###### Clovis WWTP Reuse Facility CITY OF CLOVIS | CLOVIS, CA

<!-- ¶2 -->
The 3.1-mgd, $37-million reuse facility was implemented to fulfill the city’s growing population and provide highly treated recycled water. We saved the city more than $100,000 through value engineering.

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **_Detailed Description of the Work of the Proponent on This Project_**

<!-- ¶4 -->
Jacobs provided facilities planning and engineering, design, construction, obtaining governmental approvals, permitting, acceptance testing, startup, O&M, and warranty for the WWTP reuse facility.

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### **RELEVANT EXPERIENCE**

<!-- ¶6 -->
The selected treatment included both MBR as well as Siemens’ Cannibal™ process. MBR is delivering the highest quality water and provides exceptional effluent quality, creates the least environmental impact by use of a small footprint, significantly reduces costs, and offers simplified expansion for future needs. In addition to MBR, Siemens’ Cannibal solids reduction process has been incorporated into the new facility. The Cannibal process greatly reduces solids production with consequent capital and operating cost savings. It virtually eliminates biosolids production, which eliminates the need for expensive dewatering and digestion equipment and associated costs.

<!-- ¶7 -->
###### **_Detailed description of the facilities operated_**

<!-- ¶8 -->
The Clovis Sewage Treatment/Water Reuse Facility (Clovis ST/WRF) was built in anticipation of the future needs of the City of Clovis community. Prior to its construction, the City of Clovis’s sewage was conveyed to the Fresno-Clovis Regional Wastewater Treatment Plant.

<!-- ¶9 -->
The Clovis ST/WRF is a California Title 22 Quality Treatment Facility with effluent suitable for discharge into the environment. The facility is a membrane bioreactor (MBR) system, using the Siemens Cannibal<sup>TM</sup> solids reduction process.
