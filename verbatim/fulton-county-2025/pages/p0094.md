###### **SECURITY PLAN**

<!-- ¶2 -->
We are committed to providing a safe and secure work environment for our employees, clients, and the community while protecting critical infrastructure and public assets. In full compliance with Article 50.8 of the Contract and all requirements outlined in Exhibit E and Exhibit 2, we will develop and implement a comprehensive, site-specific Security Plan within 30 days of contract start, with updates provided annually.

<!-- ¶3 -->
As the Operator, JC Solutions will assume full responsibility for the security of all wastewater treatment facilities and pump stations in the North Fulton Service Area. This includes enforcing security protocols without exception, maintaining secure perimeter fencing and gated entry points, and ensuring all doors, access panels, and entryways remain locked unless actively monitored. All personnel and visitors will be required to present valid identification and documentation authorizing access, with entry logs maintained at each facility.

<!-- ¶4 -->
Our Security Plan will address the following components:

<!-- ¶5 -->
- µ<sup>**Access Control and Surveillance.**Continuous oversight of all entry points and secure</sup> zones using surveillance cameras, intrusion detection systems, and access badge or code-based entry, as required. Security systems will be tested and maintained regularly.

<!-- ¶6 -->
- µ<sup>**Security Awareness and Training.**All staff will receive security training during</sup> onboarding, with periodic refresher courses conducted throughout the contract period.

<!-- ¶7 -->
- µ<sup>**Incident Reporting and Response.**All incidents involving theft, vandalism,</sup> unauthorized access, property damage, or threats will be immediately reported, investigated, and documented in accordance with contract protocols. The County’s personnel will be notified as required under Article 50.8.

<!-- ¶8 -->
- µ<sup>**Annual Security Assessments.**The Project Manager will lead annual physical security</sup> and asset protection assessments to identify risks, ensure system readiness, and continuously improve site resilience.

<!-- ¶9 -->
- µ<sup>**Zero-Tolerance Policy Enforcement.**We strictly enforces policies related to workplace</sup> violence, illegal drug or alcohol use, and any behavior threatening employee or community safety.

<!-- ¶10 -->
- µ<sup>**Information Protection.**SCADA/IT system access and data protection protocols will be</sup> implemented in accordance with the County’s and our standards, ensuring alignment with cybersecurity policies and infrastructure protections.

<!-- ¶11 -->
- µ<sup>**Construction Coordination.**Security will be maintained during capital improvement</sup> or construction activities, with access controls coordinated between JC Solutions, the County’s staff, and third-party contractors.

###### SITE SECURITY ASSESSMENT/ EVALUATION AND RECOMMENDATIONS

<!-- ¶15 -->
We are committed to protecting the critical infrastructure under our care by developing a robust, proactive security framework that reduces vulnerabilities and enhances resiliency. Our Site Security Assessment (SSA) methodology provides a comprehensive review of existing physical security measures across all vertical assets, identifies gaps or potential risks, and defines targeted mitigation

<!-- ¶16 -->
strategies. This proactive approach supports our larger goal of minimizing exposure to security threats which are growing in frequency and complexity across water and wastewater utilities nationwide while maintaining continuity of operations and employee safety. A well-rounded utility security program is built on three important components: **security monitoring, governance, and technology.** These work in tandem to support daily operations, detect threats in real time, and ensure effective oversight and accountability.
