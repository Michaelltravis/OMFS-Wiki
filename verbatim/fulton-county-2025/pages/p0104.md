<!-- ¶1 -->
- µ<sup>**Monthly Discharge Monitoring Report.**We’ll confirm the timely</sup> submission of the Georgia EPD Discharge Monitoring Report each month, with immediate communication of any permit violations or regulatory concerns, demonstrating our commitment to transparency and regulatory compliance.

<!-- ¶2 -->
- µ<sup>**Annual Report.**Our annual report will be a comprehensive review</sup> of the previous year’s operations, including capital improvements, financial performance, and plans for the upcoming year. This will be submitted by the end of the first quarter and will help guide longterm planning and investment decisions, with full transparency and partnership at the forefront.

<!-- ¶3 -->
- µ<sup>**Additional Reports.**We’ll also prepare and provide additional reports</sup> as needed, including severe weather reports, wastewater verification, industrial monitoring, inspection reports, executive summaries, and cost reviews to ensure the County has full access to all necessary information. In addition to the regular reports, we will prepare specialized reports such as when unique circumstances warrant it.

<!-- ¶4 -->
The County will have its own customized reporting dashboard, discussed earlier in subsection **_2.4 Approach to Project Execution and Administration_** and illustrated in **Exhibit 2-2** . It will track KPIs, providing the County with real-time and historical trending information at your fingertips, including maintenance activity and work order status; chemical usage; sampling, testing and compliance data; safety stats, and much more. It will give the County improved access and transparency into the day-to-day status of your facilities and complements the written reports.

<!-- ¶5 -->
Our reporting approach underscores our commitment to maintaining a transparent, collaborative partnership with the County, affirming that communication and reporting are always aligned with your needs and expectations.
