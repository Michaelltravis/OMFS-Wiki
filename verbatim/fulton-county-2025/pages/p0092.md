Exhibit 2-41. _Key Elements of JC Solutions’ Safety Program and Planning_

<!-- ¶2 -->
|**H&S WORK CONTROL PLAN**|Used to direct, conduct, and document work relative to H&S requirements. With a focus on accident prevention, the plan<br>directs how to classify the type of work and uses activity hazard analysis and pre-task planning, daily safety meetings, and<br>stop-work authority for unsafe activity afforded every associate without fear of reprisal.|
|---|---|
|**ACTIVITY HAZARD ANALYSES**|In addition to preparing SOPs for operating system equipment, the project team is required to perform an activity hazard analyses for all high-hazard tasks.|
|**PRE-TASK PLANS (PTP)**|Used to discuss the work to be performed and comply with the OSHA regulation, PTPs make sure hazards are identified and the controls (work practices, personal protective equipment [PPE], training, etc.) involved with the safe performance of work are applied.|
|**INSPECTIONS/BZOS**|Managers’ and supervisors’ observations, coaching, and positive reinforcement allow employees to identify and correct site physical and health hazards. This program provides a real-time opportunity for leaders to engage with associates and provide feedback on safety program implementation.|
|**PPE**|Provides PPE to employees and others as required by each task.|
|**SUBCONTRACTOR MANAGEMENT**|Manages risks associated with subcontractors’ H&S using a systematic method that includes contract language plus prequalification based on safety performance. Requires most subcontractors to submit safety documents.|
|**H&S IMPROVEMENT PLANNING**|Uses our safety audit scoring checklist and customized safety scorecard to look at parameters such as total recordable rate (TRR), at-fault motor vehicle incidents, training completion, Behavior Zero observations, and other performance data.|

<!-- ¶3 -->
|**COMPREHENSIVE, ONGOING SAFETY TRAINING AND CERTIFICATION**|Assessment of safety training needs for each worker and project site starting at transition and then annually.<br>Real-time data to track and verify that each employee has received the required training using our corporate employee learning and tracking program.<br>100+ online safety courses and classroom or practical hands-on safety training programs, some of which are designed to meet specific OSHA regulations (Example training topics: hazard communication, permit required confined space entry, bloodborne pathogens, lead, benzene, lockout/tagout, working from heights, PPE, and electrical safety).<br>Prior to performing high-risk tasks, our safety team conducts classroom/practical training (NFPA 70E, permit required confined space, fall protection, scaffold, etc.).|
