###### **UTILIZATION PLAN**

<!-- ¶2 -->
JC Solutions is committed to providing opportunities designed to have a positive impact on the growth and development of Minority, Female, Small, Disadvantaged, and Veteran Owned Businesses throughout the country.

<!-- ¶3 -->
Over the last 50 years in the metro Atlanta market, Jacobs has consistently looked to develop strong, long-standing working relationships and teaming arrangements with the Minority and Female Business Enterprises (MFBE) community. We have successfully teamed with a majority of these firms over the years, earning a reputation for being a responsive and helpful teaming partner, often strengthening and expanding their capabilities to enhance their technical expertise. We invite them to be a part of the overall project planning and execution process for them to gain vital experience in the implementation of large complex projects. Our reputation is such that MFBE firms often team with us exclusively. In addition, we often not only meet clients’ MFBE goals, but exceed them.

###### **MINORITY AND FEMALE BUSINESS ENTERPRISE GOOD FAITH EFFORTS**

<!-- ¶5 -->
In order to maximize MFBE participation on our projects including the Operation & Maintenance Services for Wastewater Facilities & Pump Stations in the North Fulton Service Area, we have a robust program focused on recruiting firms that are Fulton County certified and meet our qualifications. While goals are established for most projects, we go above and beyond to reach firms that can be of value to JC Solutions and the County, while giving them the opportunity to grow. We are deeply committed to maximizing the participation of MFBE firms on our team for the Operation & Maintenance Services for Wastewater Facilities & Pump Stations in the North Fulton Service Area.
