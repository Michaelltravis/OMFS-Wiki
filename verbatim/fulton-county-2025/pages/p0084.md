4. Lifecycle Delivery and Maintenance Strategy - JC Solutions will manage the entire asset lifecycle—from acquisition to disposal—through a structured maintenance strategy and Long-term Maintenance Plan that combines PdM and PM. Our preventive maintenance optimization (PMO) and Level 3 PdM programs will shift the focus from reactive maintenance to proactive asset management. Routine maintenance inspections will allow operations staff to monitor equipment daily, identifying early signs of failure. PdM will be enhanced using advanced diagnostics such as ultrasound testing, vibration analysis, and motor signature assessments, helping to extend equipment life and reduce downtime. All PdM and PM tasks will be completed by JC Solutions’ in-house resources—either by the onsite team or with support from the regional maintenance team and technical support resources.

6. Organization and People - Building a strong maintenance culture is critical to ensuring the long-term success of an asset management program. JC Solutions will align personnel structures and training programs with North Fulton’s asset management goals. Asset Management Lead, Graham Knowles, and his team of industry experts, will provide training to equip staff with best practices in PdM, asset lifecycle planning, and data-driven decision-making. Maintenance Manager, Nathan Dahl, will oversee the day-to-day implementation of PdM and PM maintenance strategies and associated work orders. PdM and PM tasks requiring skills beyond those of the onsite team will be supported by Jacobs’ regional asset management, CMMS, and maintenance technology experts, including Maintenance Lead, Michael Idzior, CMRT, CRL and his team.
