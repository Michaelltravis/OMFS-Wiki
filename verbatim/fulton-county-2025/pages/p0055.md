<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **_Wastewater Operator Certification and Renewal Training_**

<!-- ¶4 -->
Jacobs has developed its own wastewater operations training content through our Institute of Learning platform, covering a wide range of topics but generally aligned to the national Association of Boards of Certification (ABC) “Need to Know” set of standards per certification grade level. These classes were developed with the combined expertise of our operators and engineers, and provided through classroom, field, and online teaching methods. Online quizzes are used to allow students to demonstrate their learning, specific for each subject area. The intent is to improve the skills and competency of our operations staff and support their career growth. Providing training for operators developed by operators is an effective way to accomplish this.

###### **_Maintenance Training_**

<!-- ¶6 -->
JC Solutions’ approach to maintenance skills training and predictive maintenance techniques improves uptime and reduces corrective maintenance:

<!-- ¶7 -->
- µ<sup>**Certified Maintenance Reliability Technician (CMRT)**exam opportunities at least once a year</sup> for all maintenance staff.

<!-- ¶8 -->
- µ<sup>**Hands-on, in-person instruction for vibration analysis, alignment, oil analysis, CMMS**</sup> **best practices, ultrasound, and troubleshooting.** This is taught by our regional maintenance experts in each subject area.

<!-- ¶9 -->
- µ<sup>**Skills training**—from basic mechanic-in-training to advanced predictive maintenance</sup> training—supports continuous improvement of staff skills and maintenance processes.

<!-- ¶10 -->
Our commitment to these programs has resulted in Jacobs employing more CMRT-certified maintenance staff than any other water/wastewater service provider in the U.S.

###### **_Workforce Development and Staffing Strategy_**

<!-- ¶12 -->
JC Solutions is committed to building and sustaining a high-performing operations team at the County’s facilities. Through ongoing professional development, workforce partnerships, and a dedicated staffing strategy, we ensure operators are prepared, empowered, and supported at every stage of their careers.

###### **_Workforce Development and Professional Growth_**

<!-- ¶14 -->
CERM brings deep expertise in workforce development, with a strong focus on cultivating the next generation of water utility professionals. **The firm co-developed Georgia Piedmont Technical College’s Water Utility Apprenticeship program and Atlanta Technical College’s Civil Engineering Technologist program—both designed to meet the evolving needs of the water sector.**
