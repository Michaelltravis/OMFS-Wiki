#### **Section 10**

<!-- ¶2 -->
The County needs a strong, stable O&M partner with the financial resources and proven business resiliency and management to assure service continuity to its customers. At Jacobs, consistent client satisfaction has fueled our growth over the last 75 years and over 40 years in our O&M business unit. With $12 billion in annual revenue and a robust backlog, Jacobs’ historical growth, delivery excellence, and solid financial history have provided us the strength to meet financial requirements of the largest and most complex programs in the world.

<!-- ¶3 -->
Our award-winning O&M business is a critical and valued component of our full-service identity. The operations teams generate over $2.4 billion in backlog through over 2,300 employees serving hundreds of federal, municipal, and private-sector clients with a diverse array of services that ranges from providing water and wastewater operations to leading cities, such as **Tucson, San Diego, and Seattle** ; to providing facilities management services at one of the world’s busiest airports in Atlanta. **In short, we have the experience and resources to improve Fulton County’s operations.**

<!-- ¶4 -->
Jacobs’ corporate stability provides the County with confidence in knowing that our firm’s guarantor will be unchanged throughout the term of this partnership and it is a single-entity guarantor versus a joint venture organization.

<!-- ¶5 -->
Jacobs has generated over $32 billion in revenue in the last three years, as demonstrated in our 10-K documents. We possess a strong financial position and credit rating. Our stable revenue and strong backlog, continuing profitability, and overall financial resources demonstrate our financial strength to the County. Our revenue has been steady over our history, based on successful project delivery, sound risk management strategies, and exceptional client satisfaction. We have strong retained earnings and a reasonable debt-to-capital ratio that leads to financial stability, reflected in our good credit ratings and banking relationships that demonstrate our financial flexibility. Our robust credit rating of 5A3 and assessment of “Stable Condition” and “LowModerate Business Risk” is demonstrated in the Dun & Bradstreet Credit Report for Jacobs.

CERM was founded in 1995 as a minority-owned engineering, environmental, and program management consulting firm. From its humble beginnings in a rented space at the South DeKalb Business Incubator, CERM has grown into an award-winning organization with over 100 professionals serving the southeastern US.

<!-- ¶7 -->
Since its inception, CERM has provided customized solutions in environmental, water resources, and facilities sectors, as well as federal and transportation projects. The firm’s interdisciplinary approach combines management, design, engineering, and construction expertise to address clients’ specific needs. CERM’s commitment to workforce and economic development, along with its focus on equity, inclusion, and sustainability, has been integral to its growth and success over the past three decades.
