Many facilities and utilities do not have adequate backup systems in place. During our brief review of the core control systems for the wastewater facilities, it is unclear if the utility has an offsite backup system. We will implement an improved backup strategy following the 3-2-1-1 rule, including offsite and immutable cloud storage backups. This approach minimizes downtime and data loss in the unfortunate event of server failure or malicious attack.

<!-- ¶6 -->
A robust and thorough disaster recovery plan depends on complete and validated backups of all systems. Establishing a baseline backup of all systems will be the Operational Technology (OT) team’s highest priority in the first 90 Days. These backups will primarily be stored onsite, and immutable encrypted copies will be made offsite to ensure availability during a disaster. Combined with a robust emergency response plan, this will provide operational continuity even through the worst natural or man-made disasters.

<!-- ¶7 -->
We will also provide secure integration of SCADA, LIMS, CMMS, GIS, and other critical systems. Data will be stored and shared via encrypted, access-controlled systems that are fully auditable and aligned with both County IT protocols and Article 13 confidentiality requirements .

<!-- ¶8 -->
This cybersecurity framework, combined with Jacobs’ history of successfully defending client assets from sophisticated attacks, ensures secure and uninterrupted service delivery for the County’s wastewater facilities and pump stations.
