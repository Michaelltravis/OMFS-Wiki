###### **ASSET MANAGEMENT AND MAINTENANCE**

<!-- ¶2 -->
JC Solutions recognizes that the County seeks a proactive partner to maintain its facilities in a manner that ensures reliability, extends asset longevity, and protects its infrastructure investments. We are committed to preserving the County’s capital investments—especially with the new facilities at Big Creek coming online soon. We will develop an Asset Management System (AMS), initiating development at Transition, and it will include the required Longterm Maintenance Plan and Field Performance Testing Plan, developed in an integrated process to maximize the value of these plans. Our AMS will provide a comprehensive asset management strategy that applies preventive maintenance (PM) and predictive maintenance (PdM) with data-driven decision-making.
