###### **_Proven Approach to Supporting Existing Employees and Transition Success_**

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### JC SOLUTIONS’ PROVEN STAFF TRANSITION PROCESS ADDRESSES EMPLOYEE CONCERNS AND ENSURES SMOOTH TRANSFER OF WORKFORCE

<!-- ¶3 -->
In the first days of the transition, we will refine job descriptions based on a finalized needs assessment. A revised skillset list will outline the ideal experience and skills for each role, helping evaluate current employees, identify their best-fit positions, and highlight development or training opportunities to maximize potential.

<!-- ¶4 -->
###### **_Care and Sensitivity Toward Transitioning Employees_**

<!-- ¶5 -->
JC Solutions also understands the sensitivity of employees regarding transition and change and will provide inviting and welcoming informational workshops for employees and their families to better communicate who we are, how we are organized, what to expect in the coming weeks and months, our benefits package, and the transition processes. **JC Solutions’ unique transition process is patterned around listening to employees' concerns with a specific focus on people. We routinely and successfully manage such transitions with care and will apply all our experience, qualified human resources, labor relations staff, and lessons learned to ensure it is smooth and seamless.**

###### **_Compensation Package_**

<!-- ¶7 -->
JC Solutions will offer employment to all interested and qualified current employees who would like to continue working at the County’s facilities. We recognize the importance of competitive wages and

<!-- ¶8 -->
- 1<sup>**Retain and Recruit Local Talent:**Our top priority is to retain the majority of the existing local team</sup> at the North Fulton facilities. We will begin by thoroughly assessing the current staff to identify key talent and areas of expertise. By offering these individuals opportunities for continued employment and career growth with JC Solutions, we aim to ensure continuity and stability. Alongside this retention effort, we will also implement a targeted recruiting process to fill any remaining gaps. This involves leveraging local job boards, professional networks, and outreach initiatives to ensure we attract qualified candidates who align with the County’s needs and JC Solutions’ values.

<!-- ¶9 -->
- 2<sup>**Host Introduction Workshops:**After identifying the key members of the existing local team and</sup> potential new hires, we will host introduction workshops. These workshops will provide an opportunity for the local team and new recruits to learn about JC Solutions’ culture, values, and operational expectations. By involving existing staff in these workshops, we foster a sense of inclusion, community, and collaboration from the outset. This ensures that everyone, both retained employees and new hires, are aligned with the project’s goals.

<!-- ¶10 -->
- 3<sup>**Assess and Interview Remaining Candidates:**We will conduct structured assessments and</sup> interviews for any remaining open positions or for employees interested in expanding their roles. This process allows us to evaluate each candidate’s technical skills, cultural fit, and alignment with the project’s long-term goals. By doing so, we ensure that those who join or remain on the team are not only qualified but also motivated to contribute to the ongoing success of the County and JC Solutions.

<!-- ¶11 -->
- 4<sup>**Make Employment Offers:**For both the retained local staff and any new recruits, we will extend</sup> competitive employment offers that reflect their value to the organization. These offers will incorporate competitive salaries, comprehensive benefits, and opportunities for growth. For the existing staff, special attention will be given to recognizing their experience and contributions, which will support their smooth transition to JC Solutions. This approach demonstrates our commitment to retaining talent and maintaining continuity.

<!-- ¶12 -->
- 5<sup>**Implement Contract Startup and Operation:**Once employment offers are accepted, we will initiate</sup> the contract startup and operational processes. This step involves finalizing paperwork, clarifying roles and responsibilities, and ensuring compliance with all regulatory and contractual requirements. By retaining local staff who are already familiar with the County’s operations, we will streamline the startup phase, ensuring minimal disruption and a smooth transition.

<!-- ¶13 -->
- 6<sup>**Initiate Continuous Training and Development Program:**To ensure ongoing success, we will</sup> implement a continuous training and development program, designed to support both retained and newly hired staff. This program will include tailored training for specific job functions, mentorship opportunities, and ongoing professional development. By fostering a culture of learning and growth, we empower the team to adapt to new challenges, remain engaged, and continually improve performance.
