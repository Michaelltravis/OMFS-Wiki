###### **ENERGY MANAGEMENT STRATEGY**

<!-- ¶7 -->
JC Solutions will develop a comprehensive Energy Management Strategy for the North Fulton wastewater facilities and associated pump stations. Our approach goes beyond traditional energy efficiency measures to identify significant cost-saving opportunities through strategic rate structure optimization, demand management, and innovative operational strategies.

<!-- ¶8 -->
###### **_Comprehensive Rate Structure Analysis_**

<!-- ¶9 -->
Our team will conduct a detailed review of current electricity rate structures for all three treatment plants and 28 pump stations within the Fulton County system. We will model electric utility costs across all relevant Georgia Power rate structures to identify least-cost options for each facility, taking into account the unique operational characteristics of each site. By analyzing historical consumption patterns, we can determine significant opportunities for rate optimization and identify facilities where alternative rate classifications could yield immediate savings without capital investment.

<!-- ¶10 -->
###### **_Strategic Demand Management_**

<!-- ¶11 -->
JC Solutions will develop facility-specific strategies to reduce peak demand charges across the North Fulton facilities. Our approach includes modeling backup generator deployment for strategic peak shaving, with careful consideration of emissions thresholds and noise constraints to ensure regulatory compliance and community harmony. We will evaluate battery energy storage systems (BESS) for peak demand reduction through detailed financial feasibility analysis that considers current rates and future projections. For viable energy asset deployments, we will create automated control strategies that optimize battery charging/discharging cycles aligned with rate structures and operational needs.

<!-- ¶12 -->
###### **_Georgia Power Partnership_**

<!-- ¶13 -->
Leveraging our established relationship with Georgia Power, JC Solutions can negotiate preferred rate classifications for the North Fulton facilities to maximize cost savings. Our team will coordinate directly with utility representatives to identify interconnection requirements for any proposed energy assets, ensuring smooth implementation of recommended solutions.
