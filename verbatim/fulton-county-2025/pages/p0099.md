###### **_Resources and Regional Response_**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Our extensive national and regional network including more than 4,000 O&M and engineering professionals provides scalable emergency support. Our local Atlanta-based team and South Region staff are trained and equipped for rapid deployment to handle:

<!-- ¶3 -->
- Pump and pipe failures

<!-- ¶4 -->
- SCADA outages

<!-- ¶5 -->
- Generator and backup power needs

<!-- ¶6 -->
- Emergency procurement and logistics

<!-- ¶7 -->
- Technical troubleshooting and compliance guidance

<!-- ¶8 -->
Our FEMA experience and certified teams have supported post-disaster recovery efforts across the U.S., including responses to Hurricanes Irma, Harvey, Maria, and major regional flooding events. We apply these lessons learned to tailor resilient emergency plans for the County.

<!-- ¶9 -->
_When super storm Sandy hit the New York harbor and the magnitude of the disaster became apparent, you were the first person that I reached out to for help. Fortunately, for the North Hudson Sewerage Authority [NHSA], Jacobs, our partner of 22 years at the time, came to our assistance and in 24 hours provided an expert team to address the damage caused by the disastrous storm surge. Our main Sewage Treatment Plant in Hoboken was pumped out, some 24 pumps were removed, rebuilt and replaced and temporary electrical control systems constructed to enable primary treatment 48 hours later with full secondary treatment restored within 5 days of the system failure. It was teamwork at its best.”_

<!-- ¶10 -->
_– Fredric J. Pocci, PE_<br>_North Hudson Sewerage Authority Engineer_
