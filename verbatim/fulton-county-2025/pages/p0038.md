AquaDNA: Smart Technology for System Optimization<br>While the collections system beyond the pump stations is not part of the<br>scope of services for this contract, we recognize that overflows present<br>significant management challenges that can lead to compliance issues and<br>operational inefficiencies, especially during wet weather events. To address<br>these challenges, we’re including Jacobs’ Palantir-driven AquaDNA tools as<br>part of our comprehensive solution for North Fulton facilities.<br>AquaDNA uses smart technology and sensors for wastewater collection<br>systems to understand performance in real time, alert operators to potential<br>incidents, and help avoid spills, floods, overflows, and backups that could<br>harm customers and the environment. While wet weather is primarily<br>managed through the collection system—with wet weather management<br>tanks only becoming available at Big Creek after construction completion—<br>AquaDNA offers an immediate solution by visualizing pump station<br>operations, monitoring critical sewer levels, and providing predictive alarms.<br>Our offering integrates near real-time data from multiple County systems<br>onto the AquaDNA platform, powered by Palantir’s Foundry. These integrated<br>data sources include pump station SCADA information, available rain<br>gauge data, level sensors, flow meters, and public weather forecasts. After<br>integration, both the onsite JC Solutions team and County personnel will<br>receive customized software access to this powerful monitoring system.<br>Enhanced Pump Station Management with AquaDNA “Deragger”<br>As part of our solution, we propose installing automatic deragging devices<br>at four of the most vulnerable wastewater pump stations, one in each<br>quadrant, to proactively detect and address potential ragging issues and<br>help mitigate sewer overflows in advance. Our AquaDNA “Deragger” is an<br>exclusive partnership with a sewage pump deragging vendor that implements<br>pump reversals upon detection of flow or power changes through dynamic<br>waveform monitoring—a unique approach in the industry.<br>This technology has been successfully implemented at several sites, including<br>our newest project in Wilmington, DE. With nearly 100 installations in the U.S.<br>and a decade of operating experience, this is a proven approach that delivers<br>dramatic results.
