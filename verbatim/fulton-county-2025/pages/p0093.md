###### **_Continuous Improvement and Learning_**

<!-- ¶9 -->
To keep complacency at bay, Jacobs constantly evolves its safety program by **integrating real-time data, continuous training, and performance tracking**. Our **safety scorecard**, for example, measures both leading indicators (such as proactive safety behaviors) and lagging indicators (such as incident rates), providing our leadership team with insights into emerging trends and opportunities for improvement. This allows us to address potential safety issues before they result in incidents, promoting ongoing vigilance and improvement across the team.
