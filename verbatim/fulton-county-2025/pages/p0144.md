###### Nathan Callison, CRL, CMRT | Johns Creek Plant Manager (continued)

<!-- ¶2 -->
Wastewater Plant Operator | WWTP Operations, City of Midland | Midland, MI. Responsible for monitoring, maintenance, and operation of the wastewater plant, collection system, pump stations, and gas to energy building. Duties included sample collection, laboratory testing, process adjustments, QA/QC analysis, equipment maintenance, data entry, biosolids management, collection system maintenance, chemical preparation, and ensuring effluent compliance with Department of Environmental Quality (DEQ) permitted limitations. Operated specialized equipment including belt filter press, vactor and flusher trucks, and handled 2000 lb. chlorine cylinders. Responded to citizen concerns and often worked independently as the sole on-duty personnel.

<!-- ¶3 -->
Lead O&M Technician | Wastewater Facilities Operations, Village of Suttons Bay and Village of Northport | Traverse City, MI. Operated two municipal wastewater facilities, three lift stations, and approximately 120 residential grinder pump systems. Duties included sample collection, process lab testing, data recording, equipment maintenance, safety audits, stormwater assessments, chemical preparation, residential callouts, and system troubleshooting. Operated the Suttons Bay Class B Extended Aeration Treatment Plant (up to 0.300-mgd) and the Northport WWTP Moving Bed Biofilm Reactor (MBBR) plant (0.04-0.100-mgd). Responsible for process reporting, biosolids management, permit sampling, discharge monitoring reports, and process control. Attended village council meetings, performed maintenance on various equipment, managed inventory, and handled on-call duties. Conducted quarterly monitoring well sampling and managed sustainability initiatives for northern Michigan projects.

<!-- ¶4 -->
Plumber, Foreman, HVAC, and Boiler Tech | Installation and Maintenance of Plumbing and HVAC, Dales Plumbing and Heating | Bay City, MI. Installed and maintained various plumbing and HVAC systems including sanitary sewers, potable water systems, venting systems, irrigation piping, backflow preventers, furnaces, boilers, in-floor radiant heat tubing, air conditioning systems, and gas piping. Supervised plumbers and HVAC techs, read blueprints, and was responsible for project design. Managed material ordering, employee and tool transportation, and customer billing and payment collection.
