###### **Data Management**

<!-- ¶3 -->
Data collection will be driven by the Sampling Plan and will be collected through lab analysis or online instrumentation and entered manually or directly from SCADA. We’ll install and use Hach WIMS to allow for the use of Jacobs’ process control system (PCS). We utilize a Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS) in conjunction with HACH WIMS to centralize data collection, storage, and analysis. This system offers real-time access, customizable reporting and trend analysis, which helps the County monitor water quality metrics seamlessly.

<!-- ¶4 -->
Direct access to the data management software will allow the North Fulton facilities to “mine” the database for specific data. Regular reports will provide the County with a quick summary of how the plant and individual unit processes are performing. Exporting data into our PCS will allow our regional support staff to analyze data and trends to assist the onsite operations staff in real-time decisions and to drive optimization.

<!-- ¶5 -->
**Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)**

<!-- ¶6 -->
SOPs are developed to ensure safe work practices and consistent operations of equipment and that systems are implemented and followed by O&M staff. SOPs typically contain precautionary safety steps, followed by startup, shutdown, and routine monitoring of the equipment, as well as the detailed information needed to complete the work properly, safely, and efficiently.

<!-- ¶7 -->
SOPs provide consistent and standard procedures and ensure staff safety, operations performance, and equipment reliability. SOP development of SOPs is an ongoing process, and the current SOPs in place at the North Fulton facilities will be reviewed and updated as needed. Critical SOPs will be developed first, and as new SOPs are required.

<!-- ¶8 -->
Daily round sheets record readings, observations, and preventive maintenance tasks that typically identify potential upsets or problems with equipment before they arise. Round sheets are printed out daily **Operator** through the CMMS and completed by the operator at least daily. **Round Sheets** Unusual observations are documented, and the data is uploaded into the CMMS. Unusual observations or readings will be reported immediately to the Operations and Compliance Manager so appropriate action can be taken in a timely manner.

<!-- ¶9 -->
One of the most effective ways to maintain control of the treatment process and operational equipment is to perform and document daily rounds on the treatment process. This allows the operator to make any necessary process adjustments or create maintenance work orders to proactively correct a maintenance item. Operator round sheets also identify general housekeeping actions for keeping the facility safe and clean.

<!-- ¶10 -->
A monthly test of peak membrane capacity will be performed on each membrane train. Success will be demonstrated by operation at design **Peak Flow Tests** peak flow for one hour without the train switching to transmembrane pressure control.

<!-- ¶11 -->
The County will know that its membranes are ready to handle design peak flows.
