###### **_Environmental Management Plan_**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Our commitment to environmental management is fully defined in our PlanBeyond Environmental Management System, which addresses the management and reduction of environmental impacts from our company’s operations. However, most significant environmental impacts and opportunities arise from the projects we deliver for our clients, which will be addressed in our Environmental Management Plan, which conforms to ISO 14001.

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **_Communication and Reporting Transparency_**

<!-- ¶4 -->
Transparency in reporting is central to our commitment to open communication. We’ll maintain clear and thorough records of all operations, verifying compliance with regulatory standards and full visibility for the County. We summarize the full breadth of reports and documentation we will provide the County later in this section under **_Communications and Reporting_** . As part of our approach to regulatory compliance reporting, we will specifically provide timely submission of the **Georgia EPD Discharge Monitoring Report** through NetDMR each month, with immediate communication of any permit violations or regulatory concerns, demonstrating our commitment to transparency and regulatory compliance.

###### **LABORATORY MANAGEMENT AND SAMPLING PLAN**

<!-- ¶6 -->
###### **_Overview and Compliance Strategy_**

<!-- ¶7 -->
Our Sampling and Laboratory Testing Plan is designed to ensure that operations in the North Fulton Service Area meet or exceed all applicable regulatory requirements while delivering highly defensible, timely, and transparent data. The program is rooted in practices successfully implemented on similar projects, including Spokane County, WA WWTP, Clovis, CA WWTP, and Traverse City, MI WWTP, and integrates EPA-compliant sampling protocols, rigorous QA/QC procedures, and ongoing training programs.

<!-- ¶8 -->
Jacobs operates seven certified laboratories under the national TNI standard and is wellversed in the specific expectations of Georgia EPD and the County. Our plan ensures full compliance with NPDES permits, reuse permits, stormwater and SPCC requirements, sludge disposal regulations, and state and federal rules under the Clean Water Act. In alignment with Georgia EPD’s zero-tolerance policy for Sanitary Sewer Overflows (SSOs), our monitoring program includes controls to detect early warning signs and drive rapid corrective action .
