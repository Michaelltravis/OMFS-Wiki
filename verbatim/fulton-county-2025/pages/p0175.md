###### **_Accomplishment in Terms of Cost Savings, Capital, or Other Improvements Implemented_**

<!-- ¶2 -->
We worked on two plant upgrades, doubling the plant’s biochemical oxygen demand treatment capability. The first, completed in 1999 for $1.5 million, improved the plant’s biological nutrient removal (BNR) capability to meet new regulations related to nitrification. The second upgrade, valued at $31 million and completed in 2004, installed the newest and largest MBR in North America. The plant can handle a peak flow of 17-mgd, making it capable of processing the largest instantaneous flow with MBR technology in the world. The project also added a new digester and sludge dewatering and storage facilities.

<!-- ¶3 -->
In 2022, Jacobs prepared a Michigan Public Service Commission Grant Application on behalf of the City of Traverse. The City was awarded over $1.6 million in 2023 to construct rooftop solar and a battery energy storage system, producing 10% of annual energy usage. This is currently under construction.

<!-- ¶4 -->
After the upgrade to a membrane bioreactor MBR system, Jacobs’ staff embarked on an optimization effort spanning several years that sought out and addressed opportunities to reduce energy and chemical consumption. Staff worked to reduce electrical consumption by over 30%. These savings, along with similar savings in chemicals and natural gas consumption, allowed Jacobs to lower the fee to the city over a 5-year period.

<!-- ¶5 -->
Our technology solution and DBO capability brought the wastewater facility into compliance and met the public’s demand for higher-quality effluent to help enhance the local environment while providing best overall value to the City.
