###### **_Plant Tours_**

<!-- ¶9 -->
Inviting people into the facility can broaden engagement efforts. Open houses and tours can be planned around specific events/celebrations such as Clean Water Week, Public Works Week, Earth Day, or other related events. Getting creative and linking them to something less obvious such as a “spooky tour” on Halloween or a spring tour that celebrates new vegetation at the facility is a fun way to engage people with the process.

<!-- ¶10 -->
Regardless of the timing or even if they are regular events, facility tours should be planned and organized to provide a highlight of the operations and connect them to people’s everyday lives. Print and digital maps highlighting key areas, interactive or hands on displays that explain “hidden” processes (e.g. membranes), and well-versed, personable guides go a long a way to creating a memorable experience. Our team would assess current practices for tours and then based on experience

<!-- ¶11 -->
supporting other facilities and insights from industry peers would develop a specific tour plan for each facility, help select the best guides, and support training as needed.

###### **_Media Relations Program_**

<!-- ¶20 -->
Our media relations strategy emphasizes proactive, accurate, and responsible communication. Our team can create print and digital content starting with a basic media kit for each facility covering the history, basics of operations, role in environmental sustainability and regional water management, and the communities it serves. This will serve as the foundation for media outreach and help ensure consistency across north Fulton communities. We will look for positive stories about staff, community partnerships, special events, behind-the-scene tours, and more to craft tailored media releases that generate coverage and can be amplified through social media.

<!-- ¶21 -->
Our focus would be on hyperlocal outlets, particularly the Appen Media Group, which has a robust multi-media presence in the service area of all facilities and presents a variety of opportunities to share stories. These include the newspapers Alpharetta-Roswell Herald, Johns Creek Herald, and Milton Herald; online news hub AppenMedia.com; social media channels with 50,000 followers/subscribers, and the Appen Podcast Network.

<!-- ¶22 -->
Additional outlets include North Fulton Neighbor, North Fulton News (AJC), GPB Radio and WABE (public broadcasting). Atlanta-based print and broadcast media would also be targeted but the chance of success is greater with local outlets.
