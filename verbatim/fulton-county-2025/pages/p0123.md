Additionally, we strive to maintain competitive wages and benefits, recognizing it is a critical aspect of employee retention. **_Train to Retain._** By providing numerous internal opportunities, highlighted

<!-- ¶2 -->
in **Exhibit 2-48** for our O&M staff to grow in their jobs and advance their careers, we see many of our staff remain long-term employees with JC Solutions. This approach benefits clients like the County by providing a stable workforce and elevating existing staff’s skills and quality.

<!-- ¶3 -->
|**Technical**<br>**Training and**<br>**Certifications**|Staff keep up to date with our online electronic bookshelf of training materials and in-person training ranging from operations,<br>maintenance, safety, compliance and more to leadership development such as project management fundamentals, leading<br>people, and persuasive public speaking.|
|---|---|
|**Future**<br>**Leadership**<br>**Development**|Our internal two-year training program focuses on project manager fundamentals, management, and leadership skills plus<br>mentoring and networking opportunities.|
|**Global Future**<br>**Talent Team**|By partnering with public and private universities, community colleges, and technical schools, our 20+ talent development staff<br>manage strategies to attract and select graduates, interns, co-ops, and apprentices with positions that grow into full time upon<br>graduation.|
|**Project**<br>**Manager Intern**<br>**Program**|This rotational program aims to give high-potential staff a three-month immersion on two different O&M project sites. They<br>collaborate and build relationships with senior project managers while developing project management skills to take on new<br>project management duties or assignments. After this six-month endeavor, participants should have a breadth of experience<br>across our primary service delivery areas and could provide opportunities to take on new project management duties or<br>assignments—helping JC Solutions build project manager bench strength to support growth.|
|**Leadership**<br>**Team Mentor**<br>**Program**|A selected group of 35 staff are paired with our global leaders for a year-long leadership training program focusing on a case<br>study challenge that’s presented at a capstone event.|
|**Maintenance**<br>**Excellence**<br>**Initiative**|A long-term strategy to significantly reduce maintenance costs and improve safety, reliability, and regulatory compliance, this<br>program partners our leaders with staff to implement successful reliability-based strategies that improve our projects’ operations<br>as well as enhance our high-performance culture.|
|**Cross-training**|JC Solutions will equip employees with cross-training at large sites as well as other local and regional projects through “walk in<br>my shoes” opportunities.|
|**Succession**<br>**Planning**|JC Solutions will prepare succession plans to help employees reach desired advancement opportunities and goals. Managers<br>use a formalized path and specific tools to further conversation on growth and development, leading to both linear and cross-<br>departmental opportunities.|
