###### ENERGY MANAGEMENT STRATEGY STUDY AND RECOMMENDATIONS

<!-- ¶5 -->
Our Energy Management Strategy provides a comprehensive analysis of all three of the WRFs and the 28 pump stations to optimize electricity costs without capital investment. Our specialized team will review complex Georgia Power rate structures, evaluate strategic demand management opportunities, negotiate preferred utility classifications, and implement operational timing adjustments that align energy consumption with the most favorable rate periods.

<!-- ¶6 -->
This strategic initiative delivers immediate financial impact with projected annual savings of $235,000-$365,000 across your wastewater system. Benefits extend beyond cost reduction to include enhanced operational visibility, improved system resilience, reduced exposure to rate volatility, and the foundation for future sustainability initiatives—transforming energy from an uncontrollable expense into a strategic asset for Fulton County.

###### **_Operational Optimization_**

<!-- ¶9 -->
Our Energy Management Strategy will include comprehensive analysis of process timing and sequencing to minimize coincident peak demands across all facilities. We will identify strategic load-shifting opportunities that take advantage of time-of-use rate differentials while maintaining operational performance. To ensure long-term success, JC Solutions will develop staff training protocols for ongoing energy management best practices that empower the North Fulton team to maintain optimization over time. Additionally, we will create recommendations for a real-time monitoring and response system capable of managing daily and hourly energy consumption in alignment with rate structures and operational priorities.
