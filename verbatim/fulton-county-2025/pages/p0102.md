###### **COMMUNICATIONS AND REPORTING**

<!-- ¶4 -->
JC Solutions is dedicated to open and transparent communication with the County, recognizing that this is key to a successful and collaborative partnership. Our communication plan aims to keep the County informed, involved, and confident in the management of the project, fostering a productive working relationship. Our commitment to transparency ensures that the County is fully aware of plant performance, maintenance activities, and any significant developments.

###### **_Communication Plan_**

<!-- ¶7 -->
Beginning at Transition, JC Solutions  will work with the County to establish seamless communication channels and proactive engagement. These will include informal daily updates to support informed decision-making and rapid responses to any emerging issues and formal weekly, monthly, and quarterly meetings to discuss performance, maintenance schedules, and service updates, as illustrated in **Exhibit 2-43** . Project Manager **Anthony Benavidez** will serve as the key point of contact, ensuring regular communication with the Fulton County Public Works Director and/or their designated representatives as well as County leadership and the public, as directed.
