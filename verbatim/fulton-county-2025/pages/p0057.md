###### **_Recruiting, Retention, and Succession Planning_**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Jacobs’ dedicated Resource Planning Group focuses on attracting, developing, and retaining top talent in the O&M industry. Over the past year, their efforts have doubled new hires, reduced turnover, and helped promote high-performing staff into new roles.

<!-- ¶3 -->
- µ<sup>**Internal Transfers:**Open positions will be offered to current qualified Jacobs and CERM O&M</sup> staff, many of whom view Fulton County as a highly desirable location. We promote internal mobility through our digital platforms and provide relocation assistance where needed.

<!-- ¶4 -->
- µ<sup>**External Recruiting:**Our full-time recruiting team leverages mobile-optimized tools and</sup> platforms targeted to O&M professionals. We also engage with veteran hiring programs such as Hiring Our Heroes and use targeted social media outreach to attract skilled talent.

<!-- ¶5 -->
- µ<sup>**Retention and Succession:**In addition to structured training and certification incentives,</sup> we offer advancement pathways, performance recognition, and leadership development programs—creating a long-term strategy to support continuity and institutional knowledge.

<!-- ¶6 -->
- Through this comprehensive approach, we will ensure North Fulton has a stable, skilled, and future-ready workforce.
