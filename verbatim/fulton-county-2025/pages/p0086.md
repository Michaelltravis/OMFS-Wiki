Implementing a best-in-class maintenance practices is essential for the efficient and reliable operation of the Big Creek WRF's Kubota MBR system. Regular maintenance ensures optimal system performance, extends equipment lifespan, and maintains high effluent quality. Key maintenance activities include:

<!-- ¶8 -->
- µ<sup>**Preliminary Treatment:**Regular inspection and</sup> maintenance of the grit and screening facilities are paramount to effective treatment and life span of the Kubota membranes. In our experience the most susceptible membranes to damage are those on the bottom half of the stack. During the turbulence of the air scour cycle any grit or screenings in the tank encounter the lower units first creating the most damage. Regular screening effectiveness tests will ensure longevity of the equipment.

<!-- ¶9 -->
###### µ<sup>**Pumps, Motors, and Blowers:**</sup>

<!-- ¶10 -->
JC Solutions will be implementing PdM activities using data and monitoring tools to assist in timely and cost-effective maintenance.

<!-- ¶11 -->
- µ<sup>**Routine Monitoring and Cleaning:**Regular inspections and cleaning</sup> prevent membrane fouling, a common challenge in MBR systems that can lead to reduced filtration efficiency and increased operational costs.

<!-- ¶12 -->
- µ<sup>**Disinfection:**Maintaining the UV</sup> systems for operation effectiveness, making sure hydraulic units are functioning properly for bulb cleaning and keeping an inventory of spare parts on hand.

<!-- ¶13 -->
- µ<sup>**Air Scouring:**Utilizing air scouring as a primary cleaning method</sup> effectively removes debris from membrane surfaces, reducing the need for chemical cleaning and simplifying maintenance procedures.

<!-- ¶14 -->
- µ<sup>**Chemical Cleaning:**Periodic chemical cleaning is necessary to address</sup> persistent fouling and maintain membrane permeability, ensuring consistent treatment performance. Noted issues with the chemical feed system for the membrane treatment. Piping appears to be leaking around multiple fittings. An investigation of the entire feed system is required.

<!-- ¶15 -->
- µ<sup>**Solids Handling:**Identify issues with</sup> screw press operations and repairs needed. Finalize project for the additional screw presses that are out of service.
