### **Section 5**

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### **ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AND MITIGATION**

<!-- ¶3 -->
_We guarantee the quality of our reporting and pledge full transparency in fulfilling the County’s objective to operate in full compliance with all environmental regulations, statutes, and permits._

<!-- ¶4 -->
Our goal for the County is perfect environmental compliance and reporting. We will strive to achieve that by tailoring our environmental compliance program to your needs, and then fully implementing it with integrity and transparency. This program is a proven blueprint for maintaining compliance with environmental, legal, and business requirements that will:

<!-- ¶5 -->
- µ<sup>Generate consistent, predictable compliance with all environmental</sup> applicable laws, regulations, and standards

<!-- ¶6 -->
- µ<sup>Alert the County’s staff to situations that require special attention and possibly</sup> require calling on assistance from regional, corporate, or other specialists to support onsite staff

<!-- ¶7 -->
- µ<sup>Generate legally defensible data and records</sup>

<!-- ¶8 -->
A blueprint is one thing. Applying it to its full potential is another! Our environmental compliance team brings decades of experience and a consistent record of success with attaining environmental compliance for clients. These professionals have expertise in all pertinent areas of environmental regulations—including the Clean Water Act, Clean Air Act, Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act, Resource Conservation and Recovery Act, as well as in biosolids management, industrial pretreatment, laboratory management, and stormwater regulations. Beyond in-depth understanding of the regulations, their strong working relationships with regulators at the federal, state, and local levels will translate into effective advocacy for the County.

<!-- ¶9 -->
We are committed to providing complete transparency in our reporting to the regulators and communities we serve, and we guarantee the accuracy of the compliance and facility-performance data we gather and report to regulators, the County, and stakeholders.
