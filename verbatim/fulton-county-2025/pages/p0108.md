###### **_Community Outreach Plan_**

<!-- ¶3 -->
Jacobs will develop and implement community relations and education programs that foster engagement while supporting and enhancing the County’s existing community engagement efforts. The approach will operate on parallel paths providing broad-based knowledge to increase community awareness of the critical role the three treatment facilities play in the County and targeting strategic opportunities to collaborate with local organizations to promote social responsibility and support underserved populations.

<!-- ¶4 -->
To develop specific community relations and education programs for the County service area, we recommend holding a workshop to gather input from staff to gain a better understanding of the various communities’ needs, how current programs are meeting them, and where adjustments or new approaches could amplify these efforts. At a minimum, the community relations plan would include an overall goal, objectives, key messages, audiences, strategies and tactics, content and collateral, distribution channels, metrics, and an implementation schedule. However, a final Community Outreach Plan will be submitted to the County within 90 days of the Commencement Date. This plan will be updated periodically to align with facility milestones, community events, and feedback received from stakeholders.

###### **_Stakeholders Directory_**

<!-- ¶6 -->
We will create and maintain a comprehensive Stakeholders Directory that includes:

<!-- ¶7 -->
- µ<sup>Homeowners associations and neighborhood groups</sup>

<!-- ¶8 -->
- µ<sup>Elected officials</sup>

<!-- ¶9 -->
- µ<sup>Local school administrators and educators</sup>

<!-- ¶10 -->
- µ<sup>Media contacts</sup>

<!-- ¶11 -->
This directory will be updated at least twice per year and serve as a primary outreach resource for the County.

<!-- ¶12 -->
As most of the adjacent neighborhoods and even those farther out have HOAs, establishing points of contact with the closest communities and maintaining a directory would facilitate the distribution of information since the neighborhoods likely have their own communications channels (Google Groups, Facebook Groups, etc.). Staff could also look for opportunities to present on the individual facility operations at regular HOA meetings, emphasize steps taken to minimize community impacts, and explain the reporting system. This information could also be shared through neighborhood websites, social channels, etc. Surveying residents on their topics of interest about the facility and how they would like to be communicated with should also be factored in the response system and proactive outreach. Being transparent and providing factual details in a calm setting will help build community goodwill when issues arise. Additionally, having staff participate in neighborhood events, cleans ups, open houses, etc. can build even more goodwill and let residents see operators as neighbors.
