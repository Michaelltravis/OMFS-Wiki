###### **_Coordinated Transition and Commissioning of New Facilities at Big Creek_**

<!-- ¶4 -->
We recognize that your facility at Big Creek is currently undergoing a construction upgrade, with ongoing work likely to continue after the awarding of a new operations contract. During the commissioning phase of the construction project, transition to a new O&M provider will require a seamless process to manage the operational aspects of the facility alongside the commissioning team with a goal to continue reliable, compliant and safe operations for the facilities, the onsite personnel and the community.

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### **APPROACH TO ACHIEVING THE COUNTY’S GOALS**

<!-- ¶6 -->
**We are all in on delivering cost-effective, reliable O&M services that help the County protect and optimize its investments** in critical wastewater infrastructure. Our approach blends deep technical expertise—especially in advanced treatment and MBR systems—with a strong commitment to compliance, transparency, and community partnership. We bring a people-first culture, proven performance, and innovative tools that drive efficiency, improve decision-making, and support long-term operational excellence. With a focus on asset stewardship, workforce development, and continuous improvement, we’re ready to be the County’s trusted partner—today and into the future. **Key elements of our approach are detailed below.**

<!-- ¶7 -->
###### **1. Meaningful partnership built on transparency, accountability, and investment in community**

<!-- ¶8 -->
Our approach to partnering with the County centers on transparency and shared accountability for operational excellence. Through a KPI dashboard (see **Exhibit 2-2)** , we will provide County’s staff with realtime access to comprehensive performance metrics across all three facilities, enabling collaborative decision-making based on accurate, current data. This transparency extends beyond digital tools to our

<!-- ¶9 -->
communication practices—we commit to regular operations meetings, proactive issue notifications, and open-door access to our management team. Meaningful partnership requires more than contractual compliance; it demands genuine collaboration in addressing operational challenges, implementing innovations, and planning for future needs. Our team will actively engage with County staff to transfer knowledge, develop solutions jointly, and celebrate shared successes. This collaborative approach creates a foundation for continuous improvement as we work together to elevate operations across all North Fulton facilities, ultimately enhancing service to the community while optimizing resource utilization and ensuring regulatory compliance with appropriate safety margins.

<!-- ¶10 -->
Providing high-quality, compliant O&M services is part of our company’s DNA—not a trend or transitional strategy subject to ownership changes or shifting priorities. As a stable, publicly traded firm—not backed by short-term venture capital interests—our commitment to the County will remain unwavering year after year. You won’t have to worry about us being sold off, reorganized, or reassessing our obligations halfway through the contract.

<!-- ¶11 -->
**At virtually all of our O&M projects, we are active members in the communities we serve.** Community involvement is a foundational element of our O&M approach because it helps build and support sustainable communities. Our staff are residents who live and work in the communities ourselves and serve as stewards of public health and safety. We recognize the importance of connecting with citizens and customers and providing the commitment that their utilities are operated by a provider who cares about their wellbeing.

<!-- ¶12 -->
We recognize that the Johns Creek Environmental Campus is highly visible to the public through tours, environmental programming, and community spaces. The plant’s appearance and performance affect the community’s impression of the facility and also reflects the onsite team’s pride in the facility. We will build on the impressive public education and community involvement program as well as build further partnerships in the community to elevate the stature of the County’s facilities and community programs. Additionally, we will establish facility appearance standards into our preventive maintenance tracking and work order system through CMMS beginning at Transition to ensure the continued reputation of the facility in the community and with other stakeholders.
