###### 2.7 **APPROACH TO PROVIDING QUALIFIED AND LICENSED PERSONNEL**

<!-- ¶3 -->
Jacobs’ Atlanta office was established in 1958 and houses 500 professional staff in water, transportation, IT, environmental permitting, and other markets. We have a long history of serving both the County and the City of Atlanta. Our JV partner, CERM, is headquartered in Atlanta and has completed numerous projects for the City. As Jacobs’ partner, CERM will focus on workforce development.

<!-- ¶4 -->
###### **LONG HISTORY OF SERVICE IN ATLANTA AND THE COUNTY**

<!-- ¶5 -->
We are proud to offer the County a proven, locally rooted team with the stability, expertise, and fresh energy to deliver world-class O&M services. Since establishing our Atlanta office in 1958, Jacobs has partnered with the County and the City of Atlanta across water and infrastructure programs, backed by 500 local professionals. Our JV partner, CERM—also Atlanta-based—brings a strong track record and will lead workforce development efforts.

<!-- ¶6 -->
###### **INTEGRATED, FULL-SERVICE O&M SERVICES TEAM**

<!-- ¶7 -->
Through this partnership, the County can expect safe, compliant, and reliable operation of its wastewater facilities. We fully recognize the importance of meeting the County’s expectations, including compliance with all applicable laws and contractual requirements. To deliver on these expectations, Jacobs has handpicked a highly experienced project management team, supported by an extensive network of SMEs, to deliver superior O&M services.

<!-- ¶8 -->
Together, we offer a full-service, compliant, and reliable O&M program led by licensed professionals with deep experience in advanced wastewater treatment. Our leadership approach emphasizes empowerment, accountability, and continuous growth to ensure longterm success. As stable, long-standing firms —not driven by short-term market shifts—we are fully committed to serving today County today and into the future.
