Lead Process Engineer | Yellow River Water Reclamation Facility On-Call Process Support | Department of Water Resources | Gwinnett County, GA. Worked with GCDWR staff to address operational issues at the plant, including its MBR process. Reviewed and analyzed data to investigate occasional episodes of increased effluent ammonia. Developed strategies to maximize biological phosphorus removal, thereby minimizing the expense of chemical phosphorus removal. Updated the plant’s Operations Model, which is built on Jacobs’ proprietary Pro2D2 simulation platform.

<!-- ¶4 -->
Lead Process Engineer | Yellow River Water Reclamation Facility Expansion | Department of Water Resources | Gwinnett County, GA. As part of expansion of the County’s Yellow River Water Reclamation Facility from 14- to 22-mgd capacity, compared an MBR process to a conventional activated sludge process followed by granular media filters, demonstrating that the MBR process would have lower lifecycle cost. Prepared a request for proposals to pre-select and procure a membrane system for the MBR process. Performed simulation modeling to develop the project mass balance. Evaluated capacity of existing treatment facilities with and without aeration improvements to develop a plan for construction sequencing.

<!-- ¶5 -->
Subject Matter Expert | Assessment of Existing Tertiary Treatment Facilities | Department of Water Resources | Gwinnett County, GA. Assessed existing physical/chemical treatment facilities, including solids contact clarifiers, rapid mix and flocculation tanks, high-rate clarifiers, and granular media filters. Developed alternatives to improve or replace these facilities. Presented a workshop on chemical phosphorus removal, alternative coagulants, and tertiary treatment approaches at other facilities. Advised DWR on jar testing protocols.

<!-- ¶6 -->
Senior Technical Consultant | W.B. Casey Water Resource Recovery Facility Capacity Analysis and Plant Expansion Evaluation | Clayton County Water Authority | Clayton County, GA . Evaluated plant capacity constraints, developed influent and effluent design bases, and evaluated liquid and solids treatment process alternatives using economic and non-economic criteria. Selected liquid and solids processes for plant expansion from 24 to 32-mgd capacity. Developed a preliminary engineering report for solids improvements that include primary sludge thickening, sludge screening, anaerobic digestion, sludge storage, dewatering, cake receiving, and thermal drying. Part of the Jacobs team acting as owner’s agent for implementation of biosolids improvements using a progressive design build approach.

<!-- ¶7 -->
Wastewater Process Specialist | Linwood Water Reclamation Facility Process Analysis | Department of Water Resources | Gainesville, GA. Performed facility analysis of this 5-mgd MBR plant, which was experiencing nitrification inhibition. Characterized raw wastewater and developed process simulation model. Determined that the most likely cause of inhibition was quaternary ammonium compounds (QACs). Developed strategies to mitigate the impact of QACs. A year later, updated the evaluation and developed a plan to expand aerated sludge holding and dewatering.

<!-- ¶8 -->
Lead Process Engineer | Regional Water Reclamation Facility | Spokane, WA. Investigated seasonal decline in membrane performance that had affected plant capacity. Developed and implemented capital improvements to achieve required plant capacity. These improvements include - increased membrane area, increased mixed liquor pumping capacity, polymer feed to secondary treatment to improve mixed liquor filterability, and bioreactor swing zones to increase aerobic volume and improve winter nitrification.

<!-- ¶9 -->
Lead Process Engineer | Regional Water Pollution Control Plant Membrane Replacement | Traverse City, MI. In 2013, Suez (now Veolia) 500C membranes that had been installed in 2004 were approaching end of life. Developed a plan for phased replacement with 500D membranes to maximize use of existing assets while minimizing risk associated with impaired permeability due to gram positive bacteria. Negotiated pricing and scope of supply with Suez to avoid cost and time associated with competitive membrane procurement.
