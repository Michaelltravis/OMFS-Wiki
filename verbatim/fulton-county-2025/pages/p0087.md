Adhering to these maintenance practices not only enhances the efficiency of the Kubota MBR system but also contributes to the facility's compliance with environmental regulations and its commitment to sustainable wastewater treatment.

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### **_JCEC_**

<!-- ¶3 -->
The biggest focus at this facility is on the odor control system, which requires changing the media and regular checks to ensure the effectiveness of the system. The Xenon membrane system using Pneumatic valves requires special attention to the air dryer system. Effective moisture removal ensures consistent system operation and equipment longevity, along with repairing any air leaks in the compressed air system and blowers for the membranes. Any air leaks would disrupt the operational effectiveness of the system and will need to be addressed promptly.

<!-- ¶4 -->
In summary, our maintenance approach at JCEC will focus on:

<!-- ¶5 -->
- µ<sup>**Air dryer maintenance**for keeping the pneumatic valves working</sup> properly.

<!-- ¶6 -->
- µ<sup>**Blower maintenance**(vibration, precision alignment, continuous</sup> monitoring) for membrane system.

<!-- ¶7 -->
- µ<sup>Special attention to the</sup><sup>**grit and screenings equipment**.</sup>

<!-- ¶8 -->
- µ<sup>**UV disinfection system**maintenance and inventory control.</sup>

<!-- ¶9 -->

<!-- ¶10 -->

<!-- ¶11 -->

<!-- ¶12 -->

<!-- ¶15 -->
###### **_Little Creek WRF_**

<!-- ¶16 -->
This smaller membrane facility is a converted plant utilizing Xenon ZeeWeed technology. Again, effective grit and screenings removal will be especially important as will repairing failing units promptly to maintain compliance. Tank mixers will need to be checked regularly for proper mixing, and we will use oil analysis to determine when to change the lubrication to ensure proper maintenance with an eye to costeffectiveness. The UV system is important and requires maintaining a proper inventory level of bulbs and replacement parts for timely repairs.

<!-- ¶17 -->
Other areas of focus for our PM and PdM program will include:

<!-- ¶18 -->
- µ<sup>**Infrastructure maintenance:**As this is an older facility, we will regularly</sup> inspect and maintain the physical infrastructure of the plant, addressing issues like concrete deterioration, pipe corrosion, and structural integrity before they become critical problems.

<!-- ¶19 -->
- µ<sup>**Safety Systems:**Emergency Power generators and transfer switches</sup> will be tested monthly and maintained according to manufacturer specifications to ensure continuous operation during power outages, protecting both treatment processes and facility personnel.

<!-- ¶20 -->
- µ<sup>**Corrosion Prevention:**We will implement a comprehensive corrosion</sup> monitoring and prevention program including regular inspections, protective coatings application, and cathodic protection systems where appropriate to extend equipment life and maintain facility integrity.

<!-- ¶21 -->
- µ<sup>**Alarm Systems:**We will ensure</sup> alarms are functional and correctly calibrated through regular testing and verification procedures, maintaining accurate monitoring of critical parameters to prevent compliance issues and equipment damage.
