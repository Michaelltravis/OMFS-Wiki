###### **TEAM ORGANIZATION OPTIMIZES AND ELEVATES O&M**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Our organizational structure, shown in **Exhibit 3-1** , outlines a comprehensive staffing approach that meets and exceeds the County’s requirements. Our staffing approach maintains current staffing levels and includes key RFP-mandated roles. We do not anticipate a change to planned staff after year one. However, we do intend to retain all the existing employees that the County desires, preserving their invaluable institutional knowledge. For any staff who do not wish to join the JC

Similarly, we have resources for all other positions should we need to replace any current staff in those positions.
