###### **STAFFING PLAN**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Our approach to delivery of the management, operations, and maintenance services of the County’s facilities focuses on high-caliber leadership, reliability, compliance, and efficiency. In **_Section 3: Project Team Qualifications, Qualifications of Key Personnel_** , we discuss our team’s overall organization ( **Exhibit 3-1** ), roles, and identified key personnel. In this section, we discuss our staffing approach for the County’s facilities, including a summary of the total proposed staff (shown in **Exhibit 2-13** ) and shift schedules. Our staffing approach includes all of the required positions in the RFP, and we anticipate offering current qualified staff positions with JC Solutions. We discuss our approach to transitioning current staff later in **_Section 2.9 Transition Plan with Approach and Timeline of Activities Necessary to Achieve the Commencement Date_** .

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **_Summary Of Delivery Staffing Approach_**

<!-- ¶4 -->
- µ<sup>**Management, administrative and safety staff**</sup> will work eight-hour shifts, Monday through Friday, as shown in **Exhibit 2-14** . We will have two Administrative Assistants who will provide dayto-day support to the project, one located at Big Creek and the other at JCEC. A Health & Safety Specialist will also be located onsite full-time and be responsible for all the the County’s facilities.

<!-- ¶5 -->
- µ<sup>**Big Creek WRF operations staff**will be overseen</sup> day-to-day by the Plant Manager, who will work eight-hour shifts, Monday through Friday. (See **Exhibit 2-15** .) There will be two groups of dayshift operators who will all work 12-hour shifts. A Main Lead Operator along with two Operators will work a total of seven days across two weeks, covering three days during the first week and four days the next week. Similarly, a Secondary

<!-- ¶6 -->
Lead Operator and two Operators will also work shifts across two weeks, and their shifts will cover the four days in the first week and the three days in the second week not covered by the Main Lead Operator group. This ensures three day-shift Operators onsite every day of the week. For night shifts, we will have two groups, each consisting of one Lead Operator and one Operator. They will follow the same shift patterns across two weeks as the two day-shift groups. This ensures two Operators are onsite every night shift of the week.

<!-- ¶7 -->
- µ<sup>**JCEC operations staff**will be overseen day-to-day</sup> by the Plant Manager, who will work eight-hour shifts, Monday through Friday. (See **Exhibit 2-16** .) Lead Operators and Operations will work 12-hour shifts and will follow the same shift patterns as at Big Creek WRF, ensuring the required minimum staffing of three Operators during day shifts and two Operators during night shifts for every day of the week.

<!-- ¶8 -->
µ<sup>**Little River WRF operations staff**will be overseen</sup> day-to-day by the Plant Manager, who will work eight-hour shifts, Monday through Friday. (See **Exhibit 2-17** .) Two day-shift Lead Operators alternate overlapping schedules, blending shorter shifts of 4 or 8 hours with longer 12-hour shifts to maintain daily supervisory coverage. Four day-shift Operators work rotating 12-hour shifts, typically structured to provide consecutive days of work followed by multiple off days. Similarly, two night-shift Lead Operators alternate overnight supervision, each paired closely with a night-shift Operators who mirror their rotation, working 12-hour shifts with alternating days on and off. This shift pattern provides balanced staffing at the smaller Little River WRF while ensuring a minimum of three day shift operators and two night-shift Operators seven days per week.
