###### 2.6 **BENEFITS OF JC SOLUTIONS’ APPROACH TO OPERATIONS AND MAINTENANCE**

<!-- ¶2 -->
JC Solutions’ mission is to work alongside the County to delivery on your expectations and continue the important legacy of these vital facilities. Our SMEs in operations, maintenance, optimization, asset management, lab, SCADA/Operations Technology (OT), and safety have studied the facilities on our site visit and carefully reviewed the data provided. Many have prior experience with the facilities as well. Drawing on these resources, we have crafted a custom approach that includes numerous
