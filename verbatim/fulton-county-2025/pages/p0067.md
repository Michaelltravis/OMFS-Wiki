The procedures described above relate to membrane production capacity. Permeate quality is also important. At the three North Fulton plants, turbidity is monitored for each membrane train, and low turbidity indicates membrane integrity is intact. Big Creek WRF and Little River WRF (the newer plants) have a degassing column upstream of each turbidimeter to reduce false positive readings due to air bubbles. JCEC does not have this feature, and we would like to discuss with the County the possibility of adding them.

###### **_Properly Operating Scrubbers to Avoid Offsite Odor_**

<!-- ¶8 -->
Odor control is extremely important to being a good neighbor, and our goal will be to have zero odor complaints. At Big Creek WRF and JCEC, chemical scrubbers are used to treat air from the most odorous parts of the plant. Granular activated carbon (GAC) is used for other areas and as polishing after chemical scrubbers ( **Exhibit 2-30** ). Given the smaller plant size, Little River WRF has only GAC.

<!-- ¶9 -->
