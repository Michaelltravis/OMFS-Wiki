**4. Regulatory Compliance and Safety Transition.** All active permits, compliance documentation, and safety procedures will be transferred to the County. Jacobs will also:

<!-- ¶7 -->
- –Conduct a comprehensive compliance audit within 30 days of departure, as required under Exhibit 6, to confirm adherence to environmental, safety, and operational regulations.

<!-- ¶8 -->

**5. Operational Continuity.** JC Solutions will ensure uninterrupted service throughout the transition. Three months prior to the contract end, we will conduct an operational readiness review to confirm:

<!-- ¶10 -->
- –Tools, spare parts, and consumables are stocked for at least four weeks post-handover.

<!-- ¶11 -->
- –Vendor, service, and utility accounts are reconciled and properly closed.

<!-- ¶12 -->
- –All final accounting tasks—including final payroll, procurement card reconciliation, and the closeout invoice—are completed and documented.

<!-- ¶13 -->
As permitted by Article 6.6(C), JC Solutions is prepared to provide up to 180 days of extended support post-exit, at the County’s request, to ensure a smooth and professional handover.

<!-- ¶14 -->
**6. Proactive Risk Management.** We proactively address key transition risks such as service disruption, compliance gaps, and knowledge loss through:

<!-- ¶15 -->
- –Safety: Pre-handover safety audits and full protocol transfer.

<!-- ¶16 -->
- –Environment: Verified transfer of compliance data and updated permits.

<!-- ¶17 -->
- –Continuity: Operational readiness checks and inventory stock reviews.

<!-- ¶18 -->
- –Human Resources: Early coordination of interviews, training, and knowledge sharing.

###### **_Maintaining Exit Transition Readiness_**

<!-- ¶20 -->
JC Solutions will maintain transition readiness throughout the contract by conducting annual updates to the Exit Transition Plan. These updates will reflect operational changes, system upgrades, or new regulatory obligations, ensuring the final handover is always well-supported and executable with minimal disruption.

<!-- ¶21 -->
###### **_Lessons Learned_**

<!-- ¶22 -->
JC Solutions’ experience with exit transitions, such as Coos Bay, has highlighted the importance of early planning, thorough documentation, and proactive risk management. Structured communication protocols, detailed asset assessments, and collaborative planning with clients have proven key to success. These lessons will directly inform our approach at the County’s facilities, ensuring a seamless transition.
