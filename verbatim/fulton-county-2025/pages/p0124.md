###### **_Exit Transition Plan_**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Although an Exit Transition Plan is something we hope will not be necessary, JC Solutions has developed a robust plan to ensure continuity of operations, seamless data and knowledge transfer, and accurate documentation. In accordance with Article 49 and Exhibit 6 of the Contract, JC Solutions will develop a detailed Exit Transition Plan within 90 days of the Commencement Date. This plan will outline all handback requirements and initiate transition activities no later than nine months prior to the end of the O&M term. The plan will ensure that all transition activities—including operational, technical, administrative, and regulatory elements—are coordinated to maintain service reliability and comply with all contract expectations.

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **_JC Solutions’ Approach to the Exit Transition Process_**

<!-- ¶4 -->
JC Solutions’ approach to the exit transition process at the County emphasizes proactive planning, seamless knowledge transfer, clear documentation, and preservation of asset quality. While large-scale exit transitions are not common for Jacobs, our experience including recent transitions such as Coos Bay has informed key priorities and challenges, guiding us in developing robust processes to ensure an orderly, compliant, and thorough handover.

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### **_Comprehensive Exit Transition Strategy_**

<!-- ¶6 -->
JC Solutions’ Exit Transition Plan will align with all requirements outlined in the RFP, Exhibit 6, and Articles 6.6, 49, 50.6, and 53.2 of the Contract. Our plan includes detailed roles, responsibilities, and timelines to coordinate effectively with the County and any successor operator.

<!-- ¶7 -->
###### **_Key Elements of the Exit Transition Plan_**

<!-- ¶8 -->
**1. Documentation and Data Handover.** JC Solutions will provide the County with a complete set of updated management plans, operating manuals, and historical maintenance records. In compliance with Article 50.6 and 50.12, this includes:

<!-- ¶9 -->
- –A master inventory of all project-related records, including compliance reports, vendor invoices, operator logs, and laboratory data—indexed and converted into accessible electronic formats.

<!-- ¶10 -->
- –Identification and return or archival of proprietary Jacobs materials, clearly distinguished from County-owned documentation.

<!-- ¶11 -->
- –Complete transfer of regulatory documentation such as permits, inspection reports, and Discharge Monitoring Reports (DMRs), ensuring legal and regulatory compliance.

<!-- ¶12 -->
**2. Asset Condition and Inventory Management.** In accordance with Article 53.2, JC Solutions will conduct a thorough assessment of all facility assets and generate detailed condition reports. This will include:

<!-- ¶13 -->
- –Final inventory assessment of tools, equipment, spare parts, and consumables, with supporting documentation on all transfers or decommissioned items.

<!-- ¶14 -->
- –Verification and documentation of vehicles and equipment registration, transfer, or termination.

<!-- ¶15 -->
- –A comparison of final asset conditions to baseline records established at the project’s start, with recommendations for capital improvements where applicable.
