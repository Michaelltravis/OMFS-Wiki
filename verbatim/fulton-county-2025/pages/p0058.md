###### **OPERATION & MAINTENANCE PLANS**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Our overall approach to developing a comprehensive O&M Plan for Fulton County's North Fulton Wastewater Facilities will build upon current operations, emphasizing reliability, efficiency, and continuous improvement. By thoroughly analyzing existing facility conditions and operational procedures, we will establish a robust framework that integrates process control, proactive maintenance and asset management, rigorous QA/QC standards, systematic sampling and laboratory oversight, effective sludge management strategies, and proven odor control measures. Additionally, the plan will incorporate a clear and timely reporting and communications protocol to ensure transparency and responsiveness to the County. Our emphasis on health and safety, reinforced by stringent site security and advanced cybersecurity measures, will protect personnel, assets, and critical data. Detailed, facility-specific Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) will document all facets of the O&M Plan, enabling consistent execution and continuous optimization to meet current and future facility performance objectives.

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **_Process Control_**

<!-- ¶4 -->
We’ll implement our proven approach to process control. Jacobs’ Companywide Process Control System (CPCS)—one of our newest innovations—makes full use of process data and modeling for a fully scientific approach to process control. The core of the CPCS focuses on control and results. CPCS reports ensure processes are well controlled and operated in an optimized manner to achieve regulatory compliance and cost efficiency. Both in-house staff and regional technical specialists review the CPCS reports, supporting our QA/QC performance monitoring commitments.
