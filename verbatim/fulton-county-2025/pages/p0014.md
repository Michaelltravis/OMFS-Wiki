###### 2.2 **MEETING AND EXCEEDING THE COUNTY’S REQUIREMENTS AND OBJECTIVES**

<!-- ¶2 -->
We confirm we meet all contract requirements and understand the County seeks cost effective and reliable wastewater system O&M management services. Within 10 days following Notice to Proceed, we’ll submit a detailed Transition Plan identifying key milestones required to ensure completion of the Transition and readiness for full operations within the specified 45-day timeframe.

<!-- ¶3 -->
During this period, we’ll also sit down with the County to develop and agree on a summary checklist of all commitments, timelines, and documents to be provided throughout the term, as well as our process for fulfilling the requirements. This exercise will ensure agreement on the right timelines for when things must be developed, delivered, submitted, and reviewed.
