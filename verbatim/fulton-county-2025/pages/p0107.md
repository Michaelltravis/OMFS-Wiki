The system will include, at a minimum, a dedicated phone line; we’ll explore an online form, mobile app and other options to establish a clear and accessible means for reporting.

<!-- ¶8 -->
**Response Protocol.** We will receive and acknowledge complaints as required. We will follow up each complaint with a full investigation and action plan, as required. Creating a rapid response team to investigate and address odor complaints as soon as they are reported supports transparency and credibility. There would be a feedback mechanism to ensure that residents feel heard and that their concerns are being addressed featuring a means to follow up with complainants and inform them of the actions taken, anticipated duration, and other relevant details.

<!-- ¶9 -->
**Reporting and Follow-Up.** Monthly summaries of all public complaints, including response actions and resolutions, will be submitted to the County. Follow-up communication with the complainant will be conducted to confirm satisfaction with the resolution.

<!-- ¶10 -->
To support facility operations, odor complaint data and locations could be analyzed to identify patterns and sources. This information could support targeted solutions and improve overall odor management in the event of ongoing or longer-term issues.

<!-- ¶11 -->
**Sludge or Biosolids Spills.** In the event of a spill impacting roadways or public spaces, emergency containment procedures will be activated, and local authorities will be notified. Spill updates will be communicated to the public through pre-approved channels.

<!-- ¶12 -->
**Noise Complaints.** We will minimize noise through sound-dampening technology and restrict noisy operations to designated hours. Should complaints arise, we will reassess equipment and processes and communicate mitigation steps to the affected parties.

<!-- ¶13 -->
###### **_Stakeholder Engagement and Regulatory Coordination_**

<!-- ¶14 -->
###### **_Interagency Collaboration_**

<!-- ¶15 -->
We are committed to maintaining strong relationships with County personnel, Georgia EPD, EPA Region 4, and other relevant authorities. Regular coordination meetings will be held to review operational status, compliance, and community concerns.

<!-- ¶16 -->
###### **_Participation in Public Meetings_**

<!-- ¶17 -->
We will participate in all public meetings as required by the County, providing updates on facility performance, environmental stewardship, and capital improvements. Our team will prepare professional visual aids, educational materials, and staff subject matter experts to engage directly with community members.

<!-- ¶18 -->
###### **_Industry Involvement_**

<!-- ¶19 -->
As a long-standing and active Corporate Member of the Georgia Association of Water Professionals (GAWP), we will remain engaged with industry best practices and collaborate with peers across the state. Participation in GAWP events will also serve as a platform to share the success of our community outreach efforts.

<!-- ¶20 -->
###### **_Incident-Specific Protocols_**

<!-- ¶21 -->
**Odor Complaints.** We will utilize real-time monitoring equipment and conduct regular odor inspections. A rapid response team will investigate any complaint and implement immediate corrective measures. Communication will be maintained with the reporting party throughout the resolution process.
