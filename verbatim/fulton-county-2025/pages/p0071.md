Because a great deal of the information necessary for permit compliance begins in the laboratory, we’ve developed a suite of quality control tools for laboratory testing, including a QA/QC manual and the QC-Stats software program. We also participate in third-party proficiency testing programs to ensure laboratory performance yields data of unquestionable integrity. The laboratory will be operated in compliance with the 2016 TNI standard. We also administers a comprehensive internal auditing program to verify the adherence of our projects to permitting and other regulatory requirements.
