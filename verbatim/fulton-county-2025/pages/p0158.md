###### Scott Levesque, PE | MBR Specialist and Wastewater Process (continued)

<!-- ¶2 -->
Senior Technical Consultant | Fowler Water Reclamation Facility Expansion | Forsyth County, GA. Oversaw expansion of an existing MBR facility from 2.5 to 5-mgd (max monthly average). Existing membrane tanks were reconfigured to maximize capacity. Older generation membranes (Suez 500C) membranes were replaced with the latest generation membranes (Suez 500D). Designed a sidestream process for mixed liquor fermentation. Assisted with membrane performance testing and enhanced biological phosphorus removal process commissioning. Wrote an Operations Guide for the plant.

<!-- ¶3 -->
MBR Facility Lead | Changi WRP Expansion | Singapore Public Utilities Board, Singapore. Led the Liquid Module 3 MBR design, which increased plant capacity by 53 mgd (annual average), making it one of the five largest MBR facilities in the world. The MBR process uses center-flow band screens, deep step feed bioreactors, and DuPont immersed membranes.

<!-- ¶4 -->
MBR Subject Matter Expert | Rikers Island Feasibility Study | New York City, NY. Developed concepts to convert four existing wastewater treatment plants to MBR technology. Capacity of existing plants varied from 80 to 275-mgd (annual average). Also developed concepts for a new, 705-mgd MBR facility to replace those four WWTPs. Results of this study will inform decision making by NYC Environmental Protection relating to replacement or upgrade of existing WWTPs.

<!-- ¶5 -->
MBR Subject Matter Expert | Technical Analysis of Biological and Advanced Water Treatment Processes at the Joint Water Pollution Control Plant | Sanitation Districts of Los Angeles County, CA. Advised Jacobs staff on implementation of large MBR designs. Performed a survey of 14 large, North American MBR facilities. Conducted a literature review focused on wet weather performance and biofouling control of MBR processes.

<!-- ¶6 -->
MBR Subject Matter Expert | Tapia Water Reclamation Facility Membrane Bioreactor Evaluation | Las Virgenes-Triunfo Joint Powers Authority | Malibu, CA. Evaluated conversion of conventional activated sludge to MBR technology. Due to space limitations, developed a concept to integrate an immersed membranes facility into existing rectangular secondary clarifiers. Coordinated with several engineering disciplines to define the concept in sufficient detail for cost estimation.

<!-- ¶7 -->
MBR Subject Matter Expert | Mary’s Creek Water Reclamation Facility | Fort Worth, TX. Advising project team on matters relating to preliminary treatment, biological nutrient removal, process simulation, MBR process configuration, and membrane pre-selection for a 10-mgd (annual average) greenfield WRF that will produce reuse water for nearby customers.

<!-- ¶8 -->
Subcommittee Chair | Membrane Bioreactor Systems Standard (ANSI/AWWA B130). In 2023, oversaw revision of the MBR standard first published in 2013.
