###### **_JC Solutions’ AMS Elements Built on the 10-Box Model_**

<!-- ¶5 -->
Strategy and Planning - JC Solutions will establish a strategic asset management framework that aligns with the County’s operational objectives and integrates lifecycle cost analysis, capital investment prioritization, and risk-based decision-making. The first step in this process is conducting a baseline condition assessment, which will provide a risk-based ranking of all critical assets. This assessment will help optimize capital planning and ensure that maintenance and asset renewal efforts are focused on high-priority infrastructure. Additionally, JC Solutions will update and validate the North Fulton CMMS with our Maintenance Connection system during transition and ensure that the asset registry is complete, accurate, and reflective of real-world conditions.

###### Asset Management Decision-Making -

<!-- ¶9 -->
Data-driven decision-making is at the core of JC Solutions’ approach to asset management. By leveraging CMMS tracking and analytics, we will centralize asset data, enabling optimized work order generation, cost analysis, and maintenance prioritization. The system will facilitate risk-based asset prioritization, allowing the County to direct resources toward the most critical infrastructure needs. The CMMS will also support planned obsolescence strategies, ensuring that asset replacements and upgrades are proactively managed, avoiding unnecessary costs associated with emergency repairs.
