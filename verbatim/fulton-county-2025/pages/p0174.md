###### Regional WWTP Design-Build-Operate (DBO) CITY OF TRAVERSE | TRAVERSE, MI

<!-- ¶2 -->
Since its establishment in 1932, the City of Traverse Regional WWTP has been a beacon of environmental stewardship and community service, currently catering to 50,000 residents and local industries with an 8.5mgd capacity. The partnership between Jacobs and the City of Traverse (City), growing since 1990, has been instrumental in transforming the plant into a model of efficiency, compliance, and innovation.

<!-- ¶3 -->
The collaboration between Jacobs and the City of Traverse City led to significant capital improvements, including two major plant upgrades that not only enhanced treatment capabilities but also introduced North America’s largest MBR system by 2004, increasing the plant’s peak flow capacity to 17-mgd. Moreover, Jacobs has expanded its environmental and operational expertise to five satellite projects in the region, managing diverse facilities ranging from a 0.069-mgd WWTP for Shorts Brewing to a 0.132-mgd facility for the Northport/Leelanau Township Utility Authority. This comprehensive approach to wastewater management and the journey of continuous improvement underscore Jacobs’ commitment to excellence and its pivotal role in supporting Traverse City’s mission to protect its sensitive environment and sustain a high quality of life.

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### **_Detailed Description of the Facilities Operated_**

<!-- ¶6 -->
Jacobs operates nine lift stations in addition to the treatment plant. The Front St. Lift Station is capable of delivering over 1-mgd and has a capacity to deliver 8.7-mgd.

<!-- ¶7 -->
###### **_Detailed Description of the Work of the Proponent on This Project_**

<!-- ¶8 -->
Addressing initial compliance challenges, Jacobs introduced a sound operational plan and a CMMS that restored the plant’s standing with the Michigan Department of Natural Resources (DNR) and expanded its services to include a comprehensive IPP. Our technology solution and design-build-operate capability brought the wastewater facility into compliance and met the public’s demand for higherquality effluent to help enhance the local environment while providing the best overall value to the City.
