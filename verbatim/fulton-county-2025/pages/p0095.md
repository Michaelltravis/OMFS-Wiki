We recognize that utility infrastructure requires a balanced security approach—one that protects critical systems without interrupting essential operations. Jacobs draws on extensive experience securing water and wastewater facilities nationwide. Our integrated approach pairs physical safeguards with utility-specific expertise to proactively mitigate risk and ensure service continuity.

<!-- ¶2 -->
By embedding a culture of safety and security into every aspect of operations, we will deliver a secure, compliant, and resilient environment that protects the County’s critical assets and the community they serve.

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **_Security Monitoring_**

<!-- ¶4 -->
Security monitoring plays a critical role in early detection and response to security threats. At utility sites, it functions as the “eyes and ears” of the operation, enabling rapid awareness of intrusions or unusual activity. JC Solutions will evaluate monitoring practices across key areas including perimeter security, facility access, fire alarm integration, physical and electronic access control systems, and sensitive in-building areas. Effective monitoring also contributes to employees’ safety by enabling timely emergency notifications and ensuring that intrusion detection systems are responsive and well-integrated. Our assessments will review how these elements work together to detect unauthorized access and support quick, coordinated responses to incidents.

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### **_Security Governance_**

<!-- ¶6 -->
Security governance provides the organizational framework for implementing and maintaining a successful security program. A strong governance model starts with leadership buy-in at all levels, from upper management to frontline staff, and includes the development and enforcement of policies and procedures that clearly define responsibilities during normal operations and during security events. In addition to policy development, we will evaluate the presence of training programs, employee awareness efforts, and administrative oversight to ensure a cohesive, system-wide security culture. This includes ensuring that daily operations, staff behavior, and equipment maintenance are guided by clearly defined standards and consistently reinforced expectations.

<!-- ¶7 -->
###### **_Security Technology_**

<!-- ¶8 -->
Technology supports both the monitoring and governance components of a security program by providing tools that increase visibility, improve access control, and ensure secure perimeters. During our assessments, we will examine the effectiveness of installed systems such as surveillance cameras, card readers, intrusion alarms, lighting, and video analytics. We will also consider how well these systems support both daytime and nighttime security, and how they align with the facility’s operational needs. We will ensure that each facility has the right mix of technological safeguards to control and monitor access, detect suspicious behavior, and support rapid response.

<!-- ¶9 -->
These technologies will be selected and applied to support the unique needs of North Fulton facilities, emphasizing reliability, scalability, and ease of integration with existing systems.
