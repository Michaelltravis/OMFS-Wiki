###### **_Key Findings of Our Initial Facilities Assessments_**

<!-- ¶4 -->
Our evaluation of the pump station locations during our site visits identified a number of areas of concern that we will further investigate and identify how to address if selected, including:

<!-- ¶5 -->
- µ<sup>Our evaluation highlighted that certain pump stations utilize outdated “can” type designs.</sup> Transitioning these to modern submersible stations is advised to improve operational efficiency, reduce maintenance needs, and enhance safety for maintenance personnel.

<!-- ¶6 -->
- µ<sup>Reliable telemetry systems are vital for real-time monitoring and efficient management</sup> of pump stations. Continuous maintenance and periodic upgrades of communication infrastructure will support prompt detection and resolution of operational issues.

<!-- ¶7 -->
- µ<sup>The emergency generators at the stations, many of which are new, will be a major focus.</sup> Ensuring continuous operation during power outages is critical. Regular maintenance and testing of emergency generators are necessary to provide reliable backup power. This includes load-bank testing ensuring generators can supply the power needed.

<!-- ¶8 -->
- µ<sup>Odor control systems at the pump stations are another focus area. Effective odor control is</sup> essential for community relations and environmental compliance. Routine inspections and timely replacement of odor control media are recommended to maintain optimal performance.
