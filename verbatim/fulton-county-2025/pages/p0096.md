###### **_Site Visits and Field Assessment_**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Jacobs will perform in-person assessments of each vertical asset as part of the SSA. Each site will be documented, photographed, and evaluated using a structured, security-specific data collection form. Assets are grouped into the following categories:

<!-- ¶3 -->
- µ<sup>Water Intake Structures</sup>

<!-- ¶4 -->
- µ<sup>Water Source Wells</sup>

<!-- ¶5 -->
- µ<sup>Water Treatment Plants (WTPs)</sup>

<!-- ¶6 -->
- µ<sup>Water Booster Stations</sup>

<!-- ¶7 -->
- µ<sup>Water Storage Tanks</sup>

<!-- ¶8 -->
During site assessments, we will evaluate the following security categories, where applicable:

<!-- ¶9 -->
- µ<sup>**Perimeter Deterrence:**Fencing, barbed wire, lighting, signage, guard</sup> presence, housekeeping, and visibility

<!-- ¶10 -->
- µ<sup>**Perimeter Delay:**Gates, bollards, secure locks, and vehicle/pedestrian</sup> control

<!-- ¶11 -->
- µ<sup>**Perimeter Detection:**Fence sensors, radar, surveillance analytics, and</sup> staff-based monitoring

<!-- ¶12 -->
- µ<sup>**Surveillance and CCTV Response:**Notification protocols, law</sup> enforcement liaison, emergency response

<!-- ¶13 -->
- µ<sup>**Secondary Layer Delay:**Building entry points, hatches, vaults, ladder</sup> cages, and key control systems

<!-- ¶14 -->
- µ<sup>**Secondary Layer Detection:**Door/window/hatch/vault alarms and</sup> internal motion detection systems

<!-- ¶15 -->
This assessment follows Crime Prevention Through Environmental Design (CPTED) principles, focusing on:

<!-- ¶16 -->
- µ<sup>**Natural Surveillance**– Maximizing visibility through site layout and design</sup>

<!-- ¶17 -->
- µ<sup>**Natural Access Control**– Using landscape, lighting, and physical</sup> features to guide entry

<!-- ¶18 -->
- µ<sup>**Territorial Reinforcement**– Defining boundaries with signage, fencing,</sup> and landscaping

<!-- ¶19 -->
- µ<sup>**Maintenance**– Preserving intended site use and eliminating</sup> obstructions to visibility

<!-- ¶20 -->
Our commitment to safety and security extends beyond assessment— we will implement what we evaluate. Findings from the SSA will directly inform site-specific updates to the County’s Security Plan and guide improvements in facility design, operations, and monitoring systems. By combining local risk analysis with nationally recognized security principles, we will ensure that the County’s water and wastewater infrastructure remains resilient, well-protected, and ready to meet future challenges.

<!-- ¶21 -->
###### **CYBERSECURITY**

<!-- ¶22 -->
JC Solutions understands that cybersecurity is critical to the reliability and resilience of wastewater treatment operations. As cyber threats targeting water and wastewater utilities increase, our team will apply a proven and comprehensive strategy to safeguard Industrial Control Systems (ICS), SCADA, and business systems across the North Fulton Service Area.

<!-- ¶23 -->
Jacobs brings deep expertise in ICS cybersecurity, shaped by years of work with federal, municipal, and confidential clients—including the EPA. Our approach is aligned with NIST SP 800-series guidance and includes both proactive defense strategies and rapid response protocols to protect infrastructure, data, and public health. Using the Purdue model, assets are divided based on their criticality, and separate process areas are isolated to prevent lateral movement between systems. Security controls and network inspection are added between areas to monitor and manage access. Real-time monitoring is used to ensure that security controls are operable, and issues are detected before operations are impacted. Systems are kept patched and updated to ensure mitigation against the latest vulnerabilities.

<!-- ¶24 -->
Our cybersecurity program for the County will include:

<!-- ¶25 -->
- µ<sup>**Risk and resilience assessment**to identify cybersecurity risks and</sup> provide recommended mitigations.

<!-- ¶26 -->
- µ<sup>**Development of a site-specific cybersecurity and SCADA Security**</sup> **Plan** as part of our overall safety and risk management approach, as required by Article 50.8 and Exhibit 4 of the contract .

<!-- ¶27 -->
- µ<sup>**Implementation of ICS-focused cybersecurity policies and**</sup> **procedures** , including logical and physical network architecture reviews, remote access controls, endpoint protection, and penetration testing of WAN systems.
