- **MBR Excellence to Protect Your Investment:** We recognize the County's significant investment in membrane bioreactor technology across all three facilities and are uniquely positioned to protect and optimize that investment. Our MBR subject matter experts bring specialized knowledge in Kubota and other membrane systems, offering immediate support and operational optimization. These specialists, including members of our onsite team, have commissioned, operated, and provided troubleshooting for MBR systems worldwide. They bring expertise in extending membrane life, optimizing chemical usage, and enhancing performance, as well as developing customized cleaning protocols, optimizing flux rates and aeration sequences, and implementing fouling mitigation strategies that maximize treatment capacity while minimizing energy consumption. This specialized knowledge ensures operations at your facilities maximizes return on your MBR investment while maintaining exceptional effluent quality.

- **Proactive Maintenance for Uninterrupted Operations:** Our preventive maintenance program incorporates condition-based monitoring and predictive analytics to identify and address potential issues before they impact operations. We deploy vibration analysis, infrared thermography, oil analysis, and acoustic monitoring to track equipment health in real-time, particularly for critical assets like blowers, pumps, and membrane
