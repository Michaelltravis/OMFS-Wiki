###### **_Equipment Performance Testing Plan_**

<!-- ¶3 -->
This Equipment Performance Testing Plan establishes the procedures, schedules, and protocols for evaluating the performance of all wastewater process equipment against the Minimum Equipment Performance Standards and Contract Standards as specified in the Draft Services Agreement. This plan covers all Managed Assets at the facilities, including but not limited to membrane systems (Kubota Membranes, Zenon ZeeWeed), aerobic and conventional digestion systems, dewatering equipment (centrifuges, screw presses), blowers, grit/screening equipment, lift pumps, UV disinfection systems, and associated control systems.

<!-- ¶4 -->
JC Solutions has developed an initial draft of the Equipment Performance Testing Plan outlined below. Our Maintenance Manager and Project Manager will work with our onsite team and the County to complete and implement the plan within 180 days from the contract commencement date.

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### **_Equipment Performance Testing Plan Outline_**

<!-- ¶6 -->
**1. Introduction:** Establishes procedures, schedules, and protocols for evaluating equipment performance against Minimum Equipment Performance Standards. Covers all managed assets including membrane systems, digestion systems, dewatering equipment, blowers, grit/screening equipment, lift pumps, and UV disinfection systems.

<!-- ¶7 -->
**2. Roles and Responsibilities:** Delineates responsibilities between Owner (providing notice for inspections, approving the plan, witnessing tests) and O&M Services Contractor (implementing the plan, conducting tests, documenting results). Identifies key testing personnel including specialists for membrane systems, solids handling, mechanical systems, digestion, and UV.

<!-- ¶8 -->
**3. Equipment Inventory and Classification:** Maintains comprehensive equipment inventory with classification based on criticality (Class A, B, C). Categorizes specialized equipment into functional groups: membrane systems, solids handling, air supply, preliminary treatment, pumping, and disinfection.

<!-- ¶9 -->
**4. Minimum Performance Standards:** Details performance criteria for each equipment type (capacity, pressure, efficiency, etc.) with reference to standard test methods from industry authorities (Hydraulic Institute, ASCE/WEF, EPA, NFPA).

<!-- ¶10 -->
**5. Testing Procedures:** Outlines general requirements and equipmentspecific test procedures for:

<!-- ¶11 -->
- µ<sup>Membrane Systems: TMP measurement, permeability calculation,</sup> integrity testing

<!-- ¶12 -->
- µ<sup>Aerobic Digestion: VSR percentage, oxygen uptake rates, DO levels</sup>

<!-- ¶13 -->
- µ<sup>Dewatering Equipment: Cake solids percentage, capture rates, polymer</sup> consumption

<!-- ¶14 -->
- µ<sup>Blowers: Pressure and flow rates, efficiency, vibration analysis</sup>

<!-- ¶15 -->
- µ<sup>Grit/Screening: Capture efficiency, hydraulic capacity</sup>

<!-- ¶16 -->
- µ<sup>Lift Pumps: Pressure measurements, flow rates, efficiency</sup>

<!-- ¶17 -->
- µ<sup>UV Disinfection: UVT measurement, intensity verification, bioassay</sup> testing

<!-- ¶18 -->
**6. Test Scheduling and Frequency:** Establishes testing frequency based on equipment classification with special requirements for critical systems. Includes procedures for coordinating owner inspections.

<!-- ¶19 -->
**7. Documentation and Reporting:** Requires comprehensive documentation using standardized forms, detailed test reports, and maintenance of testing records for the contract duration.

<!-- ¶20 -->
**8. Compliance Management:** Establishes procedures for handling noncompliance issues, including documentation, root cause analysis, corrective action planning, and retesting. Addresses equipment-specific compliance considerations and prioritization methodology.

<!-- ¶21 -->
**9. Continuous Improvement:** Commits to annual plan review and updates following major equipment modifications or process changes. Focuses on performance optimization of key systems and maintains a lessons learned database.

<!-- ¶22 -->
**10. Appendices:** Includes comprehensive supporting documentation: equipment inventory, performance standards, test methods, detailed procedures, testing schedule, standard forms, test equipment records, and system-specific evaluation guidelines.
