### **Section 6**

## Availability of Key Personnel

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### **KEY PERSONNEL AND PLAN FOR COMMITMENT**

<!-- ¶3 -->
_JC Solutions recognizes the importance of having a fully trained, motivated, and certified staff on-site supported by a strong network of off-site expert resources to deliver high-quality and reliable services to the County and its customers. Our on-site staff will be fully dedicated once they transition to this project, and their current roles will no longer require their involvement. We anticipate that we will transition all of the existing staff on-site that the County wishes to retain. With our deep bench of resources, we are able to propose qualified staff for all of the required positions. JC Solutions fully intends to maintain our proposed key personnel throughout the duration of the Contract._ **_Exhibit 6-1_** _shows the key personnel proposed._
