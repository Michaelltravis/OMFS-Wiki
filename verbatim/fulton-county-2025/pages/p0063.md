###### **_Chemical Cleaning to Maintain Membrane Performance_**

<!-- ¶8 -->
Chemical cleaning is necessary to maintain membrane performance. The membranes at Big Creek WRF are new; those at Little River WRF are six years old; and those at JCEC are 16 years old. The JCEC membranes are well past expected life, and an intense program of chemical cleaning is being used to maintain productivity. We understand that the County is evaluating membrane replacement, after which chemical quantities can be determined.
