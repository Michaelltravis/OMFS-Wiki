###### **CUSTOMER SERVICE PLAN**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Customer service and client centricity are at the core of JC Solutions’ approach and represent a key reason why clients and communities trust us to operate and maintain essential infrastructure. Our commitment to exceptional service extends beyond daily operations to include thoughtful, responsive engagement with County’s staff, residents, and businesses.

<!-- ¶3 -->
We will implement a strategic Customer Service Plan tailored to the expectations outlined in Article 50.5(G) and Exhibit 4 of the contract. The Project Manager will work closely with Transition Manager Mike Boven to initiate development of the plan, which will be finalized prior to the Commencement Date and updated annually to ensure ongoing alignment with County expectations. This plan will ensure timely, professional, and accountable interactions with all customers and stakeholders. Key components include:

<!-- ¶4 -->
- µ<sup>**Behavioral Guidelines and Expectations:**All JC Solutions</sup> personnel will be trained in professionalism, courtesy, and responsiveness to build trust and confidence with the public and County’s staff.

<!-- ¶5 -->
- µ<sup>**Accountability for Damages:**In the event JC Solutions is responsible</sup> for property damage to a customer’s home or business (e.g., due to a backup or overflow), we will bear the full cost of repair and maintain open communication with both the customer and the County throughout the resolution process.

<!-- ¶6 -->
- µ<sup>**Standards for Responsiveness and Follow-Through:**We will respond</sup> to urgent service issues—such as spills or backups—within one hour, and all customer concerns will be logged, tracked, and resolved promptly. Daily summaries of complaints and actions taken will be submitted to the County.

<!-- ¶7 -->
- µ<sup>**Community Relations Program:**Our team will support the County’s</sup> outreach and education initiatives, ensuring residents understand how to report service issues and how their feedback is used to improve operations.

<!-- ¶8 -->
- µ<sup>**24/7 Accessibility:**We will maintain a dedicated phone number and</sup> email address for customer contact. Signage at each facility will display this information clearly, along with emergency contact protocols.

<!-- ¶9 -->
This plan fosters a consistent, thoughtful, and transparent service experience for every customer interaction. By combining Jacobs’ national best practices with localized, contract-specific procedures, we will help uphold the County’s reputation for quality service and responsiveness.

<!-- ¶10 -->
- µ<sup>**Reporting and Transparency:**A summary of customer service</sup> interactions will be included in the monthly operations report, along with proactive steps taken to prevent recurring concerns.
