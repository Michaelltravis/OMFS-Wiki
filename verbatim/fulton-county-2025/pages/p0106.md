###### **_Handling Public Concerns and Complaints_**

<!-- ¶24 -->
Because many of these neighborhoods already have a history with a nearby facility, developing and implementing a standard process to handle complaints about operations, especially odor, is critical to being a good neighbor. Working with operational staff, we will develop a complaint reporting system that gives residents and citizens a direct way to communicate concerns.

<!-- ¶25 -->
**Complaint Intake and Tracking.** We will provide a system for 24/7 odor complaint notification and response. We will maintain a record of the number of calls received and information on the odor complaints using a log form that will include, at a minimum, the following information:

<!-- ¶26 -->
- µ<sup>Name of Contractor’s employee that received the complaint</sup>

<!-- ¶27 -->
- µ<sup>Nature of complaint</sup>
