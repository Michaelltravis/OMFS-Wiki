###### **_Chemical Use Optimization_**

<!-- ¶3 -->
Jacobs has applied data science and AI supported techniques to chemical optimization, with a similar philosophy as we apply for power optimization. And like power use cases, it works spectacularly well even at sites that have been optimized using traditional industry methods, and on systems using best practice designs. We **typically see 10-30% improvement in dosage rates across all types of chemicals where we have applied this.**

<!-- ¶4 -->
The premise approach is similar to what was described above: Use your plants’ history and correlations to actual operating conditions to inform recommendations pushed directly to O&M staff on the best setpoints for

<!-- ¶5 -->
the given situation. It’s all based on what has already worked best in the past at that site, so user adoption is much simpler to support—frankly speaking, operators have trust in it if they know it is something the plant and their own team has already done before, rather than a mysterious “black box”. Indeed, user adoption rates are between 75%-100% for this kind of use case. All the AI and machine learning is doing is matching current conditions to what has worked in the past to bring that recommendation forward, and at speeds and accuracy that no operator has time to manage. The available instruments designed into the

Based on our rollout of over 80 use cases to date, we can begin push notifications for dosage recommendations within weeks of obtaining detailed plant data ( **Exhibit E-2** ). ###### **DRAGONFLY – ADVANCED COLLECTIONS SYSTEM DEFECT CODING**

<!-- ¶11 -->
While the collections system is not part of the scope for this contract, JC Solutions recognizes the immense amount of collection system pipe under the County’s stewardship. In the spirit of building a strong partnership, we can assist the County, if it desires, in improving efficiencies related to CCTV defect coding during sewer inspections with our advanced Dragonfly tool.

<!-- ¶12 -->
Dragonfly is a web-based, cloud-hosted system integrated with AI for enhanced condition assessment and guidance to cost-effectively manage collection system infrastructure, built jointly by a Jacobs and Hitachi partnership. It uses AI to provide NASSCO PACE coding of inspection video with NASSCO certified inspectors providing QA /QC of the AI output ( **Exhibit E-3** ). With the double layer of accuracy Dragonfly provides through AI and human QA/QC, we can deliver insights that are rapid, timely, and backed with rigorous traceable data.
