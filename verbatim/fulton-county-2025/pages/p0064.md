###### **_Sodium Hydroxide for Alkalinity Addition at Little River WRF_**

<!-- ¶5 -->
Monthly reports include influent data for BOD and TSS but lack data for total Kjeldahl nitrogen (TKN), ammonia, and alkalinity. While historical measurements are those required by the NPDES permit, there is insufficient information on which to base process simulations using historical wastewater characteristics. Using the original design basis for TKN, ammonia, and alkalinity, process simulation indicates that far more sodium hydroxide would be required for alkalinity addition (82 gal./ MG) than is reimbursable (6 gal./MG). Our figure already considers the sodium hydroxide contained in Phos-Zorb. In fact, high historical usage of Phos-Zorb at this plant suggests that the incumbent may be overfeeding Phos-Zorb for alkalinity addition, which is not a cost-effective operating strategy.

###### **_Maintaining Good Mixed Liquor Filterability_**

<!-- ¶7 -->
While sludge settleability is not important to an MBR process, mixed liquor filterability is because it’s affected by operation of the biological process, especially temperature and solids retention time (SRT). Longer SRT is needed at lower temperature to ensure adequate removal of organic foulants ( **Exhibit 2-28** ). In addition, the best filterability is achieved when surface wasting is practiced.

<!-- ¶8 -->
JCEC is designed for effective surface wasting, with mixed liquor from all bioreactors passing the foam wasting pump station before it splits to the membrane tanks. Wasting can also be done from RAS, which is at a higher concentration. While surface wasting is beneficial to plant housekeeping (by avoiding foam) and membrane performance (by improving filterability), sludge wasting from RAS is beneficial to dewatering because higher dewatering feed concentration may increase cake concentration and reduce disposal costs. We plan to begin plant operation in the same manner as the incumbent and gradually shift toward surface wasting, ultimately finding the best balance between the two wasting locations.
