###### **WE’RE MORE THAN AN OPERATOR: WE’RE YOUR STRATEGIC PARTNER FOR WASTEWATER EXCELLENCE & COMPLIANCE**

<!-- ¶4 -->
Jacobs and Atlanta-based, minority-owned firm CERM have formed a joint venture, JC Solutions (A Jacobs/CERM JV), to deliver compliant operations and maintenance services for wastewater facilities and pump stations in the North Fulton County. This joint venture is a natural evolution of our partnership and a perfect alignment of complementary strengths. Built on over 20 years of combined experience across water, education, aviation, and transportation projects, our partnership brings together complementary strengths: Jacobs’ deep O&M expertise and national best practices, and CERM’s strong local presence, workforce development programs, and community partnerships. Jacobs is the only fully integrated O&M firm in North America—U.S.-based, Fortune 500, and built for stability. With $12 billion in FY24 revenue and a $2.4 billion O&M backlog, we bring unmatched scale and staying power. Our 4,000+ U.S. O&M professionals and 45,000 global staff make us the industry’s largest and most diverse operations partner. CERM was founded in 1995 as a minority-owned engineering, environmental, and program management consulting firm, and has grown into an award-winning organization with 100+ professionals serving the southeastern U.S. CERM offers robust workforce development programs and strong local and regional partnerships. Together, we bring a shared commitment to regulatory compliance, environmental stewardship, and operational excellence.
