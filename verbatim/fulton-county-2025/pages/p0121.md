###### **_Full Transition and Integration (90 Days from Start Date)_**

<!-- ¶3 -->
Following the initial transition, we will enter a comprehensive integration phase. This critical period focuses on aligning all systems and processes with JC Solutions’ operational framework. Key activities will include extensive training sessions for all staff, updates to SOPs, and complete system integration to ensure everyone is equipped to perform effectively in their new roles.

<!-- ¶4 -->
###### **_Long-Term Transition (12 Months from Start Date)_**

<!-- ¶5 -->
The final phase spans 12 months and is dedicated to finalizing all transition activities. This period will focus on ensuring that the project operates smoothly under new management and that any remaining integration tasks are addressed. This will enable the project team is not only capable of fully delivering the contracted services well but that they are also fully trained on the tools and strategies that JC Solutions will bring to the project and are seamlessly integrated with the regional and technical support teams. We will continue to monitor performance, gather feedback, and adjust as needed to fully realize the benefits of the new management structure.

<!-- ¶6 -->
Prior to implementation, our transition leadership will review the proposed transition schedule with the County and modify it based on your guidance and see that it’s consistent with the final contract award and execution dates. This collaborative approach will ensure alignment with the existing project organization, verify that all requirements are met, and that mutually agreed-upon success factors are achieved.

<!-- ¶7 -->
This phased approach facilitates a smooth transition to the new project management and emphasizes our commitment to retaining local knowledge.

###### **_Long-Term Staffing/Succession Planning Practices_**

<!-- ¶9 -->
**_Building a strong culture is Step 1._** Effectively recruiting and proactively growing and retaining quality staff—while building future leaders—is critical to sustained O&M excellence. We have a plan for that!

<!-- ¶10 -->
Jacobs has created a Resource Planning Group focused on attracting, developing, and retaining high-performing O&M staff. Their efforts have

<!-- ¶11 -->
**doubled the number of new Jacobs’ O&M employees over the past year, lowered our turnover numbers, and focused on the promotion of our high-potential staff.** As a result, we have a proven approach to retaining our staff over time.

<!-- ¶12 -->
###### **_We have already begun recruiting efforts for this important project._**

<!-- ¶13 -->
Internally we promote these opportunities via a range of internal communications, including virtual tools and onsite efforts. Externally, we use our national recruiting resources and will continue these practices in the future to fill vacant positions. Our efforts include:

<!-- ¶14 -->
**Partnering with local high schools, community colleges, and technical schools** such as Atlanta Metropolitan State College and ATC to create a pipeline of talent and offer intern and apprentice programs, as well as water and wastewater coursework to prepare students for the Operator Certification Exam and a career in the industry.

<!-- ¶15 -->
**At Ease, Jacobs’ Military Recruitment Program.** The Jacobs’ at Ease program partners with Hiring our Heroes and the US Department of Defense Skillsbridge programs for those nearing completion of military service, veterans, or their spouses with on-the-job experience over a 90– 180 period. They gain familiarity with O&M, Jacobs’ systems and culture, and position competencies before officially joining the Jacobs workforce. This great new pool of talent for JC Solutions also benefits the men and women who have served our country.

<!-- ¶16 -->
**Recruiting tools best suited to O&M-focused candidates.** We focus on platforms such as Indeed and Zip Recruiters that work on smartphones instead of requiring a computer.

<!-- ¶17 -->
**_Incentive and Recognition Programs._** Jacobs provides a wide range of incentive and recognition programs to employees related to **job performance, safety-related behaviors, and exceptional team efforts.** Jacobs also provides **powerful rewards and recognitions to employees who obtain advanced certifications** in an operations, maintenance, or related technical field. Jacobs **helps employees cover the cost of training** , preparation and practice materials, and examination fees so they can concentrate on learning and developing and provides **promotions and cash bonuses up to $1,000 for employees who pass certifications or earns an advanced degree** in a field related to their position from an accredited college or university degree program. JC Solutions can provide annual tuition reimbursement to help employees.
