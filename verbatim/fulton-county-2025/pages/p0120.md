###### **_45-Day Schedule for Smooth Transition of Operations and Related Systems_**

<!-- ¶13 -->
JC Solutions is fully confident in our ability to deliver a comprehensive transition within the 45-day period. Our team is well-equipped to mobilize quickly, leveraging proven tools, experienced personnel, and established processes to ensure all critical aspects of the handover are addressed efficiently and effectively. We will dedicate additional resources and maintain our focus on quality, safety, and continuity throughout the transition.

<!-- ¶14 -->
While the RFP does not specify a required transition duration, we have developed a focused 45-day transition plan beginning May 22nd to meet the project’s timeline. As outlined in **Exhibit 2-47** , our phased and targeted approach emphasizes early engagement, structured onboarding, and clear communication to align goals and expectations from the outset. The plan also prioritizes retention of local knowledge and allows flexibility to bring in new talent as needed.

<!-- ¶15 -->
**This structured approach enables a smooth and well-coordinated transition, with no disruption to operations or service delivery.**
