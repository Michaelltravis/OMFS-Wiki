###### **_School Program_**

<!-- ¶11 -->
Leveraging the educational opportunities the County already has in place and finding ways to make them specific to each facility can help build programming at each location. County staff already have existing relationships with area schools and can support efforts to strengthen the connection to JCEC, Big Creek WRF, and Little River WRF.  This could

<!-- ¶12 -->
include educational workshops on pollution prevention and sustainable practices as well as student design competitions on innovative solutions for water reclamation.

<!-- ¶13 -->
The workshops could also serve as an introduction to the Stockholm Junior Water Prize for local students or support their efforts in pursuing it. The SJWP is a prestigious competition for high school students who conduct research projects related to water issues. The GA Association of Water Professionals sponsors it and selects a winner to compete nationally and potentially internationally.

<!-- ¶14 -->
These educational programs will be designed to inspire future careers in environmental services and support local Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics (STEM) education initiatives. Here are some additional educational opportunities we have identified:

<!-- ¶15 -->
- µ<sup>**“First Day Fulton” Back-to-School Events and Supplies Program:**</sup> Provide financial or in-kind donations to back-to-school supply drives, emphasizing STEM programs that connect with water and environmental sciences. Include educational materials on water treatment and sustainability in back-to-school kits.

<!-- ¶16 -->
- µ<sup>**Field Trips:**Integrate facility tours (Big Creek WRF, JCEC, or Little River</sup> WRF) into CNC’s school field trip offerings.

<!-- ¶17 -->
- µ<sup>**Joint STEM and career exploration initiatives around “Careers in**</sup> **Clean Water”**

<!-- ¶18 -->
- –Host a career day featuring water treatment engineers, operators, and environmental scientists, showing students potential pathways into water and wastewater management.

<!-- ¶19 -->
- **Summer Youth Job Training/Shadowing Program:** Collaborate with youth job training programs to offer summer internships to County high schools at the plant, allowing young people to gain practical experience in environmental sciences, water management, and engineering.

<!-- ¶20 -->
- µ<sup>**Digital Learning:**Develop video tours of Big Creek WRF and JCEC,</sup> hosted by CNC’s educators, for use in schools and online learning platforms. These video tours would highlight innovative technologies like membrane filtration, UV disinfection, and gravity-fed treatment.

<!-- ¶21 -->
- µ<sup>**Atlanta Science Festival:**Continue successful partnership to explain</sup> “Journey of our Water” and connect it the plant.
