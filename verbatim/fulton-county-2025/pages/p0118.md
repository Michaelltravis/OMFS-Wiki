All key staff will participate in transition activities so everyone is familiar with the systems and tools being implemented, as well as with the County’s assets. At the same time, we will establish all necessary programs related to laboratory practices and testing, regulatory notification, and on-site communications plans. We will also finalize emergency response plans while having all required notifications, communications, and operational requirements fully functional by the commencement date. These plans are developed using JC Solutions’ expertise on the topics but are tailored to each of our clients by having open discussions to ensure goals and expectations are defined, understood, and aligned.

###### **_Transition Team_**

<!-- ¶11 -->
To ensure the success of this important partnership, we offer an onsite transition leadership team led by Transition Manager, **Mike Boven** , one of our most seasoned transition managers with a proven track record of smooth transitions. **Mike also holds a Georgia Wastewater Operator Class 1 Certification** . By having a fully staffed transition team, shown in **Exhibit 2-46** , we minimize distractions to the onsite team so that they can focus on day-to-day operations while JC Solutions' systems, tools, and processes are implemented along with training the onsite team.

<!-- ¶12 -->
The transition team is comprised of seasoned experts who have successfully transitioned numerous O&M facilities and who bring specific expertise in operations, maintenance, asset management, safety, SCADA control, sludge management, odor control, human resources, labor relations, and administration support. They will remain available after the transition period for questions, training, follow-up assistance, and continued support to the onsite project staff.

<!-- ¶13 -->
**Kelly Irving** is our Senior Operation Director and will assist Mike Boven with the transition. Kelly brings more than 30 years of experience in water and wastewater operations, management, and executive leadership— including successfully leading multiple transitions from public to private management and between contract operators. Most recently, she oversaw the complex transition of a regional utility serving 13 communities and approximately 50 employees under a 20-year contract, navigating significant deferred maintenance and operational challenges with confidence and care.
