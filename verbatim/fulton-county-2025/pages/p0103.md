###### **_Reporting and Transparency_**

<!-- ¶8 -->
We will also deliver a comprehensive suite of reports to ensure transparency into our operational performance and regulatory compliance— providing the County with tools for proactive management and a successful partnership as outlined in outlined in **Exhibit 2-44** .

<!-- ¶9 -->
Below we briefly summarize our approach to key reports:

<!-- ¶10 -->
- µ<sup>**Monthly Operating Report.**By the fifth day of each month, we’ll deliver a comprehensive</sup> operating report, providing the County with full visibility into key operational data, compliance, staffing updates, safety metrics, maintenance activities, and any significant alarms. This regular reporting fosters transparency and builds trust in our shared commitment to operational excellence.

<!-- ¶11 -->
- µ<sup>**Monthly Operations, Maintenance, and Asset Management Report.**Our project manager</sup> will provide a detailed monthly report covering all key areas of operations, including O&M activities, staffing changes, training, health and safety, daily inspections, open maintenance work orders, equipment status, and permit compliance. This report will be designed to keep the County fully informed and engaged in all aspects of facility management ( **Exhibit 2-44** ).
