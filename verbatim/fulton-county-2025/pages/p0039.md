###### AQUADNA SMART SYSTEM OPTIMIZATION

<!-- ¶6 -->
Our AquaDNA solution delivers comprehensive protection against costly overflows and system failures through real-time monitoring and predictive analytics powered by Palantir’s Foundry platform. JC Solutions’ investment includes annual licensing costs, equipment/installation for automatic deragging devices at four vulnerable pump stations, flow and level measurement instrumentation, and ongoing calibration and optimization by JC Solutions experts. The system’s dynamic waveform monitoring technology proactively implements pump reversals upon detection of flow or power changes—a unique approach proven across nearly 100 U.S. installations that dramatically reduces emergency callouts, decreases power consumption, extends equipment life, and prevents sanitary sewer overflows. By integrating SCADA information, rain gauge data, level sensors, and weather forecasts, our machine learning algorithms continuously optimize pump station setpoints, transforming raw data into actionable intelligence that enhances operational efficiency and maximizes system performance.
