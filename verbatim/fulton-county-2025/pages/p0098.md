###### **EMERGENCY RESPONSE AND DISASTER PREPAREDNESS PLAN**

<!-- ¶2 -->
The JC Solutions team is committed to ensuring the County’s wastewater facilities are fully prepared for any emergency. As a FEMA-certified Disaster Response and Recovery contractor, we bring proven expertise in managing critical events—including hurricanes, flooding, pandemics, power loss, and system failures—through comprehensive planning, real-time response, and rapid recovery.

<!-- ¶3 -->
Our Emergency Response Plan (ERP), developed in compliance with Article 50.14 and Exhibit 4, will address federal, state, and local regulatory requirements while aligning with the County’s specific expectations. This includes coordination protocols, contingency planning, crisis communication, and clear lines of authority for swift, unified emergency response. The ERP will be finalized within 60 days of contract award and updated annually per the contract.

<!-- ¶4 -->
**Exhibit 2-42.** _Key Topics in the ERP_

<!-- ¶5 -->
|**EVENT**|**APPROACH**|
|---|---|
|**Chemical Spill**|Site-specific SDS and emergency cleanup protocols; immediate reporting per OSHA and local law.|
|**Asset/Equipment Failure**|Initial and periodic condition assessments; spares strategy for critical systems to enable quick restoration.|
|**Fire Response**|Staff trained in fire prevention/suppression; coordination with local fire departments; recovery planning in place.|
|**Flooding**|Storm hardening and drainage maintenance; regional staff available for response and staffing support.|
|**Hurricane**|Emergency power readiness; mock events (“dry runs”); continuity of operations during outages.|
|**Pandemic/Health Emergency**|Health and safety protocols, quarantine guidelines, emergency staffing, and access to regional support.|
|**Process Failure**|Real-time SCADA and trend monitoring, immediate testing, chemical optimization, and use of regional SMEs.|
|**Power Failure**|Coordination with the County to deploy generators for 60 MGD capacity; 24/7 staffing until power resumes.|
|**Emergency Contacts**|Posted emergency numbers, including key JC Solutions and County contacts, 911, hospitals, and first responders.|
|**Emergency Equipment**|Pre-arranged 24/7 service contracts for vendor-supplied specialty equipment or rentals.|
|**Continuity of Operations Plan (COOP)**|Clear succession, staffing augmentation plan, subcontractor coordination, and emergency notification protocols.|

###### **_Emergency Notification and Reporting Protocols_**

<!-- ¶7 -->
Per Article 50.14(C) of the contract, Jacobs will:

<!-- ¶8 -->
- Provide immediate verbal notice to the County’s Contract Administrator following any emergency.

<!-- ¶9 -->
- Submit a written report within five (5) business days outlining the event, actions taken, and recovery progress.

<!-- ¶10 -->
- In the case of personal injury or a major safety incident, Jacobs will immediately report known details and follow up with full documentation.
