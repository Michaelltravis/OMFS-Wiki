**Records and Version Control.** All documents—including SOPs, QA/QC logs, calibration records, and compliance reports—will be maintained within a secure, version-controlled SharePoint site accessible to authorized County personnel. Document management will comply with the Open Records Act and all audit requirements specified in Article 50.12 and Article 14.

<!-- ¶3 -->

<!-- ¶4 -->
- µ<sup>Early detection of risks</sup>

<!-- ¶5 -->
- µ<sup>Immediate implementation of corrective actions</sup>

<!-- ¶6 -->
- µ<sup>Alignment with Article 53 performance standards</sup>
