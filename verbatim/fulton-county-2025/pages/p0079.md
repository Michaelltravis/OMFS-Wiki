###### **ODOR AND NOISE MITIGATION**

<!-- ¶2 -->
JC Solutions recognizes the importance of odor control at the North Fulton facilities, especially given the residential, recreational, and commercial nature of adjacent areas. Significant investments in odor control have been made at all three sites, and particularly at Big Creek WRF and JCEC which cover most, if not all, processes to capture and treat odors from plant operations. This approach, however, is not bulletproof. Although Monthly Operating Reports show no odor complaints in 2024, the conditions for which the existing odor control facilities were designed may have changed (e.g., odor characteristics, meteorological conditions), and as such, opportunities may exist to improve the performance of existing systems. As part of our proactive approach to O&M, we are including a value-add to study odor mitigation strategies to ascertain effective odor mitigation.

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **_Odor and Noise Control Program_**

<!-- ¶4 -->
JC Solutions will routinely monitor the plants for odors including the presence of hydrogen sulfide (H2S) and nuisance odors using a portable meter. Performance of the existing odor control facilities will also be monitored. Operators will make routine patrols of the plant and the boundary using both the portable meters as well as their own senses to determine if H2S or other odor conditions are detectable. Once per shift, JC Solutions will make an off-site patrol to specified potential odor source locations in neighboring vicinities during the known high odor season using a portable H2S meter. Any

<!-- ¶5 -->
detection of abnormal odor or H2S readings will be cause for JC Solutions to implement a corrective action plan. Written guidelines will be prepared for operators to follow if any of these elements are detected. JC Solutions will record each plant patrol finding, including any measured readings of noise, H2S, or detection of odor on an Odor Inspection Log. JC Solutions will sign the log for each patrol indicating time of patrol, weather conditions, wind speed, and direction.

###### ODOR EMISSIONS STUDY

<!-- ¶12 -->
The JC Solutions team will complete an Odor Emissions Study, at contract start. Jacobs’ odor control experts will perform source sampling and odor dispersion modeling to assess likely sources of odor and performance of existing odor control facilities, and these studies are included in our offer (a value at no added cost). Samples will be collected from odorous treatment processes and analyzed for odor concentrations and reduced sulfur compounds. The sample results will be used in the odor dispersion model along with site specific hourly atmospheric conditions from the past five years to assess the offsite impact from the odor sources. This is particularly relevant for JCEC where the last odor study was performed in 2009 and conditions may have changed, from meteorological conditions to the odor characteristics and loadings to the existing facilities. Modeling will allow us to quickly identify the odor sources with the risk of causing off-site odor complaints and take a proactive approach on odor control.

<!-- ¶13 -->
Our step-by-step process will identify sources of odor including the expected dispersion. Using this information, improvements can be prioritized to have the most significant positive impact on the neighboring community. An example of these improvements include buffer zones where vegetation can further support odor mitigation measures.
