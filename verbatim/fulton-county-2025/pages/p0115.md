###### 2.9 **TRANSITION PLAN WITH APPROACH AND TIMELINE OF ACTIVITIES NECESSARY TO ACHIEVE THE COMMENCEMENT DATE**

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **_Fluid Continuity: Enabling Uninterrupted Excellence in Water Services_**

<!-- ¶4 -->
A smooth transition with a detailed plan and a dedicated transition team is one of the first steps in a successful partnership between the County and JC Solutions. We have a thorough understanding of the County’s needs and expectations and have developed a plan to deliver a high level of service on day one. We leverage the expertise of our transition experts to develop an approach that is tailored to the specific needs of the project and client, supported by additional resources that will supplement the local team. This helps confirm transition activities occur in parallel while project tasks are seamlessly implemented. **Our transition plan described in this section reflects a highly organized process that couples our past experience transitioning operations with a focus on addressing specific project and client needs.** It efficiently organizes the local staff and provides sufficient qualified resources in all disciplines to assure the County of superior operations, maintenance, and administrative coverage for the managed assets. At the same time, this additional team of SMEs enables all Jacobs’ tools and strategies to be implemented at the project level without impacting the workload or effectiveness of the local team.
