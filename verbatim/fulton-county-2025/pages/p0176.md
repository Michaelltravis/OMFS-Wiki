Jacobs designed and built the Spokane County Regional Water Reclamation Facility (SCRWRF) and now operates and maintains the facilities. The 8.5-mgd facility is expandable to 24-mgd and uses state-of-theart membrane bioreactor and chemically enhanced primary treatment to reduce phosphorus levels in the effluent to less than 50 parts per billion (PPB).

In 1999, Spokane County Utilities began planning a new water reclamation facility when the City of Spokane decided that its existing facility would not have adequate capacity to serve the long-term regional wastewater treatment needs. Excessive phosphorus in the Spokane River was causing severe dissolved oxygen depletion in the Spokane River and Lake Spokane. Phosphorus and other nutrients, such as ammonia, encourage the growth of algae and other aquatic plants. When the plants decompose, they use up oxygen in the water, suffocating fish. After several years of planning, Spokane County Utilities selected Jacobs in 2008 to design, build, and operate a new treatment plant that will significantly reduce pollution going to the Spokane River and Lake Spokane.
