###### Terrell Gibbs, PhD, PE | Workforce Development and Training (continued)

<!-- ¶2 -->
Project Principal | Municipal Separate Storm Sewer System (MS4) and Stormwater Management Plan Compliance Program, City of Lithonia | Lithonia, GA. Responsible for successful resource planning and project delivery for the City of Lithonia’s MS4 Management Plan and Compliance Program. CERM’s effort included conducting the City’s MS4 Inspection and Inventory Updates, developing the City’s maintenance and repair plan, and producing the City’s annual report.

<!-- ¶3 -->
Deputy Commissioner Office of Linear Infrastructure Operations | City of Atlanta Department of Watershed Management | Atlanta, GA. Responsible for providing executive level technical and operational department direction for the Office of Linear Infrastructure Operations, an operation responsible for the repair and maintenance of City of Atlanta’s 2,700+ miles of water lines, 2,200+ miles of sewer lines 1,100+ miles of storm lines 24,000+ hydrants, 47,000+ manholes, 35,000+ catch basins, and 62,000+ valves. Responsible for managing a $45 million operating budget as well as implementing operating policies and procedures, planning and managing budgets, directing operations, developing leadership and technical talent, developing and implementing capital projects, and monitoring and measuring performance of the operations of the Office to ensure efficiency and effectiveness.

<!-- ¶4 -->
Watershed Director – Distribution Systems | City of Atlanta Department of Watershed Management | Atlanta, GA. Responsible for managing a $15 million operating budget as well as providing direct management responsibility for the day-to-day operations of the water distribution system (2,700+ miles of water lines, 24,000+ hydrants, and 62,000+ valves) for the City of Atlanta.

<!-- ¶5 -->
Deputy Director of Operations and Engineering | Rockdale County Department of Water Resources | Conyers, GA. Managed the day to day operations of the engineering, water distribution and collections O&M program, treatment works (which to include a 22.1-mgd water treatment plant, five wastewater treatment facilities) and the County’s Water and Sewer Capital Improvement Program.
