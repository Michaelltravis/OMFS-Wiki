###### **_Detailed Schedule of Audits_**

<!-- ¶18 -->
The project will be audited by an experienced, independent auditor approved by the County for compliance with the requirements of the Services Management System. The auditor will prepare and implement an audit schedule for each year of the contract, with at least one audit being performed yearly. Typically, our new projects are audited six months after startup, and the frequency of follow-up audits is determined by the project’s score across O&M and management areas.

<!-- ¶19 -->
The auditor will submit the audit schedule to the County for approval no later than 60 business days prior to the commencement of each fiscal year. The auditor’s report will be submitted to the County within five business days after JC Solutions receives the report.

###### **_Environmental Risk Management Approach_**

<!-- ¶21 -->
We evaluate project risk from a number of perspectives, including regulatory compliance, financial, asset management, safety, client relations, project manager’s strength, and general contract compliance, as measured by the annual review process (C3PR). Each of those areas is overseen by SMEs, tracking information and metrics that are collected, summarized, and aggregated to generate a composite risk score. Reports are then shared with regional leadership to validate the data and develop resolutions to address issues and bring projects into compliance.
