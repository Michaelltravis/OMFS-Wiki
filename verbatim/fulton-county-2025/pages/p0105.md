###### **PUBLIC EDUCATION AND COMMUNITY OUTREACH PLAN**

<!-- ¶2 -->
JC Solutions is committed to fostering a transparent, responsive, and proactive relationship with the citizens of the County, County staff, regulatory agencies, and other key stakeholders. Our Public Relations and Community Outreach Plan is designed to ensure that all community concerns as they relate to the County’s managed assets described in RFP Section 3.3.2 are addressed with professionalism, empathy, and urgency.

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **_A Team that is Community-Focused and Ready to Deliver_**

<!-- ¶4 -->
We bring you in-house communications professionals embedded within the technical team of engineering and operations experts who are also well-versed in the latest trends and tools in community engagement and outreach. We also understand the technical considerations in operating water reclamation facilities and how to best explain them. Proposed Community Relations/PR Lead and Jacobs’ national Communications Practice Leader Marci Davis crafted the approach and supported outreach and engagement during the construction of the Johns Creek Environmental Campus. She is also an active volunteer on the Public Education Committee for the GA Association of Water Professionals and has an extensive network of utility communicators and educators, which offers access and insights into best practices for other utilities across the state.
