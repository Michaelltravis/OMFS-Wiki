###### **2. Commitment to meeting regulatory compliance with expertise and track record to back it up**

<!-- ¶2 -->
JC Solutions will assume responsibility for meeting permit requirements, including NPDES, air quality, water quality, and stormwater. We will ensure full compliance with EPA, Georgia EPD, and County regulations to avoid permit violations, fines, or environmental risks. We have a past record of stellar regulatory compliance and environmental stewardship. The County can rely on our robust internal regulatory audit processes and QA/QC protocol that are driven by leadership oversight, as well as the extensive compliance training, we offer our onsite teams. Our approach ensures compliance including routine and emergency sampling/testing as required by state and federal regulations and provides proactive management of odor control and noise reduction.
