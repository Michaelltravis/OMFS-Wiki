###### 2.4 **APPROACH TO PROJECT EXECUTION AND ADMINISTRATION**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Our approach to project execution and administration is built on decades of delivering compliant, cost-effective O&M services for facilities similar in size and complexity—including deep expertise with membrane bioreactor (MBR) technology. As North America’s second-largest O&M service provider, Jacobs uniquely integrates operations, engineering, consulting, program management, and construction services, enabling us to deliver significant value across every phase of you’re the North Fulton facilities’ lifecycle. Beyond technical proficiency, we bring a partnership mindset grounded in accountability, transparency, and a community-focused, people-first culture.

<!-- ¶3 -->
Fulton County can confidently rely on us for 100% compliance as we drive operational excellence, optimized performance, reduced risk, and sustained asset reliability well into the future. We’ve developed structured frameworks, processes, and a comprehensive suite of tools to plan, execute, monitor, and continually improve our operations, summarized in **Exhibit 2-1** . More detail on the key elements of our project delivery approach is given at the end of **_Section 2_** .

<!-- ¶7 -->
###### **Health, Safety and Security**

<!-- ¶8 -->
A framework for compliance with Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) and other safety regulations, vulnerability assessments, job safety analysis, safety training, emergency preparedness and response, and other safety related functions.

<!-- ¶9 -->
###### **Compliance and Reporting**

<!-- ¶10 -->
A program of compliance for pollution prevention and waste minimization. Includes analysis and review checklists for all permits, report preparation for regulatory agencies, and environmental compliance training for all employees.

<!-- ¶11 -->
###### **Operations Management**

<!-- ¶12 -->
Documents treatment strategies and process control functions through standard operating procedures (SOPs), unit process control procedures (UPCPs), and treatment system optimization.

<!-- ¶13 -->
###### **Maintenance and Asset Management**

<!-- ¶14 -->
Integrates maintenance scheduling, documentation, and tracking; inventory control; and equipment condition assessment into a computerized database that allows us to maximize equipment performance and minimize downtime.

<!-- ¶15 -->
###### **Laboratory Management**

<!-- ¶16 -->
Standard procedures for sample collection, analysis, and documentation with our quality assurance/quality control (QA/QC) and chemical hygiene plans.

<!-- ¶17 -->
###### **Human Resources**

<!-- ¶18 -->
A program for the success of all of our employees as individuals and as a team, including recruitment, orientation, and training; standard training needs assessments for all employees; training programs for certi'ication, ethics, compliance, safety, management and succession; and performance appraisals and professional development.

<!-- ¶19 -->
###### **Financial Management**

<!-- ¶20 -->
Systems and approaches to manage project cost through national purchasing power, documentation of contractual requirements, and continuous monitoring.

<!-- ¶21 -->
###### **Project Management**

<!-- ¶22 -->
Transparent communication including daily, weekly, monthly, severe weather, and annual client reports, internal measures of project performance, and plans to improve project performance beyond the requirements of our contract.

<!-- ¶23 -->
###### **Public Education, Community Involvement, and Media Relations**

<!-- ¶24 -->
A robust public education and community involvement program developed in partnership with the County, including volunteering, charitable donations, and facility tours to bene'it the community.

<!-- ¶25 -->
###### **Customer Service**

<!-- ¶26 -->
A robust program to demonstrate our commitment to be a good neighbor and responsiveness to community concerns.
