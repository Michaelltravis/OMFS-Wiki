###### **_Thoughtful and Smooth Step-by-Step Transition Process for New Employees_**

<!-- ¶3 -->
While the physical process of the transition is hugely important, we view this step as more than a transfer of responsibilities from the County to us. **JC Solutions’ transition is the first step in the transformation of the County’s operations to a high-performing utility. One that is focused on leveraging the tools and capabilities of the larger Jacobs and driving ongoing improvement. As such, we invest significantly in the implementation of our systems, tools, and processes, coupled with a strong focus on training and the development of our culture.** The result is a high-performing local team that is engaged, empowered to deliver on all project needs, fully integrated into the region, and well equipped to leverage the strength of Jacobs’ technical capabilities.

<!-- ¶4 -->
Transition planning will start immediately after notification of award to allow SMEs and teams to assemble, organize, and prepare to address all aspects of the transition on the contract date. Nevertheless, our approach includes the integration of due diligence and transition resources so that even prior to award, our transition experts are already engaged in the project. A key aspect of our transition is to provide full transparency through open and frequent communications in a structure that will be adapted to the specific needs and expectations of the County.

###### **_Tapping the Value of Existing Staff_**

<!-- ¶6 -->
**We believe that the institutional knowledge of existing staff is a vital asset, and we are committed to offering competitive wages and benefits to qualified and interested candidates.** At JC Solutions, we recognize the knowledge and invaluable contributions of the employees currently working at the facility. **Our goal is to retain as many of these talented individuals as possible, leveraging their qualifications, capabilities, and the specific needs of the County.** Our commitment to local teams is evident in seamlessly transitioning over a dozen projects in the last three years, with a 90% conversion rate for qualified employees receiving job offers in the water sector's O&M services.

<!-- ¶7 -->
Our approach to building a strong team focuses on a blend of existing employees who will transition to JC Solutions, existing JC Solutions resources, and targeted recruitment efforts to fill any potential gaps. While unlikely, should only a limited number of current staff choose to join us, we will implement a comprehensive recruiting strategy that starts with leveraging existing JC Solutions employees and is supplemented externally, as detailed in our **_Long-term Staffing and Succession Planning Practices_** . To proactively address any staffing needs, we have identified several existing resources that would help us fill project roles and have initiated a focused pipeline search to identify local talent, so that we can fill any critical roles effectively. We also maintain a robust network of local providers who can support our team as necessary.

<!-- ¶8 -->
**During the transition phase, we will leverage our extensive regional and technical resources to temporarily fill any unexpected vacancies, ensuring that operations remain uninterrupted from day one.** This proactive strategy not only addresses unexpected staffing needs but also showcases the reliability and depth of JC Solutions' resources, reinforcing our commitment to seamless operations for the County. This approach also allows us to help the local team assimilate the strategies, tools, and the approach the team uses to deliver superior operations and provide a high-quality service.

<!-- ¶9 -->
Both existing and new employees will be supported with resources drawn from within JC Solutions. We will implement a proven six-step process to provide a high level of communication which in turn minimizes stress and results in a smooth workforce transition.
