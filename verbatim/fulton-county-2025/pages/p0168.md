A project of this magnitude needs a reliable and experienced partner, and we have the right resources, expertise, and knowledge to be yours. ###### **JACOBS WASTEWATER ENGINEERING AND MBR EXPERIENCE**

<!-- ¶6 -->
Jacobs’ membrane bioreactor experience is unparalleled. Our thought leadership began in the late 1990s, when we performed demonstration testing and designed several small MBR facilities. In 2003, Jordan Jones and Goulding (now Jacobs) assisted the County in soliciting proposals for technology and design/ build services for the JCEC. In 2004, we converted a conventional treatment plant in Traverse City, MI to MBR. About the same time, we designed our first MBR in Georgia, the 2.5-mgd plant in Woodstock. Many larger plant designs followed, including Gwinnett County’s 22-mgd Yellow River WRF and even larger plants in King County, WA and North Las Vegas, NV.
