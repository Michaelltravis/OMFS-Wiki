**EXHIBIT 1-14.** _Jacobs’ Tools for Operational, Process, and Compliance Success Built on Best Practices and Decades of Experience_

<!-- ¶2 -->
TOOL/PROCEDURE WHAT JACOBS WILL DO  BENEFIT TO OCWUT<br><!-- End of picture text -->

<!-- ¶3 -->
|**UPCPs**|UPCPs outline our operations road map, establish the KPIs and parameters we<br>track, drive process adjustments, direct continuous improvement, and clearly<br>establish lines of communication and control. UPCPs also provide the detail<br>operators and maintenance staff need to make informed decisions.|Documented procedures and process targets shared with OCWUT,<br>management, O&M, and laboratory staff confirm a common purpose and<br>goal. Detailed information maximizes efficiency and minimizes errors<br>and downtime.|
|---|---|---|
|**Mass Balance**|A mass balance solids report provides a comprehensive accounting of solids<br>entering, accumulating, and leaving the system at each facility.|Enables accurate solids inventory control and identifies accumulation<br>points relative to design capacity. Supports informed process adjustments,<br>including wasting rates and dewatering schedules, and aids in forecasting<br>solids handling needs—particularly important given the interdependencies<br>between North Canadian, Deer Creek, and Chisholm Creek solids programs.|
|**Sampling Plan**|Defines sampling locations, sampling map, parameters at each location,<br>analytical methods, chain-of-custody techniques, and preservation procedures.<br>Developed in coordination with the Sampling and Testing QA/QC Plan<br>(Schedule 3).|A properly written and implemented sampling plan is critical to compliant,<br>efficient facility operation. Reliable, accurate data starts with representative<br>sampling—the foundation of effective solids balances and process control.|
|**Sample Tracking Tool (STT)**|Used by operations staff to monitor and track process and compliance<br>sampling and analysis requirements. Developed at the start of each year;<br>samples are checked off by the collector and verified same day by a second<br>independent party.|Confirms that samples and analyses are performed at the locations and<br>frequencies outlined in the NPDES permits and Scope of Work. Provides<br>quality assurance to make sure nothing is missed.|
|**Data Management**<br>**(Lab + SCADA/ Instruments)**|Data collection driven by the Sampling Plan, collected through lab analysis or<br>online instrumentation and entered manually or directly from SCADA. Jacobs will<br>use Hach WIMS to support our process control system (PCS).|Direct access to data management software allows OCWUT to query the<br>database for specific data. Regular reports provide a clear summary of how<br>each plant and unit process performs is performing. Data exported into<br>our PCS enables regional support staff to analyze trends and assist on-site<br>operations in real-time decisions and optimization.|
|**SOPs**|SOPs document safe work practices and consistent operations of equipment and<br>systems. They contain precautionary safety steps followed by startup, shutdown,<br>and routine monitoring procedures, plus the detailed information needed to<br>complete work properly, safely, and efficiently.|Critical SOPs will be developed first during transition, with new SOPs<br>added as required, such as when new CIP equipment starts up or regulatory<br>requirements evolve. SOPs maintain consistent procedures across all four<br>facilities and Witcher, supporting staff safety, operations performance, and<br>equipment reliability.|
|**Operator Round Sheets**|Daily round sheets consist of readings, observations, and preventive maintenance<br>tasks that give early warning of potential process upsets or equipment problems.<br>Round sheets also capture housekeeping and safety actions.|Printed daily through the CMMS and completed at least once per shift.<br>Unusual observations are recorded and reported immediately to the<br>operations supervisor. Data is uploaded into the CMMS, turning field<br>observations into timely process adjustments and work orders.|
|**Operations Resiliency Plan**|Establishes a proactive framework for managing hydraulic and solids<br>loading during storm events that push facilities beyond normal operating<br>conditions. Defines strategies for flow equalization, process optimization, and<br>solids handling.|Supports smooth coordination of key actions—adjusting return sludge<br>rates, chemical dosing, and storage utilization—based on forecasted or<br>real-time conditions. Minimizes risks of untreated bypasses and effluent<br>quality degradation at all four facilities and Witcher.|
