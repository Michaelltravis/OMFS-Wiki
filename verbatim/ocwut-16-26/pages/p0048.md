<!-- ¶3 -->
###### SAFETY AND SECURITY EXPERTISE BEHIND YOUR ON-SITE TEAM

<!-- ¶4 -->
Safe, secure facilities protect everything that matters to OCWUT—your people, your service, your compliance record, and your community’s trust. Jacobs will build that protection into daily operations from the start. We will implement and maintain a Safety Program and Plan, Security Plan, Drug Detection and Deterrence Plan, and Emergency Operating Plan that aligns with the RFP, the Service Agreement, OSHA requirements, applicable local requirements, and OCWUT’s site-specific operating conditions. **Our approach will turn those plans into active operating tools that guide field work, control access, strengthen accountability, and support reliable performance every day.** We are committed to providing OCWUT with the same proven industry-low operational safety record that we take pride in at facilities we operate nationwide. **Safety at your facilities will be our #1 focus – we want everyone to go home to their families safely every day.**

###### **Leadership, Accountability, and Safe Work**

<!-- ¶10 -->
Strong safety performance starts with visible leadership and clear accountability. Our project leaders, Safety Manager, and site leaders will set expectations early, stay engaged in the field, and reinforce safe work practices through daily communication, routine observations, and disciplined follow-through. Supervisors will review the work ahead, confirm staff understand the hazards and controls, and step in quickly when conditions change.

<!-- ¶11 -->
We’ll use activity hazard analyses, pre-task plans, standard operating procedures, and stop-work authority to keep work controlled and consistent. For high-risk tasks, we will define the work steps, identify the hazards, assign the controls, and verify readiness before work begins. We’ll support this effort with regular inspections, formal audits, corrective action tracking, and organized documentation, including OSHA 300/300A logs, hazard communication records, training records, and audit findings. **This structure will give OCWUT a practical, auditable safety program that supports safe execution and continuous improvement.**
