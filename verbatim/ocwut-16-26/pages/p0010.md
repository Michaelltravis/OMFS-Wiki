###### **REQUIRED PLANS: BUILT FOR OCWUT, READY ON DAY ONE**

<!-- ¶2 -->
**Draft Transition Plan:**  Transition Manager Kelly Irving to lead a phased mobilization—from governance and due diligence through full operational control—with risk-based management addressing the 10 highest-probability risks Positions offered to all qualified incumbent staff A jointly signed baseline condition assessment completed before the Commencement Date

<!-- ¶3 -->
**Staffing & Training Plan (Schedule 10):**  Jacobs workforce of 96 FTEs (plus another 13 biosolids staff from our subcontractor) with 24/7 coverage at every facility  A high-performance culture built on competitive compensation, BeyondZero® safety, certification incentives for operators and mechanics, and cross-training across facilities—shifting the team from reactive response to proactive ownership of plant performance  Jacobs’ dedicated Resource Planning Group to fill vacancies fast and prevent turnover that has plagued current O&M

<!-- ¶4 -->
**Sludge Management Plan (Schedule 14):**  An integrated, generation-throughdisposal program covering all four facilities and ~170 permitted land application sites  Facility-specific strategies for North Canadian’s H₂S and plastics issues, Deer Creek’s hydrogen peroxide dosing protocol, Chisholm Creek’s liquid transfer coordination, and South Canadian’s construction-period continuity  All tied to a tiered Storage Management Protocol that keeps pad utilization well below Schedule 21 thresholds

<!-- ¶5 -->
**Solids Management Plan (Schedule 21):**  Strategic framework for dewatering, storage, hauling, and disposal as one coordinated system with clear operational triggers and escalation protocols aligned with our Odor Control and Process Control Plans  Solids, process performance, and odor managed together—and positioned to guide OCWUT through the transition from land application to landfill disposal and the Deer Creek heat drying integration

<!-- ¶6 -->
**Operational Integration Plan (Schedules 16 & 18):**  Structured, repeatable process for bringing each major CIP project—onsite hypochlorite generation at North Canadian and Deer Creek, the Deer Creek heat drying facility, and the systemwide biosolids disposal transition—from construction into daily operations  Detailed staffing impacts, maintenance adjustments, training sequences, and risk mitigation for each project  Backed by direct PSI MicroClor® operating experience at similar projects

<!-- ¶7 -->
BENEFIT TO OCWUT: _Plans built from verified, facility-specific intelligence—not boilerplate. Delivered on schedule, coordinated with the OCWUT General Manager, and structured to drive accountability from day one._
