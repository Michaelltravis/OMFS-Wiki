###### _QA/QC Plan Development and Internal Audit Program_

<!-- ¶2 -->
Upon contract award, Jacobs will develop a comprehensive QA/QC Plan (draft due November 2, 2026; final due December 31, 2026, per Schedule 3). The plan will define how we monitor daily operations, maintenance activities, regulatory compliance, safety practices, and reporting obligations. It will specify internal review procedures prior to report submittal and methods for tracking every required deliverable.

<!-- ¶3 -->
Our QA/QC program is anchored by an Annual Quality Audit—a structured process we’ve developed and refined over our O&M group’s 40-year history. This audit is quantifiable, repeatable, and leads to actionable results. A dedicated QA/QC team investigates 70 specific topics with a focus on contractual requirements, including training, project documentation, succession planning, communication plans, inventory, CMMS maintenance, work order status, sampling plan status, process control plans, data management, and business and budget management. The audit follows a four-step process, shown in **Exhibit 1-6** .

###### _Compliance Verification Before Report Submittal_

<!-- ¶14 -->
Every report delivered to OCWUT, including monthly operating reports, NPDES Discharge Monitoring Reports , Payment Requests, Maintenance and Repair Fund expenditure reports, Rehabilitation and Replacement expenditure reports, will undergo internal review before submittal. QA/QC Manager Josh will verify data accuracy, completeness, and consistency against source records. This is especially critical for the Monthly Payment Request, which includes self-reported compliance with Performance Requirements. The RFP makes clear that failure to accurately report compliance will result in consequences up to and including breach. We take that standard seriously and will build verification checkpoints into every reporting workflow.

<!-- ¶15 -->
###### **Reporting and Communication**

<!-- ¶16 -->
Transparency is the foundation of our partnership with OCWUT. From day one, we’ll establish a communication framework designed around a simple principle: no surprises. You’ll know what’s happening at your facilities, why it’s happening, and what we’re doing about it before you have to ask.

<!-- ¶17 -->
Our communication approach connects Jacobs leadership to the appropriate OCWUT counterparts at every level—what we call a “zippering” model ( **Exhibit 1-2** ). Project Director **Steve** and Executive Sponsor **Kevin Dahl** will engage directly with OCWUT’s General Manager and OCWUT leadership. Project Manager **David** will work closely with the General Manager’s designee. Plant managers and functional leads will coordinate with OCWUT’s operational and engineering staff. **Rick Warner** , as Strategic Advisor, will provide additional senior-level support as needed.
