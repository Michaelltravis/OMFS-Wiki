###### **FROM PROCESS CHALLENGE TO RELIABLE BIOSOLIDS PERFORMANCE**

<!-- ¶9 -->
In Vancouver, Jacobs turned biosolids processing into a dependable, high-performing operation. Through a strong O&M program, the plant processed 100% of the biosolids it produced through the incinerator year over year, excluding only planned diversions during annual outages. The team also drove measurable efficiency gains, including a recent 20% reduction in polymer costs by bypassing the Mannich system for emulsion totes. To strengthen reliability and control across the facility, Jacobs also leveraged broader technical resources to implement a plantwide SCADA and PLC upgrade with new automated programming—helping improve performance, reduce manual intervention, and keep this critical process under control.

In 2024, Jacobs rapidly stabilized and transformed the solids operations at Baltimore’s Back River WWTP—an underresourced, consent-driven facility facing an aggressive compliance timeline.
