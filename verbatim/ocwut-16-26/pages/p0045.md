###### **Process Control System Phased Performance Improvement Plan**

<!-- ¶7 -->
**Our operational approach to treatment process automation and intelligence is designed to deliver consistent regulatory compliance, resilient and adaptable treatment performance, and sustainable operational efficiency across OCWUT facilities.** We will integrate proven process engineering and operational discipline with modern automation and intelligence capabilities through a deliberate, phased roadmap. This approach emphasizes repeatable, measurable improvements that endure over the life of the facilities. Central to this strategy is the principle that automation and intelligence perform only as well as the process definition and data foundation beneath them. We will begin by clearly documenting treatment objectives, operational constraints, and process variability; validating measurement and instrumentation; and confirming data quality. Only once this foundation is established will we apply automation and advanced control strategies to reduce variability, improve responsiveness, and drive predictable outcomes without introducing unnecessary complexity or operational risk.
