###### FACILITY OVERVIEW

<!-- ¶5 -->
Jackson's water and wastewater systems serve nearly 150,000 residents and represent one of the most complex municipal utility turnarounds in the U.S. In 2022, the City of Jackson faced major reliability and compliance issues due to aging infrastructure, long-term underinvestment, and staffing shortages. The EPA appointed an interim third-party manager (ITPM) to oversee the system, and the ITPM established JXN Water to conduct the business of the ITPM. The water system includes the **50-MGD O.B. Curtis membrane water treatment plant (WTP), the 30-MGD J.H. Fewell conventional WTP, a well system, booster stations, and elevated and ground storage tanks** . The wastewater system includes **three wastewater treatment plants (WWTPs) totaling 65 MGD and 99 pump stations** .

<!-- ¶6 -->
###### **OWNER INFORMATION**

We also serve as **Lead and Copper Rule Compliance Program Manager** for JXN Water’s treatment plants, distribution tanks, and booster pumping stations. We have developed reporting frameworks to support the LSL inventory and upcoming replacement planning efforts for the Mississippi State Department of Health and EPA.

<!-- ¶8 -->
###### JACOBS' ROLE AND RELEVANCE TO OCWUT

<!-- ¶9 -->
Between December 2022 and August 2023, we provided interim O&M services as JXN Water identified the best path forward for the facilities under direction from the U.S. Department of Justice. Our initial support began in November 2022 with two maintenance specialists and **Steve Carpenter** serving as an advisor. In fall 2023, Jacobs began a long-term O&M contract valued at approximately $30M annually. Our sole-source selection reflected our performance during the interim operations phase, as well as our reputation, integrated capabilities, experience, and deep bench of skilled resources needed to keep the WTPs operating while coordinating with engineers and contractors on major upgrades. JXN Water engaged Jacobs to provide O&M and program management services for the City’s WTP, well system, booster stations, and water storage tanks. We manage **O&M, safety, compliance, laboratory services, asset management** , and **community engagement** .
