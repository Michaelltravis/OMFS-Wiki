###### **MANAGEMENT TEAM AND ADDITIONAL KEY PERSONNEL**

<!-- ¶4 -->
As your single point of accountability, **David** will be on site, engaged daily, and responsible for operations, maintenance, compliance, and client communication. He knows these facilities firsthand from **his previous work as project manager for this program** where he led targeted process improvements and strengthened operational reporting and communication. David brings **30 years of wastewater O&M leadership experience** managing similar multi-facility systems, including direct oversight of four regional facilities with 100+ MGD treatment capacity, peak wet-weather flows above 250 MGD, and 80+ staff. His experience aligns directly with OCWUT’s priorities. He has taken over underperforming contracts, stabilized operations, restored compliance, eliminated maintenance backlogs, and improved client communication and reporting. He’s supported transitions with staff training, SOP implementation, and maintenance of continuous operations. His hands-on leadership focuses on strong teams, reliability, and measurable results.
