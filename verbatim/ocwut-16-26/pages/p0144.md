###### **Experience with OSHG**

<!-- ¶2 -->
The planned transition to OSHG at multiple OCWUT facilities is one of the most operationally significant changes in this contract. It eliminates gaseous chlorine—and with it, the PSM/RMP compliance requirements at North Canadian and Deer Creek—while introducing a fundamentally different chemical management approach. Jacobs brings direct, relevant experience to this transition. **See Section 3 – Experience and Qualifications of the Firm and Management Team** for **detailed discussion of our OSHG O&M experience.**

<!-- ¶3 -->
Regardless of the specific systems OCWUT ultimately selects for Deer Creek and North Canadian, the core operational and maintenance considerations are consistent across OSHG technology. Our experience gives us direct knowledge of:

<!-- ¶4 -->
- <sup>**Electrolytic cell maintenance and replacement cycles**,including the proprietary cartridge systems (such as PSI/Microclor® units, where cartridges cost approximately</sup> $20,000 each) that require careful lifecycle management and warranty tracking.

<!-- ¶5 -->
- <sup>**Brine quality management and softening system upkeep**—critical for protecting cell life and maintaining consistent hypochlorite production.</sup>

<!-- ¶6 -->
- <sup>**Sodium hypochlorite storage and degradation management**,including strength monitoring, turnover rate optimization, and temperature control to maintain effective</sup> chlorine concentration.

<!-- ¶7 -->
- <sup>**Hydrogen gas ventilation and safety monitoring**—a key safety consideration for on-site generation systems that require dedicated monitoring and response protocols.</sup>

<!-- ¶8 -->
- <sup>**Integration with existing disinfection control systems and SCADA**—transitioning from gas feed control to liquid chemical dosing with automated residual feedback.</sup>

<!-- ¶9 -->
- <sup>**PSM/RMP transition**—asignificant benefit of the conversion is the elimination of gaseous chlorine PSM requirements at both North Canadian and Deer Creek. Currently,</sup> both facilities maintain PSM/RMP programs for their chlorine gas systems. Once OSHG replaces gas, those requirements and their associated compliance burden, training requirements, and safety risk are eliminated. This is a meaningful improvement in both worker safety and regulatory simplification for OCWUT.

<!-- ¶10 -->
We will leverage the direct operating experience we’ll build at South Canadian’s new PSI/Microclor® units (online by contract start) to inform our integration planning for the Deer Creek and North Canadian installations. This gives us facility-specific knowledge of the same equipment family before the next generation systems are commissioned.

###### **Transition Plan and Operational Readiness Approach**

<!-- ¶13 -->
A successful transition is about protecting performance, maintaining compliance, and giving OCWUT confidence that nothing slips during change. From the moment of award through day one and beyond, **Jacobs will deliver a structured, transparent, and fully resourced transition that keeps your facilities stable** while building the foundation for long-term performance.

<!-- ¶14 -->
We’ll **mobilize immediately upon notification of award** , activating a dedicated transition team supported by regional and technical specialists. This will allow transition activities to move forward in parallel with no disruption to ongoing operations. Every action during this period will focus on one outcome: **uninterrupted, compliant, and fully staffed operations on day one.**

<!-- ¶15 -->
We also encourage OCWUT to speak directly with clients who have selected Jacobs as their operating partner. Their firsthand experience with our transition process and resulting strong outcomes provides valuable insight into what partnering with Jacobs truly looks like.

<!-- ¶16 -->
###### **Structured, Phased Transition with Clear Accountability**

<!-- ¶17 -->
Jacobs will execute the transition through **coordinated phases that move from planning to full operational control** while maintaining continuous alignment with OCWUT.

<!-- ¶18 -->
During early transition, our team will establish governance, confirm communication protocols, and begin detailed validation of permits, compliance history, SOPs, asset data, and maintenance records. We’ll finalize staffing assignments, confirm certification coverage, coordinate facility and systems access, advance required draft and final plans, and maintain disciplined tracking of all transition deliverables and commitments.
