###### **Continuous Improvement and Performance Reporting**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Our maintenance program will evolve over the contract term through a deliberate three-phase progression:

<!-- ¶3 -->
- <sup>**Year 1: Stabilization.**Address deferred maintenance backlog,</sup> complete the baseline condition assessment, establish PM and PdM routines, and calibrate staffing to workload. Build the data foundation in NexGen that enables everything that follows.

<!-- ¶4 -->
- <sup>**Years 2–3: Optimization.**Right-size PM frequencies based on</sup> condition data and failure trends. Expand PdM coverage to additional equipment classes where cost-benefit is demonstrated. Conduct quarterly PMO checkpoints and integrate CIP-delivered assets into steady-state maintenance. Regional Maintenance Team support transitions from heavy supplemental involvement to targeted specialist deployment.

<!-- ¶6 -->
- <sup>**Years 4+: Sustained Performance.**Condition-based maintenance</sup> driven by real-time asset health data. Annual benchmarking of OCWUT’s maintenance KPIs against Jacobs’ portfolio of comparable wastewater O&M projects, sharing proven practices across the network. Lifecycle cost data informs capital planning recommendations, and the AMS is a mature, continuously improving system.

<!-- ¶7 -->
Monthly maintenance reports will document all completed work, asset condition updates, KPI performance against Schedule 5 targets, and M&R Fund expenditure status. Quarterly reviews with OCWUT will measure progress, identify improvement opportunities, and adjust strategies based on trending data. Our annual internal audits will provide independent assessment and bring best practices from across our national O&M portfolio. **The result is a disciplined, risk-driven maintenance program that protects performance, controls cost, and ensures long-term sustainability of OCWUT’s assets.**

Capital dollars go further when investment decisions are determined by actual asset conditions, risk, and lifecycle cost—not emergency-driven spending or deferred maintenance backlogs. **The AMS described in our Maintenance Plan generates a continuous stream of data that directly informs capital planning: condition scores, failure trends, cumulative repair costs, and remaining useful life estimates.** That data gives OCWUT the objective basis to fund the right projects at the right time.

- <sup>**Asset Age:**actual age versus typical useful life for the asset class, adjusted for</sup> site-specific conditions like H₂S exposure
