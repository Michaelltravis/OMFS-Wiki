We recognize that long-term CIP improvements are planned to address the severe H₂S conditions in the belt press room. In the short term, our focus will be on operational adjustments that we can implement immediately. We’ll evaluate existing solids handling strategies for both WAS and primary sludge to reduce excessive detention times and uneven loading to the sludge holding tanks, which currently receive both streams with no mixing or aeration, creating the septic conditions that drive H₂S levels. By improving the timing, blending, and frequency of sludge transfers and pressing operations, we’ll limit septic conditions and reduce sulfide generation, providing rapid relief from localized odor impacts while longerterm solutions are evaluated.

<!-- ¶2 -->
Near term enhancements will include re-implementing the existing anoxic selector zones—offline since 2019 but with submersible mixers replaced and ready to return to service—to improve settling performance and reduce aeration demand. Expanded DO monitoring will address the over-aeration observed during our site visit (baseline energy intensity averages 1,763 kWh/MG at Deer Creek) while laying out the groundwork for future aeration control optimization. Longerterm efforts will focus on refining aeration efficiency, improving solids handling reliability, and using operating data to enhance performance while maintaining compliance margins.

###### _Chisholm Creek WWTP—Protecting What’s Working, Reducing What’s at Risk_

<!-- ¶4 -->
Chisholm Creek has experienced fewer historical permit excursions but faces ongoing operational risk from aging equipment, filtration reliability challenges, and clarifier limitations. The process control strategy here emphasizes proactive risk reduction to prevent future compliance issues rather than recovering existing ones.

<!-- ¶5 -->
Immediate efforts will focus on restoring dependable filtration automation and backwash control, supported by turbidity monitoring to protect effluent quality and downstream disinfection performance. The backwash returns tank overflow— currently piped directly to the effluent—will be closely managed to mitigate compliance risk during overflow conditions. Secondary clarifier operation will be strengthened through routine sludge blanket monitoring and trending.

<!-- ¶6 -->
SRT-based wasting will provide consistent control of biological inventory, improve hydraulic loading through the treatment process, and stabilize sludge management. This will also support production of thicker waste sludge, reducing the volume of water hauled off site to North Canadian and improving operational efficiency. The over-aerated conditions we observed—excessive DO with all blowers running (baseline energy intensity averages 2,292 kWh/MG)—will be addressed by rightsizing aeration to actual demand.

<!-- ¶7 -->
Over the near to long term, process control improvements at Chisholm Creek will focus on incremental enhancements that improve reliability without adding unnecessary complexity via practical, operator-driven, process control tools that are sustainable with existing staff and resources.

###### _South Canadian WWTP— New Plant, Right Habits from Day One_

<!-- ¶9 -->
South Canadian will enter service as a new 8.66-MGD activated sludge facility during the term of this contract. While the plant will benefit from modern design and new equipment, the primary objective will be to establish strong process control fundamentals from the outset and set a high bar for regulatory compliance and consistency.

<!-- ¶10 -->
Early operations will prioritize stabilizing the biological process through SRT-based wasting, providing operators with clearly defined daily wasting targets as influent characteristics and loading patterns are established. This disciplined approach will protect secondary clarifiers and support reliable wet weather performance during startup and early operation. Wet weather management will be emphasized through careful monitoring of hydraulic loading, flow distribution, and solids inventory, with proactive adjustments to preserve treatment performance and compliance margins.

<!-- ¶11 -->
Strong process monitoring and operator engagement will be integral to embedding good operating practices early in the facility’s lifecycle. Routine reviews of biological, hydraulic, and solids data will establish clear operating targets and standard responses, supporting consistent decision-making and operational confidence.

<!-- ¶12 -->
South Canadian will include **on-site sodium hypochlorite generation (OSHG)** using PSI MicroClor units, **a system our team has direct operating experience with at our Pampa, Texas, facility** . We’ll leverage that experience to optimize performance from the outset, including maintaining consistent hypochlorite strength, maximizing system reliability, managing the proprietary cartridge replacement cycle, and integrating hypochlorite production with plant disinfection demand. Preventive maintenance, performance monitoring, and best practice SOPs will maintain safe operation and reliable disinfection capacity.

<!-- ¶13 -->
As operational data becomes available, process control strategies will be refined to optimize SRT targets, wet weather response, and disinfection efficiency. By embedding disciplined process control practices early and leveraging proven operating experience, we’ll position South Canadian for reliable compliance, operational consistency, and long-term sustainability.
