###### _Regional Maintenance Support: The Jacobs Advantage_

<!-- ¶17 -->
Beyond the on-site teams, OCWUT’s maintenance program will be backed by Jacobs’ Regional Maintenance Team as illustrated in **Exhibit 1-32** . This dedicated group of specialists support projects across the southern region, including Baton Rouge, Jackson, and other Jacobs O&M operations. This team handles heavy equipment overhauls, major pump and blower rebuilds, crane-served work, and other tasks that would typically require hiring outside contractors with the associated delays, unfamiliarity, and communication gaps.

###### **Warranty Management**

<!-- ¶19 -->
With South Canadian’s new oxidation ditch, hypochlorite generation system, disc thickener, and cloth filtration expected online in summer 2026, and Deer Creek’s major CIP upgrade planned for 2027, warranty management will be a critical focus from the start of this contract.

<!-- ¶20 -->
What makes this a genuine differentiator is how the team is structured: regional maintenance specialists are separate and standalone from our project teams. They don't draw resources away from other projects when OCWUT needs support, and other projects don't draw them away from OCWUT. They're Jacobs personnel who will know your facilities, your CMMS, and your safety protocols— and they're available without the procurement delays and onboarding friction that come with third-party contractors. Unlike typical operators, we combine on-site delivery with regional and national support structures, bringing specialized expertise, benchmarking, and proven practices from more than 100 Jacobs-operated facilities.

<!-- ¶21 -->
We’ll maintain a comprehensive warranty register within NexGen, tracking warranty terms, expiration dates, coverage conditions, and required maintenance actions for every applicable asset. NexGen will flag assets under warranty when corrective maintenance is requested, triggering a review of warranty applicability before any repair or replacement work proceeds. PM tasks required to maintain warranty coverage will be built into the PM schedule as non-negotiable items—because a voided warranty doesn’t just cost money; it transfers risk from the manufacturer to the operator.
