###### **ON-SITE SODIUM HYPOCHLORITE GENERATION**

<!-- ¶2 -->
On-site sodium hypochlorite generation (OSHG) is already part of how Jacobs delivers compliant disinfection. We operate PSI MicroClor® OSHG systems at active O&M contracts today, and that experience translates directly to what OCWUT is building. South Canadian’s two PSI MicroClor® units are currently under construction; North Canadian and Deer Creek will follow in 2028 and 2029. When those systems come online, OCWUT won’t need a contractor that’s still figuring out the platform, as **our team already knows how PSI MicroClor® systems perform, where they’re sensitive, and what it takes to keep them running reliably** .

<!-- ¶3 -->
Beyond operating these systems, **we’ve designed and commissioned them** . We’ve delivered PSI MicroClor® OSHG installations across the country, including the Hayden Bridge WTP for Eugene Water and Electric Board in Oregon (50 MGD, 1,500 ppd, commissioned 2019), Paradise Point WTP for Clark County Public Utilities in Ridgefield, Washington (7.5 MGD, commissioned 2016), the Rockwood Public Utility District’s Main WTP in Portland (20 MGD, commissioned 2025), and Nashville Metro Water Services’ Omohundro WTP (150 MGD, 9,600 ppd) and K.R. Harrington WTP (120 MGD, 7,200 ppd), both currently under construction. That design depth— knowing not just how to run these systems but how to specify, site, and commission them—means **we can support OCWUT through each capital improvement project from pre-construction coordination through startup and steady-state operations** . PSI MicroClor® equipment is proprietary. That makes disciplined preventive maintenance, not reactive repair, the only responsible approach. We also understand the safety risks these systems carry and the operational requirements to manage them. Our teams in Pampa, Texas; Ontario, Oregon; Fort Irwin, California; and Rio Rancho, New Mexico, **(Exhibit 3-4)** have built their O&M programs around

<!-- ¶4 -->
exactly that discipline: short-cycle PM on the failure points that matter most, tight residual control under variable demand, safe chemical handling protocols, and the documentation practices that keep compliance clean. We’ll bring that same approach to OCWUT’s system.

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### **MAINTENANCE**

<!-- ¶6 -->
Jacobs has implemented more than 100 asset management programs across North America, and our approach is built on a straightforward progression: get preventive maintenance locked in first, layer in predictive technologies (i.e., vibration analysis, motor signature analysis, infrared thermography, ultrasound, and oil analysis), and use the data to move toward condition-based decisions rather than fixed-interval assumptions. Corrective work orders open within 24 hours; we target closure within 30 days.

<!-- ¶7 -->
NexGen, being implemented now through Jacobs consulting, gives OCWUT full visibility into work order status, asset history, and maintenance costs. Our Asset Replacement Modeling (ARM) tool uses condition scores, asset age, and maintenance history to support capital planning decisions.

<!-- ¶8 -->
When Jacobs assumed operations of **Baton Rouge's North and South WWTPs** in 2023, the facilities faced a significant backlog of deferred maintenance, repair, and replacement needs. We immediately conducted a detailed inventory and condition assessment, developed targeted strategies to address critical equipment deficiencies, and launched a rigorous belt filter press review and repair program, **bringing the presses back to full effectiveness and significantly improving the system's capacity to handle biosolids** . We also conducted a comprehensive facility condition assessment and capital repair and replacement plan, aligning recommendations with the City's funding availability.
