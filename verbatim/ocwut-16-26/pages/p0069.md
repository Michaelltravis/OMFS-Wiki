###### _Maintenance of Plant Operations (MOPO)_

<!-- ¶7 -->
MOPO is the highest-risk operational element of any capital project at a working wastewater facility. We treat MOPO as a living document that evolves through each design phase, not a static plan developed once and filed away. We’ll identify critical process interdependencies and capacity constraints before construction begins, sequence construction activities to maintain treatment capability and permit compliance, develop contingency plans for equipment outages including temporary process configurations, and coordinate shutdown windows with seasonal flow patterns, wet weather risk, and OCWUT’s operational priorities.

<!-- ¶8 -->
Jacobs remains responsible for meeting all Schedule 5 Performance Requirements during CIP construction. That accountability is why MOPO planning starts at 35% design and receives dedicated attention at every subsequent phase, and why the CIP Construction Manager coordinates directly with operations leadership to avoid conflicts between construction schedules and treatment obligations.

<!-- ¶10 -->
