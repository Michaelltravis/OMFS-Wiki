###### _Jacobs Annual Innovation Workshop Drives Continuous Improvements and Elevated O&M_

<!-- ¶9 -->
Our **Annual Innovation Workshops—provided at no cost to OCWUT** —bring leading-industry experts to your team each year to review the prior year’s achievements and co-develop new goals aligned with OCWUT’s evolving needs and long-term vision. OCWUT staff, stakeholders, our on-site O&M team, and our utility experts all take part.

<!-- ¶10 -->
This forward-looking session keeps OCWUT at the leading edge of treatment technologies, optimization strategies, and modern O&M practices. We’ll handpick industry leaders in process engineering, biosolids and dryer technologies/ operations, odor control, hydraulics, wet weather flow management, pump station operations, digital optimization, and energy management to match your current challenges and opportunities.

<!-- ¶11 -->
Ahead of the workshop, we’ll co-develop a full-day agenda with you that reflects your priorities and sharpens the focus on your priorities. Our specialists then prepare by learning about your facilities, analyzing performance data, and pinpointing improvement opportunities so they arrive with actionable ideas tailored to OCWUT. During the session, we listen first, then co-create plans for the coming year. The result is measurable gains in operations, asset lifecycle performance, and cost efficiency. **For example, a previous workshop at the Wilmington WWTP identified opportunities for more than $1M in energy savings.**
