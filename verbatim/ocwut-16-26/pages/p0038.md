**Sampling – Compliance and Process Control Jacobs operates two distinct but integrated sampling programs, and both matter at OCWUT.** Compliance sampling covers all NPDES permitrequired sampling locations, parameters, and frequencies—influent, effluent, and in-process points at each facility—and stormwater and biosolids sampling. All compliance samples will follow EPA-approved methods with proper chain-of-custody, preservation, and documentation. Schedule 9's daily data reporting forms define the specific parameters OCWUT expects to be tracked at each facility. Jacobs will use these forms as the operational floor, not the ceiling.

<!-- ¶5 -->

**To make sure nothing falls through the cracks, Jacobs uses a Sample Tracking Tool at every project.** This documented system records sampling locations, frequencies, parameters, and individual accountability for each sample pulled. Samples are checked off at collection and verified by a second independent party the same day, providing assurance that every compliance and process sample is taken as required, every time.

We will conduct and manage biomonitoring, including whole effluent toxicity testing as required by NPDES permits, as a priority compliance obligation. Given recent WET test performance at North Canadian and Deer Creek, Jacobs will monitor these tests closely, maintain operational controls that minimize toxicity risk, and work proactively with ODEQ if additional testing or investigation is warranted.
