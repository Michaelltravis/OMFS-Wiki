We’ll bring the full depth of that capability to OCWUT.

###### _Training Topics by Role_

<!-- ¶19 -->
Our training content is tailored to each role and will be aligned with how the work actually gets done at the OCWUT facilities, as summarized in **Exhibit 5-6** . **Safety training, anchored in our BeyondZero® culture, is the foundation for every employee.** This starts with site-specific orientation before any work begins and is reinforced through ongoing refreshers, stand-downs, and practical drills. Technical and process training will leverage Jacobs' Institute of Learning platform with 36 instructional volumes aligned to the ABC "Need to Know" standards. This has been developed by operators and engineers and will be delivered through classroom, field, and online methods. Maintenance staff will receive hands-on instruction from Jacobs' regional maintenance experts with annual CMRT exam
