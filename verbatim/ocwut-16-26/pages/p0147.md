RISK AREA POTENTIAL IMPACT JACOBS MITIGATION APPROACH OUTCOME FOR OCWUT<br><!-- End of picture text -->

<!-- ¶3 -->
|**Staffing**<br>**Continuity**|Loss of institutional knowledge,<br>certification gaps, or<br>insufficient coverage|Offer positions to qualified incumbent personnel, confirm<br>certification coverage early, recruit and onboard during transition,<br>and maintain regional backup and contingency staffing resources.|Fully staffed, qualified team in place on operational start<br>date with no service disruption|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Knowledge**<br>**Transfer Gaps**|Incomplete understanding of<br>facility operations leading to errors<br>or inefficiencies|Structured knowledge transfer through document review, shadowing,<br>walkthroughs, SOP review, and multi-shift monitoring; 7-day<br>operational monitoring before startup|Effective transfer of operational knowledge and consistent<br>performance from day one|
|**Regulatory**<br>**Compliance**|Missed sampling, reporting<br>errors, or permit noncompliance<br>during transition|Validate sampling programs, laboratory methods, reporting<br>workflows, and compliance obligations during transition; align tools<br>and responsibilities before startup.|Continuous compliance with no transition-related gaps|
|**Asset Condition**<br>**Uncertainty**|Unknown equipment condition<br>or unexpected failures affecting<br>startup reliability|Complete Baseline Physical Condition Assessment; review<br>maintenance history and backlog; jointly sign the Baseline Physical<br>Condition Report with OCWUT before startup|Clear, jointly understood baseline and reduced startup<br>reliability risk|
|**Initial**<br>**Inventory Gaps**|Insufficient spare parts, chemicals,<br>supplies, or equipment at startup|Jointly validate initial inventory during transition, establish<br>minimum inventory levels, identify and address deficiencies before<br>startup, and integrate inventory controls into CMMS.|Required inventory in place to support uninterrupted startup|
|**Chemical,**<br>**Polymer, and**<br>**Biosolids Readiness**|Delays affecting process<br>performance, odor control,<br>dewatering, or solids compliance|Complete polymer and chemical testing before startup, secure<br>preferred vendors, and confirm biosolids equipment and rolling<br>stock within required timeframes.|Confirmed chemical and biosolids readiness supporting<br>stable, compliant startup operations|
|**Systems**<br>**Integration**|Loss of visibility, alarm<br>failures, data errors, or<br>cybersecurity vulnerabilities|Coordinate early access to systems and data; validate SCADA,<br>CMMS, and IT workflows; develop and submit ITS Implementation<br>and Use Plan; and train staff before startup.|Fully functional systems supporting safe, controlled<br>operations from day one|
|**Transition**<br>**Milestone**<br>**Slippage**|Missed Schedule 19 deadlines<br>affecting startup readiness|Manage transition through a milestone-driven schedule with clear<br>owners, checkpoints, and escalation protocols; track all deliverables<br>in a centralized transition log.|On-time completion of required deliverables and confidence<br>in operational start date readiness|
|**Communication**<br>**Breakdowns**|Misalignment between OCWUT,<br>Jacobs, and incumbent operator|Establish governance at commencement and implement a transition<br>communications plan with defined roles, meeting cadence, and<br>action tracking.|Transparent communication and aligned execution<br>throughout transition|
|**Operational**<br>**Disruption**<br>**at Startup**|Instability from simultaneous<br>changes in staff, systems,<br>or responsibilities|Sequence activities to support continuity first, complete<br>transfer-of-service planning for the month before and month after<br>startup, and prioritize stabilization before optimization.|Controlled startup that protects performance, compliance,<br>and continuity|
|**Safety Risks**<br>**During Transition**|Increased incident potential from<br>unfamiliarity with site conditions<br>or equipment|Conduct site-specific safety assessments, incorporate safety<br>requirements into onboarding and training, and provide role-specific<br>hazard awareness before independent work.|Safe work environment and strong safety culture from the<br>start of operations|

<!-- ¶4 -->
###### **Workforce Transition, Staffing Readiness, and Retention**

<!-- ¶5 -->
**People are the foundation of a successful transition.** Our workforce transition approach is designed to retain critical institutional knowledge, reduce uncertainty for employees, and ensure OCWUT has a qualified, fully prepared team ready to perform on day one.

<!-- ¶6 -->
From the start of the transition period, we’ll treat staffing, onboarding, and training as a core transition workstream with clear accountability, defined milestones, and direct alignment with the staffing plan and Schedule 19 requirements.
