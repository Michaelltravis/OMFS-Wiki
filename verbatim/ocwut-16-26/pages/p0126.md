###### **EMERGING REGULATORY RISK: OKLAHOMA'S BIOSOLIDS LEGISLATION**

<!-- ¶5 -->
**One of the most significant regulatory developments affecting OCWUT’s program is the continued legislative effort in Oklahoma to regulate, restrict, or potentially prohibit the land application of biosolids** , building on the momentum that began with Senate Bill 3 during the 2025 session. In the 2026 legislative session, multiple measures have been introduced that reflect this ongoing push, including House Bill 3411, Senate Bills 1799, 1818, and 2141. Collectively, these bills signal sustained legislative scrutiny of biosolids management through expanded PFAS testing and disclosure requirements, increased transparency and public notification, tighter application restrictions, and, in some cases, outright prohibition.
