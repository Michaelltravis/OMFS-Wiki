<!-- ¶4 -->
###### **Four-layer odor control strategy:**

<!-- ¶5 -->
**1) Source control:** Evaluate dissolved sulfide levels arriving at each plant; chemical dosing at Witcher (ferrous chloride or peroxide in wet well) to reduce H₂S before it reaches NC. **2) Equipment restoration:** Assess and rehabilitate BioWay tower at NC (engage Bio Air or ECS); replace exhausted carbon media with properly sized biological or chemical scrubbers where H₂S exceeds 10 ppm.

<!-- ¶6 -->
**3) In-plant process control:** Maintain aerobic conditions in critical process areas, actively manage sludge age and solids inventory to prevent H₂S formation at the source, and minimize detention time in sludge handling across all facilities. At DC specifically, separate WAS/primary sludge storage and optimize peroxide dosing ahead of the belt press.  Eliminate masking agents in favor of targeted source treatment.
