###### **SEPTAGE RECEIVING**

<!-- ¶11 -->
Running a compliant, efficient septage receiving program takes more than a staffed attendant and a flow meter. It takes rigorous screening, clear hauler accountability, and active program management. We bring both the operational discipline and the programmatic know-how to do this well.

<!-- ¶12 -->
Jacobs has **operated the dedicated Grand Traverse County Septage Receiving and Treatment Facility since 2012** , managing all aspects of receiving, treating, and reporting on septage loads, including manifesting, sampling, Discharge Monitoring Report (DMR) submittals, and permit compliance. This purpose-built receiving station serves the broader Traverse City region, giving our team direct, hands-on experience with exactly the type of program OCWUT runs at Witcher Pump Station.

<!-- ¶13 -->
We also know how to turn a receiving program into a revenue driver. Jacobs has been the O&M partner to the **Lehigh County Authority (LCA) in Allentown, Pennsylvania** for 30 years, and we **overhauled their hauled waste program from the ground up** . That meant a market analysis, revised hauler permitting, updated metals limits, and a shift in lab testing accountability to the waste generators. The results speak for themselves: revenue from hauled waste increased **38% in the first year** and has grown **63% cumulatively** , generating nearly **$2.8M in offset annual revenue** for LCA.
