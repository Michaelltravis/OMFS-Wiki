###### **Transparency You Don’t Have to Chase**

<!-- ¶5 -->
Our management approach is anchored in the belief that OCWUT shouldn’t have to chase its operator for information. You’ll have real-time access to performance data, structured reports delivered on schedule, and proactive communication about issues before they become problems. That’s the standard we hold ourselves to and it’s the standard this Management Plan describes.
