###### **Advanced Monitoring and Data-Driven Control**

<!-- ¶5 -->
We will implement a targeted monitoring strategy at high-risk locations across all four facilities, including key process areas, pump stations, force main discharge points, and representative fence-line locations. This data will establish action thresholds that trigger operational responses, replacing the inconsistent manual process control observed during our site visits with clear operating targets, realtime visibility, and defined response actions ( **Exhibit 1-22** ).

<!-- ¶6 -->
**As part of our value-added investment in OCWUT's odor program, we will install Sulfilogger sulfide monitors at each facility and weather stations to monitor wind speed and direction. This $30K investment delivers two immediate operational benefits.** First, Sulfilogger monitors will allow ferric chloride dosing to be automated and controlled proportionally to actual sulfide concentrations in real time. Currently, plant staff wait for odors to occur before activating ferric at a constant rate, which results in either overdosing or insufficient feed during concentration peaks. Sulfilogger-driven automation eliminates that reactive cycle, improving odor control while reducing ferric consumption and cost. Second, weather station data, combined with odor monitors mounted near the plant perimeter, will feed directly into an air dispersion model that generates a real-time graphic understanding of plume dispersion and travel into adjacent neighborhoods. This capability moves odor management from reactive complaint response to predictive community protection, giving operators advance warning when conditions are likely to push odors toward residential areas and time to act before complaints occur.

**As part of Jacobs' startup investment, all O&M staff will be equipped with Blackline personal gas monitors.** Given the hydrogen sulfide levels documented across OCWUT's facilities, including areas where H₂S prevented access during our site visit and buildings where existing monitors were unprogrammed or sensorsaturated, **personal gas monitoring is a baseline safety requirement.** Blackline devices provide continuous H₂S monitoring and automatic man-down detection, alerting the control room immediately if an operator becomes incapacitated. This capability is especially critical at North Canadian and Witcher Pump Station, where elevated H₂S conditions exist across multiple work areas and rapid response to an operator-down event could be the difference between a rescue and a fatality.
