###### **OCWUT-Specific Operational Readiness**

<!-- ¶8 -->
Jacobs will tailor transition activities to the scope and operating profile of OCWUT's wastewater facilities, applying a wastewater-focused startup approach rather than a one-size-fits-all transition model. Early efforts will focus on understanding and validating the systems most critical to reliable startup: biological treatment processes, biosolids handling and dewatering, lime stabilization, chemical feed systems, electrical infrastructure, plant control systems, laboratory operations, certification

<!-- ¶11 -->
requirements, and QA/QC procedures. We’ll also prioritize the collection system interface at Witcher Pump Station, the biosolids land application program, and the elevated H2S conditions documented at North Canadian and Deer Creek, which will require site-specific safety protocols and equipment readiness verification during transition.

<!-- ¶10 -->
South Canadian presents a distinct transition challenge and opportunity. With the new 8.6-MGD activated sludge facility anticipated to come online in July 2026, we’ll take over a newly commissioned plant mid-transition. Our transition planning accounts for coordination with the construction contractor on equipment commissioning and warranty management, new equipment familiarization for operations staff, and startup protocol development for processes that will have limited operating history by January 1, 2027.
