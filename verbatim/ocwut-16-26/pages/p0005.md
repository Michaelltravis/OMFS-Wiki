- **Process performance and compliance.** Fecal compliance exceedances, inadequate chlorination/dechlorination balance, and untended SCADA alarms and inaccurate instrumentation all point to a process control program that needs structure and accountability. Our structured process control program will have defined operating targets, flow-paced chemical dosing, and restored instrumentation—giving operators the tools to prevent permit excursions.

<!-- ¶2 -->
- **Deferred maintenance and asset condition.** OCWUT's facilities’ deferred maintenance has compounded over time, and the new contract rightly holds the operator accountable for thorough documentation. Our staffing approach will shift performing the tank drain and clean program along with minor PMs to operators. This shift allows our maintenance team to focus on preventative, predictive and corrective maintenance throughout the facilities. Within the first 90 days, we’ll complete a comprehensive condition assessment, establish a defensible baseline, and transition maintenance from reactive to proactive using our Asset Replacement Modeling (ARM) approach.

<!-- ¶3 -->
- **Staffing stability and accountability.** Staff turnover and wage competitiveness have been well-documented challenges under the current operator. We'll prioritize retaining qualified incumbent staff, implement competitive compensation and career development pathways, and leverage regional support resources to ensure consistent, reliable operations.

<!-- ¶4 -->
- **Transparent communication and performance measures that drive results.** We’ll implement a robust communication framework supported by real-time dashboards and structured reporting aligned with OCWUT’s performance requirements. This approach provides full visibility into safety, compliance, maintenance, and cost ensuring OCWUT always remains informed and in control.
