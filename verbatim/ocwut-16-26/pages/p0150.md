###### **Communication and Knowledge Transfer**

<!-- ¶6 -->
We’ll implement a transition communications plan that defines roles, responsibilities, communication pathways, and meeting cadence and regular coordination meetings with OCWUT to review progress, resolve issues, and confirm readiness. Knowledge transfer will follow a structured approach combining **documentation review, staff engagement, and direct observation of operations across all shifts,** capturing both formal procedures and the practical, experiencebased knowledge that only comes from working alongside people who know these facilities.

<!-- ¶7 -->
###### **Training and Operational Readiness**

<!-- ¶8 -->
During the transition period, we’ll prepare staff to assume their responsibilities safely, confidently, and effectively on the operational start date. Before performing independent duties, personnel will complete required safety training, job-specific operational training, and training on OCWUT systems, procedures, and work processes in accordance with the training plan.

<!-- ¶9 -->
During transition, our staff will shadow existing personnel and maintain a strong field presence across shifts to develop a detailed understanding of facility operations, maintenance practices, system behavior, and site-specific requirements. **This boots-on-the-ground approach will allow our team to observe operating conditions, workflows, decision points, and coordination practices in real time while respecting that the incumbent remains responsible for performing the work until the operational start date.** This familiarization will be reinforced through cross-training and systems training so personnel are prepared not only for their assigned roles but to provide reliable coverage across all facilities.

<!-- ¶10 -->
Training will continue beyond day one through refreshers, role-specific development, and ongoing reinforcement of safe operating practices, compliance requirements, and standard procedures, which will build confidence, promote consistency, and support reliable performance.

<!-- ¶11 -->
###### **Asset Validation and Capital Planning**

<!-- ¶12 -->
During the transition period, we’ll work with OCWUT to validate the baseline physical condition of the facilities as required under Schedule 19. This will include review and field verification of process equipment, structures, electrical systems, instrumentation, controls, and other critical assets so that facility condition is clearly documented and mutually understood before startup. Our team will use this process to identify discrepancies, confirm asset conditions, and support development of a defensible baseline for future accountability and asset management. We’ll coordinate the baseline physical condition of facilities report with OCWUT, and it will be jointly signed prior to the operational start date in accordance with transition requirements.

<!-- ¶13 -->
Building directly on the baseline condition assessment findings, we’ll provide prioritized recommendations for capital improvements and reliability enhancements within 90 days of the operational start date. These recommendations will be grounded in documented field conditions, aligned with life-cycle planning, and structured to support OCWUT's annual CIP budget development process consistent with Schedule 20 requirements.
