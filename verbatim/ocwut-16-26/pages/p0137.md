**Chisholm Creek** transfers liquid sludge to North Canadian for downstream processing, a dependency that requires active coordination to avoid hauling inefficiencies and storage pressure at the receiving facility. Jacobs will manage liquid hauling to minimize truck traffic and the safety risks that come with frequent tractor-trailer operations at the facility. ###### **Sludge Generation, Processing, and Class B Treatment**

<!-- ¶6 -->
OCWUT's sludge program operates under four ODEQ-approved Sludge Management Plans, 40 CFR Part 503 Class B requirements, and Schedule 14 and 21 obligations that carry direct financial consequences when storage thresholds are exceeded or reporting lapses **(Exhibit 5-8)** . With four facilities, around 170 permitted land application sites, and roughly 13,500 acres under management, the compliance exposure is continuous, and the only way to stay ahead of it is to manage solids as one fully integrated system. Jacobs will connect every stage from generation through final disposal under a single coordinated program, where dewatering performance, storage levels, hauling schedules, and land application decisions all inform each other. That coordination protects OCWUT's compliance record and drives direct cost savings through optimized polymer use, reduced hauling volume, and the elimination of emergency response premiums.
