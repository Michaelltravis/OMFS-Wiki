###### **MANAGEMENT PLAN**

<!-- ¶2 -->
**An Integrated Management Approach Built for OCWUT’s Complexity** OCWUT’s wastewater system—spanning four treatment plants, the Witcher Pump Station, and a complex solids management program—requires more than routine contract operations. **It demands an integrated management approach built on transparency, accountability, and a partnership structure that keeps OCWUT leadership always informed and in control** .

<!-- ¶3 -->
Jacobs will bring that approach. **Drawing on extensive expertise in delivering program management refined across large-scale contracts nationwide, we’ll establish a governance structure, communication cadence, and performance monitoring framework that gives OCWUT clear visibility into every aspect of facility operations, maintenance, compliance, and cost management** from day one and every day thereafter. This isn’t a bolt-on management layer; it’s how we operate. Structured accountability, proactive reporting, and collaborative decision-making are built into our management DNA.

<!-- ¶4 -->
###### **Leadership That Knows Oklahoma City**

<!-- ¶5 -->
**Leading this effort will be Project Director Steve Carpenter—a trusted name in Oklahoma City’s water community.** Steve ran the Hefner WTP and brings deep relationships with OCWUT staff built over years of local service. He’ll provide management oversight, drive compliance and communication, and serve as the independent quality assurance/quality control (QA/QC) voice on the project—reporting above the day-to-day project management structure to give OCWUT confidence that oversight is genuine and unfiltered.

<!-- ¶6 -->
Daily operations will be led by **Project Manager David Pitocchelli, who will lead daily operations and serve as OCWUT’s primary point of contact, and Assistant Project Manager Alex Rodriguez, who brings firsthand knowledge of OCWUT’s operations from his previous role in Oklahoma.** Together with Steve and our corporate support team, we’ll maintain a “zippering” communication approach, illustrated in **Exhibit 1-2** , that connects the right Jacobs leaders to the right OCWUT counterparts at every level of the organization.
