###### _Continual Improvement and Learning Loops_

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### MAINTENANCE EXECUTION EXPERTISE BEHIND OUR ON-SITE TEAM

<!-- ¶3 -->
We’ll institutionalize learning at every level with monthly KPI reviews with the Maintenance Committee, quarterly Preventive Maintenance Optimization (PMO) checkpoints, annual internal audits by dedicated technical experts, and after-action reviews for major events. All process changes, including task edits, new PdM routes, priority rule adjustments, follow change control through governance, so improvements stick and institutional knowledge increases rather than erodes. **Our objective is to transition OCWUT from reactive maintenance to a predominantly planned environment, where most work is scheduled in advance, and backlog is actively managed.**

<!-- ¶4 -->
Strong maintenance execution turns asset management framework into field results. Our on-site Maintenance Manager Brian Daniels will lead day-to-day planning, scheduling, and work execution, backed by Jacobs’ Regional Maintenance Support Team — condition assessment and reliability specialists who move between Jacobs O&M projects wherever targeted expertise is needed.

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### **Preventive Maintenance Program**

<!-- ¶6 -->
###### BRIAN DANIELS

<!-- ¶7 -->
PM is the backbone of asset protection—but only when it’s calibrated to the actual condition and criticality of the equipment. Over maintaining wastes resources; under maintaining invites failure. We will implement a PMO strategy that uses asset data, condition monitoring results, and operating analytics to right-size PM frequencies for every asset class at every facility. **All maintenance work is planned, prioritized, and scheduled through a disciplined weekly planning process that aligns available resources to risk-based priorities and KPI commitments.**

###### _Drain and Clean Program_

<!-- ¶22 -->
Schedule 15 defines a comprehensive drain and clean scope across all facilities: biannual, triannual, annual, and once-per-5-year-term frequencies for dozens of structures. We’ll develop the Annual Drain and Clean Plan in coordination with OCWUT, integrating each activity into the operations schedule to minimize process disruptions and maximize the inspection and repair opportunities that draindown events create. This plan directly supports PM-1 KPI compliance: 100% completion of the annual Drain and Clean Plan, with a fee at risk per Schedule 5.
