<!-- ¶5 -->
###### **Dear Tasha and Members of the Selection Committee:**

<!-- ¶6 -->
This procurement represents a critical opportunity for the Oklahoma City Water Utilities Trust (OCWUT) to elevate operations and maintenance (O&M) through a more proactive, transparent, and asset-focused approach. Over the past decade, Jacobs has worked closely with every division within the Utilities Department, building a deep understanding of your facilities, business processes, and the people who operate them.

<!-- ¶7 -->
Through our site visits and due diligence, we have identified the key challenges facing the system today—and we understand the future challenges OCWUT must also navigate. Over the term of this partnership, OCWUT will be managing a changing biosolids and disposal strategy, major treatment expansion and disinfection upgrades, increasing pressure to optimize power and chemical use, and the need to protect significant capital investments as new assets are brought online. Jacobs brings the accountability, structure, operational discipline, and integrated technical resources to address both priorities: delivering measurable improvements from day one while helping OCWUT plan, integrate, and sustain the improvements that will define the system’s long-term performance.

<!-- ¶8 -->
With more than 40 years of O&M experience in Oklahoma and ongoing engineering work across the state, we have established strong working relationships with the Oklahoma Department of Environmental Quality (ODEQ) and other regulatory agencies. As shown in the timeline on the following page, we have a long history of supporting OCWUT—including the Hefner Water Treatment Plant (WTP) expansion, North Canadian Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) work, the SCADA System Master Plan, and Deer Creek improvements—meaning we understand not only your system, but also your expectations. This familiarity enables us to transition seamlessly and begin delivering results immediately.

<!-- ¶9 -->
OCWUT is ambitious and positioned for performance excellence. As the only company in the water and wastewater industry to receive the Malcolm Baldrige National Quality Award, we understand what it takes to build a culture of accountability, discipline, and continuous improvement. Through our partnership, we’ll help position OCWUT to achieve that level of recognition in a way that reflects your people, priorities, and long-term vision.

<!-- ¶11 -->
- **Addressing the highest priorities from day one.** Our due diligence provided a clear view of the operational gaps, and we are prepared to act immediately, by providing:

<!-- ¶12 -->
- **Odor and H₂S control.** The H₂S conditions at North Canadian and Deer Creek affect nearby residents, reduce asset life, and present a significant safety hazard to personnel. We’ll address these conditions through targeted chemical addition at key points within the WWTFs, improve air changes with secondary auxiliary equipment, and point-source odor identification and remediation. These measures will support safe, reliable O&M activities across the facilities. Blackline Safety H₂S devices will serve as the primary personnel safety tool, providing continuous air monitoring and man-down notifications.
