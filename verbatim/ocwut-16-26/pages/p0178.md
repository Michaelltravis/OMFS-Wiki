<!-- ¶6 -->
###### **Alternative Escalation Index Methodology – Saving OCWUT $620,000 Over the First Contract Term**

<!-- ¶7 -->
As an alternative to the RFP’s CPI-U South escalation, Jacobs proposes an approach that better reflects actual wastewater industry cost drivers. Using an index such as CPI Water/Sewer, or a blended CPI/ECI methodology, more closely aligns annual adjustments with real labor and operating cost trends, reducing the need to carry contingency for mismatches between general CPI and utility-specific escalation.

<!-- ¶8 -->
By aligning escalation with actual cost conditions, we can lower the Base Fee through reduced pricing contingency. This approach provides an estimated savings to OCWUT of approximately $155,000 annually, or $620,000 over the first contract term.

###### _Odor Study Gap Analysis and Updated Odor Control Report_

<!-- ¶13 -->
We’ll take a structured, action-oriented approach to odor management planning. We’ll review prior OCWUT odor studies, validate current findings against field conditions, and identify where data or scope gaps limit decision making. From there, we’ll complete targeted fieldwork and technical analysis to close those gaps and develop an updated, implementation-ready plan with prioritized odor control actions.

Because odor control is a core performance requirement, this effort will focus on practical, cost-effective solutions that improve compliance, safety, asset protection, and community outcomes. Work will be delivered in phases, allowing OCWUT to act quickly on the highest-impact improvements while building toward longer-term systemwide optimization.
