###### **Staffing Approach and Facility Coverage**

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### _Staffing Model_

<!-- ¶3 -->
We will deploy a workforce sized and structured to operate all OCWUT facilities safely, compliantly, and without interruption, as summarized in **Exhibit 5-3** . Our staffing approach will be built on three principles: **retain qualified incumbent staff, right-size coverage for each facility’s operational complexity, and build resilience so that vacancies, absences, and emergencies don’t compromise performance.**

<!-- ¶4 -->
We will retain qualified staff currently working at the facilities who wish to continue, preserving institutional knowledge and operational continuity. Details of our employee transition approach are provided later in this section under our **Draft Transition Plan** . We’ll recruit for any positions not filled through retention through our dedicated resource planning group, local hiring networks, and national O&M talent pipeline (see discussion on these later in this section).

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### _Fewer Managers, More Doers_

<!-- ¶6 -->
**A staffing model built around wrench time and process control.** We’ve intentionally restructured the workforce mix from what’s in place today, shifting investment out of management overhead and into the operators and technicians who keep the plants running. The result is a leaner leadership layer paired with the hands-on capacity needed to meet OCWUT’s drain-and-clean, process control, and 24/7 coverage expectations as illustrate in below.
