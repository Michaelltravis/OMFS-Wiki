###### FACILITY OVERVIEW

<!-- ¶5 -->
Jacobs operates the City of Waterbury's **27-MGD activated sludge WWTP,** approximately **310 miles of sanitary sewer,** and **20 pump stations** under a 10-year agreement that began in November 2018. The treatment process includes **screening and grit removal, primary treatment, secondary aeration and clarification, UV disinfection,** and **chemical phosphorus removal** , with wet weather capacity above 54 MGD. We are also supporting a **$25M phosphorus upgrade** and, in July 2023, were awarded the City's **38-MGD surface water filtration plant (WFP) O&M** contract. Across both facilities, we use Maintenance Connection CMMS, SEDARU and SCREAM for collections management, and Hach WIMS for operations data. **The facility has maintained a 99.57% permit compliance rate over the past 5 years** .

###### JACOBS’ ROLE AND RELEVANCE TO OCWUT

<!-- ¶9 -->
Waterbury selected us after combined sewer overflows in late 2017 impacted the Naugatuck River—a high-visibility compliance issue that required a structured operational response. We were chosen based on a comprehensive facility assessment and improvement strategy, and the City has since estimated up to **$12.7M in savings compared with its prior operating approach** . The scope is directly relevant to OCWUT: **multi-facility operations, active capital work, a client that retains ownership and decision-making authority, and a long-term partnership built on transparent, accountable O&M** . Waterbury also demonstrates our ability to expand scope based on performance, with the City’s water O&M contract awarded competitively in 2023.
