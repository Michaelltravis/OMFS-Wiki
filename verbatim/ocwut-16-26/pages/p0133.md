###### **Vacancy Management, Retention, and Staffing Resiliency**

<!-- ¶11 -->
OCWUT’s service agreement is clear: inability to fill vacancies is not an excuse for failing to meet performance requirements. We take that seriously, and it dovetails with our overarching staffing philosophy. We’ll design our staffing model to prevent vacancies before they occur, fill them quickly when they do, and maintain full operational coverage in the interim.

<!-- ¶12 -->
###### _Preventing Vacancies: Train to Retain_

<!-- ¶13 -->
The most effective vacancy strategy is keeping good people. Our retention approach will start with competitive compensation and benefits, then layer on career development, recognition, a positive culture, and the kind of leadership that makes people want to stay. This includes:

<!-- ¶14 -->
- <sup>**Career advancement pathways** from entry-level through licensed operator,</sup> specialized maintenance, and supervisory positions, giving every employee a clear line of sight to growth

<!-- ¶15 -->
- <sup>**Certification incentives** that cover exam fees and preparation costs and provide</sup> promotions and bonuses for achieving advanced certifications (details in the Licensing and Certification subsection)

<!-- ¶16 -->
- <sup>**Leadership development** through Jacobs University, including workshops on</sup> communication, coaching, team performance, and management fundamentals

<!-- ¶17 -->
- _Filling Vacancies Promptly_

<!-- ¶18 -->
When vacancies do occur, our dedicated resource planning group, which is focused exclusively on O&M recruiting, will act immediately. Over the past year, their efforts have doubled new hires across our O&M portfolio, reduced turnover, and promoted high-performing staff into new roles. Our vacancy management approach includes:

<!-- ¶19 -->
- <sup>**Immediate posting and recruiting** through our centralized recruiting system,</sup> local job networks, veteran hiring programs, and targeted social media outreach

<!-- ¶20 -->
- <sup>**Internal mobility** including offering open positions first to qualified Jacobs</sup> O&M staff across our national portfolio, many of whom view Oklahoma City as a desirable location

<!-- ¶21 -->
- <sup>**Regional O&M bench deployment** to provide temporary or emergency coverage</sup> within days, bridging gaps while permanent hires are recruited

<!-- ¶22 -->
- <sup>**Succession planning** for every critical role, with cross-trained backups identified</sup> and prepared in advance

<!-- ¶23 -->
###### _Staffing Transparency_

<!-- ¶24 -->
We’ll track hiring status, onboarding, certifications, and license renewals through our HR information system and report staffing metrics to OCWUT as part of regular governance meetings. **OCWUT will know about staffing issues early, and every decision will be traceable.**
