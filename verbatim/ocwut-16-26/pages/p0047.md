<!-- ¶3 -->

Our focus will be on field-level OT assets and their interaction with OCWUT’s enterprise systems, ensuring that security controls are practical, enforceable, and fully transparent to OCWUT system owners.

###### **CIP Integration and Regional OT Support**

<!-- ¶10 -->
OCWUT's active capital program will bring significant new OT systems online during the contract term. Per Schedule 16, our team will support these projects from design input through startup, commissioning, and ongoing O&M—developing SOPs, operating new equipment during proofing, incorporating new assets into the CMMS, managing warranties, and coordinating with OCWUT on SCADA integration.

<!-- ¶11 -->
###### <mark>ON-SITE DEPTH. LOCAL AND REGIONAL BACKUP. OEM MUSCLE.</mark>

<!-- ¶12 -->
When a PLC throws an unexpected fault at 2 a.m., your on-site I&C technicians won't have to go solo. They'll have 24/7 access to Jacobs' local SCADA and regional OT team—and supporting them, one of North America's largest SCADA integration practices for water and wastewater, with direct experience on the same Emerson ControlWave and Rockwell Allen-Bradley platforms running at your facilities. Our SCADA team supports pre-deployment testing before changes reach production, and OEM partnerships with Rockwell, Emerson, Siemens, and Cisco deliver priority parts and technical support when it counts.
