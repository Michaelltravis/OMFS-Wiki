###### PROJECT CHALLENGES AND HOW WE RESPONDED

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **WHAT MADE THIS PARTNERSHIP WORK:** **<u>UNIQUE CHARACTERISTICS THAT BENEFITED BOTH PARTIES</u>**

<!-- ¶4 -->
**Transition:** We **transitioned City O&M staff to Jacobs** , providing them with upgraded safety equipment, crucial training, and introduction to our culture of performance and accountability and our proven O&M systems and processes. **Kelly Irving led transition efforts** , coordinating workforce continuity, regulatory readiness, operational control, and transition activities associated with turnover from the incumbent provider.

<!-- ¶5 -->
This partnership worked because we combined immediate operational action with direct, transparent communication. Maintenance specialists, program managers, compliance leads, and operations staff mobilized together from the outset, giving JXN Water immediate access to both field response and clear reporting. By documenting conditions clearly and linking findings to corrective action, we helped stabilize service for 150,000 residents.

<!-- ¶6 -->
**Wastewater System Stabilization and Reporting:** At takeover, the facilities and lift stations had deferred maintenance, limited functionality, and incomplete visibility into system condition. We immediately began a condition assessment across the three WWTPs and 99 pump stations, documented inherited deficiencies, restored pumps, and communications. **Our transparent approach gave JXN Water a clear path to reducing bypasses and improving reliability.**

<!-- ¶7 -->
An additional outcome of this partnership has been the cultural shift within the workforce. We have integrated staff from the City and the prior operating team, investing in professional development. The result has been **measurable improvements in employee engagement and skill development underpinning long-term system reliability.**

<!-- ¶8 -->
###### **<u>PROVEN RESULTS</u>**

<!-- ¶9 -->
**Odor Control:** We restored core process control by **returning aeration equipment to reliable operation** , **improving dewatering performance, and implementing routine wet well cleaning and pump station inspections** . We also **resolved isolated odor concerns** related to onsite biosolids storage through **improved hauling and prompt offsite removal** . Severe odor issues did not become a recurring problem across the plants. Any residual odors were brief, associated only with spill-related events, and improved rapidly as lift stations were repaired and restored to standard Jacobs operating parameters.

<!-- ¶10 -->
- Lift station functionality improved from ~30% operational at takeover to **90% fully operational**

<!-- ¶11 -->
- **Reliability restored across a system** where ~70% of lift stations initially had only one pump and/or no communication system

<!-- ¶12 -->
- **Staff integration from City and prior operator** , with improvements in staff engagement and technical capability

<!-- ¶13 -->
- **50+ person team mobilized and operational within days of interim contract execution**

<!-- ¶14 -->
**Maintenance and Reliability Recovery:** We shifted the system from reactive response to structured asset recovery. We used the condition assessment to identify critical failures, prioritize repairs, and **established a strategic improvement plan focused on the assets that most affected compliance and continuity** . We also deployed our CMMS at contract start, using assessment data to create a live, tracked maintenance program from day one.
