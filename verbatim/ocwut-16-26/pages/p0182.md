<!-- ¶1 -->
###### **STATUTORY CLAUSES**

<!-- ¶2 -->
The following provisions are required and apply when public funds are expended by the Contracting Entity for any agreement resulting from the procurement process. The Services Provider must acknowledge and agree to the provisions below:

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **1. Energy Discrimination Elimination Act of 2022 - 74 O.S. § 12001 et seq.**

<!-- ¶4 -->
- A. I hereby certify that the Energy Discrimination Elimination Act of 2022, as amended, either:

<!-- ¶5 -->
- i. Does not apply to me or my Entity, including but not limited to circumstances where:

<!-- ¶6 -->
- a. The Entity employs fewer than ten (10) full-time employees;

<!-- ¶7 -->
- b. The value of this Contract is less than, and not expected to exceed, One Hundred Thousand Dollars ($100,000.00); **<u>or</u>**

<!-- ¶8 -->
- ii. To the extent it does apply, I am in full compliance with its requirements.

<!-- ¶9 -->
###### **2. Firearm Non-Discrimination Verification** - **21 O.S. § 1289.31 (Senate Bill 500, 2025)**

<!-- ¶10 -->
- A. I hereby certify that the Firearm Non-Discrimination Act of 2025, as amended, either:

<!-- ¶11 -->
- i. Does not apply to me or my Entity, including but not limited to circumstances where:

<!-- ¶12 -->
- a. Entity is a sole proprietorship;

<!-- ¶13 -->
- b. The Entity is a sole-source provider under this Contract;

<!-- ¶14 -->
- c. The Entity employs fewer than ten (10) full-time employees; or

<!-- ¶15 -->
- d. The value of this Contract is less than, and not expected to exceed, One Hundred Thousand Dollars ($100,000.00); **<u>or</u>**

<!-- ¶16 -->
- ii. To the extent it does apply, I am in full compliance with its requirements.
