•<sup>**Warranty Tracking and Compliance.**New CIP equipment carries manufacturer warranties that represent significant value to OCWUT. We’ll enter all warranty terms,</sup> conditions, and expiration dates into NexGen during commissioning and flag warranty-covered assets in the work order system, so corrective maintenance triggers a warranty review before any repair work proceeds. We’ll build warranty-required maintenance tasks into the preventive maintenance (PM) schedule and manage defective work claims through the 2-year maintenance bond per Schedule 16. Updated warranty lists will be included in monthly reports.

<!-- ¶2 -->
- <sup>**Long-Term Lifecycle Maintenance Planning.**We will update the Asset Management Plan after each CIP project, incorporating new assets into lifecycle cost projections</sup> and our Asset Replacement Modeling (ARM) tool. We’ll adjust risk profiles and criticality rankings, develop optimized PM/predictive maintenance (PdM) strategies starting with manufacturer recommendations and refining with NexGen operating data, and feed updated projections into the annual Facilities Status Report and CIP recommendations due each October 15.
