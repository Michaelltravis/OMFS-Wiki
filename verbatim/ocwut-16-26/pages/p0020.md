**EXHIBIT 1-4.** _Jacobs’ Layered Approach to Performance Monitoring_

<!-- ¶10 -->
###### _How We’ll Track, Measure, and Report Performance_

<!-- ¶11 -->
Jacobs will implement an **integrated performance monitoring system that connects real-time operational data to the specific metrics** OCWUT cares about most. Our approach combines three layers of oversight, illustrated in **Exhibit 1-4** . _Corrective Action and Escalation_

<!-- ¶12 -->
When performance deviates from standards, our response will be immediate, documented, and traceable. Our process follows a structured escalation:

<!-- ¶13 -->
1. **Detection:** Real-time dashboards and daily operations monitoring flag deviations automatically

<!-- ¶14 -->
2. **Root Cause Analysis** : Operations and maintenance staff investigate and document the cause within 24 hours

<!-- ¶15 -->
3. **Corrective Action:** Project Manager/Operations Supervisor implements the fix and documents it in the CMMS

<!-- ¶16 -->
4. **Verification** : QA/QC Manager verifies the corrective action closed the issue

<!-- ¶17 -->
5. **Reporting** : Exception report provided to OCWUT with root cause, action taken, and prevention steps

<!-- ¶18 -->
###### **Jacobs’ QA/QC Program**

<!-- ¶19 -->
###### _Management Oversight_

<!-- ¶20 -->
Quality doesn’t happen by accident—it’s built into every process, verified through independent review, and corrected promptly when gaps are found. Jacobs’ QA/ QC Program for OCWUT will be led by Project Director **Steve** , who will provide management oversight independent of day-to-day operations, and supported by the full-time QA/QC Manager **Josh Alleman** reporting to Project Manager **David** with a direct line to **Steve** .

<!-- ¶21 -->
This structure, illustrated in **Exhibit 1-5** , directly addresses OCWUT’s Schedule 3 requirement for a Management Oversight and QA/QC Plan that goes “beyond general policy statements” and “clearly identifies the processes, responsibilities, and tools” used to manage performance, verify compliance, and correct deficiencies. This structure will further support oversight of Jacobs’ internal audit program, discussed below, and ensure audit results are acted upon to deliver results.
