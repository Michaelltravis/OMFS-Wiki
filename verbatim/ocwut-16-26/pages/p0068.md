Assets are continuously evaluated to determine the optimal intervention—maintain, repair, rehabilitate, or replace—based on condition, risk, and lifecycle cost. **The ARM output is a prioritized, risk-ranked reinvestment plan—a multi-year look-ahead that gives OCWUT a defensible, transparent basis for capital budgeting.** The tool also identifies when an asset crosses from routine maintenance (M&R Fund) into rehabilitation or replacement territory (R&R Fund or CIP), flagging the transition early enough for OCWUT to plan accordingly.

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### _Annual Facilities Status Report_

<!-- ¶3 -->
By October 1 of each year, we’ll submit recommendations to retire, rehabilitate, replace, or upgrade/expand facility components as part of the Annual Management, Operations, and Maintenance Summary as required in Schedule 1 and Schedule 16. Each recommendation will include a cost estimate, the anticipated benefits, and projected cost savings per Schedule 20—supported by condition assessment data, maintenance cost trends, risk rankings, and lifecycle projections from NexGen and the ARM tool. These recommendations will align with OCWUT’s budget development cycle, positioning capital requests to compete effectively for funding. OCWUT retains sole discretion on acceptance of any recommendations.

###### _Dedicated CIP Construction Manager_

<!-- ¶13 -->
The CIP Construction Manager is a dedicated, required position (Schedule 10) whose sole responsibility is managing and coordinating the CIP scope defined in Schedule 16. This position serves as the interface between the General Manager, OCWUT’s consultants, construction contractors, and our on-site operations and maintenance team, coordinating activities so construction doesn’t disrupt treatment processes or permit compliance. The CIP Construction Manager will be supported by our engineering and capital program management resources, bringing experience integrating major capital upgrades at operating wastewater facilities.
