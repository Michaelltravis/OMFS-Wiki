**Maintenance:** Taking over a brand-new facility midconstruction meant there were no historical maintenance records, equipment baselines, or schedules to rely on. **We built the maintenance program from the ground up** by implementing the CMMS, loading a field-verified asset registry based on Phase 1 inspections, and developing preventive maintenance schedules for equipment throughout the facility and all 18 lift stations. Starting with accurate as-built data rather than design drawings gave us a stronger maintenance foundation and reduced risks including wrong spare parts and unplanned downtime.

###### **LESSONS LEARNED:** **<u>INTEGRATED EXPERIENCE IMPROVING PLANS & DELIVERY</u>**

<!-- ¶11 -->
With the South Canadian WWTP under construction, we’ll bring the same phased, hands-on startup approach used at Bixby: **early engagement during construction, telemetry and CMMS deployment ahead of full commissioning, and an operations team experienced in resolving equipment issues while maintaining compliance** . Our **One Jacobs model** gives OCWUT access to engineering and technical specialists across the firm—the same bench we relied on at Bixby when the facility required an alternative solution. Because our team already operates within ODEQ’s regulatory framework, we can focus on execution from day one.

<!-- ¶12 -->
###### **WHAT MADE THIS PARTNERSHIP WORK:** **<u>UNIQUE CHARACTERISTICS THAT BENEFITED BOTH PARTIES</u>**

<!-- ¶13 -->
The City of Bixby needed an operator that could be a true partner, and we were involved from the start. **By having our operations team onsite during construction, we identified issues early, documented as-built conditions, and built a CMMS based on the actual facility** rather than design drawings. That foundation supported reliable, cost-effective long-term operations, and the City trusted us to lead that transition from the old holding pond system to the new BWRF. When the under-design issue emerged, we brought in technical resources from across the company to evaluate the problem, develop engineering solutions and cost estimates, and work with the City on compliance alternatives. Throughout the process, **we maintained transparent reporting and gave the City direct access to OMFS and Engineering leadership for timely coordination and decision making** .
