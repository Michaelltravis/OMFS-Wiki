###### _Wet Weather Management at Witcher_

<!-- ¶2 -->
When storm flows exceed main station capacity, the retention lift station diverts flow to the three wet weather basins. We’ll coordinate basin activation and drawdown with North Canadian headworks loading to prevent hydraulic shock to the treatment process, protecting downstream permit compliance during storm events, and maintain basin readiness through scheduled drainand-clean cycles per the Annual Drain and Clean Plan (Schedule 15), earthen embankment inspections, and drainage controls that minimize stagnant water and associated odor between storm events.

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **Septage Receiving Program**

<!-- ¶4 -->
As the sole septage receiving point for Oklahoma City, we’ll take a disciplined approach to operating Witcher station under Schedule 6 , recognizing it directly shapes the quality of your biosolids land application program. We’ll staff a trained attendant at the receiving station during all required hours and execute the process show in **Exhibit 1-27.**

<!-- ¶5 -->
We’ll also enforce hauler compliance with site rules: exterior-only tanker washing, no interior hose use, and water-fill privileges limited to portable toilet haulers under strict hose protocols. Any hauler who fails to comply will have water use suspended. Beyond Schedule 6 compliance, Jacobs will evaluate  septage receiving logs for trends in load characteristics that may contribute to downstream odor or solids quality issues—connecting what enters at Witcher to what ultimately reaches the biosolids program. This data-driven feedback loop will strengthen both the septage program and overall solids management. **See our Sludge and Solids Management Plans** in **Section 5 – Required Plans Submitted with the Proposal** .
