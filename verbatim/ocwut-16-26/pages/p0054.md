###### **Proven Results at Comparable Facilities**

<!-- ¶9 -->
This approach has delivered measurable outcomes at other Jacobs-operated facilities:

<!-- ¶10 -->
- µ<sup>At a comparable facility in Pima County, Arizona, demand-responsive aeration</sup> control **delivered approximately $300,000 in annual energy savings while maintaining full compliance** .

<!-- ¶11 -->
- µ<sup>At a 3-MGD facility in Carol Stream, Illinois,</sup><sup>**36 energy efficiency projects over**</sup> **5 years reduced electricity costs by $52,000 annually** and **earned $33,000 in utility incentives** , with a secondary benefit of 466 metric tons CO₂-equivalent in avoided greenhouse gas emissions.

<!-- ¶12 -->
At OCWUT, we'll establish current kWh/MG baselines at each facility, identify the highest-return opportunities, implement improvements systematically, and track results in monthly reporting.

<!-- ¶13 -->
###### **Additional Opportunities Beyond the O&M Scope**

<!-- ¶14 -->
Through our due diligence, process modeling, and site observations, we’ve identified additional opportunities for energy and chemical optimization that go beyond the day-to-day O&M scope described above. These include data-driven tools and advanced analytics that have delivered measurable results at comparable facilities. Because these opportunities involve approaches, technologies, and investment outside the base O&M scope, they are presented in **Section 8 — Innovative and/or Alternative Recommendations** , where OCWUT can evaluate them on their individual merits and potential return on investment.
