###### **Coordination With OCWUT and Local Responders**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Effective emergency response depends on strong coordination before, during, and after an event. We will align closely with OCWUT leadership and integrate our procedures with local emergency response agencies to support a unified response. We’ll establish clear communication protocols and escalation procedures so information flows quickly and accurately during an event. Our team will coordinate with fire departments, emergency management, and public safety agencies and participate in joint training exercises when appropriate. This coordinated approach will help OCWUT avoid confusion during critical situations, strengthen decision making, and keep the right people engaged at the right time.

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **Elements of Our Emergency Operating Plan**

<!-- ¶4 -->
Our team will operate and maintain OCWUT facilities and utilities under emergency conditions with a plan built around reliability, safety, communication, and continuity of operations. Our emergency operating plan will include staff training, emergency equipment readiness, defined response roles, and coordination procedures with the City and external agencies. Drawing on our experience supporting utilities through severe weather, flooding, and other system disruptions, we will establish immediate response actions that protect people first, stabilize operations quickly, and support efficient recovery.

<!-- ¶5 -->
At a minimum, the plan will address fires, floods, tornadoes, and power outages as required by the RFP and will also cover the broader emergency conditions most relevant to OCWUT’s facilities and collection system operations. These include chemical spills, asset and equipment failures, process failures, emergency communications, emergency equipment access, continuity of operations, and pipeline failures or blockages ( **Exhibit 1-25** ).
