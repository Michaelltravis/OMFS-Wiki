###### **STAFFING AND TRAINING PLAN**

<!-- ¶4 -->
OCWUT’s facilities demand reliable 24/7 operations, rigorous regulatory compliance, proactive asset stewardship, and a workforce that performs with excellence under both normal and emergency conditions. Meeting these demands will start with bringing in experienced leaders, qualified operators, and maintenance technicians and implementing a support structure that prevents gaps before they become problems.

<!-- ¶5 -->
Our staffing and training plan, which we developed in accordance with Schedule 10, will **deliver a stable, well-qualified workforce backed by structured training, cross-training across facilities and functions, on-call coverage, and regional resources** . Our plan reflects what we learned during our site visits to all five OCWUT facilities, analysis of the RFP and Service Agreement, and decades of experience staffing and managing multi-site wastewater O&M contracts of similar size and complexity across the country. The result will be **dependable operations without service gaps** and a team culture where people take pride in their work, stay for the long term, and grow.

<!-- ¶6 -->
###### **Organization Structure and Leadership Team**

<!-- ¶7 -->
**Strong O&M performance begins with strong leadership.** We have assembled a leadership team with deep experience operating multi-facility wastewater systems, managing 24/7 workforces, and building highperforming cultures, because the caliber of leadership will directly shape the quality of daily operations, staff retention, and responsiveness to OCWUT. Project Manager **David Pitocchelli and our leadership team** will set the tone for a culture of accountability, responsiveness, and continuous improvement. They’ve done this before at complex, multi-facility systems, and they’ll bring that same leadership presence and discipline to OCWUT from day one.

<!-- ¶8 -->
**Exhibit 5-1 summarizes our proposed leadership positions, each of which will meet or exceed the qualification and experience requirements defined in Schedule 10.** Our identified leadership team hold these qualifications, and any future vacancies will be filled with individuals who bring these credentials and certifications and the depth of experience the OCWUT’s facilities demand. For detailed qualifications and resumes, **see Section 3, Experience and Qualifications of the Firm and Management Team** .
