<!-- ¶1 -->
Prior to the operational start date, we’ll complete the baseline physical condition assessment and initial inventory jointly with OCWUT, identify chemicals and polymer with sourcing agreements, secure biosolids management equipment by December 1, 2026, review existing SOPs and standard maintenance procedures and update them to the more stringent of Jacobs' standards or OCWUT's Standards of Maintenance, with all procedures finalized by December 21, 2026, and confirm all staff positions are filled and transition training is complete—all before January 1, 2027.

<!-- ¶2 -->
Transfer-of-service activities will follow three defined windows per Schedule 19. In the month prior to startup, we’ll complete operational monitoring on multiple shifts, verify systems and compliance readiness, and confirm inventories are in place. During the first week after startup, we’ll execute the operational handoff and verify all workflows are functioning as intended. For the remainder of the first month, we’ll stabilize operations, reinforce training, and confirm that performance, compliance, and reporting meet the required standards.

<!-- ¶3 -->
Jacobs will meet every Schedule 19 deliverable deadline, not because they are contractual requirements, but because each one represents a meaningful step toward **giving OCWUT a fully prepared, compliant, and stable operation on day one**. By the established deadlines on October 15, November 2, and December 1 and the operational start date, all required plans, staffing actions, equipment, inventories, and jointly signed reports will be complete and delivered.

<!-- ¶4 -->
Within 90 days of startup, Jacobs will deliver prioritized capital and reliability recommendations grounded in baseline condition assessment findings and aligned with OCWUT's CIP budget development process.

<!-- ¶5 -->
Jacobs has successfully transitioned multiple complex water and wastewater systems from previous operators. Recent examples include West Basin Municipal Water District and Jackson Water (JXN Water), where Jacobs assumed operational responsibility while maintaining uninterrupted service and workforce continuity. In both cases, utility leadership has recognized the effectiveness of Jacobs’ disciplined transition approach and the positive impact on employee engagement and operational confidence.

<!-- ¶6 -->
###### PROVEN SUCCESS: TRANSITIONING COMPLEX PROJECTS

<!-- ¶7 -->
**Given the complexity of West Basin’s operations, the transition period required extensive coordination and technical expertise. Jacobs deployed a deep bench of subject matter experts and worked collaboratively with District staff to ensure a smooth transition from the previous contract operator of more than 20 years. Overall, the transition was managed very effectively, with minimal challenges, and resulted in a smooth and successful onboarding for a system of this complexity.”**

We look forward to expanding their mission, applying that same commitment to the people of Jackson with a focus on protecting public health and the environment with their wastewater expertise.”**
