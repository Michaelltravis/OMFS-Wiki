**Maintenance:** We implemented an asset management framework that integrates Maintenance Connection CMMS with our proprietary asset management platform. Repairs and replacements are prioritized based on risk, condition, and return on investment, aligned with ISO 55001 asset management standards. Predictive maintenance includes Augury vibration analysis for rotating equipment to detect imbalance, misalignment, and bearing wear before failures occur, and Fluke SmartView thermal imaging for electrical systems to identify overloads and insulation issues. **This layered approach, CMMS-tracked schedules, conditionbased predictive tools, and capital planning informed by asset risk scores, gives Vancouver visibility into equipment health across the full facility portfolio.**
