WE OFFER ACCESS TO INNOVATIVE FINANCING APPROACHES

<!-- ¶14 -->
Jacobs has an experienced team that can assist in identifying and securing funding sources for biosolids treatment including thermal drying and other thermal treatment technologies.

<!-- ¶15 -->
Jacobs recently secured a $9.8 million grant from the USDA for Tulsa, Oklahoma, to help offset the cost of building a $45 million biosolids fertilizer manufacturing center. We also assisted the City of Hendersonville, North Carolina, to secure a $14.5 million principal forgiveness grant from the North Carolina Clean Water State Revolving Fund to pay the entire cost of building a new drying facility to eliminate the need for landfilling. In both cases, funding is being used to build facilities to produce marketable biosolids products instead of relying on disposal options such as landfilling. In addition, Jacobs has developed partnership opportunities with technology providers to build and operate thermal drying/gasification facilities that can be funded by utilities or by a capital investment group to minimize capital outlay for our clients. We look forward to discussing these innovative funding options with you to secure biosolids management security for years to come.
