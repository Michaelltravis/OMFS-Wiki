###### **Management Systems**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Effective O&M depends on integrated management systems that connect operations, maintenance, compliance, safety, and administration into a coordinated whole. Jacobs will organize our management systems around the critical areas of service delivery shown in **Exhibit 1-9** , with each system supported by detailed plans developed per Schedule 3.

<!-- ¶3 -->
_Required Plans — Integration into an Overall O&M Framework_ Schedule 3 requires Jacobs to develop 15 new plans and update nine existing plans; all coordinated with the OCWUT General Manager. We’ll develop each plan as an integrated component of our overall O&M management system—not as standalone documents that sit on a shelf. Our Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) will flow from these plans and connect directly to CMMS work orders, operator round sheets, and training curricula.

###### **HEALTH, SAFETY, AND SECURITY**

<!-- ¶36 -->
A framework for compliance with the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) and other safety regulations, vulnerability assessments, job safety analysis, safety training, emergency preparedness and response, and other safety-related functions.

<!-- ¶38 -->
