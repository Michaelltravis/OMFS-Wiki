###### **Staffing Resilience and Continuity of Operations**

<!-- ¶3 -->
Maintaining operations during an emergency depends on having qualified staff available under all conditions. We will implement a staffing approach that supports continuity during extended or severe events by cross-training personnel to perform critical functions across facilities and maintaining emergency staffing plans with clear activation criteria. When additional support is needed, we’ll deploy experienced regional personnel who are familiar with utility operations and emergency response.

<!-- ¶4 -->
–<br>DUNCAN WWTF   2018 OKLAHOMA FLOOD EVENT<br><!-- End of picture text -->

<!-- ¶5 -->
During the September 21, 2018 severe rain event, Duncan WWTF received 7.7 inches of rain in 9 hours, causing flash flooding and influent at the WWTP to peak max of 10,400 GPM for 22 hours.Ahead of this serious storm event, our team implemented the facility’s emergency preparedness and response plan, secured the facility, and procured repair supplies and equipment. When the rain storms hit, we staffed the WWTP overnight to position for immediate emergency response. Flash flooding filled the facility’s yards. To save the pumps, PLC, and electrical systems, our team acted quickly and installed a temporary pump station to continually pump our approximately 200 GPM of rushing water. Our on-site staff and SMEs’ rapid response saved approximately $10K in damages.

###### **Local Response Backed By Regional Support**

<!-- ¶9 -->
OCWUT will have a dedicated local team that understands your system and can respond immediately. That team will be backed by our regional network, giving you access to additional personnel, specialized expertise, and emergency support when needed. This structure will allow us to respond quickly at the local level while scaling resources for larger or more complex events.

<!-- ¶10 -->
That support matters most during widespread emergencies, when competition for labor, equipment, and specialty services is high. With Jacobs, OCWUT will already have access to the broader response capability needed to protect operations and accelerate recovery.

<!-- ¶11 -->
###### **Continuous Improvement and Ongoing Readiness**

<!-- ¶12 -->
Emergency preparedness is an ongoing operating discipline. We will update plans based on lessons learned, conduct regular training and exercises, and incorporate improved tools and practices into the program over time. We have direct experience supporting emergency operating planning, risk and resilience assessments, continuity planning, and incident command and tabletop exercises for Oklahoma City utility systems. We’ll bring that experience to OCWUT to strengthen readiness, improve coordination, and help your team respond confidently to any situation.
