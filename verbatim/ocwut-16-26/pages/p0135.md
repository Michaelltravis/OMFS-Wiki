###### **Licensing, Certification, & Professional Development**

<!-- ¶9 -->
We will obtain and maintain all operator and laboratory certifications required to operate OCWUT’s facilities in Schedule 12. Our leadership team will hold the required Oklahoma licenses by the operational start date. We’ll actively support **EXHIBIT 5-7.** every team member’s professional development throughout the contract term, including significant incentives and support for certification advancement, such as bonuses for advancement through progressively higher certification levels and as summarized in **Exhibit 5-7.** We’ll also establish structured career pathways from entry-level roles through licensed operator, specialized maintenance, and supervisory leadership positions with defined competencies, measurable promotion criteria, and clear expectations at each level.
