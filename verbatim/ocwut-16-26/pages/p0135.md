###### **Licensing, Certification, & Professional Development**

<!-- ¶7 -->
We will obtain and maintain all operator and laboratory certifications required to operate OCWUT’s facilities in Schedule 12. Our leadership team will hold the required Oklahoma licenses by the operational start date. We’ll actively support every team member’s professional development throughout the contract term, including significant incentives and support for certification advancement, such as bonuses for advancement through progressively higher certification levels and as summarized in **Exhibit 5-7.** We’ll also establish structured career pathways from entry-level roles through licensed operator, specialized maintenance, and supervisory leadership positions with defined competencies, measurable promotion criteria, and clear expectations at each level.

<!-- ¶8 -->
###### OPERATOR CERTIFICATION INCENTIVES

<!-- ¶9 -->
Jacobs provides **significant incentives and rewards for advanced certifications to elevate operations employees’ skills**, including:

<!-- ¶10 -->
- **Covering the cost of training, preparation, and exam fees**
- **Potential promotions for operators-in-training (OITs)** that complete the OIT period of 6 to 12 months and get their first operations or maintenance certification
- **Providing a range of incentives** for Advanced Water Treatment Operator (AWTO)® certifications, including covering costs and offering opportunities for career advancement

<!-- ¶11 -->
###### MAINTENANCE CERTIFICATION PROGRAMS

<!-- ¶12 -->
We will help **staff reach the next level** and reinforce using the best practices that maximize the asset life **by driving certifications**, including:

<!-- ¶13 -->
- **Certified Maintenance Reliability Technician** (CMRT) program provided by the Society of Maintenance and Reliability Professionals (SMRP)
- **Certified Reliability Leader** (CRL) program provided by the Association of Asset Management Professionals
- **Potential bonuses for advancement** in those programs for employees that complete next level certifications

###### **Regional & SME Support**

<!-- ¶16 -->
Our on-site team won’t operate in isolation. We’ll supplement OCWUT staff with 7,800+ hours per year of **dedicated support from our regional O&M resources and national SMEs**—providing depth, resilience, and specialized expertise that no standalone staffing model can match. This support structure will include:

<!-- ¶17 -->
- **Regional operations support** able to deploy on short notice for temporary staffing coverage, emergency support, or specialized operational assistance
- **Maintenance and asset management SMEs** to provide hands-on instruction, reliability assessments, predictive maintenance support, and asset management planning, strengthening on-site capability
- **Process engineering and optimization SMEs** to help with process troubleshooting, data-driven optimization, and process analysis, translating data into operational improvements
- **Compliance, safety, and regulatory SMEs** to provide audit support, regulatory strategy, and training, giving OCWUT an additional layer of expertise beyond the on-site team

<!-- ¶18 -->
- **Specialized and emergency support** for odor control, biosolids management, I&C/SCADA, energy optimization, and other specialty needs from our national network of 40,000+ professionals to address challenges that go beyond the day-to-day

<!-- ¶19 -->
**This regional depth will be a strategic advantage for OCWUT.** We’ll solve problems more quickly and cover vacancies reliably, and the on-site team will have a safety net that prevents them from being overwhelmed during emergencies, major maintenance events, or unexpected staff departures.

<!-- ¶20 -->
**See Section 3 – Experience and Qualifications of the Firm and Management Team** for the SMEs we’ve identified for OCWUT and their qualifications.

<!-- ¶21 -->
###### **Subcontractors**

<!-- ¶22 -->
Our staff will self-perform most O&M services at the OCWUT facilities. Between our on-site workforce and regional support resources, we have the capability to handle nearly all operational, maintenance, compliance, and administrative functions in-house, providing OCWUT with consistent quality, faster response times, and direct accountability.

<!-- ¶23 -->
We will engage a key subcontractor, Synagro, for biosolids hauling and disposal. We may work with other subcontractors to cover specialty services that fall outside our core O&M scope. We’ll integrate all subcontractors into our safety, quality, and performance management systems, and they’ll comply with all applicable service agreement requirements.
