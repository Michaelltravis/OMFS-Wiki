###### FACILITY OVERVIEW

<!-- ¶5 -->
The Bixby Water Reclamation Facility (BWRF) is a 2.8-MGD sequencing batch reactor (SBR) with an ultimate design capacity of 5.0 MGD, serving approximately 28,609 residents. Jacobs operates the BWRF 7 days a week and manages 18 associated telemetry-equipped lift stations. The scope includes **industrial pretreatment program (IPP) administration; fats, oils, and grease (FOG) management program; and CMMS tracking all maintenance work orders, labor, parts, and inventory costs** .

###### JACOBS' ROLE AND RELEVANCE TO OCWUT

<!-- ¶9 -->
Jacobs holds a 10-year, $8.3M contract for **full O&M of the BWRF** , beginning before the facility reached final acceptance. The contract has three phases: construction inspection coordination (Phase 1); SBR startup with initial staffing and telemetry installation on seven lift stations (Phase 2); and ongoing full O&M with seven full-time employees (Phase 3). When post-commissioning operations revealed that the facility was under-designed for actual loading conditions, we **mobilized our broader technical resources through our One Jacobs model** to develop alternative compliance strategies with the City.
