###### _Three Customers, Three Facilities, One Standard_

<!-- ¶9 -->
OCWUT's reuse program serves three customers across three facilities: the OG&E Red Bud Facility at North Canadian WWTP, the OG&E McClain Facility at South Canadian WWTP, and the Gaillardia Golf and Country Club at Deer Creek WWTP **(Exhibit 1-16)** .

<!-- ¶10 -->
###### **Each carry distinct operational demands.**

<!-- ¶11 -->
At Deer Creek, the Gaillardia agreement requires on-site water sampling at designated sampling points to comply with reuse permit requirements— a more hands-on compliance obligation than the industrial reuse deliveries at the OG&E facilities. All three locations require chlorine treatment of reuse water in accordance with permit regulations.

###### **Our Approach: Reliable Reuse, Every Delivery**

<!-- ¶16 -->
Jacobs will operate OCWUT's reuse program as a **regulated, continuously monitored service** that reliably meets reuse agreement water quality and quantity commitments while protecting public health and system integrity. Our approach combines **robust treatment and disinfection, distribution system control, targeted monitoring, and operational readiness** so reuse deliveries, including site-specific obligations at Gaillardia, are met.
