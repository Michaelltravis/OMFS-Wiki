###### **Monitoring, Data, and Reporting**

<!-- ¶18 -->
Every report Jacobs submits will be complete, accurate, and defensible. We will use centralized digital tools to consolidate data from SCADA, laboratory systems, and maintenance platforms. We’ll make sure data is validated daily, trended to identify emerging risks, and reviewed by operations and compliance staff before any report is finalized. Dashboards will provide visibility into permit performance, parameters approaching regulatory limits, and the connection between process conditions and compliance outcomes. Jacobs' tracking toolkit includes our Sample Tracking Tool for compliance and process sampling accountability, CMMS-integrated work order and reporting workflows, and real-time performance dashboards that can give OCWUT direct visibility into permit performance, corrective actions, and operational trends at any time ( **Exhibit 1-17** ).

<!-- ¶19 -->
###### **Governance and Accountability**

<!-- ¶20 -->
Reliable compliance starts with clear ownership, rather than shared responsibility that diffuses accountability until something goes wrong. Jacobs will establish a defined compliance leadership structure with clear roles across all functions. An environmental compliance manager will lead the program and serve as the primary interface with OCWUT and ODEQ, supported by facility operations leadership, our laboratory manager, and our regional and national compliance specialists.

<!-- ¶21 -->
**Compliance responsibility will be embedded across operations, maintenance, laboratory services, and management.** This structure creates clear ownership, rapid escalation pathways, and consistent accountability across the entire system.
