###### **Risk-Based Transition Management**

<!-- ¶19 -->
Jacobs will actively manage transition risk from the commencement date through the operational start date using **a disciplined process built on early identification, clear ownership, and proven mitigation strategies** . During the transition period, we’ll track and manage risks across staffing, knowledge transfer, systems access, compliance readiness, asset validation, inventory, and transfer of service so that OCWUT enters day one with stable, compliant, and fully prepared operations. Our approach aligns with Schedule 19’s required transition services and milestone-driven readiness framework. All OCWUT-specific deliverables will be incorporated into our robust checklist of standard action items, ensuring that risks are addressed before they affect performance, compliance, or continuity of service **(Exhibit 5-13)** .
