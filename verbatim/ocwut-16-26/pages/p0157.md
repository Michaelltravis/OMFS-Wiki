###### **Integrated Solids Management Strategy**

<!-- ¶4 -->
###### **Biosolids Processing and Dewatering Operations**

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### **Jacobs will manage OCWUT’s solids program as one coordinated system from generation through final disposal.**

<!-- ¶6 -->
**Reliable dewatering performance is the foundation of the solids program.** When dewatering performs consistently, storage remains controlled, hauling stays on schedule, and land application remains compliant. When it does not, issues escalate quickly across the system.

<!-- ¶7 -->
Our approach begins with understanding how solids are produced, how they move between facilities, and how they are processed and managed in real time. We’ll balance solids production and transfer across facilities so that no single site becomes a bottleneck. Hauling schedules will be aligned with daily production and storage conditions rather than fixed assumptions.

<!-- ¶8 -->
We’ll monitor key process parameters daily, including sludge blanket levels, holding tank levels, belt press performance, cake solids, polymer usage, lime usage, and tons processed. These metrics will be tracked through daily operating logs and reviewed by our supervisors each day.

<!-- ¶9 -->
We’ll integrate solids management with the biosolids land application program, across approximately 170 permitted sites covering about 13,500 acres, requiring daily coordination of hauling, site selection, regulatory documentation, and communication with stakeholders. We’ll account for facility-specific logistics, including transport times and construction constraints at South Canadian. Contingency protocols for equipment downtime and site unavailability will be established in advance, so the system remains stable under changing conditions.

<!-- ¶10 -->
Our operators will maintain stable feed conditions, optimize polymer dosing to maximize efficiency and reduce cost, and maintain lime addition to meet Class B requirements. Production will be tracked against storage and hauling capacity in real time, so adjustments can be made before conditions deteriorate. Processlevel targets, including minimum cake solids, polymer dosing efficiency, and daily throughput will be established during transition and tracked as leading indicators of storage risk, enabling our team to catch performance drift before it affects pad utilization.

<!-- ¶11 -->
A well-managed solids program also strengthens OCWUT's wet weather resilience. When storage is actively maintained well below thresholds and contingency disposal options are pre-arranged, the system can absorb the pressure of wet weather land application restrictions without creating compliance risk or backlog.

<!-- ¶12 -->
Dewatering performance will be actively managed through daily review, trend tracking, and proactive maintenance.
