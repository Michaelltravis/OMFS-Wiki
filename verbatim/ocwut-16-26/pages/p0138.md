###### **Odor Management in Sludge Operations**

<!-- ¶15 -->
Sludge handling is one of the highest contributors to odor risk across the OCWUT system, and Schedule 14 requires sludge and biosolids management to minimize offensive odors at facilities, along transport routes, and at land application sites. We describe detailed odor control strategies in the Odor Control Plan. Here, we focus on how sludge management decisions directly influence odor outcomes, as sludge management decisions and odor outcomes are inseparable. **We manage them as one system.**

<!-- ¶16 -->
###### **Transport and Hauling Controls**

<!-- ¶17 -->
Jacobs will transport sludge and biosolids safely, cleanly, and in full compliance with Schedule 14 requirements. All loads will be properly contained, hauling equipment will be maintained to prevent leaks or spills, routes will be selected and managed to minimize community impact, and drivers will follow defined procedures for safe transport and documentation. Where subcontract haulers are used, Jacobs will maintain full oversight and accountability for performance.

<!-- ¶18 -->
**We’ll conduct transport in a manner that eliminates spill potential with immediate cleanup and required notifications in the event of any release, consistent with Schedule 14 requirements.**

<!-- ¶19 -->
###### **Land Application Program Management**

<!-- ¶20 -->
Managing OCWUT's land application program across approximately 170 sites and 13,500 acres requires daily coordination and disciplined execution. Jacobs will schedule and rotate sites based on suitability, weather conditions, soil conditions, hauling logistics, and storage levels. We’ll maintain all required records and coordinate closely with landowners to support reliable operations.

<!-- ¶21 -->
We will work with OCWUT to develop and maintain a Sensitive Sites list, providing prior notification before any biosolids application at identified locations and applying additional controls to reduce community and regulatory risk. When conditions are not favorable at any site, we’ll adjust or delay operations rather than create risk. When additional capacity is needed, we will support identification and permitting of new sites with OCWUT approval.

<!-- ¶22 -->
###### **Testing, Monitoring, and Compliance Reporting**

<!-- ¶23 -->
Jacobs will complete all required sludge and biosolids testing and reporting in accordance with permits and Schedule 14 requirements. We’ll collect samples at required frequencies, manage them under chain-of-custody procedures, and analyze them using approved methods. We’ll review data for compliance before submission. We will maintain organized, accessible records to support audits, inspections, and regulatory review and will use this data to identify trends and adjust operations before issues develop **.**

<!-- ¶24 -->
###### **External Communications Protocol**

<!-- ¶25 -->
All external communications related to sludge and biosolids management will be directed through OCWUT's Public Information staff, as required by Schedule 14. Jacobs will train all staff and subcontractors on this requirement and establish clear internal escalation procedures. We will document all inquiries and complaints and track them to resolution.
