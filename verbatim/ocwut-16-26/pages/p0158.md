Jacobs will implement a structured Storage Management Protocol built on daily monitoring and proactive intervention at our 35 percent internal trigger. Our operators will track pad utilization each day, and our supervisors will review conditions daily. When utilization approaches 35 percent, the Operations Manager will evaluate hauling schedules, dewatering performance, and land application availability, and implement adjustments before risk develops.

<!-- ¶3 -->
If utilization approaches 50 percent, we’ll seek prior written authorization from the General Manager, submit the required Service Approval Form with an action response plan, and implement corrective actions consistent with Schedule 21, protecting OCWUT from fee exposure while maintaining operational transparency. **We will not manage to the limit. We will manage well below it.**

<!-- ¶4 -->
When land application is not feasible, contingency disposal strategies will be activated to maintain system stability and prevent backlog.

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### **Hauling, Land Application, and Disposal Coordination**

<!-- ¶6 -->
**OCWUT’s biosolids program requires continuous coordination to remain compliant and efficient.** We’ll coordinate hauling to maintain storage within target ranges and adjust schedules based on production and site conditions. We’ll maintain all required documentation for land application, including volumes, site locations, and compliance parameters required for Class B biosolids. We’ll manage site selection and application sequencing in coordination with OCWUT, while supporting the identification and development of additional permitted sites when needed.

<!-- ¶7 -->
We fully support OCWUT's plan to integrate landfill disposal of biosolids by the 2029 target date. We’ll monitor legislative activity that may influence this timeline and adjust planning accordingly. We have already begun building relationships with local landfill operators and will leverage our national project experience to establish a reliable and effective program.

<!-- ¶8 -->
**Schedule 14 requires biosolids to be managed to minimize odor at facilities, along transport routes, and at land application sites.** We’ll implement controlled loading practices, covered transport, and strict manifest tracking. Spill prevention and immediate cleanup procedures will be enforced to meet regulatory requirements. **All external communications regarding biosolids will be coordinated through OCWUT's Public Information staff as required by Schedule 14.** We’ll maintain ready-to-deploy contingency disposal options to support operations when land application is not feasible.

<!-- ¶9 -->
###### **Odor and Environmental Controls**

<!-- ¶10 -->
Solids handling is one of the highest odor risks across the OCWUT system, and process control decisions related to sludge age, detention time, and solids handling directly influences odor generation. **We’ll manage solids, process control, and odor as one integrated system.** This Solids Management Plan is fully aligned with the Odor Control Plan and Process Control Plan as required by Schedule 21.

<!-- ¶11 -->
###### **Monitoring, Reporting, and Compliance Assurance**

<!-- ¶12 -->
Solids compliance is achieved through disciplined monitoring, review, and action. We’ll track daily solids production, processing, hauling, storage levels, and dewatering performance at each facility. Our supervisors will review this data daily and initiate corrective actions before conditions create compliance risk.

<!-- ¶13 -->
Monthly reporting will provide clear visibility into solids performance, including production trends, hauling activity, storage conditions, and corrective actions, structured to align with OCWUT's requirements while providing additional transparency.

<!-- ¶14 -->
**This plan is specifically designed to maintain compliance with both COM-3 and COM-5 through proactive management of solids and odor conditions.**

<!-- ¶15 -->
###### **Continuous Improvement and Risk Management**

<!-- ¶16 -->
**We’ll treat OCWUT's solids program as a long-term performance opportunity.** We’ll continuously evaluate dewatering performance, chemical usage, equipment condition, hauling efficiency, and process integration. **Every improvement will be evaluated based on its impact on compliance, cost, and community.**

<!-- ¶17 -->
###### **Related Program Support**

<!-- ¶18 -->
OCWUT’s solids responsibilities extend beyond wastewater facilities to include residuals management, septage receiving, and industrial pretreatment support. Jacobs will execute each of these programs with the same discipline applied to biosolids operations.

<!-- ¶19 -->
At the Hefner Water Treatment Plant, we’ll coordinate the collection and transport of lime residuals to approved recipients or City-designated locations while maintaining all required documentation and compliance tracking.

<!-- ¶20 -->
At the Witcher Pump Station, we’ll operate the Septage Receiving Program in accordance with Schedule 6, including pH testing, manifest verification, load inspection, chain-of-custody documentation, and daily reporting. **This is a frontline compliance function that we’ll manage with discipline every day.**

<!-- ¶21 -->
We’ll also support the Industrial Pretreatment Program by providing qualified staff, coordinating inspections and sampling, and maintaining required documentation in alignment with program requirements.
