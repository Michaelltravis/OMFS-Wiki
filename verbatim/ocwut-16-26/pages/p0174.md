###### **AWARDS**

<!-- ¶3 -->
To support a **smooth transition** at Agua Nueva, **we brought approximately 80% of the Pima County personnel who chose to join the project** . That approach strengthened our relationship with the County, kept valuable institutional knowledge in place, and helped maintain the facility to a very high standard. **Across both announced and unannounced ADEQ inspections, the site received only explanatory remarks** .

<!-- ¶4 -->
**Maintenance:** Agua Nueva relies on advanced, highhorsepower equipment, especially large aeration blowers. We use **predictive and preventive maintenance, supported by an Intelligent O&M platform** developed with Palantir, integrating SCADA data, facility sensors, CMMS records, and cost data into a single operational view, generating specific, actionable recommendations for field staff on blower DO setpoints, maintenance scheduling, and process adjustments. **This data-driven maintenance model reduces downtime, extends equipment life, and cuts energy costs by 20% plant-wide** .

###### **LESSONS LEARNED:** **<u>INTEGRATED EXPERIENCE IMPROVING PLANS & DELIVERY</u>**

<!-- ¶14 -->
The Agua Nueva DBO experience demonstrates what's possible when operations expertise is embedded in capital project delivery from the start. We'll apply that same integrated thinking to OCWUT's CIP pipeline: **operational staff informing design decisions, constructability reviewed through an O&M lens, and capital investments sized for longterm performance** rather than upfront cost alone. Agua Nueva cemented our approach to datadriven operations. We'll look to deploy **Intelligent O&M** at OCWUT's facilities to **drive energy savings, reduce chemical consumption, and improve process consistency** , all without adding operational complexity or capital expenditure.
