###### **OKLAHOMA EXPERIENCE AND RESOURCES**

<!-- ¶5 -->
In Jacobs, OCWUT will have a partner that already knows how to operate in Oklahoma. **We’ve operated a wastewater treatment facility for the City of Duncan continuously since 1995. Our 30-year relationship spans multiple contract renewals and represents one of our longest-standing municipal wastewater partnerships in the south-central U.S.** Our Duncan team has also earned recognition as **Oklahoma Water Environment Association’s 2025 Municipal Plant of the Year.** We administer the City’s Industrial Pretreatment Program (IPP), managing seven significant industrial users and four categorical industrial users under Oklahoma Department of Environmental Quality (ODEQ) oversight. Our team understands Oklahoma’s regulatory environment and how ODEQ operates, and we’ve navigated the permitting, compliance reporting, and agency relationships that OCWUT relies on every day. Our Oklahoma experience also includes current operation of the **City of Bixby’s water reclamation facility and 18 lift stations** — proof that we’ll bring both active in-state operating depth and proven performance to OCWUT.
