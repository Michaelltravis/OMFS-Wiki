###### _Potential Chemical and Energy Savings Through Intelligent O&M_

<!-- ¶2 -->
OCWUT’s system generates a large amount of operating data every day. The value comes from turning that data into timely decisions that help operators protect compliance, reduce costs, and keep critical assets available. We use Intelligent O&M to connect our wastewater operations experience with advanced data analytics, machine learning, and AI-enabled tools that support practical decisions in the field. If interested, we can use Intelligent O&M to evaluate opportunities for power and chemical optimization while maintaining full regulatory compliance and process reliability. Across OCWUT’s facilities, that means focusing on high-impact systems

<!-- ¶3 -->
such as aeration, pumping, disinfection, odor control, sludge handling, polymer dosing, lime stabilization, and chemical feed systems. Our team will review operating history, SCADA information where available, lab results, weather patterns, equipment status, and chemical and energy trends to identify where adjustments can improve efficiency without creating risk. Operators will remain in control of the process; Intelligent O&M will give them better insight, earlier warning signs, and data-supported recommendations.

<!-- ¶4 -->
**We look forward to further discussing these innovations with you.**
