###### **Contingency Planning and Upset Response**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Jacobs will maintain contingency plans for the conditions most likely to create operational pressure, including equipment outages, dewatering upsets, weather-related restrictions, hauling disruptions, and site unavailability. Wet-weather events represent one of the most predictable contingency scenarios for OCWUT's program. Extended rain periods limit land application availability while sludge generation continues, creating storage pressure. We will maintain pre-identified contingency disposal options, including landfill arrangements consistent with Exhibit B, so that wet-weather restrictions never result in storage threshold violations or fee exposure. Each contingency plan will define clear triggers, response actions, and communication steps so that response is immediate rather than improvised.

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **Performance Tracking and Continuous Improvement**

<!-- ¶4 -->
Jacobs will track key performance indicators (KPIs) including pad utilization, tons processed and hauled, odor incidents, hauling performance, compliance metrics, and site utilization. Corrective actions will be implemented when trends indicate risk, and monthly reporting will provide OCWUT with clear visibility into program performance.

<!-- ¶5 -->
**This approach maintains compliance with COM-3 and COM-5, protects OCWUT's budget from fee exposure, and builds a sludge management program that becomes more reliable, efficient, and resilient over time.**

###### **OPERATIONAL INTEGRATION PLAN**

<!-- ¶7 -->
###### **Integration Approach and OCWUT’s Planned Capital Improvements**

<!-- ¶8 -->
OCWUT’s planned capital improvements represent a significant investment in system capacity, reliability, safety, and modernization. These projects will reshape how your facilities handle disinfection, biosolids processing, and solids disposal—and each one needs to transition smoothly from construction to daily operations without disrupting treatment performance or permit compliance. That’s what this Operational Integration Plan is designed to deliver.

<!-- ¶9 -->
Jacobs will provide a structured, repeatable process for bringing each new facility or upgrade into full operational status with the staffing, training, maintenance systems, and risk management in place before day one of operations. **We’ve built this framework around the specific CIP projects, and it will adapt as OCWUT’s capital program evolves (Exhibit 5-10)** .
