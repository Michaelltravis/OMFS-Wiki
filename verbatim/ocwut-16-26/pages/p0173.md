###### FACILITY OVERVIEW

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### **OWNER INFORMATION**

<!-- ¶6 -->
The 32-MGD Agua Nueva Water Reclamation Facility is the centerpiece of Pima County's Water Campus, **the largest capital program in the County's history** , valued at $750M. The facility treats wastewater through a full advanced treatment train: **new headworks, influent pumping, combined flocculation and grit removal, dissolved air flotation (DAF) clarification for primary treatment and sludge thickening, five-stage Bardenpho activated sludge secondary treatment with step-feed aeration, tertiary filtration, and chloramine-based disinfection** . The facility produces 25 MGD of Arizona Class A+ reclaimed water for reuse and is partially powered by solar energy. Jacobs holds full DBO responsibility, from concept and design through permitting, construction, and long-term O&M, with an annual operations budget of $4.1M.
