- **Q #20:** _As per the RFP, the contractor is responsible for servicing the grinder pumps and low-pressure sewer system and budgeting the replacement of 17 grinder pumps annually, as part of the service agreement. Does the cost of a new (or repair of an existing) grinder pump come out of the annual M&R fund?_

<!-- ¶32 -->
- **A #20:** Contractor’s labor shall be included in the base fee for servicing, repairing, or replacement of grinder pumps/low-pressure sewer systems. Any material costs to repair and/or replace grinder pumps/low-pressure sewer systems or costs related to any specialty contractors shall come out of the M&R fund.
