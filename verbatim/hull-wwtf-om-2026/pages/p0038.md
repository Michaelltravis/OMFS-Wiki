###### **_Documentation and Reporting_**

<!-- ¶2 -->
We will maintain detailed records of monitoring activities, complaints, corrective actions, and chemical usage. Odor trends will be reviewed periodically to identify recurring conditions and refine control strategies. Through this proactive, responsive, and data‑driven approach, we will protect neighborhood quality of life while maintaining regulatory compliance and operational excellence. This approach also supports identification of equipment improvement needs.

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **_Innovation and Sustainable Odor Control Strategies_**

<!-- ¶4 -->
While the odor control plan focuses on effective management of existing infrastructure, we will identify **long-term, sustainable odor control improvements** where operational measures alone are insufficient. Consistent with the evaluation framework shown in **Exhibit 5-8** above, additional monitoring, process modeling, or engineering evaluation may be recommended to better quantify odor sources and determine the most effective long‑term mitigation strategies.

<!-- ¶5 -->
Evaluations may include sewer process modeling, air dispersion analysis, or lifecycle evaluation of alternative odor control technologies, where warranted. We will apply advanced sewer process modeling tools, such as WATS modeling, to **better understand odor generation and evaluate system-wide mitigation strategies** , as further described in **Appendix F** . Any recommended improvements will be coordinated with the Town through the CIP process to ensure that odor mitigation strategies align with operational needs, infrastructure protection, and fiscal priorities.

<!-- ¶6 -->
###### **OPERATIONAL TECHNOLOGY (OT)/SCADA/ INTELLIGENT O&M**

<!-- ¶7 -->
We view the Town’s OT/SCADA as **mission-critical operational infrastructure** that must be **reliable, recoverable, and easy for operators to use** , particularly given the Town’s coastal and flood exposure and its stated expectations for stronger communication, leadership, and transparency. The OT/SCADA platform will serve as the **shared source of truth** for real‑time status, alarms, and performance trends, allowing the Town and on‑site Jacobs team to **identify emerging risks early** through timely, consistent, and defensible reporting.

<!-- ¶8 -->
###### **_Three-Phase Roadmap to Strengthen Visibility, Resilience, and Asset Performance_**

<!-- ¶9 -->
OT and SCADA improvements will be implemented through a **deliberate three-phase roadmap** (as shown in **Exhibit 5-9** on the following page) designed to strengthen **permit compliance, operational resiliency, and long-term asset performance** across the Town’s WWTF and collection system. The roadmap begins with core fundamentals, recognizing that automation performs only as well as the instruments, controls, and data that support it. Improvements will be implemented deliberately so they remain **transparent, maintainable, and sustainable** over the full contract term.
