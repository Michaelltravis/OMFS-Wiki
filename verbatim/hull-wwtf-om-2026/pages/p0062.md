###### **<u>Statement(s) to RFP document:</u>**

<!-- ¶13 -->
- **S #1:** The Town has previously discussed the potential implementation of Wet Well Wizard control systems at several wastewater pump stations. The Town would like to clarify that it has independently moved forward with the installation of these systems at five (6) pump stations (Stations A, 1, 3, 4, 5, and 9). These installations are being installed under a separate Town initiative and shall not be included in the proposal for Operations and Maintenance services under this RFP. Bidders should assume these systems will be in place and operational as part of the existing infrastructure at the identified pump stations.

<!-- ¶14 -->
- **S #2:** For firms to plan appropriately, the Town will conduct all interviews on April 21, 2026. The 60minute interviews will include 30 min for presentation and 30 minutes for questions. Specific start times for each firm will be determined, but will be between 10am and 3pm.

<!-- ¶15 -->
- **S #3:** Each proposer shall provide their annual budget for sewer cleaning and CCTV per the scope described in the RFP. The Town is seeking this for informational purposes, not for selection.  There has been an update to the RFP attachment H, Fee Proposal Form provided as part of this addendum.

###### **<u>Response(s) to bidder(s) question(s):</u>**

<!-- ¶19 -->
- **Q #1:** _Regarding biosolids disposal - is this meant to be done at cost, or can the proposer issue a markup?_

<!-- ¶20 -->
- **A #1:** The Town expects each proposer to reveal its cost for Biosolids hauling and disposal, without markup, to establish a baseline should there ever be a change in disposal options that would significantly impact the Contractor’s biosolids disposal cost.
