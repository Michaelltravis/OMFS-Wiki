###### **_Safety Leadership and Culture_**

<!-- ¶4 -->
The Town’s facilities will be operated under our **BeyondZero® safety philosophy** , which emphasizes proactive risk management and shared accountability. Safety leadership begins with the Project Manager and extends to all employees and subcontractors. Daily work activities will include pre‑task planning, activity hazard analyses (AHAs), and clearly defined safe work procedures. All personnel will have **stop-work authority** and will be expected to exercise it whenever unsafe conditions are identified.

<!-- ¶5 -->
Safety metrics will be reviewed with the Town during routine meetings to support transparency and continuous improvement. Jacobs maintains an **Experience Modification Rate (EMR) below 1.0** , exceeding the RFP safety requirement, and our industry‑leading safety performance is further illustrated in **Exhibit 5-10** .

<!-- ¶6 -->
###### **_Site-Specific Safety and Security Planning_**

<!-- ¶7 -->
Within **30 days of Notice to Proceed** , Jacobs will submit our Site‑Specific Safety Plan and Site Security Plan for Town review. The Safety Plan will address confined space entry, lockout/ tagout, electrical safety, chemical handling, fall protection, gas monitoring, contractor coordination, and emergency response procedures. Given the Town’s vulnerability to coastal storms and flooding, the plan will also define **storm preparation protocols, staffing contingencies, and flood response procedures** to maintain operational continuity.

<!-- ¶8 -->
Security measures address both administrative and physical controls, including controlled facility access, secured gates and doors, visitor verification procedures, and annual security assessments. We will coordinate with local emergency responders to ensure familiarity with facility operations and **rapid response during emergencies** .

<!-- ¶9 -->
Physical security across the WWTF, pump stations, and collection system will also be evaluated. Existing deficiencies, such as holes in the WWTF perimeter fencing that allow

<!-- ¶10 -->
potential unauthorized access, will be addressed through **immediate repairs and implementation of routine perimeter inspections** . Additional enhancements, including improved gate locking, signage, lighting, and surveillance, will be considered to strengthen long‑term site security.

<!-- ¶11 -->
###### **_Cybersecurity and SCADA Protection_**

<!-- ¶12 -->
We will implement a cybersecurity plan aligned with industry best practices for OT and industrial control systems, using a **defense-in-depth approach** to protect system integrity and maintain operational continuity. The plan will include system risk assessment, role‑based access controls, secure network segmentation between business IT and SCADA environments, secure remote access protocols, and password governance. Continuous monitoring will identify abnormal activity and incident response procedures will define notification, containment, and recovery actions.

<!-- ¶13 -->
System backups and recovery capabilities will also be maintained to support **rapid restoration of operations** following a cyber or system event. Annual cybersecurity awareness training will be provided to on‑site personnel to reduce risks associated with phishing, credential compromise, and other cyber threats.
