### **Westerly Wastewater Treatment Plant** O&M AND DESIGN-BUILD-OPERATE (DBO) SERVICES | TOWN OF WESTERLY, RI

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### **DESCRIPTION OF SERVICES PROVIDED**

<!-- ¶3 -->
Jacobs operates the Town’s 3.3-MGD wastewater treatment facility, including nine pump stations and associated utility equipment. The plant accepts hauled commercial and domestic septage, with Jacobs managing an industrial pretreatment program (IPP).

<!-- ¶4 -->
###### **DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE FACILITIES MANAGED**

<!-- ¶5 -->
The town officials wanted to find ways to save additional costs and risk. The original contract, long held by SUEZ Group, didn’t include biosolids management and disposal, but Jacobs saw an opportunity to help Westerly realize additional cost savings. We adjusted hauling schedules, monitored energy use, and deployed quality systems to make sludge handling more efficient. This achieved more than $500,000 savings against the original 6-year forecast, and about $250,000 savings against the adjusted forecast over the last 3 years.

<!-- ¶6 -->
In the first 18 months of our contract, we have improved the facility by:

<!-- ¶7 -->
- µ<sup>Implementing “in-house” clarifier rehabilitations.</sup>

<!-- ¶8 -->
- µ<sup>Completing corrective action items inherited from the previous contract operator</sup> (DEM-mandated corrective action items from 2016 facility inspection).

<!-- ¶9 -->
- µ<sup>Improving the disinfection system upgrades. Jacobs eliminated the callouts the</sup> previous contract operator estimated, including approximately 32 callouts per year for sodium hypochlorite dosing problems.

<!-- ¶10 -->
- µ<sup>Removing six 30-yard containers of trash and debris from throughout the facility</sup> and pump stations, including scrap metal that was recycled with the money refunded to the client.

<!-- ¶11 -->
- µ<sup>Improving general facility and pump station appearance through landscaping,</sup> painting, and vegetation removal.

<!-- ¶12 -->
**Progressive Design-Build.** In 2021, under a consent agreement with RIDEM, Jacobs helped the Town lower nitrogen limits and update the Facilities Plan. Our role included running hydraulic models, assessing equipment, and making recommendations. We were chosen as the designer due to our operational knowledge.
