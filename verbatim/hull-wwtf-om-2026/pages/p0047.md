Personnel integration, systems alignment, safety programs, compliance protocols, and emergency procedures will be coordinated in advance, **making the transition seamless** for regulators, residents, and the community.

<!-- ¶2 -->
Refined through numerous municipal transitions and successfully applied at comparable wastewater facilities across EPA Region 1 and the Northeast, this framework ensures Day 1 readiness, proactive risk management, and stabilized workforce culture. Our transition plan specifically **mitigates common operational risks** such as staff turnover, incomplete asset records, SCADA access limitations, and emergency response readiness. A high‑level Gantt chart outlining transition activities, including the job‑shadowing period, is provided in Appendix G.

<!-- ¶3 -->
We encourage the Town to speak directly with Jacobs’ municipal clients who have experienced our transition process firsthand. Their feedback consistently **reflects strong communication, organized mobilization, and early operational improvements** that set the foundation for a high‑ performing, long‑term partnership.

<!-- ¶4 -->
###### **_Immediate Mobilization and Governance_**

<!-- ¶5 -->
Upon receiving award, Jacobs will activate our Transition Leadership Team and establish a formal governance structure with the Town. Decision authority, communication cadence, issue escalation, and accountability assignments will be

<!-- ¶6 -->
confirmed at the outset, so **progress remains transparent and measurable** . We will immediately complete structured due diligence, including:

<!-- ¶7 -->
- µ<sup>Review of NPDES permit requirements, monitoring/</sup> reporting obligations, and compliance history

<!-- ¶8 -->
- µ<sup>Detailed review of O&M manuals, SOPs, CMOM</sup> documentation, and historical performance data

<!-- ¶9 -->
- µ<sup>Verification of certified operator coverage and license/</sup> grade requirements

<!-- ¶10 -->
- µ<sup>Asset inventory validation and critical equipment condition</sup> assessment (including maintenance backlog)

<!-- ¶11 -->
- µ<sup>SCADA/telemetry review, remote access validation, alarm</sup> management testing, and cybersecurity verification

<!-- ¶12 -->
- µ<sup>Flood control readiness review (pumps/gates), standby</sup> power verification, and storm response procedures

<!-- ¶13 -->
All readiness milestones will be tracked against the Transition Schedule to ensure the Town’s facilities remain **stable and compliant throughout transition.**
