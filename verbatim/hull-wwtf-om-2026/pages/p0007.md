##### **THE JACOBS DIFFERENCE: EXPERTISE THAT DRIVES RESULTS**

<!-- ¶3 -->
Jacobs delivers more than traditional O&M services by combining **experienced on‑site leadership with a deep bench of regional and national Subject Matter Experts (SMEs)** . Our team includes specialists in process optimization, biosolids management, odor control, asset management, regulatory compliance, maintenance reliability, and digital systems. These experts actively support the on-site team, providing **targeted solutions and accelerating implementation of improvements** . By connecting real-time operational insights with engineering and asset management expertise, we **translate daily performance data into informed capital planning and system improvements** .

<!-- ¶4 -->
Our approach aligns operational decisions with long-term investments, **enhancing system reliability, extending asset life, and delivering cost‑effective performance** .

At the Town of Southbridge’s WWTP and collection system, our team improved reporting transparency, reduced long-standing odor issues related to biosolids composting, and optimized chemical and power usage, **enhancing system performance, community conditions, and operational efficiency** .

<!-- ¶11 -->
The Town of Hull’s wastewater system operates under conditions that require disciplined execution. Seasonal population changes, tidal infiltration, and wet-weather variability introduce operational complexity that must be actively managed. We address these challenges through **proactive monitoring, coordinated operations, and disciplined execution** . Our team strengthens reliability

<!-- ¶12 -->
through standard operating procedures (SOPs), preventive and predictive maintenance, and improved monitoring of pump stations and collection system assets.

<!-- ¶13 -->
For the Town of Westerly’s WWTP, Jacobs improved odor control by upgrading an aging biofilter system from organic media to engineered media with a significantly longer service life. This reduced replacement frequency, **improved system reliability, and delivered over $134k in cost savings** . Our disciplined approach improves compliance stability, reduces variability, and supports **predictable long‑term operating costs** .

<!-- ¶14 -->
##### **CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT THROUGH AN ANNUAL INNOVATION WORKSHOP**

<!-- ¶15 -->
We believe successful wastewater operations are built on **strong partnerships.** Our team will operate as an **extension of the Town’s staff** , maintaining open communication, transparency, and alignment with Town priorities while proactively identifying opportunities to strengthen long-term system performance and resilience.

<!-- ¶16 -->
We reinforce this approach through our Annual Innovation Workshop, a structured, collaborative forum that brings together Town staff and Jacobs SMEs to review system performance, evaluate prior-year accomplishments, and establish **targeted goals for the coming year focused on continuous improvement** while avoiding complacency over time. Drawing on expertise in process engineering, biosolids, collections, odor control, energy management, and digital optimization, these sessions focus on high-value opportunities aligned with the Town’s priorities.

<!-- ¶17 -->
By connecting **day‑to‑day operations with technical expertise and forward‑looking planning** , we help the Town move beyond reactive operations toward **continuous, measurable improvement** . This process ensures alignment with evolving regulatory requirements, improves decision-making, and provides a **clear path from operational challenges to implementable solutions** .
