This effort led to targeted operational improvements,<br>including enhanced solids management, optimized<br>aeration and process control, and refined residuals<br>handling practices. These changes addressed the<br>root causes of odors without requiring immediate<br>capital investment.<br><!-- End of picture text -->

<!-- ¶3 -->
As a result, the facility experienced a measurable<br>reduction in odor complaints and improved process<br>stability, demonstrating Jacobs’ ability to quickly<br>diagnose complex odor issues and implement cost‑<br>effective solutions in partnership with the client. <!-- ¶4 -->
###### **_Rapid Complaint Response and Investigation_**

<!-- ¶5 -->
We will respond to all odor complaints **within 1 hour of notification** . Upon receipt of a complaint, qualified personnel will assess the reported location, document odor characteristics and weather conditions, and review plant and collection system operating conditions, including pump station performance, wet well conditions, and force main detention times.

<!-- ¶6 -->
Corrective actions will be implemented promptly when warranted. All complaints and responses will be logged, and summaries of odor monitoring activities, investigations, and corrective actions will be provided to the Town monthly. The Project Manager will serve as the primary point of contact to ensure **timely communication and transparency** .

<!-- ¶7 -->
###### **_Wastewater Treatment Facility Odor Management_**

<!-- ¶8 -->
Within the WWTF, odor generation will be minimized through **optimized process control and solids management** . DO levels will be maintained to prevent septic conditions, sludge age and solids retention time will be managed to reduce sulfide formation, and biosolids processing frequency will be adjusted as needed to limit storage duration.

<!-- ¶9 -->
We will evaluate the performance and capacity of the existing ventilation and odor control systems serving the headworks, thickening areas, and sludge storage infrastructure. Particular attention will be given to confirming adequate air movement to meet safety and odor control requirements. High sulfide concentrations in confined areas, such as the gravity thickener, may indicate insufficient ventilation or undersized odor treatment systems. Where warranted, we will identify operational improvements and recommend potential capital upgrades through the Town’s CIP process.

<!-- ¶10 -->
###### **_Collection System and Pump Station Odor Control_**

<!-- ¶11 -->
The Town’s long force mains and pump stations are major contributors to hydrogen sulfide formation and odor risk. Wet wells will be inspected routinely for grease buildup and septicity, and cleaning frequencies will be adjusted proactively. Pump cycling strategies will be optimized to reduce detention time and limit hydrogen sulfide generation.

<!-- ¶12 -->
The Town currently uses chemical treatment for hydrogen sulfide and FOG control. We will review existing chemical dosing practices, feed locations, and chemical selection to ensure that dosing remains **effective and cost-efficient** . Industry best practices will be applied to verify that chemical dosing rates are appropriate and not excessive relative to sulfide loading conditions. We will also monitor, control, and periodically evaluate the Town's current use of bioxide, In‑Pipe Technology, and Wet Well Wizard‑style optimization practices, adjusting feed locations, dosing rates, cleaning triggers, and field routing/ priorities based on measured conditions and observed results.

<!-- ¶13 -->
As part of our SmartCover® implementation, the units can also be modified from monitoring I/I and incorporated with H2Scents®, a specialized sensor that provides **reliable, real-time measurement of hydrogen sulfide levels** over extended periods. H2Scents® allows real‑time identification of hydrogen sulfide levels, supporting more accurate dosing that reduces chemical use, minimizes odor, and extends the life of the collection system assets. H2Scents® can be used as a diagnostic tool to help minimize or eliminate the use of bioxide and In‑Pipe Technology.

<!-- ¶14 -->
Hydrogen sulfide trends will be reviewed as part of preventive maintenance planning to support corrosion prevention and long‑ term asset preservation.
