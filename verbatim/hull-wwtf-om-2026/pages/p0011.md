##### **TECHNICAL QUALIFICATIONS**

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### **YEARS OF EXPERIENCE PROVIDING O&M SERVICES FOR WASTEWATER FACILITIES**

<!-- ¶3 -->
Jacobs has more than **45 years of experience providing O&M services** for water and wastewater systems, supporting complex facilities from startup and commissioning through long-term operations. As shown in **Exhibit 3-1** , our O&M portfolio spans facilities across the United States, including a **strong presence in the Northeast** , demonstrating our ability to deliver **consistent, highquality operations across diverse systems and regulatory environments** . Our portfolio of **more than 300 O&M projects** includes water and wastewater treatment facilities, collection and distribution systems, water quality laboratories, stormwater management programs, and groundwater remediation systems.

<!-- ¶4 -->
As a multidisciplined O&M provider, we bring a **“more-than-an-operator” approach** , integrating operations, process engineering, asset management, and technical services to **optimize system performance, reliability, and compliance** . This approach directly supports the Town’s goals for reliable operations, regulatory compliance, and long-term system performance. Our experience **directly aligns with the Town’s requirements** , including operation of wastewater treatment facilities exceeding **3 MGD** , management of collection systems greater than **40 miles** , and operation of systems with **multiple pump stations and lowpressure sewer networks** .
