###### **TRANSPARENT COMMUNICATIONS AND REPORTING**

<!-- ¶2 -->
From Day 1, we will improve communication between the project team and the Town by operating with a **no-surprises, transparent partnership,** providing clear points of contact, rapid escalation paths, and consistent documentation of actions and outcomes. Project Manager Nathan Callison will maintain open day‑to‑day coordination with Town staff to accelerate decisions, identify risks early, and resolve issues before they affect service. We treat communication as an operational control: **the right information will reach the right decision-maker at the right time** to protect safety, compliance, and uninterrupted service.

<!-- ¶3 -->
The Communication Protocols shown in **Exhibit 5-3** will be implemented during transition and early operations. These protocols will combine frequent day‑to‑day coordination with structured forums to review performance, maintenance execution, risks, and upcoming work. During recurring meetings with the Town, we will provide written monthly reporting aligned to the required scope of work, including operational and process control results, compliance/near‑exceedances, maintenance and repair activity and costs, M&R fund tracking, power utilization (kWh/MG), significant/imminent events, and collection system activities. We will also manage required permits and reporting and will route media inquiries to the Town’s authorized representative, as directed. Maintenance and compliance reporting are discussed in more detail later in this approach.

###### **_Annual Innovation Workshops Drive Continuous Improvement and Innovation_**

<!-- ¶5 -->
Our **Annual Innovation Workshops** , provided at no cost to the Town, will bring together Town staff, stakeholders, and our O&M staff, as well as industry recognized leaders in areas such as process engineering, biosolids, hydraulics, wet weather flow management, pump station design and operations, digital optimization, and energy management. We will review the prior year’s accomplishments and establish new goals and innovations to address the Town’s evolving needs and long‑ term vision. We’ll work closely with the Town to co‑develop a full day agenda that reflects your priorities and expectations, focusing the session on your desired outcomes. This important annual meeting ensures we continuously look forward to future needs and trends; help the Town address emerging challenges, technology, and O&M practices; and eliminate complacency over time.
