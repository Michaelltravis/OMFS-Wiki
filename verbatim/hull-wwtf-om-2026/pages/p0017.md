###### **_Environmental Compliance_**

<!-- ¶17 -->
Jacobs is the industry leader in environmental compliance with a **20-year compliance record of 99.8% with the NPDES permit requirements** across the wastewater treatment plants that we operate nationwide. Jacobs may, from time to time, have minor environmental violations, as customarily occurs in the ordinary course of business in operating water and wastewater utilities. However, all such violations are **minor in nature and will not impact Jacobs' ability to deliver the services** described in the RFP. In the rare instances Jacobs is the subject of a fine or enforcement action, we work with the regulator to resolve the issue **quickly and transparently** . The limited number of fines and enforcement actions encountered in our O&M business over the past 5 years is summarized in **Exhibit 3-9** . These matters were administrative in nature, **promptly addressed, and resolved in coordination with regulators, with no impact to our ability to deliver services** .

<!-- ¶18 -->
###### **_Meeting and Guaranteeing Permit Compliance_**

<!-- ¶19 -->
Jacobs assumes **full responsibility for meeting all permit requirements** , including NPDES, air quality, biosolids, and stormwater permits. We perform all required sampling, analysis, and reporting in accordance with current state and federal regulations. Plant staff conduct routine NPDES analyses, including pH, dissolved oxygen, and temperature monitoring, while specialized permit-required testing is completed by certified commercial laboratories. Samples are transported via courier to ensure compliance with hold-time requirements and data integrity standards.

<!-- ¶20 -->
Beyond permit-required sampling and compliance monitoring, operators conduct routine in-house process control testing on representative samples collected throughout the facility. These analyses provide **real-time operational data that support proactive, data-driven decision-making** , allow our team to **identify trends, anticipate process variability, and optimize treatment performance before issues arise** .

<!-- ¶21 -->
Compliance performance is actively tracked by our regional process specialists, who work closely with the on-site team to monitor trends, verify reporting accuracy, and address potential issues before they escalate. Through proactive monitoring, internal compliance audits, structured reporting controls, and regulatory engagement, Jacobs ensures **continuous compliance and rapid corrective action** should performance trends indicate potential risk **.**
