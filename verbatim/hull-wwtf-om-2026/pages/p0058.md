- **Q #2:** _1- Definition of “Actual Unit Price” The RFP states that the “actual unit price for power in any given year will be used in the sharing calculation.” Please confirm that “actual unit price” is defined as the total annual electric cost divided by total annual kWh consumed at the WWTF (i.e., the all-in effective rate including distribution and supply charges)._

<!-- ¶18 -->
- **A #2:** Yes, the Town will use the average rate of the power consumed (including transmission charges) for the treatment plant in this calculation, with the aim of reflecting the actual kWh cost over any given year.
