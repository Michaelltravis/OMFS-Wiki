**Lead Operations and Maintenance Technician | Grand Traverse Regional WWTP, Traverse City and Grand Traverse County |** _Traverse City, MI._ Responsible for all aspects of operations at the Grand Traverse Regional WWTP which is a membrane bioreactor wastewater treatment facility with average flows of 5.5 MGD and wet weather flows up to around 15 MGD. Responsible for maintenance and recovery cleaning of the Zeeweed 0.4 micron membrane cassettes, all plant preventive and corrective maintenance, sampling, lab analysis, adjustment of chemical dosing rates, cleaning of bar and fine screens, adjusting key performance indicator (KPI) targets such as MCRT, F:M ratio, MLSS targets, etc., troubleshooting operational issues, on-call rotation to respond to alarms, O&M of lift stations, O&M of the Grand Traverse Septage Receiving Station, completion and submittal of monthly operating reports (MORs), discharge monitoring reports (DMRs), IPP reports, annual biosolid reports, and essentially any and all activities related to successful operation of the POTW to maintain consistent compliance.

**Lead Operator | WWTP Operations, Village of Elk Rapids |** _Elk Rapids, MI._ Responsible for all operational decisions and adjustments, including oversight of the wastewater laboratory and QA/QC program. Managed all EGLE reporting including DMR, residuals management program (RMP), per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS), and annual IPP reports. Oversaw and enforced the Village IPP program. Planned and performed all plant preventive, predictive, and corrective maintenance. Conducted laboratory analysis, gave plant tours, and participated in monthly Village council meetings. Responsible for procurement of laboratory and plant supplies, budget preparation and submission, and creation and implementation of standard operating procedures (SOPs) for plant operations and lift stations.

**Wastewater Plant Operator | WWTP Operations, City of Midland |** _Midland, MI._ Responsible for monitoring, maintenance, and operation of the wastewater plant, collection system, pump stations, and gas-to-energy building. Duties included sample collection, laboratory testing, process adjustments, QA/QC analysis, equipment maintenance, data entry, biosolids management, collection system maintenance, chemical preparation, and ensuring effluent compliance with Department of Environmental Quality (DEQ) permitted limitations. Operated specialized equipment including belt filter press, vactor and flusher trucks, and handled 2,000-pound chlorine cylinders. Responded to citizen concerns and often worked independently as the sole on-duty personnel.

**Lead O&M Technician | Wastewater Facilities Operations, Village of Suttons Bay and Village of Northport |** _Traverse City, MI._ Operated two municipal wastewater facilities, three lift stations, and approximately 120 residential grinder pump systems. Duties included sample collection, process lab testing, data recording, equipment maintenance, safety audits, stormwater assessments, chemical preparation, residential callouts, and system troubleshooting. Operated the Suttons Bay Class B Extended Aeration Treatment Plant (up to 0.300 MGD) and the Northport WWTP Moving Bed Biofilm Reactor (MBBR) plant (0.04-0.100 MGD). Responsible for process reporting, biosolids management, permit sampling, discharge monitoring reports, and process control. Attended village council meetings, performed maintenance on various equipment, managed inventory, and handled on-call duties. Conducted quarterly monitoring well sampling and managed sustainability initiatives for northern Michigan projects.
