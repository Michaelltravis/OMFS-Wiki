###### **COMMUNITY STEWARDSHIP**

<!-- ¶7 -->
We approach community stewardship in the Town as an **operating responsibility, not an occasional outreach effort** . The Town wants a partner that aligns with its values, communicates well, and works collaboratively with Town staff and the broader community, as demonstrated through our **proven community stewardship practices** shown **in Exhibit 5-21** .

<!-- ¶8 -->
The WWTF sits in a residential area in a dense coastal town where severe weather can quickly affect operations. We will focus on **practical actions that matter locally** : clear communication, visible follow‑through, support for appropriate Town sponsorship opportunities, joint facility familiarization, and emergency training with police, fire, and other first responders. Our goal is to help the community understand how **reliable wastewater operations protect public health** , reduce neighborhood impacts, and support quality of life. Activities may include:

<!-- ¶9 -->
- µ<sup>**Coordinating facility familiarization sessions and**</sup> **emergency-response training with the Town’s police, fire, and other personnel** so first responders understand the plant and collection system before an incident occurs.

<!-- ¶10 -->
- µ<sup>**Hosting periodic plant tours or small open-house events**</sup> for residents, schools, and community groups to show how wastewater operations protect public health, nearby waters, and neighborhood quality of life.

<!-- ¶11 -->
- µ<sup>**Supporting classroom visits, STEM outreach, and**</sup> **educational partnerships** that help local students understand treatment processes, water stewardship, and water‑sector careers.

<!-- ¶12 -->
- µ<sup>**Participating in community events with simple, practical**</sup> **public education materials** that explain how proactive operations reduce odors, minimize service disruptions, and support reliable service.

<!-- ¶13 -->
- µ<sup>**Joining local environmental stewardship and**</sup> **cleanup efforts** that reinforce the connection between reliable utility operations and protection of the Town’s coastal environment.

<!-- ¶14 -->
- µ<sup>**Creating internships, mentoring, and career-awareness**</sup> **opportunities** with local or regional schools to build awareness of water‑sector careers.

<!-- ¶15 -->
- µ<sup>**Partnering with local schools for murals and exploring**</sup> **native planting options** to support plant beautification.

<!-- ¶16 -->
- µ<sup>**Supporting appropriate Town initiatives and community**</sup> **organizations** in ways that are visible, practical, and consistent with the Town’s close‑knit character.

<!-- ¶17 -->
###### **INNOVATION**

<!-- ¶18 -->
Jacobs brings the Town more than routine O&M. We will pair disciplined day‑to‑day execution with **practical, proven innovation** that helps improve reliability, reduce nuisance conditions, and support **better long-term investment decisions** . Through structured innovation workshops, direct connectivity between O&M leaders and Jacobs’ regional and national SMEs, and a strong link between field operations and capital planning, we will help the Town turn recurring issues and operating data into **actionable improvements rather than isolated fixes** . **Exhibit 5-22** demonstrates the breadth of value‑added innovations we propose to bring the Town.
