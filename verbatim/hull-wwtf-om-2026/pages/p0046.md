###### **TRANSITION PLAN**

<!-- ¶9 -->
###### **_Transition Plan with Approach and Timeline of Activities Necessary to Achieve the Commencement Date_**

<!-- ¶10 -->
Transitioning operations of the Town’s WWTF, collection system, pump stations, and flood control infrastructure is a critical responsibility requiring careful planning, experienced leadership, and disciplined execution. Jacobs brings proven experience successfully transitioning complex wastewater systems while **maintaining uninterrupted service** as workforce continuity (see **Exhibit 5-18** ). The Town must be confident that service levels, permit compliance, communication, and workforce stability will not be compromised. Our transition approach is designed to eliminate risk through disciplined planning, experienced leadership, and a structured operational handoff that establishes a culture of accountability from Day 1.

<!-- ¶11 -->
Jacobs has successfully transitioned multiple complex water and wastewater systems from previous operators. Recent examples include West Basin Municipal Water District and Jackson Water (JXN Water), where Jacobs assumed operational responsibility while maintaining uninterrupted service and workforce continuity. In both cases, utility leadership has recognized the effectiveness of Jacobs’ disciplined transition approach and the positive impact on employee engagement and operational confidence.

<!-- ¶12 -->
Transition activities will begin immediately upon award and continue through Commencement and the early months of operation. Organized into three phases: Pre‑Start Transition, Initial Transition (Days 1–30), and Extended Transition (Days 31–120), this structured approach ensures **continuity, leadership alignment, and uninterrupted regulatory compliance** . Key milestones include completion of due diligence within 30 days of award, workforce integration prior to Commencement, SCADA and alarm validation before July 1, and confirmation of regulatory reporting systems prior to assumption of operations. We will coordinate with MassDEP and EPA Region 1 to confirm operator certification coverage, reporting contacts, and regulatory communication protocols prior to Commencement.
