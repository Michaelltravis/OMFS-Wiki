##### **CORPORATE EXPERIENCE**

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### **SIMILAR FACILITIES**

<!-- ¶3 -->
We operate and maintain wastewater facilities in **coastal communities comparable to the Town of Hull** , including Traverse City, Michigan; Key West, Florida; and Westerly, Rhode Island. These systems include similar regulatory requirements, residential proximity, wet-weather conditions, and odor sensitivity, requiring **responsive operations and strong community stewardship** . **Appendix C** provides additional examples demonstrating our ability to manage facilities of similar size, complexity, and operating conditions. By partnering with Jacobs, the Town will gain an **experienced operator with a proven track record** managing systems of similar size and complexity, supported by the **resources, operational discipline, and depth of expertise** needed to deliver consistent performance and adapt to changing system demands.

<!-- ¶4 -->
###### **_Full O&M Services for Water and Wastewater Treatment Facilities_**

<!-- ¶5 -->
We deliver **comprehensive O&M services that extend beyond day-to-day operations** , integrating regulatory compliance, asset management, process optimization, and capital planning into a coordinated approach. Our teams are responsible for maintaining **permit compliance and reliable system performance** , while aligning operations with regulatory requirements, budget priorities, and community expectations, particularly in coastal and residentially sensitive environments. This **“more-than-anoperator” approach** supports the Town’s long-term infrastructure investment and directly reinforces the on-site team described in Section 4, ensuring **consistent, responsive, and reliable service** .

<!-- ¶6 -->
###### **ADDITIONAL TECHNICAL RESOURCES AND CAPITAL PLANNING SUPPORT**

<!-- ¶7 -->
We provide the Town of Hull with **direct access to extensive technical troubleshooting and capital planning resources** beyond the on-site O&M team. Capital improvement planning (CIP) is fully integrated into our Asset Management program, providing a **structured and defensible framework for prioritizing improvements, managing risk, and aligning investments** with operational needs. We have successfully delivered CIP programs and technical evaluations for wastewater facilities comparable to the Town’s.

<!-- ¶8 -->
These resources are primarily sourced from Jacobs’ in-house engineering, construction, and water and wastewater consulting teams, providing **immediate access to subject matter experts** in process engineering, electrical and controls, structural, mechanical, regulatory compliance, and asset management. When specialized expertise is required, we supplement our team with pre-qualified affiliate partners and subcontractors. Routine technical troubleshooting and operational support are included as part of our base operating services. Larger studies, detailed design efforts, or capital project implementation support are provided as additional services upon Town authorization, ensuring **transparency and cost control** .

<!-- ¶9 -->
Our vast network of resources, as shown in **Exhibit 3-6** , enables **rapid response to operational and compliance challenges** and supports continuous system improvement. These resources directly support the on-site team described in Section 4, providing **additional capacity, technical depth, and rapid response capability.**
