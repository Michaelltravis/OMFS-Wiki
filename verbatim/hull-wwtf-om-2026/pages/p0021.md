###### **TECHNICAL SUPPORT TEAM**

<!-- ¶2 -->
While our on-site team manages daily operations, our Technical Support Team will deliver **targeted, hands-on support and datadriven technical oversight** to accelerate maintenance improvements, support staff training, and proactively **identify and address risks before they impact compliance** . This support includes approximately **2,000 hours of dedicated support annually in our base fee** , providing the Town with **consistent access to specialized expertise** when and where it is needed most. These resources are available during normal business hours and can **be mobilized as needed to support emergency response, system upsets, or critical operational needs.**
