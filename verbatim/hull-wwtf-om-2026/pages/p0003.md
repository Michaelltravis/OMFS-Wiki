**Subject: Contract FY26-02 Wastewater Treatment Facility and Collection System Operation and Maintenance Services**

<!-- ¶6 -->
Dear Mr. Molinari and the Selection Committee,

<!-- ¶7 -->
The Town of Hull's residents, businesses, and coastal environment depend on wastewater operations that protect public health, maintain regulatory compliance, and support the community's quality of life. **The Town's coastal setting, proximity of residences to the treatment system, and the importance of storm preparedness, odor control, and effective biosolids management** require a disciplined and responsive operating partner. Addressing challenges such **as inflow and infiltration (I/I) and wet weather peak flows**, and coordinating capital improvements with daily operations also demands an experienced and collaborative team. Jacobs understands these priorities and has tailored our approach to support the Town's operational goals, protect Town assets, and deliver **resilient, high-quality service.**

<!-- ¶8 -->
**Project Manager Nathan Callison and Regional Operations Manager Scott Mangold** bring hands-on experience managing our municipal wastewater operations across New England. Their leadership will **strengthen operational accountability, safety culture, and process reliability** while promoting strong housekeeping practices, regulatory compliance, and clear communication with the Town. **Jacobs also brings regional and national wastewater specialists and industry-leading tools to reinforce our local team and expand available resources.** During our site visits, we engaged subject matter experts to complement our team's evaluation of the facility's current condition, regulatory drivers, and long-term opportunities for improvement. This combined perspective allows us to tailor our services to the Town's needs and focus on delivering **operational stability** while identifying opportunities to optimize **energy use, odor control, biosolids management, and chemical efficiency.**

<!-- ¶9 -->
**A successful contract begins with a smooth transition and stable, supported workforce.** Jacobs will implement **a structured transition plan focused on knowledge transfer, workforce continuity, safety alignment, and seamless, uninterrupted service**, while advancing **staff training, succession planning, and alignment with the Town's culture, goals, and community values**. This approach **minimizes operational risk, maintains continuity of service, and positions the team for long-term performance and resiliency.** From Day 1, we will maintain regular communication with Town leadership and support continuous improvement through initiatives such as our **Annual Innovation Workshop.**

<!-- ¶10 -->
Jacobs is committed to being a **trusted, long-term partner** to the Town of Hull. We look forward to working collaboratively with the Town to deliver **safe, reliable, and forward-looking wastewater services** for the community.

Jacobs acknowledges receipt and review of all addenda issued for this solicitation.
