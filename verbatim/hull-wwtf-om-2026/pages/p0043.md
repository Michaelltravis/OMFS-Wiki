###### **ASSET MANAGEMENT/MAINTENANCE**

<!-- ¶6 -->
###### **_The Town’s Asset Environment_**

<!-- ¶7 -->
Jacobs understands that the Town’s wastewater system must perform in a demanding coastal setting. The Town operates a 3.07‑MGD activated‑sludge WWTF, seven wastewater pump stations, the D Street stormwater station, approximately 42 miles of gravity and force mains, and a low‑pressure grinder system, all within a flood‑prone peninsula environment where storms, access constraints, and community sensitivity heighten the importance of **reliable assets and disciplined maintenance** .

<!-- ¶8 -->
While observations suggest the facilities are generally maintained, the Town is clearly seeking more than routine maintenance support. The Town wants a partner that will protect its assets through cleaner facilities, more visible accountability, stronger self‑performance, better use of Cartegraph and CMMS data, and closer linkage between daily maintenance and future capital decisions. Our **proactive asset management program** improves day‑to‑day execution while giving the Town **clearer visibility into condition, risk, and renewal priorities** .

<!-- ¶9 -->
As shown in **Exhibit 5-13** , we bring **visible change** to how the Town’s assets are managed, maintained, and improved, shifting **from reactive, fragmented practices to a planned, accountable, and self-performed program** .

###### **_Our Asset Management and Maintenance Approach for the Town_**

<!-- ¶15 -->
###### **_CMMS-Driven Asset Visibility_**

<!-- ¶16 -->
A **preventive-first asset management program** will be implemented, anchored by a CMMS strategy developed to align with the Town’s adopted systems, workflows, and reporting needs. The Town has been moving toward Cartegraph, which, through its GIS‑enabled environment, offers strong value for linear assets such as the collection system, pump stations, low‑ pressure sewer infrastructure, and flood‑control assets.

<!-- ¶17 -->
We bring experience across a broad range of CMMS and EAM platforms, including Cartegraph and Maintenance Connection. Maintenance Connection has proven especially effective for vertical assets such as WWTF equipment because it supports disciplined work order management, preventive maintenance planning, asset history, and KPI reporting. Rather than forcing a one‑size‑fits‑all solution before transition, we’ll confirm the most effective CMMS approach for all assets—whether that means using Cartegraph as the primary platform, supporting a complementary plant‑focused workflow, or integrating value‑ add systems, so the Town receives one **clear, reliable view of asset condition, maintenance status, and performance** .
