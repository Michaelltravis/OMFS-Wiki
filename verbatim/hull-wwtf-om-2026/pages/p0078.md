### **Southbridge Wastewater Treatment Plant** TOWN OF SOUTHBRIDGE | SOUTHBRIDGE, MA

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### **DESCRIPTION OF SERVICES PROVIDED**

<!-- ¶3 -->
Jacobs was unanimously selected by the Town to operate and maintain the 3.77-MGD WWTP, 48 miles of collection system, and 11 lift stations. Under a 5-year contract, Jacobs transitioned the operations of the WWTP from the previous contractor and assumed full operating responsibility on February 1, 2025.

<!-- ¶4 -->
###### **DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE FACILITIES MANAGED**

<!-- ¶5 -->
As part of the partnership, we are leveraging our unique strengths to make significant investments in the Town’s facilities and system upgrades to improve operability and performance. This investment will allow for more efficient and timely distribution of compost and the potential for revenue generation through product sales. Some of these upgrades include:

<!-- ¶6 -->
- µ<sup>Improving composting operations by implementing new screening equipment to</sup> reduce bulking material requirements and produce a higher quality product that is more marketable

<!-- ¶7 -->
- µ<sup>Conducting an odor control and dispersion study and value engineering</sup> improvements to reduce odors as a responsible neighbor of the community

<!-- ¶8 -->
- µ<sup>Implementing innovative technologies and enhancements, such as</sup> Dragonfly AI enhanced CCTV monitoring and analysis, so employees’ time is maximized efficiently

<!-- ¶9 -->
- µ<sup>Providing technical expertise support to the plant staff throughout operations and</sup> during annual innovation workshops to focus on continuous improvement and future planning
