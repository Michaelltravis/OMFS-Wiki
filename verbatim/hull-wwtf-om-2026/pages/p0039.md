We will also build on the Town’s demonstrated interest in digital tools for optimization and transparency, including its current use of Aquasight/APOLLO, by ensuring OT data are **trustworthy and actionable** , which supports better process insight, faster troubleshooting, and clearer reporting to Town stakeholders.

<!-- ¶9 -->
###### **INTEGRATED SAFETY, SECURITY, AND CYBER RESILIENCE**

<!-- ¶10 -->
Jacobs will implement a **fully integrated safety, security, and cybersecurity program** designed to protect personnel, safeguard Town assets, and maintain **uninterrupted wastewater operations** in the Town’s coastal environment. The facilities operate in a flood‑prone area, serve a dense residential community, and must meet strict NPDES permit requirements. Our program will support **safe, reliable operations under both routine and extreme conditions** .

<!-- ¶11 -->
Our objective is simple: **zero injuries, zero security breaches, and zero service interruptions.** Safety, physical security, and cybersecurity will be integrated into daily operations, maintenance planning, and emergency preparedness to **reduce risk and strengthen system resiliency** .
