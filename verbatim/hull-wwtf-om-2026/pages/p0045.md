<!-- ¶4 -->
###### **_Self-Performance with Specialist Depth_**

<!-- ¶5 -->
Routine maintenance and most corrective work will be **self-performed with support from regional specialists** . Subcontractors will be used only for specialty electrical work, OEM or warranty work, heavy rigging, certified repairs, or other discrete services requiring specialized equipment or credentials. This approach improves responsiveness, reduces handoffs, and aligns with the Town’s desire for less reliance on subcontractors for routine work. As noted in the Collections subsection above, specialty collection system CCTV and sewer cleaning services will be delivered through qualified vendors when that model is more economical, while Jacobs retains planning, oversight, QA/ QC, and single‑point accountability to the Town.

<!-- ¶6 -->
###### **_Housekeeping Focused on Clean, Orderly Facilities_**

<!-- ¶7 -->
Housekeeping will be treated as part of the maintenance program, not as work completed only when time allows. Cleanliness, organization, labeling, and minor site upkeep will be embedded into rounds, check sheets, and CMMS tasks, resulting in **safer facilities, better work preparation, and stronger pride of ownership** across the WWTF and pump stations.

<!-- ¶8 -->
###### **_Whole-System Asset Planning_**

<!-- ¶9 -->
We will apply **one integrated asset strategy** across the Town’s WWTF, pump stations, standby power systems, flood‑control assets, and low‑pressure grinder system. CMMS history, observed condition, repeat failures, and obsolescence risk will be used to develop **practical, decision-ready capital recommendations** and help standardize future upgrades where appropriate. Grinder pumps will also be managed as a distinct asset class, using work‑order history and failure data to improve repair planning, replacement forecasting, and lifecycle decision‑ making.

<!-- ¶10 -->
Proactive maintenance planning will be paired with **transparent cost control** . As summarized in **Exhibit 5-17** , Jacobs will manage the Town’s M&R fund in accordance with approval thresholds and reporting requirements, giving the Town **clear visibility into expenditures, balances, and decision points** .

###### **_Integrating SOPs, Training, and Storm Readiness into Maintenance Practices_**

<!-- ¶14 -->
SOPs and O&M procedures will be kept current, reinforced through supervisory review and shift turnover, and supported by structured skills assessment and cross‑training to strengthen long‑term workforce capability. Because the Town’s facilities operate in a storm‑exposed coastal environment, early priority will also be given to **standby power validation, flood-control readiness, alarm verification, and contingency procedures** so maintenance planning supports system resilience and equipment reliability.

<!-- ¶15 -->
###### **_Technical Expertise for Capital Planning Efforts_**

<!-- ¶16 -->
Using our technical expertise, our contributions to capital planning include using the initial asset assessment and ongoing maintenance data to turn asset condition, criticality, lifecycle cost, and operational risk into a **practical, risk-ranked capital plan** for the Town. Supported by integrated O&M, engineering, process, asset management, and digital resources, we will help prioritize renewal, resiliency, and strategic investments with defensible recommendations on scope, timing, sequencing, and funding needs. This ensures **day-to-day mainteance directly informs long-term capital decisions** .

<!-- ¶17 -->
###### **_Support for Planning and Executing Construction Projects and Facility Upgrades_**

<!-- ¶18 -->
Jacobs offers expertise in actively supporting, planning, and executing construction projects and facility upgrades as desired by the Town, including providing structured O&M input from project development through design, construction, startup, commissioning, and warranty. We regularly help clients with these types of improvements, helping ensure they are **practical to operate with minimal disruption to ongoing service** . Supported by Jacobs’ broader engineering, construction management, and design‑build bench, we can coordinate constructability reviews, outage and bypass planning, field support, testing, turnover, and asset record updates so new and modified assets are **fully integrated into the Town’s asset management program and ready for reliable operation on Day 1** .
