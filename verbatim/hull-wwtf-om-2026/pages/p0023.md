<!-- ¶1 -->
Jacobs will prioritize **continuity of leadership and institutional knowledge** by retaining the existing WWTF and collection system workforce to the greatest extent possible. We will begin transition planning immediately upon Notice of Award, with the primary objective of maintaining **stable day-to-day management and minimizing disruption** for Town staff and the operating team.

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### **_Retention‑First Staffing Strategy_**

<!-- ¶3 -->
- µ<sup>**Workforce retention:**Offer employment to all qualified,</sup> interested incumbent staff to retain facility-specific expertise, relationships, and operating history.

<!-- ¶4 -->
- µ<sup>**License and certification continuity:**Maintain required</sup> licenses and certifications by confirming credential status early and supporting renewals and training to ensure uninterrupted compliance coverage.

<!-- ¶5 -->
- µ<sup>**Targeted staffing support:**Supplement staffing where</sup> needed (e.g., vacancies, coverage gaps, or specialized needs), with qualified resources to ensure management continuity from Day 1.

<!-- ¶6 -->
###### **_Management Handoff and Knowledge Retention_**

<!-- ¶7 -->
We will implement a **structured leadership transition** that preserves what is working today and strengthens governance over time, which includes:

<!-- ¶8 -->
- µ<sup>**Early leadership alignment:**Project Manager Nathan</sup> Callison will meet with Town leadership and current facility leadership to confirm near-term priorities, reporting expectations, decision authority, and communication cadence.

<!-- ¶9 -->
- µ<sup>**Role clarity and continuity:**We will maintain clear lines</sup> of authority and responsibilities while integrating the management team into Jacobs’ safety, reporting, and performance systems.

<!-- ¶10 -->
- µ<sup>**Institutional knowledge capture:**We will formalize</sup> knowledge transfer through structured interviews, job shadowing, review of existing SOPs and logs, and documentation of critical operational “tribal knowledge” (e.g., seasonal conditions, equipment quirks, alarms, vendor nuances, and historical maintenance pain points).

<!-- ¶11 -->
- µ<sup>**Employee-focused communications:**We will conduct</sup> onboarding discussions and transition check-ins so staff understand expectations, benefits, training pathways, and how Jacobs will support them.

<!-- ¶12 -->
###### **SUBCONTRACTORS**

<!-- ¶13 -->
We will utilize qualified subcontractors for specialized services such as biosolids management and CCTV inspection. All subcontractors will be selected based on **demonstrated performance, cost-effectiveness, and familiarity with Massachusetts regulatory requirements** , and will be **subject to Town review and approval** prior to engagement.

<!-- ¶14 -->
###### **BACKUP RESOURCE AVAILABILITY TO AUGMENT ONSITE STAFF AND FILL TEMPORARY POSITIONS**

<!-- ¶15 -->
The off-site Regional and Technical Resources shown in **Exhibit 4-1** represent a portion of Jacobs' broader internal bench available to support **continuous, safe, compliant, and reliable operations** . These resources provide technical expertise, supplemental staffing for special projects, or support for innovation and process optimization, and can be **rapidly mobilized to respond to surge conditions, including emergencies or severe weather events** .

<!-- ¶16 -->
Planned absences and short-term vacancies will be covered by qualified personnel from nearby Jacobs projects or our regional and national staff pool. For unexpected absences, we will deploy trained personnel from across our O&M organization to **maintain safe and compliant operations with minimal disruption** .

<!-- ¶17 -->
Jacobs maintains a **dedicated Resource Planning Group (RPG)** focused on attracting, developing, and retaining highperforming O&M staff. In coordination with our regional management team, the RPG supports **rapid mobilization of qualified personnel to address both planned and unplanned staffing needs** .

<!-- ¶18 -->
