We've reviewed the Woodard & Curran Resiliency Report, including the evaluation of acquiring a jetter and CCTV capable vehicle, and agree that a subcontracting approach is the most economical, including cleaning 45,000 feet of collection system. To supplement this approach, Jacobs intends to lease a jetter to provide a **dedicated on-site resource for urgent or hot spot needs** , improving responsiveness and helping offset subcontracting costs.

<!-- ¶2 -->
Specialty services will be delivered through a **competitively priced vendor model with direct Jacobs oversight** , a delivery approach we commonly use on other collection system O&M projects. Based on pricing received from qualified CCTV and cleaning providers, this approach ensures **cost-effective service delivery while maintaining quality and control** . Jacobs retains responsibility for scope definition, schedule management, QA/QC, NASSCO deliverables, and integration of findings into the Town's CMMS, annual work planning, and rehabilitation/CIP prioritization.

<!-- ¶3 -->
We will also evaluate practical innovations that improve **collection system visibility, response, and cost control** without adding unnecessary complexity. Innovations may include enhanced use of SCADA/alarm trend data, stronger CMMS integration, mobile field documentation, and more flexible cleaning and wet well maintenance tools that allow crews to respond more quickly to emerging issues and recurring hotspots. InfoSense’s SL‑RAT will be used to assist with these efforts. This tool requires only two operators, minimal setup time, and no confined‑space entry. SL‑RAT helps the WWTF team **prioritize cleaning** , reduce unnecessary Vactor truck deployments, and **reduce overall maintenance costs** .

###### **_Low-Pressure System/Grinder Pumps_**

<!-- ¶5 -->
The low‑pressure system will be managed through a **structured grinder pump program** focused on responsive service, asset tracking, stocked spares, and proactive replacement planning. With about 175 grinder pumps in service, we will use work‑ order and failure data to identify repeat issues and support forecasting of renewal needs, **reducing repeat callouts and improving customer service** . Our approach ensures the low‑ pressure system is managed as a distinct and important part of the Town’s overall collection system.

###### **_Proactive Monitoring and Early Detection_**

<!-- ¶14 -->
A structured odor monitoring program will focus on **early detection and prevention** . Routine odor patrols will be conducted at the WWTF, pump stations, force main discharge locations, and facility perimeter areas, with increased frequency during warm weather and low‑flow conditions when detention times rise and septicity risks are greatest.

<!-- ¶15 -->
Operators will use portable hydrogen sulfide detection equipment and field observations to assess odor intensity and potential off‑site migration. Monitoring data, weather conditions, and observations will be documented and reviewed for trends. Internal hydrogen sulfide action thresholds will trigger **proactive operational response before off-site odor impacts occur** . Where appropriate, we will evaluate installation of fixed hydrogen sulfide sensors at strategic locations to support **real-time monitoring and improved process control** .
