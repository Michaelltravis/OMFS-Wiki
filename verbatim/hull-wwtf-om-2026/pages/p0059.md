- **Q #4:** _3- Maintenance & Repair (M&R) Budget – Procurement & Mark-Up The RFP references an annual Maintenance & Repair (M&R) budget of $450,000, with materials and subcontract services to be procured by the Contractor and subject to that budget. Question: Please clarify whether materials, subcontract services, equipment rental, and other M&R-funded expenditures are intended to be reimbursed as direct pass-through costs at invoice value, or whether the Contractor is permitted to apply a reasonable procurement and administrative mark-up consistent with industry practice. Additionally, please confirm the process for review and approval of such expenditures and the timing of reimbursement._

<!-- ¶4 -->
- **A #4:** The M&R fund is to be included in your fee.  All repair expenses that are paid from that fund will be accounted for in your monthly and annual reports.  Markup on these charges is not permitted in the Contractor’s monthly accounting for these expenses.  All repair expenses below $ 3,000 do not require Town advance approval, and all expenses above $3,000 require Town approval.  The Town anticipates that  approval of the over $3,000 in expenses would occur swiftly, typically with a phone call or a quick meeting with onsite Town staff and follow-up email.

- **Q #5:** _4- Collection System Chemicals – Procurement & Mark-Up The RFP includes an annual allowance of $100,000 for collection system chemicals, with a 50/50 sharing mechanism for savings or overruns. Question: Please confirm whether chemical procurement under this allowance is intended to be reimbursed at direct invoice cost, or whether the Contractor may apply a reasonable handling, storage, and procurement mark-up. Additionally, please confirm whether freight, emergency delivery charges, and supplier surcharges are considered part of the reimbursable chemical cost for purposes of the annual reconciliation._

<!-- ¶7 -->
- **A #3:** Like the M&R fund, the $100,000 for collection system chemicals is included in the Contractor’s fee. The expenses of the procurement of those chemicals should reflect the actual cost, without the Contractor’s mark-up. Also, like the M&R fund, the contractor will account for the expenses and work closely with the Town in the optimization of the odor, H2S and FOG issues that affect the collection system. The Town views this as a collaboration and wants to work closely with the Contractor.

- **Q #6:** _1- Collection System – Jet / CCTV / Emergency Response Please clarify whether collection system Jetting & CCTV are expected as part of the Base Fee. 2- Billables Beyond Base Fee Please clarify whether emergency response services, including overtime labor required outside normal routine operations, are to be included in the annual base fee or compensated separately at agreed hourly rates. Please clarify if prevailing wage tasks are to be included in the annual base fee._

<!-- ¶10 -->
- **A #6:** The Collection system cleaning (cleaning/CCTV) is to be included in the Contractor’s base fee. As part of the base fee the Town anticipates the contractor will respond to emergencies as part of your base fee, including overtime. The Town is requesting for the 24/7/365 management of the utility. That said, should the emergency repair require expertise beyond your staff’s capability, the M&R fund could be used to address the repair, subject to M&R fund approvals. In extenuating circumstances that require the contractor’s staff beyond a typical emergency condition, could with approval from the Town, be compensated for any overtime.
