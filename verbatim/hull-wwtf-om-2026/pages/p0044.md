At commencement, we will validate the asset registry across the WWTF, pump stations, flood‑control infrastructure, grinder pump system, and collection system; confirm asset hierarchy, GIS linkages, PM libraries, inspections, work history, spare‑ parts records, and reporting workflows; and implement the agreed‑upon CMMS configuration within the required startup period. We will ensure the system supports systematic evaluation of plant performance, well‑documented O&M records, Town ownership of records, and real‑time or on‑ demand Town access. Within the first 90 days, a baseline condition assessment will be completed with **prioritized, data-driven recommendations for renewal, reliability improvement, and capital planning** .

<!-- ¶4 -->
###### **_Planned Maintenance on Critical Assets_**

<!-- ¶5 -->
Focusing first on critical assets, we’ll use reliability‑centered maintenance principles to shift the Town’s wastewater facilities **from reactive work to planned PM and targeted PdM** . Regardless of platform, job plans, manufacturer requirements, inspections, and failure history will be built into the CMMS so it functions as an **operating tool for decision-making** , not just a work order log, as illustrated in **Exhibit 5-15** . Operator rounds will be used to identify issues early, update PM tasks and intervals based on asset performance, and track practical maintenance KPIs such as PM completion, backlog aging, repeat failures, and response time to **support continuous improvement** and monthly review with the Town.
