<!-- ¶1 -->
###### **REQUIRED DOCUMENTATION DELIVERY**

<!-- ¶2 -->
We have identified the required documentation and will develop, finalize, and implement these deliverables during Transition. These living documents will be maintained throughout the contract, consistent with our Transition approach described in the Transition subsection. All identified deliverables are items we commonly produce for our O&M projects and will be included in our final O&M plan, along with their delivery schedule, as follows:

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **Day-to-Day/Ongoing**

<!-- ¶4 -->
- µ<sup>24/7 O&M, monitoring, emergency response, daily</sup> performance evaluation, records/data management, remote SCADA/process access, record drawings, equipment inventory, permit/report management

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### **Monthly**

<!-- ¶6 -->
- µ<sup>Written monthly report to the Town, including, at a</sup> minimum:

<!-- ¶7 -->
- Influent/effluent, process control, and discharge information

<!-- ¶8 -->
- Violations or near‑exceedances

<!-- ¶9 -->
- Safety incidents

<!-- ¶10 -->
- Maintenance and repair work conducted

<!-- ¶11 -->
- Maintenance expenditures and M&R fund balances

<!-- ¶12 -->
- WWTF power utilization in kWh/MG

<!-- ¶13 -->
- Significant and imminent significant events

<!-- ¶14 -->
- Wastewater collection system activity

<!-- ¶15 -->
- µ<sup>Monthly reports from the process‑control/CMMS system, in</sup> sufficient detail for the Town

<!-- ¶16 -->
- µ<sup>Monthly billing/financial reporting</sup>

<!-- ¶17 -->
###### **Annual**

<!-- ¶18 -->
- µ<sup>Staffing and organizational structure</sup>

<!-- ¶19 -->
- µ<sup>Recommended capital improvements or operational</sup> changes as appropriate

<!-- ¶20 -->
- µ<sup>5‑year CIP and annual updates as appropriate</sup>

<!-- ¶21 -->
###### **One-Time/Startup Deliverables**

<!-- ¶22 -->
- µ<sup>Transition Plan (before/at Commencement)</sup>

<!-- ¶23 -->
- µ<sup>Initial asset condition assessment</sup>

<!-- ¶24 -->
- µ<sup>5‑year CIP</sup>

<!-- ¶25 -->
- µ<sup>Cybersecurity Plan</sup>

<!-- ¶26 -->
- µ<sup>Site Security Plan / Approach</sup>

<!-- ¶27 -->
- µ<sup>Site‑Specific Odor Control Plan</sup>

<!-- ¶28 -->
- µ<sup>Storm Preparation Plan</sup>

<!-- ¶29 -->
- µ<sup>Emergency response protocol</sup>

<!-- ¶30 -->
- µ<sup>O&M Plan</sup>

<!-- ¶31 -->
- µ<sup>Implementation of process‑control data management</sup> system and CMMS (within 120 days)

<!-- ¶32 -->
- µ<sup>Initial physical inventory of spare parts, materials, and</sup> supplies (within 90 days)

<!-- ¶33 -->
###### **As Needed/Event-Driven/Periodic Based on Requirements**

<!-- ¶34 -->
- µ<sup>Update O&M Manuals, as required</sup>

<!-- ¶35 -->
- µ<sup>Update SOPs, including high‑flow management plan and</sup> pump station bypassing procedures

<!-- ¶36 -->
- µ<sup>Maintain contingency plans for treatment processes and</sup> collection system operations, including storm and high‑ flow events

<!-- ¶37 -->
- µ<sup>Perform I/I tracking investigations</sup>

<!-- ¶38 -->
- µ<sup>Prepare and submit all regulatory reports in accordance</sup> with required schedules and events

<!-- ¶39 -->
- µ<sup>Develop and maintain emergency response, hazardous</sup> materials, and OSHA programs in compliance with applicable requirements

<!-- ¶40 -->
- µ<sup>Respond to odor complaints as they occur</sup>

<!-- ¶41 -->
- µ<sup>Issue public notice within 24 hours of unauthorized</sup> discharges, as required

<!-- ¶42 -->
- µ<sup>Notify downstream water systems within 4 hours of</sup> qualifying emergency conditions, with written follow‑up within 5 days

<!-- ¶43 -->
- µ<sup>Perform effluent diffuser inspection and video survey every</sup> 5 years (first within 12 months of permit authorization)

<!-- ¶44 -->
- µ<sup>Submit diffuser inspection report to EPA and MassDEP</sup> within 60 days of each inspection
