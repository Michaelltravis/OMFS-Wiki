- **Q #9:** _The Town provided two years of DMR reports submitted to MassDEP under Addendum 3 (Question 25). In the section presenting Hull Wastewater Treatment Plant operations data, the quantity of grit removed (lbs.) is reported as zero for 2025 and for portions of 2024. Could the Town confirm whether any grit was removed during this period, whether it was comingled with screenings, and whether improvements in grit system performance are anticipated in the near term?_

<!-- ¶20 -->
- **A #9:** The grit removal system had not been operational for several years, which is why zero grit removed was reported during that period. The system was repaired and returned to service in January 2026. Since being placed back in operation, the current operator has been removing approximately 25 lbs. of grit per day, resulting in the disposal of approximately one (1) cubic yard dumpster of grit per week (not filled to capacity).
