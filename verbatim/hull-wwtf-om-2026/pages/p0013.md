###### **EXPERIENCE WITH LOW-PRESSURE SEWER SYSTEMS**

<!-- ¶5 -->
Building on our experience managing complex collection systems, we bring **extensive experience evaluating, operating, and maintaining low-pressure sewer systems** , including grinder pump networks integrated with broader wastewater infrastructure. Our team understands the **unique operational, maintenance, and asset management considerations** associated with these systems, including pump reliability, pressure system performance, electrical and controls integration, and lifecycle planning.

<!-- ¶6 -->
We have supported utilities with system assessments, capital planning, development of O&M programs, SOPs, and asset management strategies tailored to low-pressure infrastructure. This experience **directly aligns with the Town’s system of approximately 175 grinder pumps and associated low-pressure piping** , including budgeting for routine pump replacement, SCADA and asset management integration, odor and grease control, and emergency response.

<!-- ¶7 -->
In Westerly, Rhode Island, we are assisting a client in addressing performance challenges within a low-pressure area of its collection system. Our team initiated a **targeted, out-of-scope evaluation to identify root causes and develop practical, long-term solutions** to improve system reliability and performance.

<!-- ¶8 -->
Proposed Project Manager Nathan Callison brings **direct experience managing dual-pressure systems** that incorporate both residential grinder pumps and septic tank effluent pump (STEP) units. Jacobs currently operates the Northport–Leelanau Township Utility Authority collection system, which includes integrated force main and gravity infrastructure, multiple lift stations, and approximately **160 residential grinder pump installations** . This hands-on experience ensures a **strong technical foundation for supporting the Town’s system** .
