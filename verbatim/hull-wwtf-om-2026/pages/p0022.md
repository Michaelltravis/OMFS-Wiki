###### **CERTIFICATIONS AND TRAINING**

<!-- ¶15 -->
###### **_Leadership Credentials_**

<!-- ¶16 -->
Project Manager Nathan Callison and Assistant Project Manager Tony Rose each hold a Massachusetts Class 6C Wastewater Operator License, **exceeding the licensing requirements for the Town’s facility.**

<!-- ¶17 -->
###### **_Staff Certifications and Training_**

<!-- ¶18 -->
Operators and collections technicians will obtain and maintain **all required Massachusetts licenses** , collections staff will hold **NASSCO PACP/MACP certifications** , and all staff will complete **OSHA 10 or greater safety training** with documented certification. Startup training includes site-specific standard operating procedures (SOPs), high-flow and bypass plans, storm preparation, odor response, and emergency response procedures to ensure **Day 1 operational readiness** .

<!-- ¶19 -->
###### **_Operator Certification Incentives_**

<!-- ¶20 -->
Jacobs provides meaningful incentives that encourage operators to pursue advanced certifications and continue developing their skills. We support this through training, preparation, and exam fee coverage; career advancement opportunities for operators-in-training (OITs) who complete the 6- to 12-month OIT period and obtain certification; and certification bonuses for achieving higher license levels.

<!-- ¶21 -->
###### **_Maintenance Certification Programs_**

<!-- ¶22 -->
Jacobs provides similar support to maintenance staff to advance their technical expertise and apply best practices that **extend asset life and improve reliability** . We support this through certification programs such as CMRT (Society of Maintenance and Reliability Professionals) and CRL (Association of Asset Management Professionals), along with incentives for achieving certification levels.

<!-- ¶23 -->
###### **STAFFING PLAN**

<!-- ¶24 -->
Our staffing plan ensures **adequate staffing of the facilities** in compliance with the NPDES permit requirements and to meet its staff certification requirements, as demonstrated in our Key Staff and Technical Support Team profiles shown above.

<!-- ¶26 -->

<!-- ¶27 -->

<!-- ¶28 -->
- µ<sup>**24/7 On-Call:**One WWTF operator and one Collections</sup> Technician on rotation, with ≤20-minute phone response and ≤60-minute site response, meeting RFP requirements.

<!-- ¶29 -->
- µ<sup>**Storm Events:**Pre-staged teams at the WWTF and critical</sup> pump stations (L Street and PS #3/#5), with provisions and sleeping arrangements maintained for extended events.

###### **PLAN TO TRANSITION MANAGEMENT AND OPERATION OF FACILITY**

<!-- ¶31 -->
###### **_Proven Transition and Mobilization Strategy_**

<!-- ¶32 -->
We recognize that maintaining **uninterrupted compliance and operational stability** during contract transition is critical. Our mobilization plan is structured around a **disciplined, phased approach** that is discussed in detail in Section 5 – Project Understanding and Technical Approach. Our transition approach is specifically designed to **eliminate compliance risk, maintain operational continuity, and provide immediate stability** for the Town, facility staff, and stakeholders.
