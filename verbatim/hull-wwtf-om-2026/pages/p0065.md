- **Q #18:** _Kindly confirm where the water supply for Jet-Vac'ing is pulled from, and if there are water charges that are the responsibility of the contractor._

<!-- ¶2 -->
- **A #18:** A fire hydrant located at the wastewater treatment facility is available for vactor truck use. This hydrant, along with other approved hydrants in town, with Weir River Water System’s (water company) coordination, will be metered. Water usage from the water meters will be paid by the Town through either the M&R fund (with Town approval) or the Sewer Department operating budget.
