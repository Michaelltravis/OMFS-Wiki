_______________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________ **Q #4:** _Pages 19–20 of the RFP state that project descriptions are excluded from the 45-page proposal limit. However, Page 21 requires inclusion of five reference projects within Section 3, Firm Qualifications and Demonstrated Experience. Please clarify how the Town would like these five required project descriptions to be presented. Should they be included within Section 3 but excluded from page numbering, included as an appendix, or does the exclusion from the page limit apply only to additional project descriptions beyond the five required in Section 3?_

<!-- ¶5 -->
**A #4:** Within the 45-page limit, list your firm’s references with the required information as detailed in section 3.3 of the RFP.  You may also add more detailed project descriptions for your firm’s 5 reference projects, as well as others, in the appendix of your proposal.
