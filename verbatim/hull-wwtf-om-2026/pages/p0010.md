##### **CORPORATE PROFILE AND LEGAL QUALIFICATIONS**

<!-- ¶4 -->
###### **CORPORATE PROFILE**

<!-- ¶5 -->
_Jacobs is a leading provider of water and wastewater operations and maintenance (O&M) services in North America and worldwide. Established in 1947 and headquartered in Dallas, Texas, Jacobs is a U.S.-based Fortune 500 company with approximately $12 billion in revenue in FY24 and a backlog of more than $2.4 billion in O&M services. We have approximately 700 staff in New England, 29,000+ across the U.S., and 42,000+ worldwide, providing the resources and financial strength to successfully deliver all aspects of the Town of Hull’s scope of services._

<!-- ¶6 -->
_We bring more than 45 years of wastewater O&M experience, including extensive experience in EPA Region 1 and New York. We operate and maintain facilities and collection systems comparable in size and complexity to the Town of Hull, including systems exceeding 3 MGD, 40 miles of collection piping, multiple pump stations, and low-pressure sewer systems._

<!-- ¶7 -->
Our commitment to client partnership delivers **strong performance, cost efficiency, and long-term relationships** . We prioritize safety, ethics, and workforce development, creating a culture that attracts and maintains top talent. Jacobs has been recognized as one of the **“50 Best Companies to Work for,”** and our investment in our people results in **one of the lowest turnover rates in the industry** .
