<!-- ¶4 -->
###### **REGULATORY COMPLIANCE**

<!-- ¶5 -->
Operations of the Town’s wastewater facilities are conducted in full compliance with all applicable federal and Massachusetts environmental requirements. Our objective is **predictable, uninterrupted compliance** that protects public health, coastal receiving waters, and long‑term Town interests. Jacobs’ **SixPoint Compliance Program** , shown in **Exhibit 5-6** (on the following page), integrates ethics, disciplined process control, laboratory practices, data integrity, auditing, and transparent reporting to deliver **defensible NPDES compliance** . This structured framework embeds compliance into daily operations rather than verifying it after the fact.

<!-- ¶6 -->
A centralized, auditable compliance management system integrates operational, laboratory, and maintenance data into a single record. As illustrated in **Exhibit 5-7** (on page 26), Town leadership receives **real-time visibility into permit parameters, reporting status, and performance trends** . Automated alerts identify approaching limits, enabling **proactive response** .

<!-- ¶7 -->
All required regulatory submission, including discharge monitoring reports (DMRs), NetDMR filings, biosolids reports, PFAS sampling documentation, combined sewer overflow (CSO) notifications, and other filings, will be prepared accurately and submitted on time in accordance with MassDEP and EPA Region 1 requirements. Records remain **secure and audit-ready at all times** .
