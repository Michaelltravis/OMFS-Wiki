<!-- ¶5 -->
###### **_Regulatory Oversight and Change Management_**

<!-- ¶6 -->
Emerging regulatory developments, including nutrient limits, biosolids requirements, PFAS standards, and cybersecurity guidance, are actively monitored, with impacts evaluated and communicated to the Town. This **forward-looking approach** supports proactive planning rather than reactive compliance.

<!-- ¶7 -->
###### **_Incident Response and Continuous Improvement_**

<!-- ¶8 -->
In the event of a permit deviation due to extreme weather, equipment failure, or abnormal influent conditions, response actions will be **immediate and transparent** . Corrective actions will be implemented promptly, required notifications will be completed, and each event will undergo documented root cause analysis. Findings will be incorporated into updated procedures to strengthen resilience and prevent recurrence.

<!-- ¶9 -->
###### **_Laboratory Management and Data Integrity_**

<!-- ¶10 -->
Reliable compliance begins with **defensible laboratory data** . A structured laboratory management program aligned with 40 CFR Part 136, the Town’s NPDES permit, and MassDEP requirements includes:

<!-- ¶11 -->
- µ<sup>Required influent and effluent sampling at</sup> specified frequencies

<!-- ¶12 -->
- µ<sup>Use of EPA‑approved analytical methods</sup>

<!-- ¶13 -->
- µ<sup>Coordination of quarterly PFAS composite sampling with</sup> certified laboratories

<!-- ¶14 -->
- µ<sup>Documented QA/QC procedures including calibration,</sup> duplicate sampling, and control charting

<!-- ¶15 -->
- µ<sup>Integration of laboratory data into process control and</sup> compliance dashboards

<!-- ¶16 -->
All analytical data will undergo internal QA/QC review prior to submission. Laboratory equipment will be maintained under preventive maintenance schedules, and staff will maintain appropriate training and certifications.
