# **Section 5**

<!-- ¶2 -->
##### **PROJECT UNDERSTANDING**

<!-- ¶3 -->
The Town’s wastewater facilities operate within a challenging coastal, flood‑prone, peninsula setting that can restrict plant access during severe nor’easters, blizzards, and hurricanes. Wet‑weather peaks, tidal influence, and odor sensitivity from close residential neighborhood proximity require disciplined operations and strong coordination. The Town’s objectives emphasize collaborative governance, regulatory excellence, asset stewardship, workforce development, transparency, community quality of life, and innovation with financial optimization. These objectives directly shape our plan.

<!-- ¶4 -->
The Town seeks a collaborative partner to improve accountability, compliance, asset stewardship, resiliency, and community engagement. **Jacobs delivers on all fronts** . We bring visible leadership, proactive maintenance, disciplined process control, and data‑driven decision‑making to support **consistent, defensible permit compliance** . Our approach also addresses odor management, resilience, and community impact with intentionality. As summarized in **Exhibit 5-1** , the Town will gain more than compliant operations and a partner committed to continuous improvement and long‑term asset stewardship.
