<!-- ¶21 -->
- **Q #17:** _If the services of a consulting engineer are required to secure and review 3 or more cost quotes for maintenance, repairs, and upgrades, will these costs be recoverable by the contractor, funded by the M&R Fund?_

<!-- ¶22 -->
- **A #17:** The Town expects the Contractor to manage the procurement of routine maintenance and repair work for the system, including soliciting quotes when necessary. The Town also currently has an on-call emergency contractor under contract to address emergency repair needs. For preventative or proactive maintenance and repair work, the Contractor would be expected to handle the procurement as part of their management responsibilities. If an extraordinary situation arises that requires significant engineering involvement beyond typical operational needs, such work would likely exceed the Contractor’s normal responsibilities and would require prior approval from the Town for potential use of M&R funds.
