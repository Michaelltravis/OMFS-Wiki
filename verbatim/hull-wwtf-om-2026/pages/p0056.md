**Q #1:** _Could the Town kindly provide copies of the following: Current contract with O&M provider - Rolling 12 months of invoices subject to above - Rolling 12 month of invoices / pass through for any sub-suppliers AND/OR third-party contractors not subject to the responsibilities of the current agreement - Rolling 12 months of bio-solids generation/tickets/invoices._

<!-- ¶18 -->
**A #1:** The current contract is not relevant to this procurement and will not be shared. Regarding invoicing, the town has already included the power invoices for the past 3 years which are included into the Document Room within BidNet, and the bio-solids generation information will be in the “2024 - Annual Sludge Characterization” made available via this addendum and in the annual W&C MOR which will be located in the document room.

<!-- ¶19 -->
_____________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________ **Q #2:** _Within 1.3 Minimum Qualifications, the RFP asks firms to have experience operating wastewater treatment facilities greater than 3-MGD as well as minimum O&M experience in EPA Region 1 and New York. Within the Evaluation Factors however, it conflates these two, asking for the firm's experience with 3-MGD+ facilities within EPA Region 1 and New York. We respectfully request that the Town separate these Evaluation Factors and evaluate experience with wastewater treatment facilities greater than 3- MGD independently from experience within EPA Region 1 and New York. This would ensure consistency with the Minimum Qualifications and allow each criterion to be assessed on its own merit._
