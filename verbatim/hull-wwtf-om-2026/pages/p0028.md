##### **OPERATIONAL APPROACH**

<!-- ¶7 -->
Jacobs will develop and implement a comprehensive O&M Plan tailored to the Town’s facilities, contract scope, and regulatory requirements. The plan establishes clear procedures for operations, maintenance, safety, compliance/laboratory, emergency response, housekeeping, reporting, and other critical O&M elements, as discussed below. The plan defines roles, routines, and escalation paths, and include an SOP library so every critical task is performed **consistently,**

<!-- ¶8 -->
**safely, and defensibly** including startup/shutdown, routine monitoring, abnormal operating conditions, and work practices. SOPs will remain **living documents** , updated at least annually or as equipment, permits, and performance priorities evolve, and reinforced through shift turnover, training, and routine supervisory review.

<!-- ¶9 -->
###### **PROCESS TOOLS AND OVERSIGHT**

<!-- ¶10 -->
Jacobs’ process control strategy serves as the focal point for daily operations, maintaining steady‑state treatment, preventing upsets, and supporting **permit compliance** with monitoring and reporting requirements. UPCPs define targets, control limits, monitoring points, and response actions by unit process, grounded‑in‑plant design, influent characteristics, performance goals, and applicable permit requirements (frequency/sample type).

<!-- ¶11 -->
Process control is executed through a **disciplined weekly rhythm** supported by a focused set of high‑value tools (see **Exhibit 5-4** ). A sampling plan and tracking controls to ensure no missed monitoring events, while integrated data management trends lab, SCADA, and instrument data to provide early warning. Operator rounds and check sheets translate observations into timely process adjustments and work orders. Weekly process‑control decisions are documented, and the process control plan is formally audited and refreshed at least annually to drive continuous improvement and incorporate innovations. Staff are equipped with electronic tablets to streamline reporting and provide immediate access to system information.
