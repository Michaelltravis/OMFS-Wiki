###### **_Training and Operational Readiness_**

<!-- ¶9 -->
Training is central to transition success and long‑term performance. After onboarding, structured skills assessments will be conducted and targeted training will be implemented to reinforce process control, safety procedures, emergency response, and regulatory compliance requirements. Cross‑training across plant and collection operations will be emphasized to **build redundancy and resilience** .

<!-- ¶10 -->
###### **_Town-Specific Operational Integration_**

<!-- ¶11 -->
Focused attention will be given to the Town’s coastal operating environment, and key site‑specific procedures will be validated early in transition, including:

<!-- ¶12 -->
- µ<sup>Activated sludge process performance under variable</sup> loading and seasonal conditions

<!-- ¶13 -->
- µ<sup>High‑flow and wet‑weather management procedures</sup> (including contingency/bypass protocols as applicable)

<!-- ¶14 -->
- µ<sup>Pump station reliability, wet well condition, and telemetry</sup> functionality

<!-- ¶15 -->
- µ<sup>Flood control system readiness and gate/pump operations</sup>

<!-- ¶16 -->
- µ<sup>Cleaning priorities and I/I investigation approach</sup>

<!-- ¶17 -->
- µ<sup>Odor monitoring, control, and complaint response</sup> procedures at the WWTF and within the collection system

<!-- ¶18 -->
Given the Town’s exposure to storm events and coastal conditions, emergency preparedness and standby power validation will receive early priority. Alarm notifications, communication protocols, and contingency response procedures will be confirmed prior to July 1, 2026.

<!-- ¶19 -->
###### **_Asset Inventory and Condition Assessment_**

<!-- ¶20 -->
At Commencement, a comprehensive asset inventory and baseline condition assessment will be conducted for process equipment, electrical systems, instrumentation, pump stations, and flood control infrastructure. Within the first 90 days, **prioritized, data-driven recommendations** will be provided for asset renewal, reliability enhancements, and capital planning aligned with risk, lifecycle principles, and the RFP’s required planning deliverables.

<!-- ¶21 -->
###### **_Communication and Knowledge Transfer_**

<!-- ¶22 -->
A formal Transition Communications Plan will be implemented immediately following award, establishing stakeholder contacts and meeting cadence, documentation standards, and readiness verification procedures. Regular coordination meetings with Town leadership will **confirm progress and resolve issues proactively** .

<!-- ¶23 -->
Knowledge transfer will be **deliberate and structured** . Our team will work alongside existing staff during familiarization to capture process operations, maintenance practices, SCADA/ telemetry procedures, reporting workflows, and document baseline conditions.

<!-- ¶24 -->
###### **_Assurance of Continuity and Compliance_**

<!-- ¶25 -->
From Day 1, we **assume full responsibility for regulatory compliance** , including monitoring, sampling, laboratory coordination, reporting, and regulatory communication. Sampling programs, reporting workflows, and alarm systems will be validated in advance to prevent any lapse in obligations. With redundant coverage and regional backup resources identified before July 1, the Town’s wastewater and collection system operations will remain **safe, compliant, and reliable** throughout transition.

<!-- ¶26 -->
We will enter the contract fully prepared to operate at a highly advantageous level from the first day of service (July 1, 2026).
