Our approach to successful operations is built around what matters most to MMSD: safe, compliant, cost-efficient, and reliable service—every day and in every condition. We’ll go beyond routine operation to continuously optimize performance, regulatory outcomes, and chemical and energy use. Using real-time dashboards and Intelligent O&M tools, we’ll give operators and MMSD clear visibility into system conditions and performance drivers, with shared buy-in on how we act on the data. Just as important, we’ll maintain clear communication and operating protocols between the water reclamation facilities (WRFs) and the collections team to manage wet-weather events smoothly and effectively— while protecting MMSD’s reputation through environmental stewardship, responsive communication, odor control, and sustainable operations.

Our strategies directly support MMSD’s priorities outlined in **Exhibit IV-10** and integrate process control, proactive maintenance, asset management, QA/QC, systematic sampling, real-time capacity management, odor control, and targeted energy and chemical optimization. We’ll prioritize the system areas needing improvement, identified earlier in **Exhibit IV-4** page IV-4), and our extensive offsite support resources will routinely audit the project and provide the onsite team with lessons learned, including those from our broader O&M work.
