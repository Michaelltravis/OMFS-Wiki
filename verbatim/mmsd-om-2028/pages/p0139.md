We know that this plan works. It’s rooted in our team’s history of building relationships and leading effective outreach activities in the region, in particular, those with a focus on water quality improvements. We’ve delivered critical work to make a positive environmental, societal, and economic difference in the Milwaukee area — from Milwaukee’s first Riverwalk design guidelines to the Waukesha Water Transition Project, the ongoing remediation and restoration of the Milwaukee River, and Estuary Area of Concern, and improvements at MMSD’s Jones Island Water Reclamation Facility. These projects (captured in **Exhibit V-2** ) reflect our commitment to building a better future for the City and the surrounding region.
