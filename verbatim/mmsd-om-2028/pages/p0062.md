**JIWRF Aeration - Unlocking Energy and Process Gains.** JIWRF has a strong track record of meeting or exceeding permit requirements with the existing configuration. However, the amount of air used for bioreactor aeration and channel mixing **provides an opportunity to further reduce energy-related operating costs** . We’ll work with MMSD to continue improving these systems. Key dynamics impacting aeration blower efficiency at JIWRF include:

<!-- ¶3 -->
- Big bubble channel mixing will bring new anaerobic/anoxic volume to the biological process. This may improve biological phosphorus removal but could also impact the loading on the aeration basins.

<!-- ¶4 -->
- Dissolved oxygen (DO) variability observed across the aeration basins in service (See **Exhibit IV-18** ) indicate that individualized basin setpoints, **improved load balance, and refined airflow distribution to the aeration basins would yield substantial energy and process benefits.**

- **Expanded Blower VFDs Capabilities:** Only one blower at JIWRF is currently equipped with a VFD and is primarily used as the fast-start standby unit.
