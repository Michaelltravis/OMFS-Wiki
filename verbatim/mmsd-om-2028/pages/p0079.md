<!-- ¶11 -->
**2.4.1. Jacobs OT Asset Management Software Platform**

<!-- ¶12 -->
MMSD increasingly relies on advanced automation and control systems to operate its treatment and conveyance network. These OT assets—analytical instruments, PLCs, networked motor controls, HMIs, and field communications—are mission-critical, yet traditional CMMS platforms aren’t designed to manage their complexity, interdependencies, and rapid configuration change.

<!-- ¶13 -->
Jacobs will close this gap with a comprehensive OT asset management platform tailored to MMSD’s needs. Our system captures the full OT environment, from major components down to connectors, cabling, firmware, and configuration files, so teams can see asset condition, configuration health, and life-cycle risk in one place.

<!-- ¶14 -->
This end-to-end visibility strengthens resiliency by enabling smarter decisions, proactive maintenance, and a clearer understanding of how each OT component supports critical treatment and conveyance processes, as summarized in **Exhibit IV-31** .
