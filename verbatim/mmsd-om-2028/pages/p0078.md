###### EXHIBIT IV-30. DIGITAL TOOLS FOR MORE SUSTAINABLE AND COST EFFICIENT OPERATIONS

<!-- ¶10 -->
|**Digital Tool**|**How is it Implemented**|**Example Benefits for MMSD**|
|---|---|---|
|**Intelligent O&M**<br>Advance AI and Data Analytics<br>Digital Tool|▪ Turns MMSD’s historical SCADA and laboratory data into predictive,<br>operator-ready recommendations that reduce energy and chemical use<br>while maintaining compliance and process stability.|▪ Supports more efficient day-to-day operations and helps achieve sustainability and<br>cost-reduction goals. At facilities managed by Jacobs, Intelligent O&M has contributed<br>to a 10–30% reduction in power and chemical consumption.|
|**AquaDNA**<br>Smart Technology & Sensors<br>for Collection Systems &<br>Pump Stations for Real Time<br>System Performance|▪ Integrates real-time data from MMSD sensors, gauges, and weather inputs<br>into a machine-learning platform.<br> ▪ Detects and alerts CSO crew of blockages, I/I anomalies, and CSO risk<br>conditions and visualizes MIS, ISS, and pump station performance for<br>operator response.|▪ Strengthens ISS readiness and extends the useful capacity of the Deep Tunnel.<br> ▪ Prevents dry- and wet-weather overflows through early detection of pump or pipe<br>anomalies. Improves pump station reliability and reduces emergency callouts.<br> ▪ Reduces manual field inspections and increases staff safety.|
|**AquaDNA Deragger**<br>Automated Clearing of Pump<br>Ragging|▪ Installs smart algorithms that detect rag buildup and automatically clears<br>pump impellers.<br> ▪ Provides performance monitoring and alerts through SCADA.|▪ Reduces pump station failures and ISS bypass risks.<br> ▪ Lowers maintenance labor and emergency callouts.<br> ▪ Improves reliability at historically high-ragging locations across MMSD’s network.|
|**Digital Twin**<br>Whole Plant Process and<br>Hydraulic Model Connected<br>to/from Actual SCADA<br>Data System|▪ Real-time model of MMSD’s entire system (ISS, conveyance, WRFs).<br>Integrating SCADA, weather forecasts, flows data, and process models to<br>quickly troubleshoot hydraulics and process issues/changes.<br> ▪ Simulates storage strategies, flow distributions, solids routing, and wet-<br>weather responses.|▪ Provides operators with a deeper understanding of the system and opportunities to test<br>operating scenarios and controls.<br> ▪ Improves tunnel dewatering strategies to minimize CSOs and basement backups and<br>enhances readiness for large storms and operational shocks.|
|**Hybrid Optimizer**<br>Chemical & Energy<br>Optimization Engine|▪ Use the calibrated models together to optimize chemicals/energy and<br>operations with real-time operating data.<br> ▪ Continuously recommends aeration, chemical, and solids strategies.<br> ▪ Provides operator dashboards to validate all recommended adjustments.|▪ Advises operators on flow setpoints for WAS, digester feed, centrifuge feed etc.<br> ▪ Lowers aeration energy at both WRFs, reducing shared-cost power bills.<br> ▪ Decreases disinfectant, bisulfite, ferric, and RAS chlorination chemical usage.<br> ▪ Improves forecast accuracy for high-load or wet-weather periods.|
|**WATS Model**<br>Sewer Process, Odor &<br>Corrosion Modeling|▪ Sewer-process model simulates biological, chemical, and physical<br>transformations in MMSD’s conveyance system.<br> ▪ Integrates MMSD odor complaints, H₂S data, corrosion records, and<br>hydraulics to identify high-risk segments and evaluates impacts of flow<br>routing, pumping changes, and industrial contributions.|▪ Reduces odor complaints and corrosion at known MMSD hotspots.<br> ▪ Prioritizes targeted, cost-effective odor and corrosion mitigation investments.<br> ▪ Provides early insight into upstream impacts on JIWRF and SSWRF performance.<br> ▪ Supports seasonal optimization of phosphorus removal for both WRFs.|
|**Dragonfly and Argon**<br>CCTV Defect Coding and Asset<br>Rehab Planning Tools|▪ Dragonfly improves quality and cost-effectiveness of pipeline defect<br>coding, helping optimize repair and replacement effort and with more<br>consistent, objective data.<br> ▪ Argon supports more effective conveyance maintenance and can be used<br>as a CIP support tool for linear assets.|▪ Advanced data analysis and reporting provides insights into rehabilitation, repair,<br>reinspection, defects, next steps, and cost estimates.<br> ▪ Approximately 30-40% cost savings compared to manual detect coding.|
