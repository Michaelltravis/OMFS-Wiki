We’ll work hard to foster day-to-day communication between MMSD and Jacobs staff to empower decision making on all levels and ensure immediate attention and response. Project Manager Kevin Dahl and the entire team will promote the flow of information and transparency. In addition to frequent informal daily communications, our PgM approach will include formal weekly, monthly, and quarterly meetings to review plant performance and maintenance activities in addition to the formal Governance Committees' meetings. Kevin will also ensure effective and transparent communication with the Commission and at public meetings as requested and directed by MMSD. Our Executive Sponsors will support Kevin with these important communications as necessary. We provide information on how we’ll communicate with the community/ratepayers in **_Section 2.2.6.
