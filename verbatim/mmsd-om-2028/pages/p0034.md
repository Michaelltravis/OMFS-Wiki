###### 2. DEDICATED WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT TEAM AND REPORTING

<!-- ¶4 -->
Rick Warner, Director of Regional Partnerships, will direct workforce development planning. Toyin Ogunfolaju will report to Rick and manage workforce development program planning and implementation. Our workforce development team will collaborate with our HR and operations leadership team and MMSD to align career development initiatives with staffing needs, and to assure that internship and other entry-level trainees are onboarded in accordance with our hiring guidelines.

<!-- ¶5 -->
- PRISM will provide workforce development coordination and support our team in program planning, partnerships, tracking, and reporting.

<!-- ¶6 -->
- P3 will support our career awareness outreach efforts and capacity building activities.

<!-- ¶7 -->
We’ll provide MMSD with transparent reporting regarding workforce development that defines and tracks measurable outcomes. We’ll submit a quarterly report/review to the governance committees and an annual report to MMSD to maximize transparency, compliance, and accountability. The Workforce Development Coordinator will submit updates to the annual Workforce Development Plan by January 31 of each contract year and an annual report by March 31 of each contract year that details workforce development activities from the previous contract. **Exhibit II-21** provides examples of our outreach and mentoring strategies to help small businesses develop.

<!-- ¶8 -->
**Jacobs supports the growth of emerging small businesses through creative outreach and mentoring. Small businesses represent on average 30-35% of our total supply chain spend.**

Construction Academy Helps Increase SBE Participation
in Omaha
We established the Jacobs Construction Academy to provide
technical assistance to small businesses in Omaha as part of our
program management contract for Omaha Public Schools and
our Omaha Public Schools Economic Inclusion Plan. Our team led
3-hour classes, twice a week on a variety of construction topics.
As a result, local firms received nearly $43M in contracts and
subcontracts.
