###### 3. PM STAFFING AND SCHEDULE DISCIPLINE

<!-- ¶7 -->
We’ll assign PM staff based on the asset-criticality scale we develop together, including the skill sets required for each task. Supervisors will be accountable for weekly completion and documentation standards in the CMMS. Our planners will manage a rolling 4- to 6-week planning cycle that locks in craft hours, access windows, and outage coordination—ensuring high-priority PMs are completed on time without disrupting critical corrective work.

<!-- ¶8 -->
To keep crews ready and productive, procurement and warehouse staff will participate in weekly planning, pre-kit jobs, and manage inventory aligned with the schedule. Pairing planning with kitting ensures maintenance teams are never stalled waiting for parts or materials, an essential factor in consistently meeting PM schedules.

<!-- ¶9 -->
###### 4. CM STAFFING—RAPID RESPONSE WITHOUT DIMINISHING PM

<!-- ¶10 -->
We’ll classify and manage CM under the contract’s Level A/B model. Closeout will capture failure mode, root cause, corrective action, and steps to avoid recurrence. Supervisors will triage issues and assign work promptly, while the planner/ scheduler will preserve the PM plan by slotting CM into reserved capacity and using the kits/parts pipeline to minimize downtime.

<!-- ¶11 -->
We’ll prepare for wet weather/emergency coverage and minimize impacts on the overall PM schedule. This includes storm rosters, on-call rotations, and clear activation triggers coordinated with Operations. Details on wet weather/ emergencies will be documented in our Emergency Response Plan, provided in **_Section IV.A.4 Safety & Security_** .

###### 5. PDM INTEGRATION WILL MAXIMIZE PM VALUE

We’ll provide experts to work with the onsite maintenance teams to make sure MMSD’s desired Level 3 PdM additive elements are optimized to deliver the maximum PM benefits of these tools. ###### D. Use of Subcontractors

<!-- ¶15 -->
We'll balance self-performance requirements with the goal of finding meaningful opportunities for Milwaukee's local and small businesses to support this contract. Our subcontractor plan aims to build lasting capacity within the community through participation in MMSD’s business capacity development initiatives, joint networking events, and mentor–protégé programs. Through close coordination with MMSD’s procurement team and leveraging our subconsultants PRISM and P3 Development Group, we’ll align strategies, share best practices, and expand opportunities for local suppliers and contractors. **Exhibit II-10** lists local strategic partners who will enhance Jacobs’ services by contributing their expertise and insights into MMSD’s facilities and service area.
