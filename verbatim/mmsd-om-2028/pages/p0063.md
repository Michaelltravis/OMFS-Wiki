**2.2.3. Optimizing JIWRF and SSWRF Solids Processing and Milorganite Production - Focus Area** MMSD has operated its solids processing and Milorganite **SOLIDS PROCESSING** production systems very effectively for almost a century, **& MILORGANITE** consistently meeting production and product-quality objectives. **PRODUCTION** Even with this strong performance, the system remains uniquely complex: sludge streams from JIWRF and SSWRF are highly strategic risk management. interdependent, and Milorganite quality and quantity depend directly on the relative sources and proportions of these solids. Balancing solids transfers through the IPS adds additional operational challenges, as these decisions influence treatment performance and energy demands across both plants. At the same time, the integrated energy system—biogas, heat recovered from engine generators, renewable landfill gas, and supplemental natural gas—presents both constraints and opportunities within the overall production process. Building on MMSD’s already strong operations, our approach offers targeted ideas to further optimize solids handling, energy use, and Milorganite production in a coordinated, systemwide manner.

<!-- ¶5 -->
Our approach focuses on identifying process and energy optimization while maintaining clean, well-managed facilities that support sustainable solids processing and ensure reliable Milorganite production that consistently meets product specifications and Class A compliance through strategic risk management.
