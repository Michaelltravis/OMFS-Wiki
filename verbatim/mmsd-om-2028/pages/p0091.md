###### **3.2. Jacobs’ Maintenance Philosophy in Action– From Asset Management to Daily Execution**

<!-- ¶5 -->
We’ll work closely with MMSD to develop a maintenance program built on a practical **Asset Management System (AMS) aligned to ISO 55001.** The Maintenance Committee will provide ongoing governance and oversight with input from the CMMS and SCADA subcommittees. The AMS will be documented in a **Strategic Asset Management Plan (SAMP)** that defines policy, roles, decision rights, and resources and timeframes for implementation with the line of site MMSD desires, as illustrated in **Exhibit IV-42** .

###### **3.2.1. Our ISO-aligned, 10-Box AMS is tailored to MMSD**

<!-- ¶21 -->
We’ll tailor **Jacobs’ proven 10-Box AMS framework (Exhibit IV-43)** —based on the IAM 10-Box Excellence Model and consistent with Global Forum on Maintenance and Asset Management guidance—to MMSD’s scope and priorities. Each element is mapped to concrete deliverables, owners, and committee touchpoints so the program is auditable and durable. The collaboration with MMSD and the governance committees will drive outcomes from the AMS.

<!-- ¶22 -->
###### <mark>1. Leadership & Governance</mark>

<!-- ¶23 -->
**Establish an Asset Management Policy and Maintenance Governance Charter** that define objectives, decision rights, and change control. The Maintenance Committee (with CMMS and SCADA subcommittees) will be the forum for approving PM list changes, data standards, integrations, and exceptions. Outcomes include clear accountability, faster decisions, and one source of truth.

<!-- ¶24 -->
###### <mark>2. Strategy & Planning</mark>

<!-- ¶25 -->
**Publish the SAMP, which will include measurable objectives** for reliability, risk, safety, and cost. **Complete a baseline condition and criticality assessment** across WRF and conveyance assets; produce a risk-ranked register that drives the Long-Term Maintenance Plan and informs capital improvement planning (see No. 7 ). Tie staffing plans and materials strategy to asset risk and workload.

<!-- ¶26 -->
###### <mark>3. Asset Management Decision-Making</mark>

<!-- ¶27 -->
**Use risk, criticality, and lifecycle cost to pick the right action at the right time** —PM, PdM, CM, refurbishment, or replacement. Decision standards will be embedded into NEXGEN (job plans, priority rules, approval thresholds) so choices are consistent and traceable. Where trade-offs exist (cost vs. risk vs. performance), we’ll document the rationale and the trigger to revisit.
