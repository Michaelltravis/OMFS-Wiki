Backed by decades of wastewater O&M experience, our approach will deliver practical operating strategies grounded in data and reinforced by disciplined monitoring and control across all facilities. During the pre-term period and throughout the operational term, we’ll combine our team’s expertise with the knowledge of the local staff and MMSD personnel to review, refine, and strengthen these strategies. **Exhibit IV-15** summarizes our core practices and provides representative control strategies for MMSD’s key operational focus areas. Our operators will also use Jacobs’ digital tools to make day-to-day decisions easier in a system that is interconnected and constantly changing.
