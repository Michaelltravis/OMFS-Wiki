<!-- ¶3 -->
###### IV. APPROACH

<!-- ¶4 -->
▪ **Infrared/thermal Imaging (infrared thermography)** identifies thermal patterns associated with electrical resistance, loose connections, overloaded circuits, and mechanical stress. It is particularly valuable for motor control centers, switchgear, and rotating equipment. By regularly trending thermal profiles, MMSD can detect risk conditions long before they lead to electrical faults or mechanical degradation. Thermal imaging complements both vibration and MCSA data by providing a non-contact indicator of emerging failure modes.

<!-- ¶5 -->
▪ **Enhanced oil and tribology analysis** provides insight into internal wear mechanisms, lubricant degradation, contamination, and overall equipment health. MMSD already performs oil sampling, but under the Level 3 PdM program, Jacobs will integrate these samples into a broader asset condition framework, correlating oil characteristics with vibration, thermal, and MCSA insights. This strengthens diagnostics, improves trend reliability, and enhances predictive accuracy for gearboxes, motors, blowers, and turbine systems.

<!-- ¶6 -->
Based on our analysis of the WRFs’ assets, we’ve identified and prioritized the types of assets where continuous monitoring is most beneficial based on asset criticality, as shown in **Exhibit IV-61** . We’ll work with MMSD to confirm the final selection of assets for Level 3 PdM deployment.
