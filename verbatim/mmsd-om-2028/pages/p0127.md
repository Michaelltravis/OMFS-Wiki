###### C. Computerized Maintenance Management System (CMMS) Approach

<!-- ¶6 -->
###### 1. JACOBS’ DIFFERENTIATING STRENGTH IN CMMS IMPLEMENTATION

<!-- ¶7 -->
Jacobs offers MMSD a capability unmatched by any other proposer. As a fully integrated firm, we offer O&M, digital and data solutions, engineering, and asset management expertise. As discussed in **_Section A.iii Maintenance Approach_** , we align our approach to industry standards such as the Society of Maintenance and Reliability Professionals, IAM, ISO 55000, and PAS55 and consider each client’s unique needs and requirements. This unique combination of skills allows MMSD to benefit from a partner that not only operates and maintains major wastewater systems but also designs, configures, and optimizes CMMS platforms for utilities worldwide and works with software companies and implementors to ensure system configurations meet organizational goals.

<!-- ¶8 -->
Jacobs will collaborate with MMSD and NEXGEN’s implementation team to:

<!-- ¶9 -->
- Configure a unified system covering both WRFs and conveyance assets.

<!-- ¶10 -->
- Align PM and CM workflows with MMSD’s Maintenance Resource Guide and governance requirements.

<!-- ¶11 -->
- Integrate CMMS data with MMSD and Jacobs systems for complete operational visibility and PdM systems to provide escalations to intercept issues.

<!-- ¶12 -->
- Develop dashboards and analytics that provide real-time insight into asset performance, staffing, and costs.

<!-- ¶13 -->
###### JACOBS BRINGS PROVEN NEXGEN IMPLEMENTATION EXPERIENCE

<!-- ¶14 -->
Jacobs’ consulting and technology teams have implemented or managed dozens of large-scale CMMS technology solutions—many using MMSD’s chosen software, NEXGEN CMMS. We are experienced with NEXGEN’s implementation and training process. We understand how its technology solution integrates with SCADA, GIS, and laboratory systems, how its data architecture is configured, and how to leverage its full capabilities to drive measurable results. Our experience with MMSD’s prior SCADA modernization and conveyance risk assessments give us firsthand knowledge of MMSD’s digital ecosystem, positioning us to help MMSD realize immediate and sustained value from NEXGEN.

<!-- ¶15 -->
The NEXGEN CMMS is required to be online by March 1, 2028. Jacobs has a proven track record of successful implementations with NEXGEN and can work together effectively and efficiently to meet that deadline.

<!-- ¶16 -->
###### 2. CURRENT CONTEXT AND PARTNERSHIP FRAMEWORK

<!-- ¶17 -->
MMSD’s new NEXGEN CMMS will become the foundation for your asset management, operations, and maintenance transparency goals. It’s the digital backbone of your asset management activities, with primary functionality that will support the line-of-sight framework that drives effective maintenance delivery. Data entered into NEXGEN is used to inform asset reliability, O&M optimization, rapid response to corrective issues, optimized inventory management, planning and scheduling, regulatory performance, asset risk management, and lifecycle forecasting. Consequently, it’s critical to understand desired data reporting output needs, during system configuration. Jacobs can provide input on that process.
