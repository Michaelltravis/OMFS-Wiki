###### **4.1. Safety**

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### **4.1.1. Safety First: A Culture of Accountability and Prevention**

<!-- ¶6 -->
Jacobs will bring MMSD a safety program built on personal accountability, strong leadership, and a commitment to preventing injuries and incidents. Our BeyondZero® philosophy guides day-to-day decisions, reinforces individual responsibility, and promotes a work environment where every employee is empowered to identify hazards and act before they cause harm. Based on our site familiarity, MMSD’s facilities are generally well maintained and clean, providing a strong foundation on which to further reinforce consistent safety practices. At MMSD’s facilities, this approach will translate into safe and reliable operations for staff supporting wastewater facilities, maintenance activities, laboratory functions, solids handling, and capital project interfaces. Our objective is clear: **zero injuries, zero incidents, and zero harm to people, property, or the environment** .

<!-- ¶11 -->
###### **4.1.2. SMS and Required Plans**

<!-- ¶12 -->
Jacobs’ SMS complies with OSHA 29 CFR 1910 and will be aligned with MMSD procedures. Thirty days prior to the Start Date, we’ll submit a complete Safety Plan that includes:

<!-- ¶13 -->
- Confined space entry requirements, including permitting, continuous monitoring, communication, and rescue readiness

<!-- ¶14 -->
- Gas detection practices and calibration, as well as documentation of fixed and portable monitoring equipment

<!-- ¶15 -->
- Lockout and tagout procedures and verification of isolation

<!-- ¶16 -->
- Arc-flash protection, safe electrical work practices, and labeling compliance

<!-- ¶17 -->
- Chemical hygiene requirements and laboratory safety protocols consistent with MMSD standards

<!-- ¶18 -->
- Housekeeping and combustible dust control in Milorganite drying areas

<!-- ¶19 -->
- Daily pre-task planning, Activity Hazard Analyses, and safe work permitting

<!-- ¶20 -->
- Expectations for subcontractor safety, coordination, and oversight

<!-- ¶21 -->
Jacobs will work in close coordination with MMSD’s Risk Manager and Safety Advisor to ensure consistent implementation, documentation, inspection practices, and reporting.
