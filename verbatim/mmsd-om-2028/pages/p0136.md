This program is designed to **build on MMSD’s existing systems** . Any required sensors are included as part of the PdM technologies themselves, and Jacobs assumes the MMSD's PLCs and control systems can support the necessary routing of PdM data to SCADA, the historian, and NEXGEN. Jacobs’ SCADA/OT specialists will configure tag structures, historian routing, and alarm strategies in accordance with Exhibit Q cybersecurity protocols.

<!-- ¶3 -->
Working with MMSD, Jacobs will establish automated rulesets that interpret patterns in vibration signatures, electrical anomalies, hydraulic performance changes, temperature trends, and operational behavior. These rulesets will generate alerts, create automated NEXGEN work orders, update asset condition ratings, and drive PM optimization. We’ll routinely review PdM insights with MMSD through the Operations Committee, Maintenance Committee, and CMMS Governance Committee to ensure transparency, alignment, and continuous improvement.
