###### IV. APPROACH

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **2.7.6. Wet Weather Coordination and System Modeling**

<!-- ¶4 -->
Jacobs will use real-time hydraulic and treatment-performance data to coordinate wet-weather operations across the ISS, conveyance system, and both WRFs. Monitoring tunnel capacity, pump back timing, and plant loading will allow operators to manage flows effectively, reduce the risk of combined sewer overflows, and maintain high treatment efficiency during storm events.

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### **2.7.7. Cross-Facility Process Optimization**

<!-- ¶6 -->
Jacobs will align operating strategies between JIWRF and SSWRF to improve process stability, chemical efficiency, and energy use. This includes coordinating treatment strategies, sharing process knowledge, and applying predictive tools that identify developing process issues before they affect effluent quality.

<!-- ¶7 -->
###### **2.7.8. Knowledge Sharing and Operational Consistency**

<!-- ¶8 -->
Operations and maintenance practices will be documented within a shared knowledge framework that captures SOP updates, lessons learned, and systemwide best practices. This promotes consistent operations between facilities and supports staff development and cross-training.

###### **2.7.9. Unified Compliance Oversight and Continuous Improvement**

<!-- ¶13 -->
Within the first 90 days, Jacobs will complete a baseline environmental compliance audit covering permit conditions, monitoring requirements, reporting processes, and site-specific environmental risks. Recurring self-assessments and governance reviews with MMSD will ensure timely corrective actions and continuous improvement. Corrective actions and audit results will be tracked to closure through Jacobs’ enterprise quality assurance system.

<!-- ¶14 -->
**We’ll manage air-permit requirements, chemical handling standards, solids management obligations, and odor-control systems according to MMSD procedures. The team will use predictive analytics to identify potential upsets early and will conduct semi-annual compliance reviews with MMSD to confirm progress and verify compliance with all regulatory commitments.**

<!-- ¶15 -->
###### **2.7.10. Ongoing Stewardship and Reliability**

<!-- ¶16 -->
Jacobs’ Environmental Compliance and Stewardship Program provides MMSD with a coordinated, transparent, and data-driven approach to maintaining high regulatory performance. By combining integrated system management, rigorous QA/QC processes, and continuous improvement practices, Jacobs will safeguard MMSD’s compliance record and support MMSD’s leadership in environmental protection.
