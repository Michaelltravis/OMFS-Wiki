**Confidentiality Designations and Legal Basis under Wisconsin Open Records Laws -** Consistent with RFP Section 3.4, we’ve labeled only specific pages of this proposal as confidential where we believe the information involves proprietary “trade secrets” exempt from public disclosure under Wisconsin public records law§ 19.36(5); § 134.90(1)(c). Identified pages include one or more of the following types of proprietary trade secrets:

<!-- ¶8 -->
- Proprietary value/innovation packages derived from Jacobs-funded investments

<!-- ¶9 -->
- Client-identifying, client-confidential, or non-public client-specific information

<!-- ¶10 -->
- Proprietary technologies, tools, methods, processes, and “how we do it” internal frameworks

<!-- ¶11 -->
- Proprietary reporting tools and processes including custom computer programs, designs, and interfaces

<!-- ¶12 -->
- Internal pricing, cost approaches, and pricing assumptions

<!-- ¶13 -->
- Proprietary transition mobilization sequencing, risk controls, and internal tools and processes

<!-- ¶14 -->
Jacobs derives economic value from these proprietary trade secrets not being generally known, and Jacobs takes reasonable measures to maintain the confidentiality of the information on the identify pages. Public disclosure would likely (1) allow competitors to replicate, reverse-engineer, or underbid Jacobs, causing competitive harm, or (2) reveal details about our other clients that may be regarded as confidential or non-public information.
