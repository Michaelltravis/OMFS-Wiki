###### **1.2.1. Care and Sensitivity Toward Transitioning Employees**

<!-- ¶9 -->
Jacobs understands the sensitivity that comes with organizational transition and the uncertainty it can create for employees and their families. Our approach emphasizes **care, transparency, and respect** for every team member. To support this, we’ll host informational workshops designed for MMSD employees and their families to introduce Jacobs, explain how we’re organized, outline what to expect during the transition, and review our benefits and the onboarding process.

###### **1.2.2. Compensation and Benefits**

<!-- ¶14 -->
Jacobs will offer employment to all qualified and interested Veolia employees who wish to continue their careers under our management. We recognize that competitive wages and benefits are critical to retaining and attracting quality staff. As part of our commitment to MMSD’s workforce, Jacobs has performed a **regional salary analysis** and will implement a compensation structure tailored to the Milwaukee market and wastewater industry. This ensures fair, competitive pay and benefits that improve recruiting success and long-term employee retention. Our approach is designed to support continuity and stability for transitioning employees.
