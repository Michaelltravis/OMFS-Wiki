<!-- ¶10 -->
###### SUSTAINABILITY AND RESILIENCE

<!-- ¶11 -->
_We support MMSD’s position as an industry model for climate resilience and sustainable water management and will promote, accelerate, and embed practices that create long-term environmental, social, and economic stability, while preparing systems and communities to withstand future shocks and stresses such as climate change, flooding, and resource scarcity._

<!-- ¶12 -->
- Combine MMSD’s leadership with Jacobs’ expertise to elevate land protection as a cornerstone of regional climate resilience and sustainability.

<!-- ¶13 -->
- **Nature Positive Solutions:** Engage Jacobs’ SMEs to advance MMSD’s shift toward regenerative, nature-positive solutions that support climate resilience and adaptation, sustainability, and progress toward the 2035 Vision and the 2025-2027 Strategic Plan.

<!-- ¶14 -->
- Combine our nature-focused expertise and established regional partnerships to enable MMSD to acquire and protect river corridor lands.

<!-- ¶15 -->
- Use Great Lakes and Milwaukee Watershed collaborations to identify priority conservation areas and integrate nature-based solutions into land acquisition strategies.

<!-- ¶16 -->
- � Focus with MMSD's input on where we can deliver ecological and community benefits by embedding biodiversity restoration, flood risk reduction, and water quality improvements.

<!-- ¶17 -->
- **Leverage Partnerships** with the Great Lakes Watershed and Milwaukee Watershed initiatives to strengthen regional sustainability and resilience leadership.

<!-- ¶18 -->
- **Expand Integrated Watershed Planning:** Improve water quality and enhance flood risk management on a regional scale.

<!-- ¶19 -->
- Work with our subconsultant partners P3 and PRISM to create green job opportunities resulting from nature-positive planning efforts and enhance public engagement by involving community-facing organizations.

<!-- ¶20 -->
###### **Support Business Case Development**

<!-- ¶21 -->
- Quantify social and environmental returns on investment for GI (e.g. Greenseams), renewable energy, and resilience projects, using Jacobs’ tools, such as Simetrica-Jacobs.

<!-- ¶22 -->
- Practice transparent reporting aligned with global sustainability standards.

<!-- ¶23 -->
- Embed social value in decision making to attract funding and enhance partnerships.
