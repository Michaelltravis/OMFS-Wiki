###### **1.3.1. Kelly Irving – Transition Manager**

<!-- ¶4 -->
Kelly has more than 30 years of experience in water and wastewater operations, management, and executive leadership. She has led multiple transitions across both treatment and conveyance systems. Most recently, Kelly managed the transition of the City of Jackson, MS’s wastewater operations (a Veolia transition), where 31 of 34 employees joined Jacobs and remaining vacancies were quickly filled, all while maintaining uninterrupted service and compliance. Her deep experience with startup planning, workforce integration, and organizational change will support a smooth, people-centered transition for MMSD.

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### **1.3.2. Mike Boven – Transition Deputy Manager**

<!-- ¶6 -->
Mike is one of Jacobs’ most experienced transition specialists, recognized for his hands-on approach to field implementation, technical coordination, and logistics. His background spans operations, maintenance, asset management, safety, SCADA, sludge management, odor control, and workforce development. He recently led the West Basin Municipal Water District transition (a Veolia transition), delivering a seamless badge flip for 49 employees while maintaining full operational and safety compliance. Mike’s practical field-focused leadership will help ensure MMSD’s transition is orderly, safe, and aligned with both field operations and project objectives.
