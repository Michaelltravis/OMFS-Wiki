###### **4.2.6. Innovative Security Enhancements**

<!-- ¶4 -->
Jacobs understands that MMSD is implementing important upgrades, including new video cameras, the **Genetec Security Center** platform, and a visitor-enrollment and badging system. These investments establish a strong foundation for an effective, modernized security program. Based on our experience with similar utilities, Jacobs can offer several value-added recommendations that could further strengthen MMSD’s protection and response capabilities.

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### _Shoreline Intrusion Detection_

<!-- ¶6 -->
Jacobs will evaluate opportunities to enhance perimeter protection at JIWRF, including **potential shoreline intrusion-detection solutions** identified through a site-specific vulnerability assessment. Technologies that may be considered include ground-based radar or microwave detection units integrated with existing camera systems. When configured, these systems can automatically cue corresponding camera views in the security office for rapid assessment.

<!-- ¶7 -->
Such technologies are effective in darkness, fog, snow, and other adverse conditions, and may be supplemented with optional acoustic loudspeakers to issue automated voice warnings and deter trespassers. Jacobs has implemented similar shoreline-protection solutions at waterfront utilities to improve early detection and response to vessels approaching restricted areas and would work collaboratively with MMSD to determine the appropriate application based on site conditions, risk prioritization, and operational preferences.

<!-- ¶8 -->
###### _Mobile Video Capability_

<!-- ¶9 -->
Jacobs recommends enabling Genetec Mobile for MMSD security personnel. This feature allows staff to view live camera feeds and capture video directly from iOS or Android devices, with recordings automatically uploaded to the PMR. Mobile access improves situational awareness for roving officers and strengthens real-time incident documentation.

###### **4.2.7. Jacobs Business Networks & Infrastructure Cybersecurity Preparedness**

<!-- ¶11 -->
Jacobs applies a unified cybersecurity approach that integrates automation controls, SCADA, and OT cybersecurity under a single leadership structure. This alignment eliminates silos, strengthens visibility across IT and OT environments, and ensures consistent application of security controls. While protection of MMSD-owned systems is addressed in the Operational Technology Approach section, this section focuses on Jacobs’ enterpriselevel business cybersecurity capabilities that protect corporate networks, data, and collaboration platforms supporting MMSD operations.

<!-- ¶12 -->
Recognizing the evolving cyber threat landscape facing water and wastewater utilities, Jacobs’ integrated governance framework enhances resilience, accelerates incident response, and provides MMSD confidence that cybersecurity risks are managed consistently across all layers of infrastructure.

<!-- ¶13 -->
###### _Cybersecurity Framework, Governance, and Incident Response_

<!-- ¶14 -->
Jacobs’ cybersecurity program is aligned with ISO 27001 and NIST/CISA standards, providing a globally recognized framework for managing information security risk through documented policies, regular audits, and continuous improvement. Jacobs maintains a comprehensive Cyber Incident Response Plan that defines roles, communication protocols, and restoration procedures, supported by a 24/7 global Security Operations Center for monitoring and rapid containment.

###### _Automated Security Workflows_

<!-- ¶21 -->
Jacobs also recommends configuring Genetec Mission Control to establish predefined security-response workflows. During an incident, the system can guide personnel through required actions such as dispatching guards, notifying law enforcement, alerting nearby staff, locking doors, and activating relevant cameras. Automated workflows support a consistent, timely, and fully auditable response.

###### _Vulnerability Management and Network Protection_

<!-- ¶24 -->
Jacobs employs automated patch management and vulnerability scanning to maintain systems within two major vendor releases, with all patches validated for authenticity. Layered defenses include next-generation firewalls, intrusion prevention systems, endpoint detection and response, and continuous network monitoring.
