###### <mark>4. Lifecycle Delivery & Maintenance Strategy</mark>

<!-- ¶4 -->
Deliver a **Long-Term Maintenance Plan** that combines class-based PM with **PdM** options and standard corrective practices. We’ll formalize **PM Optimization (PMO)** to review frequencies, failure modes, and job plans at least annually; escalate chronic issues to root cause analysis (RCA) and, where justified, to PdM investments (see IV.D). Operator inspections provide early warning; job plans specify acceptance criteria and proof-of-work evidence.

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### <mark>5. Asset Information (Knowledge Enablers)</mark>

<!-- ¶6 -->
During mobilization, **complete asset registry validation and QR/barcode tagging using a NEXGEN-aligned data dictionary** . Integrate **GIS for linear/conveyance assets** and link **SCADA/Historian tags to equipment records** . Field staff will use mobile devices to scan, execute, and close work with photos, meter reads, parts, test results—creating a durable audit trail. A CMMS Governance standard will standardize naming conventions, hierarchies, and required fields.
