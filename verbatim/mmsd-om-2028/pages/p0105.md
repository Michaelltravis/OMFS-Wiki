###### **4.1.6. Auditing, Inspections, and Continuous Improvement**

<!-- ¶3 -->
Jacobs will apply a structured program of inspections and reviews to identify hazards before they lead to incidents. Supervisors and field leaders will conduct weekly inspections. Monthly reviews of safety performance will evaluate trends and corrective action progress. We’ll complete a comprehensive annual audit at JIWRF and SSWRF. All findings will be documented, assigned, and tracked through Safety360 until closure. This consistent review process drives improvement and supports safe operations.
