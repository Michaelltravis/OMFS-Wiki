###### **3.6. Fleet Maintenance**

<!-- ¶3 -->
Fleet maintenance represents an additional skillset to the wastewater and D&D facilities maintenance, and our approach will ensure all MMSD vehicles and equipment remain reliable, safe, and fully available to support operations across the WRFs, conveyance system, and field environments. We’ll leverage Jacobs’ national fleet management expertise, our CMMS/asset management discipline, and a local team deeply familiar with MMSD’s operating conditions to deliver the following:

<!-- ¶4 -->
� **Comprehensive Coverage of MMSD’s Fleet:** Jacobs will maintain all vehicles and equipment listed in RFP Exhibit A. Our Fleet Maintenance staff will perform all required PM, approved PdM, and CM activities consistent with RFP Exhibit B requirements, covering on-road vehicles (sedans, pickups, vans, utility vehicles); heavy equipment (front-end loaders, forklifts, man-lifts); sewer maintenance vehicles (vacuum trucks, CCTV trucks); and plant vehicles (burden carriers, bicycles). This includes full compliance with MMSD requirements for vehicle cleanliness, safety equipment, warranty coordination, and NEXGEN CMMS-based documentation as discussed above.

<!-- ¶5 -->
� **CMMS-Driven, Condition-Based Fleet Management:** Based on our experience implementing NEXGEN, we understand its capabilities and intend to use them, as appropriate, as the backbone of our Fleet Maintenance program. We’ll use NEXGEN’s Asset Inventory module to maintain a complete record for each vehicle—specifications, work order history, and associated PM schedules—all in one place. PM and PdM modules will drive smart scheduling of work orders based on time, meter readings, and condition so we can address emerging issues before they become breakdowns. All work orders—PM, CM, PdM, inspections, and repairs—will be created and closed in real time via mobile devices, with cost, labor, and parts tracked at the asset level to support transparent decisions and optimized lifecycle cost. Using NEXGEN’s AM Planning module, we’ll turn this condition and cost data into forward-looking forecasts of capital and O&M needs, including visuals of expected renewal timing based on useful life, financial life, and inflation assumptions, so MMSD can clearly see fleet reliability risks and replacement priorities.

<!-- ¶6 -->
� **Reliable PM and CM:** Jacobs will implement a structured PM program that’s integrated with the facilities PM systems. For CM activities, we’ll self-perform repairs wherever possible to minimize downtime, obtain MMSD pre-authorization for outsourced repairs or parts exceeding service agreement thresholds, and maintain spare parts inventory consistent with the Initial Inventory requirements. Additionally, we’ll provide MMSD with a temporary vehicle whenever an MMSD vehicle requires more than1 day of service, ensuring uninterrupted field operations.

� **Annual Vehicle Assessments and Budget Collaboration:** In accordance with RFP Exhibit B requirements, Jacobs will conduct annual vehicle condition assessments, prepare detailed specifications for vehicle replacements and maintain a technical specification library in the CMMS, and collaborate with MMSD each April in the annual Fleet Maintenance and replacement planning meeting. Jacobs will use NEXGEN’s available tool modules such as AM Planning for Intelligent Asset Management to forecast the fleet’s capital and operational costs over the upcoming years.

<!-- ¶10 -->
� **Safety, Compliance, and Operational Readiness:** Jacobs embeds safety and reliability into all Fleet Maintenance activities, including equipping our vehicles with fire extinguishers, first aid kits, and safety gear and providing staff training in equipment inspections, heavy-vehicle safety, and CDL-relevant operations, as needed. We’ll also ensure garage equipment (lifts, cranes, tools, etc.) at the Thirteenth Street Vehicle Garage are properly maintained to ensure safe and compliant repair operations. Additionally, we’ll provide towing services for MMSD.

###### **3.7. Transparency, Reporting, and Governance**

<!-- ¶12 -->
We’ll proactively establish the required **Maintenance Committee with two subcommittees—CMMS Governance and SCADA Governance—to co-manage standards, integrations, configuration, and change control.** These bodies are the forum for PM list changes, PMO results, and cross-functional decisions connecting maintenance to capital planning.

<!-- ¶13 -->
MMSD will have **live dashboard access to PM compliance, CM backlog, work-order aging, labor/cost, asset availability, and risk** . We’ll clearly delineate MMSD-funded vs. Jacobs-funded work under the Agreement’s threshold model, and we’ll **review Standards of Performance** (e.g., on-time PM completion, closeout lag, MTBF/MTTR, availability) **at set cadences with documented actions and owners** .

<!-- ¶14 -->
**Specific to fleet maintenance** , Jacobs will maintain open, frequent communication with MMSD regarding vehicle repair status and availability, any outsourced repair recommendations, warranty claims and manufacturer coordination, fleet disposal preparation and recommendations, and any operational risks affecting safety, mobility, or mission-critical service. Our approach ensures MMSD’s leadership, governance committees, and operational teams will always have visibility into fleet condition, risks, and costs just as they will with the facilities.
