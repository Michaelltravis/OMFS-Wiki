###### **2.6.5. Shared-Cost Transparency and Reporting**

<!-- ¶6 -->
Jacobs will maintain a complete, auditable record of shared-cost items, including energy, chemicals, fuel, and biosolids handling, and will provide detailed reporting that supports collaborative cost management with MMSD. **Using integrated dashboards, Jacobs and MMSD will jointly review data, KPI trends, and sharedcost metrics during regular governance meetings.** All monitored parameters will align with MMSD’s **Standards of Performance** in Exhibit C, ensuring comprehensive visibility into treatment, conveyance, and solids operations as well as the cost and efficiency drivers behind them.

<!-- ¶7 -->
Jacobs will automatically flag alarm conditions or KPI deviations and document corrective actions in a shared log visible to MMSD. Any process condition that could threaten permit compliance will trigger immediate notification to MMSD and a written summary of corrective steps. Daily operations logs and monthly performance reports will summarize flow, loading, power, chemical, and solids data. We’ll reconcile monthly shared-cost reports with invoices, inventory, and consumption data. Quarterly and annual performance summaries will be prepared for **Operations and Maintenance Committee** review, including efficiency trends, performance indicators, and improvement actions. An **Annual Performance and Optimization Report** will consolidate operational parameters, KPIs, and shared-cost metrics. All reports will be generated from validated datasets in compliance with RFP Exhibits B and Q and will be available to MMSD through secure dashboards.

###### **2.6.6. Continuous Improvement**

<!-- ¶14 -->
Jacobs’ program fosters ongoing optimization through periodic audits, process modeling, and analytics-driven performance reviews. Each quarter, our team will evaluate process efficiencies and develop targeted improvement actions, such as optimizing aeration control, refining chemical feed curves, and using existing SCADA-integrated dryer and product temperature data to fine-tune dryer operating parameters. This includes focused review of product temperature trends monitored and archived to demonstrate compliance with EPA 503 requirements, while identifying opportunities to improve energy efficiency and operational consistency.
