EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### Programmatic Framework for Unified Governance, Accountability, and Delivery

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### High-Caliber Leadership Will Create a People-First, High Performance Team

<!-- ¶4 -->
We’ve assigned **Kevin Dahl as Project Manager and Rick Warner, PE as Deputy Project Manager (DPM), two of our highest-caliber water management leaders** , who will align with MMSD’s management and remain available 24/7. **Kevin will lead day-to-day operations with a steady, proven, hands-on presence. Rick’s Above and Beyond role is a key differentiator: in addition to other responsibilities, we’re intentionally elevating regional partnership and public outreach to the DPM level so these priorities receive dedicated leadership attention** —while also strengthening the cross-functional disciplines that directly support your operations and maintenance teams. In this way, Rick will back up Kevin with selected management needs, so Kevin can stay focused on daily O&M performance and culture building.

<!-- ¶5 -->
MMSD structured this contract to deliver accountability and transparency, especially for shared costs and maintenance outcomes, across its complex, interconnected system. Jacobs’ program management (PgM)-based operating model will do just that—unifying governance, delivery management, and cost control across MMSD’s facilities. Our PgM framework creates a single source of truth for operating, maintenance, and financial systems and ties performance and cost to verifiable data, shared key performance indicators (KPIs), and disciplined reporting— with dashboards, exception tracking, and structured monthly/quarterly reviews that drive timely corrective action.

<!-- ¶6 -->
Kevin and Rick will build a **people-first, high-performance culture** grounded in employee development and respect—because strong culture drives safety, retention, and results. Our team has engaged MMSD staff, stakeholders, and union leadership and understands the workforce and labor-relations realities that must be managed proactively. **We’ll be ready to perform from Day 1.** Our robust training approach is tailored to staff roles and career pathways and includes a workforce development program to build a
