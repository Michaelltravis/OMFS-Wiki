###### D. Potential Additive Work - Level 3 PdM

<!-- ¶4 -->
###### 1. JACOBS UNDERSTANDS AND AGREES WITH MMSD’S GOALS FOR PdM

<!-- ¶5 -->
Today’s PdM environment at MMSD is fragmented. Multiple vendors provide vibration, oil, and OEM-specific analytics, but results are not consistently visible, comparable, or actionable. MMSD recognizes that its current data architecture does not yet support asset-level traceability of condition, work history, and failure modes—limiting the value of predictive analytics. MMSD is therefore seeking a PdM program that consolidates disparate data sources, removes black-box vendor dependencies, strengthens data quality, and produces reliable, automated insights tied to specific assets and components.

<!-- ¶6 -->
Jacobs understands that MMSD wants a **coherent, transparent, and scalable strategy** for PdM—one that begins with the assets of highest consequence and expands as value is demonstrated. MMSD expects Level 3 PdM to be priced separately from the base O&M fee, consistent with the RFP, and to leverage its existing infrastructure rather than adding burdensome hardware or software. The program must align with MMSD’s criticality rankings, integrate analytics into Jacobs’ workflows, and support MMSD’s formal governance bodies through clear reporting and structured decision-making.

<!-- ¶7 -->
Jacobs’ approach reflects MMSD’s goals: PdM architecture that builds on MMSD’s current systems, elevates the value of data they already collect, and adds targeted advanced technologies where they create meaningful gains in reliability, cost efficiency, and operational resilience.

<!-- ¶8 -->
###### 2. SUMMARY OF LEVELS 1–3 PdM

<!-- ¶9 -->
**Level 1 – MMSD-Owned Data and Analytics.** Level 1 consists of asset and operational data collected and monitored directly by MMSD—primarily SCADA, historian trends, alarms, and operator observations. While not priced as PdM work, these datasets are essential inputs that Jacobs will incorporate into Level 3 analytics. Level 1 data provides the operational context needed to interpret condition signatures, identify trends, and refine predictive thresholds.

<!-- ¶10 -->
**Level 2 – Jacobs’ Standard PdM/PM (Included in Base Fee).** Level 2 includes Jacobs’ traditional predictive and preventive technologies: handheld vibration readings, infrared thermography, oil and tribology analysis, static electrical testing, and vendor/OEM-prescribed PM activities. MMSD classifies these activities as PM, and they are fully included in the base O&M price. Level 2 provides measurable condition indicators and early warnings and serves as a foundation on which Level 3 is built.

**Level 3 – Advanced, Real-Time, Integrated PdM (Additive Scope).** Level 3 PdM is an additive, on-demand scope that integrates continuous monitoring technologies, automated diagnostics, and NEXGEN-triggered workflows. Rather than relying on periodic measurements, Level 3 delivers **continuous or high-frequency analytics** that detect subtle changes in equipment behavior long before failure modes develop. Tools such as VibeCloud (real-time vibration and temperature), Artesis (motor current signature analysis and sensorless pump performance analytics), and complementary technologies lik **e Sewer Line Rapid Assessment Tool (SL-RAT)** for acoustic inspection provide early insight into mechanical, electrical, and hydraulic degradation.

<!-- ¶13 -->
The distinguishing characteristic of Level 3 is not the sensor alone—it's the automated analysis, continuous diagnostics, and integration with NEXGEN to generate actionable work orders. Level 3 unifies MMSD’s Level 1 and Level 2 data with new real-time insights to form a complete, predictive view of asset health.
