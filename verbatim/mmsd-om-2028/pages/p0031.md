###### 5. A CULTURE THAT CELEBRATES TEAMWORK AND ACHIEVEMENT

<!-- ¶12 -->
We recognize what matters: safety, innovation, teamwork, and community service. **Teamwork and empowerment are at the heart of Jacobs’ culture and will power results at MMSD.** We strive to pursue industry recognition that reflects our teams’ operational excellence and client satisfaction. These recognitions reinforce the value of collaboration across functions and levels.

**Jacobs’ commitment to social impact.** Through our **Collectively℠ program** , Jacobs empowers employees to give back via volunteering and charitable contributions. The company matches donations, rewards volunteer hours, and promotes campaigns aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goals. Since 2020, Jacobs employees have contributed over $10.9M to global causes. Each year our teams donate thousands of hours to benefit the local community, from river cleanups and community beautification efforts to charitable golf tournaments and holiday gift and meal drives. **At MMSD, we’ll organize and sustain these efforts with the same discipline we bring to operations, turning community service into a standing practice that provides tangible benefits to Milwaukee, rather than a one-off event.**
