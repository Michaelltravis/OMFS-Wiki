###### _Effective and Efficient Wet Weather Operations_

<!-- ¶9 -->
During transition, we’ll confirm the ISS, MIS, and WRFs are ready for wet weather. We’ll align MMSD’s forecasting framework with our predictive tools to maximize available tunnel storage, confirm pump readiness, and coordinate WRF operations. This is central to achieving zero CSOs and reducing basement backups. During events, Jacobs will provide clear, defensible, real-time recommendations. To achieve this, we’ll use MMSD’s root cause analyses, including those following the August 2025 flooding event, to strengthen decision-making and performance during future events. Jacobs and our Mead & Hunt partners bring a systemwide view informed by recent CSO/SSO and basement-backup reduction work, including the Mead & Hunt-led Mill Road Near Surface Collector System Design, NS12 Collector System Improvements, Hampton MIS Analysis, and Impact of Water Levels on District Conveyance System Assets Study. That perspective will help us anticipate how new connections to the ISS may change how quickly the tunnel fills—and what that could mean for how often the ISS reaches full capacity.
