###### IV. APPROACH

<!-- ¶3 -->
� **Predictive odor modeling and community impact assessment.** While influent sulfide mass provides critical process insight, it does not indicate if odors will reach nearby receptors at concentrations that could cause complaints. To address this, we propose installing a system that will continuously estimate odor concentrations at locations surrounding the WRFs. These emissions will then be used in an AERMOD dispersion model along with real-time wind speed and direction data from a Jacobs-installed meteorological station. The model results will be displayed graphically to illustrate the shape and extent of odor plumes and identify potential offsite areas impacted.

<!-- ¶4 -->
� When modeling indicates a risk of offsite odor impacts, notifications can be sent to residents, alderpersons, and neighborhood associations using multiple communication channels, including social media, email, and opt-in text messages. While we recognize the inherent uncertainties associated with modeling, our experience shows that with proper design and calibration, this integrated system provides a reasonably reliable early warning that is often a strong indicator of conditions likely to result in odor complaints. **Exhibit IV-26** illustrates how we calibrated our ATS model.

**Disciplined Plant Operations and Equipment Performance.** Jacobs will operate treatment processes with practices designed to minimize sulfide generation and prevent fugitive emissions, including maintaining appropriate sludge blanket depths, optimizing sludge storage durations, and closely monitoring primary and solids-handling conditions during warm weather. We understand that there may be a need under some circumstances to operate with high blankets, but operators will be trained to more closely monitor for odors when blankets are high. Operations of all covered processes will follow SOPs that ensure negative pressure is maintained, covers are secure, gasketed, and intact, and fans and ventilation systems are functioning as designed. We’ll provide ISO-aligned PM and testing for odor control units—carbon, chemical scrubbers, blowers—to maintain performance and extend media life. If ventilation systems are not operating properly, leaks can occur, which can be a significant source of odors.
