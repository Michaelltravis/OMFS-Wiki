###### **2.3.1. Digital Twin: Enabling System-Wide Optimization**

<!-- ¶7 -->
At the core of our approach is giving operators clear, actionable insights while giving MMSD full visibility into what we see, decide, and do. Jacobs’ Digital One Water platform turns system data into decision-ready information so operators can manage wet-weather flows, optimize treatment processes, sustain reliable Milorganite production, and anticipate equipment issues before they occur.

<!-- ¶8 -->
A key component of our toolkit is a digital twin, which provides a dynamic, real-time model of MMSD’s infrastructure. It enables holistic management under varying conditions and supports adaptive operational decision-making, continuous compliance improvement, optimization of energy, nutrient removal, resource recovery, and strategic CSO mitigation. The digital twin does not automate plant operations. It elevates situational awareness by allowing MMSD and Jacobs to evaluate operational scenarios together and respond proactively to changing conditions. **Exhibit IV-29** presents the benefits of Jacobs’ digital twin to MMSD.

<!-- ¶9 -->
Real-time monitoring, PdM, and integrated controls will help the teams quickly understand current conditions, identify root causes, and take timely action. These tools don’t replace operator judgment—they reinforce it by pairing real-time analytics and machine learning with proven operating experience to support proactive decision-making.
