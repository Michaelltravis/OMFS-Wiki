###### **1.5. Mobilization Overview**

<!-- ¶4 -->
Jacobs will implement a structured 200-point transition and mobilization plan immediately after contract award and concluding on March 1, 2028. This plan ensures all staffing, systems, governance, and readiness activities are completed before operations commence. The approach aligns with Exhibit E requirements and is anchored in Jacobs’ steps to setup a PgM framework for success, provided in **Exhibit IV-8** in **_Section IV.A.1.2.1_** . The detailed plan will be finalized jointly with MMSD at contract award.

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### **1.6. Transition Governance and Communication**

<!-- ¶6 -->
Jacobs will establish a three-tiered transition governance structure aligned with Exhibit P, including Executive, Operations, and Maintenance Committees. These committees will begin meeting within 10 business days of contract execution to guide implementation, ensure alignment, and maintain consistent communication between MMSD and Jacobs leadership.

###### **1.6.1. Transition Communications Plan**

<!-- ¶10 -->
Jacobs will implement a transition communications plan to provide transparency, clear expectations, and consistent engagement throughout the pre-term period. The plan defines stakeholder roles, meeting cadence, knowledge transfer protocols, documentation standards, and progress reporting. Key elements include:

<!-- ¶11 -->
- Defined stakeholders and points of contact

<!-- ¶12 -->
- Weekly progress meetings and bi-weekly governance reviews

<!-- ¶13 -->
- Structured knowledge transfer and documentation protocols

<!-- ¶14 -->
- A shared digital workspace for deliverables and records

<!-- ¶15 -->
- Dashboard reporting on schedule, staffing, and risk mitigation.

###### **1.8. Integrated Collection and Treatment System Modeling and System Readiness**

<!-- ¶19 -->
During the transition period, Jacobs will collaborate with MMSD to evaluate the operation of the ISS and WRFs as an integrated system, particularly under increasingly intense rainfall conditions. Coordination with Storm Harvester will support understanding of system behavior before, during, and after significant storm events. Additional details are provided in **_Section IV.A, 2.2.1 Collection System, Tunnels, and Wet Weather Flow Management Focus Area_** .

<!-- ¶20 -->
###### **1.9. Assurance of Continuity**

<!-- ¶21 -->
Through proven systems, experienced personnel, and local familiarity, we’ll maintain continuity across MMSD’s facilities and services throughout the transition. Drawing on lessons learned from dozens of complex treatment and conveyance transitions, Jacobs will deliver a seamless, non-disruptive handover that is reassuring to employees and invisible to the public.

<!-- ¶22 -->
###### **1.10. Schedule for Smooth Transition of Operations and Related Systems**

<!-- ¶23 -->
Jacobs’ transition approach is governed by a clearly defined, phased schedule that ensures all operational, staffing, and system milestones are achieved before March 1, 2028. While the Draft Contract does not specify a transition duration, Jacobs has developed an approximately 18-month pre-term schedule consistent with §3.02 (Mobilization and Staffing Transition) and §3.03(a) (CMMS Implementation).

<!-- ¶24 -->
The phased schedule emphasizes early engagement, structured onboarding, retention of institutional knowledge, and flexibility to deploy additional expertise as needed. Each phase is supported by Jacobs’ governance and communication framework, providing MMSD with transparency, accountability, and confidence in progress. The accompanying high-level schedule ( **Exhibit IV-58** ) illustrates how key activities will be sequenced and monitored to ensure full operational readiness.

<!-- ¶25 -->
All transition activities, decisions, and milestones will be documented and accessible, supporting a smooth progression to the operational start date.
