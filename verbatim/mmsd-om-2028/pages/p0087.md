**2.7.3. Compliance Execution: Tracking, Reporting, and Dashboards** Jacobs will maintain a transparent and auditable system that consolidates operational, laboratory, and maintenance data into one defensible record. Using **Hach WIMS** integrated with MMSD’s **SCADA** and **CMMS** platforms, compliance data will be validated daily, trended monthly, and summarized quarterly for joint review.

<!-- ¶11 -->
Dashboards will visualize permit performance, approaching limits, and parameter trends while linking results to process or maintenance events. Every report, including discharge monitoring reports, compliance maintenance annual reports, pretreatment program summaries, and air-permit documentation, will be prepared in MMSD’s established formats and delivered 100% on time and in compliance.

<!-- ¶12 -->
All calibration records, chain-of-custody forms, and documentation will be managed through a secure, SharePoint-based electronic document management system accessible to MMSD and Jacobs staff.

<!-- ¶13 -->
###### **2.7.2. Governance and Compliance Team**

<!-- ¶14 -->
An **Environmental Manager** will lead permit compliance, supported by operations and laboratory QA/QC leads and Jacobs’ national **compliance and reporting team** . We commit to maintaining full compliance with all requirements included in applicable federal, state, and local regulations, including WPDES and air permits. Where MMSD’s Standards of Performance exceed minimum regulatory obligations, Jacobs affirms that those higher performance standards shall govern. We’ll consistently meet or surpass all regulatory requirements while aligning with the MMSD’s environmental stewardship.

<!-- ¶15 -->
Jacobs will promptly notify MMSD of any conditions requiring attention and will engage regional or corporate resources as needed to support on-site staff. Compliance reporting SMEs will assist with reporting, audits, and regulatory coordination, and maintaining strong relationships with regulators to ensure transparency and advocacy on behalf of MMSD.

<!-- ¶16 -->
Training under this program will include WPDES permit compliance, biosolids, pretreatment, and laboratory data integrity. It will be delivered through a combination of onsite and web-based modules. All training completion and competencies will be documented and shared with MMSD to verify readiness and accountability.
