EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### IS0-55001-Aligned Asset Management System for Preventive Maintenance (PM)/ Corrective Maintenance (CM) Excellence

<!-- ¶3 -->
MMSD wants a rigorous and accountable maintenance approach with the visibility needed to manage risk and maximize asset life. **We’ll implement a practical ISO 55001-aligned Asset Management System** , governed through MMSD-facing committees. It will drive reliability-centered maintenance and use NEXGEN as the system of record, with work orders closed using field evidence, supervisor verification, and timely closeout. Shared dashboards will track PM compliance, CM backlog/aging, cost/labor, and asset signals—so performance is auditable and actionable.

<!-- ¶4 -->
**Maintenance Certainty from Enhanced Leadership and Deep Bench.** As another Above-and-Beyond enhancement, **Jacobs has added a Director of Maintenance & Asset Management, John Loucks-Powell, CRL** —a proven leader who will align the maintenance teams and drive an asset-management-centered PM/CM delivery approach. John is backed by Jacobs’ regional maintenance and specialty resources to support onsite crews during peaks, complex work, and emergencies— so potential staffing constraints won’t impede execution. Jacobs’ national asset management team will also provide tools, analytics, and implementation support to strengthen onsite capability.

<!-- ¶5 -->
**Reliability-Centered Maintenance to Eliminate Repeat Failures.** We’ll call on our regional and national specialists to also bring their expertise in reliability-centered maintenance, PM optimization, predictive maintenance (PdM) strategies, CMMS/ NEXGEN, and risk-based prioritization to our failure analysis. This ensures we identify true root causes, update work plans and maintenance intervals, prevent repeat issues, and embed lessons learned into ongoing operations.

<!-- ¶6 -->
**Jacobs’ Closed-loop Work Order Management Approach Will Drive PM/CM Excellence**

<!-- ¶7 -->
**Reliability Enhancement Programs for Jones Island Water Reclamation Facility (JIWRF) and South Shore Water Reclamation Facilities (SSWRF).** We’ve developed reliability enhancement plans for both WRFs to focus on the assets and systems that have commonly affected uptime. These programs will connect disciplined work execution with reliability analytics to improve equipment availability, reduce unplanned disruptions, and lower associated compliance risk.

###### Elevated Operations Driven by Technical Expertise and Innovation

<!-- ¶12 -->
###### COST-EFFECTIVE, COMPLIANT OPERATIONS THAT PROTECT MMSD’S REPUTATION

<!-- ¶13 -->
We’ve listened carefully to MMSD staff to understand your system and what success needs to look like in the future. Our approach is built to protect what MMSD values most: permit performance, zero overflows, Milorganite quality and reliable production, safe and resilient operations, cost effectiveness, and progress toward your 2035 Vision. We’ll operate MMSD’s complex network as one interconnected system—so decisions are coordinated, accountability is consistent, and performance improves across your facilities.

<!-- ¶14 -->
###### **Technical Excellence, Optimization, and Community‑Facing Performance**

<!-- ¶15 -->
Jacobs’ comprehensive O&M approach brings deep expertise in asset management, process optimization, digital operations, and system-wide process control. Operating as One Water, One Team, One Vision with openbook reporting and real-time monitoring for MMSD staff, you’ll always have clear visibility into what’s happening, why it’s happening, and what we’re doing about it.

<!-- ¶16 -->
Additionally, MMSD’s reputation depends on **consistent compliance, reliable operations, and odor management to drive community trust—plus maintaining Milorganite quality** that extends beyond the local community. Our operating plan protects these outcomes through disciplined process control, transparent monitoring, and continuous optimization within a structure that MMSD can audit and govern.

<!-- ¶17 -->
###### **Commitment to Compliance and “No Surprises” Performance**

<!-- ¶18 -->
Jacobs will operate every facility and control system to strictly adhere to permit conditions and MMSD’s performance standards—supported by dedicated technical leadership, compliance tools, and a culture of accountability. PgM tools such as dashboards and committee-ready reports will connect operational conditions to outcomes and costs, so issues are identified early, communicated clearly, and resolved with documented corrective actions.
