- has a stronger track record—than Jacobs. We take environmental compliance extremely seriously, and our optimization methods reflect this. Chemical dosages are intentionally set with a safety factor to ensure _consistent_ 100% regulatory compliance under all operating conditions. Our analysis of historical best practices at your facilities under all recorded past operating conditions where compliance was _always_ achieved informs our future dosage settings, not where it was achieved 99% of the time. If a lower-cost option introduces compliance risk, we do not pursue it.

<!-- ¶8 -->
- **Transparency** – Jacobs values MMSD’s technical expertise, and transparent decision-making is fundamental to a strong partnership. We’ll openly share the rationale behind all chemical optimization decisions through participation in process control meetings, real-time performance dashboards, open-book vendor pricing, and discussion of potential operational approaches. We are proud operators who respect and learn from the insights our client partners bring. Optimization will be a shared process, not a black box, and building trust with operational staff takes time and constant attention.

- **Cost Management** – MMSD and Jacobs share the goal of producing fully compliant water at the lowest responsible cost. We achieve this through both **passive** strategies in the form of one-time process alterations (e.g., refining chemical injection points, improving mixing, optimizing chemical selection) and **active** strategies that rely on our professional operations staff to implement dosage adjustments as plant conditions change. Our proposal outlines several opportunities in both categories that can deliver measurable savings without compromising performance.
