###### **1.4. Bolstering Career Awareness**

<!-- ¶19 -->
###### **1.3. Internships Peak Interest in Careers**

<!-- ¶20 -->
In support of MMSD’s goals for inclusive growth and technical advancement, we’ll work with MMSD to reinforce its brand and raise the awareness of job and career opportunities, particularly for under-represented communities through targeted outreach activities. We’ll continue to invest in local partnerships to help build the next generation of water professionals to carry MMSD’s legacy forward. This plan builds on the many outreach programs that MMSD and Jacobs have developed or have supported in the region, such as _Camp RISE, Business Capacity Development Program, Waves of Change, the Water & Sewer School, Wisconsin Rural Water Association_ and _Fresh Coast Ambassador Program_ .

<!-- ¶21 -->
Internships are critical to our recruitment strategies and have been a highly effective component of our operating model in Milwaukee and the surrounding geographic area, where we’ve engaged local colleges, technical associations, and nonprofit organizations such as the UWM and MATC, to attract students and provide insights into career pathways. We’ll continue to work with the City and MMSD as a participant in Camp RISE internship programs, and will review other proposed activities with MMSD, including participation in regional workforce programs, hosting career fairs and plant tours, developing bilingual recruitment tools, and supporting targeted outreach to underserved communities. Jacobs will provide technical instruction, facility tours, and mentoring opportunities that expose Milwaukee youth to hands-on STEM and sustainability careers.

One successful program we propose to replicate under this MMSD contract is a **Wastewater Career Internship Pipeline Program** , which we designed to create a direct pathway for high school graduates to enter apprenticeships or entry-level positions in wastewater operations and maintenance activities, laboratory services, and green infrastructure (groundskeeping and BMP maintenance). The program transitions into paid internships that can lead to long-term careers in the water sector. The program consists of two phases, as described in **Exhibit II-19** . **Exhibit II-20** provides details on our planned partnership with UWM.
