###### **4.1.3. Leadership, Roles, and Integration**

<!-- ¶4 -->
Safety leadership is embedded in our on-site team. The Project Manager will hold overall accountability for safety performance and will serve as the primary point of communication with MMSD’s Risk Manager and Safety Advisor. A dedicated **Safety and Security Manager** will implement the SMS, conduct inspections, coordinate training, and oversee investigations and documentation. Each facility will also have a safety coordinator to reinforce daily safe work practices and encourage peer engagement. Supervisors will lead pre-task briefings, hazard identification, and verification that safety procedures and permits are correctly implemented.

<!-- ¶5 -->
Jacobs and MMSD will jointly review incidents, corrective actions, and trends during quarterly **Operations Governance Committee** meetings to promote continuous improvement.

<!-- ¶6 -->
###### **4.1.4. Training and Workforce Engagement**

<!-- ¶7 -->
Employees assigned to MMSD’s facilities will complete required OSHA and MMSD-specific training, including confined space entry, lockout and tagout, fall protection, respiratory protection, combustible dust awareness, hazard communication, emergency response, chemical hygiene, and first aid and CPR. Job-specific and site-specific modules will supplement these core requirements. Annual refresher training will be tracked in the LMS and made available to MMSD.

<!-- ¶8 -->
Regular engagement through daily start-up discussions, weekly toolbox talks, and safety observations will reinforce awareness, support proactive hazard identification, and encourage open communication. All personnel will have stop-work authority.

<!-- ¶9 -->
###### **4.1.5. Incident Reporting and Investigation**

<!-- ¶10 -->
Jacobs will meet MMSD’s notification and reporting procedures for all safety incidents, near misses, injuries, or hazardous conditions. MMSD’s Risk Manager, Safety Advisor, and Operations Officer will be notified immediately by phone. We’ll provide a written summary within 12 hours, followed by a full investigation, including root cause analysis and corrective actions, within the required 48 to 72-hour period. All findings will be managed through Safety360 and reviewed with MMSD during routine performance discussions.

###### **<mark>How Do We Do It?</mark>**

<!-- ¶16 -->
- **Training and skill development** —We empower our teams with the knowledge and tools to navigate potential hazards, creating a workforce that is proficient and safety conscious at every step.

<!-- ¶17 -->
- **Robust safety protocols** —We perform regular safety audits, hazard assessments, and real-time monitoring to identify and address potential risks.

<!-- ¶18 -->
- **Engaged leadership** —Our projects are supported by regional and national safety leaders. Each leader actively engages with their teams to reinforce safety messages, provide on-the-job and industry training, listen as concerns arise, and motivate teams to work safely. EH&S Specialist and Safety & Security Manager Travis Leech will fill this role for MMSD.

<!-- ¶19 -->
- **Established culture of accountability** — Staff are empowered to speak up if they see unsafe practices, allowing everyone to feel comfortable raising concerns without fear of repercussion.

<!-- ¶20 -->
- **Inspiring safety communication** —Staff volunteer to share simple daily messages focused on our culture of caring, distributed to all O&M staff, often reflecting on deeply personal events.

<!-- ¶21 -->
- **Staff recognition and reward** —Behind every incident-free year is a team of dedicated professionals who prioritize safety in their daily operations. Our people are the heartbeat of our success. We regularly seek out industry safety awards and host project parties to foster a sense of accomplishment and reinforce our desired safe behaviors.
