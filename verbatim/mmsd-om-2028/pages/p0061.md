- **Step Feed Optimization** . Upgrading the existing step feed process to step feed biological nutrient removal (BNR) introduces flow into a downstream anoxic zone, enabling denitrification and reducing settleability impacts. This approach increases selector zone hydraulic retention time, lowers NO₃ loading in RAS, and improves phosphorus removal. **A two-pass step feed configuration could boost capacity by 10–15%.**

<!-- ¶7 -->
- **Mobile Biofilm Activated Sludge (MBAS).** MBAS, developed by Jacobs and Nuvoda, uses mobile plant-based media integrated with MLSS to enhance treatment without major footprint expansion. Unlike fixed-film systems, MBAS media circulates with RAS and is retained and returned to the aeration basins via screening of the WAS stream. At SSWRF, MBAS could increase capacity to improve wet-weather handling, increase nitrification, improve settleability and enable biological phosphorus removal. While blower upgrades and aeration control remain necessary, **MBAS offers a cost-effective retrofit with significant performance gains. It could increase treatment capacity by up to 40% at South Shore.**
