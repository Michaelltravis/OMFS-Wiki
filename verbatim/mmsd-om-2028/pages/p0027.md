<!-- ¶21 -->
###### E. Technical Training Program

<!-- ¶22 -->
Jacobs’ proposed training programs are structured to develop and sustain a high-performing, certified workforce that operates safely, efficiently, and in compliance with national standards and MMSD’s performance standards and technical training requirements under RFP §3.02(e,f) and Exhibit M. Our approach is data-driven and outcome-focused, providing the foundation for operational excellence, regulatory reliability, and employee growth.

<!-- ¶23 -->
###### 1. COMMITMENT TO LEARNING, SAFETY, AND CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT

<!-- ¶24 -->
We plan to exceed the contractual requirement of 24 hours of technical training per employee per year, supported by comprehensive safety and leadership development. Training completion and certifications will be tracked through Jacobs’ **Learning Management System (LMS)** and integrated with performance evaluations and project KPIs. This assures accountability, transparency, and measurable progress for both Jacobs and MMSD.
