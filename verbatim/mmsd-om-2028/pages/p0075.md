###### IV. APPROACH

<!-- ¶5 -->
**Clear, Timely, Documented Response to Public Complaints.** While we believe our proactive approach described above will decrease complaints, there still will be complaints until all odorous sources are covered and treated. **Jacobs will implement a proactive, consistent, well-documented odor complaint protocol that exceeds MMSD’s Scope of Services requirements.** Our approach integrates prevention, rapid response, and community engagement—ensuring that odor issues are minimized, investigated promptly, and communicated effectively to both MMSD and the public. Complaints received through MMSD systems, Jacobs’ website, hotlines, or social media will be logged into a centralized database with location and observed conditions. Trained staff—validated through odor sensitivity testing—will investigate each complaint using onsite observations, low-level H₂S meters, and data from the odor early warning system. We’ll assess root causes and implement corrective action when appropriate. Each complaint will generate a documented summary with findings, mitigation steps, and lessons learned, which we’ll leverage to drive continuous improvements. Residents will receive courteous follow-up, and we’ll review trends with MMSD to refine procedures and identify systemic improvements. A preliminary outline of the protocol approach is shown in **Exhibit IV-27** .

**Continuous Improvements to Improve Community Outreach and Confidence.** Odor control performance, complaint trends, and system data will be evaluated through MMSD/Jacobs governance meetings, annual odor reviews, and dashboard reporting. Jacobs will apply insights from the WATS model, early warning analytics, SCADA trends, and operator feedback to continuously improve odor mitigation strategies and prevent recurrences. In addition to proactive operations, we’ll maintain a visible community presence, overseen by our Deputy Project Manager Rick Warner, by participating in local meetings, events, and neighborhood associations to conduct outreach, increase community confidence, and ensure that residents clearly know who to contact if concerns arise. We’ll prepare quarterly odor reports that can be shared with the public. This proactive, transparent approach strengthens regulatory compliance, reduces community impacts, and ensures MMSD maintains its role as a responsible steward of public health and neighborhood quality of life.
