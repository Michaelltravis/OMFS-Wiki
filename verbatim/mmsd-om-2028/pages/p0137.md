###### 8. INTEGRATION WITH SCADA AND NEXGEN CMMS

<!-- ¶4 -->
Successful Level 3 PdM depends on tight integration between predictive technologies, SCADA, the SCADA historian, and NEXGEN. Jacobs will configure SCADA points and data flows for PdM inputs, ensuring alignment with MMSD’s OT architecture and cybersecurity requirements. PdM tools will route their analytics to the historian or directly into NEXGEN via open APIs, where automated work orders will be generated based on agreed thresholds.

<!-- ¶5 -->
NEXGEN will be configured to accept condition levels, alerts, and asset health scores and to associate them with specific assets. This will improve work planning, enhance prioritization, and eliminate the manual interpretation of PdM reports that currently burdens MMSD.
