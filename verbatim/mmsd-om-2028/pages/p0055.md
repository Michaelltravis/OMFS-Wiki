**2.2. Effective Process Monitoring and Control for System-Wide Insight and Operational Excellence** **Effective process monitoring and control for system-wide insight for operational excellence** 373_007CAM_1 MMSD has one of the nation’s most complex wastewater systems, including a 4,800-mile collection network, numerous pump stations, interplant sludge pumping, an ISS for wet weather flow, the SSWRF, and JIWRF with significant Milorganite fertilizer production. These assets function as a single, interconnected system, and operational decisions in one area directly influence performance, reliability, and compliance elsewhere. Achieving operational excellence requires system-wide insight into how each element interacts with another. To translate this into actionable strategies and to enable operators to make informed and timely decisions, our approach focuses on the following:

<!-- ¶5 -->
- **Integrated Cross-Facility Monitoring and Best-Practice Control:** We’ll combine SCADA data, online sensors, laboratory results, process models, and operator observations into a single unified view that enables early detection of upsets, rapid troubleshooting, and precise control of aeration, clarifiers, chemical dosing, sludge handling, and solids processing. Because SSWRF and JIWRF are operationally linked through the ISS, solids transfers, and system hydraulics, we’ll emphasize coordinated decision-making across facilities. Operators at both WRFs will work from shared dashboards, common data sets, and aligned control strategies to protect compliance while optimizing energy use, hydraulics management, and solids management.

<!-- ¶6 -->
- **Predictive Operating Insight:** To anticipate operating conditions before they develop, we pair operator knowledge with advanced digital tools—such as Intelligent O&M analytics, process models, and a digital twin—allowing staff to prepare for changes in loading, tunnel discharges, equipment stress, or solids characteristics and maintaining stability across the system. Details on these digital tools are included in **_Section 2.3_** .

<!-- ¶7 -->
- **Optimization of Energy, Chemicals, Solids, and Process Stability** : Adjustments to aeration strategies, chemical addition, wasting rates, solids loading, and dryer feed management have direct impacts on energy consumption, biosolids quality, wastewater treatment, odor, and overall system resilience. Jacobs’ O&M and technical teams will continuously evaluate and optimize these areas while preserving stable, compliant operations.

<!-- ¶8 -->
We’ve identified **key operational focus areas in Exhibit IV-14** that offer the biggest opportunities to improve performance across MMSD’s system. Each requires specialized expertise and proven digital tools for success. We introduce each key focus area in this exhibit, then describe our approach for each in more detail.

###### EXHIBIT IV-14. KEY OPERATIONAL FOCUS AREAS

<!-- ¶12 -->
###### **COLLECTION SYSTEM &  WET-WEATHER FLOW MANAGEMENT**

<!-- ¶13 -->
We will apply predictive forecasting, coordinated tunnel and WRF operations, and disciplined decision-making to support MMSD’s goals of zero overflows, reduced basement backups, and optimized energy use. Our approach emphasizes proactive readiness, resilient wet-weather response, and close coordination with MMSD management.

<!-- ¶14 -->
###### **LIQUID TREATMENT PROCESS CONTROL**

<!-- ¶15 -->
Using disciplined, data-driven process control, we will integrate primary, secondary, and disinfection processes to optimize biological performance, aeration, solids management, energy use, and effluent quality—ensuring stable operations and continuous compliance.

<!-- ¶16 -->
###### **SOLIDS PROCESSING & MILORGANITE  PRODUCTION**

<!-- ¶17 -->
Building on MMSD’s strong performance, we will optimize sludge handling, energy recovery, and product quality across both WRFs. Our approach enhances reliability, maximizes renewable energy use, and ensures consistent Milorganite production through targeted optimization and disciplined risk management.

<!-- ¶18 -->
###### **ENERGY OPTIMIZATION**

<!-- ¶19 -->
We will operate power generation, heat recovery, and high-energy treatment processes as a single integrated system. Through predictive analytics and systemwide energy modeling, we will reduce energy costs and demand charges while supporting MMSD’s 2035 Vision for increased renewable energy without compromising treatment performance.

<!-- ¶20 -->
###### **CHEMICAL  OPTIMIZATION**

<!-- ¶21 -->
Guided by regulatory compliance, transparency, cost management, and innovation, Jacobs uses data science and historical best-practice analysis to deliver defensible reductions in chemical use while maintaining stable operations, full compliance, and clear operator visibility into dosing decisions.

<!-- ¶22 -->
###### **ODOR  CONTROL**

<!-- ¶23 -->
Our proactive, predictive, and community-focused approach emphasizes odor prevention, rapid identification of potential odor issues, and disciplined response. By combining advanced H2S modeling, real-time monitoring, and transparent communication, we reduce odor incidents and strengthen public trust.
