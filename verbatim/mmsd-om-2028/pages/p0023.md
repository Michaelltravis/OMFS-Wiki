###### **4.1. Integration: One Team, One Standard**

<!-- ¶16 -->
We begin by integrating qualified incumbent staff you wish to retain alongside new hires, pairing senior staff with junior operators for skills transfer, and aligning everyone to common performance and safety expectations. Orientation reinforces our code of conduct and a “One Water, One Team, One Vision” culture. Supervisors will conduct regular check-ins to assure people feel respected, informed, and supported. We’ll use the same respectful and transparent approach with labor partners that we’ve used with other unionized workforces, some of which are listed in **Exhibit II-8** .

###### **4.3. Succession Planning—A Structured, Visible Path to Advancement**

<!-- ¶18 -->
Our team is structured to provide pathways to advanced positions and fill vacancies as they occur. With a national shortage of certified wastewater operators, proactive succession planning and job marketing is essential. Each department will identify critical roles and designate successors with readiness plans tied to skills, licenses, and leadership milestones. We align these plans with **_Section II.E Technical Training_** and the annual Workforce Development Plan discussed in **_H. Workforce Development Program_** , so training investment is aimed at the roles MMSD needs most. Tools from our Resource Planning Group—career succession planning, leadership development guides, one-on-one career counseling, project manager internship, and leadership-engaged mentor programs—give supervisors and employees a common language and playbook for growth.
