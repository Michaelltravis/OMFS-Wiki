###### **2.5. Leveraging Corporate Resources for Operational Excellence**

<!-- ¶5 -->
**Leveraging**<br>**corporate resources for**<br>**operational excellence**<br>376_007CAM_1

Jacobs will leverage the full breadth of our corporate resources to strengthen operations and management of MMSD’s complex facilities, drawing on our broader technical, operational, and digital capabilities to enhance local performance. MMSD’s system requires precise 24/7 monitoring and control of the ISS, pump stations, and interceptors as well as large and aging WRFs and the associated Milorganite production facilities. To meet these needs, Jacobs will integrate our local operations team with the expertise

<!-- ¶6 -->
of our Operations Technical Services Group and Global Water Technology Organization. These groups include national experts in tunnel hydraulics, wet weather operations modeling, SCADA and instrumentation, energy optimization, and PdM, to name only a few as illustrated in **Exhibits IV-34 and 35** . They’ll provide MMSD with on-call and remote support, data review, process optimization guidance, and escalation assistance—ensuring operational resiliency during both routine and extreme wet weather conditions.

<!-- ¶7 -->
By leveraging these corporate resources, Jacobs ensures MMSD’s operations remain resilient, data-driven, and adaptive. Our unique delivery model provides both the agility of a dedicated local team and the depth of a global O&M, engineering, consulting, technology, and program management enterprise—ensuring continuity, redundancy, and innovation. Through this structure, MMSD gains a high-performing partner committed to measurable reliability, O&M excellence, and continuous improvement throughout the contract.
