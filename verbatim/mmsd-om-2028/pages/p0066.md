We’ll also work with MMSD to evaluate and integrate capital projects that support long-term energy goals. To guide day-to-day decisions, we propose developing a system-wide, dynamic energy balance that builds on the “Sankey” model Jacobs developed for MMSD’s Energy Plan (see **Exhibit IV-19** above). We’ll expand the model to include solids loading, interplant transfers, turbine heat recovery, and gas distribution. The result will function as an operational dashboard across both plants. It will help operators compare options in real time—such as when to transfer sludge between plants, how to run GE turbines to manage peak demand, and how best to route landfill gas to the Solar Turbines and dryers.

<!-- ¶6 -->
_JIWRF Solar Turbines: Targeted PdM will increase turbine uptime and renewable energy generation_

<!-- ¶7 -->
**JIWRF Power Generation.** JIWRF’s complex energy system relies heavily on landfill gas and natural gas to fuel the Solar and GE turbines that supply both electricity and critical waste heat for Milorganite drying—MMSD’s single largest energy demand. Jacobs’ operating strategy focuses on maximizing renewable fuel use, heat recovery, and turbine uptime while minimizing demand charges as such:

<!-- ¶8 -->
- **Enhance demand management** , including coordinated load shedding and exploring short-duration aeration adjustments where appropriate and compliant.

<!-- ¶9 -->
- � **Maximize the use of available landfill gas** , directing it to the turbines whenever possible, and utilizing any excess in the four dual-fuel dryer burners.

<!-- ¶10 -->
Together, these strategies will **increase renewable heat utilization, reduce purchased power, and improve resilience of the drying process** .

<!-- ¶11 -->
- **Maximize Solar Turbine availability** through targeted PM/PdM, compressorsystem reliability improvements, and optimized landfill gas moisture control (including evaluating potential gas conditioning enhancements such as upstream chilling).

<!-- ¶12 -->
- **Use waste heat efficiently** , prioritizing waste heat use first in the dryers, then the waste heat boiler, while minimizing waste heat blown off for pressure control.

<!-- ¶13 -->
- **Operate the GE turbine strategically** to mitigate peak electrical demand, while coordinating with MMSD on future Solar Turbine expansion based on current feasibility studies.
