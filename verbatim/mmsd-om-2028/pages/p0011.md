EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### Seamless Transition Will Lay the Foundation for Uninterrupted and Continuous O&M Services

<!-- ¶3 -->
MMSD’s schedule provides approximately 18 months for pre-term transition, intentionally structured to minimize risk and protect uninterrupted operations. **Jacobs will execute our proven transition approach that delivers continuity, staffing stability, and strong Day 1 performance.** We’ll sequence critical staffing, systems, and governance activities well ahead of the contract start, so **handover occurs without disruption to service, safety, or compliance.**

<!-- ¶4 -->
**Expert Transition Team, Leadership, and Processes Ensure Day 1 Readiness.** Our transition will be led by **senior transition manager Kelly Irving** and a dedicated team experienced with complex wastewater O&M handovers. We emphasize early engagement, clear communication, and disciplined readiness so people, processes, and systems are fully prepared before operations begin—establishing a strong foundation to elevate performance throughout the contract term.

<!-- ¶5 -->
**Workforce continuity is central to success. Jacobs brings deep experience transitioning and supporting unionized workforces** , and we will focus on continuing to build and align our relationship with the local Milwaukee unions to ensure success.  In parallel, our PgM team will mobilize during the pre-term period to establish governance, control tools, and management processes—positioning MMSD for a seamless handover on March 1, 2028.

<!-- ¶6 -->
###### Regional Partnerships and Public Outreach for Community Benefits

<!-- ¶7 -->
We’re inspired by your vision—and your ongoing partnership and outreach initiatives. We’ll build on your foundation and our partnerships in the region. We’ll explore above and beyond ideas with you within four strategies—community health and environmental enhancement, workforce development and economic growth, sustainability and resilience, and research and development. Our goal is to identify those ideas within each strategy that have the greatest impact on equity, sustainability, and economic growth and bring the most value to MMSD and ratepayers. Examples of these ideas are captured on the next page, and are elaborated upon in **_Section V_** .
