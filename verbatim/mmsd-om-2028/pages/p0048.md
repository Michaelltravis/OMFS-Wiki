###### 1. APPROACH TO MANAGEMENT OF FACILITIES

<!-- ¶6 -->
Sustaining strong, long-term partnerships is at the heart of our client-for-life mindset. Effective management requires a dynamic approach—one that evolves alongside changing needs and goals. We leverage the full breadth of our integrated resources to deliver the high-quality, cost-effective performance that clients like MMSD expect. We are deeply committed to our clients, our staff, and the communities we serve. By building trust and staying attuned to the evolving priorities of each stakeholder, we maintain alignment that drives shared success.

<!-- ¶7 -->
###### **1.1. Key Focus Areas in Delivering Excellence**

<!-- ¶8 -->
Our approach to delivering exceptional results is anchored in five tenets that define how we operate, collaborate, and grow. The tenets, shown in **Exhibit IV-5** create a holistic framework that powers excellence, lasting partnerships, and meaningful impacts.

<!-- ¶9 -->
###### **1.2. PgM Framework is the Foundation of Our Shared Success**

<!-- ¶10 -->
**Delivering the scope of services for MMSD—spanning O&M of multiple large facilities, large-scale capital improvement coordination and support, system optimization, regulatory compliance, and long-term environmental stewardship—demands a strategy that goes beyond the current approach.** MMSD’s portfolio is among the most complex and interconnected in the country. Decisions in one area influence operations, customer service, maintenance, and community outcomes across the entire service territory.

<!-- ¶11 -->
**To meet this level of complexity, innovative and integrated PgM is essential.** Our methodology is built for environments like MMSD’s, where success depends on aligning diverse stakeholders, managing interdependent workstreams, and maintaining transparency across broad and evolving priorities. By applying a program-wide lens, we create the structure, governance, and collaborative culture needed to deliver consistent, predictable, and high value outcomes that support our shared mission of protecting public health and the environment.

<!-- ¶12 -->
Our PgM approach is built on collaboration, transparency, and accountability and forms the foundation for true stakeholder alignment, as illustrated in **Exhibit IV-6** . This framework integrates proven principles, platforms, procedures, processes, and tools—each refined through a comprehensive readiness assessment during the pre-term contract phase, including team chartering with MMSD and Jacobs’ leadership. The chartering process establishes a clear project vision, goals, and objectives, which shape the governance model—the central structure that drives a collaborative program organization and a transparent culture.

<!-- ¶13 -->
By viewing contract execution through a PgM lens, we strengthen collaboration and eliminate common disconnects across capital development, customer service, operations, and maintenance. Together, this approach ensures alignment, reinforces a culture of openness, and delivers successful outcomes for all stakeholders.
