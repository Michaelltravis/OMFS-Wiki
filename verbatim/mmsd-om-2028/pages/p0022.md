###### **3.1. Process for Transitioning Unionized Employees**

<!-- ¶4 -->
- Develop a competitive benefits and compensation package that complies with negotiated CBA terms and that the unions will support. Negotiate change in health benefits, retirement, and other aspects of the CBA with the unions to confirm support for changes.

<!-- ¶5 -->
- Continuously communicate with union representatives and MMSD to encourage partnership and early issue resolution.

<!-- ¶6 -->
- Consult with management, HR, and the union to help oversee changes.

<!-- ¶7 -->
###### **3.2. Integrating Union Workforce to Build a Positive Culture from Day 1**

<!-- ¶8 -->
We’ll communicate with potential employees through the union about our hiring plan, open positions, application procedures, and interview process. Early in the process, our benefits specialists will host workshops to explain our program and answer questions from employees or their family members. Our process for hiring represented employees is outlined below:

<!-- ¶9 -->
- Notify union of contract start date and our intent to retain represented employees.

<!-- ¶10 -->
- Request represented employees contact information, classifications, seniority, and training.

<!-- ¶11 -->
- Request the current union benefits package.

<!-- ¶12 -->
- Hold union-specific workshops to introduce our management team and answer questions.

<!-- ¶13 -->
- Notify the union weekly about the hiring process, including the numbers interviewed and the numbers, names, and classifications selected.

<!-- ¶14 -->
- Following contract award, enter into a successor agreement with the union.

<!-- ¶15 -->
We’ll schedule periodic labor/management meetings to increase communication, improve safety and productivity, and create a partnering relationship. **Twice yearly, we’ll host a roundtable with MMSD and union representatives to review project performance.** ( **See details on our robust employee transition approach built on decades of experience in** **_Section IV.B. Pre-term Transition Activities_** .)

###### **3.3. Building the Pipeline with Apprenticeships and Union Partnerships**

<!-- ¶21 -->
We see an immense opportunity to build apprenticeship programs customized to MMSD’s needs and focused on recruiting to attract and build an industry labor pipeline. Our plans include:

<!-- ¶22 -->
- Continue to foster union partnerships we’ve formed to engage people from all workforce demographics.

<!-- ¶23 -->
- Help connect unions to students at the high school and trade school levels, help articulate what membership in a union could do for careers, and encourage students to consider a career path in the trades needed at MMSD’s facilities.

<!-- ¶24 -->
- Partner with local area chambers of commerce and participate in tradefocused high school technical programs and job fairs.

<!-- ¶25 -->
- Expand on our internal training programs to include the MMSD-custom apprenticeship model.
