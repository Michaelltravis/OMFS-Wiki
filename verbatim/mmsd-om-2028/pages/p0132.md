###### 3. CURRENT PdM AT MMSD

<!-- ¶4 -->
MMSD already performs important PdM activities, but they remain siloed across multiple vendor platforms. Vibration and oil analyses cover more than 200 assets, while OEM systems for equipment such as Solar Turbines, Caterpillar engines, and Bentley aeration provide additional diagnostic insights. Although these datasets contain valuable information, they are not consistently integrated into maintenance decision-making. Reports may not be reviewed in real time, and corrective work is not always tied to specific assets within the CMMS.

<!-- ¶5 -->
Today, many maintenance activities are recorded at higher-level locations rather than at the individual asset or component level. This limits MMSD’s ability to correlate PdM findings with past work, trend condition data over time, or identify systemic degradation patterns. A key benefit of the Level 3 PdM program is the opportunity to strengthen asset-level documentation within NEXGEN, improving the visibility, traceability, and long-term value of PdM insights.

<!-- ¶6 -->
Jacobs’ Level 3 PdM program addresses these challenges directly by consolidating all available PdM inputs, strengthening data discipline, and making insights visible and actionable through NEXGEN.

<!-- ¶7 -->
###### 4. JACOBS’ RECOMMENDED LEVEL 3 PdM PLAN

<!-- ¶8 -->
Jacobs recommends a suite of proven, cost-effective PdM technologies that complement MMSD’s existing systems and provide actionable, real-time insights for the treatment facilities and conveyance system assets.

<!-- ¶9 -->
###### **4.1. Proposed PdM Technologies for MMSD’s Treatment Facilities**

<!-- ¶10 -->
Illustrated in **Exhibit IV-60** , our recommended Level 3 PdM technologies for the WRFs include:

<!-- ¶11 -->
- **Continuous vibration and temperature monitoring (VibeCloud or Augury)** will provide continuous insight into the mechanical condition of rotating equipment by measuring changes in vibration amplitude, frequency signatures, and thermal behavior. These sensors detect early signs of imbalance, misalignment, bearing wear, lubrication issues, and electrical or mechanical stress long before they affect performance. Continuous monitoring offers a far richer understanding of equipment behavior than periodic handheld readings and enables automated diagnostics to identify trends, anomalies, and emerging failure modes under real operating conditions. When integrated with SCADA and NEXGEN CMMS, vibration and temperature monitoring systems can generate condition-based alerts and work orders, strengthen asset health scoring, and support a proactive, PdM strategy that improves reliability and reduces unplanned downtime.

<!-- ¶12 -->
- **Artesis – motor current signature analysis (MCSA) and sensorless pump performance analytics** will deliver high-resolution electrical signature analysis that detects mechanical, electrical, and power-quality–related degradation within motors and pump systems. This MCSA approach identifies bearing issues, rotor defects, stator problems, and load-related inefficiencies long before they manifest as operational failures. Artesis also offers a sensorless pump performance capability that compares real-time operating conditions to ideal pump curves, enabling utilities to identify hydraulic inefficiencies and energy waste. These insights can be integrated with NEXGEN to automatically generate investigative work orders when deviations occur. We would work collaboratively with MMSD to prioritize the most critical assets where Artesis would provide the greatest value based on asset condition and risk scoring.
