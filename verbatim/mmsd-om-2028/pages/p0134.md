###### **4.2. Proposed PdM Technologies for MMSD’s Conveyance System**

<!-- ¶4 -->
###### **4.3. Additional PdM Technologies Included in Our Base Fee**

<!-- ¶5 -->
**AquaDNA Deragger** is an intelligent pump optimization technology that actively prevents ragging and blockage events by automatically adjusting pump operation to clear debris before it accumulates. Installed at high-consequence conveyance pump stations, the Deragger improves hydraulic performance, reduces pump starts and energy use associated with rag-induced inefficiency, and minimizes emergency callouts and maintenance interventions. For MMSD, Jacobs proposes deploying AquaDNA Deraggers at two jointly selected pump stations as a proof of concept, targeting locations with a history of clogging or elevated operational risk. Integrating this technology into the Level 3 PdM framework allows pump performance anomalies to be detected earlier and addressed proactively, enhancing reliability across the conveyance system. See **Exhibit IV-62** for an example dashboard.

<!-- ¶6 -->
Beyond the recommended Level 3 PdM technologies listed above, we’ve included two Level 3 PdM technologies as part of our Base Fee. We intend to implement them as part of our value-added innovations discussed above in **Exhibit IV-52** under **_Section 5.3 Value-Added Improvements and Innovations Included as Part of the Base Fee._** These two technologies will support more cost-effective maintenance of the conveyance system and include:

<!-- ¶7 -->
- **InfoSense SL-RAT** uses acoustics, rather than CCTV or cleaning, to rapidly assess blockages in gravity sewer lines. A transmitter and receiver placed at adjacent manholes measure sound transmission to determine whether the pipe segment is clear, partially obstructed, or likely blocked. The tool requires only two operators, minimal setup time, and no confined-space entry. SL-RAT helps MMSD prioritize cleaning, reduce unnecessary Vactor truck deployments, and lower overall conveyance maintenance costs.
