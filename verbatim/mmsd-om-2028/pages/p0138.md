## STATEMENT ON REGIONAL PARTNERSHIP

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **Jacobs looks forward to collaborating with MMSD to develop creative regional partnership strategies that will foster community trust and provide economic growth opportunities for local and regional residents and businesses.**

<!-- ¶4 -->
S This O&M contract represents far more than the operation and maintenance of MMSD’s sewerage infrastructure. It is a long-term commitment by Jacobs and our entire proposed team to serve as a fully integrated partner—one aligned with MMSD’s culture, priorities, and mission. Together, we are dedicated to protecting public health and safety, prioritizing the communities we serve, and preserving and enhancing the environment for future generations.

<!-- ¶5 -->
Jacobs’ regional partnership strategies will contribute to equity, sustainability, and economic growth for MMSD’s service area and regional communities. We’ll support and complement the outreach and regional initiatives that MMSD is currently spearheading (community engagement, workforce development, small business and supplier development, and research and development). While we view these activities as critical, we’ve also proposed above and beyond ideas that will catalyze partnerships and produce measurable regional benefits.
