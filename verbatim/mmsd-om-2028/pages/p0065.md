**Reliable Milorganite Production with Strategic Risk Management.** To help meet Milorganite production and distribution goals, we intend to collaborate with MMSD to evaluate the following opportunities to make production more reliable and consistent. To minimize risk to product quality, we’ll work closely with MMSD compliance and Milorganite marketing staff to plan and execute any proposed changes in a stepwise manner while carefully monitoring any changes.

###### **Collecting Chaff from Milorganite Production in Specialized Dumpsters for**

<!-- ¶8 -->
- **Develop detailed and effective drying and dewatering SOPs and train operators in their execution to** mitigate the risk of producing Milorganite that is not compliant with the specifications. For example, operators must be trained on the new, more robust sieve analysis.

<!-- ¶9 -->
**Disposal in a Landfill.** We’ll work with MMSD to investigate options to minimize chaff production, which would lower disposal costs. For transport and disposal, chaff could be hauled in standard vehicles or placed in roll-off boxes using disposable filter “socks” (the same type planned for other wastes). The sock is attached to the box and filled through an extended neck that keeps the dusty material contained. The roll-off is then hauled to the landfill, where the entire filled sock is tipped and disposed. This approach helps control dust during loading, transport, and unloading.

<!-- ¶10 -->
- **Carefully monitor silo storage capacity and work closely with MMSD and its packaging contractor to mitigate the risk of reaching silo storage capacity** during low agricultural demand seasons, ensuring dryer production does not have to be curtailed.

<!-- ¶11 -->
- **Provide effective training and SOPs for staff on properly staging the product for distribution and delivering the product to the packaging facility** so they adhere to the requirements described in the RFP.

<!-- ¶12 -->
**Maintaining Clean Facilities** . The D&D and silo facilities are difficult to keep clean because of dust and fine particles that escape during drying, processing, and storage. We’ll develop detailed, OSHA-compliant SOPs so cleaning is performed consistently and effectively in accordance with housekeeping to manage dust as required in the Scope of Services. The D&D dust control system is aging, and leaks and plugging can worsen dust buildup. We understand MMSD has ongoing and planned capital improvements. If those upgrades are not in place at contract start, Jacobs will evaluate system performance and recommend practical, low-cost actions to reduce dust, such as rebalancing or adjusting airflow rates.
