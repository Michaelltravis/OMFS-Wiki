## STATEMENT ON STAFFING, TRAINING, AND CORPORATE CULTURE

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### A. Staffing Strategy and Key Positions

<!-- ¶4 -->
Our conversations with MMSD staff, stakeholders, and union leadership over the past 2 years have given us a clear understanding of your operations and the condition of your facilities, as well as your expectations for a partnership throughout the contract. Beyond simply meeting permits and maintaining system reliability, you’re seeking a partner that will lead by energizing the workforce, strengthening their sense of belonging, and advancing MMSD’s long-term goals through the focused engagement of a high-performing, collaborative team. We’ve created our staffing approach to meet those needs. It’s designed to deliver real, practical solutions to the staffing, training, cultural, and labor relations challenges you face—and to assure MMSD of the leadership, workforce stability, and operational support you’ll require for long-term success.

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### 1. ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE

<!-- ¶6 -->
Jacobs’ organizational structure, shown on **Exhibit II-2,** illustrates our staffing plan that complies with MMSD’s Exhibit E and the pivotal roles we’ve added, which engages some of our top caliber staff to deliver transformative services and outstanding performance. Each leadership position is supported by a team to fulfill the requirements of the contract, and includes staff from within Jacobs, the current O&M employees who will transition to the Jacobs’ team, our subcontractors, and our workforce development, internship, and apprenticeship programs, as presented in **Exhibit II-3** . Staff that represent positions Above and Beyond (AB) the required minimum staffing requirements are denoted on **Exhibits II-2** and **II-3** with the AB icons. **Staff resumes are included at the end of this section on page II-20** .

<!-- ¶7 -->
Transparency is a cornerstone of our approach. From staffing dashboards to key performance indicators (KPIs) and reporting on maintenance performance, we’ll provide clear, consistent visibility into your operations. This openness will build trust, accelerate decision making, and make sure there are no surprises. Through decades of experience managing utility facilities, including nearly 200 current contracts, and augmented by more than 75 years in wastewater engineering, we understand the responsibility that comes with managing critical public infrastructure. **Our commitment is straightforward: deliver exceptional service from Day 1, while strengthening the workforce, systems, and culture that make MMSD a nationally respected utility.** With a dedicated local Human Resources (HR) team, deep union partnerships, culture of belonging, and robust operations and maintenance (O&M) and consulting bench, we’ll stabilize staffing, accelerate hiring, optimize operations, and build a workforce pipeline through apprenticeships, internships, and career pathways.
