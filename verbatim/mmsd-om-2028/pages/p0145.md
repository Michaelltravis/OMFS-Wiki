###### A. Exceptions

<!-- ¶4 -->
Jacobs proposes the following alternatives from the terms and conditions for MMSD’s consideration, each of which we believe offers MMSD a more advantageous and balanced contractual arrangement. In alignment with MMSD’s response to question 37, Jacobs anticipates future discussions with MMSD to address potential areas requiring clarification as we refine the final agreement. In addition, Jacobs would like to underscore two items found at the bottom of the following table that are of particular importance to our team. They are key considerations that we believe are essential to establishing a successful, long-term partnership and that we have intentionally highlighted within our proposal.
