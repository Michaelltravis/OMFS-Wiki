###### B.  Pre-term Activities

<!-- ¶7 -->
###### 1. TRANSITION PLAN WITH APPROACH AND TIMELINE OF ACTIVITIES NECESSARY TO ACHIEVE THE COMMENCEMENT DATE

<!-- ¶8 -->
We recognize that transitioning to a new operator is a significant decision, and it’s natural to have concerns—whether about potential impacts on service levels or the effect on the dedicated staff who keep your facilities running. We’ve addressed these considerations throughout our proposal so MMSD can weigh them against the many benefits that Jacobs’ fresh perspective, proven processes, and commitment to people bring to MMSD and its customers. Jacobs is sensitive to the perception that risk is introduced during a transition, and we dedicate substantial resources to minimize impacts and respond to any challenge that may arise.

<!-- ¶9 -->
**We also encourage MMSD to speak directly with clients who have selected Jacobs as their operating partner** . Their firsthand experience with our transition process, and the strong outcomes that followed, provides valuable insight into what partnering with Jacobs truly looks like.

<!-- ¶10 -->
###### **1.1. Continuity: Enabling Uninterrupted Excellence in Water Services**

<!-- ¶11 -->
**With Jacobs, transition is more than a handoff—it’s the moment we begin building the foundation that will take your best-in-class utility to the next level. As we assume operational responsibility, we collaborate with MMSD to ensure every step aligns with MMSD’s priorities, values, and long-term vision. At the same time, we strengthen processes, elevate workforce capabilities, and introduce tools and practices that empower MMSD and our joint staff for sustained success well beyond Day 1.**
