###### <mark>6. Organization & People</mark>

<!-- ¶4 -->
**Staff for outcomes:** Provide a dedicated **Planner/Scheduler** , cross-trained maintenance crews, and **CMMS Champions** on each crew to coach mobile use and data quality. Provide targeted training (CMMS, PMO methods, RCA, electrical/ mechanical specialties) and embed **Jacobs’ housekeeping standards** as preventive tasks. Align warehouse roles to planning (kitting, long-lead tracking, parts QA), so equipment, parts, and materials are ready for scheduled tasks.”

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### <mark>7. Risk Management</mark>

<!-- ¶6 -->
**Use a common consequence-by-likelihood matrix for WRF and conveyance to drive prioritization** . Apply failure mode libraries and vulnerability scenarios (e.g., wet-weather pumping, tunnel access, critical MCCs, high-risk interceptors) to pre-plan contingencies and spares strategies. Risk reports are reviewed in Governance and feed the Capital Improvement Project (CIP) candidate list.

###### <mark>8. Review & Continual Improvement</mark>

<!-- ¶12 -->
**Institutionalize learning loops.** Hold monthly KPI reviews with the Maintenance Committee, quarterly PMO checkpoints, annual internal audits by dedicated technical experts, and after-action reviews for major events. All process changes (task edits, new PdM routes, priority rules) follow change control through the committees so improvements stick.

<!-- ¶13 -->
###### <mark>9. Performance Management</mark>

<!-- ¶14 -->
**MMSD will have live access to dashboards** showing **PM compliance** , **CM backlog and aging** , mean time between failures/mean time to repair ( **MTBF/MTTR)** , **asset availability** , **labor/parts cost** , and **evidence lag (time to close with proof)** . We’ll align thresholds to Standards of Performance and publish exception lists with owners and due dates.

<!-- ¶15 -->
###### <mark>10. Values & Outcomes</mark>

<!-- ¶16 -->
**The AMS converts field work into measurable results:** higher uptime, fewer repeats, safer plants and tunnels, defensible budgeting, and risk-ranked reinvestment. The same data stream builds **obsolescence roadmaps** and **CIP business cases,** so MMSD invests where **reliability per dollar** is highest.
