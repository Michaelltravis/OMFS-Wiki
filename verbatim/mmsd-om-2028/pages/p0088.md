###### **2.7.5. Holistic, Systems-Based Approach**

<!-- ¶12 -->
MMSD’s wastewater system functions as a single, highly integrated network in which conveyance, the ISS, and the JIWRF and SSWRF operate together to achieve permit compliance. Jacobs’ **One Water, One Team** approach ( **Exhibit IV-40** ) reflects this reality by promoting unified operational control, coordinated decision-making, and clear communication across the entire system. By managing the system as one interconnected process, staff can anticipate system interactions, respond proactively to changing conditions, and maintain consistent environmental performance.
