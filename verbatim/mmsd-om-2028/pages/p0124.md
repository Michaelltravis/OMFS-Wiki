###### **1.3.4. Program Management Office (PMO) Role**

<!-- ¶6 -->
Supporting the Transition Leadership Team, Jacobs’ PMO will provide oversight, structure, and coordination needed to manage all transition workstreams. The PMO will track deliverables, manage schedules, monitor risks, and ensure seamless integration between staffing, systems, training, and governance activities. It will maintain the master transition schedule, facilitate reporting dashboards, and prepare weekly updates and performance summaries for MMSD and the Transition Leadership Team.

<!-- ¶7 -->
By centralizing progress tracking and documentation, the PMO ensures full transparency, disciplined execution, and seamless alignment with MMSD’s governance framework and communication expectations.

###### **1.3.5. Comprehensive Transition Support**

<!-- ¶9 -->
As depicted in our transition team organization chart, Jacobs’ transition team includes specialized experts across all key functions. These professionals bring a disciplined yet people-centered approach to mobilization and startup. During the transition period, this team will work side-by-side with MMSD staff to ensure a smooth and efficient handoff. Their focus includes:

<!-- ¶10 -->
- **Leading the implementation of Jacobs’ systems, tools, and standard processes** while maintaining focus on uninterrupted day-to-day operations.

<!-- ¶11 -->
- **Coordinating onboarding and training programs** that introduce employees to Jacobs’ culture, safety principles, and operational excellence standards.

<!-- ¶12 -->
- **Integrating asset and data management systems** to align with MMSD’s CMMS, SCADA, and reporting requirements.

<!-- ¶13 -->
- **Providing continuity and follow-through beyond the initial handover** , remaining available to MMSD for ongoing support, optimization, and knowledge sharing.

<!-- ¶14 -->
This hands-on leadership approach ensures that MMSD’s transition is guided by seasoned professionals who understand both the human and technical dimensions of change. By maintaining a dedicated presence throughout the process, Jacobs reduces risk, accelerates integration, and delivers a transparent and collaborative transition that reflects the professionalism and partnership MMSD expects.

###### **1.4. Transition Training Period**

<!-- ¶18 -->
Training is a critical bridge between mobilization and long-term operational excellence. During the transition period, Jacobs’ O&M staff will complete structured onboarding and hands-on familiarization across MMSD facilities, including the WRFs, collection system, tunnels, and pumping systems. Our objective is to ensure all employees, both transitioning and new, are fully prepared, credentialed, and confident before the March 1, 2028, Operational Start Date.

<!-- ¶19 -->
Following onboarding, Jacobs personnel will shadow MMSD staff to capture institutional knowledge and gain firsthand understanding of process operations, data and reporting protocols, performance metrics, CMMS workflows, asset management practices, and Milorganite production. Training will cover biosolids blending, dewatering, drying, product classification, and silo storage, with a consistent focus on safety, reliability, and product quality.

<!-- ¶20 -->
Jacobs and trusted partners such as Mead & Hunt will provide collection system expertise and work closely with MMSD’s laboratory staff to align quality systems and QA/QC procedures. All staff will complete required safety and operational training prior to startup, and role-specific development needs identified during transition will be incorporated into ongoing training plan.
