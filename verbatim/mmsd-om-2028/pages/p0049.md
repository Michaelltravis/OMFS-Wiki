###### **1.2.2. PgM-Driven Cost Control, Monitoring, Communications, and Reporting Tools for Transparency and Accountability**

<!-- ¶8 -->
Jacobs’ PgM framework will be the backbone for how we **monitor, control, and communicate operational performance and costs** across MMSD’s operations. In partnership with MMSD, we’ll **align operational monitoring to RFP Exhibit C Standards of Performance** , jointly define key performance indicators (KPIs), and use integrated data from NEXTGEN, SCADA, CMMS, Hach WIMS/LIMS, and financial systems to produce **one validated, single source of truth.** We’ll provide MMSD with real-time dashboards, automated exception tracking, and structured monthly/quarterly reviews that will drive corrective actions and continuous improvement.

<!-- ¶9 -->
**Quitterie Cotten** , who offers 28 years of wastewater experience, will help Keoki implement this program. See **_Section II – Statement on Staffing, Training, and Corporate Culture_** for more information on our Program Management team structure.

<!-- ¶10 -->
A critical PgM outcome is **shared-cost transparency and reporting** . Our framework establishes **complete, auditable shared-cost controls** for energy, chemicals, fuel, and biosolids handling—linking consumption and performance drivers directly to invoicing, inventory, and operational conditions. The system will **automatically flag KPI deviations** , maintain a **shared corrective-action log visible to MMSD** , and trigger **immediate notifications for any condition that could impact permit compliance** , followed by written corrective summaries. PgM-led reporting will include **daily operations logs** , **monthly performance and reconciled shared-cost reports** , **quarterly and annual committee-ready summaries** , and an **Annual Performance and Optimization Report.**
