###### **5.4 Additional Improvements and Innovations for Future Savings**

<!-- ¶2 -->
In addition to the offerings included in our Base Fee and summarized in **Exhibit IV-52** , Jacobs will partner with MMSD to explore more opportunities to improve and innovate across MMSD’s facilities with the goal of optimizing performance and further reducing shared O&M costs and risks. Our team’s operators and SMEs have brainstormed more than 25 ideas focused on process improvements, intelligent O&M optimizations and security/SCADA/OT improvements that we would like to develop in partnership with MMSD. **These insights are just our initial takeaway from a handful of visits over the past months. Once we're hands-on and embedded with your system, we'll keep coming up with fresh ideas to optimize it.** The ideas presented in **Exhibit IV-53** , have the potential for significant optimization and cost savings. **We believe these ideas can bring MMSD between $21M and $53M in additional value-adds and savings over the next 10 years.**
