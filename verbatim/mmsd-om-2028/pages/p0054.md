###### EXHIBIT IV-13. DECISION-MAKING HIERARCHY

<!-- ¶7 -->
###### **01 ONSITE PROCESS CONTROL AND OPERATIONAL AND MAINTENANCE DECISIONS**

<!-- ¶8 -->
Day to day process adjustments or maintenance decisions made by operators under the direction of Project Manager and onsite leadership **Decision Forum:** Daily or Weekly Meetings

<!-- ¶9 -->
This includes decisions guided by real-time data from SCADA, laboratory, and instrumentation systems. They are verified against established SOPs and compliance requirements such as the governing permits and the stipulated standards of performance. Key operating parameters including aeration, flow distribution, chemical dosing, and energy use are continuously monitored through dashboards, trend analysis, and performance alerts to support timely, defensible decisions.

<!-- ¶10 -->
Maintenance decisions impacting operations are guided by PM and PdM data, complemented by real-time insights from our team monitoring equipment daily. This approach helps identify factors that could compromise data accuracy, such as faulty or uncalibrated instruments. All staff are empowered to share observations during weekly meetings or as needed, enabling timely decisions or escalation to MMSD when broader implications arise.

<!-- ¶11 -->
###### **02 COLLABORATIVE OVERSIGHT AND OPTIMIZATION**

<!-- ¶12 -->
Operational decisions with broader implications evaluated collaboratively through regular meetings and discussions with subject matter experts and MMSD

<!-- ¶13 -->
**Decision Forum:** Operations and Maintenance Committees

<!-- ¶14 -->
###### **03 STRATEGIC AND CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT**

<!-- ¶15 -->
Longer term or cross functional decisions evaluated by Jacobs SMEs and analytics specialist, discussed and reviewed with MMSD **Decision Forum:** Executive Committee

<!-- ¶16 -->
This includes energy management strategies, wet weather response, tunnel operations, and Milorganite production. These decisions are data-driven, have shared accountability, focus on system improvements, and ensure alignment with MMSD’s financial, environmental, and reliability goals.

<!-- ¶17 -->
Operational and maintenance performance metrics are reviewed monthly with MMSD to confirm alignment, document decision rationale, and evaluate outcomes. Jacobs’ operations, maintenance, and technical leadership will work directly with MMSD, ensuring coordinated decision-making and consistent application of agreed strategies.

<!-- ¶18 -->
This includes decisions such as process optimization, resource recovery initiatives, or technology implementation. SMEs review data trends, conduct root cause and cost-benefit analyses, and provide recommendations that are formally presented through the Annual Innovation Workshop and, as appropriate, the Executive Committee defined in Exhibit P.

<!-- ¶19 -->
Strategic recommendations are grounded in predictive analytics, regulatory outlooks, and life-cycle cost assessments to ensure sustainable, long-term value for MMSD. They ensure operational and investment decisions reflect MMSD’s commitment to stewardship of public funds.
