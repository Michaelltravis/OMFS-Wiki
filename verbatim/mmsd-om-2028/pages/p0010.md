###### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### **Wet Weather Flow Management to Support MMSD’s Goal for Zero Overflows**

<!-- ¶3 -->
We’ll apply predictive forecasting, coordinated tunnel and WRF operations, and disciplined decision-making to prevent overflows and basement backups. Our approach emphasizes proactive readiness, resilient wet weather response, and close coordination with MMSD management. **Susan Moisio, PE as Wet Weather and Conveyance Director,** will direct our conveyance experts to help MMSD innovate and address current and future challenges. We’ve also proposed deploying digital tools which provide a real-time view of the network for improved performance.

<!-- ¶4 -->
###### **Optimization that Delivers Value for MMSD’s Budget**

<!-- ¶5 -->
**Leveraging our intelligent O&M tools, we have already identified reductions of 20-30% in power and chemical use compared to prior years—efficiencies that are directly reflected in our price.** We’ll continue to target additional optimization opportunities in MMSD’s highest shared cost areas, including power, chemicals, fuel, and biosolids handling, using data analytics, SCADA insights, process modeling, and targeted pilots. Our digital tools that use data science and machine learning have helped achieve 20–30% chemical and power savings for existing O&M clients, such as Wilmington, DE, and West Basin, CA. For example, in taking over the West Basin operations (the largest reuse facility in North America) from your current service provider, we reduced chemical consumption by 15-40% and at Wilmington by 23%. Ultimately, our focus is to continuously optimize cost and risk, while sustaining compliance and performance.

<!-- ¶6 -->
###### **Milorganite Production: Precision, Continuity, and Brand Protection**

<!-- ¶7 -->
Milorganite requires precise coordination across multiple processes and an experienced workforce. Our transition and operating approach preserves institutional knowledge and strengthens best practices, safety systems, and process controls to protect continuity and product quality. We’ll track the operating parameters that matter—feeds, dewatering, polymer, dryer energy, and production output—so trend data confirm compliance and reveal efficiency opportunities.

<!-- ¶8 -->
###### **Odor Control: Prevention, Early Warning, and Documented Response**

<!-- ¶9 -->
We’ll take a proactive and community-sensitive odor approach that reduces the likelihood of offsite impacts and improves responsiveness—investing in an early warning system with predictive indicators and models to anticipate elevated-risk conditions. If a complaint occurs, we’ll follow a consistent, documented protocol: log, investigate, measure, diagnose root cause, implement and communicate corrective action, and capture lessons learned to prevent recurrence.

<!-- ¶10 -->
###### ACCESS TO A FULL BREADTH OF TECHNICAL EXPERTISE TO STRENGTHEN OPERATIONS

<!-- ¶11 -->
By leveraging Jacobs’ vast O&M and technical experts, our delivery model provides both the cost effectiveness and agility of a dedicated local team and the depth of a global enterprise—ensuring continuity, redundancy, and innovation. Through this structure, MMSD gains a high-performing operations partner committed to delivering reliability, operational excellence, and continuous improvement throughout the agreement. **A proof of our commitment is our Annual Innovation Workshop where we bring our industry SMEs directly to your team** to share best practices and new technologies, explore future improvement initiatives and **proactively combat operator complacency over time.**
