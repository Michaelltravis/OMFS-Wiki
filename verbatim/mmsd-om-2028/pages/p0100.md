Our CIP process focuses on three improvement and investment streams:

<!-- ¶6 -->
Working with MMSD project managers and third-party engineers, our Capital Project Coordinators will:

<!-- ¶7 -->
- **Asset Expansion:** Upgrading, creating, or purchasing new assets based on growth or elevated service levels.

<!-- ¶8 -->
- Support the use of the AMS to identify common assets or adjacent assets that will likely need replacement shortly after a planned capital improvement, especially where there is the potential to save money in the long run.

<!-- ¶9 -->
- **Asset Renewal:** Upgrading existing assets to maintain required service levels and manage risk.

<!-- ¶10 -->
- **Strategic Investment:** Providing financial returns, resiliency, or future opportunities.

<!-- ¶11 -->
New-build CIP initiatives focus on bridging service level gaps and accommodating growth, while ensuring investments consider full lifecycle costs and MMSD’s funding capabilities for operations, maintenance, and asset renewal. Our asset renewal forecasting approach incorporates historic data analysis, lifecycle estimates, predictive modeling, and direct asset observation to prioritize funding effectively. Maintenance data will flow into risk ranking and obsolescence tracking to identify assets approaching end-of-life or due for upgrade. The output is a living reinvestment list ranked by risk, consequence, and lifecycle costs so MMSD can allocate funds where reliability per dollar is maximized.
