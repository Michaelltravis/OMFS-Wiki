###### 2. MAINTAINING A RESPECTFUL WORKPLACE OF INCLUSION AND BELONGING

<!-- ¶12 -->
Inclusion is about tangible action that drives meaningful, measurable change, both in our company and in the communities we serve. It means creating a workplace where our people are curious, embrace different perspectives, and harness new ideas to bring innovative solutions our clients expect. **Exhibit II-14** describes our peer-to-peer culture coach program, designed to make employees feel valued and included.

<!-- ¶13 -->
**TogetherBeyond℠** is our company-wide approach to drive measurable outcomes for our teams and the communities we serve and reinforcing staff retention. Our leadership champions inclusion by annually re-signing a Leadership Commitment Statement that is embedded in performance systems. Jacobs’ employees, including the MMSD O&M team, can connect and grow through more than 260 Communities of Practice and eight Jacobs Employee Networks (JENs), shown in **Exhibit II-15** .

<!-- ¶14 -->
###### EXHIBIT II-15. <mark>FIND YOUR COMMUNITY</mark>

<!-- ¶15 -->
In FY25, we launched “Find Your Community” to increase visibility, connectivity, and impact across Jacobs’ rich mix of community offerings, including JENs, Communities of Practice, Positive Mental Health Champions, STEAM Ambassadors, Collectively Ambassadors, Sustainability Network, Evolve Champions, and GlobeSmart Community. In FY25, 54% of our employees were members of one or more Communities of Practice and 40% of were members of one or more JEN.
