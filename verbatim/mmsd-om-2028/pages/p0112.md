###### **5.3. Value-Added Improvements and Innovations Included as Part of the Base Fee**

<!-- ¶9 -->
Jacobs is committed to delivering meaningful, measurable improvements from the start of the contract. As part of our Base Fee, we are investing, at **no additional cost to MMSD, a suite of value-added enhancements that directly strengthen day-to-day operations, improve system reliability, and reduce costs.** We developed these offerings through site visits, data review, and discussions with MMSD staff, along with ideas from Jacobs operators, maintenance experts,

<!-- ¶10 -->
digital specialists, and process SMEs. Together, they reflect our commitment to a performance-driven O&M partnership that will deliver both immediate and long-term operational benefits to MMSD.

<!-- ¶11 -->
These value-added improvements involve onsite and offsite operational support, application of proven best practices, targeted operational and engineering studies, energy and chemical optimization pilots, solids and Milorganite process enhancements, odor and corrosion modeling, OT modernization and cybersecurity strengthening, and investments that improve system performance and operator effectiveness. **Many of these services—such as Intelligent O&M optimization, WATS modeling, solids-handling improvements, and OT configuration management—are typically delivered through stand-alone consulting engagements. By embedding them directly into our Base Fee, Jacobs provides MMSD with millions of dollars of added value,** accelerates efficiency gains, and gives operators practical tools and expert support that enhance performance at every step of the system.
