The value-added offerings we highlight in **Exhibit IV-52** are included in our Base Fee. The column labeled “10-Year Value of Investments” indicates the typical price of these services if they were provided separately from the O&M contract under a stand-alone engineering/consulting services contract. The column labeled “10-Year Value of Potential Savings” shows the expected financial benefit of these investments to MMSD where applicable. **Our intent is to demonstrate the breadth of additional capabilities and services our team will provide in support of MMSD’s objectives. They reflect our commitment to exceed your expectations.**

<!-- ¶2 -->
We’ll target completion of the investments within the first 24 months of the O&M contract. Items under “Studies” are one-time investments. All other investments represent the total value of annual, ongoing investments and savings over the 10-year contract term. During transition, we’ll collaborate with MMSD to identify your priorities and preferred timing for each of these investments.

<!-- ¶3 -->
**Together, these O&M-focused investments create a strong foundation for safer, more resilient, and more efficient operations— positioning MMSD to capture early wins, streamline ongoing performance, and advance MMSD’s 2035 Vision with confidence and measurable results.**
