###### 5. CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT AND INNOVATION

<!-- ¶2 -->
Jacobs brings a proven, research-driven approach to continuous improvement through innovation, tailored to MMSD’s evolving challenges. Through collaboration with MMSD and local partners, we’ll co-develop solutions that enhance compliance, resilience, and sustainability. Leveraging our operational expertise and global technology network, we’ll focus on continuous improvements, deliver innovations aligned with MMSD’s 2035 Vision, and ensure knowledge is shared and leveraged across all facets of the contract. Innovation has long been the driving force behind progress in municipal wastewater collection and treatment—making systems more effective, resilient, and sustainable. At Jacobs’, our role as facility operators, consultants, and constructors is deeply rooted in a commitment to innovation through applied research. This hands-on experience has been instrumental in helping our clients design, build, and operate more sustainable and cost-effective wastewater management systems. Our direct involvement in research enables us to develop new knowledge, test and pilot emerging technologies at facilities we operate such as Wilmington and inform policy and decision-making. Our research provides municipalities with the data and insights needed to choose the most effective and sustainable strategies.

<!-- ¶3 -->
Our approach to continuous improvement and to leveraging innovation during this contract is anchored by several strategic and practical reasons:

<!-- ¶4 -->
� **Aligning Innovation with MMSD’s Strategic Vision.** MMSD’s 2035 Vision emphasizes sustainability, decarbonization, and resilience. By working together with you, we can ensure that all operational improvements and innovations directly support these long-term goals. This alignment helps prioritize initiatives that deliver the greatest value to MMSD and its stakeholders.

<!-- ¶5 -->
- **Accelerating Problem-Solving and Adaptation.** MMSD faces evolving challenges, PFAS regulations, climate impacts, aging infrastructure, and workforce transitions. A collaborative innovation model between Jacobs and MMSD allows for rapid identification, piloting, and scaling of solutions tailored to MMSD’s unique context, especially at critical facilities like SSWRF.

<!-- ¶6 -->
- **Leveraging Complementary Expertise.** MMSD brings deep institutional knowledge and local insight. Jacobs contributes global research, technical depth, and operational experience. Together, this partnership fosters co-creation of solutions that are both technically sound and locally relevant.

<!-- ¶7 -->
- **Enhancing Transparency and Trust.** Our successful partnerships in other operations contracts highlight the need for a more integrated and communicative relationship. Joint innovation efforts build trust, improve transparency, and ensure that MMSD remains actively engaged in shaping operational strategies.

<!-- ¶8 -->
- **Ensuring Knowledge Transfer and Capacity Building.** Collaborative innovation supports shared learning, helping MMSD staff stay informed about emerging technologies and best practices, and in discerning how to properly consider them for the benefit of the partnership. It also ensures that innovations are not siloed but embedded into MMSD’s organizational knowledge and future planning.

<!-- ¶9 -->
###### **5.1. Jacobs Annual Innovation Workshop Drives Continuous Improvements and Elevated O&M**

<!-- ¶10 -->
**Our Annual Innovation Workshops, provided at no cost to MMSD** , use a proven innovation framework that brings together leading experts to share the most relevant, cutting-edge knowledge in the industry. A clear demonstration of the depth and relevance of our expertise is our unmatched participation in national knowledge sharing events such as the Water Environment Federation Technical Exhibition and Conference (WEFTEC), where Jacobs consistently contributes more technical content and thought leadership than any other O&M provider. For example, Jacobs had 27 papers at the 2024 WEFTEC where our major O&M competitors had none. In 2025, Jacobs presented more than 60 workshops and remained a long-time sponsor of the annual operations challenge, where top wastewater professionals demonstrate their skills in collections, laboratory work, process control, maintenance, and safety. This level of engagement underscores the breadth and relevance of the expertise we can bring.

<!-- ¶11 -->
Each year, we’ll convene MMSD staff, stakeholders, our onsite O&M team, and Jacobs’ utility experts to review the prior year’s achievements and co-develop new goals and innovations aligned with MMSD’s evolving needs and long-term vision. This forward-looking workshop will help ensure MMSD remains at the forefront of treatment technologies, optimization strategies, and modern O&M practices.

<!-- ¶12 -->
Jacobs will bring industry recognized leaders in process engineering, biosolids, hydraulics, wet weather flow management, pump station design and operations, digital optimization, and energy management. They are all hand selected to match MMSD’s current challenges and opportunities. Before the workshop, we’ll work closely with MMSD to co-develop a full day agenda that reflects your priorities and expectations, focusing the session on your desired outcomes. Once the agenda is set, our specialists will begin their preparation in advance, learning your facilities, analyzing performance data, and identifying improvement opportunities so they arrive equipped with realistic, actionable ideas tailored specifically to MMSD.
