Jacobs brings real-world experience and an unmatched track record of success with the most complex and high-profile transitions in the industry. Our proven transition model, which focuses on supporting existing employees, has consistently produced exceptional outcomes. **In 2025, four transitions from Veolia resulted in an overwhelming number of staff choosing to join Jacobs, as shown on Exhibit IV-55.** These transitions included the West Basin Municipal Water District, the largest water reuse facility in North America with 58 FTEs, and the City of Jackson’s wastewater system with 39 FTEs, which Veolia highlighted as a reference project during MMSD’s SOQ phase.

Our 200-point transition plan sequences critical activities-staffing, safety, SOP alignment, SCADA/IT readiness, asset-management integration, and other important activities well in advance of March 1, 2028. For MMSD, this translates into a structured, transparent, and proactive approach that ensures a seamless transfer of responsibility while safeguarding safety, compliance, and workforce stability.

**Jacobs recognizes that MMSD’s top priorities during transition are uninterrupted operations, full regulatory compliance, and staff continuity** . MMSD’s schedule reflects this commitment by allowing an unprecedented 18-month transition period, far exceeding the industry-standard 90 days. Combined with your procurement schedule and the extended shadowing period, this creates the conditions for an exceptionally smooth and low-risk transition.
