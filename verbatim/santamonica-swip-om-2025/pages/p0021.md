###### **2.3 REFERENCES**

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### **OPERATIONAL EXCELLENCE BACKED BY DIVERSE PROJECT EXPERIENCE**

<!-- ¶3 -->
Jacobs brings unmatched depth across water, wastewater, and advanced treatment facilities, demonstrated through a diverse portfolio of complex O&M projects. At West Basin’s Edward C. Little Water Recycling Facility, the nation’s largest and most technically sophisticated reuse system, Jacobs manages the O&M of nine advanced treatment trains that deliver five distinct grades of fit-forpurpose recycled water. For Soquel Creek Water District, Jacobs supported the design, construction, and startup, and will continue to partner with the District for long-term O&M of the Pure Water Soquel Advanced Water Purification facility, applying reverse osmosis, UV-AOP, and granular activated carbon filtration to safeguard groundwater and prevent seawater intrusion.

<!-- ¶4 -->
Jacobs’ experience also extends to industrial systems such as the Turlock Irrigation District Zero Liquid Discharge Facility, where Jacobs optimizes advanced RO, crystallizers, and exchangers to support a 250 MW power plant. For the City of Clovis, Jacobs has provided long-term operations of its 2.8-mgd Water Reuse Facility since 2009, applying advanced membrane bioreactor, reduce costs, and reliably meet stringent Title 22 reuse and NPDES permit requirements.

<!-- ¶5 -->
Together, these projects illustrate Jacobs’ proven track record in optimizing treatment reliability, compliance, and sustainability, ensuring Santa Monica benefits from the collective strength of Jacobs’ national delivery network.
