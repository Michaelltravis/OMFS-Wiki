###### **SANTA MONICA’S VISION FOR SWIP AND DPR**

<!-- ¶3 -->
Jacobs understands the City’s integrated water-reuse system and what success will look like for Santa Monica. Sustainable Water Infrastructure Project (SWIP) centers on an underground Advanced Water Treatment Facility (AWTF) that treats blended sources (wastewater and stormwater) and supports the Groundwater Replenishment Reuse Project (GRRP) via injection wells SM-10i and SM-11i—while the Santa Monica Urban Runoff Recycling Facility (SMURRF), stormwater diversions/lift stations, and Ocean Avenue Pump Station round out the SWIP infrastructure portfolio. The City’s goals include: reliable Product Water to GRRP, seawaterintrusion protection, transparent key performance indicator (KPI) reporting, and a practical pathway to Direct Potable Reuse (DPR). Key challenges will include documenting SCADA/OT baselines, tightening alarm/remote response, sustaining wet-weather readiness, preserving SMURRF assets while upgrades proceed, and coordinating well performance monitoring as injection commences—all under Title 22/GRRP and State Board Orders.

<!-- ¶4 -->
Jacobs will operate the City’s underground AWTF and associated SWIP assets to reliably produce Product Water today while positioning Santa Monica to lead on DPR tomorrow. We will run the full screening/MBR → cartridge filter → RO → UV-AOP train to permit compliance, protect GRRP objectives, and maintain transparent performance trending for City leadership. Our team will operate to State Board Orders R4-2021-0044 and R4-2023-0366 and Title 22 GRRP requirements, consistent with the SWIP Engineering Report, so the City will advance aquifer replenishment, seawater-intrusion control, and long-term water independence.
