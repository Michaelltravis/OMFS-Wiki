###### **Turlock Zero Liquid Discharge Facility O&M**

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### TURLOCK, CA

<!-- ¶3 -->
Jacobs is providing O&M support for the 0.75-MGD Zero Liquid Discharge Facility for Turlock Irrigation District (TID)’s combined cycle 250 MW power plant.

<!-- ¶4 -->
###### **RELEVANT EXPERIENCE**

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### Detailed Description of the Facilities Operated

<!-- ¶6 -->
The TID industrial zero liquid discharge system includes micro filtration, sodium zeolite softeners, weak acid cation (WAC) exchange softeners, a de-carbonation unit, high efficiency RO systems, a demineralized RO system, mixed bed units, and crystallizer evaporators.

<!-- ¶7 -->
###### Challenge

<!-- ¶8 -->
TID invested in a zero-liquid discharge, RO water treatment system at its power plant in Turlock, CA. The facility treats process water from the cooling tower and supplies demineralized water to the steam generators. The water balance is crucial to proper operation of the power plant. The District needed a skilled O&M partner to operate and maintain the new system.

<!-- ¶9 -->
###### Solutions/Outcome

<!-- ¶10 -->
The District partnered with Jacobs as its contract O&M provider. Upon assuming O&M responsibility of the new facilities, we discovered operational challenges associated with the facility design and are helping resolve them while making the system run more efficiently and effectively. Programmable logic controllers (PLCs) and a human machine interface (HMI) computer system control the power plant’s water balance, and they require constant monitoring.
