###### **LABORATORY MANAGEMENT AND SAMPLING PLAN**

<!-- ¶3 -->
During our site visit, Jacobs observed that the onsite laboratory is in excellent condition and is a credit to the City’s commitment to water quality and operational excellence. We recognize that the primary role of this facility is to support process control and operational decision-making through in-house sampling and analysis. Regulatory compliance and reporting analyses will continue to be conducted by the City’s third-party certified laboratory contractor. Jacobs will align our laboratory management approach with this framework, ensuring that onsite analyses provide timely, accurate data for operations, while maintaining seamless coordination with the City’s external laboratory partners to meet all regulatory reporting requirements.

<!-- ¶4 -->
**Jacobs’ Sampling and Laboratory Testing Plan** will ensure all SWIP facilities meet applicable regulatory requirements while generating timely, accurate, and defensible data. Rooted in proven best practices and regulatory expectations, our plan includes EPA-compliant sampling protocols, rigorous QA/QC procedures, and regular training for laboratory and field personnel.

<!-- ¶5 -->
Jacobs will perform all in-house process control sampling and laboratory testing necessary to effectively manage facility performance. For regulatory compliance, Jacobs will collect required samples and deliver them to the City’s certified third-party contract laboratory in accordance with our rigorous sampling protocols.

<!-- ¶6 -->
Our Sampling and Laboratory Testing Plan will:

<!-- ¶7 -->
- µ<sup>**Maintain strict quality assurance/quality control (QA/QC)**</sup> **procedures** to ensure accuracy and consistency of results.

<!-- ¶8 -->
- µ<sup>**Follow chain-of-custody protocols**for all regulatory</sup> samples, providing clear documentation from collection through delivery to the contract laboratory.

<!-- ¶9 -->
- µ<sup>**Calibrate and maintain laboratory instruments**on a defined</sup> schedule to ensure precision and reliability.

<!-- ¶10 -->
- µ<sup>**Integrate laboratory results into the SCADA and CMMSfor**</sup> **real-time visibility** and long-term performance tracking.

<!-- ¶11 -->
- µ<sup>**Conduct routine data validation and trend analysis**to</sup> support operational decision-making and early identification of process issues.

<!-- ¶12 -->
- µ<sup>**Prepare and organize laboratory records**to support City</sup> reporting requirements, audits, and regulatory inspections.

<!-- ¶13 -->
- µ<sup>**Provide and maintain laboratory equipment**at all</sup> SWIP locations.

<!-- ¶14 -->
- µ<sup>**Perform all required sampling per the City’s permits**</sup> for the AWTF, SMURRF, and stormwater systems and send all regulatory-required analyses to the City’s third-party certified laboratory.

<!-- ¶15 -->
- µ<sup>**Conduct special sampling as needed for Title 22, potable**</sup> **reuse, or stormwater events** , with analysis performed by the City’s third-party certified laboratory as required.

<!-- ¶16 -->
- µ<sup>**Provide support for toxicity, geochemical, or microbial**</sup> **sampling** based on regulatory conditions.
