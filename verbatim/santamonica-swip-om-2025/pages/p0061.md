###### **COMMUNICATIONS AND REPORTING**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Jacobs is dedicated to open and transparent communication with the City, recognizing that this is key to a successful and collaborative partnership. Our communications plan will ensure the City remains fully informed, engaged, and confident in the management of the SWIP facilities—fostering trust, responsiveness, and mutual accountability. From day one, Jacobs will prioritize real-time visibility into plant performance, maintenance status, and regulatory compliance.

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **_Communication Plan_**

<!-- ¶4 -->
Starting at transition, Jacobs will collaborate with the City to establish clear communication protocols and engagement schedules. These will include informal daily check-ins to support responsive decision-making and issue resolution, along with formal weekly, monthly, and quarterly meetings to review performance, discuss maintenance schedules, and provide project updates, as illustrated in **Exhibit 2-26** .

###### **_Reporting and Transparency_**

<!-- ¶11 -->
Jacobs will deliver a comprehensive suite of reports to ensure transparency into operational performance and regulatory compliance, providing the City with tools to proactively monitor and guide SWIP facility operations. These reports will also support planning, budgeting, and community engagement.

<!-- ¶12 -->
Below is a summary of our key reporting deliverables:

<!-- ¶13 -->
- µ<sup>**Monthly Operating Report.**Delivered by the fifth day of</sup> each month, this report will summarize key operational data including flows, volumes, pressures, fouling, compliance status, staffing, safety metrics, maintenance activities, and notable events or alarms. It reinforces our commitment to visibility, accountability, and continual performance review.

<!-- ¶14 -->
- µ<sup>**Monthly Operations, Maintenance, and Asset**</sup> **Management Report.** Prepared by the CPO, this report will cover all core operational areas, including PM/PdM activities, asset performance, staff training, safety meetings, inspection findings, work order status, and permit compliance. It will ensure the City has a clear understanding of asset health and facility status.

<!-- ¶15 -->
- µ<sup>**Monthly Discharge Monitoring Report.**Jacobs will confirm</sup> timely submission of the SWIP DMR to the State Water Board through CIWQS/NetDMR. Any permit exceedances or compliance concerns will be communicated immediately, along with corrective actions and follow-up.

<!-- ¶16 -->
- µ<sup>**Annual Report.**Our annual report will be a comprehensive</sup> review of the previous year’s operations, including O&M performance evaluations, capital improvements, financial performance, and plans for the upcoming year. We will furnish the City a draft within 30 days and final within 60 days after completion of each calendar year of operations.
