<!-- ¶1 -->
###### **COMMUNITY INVOLVEMENT AND OUTREACH**

<!-- ¶2 -->
_Jacobs is committed to being an active and visible partner in the Santa Monica community. We understand that successful advanced water reuse projects are built not only on technical excellence, but also on public understanding, trust, and support. Our outreach will go beyond meeting contractual requirements — we will bring passion, creativity, and decades of leadership in water reuse education to engage the community._

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **BUILDING ON CURRENT MOMENTUM**

<!-- ¶4 -->
The current CPO at SWIP has already demonstrated the power of effective community engagement, providing tours that resonate with public and school groups. Jacobs will build on this strong foundation, supporting the continuation and expansion of existing outreach efforts. We will work closely with the City to align outreach with Santa Monica’s sustainability goals and to ensure consistent, positive messaging about water reuse.

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### **STRATEGIC IMPORTANCE OF OUTREACH**

<!-- ¶6 -->
Outreach and education are now considered industry standard at advanced reuse facilities, as we’ve seen demonstrated at West Basin, our most recent addition in California. As utilities move toward **IPR and DPR** , proactive public engagement is critical to building trust and acceptance. Our approach reflects this strategic importance: community education is not an optional add-on, but a core component of long-term project success.

<!-- ¶7 -->
###### **_Jacobs’ Leadership in Water Reuse Education_**

<!-- ¶8 -->
Jacobs has been at the forefront of water outreach for decades (see **Exhibit 2-4** ). We have helped shape industry best practices and consistently demonstrated that effective education programs build community trust and excitement around water reuse.

<!-- ¶9 -->
###### **_Commitment to Community_**

<!-- ¶10 -->
Jacobs is committed to being an active member of the communities we serve (see **Exhibit 2-29** ). Our engagement builds and supports sustainable communities through a wide variety of programs, including charitable contributions, volunteering, internships and mentoring partnerships, and educational outreach. These activities reflect Jacobs’ culture of caring and demonstrate our commitment to leaving a positive legacy wherever we work. For Santa Monica, we will build on this foundation by tailoring a comprehensive and customized community involvement program that reflects local values and priorities.
