All Schedule B activities are subject to prior written approval by the City. No work will be initiated, performed, or billed under Schedule B without the City’s authorization. This process ensures transparency, protects the City’s budget, and reinforces our commitment to collaborative decision making.

In the following, please find further details to each cost category shown in Schedule B, City Controlled Allowances:

<!-- ¶7 -->
1. **Repair Parts – Maintain Spare Parts Inventory –** Jacobs' maintenance engineers evaluated the construction drawings and developed an asset list with all the equipment, meters, and sensors that require corrective maintenance at the SWIP. They developed a corrective maintenance schedule based on projected asset life and the parts that will be required to maintain the facility for the next 5 years. The scope of this effort was filtered for costs less than $10,000 to be consistent with the City’s RFP requirements. The output of our Maintenance Model’s corrective maintenance cost is our best estimate of the Schedule B Item 1 – Repair Parts – Maintenance Spare Parts Inventory.

<!-- ¶8 -->
2. **In-House Lab Equipment and Supplies –** Jacobs laboratory and California regulatory subject matter experts reviewed the permit-required sampling, plant instrumentation, and laboratory equipment and developed the cost of reagents to keep the online analyzers properly calibrated and maintained. The number and type of process samples for onsite analysis was estimated, and a daily count of process samples was developed. The sample count for each was multiplied by the cost per sample analysis to develop the cost for process samples and online analyzers. This budget also includes costs for measurement items and noncapital laboratory equipment replacement that will occur throughout the contract period.
