<!-- ¶1 -->
###### **PROCESS CONTROL**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Jacobs will implement our Companywide Process Control System (CPCS)—a data- and results-driven framework that leverages modeling, analytics, and standardized controls to optimize treatment processes. CPCS reports are reviewed by onsite staff and Jacobs’ regional technical specialists to verify compliance and identify performance optimization opportunities.

<!-- ¶3 -->
Supporting the CPCS is a suite of proven tools based on decades of operational experience and industry best practices, summarized in **Exhibit 2-10** .

<!-- ¶4 -->
