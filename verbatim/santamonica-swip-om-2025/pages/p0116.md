<!-- ¶3 -->
Deviations from Terms and Conditions (Item 19)

<!-- ¶4 -->
1. Term (Section 1)- It is customary industry practice for the contract to be executed some time prior to the commencement of operations. This enables the Contractor to be able to mobilize resources prior to the commencement of full contract operations.

<!-- ¶5 -->
2. Compensation (Section 4) – The section will be revised so that the not to exceed amount is limited to the services accounted for in Schedule A. All Schedule B services shall be estimated budgetary numbers but not subject to a not to exceed, as further set forth in Contractor’s proposal.
