###### Key West WWTP, FL – UV System Replacement

<!-- ¶3 -->
Jacobs successfully installed a new UV disinfection system in Key West, replacing an obsolete unit while maintaining uninterrupted treatment and full regulatory compliance. The project involved phased replacement of two channels—keeping one operational at all times—along with mechanical installation, electrical upgrades, and complete reprogramming of lost automation. With vendor coordination for programming and warranty support, the project was completed without violations and delivered very positive operational results.

<!-- ¶4 -->
###### North Hudson Sewerage Authority – 18th St Pump Station ATS Replacement

<!-- ¶5 -->
Jacobs planned and implemented a temporary generator and Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) solution after the primary ATS failed. The team coordinated closely with site staff to maintain operations while developing a permanent replacement plan, which includes full removal and installation of a new ATS.

###### Traverse City WWTF – New Equipment Room and HVAC Installation

<!-- ¶8 -->
Jacobs managed the layout and construction of a new equipment room at the Traverse City WWTF, working with a subcontractor to complete the structure. Following construction, we installed a mini-split HVAC system with heat pump to ensure proper climate control in the new space.
