**Chris Catlin, PE |** Manager of Operations (continued)

<!-- ¶2 -->
Superintendent: Operations and Maintenance | Minneapolis WTP, Minneapolis Public Works Water Treatment and Distribution Services | **_Minneapolis, MN._** Chris managed a 120-MGD surface water treatment plant, including a staff of 140 comprising operations, maintenance, and water quality departments within the water utility. Plant included lime softening, membrane and conventional filtration, activated carbon adsorption, recarbonation, chloramination, and fluoridation. Chris optimized treatment and residuals handling and trained operators, supervisors and lab staff in state of the art operations. Chris also created a Renewal and Replacement program for sustainability.

<!-- ¶3 -->
Treatment Operator | Seattle Cedar Water Plant, Seattle Public Utilities | **_Seattle, WA._** Chris operated the treatment plant during staffing shortage. Treatment included chlorination, ozonation, UV disinfection, and pH adjustment.

<!-- ¶4 -->
Design Engineer and Project Manager | Howard R. Green Consulting Engineers | **_Saint Paul, MN._** Chris provided design engineering, engineering study and analysis, master planning, and construction oversight services for municipal clients. Work included a variety of design, construction oversight, and start up of surface water treatment plants, groundwater treatment plants, and membrane treatment plants. Plant flow rates ranged from 1- to 30-MGD.

<!-- ¶5 -->
Startup and Commissioning Operator | Joint Water Purification Plant (JWPP), Arapahoe County Water and Wastewater Authority (ACWWA)/Cottonwood Water and Sanitation District | **_Denver, CO._** Chris served as start-up and commissioning operator for this plant utilizing reverse osmosis, microfiltration, advanced oxidation. Chris provided start up and commissioning services, operations, and operator training.

<!-- ¶6 -->
Startup and Commissioning Operator | Catskill-Delaware Water Ultraviolet Disinfection Facility, NYC Department of Environmental Protection (NYCDEP) | **_New York City, NY._** Chris provided start up and commissioning services for this 2,200-MGD UV treatment plant. He operated the plant during start up and trained City staff on operations.
