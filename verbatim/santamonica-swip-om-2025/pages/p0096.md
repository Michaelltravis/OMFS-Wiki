###### **3.4 SEAMLESS TRANSITION: ADMINISTRATIVE AND OPERATIONAL PLAN FOR TRANSITION TO JACOBS**

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### **COMMITMENT TO A SMOOTH TRANSITION**

<!-- ¶3 -->
A smooth transition with a detailed plan and a dedicated transition team is the foundation of a successful partnership between the City of Santa Monica and Jacobs. We have a thorough understanding of the City’s needs and expectations and have developed a plan to deliver a high level of service on day one. Leveraging the expertise of our transition specialists, we will tailor our approach to the specific requirements of this project while supplementing the local team with additional resources when needed. This ensures transition activities progress in parallel with ongoing operations so that project tasks are seamlessly implemented.

<!-- ¶4 -->
Our transition plan reflects a highly organized process that builds on Jacobs’ proven experience managing complex transitions while addressing Santa Monica’s unique needs. It efficiently organizes local staff and provides sufficient qualified resources in all disciplines to assure the City of superior operations, maintenance, and administrative coverage for all managed assets. At the same time, our subject matter experts will enable Jacobs’ tools and strategies to be implemented at the project level without impacting the workload or effectiveness of the local team.
