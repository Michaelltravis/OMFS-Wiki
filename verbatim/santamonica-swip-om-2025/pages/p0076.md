Chief Plant Operator (CPO)<br>Mack Mckenzie<br><!-- End of picture text -->

<!-- ¶2 -->
Mack MacKenzie will serve as the designated Chief Plant Operator for the SWIP facilities. He holds a Grade V Wastewater Operator certification and AWT3™, bringing over 11 years of operational leadership across a range of advanced treatment plants throughout California.

<!-- ¶3 -->
Mack has served as CPO at multiple facilities, including his current role as project manager/CPO at the Soquel Creek Advanced Water Purification Facility, the 7-MGD Novato Sanitary District WWTP and the 16-MGD Richmond Water Pollution Control Plant. He has extensive experience managing MBR, ultrafiltration, RO, and UV disinfection systems—technologies core to the SWIP facility. At Santa Paula and Fillmore, Mack led operations at full-scale MBR facilities, overseeing advanced automation systems, UV disinfection, chemical dosing, and biosolids handling. At Soquel Creek Advanced Water Purification Facility, Mack oversees the operations including membrane filtration, RO, and UV-AOP processes.

<!-- ¶4 -->
He has managed both day-to-day operations and long-term capital projects, supported regulatory compliance across multiple jurisdictions, and has trained operations staff in both theoretical and hands-on treatment processes. Mack has also implemented safety programs and served as a Site Safety Coordinator, contributing to strong compliance records.

<!-- ¶5 -->
His proven ability to supervise multidisciplinary teams, maintain asset performance, and navigate complex permit requirements will ensure the SWIP facility operates reliably and in full regulatory compliance from day one.
