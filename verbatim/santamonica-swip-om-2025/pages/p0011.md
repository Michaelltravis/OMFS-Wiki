###### **ASSET MANAGEMENT, CMMS, INVENTORY, AND 72-HOUR REPAIR RECOMMENDATIONS**

<!-- ¶6 -->
We will implement an ISO 55001-aligned asset management program with CMMS-driven preventive maintenance (PM)/ predictive maintenance (PdM) using the City’s Mentor APM system, then track PM compliance and reliability KPIs, and deliver recommendations within 72-hour for large/emergency repairs to protect warranties and asset life. We will stand up a modern inventory program, including barcoding/QR codes, labeled bin locations, mobile access, reorder & obsolescence KPIs and support annual budget planning for maintenance, replacements, and capital improvements.

<!-- ¶7 -->
BENEFITS TO<br>SANTA MONICA<br><!-- End of picture text -->

<!-- ¶8 -->
**_Longer asset life, fewer reactive repairs, clearer budget forecasts, and faster parts availability to shorten outages._**

###### **COMPLIANCE SYSTEMS AND LABORATORY PROGRAM**

<!-- ¶10 -->
We will deploy Hach WIMS integrated with LIMS and our Sample Tracking Tool (STT) to centralize data, automate reporting, and ensure no sampling requirement is missed. Our ELAP-aligned sampling plan, chain-of-custody, and regular audits will produce defensible data and on-time submittals for AWTF, SMURRF, and stormwater.
