###### **PROJECT TEAM AND THEIR QUALIFICATIONS**

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### **_Onsite O&M Team_**

<!-- ¶3 -->
We have hand-picked a high-caliber team, reflecting our commitment to the success of the City’s facilities. Key attributes of our team include:

<!-- ¶4 -->
Director of Operations<br>Howard Brewen<br><!-- End of picture text -->

<!-- ¶5 -->
Howard Brewen will serve as Director of Operations, bringing more than 22 years of experience in wastewater facility operations and management. He holds a California Wastewater Treatment Operator Grade V certification and a California Wastewater Collection Systems Operator Grade III certification, equipping him with the regulatory and technical expertise required to oversee SWIP’s complex infrastructure.

<!-- ¶6 -->
At the City of San Luis Obispo Water Resource Recovery Facility (WRRF), Howard managed daily O&M and led initiatives to improve energy efficiency and sustainability, including a $9.75M PG&E energy partnership and a $140M facility upgrade project. His experience managing $3.5M+ O&M budgets, integrating advanced treatment technologies, and establishing a successful wastewater operator intern program will directly benefit Santa Monica. Howard will provide strategic oversight, lead coordination with City staff, and ensure reliable performance and regulatory compliance across the SWIP facilities.

<!-- ¶7 -->
Manager of Operations<br>Chris Catlin<br><!-- End of picture text -->

<!-- ¶8 -->
Chris Catlin will serve as Manager of Operations, bringing over 30 years of experience in water treatment and distribution, including leadership roles as plant manager, superintendent, and startup operator at advanced treatment facilities nationwide. He recently supported commissioning of the Soquel Creek Advanced Water Purification Facility, training operators and initiating compliance systems for membrane filtration, RO, and UV-AOP processes. Chris has extensive experience managing operations at facilities up to 120 MGD and has trained staff across multiple treatment technologies, from conventional surface water treatment to advanced reuse. He holds a California Water Treatment Operator T3 certification and an AWT5™, providing the highest level of compliance and technical expertise required for SWIP’s advanced treatment systems.

incentive plans for all California operators.
