###### **COST PROPOSAL**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Our offer is based on a careful review of the technical specifications in the RFP and addenda. It is important to note we recognize you may have questions about our assumptions, and we remain fully flexible working together to fine-tune our offer, so it is consistent with your exact needs.

<!-- ¶3 -->
To develop our approach and cost, we engaged a large due diligence team comprised of some of our most talented associates with diverse skill sets. They, in turn, reviewed the information we received looking closely at the facility operations, all associated components, the permits, and other data provided. Where information gaps existed, we used industry benchmarks and conservative data from our own operations to establish the true cost of operations. To confirm costing accuracy, individual subject matter experts were tasked with developing estimates for specific items or categories, and these estimates were then compiled and integrated into one comprehensive approach and cost. Jacobs will staff the facilities with 6 FTEs and has provided hourly labor rates for each position as well as the total annual cost in Schedule A. In lieu of budgeting for I&C and Maintenance Tech apprentice positions on lines 8 and 9, Jacobs will contribute to the California Apprentice Council each year. This is a standard practice at Jacobs and satisfies the prevailing wage requirement. In compliance with the pricing form, the total annual cost includes wages + burden + indirect + fee and includes all the items mentioned in Exhibit A, Scope of Work, section 1.5.1 including facility data management, field office equipment, safety supplies, IT services for maintaining Jacobs hardware and software, contractor support staff, and vehicles.

<!-- ¶15 -->
It is important to note, a major differentiator of Jacobs is how we deliver work to our partner communities through our robust regional support network. This approach is our secret sauce to long-term client satisfaction and employee engagement. This regional support includes our management team and support for compliance, process control, CMMS, IT/OT, HR, safety, internal audits to ensure quality of service, procurement, and administration. We have 1,387 hours each year included from these functions to support the local team, thereby increasing greatly the capacity of 6 FTEs to deliver great work to Santa Monica. The cost of this regional support function has also been included in the total annual cost for each position in Schedule A. It’s worth mentioning that while the 1,387 hours of annual regional support is our commitment to helping the onsite team, Jacobs will pay for any additional cost for deploying additional resources if needed to successfully deliver our required scope of services.
