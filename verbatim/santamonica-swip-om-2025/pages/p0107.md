###### **_Inventory Management for Arcadia_**

<!-- ¶6 -->
In recognizing the importance of the Arcadia WTP, especially considering its role for DPR, Jacobs can extend our CMMS-integrated inventory management services to this facility, providing the City a unified asset management system. This proactive system will support accurate part forecasting, timely reorders, prevent obsolescence, and ensure the City gets the maximum value from these important assets. Dashboards and reports will provide real-time visibility into inventory status, usage trends, and procurement needs, empowering the team to make informed decisions while minimizing downtime and lifecycle costs. **Jacobs’ approach will transform inventory control from a reactive function into a strategic asset management tool aligned with ISO 55001 standards and the City’s operational goals.**
