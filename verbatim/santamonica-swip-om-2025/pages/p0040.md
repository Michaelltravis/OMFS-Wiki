Jacobs operates one of the most comprehensive training and certification programs in the
industry, supporting more than 300 O&M sites across North America. A centerpiece of our staff
development is building the largest bench of Advanced Water Treatment certified operators in
California, a credential that has become increasingly vital as utilities expand into water reuse,
indirect potable reuse, and direct potable reuse. We currently have 12 California AWTO certified
employees, with several more on track, demonstrating our commitment to expanding this
expertise and ensuring depth of coverage.

<!-- ¶12 -->
We are proud of the strong bench of AWTO certified employees we have already developed, as well
as the large number of staff who have committed to joining our bonus-supported certification
pool. This ensures we maintain a steady pipeline of skilled operators ready to meet the City’s needs
today and as regulations and treatment requirements evolve. Jacobs will leverage this proven
training and certification model, along with our extensive O&M training systems, to strengthen
staff capabilities at the Santa Monica facilities and ensure long-term operational resilience.
