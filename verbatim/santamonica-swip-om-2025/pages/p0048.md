As part of our commitment to energy savings and reduced greenhouse gas emissions, Jacobs will also incorporate fuel-efficient fleet management into project operations. Specifically, we have included two hybrid vehicles to support daily operations and maintenance of the SWIP facilities. These vehicles will be used for site inspections, sampling, and other routine activities, reducing fuel consumption and lowering the project’s overall carbon footprint. By integrating hybrid vehicles alongside process-level energy optimization, Jacobs will support the City in realizing benefits not only within facility operations but also across the broader support systems that keep the SWIP running reliably and sustainably.

###### **_Chemical Efficiency_**

<!-- ¶3 -->
Jacobs will use titration-based dosing, predictive cleaning schedules, and analyzer optimization to ensure minimal chemical use without compromising water quality or regulatory compliance. For the SWIP facilities, we will evaluate log reduction value (LRV) targets in relation to influent variability and optimize chemical feed rates accordingly.

<!-- ¶4 -->
###### **At the Pure Water Monterey facility, Jacobs reduced RO CIP frequency by 35% through membrane monitoring and refined pre-treatment practices—an approach we will replicate at SWIP using performance normalization tools and vendorintegrated service contracts.**

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### **_Customized Digital Reporting Dashboards_**

<!-- ¶6 -->
Jacobs will implement a secure, web-based dashboard tailored to the City’s facilities and KPIs. This tool will provide realtime and historical visibility into key performance indicators, maintenance activity, chemical usage, sampling and compliance results, safety metrics, and operational alerts. The dashboard will complement formal written reports, equipping City staff and leadership with immediate access to facility performance data and reinforcing Jacobs’ commitment to transparency, accountability, and operational excellence (see **Exhibit 2-19** for an example KPI dashboard).
