<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **_Exceptional Reach-back to Regional and Technical O&M Resources_**

<!-- ¶4 -->
What sets Jacobs apart is our dedicated support model with over 100 O&M and technical specialists focused solely on supporting projects like Santa Monica’s. Unlike firms that shuffle staff between sites to cover gaps, Jacobs keeps each team fully staffed and focused on their assigned project so your budget, resources, and institutional knowledge stay dedicated to your facility, not stretched across others. Behind every site team is a deep bench of regional and technical O&M experts who provide consistent, proactive support to maintain performance and solve problems before they escalate.

<!-- ¶5 -->
Jacobs will deliver proactive, full-spectrum support across compliance, asset management, process optimization, and more. Our teams don’t just respond to issues—they anticipate them. We apply regulatory expertise, asset management-based maintenance, and advanced analytics to extend asset life, reduce costs, and ensure consistent performance. With immediate access to Jacobs’ national reach-back network—detailed in **Exhibit 2-2** —our site teams can quickly tap into specialized expertise across all aspects of O&M.

<!-- ¶6 -->
With 15 offices across California, including a strong presence in Los Angeles, Jacobs will provide daily, local support while maintaining responsive communication with City staff. When specialized expertise is needed, our deep regional and national bench of engineers, operators, and maintenance and compliance professionals will mobilize quickly.

<!-- ¶7 -->
This integrated model ensures Santa Monica receives both hands-on local attention and the full strength of Jacobs’ technical depth and operational excellence.
