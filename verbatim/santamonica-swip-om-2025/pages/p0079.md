###### **_Regional and Technical Support Resources_**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Jacobs will supplement the full-time SWIP operations team with a regional bench of over 100 subject matter experts in safety, regulatory compliance, advanced maintenance, asset management, and water reuse operations. This team is not committed to any one project but is a bench of shared resources we leverage to ensure each project is fully supported. This team provides on-demand support, ensuring projects receive specialized expertise and best practices. The team will assist with operations, maintenance, compliance, highquality reporting aligned with State permit requirements, and engineering support to the Santa Monica team. These experts will be available to City staff on an as-needed basis, providing immediate access to specialized technical expertise and industry-leading best practices. This in-house team will ensure Santa Monica’s facilities operate at peak performance and efficiency, with direct access to specialized expertise when needed. We’ve shown some of the individuals in this large team who will be supporting Santa Monica in **Exhibit 3-1** (SWIP Project Team Organization).

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **STAFFING PLAN**

<!-- ¶4 -->
Jacobs will staff the City’s SWIP facilities with the right balance of skill, experience, and coverage to ensure operations are not only in compliance but consistently meet the highest standards of safety, reliability, and performance. We will take into account the unique needs of each SWIP facility, the current condition of assets, and the City’s operational objectives to ensure our staffing plan aligns with your priorities.

<!-- ¶5 -->
We anticipate that many existing operations and maintenance personnel may wish to continue serving the City. Jacobs will welcome qualified incumbent staff into our team, recognizing the value of their facility knowledge and dedication. Our experience shows that transitioned employees thrive with

<!-- ¶6 -->
Jacobs, benefiting from our collaborative culture, robust safety program, and comprehensive training opportunities.

<!-- ¶7 -->
Our proposed staffing and shift schedule will deliver:

<!-- ¶8 -->
1. The right number of staff per shift to complete work proactively

<!-- ¶9 -->
2. Team capacity to assist one another on complex or urgent tasks

<!-- ¶10 -->
3. No lone shift operations, ensuring both safety and operational resilience

<!-- ¶11 -->
###### **_Shift Schedules_**

<!-- ¶12 -->
Jacobs will implement a hybrid staffing model that combines 4/10 and 5/8 work schedules to maximize operational efficiency, ensure comprehensive coverage, and accommodate employee work-life preferences. As illustrated in **Exhibit 3-2** , operations personnel will follow a 4/10 schedule, providing extended daily coverage with fewer shift transitions, while maintenance and technical staff will work standard 5/8 shifts to align with typical task durations and vendor support availability.

<!-- ¶13 -->
The operators’ 4/10 schedule assumes Jacobs will be granted an Alternative Work Schedule by the California Department of Industrial Relations (DIR).

<!-- ¶14 -->
In addition to full-time, on-site operations and maintenance personnel, Jacobs’ leadership team, including key project management, engineering, and technical support staff, will remain engaged on this project at an estimated level of 20 percent of their time. This sustained leadership involvement will provide continuity, oversight, and immediate access to Jacobs' broader technical bench throughout the contract term.

<!-- ¶15 -->
###### **_On-Call Staffing_**

<!-- ¶16 -->
We will designate on-call staff for after-hours emergencies at each SWIP facility to ensure rapid response to SCADA alerts, security calls, or other urgent conditions.
