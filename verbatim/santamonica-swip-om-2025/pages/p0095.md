Each team member brings hands-on expertise operating these systems under California’s rigorous Title 22 and Groundwater Replenishment Reuse Project (GRRP) regulations.

BENEFITS TO<br>SANTA MONICA<br><!-- End of picture text -->

<!-- ¶7 -->
**_Collectively, the Jacobs team brings a deep understanding of how MBR, RO, and UV-AOP systems must be operated, monitored, and maintained under Title 22 and GRRP conditions. Their direct experience with data validation, critical control point monitoring, and log removal value (LRV) tracking ensures that SWIP will maintain continuous compliance and readiness for evolving DPR pathways._**

<!-- ¶8 -->
**_We can’t stress it enough: no Jacobs project is an island. Jacobs’ operators at SWIP will be backed by a robust California delivery network, including nearby projects such as West Basin, Soquel Creek, and other advanced reuse facilities. This statewide bench strengthens resiliency, supports cross-training across teams, and expands the pool of AWTO certified employees available to Santa Monica. This approach not only builds operational depth for the City but also advances our broader effort to expand potable reuse capacity._**

<!-- ¶9 -->
**_In addition, Jacobs will align with Santa Monica’s intern program, providing structured training, mentorship, and progress feedback to help develop the next generation of advanced water treatment professionals._**

<!-- ¶10 -->
**_Together, the Jacobs SWIP team is well-positioned to ensure compliance with California’s evolving potable reuse regulations while delivering high-quality product water to meet the City’s groundwater recharge goals._**
