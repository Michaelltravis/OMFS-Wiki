##### **Section 5**

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### **JACOBS’ COMMITMENT TO VALUE, PERFORMANCE, AND PARTNERSHIP**

<!-- ¶3 -->
The City of Santa Monica is leading the way in the water reuse community with its sustainable water infrastructure project (SWIP) and deserves a partner that not only understands the demands of complex advanced water treatment operations, but one that can be trusted to elevate performance, address issues as they arise, and has the depth of resources to not only competently operate and maintain the current facilities, but to also support and prepare for the potential of direct potable reuse (DPR) during the term of this contract. Jacobs is that partner.

<!-- ¶4 -->
Following meetings with staff and two days of due diligence at the City’s SWIP facilities, bringing a team of subject matter experts with broad skillsets, our team has developed a thorough understanding of the nuances of your facilities. The meetings with staff and in-depth visits to your facilities, along with careful review of all the other pertinent information provided as part of the procurement, has given us a comprehensive understanding of current challenges and future opportunities.

<!-- ¶5 -->
In developing this cost proposal, our approach was not to “drive to the low price” but rather to deliver the highest quality and reliable service for the best value while recognizing the importance of cost and its impact to the City and its customers. As highlighted in our proposal, we have identified numerous ways to provide value to Santa Monica. We are offering an experienced and qualified team, with a deep bench of support behind them to closely collaborate with the City’s staff in achieving your vision for the future. We are excited to have the opportunity to partner with the City and bring you the full capabilities of Jacobs.
