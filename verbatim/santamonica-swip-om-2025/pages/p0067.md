###### **_Local and Regional Resources for Diversified Emergency Response_**

<!-- ¶3 -->
In a major emergency, it’s wise not to have “all your eggs in one basket.” A large event such as a wildfire or earthquake can result in fierce competition for operators, maintenance personnel, contractors, and materials. We bring Santa Monica diversified support, with regional and national resources that we routinely deploy to support projects across the country.

<!-- ¶4 -->
When emergencies exceed local capacity, we deploy specialized crews with experience in critical pump replacements, pipe repair, generator failure, and SCADA restoration. These teams have responded to major emergency events across the country and are trained to act under pressure while adhering to safety and regulatory protocols.

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### **Jacobs’ Invaluable Regional Resources for Emergency Response**

<!-- ¶6 -->
At the request of the City of Waterbury, CT, during a water supply emergency in 2019, Jacobs staff (including Regional Director Kevin Dahl), mobilized and engaged with the City at no cost to help troubleshoot water supply restrictions and offer solutions.

<!-- ¶7 -->
Having a diversified response capability prevents over-reliance on a single resource pool—a vital advantage during widespread regional incidents where labor and materials are in high demand.

<!-- ¶8 -->
###### **_Proven Emergency Preparedness and Response for the Most Challenging Situations_**

<!-- ¶9 -->
Jacobs is the preeminent emergency response firm and a **certified FEMA Disaster Response and Recovery contractor** . Our regional team has successfully prepared for and managed a wide range of emergencies and will support our onsite teams with regional O&M and consulting resources to handle any challenge presented. We are extreme weather experts and have received awards and client recognition for keeping systems in operation during disasters. As a FEMA contractor, we also bring the City deep access to specialized skills, contractors, and materials to handle even the most challenging emergencies.

BENEFITS TO<br>SANTA MONICA<br><!-- End of picture text -->

<!-- ¶11 -->
**_By aligning facility-specific Emergency Response Plan (ERP) procedures with the City’s broader emergency management framework, Jacobs will enhance the City’s ability to respond quickly and effectively to unplanned events. Close coordination with fire, police, and utility providers will reduce response times, improve communication, and ensure public safety in a highly populated urban setting. Integrating ERP procedures with the City’s Operations Optimization Plan, Asset Register, and updated Exhibit H resources will provide a comprehensive view of critical assets, preventive measures, and available tools. This integrated approach will reduce downtime, safeguard public health, and strengthen Santa Monica’s overall resiliency in the face of emergencies._**
