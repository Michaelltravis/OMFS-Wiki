###### **SCADA, I&C, AND CYBERSECURITY**

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### **_Site Visit Findings_**

<!-- ¶3 -->
During our recent visits to SWIP, Arcadia WTP, and the supporting remote facilities, Jacobs observed that the City's SCADA and instrumentation systems are relatively modern and well maintained. Across all sites, systems are built on Allen Bradley PLCs and touchscreen HMIs, with Ignition SCADA software serving as the primary interface. At Arcadia WTP, the recent conversion from Wonderware to Ignition demonstrates the City’s commitment to modernization and system standardization.

<!-- ¶4 -->
However, we noted critical gaps in system resilience. Most notably, the SCADA system at SWIP currently lacks redundancy—a single server failure would disrupt all monitoring and control capabilities. The server in use is a Dell R340, a minimal model without built-in failover protection. Additionally, we were unable to verify licensing, remote access configurations, and spare part inventories—underscoring the need for a comprehensive assessment early in the contract term to fully map the system architecture and risks.

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### **_Cybersecurity Survey_**

<!-- ¶6 -->
Cybersecurity is a foundational element of Jacobs’ approach to operational resilience. We recognize that water and wastewater control systems are increasingly targeted by cyber threats, and that safeguarding these systems is critical to protecting public health, compliance, and reliability of service.

<!-- ¶7 -->
For Santa Monica, Jacobs will work with the City during the **transition phase** to confirm the appropriate cybersecurity frameworks are in place. This structured survey includes reviewing existing systems, clarifying roles and responsibilities, and ensuring right-sized safeguards are established. Our focus is on **practical, cost-effective protections** that align with the City’s risk tolerance and regulatory requirements, without overengineering or introducing unnecessary complexity.

###### **_Dedicated I&C Technician with Regional Support_**

<!-- ¶12 -->
To directly address these vulnerabilities and support longterm system reliability, Jacobs will allocate a dedicated Instrumentation & Controls (I&C) Technician to our regional maintenance team in southern California. This technician will be based in the Los Angeles area and will prioritize serving the City of Santa Monica facilities and our West Basin project in El Segundo, while spending their remaining time helping our other projects in California. This position is in addition to the onsite I&C Technician also included on our Santa Monica team.

<!-- ¶13 -->
This cross-support model ensures high availability and responsiveness while leveraging regional expertise and cost efficiency. For Santa Monica, this approach provides the following key benefits:

<!-- ¶14 -->
- µ<sup>**Site-level familiarity with Arcadia WTP and SWIP SCADA**</sup> **infrastructure** , enabling proactive diagnostics, routine calibration, and software/hardware troubleshooting.

<!-- ¶15 -->
- µ<sup>**Rapid on-site response**for SCADA failures or instrument</sup> alarms, reducing system downtime and operational risk.

<!-- ¶16 -->
- µ<sup>**Consistent I&C coverage across all remote facilities**,</sup> including injection wells and lift stations, helping to maintain regulatory compliance and permit-required data integrity.

<!-- ¶17 -->
- µ<sup>**Backup staffing and knowledge continuity**, reducing risks</sup> associated with vacation or turnover.

<!-- ¶18 -->
The technician will collaborate closely with our operations team and Jacobs' deep bench of SCADA engineers to ensure robust system performance across all facilities.
