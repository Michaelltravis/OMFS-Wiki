###### **_Proven Advanced Treatment and Reuse Experience in California_**

<!-- ¶2 -->
In Southern California, our team members provide treatment process support for projects like the Pure Water Project Las VirgenesTriunfo and Chino Basin Programs, and Jacobs is the progressive design-builder for the Donald C. Tillman Advanced Water Purification Facility. Our subject matter experts in potable reuse and advanced treatment include Melanie Holmer, Michael Hwang, Jim Lozier, Larry Schimmoller, and Paul Swaim, who support these projects and will be available to your project to provide process expertise should challenges or opportunities arise.

<!-- ¶3 -->
Our Southern California teams have built strong relationships with the Los Angeles Regional Water Quality Control Board, the Division of Drinking Water, and have been very involved in the development of direct potable reuse regulations. Melanie Holmer served on WateReuse California’s DPR working group, reviewing every draft of the DPR regulations and providing input on potential comments and project impacts. She is also supporting clients who are exploring the potential to shift from an Indirect Potable Reuse (IPR) project to a DPR project, similar to the City’s ambitions. Melanie has also served as permitting lead or support for multiple IPR projects in planning, design, or operations in California. This familiarity will help streamline reporting, permitting, and compliance at every stage of the contract.

<!-- ¶4 -->
###### **_Representative Operations Experience with Advanced Water Treatment and Potable Reuse Facilities_**

<!-- ¶5 -->
Jacobs will bring decades of hands-on experience operating some of the most complex and innovative advanced water treatment facilities in the country—from MBR and RO to UV advanced oxidation and indirect potable reuse. Our work also consistently involves large-capacity plants with multiple treatment trains, regulatory complexity, and the need for 24/7 performance assurance. These conditions are directly aligned with the City of Santa Monica’s SWIP facilities. The projects highlighted in **Exhibit 2-5** (and described in detail in **Section 2.3 References** ) demonstrate Jacobs’ proven ability to operate and optimize facilities of similar scope, scale, and technical complexity. Through this work, Jacobs has developed an award-winning O&M approach that blends regulatory precision with operational innovation—ensuring high performance, low risk, and long-term value for the communities we serve.
