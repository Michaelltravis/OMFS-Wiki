<!-- ¶1 -->
- h. **Calibration Services with Licensed Engineer Signoff –** Magnetic flow meters are installed on influent/effluent lines and many process streams including MBR, RO feed, RO concentrate, AOP feed, and clear well distribution. Jacobs will have a contract for annual calibration/verification (wet flow test or electronic verification using manufacturer’s certified tool). NIST traceable certificates are provided. It will require engineering review and stamp/sign-off to meet regulatory QA/QC.

<!-- ¶2 -->
- i. **Davit Crane –** All davit and overhead cranes will be inspected and certified annually as required by OSHA. Preventive and predictive maintenance will be done with the inspection. We counted 10 cranes that need to be inspected.

<!-- ¶3 -->
- j. **Elevator –** The elevator will be inspected and certified annually as required by OSHA and any local permits. The elevator service agreement will include certified inspection as well as predictive and preventive maintenance.

<!-- ¶4 -->
- k. **Electrical High Voltage, 100 Volts and Above –** Jacobs has included high voltage maintenance service utilizing a specialized outside contractor service on every other year frequency.  Licensed electricians will be required to go through each MCC for cleaning and infrared camera recording of the lugs, buses, and switch gear, with any minor problems corrected on the spot.

<!-- ¶5 -->
- l. **Purchase Orders for Process or Membrane Cleaning Chemical Other Than Bulk Process Chemical Supplied by the City –** Jacobs has estimated the chemicals for clean-in-place activities, and the costs are included in the chemicals category below.

<!-- ¶6 -->
- m. **Vac-truck Services –** This description is already listed in item 4 above for Vac-truck Services.

<!-- ¶7 -->
7. **Contractors Craft Labor Support: Salary + Burden and Materials –** Contract Labor Support is based on over 900 annual hours of Jacobs Regional Maintenance mechanics’ or I&C Technicians’ time (this is above the 1,387 hours of regional support discussed above) and equipment at California Prevailing Wage rates. Jacobs will use this allocation for corrective maintenance activity that is beyond the capabilities of our onsite staff. The materials to be used will be billed to either the Preventive Maintenance or the Corrective allocation identified in Schedule B 1 and 3 above. This allocation will be billed on a time and material basis as the services are used.

<!-- ¶8 -->
8. **Janitorial Services –** This has been estimated based on weekly cleaning by an outside service provider. We expect this contract will include cleaning the bathrooms and moping the floors in common areas. Process areas, MCC rooms, pipe galleries, shop, and storage areas will be kept in a neat and orderly condition and cleaned as needed by Jacobs staff.

<!-- ¶9 -->
9. **Chemicals –** Specialty chemicals including antiscalant, calcium chloride, citric acid, RO CIP chemicals, and MBR CIP chemicals have been estimated per usage information provided in the optimized operations plan assuming 0.5 - 1.0 MGD treated flow. All other bulk chemicals will be paid directly by the City.

<!-- ¶10 -->
10. **Membrane Performance Monitoring (Optional Services, section 2.7.3.1) –** Jacobs did not develop a separate cost for Membrane Performance Monitoring as these services are inclusive with and provided by the annual OEM Service Contracts with Veolia and H2O Innovations identified above in c and d.

<!-- ¶11 -->
11. **Direct Potable Reuse (DPR) Operations (Optional Services, section 2.7.3.2) –** Based on Jacobs knowledge of DPR in California, during the piloting phase, the SWIP AWTF will be required to be manned 24 hours/day for at least 1 year. Since this pilot is expected several years from now, we have put the cost in year five and are flexible to when the pilot is started. The cost includes two additional AWT3<sup>TM</sup> operators and one additional AWT5<sup>TM</sup> lead operator, benefits, training, license renewals and CEU, uniforms, and safety equipment. Please note that additional regulatory sampling that will be required is assumed to be paid by the City.

<!-- ¶12 -->
12. **Mechanical and I&C Support at Other City Facilities (Optional Services, section 2.7.3.3) –** Mechanical and I&C support is based on Prevailing Wage that is required for Public Works services. The current rate for Inside Wireman (I&C Tech) is $99.08 and Maintenance Mechanic (Carpenter/Millwright) is $78.52. We have allocated 400 hours (1 day per week) for each trade annually (800 hours total) for this budget category. These hours are in addition to both the 1,387 hours of regional support and 900 hours of contract labor support mentioned above. The actual cost will be based on hours worked.

<!-- ¶13 -->
13. **SM-10i and SM-11i Performance Monitoring (Optional Services, section 2.7.3.5) –** A Jacobs hydrogeologist with 30 years of experience in the LA Basin will develop the Injection Well Performance Monitoring Program. The program will be developed in year one and implemented in years two – five.  Keeping the injection screens clean and removing buildup of precepted minerals is the purpose of this program. We expect periodic sampling of particulates in the injection water will be performed to monitor the injected water and pressure. Backwashing of the well screens will be done regularly on data collected. More aggressive screen brushing and chemical treatment of the well will be recommended as needed.

<!-- ¶14 -->
14. **Discounted Engineering Services (Optional Service) –** We are offering the City 1,000 hours of discounted engineering at a 2.3 multiplier each year of our partnership. We have included $250,000 on Schedule B to provide this service to be used at the City’s option for engineering studies such as preparation for direct potable water reuse, leech management, process engineering support, or other engineering studies the City requests.

<!-- ¶15 -->
**All items in Schedule B will be billed each month as costs are incurred and subject to contractor’s markup and annual escalation.**
