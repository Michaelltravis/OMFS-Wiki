###### **EMERGENCY RESPONSE PLAN**

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### **_Prevention of and Response to Emergencies_**

<!-- ¶3 -->
Protecting the safety of our employees, the public, and the environment is Jacobs’ highest priority and a core value of our operations culture. Jacobs takes emergency preparedness seriously and brings the City of Santa Monica direct experience managing a wide range of emergency situations across the country.

<!-- ¶4 -->
Jacobs has extensive experience in the development and implementation of Emergency Response Plans (ERP). A wellrehearsed and actively maintained ERP ensures our teams are prepared to prevent hazardous conditions, minimize service disruptions, and restore operations quickly and safely while protecting public health, the water supply, and critical infrastructure assets. Our ERP will define response, recovery, and remediation actions for all anticipated emergency scenarios, incorporating reliability and communication features that enhance safety, coordination, and efficiency.

<!-- ¶5 -->
Sometimes the unexpected occurs. When that happens, Jacobs stands ready to respond with the superior services that are the cornerstone of our O&M project delivery. Jacobs has 2,400+ associates in the region who can be quickly deployed for routine needs, troubleshooting, or emergency support of this partnership when needed. We currently operate 12 O&M projects in California, whose personnel can respond to any emergency in support of our onsite team or the City within a matter of hours. With 11 offices in California, our team is equipped and committed to providing immediate, rapid, efficient, and effective assistance—whether for earthquakes, flooding, wildfires, hazardous materials, or equipment failure. Our experts understand the level of resources essential in your time of need and will adapt to provide the most technically effective and feasible solutions while delivering work products expeditiously and within budget.

<!-- ¶6 -->
###### **_Emergency Preparedness Approach_**

<!-- ¶7 -->
Emergency preparedness begins with proactive assessment. Prior to any predicted event, Jacobs will assess expected impacts, prepare facility systems, and stage necessary staffing. Unit Process Control Procedures (UPCPs) will be reviewed to confirm equipment readiness and operational flexibility. Based on the severity of the threat, we will mobilize additional support staff from Jacobs’ regional teams—drawn from facilities across the western United States and nationwide. During the event, we will continuously monitor operational conditions and initiate mitigation or response activities as soon as safely feasible.

We recognize that Santa Monica is a densely populated urban area with constrained access in many locations, requiring careful consideration of potential public interface during an emergency. Jacobs will coordinate closely with the Santa Monica Fire Department, Police Department, and City-designated emergency coordinators to align site-specific Emergency Response Plan (ERP) procedures with City-wide emergency planning. We will also engage Southern California Edison (SCE) when appropriate to strengthen preparedness and continuity of operations. ERP procedures will be fully integrated with the City’s Operations Optimization Plan (Addendum #2, Section 1.4), the Asset Register (Addendum #2, Section 1.3), and the City-Provided Tools, Lab Equipment, and Specialty Chemicals (Exhibit H, updated per Addendum #3), ensuring that facilityspecific risks, critical assets, and preventive measures are directly addressed in emergency protocols.
