###### **<mark>8. Review and Continual Improvement</mark>**

<!-- ¶5 -->
Jacobs will foster a culture of continuous improvement through annual audits, performance reviews, and system evaluations. ###### **<mark>9. Performance Management</mark>**

<!-- ¶7 -->
Jacobs will provide monthly performance reports tracking key indicators including PM effectiveness, mean time between failures (MTBF), mean time to repair (MTTR), and overall equipment effectiveness (OEE). These metrics will support proactive management, drive operational efficiency, and provide the City with real-time insights into system performance and reliability.
