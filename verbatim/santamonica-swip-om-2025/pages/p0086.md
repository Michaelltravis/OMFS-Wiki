**Mack Mckenzie |** Chief Plant Operator (continued)

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### Chief Plant Operator (CPO)/Assistant General Manager | Richmond Water Pollution Control Plant |

<!-- ¶3 -->
**_Richmond, CA._** Mack was esponsible for the operation, maintenance, collections system, and laboratory at the Richmond Water Pollution Control Plant, a facility designed for 16-MGD treatment of the City’s sewage. He supervised 27 employees within five departments: Operations, Maintenance, Collections, Laboratory, and Administration. Mack’s duties included fostering a safe work environment, meeting contract deliverables, maintaining client satisfaction, presenting at monthly board meetings, ensuring regulatory and lab compliance, addressing routine and emergency maintenance issues, capital planning, budget compliance, managing large capital projects, implementing efficiency programs, and developing long-term strategic plans. Mack also trained operations staff in wastewater theory and practical application.

<!-- ¶4 -->
Maintenance Manager | South Bay International Wastewater Treatment Plant (SBIWTP) | **_San Diego, CA._** Mack was responsible for maintenance operations of a 25-MGD chemically enhanced primary treatment (CEPT) wastewater treatment plant with secondary clarification at a federally owned facility in San Diego. The International Boundary and Water Commission (IBWC) governs the SBIWTP, which treats sewage on the U.S. side of the Mexican border ut generated in Mexico. He supervised and directed scheduled and unscheduled maintenance activities of eight direct reports and managed very large capital improvement projects. In addition to maintenance, Mack trained wastewater operations personnel in operations theory and practical applications.

<!-- ¶5 -->
CPO | Limoneira WWTP, Limoneira Co. | **_Santa Paula, CA._** Mack was responsible for the operation and maintenance of the Limoneira WWTP, an extended aeration wastewater treatment plant where he managed two public water drinking water systems. He maintained operation and maintenance of all equipment and processes involved with the activated sludge system, including pumps, motors, controls, blower system, meters, and valving assemblies. He also provided routine and emergency maintenance of lift stations and 20 miles of sewer collection lines and manholes. Mack oversaw the dewatering system, multiple (1 MG) aeration ponds, sand filtration system, and UV disinfection. He created all standard operating procedures (SOPs), operational checklists, inventory distribution, sampling schedules, employee scheduling, and all safety training and prepared all required monthly, quarterly, and annual maintenance and sampling reports to state, federal, and local agencies for all three water systems.

<!-- ¶6 -->
###### Lead Operator/Wastewater Maintenance Supervisor | Santa Paula Water Recycling Facility (WRF) |

<!-- ¶7 -->
**_Santa Paula, CA._** Mack operated and maintained the Santa Paula WRF, a 3.4-MGD MBR facility with combined anoxic and aerobic aeration system. He supervised employees maintaining operations of all equipment and processes involved with the activated sludge system and SCADA system. Mack oversaw the Huber gravity belt thickeners, screw press dewatering system, drum type screens, MBR trains, Bray actuators, Ozonia UV disinfection trains, Flygt mixers, carbon media odor silo, Aerisa odor blowers, K-Turbo blowers, flow totalizers, meters, VFDs, PLCs, and HMIs. Mack was the designated Site Safety Coordinator, was first aid/CPR certified, and was part of the Community Emergency Response Team (CERT). He also completed state compliance reporting.

<!-- ¶8 -->
Wastewater Operator/Collections Supervisor | Fillmore Recycled Water Facility | **_Fillmore/Santa Paula, CA._** Mack operated and maintained the Fillmore Recycled Water Facility, a 2.4-MGD MBR facility. He supervised employees maintaining operations of all equipment and processes involved with the activated sludge system and SCADA system. Mack oversaw the screw press dewatering system, rake type screens, MBR trains, Bray actuators, Trojan UV disinfection trains, Flygt mixers, odor biofilters, flow totalizers, meters, VFDs, PLCs, and HMIs. He completed line cleaning and CCTV recording of 33 miles of sewer collection systems and created reports for the client. Mack was the facility’s designated Site Safety Coordinator, where he was part of the CERT and was first aid/CPR certified. He purchased all equipment and parts required for the CMMS program and managed an ultrafiltration/reverse osmosis plant treating industrial wastewater.
