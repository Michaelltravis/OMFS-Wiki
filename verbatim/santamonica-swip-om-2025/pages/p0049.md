###### **SAFETY PLAN AND MANAGEMENT**

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### **_Health and Safety_**

<!-- ¶3 -->
Jacobs has a long history of keeping our employees and partners safe, consistently outperforming industry safety averages because of our focus on training, accountability, and a culture of caring. Our employees look out for one another and provide or accept feedback when observing unsafe behaviors or conditions, which has resulted in our industry-low safety statistics ( **Exhibit 2-20** ).

<!-- ¶4 -->
Jacobs’ health and safety (H&S) program is based on the principle that all incidents are preventable. Our behaviorbased safety approach emphasizes identifying and correcting unsafe behaviors in real time. A cornerstone of this program is our BeyondZero Observations (BZOs), which are required of all supervisors and managers and encouraged across all employees. These observations, paired with coaching and positive reinforcement, provide immediate opportunities to improve safety. Jacobs also facilitates knowledge sharing across projects and regions, ensuring lessons learned are widely applied and our H&S program continues to evolve.

<!-- ¶5 -->
The project team will prepare a customized safety plan for SWIP facilities that reflects Jacobs’ proven BeyondZero® approach and incorporates site-specific needs, including the Cal/OSHArequired Injury and Illness Prevention Program (IIPP). This plan will deliver a culture of safety that engages all employees, reinforces accountability, and protects both staff and the public.

<!-- ¶6 -->
###### **_Safety Tracking and Performance_**

<!-- ¶7 -->
Jacobs uses a Safety Scorecard to measure both leading **proactive** and lagging **reactive** indicators. Five leading indicators such as BZOs, pre-task planning compliance, and training completion help identify and address trends before incidents occur. Two lagging indicators, TRIR and DART, provide accountability for corrective action. The Safety Scorecard is shared quarterly with project and regional leadership, ensuring transparency and timely response to any deficiencies.
