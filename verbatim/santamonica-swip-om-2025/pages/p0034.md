###### **STRATEGIC O&M PLANS BUILT ON BEST PRACTICES AND INNOVATION**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Jacobs understands that effective O&M planning must be both detailed and adaptable. Our approach combines proven best practices with innovative solutions, all delivered through open, transparent communication with the City to ensure fast, coordinated responses to any issues. We view operations planning as a collaborative effort—where shared goals and teamwork drive high performance and reliability.

<!-- ¶3 -->
Jacobs’ approach to developing a comprehensive O&M Plan for the City of Santa Monica’s SWIP facilities will build upon existing operations, emphasizing reliability, efficiency, safety, and continuous improvement. Through detailed evaluation of current facility conditions and procedures, we will establish a robust framework that integrates scientific process control, proactive maintenance and asset management, rigorous QA/QC, structured sampling and lab coordination, efficient solids management, and effective odor control strategies.

<!-- ¶4 -->
**Exhibit 2-9** provides a representative list of our O&M Plan elements. These plans will be updated starting at transition and will be implemented by **Mack Mckenzie, CPO** , with ongoing support from the **Director of Operations and Jacobs’ California Geographic Manager Howard Brewen** and **Manager of Operations Chris Catlin** . Together, they will tailor and refine these plans to ensure the City’s facilities operate at peak efficiency.

<!-- ¶5 -->
The O&M Plan will also establish clear protocols for internal and City-facing reporting and communications to ensure full transparency. Emphasis will be placed on health and safety, supported by rigorous site security protocols and modern cybersecurity protections. All elements of the plan will be formalized through facility-specific Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) that document best practices for execution, troubleshooting, and compliance.
