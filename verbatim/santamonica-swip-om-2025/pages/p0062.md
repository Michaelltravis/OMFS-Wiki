###### **_Annual Innovation Workshop_**

<!-- ¶19 -->
Our Annual Innovation Workshops, provided at no cost to the City, will bring together City staff, stakeholders, our O&M staff, plus our utility experts. We will review the prior year’s accomplishments and establish new goals and innovations to address the City’s evolving needs and long-term vision. This important annual meeting ensures we continuously look forward to future needs and trends, help the City stay at the forefront of treatment technology and O&M practices, and eliminate complacency over time.
