###### **IN-HOUSE PROCEDURES TO PROVIDE ACCURACY, INTEGRITY, AND QUALITY CONTROL**

<!-- ¶2 -->
To ensure operational accuracy, data integrity, and ongoing quality control at all SWIP facilities, Jacobs will implement a robust Quality Assurance and Quality Control (QA/QC) Program tailored to the City of Santa Monica’s operational priorities and permit obligations.

<!-- ¶3 -->
Jacobs will develop a comprehensive Quality Assurance Plan outlining the processes, metrics, and verification protocols used to monitor performance across the AWTF, SMURRF, and associated diversion and lift stations. The results will be shared with the City through formal channels as detailed in the “ **Communication and Reporting** ” subection of this proposal.

<!-- ¶4 -->
Jacobs’ QA/QC procedures have been refined over four decades of O&M delivery across hundreds of facilities. Each project undergoes a standardized internal audit beginning six months after start-up. The frequency and depth of future audits are driven by performance scores in core operational and managerial areas.

<!-- ¶5 -->
At SWIP, Jacobs will deploy a dedicated QA/QC team drawn from our extensive regional and national technical support resources, as shown in **Exhibit 2-11** , to conduct comprehensive audits, covering 70+ specific criteria. These include:

<!-- ¶6 -->
- µ<sup>Compliance with WDRs and Title 22</sup> GRRP requirements

<!-- ¶7 -->
- µ<sup>Compliance with South Coast Air Quality Management</sup> District (SCAQMD/AQMD) requirements

<!-- ¶8 -->
- µ<sup>Training documentation and certification tracking</sup>
