##### **Section 3**

<!-- ¶2 -->
_To ensure the City’s SWIP facilities operate with the highest levels of safety, compliance, and reliability, Jacobs has identified a dedicated, high-caliber onsite team supported by deep regional and national expertise. Our staffing approach goes beyond the RFP minimums to provide added value in operations, maintenance, compliance, and innovation. The City will benefit from a team with a proven track record operating advanced treatment facilities, deep experience with MBRs and Title 22 compliance, and a proactive maintenance philosophy that extends asset life and reduces downtime. Our structure is designed to ensure continuity, 24/7 responsiveness, and seamless integration with City staff—providing Santa Monica with peace of mind that its critical water reuse infrastructure is in capable, committed hands._

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **3.1 STAFFING PLAN**

<!-- ¶4 -->
**Robust Team with AWTO Certifications.** Jacobs’ onsite team of six dedicated staff will be led by our highly experienced Chief Plant Operator (CPO), Mack McKenzie, who has a California WW Grade V and AWT3™. Each member of our onsite team holds the required California State certifications, including Advanced Water Treatment Operator credentials, ensuring compliance with permit and regulatory mandates from day one. This certified team brings direct experience with SWIP-relevant technologies, including MBR, RO, and UV-AOP, and will maintain robust 24/7 coverage across the SWIP facilities. Further enhancing operational resilience, the core team can call on our network of California AWTO certified employees, including nearby resources at the West Basin facility in El Segundo, ensuring immediate backup and rapid support when needed.

<!-- ¶5 -->
**Additional Regional Support Dedicated to Santa Monica.** To enhance service flexibility and reduce costs for the City, Jacobs will also hire two new LA-based technicians, one I&C and one Maintenance, into our regional maintenance team. These positions are in addition to the full-time, dedicated Maintenance and I&C Technicians already assigned to the SWIP facilities. By embedding these additional resources in the Los Angeles area, Jacobs will provide Santa Monica with rapid, on-demand technical support without the delays or costs associated with mobilization. This approach ensures the City receives responsive, expert maintenance services when needed in the most cost-effective manner, improving operational continuity while minimizing costs.

<!-- ¶6 -->
**Deep Bench of Technical Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) at the Ready.** Further strengthening this model is Jacobs’ extensive technical bench of industry experts in MBR, potable reuse, asset management, and more. Our local and national experts will provide proactive support, including CMMS optimization, permit compliance, and process troubleshooting, ensuring Santa Monica remains a leader in water reuse innovation.

<!-- ¶7 -->
**A culture of innovation and continuous improvement.** Jacobs uses real-world proven digital tools, best practices, and lessons learned across our portfolio to optimize operations. This deeply ingrained commitment cultivating excellence will empower our Santa Monica team to deliver consistent, high-performance operations while protecting the City’s investment in sustainable water infrastructure.
