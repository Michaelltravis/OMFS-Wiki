###### **Edward C. Little Water Recycling Facility O&M** WEST BASIN MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICT | EL SEGUNDO, CA

<!-- ¶2 -->
The West Basin Municipal Water District recently awarded Jacobs a five-year O&M services contract for the nation’s largest advanced water reuse treatment facility. This unparalleled system produces five distinct grades of custom water through nine complex treatment streams—making it the most technically sophisticated reuse operation in the U.S. We are now leading the transition from the District’s previous contract operator, providing operational continuity informed by our deep expertise in membrane technologies, track record of compliance excellence, and commitment to ethical, transparent operations.

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **RELEVANT EXPERIENCE**

<!-- ¶4 -->
###### Detailed Description of the Facilities Operated

<!-- ¶5 -->
The 40-MGD Edward C. Little Water Recycling Facility (ECLWRF) is the only treatment facility to exist that produces five different types of fit-for-purpose recycled water—water for irrigation, cooling towers, seawater intrusion barrier, groundwater recharge, and low and high pressure boiler feeds. This recycled water conserves enough drinking water to meet the needs of up to 80,000 households for a year.

<!-- ¶6 -->
In addition to the ECLWRF, Jacobs also provides O&M services for its four satellite facilities: the Chevron Nitrification Treatment Plant, Juanita Millender-McDonald Carson Regional Water Recycling Plant, Torrance Refinery Water Recycling Plant, and Hyperion Effluent Pump Station. These facilities serve nearly 600 connections to municipal, industrial, and commercial businesses and are crucial in providing quality recycled water as a highly sustainable resource. Major customers include Los Angeles International Airport, SoFi Stadium, and Chevron El Segundo.
