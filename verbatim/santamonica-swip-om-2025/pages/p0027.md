TURLOCK ZERO LIQUID DISCHARGE FACILITY O&M | TURLOCK, CA

<!-- ¶2 -->
Our staff keeps tank capacities at a minimum level to facilitate effective and efficient power plant operations. We are working to improve HMI system by programming and tuning loops, adjusting set point controls, adding data collection, and troubleshooting the system. Jacobs installed new valving, plumbing, instrumentation, feedback control, and trend monitoring to improve O&M. The onsite team maintains PALL micro filtration units to better control turbidity and improve backwash techniques.

<!-- ¶3 -->
Staff optimized process chemical aids using bench jar testing, experimentation, and mechanical piping configuration changes. The team refined the WAC exchanger regeneration process to operate smoothly by implementing a more accurate acid control valve, tuning, and rinse procedure. Staff developed WAC rinse procedures that use one-fifth of the water and take less time than the old procedures required. These procedure improvements led to better techniques in scrubbing and regenerating the NAZ softeners to maintain lower differential pressure and higher throughput values to reduce brine waste.

<!-- ¶4 -->
We increased mixed beds throughput to exhaustion from 480,000 gallons to 500,000 gallons, a four percent increase without loss of quality. We improved crystallizer run time by changing procedures for the belt press, flash tank, FCHX, and brine waste heat exchanger cleaning. **Jacobs’ changes translate to lower costs and more efficient O&M for the client, helping the District achieve its goals for the power plant.**

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### Sustainability + Savings

<!-- ¶6 -->
Jacobs employees focus on continuous improvement. At the Turlock project, staff researched and developed a new cleaning method for RO equipment using bulk chemicals in place of special-order RO cleaning chemicals. The new method generates $147,000 in chemical savings for the client annually.

What I appreciate most is the outstanding communication and transparency we have with their team, which is essential to having a positive and productive partnership.
