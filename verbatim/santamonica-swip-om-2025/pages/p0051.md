###### **_SWIP Site Safety Observations and Unique Safety Needs_**

<!-- ¶3 -->
Jacobs understands that the SWIP facilities present unique safety challenges due to their location and operational complexity. Of particular note, the AWTF extends three stories below ground, requiring a site-specific egress and regress plan to ensure safe access and evacuation under all conditions. To further safeguard personnel, Jacobs will maintain two-person minimum staffing at the AWTF, recognizing that confined, below-grade facilities require immediate support in the event of an incident. The sitespecific safety program prepared following transition will address the unique environments of the AWTF, SMURRF, lift stations, and stormwater components.

<!-- ¶4 -->
Chemical management will also be a key focus of our safety program at SWIP. The AWTF stores and uses several treatment chemicals, including sodium hypochlorite and calcium chloride, which require careful handling and preparedness. Jacobs will ensure that all chemical storage and feed systems are inspected and maintained as part of the preventive maintenance (PM) program, and that emergency eyewash and shower stations remain fully operational and tested on schedule. We will also implement comprehensive chemical response plans that address spill containment, neutralization, and notification procedures, tailored to the specific chemicals in use at the facilities.

**_Jacobs’ safety approach will provide the City with a program designed to meet the unique challenges of an underground, chemical-intensive water treatment facility. By requiring two-person staffing, maintaining fully functional emergency response systems, and applying tailored chemical hazard protocols, Jacobs will reduce risks to staff, prevent disruptions to operations, and ensure full compliance with state and federal safety regulations. These measures will also strengthen public confidence that the City is operating SWIP facilities with the highest standards of safety and environmental stewardship._**

<!-- ¶7 -->
###### **_Continuous Improvement and Well-Being_**

<!-- ¶8 -->
Jacobs’ safety program is built on continuous improvement, using real-time data and lessons learned to strengthen our practices and prevent complacency. Leading indicators ensure we identify risks early, while lagging indicators provide accountability.

<!-- ¶9 -->
Recognizing that employee well-being is central to safe performance, Jacobs also integrates mental health into safety planning. Our **Mental Health Matters** program provides access to more than 1,200 trained champions worldwide who support staff in crisis, connect them to resources, and foster open dialogue about mental health. Supporting employee well-being helps sustain morale, productivity, and vigilance in safety practices.
