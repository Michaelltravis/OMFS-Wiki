###### **Schedule C – One-Time Costs Associated with Transition and Handover**

<!-- ¶2 -->
|**Item No.**|**p**<br>**DESCRIPTION**|**Total Cost**|
|---|---|---|
|**1**|**Transition and Startup**|273,762.76<br>$|

<!-- ¶3 -->
To facilitate the City’s evaluation of our cost proposal relative to the incumbent operator and other potential bidders participating in this procurement, Jacobs has provided one-time costs associated with mobilization, transition, and handover of the facilities into Schedule C – Transition and Startup. These costs include support resources from a wide range of SMEs to execute a smooth and seamless transition including administrative, human resources, asset management, maintenance, environmental compliance, health and safety, procurement, finance, onboarding, training, engineering, consulting, and various specific subject matter experts. This includes more than 1,600 hours of time dedicated to a successful transition in addition to the onsite staff.

<!-- ¶4 -->
Jacobs remains flexible with how we receive compensation for the one-time transition and handover costs should the City prefer to pay the balance in year one of the contract or have costs amortized over the 5 years of the initial term or contract. We are also open to other mutually agreed to terms and look forward to discussing this in more detail.

###### **PRICING AND CONTRACT ASSUMPTIONS**

<!-- ¶6 -->
Jacobs pricing is based on the following assumptions. We welcome the opportunity to discuss our assumptions and modifying them to meet the City’s needs.

<!-- ¶7 -->
- µ<sup>Labor is based on Operators’ schedule for 10-hour workdays (4 x 10s) with the assumption Jacobs will be granted an</sup> Alternative Work Schedule by the California Department of Industrial Relations (DIR).

<!-- ¶8 -->
- µ<sup>Generator fuel will be paid by City.</sup>

<!-- ¶9 -->
- µ<sup>SMURRF is not in service and requires periodic maintenance activities only.</sup>

<!-- ¶10 -->
- µ<sup>Pico-Kenter Pump Station is not included in the current scope.</sup>

<!-- ¶11 -->
- µ<sup>Exhibit B, Schedule B – These are realistic estimates based on our due diligence, costs from similar projects, actual quotes, subject</sup> matter experts experience, best practices, and industry standards. Cost will be billed on an as-used basis, including any budget overspends. Agreed-upon procurement procedures will be followed.

<!-- ¶12 -->
- µ<sup>Preventive maintenance and light repair activities will be performed by the onsite Jacobs personnel. Repairs beyond the skill of the</sup> onsite personnel will be performed by our Regional Maintenance Team or by outside contractors with costs charged to the City in the same format as Schedule B items.

<!-- ¶13 -->
- µ<sup>Building and structures including concrete, waterproofing, doors, and drainage are not included in maintenance costs.</sup>

<!-- ¶14 -->
- µ<sup>Jacobs desires to annually negotiate the cost escalator based on mutually agreeable indices from the previous year for the</sup> different cost categories. We are assuming an escalation formula approach will be negotiated into the contract upon award, and the escalation numbers provided in our proposal are for evaluation purposes only (an example might include 60% Employee Cost Index and 40% CPI Water Sewer).

<!-- ¶15 -->
- µ<sup>Jacobs assumes mutually agreeable language for termination for cause and termination for convenience will be negotiated into the</sup> contract upon award.
