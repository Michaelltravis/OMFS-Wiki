###### **_Cross-Training for Resiliency_**

<!-- ¶2 -->
The organization chart ( **Exhibit 3-1** ) reflects coverage for the AWTF with cross-trained staff for flexibility. Jacobs believes that crosstraining is essential to reliable plant operations and employee retention. We will develop our team members to their full potential, enabling them to work across multiple facilities and disciplines. This cross-facility approach increases the City’s operational resiliency and creates a larger, more capable resource base.

<!-- ¶3 -->
In addition to onsite cross training, Jacobs will leverage our extensive network of projects across California. With nearby operations at West Basin, Soquel Creek, and other advanced treatment facilities, staff will participate in regional learning and knowledge sharing that directly benefits Santa Monica. This California-wide bench of expertise not only enhances resiliency but also supports Jacobs’ broader mission of advancing water reuse and developing the largest bench of AWTO certified employees in California. By sharing knowledge across projects, our teams strengthen each other and help “get the word out” about the value of water reuse, furthering the industry-wide effort to expand advanced water treatment capacity across the state.

Santa Monica’s strong intern program is another important part of this strategy. Jacobs will involve interns in structured training alongside our operators, pairing them with mentors and providing feedback to the City on their development. This builds the next generation of water and advanced water treatment professionals while supporting the City’s long-term workforce pipeline.

<!-- ¶8 -->
We also recognize the City’s interest in cross-training staff. While the scope of this request will be clarified during negotiations, Jacobs will work with the City to identify targeted opportunities for knowledge transfer without overextending resources.

<!-- ¶9 -->
By combining cross-training within our Jacobs team, involving interns in structured development, and drawing on our broader California delivery team, Jacobs offers Santa Monica a balanced approach that enhances resiliency, builds local talent, and strengthens long-term operational capacity.
