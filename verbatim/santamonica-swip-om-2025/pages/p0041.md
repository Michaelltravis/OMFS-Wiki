###### **_Structured Training Programs and Compliance Alignment_**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Every team member will receive training aligned with the State Water Resources Control Board, California Title 22, and AWTO certification requirements. Our programs encompass safety, ethics, operations and maintenance procedures, quality management, and certification preparation. **Exhibit 2-13** summarizes our standard training topics, with individualized and site-specific training delivered as needed.

<!-- ¶3 -->
To ensure regulatory awareness, we deliver targeted compliance training addressing permit responsibilities, reporting obligations, and the legal implications of violations under the Clean Water Act. **(Exhibit 2-14)**
