###### **HOW WE WILL DELIVER RESULTS— COMPLIANCE, RELIABILITY, TRANSPARENCY**

<!-- ¶2 -->
The City can count on Jacobs to meet and exceed your expectations for compliance, reliability and transparency, including:

<!-- ¶3 -->
- µ<sup>**Operate to Permits and KPIs:**We will run the full train, track critical control</sup> points (CCPs)/ log reduction values (LRVs), and issue monthly compliance/ KPI reports. We will verify alarms remotely and integrate alarm callouts with Ignition, documenting alarm functionality each month. We will trend injectionwell performance (head pressure, specific capacity, backwash intervals) to protect capacity and regulatory confidence.

<!-- ¶4 -->
- µ<sup>**QA/QC and Audits:**We will deploy a formal QA/QC program with internal audits</sup> and data reviews tied to corrective actions for durable improvements.

<!-- ¶5 -->
- µ<sup>**Reporting and Dashboards.**We will produce MORs/DMRs/annual reports and</sup> a customized dashboard showing KPIs, work-order status, chemical/energy intensity, compliance results, and safety/training metrics—so the City will have real-time visibility into performance.

###### **TRAINING, CROSS-TRAINING, AND KNOWLEDGE TRANSFER**

<!-- ¶10 -->
Training for our staff will consist of inhouse training, and training through water and wastewater associations such as the Water Environment Federation (WEF), the American Water Works Association (AWWA), and the WateReuse Association. We will also provide training for our associates through private sources such as the American Water College, which got its start in California in 2006 and provides water and wastewater training for all 50 states. Its many course offerings include California-approved specialized training courses such as advanced water treatment.

<!-- ¶11 -->
We will cross-train across AWTF and SMURRF, pair City interns with mentors, and implement a formal training plan (EPA/Title 22/ ELAP sampling, safety, QA/QC, SOPs) with documented completion and incentives.

###### **SITE-SPECIFIC READINESS AND RELIABILITY**

<!-- ¶15 -->
**AWTF.** We will verify PLC/SCADA versions and license ownership, finalize point lists and CCP/ LRV displays, and add server redundancy for continuous operations. We will conduct monthly peak-flow stress tests so the City will know membranes can handle design peaks.

<!-- ¶16 -->
**Stormwater/Lift Stations.** We will validate alarm point lists, implement monthly alarm testing, exercise valves, and increase Ocean Avenue vac-truck cleaning to every eight weeks; we will apply advanced de-ragging tools driven by our Intelligent O&M capabilities to maximize uptime.
