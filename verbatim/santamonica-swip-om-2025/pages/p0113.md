3. **Preventive Maintenance Tools, Supplies & Consumables –** Jacobs's maintenance engineers evaluated the construction drawings and developed an asset list with all the equipment, meters, and sensors that require preventive and predictive maintenance at SWIP and pump stations. They developed a maintenance schedule based on projected asset life and the oil, consumables, and supplies that will be required to maintain the facility for the next 5 years. A small allowance was added for replacement tools.

<!-- ¶2 -->
4. **Vac-truck Services –** We heard during due diligence that the Ocean Ave lift station wet well is cleaned every 3 months. Even with the frequent cleaning, there have been ongoing operational problems due to debris buildup. To develop this estimate, Jacobs increased the cleaning frequency from every 12 weeks to roughly 8 weeks or 6 times per year. Once onsite, we plan to implement Jacobs’ AquaDNA Deragger at the Ocean Avenue lift station to improve performance and will make recommendations on keeping the wet well in better condition with weekly wet well checks and other methods to avoid overflows and attempt to decrease the frequency of vac-truck cleanouts.

<!-- ¶3 -->
5. **AQMD Annual Testing Odor Control –** The implementation of best management practices and H2S testing according to the air quality permits is standard practice inclusive of our operations plan. The cost listed in this category is maintenance of the H2S sensors and calibration gas for the odor control system.

###### 6. **OEM Service Agreements per Exhibit A 2.7 Service Contracts**

<!-- ¶5 -->
- a. **Drum Screen by Clean Tek –** The drum screen is a common piece of equipment that Jacobs typically maintains in–house with onsite maintenance staff. Jacobs will provide preventive and corrective maintenance to the drum screen in accordance with the manufacture's requirements. We do not recommend establishing an OEM Service Agreement for the drum screen at this time.

<!-- ¶6 -->
- b. **Odor Control by ECS Environmental –** The Odor Control equipment is a common piece of equipment that Jacobs typically maintains in–house with onsite maintenance staff. Jacobs will provide preventive and corrective maintenance to the odor control equipment in accordance with the manufacture's requirements. We do not recommend establishing an OEM Service Agreement at this time.

<!-- ¶7 -->
- c. **Membrane Bioreactor by Veolia WTS –** We have included a recommended budget for a Veolia service agreement. These agreements are site specific, including the scope of work and frequency of visits. A final service agreement price will require negotiating all the site-specific details upon award of our O&M contract. Veolia WTS is the OEM service provider of the ZeeWeed membranes used in the MBR system. Jacobs is familiar with the OEM service that Veolia WTS offers including periodic visits to the process, continuous online monitoring of the membranes, making recommendations for cleaning based on their monitoring of performance, recommendations for cleaning, and replacement. The membranes typically last from 7 –10 years, and the replacement cost of the membranes is not included in this service agreement estimate. If the City is interested, they also have a service program, life cycle inclusive, that includes membrane replacement at an increased cost.

<!-- ¶8 -->
- d. **Reverse Osmosis by H2O Innovation for AWTF and SMURRF –** We have included a recommended budget for an H2O Innovations service agreement. H2O Innovations is the OEM service provider of the RO skids at SWIP. The H2O Innovations service contract will include periodic visits to the process, 1 to 2 times per year, continuous online monitoring of the membranes, recommendations for cleaning based on their monitoring of performance, and recommendations for replacement. A final service agreement price will require negotiating all the site-specific details upon award of our O&M contract. The membranes typically last from 7 –10 years, and the replacement cost of the membranes is not included in this service agreement. If the City is interested, H2O Innovations may have a service program, life cycle inclusive, that includes membrane replacement at an increased cost.

<!-- ¶9 -->
- e. **Ultraviolet Advanced Oxidation Process by Trojan (SWPI has two) –** Trojan UVPhox units provide the UV for the advanced oxidation process. Jacobs uses Trojan UV at many of the plants we operate. Jacobs onsite maintenance will perform routine preventive maintenance. We do not recommend establishing an OEM Service Agreement at this time. The bulbs and ballasts need to be replaced based on run times. The cost for bulb and ballast replacement is over $10,000 and will be a City capital expense.

<!-- ¶10 -->
- f. **Instrumentation Service by Each Respective Supplier (e.g., HACH and Veolia) –** Jacobs has included a cost estimate for the OEM instrumentation service for the numerous instruments at SWIP. This is an annual service contract that would include preventative maintenance, calibration, and priority support.

<!-- ¶11 -->
- g. **Pumps at Lift Stations –** Jacobs has a lot of experience servicing lift stations at wastewater systems throughout the country. This service will be provided by Jacobs regional staff with a service truck that has a 4-ton crane stationed in the LA area. Annually the lift stations at Ocean Ave and the Civic Center Tank will have both pumps pulled, new seals installed, impellers checked, and motors checked. We have budgeted 16 hours for a two-man crew with truck and equipment. This cost will be charged in the same manner as all Schedule B expenses.
