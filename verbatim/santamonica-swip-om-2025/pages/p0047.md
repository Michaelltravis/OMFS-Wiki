###### **_AquaDNA Deragger for Ocean Avenue Lift Station_**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Jacobs has an exclusive partnership with a sewage pump deragging vendor, coupled with the AquaDNA smart technology monitoring system, that implements pump reversals upon detection of flow or power changes. This dynamic waveform monitoring is unique in the industry and results in dramatically reduced callouts, less power consumption, and longer equipment service life. With nearly 100 installations in the US, and a decade of operating experience, this is a proven approach.  For example, at our Rio Rancho, NM project, eight sewage pumps have been fitted with this control system (no change in pumps or starters/VFDs is required) to reduce previously chronic sanitary sewer overflow (SSO) events. See **Exhibit 2-18** for an example dashboard.

<!-- ¶3 -->
**As part of our solution, we will install automatic deragging devices at Ocean Avenue lift station. The technology can be integrated with existing equipment, requiring no additional control panels. These devices will proactively detect and address potential ragging issues and help mitigate sewer overflows in advance.**

###### **_Proactive Inventory Management to Support Operational Continuity_**

<!-- ¶6 -->
Jacobs will implement a comprehensive, CMMS-integrated inventory management system to ensure uninterrupted maintenance and operations at SWIP facilities by having essential spares and equipment on hand as needed. During the transition phase, Jacobs will define key inventory roles, set up organized stockroom infrastructure, and input all parts data—including bin locations—into the CMMS for full traceability. Leveraging mobile technology and barcoded parts tracking, Jacobs will streamline inventory workflows and establish SOPs for ordering, stocking, and usage. This proactive system will support accurate parts forecasting, timely reorders, and prevent obsolescence. Dashboards and reports will provide real-time visibility into inventory status, usage trends, and procurement needs, empowering the team to make informed decisions while minimizing downtime and lifecycle costs. **Jacobs’ approach will transform inventory control from a reactive function into a strategic asset management tool aligned with ISO 55001 standards and the City’s operational goals.** Further detail on our inventory approach and integration with long-term planning is provided in the “Asset Management and Maintenance” subsection of this proposal.

For SWIP, we will offer pilot-ready support and provide recommendations for emerging solutions that align with the City’s goals and funding strategies.

###### **_Energy Optimization_**

<!-- ¶10 -->
At potable reuse and advanced treatment facilities across California—including Soquel Creek and Pure Water Monterey—Jacobs has implemented SCADA-integrated energy dashboards, VFD tuning protocols, and automated control logic to reduce energy intensity across membrane filtration, RO, and UV-AOP systems. **In Soquel Creek, our team achieved more than 10% energy reduction through membrane recovery optimization and blower reprogramming.**

<!-- ¶11 -->
For Santa Monica, Jacobs will benchmark energy use at each process step (e.g., aeration for MBR, kWh/MG for RO) and apply our proven optimization strategies, such as real-time setpoint adjustments and off-peak operations, to drive savings. These changes will be monitored through key performance indicators (KPIs) and reviewed in our monthly operations reports.
