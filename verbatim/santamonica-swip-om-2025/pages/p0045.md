###### **INNOVATION AND ADVANCED TECHNIQUES FOR OPERATIONAL EFFICIENCY**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Jacobs is committed to bringing Santa Monica not only reliable operations but also continuous innovation and advanced techniques that improve efficiency, reduce lifecycle costs, and support the City’s broader sustainability goals. Building on our national Intelligent O&M platform, Jacobs will deliver a suite of value-added services included in our base fee, as summarized in **Exhibit 2-16,** that are tailored to the City’s needs—including process optimization tools, energy and chemical efficiency strategies, advanced asset management practices, and modern compliance systems. These services will be delivered through structured programs such as our **Annual Innovation Workshop**

<!-- ¶3 -->
(see “Communications and Reporting” subsection for details) and **Annual Training and Incentives Program** (see “Training & Certification Program for O&M Personnel” subsection for details) supported by local and regional experts who provide surge coverage and subject matter expertise. Jacobs will also bring specialized tools such as the **AquaDNA Deragger,** proactive **Inventory Management systems** (see “Asset Management and Maintenance” subsection for details), and a **Cybersecurity Survey** aligned to City standards (see “ **SCADA, I&C, and Cybersecurity** ” subsection for details), along with discounted access to targeted engineering studies.
