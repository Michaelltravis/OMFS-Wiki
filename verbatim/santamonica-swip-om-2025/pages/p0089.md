Superintendant | Upper Tuscarawas RBC Replacement and Wastewater Treatment Upgrades, Summit County Department of Sanitary Sewer Services | **_Akron, OH._** For this $19M upgrade to existing wastewater treatment facility, changing biological treatment system from rotating biological contactors (RBCs) to biological nutrient removal, Christen oversaw construction of the facility while operating the existing plant. She suggested changes to allow fully independent operation of the two activated sludge trains and ensured 24/7 operations during controls programming downtime, operating the plant fully by hand. She organized sampling schedules and built spreadsheets for tracking performance throughout the facility. She troubleshot issues with biological nutrient removal, recommended operational changes, and documented success. She communicated successes and challenges to upper management and provided wastewater, process, and troubleshooting training to O&M staff on operation of the biological nutrient facility. During and after plant startup, she provided hands-on, on-the-job training. Under Christen’s operations leadership, the facility drastically improved its water quality and NPDES permit compliance.
