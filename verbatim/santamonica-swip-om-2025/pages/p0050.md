**Summary of OSHA Audits from the Past Five Years.** OSHA has issued two citations to Jacobs water/wastewater O&M organization during the past five years. The violations were issued on August 25, 2020, and January 26, 2024. The August 25, 2020, citation was for a safety complaint regarding scheduled offline maintenance around a scrubber system at a PSM facility in Seattle (WA). A letter vacating the January 26, 2024, citation was signed on March 20, 2024. OSHA has not removed the citation yet. Documentation of both instances can be provided if requested.

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### **_Leadership Engagement and Accountability_**

<!-- ¶3 -->
Jacobs’ commitment to safety starts at the leadership level. **Vice President of Operations (West) Paul Rheault, Regional H&S Manager Bobby Hammond** , and **CPO Mack Mckenzie** together with **Director of Operations and Jacobs’ California Director of Operations Howard Brewen and Manager of Operations Chris Catlin** will set expectations, while **California Safety Lead Trey Kane** will support day-to-day implementation. Leaders will conduct safety meetings, inspections, and site visits, modeling expected behaviors, and holding all staff accountable for their roles in maintaining safety.

<!-- ¶4 -->
Jacobs will also implement a **digital-enabled safety program** at SWIP. Tablet-based tools will track observations, training compliance, and incident data in real time. This allows our team to quickly assess compliance, measure performance against KPIs, and respond proactively to emerging risks.

###### **_Key Elements of Our Safety Program and Planning_**

<!-- ¶6 -->
**Our comprehensive safety program and plan to improve the safety environment at the City’s facilities is outlined below.** These elements, along with our leadership’s continuous advocacy, will help establish a whole culture of safety at your facilities. We use tablets to provide many of these documents and tools in an electronic format, improving efficiency in our day-to-day tasks and tracking of our health and safety metrics and safety scorecard data ( **Exhibit 2-23** ).
