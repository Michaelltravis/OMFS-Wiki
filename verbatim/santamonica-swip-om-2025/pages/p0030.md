###### **2.4 FIRM APPROACH**

<!-- ¶2 -->
_Jacobs will deliver an integrated, transparent, and results-driven project management approach that ensures 24/7 compliance, reliable production of advanced treated water, and seamless coordination across the City’s SWIP facilities. With decades of experience operating facilities comparable in scale and technology—including MBR, RO, UV-AOP systems, and large pump stations—Jacobs is uniquely equipped to manage the full scope of SWIP operations in accordance with State Board Orders R4-2021-0044 and R4-2023-0366. As one of the largest O&M services providers in North America, Jacobs will leverage the depth of its operations, engineering, asset management, maintenance, and compliance capabilities to meet the City’s performance standards while driving long-term value and sustainability. Our team will integrate with City staff to uphold a community-first culture rooted in accountability, safety, and regulatory excellence._

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **JACOBS UNDERSTANDS SANTA MONICA’S CHALLENGES AND GOALS FOR ITS FACILITIES**

<!-- ¶4 -->
Santa Monica's SWIP represents a significant advancement in water treatment and establishes the City as a pioneer in water reuse. The 1.0-MGD AWTF facility is the first of its kind to successfully treat both wastewater and stormwater, the firstever to receive log removal credits for pathogen reduction using MBR, and the first to do all of this in an underground facility.

<!-- ¶5 -->
SWIP plays a critical role in reducing Santa Monica’s reliance on imported water supplies and promotes sustainability through the injection of recycled water into the local aquifer as a barrier to seawater intrusion and for aquifer replenishment. In addition, Santa Monica is in a great position to be the first DPR in the country—an amazing aspiration and one the full Jacobs team would love to help the City achieve.

<!-- ¶6 -->
In preparation for this proposal, our team talked with your staff, thoroughly reviewed the RFP, scope of work, draft agreement and exhibits, and spent several days in the field visiting the facilities conducting due diligence, which has provided a deep understanding of the challenges and opportunities to innovate and improve the O&M of the facilities.

<!-- ¶7 -->
**Exhibit 2-6** provides a concise, facility-by-facility summary of our understanding of your goals, challenges, and the resources and strategies Jacobs can bring to ensure we meet and exceed your expectations for exceptional O&M services. Across all sites, Jacobs recognizes the need to operate to the City’s permits and KPI framework to reliably produce Product Water for non-potable and GRRP uses, implement CMMS-based asset management, and integrate alarms via Ignition, per the Scope of Services.
