###### **ASSET MANAGEMENT AND MAINTENANCE**

<!-- ¶2 -->
###### **_Understanding the City’s Asset Management Goals and Facility Needs_**

<!-- ¶3 -->
Jacobs recognizes that the City of Santa Monica is at the beginning of its asset management (AM) journey and we are committed to supporting the City in building a program that preserves its capital investments while ensuring long-term performance. During our site visit, Jacobs observed that while some elements of asset management are already in place, a full implementation has not yet been completed. We will coordinate closely with the City to ensure full support for the City’s Mentor Asset Performance Management (APM) program and will implement a robust Asset Management System (AMS) during the transition phase.

###### **AquaDNA Deragger**

<!-- ¶6 -->
Jacobs will work with Santa Monica during the transition to improve the performance of the Ocean Avenue Lift Station, by installing our AquaDNA Deragger at no additional cost (a $75,000 value). This technology will help ensure continuous operation and help reduce vac-truck cleaning frequency.

The City’s AWTF is in good condition; however, as a complex underground facility, it requires robust preventive maintenance (PM), predictive maintenance (PdM), and careful calibration programs to sustain current performance and reliability. By contrast, the SMURRF is not currently in active use and contains many aging assets. We assume these will not require inclusion in the AMS unless directed by the City, beyond essential pickling and periodic pH monitoring of the membranes to maintain baseline condition.

<!-- ¶9 -->
Jacobs will also integrate the stormwater diversion structures, Ocean Avenue Pump Station, and groundwater injection wells into the AMS. For the Ocean Avenue Pump Station—which requires routine vac-truck services — the AMS will be key to optimizing cleaning frequency in conjuction with utilizing **Jacobs’ AquaDNA Deragger** (discussed in more detail in the subsection “Innovation and Advanced Techniques for Operational Efficiency”) to adjust cleaning frequencies. At the groundwater injection site, Jacobs will apply the same asset management principles to ensure injection well reliability, system integrity, and regulatory compliance.

<!-- ¶10 -->
Our AMS will include a long-term Asset Management Plan developed through a collaborative, data-driven process. This plan will maximize the value of City assets by applying best practices in preventive and predictive maintenance, leveraging condition assessment data, and implementing optimization strategies that extend equipment life and reduce lifecycle costs.

<!-- ¶11 -->
###### **_Integrated Asset Management and Maintenance Drives Delivery Excellence_**

<!-- ¶12 -->
Jacobs’ projects are fully embedded into our interconnected internal network of subject matter experts, field professionals, and support teams. No project exists in isolation. This structure enables shared learning, resource efficiency, and exceptional client outcomes. The SWIP facilities will benefit from Jacobs’ integrated approach to maintenance and asset lifecycle optimization.
