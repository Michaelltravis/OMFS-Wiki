###### **<mark>3. Asset Management Decision-Making</mark>**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Data-driven decision-making is central to Jacobs’ approach. By leveraging CMMS analytics and centralized asset data, Jacobs will optimize work order generation, cost analysis, and prioritization of maintenance efforts. Our system supports proactive obsolescence planning, helping the City avoid costly emergency repairs and maintain service continuity.

<!-- ¶3 -->
###### **<mark>4. Lifecycle Delivery and Maintenance Strategy</mark>**

<!-- ¶4 -->
Jacobs will manage the complete asset lifecycle, from acquisition to decommissioning, through a proactive strategy that blends PM and PdM. Our preventive maintenance optimization (PMO) and Level 3 PdM programs will replace reactive maintenance with forwardlooking diagnostics. Routine field inspections will monitor asset condition, while PdM tools such as ultrasound, vibration analysis, and motor signature assessments will support early detection of issues. Jacobs will complete all PM and PdM activities with in-house teams, supported by regional technical staff as needed. Corrective maintenance tasks will be tracked in the CMMS, issued within 24 hours of detection, and closed within 120 days. Facility appearance and maintenance will be tracked in parallel to ensure all spaces remain safe, clean, and professional.

Corrective maintenance will be systematically tracked within the CMMS, ensuring that all failure-related work orders are issued within 24 hours of detection and closed within 120 days. Additionally, Jacobs will uphold high facility appearance standards, ensuring that all buildings, process areas, and fleet equipment remain clean, well-maintained, and professionally managed. Maintenance tasks will be scheduled and monitored through the CMMS, ensuring structured oversight and accountability.

<!-- ¶8 -->
###### **<mark>5. Asset Knowledge Enablers (Information Management)</mark>**

<!-- ¶9 -->
Accurate and accessible data is essential to asset management success. Jacobs will maintain all asset records in the City’s CMMS to support lifecycle tracking, real-time condition visibility, and maintenance scheduling. The system will also support five- and tenyear CIP planning, helping the City prioritize infrastructure investments and maintain financial transparency. Asset data will include expected lifespan, spare parts needs, and replacement history, accessible through user-friendly dashboards.

<!-- ¶10 -->
###### **<mark>6. Organization and People</mark>**

<!-- ¶11 -->
A high-performing maintenance program depends on the capabilities of its people. Jacobs will align staffing, training, and leadership roles with the City’s asset management goals. The SWIP team will receive targeted training in asset lifecycle planning, PdM strategies, and CMMS use. **CPO Mack Mckenzie** and **Maintenance Technical Richard Gwinn** will be aided by regional SMEs in condition monitoring (e.g., Artesis, Augury AI), lubrication science (Tribology), and root cause analysis. These experts will provide continuous guidance and support to ensure system optimization and staff development.
