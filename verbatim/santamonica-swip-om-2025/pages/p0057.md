###### **<mark>1. Leadership and Governance</mark>**

<!-- ¶19 -->
A successful asset management program requires clear leadership and accountability. The **CPO Mack Mckenzie** and the **Management Team** will work with **Asset Management Support Lead Graham Knowles** and his team to develop the Asset Management Plan (AMP), ensuring alignment with City goals, compliance with industry standards, and strategic resource allocation. At project initiation, Jacobs will conduct a condition assessment and facilitate a risk workshop with City staff. This session will review key deficiencies, perform risk scoring, develop mitigation strategies, and assign responsibilities for corrective actions.

<!-- ¶20 -->
###### **<mark>2. Strategy and Planning</mark>**

<!-- ¶21 -->
Jacobs will establish a strategic asset management framework that supports the City’s operational objectives and integrates lifecycle cost analysis, risk-based prioritization, and long-term capital investment planning. We will begin by conducting a baseline condition assessment and risk-ranking of critical assets. During the transition phase, Jacobs will update and validate the City’s CMMS, Mentor APM, and ensure that the asset registry is accurate, complete, and reflects true field conditions.
