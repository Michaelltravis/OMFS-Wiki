###### **_True Belonging with Diversity, Equality, and Inclusion_**

<!-- ¶2 -->
Jacobs’ TogetherBeyond global strategy creates a culture of inclusion and belonging where we all thrive by embracing many perspectives—critical elements to building a culture that retains talent. It’s built on four strategic pillars of: promoting business growth through different perspectives, building cultural awareness and engagement, developing the best talent, and leadership commitment and accountability.

<!-- ¶3 -->
Jacobs’ diversity vision is to foster fairness and respect for all people, value the differences and similarities in backgrounds and skills, and maximize everyone’s potential. Because Santa Monica is a truly diverse city, this is particularly important. Jacobs employee network groups share information, ideas, experiences, and resources. These groups provide peer support, career development, and mentoring, and raise awareness through a variety of discussion groups and activities that are open to all. Each group has a senior executive sponsor to guide and reinforce the importance of efforts and mentor staff who may not otherwise have access to senior leadership.

<!-- ¶4 -->
Jacobs is also actively involved with the Hispanic Engineer National Achievement Awards Corporation, National Society of Black Engineers, Society for Hispanic Professional Engineers, Women’s Transportation Seminar, and Society of Women Engineers.
