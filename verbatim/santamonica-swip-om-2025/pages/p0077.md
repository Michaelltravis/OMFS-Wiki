###### **_Proven, Innovative Regional Maintenance Team Approach_**

<!-- ¶7 -->
Unlike other O&M firms that claim regional support but rely on borrowing staff from nearby projects, Jacobs maintains a standalone team of highly skilled regional maintenance professionals who directly support Jacobs’ California operations. This mobile team will provide immediate, flexible support to address any of SWIP’s current or future maintenance needs as illustrated in **Exhibit 3-1** . If desired by the City, they will also assist with capital improvement project execution. This model has proven successful in addressing evolving maintenance requirements at advanced treatment facilities and will provide Santa Monica with the reliability and responsiveness it expects from a long-term partner.

<!-- ¶8 -->
To further strengthen this model, Jacobs will expand our Regional Maintenance Team with two new Los Angeles–based positions— one dedicated I&C Technician and one dedicated Maintenance Technician—whose time will be shared between Santa Monica and other local projects in the area. These new roles are in addition to the full-time Maintenance and I&C staff already assigned to SWIP, giving the City access to additional expertise without bearing the full-time cost of staffing them exclusively at the facility. By embedding these resources locally, Jacobs will eliminate mobilization delays, reduce travel expenses, and enable rapid response to both routine and urgent needs. This flexible deployment strategy provides Santa Monica with the cost efficiencies of “on-demand” specialized support, while ensuring continuity, resilience, and direct access to technicians who are familiar with the City’s systems, assets, and operational priorities.
