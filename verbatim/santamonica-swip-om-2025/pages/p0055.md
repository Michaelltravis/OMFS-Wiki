**Laboratories.** Prior to assuming operations, Jacobs will perform a full laboratory system startup, including QA/QC audits, method reviews, and data validation to establish a defensible compliance baseline. Our QA/QC program includes:

<!-- ¶2 -->
- µ<sup>Chain-of-custody tracking</sup>

<!-- ¶3 -->
- µ<sup>Use of EPA-approved methods</sup>

<!-- ¶4 -->
- µ<sup>Internal audits and data reviews</sup>

<!-- ¶5 -->
- µ<sup>Corrective action logs</sup>

<!-- ¶6 -->
**Document Control and Information Management Systems.** Jacobs will use HACH WIMS to track lab results and performance data, enabling:

<!-- ¶7 -->
- µ<sup>Real-time monitoring and alerts</sup>

<!-- ¶8 -->
- µ<sup>Automated compliance reporting</sup>

<!-- ¶9 -->
- µ<sup>Trend analysis across permit parameters</sup>

<!-- ¶10 -->
- µ<sup>Custom dashboards for the City</sup>

<!-- ¶11 -->
All regulatory documents—including SOPs, QA/QC logs, calibration records, and compliance reports—will be stored in a secure, version-controlled SharePoint site accessible to authorized City personnel. Document control will comply with all public records and audit provisions.

<!-- ¶12 -->
###### **Continuous Improvement through Regular Audits.** Jacobs will

<!-- ¶13 -->
perform routine audits of all laboratory systems and sampling protocols, including calibration verification and data accuracy reviews. Findings will be presented during performance meetings and used to implement immediate improvements, ensuring continuous alignment with regulatory standards.

<!-- ¶14 -->
**Training and Certification Programs.** All field and laboratory staff will be trained in EPA, Title 22, and SWIP-specific permit requirements. Personnel will also complete training in laboratory safety, ethics, QA/QC documentation, and spill response.

<!-- ¶15 -->
Jacobs also uses proprietary QA/QC tools for lab data verification, including QC-Stats software and an internal auditing program. We participate in third-party proficiency testing programs to validate data integrity and laboratory performance, ensuring compliance with all state and federal standards. Our internal audit process reinforces adherence to regulatory expectations and supports early identification of compliance risks.

<!-- ¶16 -->
BENEFITS TO<br>SANTA MONICA<br><!-- End of picture text -->

<!-- ¶17 -->
**_Jacobs’ laboratory management and sampling approach will provide the City with reliable, defensible data to guide daily operations and ensure compliance with all permit requirements. By combining rigorous in-house process control testing with seamless coordination with the City’s certified contract laboratory, Jacobs will reduce the risk of compliance violations while supporting proactive operational adjustments. Our integration of laboratory data into SCADA and CMMS systems will enhance real-time visibility, improve reporting efficiency, and strengthen long-term trend analysis. With a robust QA/QC program, secure document control, and ongoing staff training, the City will benefit from increased regulatory confidence, streamlined audits, and operational transparency. Ultimately, this program will safeguard public health, protect the environment, and reinforce Santa Monica’s leadership in sustainable water management._**
