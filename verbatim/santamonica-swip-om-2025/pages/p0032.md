###### **APPROACH TO DELIVERY OF THE O&M SERVICES**

<!-- ¶2 -->
In **Exhibit 2-6** , we outline specific strategies for meeting the City’s goals for SWIP and how we can address some of the systems current and future challenges. With our industry-leading best practices in O&M and depth of technical resources—from O&M to engineering and beyond, we bring the City unrivaled capabilities to deliver on these solutions. The City can rely on Jacobs to achieve 100% compliance, minimize risk, and optimize the lifecycle performance of the SWIP assets. Jacobs will apply structured processes, proven methodologies, and proprietary digital tools to plan, execute, monitor, and improve operations—summarized in **Exhibit 2-7** and detailed further in the following Approach section of this proposal.

<!-- ¶4 -->
###### MAJOR ELEMENTS OF JACOBS’ APPROACH TO PROJECT EXECUTION

<!-- ¶5 -->
###### **HEALTH, SAFETY AND SECURITY**

<!-- ¶6 -->
A framework for compliance with Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) and other safety regulations, vulnerability assessments, job safety analysis, safety training, emergency preparedness and response, and other safety related functions.

<!-- ¶7 -->
###### **COMPLIANCE AND REPORTING**

<!-- ¶8 -->
A program of compliance for pollution prevention and waste minimization. Includes analysis and review checklists for all permits, report preparation for regulatory agencies, and environmental compliance training for all employees.

<!-- ¶9 -->
###### **OPERATIONS MANAGEMENT**

<!-- ¶10 -->
Documents treatment strategies and process control functions through standard operating procedures (SOPs), unit process control procedures (UPCPs), and treatment system optimization.

<!-- ¶11 -->
###### **LABORATORY MANAGEMENT**

<!-- ¶12 -->
Standard procedures for sample collection, analysis, and documentation with our quality assurance/quality control (QA/ QC) and chemical hygiene plans.

<!-- ¶13 -->
###### **HUMAN RESOURCES**

<!-- ¶14 -->
A program for the success of all of our employees as individuals and as a team, including recruitment, orientation, and training; standard training needs assessments for all employees; training programs for certification, ethics, compliance, safety, management and succession; and performance appraisals and professional development.

<!-- ¶15 -->
###### **MAINTENANCE AND ASSET MANAGEMENT**

<!-- ¶16 -->
Integrates maintenance scheduling, documentation, and tracking; inventory control; and equipment condition assessment into a computerized database that allows us to maximize equipment performance and minimize downtime.

<!-- ¶17 -->
###### **FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT**

<!-- ¶18 -->
Systems and approaches to manage project cost through national purchasing power, documentation of contractual requirements, and continuous monitoring.

<!-- ¶19 -->
###### **PROJECT MANAGEMENT**

<!-- ¶20 -->
Transparent communication including daily, weekly, monthly, severe weather, and annual client reports, internal measures of project performance, and plans to improve project performance beyond the requirements of our contract.

<!-- ¶21 -->
###### **PUBLIC EDUCATION, COMMUNITY INVOLVEMENT, AND MEDIA RELATIONS**

<!-- ¶22 -->
A robust public education and community involvement program developed in partnership with the City, including volunteering, charitable donations, and facility tours to benefit the community.

<!-- ¶23 -->
###### **CUSTOMER SERVICE**

<!-- ¶24 -->
A robust program to demonstrate our commitment to be a good neighbor and responsiveness to community concerns.

As Jacobs partners with the City to deliver high-performance operations across the SWIP facilities, we will implement a suite of tools and protocols designed to ensure transparency, compliance, and proactive management. Early in the contract, Jacobs will co-develop a tailored communication and reporting plan with the City, designed to address both Citywide coordination needs and the specific operational nuances of the SWIP system. This plan will guide ongoing communication with City representatives, regulatory agencies, and internal stakeholders, ensuring alignment on expectations, deliverables, and performance goals. We provide additional details in the “Communications and Reporting” subsection later in this approach section.

<!-- ¶27 -->
To support contract execution, Jacobs will deploy a rigorous contract deliverables tracking system that captures all regulatory, operational, and reporting requirements. This system will provide a clear line of sight into every commitment, accompanied by a realtime dashboard that highlights key deadlines, submission statuses, and upcoming milestones—enabling both City staff and Jacobs teams to manage compliance proactively and efficiently.
