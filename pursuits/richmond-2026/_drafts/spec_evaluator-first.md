## How to use this sheet

This sheet is written backwards from the scorecard. The RFP scores four things (C-073, RFP §9): Firm Qualifications and Experience 15%, Project Staffing and Management Plan 30%, Project Understanding and Technical Approach 30%, Fee 25%. Fee opens only after the technical panel has scored the narrative and the interview (C-077), so 75 points are decided by the 40 narrative pages, the reference checks (C-075), and the 2026-10-28 interview. Every row below exists to move one of those 75 points.

Four constraints shape every decision on this sheet.

1. **No clarification is available.** The Q&A window closed 2026-09-04 (C-012). Ambiguities are settled by our own judgment, conservatively, and recorded in §11.
2. **Responsiveness is a gate, not a score.** C-072, C-039 and C-074 let the City eliminate a proposal for a missing mandatory element or a format deviation before any scoring happens. The notarized surety statement (C-047), the two named key personnel (C-054), the five references with all six fields (C-052), and the two Attachment D forms (C-057) are gate items, not narrative items.
3. **Weight is not where the pilot spent its effort.** The pilot drafted Qualifications, which carries 15 points. Staffing and Technical Approach carry 30 each. §8 rebalances the page budget accordingly.
4. **Never invent a number.** Every figure here is copied from `proof-points/registry.md`, a verbatim page, the pursuit file, the trip-report digest, or the requirement exports. Where sources conflict, all values are shown with their source and a lock is recommended with a reason. Where nothing exists, there is a gap decision in §7 — not a placeholder in body text (voice guide Rule 35: zero editorial residue).

**Source keys.** `PP-####` = `proof-points/registry.md`. `TM-####` = `testimonials/inventory.md`. `ST-####` = `stories/catalog.md`. `C-###` = compliance-matrix requirement in `pursuits/richmond-2026/reqs/`. `FP §x` = `Desktop\Richmond\Draft Sections\02 Claude Working Drafts\_work\fp_03_qualifications.md`. `TRIP` = `pursuits/richmond-2026/trip-report-digest.md`. `PURSUIT` = `pursuits/richmond-2026/pursuit.md`.

## 6. The unmatchable fact

Mack Mckenzie was the Chief Plant Operator and Assistant General Manager of the Richmond Water Pollution Control Plant. No competitor can write that sentence.

### Exact wording allowed

Only the resume's own wording may be used, from `wiki/resumes/mack-mckenzie.md` (FP §I):

> "Chief Plant Operator (CPO)/Assistant General Manager | Richmond Water Pollution Control Plant | Richmond, CA. ### Three hard rules on the wording

1. **Never print the plant capacity in the same sentence.** The resume describes a "16-MGD" Richmond WPCP; the trip-report digest records full treatment capacity of 20 MGD today (LN-64). Both are true at different dates, and a reader who spots the mismatch will read it as carelessness about the City's own plant. The sentence names the role, the four functions, and the 27 employees across five departments — nothing about capacity.
2. **Never write him as proposed.** The pursuit file records that both named key personnel are placeholders by the decision of 2026-09-05 and that Mack's role is pending. Until that decision changes, he is described as a Jacobs operations leader who held that post, in the past tense, with no implication of assignment. The pilot's editor already had to make this correction once.
3. **Never imply access.** C-014 bars contact with City employees about this solicitation, and C-043 requires disclosure of any communication with City staff or Council since the RFP release. A former Richmond WPCP leader on our team invites exactly that question. The wording states his history and nothing about relationships, and the C-043 disclosure in the cover letter is completed on the strength of a positive check, not an assumption.

### Placement rule

- **Executive Summary:** inside the first 100 words, in a boxed callout beside the opening paragraph. One box, 25 to 40 words, role and functions only.
- **Qualifications (§8.4.3):** inside the first 100 words, in a boxed callout at the head of the section, before the corporate-entity table. Same wording, same box treatment, so the two read as one fact stated twice rather than two claims.
- Nowhere else. The device budget in §8 allows these two boxes and no third. Repeating it in Staffing or the Technical Approach turns the strongest fact in the proposal into a mannerism.

### The decision that must be made

**Mack Mckenzie's role and availability.** Three options, in order of value:

1. **Named on-site Project Manager.** Highest scoring value by a wide margin: it answers C-053.2 and C-054 with the one person in the industry who has already done this job at this plant, and it converts a 15% section asset into a 30% section asset. Requires availability, relocation and a check on the C-043 and conflict-of-interest questions.
2. **Named off-site executive sponsor or transition lead.** Preserves the fact, keeps him in the interview room on 2026-10-28, and does not depend on relocation.
3. **Mentioned only as corporate history, unassigned.** The current default. It keeps the sentence but leaves the evaluator to wonder why the man who ran their plant is not on the team — which is a question a competitor's debrief will eventually ask for us.

**Default recommendation: option 2**, with option 1 held open until Roy Aristizabal has his availability. Also required, whichever option is chosen: confirm his employer of record during the Richmond tenure (City employee or contract operator staff), because that determines both the conflict question and whether "27 employees" were City or contractor staff.

## 10. Concession policy

The proposal makes admissions on purpose, early, in our own words, and always followed by the mechanism that answers them. Voice Rule: an admission is a sentence with a subject and a fix, never a hedge attached to a figure and never a self-disclaimer ("we make no claim", "it is too early", "contacts and fees are as last confirmed" are all banned).

### What may be admitted, and how

| what | how it is said | where |
|---|---|---|
| **West Basin has been ours since 2025** | State the date plainly and make the transition, not the tenure, the claim: full operating responsibility assumed 1 September 2025 after a contract operator of more than twenty years, with staff satisfaction measured up from 3.6 to 4.1 (LN-43, LN-44) | 8.4.3, 8.4.4 |

| **We self-disclose permit excursions** | The 13-row corrective-action table with its scoping sentence (PX-25), regenerated current, plus the two Oregon herbicide fines with amounts and resolutions (PX-24). Disclosure is the theme, not an exception to it | 8.4.3 |

### What is never volunteered

- **Any internal figure conflict.** One value per fact across the document (G-25). Never "99.8% (99.98% in other sources)". Never both revenue figures. Never both TRIR constructions.
- **That the 55% odor figure was ours and unsourced** (G-01). It is dropped, and dropped things are not discussed.
