## Angle: story-first

Built 2026-09-06 from `pursuits/richmond-2026/pursuit.md`, `trip-report-digest.md`, the eight `reqs/*.md` exports, `proof-points/registry.md`, `testimonials/inventory.md`, `stories/catalog.md`, `wiki/` blocks, `verbatim/<slug>/pages/`, `voice/voice-guide.md`, and the pilot diagnostics `editorial_log_03.md` / `fp_03_qualifications.md`.

**Rule of this sheet.** No number is invented. Every value is copied from a registry ID, a verbatim page, a wiki block, or a pursuit document, and carries its source. Where sources disagree, all values are listed and a lock is recommended with a reason. Where nothing exists, there is a gap decision — so no placeholder reaches body text (voice-guide Rule 35: zero editorial residue).

## 0. The narrative arc — decided before the proof

**The spine, one sentence:** *Richmond's next operator should arrive already knowing the plant, the settlement and the neighborhood — and prove it in the open, in the first 90 days.*

The story-first order of construction is: the arc below is fixed; each section's closer is fixed; then proof is attached. Any proof that does not serve a beat is cut, however good the number.

| § | Section | The beat this section carries | The closer (last thing the evaluator reads) |

| 8.4.6 | Required Forms | No narrative. Compliance only. | — |

**Why this arc wins.** The trip reports say the City's dissatisfaction is about *visibility*, not effluent quality — "Where are all the staff? Maybe saw 3 different people at plant"; City access to systems and records; M&R fund accounting; a PM "not at all knowledgeable about collection system and storm system." Every closer above is a visibility closer. The technical case is the middle of each section, not the end.

**Three signature phrases** (voice Rule 27 — each used at least twice, no more than one per paragraph): *"in the open"*, *"Day 1 and Day 90"*, *"an extension of the City"*. Do not add a fourth.
