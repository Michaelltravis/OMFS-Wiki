# Requirements — 8.4.6 Required Forms (Compliance Matrix Outline Xref 6)

Source: 20260803150224418 FINAL_City of Richmond RFP-8-3-26.pdf. 5 requirement rows.

- **C-030** [Submittal] (RFP §7.10, p.27): The Richmond Sanctuary City Contracting Ordinance No. 12-18 (Attachment D) prohibits the City from granting and/or retaining contracts with any person or entity that provides data broker or Extreme Vetting services to the U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement Division of the United States Department of Homeland Security ("ICE"). Proposers must submit the attached Sanctuary City Compliance Statement with their Proposal.
- **C-031** [Submittal] (RFP §7.11, p.27): Resolution No. 86-21 (Attachment D), approved by City Council on July 6, 2021, requires disclosure of the identities of beneficial owners of Limited Liability Corporations (LLC). Proposers must submit the attached Disclosure Statement of Limited Liability Companies Form with their Proposal. This form can be found in Attachment D of this RFP.
- **C-057** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.6, p.32): The City requires the following forms to be completed and submitted with the technical proposal as an attachment. These forms are provided in Attachment D. Sanctuary City Contracting Ordinance; Limited Liability Disclosure Affidavit
- **C-119** [Eligibility/Legal] (Attachment D SCCO, p.51): Contractor further understands and agrees that Contractor's failure to comply with the SCCO shall constitute a material default of the Contract and the City Manager may terminate the Contract and bar Contractor from bidding on future contracts with the City for five (5) years from the effective date of the contract termination.
- **C-120** [Eligibility/Legal] (Attachment D LLCS, p.52): Whenever the owner of a property or the applicant for a City permit or license is a limited liability company ("LLC"), the LLC shall provide the City with the names and business addresses of any and all shareholders, directors, officers, members, managers, other authorized persons, partners, and "Beneficial Owners" of the applying LLC.
