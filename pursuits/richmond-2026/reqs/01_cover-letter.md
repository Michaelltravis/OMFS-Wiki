# Requirements — 8.4.1 Cover Letter (Compliance Matrix Outline Xref 1)

Source: 20260803150224418 FINAL_City of Richmond RFP-8-3-26.pdf. 21 requirement rows.

- **C-014** [Eligibility/Legal] (RFP §6.1, p.22): From the date the RFP is issued until the Notice of Intent to Award is issued, Proposers shall not contact members of the City Council, evaluation committee, City employees, or City consultants concerning this solicitation, except through the City's designated procurement contact using BidsOnline. This restriction does not prohibit communications concerning unrelated existing City contracts or services. Any prohibited communication may result in disqualification.
- **C-016** [Submittal] (RFP §6.6, p.24): The proposal and any required certifications shall be signed by an individual or individuals authorized to execute legal documents on behalf of the Proposer.
- **C-040** [Format] (RFP §8.4.1, p.29): The proposal must include a letter of transmittal attesting to its accuracy. A cover letter, not to exceed three pages in length, should summarize key elements of the proposal and shall:
- **C-040.1** [Format] (RFP §8.4.1, p.29): Confirm that all elements of this RFP have been reviewed and understood
- **C-040.2** [Format] (RFP §8.4.1, p.29): Include a statement of intent to perform the services as outlined
- **C-040.3** [Format] (RFP §8.4.1, p.29): Express the company’s willingness to agree to the terms and conditions prescribed by this RFP, insurance requirements, and the Service Agreement;
- **C-040.4** [Format] (RFP §8.4.1, p.29): Submit a written description and brief history of the company’s experiences, qualifications, and successes in serving as a wastewater facilities operator.
- **C-040.5** [Format] (RFP §8.4.1, p.29): Indicate the address and telephone number of the office located nearest to Richmond, California, and the office from which the project will be managed.
- **C-040.6** [Format] (RFP §8.4.1, p.29): Stipulate that the proposal price will remain valid for at least 180 days.
- **C-040.7** [Format] (RFP §8.4.1, p.29): Identify a single point of contact for the RFP review process.
- **C-040.8** [Format] (RFP §8.4.1, p.29): An authorized company official shall sign the cover letter.
- **C-041** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.1, p.29): The proposal must include a letter of transmittal attesting to its accuracy.
- **C-042** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.1, p.29): An authorized company official shall sign the cover letter.
- **C-043** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.1, p.29): Proposers must disclose whether they or any of their principals, officers, employees, agents, or subcontractors have had any communication with members of the City staff or the City Council regarding this solicitation, the subject matter of this solicitation, or related City business since the release date of this RFP. If any such communication has occurred, the Proposer must provide the following information:
- **C-043.1** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.1, p.29): Name of the City Council member(s)
- **C-043.2** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.1, p.29): Date(s) of communication
- **C-043.3** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.1, p.29): Method of communication (e.g., meeting, email, phone)
- **C-043.4** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.1, p.29): General subject matter discussed
- **C-043.5** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.1, p.29): Disclosure of such communications will not, by itself, disqualify a Proposer or affect the evaluation of proposals. However, failure to disclose required information may result in a proposal being deemed non-responsive or rejected.
- **C-045** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.1, p.29): Stipulate that the proposal price will remain valid for at least 180 days.
- **C-076** [Submittal] (RFP §10, p.38): The Proposer shall include in the cover letter a statement confirming that it has reviewed this draft agreement.
