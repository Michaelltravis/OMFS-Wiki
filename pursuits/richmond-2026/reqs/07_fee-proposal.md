# Requirements — 8.4.7 Fee Proposal (Compliance Matrix Outline Xref 7)

Source: 20260803150224418 FINAL_City of Richmond RFP-8-3-26.pdf. 15 requirement rows.

- **C-022** [Scope/Technical] (RFP §7.2, p.25): The City shall not be liable for any expenses incurred by potential Contract Operators in preparing or submitting their proposals. Pre-contractual expenses are not to be included in the Proposer's fee proposal.
- **C-058** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.7, pp.32-33): Each proposal shall provide a fee for the Operations and Maintenance of the WWTP, Collection System, and Stormwater System in accordance with this RFP. The City requests that each Proposer provide a separate cost for the stormwater system as a standalone service offering. The M&R fund shall be used to fund maintenance for the WWTP, collection, and stormwater systems. Provide the O&M cost for the stormwater system, excluding the M&R fund allocation. Include this table with your Fee Proposal.
- **C-059** [Scope/Technical] (RFP §8.4.7, p.32): The initial base fee shall remain in effect through May 15, 2028.
- **C-060** [Scope/Technical] (RFP §8.4.7, p.32): The Contract Operator's annual first-year base fee covers all services specified in this RFP. The quantified Maintenance and Repair (M&R) fund is included in the base fee and is subject to complete, transparent accounting of all charges each month and at the end of each contract year. Any remaining funds will be returned to the City. The City reserves the option to roll over the excess into the following year's M&R fund.
- **C-061** [Scope/Technical] (RFP §8.4.7 Utilities, p.33): The Contract Operator will be responsible for electrical power utilization, not commodity pricing. A baseline of 2050 kWh/MG is established, and this baseline will be used to calculate gain-sharing on an annual basis, using annual averages. The City will share any efficiencies gained as a result of the Contract Operator's innovation with the Contract Operator on a 50/50 basis. Should the Contract Operator exceed the kWh/MGD performance standard, the same 50/50 cost-sharing will apply. The average unit price for power in any given year will be used in the sharing calculation. The City will consider alternative fee arrangements for utilities if the Proposer offers a more advantageous method. Any such alternatives must be identified and described in the proposal.
- **C-062** [Scope/Technical] (RFP §8.4.7 M&R Fund, p.33): The City has established a Two Million ($2,000,000.00) Dollar M&R fund for the first-year contract. These funds will be used solely for the maintenance and repair of the Facility's assets. This includes, but is not limited to, pumps, motors, the SCADA system, piping, meters, electrical equipment, office equipment, laboratory equipment, safety equipment, communication equipment, maintenance equipment, City-owned vehicles, and other equipment. Maintenance of Contract Operator-owned equipment will not be charged to the maintenance and repair budget.
- **C-063** [Scope/Technical] (RFP §8.4.7 Utilities, p.33): The City desires that the Contract Operator manage electrical power efficiently for the entire system, particularly at the wastewater treatment plant. For this reason, a performance standard for the power used at the WWTP, measured in, will be calculated annually. The City expects monthly reporting on power utilization, analysis, and energy-saving ideas. The City will pay for gas and water services for the facility. That said, it expects the Contract Operator to optimize utilization and report its efforts monthly.
- **C-065** [Scope/Technical] (RFP §8.4.7 M&R Fund, p.34): Maintenance and repair expenditures exceeding $5,000 for a single work order, repair, event, or related group of activities require prior written approval from the Public Works Director or designee. The request shall include the equipment or asset involved, a description of the failure, proposed corrective action, cost estimate, and labor and material breakdown. Related work shall not be divided to avoid the approval threshold.
- **C-066** [Scope/Technical] (RFP §8.4.7 M&R Fund, p.34): All maintenance expenditures less than $5,000 per corrective work order will be charged to the M&R fund, excluding on-site labor, and do not require City pre-approval. A work order is a single repair or replacement.
- **C-067** [Scope/Technical] (RFP §8.4.7 Fee Adjustments, pp.34-35): The Base Prices may be adjusted in an amount not exceeding the percentage change (up or down) using the Consumer Price Index U.S. city average, Water and Sewerage Maintenance, Not Seasonally Adjusted, as published by the U.S. Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics in the CPI Detailed Report Series ID: CUSR0000SEHG01.
- **C-068** [Scope/Technical] (RFP §8.4.7 M&R Fund, p.34): It is expected that the expense required for predictive and preventive maintenance will be included in the Contract's base fee. In addition, Contract Operator's labor provided for corrective maintenance shall be included in their base fee. The repair budget for subsequent years will be negotiated annually. Any unused funds will be returned to the City.
- **C-069** [Scope/Technical] (RFP §8.4.7 M&R Fund, p.34): Itemized expenditures for maintenance and repair shall be submitted to the City in the monthly report with a complete accounting of all expenses. Requests for additional repair funds above the repair budget may be considered on a case-by-case basis at the City's sole discretion. The Public Works Director or their designee may sometimes request direct expenditures for the facilities utilizing the M&R budget.
- **C-070** [Scope/Technical] (RFP §8.4.7 M&R Fund, p.34): Any subcontracted labor for repairs, which are usually performed by plant staff, will require the City's pre-approval before being paid from the M&R fund.
- **C-071** [Scope/Technical] (RFP §8.4.7 Fee Adjustments, p.34): Adjustments based on system flow, load, and system size (length of pipe, number of manholes, pump stations, hydrodynamic separators, etc.) exceeding 10% of baseline conditions, and justify a contract adjustment. The Contract Operator must demonstrate the cost impact of the increase so that the City can justify an adjustment to the contract fee.
- **C-078** [Scope/Technical] (Attachment A, p.40): The Proposal should use the flow and loading information below as the basis of their proposal and fee. WWTP Flow and Loading Assumptions for the Base Fee: Influent Flow, MGD 6.50; BOD, mg/l 375; TSS, mg/l 450
