# Requirements — 8.4.4 Project Staffing and Management Plan (Compliance Matrix Outline Xref 4)

Source: 20260803150224418 FINAL_City of Richmond RFP-8-3-26.pdf. 14 requirement rows.

- **C-053** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.4, p.31): The Proposer shall provide a project staffing and management plan to meet the service levels described in this RFP. Describe the Proposer's approach to providing management and staffing of the Facilities, including:
- **C-053.1** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.4, p.31): Provide a clear, concise organizational chart, including subcontractors.
- **C-053.2** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.4, p.31): Describe the key staff and the management team who will be directly responsible for delivering the services and meeting the City’s objectives. Include the Contract Operator’s point person responsible for the day-to-day management of the facilities and the full-time on-site staff; describe all named (key) staff with experience maintaining operations in similar facilities. The City requires at least 2 key personnel to be named: the Project Manager (the highest-ranking on-site person and direct City contact) and the Collection System Manager (the person responsible for the collection and stormwater system).
- **C-053.3** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.4, p.31): Provide a clear delineation of responsibility & authority. Fully explain which staff are on-site and off-site, as well as support staff and sub-contractors.
- **C-053.4** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.4, p.31): Describe experience, education, and certifications held by key staff.
- **C-053.5** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.4, p.31): Describe the technical team supporting the facility's operation and maintenance, including off-site support staff expertise, capabilities, and availability.
- **C-053.6** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.4, p.31): Describe the proposed plan to ensure adequate staffing of the Facilities in compliance with the permit requirements and to meet the needs of the Facilities being operated and maintained.
- **C-053.7** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.4, p.31): Describe the Proposer’s plan to transition the management and operation of the Facility to the Proposer’s control.
- **C-053.8** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.4, p.31): Identify and describe the use of any sub-contractors included in the proposal.
- **C-053.9** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.4, p.31): Backup resources and expertise are available to augment the onsite staff and temporarily fill positions.
- **C-054** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.4, p.31): The City requires at least 2 key personnel to be named: the Project Manager (the highest-ranking on-site person and direct City contact) and the Collection System Manager (the person responsible for the collection and stormwater system).
- **C-064** [Scope/Technical] (RFP §8.4.7 M&R Fund, p.34): Sub-contracted work shall be limited to work outside the scope of the Contract Operator's on-site and support staff. Subcontracted work must be identified in the Project Team and/or technical approach sections of the proposal, and subcontractors must be named.
- **C-080** [Scope/Technical] (Attachment A, p.41): Provide adequate facility staffing with personnel with the appropriate state-required certifications and qualifications in technical, laboratory, and administrative/management issues to satisfy regulatory requirements. Under the scope of the contract, provide O&M services and all other related and required services.
- **C-087** [Scope/Technical] (Attachment A, p.42): Submit a staffing and organizational structure to the City annually and note any changes in the full-time Project Manager, who shall be the Contract Operator's primary on-site agent, or Collection System Manager. The Project Manager or his or her designee shall be available by telephone at all times. Telephone numbers shall be furnished to the City and updated to ensure the list remains current. The City reserves the right to approve any change in the Contract Operator's Project Manager.
