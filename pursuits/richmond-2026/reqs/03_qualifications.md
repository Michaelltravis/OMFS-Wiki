# Requirements — 8.4.3 Qualifications (Compliance Matrix Outline Xref 3)

Source: 20260803150224418 FINAL_City of Richmond RFP-8-3-26.pdf. 20 requirement rows.

- **C-001** [Eligibility/Legal] (RFP §2, p.8): Any Contract Operator proposing for this project should meet the desired qualifications as outlined in this RFP, which include:
- **C-001.1** [Eligibility/Legal] (RFP §2, p.8): Ten (10) or more years of experience providing operation and maintenance (“O&M”) services for wastewater treatment plants
- **C-001.2** [Eligibility/Legal] (RFP §2, p.8): California regulatory and wastewater utility operations and maintenance experience
- **C-001.3** [Eligibility/Legal] (RFP §2, p.8): Experience operating five (5) activated sludge wastewater treatment plants with a capacity of 10 MGD or more
- **C-001.4** [Eligibility/Legal] (RFP §2, p.8): Management of five (5) or more collection systems of at least 50 miles of network
- **C-001.5** [Eligibility/Legal] (RFP §2, p.8): Management of stormwater collection systems
- **C-001.6** [Eligibility/Legal] (RFP §2, p.8): Annual O&M revenue of greater than $50M per year
- **C-026** [Eligibility/Legal] (RFP §7.8, p.26): Bonding. The Successful Proposer shall provide performance and payment bonds equal to 100% of the Annual Fee (as hereinafter defined) under the contract. The bonding company may not cancel the bond without thirty (30) days' notice to the Owners. However, the successful Proposer may substitute another bonding company within the notice period.
- **C-044** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.3, pp.29-30): Include the Proposer's full name, tax identification number, principal office address, telephone numbers, and principal contact person. This shall include a description of the firm or organization (corporation, partnership, etc.) that will serve as the contracting party. A project organization chart delineating lines of authority within the organization is required.
- **C-047** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.3, p.30): NOTE: Notarized statement must be from a Surety Company, not an agent or broker.
- **C-048** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.3, p.30): Include five (5) references of projects similar in scope and complexity to the City's facilities. Provide a detailed write-up for each project, a project reference, and contact information. Additional information may be requested during the evaluation of technical qualifications.
- **C-049** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.3, p.30): Corporate Experience:
- **C-049.1** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.3, p.30): Provide information about the Proposer’s overall experience with operating facilities in the United States. Provide details (size, duration of relationship, and processes utilized) on representative facilities that the Proposer operates under a contract operations and maintenance arrangement in the United States. Refer to the targeted qualifications as a reference for similar facilities.
- **C-049.2** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.3, p.30): Have the capability to provide additional resources to assist in technical troubleshooting and capital planning. Explain how these resources are sourced (i.e., from internal resources, affiliate companies, and subcontractors) and whether these services are provided as part of the services offered under the base operating fee or are billed additionally.
- **C-049.3** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.3, p.30): Have a strong record of health, safety, and environmental compliance. Provide the Proposer’s current OSHA recordable rate and Experience Modifier Rate (EMR) and information about any health, safety, or environmental violations within the last five years.
- **C-049.4** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.3, p.30): Describe the Proposer’s approach to meeting and guaranteeing permit compliance.
- **C-050** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.3, p.30): The name (s) of the person(s) authorized to represent the Proposer in negotiating and signing any agreement that may result from the proposal. Identify when the Proposer was organized, if the Proposer is a corporation, and how many years the Proposer has been engaged in providing contract operations, maintenance, and management services under that name. Demonstrate good legal standing in California and your home state (no bankruptcy in the last ten years, fraud, or illegal activities).
- **C-051** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.3, p.30): Financial Qualifications: Demonstrate the ability to furnish insurance coverage and bonding as specified in Section 7.8 of this RFP. NOTE: Notarized statement must be from a Surety Company, not an agent or broker. Submit the company's final financial statements, which demonstrate its financial health and annual revenues for O&M services.
- **C-052** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.3, p.30): References – for each of the five (5) references provide: Name and location; Owner contact, name, and contact information; A description of services provided; Description of processes and system attributes (Plant size, system length, etc.); Years of service; Annual project fee.
- **C-056** [Submittal] (RFP §8.4.3, p.31): Litigation: Indicate if the proposing Contract Operator was involved with any litigation or contract termination for cause within the past ten (10) years involving the owners of prior projects. If yes, briefly describe the nature of the litigation and the result.
