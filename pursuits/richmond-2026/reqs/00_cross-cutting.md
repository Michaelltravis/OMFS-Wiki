- **C-017** [Submittal] (RFP §6.6, p.24) xref=None: Electronic Proposals shall be submitted via the City's secure online bidding system. - **C-018** [Schedule] (RFP §6.7, p.24) xref=None: The City may elect to interview all or some of the firms that submit proposals responding to the RFP. Interviews will be conducted in person at the City's offices. The key personnel named in the proposal, including any significant subcontractors and members of the Proposer's leadership team, should attend the oral presentation. The interviews will be scheduled in advance, and each Proposer will present their proposal and answer questions.

W-9 – Current signed form W-9 (Taxpayer Identification Number & Certification) which includes Contract Operator's legal business name(s) c. Business License – Copy of business license issued by the City of Richmond
- **C-021** [Scope/Technical] (RFP §7.4, p.25) xref=None: Initial Term. This Agreement shall commence on May 15, 2027, and terminate on May 14, 2037 (the "Initial Term"). The Contract Operator shall begin work as outlined in the Scope of Work, except as may be changed in writing by the City.
- **C-023** [Eligibility/Legal] (RFP §7.5, p.26) xref=None: Documents protected by law from public disclosure will not be disclosed by the City of Richmond if clearly marked with the word "confidential" on each applicable page. Trade secrets may be marked as confidential only to the extent they meet the requirements of California Government Code section 6254.7. Only information that is claimed to be a trade secret at the time of submission to the City of Richmond and marked as "confidential" will be treated as a trade secret.
- **C-024** [Eligibility/Legal] (RFP §7.6, p.26) xref=None: The selected Contract Operator will not discriminate in performing all services based on age, race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, veteran status, or disability.
- **C-025** [Eligibility/Legal] (RFP §7.7, p.26) xref=None: All proposals will conform to the prevailing wage requirements of the State of California.
- **C-027** [Eligibility/Legal] (RFP §7.9, pp.26-27) xref=None: The successful Contract Operator and all subcontractors used in the work will be required to hold or obtain a City of Richmond business license for which the fee will not be waived.
- **C-028** [Scope/Technical] (RFP §7.4, p.26) xref=None: Renewal Option. The City and Contract Operator shall have a mutually agreeable option to renew this Agreement for up to one additional 5-year term by providing written notice of renewal to the Contract Operator no later than 180 days before the end of the initial term of the Contract. Only after any adjustments for the upcoming year have been agreed upon shall the City authorize the extension.
- **C-029** [Eligibility/Legal] (RFP §7.9, p.27) xref=None: The Contract Operator and all subcontractors shall comply with the City of Richmond Nondiscrimination Clauses in City Contracts Ordinance (Richmond Municipal Code (RMC) Chapter 2.28), Business Opportunity Ordinance (RMC Chapter 2.50), Local Employment Program Ordinance (RMC Chapter 2.56), Living Wage Ordinance (RMC Chapter 2.60) and Ordinance Banning the Requirement to Provide Information of Prior Criminal Convictions on all Employment Applications ("Ban the Box") (RMC Chapter 2.65), which are incorporated into the Contract Documents by this reference.

No such materials or properties produced in whole or in part for the Project shall be subject to private use, copyrights, or patent rights by Respondent in the United States or in any other country without the express written consent of the City.
- **C-034** [Format] (RFP §8.1, p.28) xref=None: Each proposal must be submitted using the format indicated in the Proposal format guidelines. The selected firm's proposal will be attached to the Final Agreement.
- **C-035** [Format] (RFP §8.2, p.28) xref=None: Each proposal shall be prepared economically, avoiding elaborate promotional material, to provide a complete, accurate, and reliable presentation.
- **C-036** [Format] (RFP §8.4, p.28) xref=All: Each proposal will adhere to the following order and content of sections, must provide the following information, and shall be organized into the following sections: Cover Letter; Executive Summary; Firm Qualifications and Demonstrated Experience; Project Staffing and Project Management Plan; Project Understanding and Technical Approach (Operational Approach; Maintenance Plan; Transition Plan; Innovation to improve revenue and green initiatives; Development and implementation of Specific Deliverables); Required Forms; Appendices (if applicable); Price Proposal (as a separate submission)
- **C-037** [Format] (RFP §8.4, p.28) xref=None: Narrative pages will be in electronic PDF format and printable on 8½ x 11-inch paper using a 12point font. A clear and concise presentation of information is encouraged. Limit proposals to 40 pages or fewer, excluding bios, required forms, and supplemental information such as financials or promotional materials.
- **C-038** [Format] (RFP §8.4, p.28) xref=None: The proposal should be concise, well-organized, and demonstrate the Proposer's qualifications and experience applicable to the Project.
- **C-039** [Format] (RFP §8.4, p.29) xref=None: If the Proposer fails to organize the information required by this RFP as outlined, the City may, at its sole discretion, disqualify the Proposer from further consideration.
- **C-072** [Evaluation] (RFP §9, p.35) xref=None: All proposals will first be reviewed for responsiveness to confirm compliance with the mandatory submission requirements of this RFP. Proposals that fail to meet required submittal elements or contain material omissions may be deemed non-responsive and eliminated from further consideration.
- **C-073** [Evaluation] (RFP §9, p.35) xref=None: Each proposal will be evaluated and scored independently based on the weighted criteria listed below. Scores will be based solely on the information contained in the written proposal, reference checks, interviews, and any additional materials formally requested by the City.
- **C-074** [Evaluation] (RFP §9.2, p.36) xref=None: The City may reject any proposal that it finds incomplete, unresponsive, or noncompliant with the format requirements outlined in this RFP. A proposal may be deemed unresponsive if any aspect is unacceptable or contrary to the City's best interests.
- **C-075** [Evaluation] (RFP §9.3, p.36) xref=None: The City reserves the right to conduct an independent verification of the Proposer's experience and qualifications by contacting project references, accessing public information, or contacting any client mentioned in the proposal. Prospective Proposers shall respond and provide additional information that may be requested during the evaluation of proposals.
- **C-077** [Evaluation] (RFP §9.3 Fee Proposal, p.38) xref=None: Scoring the proposals will be done in two steps. Initially, the selection panel will evaluate proposals and interviews to score them using the evaluation criteria. After this initial scoring, the City will open the fee proposals. The Proposer's Fee Proposal will be evaluated against the overall proposal and interview, and the panel will score the proposed fee based on best value, considering the Proposer's proposal and providing a numeric value reflecting the best-value fee proposal.
