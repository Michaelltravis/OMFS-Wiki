## Description of Services Provided

Since 1990, Jacobs has operated, maintained, and improved the City's wastewater treatment facilities and related systems, achieving regulatory compliance, operational excellence, cost savings, and industry recognition. We assumed operations when the facility was noncompliant and implemented a comprehensive operational strategy and maintenance management program that restored compliance and resolved enforcement actions. We have continued to optimize performance and support the City's environmental goals while providing integrated services such as operations, maintenance, asset management, capital planning, and technical support. A team of 13 staff operates the 8.5-MGD facility and supports pump stations and satellite systems serving approximately 50,000 residents and local industries.

## Detailed Description of the Facilities Managed

**Capital Improvements.** Improvements include treatment performance and compliance, comprising biological nutrient removal improvements and installation of a large MBR system. The facility also includes digesters, dewatering, and storage systems for solids handling. We operate several satellite treatment systems, providing regionalized wastewater solutions to industrial and municipal clients.

**Treatment Systems and Processes.** The plant uses MBR technology to produce high-quality effluent and handle peak flows up to 17 MGD. Processes include biological treatment, membrane filtration, digestion, and dewatering.

**Operations, Maintenance, and Optimization.** We implemented proactive maintenance and asset management practices that extended equipment life and improved reliability, including membrane performance beyond its expected lifespan. Optimization efforts following MBR installation reduced electrical consumption by more than 30%, along with additional savings in chemicals and natural gas.

**Regulatory Compliance and Program Management.** We restored and have maintained compliance with regulatory requirements and developed and administered the City's IPP, including ordinance updates, permitting of industrial users, monitoring, and implementation of cost recovery mechanisms.

**Community Involvement.** Jacobs supports the Traverse City community through environmental stewardship and education initiatives, including partnerships, student engagement, facility tours, and participation in events focused on protecting regional water resources.

## Reuse guidance

This is the answer to "will you still be a good partner in fifteen years?" — a relationship running since 1990 that began with a noncompliant facility and enforcement actions and ended with a stack of awards spread across 2004, 2006, 2007, and 2019. Cite the award spread rather than any single award: awards in four different years across two decades prove sustained performance, where one award proves a good year. The single strongest quantified claim is the greater-than-30% reduction in electrical consumption following MBR optimization, with additional chemical and natural gas savings — pair it with the client quote about membranes exceeding their life expectancy, since the two together turn "we do proactive maintenance" into a measured result. The regionalized satellite-systems angle is a distinct differentiator worth pulling out whenever a client is weighing consolidation or taking on neighboring communities' flows. One caution: the "three decades of continuity" framing can read as ironic in a pursuit driven by dissatisfaction with a long-tenured incumbent, so in that context lead with the noncompliance turnaround instead of the tenure.
