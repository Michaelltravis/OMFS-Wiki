## Facility Overview and Integrated Delivery

The 32-MGD Agua Nueva Water Reclamation Facility is the centerpiece of Pima County's Water Campus, **the largest capital program in the County's history**, valued at $750M. The facility treats wastewater through a full advanced treatment train: **new headworks, influent pumping, combined flocculation and grit removal, dissolved air flotation (DAF) clarification for primary treatment and sludge thickening, five-stage Bardenpho activated sludge secondary treatment with step-feed aeration, tertiary filtration, and chloramine-based disinfection**. The facility produces 25 MGD of Arizona Class A+ reclaimed water for reuse and is partially powered by solar energy. Jacobs holds full DBO responsibility, from concept and design through permitting, construction, and long-term O&M.

**Jacobs holds full responsibility across the entire project lifecycle, including design, permitting, construction, and long-term O&M, under a single contract.** That **integrated approach** helped deliver a facility that came in $77M under budget, opened 8 months ahead of schedule, and continues to show strong compliance and safety performance. The facility also demonstrates Jacobs' ability to support an **active capital program while maintaining uninterrupted O&M** and carrying operational considerations into capital decisions.

Our operations team was embedded in design and construction decisions from the start, shaping equipment selection, maintenance access, and process flexibility for lower lifecycle costs and smoother long-term operations.

**Regulatory Schedule and Delivery:** Arizona regulators imposed a nitrogen reduction deadline for effluent discharges to the Santa Cruz River, and traditional design-bid-build couldn't meet the schedule. We used the DBO model to front-load operational thinking into design, shorten the critical path, and **keep stakeholders aligned while protecting treatment performance and permit compliance**.

## Odor, Transition, and Maintenance

**Odor Control:** Because Agua Nueva sits in a growing mixed-use corridor, **odor performance was critical**. Rather than relying on chemical scrubbing systems that require ongoing reagent supply, maintenance, and disposal, **we designed and operated an advanced odor control system that removes virtually all facility odors without additional chemicals and with minimal ongoing maintenance requirements**. We embedded odor control into the process design by enclosing odor-generating areas, maintaining negative pressure, and sizing treatment capacity to avoid odor-causing conditions. The result is a facility that has **maintained strong community relations and no odor-related regulatory actions since opening in 2013**.

To support a **smooth transition** at Agua Nueva, **we brought approximately 80% of the Pima County personnel who chose to join the project**. That approach strengthened our relationship with the County, kept valuable institutional knowledge in place, and helped maintain the facility to a very high standard. **Across both announced and unannounced ADEQ inspections, the site received only explanatory remarks**.

**Maintenance:** Agua Nueva relies on advanced, high-horsepower equipment, especially large aeration blowers. We use **predictive and preventive maintenance, supported by an Intelligent O&M platform** developed with Palantir, integrating SCADA data, facility sensors, CMMS records, and cost data into a single operational view, generating specific, actionable recommendations for field staff on blower DO setpoints, maintenance scheduling, and process adjustments. **This data-driven maintenance model reduces downtime, extends equipment life, and cuts energy costs by 20% plant-wide**.

## Proven Results and Lessons Learned

- **Facility delivered $77M under budget; 8 months ahead of schedule.**
- **$2M reduction** in annual operating costs.
- **25 MGD of Arizona Class A+ reclaimed water produced for reuse.**
- **Zero recordable incidents for 3+ consecutive years** — Target Zero Gold Achievement Award (2017).
- **LEED Silver-certified** administration building.

Jacobs and Pima County worked as a true partnership through technical, schedule, and community challenges. That approach **brought the facility online 8 months early, more than a year before the regulatory deadline**, and **$77M under budget while reducing chemical use and keeping lifecycle operating costs low**. The secondary system incorporated **North America's first ammonia control strategy** to integrate step-feed aeration with dissolved oxygen control. The Intelligent O&M tool built in partnership with Palantir represents a continued investment in data-driven operations, using real facility data to identify efficiency gains in energy-intensive equipment rather than relying on manual observation alone. **The facility has operated for more than 3 continuous years without a recordable safety incident, earning the Jacobs Target Zero Gold Achievement Award**.

The Agua Nueva DBO experience demonstrates what's possible when operations expertise is embedded in capital project delivery from the start. We'll apply that same integrated thinking to OCWUT's CIP pipeline: **operational staff informing design decisions, constructability reviewed through an O&M lens, and capital investments sized for long-term performance** rather than upfront cost alone. Agua Nueva cemented our approach to data-driven operations. We'll look to deploy **Intelligent O&M** at OCWUT's facilities to **drive energy savings, reduce chemical consumption, and improve process consistency**, all without adding operational complexity or capital expenditure.
