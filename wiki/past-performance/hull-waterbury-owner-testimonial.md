**CITY OF WATERBURY | WATERBURY, CT**

> Protection of the Naugatuck River is a key priority for our city. We went through a careful process to select our O&M contractor, and I'm pleased that we chose Jacobs. I've seen a lot of improvements since our partnership began in 2018, and I rest easier knowing that Kevin Dahl and his team are taking excellent care of our wastewater system.
