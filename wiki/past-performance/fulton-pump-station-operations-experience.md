# Jacobs Collection System Pump Station Experience

Jacobs has extensive experience in the operation and maintenance of pump stations, providing comprehensive services that ensure the reliability and efficiency of water and wastewater systems. As one of the largest water and wastewater O&M service providers in North America, Jacobs manages pump stations for cities, counties, service jurisdictions, and industrial facilities. Our approach integrates advanced technologies and preventive maintenance practices to optimize equipment performance and minimize downtime. Jacobs' expertise includes pipeline condition assessments, asset management, and trenchless rehabilitation solutions, which are crucial for maintaining the integrity of pump stations and associated infrastructure.

Moreover, Jacobs' O&M services are tailored to meet the specific needs of each client, leveraging over four decades of successful delivery in the industry. We employ skilled staff and innovative solutions to address challenges such as leaks, corrosion, and wet weather management. By focusing on sustainability and adapting to changing environmental conditions, Jacobs ensures that pump stations operate efficiently and effectively, contributing to the overall resilience of conveyance systems. Our commitment to quality service and risk management helps clients reduce costs and improve service quality, making Jacobs a trusted partner in the operation and maintenance of pump stations.
