# Edward C. Little Water Recycling Facility O&M

**West Basin Municipal Water District | El Segundo, CA**

The West Basin Municipal Water District recently awarded Jacobs a five-year O&M services contract for the nation's largest advanced water reuse treatment facility. This unparalleled system produces five distinct grades of custom water through nine complex treatment streams—making it the most technically sophisticated reuse operation in the U.S. We are now leading the transition from the District's previous contract operator, providing operational continuity informed by our deep expertise in membrane technologies, track record of compliance excellence, and commitment to ethical, transparent operations.

**Owner:** West Basin Municipal Water District, El Segundo, CA
**Contract dates:** 2025 – Ongoing
**Reference:** Susanna Li, Manager of Engineering, West Basin Municipal Water District, 17140 Avalon Blvd., Carson, CA 90746, 310.660.6238, susannal@westbasinca.gov
**Plant size (max monthly average):** 40 MGD

## Relevant experience

### Detailed description of the facilities operated

The 40-MGD Edward C. Little Water Recycling Facility (ECLWRF) is the only treatment facility to exist that produces five different types of fit-for-purpose recycled water—water for irrigation, cooling towers, seawater intrusion barrier, groundwater recharge, and low and high pressure boiler feeds. This recycled water conserves enough drinking water to meet the needs of up to 80,000 households for a year.

In addition to the ECLWRF, Jacobs also provides O&M services for its four satellite facilities: the Chevron Nitrification Treatment Plant, Juanita Millender-McDonald Carson Regional Water Recycling Plant, Torrance Refinery Water Recycling Plant, and Hyperion Effluent Pump Station. These facilities serve nearly 600 connections to municipal, industrial, and commercial businesses and are crucial in providing quality recycled water as a highly sustainable resource. Major customers include Los Angeles International Airport, SoFi Stadium, and Chevron El Segundo.

### Membrane treatment expertise

Our specialization in membrane treatment systems forms the backbone of West Basin's advanced reuse process. Our experts bring proven strategies to maintain peak membrane performance, maximize system reliability, and optimize operating costs. In parallel, we are upgrading maintenance and asset management protocols to extend equipment life, improve long-term resiliency, and drive operational efficiencies. Our focus on data-driven optimization helps identify cost-saving opportunities while maintaining the highest standards of service delivery.

### A seamless transition

We are currently leading the transition from the District's previous contract operator, Veolia. Our team is managing this critical handoff with care, providing operational continuity while introducing fresh ideas and a culture of iterative improvement. With Jacobs, West Basin gains a trusted partner who delivers on our promises and elevates performance through both innovation and integrity.

### Process optimization

Our services include use of our Digital OneWater suite of solutions, including Intelligent O&M, to optimize chemical and power consumption while improving sustainability and operational efficiencies. We also use in-house engineering capabilities to streamline projects and elevate critical infrastructure maintenance, improving reliability, and providing cost-effective high-quality water.

> West Basin contributes to one of the most vital roles in the communities we serve: Supplying quality recycled water as a highly sustainable resource to our customers. This O&M contract reflects our fervent commitment to investing in the long-term performance of our recycled water treatment facilities. The municipalities and businesses we serve have relied on West Basin's quality of service and product waters for decades. We look forward to working with Jacobs to continue that high-quality production our customers have come to know and trust.
>
> — Gloria Gray, West Basin Board President and Division II Director
