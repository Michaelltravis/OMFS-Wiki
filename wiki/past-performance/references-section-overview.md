# References Section Overview (Santa Monica SWIP Operations RFP, 2025)

## 2.3 References — Operational excellence backed by diverse project experience

Jacobs brings unmatched depth across water, wastewater, and advanced treatment facilities, demonstrated through a diverse portfolio of complex O&M projects. At West Basin's Edward C. Little Water Recycling Facility, the nation's largest and most technically sophisticated reuse system, Jacobs manages the O&M of nine advanced treatment trains that deliver five distinct grades of fit-for-purpose recycled water. For Soquel Creek Water District, Jacobs supported the design, construction, and startup, and will continue to partner with the District for long-term O&M of the Pure Water Soquel Advanced Water Purification facility, applying reverse osmosis, UV-AOP, and granular activated carbon filtration to safeguard groundwater and prevent seawater intrusion.

Jacobs' experience also extends to industrial systems such as the Turlock Irrigation District Zero Liquid Discharge Facility, where Jacobs optimizes advanced RO, crystallizers, and exchangers to support a 250 MW power plant. For the City of Clovis, Jacobs has provided long-term operations of its 2.8-mgd Water Reuse Facility since 2009, applying advanced membrane bioreactor technology to reduce costs and reliably meet stringent Title 22 reuse and NPDES permit requirements.

Together, these projects illustrate Jacobs' proven track record in optimizing treatment reliability, compliance, and sustainability, ensuring Santa Monica benefits from the collective strength of Jacobs' national delivery network.
