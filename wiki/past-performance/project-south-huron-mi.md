## Description of Services Provided

Jacobs provides 24/7 operations, maintenance, lab, regulatory compliance, industrial pretreatment, administrative, and management services for SHVUA's wastewater system, which supports a 24-MGD treatment facility and associated collection system assets. We also perform O&M of 36 miles of interceptor piping and two lift stations, including monitoring and preventive maintenance. Since 2019, Jacobs has led a successful staff transition and implemented targeted training programs to strengthen workforce capability. We improved plant performance by optimizing biosolids management and reducing solids volumes, chemical use, and energy demand, as well as enhancing automation, alarms, VFD installations, and recommended SCADA upgrades.

## Detailed Description of the Facilities Managed

**Capital Improvements.** Jacobs has supported capital planning and implementation for biosolids management, including alternatives evaluation and implementation of thermal hydrolysis and supporting infrastructure for Class A biosolids production. These improvements reduced residuals, improved efficiency, and provided a more sustainable disposal approach while maintaining continuous operations. Jacobs also supported automation upgrades, including VFD installations and SCADA enhancements.

**Treatment Systems and Processes.** The WWTP has a design capacity of 24 MGD, treats an average of 9.99 MGD, and serves roughly 90,000 residents. Treatment processes include screening, grit removal, primary clarification, and multi-stage anoxic/oxic activated sludge treatment with secondary clarification and disinfection. Solids are managed through thickening and stabilization.

**Collection System and Supporting Infrastructure.** The system includes 36 miles of interceptor piping and two lift stations. One LS conveys up to 1.3 MGD and the other can convey up to 39 MGD with VFD-equipped pumps. These assets are integrated through SCADA monitoring and control.

**Community and Workforce Engagement.** Jacobs successfully transitioned staff and maintained a collaborative work environment. Training programs and responsive workplace practices supported employee retention and operational continuity.

## Reuse guidance

Reach for this project when the evaluator's real worry is people, not process: an incumbent workforce that may or may not come across, institutional knowledge walking out the door, or a union transition. The narrative names staff transition, targeted training, retention, and operational continuity as credited outcomes rather than folding them into a generic "smooth transition" line, which is exactly what a transition-risk-driven evaluation wants to see. The second reusable thread is the biosolids modernization path — alternatives evaluation through thermal hydrolysis to Class A production, delivered while the plant stayed in continuous operation — which shows capital-planning contribution beyond routine O&M. Scale numbers to keep: 24 MGD design against 9.99 MGD average flow, ~90,000 residents served, 36 miles of interceptor, and the two lift stations at 1.3 MGD and 39 MGD.
