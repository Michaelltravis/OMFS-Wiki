# Soquel Creek Advanced Water Purification O&M

**Soquel, CA**

Jacobs provided support for the design, construction, start-up, and commissioning of Soquel Creek's 1.3-MGD advanced water treatment facility, and is now leading long-term operations of the facility. The Soquel Creek Water District utilized an innovative Operations Management At Risk (OMAR) contracting approach, with Jacobs as the intended O&M service provider engaged in the design phase for review and recommendations and provided substantial involvement in the construction phase and acceptance testing of the new facilities. We provided ongoing operability reviews throughout the multiple design milestones and worked very closely with the design-build (DB) team, the District, and their engineer to balance the upfront capital cost of the project and long-term operating cost.

**Owner:** Soquel Creek Water District
**Contract dates:** 2020 – Ongoing
**Reference:** Cameron Kostigen Mumper, Associate Manager – Water Resources, Soquel Creek Water District, 5180 Soquel Dr., Soquel, CA 95073, 831.475.8501 x115, cameronkm@soquelcreekwater.org
**Plant size (max monthly average):** 1.3 MGD

## Relevant experience

### Detailed description of the facilities operated

The facility is a component of the Pure Water Soquel Advanced Water Purification for Groundwater Replenishment and Seawater Intrusion Prevention Program and includes ozonation, membrane filtration, RO, and UV disinfection with advanced oxidation and post-treatment stabilization. The program treats municipal secondary effluent from the City of Santa Cruz for injection into its aquifer. This process addresses the seawater intrusion and groundwater contamination challenges in the aquifer.

Following acceptance testing of the DB facility, designed and constructed by Black & Veatch/Filanc Construction, Jacobs began operating and maintaining the facility for a 15-year initial term.

### Challenges and support from Jacobs

The Santa Cruz Mid-County Groundwater Basin is in a state of critical overdraft. To help address this challenge while also increasing the sustainability of its groundwater supply, this complex infrastructure program included the design and construction of multiple components, including new treatment facilities, conveyance, and injection wells. Our network of nearby California O&M projects, personnel, resources, and engineering offices have provided reliable support for these upgrades as well as long-term operations. Our advanced treatment technologists and senior O&M staff provided critical insights into operability during the design phase, and our experienced start-up and commissioning team supported the design-builder during that critical phase.

> From design through start-up and now operations, Jacobs has provided valuable support and service to Soquel Creek Water District. Their expertise in advanced treatment and regulatory compliance, combined with their collaborative approach with us and our design builder, helped bring Pure Water Soquel to life. We've welcomed Jacobs as an extension of our team and look forward to building a stronger relationship with them as we showcase this important project for our community.
>
> — Melanie Mow Schumacher, General Manager, Soquel Creek Water District
