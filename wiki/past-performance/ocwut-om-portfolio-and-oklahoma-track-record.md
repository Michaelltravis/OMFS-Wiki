# O&M Portfolio Scale, Oklahoma Track Record, and Reference Project Framing

*Delivering reliable wastewater O&M across multiple facilities simultaneously while navigating active capital programs, complex biosolids management, and evolving regulatory requirements takes more than operational know-how. It takes a partner with a proven track record doing exactly that, at scale, over the long term.* **That's what Jacobs brings to OCWUT** — relevant experience managing some of the largest and most complex treatment facilities, pumping assets, and solids programs, with the operational coordination to keep critical infrastructure running well.

Our **O&M portfolio spans more than 300 projects** across water and wastewater treatment, collection and distribution systems, water quality laboratories, stormwater management, biosolids, and facility services — covering the full breadth of municipal infrastructure. We've built lasting partnerships with major clients like Baltimore, Baton Rouge, Seattle, San Diego, Tucson, and Wilmington, with an **average relationship of 18 years and a 98% contract renewal rate**. Those aren't just numbers. They reflect the trust our clients place in us to protect their assets, maintain permit compliance, and deliver reliable service to the communities they serve. At **Baltimore's Back River WWTP (180 MGD)**, one of the largest outsourced wastewater treatment programs in the country, we stepped in, cleared a massive biosolids backlog, and restored operational performance under an active consent decree. In **Baton Rouge**, we operate two plants with combined peak capacity exceeding **300 MGD**, serving more than 222,000 residents. In **Wilmington, Delaware**, our team runs a **134-MGD** facility alongside a complex renewable energy biosolids program. In **Jackson, Mississippi**, our **multi-facility contract spans 145 MGD** across two plants and is larger in both scope and contract value than OCWUT's. These programs are among the most demanding outsourced O&M contracts in the country, and we've delivered on every one of them.

Over our **44 years serving clients across Oklahoma**, we've built a deep understanding of the regulatory, operational, and infrastructure landscape that shapes successful utility programs in the region, including permitting requirements, ODEQ compliance, and water quality standards.

We have **operated the City of Duncan's wastewater facilities since 1995**, administering its industrial pretreatment program (IPP) under ODEQ oversight. That's 30 years of working within the same state regulatory environment OCWUT operates every day and the relationships, institutional knowledge, and compliance track record that come with it. Our Oklahoma experience goes beyond operations: **we've worked directly with OCWUT's Utilities Department for more than a decade**, providing subject matter expertise in risk and resilience, human-machine interface (HMI) programming, cybersecurity, and business process improvements. We've **developed training plans for new OCWUT staff across multiple divisions**, meaning we already know your systems, your processes, and your people. In **Bixby**, we hold a **10-year contract to operate and maintain** a 2.8-MGD sequencing batch reactor (SBR) water reclamation facility and 18 lift stations.
