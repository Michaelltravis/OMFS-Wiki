# Spokane County Regional Water Reclamation Facility DBO — Delivery and Performance

Jacobs was the lead DBO firm, responsible for overall project management, design, permitting, construction general conditions, safety, quality, equipment procurement, instrumentation and control system integration, and facility commissioning. Major subcontracted work packages included site/civil, structural, architectural, mechanical, and electrical construction.

Jacobs operates and maintains the SCRWRF. The constructed facility’s treatment process includes chemically enhanced primary treatment in conjunction with membrane bioreactor technology to produce Class A effluent reuse water quality for initial discharge into the Spokane River. The plant’s sustainable design allows energy recovered through digester gas production and co-generation systems to produce electricity to help run the facility. All non-process buildings are designed to meet LEED™ Silver criteria.

Jacobs started up the facility approximately seven months ahead of schedule. The facility received its National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System (NPDES) permit on November 30, 2011, and effluent discharge to the Spokane River began on December 1, 2011. The facility additionally holds a permit for air quality from Spokane Regional Clean Air Agency. Additionally, the facility has remained in compliance, including through capital improvements and construction, with all effluent nutrient limits (50 PPB phosphorus and 0.25 PPM ammonia limits)—some of the most stringent limits in the nation, especially during summer months.

Jacobs conducts out-of-scope work to identify potential capacity improvements using models. We have already completed capital improvements to the influent screening, post-aerobic digester, and supplementary carbon systems. The facility was commissioned in 2011 and has since been under construction.

Due to a shortage of qualified local staff in the Spokane area, we’ve staffed the facility as leanly as possible. To ensure continued success, Jacobs has proactively supplemented the team by drawing on our deep bench of talent—including experienced operators from other projects, as well as regional technical and maintenance managers, compliance specialists, and safety professionals. This strategic support from across the company allows us to maintain high performance despite local staffing constraints.

Section 5: Environmental Compliance Record provides a thorough report of any and all permit exceedances, citations, violations, penalties, and defaults throughout the Contract.

Jacobs has been operating the SCRWRF since 2011, and has excelled at creating a culture of safety, enhancing efficiency and performance through flow capacity optimization, achieving sustainable advancements with calcium carbonate implementation, implementing adaptive maintenance strategies, and creating positive environmental impacts. The team has been working on piloting the new fine screen at the headworks, cleaning the anaerobic digester, and replacing the foul air collection piping in the headworks.

> “A key to the delivery success, and to accomplishing the three major goals, was the partnering sessions at the beginning of the project, and the true partnership between the County’s project manager and the Jacobs management team that continued throughout the design-build phase of the project. This was the first Design-Build-Operate project for Spokane County, and the largest public works project ever completed by the County, and we are very satisfied with the results.”
>
> — Bruce Rawls, PE, Spokane County Utilities Director
