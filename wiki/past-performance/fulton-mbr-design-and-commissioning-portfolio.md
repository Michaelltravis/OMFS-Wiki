# Jacobs Wastewater Engineering and MBR Experience

Jacobs’ membrane bioreactor experience is unparalleled. Our thought leadership began in the late 1990s, when we performed demonstration testing and designed several small MBR facilities. In 2003, Jordan Jones and Goulding (now Jacobs) assisted the County in soliciting proposals for technology and design/ build services for the JCEC. In 2004, we converted a conventional treatment plant in Traverse City, MI to MBR. About the same time, we designed our first MBR in Georgia, the 2.5-mgd plant in Woodstock. Many larger plant designs followed, including Gwinnett County’s 22-mgd Yellow River WRF and even larger plants in King County, WA and North Las Vegas, NV.

Our largest MBR plant in operation is the 53-mgd Changi WRP in Singapore. By now, 44 of our MBR designs have been commissioned, and additional designs are underway. Once the 212-mgd Tuas WRP in Singapore commissions in several years, it will be the largest in the world.

Most of these plants use Veolia hollow fiber membranes. Our designs in Piedmont, SC and several others use Kubota flat sheet membranes. Changi WRP and several others use DuPont hollow fiber membranes.

Our MBR projects in Traverse City, MI, Spokane County, WA, and Clovis, CA have been selected as references for this proposal. All three are examples of design/build/operate projects, demonstrating our breadth of MBR expertise.
