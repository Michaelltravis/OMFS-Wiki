# Waterbury Wastewater System O&M — Treatment Systems, Processes, and Community Involvement

**CITY OF WATERBURY | WATERBURY, CT**

**Treatment Systems and Processes.** The system uses preliminary screening and grit removal followed by primary treatment, secondary aeration and clarifiers, seasonal UV, and wet weather sodium hypochlorite disinfection. Tertiary filters are under construction. The plant has a 27-MGD design flow and <54-MGD wet weather flow. There is also chemical addition for total phosphorus (TP) removal.

**Community Involvement.** The Waterbury team decided to sponsor a newer program called the Naugatuck River Brigade focused on the health of the Naugatuck River and protecting this valuable resource. Jacobs plans to volunteer staff hours to help provide riverbank cleanups in the coming year.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the two-part structure — a tight unit-process inventory that an evaluator can match against their own plant, followed by a short community-involvement paragraph naming a specific local program the operating team chose to sponsor. The community paragraph is the reusable move; it converts a project description from a capability list into evidence that the operating staff behave like residents of the watershed.

Pursuit-specific: every process element (seasonal UV, wet weather sodium hypochlorite, tertiary filters under construction, chemical TP removal) and both flow figures belong to Waterbury and must be swapped for the reference facility actually being cited. The community program must be named accurately and its sponsorship confirmed before the claim is repeated.
