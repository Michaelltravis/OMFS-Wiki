# Pump Station Fleet Scale and Station-Specific Maintenance

**Jacobs operates more than 700 lift stations at 75 plants, which includes 2,490 pumps. Pumps range from 0.5 to 200 hp, and include submersible pumps, dry well submersible pumps, and are from manufacturers ABBA to Zoeller and everything in between, including, Flyght, Trimmer, and Vaughn.**

Each pump station presents its own set of unique characteristics and challenges. Our team's extensive experience and deep knowledge will be instrumental in addressing these challenges effectively. By leveraging our expertise, we can ensure successful system maintenance, tailored to the specific needs and conditions of each pump station. This approach not only enhances operational efficiency but also ensures the longevity and reliability of the pump stations.
