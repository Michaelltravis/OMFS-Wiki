# Deliverables Commitment: Annual, Transition, and Asset-Management Plans

We confirm we meet all contract requirements and understand [CLIENT] seeks cost effective and reliable wastewater system O&M management services. Within 10 days following Notice to Proceed, we’ll submit a detailed Transition Plan identifying key milestones required to ensure completion of the Transition and readiness for full operations within the specified 45-day timeframe.

During this period, we’ll also sit down with [CLIENT] to develop and agree on a summary checklist of all commitments, timelines, and documents to be provided throughout the term, as well as our process for fulfilling the requirements. This exercise will ensure agreement on the right timelines for when things must be developed, delivered, submitted, and reviewed.

Our in-depth understanding of the Project Deliverables listed in Sample Agreement Article 61, combined with our extensive experience providing comprehensive O&M services—including at membrane wastewater treatment facilities of comparable size, enables us to efficiently and effectively prepare these plans and reports, leveraging proven approaches to accelerate development and deliver high quality.

|Deliverable or plan|JC Solutions’ understanding|Due|Source approach|
|---|---|---|---|
|Annual Budget Projection|Submit an annual detailed budget projection, including expected expenditures and operational costs, for [CLIENT]’s review and approval.|120 days preceding each contract year|Page 2-90|
|Annual Operations & Maintenance Report|Submit a comprehensive report annually detailing operational performance, maintenance activities, compliance records, and significant operational events.|Within 60 days of end of each contract year|Page 2-90|
|Annual Settlement|Provide reconciliation of actual expenditures versus budgeted amounts, with adjustments made annually between [CLIENT] and JC Solutions based on documented expenditures.|Within 45 days after the end of each contract year.|Page 2-90|
|Capital Improvement Plan|Prepare and update annually a multi-year capital improvement plan addressing major upgrades, replacements, or expansions required at facilities.|By August 1 of each year|Page 2-70|
|Customer Service Plan|Develop a customer service plan outlining procedures for addressing public inquiries, service complaints, and facilitating communication with stakeholders.|Final before commencement date, and updated annually|Page 2-89|
|Daily, Weekly, Monthly and Severe Weather Operations Reports|Submit detailed operational status reports daily, weekly, monthly, and additional specific reports during severe weather or critical events.|As indicated|Page 2-90|
|Emergency Response & Disaster Preparedness Plan|Submit and implement a detailed emergency response and disaster preparedness plan, addressing contingencies and ensuring operational continuity.|Final within 60 days of the contract date, and updated annually|Page 2-86|
|Enterprise Asset Management/CMMS & Operations Software Contract|Maintain an enterprise asset management or CMMS integrated with operational software for asset tracking and maintenance scheduling.|Software contract within 15 days of commencement date; fully functional within 60 days from the commencement date|Page 2-69|

## Reuse guidance

Use this crosswalk when an RFP scores plan and report commitments. Reconcile every due date, reporting cadence, and commercial row to the contract before reuse. The source table continues with equipment, exit, records, safety, and staffing deliverables on pages 15–16.
