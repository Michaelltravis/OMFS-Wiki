## Reuse guidance

Universal: the OT/cybersecurity block of four rows (asset platform, backup and version control, privileged access, plus the asset-tag fabricator) transfers to nearly any water or wastewater O&M pursuit with minimal edit, and each carries a paired savings number. "Deliver the full value of ISO 55001 standards without certification costs" is the reusable formulation of the asset-management offer.

Pursuit-specific: the advocacy and workforce-development row, the interplant pump shims, and the odor early-warning configuration.
