# On-Site I&C Technicians Backed by Regional OT/SCADA and Cybersecurity SMEs

## Robert Coons and the On-Site I&C Technicians

This team will bring day-to-day field presence at the plants — calibrating instrumentation, maintaining PLCs, troubleshooting SCADA-connected field issues, and handling electrical work. Robert has extensive I&C experience and will work within [CLIENT]'s standards on every system he touches.

## Mayo Miller — O&M OT/Cybersecurity SME

Mayo is Jacobs' regional O&M expert for OT and cybersecurity, drawing on his current SCADA supervisor role across multiple WWTPs and pump stations. He'll support the on-site team on OT operational issues and cybersecurity compliance questions, and coordinate Jacobs' broader O&M resources whenever [CLIENT] needs them.

## Yash Tanna — SCADA/OT Engineering SME

Yash has been directly supporting [CLIENT]'s SCADA infrastructure since 2024, giving him firsthand familiarity with the Wonderware/AVEVA Galaxy architecture in use today. AVEVA-certified on Application Server, Historian Server, and InTouch, he'll be available on-call for SCADA programming, PLC troubleshooting, HMI design, and integration support whenever [CLIENT] needs the bandwidth.
