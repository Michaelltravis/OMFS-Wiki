# Day-to-Day Communication, Governance Meetings, and Public Engagement

We'll work hard to foster day-to-day communication between [CLIENT] and Jacobs staff to empower decision making on all levels and ensure immediate attention and response. Project Manager Kevin Dahl and the entire team will promote the flow of information and transparency. In addition to frequent informal daily communications, our PgM approach will include formal weekly, monthly, and quarterly meetings to review plant performance and maintenance activities in addition to the formal Governance Committees' meetings. Kevin will also ensure effective and transparent communication with [CLIENT]'s Commission and at public meetings as requested and directed by [CLIENT]. Our Executive Sponsors will support Kevin with these important communications as necessary. We provide information on how we'll communicate with the community and ratepayers in *Section 2.2.6, Odor Control and Responding to Odor Complaints* and in *Section V, Regional Partnerships*. **Exhibit IV-9** outlines communications with [CLIENT].

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-layer communication model — informal daily contact, a formal weekly/monthly/quarterly performance rhythm, and chartered governance committees — plus the named project manager owning public and governing-body communication with executive sponsor backup. Reuse the structure in any management-approach section.

Pursuit-specific: the project manager's name, the governing body's name (commission, board, council, authority), and the cross-references to the odor-complaint and regional-partnership sections.
