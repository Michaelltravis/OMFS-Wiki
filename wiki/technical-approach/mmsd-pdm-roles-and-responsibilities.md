# Roles and Responsibilities for a Disciplined PdM Program

A disciplined PdM program relies on clearly defined roles. [CLIENT] is a regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities and a regional conveyance system.

- The **Reliability Engineer** will serve as the technical and analytical lead, integrating Level 1, 2, and 3 data, refining rulesets, interpreting trends, and coordinating with [CLIENT]'s asset management lead. The Reliability Engineer will also partner with SCADA and asset management specialists to ensure data quality, alert accuracy, and asset condition scoring.

- The **SCADA/OT team** will support configuration of PdM points, historian routing, and alarm parameters, working within [CLIENT]'s cybersecurity expectations.

- The **Maintenance and Asset Management Teams** will ensure that PdM-driven work orders are executed, documented, and tied to the appropriate asset-level records in the CMMS.

- **Jacobs' SMEs**, including Monty Sedlak, Matt Crowley, Amy Dembinski, John Rickermann, and John Loucks-Powell, will support the onsite team at each phase with technical depth in analytics, maintenance strategy, SCADA integration, and governance support.
