# Transparent Partnership, Community Stewardship, and Facility Appearance

We are all in on delivering cost-effective, reliable O&M services that help [CLIENT] protect and optimize its investments in critical wastewater infrastructure. Our approach blends deep technical expertise—especially in advanced treatment and MBR systems—with a strong commitment to compliance, transparency, and community partnership. We bring a people-first culture, proven performance, and innovative tools that drive efficiency, improve decision-making, and support long-term operational excellence. With a focus on asset stewardship, workforce development, and continuous improvement, we’re ready to be [CLIENT]'s trusted partner—today and into the future.

Our approach to partnering with [CLIENT] centers on transparency and shared accountability for operational excellence. Through a KPI dashboard, we will provide [CLIENT] staff with realtime access to comprehensive performance metrics across all three facilities, enabling collaborative decision-making based on accurate, current data. This transparency extends beyond digital tools to our communication practices—we commit to regular operations meetings, proactive issue notifications, and open-door access to our management team. Meaningful partnership requires more than contractual compliance; it demands genuine collaboration in addressing operational challenges, implementing innovations, and planning for future needs. Our team will actively engage with [CLIENT] staff to transfer knowledge, develop solutions jointly, and celebrate shared successes. This collaborative approach creates a foundation for continuous improvement as we work together to elevate operations across all [CLIENT] facilities, ultimately enhancing service to the community while optimizing resource utilization and ensuring regulatory compliance with appropriate safety margins.

At virtually all of our O&M projects, we are active members in the communities we serve. Community involvement is a foundational element of our O&M approach because it helps build and support sustainable communities. Our staff are residents who live and work in the communities ourselves and serve as stewards of public health and safety. We recognize the importance of connecting with citizens and customers and providing the commitment that their utilities are operated by a provider who cares about their wellbeing.

We recognize that [FACILITY B] is highly visible to the public through tours, environmental programming, and community spaces. The plant’s appearance and performance affect the community’s impression of the facility and also reflects the onsite team’s pride in the facility. We will build on the impressive public education and community involvement program as well as build further partnerships in the community to elevate the stature of [CLIENT]'s facilities and community programs. Additionally, we will establish facility appearance standards into our preventive maintenance tracking and work-order system through CMMS beginning at Transition to ensure the continued reputation of the facility in the community and with other stakeholders.

## Reuse guidance

Use the real-time-access and visible-facility language only where the client authorizes it and a specific dashboard/CMMS implementation plan exists. Replace the outreach and appearance commitments with the target facility’s actual public interface.
