# Phase 3 Automation Integration Strategies, Results, and Benefits (Exhibit)

**EXHIBIT 1-24.** *Phase 3 Automation Integration Strategies and Benefits*

| Strategy | What it does | Result | Benefit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Resilience automation** | Keep critical equipment running predictably through routine variability and abnormal events. | Automatic pump failover, run-time and wear leveling, and clear in-service/out-of-service tracking in SCADA will expose weak points and prevent cascading failures. | Fewer cascading emergencies; clearer picture of asset resilience. |
| **Process flow responsive control** | Continuously adjust chemical feed and air delivery to match real-time demand. | Operators will stop chasing valves and pump speeds — the system will hold setpoints tied to lab and analytical data (e.g., mg/L dose), even through wet-weather transitions, influent shifts, or mode changes. | Fewer manual interventions; operators available for higher-value oversight. |
| **Process feedback control loops** | Automatically respond to analytical measurements (DO, disinfectant residual, ammonia), tightening control around KPIs. | Deviations self-correct in a traceable way, stabilizing treatment performance and strengthening compliance margins. | Predictable performance; smoother path to full intelligent optimization. |
