# Fermentation Zone Operating Variables and Full-Scale Testing

At [FACILITY A], a county water reclamation facility, the fermentation process is affected by several interrelated operational decisions. First, the fermentation process can be operated with one, two, or three tanks in series (**Exhibit 2-23**). Second, the flow rate of its two inputs can be adjusted—fine screen effluent (FSE) and return activated sludge (RAS). Third, the two mixers in each tank can be cycled on and off to allow biomass to accumulate on the floor.

With respect to testing, JC Solutions will pick up where our predecessor leaves off and systematically explore the options until the best combination is found.

The strategy of starting and stopping mixers could also be used at [FACILITY B] and [FACILITY C], the other two water reclamation facilities in the system. Modifying a control system to start and stop mixers using timers is straightforward, and Jacobs has experience doing this at many plants.
