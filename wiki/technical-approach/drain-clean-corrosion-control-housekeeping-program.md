# Drain and Clean, Cathodic Protection, and Buildings and Grounds Program

## Drain and Clean Program

Schedule 15 defines a comprehensive drain and clean scope across all facilities: biannual, triannual, annual, and once-per-5-year-term frequencies for dozens of structures. We'll develop the Annual Drain and Clean Plan in coordination with [CLIENT] — a southcentral US municipal water utility trust operating four wastewater treatment plants and a major pump station — integrating each activity into the operations schedule to minimize process disruptions and maximize the inspection and repair opportunities that draindown events create. This plan directly supports PM-1 KPI compliance: 100% completion of the annual Drain and Clean Plan, with a fee at risk per Schedule 5.

## Cathodic Protection and Corrosion Control

We'll inspect cathodic protection systems per RFP §2.2.3 to verify integrity and operability and apply protective coatings during drain and clean activities to minimize corrosion and extend structural life. Proactive corrosion monitoring is particularly important given the elevated H₂S environment at North Canadian and Deer Creek, where we observed significant concrete deterioration along primary clarifier weirs and structural corrosion in headworks buildings during our site visits.

## Building, Grounds, and Housekeeping

Schedule 3 defines detailed housekeeping standards, and we'll integrate every one of them into NexGen as scheduled work orders so they're tracked, measurable, and auditable alongside our mechanical PM program. Key elements include daily interior cleaning and litter removal, bi-weekly floor maintenance, semi-annual window washing and wall cleaning, HVAC system maintenance, pest control, and exterior painting and corrosion touch-up.

For grounds maintenance across the extensive acreage at North Canadian, Deer Creek, and Chisholm Creek, we'll subcontract lawn and landscaping services based on our assessment that dedicated grounds subcontractors deliver more consistent results than in-house mowing programs at facilities of this scale. South Canadian's smaller footprint will be maintained in-house. Any housekeeping issues identified during [CLIENT]'s monthly inspections that remain uncorrected for 30 days trigger fees at risk — **our integrated CMMS tracking prevents items from falling through the cracks by assigning owners, due dates, and escalation triggers to every task.**

## Reuse guidance

Universal: framing draindowns as inspection and repair opportunities rather than a cleaning obligation; applying protective coatings while structures are already drained; loading housekeeping standards into the CMMS as tracked, auditable work orders; and the owners-due-dates-escalation triggers answer to any client worried about items falling through the cracks.

Pursuit-specific: schedule and RFP section references, KPI identifiers and at-risk fee mechanisms, inspection cadence and the 30-day correction window, facility names, and the specific corrosion observations. Note the housekeeping element list is drawn from one contract's standards — rebuild it from the target scope.
