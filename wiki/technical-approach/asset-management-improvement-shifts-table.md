# Asset Management Improvement Shifts

As shown in **Exhibit 5-13**, we bring **visible change** to how [CLIENT]'s assets are managed, maintained, and improved, shifting **from reactive, fragmented practices to a planned, accountable, and self-performed program**. The exhibit is laid out in three columns — Current, Jacobs' Approach, and Benefit to [CLIENT] — with an arrow between the first two and an equals sign before the third:

| Current | | Jacobs' Approach | | Benefit to [CLIENT] |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reactive work | → | Planned PM/PdM | = | Fewer repeat failures |
| More subcontracted routine work | → | Greater self-performance | = | Faster response and accountability |
| Fragmented asset information | → | Cartegraph-centered visibility | = | Better [CLIENT] decision-making |
| Housekeeping as time allows | → | Inspection-ready facilities | = | Stronger stewardship and public confidence |

**Proven asset management and maintenance success.** We successfully implement the level of asset management and maintenance [CLIENT] is seeking as a **standard, proven approach across our wastewater O&M projects**. Significant maintenance projects are also supported through regional O&M maintenance resources that supplement on-site teams. Examples of maintenance successes on other O&M projects are included in Exhibit 5-14.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-column "current state → proposed practice → client benefit" grammar, and the four themes it covers (reactive vs. planned maintenance, subcontracted vs. self-performed routine work, fragmented vs. single-platform asset information, and housekeeping as discretionary vs. inspection-ready facilities). These four map to the complaints municipal clients most often voice about an incumbent operator, which is why the device works so well in a displacement pursuit. Pursuit-specific: every entry in the "Current" column must come from an actual site walk, the RFP's own language, or the client's stated frustrations — inventing a current state the evaluators do not recognize destroys the exhibit's credibility; and the named CMMS platform in row three must be the platform the target client actually uses or is moving toward. Pair with [CMMS-Driven Asset Management and Maintenance Program](cmms-driven-asset-management-maintenance-program.md), which is the prose this exhibit sits inside.
