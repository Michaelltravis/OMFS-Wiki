# Innovation Workshop Appendix — Proposed Agenda and Delivered-Workshop Examples

## Appendix D — Innovation Workshop Agenda and Examples

Potential agenda topics for [CLIENT]'s first Annual Innovation Workshop are presented below, demonstrating how the session can be structured to align with [CLIENT]'s priorities and drive practical, results-oriented discussions. [CLIENT] is a coastal New England municipality operating a 3.07-MGD wastewater treatment facility and a 42-mile collection system.

**Sample agendas from previous Innovation Workshops for Traverse City, MI and Wilmington, NC are included below, demonstrating how these sessions have successfully driven alignment on priorities and implementation of actionable improvements.**

## Delivered-workshop agenda — June 5, 2024

Workshop 8:30 a.m. – 4:30 p.m.; Happy Hour 4:30 p.m. – 6:00 p.m. The Westin Wilmington, Caesar Rodney Ballroom, 818 Shipyard Dr, Wilmington, DE 19801.

| Time | Session |
|---|---|
| 8:30 AM – 8:45 AM | Welcome and Introductions |
| 8:45 AM – 9:30 AM | Keynote — Becoming a World Class Utility While Properly Managing an Uncertain Future |
| 9:30 AM – 10:30 AM | The Role of Process Intensification in Meeting New Regulatory Requirements |
| 10:30 AM – 10:45 AM | Break |
| 10:45 AM – 11:30 AM | PFAS: Potential Impacts and Solutions for the Water Sector |
| 11:30 AM – 12:00 NN | Energy Optimization and Sector Decarbonization Part I |
| 12:00 NN – 1:00 PM | Lunch |
| 1:00 PM – 1:45 PM | Energy Optimization and Sector Decarbonization Part II |
| 1:45 PM – 2:45 PM | Leveraging Digital OneWater for Improved Utility Operation |
| 2:45 PM – 3:00 PM | Break |
| 3:00 PM – 3:45 PM | Adaptive Planning: De-risking Future Uncertainty |
| 3:45 PM – 4:30 PM | Additional Discussion & Closing Remarks |

The shape of the day is the reusable part: a short welcome, a keynote that frames the utility's strategic position, three to four SME-led technical blocks of 45 to 60 minutes each (with a single theme allowed to run across two sessions), a working lunch, a forward-looking planning block late in the afternoon, and open discussion to close. Topics span the regulatory horizon (PFAS, new permit requirements and process intensification), the cost and carbon horizon (energy optimization and sector decarbonization), the digital horizon (digital OneWater for improved utility operation), and the planning horizon (adaptive planning and de-risking future uncertainty). An optional post-session happy hour keeps client staff, utility leadership, and the operating team together informally after the agenda closes.

## Client validation

A client whose utility has hosted these sessions describes the value in their own words: the workshops "provide a wealth of knowledge from industry experts who brainstorm ideas for the future direction of our utility that are of great value to the City and our ratepayers."

— Vincent Carroccia, Deputy Commissioner of Public Works, 302.576.2620

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the appendix device itself — proposed agenda plus at least one real, dated agenda from a workshop already delivered — is what turns an innovation-workshop commitment from a promise into a demonstrated practice, and it transfers to any O&M pursuit that offers an annual technical forum. The day's structure and the four topic horizons (regulatory, energy/carbon, digital, adaptive planning) are reusable as a template.

Pursuit-specific: the proposed first-workshop topics must be drawn from the target client's stated priorities, not copied from this list — the credibility of the device depends on the topics obviously belonging to that client's system. Verify that named reference utilities are willing to have their agendas reproduced, and re-confirm the client quote, title, and phone number with the speaker before external use.
