# Six Key Operational Focus Areas (Exhibit Summaries)

**EXHIBIT IV-14. KEY OPERATIONAL FOCUS AREAS** (graphic asset `355_007CAM_2`)

**COLLECTION SYSTEM & WET-WEATHER FLOW MANAGEMENT.** We will apply predictive forecasting, coordinated tunnel and WRF operations, and disciplined decision-making to support [CLIENT]'s goals of zero overflows, reduced basement backups, and optimized energy use. Our approach emphasizes proactive readiness, resilient wet-weather response, and close coordination with [CLIENT] management.

**LIQUID TREATMENT PROCESS CONTROL.** Using disciplined, data-driven process control, we will integrate primary, secondary, and disinfection processes to optimize biological performance, aeration, solids management, energy use, and effluent quality—ensuring stable operations and continuous compliance.

**SOLIDS PROCESSING & BIOSOLIDS PRODUCT PRODUCTION.** Building on [CLIENT]'s strong performance, we will optimize sludge handling, energy recovery, and product quality across both WRFs. Our approach enhances reliability, maximizes renewable energy use, and ensures consistent biosolids product production through targeted optimization and disciplined risk management.

**ENERGY OPTIMIZATION.** We will operate power generation, heat recovery, and high-energy treatment processes as a single integrated system. Through predictive analytics and systemwide energy modeling, we will reduce energy costs and demand charges while supporting [CLIENT]'s 2035 Vision for increased renewable energy without compromising treatment performance.

**CHEMICAL OPTIMIZATION.** Guided by regulatory compliance, transparency, cost management, and innovation, Jacobs uses data science and historical best-practice analysis to deliver defensible reductions in chemical use while maintaining stable operations, full compliance, and clear operator visibility into dosing decisions.

**ODOR CONTROL.** Our proactive, predictive, and community-focused approach emphasizes odor prevention, rapid identification of potential odor issues, and disciplined response. By combining advanced H2S modeling, real-time monitoring, and transparent communication, we reduce odor incidents and strengthen public trust.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the abstract pattern — method clause, then benefit clause, then the client's own stated goal — and the deliberate compliment to the incumbent operation ("building on [CLIENT]'s strong performance") that lets a challenger propose optimization without criticism. Pursuit-specific: the focus areas themselves, the client's goal years and targets, and the exhibit number. Pairs with [Process monitoring and control for system-wide insight](mmsd-process-monitoring-control-system-wide-insight.md), which introduces this exhibit, and with the focus-area detail blocks that follow it.
