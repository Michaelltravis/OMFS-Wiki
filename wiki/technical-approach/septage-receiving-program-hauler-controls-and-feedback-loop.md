# Septage Receiving Program — Intake Discipline, Hauler Controls, and Solids Feedback Loop

## Septage receiving rigor

Site observations noted limited testing of received septage loads beyond pH measurement and visual inspection. Several porta-potty loads were received during our visit. The septage program is regulated under Schedule 6 and tied directly to the biosolids program, so intake discipline has direct downstream consequences.

## Septage receiving program

As the sole septage receiving point for the [CLIENT] service area, we'll take a disciplined approach to operating the receiving station under Schedule 6, recognizing it directly shapes the quality of your biosolids land application program. We'll staff a trained attendant at the receiving station during all required hours and execute the process shown in **Exhibit 1-27** (**EXHIBIT 1-27.** *Daily Process for Septage Receiving Jacobs Will Follow*).

We'll also enforce hauler compliance with site rules: exterior-only tanker washing, no interior hose use, and water-fill privileges limited to portable toilet haulers under strict hose protocols. Any hauler who fails to comply will have water use suspended. Beyond Schedule 6 compliance, Jacobs will evaluate septage receiving logs for trends in load characteristics that may contribute to downstream odor or solids quality issues—connecting what enters at the receiving station to what ultimately reaches the biosolids program. This data-driven feedback loop will strengthen both the septage program and overall solids management. **See our Sludge and Solids Management Plans** in **Section 5 – Required Plans Submitted with the Proposal**.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the causal argument that septage intake discipline determines biosolids quality and station odor; the staffed-attendant commitment during all required receiving hours; the three enforceable hauler site rules (exterior-only tanker washing, no interior hose use, water-fill privileges limited to portable toilet haulers) with a stated consequence for non-compliance; and the log-trending feedback loop from receiving records into the solids program. This is strong incumbent-displacement material wherever the current operator's intake screening is thin.
