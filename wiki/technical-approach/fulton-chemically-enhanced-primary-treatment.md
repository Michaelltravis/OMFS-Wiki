# Chemically Enhanced Primary Treatment — Metal Salt Selection and Jar Testing

Chemically enhanced primary treatment (CEPT) uses chemicals to improve primary clarifier performance. Typically, ferric chloride and anionic polymer are used. Alum is not typically used because of its impact on anaerobic digestion. However, [FACILITY A], a county water reclamation facility, lacks anaerobic digestion, so alum could be considered.

The [FACILITY A] design included ferric chloride feed upstream of the primary clarifiers, suggesting that operation in a CEPT mode had been considered. Now, the plant operates with Phos-Zorb, an aluminum-based product, which is better than iron-based products for membranes and UV lamps. However, when ferric chloride is fed to primary treatment rather than secondary treatment, it has minimal impact on membrane and UV lamps. Use of two metal salts at the plant, one for primary treatment and other for secondary treatment, could be considered.

**How It Benefits [CLIENT]:** *Potentially reduces chemical requirements and associated costs, enhancing cost efficiency and reducing the amount of truck deliveries to the plant.*
