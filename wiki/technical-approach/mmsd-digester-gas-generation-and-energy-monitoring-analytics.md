# Digester Gas Power Generation, Energy Monitoring, and AI-Powered Analytics

**Power Generation at the Second Facility.** Digester gas produced at the second water reclamation facility has the potential to meet nearly all the facility's power and heat needs for the digesters and the buildings if gas production and engine uptimes are maximized. Our approach includes:

- **Maximizing engine uptime** through disciplined PM/PdM routines, targeted parts inventory management, and operational optimization including root cause failure analysis and action plans.

- **Optimizing demand charges,** including an enhanced load shedding program using predictive analytics and coordinated operation of high-energy equipment.

- **Increasing digester production** in alignment with [CLIENT]'s ongoing efforts by evaluating additional high-strength waste streams, optimizing mixing, and integrating planned capital projects such as adding more active digestion capacity.

*Digester gas may meet all of the facility's power needs.*

**Energy Monitoring and Optimization.** Energy is one of [CLIENT]'s most significant shared-cost items and a key metric for sustainability. Jacobs will implement a comprehensive monitoring and analytics program for [CLIENT], a regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities, using SCADA data, power meters, and Jacobs' Intelligent O&M tools (**AI-powered analytics)** to:

- Track daily and cumulative energy consumption by facility (depending on power meter availability), process area, and major equipment such as aeration blowers, pumping stations, sludge dryers, and dewatering systems.

- Identify the most energy-intensive processes and recommend operational adjustments to reduce peak-demand charges and overall usage.

- Forecast future energy demand using Jacobs' Intelligent O&M and recommend control adjustment guidance to operators in real time to lower total energy costs without compromising performance.

- Continue [CLIENT]'s efforts to reduce both energy usage and demand charges while maintaining full compliance.

At comparable large-scale utilities, this approach has provided **20% energy savings** and will help [CLIENT] meet its 2035 Vision target of 80% renewable-energy use from internal sources and 100% renewable from all sources. [CLIENT] will receive monthly variance reports and optimization recommendations through the Energy Purchasing Committee.
