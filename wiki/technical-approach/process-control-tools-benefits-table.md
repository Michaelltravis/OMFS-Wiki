# Process Control Tool Set and Client Benefits

**EXHIBIT 1-14.** *Jacobs' Tools for Operational, Process, and Compliance Success Built on Best Practices and Decades of Experience*

| TOOL/PROCEDURE | WHAT JACOBS WILL DO | BENEFIT TO [CLIENT] |
|---|---|---|
| **UPCPs** | UPCPs outline our operations road map, establish the KPIs and parameters we track, drive process adjustments, direct continuous improvement, and clearly establish lines of communication and control. UPCPs also provide the detail operators and maintenance staff need to make informed decisions. | Documented procedures and process targets shared with [CLIENT], management, O&M, and laboratory staff confirm a common purpose and goal. Detailed information maximizes efficiency and minimizes errors and downtime. |
| **Mass Balance** | A mass balance solids report provides a comprehensive accounting of solids entering, accumulating, and leaving the system at each facility. | Enables accurate solids inventory control and identifies accumulation points relative to design capacity. Supports informed process adjustments, including wasting rates and dewatering schedules, and aids in forecasting solids handling needs — particularly important given the interdependencies between the North Canadian, Deer Creek, and Chisholm Creek solids programs. |
| **Sampling Plan** | Defines sampling locations, sampling map, parameters at each location, analytical methods, chain-of-custody techniques, and preservation procedures. Developed in coordination with the Sampling and Testing QA/QC Plan (Schedule 3). | A properly written and implemented sampling plan is critical to compliant, efficient facility operation. Reliable, accurate data starts with representative sampling — the foundation of effective solids balances and process control. |
| **Sample Tracking Tool (STT)** | Used by operations staff to monitor and track process and compliance sampling and analysis requirements. Developed at the start of each year; samples are checked off by the collector and verified same day by a second independent party. | Confirms that samples and analyses are performed at the locations and frequencies outlined in the NPDES permits and Scope of Work. Provides quality assurance to make sure nothing is missed. |
| **Data Management (Lab + SCADA/Instruments)** | Data collection driven by the Sampling Plan, collected through lab analysis or online instrumentation and entered manually or directly from SCADA. Jacobs will use Hach WIMS to support our process control system (PCS). | Direct access to data management software allows [CLIENT] to query the database for specific data. Regular reports provide a clear summary of how each plant and unit process is performing. Data exported into our PCS enables regional support staff to analyze trends and assist on-site operations in real-time decisions and optimization. |
| **SOPs** | SOPs document safe work practices and consistent operations of equipment and systems. They contain precautionary safety steps followed by startup, shutdown, and routine monitoring procedures, plus the detailed information needed to complete work properly, safely, and efficiently. | Critical SOPs will be developed first during transition, with new SOPs added as required, such as when new CIP equipment starts up or regulatory requirements evolve. SOPs maintain consistent procedures across all four facilities and Witcher, supporting staff safety, operations performance, and equipment reliability. |
| **Operator Round Sheets** | Daily round sheets consist of readings, observations, and preventive maintenance tasks that give early warning of potential process upsets or equipment problems. Round sheets also capture housekeeping and safety actions. | Printed daily through the CMMS and completed at least once per shift. Unusual observations are recorded and reported immediately to the operations supervisor. Data is uploaded into the CMMS, turning field observations into timely process adjustments and work orders. |
| **Operations Resiliency Plan** | Establishes a proactive framework for managing hydraulic and solids loading during storm events that push facilities beyond normal operating conditions. Defines strategies for flow equalization, process optimization, and solids handling. | Supports smooth coordination of key actions — adjusting return sludge rates, chemical dosing, and storage utilization — based on forecasted or real-time conditions. Minimizes risks of untreated bypasses and effluent quality degradation at all four facilities and Witcher. |
