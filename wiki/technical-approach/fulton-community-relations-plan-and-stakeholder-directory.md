# JV Community Relations Plan and Stakeholder Directory

Jacobs will develop and implement community-relations and education programs that foster engagement while supporting and enhancing [CLIENT]'s existing efforts. The approach will operate on parallel paths: broad-based knowledge that increases awareness of the critical role of the three treatment facilities, and targeted collaboration with local organizations that promotes social responsibility and supports underserved populations.

To develop specific programs for the [CLIENT] service area, JC Solutions recommends a workshop to gather staff input about community needs, how current programs meet those needs, and where adjustments or new approaches could amplify them. At a minimum, the community-relations plan will include an overall goal, objectives, key messages, audiences, strategies and tactics, content and collateral, distribution channels, metrics, and an implementation schedule. A final Community Outreach Plan will be submitted to [CLIENT] within 90 days of the Commencement Date and updated periodically to align with facility milestones, community events, and stakeholder feedback.

JC Solutions will create and maintain a comprehensive Stakeholders Directory that includes homeowners associations and neighborhood groups, elected officials, local school administrators and educators, and media contacts. This directory will be updated at least twice each year and serve as a primary outreach resource for [CLIENT].

Because adjacent and more distant neighborhoods commonly have HOAs, establishing points of contact with closest communities and maintaining the directory will facilitate information distribution through their own channels, such as Google Groups and Facebook Groups. Staff can present on individual-facility operations at HOA meetings, emphasize measures that minimize community impacts, and explain the reporting system. Information can also be shared through neighborhood websites and social channels. Resident surveys on facility-interest topics and preferred communication methods should inform both the response system and proactive outreach. Transparent, factual information in a calm setting helps build goodwill when issues arise; staff participation in neighborhood events, cleanups, and open houses builds further goodwill and lets residents see operators as neighbors.

Full Circle Communications supported Jacobs in Baton Rouge for more than ten years with strategic communications, community investment, and local workforce development on the City-Parish's consent-decree work. The firm developed and implemented a comprehensive communications plan, hired and trained local staff, participated in service projects, and engaged small businesses for support services.

## Reuse guidance

Use as a community-relations planning and stakeholder-directory pattern. Tailor plan timing, stakeholder segments, preferred channels, facility milestones, and local events. Reconfirm all third-party roles, social channels, and case-study permissions before external use.
