# Transitioned-Staff Satisfaction and Industry Recognition

Staff members that have joined our firm after a transition are uniformly more satisfied with their work environment at Jacobs. Our most recent round of new-employee surveys confirms this historic trend. Employee-satisfaction survey results after Jacobs assumes operations were 3.4 before and 4.6 with Jacobs in Pembroke Pines, FL; 3.3 before and 4.7 with Jacobs in Vancouver, WA; and 2.7 before and 4.5 with Jacobs in Ontario, OR.

Jacobs' commitment to valuing and empowering our people creates an environment where employees thrive with the skills, tools, and support needed for success, as demonstrated by our high retention rates and positive employee-satisfaction scores from transitioned staff across our operations.

This spring, Jacobs was honored to be shortlisted for the Global Water Intelligence (GWI) Water Company of the Year award, which celebrates excellence in the global water sector. We’ve been recognized for our commitment to developing industry-leading water solutions that benefit people and the planet. Our O&M project at the Jackson, MS, WTP was also shortlisted. At this year’s national Water Environment Foundation conference (WEFTEC), four Jacobs O&M projects won “Utility of the Future” awards. These awards recognize industry leadership and best practices.
