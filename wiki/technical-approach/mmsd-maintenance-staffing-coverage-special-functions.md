# Maintenance Staffing, Shift Coverage, and Special Maintenance Functions

We recognize that the Minimum Staffing Plan in RFP Exhibit E is structured to ensure the vast majority of PM and CM is self-performed. Our staffing approach, detailed in **Section II – Statement on Staffing, Training, and Corporate Culture**, is to not just fill positions—we'll staff to outcomes with a Planner/Scheduler model, CMMS champions, and cross-trained crews for 24/7 reliability.

We'll structure shifts/hours for complete coverage, including capital project support, routine and special requests, wet-weather events, and emergencies. Because the CBA currently directs maintenance shifts in an 8-hour day, Monday through Friday structure, we have followed that structure in our proposed shift schedule.

In addition to this typical shift schedule, we'll designate staff who will be on standby/on-call to address emergency, wet weather, and related situations to ensure complete coverage as needed. Additionally, we recognize that implementing a shift schedule with 7-days-per-week scheduling may be of interest to [CLIENT], and we'll explore this option with [CLIENT] and the unions if appropriate.

Our approach is a core of self-performing maintenance crews, supported by a dedicated Planner/Scheduler team. Industry baseline is roughly one planner per 20 craft; with NEXGEN and disciplined routines we intend to trend toward a 1:30–1:40 ratio as the system stabilizes. Each crew will designate a CMMS Champion responsible for field data quality and photo evidence. Separate facility and conveyance teams will coordinate through weekly planning meetings to balance resources and backlog.

We'll track housekeeping, grounds, coatings, tunnel cleaning, and fleet as preventive tasks in the CMMS. Jacobs' good-housekeeping standards will be embedded in job plans. We'll use subcontractors (e.g., specialty deep-cleaning/coatings) when it is clearly more efficient, but plan, inspect, and close out subcontractor work through the same workflow and governance.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: "staff to outcomes, not positions"; the Planner/Scheduler model with a stated planner-to-craft ratio and a path to improve it; the per-crew CMMS Champion accountable for field data quality and photo evidence; and tracking housekeeping, grounds, coatings, and fleet as preventive tasks rather than as unmanaged side work. The subcontractor sentence — same workflow and governance as self-performed work — answers the common evaluator worry about subbed specialty work.

Pursuit-specific: the RFP Exhibit E minimum staffing reference, the CBA-driven 8-hour Monday–Friday shift structure, the offer to explore 7-day scheduling with the unions, and tunnel cleaning (a conveyance-specific special function). The 1:30–1:40 planner ratio is a forward-looking intent tied to this CMMS and these routines — validate it before reusing.
