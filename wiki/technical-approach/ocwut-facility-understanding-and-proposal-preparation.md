# Facility Understanding and Due-Diligence Preparation (Multi-Plant Wastewater O&M, >110 MGD)

## Jacobs understands [CLIENT]'s facilities and its goals for this O&M contract

[CLIENT]'s wastewater system — a southcentral US municipal water utility trust — is one of the largest contract-operated programs in the country: four treatment plants and the Witcher Pump Station with a combined design capacity exceeding 110 MGD and peak flows above 180 MGD, a biosolids land application program spanning roughly 170 permitted sites, Hefner Water Treatment Plant (WTP) residuals management, and a septage receiving program. These facilities serve a growing metropolitan area entering a period of significant change, with nearly $200M in planned capital improvements over the next decade, including plant expansions, new disinfection systems, and a transition in biosolids handling.

In preparing this proposal, the Jacobs team thoroughly reviewed the RFP, Exhibit A schedules, and the Sample Service Agreement; attended both mandatory Pre-Proposal Conferences; conducted multi-day site visits across all five facilities; completed background document review; and held focused strategy sessions with process, odor control, solids, maintenance, laboratory, automation, and safety specialists. That preparation gave us a detailed, facility-specific understanding of where things stand today and what it will take to deliver the level of service [CLIENT] is looking for. Many of the challenges we identify below are also the drivers behind [CLIENT]'s significant CIP investments, and our approach is built to operate effectively through the transition while contributing O&M insight to the capital program. **Exhibit 1-1** provides a concise summary of our understanding of [CLIENT]'s goals, the key challenges facing these facilities, and the strategies Jacobs will bring to deliver results. Our strategies reflect the depth of our integrated O&M and engineering expertise, and we present each in detail throughout the corresponding sections of our Technical Approach.
