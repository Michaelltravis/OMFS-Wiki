# Recommended Level 3 PdM Technologies for the Treatment Facilities

Jacobs recommends a suite of proven, cost-effective PdM technologies that complement [CLIENT]'s existing systems and provide actionable, real-time insights for the treatment facilities and conveyance system assets. [CLIENT] is a regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities, a regional conveyance system, and a biosolids production operation.

## 4.1. Proposed PdM technologies for the treatment facilities

Illustrated in **Exhibit IV-60**, our recommended Level 3 PdM technologies for the two water reclamation facilities include:

- **Continuous vibration and temperature monitoring (VibeCloud or Augury)** will provide continuous insight into the mechanical condition of rotating equipment by measuring changes in vibration amplitude, frequency signatures, and thermal behavior. These sensors detect early signs of imbalance, misalignment, bearing wear, lubrication issues, and electrical or mechanical stress long before they affect performance. Continuous monitoring offers a far richer understanding of equipment behavior than periodic handheld readings and enables automated diagnostics to identify trends, anomalies, and emerging failure modes under real operating conditions. When integrated with SCADA and the CMMS, vibration and temperature monitoring systems can generate condition-based alerts and work orders, strengthen asset health scoring, and support a proactive, PdM strategy that improves reliability and reduces unplanned downtime.

- **Artesis — motor current signature analysis (MCSA) and sensorless pump performance analytics** will deliver high-resolution electrical signature analysis that detects mechanical, electrical, and power-quality–related degradation within motors and pump systems. This MCSA approach identifies bearing issues, rotor defects, stator problems, and load-related inefficiencies long before they manifest as operational failures. Artesis also offers a sensorless pump performance capability that compares real-time operating conditions to ideal pump curves, enabling utilities to identify hydraulic inefficiencies and energy waste. These insights can be integrated with the CMMS to automatically generate investigative work orders when deviations occur. We would work collaboratively with [CLIENT] to prioritize the most critical assets where Artesis would provide the greatest value based on asset condition and risk scoring.

- **Infrared/thermal imaging (infrared thermography)** identifies thermal patterns associated with electrical resistance, loose connections, overloaded circuits, and mechanical stress. It is particularly valuable for motor control centers, switchgear, and rotating equipment. By regularly trending thermal profiles, [CLIENT] can detect risk conditions long before they lead to electrical faults or mechanical degradation. Thermal imaging complements both vibration and MCSA data by providing a non-contact indicator of emerging failure modes.

- **Enhanced oil and tribology analysis** provides insight into internal wear mechanisms, lubricant degradation, contamination, and overall equipment health. [CLIENT] already performs oil sampling, but under the Level 3 PdM program, Jacobs will integrate these samples into a broader asset condition framework, correlating oil characteristics with vibration, thermal, and MCSA insights. This strengthens diagnostics, improves trend reliability, and enhances predictive accuracy for gearboxes, motors, blowers, and turbine systems.

Based on our analysis of the water reclamation facilities' assets, we've identified and prioritized the types of assets where continuous monitoring is most beneficial based on asset criticality, as shown in **Exhibit IV-61**. We'll work with [CLIENT] to confirm the final selection of assets for Level 3 PdM deployment.

## Exhibit IV-60 — Jacobs' PdM technologies support cost-effective, reliability-centered asset maintenance

- **Vibration and temperature monitoring** for precision alignment of rotating equipment effectively corrects misalignment, a leading cause of bearing failure in rotating equipment.
- **Infrared thermography** to spot anomalies not otherwise visible to the eye, finding potential faults in electrical and mechanical applications before they lead to equipment failure.
- **Enhanced oil and tribology** to monitor the condition and degradation of the asset, informing decisions for continued operation, filtering, or oil volume change.
- **Continuous vibration and temperature monitoring** to identify misalignments or imbalances before issues or failures develop, including critical, high-dollar equipment that traditional RMS vibration measurement cannot detect.
