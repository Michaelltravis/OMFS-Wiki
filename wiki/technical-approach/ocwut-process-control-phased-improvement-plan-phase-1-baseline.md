# Phased Process Control Performance Improvement Plan — Phase 1 Shared Baseline

## Process Control System Phased Performance Improvement Plan

**Our operational approach to treatment process automation and intelligence is designed to deliver consistent regulatory compliance, resilient and adaptable treatment performance, and sustainable operational efficiency across [CLIENT] facilities.** We will integrate proven process engineering and operational discipline with modern automation and intelligence capabilities through a deliberate, phased roadmap. This approach emphasizes repeatable, measurable improvements that endure over the life of the facilities. Central to this strategy is the principle that automation and intelligence perform only as well as the process definition and data foundation beneath them. We will begin by clearly documenting treatment objectives, operational constraints, and process variability; validating measurement and instrumentation; and confirming data quality. Only once this foundation is established will we apply automation and advanced control strategies to reduce variability, improve responsiveness, and drive predictable outcomes without introducing unnecessary complexity or operational risk.

## Phase 1 — Establishing a Shared Baseline for Process Control Performance

Phase 1 will establish a complete, shared understanding of how each [CLIENT] treatment system is expected to perform, what drives that performance, and what capabilities currently exist. This phase will create the defensible baseline required for disciplined management, maintenance, and future optimization of process control assets.

We'll document both the demands on the treatment system, including influent variability, permit requirements, operational constraints, and seasonal impacts, and the means available to meet those demands, such as process configuration, equipment capacity, control strategies, instrumentation, and operational workflows. By evaluating these elements together, our team will define how performance is currently achieved, identify constraints and vulnerabilities, and provide the technical clarity needed for informed decision making.

This effort will be tailored to the life cycle stage and conditions of each [CLIENT] facility:

- **[FACILITY A], [FACILITY B], and [FACILITY C] (the established treatment plants).** Because assets span multiple lifecycle stages, Phase 1 will emphasize comprehensive identification and documentation of process control assets, including "shadow assets" (subcomponents, package systems, and auxiliary devices that materially influence treatment performance but often sit outside formal asset registers). This both strengthens [CLIENT]'s asset management program and ensures all contributors to treatment performance are fully visible and understood.

- **[FACILITY D] (the newly constructed plant).** Most process control assets will be new, so Phase 1 will focus on a seamless operational handoff. We'll validate control strategies and instrumentation, align with design intent, document baseline performance, and capture any deviations from design assumptions so future improvements are grounded in actual operating conditions.
