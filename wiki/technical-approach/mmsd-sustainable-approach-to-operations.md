# Sustainable Approach to Operations

Jacobs is committed to delivering solutions that make sustainability a core operating discipline—not an aspiration. Under our leadership, sustainable performance becomes measurable, repeatable, and embedded in daily decision making. We'll establish clear sustainability KPIs such as energy and chemical use per million gallons treated, energy and chemical use per ton of solids processed, MMBtu per ton of [CLIENT]'s biosolids product, and natural gas MMBtu per ton of that product, along with greenhouse gas (GHG) intensity and renewable energy contribution. Combining innovation, collaboration, and disciplined execution, we will help [CLIENT] advance its 2035 Vision and position [CLIENT] as a national model for climate-smart, high-performance wastewater operations.

## Energy optimization and decarbonization

As discussed earlier, Jacobs has identified significant opportunities for chemical and power optimization at [CLIENT]'s facilities that will **reduce operating costs and greenhouse gas emissions**. Using advanced process modeling, SCADA analytics, and predictive algorithms, Jacobs will optimize aeration, pumping, and solids handling systems. Based on results at comparable large utilities, these measures have achieved **power reductions of 15–25 percent**, with Scope 2 and Scope 3 greenhouse gas reductions occurring proportionally as power and chemical use declines.

Jacobs will update [CLIENT]'s greenhouse gas inventory and identify priority **emissions-reduction strategies**. Additional Scope 1 and Scope 3 reductions will be evaluated through targeted assessment of resource recovery, fugitive methane, flaring reduction opportunities, process-related N₂O, and other optimization measures. Industry-leading tools will be used to quantify emissions and track progress in support of [CLIENT]'s long-term decarbonization objectives.

Jacobs also brings extensive experience optimizing UV disinfection systems to balance treatment performance, energy demand, and lifecycle cost. As [CLIENT] expands UV use, Jacobs will collaborate with [CLIENT] to manage increased electrical demand through process optimization and alignment with [CLIENT]'s goals for expanding on-site solar generation at the secondary-treatment water reclamation facility, supporting progress toward energy self-sufficiency and climate-smart operations.

## Resource recovery and biosolids optimization

Jacobs will enhance [CLIENT]'s longstanding leadership in sustainable resource recovery by **improving the efficiency of biosolids, energy, and reuse operations**. We'll optimize digester performance and gas production, increase the efficiency of dewatering and drying systems, and reduce fossil fuel demand in production of [CLIENT]'s biosolids product. Working collaboratively with [CLIENT], we'll evaluate opportunities to divert high-energy side streams, such as primary scum and high-strength organic waste, into the digesters to boost renewable gas generation and advance **resource recovery**.

## Green infrastructure integration and climate resilience

Jacobs will collaborate to support [CLIENT] in meeting its green infrastructure detention capacity goals and adapt to increasing climate variability. We will ensure that treatment and conveyance assets continue to operate sustainably and reliably under changing climate conditions, protecting public health, safeguarding water resources, and strengthening the region's long-term resilience.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the opening move — "sustainability as a core operating discipline, not an aspiration" — followed immediately by a named KPI list. Naming intensity metrics (per million gallons, per ton of solids) rather than absolute totals is what makes the commitment operable for an O&M contractor who does not control loading.

Pursuit-specific: KPI selection, the client's strategic plan, the solar and UV specifics, and the green infrastructure detention goal.
