# NexGen EAM — CMMS Governance and Implementation-to-Operations Continuity

## The CMMS Advantage: NexGen and Jacobs' Consulting Relationship

**[CLIENT] — a southcentral US municipal water utility trust operating four wastewater treatment plants and a major pump station — has selected NexGen EAM as the enterprise AMS, and our team is [CLIENT]'s implementation consultant for the platform.** This relationship gives our O&M team an opportunity that competitors simply don't have: experienced, direct collaboration with the people building and configuring the system. **Amy** and our asset management team will work alongside the implementation consultants to align the NexGen configuration with operational workflows, maintenance strategies, and reporting requirements, creating a seamless link between how the system is designed and how it's used in the field every day.

We'll manage NexGen for all maintenance functions, including preventive, predictive, and corrective work orders, the asset registry, condition updates, parts inventory, Maintenance and Repair (M&R) Fund tracking, and reporting outputs. Field staff will use mobile devices to scan, execute, and close work with photos, meter reads, parts consumption, and test results, creating a durable audit trail that protects both [CLIENT]'s assets and our program's financial integrity. A work order is not considered complete until labor, materials, failure coding, and condition updates are fully documented, ensuring data integrity, auditability, and continuous improvement.

A CMMS Governance standard will standardize naming conventions, hierarchies, and required fields. GIS integration will bring linear and conveyance assets into the same view as vertical plant assets, and SCADA/Historian tags will link to equipment records so that process performance and maintenance history inform each other. **Exhibit 1-30** illustrates our typical CMMS governance approach, which we'll bring to [CLIENT]'s facilities, customizing it to NexGen's level of capabilities.

**Exhibit 1-30.** *Jacobs' CMMS Systems Serve as the Centerpoint of Our Asset Management Delivery* — the Computerized Maintenance Management System sits at the center, connected to the preventive maintenance schedule, corrective maintenance plan, inventory activity and inventory status, maintenance performance reports, maintenance review, asset performance management, and capital planning.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the CMMS governance standard (naming conventions, hierarchies, required fields), GIS integration bringing linear and conveyance assets into the same view as vertical plant assets, SCADA/Historian tag linkage, mobile scan-execute-close with photographic evidence, and the work-order completion rule that ties data integrity to financial integrity. The Exhibit 1-30 hub-and-spoke concept is platform-agnostic.

Pursuit-specific and unverified for reuse: the claim that the proposer is the client's implementation consultant for its selected EAM platform. That claim, the platform name, and the named asset management staff must be confirmed for each pursuit — it is the whole force of this passage and cannot be asserted generically.
