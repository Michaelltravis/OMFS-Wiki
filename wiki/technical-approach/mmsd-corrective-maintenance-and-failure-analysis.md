# Corrective Maintenance, Work Order Closeout, and In-House Failure Analysis

We won't just fix failures—we'll prevent recurrence by embedding root-cause analysis in every corrective work order:

All corrective work will be tracked by Level A/B per the Agreement/Exhibits and managed to [CLIENT] approval thresholds.

Each CM work order will capture the failure mode, root cause, corrective action, and preventive measure ("what we'll do so it doesn't happen again").

Supervisors will enforce closeout discipline and data quality; overdue or incomplete closeouts trigger follow-ups visible on shared dashboards.

**In-House Failure Analysis Expertise – From Root Cause to Lasting Fixes**

We'll bring our failure analysis expertise that includes Jacobs' O&M regional and heavy maintenance teams, combined with our deep wastewater consulting bench to investigate complex events and train crews to prevent recurrence. Our internal annual audits and monthly cross project CMMS reporting add QC and benchmarking, so performance gaps are surfaced and corrected quickly.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the four-field CM work order capture (failure mode, root cause, corrective action, preventive measure), supervisor-enforced closeout discipline with dashboard-visible overdue follow-ups, and the in-house failure analysis bench paragraph. The internal annual audit plus monthly cross-project CMMS reporting is a strong, reusable QC claim that distinguishes a national operator from a standalone site team.

Pursuit-specific: the Level A/B corrective work classification and approval thresholds, which come from this contract's agreement and exhibits.

This block is short by design — it is the corrective half of the maintenance story. Pair it with [PM Optimization, Operator-Driven Requests, and Work Planning](mmsd-pm-optimization-and-work-planning.md) for the preventive half.
