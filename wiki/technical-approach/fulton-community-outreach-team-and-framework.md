# JV Community Outreach Team and Public Relations Framework

JC Solutions is committed to a transparent, responsive, and proactive relationship with [CLIENT] citizens, staff, regulatory agencies, and other stakeholders. Our Public Relations and Community Outreach Plan ensures that community concerns related to [CLIENT]-managed assets are addressed with professionalism, empathy, and urgency.

We bring in-house communications professionals embedded in the technical team of engineering and operations experts, who understand current community-engagement trends and tools as well as the technical considerations of operating water reclamation facilities. Proposed Community Relations/PR Lead and Jacobs national Communications Practice Leader Marci Davis crafted this approach and supported outreach and engagement during construction of [FACILITY B], an environmental campus. She is an active volunteer on the Public Education Committee for the Georgia Association of Water Professionals and brings an extensive network of utility communicators and educators that provides access to best practices across the state. Marci brings more than 25 years of experience developing and executing outreach strategies for public-infrastructure projects. A former journalist and Jacobs national communications lead, she brings a perspective on stakeholder engagement, media strategy, and community relations that she has applied across hundreds of water, wastewater, and utility projects. Her experience across Georgia, including [CLIENT], combined with national expertise, positions her to lead responsive outreach strategies for diverse communities.

JC Solutions has partnered with Full Circle Communications, a metro-Atlanta firm supporting community outreach and education, public relations, and media relations. As a [CLIENT]-certified MBE, FBE, and SBE firm, Full Circle has specialized in stakeholder engagement for more than two decades, has deep community roots, and has a record of collaboration with both Jacobs and CERM. The firm has supported [CLIENT] initiatives including a jail feasibility project, where it led focus groups and stakeholder interviews, as well as a solid-waste master plan. Its outreach work at [FACILITY A], a water reclamation facility; [FACILITY B], an environmental campus; and [FACILITY C], a water reclamation facility demonstrates its ability to engage diverse audiences around important public assets.

Full Circle specializes in helping clients become effective environmental stewards, trusted collaborators, and engaged community partners. Its successful outreach at [FACILITY A], [FACILITY B], and [FACILITY C] provides deep local knowledge and the ability to mobilize, including at [FACILITY B]. As JC Solutions elevates public education and community engagement around the upgraded [FACILITY A], Full Circle brings strategic insight, trusted relationships, renewed vigor, and the resources to support [CLIENT]'s vision without missing a beat.

Together, JC Solutions will bring renewed energy and the expertise, creativity, and resources needed to elevate [CLIENT]'s public education and outreach. [CLIENT] deserves a program that is responsive and forward-looking; our team is prepared to deliver a full-service approach that informs, inspires, and builds trust across the community. In addition to engaging the closest neighbors to [FACILITY B], [FACILITY A], and [FACILITY C], we will expand [CLIENT]'s environmental-service and stewardship narrative. The approach builds on methods that work, expands outreach through strategic partnerships, and layers creative tactics that take the story outside individual facility gates to the communities that rely on them.

The objectives are to increase public awareness of facilities' roles in water reclamation and environmental sustainability; highlight projects, improvements, and staff to demonstrate commitment to community and environmental health; and engage local communities to foster positive relationships and transparency. Strategies focus on being a good neighbor next door and across the service area, proactive media relations, and strategic partnerships. The comprehensive plan is aligned with applicable contract components.

The Public Relations Plan will promptly and respectfully address public concerns and complaints, maintain open communication with all stakeholders, and strengthen community understanding of the facilities' role in protecting public health and the environment. Because the facilities are surrounded by high-end neighborhoods, parks and natural-land amenities, and adjacent counties, maintaining a good-neighbor ethos is key to [CLIENT]'s success as a service provider, environmental steward, and regional partner.
