## Reuse guidance

Universal: the table architecture — innovation, location, 10-year value of investment, 10-year value of savings, what we will do, expected benefit, and a cross-reference to the section that explains it. The cross-reference column is what stops a value-add table from reading as a detached appendix.

Pursuit-specific: every row. Reuse the row *types* (a security study, an energy rate study, regional reach-back hours, training hours, discounted engineering, staffing above the minimum, a local rapid-response team, local subconsultants, a community project) and re-derive the numbers.
