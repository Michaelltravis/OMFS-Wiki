# Value-Added Investments and Savings Included in the Base Fee

**Jacobs will deliver meaningful, measurable improvements from Day 1.** Our Base Fee includes investments, at no additional cost to [CLIENT], that strengthen system resilience, optimize operations, and unlock long-term savings. Summarized here and detailed in **Exhibit IV-52**, these investments leverage Jacobs' unique ability to integrate O&M delivery with our engineering, consulting, and advanced technology capabilities. As highlighted in the graphic, **our investments and efficiencies provide [CLIENT] $107M in combined savings and added value over the first 10 years** of this partnership. Working collaboratively with [CLIENT], **we'll prioritize and implement these enhancements within the first 24 months**, positioning our partnership to quickly improve efficiency, reduce costs, accelerate performance, and establish a strong foundation for the decade ahead.

## Further Potential Savings Through Future Collaboration

Beyond the Base Fee investments, **Jacobs will partner with [CLIENT] to explore more than 25 future opportunities with the potential to generate an additional $21–53M in savings, creating a pipeline of continuous improvement that we'll refine jointly throughout the partnership, as shown in Exhibit IV-2.** Detailed in *Section 5.4 — Additional Improvements and Innovations for Future Savings*, these opportunities draw on Jacobs' deep O&M expertise, engineering capabilities, and digital innovation leadership. While these enhancements fall outside the Base Fee, they demonstrate our commitment to innovating and elevating system performance for years to come.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the two-tier value argument — a quantified package of improvements already funded inside the fee, then a named, sized pipeline of jointly-explored future savings. The "from Day 1 / within the first 24 months" pacing and the explicit statement that the second tier sits outside the fee are what make this credible rather than promotional.

Pursuit-specific: all dollar figures, the opportunity count, the term length, and the cross-references to the detailed savings exhibit and the future-improvements section. Rebuild the figures from the pursuit's own value-engineering workshop and register each in `proof-points/registry.md` before the numbers appear in a draft.
