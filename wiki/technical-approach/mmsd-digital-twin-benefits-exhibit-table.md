# Digital Twin Benefits Exhibit (Exhibit IV-29)

Graphic asset ID `304_007CAM_2`.

| Benefit | What the digital twin does for [CLIENT] |
|---|---|
| **Optimized Wet Weather Management** | Anticipates and simulates wet weather events to help evaluate strategies for emptying the inline storage system while keeping plant effluents in compliance and minimizing CSOs in the interceptor system. For example, operators decide when to start emptying the tunnel and how to split flow between the two plants by accounting for each plant's different sludge settling behavior, thereby reducing final clarifier sludge blanket upsets—the driver of recent compliance issues. |
| **Enhanced Dry Weather Operations** | Can guide our tunnel use with real-time data for dry weather load normalization and flow distribution between the two water reclamation facilities to maximize nutrient removal efficiency, ensuring compliance across both facilities. |
| **Improved Energy Utilization** | Helps us balance raw WAS and digested sludge that feeds the thermal dryers, maintaining biosolids product quality while enhancing energy recovery from landfill, waste dryer heat, and digestion biogas. |
| **Regulatory Compliance Assurance** | Helps ensure compliance by forecasting effluent quality under future conditions and recommending adjustments needed to meet requirements, reducing the risk of fines and penalties. |
| **Enhanced Predictive Maintenance** | Monitors the health of assets in real-time (e.g., pumps, pipelines, sensors, and treatment systems), identifying potential failures before they occur to minimize downtime, reduce repair costs, and extend [CLIENT] asset lifespans. |
| **Stakeholder Engagement and Transparency** | Allows [CLIENT] to demonstrate system performance and improvement initiatives to internal and external stakeholders, including regulatory bodies and the public, which fosters trust and facilitates collaboration on sustainability goals. |
| **Emergency Preparedness** | Provides a critical tool for decision-making during extreme weather events or emergencies by simulating responses to possible floods or system surges and enabling proactive measures to mitigate risks. |
| **Knowledge Retention and Training** | Serves as a valuable training tool for operators, simulating real-world scenarios to prepare them for various operational challenges and helps retain institutional knowledge for [CLIENT], even as experienced staff retire. |
| **Data Integration and Analysis** | Integrates data from various sources—SCADA systems, laboratory measurements, sensors, and historical records—into a unified platform that simplifies data analysis, enabling a deeper understanding of system behavior over time. |
| **Capacity Monitoring and Trend Analysis** | Identifies patterns in flow rates, treatment performance, and storage usage by continuously analyzing data from the collection system, deep tunnel, and treatment plants and then forecasts when existing infrastructure may reach its capacity limits, providing early warnings for potential bottlenecks. |
| **Scenario Simulation for Growth and Climate Resiliency** | Simulates future scenarios, such as population growth, urbanization, or increased storm intensity due to climate change, allowing [CLIENT] to model their effects on system performance and predict when and where capacity upgrades will be needed. |
