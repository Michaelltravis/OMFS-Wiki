# PgM-Driven Cost Control, Monitoring, and Reporting for Transparency and Accountability

Jacobs' PgM framework will be the backbone for how we **monitor, control, and communicate operational performance and costs** across [CLIENT]'s operations. In partnership with [CLIENT], we'll **align operational monitoring to the RFP's Standards of Performance**, jointly define key performance indicators (KPIs), and use integrated data from NEXGEN, SCADA, CMMS, Hach WIMS/LIMS, and financial systems to produce **one validated, single source of truth.** We'll provide [CLIENT] with real-time dashboards, automated exception tracking, and structured monthly/quarterly reviews that will drive corrective actions and continuous improvement.

A critical PgM outcome is **shared-cost transparency and reporting**. Our framework establishes **complete, auditable shared-cost controls** for energy, chemicals, fuel, and biosolids handling—linking consumption and performance drivers directly to invoicing, inventory, and operational conditions. The system will **automatically flag KPI deviations**, maintain a **shared corrective-action log visible to [CLIENT]**, and trigger **immediate notifications for any condition that could impact permit compliance**, followed by written corrective summaries. PgM-led reporting will include **daily operations logs**, **monthly performance and reconciled shared-cost reports**, **quarterly and annual committee-ready summaries**, and an **Annual Performance and Optimization Report.**

**These reports are** generated from validated datasets and accessible to [CLIENT] through secure dashboards to support collaborative oversight, defensible cost allocation, and sustained performance improvement. *Section 2.6, Monitoring, Tracking, and Reporting Operational Parameters* provides a detailed discussion of our approach to delivering these tools.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the chain of commitments — one validated source of truth, auditable shared-cost controls linked to invoicing, automatic deviation flags, a client-visible corrective-action log, immediate permit-risk notification, and a fixed reporting ladder from daily logs to an annual optimization report. Reuse this chain intact; each link answers a different evaluator's objection.

Pursuit-specific: the named data platforms, the RFP exhibit reference for standards of performance, the shared-cost categories, and the report titles (match the contract's required deliverables).
