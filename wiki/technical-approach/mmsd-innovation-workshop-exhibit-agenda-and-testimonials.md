## Reuse guidance

Universal: the pattern of proving a value-add offering with a dated agenda, a venue, and two named clients who attended. A full-day agenda with named session titles reads as a real event rather than a brochure promise, and the "like attending a WEFTEC workshop in your own backyard" quote is the most quotable line in the set.

Pursuit-specific: session titles should track the evaluated client's own priorities (PFAS, decarbonization, digital, adaptive planning are the durable four).
