# Additional Improvements and Innovations for Future Savings

In addition to the offerings included in our Base Fee and summarized in **Exhibit IV-52**, Jacobs will partner with [CLIENT] to explore more opportunities to improve and innovate across [CLIENT]'s facilities with the goal of optimizing performance and further reducing shared O&M costs and risks. Our team's operators and SMEs have brainstormed more than 25 ideas focused on process improvements, intelligent O&M optimizations and security/SCADA/OT improvements that we would like to develop in partnership with [CLIENT]. **These insights are just our initial takeaway from a handful of visits over the past months. Once we're hands-on and embedded with your system, we'll keep coming up with fresh ideas to optimize it.** The ideas presented in **Exhibit IV-53** have the potential for significant optimization and cost savings. **We believe these ideas can bring [CLIENT] between $21M and $53M in additional value-adds and savings over the next 10 years.**

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the two-tier construction — tier one is priced and inside the fee; tier two is a named, quantified list of what the partnership could pursue next. Offering a second tier with a savings range, rather than a vague promise of continuous improvement, gives an evaluator something to score and gives the client a reason to start the conversation at kickoff.

Also universal: the "handful of visits" move. A challenger admits limited access and turns it into a forward-looking promise in the same sentence, which is more credible than claiming full knowledge of a system it has not operated.

Pursuit-specific: idea count, savings range, and the categories the ideas fall into. Pairs with the two Exhibit IV-53 tables ([process improvements](mmsd-exhibit-future-collaboration-process-improvements-table.md) and [intelligent O&M, SCADA/security/OT](mmsd-exhibit-future-collaboration-intelligent-om-and-ot-security-table.md)) and follows [mmsd-value-added-improvements-included-in-base-fee.md](mmsd-value-added-improvements-included-in-base-fee.md).
