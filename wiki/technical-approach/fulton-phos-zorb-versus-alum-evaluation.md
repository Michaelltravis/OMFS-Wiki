# Phos-Zorb Versus Alum — Aluminum, Alkalinity Credit, and Delivery Trade-Offs

Alum is the traditional chemical used to add aluminum. Advantages and disadvantages of Phos-Zorb relative to alum include:

- Phos-Zorb feed at [FACILITY A], a county water reclamation facility, at 15 gallons per million gallons (gal./MG) would provide the equivalent of 2 gal./MG of sodium hydroxide. This credit is small compared to the expected sodium hydroxide requirement for alkalinity addition. Alum does not provide such a credit.
- Phos-Zorb costs 60% more than alum based on aluminum content. Alum can be procured competitively, whereas Phos-Zorb is proprietary and cannot be procured completely. Phos-Zorb/Phos-Sorb is available from Southern Ionics, the manufacturer, and Polytec, a distributor.

The proposed operations contract requires Phos-Zorb, so this is the basis of our proposal. After selection, we would like to discuss our analysis with [CLIENT] to make sure the pros and cons of Phos-Zorb have been carefully considered.
