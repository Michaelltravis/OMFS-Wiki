# Facilities and Goals Understanding — Due Diligence and Shared Values

**We understand your operations, environmental stewardship priorities, regulatory mandates, and long-term vision. Through discussions and more than 16,000 hours of due diligence, we assessed how best to meet your needs. Drawing on both regional and national expertise, our tailored approach delivers innovative, cost-effective operational strategies that enhance performance and align with your goals.**

Over the past 2 years, our team, including subject matter experts (SMEs) in O&M, wastewater and solids treatment, conveyance, asset management, system optimization, safety, and security has met with [CLIENT] staff, evaluated your facilities, and carefully reviewed all data provided. Our priorities align with [CLIENT]'s, and our operating approach is built to deliver safe, compliant, and reliable service. With strong emphasis on maintenance, we'll maximize the lifespan of [CLIENT]'s assets while optimizing long-term costs. **Exhibit IV-4** provides a high-level assessment of [CLIENT]'s facilities, summary of key findings, and our proposed value-added solutions to strengthen overall system performance.

## Shared Values, Stronger Outcomes: A Collaborative Approach Aligned with the Client's Values for Resilience and Equity

Aligning our approach with shared values ensures that every step of our partnership reflects not just what we do, but who we are, together. With the One Team, One Water, One Vision approach illustrated in **Exhibit IV-3**, we align with your strategic objectives below:

- Commitment to cost-effective and compliant operations and management of [CLIENT] facilities
- Proactively manage assets to extend the life of [CLIENT]'s facilities
- Stay ahead of regulatory pressures and emerging contaminants
- Adopt climate-resilient and energy-efficient strategies
- Leadership in water quality innovation through engagement of industry leaders
- Integrated watershed management
- Regional partnerships and investments that benefit the community
- Partner with an O&M contractor with shared values

Graphic reference: Exhibit IV-3, One Team / One Water / One Vision partnership device (client-specific: true).

Universal: the pattern — quantified due-diligence effort, named disciplines that participated, an explicit "our priorities align with yours" pivot, and the client's own strategic objectives listed back as a shared-values frame. Works in any recompete or challenger bid where system knowledge is the evaluated risk.

Pursuit-specific: the hours and duration figures, the discipline list, the strategic objectives, and the One Team/One Water/One Vision theme line (replace with the pursuit's own theme).
