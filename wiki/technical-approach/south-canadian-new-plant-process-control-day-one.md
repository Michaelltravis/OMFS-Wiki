# New Plant, Right Habits from Day One

South Canadian will enter service as a new 8.66-MGD activated sludge facility during the term of this contract. While the plant will benefit from modern design and new equipment, the primary objective will be to establish strong process control fundamentals from the outset and set a high bar for regulatory compliance and consistency.

Early operations will prioritize stabilizing the biological process through SRT-based wasting, providing operators with clearly defined daily wasting targets as influent characteristics and loading patterns are established. This disciplined approach will protect secondary clarifiers and support reliable wet weather performance during startup and early operation. Wet weather management will be emphasized through careful monitoring of hydraulic loading, flow distribution, and solids inventory, with proactive adjustments to preserve treatment performance and compliance margins.

Strong process monitoring and operator engagement will be integral to embedding good operating practices early in the facility's lifecycle. Routine reviews of biological, hydraulic, and solids data will establish clear operating targets and standard responses, supporting consistent decision-making and operational confidence.

South Canadian will include **on-site sodium hypochlorite generation (OSHG)** using PSI MicroClor units, **a system our team has direct operating experience with at our Pampa, Texas, facility**. We'll leverage that experience to optimize performance from the outset, including maintaining consistent hypochlorite strength, maximizing system reliability, managing the proprietary cartridge replacement cycle, and integrating hypochlorite production with plant disinfection demand. Preventive maintenance, performance monitoring, and best practice SOPs will maintain safe operation and reliable disinfection capacity.

As operational data becomes available, process control strategies will be refined to optimize SRT targets, wet weather response, and disinfection efficiency. By embedding disciplined process control practices early and leveraging proven operating experience, we'll position South Canadian for reliable compliance, operational consistency, and long-term sustainability.
