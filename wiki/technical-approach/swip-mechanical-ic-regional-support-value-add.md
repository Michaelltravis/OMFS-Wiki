# Mechanical and I&C Regional Support Value-Add for Multi-Facility Clients

## Mechanical and I&C Support at Other Client Facilities

[Firm] regional maintenance experts can bring significant value to [CLIENT]'s other facilities as our team will already be familiar with the overall challenges and opportunities for improvement. We will utilize our local regional I&C and maintenance support to keep costs low and have budgeted 800 hours for this support. In addition to the extra one-day-a-week I&C support, [CLIENT] also has access to our full IT/OT teams for additional horsepower should they need it. **We've provided a year one value of $78,144 for the Mechanical and I&C Support at Other [CLIENT] Facilities on Schedule B of the pricing form.**

## Reuse guidance

This is a short, high-leverage value-add for any client that operates assets beyond the boundary of the O&M contract — a second treatment plant, pump stations, reservoirs, or other utility facilities. The argument is an economic one and should be kept intact when the passage is reused: the on-site team already knows the client's systems, standards, and people, so extending regional instrumentation-and-controls and maintenance support to the client's other facilities costs the client less than procuring that support separately. Three elements make the offer concrete rather than rhetorical and should each be restated with a target-pursuit value: a defined hours bank (800 hours in the source pursuit), a stated on-site cadence (one extra day a week of I&C support), and named escalation depth (access to the firm's full IT/OT teams when the routine bench is not enough). The dollar value shown here is the source pursuit's year-one Schedule B figure; reprice it with the commercial lead for a new pursuit and confirm with the pursuit team whether the value belongs in the technical narrative or only in the commercial volume. Where the client's other facilities involve control-system integration, extend this offer into a fuller OT/SCADA narrative rather than leaving it as a standalone hours commitment.
