# Mechanical and I&C Regional Support Value-Add for Multi-Facility Clients

[Firm]'s regional maintenance experts can bring significant value to [CLIENT]'s other facilities, since the on-site team is already familiar with the client's overall challenges and opportunities for improvement. [Firm] utilizes its local regional instrumentation & controls (I&C) and maintenance support bench to keep costs low, budgeting 800 hours for this support. In addition to extra one-day-a-week I&C support, the client also gains access to [Firm]'s full IT/OT teams for additional horsepower as needed.

**[Commercial figure redacted]:** The source pursuit stated a specific year-one dollar value for this offering explicitly "on Schedule B of the pricing form" (the commercial cost-proposal schedule). Per CLAUDE.md's sanitization rule, that figure has been redacted here rather than restated — reprice fresh with the pursuit team if an illustrative value is needed, and keep it in the commercial volume.

## Reuse guidance

This is a strong value-add for any client that operates multiple facilities beyond the scope of the core O&M contract (a second treatment plant, pump stations, or other City/utility assets) — the pitch is that the on-site team's existing familiarity with client systems reduces onboarding cost/time for supporting those other facilities. Reprice the hours bank and dollar value fresh per pursuit; the source's year-one dollar figure was a commercial cost-proposal schedule value and has been redacted from this block — do not restate it in a technical volume without pricing it fresh with the pursuit team. Pairs with `swip-discounted-engineering-hours-value-add.md` (same "more than an operator," no-added-cost value-add family) and the OT/SCADA modernization block if the target client's other facilities involve control-system integration.
