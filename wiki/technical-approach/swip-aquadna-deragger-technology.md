# AquaDNA DeRagger — AI-Based Pump Deragging and Lift Station Optimization Technology

Jacobs has an exclusive partnership with a sewage pump deragging vendor, coupled with the AquaDNA smart technology monitoring system, that implements pump reversals upon detection of flow or power changes. This dynamic waveform monitoring is unique in the industry and results in dramatically reduced callouts, less power consumption, and longer equipment service life. With nearly 100 installations in the US and a decade of operating experience, this is a proven approach.

**Case example:** at Jacobs' Rio Rancho, NM project, eight sewage pumps have been fitted with the system (no change in pumps or starters/VFDs is required) to reduce previously chronic sanitary sewer overflow (SSO) events.

As part of this pursuit's solution, Jacobs will install automatic deragging devices at the critical lift station. The technology can be integrated with existing equipment, requiring no additional control panels. These devices proactively detect and address potential ragging issues and help mitigate sewer overflows in advance.

**Sample dashboard capability:** the AquaDNA DeRagger dashboard (as deployed at Rio Rancho, NM) provides real-time visibility into pump performance, efficiency (power consumed, kWh/m3), health (motor health, days since last service), and blockage risk (number of cleans in the last 24h, cycles/hour, first-flush event tracking, time-to-spill prediction incorporating weather forecast data, and a forward-looking maintenance schedule for impeller/motor/bearing components).
