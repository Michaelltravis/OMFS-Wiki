# Commitment to Zero Excursions

Jacobs is committed to protecting [CLIENT]'s environmental integrity by operating every facility and control system in strict adherence to permit conditions with zero violations, zero reporting errors, and zero missed monitoring events. [CLIENT] is a regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities, a deep-tunnel conveyance and storage system, and a heat-dried biosolids product line. Our **99.98% compliance record across more than 300 O&M contracts** demonstrates our ability to meet this standard (stat callout asset `205_007CAM_1`: *99.98% — Jacobs' environmental compliance record, one of the best in the industry*).

Our approach combines technical excellence, disciplined reporting, and a culture of accountability that aligns with [CLIENT]'s vision of environmental leadership, sustainability, and transparency.

## Governance and compliance team

An **Environmental Manager** will lead permit compliance, supported by operations and laboratory QA/QC leads and Jacobs' national **compliance and reporting team**. We commit to maintaining full compliance with all requirements included in applicable federal, state, and local regulations, including the state discharge permit (WPDES) and air permits. Where [CLIENT]'s Standards of Performance exceed minimum regulatory obligations, Jacobs affirms that those higher performance standards shall govern. We'll consistently meet or surpass all regulatory requirements while aligning with [CLIENT]'s environmental stewardship.

Jacobs will promptly notify [CLIENT] of any conditions requiring attention and will engage regional or corporate resources as needed to support on-site staff. Compliance reporting SMEs will assist with reporting, audits, and regulatory coordination, and maintaining strong relationships with regulators to ensure transparency and advocacy on behalf of [CLIENT].

Training under this program will include discharge permit compliance, biosolids, pretreatment, and laboratory data integrity. It will be delivered through a combination of onsite and web-based modules. All training completion and competencies will be documented and shared with [CLIENT] to verify readiness and accountability.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three zeros, the 99.98% record, the named Environmental Manager role with lab QA/QC leads and national reach-back behind them, and the ten-year training-hours series. A decade of hours trending upward is more persuasive than a single-year figure and should be shown as the series, not averaged.

Pursuit-specific: the permit program names, the regulator, and the standards-of-performance commitment. On a challenger bid where the incumbent has a violation history, pair this block with the client's own recent record — the contrast does the work without naming the incumbent.
