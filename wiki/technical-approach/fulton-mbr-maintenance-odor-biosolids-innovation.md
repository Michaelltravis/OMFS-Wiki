# MBR Optimization, Predictive Maintenance, Odor, Biosolids, and Purposeful Innovation

We recognize [CLIENT]'s significant investment in membrane bioreactor technology across all three facilities and are uniquely positioned to protect and optimize that investment. Our MBR subject-matter experts bring specialized knowledge in Kubota and other membrane systems, offering immediate support and operational optimization. These specialists, including members of our onsite team, have commissioned, operated, and provided troubleshooting for MBR systems worldwide. They bring expertise in extending membrane life, optimizing chemical usage, and enhancing performance, as well as developing customized cleaning protocols, optimizing flux rates and aeration sequences, and implementing fouling-mitigation strategies that maximize treatment capacity while minimizing energy consumption. This specialized knowledge ensures operations at [CLIENT] facilities maximize return on the MBR investment while maintaining exceptional effluent quality.

Our preventive-maintenance program incorporates condition-based monitoring and predictive analytics to identify and address potential issues before they impact operations. We deploy vibration analysis, infrared thermography, oil analysis, and acoustic monitoring to track equipment health in real-time, particularly for critical assets like blowers, pumps, and membrane systems. This approach significantly reduces unplanned downtime and extends asset life. Our maintenance teams utilize mobile digital tools that streamline inspections and work-order processing while building comprehensive equipment histories to refine maintenance schedules. This proactive approach translates to substantial cost savings and operational stability while maximizing the service life of critical infrastructure assets.

With [FACILITY B] and other facilities located near residential areas, we understand the critical importance of effective odor control to maintain positive community relations. We will conduct a comprehensive odor-emissions study across all three facilities to identify potential sources, characterize emissions, and develop targeted mitigation strategies. Based on this assessment, we will develop facility-specific recommendations. Our approach will emphasize best practices for odor management such as training, keeping doors closed, covering trucks, etc. We will also focus on community engagement through transparent communication and responsive management, creating a foundation of trust with neighboring properties.

Recognizing the unique biosolids-management challenges [CLIENT] is facing, JC Solutions brings industry experts in solids processing, dewatering, transportation, and disposal to support [CLIENT] in evaluating sustainable biosolids-management alternatives, including beneficial-reuse options, thermal-drying technologies, and potential revenue-generating product development. Our experience implementing innovative biosolids solutions enhances environmental sustainability and regulatory compliance while identifying opportunities to reduce overall management costs.

By leveraging our deep domain expertise, we offer [CLIENT] a fresh look to the future with innovative solutions that will result in enduring outcomes. While our competitors may claim new technology similar to what we provide our client, theirs rarely represent real-world proven products. As part of our enterprise-wide innovation program, we’re actively developing and applying the latest technology in applied geospatial science, cybersecurity, automated design, Internet of Things, and predictive data analytics. We don’t innovate for the sake of novelty—we do it to solve real operational challenges, improve service delivery, and create enduring outcomes. Our expert teams on- and off-site can translate these advancements into practical, high-impact solutions that will support [CLIENT]'s goals today and position its facilities for the demands of tomorrow.

For example, we have achieved significant efficiencies and cost savings for clients such as the City of Wilmington, DE; Pima County, AZ; and the Puerto Rico Aqueducts and Sewers Authority (PRASA) through optimization efforts using advanced data-driven, machine-learning, and artificial-intelligence (AI) technologies built on partnerships with leading innovators such as Palantir. From optimizing energy and chemical use to enhancing situational awareness and asset management, we apply the right innovations with purpose and precision.
