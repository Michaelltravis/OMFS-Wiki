# Operator Training and Development Program for Client Water/Wastewater Staff

*(Presented in the source proposal as one of the "Additional Items for Consideration" — suggested modifications offered without a firm price, pending further scoping information from the client; see `swip-scada-redundancy-upgrade-recommendation.md` for the umbrella framing sentence.)*

## Training and Development of City Staff for Water/Wastewater/Advanced Water

[Firm] is aligned with [CLIENT] in its passion for staff development and training, as well as in developing the next generation of water and wastewater professionals, as demonstrated by the client's internship program. [Firm] can extend its comprehensive operator training program to the client's water and wastewater staff. The program is designed to strengthen foundational knowledge in water and wastewater operations while providing accessible, engaging, and practical learning tools.

| Program Highlights | Course Offerings |
|---|---|
| Deliver clear, accessible training on water/wastewater fundamentals in multiple formats (webinars, videos, printed materials). | Wastewater Fundamentals (2-part series) — Core unit processes in activated sludge. |
| Support both new and experienced operators with tiered training that builds skills at every level. | Wastewater Math Fundamentals — Essential operator calculations. |
| Focus on practical application — operators learn not only what to do, but why it matters to operations. | Process Lab Control & Testing — Practical insights into sampling, analysis, and optimization. |
| Include supplemental resources such as Operator Field Guides for on-the-job reference. | Wastewater Disinfection — UV, chlorination, and chloramination, including breakpoint concepts and residual testing. |

This program strengthens operator performance, improves retention and satisfaction, and builds a pipeline of skilled leaders — delivering immediate operational benefits and long-term project success.

## Reuse guidance

The program-highlights/course-offerings table structure is a reusable template for pitching a workforce-development value-add to any municipal client that has stated an interest in staff development, succession planning, or an internship/apprenticeship pipeline (lead with that framing if the client has one, as in the source pursuit). Swap course titles to match the target facility's actual treatment processes — the source list is wastewater/activated-sludge- and disinfection-focused; a drinking water, advanced treatment, or potable reuse pursuit would need a different course set (e.g., membrane processes, UV/AOP, RO). No dollar value was attached to this item in the source pursuit — confirm with the pursuit team whether to present it as a no-cost value-add (consistent with the rest of this section) or as a priced/scoped addition.
