# Operator Training and Development Program for Client Water/Wastewater Staff

*Presented in the source proposal under "Additional Items for Consideration" — modifications offered without a firm price, pending further scoping information from the client. The umbrella framing sentence is in [swip-scada-redundancy-upgrade-recommendation.md](swip-scada-redundancy-upgrade-recommendation.md).*

## Training and Development of Client Staff for Water/Wastewater/Advanced Water

[Firm] is similarly aligned to [CLIENT] in our passion for staff development and training, as well as in developing the next generation of water and wastewater professionals, as demonstrated by [CLIENT]'s internship program. [Firm] can extend our comprehensive operator training program to [CLIENT] water and wastewater staff. The program is designed to strengthen foundational knowledge in water and wastewater operations while providing accessible, engaging, and practical learning tools.

| Program highlights | Course offerings |
|---|---|
| Deliver clear, accessible training on water/wastewater fundamentals in multiple formats (webinars, videos, printed materials). Support both new and experienced operators with tiered training that builds skills at every level. Focus on practical application—operators learn not only what to do, but why it matters to operations. Include supplemental resources such as Operator Field Guides for on-the-job reference. | Wastewater Fundamentals (2-part series) – Core unit processes in activated sludge. Wastewater Math Fundamentals – Essential operator calculations. Process Lab Control & Testing – Practical insights into sampling, analysis, and optimization. Wastewater Disinfection – UV, chlorination, and chloramination, including breakpoint concepts and residual testing. |

This program strengthens operator performance, improves retention and satisfaction, and builds a pipeline of skilled leaders—delivering immediate operational benefits and long-term project success.

## Reuse guidance

The program-highlights and course-offerings table is a reusable template for pitching a workforce-development value-add to any municipal client that has signaled an interest in staff development, succession planning, or an internship or apprenticeship pipeline. Lead with that mirroring move as the source proposal does: name the client's own existing commitment first, so the offer reads as alignment rather than as a vendor add-on. Keep the four program highlights largely intact — multiple delivery formats, tiered content for new and experienced operators, the "why it matters" emphasis on practical application, and printed field guides for on-the-job reference — because together they answer the objections that sink most training offers (scheduling, mixed experience levels, relevance, and retention after the class ends). Swap the course titles to match the target facility's actual treatment processes; the source list is wastewater, activated-sludge, and disinfection focused, so a drinking water, advanced treatment, or potable reuse pursuit needs a different set covering membrane processes, UV/AOP, and RO. Close on the same three benefits — operator performance, retention and satisfaction, and a leadership pipeline — since they translate a training program into the operational and continuity terms an evaluator scores. No dollar value was attached in the source pursuit; confirm with the pursuit team whether to present it as an unpriced recommendation, consistent with the rest of that subsection, or as a priced addition.
