# Value-Added Improvements and Innovations Included in the Base Fee

## Digital tools for operational excellence

Our O&M approach incorporates the use of proven digital tools, best practices, and lessons learned developed over decades. We are excited to apply these tools to deliver greater efficiency and cost savings to [CLIENT]. Our digital tools are discussed in the operations and maintenance sections above (**Sections IV.A.2.3 and 3**).

## Value-added improvements and innovations included as part of the base fee

Jacobs is committed to delivering meaningful, measurable improvements from the start of the contract. As part of our Base Fee, we are investing, at **no additional cost to [CLIENT], a suite of value-added enhancements that directly strengthen day-to-day operations, improve system reliability, and reduce costs.** We developed these offerings through site visits, data review, and discussions with [CLIENT] staff, along with ideas from Jacobs operators, maintenance experts, digital specialists, and process SMEs. Together, they reflect our commitment to a performance-driven O&M partnership that will deliver both immediate and long-term operational benefits to [CLIENT].

These value-added improvements involve onsite and offsite operational support, application of proven best practices, targeted operational and engineering studies, energy and chemical optimization pilots, solids and biosolids-product process enhancements, odor and corrosion modeling, OT modernization and cybersecurity strengthening, and investments that improve system performance and operator effectiveness. **Many of these services—such as Intelligent O&M optimization, WATS modeling, solids-handling improvements, and OT configuration management—are typically delivered through stand-alone consulting engagements. By embedding them directly into our Base Fee, Jacobs provides [CLIENT] with millions of dollars of added value,** accelerates efficiency gains, and gives operators practical tools and expert support that enhance performance at every step of the system.

The value-added offerings we highlight in **Exhibit IV-52** are included in our Base Fee. The column labeled "10-Year Value of Investments" indicates the typical price of these services if they were provided separately from the O&M contract under a stand-alone engineering/consulting services contract. The column labeled "10-Year Value of Potential Savings" shows the expected financial benefit of these investments to [CLIENT] where applicable. **Our intent is to demonstrate the breadth of additional capabilities and services our team will provide in support of [CLIENT]'s objectives. They reflect our commitment to exceed your expectations.**

We'll target completion of the investments within the first 24 months of the O&M contract. Items under "Studies" are one-time investments. All other investments represent the total value of annual, ongoing investments and savings over the 10-year contract term. During transition, we'll collaborate with [CLIENT] to identify your priorities and preferred timing for each of these investments.

**Together, these O&M-focused investments create a strong foundation for safer, more resilient, and more efficient operations—positioning [CLIENT] to capture early wins, streamline ongoing performance, and advance [CLIENT]'s 2035 Vision with confidence and measurable results.**

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three arguments that make a value-add table land — (1) these are services other firms sell as stand-alone consulting engagements, (2) we priced them and put them inside the fee, (3) here is the dollar value of both the investment and the savings, with a completion date. Also universal is the provenance sentence: the offerings came from site visits, data review, and conversations with the client's own staff, not a catalog.

Pursuit-specific: the item categories, contract term, the 24-month completion target, and the client's strategic plan referenced in the closing sentence.
