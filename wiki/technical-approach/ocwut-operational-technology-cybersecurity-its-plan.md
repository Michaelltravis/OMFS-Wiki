# Operational Technology Cybersecurity Approach and Living ITS Implementation and Use Plan

**[CLIENT] retains ownership and control of its SCADA, IT, and cybersecurity infrastructure, and we operate within that model.** We'll partner with you to build and maintain the ITS Implementation and Use Plan required under Schedule 17 — documenting how process control assets are accessed, configured, maintained, and monitored, and explicitly defining responsibilities for implementation, oversight, and escalation so expectations, responsibilities, and boundaries stay clear and auditable. Our focus will be on field-level OT assets and their interaction with [CLIENT]'s enterprise systems, ensuring that security controls are practical, enforceable, and fully transparent to [CLIENT] system owners.

**We will leverage our deep OT operations experience and dedicated cybersecurity resources to apply industry best practices across secure configuration, asset accountability, access control, change management, and continuous monitoring.** Security controls will be practical, enforceable, and fully transparent to [CLIENT] as system owner. We'll treat the ITS Plan as a living document, reviewed regularly with you to address operational changes, emerging threats, and evolving regulatory guidance — so the PCS security posture stays resilient and adaptable while [CLIENT] keeps clear visibility and control.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the five control domains (secure configuration, asset accountability, access control, change management, continuous monitoring), the documented-responsibility framing (access, configuration, maintenance, monitoring, plus implementation, oversight, and escalation), and the living-document commitment with regular joint review. Pursuit-specific: the plan title and contract schedule citation.
