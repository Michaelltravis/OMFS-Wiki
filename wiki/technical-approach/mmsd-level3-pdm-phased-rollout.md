# Phased Rollout of Level 3 PdM

Jacobs proposes a phased deployment approach, shown in **Exhibit IV-64**, that delivers early value, controls cost, and allows [CLIENT] to expand its PdM program based on demonstrated results. [CLIENT] is a regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities and a regional conveyance system. This approach ensures [CLIENT] sees measurable benefits early, manages investment risk, and retains full control over program scaling and investment pace.

## Exhibit IV-64. Phased rollout of Level 3 PdM for early wins, cost control, and expansion based on results

**Phase 1 – Foundation**

- PdM tools deployed on approximately 50 high-criticality assets at each facility.
- SCADA routing, CMMS integration, and alert thresholds are established and validated.
- Builds the operational and data foundation for the program.

**Phase 2 – Expansion**

- As insights accumulate, PdM coverage expands to additional rotating equipment, electrical systems, pump stations, and conveyance assets.
- Remaining useful life models and refined condition scoring are introduced.

**Phase 3 – Optimization and Maturity**

- Program becomes fully integrated into [CLIENT]'s maintenance culture and governance processes.
- PdM insights inform capital planning, PM task optimization, and long-term asset strategy.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-phase arc — prove it on the critical few, expand on demonstrated value, then let the data drive capital planning — and the explicit concession that the client controls the pace. That concession is what defuses the "this is an open-ended cost" objection on any additive technology scope. Pursuit-specific: the Phase 1 asset count and which asset classes come in Phase 2.
