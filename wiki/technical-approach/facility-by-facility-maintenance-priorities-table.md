# Facility-by-Facility Maintenance Priorities Table (Exhibit 1-28)

Based on our findings, we've identified several maintenance priorities for each facility, summarized in **Exhibit 1-28**. We'll build from this starting point when we complete a baseline condition assessment at O&M startup.

**Exhibit 1-28.** *Summary of Maintenance Priorities Identified at Each Facility Based on Our Site Visits, Review of [CLIENT] Data, and Analysis of Both*

| FACILITY | KEY MAINTENANCE CHALLENGES | CRITICAL EQUIPMENT | JACOBS' PRIORITY RESPONSE |
|---|---|---|---|
| **North Canadian WWTP** | Severe H₂S preventing PM access, chronic grit failures cascading to primaries and aeration, non-functional odor control, corroded headworks structures | Grit system, influent pumps, centrifugal blowers (recent failure incident—to be confirmed), Phase 1 RAS screw pumps (1 OOS), three secondary clarifiers, belt press conveyors, solids building roof (CIP planned—year TBD) | Restore safe access through odor control coordination, grit reliability program, structured blower preventive maintenance (PM)/predictive maintenance (PdM), corrosion mitigation plan, baseline condition documentation |
| **Witcher Pump Station** | H₂S corrosion at influent, sealed junction box (no odor control) suspected as major H₂S contributor to North Canadian headworks, generator issues | Four 1,250 HP pumps, generators, force main condition | Quarterly vibration analysis, generator PM and vendor management; coordinate junction box investigation with [CLIENT] |
| **Deer Creek WWTP** | Blower issues (only two of four can run), domed primaries trapping H₂S, sludge holding without mixing driving severe BFP odors, secondary clarifier algae, sand filter blower degradation, history of influent building flooding | Blower building (2027 CIP), new belt filter presses, secondary clarifier mechanisms, sand filter blowers, new Bioway odor control system | Maintain blower availability pending CIP, warranty management for new belt filter presses, 2027 CIP integration planning, corrosion monitoring, pre-CIP condition documentation |
| **Chisholm Creek WWTP** | Both bar screens broke down (~$200K), grit building bypassed (design issues), no on-site solids processing (wet haul to North Canadian/Deer Creek), flood vulnerability at headworks | Bar screens and headworks pumps, grit system, gravity thickener, Pista Grit Turbo units | Bar screen reliability program, grit system evaluation with [CLIENT] engineering, flood preparedness; leverage Deer Creek proximity for shared specialized support |
| **South Canadian WWTP** | Major CIP replacing small batch reactors (SBRs) with oxidation ditches (online summer 2026), new hypo generation and disc thickener, existing facility at capacity, 45–50 minutes from northern facilities | New oxidation ditch equipment, Eff cloth filters (60 total), MicroClor hypo generation, Huber S-Disc thickeners, three generation sets (1,750 KW each) | Warranty-first maintenance for new equipment, dedicated on-site team, commissioning coordination; develop PM/PdM from manufacturer specifications before startup |

This assessment shapes every element of our Maintenance Plan. We aren't starting with a template; **we're starting with what your facilities need right now and building a program that evolves over the contract term.**
