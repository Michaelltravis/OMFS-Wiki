# MBR Biofouling (Leech/Physa Snail) Mitigation Case Study

**Sidebar heading:** Leech Mitigation at PWSC Demonstration Plant

The Pure Water Southern California (PWSC) Demonstration Plant, operated by Metropolitan and the Los Angeles County Sanitation Districts, experienced persistent snail (Physa) proliferation during MBR testing. Frequent cleaning managed but did not eliminate the issue, raising concerns about membrane life. Jacobs and Hazen were engaged to identify and test practical mitigation strategies. Current measures being implemented include ultra-fine influent screening, chlorinated reactor feed, anoxic zone incorporation, and free ammonia membrane soaks. These approaches are now under evaluation to inform full-scale MBR design.
