# MBR Biofouling (Leech/Physa Snail) Mitigation Case Study

The Pure Water Southern California (PWSC) Demonstration Plant, operated by Metropolitan and the Los Angeles County Sanitation Districts, experienced persistent leech (Physa snail) proliferation during membrane bioreactor (MBR) testing. Frequent cleaning managed but did not eliminate the issue, raising concerns about membrane life. Jacobs and Hazen were engaged to identify and test practical mitigation strategies. Current measures being implemented include:

- Ultra-fine influent screening
- Chlorinated reactor feed
- Anoxic zone incorporation
- Free ammonia membrane soaks

These approaches are under evaluation to inform full-scale MBR design.
