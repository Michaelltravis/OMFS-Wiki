# Three-Phase Maintenance Continuous Improvement and Performance Reporting

Our maintenance program will evolve over the contract term through a deliberate three-phase progression:

- **Year 1: Stabilization.** Address deferred maintenance backlog, complete the baseline condition assessment, establish PM and PdM routines, and calibrate staffing to workload. Build the data foundation in NexGen that enables everything that follows.
- **Years 2–3: Optimization.** Right-size PM frequencies based on condition data and failure trends. Expand PdM coverage to additional equipment classes where cost-benefit is demonstrated. Conduct quarterly PMO checkpoints and integrate CIP-delivered assets into steady-state maintenance. Regional Maintenance Team support transitions from heavy supplemental involvement to targeted specialist deployment.
- **Years 4+: Sustained Performance.** Condition-based maintenance driven by real-time asset health data. Annual benchmarking of [CLIENT]'s maintenance KPIs against Jacobs' portfolio of comparable wastewater O&M projects, sharing proven practices across the network. Lifecycle cost data informs capital planning recommendations, and the AMS is a mature, continuously improving system.

Monthly maintenance reports will document all completed work, asset condition updates, KPI performance against Schedule 5 targets, and M&R Fund expenditure status. Quarterly reviews with [CLIENT] — a Southcentral US regional water utility trust operating four wastewater treatment plants and a major pump station — will measure progress, identify improvement opportunities, and adjust strategies based on trending data. Our annual internal audits will provide independent assessment and bring best practices from across our national O&M portfolio. **The result is a disciplined, risk-driven maintenance program that protects performance, controls cost, and ensures long-term sustainability of [CLIENT]'s assets.**
