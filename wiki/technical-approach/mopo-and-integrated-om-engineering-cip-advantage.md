# Maintenance of Plant Operations (MOPO) and the Integrated O&M-Plus-Engineering CIP Advantage

MOPO is the highest-risk operational element of any capital project at a working wastewater facility. We treat MOPO as a living document that evolves through each design phase, not a static plan developed once and filed away. We'll identify critical process interdependencies and capacity constraints before construction begins, sequence construction activities to maintain treatment capability and permit compliance, develop contingency plans for equipment outages including temporary process configurations, and coordinate shutdown windows with seasonal flow patterns, wet weather risk, and [CLIENT]'s operational priorities. [CLIENT] is a Southcentral US regional water utility trust operating four wastewater treatment plants and a major pump station.

Jacobs remains responsible for meeting all Schedule 5 Performance Requirements during CIP construction. That accountability is why MOPO planning starts at 35% design and receives dedicated attention at every subsequent phase, and why the CIP Construction Manager coordinates directly with operations leadership to avoid conflicts between construction schedules and treatment obligations.

## The Jacobs Advantage: Integrated O&M and Engineering

**As both an O&M operator and one of the world's largest engineering and construction firms, Jacobs brings a perspective to CIP support that pure-play operators can't match.** Our operators bring real-world insight into design workshops, constructability reviews, and commissioning — identifying issues that look fine on paper but won't work in the field. At the Wilmington, Delaware Wastewater Treatment Facility, our operators proposed resequencing a multi-phase aeration upgrade to work on two basins simultaneously instead of one. **Our process engineers verified compliance could be maintained, and the result was a 40% reduction in the construction schedule and 20% cost savings.** For [CLIENT], this means CIP projects that are practical to operate from day one, resilient under real-world conditions, and designed with the people who run them every day.

For the complete framework addressing how new CIP facilities will be integrated into operations and maintenance programs, refer to our **Operational Integration Plan** in **Section 5 – Required Plans Submitted with the Proposal**.
