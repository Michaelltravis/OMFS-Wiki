# Successful Experience at Other Locations: Multi-Facility and MBR Operations

As a leading wastewater O&M, design, consulting, and construction-management firm, we bring a vast array of experience in all assets of O&M at facilities similar to [CLIENT], including expertise in MBR, biosolids management, odor control, advanced data-driven optimization technologies, and so much more. We provide detailed descriptions of our relevant experience, particularly with MBR facilities, in the Relevant Project Experience section. Selected facilities have additional relevance to the [CLIENT] O&M project including significant optimization and cost savings, solutions for biosolids, odor control, compliance excellence, and physical and cybersecurity, as well as robust programs for transition, community engagement, and training and workforce development.

Jacobs provides O&M services for the City of Wilmington’s 168-MGD wastewater treatment facility, operates its energy-recovery facility, and manages the City’s CSO systems and pump stations. Through our Intelligent O&M tools, we have saved the City $200,000/year in chemical costs while maintaining perfect compliance. Jacobs is delivering a $134M Capital Improvement Program. Our onsite engineering team collaborates with the City to prioritize projects and secure funding for future implementation.

**Relevance to [CLIENT]:** transition from Veolia; multiple-facilities O&M; physical and cybersecurity; S/W/MBE; local-workforce development; public information and engagement; compliance excellence; and chemical optimization.

“With Jacobs, we have had a lot of higher-level engineering support than our previous operator which has helped the City. Each project has undergone a due diligence process and been analyzed to ensure there are the right deliverables at the right time for the plant. Knowing the huge backlog of required capital O&M projects to be completed over the next ten years, spending the available dollars wisely is important to the City so we don’t put additional stress on our ratepayers..” — Vince Carroccia, Deputy Commissioner, Department of Public Works

Jacobs has provided O&M services to the City of Traverse’s Regional 8.5-MGD WWTP and nine lift stations since 1990 as well as at five satellite facilities. The collaboration between Jacobs and Traverse City led to major plant upgrades that resulted in North America’s largest membrane bioreactor (MBR) system by 2004, increasing the plant’s peak flow capacity to 17 MGD. Through extensive optimization efforts, Jacobs has reduced electrical consumption by more than 30% (approximately $120,000 annually) at the Regional WWTP and by 40% (approximately $65,000 annually) at Grand Traverse County, as well as similar savings in chemicals and natural gas consumption. One of the value-added qualities of Jacobs is the maintenance program. As an example, the Traverse City Wastewater Treatment Plant filter-membrane operation has exceeded the life expectancy because of the maintenance program Jacobs put in place.

**Relevance to [CLIENT]:** multiple-facilities O&M; MBR facility DBO; S/W/MBE; local-workforce development; public information and engagement; compliance excellence; and electrical and chemical optimization.
