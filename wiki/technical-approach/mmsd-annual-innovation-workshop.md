# Annual Innovation Workshop Drives Continuous Improvement and Elevated O&M

**Our Annual Innovation Workshops, provided at no cost to [CLIENT]**, use a proven innovation framework that brings together leading experts to share the most relevant, cutting-edge knowledge in the industry. A clear demonstration of the depth and relevance of our expertise is our unmatched participation in national knowledge sharing events such as the Water Environment Federation Technical Exhibition and Conference (WEFTEC), where Jacobs consistently contributes more technical content and thought leadership than any other O&M provider. For example, Jacobs had 27 papers at the 2024 WEFTEC where our major O&M competitors had none. In 2025, Jacobs presented more than 60 workshops and remained a long-time sponsor of the annual operations challenge, where top wastewater professionals demonstrate their skills in collections, laboratory work, process control, maintenance, and safety. This level of engagement underscores the breadth and relevance of the expertise we can bring.

Each year, we'll convene [CLIENT] staff, stakeholders, our onsite O&M team, and Jacobs' utility experts to review the prior year's achievements and co-develop new goals and innovations aligned with [CLIENT]'s evolving needs and long-term vision. This forward-looking workshop will help ensure [CLIENT] remains at the forefront of treatment technologies, optimization strategies, and modern O&M practices.

Jacobs will bring industry recognized leaders in process engineering, biosolids, hydraulics, wet weather flow management, pump station design and operations, digital optimization, and energy management. They are all hand selected to match [CLIENT]'s current challenges and opportunities. Before the workshop, we'll work closely with [CLIENT] to co-develop a full day agenda that reflects your priorities and expectations, focusing the session on your desired outcomes. Once the agenda is set, our specialists will begin their preparation in advance, learning your facilities, analyzing performance data, and identifying improvement opportunities so they arrive equipped with realistic, actionable ideas tailored specifically to [CLIENT].

During the workshop, we will engage in highly collaborative discussions, listening first, then co-creating tailored, innovative plans for the coming year. This structured approach has a **proven track record of delivering measurable results that enhance operations, improve asset life-cycle performance, and generate cost efficiencies**. For example, previous workshops have identified energy saving opportunities valued at more than $1M for the Wilmington WWTP.
