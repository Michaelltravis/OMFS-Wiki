# Award-to-Operational-Start Milestone Framework and Transition Checklist (Exhibit 5-12)

**Exhibit 5-12.** *Jacobs' Schedule 19 Milestone Framework and Transition Checklist.* (Graphic ID 105_009385.)

Exhibit banner: **From award to operational start: January 1, 2027 — every deliverable, every deadline, every commitment met.** *Jacobs transition checklist: built for [CLIENT].* **1,730 action items tracked · 30 transition workstreams · 40+ years of O&M transition experience.**

The exhibit lays out four dated milestone columns between award and the operational start date:

- **Pre-start activities — award (Aug 18, 2026) through readiness-services start (Sep 1, 2026):** activate the transition leadership team; establish governance and reporting cadence; confirm site access, badging, and IT onboarding; finalize the staffing plan and recruiting approach; complete kickoff site visits at all facilities; submit final schedules and the Transition Plan (Sep 1).
- **Milestone 1 — due Oct 15, 2026:** draft QA/QC, Odor Control, Emergency Operating, Wet Weather, and Training Plans; draft Buildings and Housekeeping Program; draft ITS Implementation and Use Plan (Oct 1); facility familiarization and knowledge transfer across all facilities.
- **Milestone 2 — due Nov 2–16, 2026:** draft Safety Program and Plan; draft Sampling and Testing QA/QC Plan; draft Process Control Plan; Transfer of Service Plan; management staff employment offers accepted (Nov 16); final treatment and equipment rotation schedules confirmed (Nov 16).
- **Milestone 3 — due Dec 1, 2026, with final-readiness actions through Dec 21:** final draft of all required plans submitted; chemicals and polymer identified with preferred vendors and sourcing agreements; biosolids management equipment secured; all staff positions filled (Dec 21); SOPs and maintenance procedures updated (Dec 21).
- **Milestone 4 — operational start date, Jan 1, 2027:** 7-day multi-shift operational monitoring complete; Baseline Physical Condition Report jointly signed; initial inventory jointly signed; all Schedule 3 plans finalized; transition training complete; full operational, compliance, and reporting responsibility assumed.

**Exhibit 5-12** illustrates Jacobs' milestone framework from award through the operational start date, showing the full sequence of Schedule 19 deliverables, key deadlines, and the depth of our transition checklist—**1,730 action items across 30 workstreams**—that drives execution behind every milestone. **Exhibit 5-16** shown at the end of this section, maps Schedule 19 milestones and key deliverables to a specific task lead and completion date, giving [CLIENT] full visibility into how we'll move from award to Operational Start Date on January 1, 2027. From day-one pre-award activities through post-start stabilization, each step is owned, sequenced, and built to keep your facilities on track without a gap in service.
