# Exhibit: Shared Priorities and the O&M Plan That Delivers Them

**Exhibit IV-1. Our shared priorities for achieving your 2035 Vision — our O&M plan delivers the results that matter most.** Graphic asset ID `352_007CAM_3`.

| O&M plan element | What it delivers for [CLIENT] |
|---|---|
| **Transparent Program Management (PgM) Framework** | Real-time, evidence-based reporting and open communication build trust and a one-team partnership aligned around shared vision, goals, and solutions |
| **Embedded Leadership & Engaged Support** | Empowered leadership team backed by national staff to strengthen accountability and performance |
| **Rigorous Cost Management** | Disciplined, transparent cost management with joint governance to validate savings and share measurable improvements over time |
| **Wet Weather Management** | Proactive, system-wide coordination that leverages forecasting to manage tunnel storage, pumping, and WRF operations |
| **Advanced Digital Tools** | Operator decisions guided and supported by digital tools for cost-effective and best-practice O&M |
| **Biosolids Management** | Optimized, energy-efficient solids processing will enhance reliability and renewable energy use |
| **Energy and Chemical Optimization** | Predictive, system-wide optimization that lowers cost, maintains compliance, and accelerates progress toward your 2035 Vision's renewable energy goals |
| **Preventive Maintenance (PM)/Corrective Maintenance (CM) Delivery** | Focus on consistent execution and reporting that drives scheduled work, extends equipment life, reduces downtime, and strengthens capital planning |
| **Regulatory Compliance Focus** | Compliance-first culture strengthened by analytics, accountability, and continuous improvement |
| **Regulatory Excellence** | Comprehensive systems for monitoring, tracking, and reporting will drive perfect permit adherence |
| **Asset Management Focus** | Reliability-focused strategy will maximize uptime and extend asset lifecycle and value |
| **Process Control** | SCADA integration, data-driven decisions, and advanced optimization will power high performance |
| **Odor Control** | Proactive, data-driven control will prevent odors, quickly resolve complaints, and protect trust |
| **Biosolids Product Production** | Stable, compliant operations that maximize quality, capacity, and energy efficiency and recovery |
| **Staffing Certainty & Workforce Development** | Proactive staffing leaves no positions unfilled and prepares the next generation of operators |
| **Physical & Cyber Security** | Intelligent, risk-based security technologies will strengthen protection and reduce costs |
| **Safety-First Culture** | Leadership-driven safety focus will build consistent safe work practices and training |
| **Successful CMMS Integration** | Longstanding relationship between Jacobs and NEXGEN ensures seamless, effective collaboration with [CLIENT] to deliver a successful CMMS implementation including configuration, integration, testing, and training |
| **Seamless Transition** | Accountable leadership and a dedicated transition team preserve critical institutional knowledge and ensure zero disruptions through proven systems and controls and by integrating Jacobs' best practices and innovative tools |
| **Emergency Preparedness** | Comprehensive Emergency Response Plan and coordinated protocols ensure resilient operations for wet weather and other emergency response |
| **Deep Support Resources** | Our experts in process, tunnels, maintenance, SCADA, and virtually every other discipline will support the onsite team with solutions whenever needed |

Universal: the device. Each tile is an element of the approach plus a single consequence sentence for the client, which makes it a compliance-map and an executive summary at once. Pursuit-specific: every tile title and the platform names (NEXGEN here).
