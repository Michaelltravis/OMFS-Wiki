# JV Customer Service Plan and Accountability

Customer service and client centricity are at the core of JC Solutions' approach and represent a key reason clients and communities trust us to operate and maintain essential infrastructure. Our commitment to exceptional service extends beyond daily operations to include thoughtful, responsive engagement with [CLIENT] staff, residents, and businesses.

JC Solutions will implement a strategic Customer Service Plan tailored to the applicable contract requirements. The Project Manager will work closely with the Transition Manager to initiate development of the plan; it will be finalized before the Commencement Date and updated annually to remain aligned with [CLIENT] expectations. The plan will ensure timely, professional, and accountable interactions with all customers and stakeholders.

All JC Solutions personnel will be trained in professionalism, courtesy, and responsiveness to build trust and confidence with the public and [CLIENT] staff. If JC Solutions is responsible for property damage to a customer's home or business, such as damage resulting from a backup or overflow, we will bear the full cost of repair and maintain open communication with both the customer and [CLIENT] throughout the resolution process.

We will respond to urgent service issues, such as spills or backups, within one hour. Customer concerns will be logged, tracked, and resolved promptly, and daily summaries of complaints and actions taken will be submitted to [CLIENT]. Our team will support [CLIENT]'s outreach and education initiatives so residents understand how to report service issues and how their feedback is used to improve operations. We will maintain a dedicated phone number and email address for customer contact; signage at each facility will clearly display that information and emergency-contact protocols.

This plan fosters a consistent, thoughtful, and transparent service experience for every customer interaction. By combining Jacobs' national best practices with localized, contract-specific procedures, JC Solutions will help uphold [CLIENT]'s reputation for quality service and responsiveness. A summary of customer-service interactions, including proactive steps taken to prevent recurring concerns, will be included in the monthly operations report.
