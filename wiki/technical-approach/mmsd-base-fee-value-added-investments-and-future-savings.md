# Value-Added Investments Included in the Base Fee, Plus a Pipeline of Future Savings

**Jacobs will deliver meaningful, measurable improvements from Day 1.** Our Base Fee includes investments, at no additional cost to [CLIENT]—a large regional sewerage district operating two water reclamation facilities and a regional conveyance system—that strengthen system resilience, optimize operations, and unlock long-term savings. Summarized here and detailed in **Exhibit IV-52** on page 70, these investments leverage Jacobs' unique ability to integrate O&M delivery with our engineering, consulting, and advanced technology capabilities. As highlighted in the graphic, **our investments and efficiencies provide [CLIENT] $107M in combined savings and added value over the first 10 years** of this partnership. Working collaboratively with [CLIENT], **we'll prioritize and implement these enhancements within the first 24 months**, positioning our partnership to quickly improve efficiency, reduce costs, accelerate performance, and establish a strong foundation for the decade ahead.

Beyond the Base Fee investments, **Jacobs will partner with [CLIENT] to explore more than 25 future opportunities with the potential to generate an additional $21–53M in savings, creating a pipeline of continuous improvement that we'll refine jointly throughout the partnership, as shown in Exhibit IV-2.** Detailed in **_Section 5.4 – Additional Improvements and Innovations for Future Savings_**, these opportunities draw on Jacobs' deep O&M expertise, engineering capabilities, and digital innovation leadership. While these enhancements fall outside the Base Fee, they demonstrate our commitment to innovating and elevating system performance for years to come.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the two-tier framing (inside the fee / beyond the fee), the Day-1 and 24-month commitments, and the "refine jointly" language that makes the second tier a partnership offer rather than a change-order threat. Pursuit-specific: every dollar figure, the opportunity count, the term length, and the cross-references to the detailed exhibit and section. Each figure must be registered in `proof-points/registry.md` with its as-of date and approval status before it appears in a draft.
