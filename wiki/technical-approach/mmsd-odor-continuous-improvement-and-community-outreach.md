# Odor Program Continuous Improvement, Governance Review, and Community Outreach

**Continuous Improvements to Improve Community Outreach and Confidence.** Odor control performance, complaint trends, and system data will be evaluated through [CLIENT]/Jacobs governance meetings, annual odor reviews, and dashboard reporting. Jacobs will apply insights from the WATS model, early warning analytics, SCADA trends, and operator feedback to continuously improve odor mitigation strategies and prevent recurrences.

In addition to proactive operations, we'll maintain a visible community presence, overseen by our Deputy Project Manager Rick Warner, by participating in local meetings, events, and neighborhood associations to conduct outreach, increase community confidence, and ensure that residents clearly know who to contact if concerns arise. We'll prepare quarterly odor reports that can be shared with the public.

This proactive, transparent approach strengthens regulatory compliance, reduces community impacts, and ensures [CLIENT] maintains its role as a responsible steward of public health and neighborhood quality of life.
