# Effective and Efficient Wet Weather Operations, with Named Wet-Weather Leadership

*Effective and Efficient Wet Weather Operations.* During transition, we'll confirm the inline storage system (ISS), metropolitan interceptor system (MIS), and WRFs are ready for wet weather. We'll align [CLIENT]'s forecasting framework with our predictive tools to maximize available tunnel storage, confirm pump readiness, and coordinate WRF operations. This is central to achieving zero CSOs and reducing basement backups. During events, Jacobs will provide clear, defensible, real-time recommendations. To achieve this, we'll use [CLIENT]'s root cause analyses, including those following the August 2025 flooding event, to strengthen decision-making and performance during future events. Jacobs and our Mead & Hunt partners bring a systemwide view informed by recent CSO/SSO and basement-backup reduction work, including the Mead & Hunt-led Mill Road Near Surface Collector System Design, NS12 Collector System Improvements, Hampton MIS Analysis, and Impact of Water Levels on District Conveyance System Assets Study. That perspective will help us anticipate how new connections to the ISS may change how quickly the tunnel fills—and what that could mean for how often the ISS reaches full capacity.

Drawing on experience with large, complex systems like Thames Tideway, we'll deliver practical, data-driven guidance for storage, pumping, and conveyance under rapidly changing conditions. Deputy Project Manager **Rick Warner** and Wet Weather and Conveyance Director **Susan Moisio** will provide strategic and operational support during critical events.

Dewatering the ISS is another critical aspect of wet weather operations. Knowing which WRF can take flow at a given time is crucial to making sure that the ISS dewatering does not negatively impact the liquid processes. The ISS pump station can pump to either WRF, so Jacobs will work with [CLIENT] on these ISS pump-out decisions.

**ABOVE AND BEYOND — VALUE-ADDED EXTRAS.** **Susan Moisio, PE** has 37 years of experience and is an expert in wet weather and conveyance. She brings a utility owner lens to [CLIENT] from her 16 years as the division lead for Cincinnati Collection Division, responsible for CSO and SSO O&M. Her role as a strategic advisor for the London Thames Tideway Tunnel CSO is an added benefit for [CLIENT]. She'll bring best practices and support for operations decisions during wet weather events. She'll direct experts in conveyance, sustainability, and wastewater technology to help [CLIENT] innovate and address current and future challenges.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the transition-period readiness check before the first storm; "clear, defensible, real-time recommendations" as the standard for event decisions; using the client's own root cause analyses rather than substituting a vendor method; and the ABOVE AND BEYOND / VALUE-ADDED EXTRAS callout format, which is the house device for offering senior expertise beyond the priced roster. Pursuit-specific: named personnel, the storage-dewatering routing question, and the partner-led study list.
