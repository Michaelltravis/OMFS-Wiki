# Collaborative Decision-Making for Safe, Compliant, and Reliable Operations

Jacobs uses system-wide data analytics and digital tools (*discussed in Section 2.3*) to support decisions. Whether related to process control, odor, energy and chemical use, biosolids product production, staffing, or maintenance, we'll make decisions with accountability and transparency.

Experienced, certified staff under the Project Manager's direction will make day-to-day O&M decisions using real-time SCADA, laboratory, and instrumentation data aligned with SOPs and permit requirements. Continuous monitoring, predictive maintenance (PdM) insights, and open team communication ensure timely, well-informed decisions and escalation to [CLIENT] when broader impacts are identified.

We'll address routine matters collaboratively through the O&M Committees, where Jacobs and [CLIENT] will review performance, trends, risks, and upcoming needs and take prompt action without unnecessary escalation. We'll elevate decisions with cost, policy, or contractual implications to the Executive Committee in accordance with the governance requirements of the contract, so [CLIENT] retains full oversight. This disciplined governance approach ensures decisions are made at the right level, by the right people, and at the right time—balancing operational agility with accountability and control.

In areas of shared risk such as energy, chemicals, odor control, solids handling and disposal, technology and IT systems, and regulatory fees, Jacobs will take a proactive decision approach with no surprises. We'll communicate issues and decisions early through the governance forums using clearly defined decision protocols, decision logs, and escalation paths to ensure accountability, traceability, and transparency.
