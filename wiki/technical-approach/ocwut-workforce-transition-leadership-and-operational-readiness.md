# Workforce Transition, Leadership, and Facility-Specific Operational Readiness

**People are the foundation of a successful transition.** Our workforce transition approach is designed to retain critical institutional knowledge, reduce uncertainty for employees, and ensure [CLIENT] has a qualified, fully prepared team ready to perform on day one. From the start of the transition period, we’ll treat staffing, onboarding, and training as a core transition workstream with clear accountability, defined milestones, and direct alignment with the staffing plan and Schedule 19 requirements.

We’ll offer employment to qualified and interested incumbent staff and work to retain the experience, plant knowledge, and site familiarity they bring to the program. Our goal is not simply to replace one workforce with another, but to carry forward the strengths of the existing team while integrating our operating practices, tools, training systems, and support resources to strengthen performance across the program.

We also understand that transition creates uncertainty for employees and their families. We address that directly through clear communication, visible leadership, and a structured onboarding process that sets expectations early. During transition, we’ll host informational sessions to introduce Jacobs, explain how we operate, review benefits and roles, and give employees a clear understanding of next steps. Our experienced HR and employee relations professionals will support this process, and employees will have direct access to regional and project leadership throughout.

Our commitment to investing in people, communicating clearly, supporting professional development, and creating a culture where staff feel supported and see a path for success means that, when employees experience a well-managed transition, they stay engaged, perform confidently, and contribute to a stable operating environment for the client.

Jacobs will provide accepted employment offers for all management positions prior to execution of the service agreement. Our proposed management team is identified in this proposal, and offers will be extended and accepted before execution, giving [CLIENT] confidence that the leadership team is committed and in place before transition begins. This is not a post-award action item. **It is a pre-execution commitment.**

We’ll recruit, onboard, train, and schedule the additional personnel needed to satisfy the staffing plan and support continuous operations by the operational start date. We’ll sequence staffing actions to support knowledge transfer, systems access, training, and transfer-of-service activities so personnel are in place early enough to contribute to readiness, not simply arrive at startup.

Workforce readiness will include verification of certifications, background checks, and facility access requirements and implementation of the required training plan. We’ll incorporate safety training, job-specific training, systems training, and cross-training to confirm adequate and competent coverage across functions. We’ll also establish the required on-call structure to ensure the facilities are continuously supported by trained and qualified personnel.

If staffing gaps occur during transition, we’ll fill them with a combination of preidentified local candidates and experienced regional resources, many of whom hold ODEQ certifications, to maintain required coverage and protect operational readiness while continuing to build a stable long-term team.

**We’ll deploy an experienced transition leadership team responsible for planning, coordination, and execution of all transition activities.** This team will serve as the single point of accountability for [CLIENT] and will maintain alignment across staffing, safety, compliance, asset management, systems integration, and communications. Their role is both strategic and hands-on, with a consistent presence throughout transition.

Kelly Irving will serve as Transition Manager, leveraging decades of leadership experience and a strong track record of delivering stable, compliant startups. Mike Boven will support execution in the field, focusing on day-to-day coordination, system readiness, and operational alignment. Kelly and Mike work closely in their roles and have teamed up on several successful transitions, bringing an extra level of efficiency and confidence to [CLIENT].

**Exhibit 5-15** shows the transition organization chart and the leadership and support structure we’ll apply to manage transition from mobilization through startup. Together, this team will keep transition activities on schedule, resolve issues quickly, and maintain clear communication with [CLIENT].

Jacobs will tailor transition activities to the scope and operating profile of [CLIENT]'s wastewater facilities, applying a wastewater-focused startup approach rather than a one-size-fits-all transition model. Early efforts will focus on understanding and validating the systems most critical to reliable startup: biological treatment processes, biosolids handling and dewatering, lime stabilization, chemical feed systems, electrical infrastructure, plant control systems, laboratory operations, certification requirements, and QA/QC procedures. We’ll also prioritize the collection system interface at Witcher Pump Station, the biosolids land application program, and the elevated H2S conditions documented at North Canadian and Deer Creek, which will require site-specific safety protocols and equipment readiness verification during transition.

South Canadian presents a distinct transition challenge and opportunity. With the new 8.6-MGD activated sludge facility anticipated to come online in July 2026, we’ll take over a newly commissioned plant mid-transition. Our transition planning accounts for coordination with the construction contractor on equipment commissioning and warranty management, new equipment familiarization for operations staff, and startup protocol development for processes that will have limited operating history by January 1, 2027. Through our extensive design-build-operate portfolio, we regularly transition directly from construction completion into full operations, managing the critical handoff between construction teams and operating staff, navigating warranty periods, and building operational protocols around equipment that is brand new. That experience is built into how we’ll approach South Canadian from day one.

To manage all of this in a disciplined way, **we’ll use our comprehensive transition checklist workbook, which includes 1,730 action items across 30 workstreams, combining [CLIENT]-specific startup tasks with our internal controls, reviews, and readiness verification.** Organized by workstream, the checklist allows SMEs and transition leaders to track responsibilities, dependencies, due dates, and readiness status at a detailed level, serving as a standing reference in every transition team meeting from award through startup.
