# ISO 55001-Aligned Asset Management System, SAMP, and the Planning-to-Delivery Line of Site

We'll work closely with [CLIENT], a regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities, to develop a maintenance program built on a practical **Asset Management System (AMS) aligned to ISO 55001.** The Maintenance Committee will provide ongoing governance and oversight with input from the CMMS and SCADA subcommittees. The AMS will be documented in a **Strategic Asset Management Plan (SAMP)** that defines policy, roles, decision rights, and resources and timeframes for implementation with the line of site [CLIENT] desires, as illustrated in **Exhibit IV-42**.

**Exhibit IV-42. "Line of site" ensures a comprehensive asset management approach for [CLIENT], from planning to delivery** (graphic 323_007CAM_2):

**Organizational Strategic Plan**
- Ensures clear understanding of the organization objectives and mission to customers
- Incorporates any existing strategic plans that relate to the assets (i.e., service goals, sustainability)

**Asset Management Policy**
- Articulates senior management commitment to asset management and continual improvement
- Provides "top down" direction regarding expectations and mandatory requirements for asset management and defines the key principles that underpin asset management at the organization

**Strategic Asset Management Plan (SAMP)**
- Defines what the organization intends to achieve from AM activities and by when
- Identifies and prioritizes key initiatives that support delivery of the AM Policy
- **SMART** asset management objectives
- High-level overview of resources and timescales for implementation

**Asset Management Plans (AMPs)**
- Provides the approach to managing the assets over the short, medium, and long term
- Outlines the long-term plan for the assets including service expectations, timelines and funding, and resource requirements

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the ISO 55001 framing, the four-rung ladder from organizational strategic plan to asset management plans, and the point that the SAMP is where policy, roles, decision rights, resources, and timeframes get written down. This is the block to lead an asset management approach with whenever the RFP asks how maintenance decisions will be governed rather than how work will be done.

Pursuit-specific: the committee/subcommittee structure and its names; whether the client already has a strategic plan or AM policy to incorporate rather than create; the exhibit graphic, which is client-branded and needs re-lettering.
