# Fleet Maintenance Program for Plant, Sewer, and Field Vehicles

Fleet maintenance represents an additional skillset to the wastewater and D&D facilities maintenance, and our approach will ensure all [CLIENT] vehicles and equipment remain reliable, safe, and fully available to support operations across the water reclamation facilities, conveyance system, and field environments. We'll leverage Jacobs' national fleet management expertise, our CMMS/asset management discipline, and a local team deeply familiar with [CLIENT]'s operating conditions to deliver the following:

**Comprehensive Coverage of [CLIENT]'s Fleet:** Jacobs will maintain all vehicles and equipment listed in RFP Exhibit A. Our Fleet Maintenance staff will perform all required PM, approved PdM, and CM activities consistent with RFP Exhibit B requirements, covering on-road vehicles (sedans, pickups, vans, utility vehicles); heavy equipment (front-end loaders, forklifts, man-lifts); sewer maintenance vehicles (vacuum trucks, CCTV trucks); and plant vehicles (burden carriers, bicycles). This includes full compliance with [CLIENT] requirements for vehicle cleanliness, safety equipment, warranty coordination, and NEXGEN CMMS-based documentation as discussed above.

**CMMS-Driven, Condition-Based Fleet Management:** Based on our experience implementing NEXGEN, we understand its capabilities and intend to use them, as appropriate, as the backbone of our Fleet Maintenance program. We'll use NEXGEN's Asset Inventory module to maintain a complete record for each vehicle—specifications, work order history, and associated PM schedules—all in one place. PM and PdM modules will drive smart scheduling of work orders based on time, meter readings, and condition so we can address emerging issues before they become breakdowns. All work orders—PM, CM, PdM, inspections, and repairs—will be created and closed in real time via mobile devices, with cost, labor, and parts tracked at the asset level to support transparent decisions and optimized lifecycle cost. Using NEXGEN's AM Planning module, we'll turn this condition and cost data into forward-looking forecasts of capital and O&M needs, including visuals of expected renewal timing based on useful life, financial life, and inflation assumptions, so [CLIENT] can clearly see fleet reliability risks and replacement priorities.

**Reliable PM and CM:** Jacobs will implement a structured PM program that's integrated with the facilities PM systems. For CM activities, we'll self-perform repairs wherever possible to minimize downtime, obtain [CLIENT] pre-authorization for outsourced repairs or parts exceeding service agreement thresholds, and maintain spare parts inventory consistent with the Initial Inventory requirements. Additionally, we'll provide [CLIENT] with a temporary vehicle whenever a [CLIENT] vehicle requires more than 1 day of service, ensuring uninterrupted field operations.

**Annual Vehicle Assessments and Budget Collaboration:** In accordance with RFP Exhibit B requirements, Jacobs will conduct annual vehicle condition assessments, prepare detailed specifications for vehicle replacements and maintain a technical specification library in the CMMS, and collaborate with [CLIENT] each April in the annual Fleet Maintenance and replacement planning meeting. Jacobs will use NEXGEN's available tool modules such as AM Planning for Intelligent Asset Management to forecast the fleet's capital and operational costs over the upcoming years.

**Safety, Compliance, and Operational Readiness:** Jacobs embeds safety and reliability into all Fleet Maintenance activities, including equipping our vehicles with fire extinguishers, first aid kits, and safety gear and providing staff training in equipment inspections, heavy-vehicle safety, and CDL-relevant operations, as needed. We'll also ensure garage equipment (lifts, cranes, tools, etc.) at [CLIENT]'s central vehicle garage are properly maintained to ensure safe and compliant repair operations. Additionally, we'll provide towing services for [CLIENT].

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the five-part structure (coverage, CMMS-driven condition-based management, reliable PM/CM with self-perform preference, annual assessment and budget collaboration, safety and readiness). It works for any O&M contract where the client's fleet comes with the scope.

Pursuit-specific: the RFP exhibit references, the vehicle and equipment classes, the CMMS platform and module names, the April planning-meeting cadence, the garage location, and the loaner-vehicle threshold (more than 1 day of service) and towing commitment — each is a real obligation and needs pursuit-team sign-off before reuse.

Pairs with [Maintenance Transparency, Reporting, and Governance](mmsd-maintenance-transparency-reporting-governance.md), which carries the fleet-specific communication commitments.
