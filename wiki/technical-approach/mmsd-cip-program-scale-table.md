## Reuse guidance

The reusable element is the device, not the data: an exhibit that puts the client's own CIP scale on the page, broken out by functional area with project counts and projected budget, under a headline that states the conclusion the evaluator should draw (an asset management approach is needed for strategic alignment and efficient resource allocation). The functional-area letter codes (C, I, J, K, M, P, S) are this client's CIP nomenclature.
