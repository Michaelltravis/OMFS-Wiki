# Operations Plan, SOP Library, and the Weekly Process Control Rhythm

## Operations Plan

Jacobs will implement a structured Operations Plan that brings consistency and control to day-to-day operations. The plan **establishes clear, repeatable procedures for operations, maintenance, safety, compliance, and reporting** — areas where variability today drives reactive performance and inconsistent outcomes.

It defines roles, routines, and escalation paths, so issues are identified and addressed early, not after they impact compliance or reliability. A formal SOP library will govern all critical activities, including startup and shutdown, routine monitoring, abnormal conditions, and work practices, so work is performed consistently across shifts, regardless of operator. SOPs will remain living documents, updated as equipment, permits, and operating conditions evolve, and reinforced through shift turnover, training, and routine supervisory oversight.

## How We Monitor, Verify, and Elevate Performance

Jacobs' process control strategy serves as the focal point for daily operations by maintaining steady-state treatment, preventing process upsets, and supporting permit compliance through effective monitoring, verification, and reporting. **We align process control with sampling, laboratory testing, field observations, and routine performance review, so operators identify changing conditions early and respond in a timely, consistent way.** Unit Process Control Plans (UPCPs) define targets, control limits, monitoring points, and response actions by unit process, grounded in plant design, influent characteristics, performance goals, and applicable permit requirements.

Our process control strategy operates in tandem with the Sampling and Testing QA/QC Plan (Schedule 3), which provides the data foundation for every process decision we make. Operators can't control what they can't measure, and compliance starts with data you can defend.

Process control is carried out through a disciplined weekly rhythm supported by the proven tools summarized in **Exhibit 1-14**. We document weekly process control decisions, formally review and refresh the process control plan at least annually, and **equip both on-site staff and [CLIENT] with tools that streamline reporting and provide immediate access to system information**, as illustrated in **Exhibit 1-15**.

Our process control tools support a robust weekly process control rhythm, with daily inputs (rounds, sampling, SCADA) feeding the weekly meetings, which drive outputs (adjustments, work orders), and provide transparent reporting via real-time dashboards, monthly process scoring reviews, and annual UPCP refresh closing the loop.
