# Level 3 PdM Technologies for the Conveyance System

## 4.2. Proposed PdM technologies for [CLIENT]'s conveyance system

**AquaDNA Deragger** is an intelligent pump optimization technology that actively prevents ragging and blockage events by automatically adjusting pump operation to clear debris before it accumulates. Installed at high-consequence conveyance pump stations, the Deragger improves hydraulic performance, reduces pump starts and energy use associated with rag-induced inefficiency, and minimizes emergency callouts and maintenance interventions. For [CLIENT], a regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities and a regional conveyance system, Jacobs proposes deploying AquaDNA Deraggers at two jointly selected pump stations as a proof of concept, targeting locations with a history of clogging or elevated operational risk. Integrating this technology into the Level 3 PdM framework allows pump performance anomalies to be detected earlier and addressed proactively, enhancing reliability across the conveyance system. See **Exhibit IV-62** for an example dashboard.

## 4.3. Additional PdM technologies included in our base fee

Beyond the recommended Level 3 PdM technologies listed above, we've included two Level 3 PdM technologies as part of our Base Fee. We intend to implement them as part of our value-added innovations discussed above in **Exhibit IV-52** under *Section 5.3 Value-Added Improvements and Innovations Included as Part of the Base Fee*. These two technologies will support more cost-effective maintenance of the conveyance system and include:

- **InfoSense SL-RAT** uses acoustics, rather than CCTV or cleaning, to rapidly assess blockages in gravity sewer lines. A transmitter and receiver placed at adjacent manholes measure sound transmission to determine whether the pipe segment is clear, partially obstructed, or likely blocked. The tool requires only two operators, minimal setup time, and no confined-space entry. SL-RAT helps [CLIENT] prioritize cleaning, reduce unnecessary Vactor truck deployments, and lower overall conveyance maintenance costs.

- **Dragonfly and ArgonLite supplemental sewer inspection technologies** are supplemental inspection tools designed for small-diameter or hard-to-access sewer segments where traditional CCTV or acoustic assessments may be impractical. These lightweight, portable systems capture visual and structural information within short pipe runs, providing additional insight into localized defects, debris accumulation, infiltration, or condition anomalies. While cost and deployment considerations are still being evaluated, Dragonfly and ArgonLite can complement [CLIENT]'s acoustic SL-RAT assessments by offering targeted visual confirmation or refined condition detail where needed. Used selectively within the Level 3 PdM framework, these tools can enhance conveyance risk assessment and support more efficient maintenance planning. See **Exhibit IV-63** for examples of this innovative defect coding tool and data-driven recommendations dashboard.

These technologies collectively support a comprehensive condition-monitoring strategy across [CLIENT]'s treatment and conveyance environments.
