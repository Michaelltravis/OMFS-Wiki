# Exhibit: Value-Added Investments and Potential Future Savings by Category

**Exhibit IV-2. $107M in value-added investments and savings over the 10-year term (included in the Base Fee), plus $21–53M in potential future savings.** Graphic asset ID `307_007CAM_6`.

### Included in the Base Fee — $107M over the 10-year term

| Category | Value | Items |
|---|---|---|
| Savings | $58M | Maintenance Optimization with Regional Support; Intelligent O&M; Chemical Optimizations; Power Demand Management; Power Generation Optimization; Digital Twin Implementation; Dragonfly and Argon; Digital Tools |
| Onsite Support | $19.2M | 9.5 additional FTEs beyond minimum requirements; region-based Rapid Assessment Response Team; [CLIENT]-knowledgeable staff |
| Offsite Support | $15M | Annual trainings; discounted rate for engineering services |
| Regional Partnership | $5.3M | Fishing pier – design, permitting, and construction oversight; advocacy support; workforce development |
| Best Practices | $4.9M | Annual Innovations Workshop; ISO 55001-aligned asset management; OT asset management software platform; OT asset backup and version control; OMFS-managed process control infrastructure; onsite asset tag fabrication; site security evaluation; Safety360 consultants |
| Investments | $4.6M | Shims for interplant pumping energy efficiency; pilot for drier feed mixers; pilot for digester H2S control; AquaDNA pump station deraggers; belt press investment at the second water reclamation facility; vehicle safety |
| Studies | $154K | New source evaluation; HSW study – evaluate additional high-strength waste (HSW) sources; landfill gas at the first water reclamation facility |

### Potential future savings, beyond the Base Fee — $21–53M

| Category | Value | Items |
|---|---|---|
| Process Improvements | $12–33M | Addition of lube rings for TWAS pumping; install pipe to connect landfill gas; new belt filter presses at the first water reclamation facility; improve influent channel mixing; anaerobic digestion / microbial hydrolysis process (MHP); sidestream treatment / sludge thickening; aeration control at both WRFs; dewatering and drying (D&D) dust minimization; sludge bulking control; waste heat recovery; mitigate fugitive biogas emissions |
| Future SCADA/OT Improvements | $8–17M | Jacobs-managed OT Security Operations Center (OT SOC); intrusion detection system; shoreline intrusion detection; mobile video capability; automated security workflows and maintenance |
| Future Optimizations | $1–3M | [CLIENT]'s biosolids product production using hybrid optimizer model; drive cost-effective natural gas purchases; dewatering and drying (D&D) odor control and odor emissions; polymer optimization; odor control optimization; AquaDNA smart technology for pump station and collection system |

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the seven-bucket "included" taxonomy (savings, onsite support, offsite support, regional partnership, best practices, investments, studies) and the three-bucket "future" taxonomy, plus the menu of items as a prompt list when scoping a new value-add offer. Pursuit-specific: every value, the FTE count, the community investment (the fishing pier here), the named digital products, and which facility receives each capital item.
