# Predictive Maintenance Technologies — Five-Technology Reliability-Centered Toolkit

Jacobs' predictive maintenance technologies support cost-effective, reliability-centered asset maintenance. Applied together, they replace reactive repair with forward-looking diagnostics, catching each of the common failure modes before it reaches the point of equipment failure. Our toolkit includes:

- **Ultrasound Analysis** — for leak detection, mechanical inspection, electrical inspection including electric arc flash detection, steam trap maintenance, valve testing, and acoustic precision lubrication of bearings.
- **Precision Alignment** — of rotating equipment, which effectively corrects misalignment, a leading cause of bearing failure in rotating equipment.
- **Infrared Thermography** — to spot anomalies not otherwise visible to the eye, finding potential faults in electrical and mechanical applications before they lead to equipment failure.
- **Lubrication/Oil Analysis** — to monitor the condition and degradation of the asset, informing the decision for continued operation, filtering, or oil volume change.
- **Vibration Measurement** — to identify misalignments or imbalances before issues or failures develop, including on critical, high-dollar equipment that traditional RMS vibration measurement cannot detect.

These technologies are deployed by field-trained maintenance specialists and are supported by regional subject matter experts in condition monitoring (for example, Artesis and Augury AI platforms), lubrication science (tribology), and root cause analysis. Findings feed the CMMS as corrective work orders, so a PdM detection becomes a scheduled, tracked, and closed repair rather than an observation. (Graphic asset ID `124_008A26`.)
