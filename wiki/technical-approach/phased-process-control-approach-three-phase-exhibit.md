## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-column, time-boxed structure itself — stabilize the fundamentals first (SRT-based wasting, DO control where instrumentation allows, restored automation and analyzers, UPCPs/sampling plans/SOPs, energy baselines), then optimize with accumulated data, then pursue capital-linked and continuous-improvement items. The pattern converts a long list of technical intentions into a schedule the evaluator can hold you to, and it is especially effective in a challenger bid where the incumbent has let instrumentation and automation lapse.

Pursuit-specific: every line item. Rebuild the three columns from the pursuit's own site-visit findings and CIP schedule, keeping the same rhythm of roughly six to eight items per phase.
