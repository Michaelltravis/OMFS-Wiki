# Tiered Communication and Reporting Protocol

From Day 1, the project team will improve communication with the client by operating with a no-surprises, transparent partnership that provides clear points of contact, rapid escalation paths, and consistent documentation of actions and outcomes. The [PROJECT MANAGER] maintains open day-to-day coordination with client staff to accelerate decisions, identify risks early, and resolve issues before they affect service. The operating principle: **the right information reaches the right decision-maker at the right time** to protect safety, compliance, and uninterrupted service.

Communication protocols are implemented during transition and carried into early operations, combining frequent day-to-day coordination with structured recurring forums:

| When | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **Day-to-Day** | Open exchange of information to ensure transparency and collaboration | [CLIENT] / project management team |
| **Weekly** | Weekly meeting reviewing the previous week's events, discussing future week plans, and documenting decisions | Project manager and client-designated representative |
| **Monthly** | Progress update and collaboration review of process and maintenance performance; capital improvement plan (CIP) and operational impact review; required monthly written reports; process control and CMMS reporting | [CLIENT] / project management teams |
| **Quarterly** | Quarterly business review of progress, future objectives, and continuous-improvement opportunities | Regional operations management and project management teams |
| **Annual** | Annual Innovation Workshop reviewing prior-year accomplishments and setting new goals; annual report with org-chart updates; 5-year CIP recommendations/updates | Executive team, external SMEs, client leadership, project management teams |

During recurring meetings, the team provides written monthly reporting aligned to the required scope of work, including operational and process control results, compliance/near-exceedances, maintenance and repair activity and costs, M&R fund tracking, power utilization (kWh/MG), significant/imminent events, and collection system activities. The team also manages required permits and reporting and routes media inquiries through the client's authorized representative, as directed.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the five-tier cadence (day-to-day → weekly → monthly → quarterly → annual) and the "right information, right decision-maker, right time" framing. Tailor per pursuit: swap in the actual required monthly report contents from the RFP's scope of work, adjust attendee titles to match the proposed org structure, and confirm whether an Annual Innovation Workshop (see companion block) is being offered as a differentiator. Pair with the O&M Management Systems Framework block (covers "Project Management" as one of its 10 categories) and the Annual Innovation Workshop Program block.
