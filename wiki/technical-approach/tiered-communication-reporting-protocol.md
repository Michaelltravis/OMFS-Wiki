# Tiered Communication and Reporting Protocol

From Day 1, we will improve communication between the project team and [CLIENT] — a coastal New England municipality — by operating with a **no-surprises, transparent partnership**, providing clear points of contact, rapid escalation paths, and consistent documentation of actions and outcomes. Project Manager Nathan Callison will maintain open day-to-day coordination with [CLIENT] staff to accelerate decisions, identify risks early, and resolve issues before they affect service. We treat communication as an operational control: **the right information will reach the right decision-maker at the right time** to protect safety, compliance, and uninterrupted service.

The Communication Protocols shown in **Exhibit 5-3** will be implemented during transition and early operations. These protocols will combine frequent day-to-day coordination with structured forums to review performance, maintenance execution, risks, and upcoming work. During recurring meetings with [CLIENT], we will provide written monthly reporting aligned to the required scope of work, including operational and process control results, compliance/near-exceedances, maintenance and repair activity and costs, M&R fund tracking, power utilization (kWh/MG), significant/imminent events, and collection system activities. We will also manage required permits and reporting and will route media inquiries to [CLIENT]'s authorized representative, as directed. Maintenance and compliance reporting are discussed in more detail later in this approach.

## Exhibit 5-3. Jacobs' communication protocols (aligned with [CLIENT] during transition) for a more collaborative, transparent partnership

| When | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **Day-to-Day** | **Meetings and Discussions** — open exchange of information to ensure transparency and collaboration | [CLIENT]/Jacobs Project Management Team |
| **Weekly** | **Weekly Meeting** — review the previous week's events; discuss future week plans; document decisions | Jacobs Project Manager and [CLIENT] Designated Representative |
| **Monthly** | **Monthly Reviews and Reports** — progress update and collaboration review of process and maintenance performance; CIP and impact to operations; required monthly written reports and process control and CMMS reporting | [CLIENT]/Jacobs Project Management Teams |
| **Quarterly** | **Quarterly Business Review** — review quarterly progress; discuss future objectives and opportunities for continuous improvement | Jacobs Regional Management; [CLIENT]/Jacobs Project Management Teams |
| **Annual** | **Annual Meetings and Reporting** — Annual Innovation Workshop to review the prior year's accomplishments while establishing new goals and objectives for the following year to ensure continuous improvement; Annual Report with any org chart updates; 5-year CIP recommendations or updates as needed | Jacobs Executive Team; External Jacobs Utility Experts; [CLIENT] Leadership Team; [CLIENT]/Jacobs Project Management Teams |

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the five-tier cadence (day-to-day → weekly → monthly → quarterly → annual), the named attendee column that shows escalation reaches regional and executive leadership, and the "communication is an operational control" framing. Tailor per pursuit: swap in the actual required monthly report contents from the RFP's scope of work, name the actual proposed project manager, adjust attendee titles to match the proposed org structure, and confirm whether an Annual Innovation Workshop is being offered as a differentiator.
