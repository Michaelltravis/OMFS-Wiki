# ISO 55001-Aligned AMS, CMMS, and Risk Workshop

JC Solutions will strive to elevate [CLIENT]’s asset management and maintenance program to the next level, leveraging industry-leading tools, strategies, and PdM technologies to enhance asset reliability, reduce failure risks, optimize cost tracking, and proactively plan for obsolescence and capital needs including CIP development. Given the mix of brand new and older infrastructure across the facilities, a structured, data-driven asset management program is critical to ensuring a highly targeted approach that fosters long-term efficiency, cost savings, and system reliability. JC Solutions’ integrated Asset Management System (AMS) aligns with [CLIENT]’s scope of work, providing a comprehensive framework for delivering strategic, tactical, and operational asset management.

As an industry leader in asset management for water utilities, we share [CLIENT]’s commitment to protecting your critical assets. We have developed asset management systems (AMS) for hundreds of clients using our proven approaches. We propose to develop and implement an ISO 55001-aligned AMS to address challenges, enhance reliability, optimize asset lifecycles, reduce risks, and drive continuous improvement through data-driven decisions—delivering measurable value to [CLIENT] and the facilities. We use the ISO-55001 standard when applying an AMS at our projects, and we have helped clients who desire official ISO 55001 certification achieve that significant credential. Should [CLIENT] desire to do so, our team can also help the facilities achieve ISO 55001 certification. Regardless of whether official certification is sought, JC Solutions’ ISO 55001-aligned delivery approach offers [CLIENT] significant value.

$300,000 WORTH OF INVESTMENTS BY JC SOLUTIONS

Our AMS for [CLIENT] will align with ISO 55001 standards and is built on the 10-Box Model, which incorporates all facets of establishing a world-class asset management program for [CLIENT]’s facilities. Key deliverables of this system will be the Long-term Maintenance Plan, which will guide decision-making, lifecycle cost analysis, and capital planning to ensure infrastructure remains reliable, resilient, and future-ready. It will provide the framework for our day-to-day maintenance planning requirements while ensuring efficient resource allocation and performance tracking.

We will hold a condition assessment and a risk workshop with [CLIENT]. A comprehensive risk workshop agenda based on condition assessment findings will include reviewing key deficiencies, analyzing risks, developing mitigation strategies, and establishing monitoring frameworks. Priority agenda items are risk categorization and scoring exercises followed by detailed action planning with assigned responsibilities and resource requirements.

Strategy and Planning - JC Solutions will establish a strategic asset management framework that aligns with [CLIENT]’s operational objectives and integrates lifecycle cost analysis, capital investment prioritization, and risk-based decision-making. The first step in this process is conducting a baseline condition assessment, which will provide a risk-based ranking of all critical assets. This assessment will help optimize capital planning and ensure that maintenance and asset renewal efforts are focused on high-priority infrastructure. Additionally, JC Solutions will update and validate the CMMS with our Maintenance Connection system during transition and ensure that the asset registry is complete, accurate, and reflective of real-world conditions.

Leadership and Governance - A successful asset management program requires clear leadership and accountability. Our governance framework will ensure that all maintenance and asset management activities align with [CLIENT]’s strategic goals. Our Project Manager Anthony Benavidez and Maintenance Manager Nathan Dahl will work with our Asset Management Lead Graham Knowles and his team to develop our AMP, driving compliance with industry best practices and regulatory requirements.

Asset Management Decision-Making - Data-driven decision-making is at the core of JC Solutions’ approach to asset management. By leveraging CMMS tracking and analytics, we will centralize asset data, enabling optimized work-order generation, cost analysis, and maintenance prioritization. The system will facilitate risk-based asset prioritization, allowing [CLIENT] to direct resources toward the most critical infrastructure needs. The CMMS will also support planned obsolescence strategies, ensuring that asset replacements and upgrades are proactively managed, avoiding unnecessary costs associated with emergency repairs.

Exhibit 2-39. *The 10-box model is a globally recognized framework that integrates strategic planning, operational management, and performance optimization. It emphasizes the interplay between technical, financial, and organizational elements, ensuring all aspects of asset management are interconnected and aligned with ISO 55001 standards.*
