# Compliance Accountability, Jacobs Reach-Back, and CERM Workforce Development

JC Solutions will assume responsibility for meeting permit requirements, including NPDES, air quality, water quality, and stormwater. We will ensure full compliance with EPA, Georgia EPD, and [CLIENT] regulations to avoid permit violations, fines, or environmental risks. We have a past record of stellar regulatory compliance and environmental stewardship. [CLIENT] can rely on our robust internal regulatory-audit processes and QA/QC protocol that are driven by leadership oversight, as well as the extensive compliance training we offer our onsite teams. Our approach ensures compliance including routine and emergency sampling/testing as required by state and federal regulations and provides proactive management of odor control and noise reduction.

Thorough tracking is key to maintaining a record of perfect compliance and reporting. To facilitate our record of near-perfect compliance, we’ll use the web-based dashboard to manage project information and communicate with our staff and clients. The dashboard will monitor and track operating parameters and regulatory deadlines, ensuring performance requirements are maintained.

Our operations will be led by a highly experienced, licensed management team with extensive MBR and advanced wastewater-treatment expertise. These leaders will build and maintain a skilled workforce fully qualified to handle the complexities of these sophisticated facilities, ensuring all required certifications and specialized knowledge are in place across all operational areas. Our management approach leverages Jacobs' unparalleled depth of technical resources, providing immediate reach-back capability to subject-matter experts (SMEs) in virtually every aspect of wastewater facilities to swiftly address any operational challenges without service disruptions. This technical foundation is enhanced by our comprehensive workforce-development program, which provides structured career-advancement pathways, technical and leadership training, and continuing-education opportunities. We invest in our people through specialized operator-certification preparation, cross-training across facilities, and access to Jacobs' global knowledge network, ensuring continuous growth while building operational resilience and succession planning that support long-term operational excellence for [CLIENT].

Our approach to staff recruitment, training, and retention, and thoughtful transition process will be led by an experienced management team and supported by a deep bench of technical experts in wastewater treatment and O&M. Highlights of our staff training and advancement program include refocusing on operational fundamentals through state-approved and JC Solutions-developed training, such as operator math, chemical feed systems, and process troubleshooting; driving and incentivizing higher-level certifications by staff for Georgia operator and maintenance certifications and Certified Maintenance Reliability Technician (CMRT) licenses, including up to $2,000 bonuses to staff for each grade of advancement or new certification achieved; an annual Project Manager Summit; diverse training opportunities including OMFS University classes, Mentor Program, and WEFTEC Ops Challenge 2025; and an innovative workforce-development program driven by JV partner CERM to grow the talent pool for today and the future.

This block intentionally distinguishes Jacobs’ technical resources and global knowledge network from CERM’s workforce-development role. Substitute the target regulator and local partner; validate all program names and incentive amounts.
