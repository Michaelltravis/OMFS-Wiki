# Predictive Maintenance Program — Vibration, Electrical Testing, and Full PdM Technology Suite

PdM catches what PM schedules can't: the early signs of degradation that happen between scheduled intervals. **We'll deploy a comprehensive PdM technology suite across [CLIENT]'s facilities — [CLIENT] being a southcentral US municipal water utility trust operating four wastewater treatment plants and a major pump station — selectively targeted at equipment based on its criticality and failure modes, ensuring the highest-value equipment receives the most advanced monitoring.**

## Vibration Analysis

We'll perform quarterly vibration analysis on all large rotating equipment: the four 1,250 HP pumps at Witcher Pump Station, ten blowers (350–800 HP) at North Canadian, South Canadian SBR blowers (and the new oxidation ditch equipment as it comes online), and lift station pumps and blowers at Chisholm Creek and Deer Creek. Annual vibration analysis will cover additional rotating equipment per Schedule 2, including retention station pumps, lift station gear boxes, sand filter pumps, and Phase 3B blowers at North Canadian.

When results identify deficiencies, we'll open corrective work orders within 5 days (PD-1 KPI). The value of this discipline is tangible: vibration trending on North Canadian's blowers, for example, will help us detect the mechanical issues that led to the recent blower failure incident, potentially preventing a recurrence.

## Electrical Testing

We'll perform the full scope of electrical testing defined in Schedule 4 for all substations, high- and medium-voltage equipment, relay testing, and calibration across the quarterly, annual, and 5-year cycles. This includes transformer inspections, switchgear and capacitor maintenance, 4160V motor control center inspections at North Canadian, protective relay testing, infrared inspection, connection torque checks, and 5-year Doble testing with oil analysis. Specialty high-voltage electrical testing beyond on-site staff capabilities, including work covered under Schedule 11, will be performed by our qualified subcontractors and fully integrated into NexGen for tracking and follow-up. Corrective work orders will be opened within 5 days of any identified deficiency (PD-3 KPI).

## Full PdM Technology Suite

Beyond vibration and electrical testing, our PdM program includes motor signature analysis to detect rotor and stator faults, particularly valuable for the aging motor fleet at North Canadian, along with ultrasound testing for leak detection, bearing lubrication monitoring, and electrical discharge detection; infrared thermography to identify abnormal heat patterns in electrical and mechanical systems; and oil analysis to evaluate lubricant condition and contamination for early wear detection.

All PdM findings feed directly into NexGen. Deficiencies identified through any predictive testing method will be addressed within 60 days (PD-5 KPI). Over time, trending PdM data will refine PM frequencies and justify targeted investments in monitoring technology where the return avoided downtime is clear.

**Exhibit — Jacobs' predictive maintenance technologies support cost-effective, reliability centered asset maintenance** (graphic asset ID 167_009385):

- **Vibration measurement** of rotating equipment to identify misalignments or imbalances before issues or failures develop.
- **Infrared thermography** to spot anomalies not otherwise visible to the eye, finding potential faults in electrical and mechanical applications before they lead to equipment failure.
- **Ultrasound analysis** for leak detection, mechanical inspection, electrical inspection, steam trap maintenance, valve testing, electric arc flash detection, and acoustic precision lubrication of bearings — including findings that traditional RMS vibration measurement cannot detect.
- **Lubrication/oil analysis** of critical, high-dollar equipment to monitor its condition and the degradation of the asset, informing decisions for continued operation, filtering, or oil volume change.
- **Precision alignment** of rotating equipment effectively corrects misalignment, a leading cause of bearing failure in rotating equipment.
