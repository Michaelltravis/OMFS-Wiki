# Outcomes of an Integrated, Reliability-Focused Maintenance Program

Our integrated, reliability-focused maintenance program will align planning, execution, and reliability analysis into a single, transparent process that drives better decisions, reduces risk, and supports the SAMP, capital planning, and improved long-term reliability. Key items for success include:

- **Connecting data across systems:** Integrating linear asset GIS and inspection history so tunnel and collection system risks are visible alongside plant and vertical process assets.
- **Standardizing asset information:** Enforcing naming conventions, hierarchy standards, and required fields in NEXGEN to ensure consistency and accuracy.
- **Leveraging operational insights:** Mapping SCADA tags to assets to capture runtime, starts, and available condition indicators such as vibration and temperature.
- **Defining evidence standards:** Establishing clear photo documentation and meter point requirements for each asset class to validate work quality and compliance.
- **Partnering for success:** Working closely with [CLIENT], a regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities, as a key partner to ensure alignment, transparency, and shared accountability across all reliability initiatives.
- **Backlog discipline and transparency:** Managing CM/PM backlog as a leading risk indicator — using NEXGEN and dashboards to track and report aging, priority, and resourcing — so critical work is executed before it becomes deferred maintenance that's not completed until the equipment fails.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: all six bullets transfer to any O&M pursuit with a CMMS and a client that wants visibility. The pattern — data, standards, operational signals, evidence, partnership, backlog — is a reusable close for a maintenance approach that needs to land in half a page.

Pursuit-specific: the CMMS name; the linear-asset scope; the evidence standards, which should match what the draft service agreement requires for payment authorization.
