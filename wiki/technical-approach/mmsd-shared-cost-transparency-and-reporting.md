# Shared-Cost Transparency and Reporting

Jacobs will maintain a complete, auditable record of shared-cost items for [CLIENT], a regional sewerage district, including energy, chemicals, fuel, and biosolids handling, and will provide detailed reporting that supports collaborative cost management. **Using integrated dashboards, Jacobs and [CLIENT] will jointly review data, KPI trends, and shared-cost metrics during regular governance meetings.** All monitored parameters will align with [CLIENT]'s **Standards of Performance** in Exhibit C, ensuring comprehensive visibility into treatment, conveyance, and solids operations as well as the cost and efficiency drivers behind them.

Jacobs will automatically flag alarm conditions or KPI deviations and document corrective actions in a shared log visible to [CLIENT]. Any process condition that could threaten permit compliance will trigger immediate notification to [CLIENT] and a written summary of corrective steps. Daily operations logs and monthly performance reports will summarize flow, loading, power, chemical, and solids data. We'll reconcile monthly shared-cost reports with invoices, inventory, and consumption data. Quarterly and annual performance summaries will be prepared for **Operations and Maintenance Committee** review, including efficiency trends, performance indicators, and improvement actions. An **Annual Performance and Optimization Report** will consolidate operational parameters, KPIs, and shared-cost metrics. All reports will be generated from validated datasets in compliance with RFP Exhibits B and Q and will be available to [CLIENT] through secure dashboards.

## Exhibit IV-36. Proven reporting framework for JXN Water (asset `280_007CAM_3`)

We'll implement a flexible performance monitoring and reporting system featuring a real-time digital dashboard to support [CLIENT]'s vision for transparent, data-driven operations. We use a similar framework for JXN Water. During startup with the City of Jackson, we developed department-level monthly reporting templates and client-facing dashboards to provide energy, chemical, and operational metrics across all facilities. These tools evolved into a quarterly performance summary and consolidated annual report which serve as a single auditable record of system performance, costs, and compliance. Our work with JXN Water demonstrates our ability to scale reporting cadence, streamline data entry for facility teams, and provide real-time data in a unified digital dashboard. We'll bring this approach to [CLIENT].

The exhibit reproduces a live Power BI dashboard from JXN Water (data as of 05/31/2025) showing daily water production year to date across J.H. Fewell, O.B. Curtis Conventional, and O.B. Curtis Membrane, with total average production of 37.56 MG/day against a 43-53 MG/day target band, plant-by-plant rolling 3- and 6-month averages, and year-over-year change.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the cadence ladder (daily, monthly, quarterly, annual) with a named deliverable at each rung, and the escalation rule that permit-threatening conditions are reported immediately in writing rather than in the next monthly. Showing a real dashboard screenshot from another client beats describing one.

Pursuit-specific: the shared-cost item list, the governance body, the report names, and the exhibit citations. If the client has an existing reporting format, commit to producing in that format rather than proposing a new one.
