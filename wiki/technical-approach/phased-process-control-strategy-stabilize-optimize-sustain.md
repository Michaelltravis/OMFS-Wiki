# Phased Process Control Strategy — Stabilize, Optimize, Sustain

Our process control strategy follows a phased approach — stabilize performance first, then optimize data and build confidence, and sustain gains over the contract term (**Exhibit 1-12**).

Each facility's Unit Process Control Plan (UPCP) will define specific target operating parameters and metrics aligned with Schedule 3 requirements — **solids retention time** (SRT) targets by season and loading condition, target MLSS concentrations for each treatment train, DO setpoints by basin zone, sludge blanket depth limits for secondary clarifiers, effluent quality targets for total suspended solids (TSS), biochemical oxygen demand (BOD), ammonia, and chlorine residual that provide operating margin below NPDES permit limits. During transition, we'll establish initial targets based on design data, historical performance, and conditions observed during our site assessments, then refine them as we collect operating data and confirm biological process response. These parameters and metrics will be documented in the UPCPs, reviewed weekly during process control meetings, and formally updated at least annually or sooner when conditions, equipment, or permit requirements change.

**EXHIBIT 1-12.** *Jacobs' Phased Process Control Strategy* (graphic 116_009385)

**PHASE 1 — STABILIZE (Transition → Month 6)**

- SRT-based wasting at all four facilities
- Expand DO profiling across all aeration basins
- Sludge blanket monitoring and clarifier protection
- Restore CL17 chlorine residual analyzers (Deer Creek, North Canadian)
- Establish UPCPs, sampling plans, and SOPs
- South Canadian startup: embed process control fundamentals from Day One
- Energy baseline tracking against Schedule 3 kWh/MG targets

**PHASE 2 — OPTIMIZE (Month 6 → Year 2)**

- Step feed implementation (North Canadian)
- DO-based aeration control where instrumentation reliability supports it
- Restore filtration automation (Deer Creek, Chisholm Creek)
- Chemically enhanced primary treatment (CEPT) evaluation for extreme wet weather
- Reactivate anoxic selector zones (Deer Creek)
- Right-size basin service at all facilities based on loading data
- Refine SRT targets from accumulated operating data
- Solids handling timing and blending improvements (Deer Creek, North Canadian)

**PHASE 3 — SUSTAIN (Year 2 → Contract Term)**

- Integrate onsite sodium hypochlorite generation (North Canadian 2028, Deer Creek 2029)
- Rehabilitate underutilized basins and filtration assets
- Incremental process improvements driven by operating data and trends
- Annual UPCP refresh and CPCS performance scoring
- CIP integration support (Deer Creek capacity expansion)
- Continuous energy optimization against established baselines
