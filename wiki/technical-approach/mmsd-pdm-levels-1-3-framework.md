# Levels 1–3 PdM Framework

**Level 1 – [CLIENT]-owned data and analytics.** Level 1 consists of asset and operational data collected and monitored directly by [CLIENT], a regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities and a regional conveyance system—primarily SCADA, historian trends, alarms, and operator observations. While not priced as PdM work, these datasets are essential inputs that Jacobs will incorporate into Level 3 analytics. Level 1 data provides the operational context needed to interpret condition signatures, identify trends, and refine predictive thresholds.

**Level 2 – Jacobs' standard PdM/PM (included in base fee).** Level 2 includes Jacobs' traditional predictive and preventive technologies: handheld vibration readings, infrared thermography, oil and tribology analysis, static electrical testing, and vendor/OEM-prescribed PM activities. [CLIENT] classifies these activities as PM, and they are fully included in the base O&M price. Level 2 provides measurable condition indicators and early warnings and serves as a foundation on which Level 3 is built.

**Level 3 – Advanced, real-time, integrated PdM (additive scope).** Level 3 PdM is an additive, on-demand scope that integrates continuous monitoring technologies, automated diagnostics, and CMMS-triggered workflows. Rather than relying on periodic measurements, Level 3 delivers **continuous or high-frequency analytics** that detect subtle changes in equipment behavior long before failure modes develop. Tools such as VibeCloud (real-time vibration and temperature), Artesis (motor current signature analysis and sensorless pump performance analytics), and complementary technologies like the **Sewer Line Rapid Assessment Tool (SL-RAT)** for acoustic inspection provide early insight into mechanical, electrical, and hydraulic degradation.

The distinguishing characteristic of Level 3 is not the sensor alone—it's the automated analysis, continuous diagnostics, and integration with the CMMS to generate actionable work orders. Level 3 unifies [CLIENT]'s Level 1 and Level 2 data with new real-time insights to form a complete, predictive view of asset health.

## Callout — Benefit to [CLIENT] of an integrated PdM approach

- Operational reliability improves as failure modes are detected earlier and equipment health is continuously monitored.
- Operational risk diminishes, particularly for high-consequence assets.
- Maintenance costs trend downward as unplanned outages and emergency responses are minimized.
- Energy efficiency improves through early detection and correction of electrical and hydraulic inefficiencies.
- Asset and capital planning decisions become more informed, supported by quantifiable condition data and demonstrated performance trends.
- Just as importantly, the program creates transparency and trust.
- [CLIENT] will no longer rely on vendor black boxes or fragmented PdM reporting. Instead, PdM insights become part of [CLIENT]'s everyday maintenance workflow, governed through established committees and fully visible in the CMMS.
