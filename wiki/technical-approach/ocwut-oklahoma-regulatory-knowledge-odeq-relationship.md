# Oklahoma Regulatory Knowledge and ODEQ Relationship

Ongoing internal audits and performance reviews will confirm compliance and identify opportunities for improvement. We'll track corrective actions to closure and verify their effectiveness.

Jacobs brings direct, current experience working within Oklahoma's regulatory framework. Our team understands ODEQ's OPDES program requirements, NPDES permit conditions applicable to Oklahoma wastewater facilities, Oklahoma's wastewater operator certification structure, land application permit processes, and the reporting obligations that define compliance under Oklahoma law.

We'll coordinate with ODEQ as a regulatory partner. **Notifying ODEQ early and proactively when issues arise, providing complete and accurate reports, and maintaining a consistent track record of good-faith cooperation is how we protect [CLIENT]'s regulatory standing and prevent minor issues from becoming enforcement actions.**
