# Lifecycle Maintenance, Risk, and Performance Management

Lifecycle Delivery and Maintenance Strategy - JC Solutions will manage the entire asset lifecycle—from acquisition to disposal—through a structured maintenance strategy and Long-term Maintenance Plan that combines PdM and PM. Our preventive maintenance optimization (PMO) and Level 3 PdM programs will shift the focus from reactive maintenance to proactive asset management. Routine maintenance inspections will allow operations staff to monitor equipment daily, identifying early signs of failure. PdM will be enhanced using advanced diagnostics such as ultrasound testing, vibration analysis, and motor signature assessments, helping to extend equipment life and reduce downtime. All PdM and PM tasks will be completed by JC Solutions’ in-house resources—either by the onsite team or with support from the regional maintenance team and technical support resources.

Asset Knowledge Enablers (Information Management) - A robust asset management program depends on accurate, accessible, and transparent data. Jacobs will ensure that all asset records are meticulously maintained in the CMMS, supporting lifecycle tracking, decision-making, and risk management. The CMMS will provide real-time visibility into asset condition, expected lifespan, and spare parts availability, ensuring that maintenance planning is based on comprehensive and up-to-date information. JC Solutions will also support five- and 10-year Capital Improvement Planning (CIP), allowing [CLIENT] to prioritize infrastructure investments and manage asset replacement budgets effectively. Through regular expenditure tracking and transparent reporting, [CLIENT] will know that all asset-related financial decisions are data-driven, accountable, and aligned with long-term goals.

Organization and People - Building a strong maintenance culture is critical to ensuring the long-term success of an asset management program. JC Solutions will align personnel structures and training programs with [CLIENT]’s asset management goals. Asset Management Lead Graham Knowles and his team of industry experts will provide training to equip staff with best practices in PdM, asset lifecycle planning, and data-driven decision-making. Maintenance Manager Nathan Dahl will oversee the day-to-day implementation of PdM and PM maintenance strategies and associated work orders. PdM and PM tasks requiring skills beyond those of the onsite team will be supported by Jacobs’ regional asset management, CMMS, and maintenance technology experts, including Maintenance Lead Michael Idzior, CMRT, CRL and his team.

Corrective maintenance will be systematically tracked within the CMMS, ensuring that all failure-related work orders are issued within 24 hours of detection and closed within 120 days. Additionally, JC Solutions will uphold high facility appearance standards, ensuring that all buildings, process areas, and fleet equipment remain clean, well-maintained, and professionally managed. Maintenance tasks will be scheduled and monitored through the CMMS, ensuring structured oversight and accountability.

These experts will provide ongoing technical support and guidance, ensuring that the onsite team has access to the latest industry innovations and methodologies, such as Artesis Condition Monitoring and PdM tools and Augury AI Sensors. Tools such as these prevent failure and drive down maintenance costs by identifying potential problems before they occur. Our technical support team will also use disciplines such as Tribology to maintain [CLIENT]’s critical assets. This technology will identify the correct lubrication for critical assets, which will reduce friction, improving energy efficiency and extending the lifespan of mechanical components.

Risk Management - Following an initial condition assessment at startup, JC Solutions will conduct regular asset condition assessments to identify potential vulnerabilities and proactively address high-risk assets before failures occur. Using a structured risk scoring methodology, we will establish network-wide risk profiles, which also support long-term CIP planning. Our risk-based approach will help protect asset value, extend infrastructure lifespan, and optimize maintenance investments.

Review and Continual Improvement - A commitment to continuous improvement is a defining feature of our AMS. We will conduct annual maintenance audits, performance reviews, and technology assessments to ensure that [CLIENT]’s asset management strategies remain efficient, adaptable, and aligned with industry advancements. Performance metrics will be tracked through real-time dashboards, providing instant insights into operational efficiency, maintenance performance, and asset health.

Performance Management - JC Solutions will deliver monthly KPI reports, regulatory updates, and maintenance tracking insights to ensure that [CLIENT] has full visibility into asset performance. These reports will include key metrics such as PM effectiveness, mean time between failures (MTBF), mean time to repair (MTTR), and overall equipment effectiveness (OEE). By monitoring these indicators, the JC Solutions team will help [CLIENT] continuously refine and improve maintenance practices to achieve higher reliability, lower costs, and greater operational efficiency.

Value and Outcomes - JC Solutions’ Integrated AMS will deliver measurable value, ensuring cost efficiencies, improved reliability, and extended asset lifespan. Our approach will align asset management activities with [CLIENT]’s long-term objectives, driving transparency, accountability, and strategic infrastructure investments. By leveraging JC Solutions’ expertise, [CLIENT] will benefit from an optimized maintenance program that enhances resilience, operational performance, and cost-effectiveness. JC Solutions will work closely with [CLIENT]’s staff in implementing data-driven asset management and maintenance strategies that ensure operational excellence, cost savings, and long-term reliability.

By integrating state-of-the-art predictive maintenance, advanced analytics, and our extensive regional expertise, we will position [CLIENT] for continued success and help elevate maintenance and asset management at the facilities now and into the future.
