# Deliverables Commitment: Records, Safety, Outreach, and Staffing Plans

|Deliverable or plan|JC Solutions’ understanding|Due|Source approach|
|---|---|---|---|
|Equipment Performance and Testing Plan|Prepare and regularly implement a plan for performance monitoring and scheduled testing of key equipment to ensure continuous compliance and efficient operation.|Within 180 days from the commencement date|Page 2-78|
|Exit Transition Plan|Submit an exit transition plan detailing procedures for smoothly transferring operations to a successor operator upon contract expiration or termination.|Within 10 days after the award of the contract|Page 2-112|
|Financial Reports|Prepare and submit regular financial reports reflecting expenditures, revenues, costs, and related financial matters for [CLIENT]’s oversight.|Within 90 days of end of each contract year|Page 2-90|
|Initial and Final Inventory|Provide comprehensive documentation of initial inventory at commencement and final inventory upon conclusion or termination of the agreement.|Chemicals and spare parts within 60 days prior to commencement date, and 90 days prior to end of contract term|Page 2-103|
|Insurance Certificates|Provide initial and ongoing verification of required insurance coverage through current insurance certificates that meet contract stipulations.|Initially due as stated in the RFP, and then annually|Appendix|
|Long-term Maintenance Plan|Develop and continuously update a long-term maintenance strategy for systematic preventive and predictive maintenance of plant assets and equipment.|Within 1 year of contract date; then annually, not later than 60 days prior to the beginning of each contract year|Page 2-69|
|Operations & Maintenance Manual and Plan|Maintain up-to-date O&M manuals and plans, providing detailed guidance for regular operation and maintenance activities at all facilities.|Within 60 days from the commencement date|Page 2-46|
|Operations & Maintenance Records|Maintain accurate, comprehensive O&M records, logs, and documentation accessible to [CLIENT]’s officials for periodic review.|To [CLIENT] and/or successor contractor within 10 days of [CLIENT]'s request|Page 2-47|
|Protocols, Policies, and Procedures|Document protocols, policies, and procedures covering all operational, safety, compliance, and administrative activities.|Draft within 60 days after Notice of the Award; implemented within 60 days of plan approval; updated by January 31 each year|Page 2-103|
|Public Relations, Education and Outreach Plan|Develop and implement a public relations and outreach strategy for community engagement, education programs, and stakeholder communication.|Final within 60 days from the contract date, then updated annually|Page 2-93|
|Safety Plan|Prepare and adhere to a robust facility-specific safety plan meeting regulatory requirements, including employee training and incident response procedures.|Final within 30 days from the contract date, then updated annually|Page 2-79|
|Sampling and Testing, QA Plan|Develop and implement a detailed sampling, testing, and quality assurance plan, addressing regulatory compliance, operational monitoring, and analytical accuracy.|Within 60 days from the contract date, then updated annually|Page 2-61|
|Security Plan|Implement a comprehensive security strategy, covering facility access, surveillance, and incident reporting protocols.|Final within 30 days from the contract date, then updated annually|Page 2-82|
|Staffing Plan|Submit an operational staffing plan, detailing personnel positions, responsibilities, qualifications, and staffing levels necessary for effective operation.|Within 10 days after the award of the contract|Page 2-35|
|Structural Evaluations|Provide regular inspection, assessment, and reporting on structural conditions of the facility infrastructure to identify and address potential issues.|Within 180 days after commencement date, and 90 days prior to end of contract term|Page 2-71|
|Subcontractor/Subconsultant List|Submit and update as needed a complete list of subcontractors and subconsultants engaged in delivering contracted services.|Each month and when changed|Page 2-38, Appendix|
|Training Program and Plan|Establish a structured training program, including ongoing personnel training and certification requirements to ensure operational efficiency, safety, and compliance.|Within 180 days from the commencement date|Page 2-42|
