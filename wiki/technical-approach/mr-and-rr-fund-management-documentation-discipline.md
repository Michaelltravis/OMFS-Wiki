# Tiered M&R Fund and R&R Fund Management with Documentation Discipline

We'll manage the tiered M&R Fund structure with the precision of documentation that [CLIENT]'s contract demands. [CLIENT] is a Southcentral US regional water utility trust operating four wastewater treatment plants and a major pump station.

- **Level A (≤$10,000):** Parts and supplies reimbursed without prior Service Approval Form (SAF); labor at Jacobs' expense, including subcontractors and overtime. Documentation submitted weekly and included in the Monthly Payment Request.
- **Level B ($10,000–$50,000):** Requires an executed SAF prior to ordering materials. [CLIENT] pays labor costs including subcontractors.
- **Level C (>$50,000):** [CLIENT] competitively procures; Jacobs cooperates fully with procurement and installation coordination.

Each year, we will develop and submit the annual M&R budget, aligning with the condition assessment cycle and capital planning process. We'll manage expenditures within 5% of the approved annual budget to avoid unrecoverable costs, a discipline that requires real-time fund tracking in NexGen with automated alerts as spending approaches thresholds. We understand that the R&R Fund addresses single asset rehabilitations or replacements exceeding $10,000 that substantially extend service life by 10 or more years. An executed SAF is required before procurement.

**We build documentation discipline into every maintenance activity — not because the contract requires it, but because it's how we protect the assets and the program's financial integrity.** Every PM completion, every inspection result, every corrective action is recorded in NexGen with timestamps, photographic evidence, and clear accountability. When a piece of equipment reaches end of life, the maintenance record speaks for itself by demonstrating the care that went into extending its service and supporting a straightforward path to M&R Fund reimbursement. That same data trail feeds our condition assessments, informs capital planning recommendations, and gives [CLIENT] full transparency into how maintenance dollars are being spent. This level of documentation ensures every maintenance dollar is traceable, justified, and aligned to asset condition, risk, and lifecycle decision-making.
