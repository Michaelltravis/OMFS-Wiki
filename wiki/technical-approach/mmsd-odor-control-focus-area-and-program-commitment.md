# Odor Control Focus Area — Understanding the Odor Challenge and Program Commitment

Like many wastewater utilities, [CLIENT]'s facilities — a regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities and their conveyance system — have become more susceptible to odor complaints, largely due to nearby residential development such as in redeveloped downtown districts or in the neighborhoods around the second water reclamation facility. In addition, the proximity of large crowd events such as regional festivals can result in complaints. Odors from collection system sources can be challenging to control because of the numerous emission points such as major pump stations and diversion structures, many of which are located close to residents. While [CLIENT] has made good progress mitigating odors, remaining odorous emissions and complaints stem mainly from uncovered/untreated processes such as primary clarifiers. Intermittent odor mitigation methods such as ferric chloride addition are used when complaints occur.

Jacobs will deliver a proactive, data-driven odor management program that focuses on preventing odors before they occur, rapidly identifying and responding to odor sources, and maintaining community trust through transparency and timely communication. Using a proposed odor early warning system that integrates sensors, process modeling, and real-time air dispersion analysis, we'll anticipate odor-forming conditions and guide operators in taking preventive actions.

When complaints do arise, trained staff will follow a structured, evidence-based investigation protocol — from initial intake and field verification through root-cause analysis and resident follow-up — ensuring consistent documentation and continuous improvement. By combining predictive tools, rigorous maintenance, proven odor-control best practices, and community-responsive communication, Jacobs will help [CLIENT] reduce odor incidents and reinforce public confidence in facility operations.

Our approach aligns directly with [CLIENT]'s goals:

- Minimize and proactively manage odor emissions from all process units and conveyance structures
- Maintain public confidence through transparent, timely communication, and response

That alignment carries through two commitments that organize everything else in this program:

- Proactively minimize odors through rigorous maintenance and monitoring
- Mitigate odors rapidly and effectively if they do occur
