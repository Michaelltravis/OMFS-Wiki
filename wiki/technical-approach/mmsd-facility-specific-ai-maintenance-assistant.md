# Facility-Specific AI Maintenance Assistant (Above and Beyond)

**ABOVE AND BEYOND: [CLIENT]-Specific AI Assistant**

**AI-assisted knowledge search.** Jacobs' Intelligent O&M includes a secure, facility-specific assistant, shown in **Exhibit IV-49**, to retrieve procedures, manuals and drawings quickly. This chat-style interface is loaded with plant specific documents only and is NOT accessible to the internet, thereby eliminating responses that are not applicable to [CLIENT] facilities. Through our partnership with advanced AI companies, we leverage the latest AI large language models (Claude, Chat GPT 5, etc.) but in a completely isolated ecosystem that adheres to Jacobs' AI security data policy and is specific to each client site, without compromising confidentiality. We'll also review them with [CLIENT] to ensure they meet your data security requirements. We've successfully used these tools for two years at our facilities for:

- Maintenance management (looking up specific equipment information and loading CMMS with PM procedures)
- Construction management (RFI and construction document search)
- Contract management (typically restricted to managers only who use them for timely access to contract requirements)

The tools provide rapid and succinct answers to questions and provide links to the documents that it sourced its answer from. The links/source of information is a critical feature we include to build trust and verify results. Like all our Intelligent O&M tools, it's intended to support and aid our O&M staff. In this case, it helps them be more efficient and spend less time searching for answers that are tucked away in thousands upon thousands of pages of documents scattered in libraries, offices, SharePoint folders and hard drives.

This is a signature feature for Jacobs—valued at over $50K/year including licensing and setup. We've refined this tool so well that it is now included in our maintenance package, as a built-in value add for this contract.

**EXHIBIT IV-49. JACOBS' AI MAINTENANCE ASSISTANT.** Jacobs' AI Maintenance Assistant, a chat-style tool that retrieves [CLIENT] facility documents quickly and cites the exact source pages—improving maintenance efficiency while supporting verification and user confidence (desktop + mobile versions shown).

**ABOVE AND BEYOND: [CLIENT]-SPECIFIC AI ASSISTANT FOR MAINTENANCE.** Jacobs' AI Assistant gives maintenance staff a search engine style interface to ask plain English questions and get trusted answers in seconds based on only facility-specific documentation. Provided in both desktop and mobile formats, this cutting-edge tool unlocks years of institutional knowledge in moments, enhances decision-making, and significantly improves speed to information that empowers greater staff efficiency.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the whole offer. The security framing (plant-specific documents only, no internet access, isolated ecosystem under Jacobs' AI security data policy), the three proven use cases (maintenance, construction, contract management), the source-citation trust mechanism, the two-year operating history, and the "valued at over $50K/year, included in our maintenance package" close. Use it as an Above and Beyond callout anywhere a client is drowning in legacy documentation.

Pursuit-specific: the model names, which date quickly; the exhibit numbering; and the commitment to review the tool against the client's data security requirements, which should name the client's actual IT review process where known.
