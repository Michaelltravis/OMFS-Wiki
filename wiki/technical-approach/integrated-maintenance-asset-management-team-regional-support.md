# Integrated Maintenance and Asset Management Team with Regional Maintenance Support

**Maintenance Manager Brian Daniels will lead day-to-day maintenance operations across all facilities**, overseeing PM/PdM/CM execution, staffing coordination, and KPI performance. **Asset Manager Amy Dembinski, CRL, will lead asset management strategy, lifecycle planning, CMMS governance, and the condition assessment program.** These are distinct, dedicated roles, not combined positions, because our experience consistently shows that combining maintenance management with asset management dilutes both functions and leads to exactly the kind of deferred maintenance we've observed at these facilities.

In addition, our O&M Asset Management Team will support the development, implementation, and ongoing improvement of the comprehensive AMS, including condition assessment protocols, lifecycle modeling, KPI benchmarking, and CMMS optimization.

For detailed staffing levels, shift structures, qualifications, and training plans, refer to our Staffing and Training Plan in **Section 5 – Required Plans Submitted with the Proposal**.

## Regional Maintenance Support: The Jacobs Advantage

Beyond the on-site teams, [CLIENT]'s maintenance program will be backed by Jacobs' Regional Maintenance Team as illustrated in **Exhibit 1-32**. This dedicated group of specialists support projects across the southern region, including Baton Rouge, Jackson, and other Jacobs O&M operations. This team handles heavy equipment overhauls, major pump and blower rebuilds, crane-served work, and other tasks that would typically require hiring outside contractors with the associated delays, unfamiliarity, and communication gaps.

What makes this a genuine differentiator is how the team is structured: regional maintenance specialists are separate and standalone from our project teams. They don't draw resources away from other projects when [CLIENT] needs support, and other projects don't draw them away from [CLIENT]. They're Jacobs personnel who will know your facilities, your CMMS, and your safety protocols — and they're available without the procurement delays and onboarding friction that come with third-party contractors. Unlike typical operators, we combine on-site delivery with regional and national support structures, bringing specialized expertise, benchmarking, and proven practices from more than 100 Jacobs-operated facilities.
