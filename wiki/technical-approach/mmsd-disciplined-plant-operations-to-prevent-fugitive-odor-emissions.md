# Disciplined Plant Operations and Equipment Performance to Prevent Fugitive Odor Emissions

**Disciplined Plant Operations and Equipment Performance.** Jacobs will operate treatment processes with practices designed to minimize sulfide generation and prevent fugitive emissions, including maintaining appropriate sludge blanket depths, optimizing sludge storage durations, and closely monitoring primary and solids-handling conditions during warm weather. We understand that there may be a need under some circumstances to operate with high blankets, but operators will be trained to more closely monitor for odors when blankets are high.

Operations of all covered processes will follow SOPs that ensure negative pressure is maintained, covers are secure, gasketed, and intact, and fans and ventilation systems are functioning as designed. We'll provide ISO-aligned PM and testing for odor control units — carbon, chemical scrubbers, blowers — to maintain performance and extend media life. If ventilation systems are not operating properly, leaks can occur, which can be a significant source of odors.
