# Exhibit 1-1 Matrix — Staffing, Performance Management, and Transition Execution

### Staffing Stability and Workforce Development

| Understanding of [CLIENT]'s Goals | Key Current/Future Challenges | Jacobs' Strategies to Deliver Results |
|---|---|---|
| **Staff all facilities per Schedule 10, including minimum FTE requirements, ODEQ certification standards, and named management positions hired before Service Agreement execution** | **Current staffing at ~88 FTEs** (increased from 85 after scope change for 24-hour coverage at DC and CC) plus an additional ~13 biosolids staff from a subcontractor.<br>**Staff retention is a persistent challenge;** competition from local employers for hourly workers; vacancy penalties escalate monthly ($5,000/position, $10,000/management, +10% each month).<br>**Schedule 10 requires dedicated full-time positions for Asset Manager and CIP Construction Manager** (historically combined into one role), signaling [CLIENT]'s intent to strengthen both functions.<br>**I&C/IT staff turnover is particularly high** under the current operator.<br>**All management positions must have accepted offers before transition**; all staff filled within 60 days of Commencement Date. | **Named key management staff committed before Notice to Proceed.**<br>**Comprehensive staffing level of 109 FTEs** (96 Jacobs plus 13 biosolids staff from subcontractor), with a leaner management structure that puts more operators on the floor. Operators will cover 24/7 operations at SC and take on Drain and Clean work, freeing mechanics for consistent PM execution — the foundation of our asset preservation approach.<br>**Competitive compensation approach** with structured pay progression to support retention.<br>**Technical training and DEQ certification support** for operators, maintenance technicians, and laboratory staff, building long-term capability and reinforcing retention.<br>**Cross-trained operators** capable of performing lower-level PMs, freeing maintenance technicians for higher-skill corrective and predictive work. |

### Performance Management

| Understanding of [CLIENT]'s Goals | Key Current/Future Challenges | Jacobs' Strategies to Deliver Results |
|---|---|---|
| **Meet all Performance Requirements in Schedule 5 with the full Fixed Monthly Fee — over 30% of the fee-at-risk across management, compliance, maintenance, and staffing metrics** | **Limited transparency and accountability** in current operations.<br>**Need to track and monitor nearly 20 individual fee-at-risk categories to drive performance requirements**, including NPDES compliance (3.0%), odor (2.0%), wet weather (1.25%), predictive maintenance (1.5%), preventive maintenance (1.5%), corrective maintenance (3.5%), staffing, and others. | **Disciplined QA/QC program with independent management oversight** (Schedule 3 requirement).<br>**Real-time tracking** of compliance, maintenance key performance indicators (KPIs), staffing levels, and reporting deadlines aligned with Schedule 1.<br>**Structured corrective action and escalation protocols** to address issues before they become deductions.<br>**Performance culture** built on visible KPIs, consistent training, and clear accountability at every level of the organization. |

### Transition Execution

| Understanding of [CLIENT]'s Goals | Key Current/Future Challenges | Jacobs' Strategies to Deliver Results |
|---|---|---|
| **Deliver a structured, milestone-based transition from Notice to Proceed through the January 1, 2027, Operational Start Date, meeting all Schedule 19 deliverables on time** | **Highly prescriptive transition** with four milestone payments totaling $200,000 tied to specific deliverables on compressed timelines (September–December 2026).<br>**Complete suite of required plans due on schedule** (QA/QC, Safety, Odor, Wet Weather, Process Control, SOPs, Solids, and more).<br>**Transfer of service from current operator requires a minimum 7 days of operations monitoring** before January 1, 2027.<br>**Award expected August 18; management staff must be hired within roughly 2 weeks** for a September 1 transition start. | **Draft Transition Plan submitted with proposal**; finalized by September 1, 2026.<br>**Named management staff in place and committed before award**, with a transparent approach for any positions to be filled during transition.<br>**Chemical and polymer testing completed, and vendors secured** before the Operational Start Date.<br>**Baseline Condition Assessment and Initial Inventory jointly certified** with [CLIENT].<br>**Detailed transition schedule** with named responsible leads for each milestone, initially developed and to be finalized in collaboration with [CLIENT].<br>**Structured staff onboarding and training program** during transition, with knowledge transfer led by incoming leadership and Jacobs' subject matter experts. |
