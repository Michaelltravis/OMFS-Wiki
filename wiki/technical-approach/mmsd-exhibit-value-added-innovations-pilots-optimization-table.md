## Reuse guidance

Universal: closing a value-add exhibit with a totals row and then a single combined headline number. The headline is what gets lifted into an executive summary or a cover letter, so keep the two component totals visible directly above it so the number is auditable.

Pursuit-specific: every row and every figure. The optimization rows depend on a gainshare mechanism existing in the commercial structure — check with the pricing lead before reusing the 40/60 language.
