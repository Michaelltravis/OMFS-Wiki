# Risk Management — Condition Assessments, Risk Scoring, and Risk Profiles That Drive Capital Investment

**7. Risk Management.** Following project startup, Jacobs will conduct regular condition assessments and apply a structured risk scoring methodology to prioritize high-risk assets and inform capital investments. Risk profiles will be maintained and used to guide both day-to-day decision-making and long-term financial planning. Our risk-based strategy protects infrastructure value, extends asset life, and supports efficient resource allocation.

**Jacobs' industry award-winning and highly efficient condition assessment process supports asset management excellence.** Condition assessments play a critical role in our industry-leading asset management and associated maintenance approach. Our field-trained maintenance specialists have completed condition assessments for 1 million water and wastewater plant assets. A 5-minute video shows our approach to condition assessment (accessed by clicking the QR code or scanning it with a mobile device).
