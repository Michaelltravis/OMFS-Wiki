# Proven Success Transitioning Complex Projects — Client Testimonial Callouts

Utility leadership has repeatedly recognized the effectiveness of Jacobs' disciplined transition approach and its positive impact on employee engagement and workforce continuity. Recent examples include West Basin Municipal Water District and Jackson Water (JXN Water), where Jacobs assumed operational responsibility from prior operators while maintaining uninterrupted service.

> "Given the complexity of West Basin's operations, the transition period required extensive coordination and technical expertise. Jacobs deployed a deep bench of subject matter experts and worked collaboratively with District staff to ensure a smooth transition from the previous contract operator of more than 20 years. Overall, the transition was managed very effectively, with minimal challenges, and resulted in a smooth and successful onboarding for a system of this complexity."
>
> — Susanna Li, Manager of Engineering, West Basin Municipal Water District, 310.660.6238

> "In the throes of the water crisis, only Jacobs was willing to answer our calls for assistance. Since that time in December 2022, they have proven to be outstanding partners, delivering on their commitments and totally bought in to our shared mission of ensuring all Jacksonians have access to safe drinking water, all the time. Once again, Jacobs has stepped up and promised operational savings as they begin to operate our wastewater facilities. We look forward to expanding their mission, applying that same commitment to the people of Jackson with a focus on protecting public health and the environment with their wastewater expertise."
>
> — Ted Henifin, JXN Water Interim Third Party Manager, 757.274.7904

> "Jacobs implemented a smooth and successful transition from our previous operator. Jacobs assembled a transition team of HR, operational, and technical professionals to implement their plan safely and efficiently. The plan included constant communication with the owner; a safe, private, and remote process for employees onboarding to Jacobs; and a technical team that applied their engineering and operational expertise to ensure that operations continued without interruption. Following a successful transition, we look forward to a continued partnership with Jacobs for many years to come."
>
> — Vincent R. Carroccia, Deputy Commissioner, Department of Public Works, City of Wilmington, DE, 302.576.3081

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the pattern of placing transition testimonials directly beside the transition narrative rather than parking them in past performance. Each of these three quotes answers a different evaluator worry — can they displace a long-tenured incumbent, will they show up when it is hard, and what actually happens to my employees — so choose the one that matches the pursuit's dominant risk rather than running all three.

Pursuit-specific: nothing in the quotes themselves; they are verbatim and stay verbatim. What changes is selection and placement, plus a lead-in sentence naming the parallel to the new client's situation.
