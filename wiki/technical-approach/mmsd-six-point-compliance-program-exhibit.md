# Six-Point Compliance Program (Exhibit IV-37)

Exhibit asset `206_007CAM_5`.

> **99.98% compliance is the result of a disciplined system, not a statistic.**

Jacobs' Six-Point Compliance Program integrates ethics, standardized processes, laboratory excellence, data integrity, auditing, and transparent reporting to deliver defensible permit compliance and support [CLIENT]'s zero-excursion goal. [CLIENT] is a regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities, a deep-tunnel conveyance and storage system, and a heat-dried biosolids product line.

| Point | What it means | What it includes |
|---|---|---|
| **SOPs and process control expertise** | Consistency and precision at every operations stage. | State environmental agency and EPA-compliant sampling plus proven QA/QC protocols; cross-functional teams for lab, reuse, stormwater, and biosolids; instrument calibration and sensor reliability for process control and regulatory monitoring. |
| **Transparency and reporting** | Insight into your system, visibility you can count on. | Immediate e-Reporting submission and permit violation alerts; real-time data feeds; [CLIENT]'s web-based dashboard. |
| **Compliance training and tools** | Empowering our people to protect the environment. | 30,000 hours of training provided in the last 3 years alone; standardized tools, tracking systems, and evaluation matrices. |
| **Audits and project monitoring** | Constant improvement through independent validation. | Internal baseline and ongoing audits drive improvement; audit results included in monthly District briefings. |
| **Culture of ethics** | We do the right thing — even when no one is watching. | Transparency in all regulatory communications; reporting culture guided by honesty and defensibility. |
| **Oversight and support by global experts** | Compliance strength driven by ongoing oversight and support. | 20+ dedicated O&M Compliance & Reporting SMEs; support for permit interpretation, training, rapid response, and future preparedness. |

**99.98% compliance** — maintained through rigorous process control, defensible data, and daily vigilance from certified experts.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the six points, the ethics point included. Naming ethics as a compliance control rather than a values statement is unusual and reads as genuine to evaluators who have been burned by self-reported data. The "disciplined system, not a statistic" line should appear in identical words in the executive summary and the compliance section so the theme recurs.

Pursuit-specific: the regulator name, the dashboard reference, and the audit-briefing cadence (here monthly briefings to the District — match the client's own governance rhythm). Where the client has had recent violations under the incumbent, the audits point is the one to expand.
