# Program Management Framework as the Foundation of Shared Success

**Delivering the scope of services for [CLIENT]—a large regional sewerage district whose scope spans O&M of multiple large facilities, large-scale capital improvement coordination and support, system optimization, regulatory compliance, and long-term environmental stewardship—demands a strategy that goes beyond the current approach.** [CLIENT]'s portfolio is among the most complex and interconnected in the country. Decisions in one area influence operations, customer service, maintenance, and community outcomes across the entire service territory.

**To meet this level of complexity, innovative and integrated Program Management (PgM) is essential.** Our methodology is built for environments like [CLIENT]'s, where success depends on aligning diverse stakeholders, managing interdependent workstreams, and maintaining transparency across broad and evolving priorities. By applying a program-wide lens, we create the structure, governance, and collaborative culture needed to deliver consistent, predictable, and high-value outcomes that support our shared mission of protecting public health and the environment.

Our PgM approach is built on collaboration, transparency, and accountability and forms the foundation for true stakeholder alignment, as illustrated in **Exhibit IV-6**. This framework integrates proven principles, platforms, procedures, processes, and tools—each refined through a comprehensive readiness assessment during the pre-term contract phase, including team chartering with [CLIENT] and Jacobs' leadership. The chartering process establishes a clear project vision, goals, and objectives, which shape the governance model—the central structure that drives a collaborative program organization and a transparent culture.

By viewing contract execution through a PgM lens, we strengthen collaboration and eliminate common disconnects across capital development, customer service, operations, and maintenance. Together, this approach ensures alignment, reinforces a culture of openness, and delivers successful outcomes for all stakeholders.

**How we'll successfully stand up our PgM framework.** We'll follow the seven-step PgM initiation process summarized in **Exhibit IV-8** to develop a strategy, budget baselines, forecasts, implementation plans, a digital platform, and training for the entire [CLIENT] organization.

**Keoki Sears** will lead our Program Management Framework for [CLIENT]. Keoki is Jacobs' global director of program management services and brings 35 years of experience leading programs around the world.

**Establishing leadership alignment (Exhibit IV-7).** Team dynamics are critical to performance. Insights Discovery provides a framework to build trust, strengthen collaboration, and create a shared language for alignment. We launched our PgM Framework by assessing the leadership team and plan to extend the assessment to [CLIENT] staff. Our goal is to build a positive, aligned culture that enables [CLIENT] and Jacobs to operate as a unified partnership.
