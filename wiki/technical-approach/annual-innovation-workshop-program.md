# Annual Innovation Workshop Program

Our **Annual Innovation Workshops**, provided at no cost to [CLIENT] — a coastal New England municipality operating a WWTF and collection system — will bring together [CLIENT] staff, stakeholders, and our O&M staff, as well as industry recognized leaders in areas such as process engineering, biosolids, hydraulics, wet weather flow management, pump station design and operations, digital optimization, and energy management. We will review the prior year's accomplishments and establish new goals and innovations to address [CLIENT]'s evolving needs and long-term vision. We'll work closely with [CLIENT] to co-develop a full day agenda that reflects your priorities and expectations, focusing the session on your desired outcomes. This important annual meeting ensures we continuously look forward to future needs and trends; help [CLIENT] address emerging challenges, technology, and O&M practices; and eliminate complacency over time. Examples of potential topics for [CLIENT]'s first Annual Innovation Workshop include the following, with **Appendix D** providing an example agenda for [CLIENT], as well as agendas from prior innovation workshops conducted for Traverse City, MI and Wilmington, NC:

- **SSES and I/I Assessment** using CCTV findings, field observations, flow patterns, maintenance history, and SmartCover® real-time monitoring data to distinguish groundwater inflow, direct inflow, and saltwater intrusion influences and prioritize corrective actions
- **Standby Power/Energy and Resiliency Roadmap** to evaluate generator upgrade strategies, connection improvements, fuel strategy, energy-management, and solar potential
- **Biosolids Strategy Review** of thickened versus dewatered solids and disposal pathways based on a Year 1 evaluation to define a resilient, phased strategy that reduces hauling, odor, emissions, and long-term market risk
- **Regionalization Opportunities** to explore partnerships to improve system efficiency, capacity, and long-term sustainability
- **Incinerator Reuse Evaluation** to assess feasible removal, repurposing, and resiliency uses that reclaim this underused space and accommodate future solids, storage, and backup power needs
- **AI in Wastewater** to identify near-term applications and future opportunities to enhance operations and decision-making
- **PFAS Strategy and Regulatory Outlook** to evaluate emerging requirements and treatment approaches to support compliance planning

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the concept of a no-cost, co-developed, full-day annual workshop that feeds the CIP and keeps the contract forward-looking rather than purely reactive, plus the closing rationale ("eliminate complacency over time"). Tailor per pursuit: select four to six topics genuinely relevant to the target facility's known issues, pulled from the RFP, site visit, or planning documents. Naming the prior workshops delivered for other contract-operations clients is a strong proof point when the agendas can actually be attached.
