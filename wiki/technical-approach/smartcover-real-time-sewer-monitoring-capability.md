# SmartCover Real-Time Satellite Sewer Monitoring — Capability Overview

*Third-party capability literature reproduced as a proposal appendix. SmartCover® is a Badger Meter® brand: "Real-Time Satellite Infrastructure Monitoring Solutions," 2110 Enterprise Street, Escondido, CA 92029 USA; (855) 291-1980; sales@smartcoversystems.com; smartcoversystems.com.*

## Full suite of solutions — offers greater visibility into your operations

SmartCover has prevented more than 40,000 sewer system overflows with real-time alarms and notifications to dispatch crews for spill avoidance. We optimize sewer cleaning by redirecting crews to clean "as needed" without sacrificing care and lower inspection costs by localizing "when and where" activities are needed.

Solution set: SSO prevention · H2S monitoring · I&I assessment · cleaning optimization · CSO monitoring · stormwater monitoring · lift station monitoring · intrusion detection.

## Trusted utility partner — protecting public health for nearly 20 years

**Protect the environment**

- Utilize real-time alarms to dispatch crews before overflows occur
- Eliminate environmental and community impacts associated with sewer spills
- Identify locations with capacity constraints to prevent future overflows

**Optimize investments**

- Schedule cleanings based on data and analytics
- Reduce chemical odor and corrosion control spend while improving performance
- Achieve 75% to 95% cleaning reduction at high-frequency locations

**Strengthen utility resiliency**

- Monitor critical infrastructure during extreme weather events using satellite communication
- Isolate inflow and infiltration to focus capital investments in high-impact areas
- Reduce carbon footprint by optimizing cleaning truck operations, decreasing chemical use, and eliminating sewer overflows

## Integrated solution — delivers all components to ensure success

The integrated system chains a manhole- or wet-well-mounted monitoring device to secure and reliable satellite communication, predictive analytics, capital investment planning, automated actionable information, and preventative asset management, with field crews dispatched on the resulting data.

## Efficient management of real-time data — actionable insights through industry-leading analytics

- Data delivered from the sewer to your web-enabled devices
- Intuitive landing dashboard summarizing location and status of manholes monitored by SmartCover sensors
- Easy access to critical events including alarms, alerts, and advisories
- Visibility to every single SmartCover unit through clicks on the map
