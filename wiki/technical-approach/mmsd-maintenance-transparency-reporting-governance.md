# Maintenance Transparency, Live Dashboards, and Governance Committees

We'll proactively establish the required **Maintenance Committee with two subcommittees—CMMS Governance and SCADA Governance—to co-manage standards, integrations, configuration, and change control.** These bodies are the forum for PM list changes, PMO results, and cross-functional decisions connecting maintenance to capital planning.

[CLIENT] will have **live dashboard access to PM compliance, CM backlog, work-order aging, labor/cost, asset availability, and risk**. We'll clearly delineate [CLIENT]-funded vs. Jacobs-funded work under the Agreement's threshold model, and we'll **review Standards of Performance** (e.g., on-time PM completion, closeout lag, MTBF/MTTR, availability) **at set cadences with documented actions and owners**.

**Specific to fleet maintenance**, Jacobs will maintain open, frequent communication with [CLIENT] regarding vehicle repair status and availability, any outsourced repair recommendations, warranty claims and manufacturer coordination, fleet disposal preparation and recommendations, and any operational risks affecting safety, mobility, or mission-critical service. Our approach ensures [CLIENT]'s leadership, governance committees, and operational teams will always have visibility into fleet condition, risks, and costs just as they will with the facilities.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: live dashboard access to the named metric set; the commitment to delineate client-funded from operator-funded work under a threshold model; and Standards of Performance reviewed at set cadences with documented actions and owners. This is the transparency proof that answers "how will we know what you are doing" in any O&M evaluation.

Pursuit-specific: the Maintenance Committee and its CMMS/SCADA governance subcommittees are structures this RFP required; check whether the target agreement has equivalents or whether Jacobs is proposing them. The fleet-specific paragraph only belongs where fleet is in scope.

Pairs with [Fleet Maintenance Program for Plant, Sewer, and Field Vehicles](mmsd-fleet-maintenance-program.md) and [PM Optimization, Operator-Driven Requests, and Work Planning](mmsd-pm-optimization-and-work-planning.md), whose PM list changes and PMO results are routed through these committees.
