# Innovation and Value-Added Offerings Overview (Included-in-Base-Fee Program Wheel)

Jacobs is committed to bringing the client not only reliable operations but also continuous innovation and advanced techniques that improve efficiency, reduce lifecycle costs, and support the client's broader sustainability goals. Building on Jacobs' national Intelligent O&M platform, Jacobs will deliver a suite of value-added services included in the base fee, tailored to the client's needs — including process optimization tools, energy and chemical efficiency strategies, advanced asset management practices, and modern compliance systems. These services are delivered through structured programs such as an Annual Innovation Workshop and an Annual Training and Incentives Program, supported by local and regional experts who provide surge coverage and subject matter expertise. Jacobs also brings specialized tools such as the AquaDNA DeRagger, proactive inventory management systems, and a cybersecurity survey aligned to the client's standards, along with discounted access to targeted engineering studies. (In the source proposal, several of these offerings are cross-referenced to fuller treatment elsewhere in the technical approach: the Annual Innovation Workshop and Annual Training and Incentives Program to the "Communications and Reporting" and "Training & Certification Program for O&M Personnel" subsections, proactive inventory management to the "Asset Management and Maintenance" subsection, and the Cybersecurity Survey to the "SCADA, I&C, and Cybersecurity" subsection — useful pointers when assembling a full section draft that pulls from multiple wiki blocks.)

## Innovation partnerships and pilots

Jacobs collaborates with the California WateReuse Association, LIFT (Leaders Innovation Forum for Technology), and regional utilities to advance novel solutions. Jacobs' teams have piloted treatment trains for potable reuse, energy recovery technologies, and low-energy RO configurations. For this pursuit, Jacobs will offer pilot-ready support and provide recommendations for emerging solutions that align with the client's goals and funding strategies.

**Benefits to the client:** Jacobs' innovation and advanced techniques package delivers measurable value well beyond day-to-day operations. By integrating tools such as the AquaDNA DeRagger, ISO 55001-aligned asset management systems, and SCADA-based compliance and reporting platforms, Jacobs helps reduce downtime, extend asset life, and streamline regulatory reporting. Annual Innovation Workshops and structured operator training programs strengthen staff capability and retention while ensuring the client remains at the forefront of industry practices. Regional surge support and discounted engineering rates give the client access to expertise and resources typically available only on large-scale programs. Finally, by embedding hybrid vehicles, energy optimization strategies, and chemical efficiency tools, Jacobs reduces greenhouse gas emissions, lowers operational costs, and advances the client's sustainability and climate goals. Together, these innovations provide the client with a resilient, future-ready water reuse system that maximizes return on investment while safeguarding public trust and environmental stewardship.
