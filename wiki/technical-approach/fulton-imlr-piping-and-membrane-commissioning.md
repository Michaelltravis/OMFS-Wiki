# IMLR Piping Evaluation and Membrane Commissioning Support

IMLR should be directed to the third compartment, making the first two compartments a fermentation zone and the third compartment an anoxic zone. This would minimize nitrate delivered to the fermentation zone.

JC Solutions will evaluate the cost and benefits of changing the IMLR piping to the anoxic zone. **How It Benefits [CLIENT]:** Decreases overall aeration and sodium hydroxide costs and improves EBPR performance.

Chemical cleaning is necessary to maintain membrane performance. The membranes at [FACILITY A] are new; those at [FACILITY C] are six years old; and those at [FACILITY B] are 16 years old. The [FACILITY B] membranes are well past expected life, and an intense program of chemical cleaning is being used to maintain productivity. We understand that [CLIENT] is evaluating membrane replacement, after which chemical quantities can be determined.

When the new membranes arrive, we will assist with their installation and commissioning. We are accustomed to doing so on Jacobs’ design-build-operate (DBO) projects and at our other MBR projects with older systems such as Traverse City, MI. **How It Benefits [CLIENT]:** Supports long-term membrane health and maintenance cost reductions.
