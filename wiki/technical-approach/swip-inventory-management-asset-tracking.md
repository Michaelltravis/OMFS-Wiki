# Proactive Inventory Management Program (CMMS-Integrated, ISO 55001-Aligned)

Jacobs will implement a comprehensive, CMMS-integrated inventory management system to ensure uninterrupted maintenance and operations at the facilities by having essential spares and equipment on hand as needed. During the transition phase, Jacobs will define key inventory roles, set up organized stockroom infrastructure, and input all parts data — including bin locations — into the CMMS for full traceability. Leveraging mobile technology and barcoded parts tracking, Jacobs will streamline inventory workflows and establish SOPs for ordering, stocking, and usage.

This proactive system supports accurate parts forecasting, timely reorders, and prevents obsolescence. Dashboards and reports provide real-time visibility into inventory status, usage trends, and procurement needs, empowering the team to make informed decisions while minimizing downtime and lifecycle costs. Jacobs' approach transforms inventory control from a reactive function into a strategic asset management tool aligned with ISO 55001 standards and the client's operational goals.
