# Approach to Management of Facilities: Client-for-Life Mindset and Five Tenets of Delivery

Sustaining strong, long-term partnerships is at the heart of our client-for-life mindset. Effective management requires a dynamic approach—one that evolves alongside changing needs and goals. We leverage the full breadth of our integrated resources to deliver the high-quality, cost-effective performance that clients like [CLIENT], a large regional sewerage district, expect. We are deeply committed to our clients, our staff, and the communities we serve. By building trust and staying attuned to the evolving priorities of each stakeholder, we maintain alignment that drives shared success.

**Key focus areas in delivering excellence.** Our approach to delivering exceptional results is anchored in five tenets that define how we operate, collaborate, and grow. The tenets, shown in **Exhibit IV-5**, create a holistic framework that powers excellence, lasting partnerships, and meaningful impacts.

**Exhibit IV-5. Five interconnected tenets that define how we deliver O&M and build a culture of high performance.** Graphic asset ID `354_007CAM_2`.

| Tenet | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Ethics & Compliance** | We uphold the highest standards of ethical conduct and regulatory compliance. This commitment safeguards our reputation and reinforces the trust placed in us by clients, partners, and communities. |
| **Communication & Transparency** | Open and honest communication is essential to building trust. We prioritize transparency in every interaction, fostering clarity, alignment, and strong collaboration across teams and with our clients. |
| **Empowerment & Accountability** | We cultivate a culture of ownership and initiative. By empowering individuals and holding ourselves accountable, we ensure responsibilities are met with integrity and that performance standards are consistently exceeded. |
| **Training & Advancement** | We invest in the growth and development of our people. Through ongoing training and career advancement opportunities, we empower our teams to expand their capabilities and stay ahead in a rapidly changing environment. |
| **Innovation & Best Practices** | We embrace innovation and continuously refine our methods by applying proven best practices. This ensures our solutions remain forward-thinking, efficient, and impactful—meeting the evolving needs of our clients and stakeholders. |
