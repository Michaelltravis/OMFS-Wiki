# Collaborative CMMS Implementation Process

## 3.1. Implementation Phase (April 2026 – February 2028)

Through our long working relationship with NexGen, we understand NexGen's implementation procedures. We'll work efficiently and effectively to achieve successful implementation for [CLIENT], a regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities and a regional conveyance system. The first step in any successful CMMS implementation is to develop a Steering Committee comprised of key Jacobs and [CLIENT] stakeholders, including operations and maintenance, engineering, finance, and asset management. The Steering Committee will establish a unified way of decision making and establish governance rules and procedures. Jacobs will perform the following activities to support the implementation:

- Jacobs will participate in the Implementation Governance Committee to provide governance and joint oversight of the implementation process. The Governance Committee will oversee NexGen's implementation and installation of the new CMMS to meet the requirements outlined in the CMMS Implementation Plan. The Governance Committee will focus on confirming that NexGen is installing and implementing the system in compliance with all integration and functional specifications as defined in the CMMS Implementation Plan.
- Our CMMS Implementation Project Manager will attend weekly NexGen project management meetings to coordinate any needs from Jacobs to support the implementation process by monitoring progress, tracking deliverables, and ensuring consistent engagement from Jacobs' broader operations team.
- Our asset management advisory staff will review and provide guidance on asset inventory, planning data, asset risk index, and life cycle planning configuration. We'll leverage our extensive expertise supporting asset management programs with the implementation or optimization of CMMS solutions in alignment with best practice standards such as the IAM and ISO 55000.
- Our CMMS support staff will partner with NexGen to integrate Jacobs' systems, identified in the integrations requirements below, and provide support for the Jacobs-side system integration requirements.
- Our operations and maintenance and system administration SMEs will define and provide Jacobs' end user requirements, business processes, and configuration specifications for NexGen and participate in development workshops. They'll support the system integration and user acceptance training process by verifying the system is fully configured, all functionality is tested, and data migration is complete. They'll review the training plan provided by NexGen, identify Jacobs operator training needs, and coordinate the training for all operators and administrators that will regularly use NexGen.

## 3.2. Operational Phase (March 2028 – 2038)

Jacobs will continue to support NexGen operations after Go-Live by:

- Delivering role-based training (refresher training and new staff training) to Jacobs operators, maintenance planners, supervisors, and field technicians and keeping training materials up to date.
- Attending routine committee governance meetings to review performance of NexGen, identify issues, and submit requests for changes to NexGen.
- Providing continuing support for post-implementation optimization and updates, changes, integrations, and enhancements to NexGen following authorization from the Governance Committee.
- Maintaining dedicated onsite system administrators to oversee the NexGen system; ensure timeliness, accuracy, and completeness of all data entered in the system, and participate in audits.
- Collaborating with [CLIENT] each budget cycle in mining NexGen for updates to the 6-year CIP.
