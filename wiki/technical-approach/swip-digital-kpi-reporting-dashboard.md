# Customized Web-Based KPI Reporting Dashboard (Digital Reporting Platform)

Jacobs will implement a secure, web-based dashboard tailored to the client's facilities and KPIs. This tool provides real-time and historical visibility into key performance indicators, maintenance activity, chemical usage, sampling and compliance results, safety metrics, and operational alerts. The dashboard complements formal written reports, equipping client staff and leadership with immediate access to facility performance data and reinforcing Jacobs' commitment to transparency, accountability, and operational excellence.

**Example dashboard navigation (Quick Links):**
- RTT (Regulatory/Reporting Tracking Tool)
- STT (Sample Tracking Tool)
- SCADA
- Water Quality
- Biosolids
- Reuse
- Maintenance
- Safety

The dashboard also pairs a map view of the service area with these Quick Links, giving stakeholders a geographic frame of reference alongside the operational data.

## Reuse guidance

Fully generic and reusable as a digital-reporting capability description for any O&M pursuit; the Quick Links category list (RTT, STT, SCADA, Water Quality, Biosolids, Reuse, Maintenance, Safety) can be trimmed or extended to match the target pursuit's actual scope (e.g., drop "Reuse" and "Biosolids" for a facility without a reuse or biosolids program).
