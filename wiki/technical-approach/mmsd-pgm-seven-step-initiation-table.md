# Exhibit: Seven Steps to Stand Up the PgM Framework

**Exhibit IV-8. Steps to set up the PgM framework for success — increasing capacity, step by step.** Graphic asset ID `309_007CAM_6`.

| Step / goal | Purpose | Results / deliverables |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Mobilize Program Management Team** | Invest in orienting and empowering the PgM team, drawing on the 16,000+ hours Jacobs staff have already applied to assessing your system and developing the proposal | Program Management Framework initiated by project team |
| **2. Establish Leadership Alignment** | Build team cohesion with INSIGHTS assessment. Charter to align on mission, vision, roles and responsibilities, conflict resolution mechanisms, and desired outcomes | Program Leadership Charter and Readiness Assessment |
| **3. Development of Holistic Strategies** | In collaboration with [CLIENT], agree on strategies for each element of management, operations, and maintenance with a focus on driving transparency and building trust | Function strategies / Program Implementation Plan (PIP), documented communication strategy |
| **4. Establish Governance for Decision-making Clarity** | Structure accountability and clearly define decision-making, lines of authority, and transparent reporting | Governance Handbook |
| **5. Deployment of Defined Ways of Working** | Establish detailed and integrated plans, processes, and tools to drive desired outcomes in compliance with the agreed overarching delivery strategy and function strategies | Program management platform and tools to be shared by the entire team |
| **6. Development of Documented Baseline** | Develop and ratify baseline scope, budget, and schedule aligned with [CLIENT] and used to measure our success | Baseline Report (scope, schedule, and budget) and Risk Register |
| **7. Charter, Align, and Train Entire Team** | Align and educate the entire team and stakeholders to foster a One Team mindset | Team Charter and training |

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the seven-step sequence and, above all, the deliverable column—naming the artifact each step produces is what makes a governance promise checkable. The step-1 purpose line is also the cleanest place in a proposal to reuse the due-diligence hour count as proof that mobilization starts from work already done.

Pursuit-specific: the hour count, the assessment tool name, and any step tied to a contractual pre-term period. Pairs with [Program Management Framework as the Foundation of Shared Success](mmsd-pgm-framework-foundation-of-shared-success.md). Read `verbatim/mmsd-om-2028/pages/p0050.md#¶3`.
