# Comprehensive O&M Approach and Transition Commitments

JC Solutions (a Jacobs/CERM JV) has developed a comprehensive O&M approach backed by deep expertise in MBR systems, asset management, and process optimization. Our partnership offers [CLIENT], a county wastewater-services organization, a focus on high levels of regulatory compliance—and SO much more. We provide a forward-looking vision, innovative solutions, and measurable value. With extensive resources from Jacobs’ extensive expert resources alongside partner CERM’s local knowledge and workforce development prowess, we are fully invested in protecting your infrastructure and serving your community for years to come.

Our full-spectrum O&M plan includes advanced MBR optimization, SCADA integration, and data-driven decision making for superior performance; an ISO 55001-aligned system with predictive maintenance, CMMS integration, and lifecycle optimization; and comprehensive monitoring, reporting, and tracking systems for perfect permit adherence. The plan also includes optimized dewatering, handling procedures, and potential beneficial reuse opportunities; QA/QC protocols, certified testing procedures, and data integrity verification systems; and proactive monitoring, chemical optimization, and responsive community engagement for odor and noise control. It is supported by a BeyondZero® culture, comprehensive training, and physical/cyber protection systems, as well as community partnerships, school programs, and transparent stakeholder communications.

Our communication, reporting, and transparency practices include daily, weekly, and monthly operational reports; a real-time performance dashboard for [CLIENT] awareness; transparent compliance tracking and notifications; and an Annual Innovation Workshop for continuous improvement. Key operations strategies apply specialized expertise in all membrane systems with strategies to extend membrane life and optimize performance; maximize efficiency with innovative process control, energy management, and chemical optimization to reduce costs; maintain the highest standards through rigorous QA/QC protocols, certified lab procedures, and multi-layered verification; and preserve and extend asset lifecycles with leading predictive maintenance technologies and strategic capital planning.

Our seamless 45-day transition is executed by a dedicated team of specialists who ensure zero service disruptions while implementing proven systems and controls. Our knowledge retention strategy values existing staff expertise while integrating Jacobs’ global best practices and innovative operational tools. A phased implementation approach uses clear milestones, parallel workstreams, and accountable leadership to ensure all critical elements are addressed methodically.

We commit to delivering the best value for [CLIENT] as highlighted in our planned investments discussed throughout our O&M approach: AquaDNA Smart Technology for and System Optimization; Odor Emissions Study; Class B Biosolids Study; Regional Support; Annual Trainings; Palantir-driven Maintenance Planner/Scheduler; Energy Management Strategy; Site Security Evaluation and Assessment; MBR/Process Optimization Evaluations and Tool Development; Cybersecurity Assessment; Annual Innovation Workshops; ISO 55001-aligned Asset Management System; Workforce Development Program; and Replica Model.

The following methodologies and strategies will drive measurable improvements across all facilities. Our approach combines proven, innovative processes, tools, and technologies to protect [CLIENT]’s infrastructure investments while delivering exceptional value.
