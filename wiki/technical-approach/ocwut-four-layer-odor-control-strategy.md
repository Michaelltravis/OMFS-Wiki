# Four-Layer Odor Control Strategy

**Four-layer odor control strategy:**

**1) Source control:** Evaluate dissolved sulfide levels arriving at each plant; chemical dosing at Witcher (ferrous chloride or peroxide in wet well) to reduce H₂S before it reaches North Canadian (NC).

**2) Equipment restoration:** Assess and rehabilitate the BioWay tower at NC (engage Bio Air or ECS); replace exhausted carbon media with properly sized biological or chemical scrubbers where H₂S exceeds 10 ppm.

**3) In-plant process control:** Maintain aerobic conditions in critical process areas, actively manage sludge age and solids inventory to prevent H₂S formation at the source, and minimize detention time in sludge handling across all facilities. At Deer Creek (DC) specifically, separate WAS/primary sludge storage and optimize peroxide dosing ahead of the belt press. Eliminate masking agents in favor of targeted source treatment.

**4) Early-contract odor assessment:** Identify highest-risk sources, develop a prioritized mitigation plan, and support [CLIENT]'s CIP planning for long-term odor control infrastructure — including evaluation of covered storage at North Canadian's underutilized 2.5-acre compost pad to reduce biosolids emissions and expand drying capacity.

**H₂S safety and odor-source identification training for operators**, with ongoing support from Jacobs' odor control, biosolids, and solids management specialists.
