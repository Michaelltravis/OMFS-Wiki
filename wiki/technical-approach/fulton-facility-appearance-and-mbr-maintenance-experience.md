# Facility Appearance Standard and MBR Maintenance Experience

In older areas of [FACILITY A], a wastewater facility, we will remove parts and materials and make areas safe. We recognized part of the issue is the ongoing construction, but we will continue to evaluate these needs as construction finishes.

Maintaining a clean, organized, and safe facility is fundamental to operational excellence and community confidence. JC Solutions is committed to upholding high standards for appearance and odor control at [CLIENT]’s wastewater facilities. To achieve this, we will establish a **Facilities Appearance Standard (FAST)**, setting clear expectations for site cleanliness, organization, and routine maintenance.

Maintenance Manager Nathan Dahl in coordination with Project Manager Anthony Benavidez will lead the development, implementation, and oversight of site housekeeping efforts, ensuring that all facilities consistently meet or exceed established standards. Senior Supervisor Tim Durham will work with our Maintenance Manager and Project Manager to foster a culture of accountability, encouraging staff ownership in facility appearance and proactively identifying areas for improvement. To maintain consistency, housekeeping tasks will be integrated into the CMMS, ensuring routine activities are scheduled and tracked. Weekly and monthly housekeeping tasks will include sweeping and mopping floors, removing cobwebs, cleaning control panels and windows, and maintaining landscaping. During our site visit, several areas for improvement were identified, which will be prioritized as part of our housekeeping and facility optimization efforts.

Our team brings extensive hands-on maintenance experience with MBR systems, demonstrated by our long-term operations at Traverse City, MI. We’ve developed a comprehensive maintenance program that has maximized system performance and extended membrane life through strategic preventative, predictive, and corrective approaches. Our capabilities include complete membrane replacement in collaboration with the Zenon system manufacturer, implementation of energy-saving upgrades, and development of innovative diagnostic techniques such as ultrasonic leak detection for vacuum systems.

We understand the critical factors that impact MBR performance and longevity. Our experience includes managing the complete life cycle of membrane systems, conducting detailed annual condition assessments with confined space entries and coating inspections, establishing robust preventative maintenance schedules for critical components like air release valves, and maintaining comprehensive spare parts inventories to prevent downtime.

By systematically addressing these housekeeping/safety needs and integrating routine site maintenance into our overall asset management plan, JC Solutions will ensure that [CLIENT]’s facilities operate safely, efficiently, and with a professional appearance that reflects [CLIENT]’s commitment to its residents.
