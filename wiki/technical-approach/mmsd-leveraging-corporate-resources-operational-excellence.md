# Leveraging Corporate Resources for Operational Excellence

Jacobs will leverage the full breadth of our corporate resources to strengthen operations and management of [CLIENT]'s complex facilities — a regional sewerage district's two large water reclamation facilities, conveyance system, and biosolids production — drawing on our broader technical, operational, and digital capabilities to enhance local performance. [CLIENT]'s system requires precise 24/7 monitoring and control of the inline storage system (ISS), pump stations, and interceptors as well as large and aging water reclamation facilities and the associated heat-dried biosolids production facilities. To meet these needs, Jacobs will integrate our local operations team with the expertise of our Operations Technical Services Group and Global Water Technology Organization. These groups include national experts in tunnel hydraulics, wet weather operations modeling, SCADA and instrumentation, energy optimization, and predictive maintenance, to name only a few, as illustrated in **Exhibits IV-34 and IV-35**. They'll provide [CLIENT] with on-call and remote support, data review, process optimization guidance, and escalation assistance — ensuring operational resiliency during both routine and extreme wet weather conditions.

By leveraging these corporate resources, Jacobs ensures [CLIENT]'s operations remain resilient, data-driven, and adaptive. Our unique delivery model provides both the agility of a dedicated local team and the depth of a global O&M, engineering, consulting, technology, and program management enterprise — ensuring continuity, redundancy, and innovation. Through this structure, [CLIENT] gains a high-performing partner committed to measurable reliability, O&M excellence, and continuous improvement throughout the contract.

## Exhibit IV-34. Our resources (asset `248_007CAM_2`; section device `376_007CAM_1`)

**Consulting** — planning; technology selection; energy management; carbon strategy and management; water management; permitting; compliance.

**Engineering** — project management; owner's engineer; alternatives evaluation; engineering design; process safety management; survey and laser scanning.

**Digital** — optimization; Intelligent O&M; digital twin; Aqua DNA system monitoring; Aqua DNA Deragger; WATS model; Dragonfly/Argon.

**Construction** — union and open shop direct hire; modular fabrication and installation; mechanical, piping and electrical construction; general contracting; commissioning and startup assistance.

**Procurement** — materials and equipment procurement; expediting and logistics; inventory management; subcontract administration.

**Operations** — planning and strategy development; plant and facility O&M; asset management; CMMS; subcontract management; technical services; planning and scheduling; project services; call center.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the two-paragraph shape — first the named corporate groups and the specific expertise they supply against the client's specific operating challenges, then the "agility of a local team, depth of a global enterprise" close. The Exhibit IV-34 capability inventory is boilerplate and reusable verbatim.

Pursuit-specific: the asset inventory in sentence two and the expertise list in sentence three. A collections-only pursuit should swap tunnel hydraulics and wet weather modeling for CMOM, CCTV analytics, and I&I. Trim the capability inventory to the columns the client's scope touches — listing construction capability on a pure O&M bid can invite change-order suspicion.
