# Community Involvement and Outreach Program

Jacobs is committed to being an active and visible partner in the [CLIENT] community. We understand that successful advanced water reuse projects are built not only on technical excellence, but also on public understanding, trust, and support. Our outreach will go beyond meeting contractual requirements — we will bring passion, creativity, and decades of leadership in water reuse education to engage the community.

## Building on Current Momentum

The current Chief Plant Operator at the facility has already demonstrated the power of effective community engagement, providing tours that resonate with public and school groups. Jacobs will build on this foundation, supporting the continuation and expansion of existing outreach efforts. We will work closely with [CLIENT] to align outreach with the community's sustainability goals and to ensure consistent, positive messaging about water reuse.

## Strategic Importance of Outreach

Outreach and education are now considered industry standard at advanced reuse facilities, as we've seen demonstrated at West Basin, our most recent addition in California. As utilities move toward IPR and DPR (indirect and direct potable reuse), proactive public engagement is critical to building trust and acceptance. Our approach reflects this strategic importance: community education is not an optional add-on, but a core component of long-term project success.

## Jacobs' Leadership in Water Reuse Education

Jacobs has been at the forefront of water outreach for decades. We have helped shape industry best practices and consistently demonstrated that effective education programs build community trust and excitement around water reuse.

## Commitment to Community

Jacobs is committed to being an active member of the communities we serve. Our engagement builds and supports sustainable communities through a wide variety of programs, including charitable contributions, volunteering, internships and mentoring partnerships, and educational outreach. These activities reflect Jacobs' culture of caring and demonstrate our commitment to leaving a positive legacy wherever we work. For [CLIENT], we will build on this foundation by tailoring a comprehensive and customized community involvement program that reflects local values and priorities.

### Proof points — Jacobs' community projects at other sites (illustrative, not [CLIENT]-specific)

- **Tulsa Metropolitan Area — Education and Environment:** volunteering with Up with Trees and Habitat for Humanity; mentoring/judging robotics competitions, Math Counts, and the Tulsa Regional STEM Alliance; annual financial sponsorships including the Tulsa Engineering Foundation's E-Week and the Metropolitan Environmental Trust (MET).
- **Dodge City, KS — Local Involvement:** horseback therapy program support and volunteer assistance; sponsorship of the Ford County Fair; work with Ford County 4-H groups repairing and painting livestock barns; support of the Southwest Cancer Benefit Rummage Sale.
- **Rio Rancho, NM — Importance of Education:** participation in and sponsorship of the city's annual Children's Water Festival and environmental outreach educational program; donated school supplies to an at-risk-student high school.
- **San Marcos, TX — Promoting Career Development:** sponsored paid internship program open to all students majoring in science-related fields at Texas State University, with interns working as lab analysts gaining exposure to laboratory safety, SOPs, sampling, analysis, QC, regulatory compliance, and the fundamentals of plant O&M.

## Customized Engagement Program — Menu of Elements

Jacobs will collaborate with [CLIENT] to design a tailored engagement program that reflects the community's values and interests, drawing on the creativity of our staff, including team members like Royce Davis, who has direct experience leading regional water outreach initiatives. Potential program elements include:

**Educational Programs & Tours**
- STEM education partnerships with local elementary, middle, and high schools to develop curricula, invite students to tour the plant, and connect classroom science with real-world applications.
- Virtual tours for schools unable to travel, making outreach inclusive and accessible.
- Expanded community plant tours and open houses to engage residents of all ages.

**Community Events & STEM Engagement**
- Participation in local events such as Earth Day, sustainability fairs, and other City-sponsored activities, where Jacobs can host interactive booths that teach children and adults about the value of wastewater treatment and reuse (including the "bugs" that make treatment possible).

**Internships & Workforce Development**
- Internship programs with local colleges and technical schools to build local workforce capacity.
- Partnerships with regional institutions (e.g., state universities and community colleges) to create meaningful employment pathways into water careers.

**Volunteering & Charitable Support**
- Active participation in local clean-ups, food pantries, holiday-giving programs, and other community causes.
- Engagement with civic organizations such as Chambers of Commerce.

We view outreach not as a requirement to be checked off, but as an opportunity to connect with people — to inspire, educate, recruit, and build lasting partnerships within the community. By bringing together Jacobs' global expertise with the passion and creativity of our local team, we will help [CLIENT] strengthen its leadership in water reuse while fostering a culture of sustainability that the entire community can take pride in.
