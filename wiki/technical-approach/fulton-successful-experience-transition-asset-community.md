# Successful Experience at Other Locations: Transition, Asset Management, and Community Partnership

In January 2016, Jacobs unseated the 37-year incumbent Veolia to manage the City of Vancouver’s wastewater services, offering a unique approach that combines operations, engineering, and capital improvements. We currently operate the City’s 28.26-MGD Westside Wastewater Treatment Plant, 16.1-MGD Marine Park Wastewater Treatment Plant, 1.7-MGD Industrial Lagoon Treatment Facility, and eight lift stations.

Jacobs successfully implemented a turnkey $24M upgrade to the City’s SCADA system and PLCs, integrating Maintenance Connection and Hach WIMS for maintenance and laboratory management. We execute a robust Asset Management program in partnership with the City, to maximize the lifecycle of assets and the City’s capital investments.

**Relevance to [CLIENT]:** transitioned from Veolia; multiple-facilities O&M; physical and cybersecurity; S/W/MBE; local-workforce development; public information and engagement; compliance excellence; Intelligent O&M support; and chemical optimization.

“As the City of Vancouver’s professional service O&M provider, Jacobs is a true partner and one that shares the same mission and vision to provide excellent customer service, quality work and a deep appreciation for protecting the community and the environment. Jacobs took responsibility of operating and maintaining Vancouver’s wastewater treatment plants in 2016. As part of the transition process, Jacobs put together a comprehensive transition plan which made the process very positive, smooth, and seamless for not only the City, but also the existing staff that became Jacobs employees. There was a high degree of transparency which continues to this day.” — Frank Dick, PE, Sewer and Wastewater Engineering Supervisor, City of Vancouver, WA

The City of Waterbury and Jacobs O&M partnership for the 27-MGD WWTP, sewer collection system, and pump stations began in 2018. Based on our success in maintaining operations during construction through major upgrades led by third-party engineers, our robust asset-management approach, and our team’s transparency and responsiveness, Jacobs was awarded the O&M contract for the water system in 2023, previously operated by Veolia. Jacobs is developing an innovative sludge-management project with the City to address issues created by poor past sludge handling at the WTP.

The Waterbury team sponsors a program called the Naugatuck River Brigade, which is focused on the health of the river and protecting this valuable resource. The Jacobs team volunteers staff hours to help provide riverbank cleanup and has been recognized for their community involvement and commitment. “Jacobs provided a team-oriented approach, utilizing their professional expertise and including their field staff to form our partnership. Their vision for long-term success provides value-added services and savings to the city by using their vast resources and, more importantly, providing the safest, highest-quality drinking water for our customers.” — Rob Langenauer, City of Waterbury Superintendent of Water.

**Relevance to [CLIENT]:** transitioned from Veolia; multiple-facilities O&M; odor-control study and recommendations; S/W/MBE; local-workforce development; public information and engagement; compliance excellence; chemical optimization; and beneficial reuse of water-treatment-plant sludge support.
