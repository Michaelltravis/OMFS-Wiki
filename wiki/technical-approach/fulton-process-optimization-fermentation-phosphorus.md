# Process Optimization, Fermentation, and Phosphorus Removal

JC Solutions’ onsite operators and industry-leading process engineers will collaborate to optimize plant operations—ensuring the high level of compliance [CLIENT] expects while minimizing energy use, chemical consumption, biosolids disposal, and membrane replacement costs.

Throughout this section, we identify more than a dozen valueadded process improvements that target operational efficiency, cost control, process reliability, enhanced compliance, and strategic facility optimization. By carefully balancing chemical and energy use, proactively managing assets and membranes, reducing operational risks, and optimizing existing infrastructure, our approach ensures reliable treatment performance, lower lifecycle costs, and greater regulatory compliance.

$150,000 worth of investment by JC Solutions.

Fermentation is used at all three plants to create conditions amenable to enhanced biological phosphorus removal (EBPR). Successful EBPR allows the effluent phosphorus limit to be met using the least amount of metal salt (Phos-Zorb, in this case). Best performance is achieved by limiting oxygen and nitrate input to the fermentation zone, which can be evaluated by process simulation. Performance can be further enhanced by starting and stopping mixers to store more solids in the zone.

While process simulation is a useful tool for exploring these settings, there are limitations, as simulators have not reached the point of accurately predicting the outcome of biomass fermentation. Instead, a combination of simulation and full-scale testing is required to optimize this process.

If required to supplement EBPR, Phos-Zorb will be used for chemical phosphorus removal. To allow biological and chemical phosphorous removal to co-exist, secondary effluent phosphorus should be maintained no lower than necessary. At very low phosphorus concentrations, the chemical interferes with EBPR. For each plant, an effluent phosphorus target agreeable to [CLIENT] will be identified, and JC Solutions will operate the plant to achieve this target.

Phos-Zorb is a blend of two chemicals, sodium aluminate and sodium hydroxide. Sodium aluminate provides aluminum for chemical phosphorus removal. Sodium hydroxide provides alkalinity, which increases biological process pH.

An effluent phosphorus target for each plant that meets [CLIENT] expectations will be identified, and we will operate the plant to achieve this target. We will also explore the options to reduce chemical requirements for phosphorus removal.

We will perform jar testing to evaluate primary clarifier performance using ferric chloride, alum, Phos-Zorb, and anionic polymer. We will use process simulation to determine benefits to secondary treatment (effluent quality, aeration energy, chemical requirements, and membrane performance).
