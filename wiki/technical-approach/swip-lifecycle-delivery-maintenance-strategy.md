# Lifecycle Delivery and Maintenance Strategy — PM/PdM Blend, CMMS Workflow, and Work-Order Service Levels

**Lifecycle Delivery and Maintenance Strategy.** Jacobs will manage the complete asset lifecycle, from acquisition to decommissioning, through a proactive strategy that blends PM and PdM. Our preventive maintenance optimization (PMO) and Level 3 PdM programs will replace reactive maintenance with forward-looking diagnostics. Routine field inspections will monitor asset condition, while PdM tools such as ultrasound, vibration analysis, and motor signature assessments will support early detection of issues. Jacobs will complete all PM and PdM activities with in-house teams, supported by regional technical staff as needed. Corrective maintenance tasks will be tracked in the CMMS, issued within 24 hours of detection, and closed within 120 days. Facility appearance and maintenance will be tracked in parallel to ensure all spaces remain safe, clean, and professional.

The CMMS (Computerized Maintenance Management System) workflow integrates a Preventive Maintenance Plan, a Corrective Maintenance Plan, and Inventory Activity as inputs, and produces a Preventive Maintenance Schedule, Maintenance Performance Reports and Maintenance Review, Asset Performance Management and Capital Planning outputs, and Inventory Status. (Graphic asset ID `OMFS_AM_102`.)

Corrective maintenance will be systematically tracked within the CMMS, ensuring that all failure-related work orders are issued within 24 hours of detection and closed within 120 days. Additionally, Jacobs will uphold high facility appearance standards, ensuring that all buildings, process areas, and fleet equipment remain clean, well-maintained, and professionally managed. Maintenance tasks will be scheduled and monitored through the CMMS, ensuring structured oversight and accountability.
