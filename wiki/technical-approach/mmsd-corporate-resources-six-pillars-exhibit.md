# Unmatched Corporate Resources for Operational Excellence (Exhibit IV-35)

Exhibit asset `286_007CAM_2`. Five pillars of corporate support surround the central claim: corporate resources for operational excellence.

| Pillar | What it delivers | Benefit to [CLIENT] |
|---|---|---|
| **Access to unmatched engineering and SME support** | Deep bench of O&M, engineering and consulting SMEs, supported by a global organization of 43,000 professionals, enables rapid escalation, specialized technical support, and quick deployment of qualified resources to address staffing, operational, maintenance, or management challenges as they arise. | Continuous operational readiness and uninterrupted service delivery across all facilities and processes, fully aligned with contract expectations. |
| **"Above and beyond" Jacobs executive support for accountability** | Provides senior-level engagement and rapid escalation to ensure [CLIENT]'s objectives are consistently achieved. They'll remove barriers, deploy resources as needed, and maintain executive accountability for safety, performance, and continuous improvement. | Reinforces a strong governance model aligned with contract expectations. |
| **Leveraging digital platforms and analytics** | Delivers above-and-beyond operational performance through continuous monitoring, benchmarking, and enterprise-level analytics that serve as the backbone of our O&M delivery. We can also bring other leading-edge, proven tools such as AquaDNA™, which provides predictive rain forecasting, anomaly detection, and energy optimization. | Enables faster, data-driven decisions, improved reliability, and enhanced situational awareness. |
| **Robust enterprise HR for staffing support** | Ensures [CLIENT]'s staffing levels are consistently met with qualified, experienced professionals. With dedicated recruiting, onboarding, succession planning, and strong union partnerships — supported by a bench of more than 5,000 in-house water professionals — we can proactively fill vacancies and maintain workforce continuity throughout the contract term. | Staffing continuity, technical competency, and a stable operations workforce throughout the contract term. |
| **Advanced OT, automation and cybersecurity** | Supports [CLIENT]'s SCADA modernization, network reliability, and cyber risk management. We'll apply our successful advanced control systems such as remote analytics, predictive diagnostics, and cloud-based visualization dashboards to support operators. | Ensures secure, optimized, and compliant operation of treatment processes, pumping systems, and remote assets — aligned with [CLIENT] IT and cybersecurity standards. |

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the five-pillar frame and the capability/benefit split. This exhibit answers the evaluator's "so what does corporate scale buy me?" question directly, which is why it scores; keep the benefit column short and outcome-shaped.

Pursuit-specific: the numbers (confirm current headcount figures), the digital tool names (drop AquaDNA if the pursuit is not wet-weather-driven), and every benefit line. On a challenger bid, the staffing pillar is the one to lead with — it answers the incumbent-transition risk the evaluator is actually worried about.
