# Standing Up the PgM Framework — Named Leadership and Insights-Based Alignment

We'll follow the seven-step PgM initiation process summarized in **Exhibit IV-8** to develop a strategy, budget baselines, forecasts, implementation plans, a digital platform, and training for the entire [CLIENT] organization.

**Keoki Sears** will lead our Program Management Framework for [CLIENT]. Keoki is Jacobs' global director of program management services and brings 35 years of experience leading programs around the world.

**Quitterie Cotten**, who offers 28 years of wastewater experience, will help Keoki implement this program. See *Section II — Statement on Staffing, Training, and Corporate Culture* for more information on our Program Management team structure.

## Above and Beyond Value-Added Extras: Establishing Leadership Alignment

Team dynamics are critical to performance. Insights Discovery provides a framework to build trust, strengthen collaboration, and create a shared language for alignment. We launched our PgM Framework by assessing the leadership team and plan to extend the assessment to [CLIENT] staff. Our goal is to build a positive, aligned culture that enables [CLIENT] and Jacobs to operate as a unified partnership.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the move of naming a corporate-level program management leader — someone above the project organization — and then naming the person who will actually implement day to day. Evaluators read that pairing as executive attention plus execution capacity. The Insights Discovery leadership-alignment step is a Jacobs-standard value-added extra offered at no cost and transfers to any pursuit.

Pursuit-specific: the two named leaders and their experience figures; the seven-step exhibit reference; the cross-reference to the staffing section.
