# Liquid Treatment Process Control — Systemwide Integration Across Two WRFs

The two water reclamation facilities are the backbone of [CLIENT]'s performance, and our strategy builds on the principle that system-wide insight will drive operational excellence, linking inline storage system (ISS) operations, plant hydraulics, and process optimization through predictive controls that enhance reliability, sustainability, and permit compliance.

*Systemwide Integration for Optimized Operations.* Our operating approach for the WRFs' liquid treatment trains focuses on disciplined and data-driven process control across [CLIENT]'s entire conveyance and treatment system. We'll coordinate flow management in the ISS and tunnel with real-time plant hydraulics to maximize throughput, prevent overloads, and achieve zero CSOs by 2035. Pump station operations will align dynamically with conveyance conditions. We'll optimize clarifier and aeration processes in tandem to balance biological loading and solids management between the two WRFs.

A **centralized digital operations dashboard** integrating SCADA, CMMS, and process analytics will make hydraulics, energy use, aeration performance, and effluent quality visible to operators. This data-informed platform will support stable operations, early risk detection, and proactive decision-making:

- **Preliminary and Primary Treatment.** Monitoring screens, grit removal, and clarifier performance will prevent headloss and ensure efficient solids capture. We'll optimize primary sludge withdrawal rates to balance downstream biological and solids loading.

- **Secondary (Biological) Treatment.** Jacobs will evaluate, in collaboration with [CLIENT], implementation of advanced dissolved oxygen (DO) control with real-time ammonia/nitrate monitoring and model-predictive control of aeration systems to reduce energy use while ensuring compliance. Aeration and RAS flows will be dynamically adjusted based on real-time loadings to maintain stable mixed liquor suspended solids (MLSS) concentrations, prevent secondary clarifier stress, and support responsive wet weather operations.

- **Tertiary and Disinfection.** To ensure reliable pathogen control and readiness for future *E. coli* permit requirements, we'll maintain automated chlorination and dechlorination systems with feedback loops tied to flow and chlorine residuals using machine learning, in combination with [CLIENT]'s new UV system. These automated systems will still require human operator review and approval.

Jacobs will measure and report performance using dashboards and KPIs aligned with [CLIENT]'s priorities, including effluent quality, energy use per MGD, chemical consumption, process stability, and minimized bypass events. Regular joint reviews with [CLIENT] staff will guide continuous improvement and proactive asset management.
