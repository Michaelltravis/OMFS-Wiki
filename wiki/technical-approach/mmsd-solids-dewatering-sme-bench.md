# Deep Bench of Dewatering and Solids Experts — Value-Added SME Support

**ABOVE AND BEYOND — VALUE-ADDED EXTRAS**

***Deep Bench of Support Resources including Industry-Leading Dewatering and Solids Experts***

**David Oerke, PE, BCEE**, a nationally recognized thickening and dewatering SME brings unmatched expertise from the evaluation/design of solids thickening and processing equipment for more than 120 municipalities nationwide. He's been key to developing internal Jacobs specifications for centrifuges, belt filter presses, screw presses, and gravity belt and rotary drum thickeners. He is a recognized solids expert at WEF.

**Drew Mearns, Biosolids O&M SME** has 30+ years of experience in biosolids management nationwide, from dewatering to disposal. He brings an operational approach focused on providing best management practices as well as lowest cost alternatives. He offers expertise in the latest disposal and transportation practices and has experience with a diverse range of beneficial reuse systems and providers.

Our team members **David Porter** and **Dave Oerke** specialize in optimizing belt filter presses and dewatering performance for facilities across the U.S.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the callout format itself — a boxed "Above and Beyond" panel of two or three named SMEs, each with a credential, a quantified reach, and the specific equipment or process they own. It answers the evaluator's unstated question about what the client gets beyond the onsite roster.

Pursuit-specific: which SMEs are offered. Match the bench to the plant's dominant risk — solids and dewatering here, membranes or odor control elsewhere. Pairs with the dewatering and drying optimization block, which carries these SMEs' actual recommendations, and with the optimization SME paragraph in the chemical optimization block.
