# Grant, Loan, and Capital Funding Strategy Support Services

As energy efficiency and consumption improvements are identified, we can also help identify potential funding sources. Water and wastewater assets require a continuous cycle of capital planning, repair, and replacement. At some point in the life cycle of [CLIENT]'s facilities, significant investment will be required. When that time comes, preparation and a clear understanding of available financing options is critical.

We routinely help clients develop funding strategies for capital projects. Our financial services team can work with [CLIENT] to explore options, avoid pitfalls, and **maximize return on investment**. Common funding solutions include grants and low-interest loans, traditional municipal funding, and user fees and other charges. The grant and loan application process can be complicated and time-consuming. Applications often require financial plans, environmental review documentation, and descriptions of planned projects, estimated costs, and schedules. Whatever the requirements, we can help [CLIENT] stay ahead of the process.

## Funding Strategy Development Approach

This structured approach to evaluating and securing funding is illustrated in **Exhibit 5-12** (graphic asset `138_HHull_0091KO_1`), a four-step sequence:

1. **Plan and prioritize** — what are your project priorities, and how much do you need?
2. **Identify options** — what are the possible funding options, and how can they be combined?
3. **Financial modeling** — what are the funding scenarios?
4. **Recommended strategy** — the final funding strategy and action plan.

A systematic process is applied to understand capital priorities, thoroughly explore funding options, and develop financial forecast models for potential scenarios that consider inflation, interest rates, project phasing, and other key factors. We can help [CLIENT] develop funding strategies and secure financing at the right time, supported by detailed schedules and milestone tracking. **We're here to help you solve the funding gap.**

## Proven Funding Track Record

Jacobs is a leader in obtaining low-interest Water Infrastructure Finance and Innovation Act (WIFIA) loans for our clients. We have achieved a **100% success rate with WIFIA loan approvals**, winning **more than $1.5B in municipal water and wastewater loans since the program's inception in 2018**, with a **cost of capital as low as 1.14%**.

## Reuse guidance

The four-step funding-strategy process (Plan and prioritize → Identify options → Financial modeling → Recommended strategy) is a fully generic, reusable framework for any capital-planning or funding-support pitch, regardless of facility size or financing program, as is the "we're here to help you solve the funding gap" closer. The funding track-record figures (100% WIFIA approval rate, more than $1.5B in loans won since 2018, cost of capital as low as 1.14%) are corporate outcome statistics, not pursuit-specific commercial pricing, so they are kept — but they are a point-in-time snapshot and must be refreshed with current, verifiable corporate data at drafting time, since the $1.5B figure grows as new loans close.
