# Grant, Loan, and Capital Funding Strategy Support Services

As energy-efficiency, asset-renewal, and other capital improvements are identified through the O&M program, funding strategy support helps the client turn those needs into fundable, financeable projects. Water and wastewater assets require a continuous cycle of capital planning, repair, and replacement; at some point in a facility's lifecycle, significant investment becomes unavoidable, and preparation combined with a clear understanding of available financing options is critical to closing the funding gap.

**Funding strategy development process.** A structured, four-step process moves a client from project prioritization to a fully developed funding strategy:

1. **Plan and prioritize** — define project priorities and quantify how much funding is actually needed.
2. **Identify options** — survey the available funding mechanisms and how they can be combined.
3. **Financial modeling** — build financial forecast models for potential funding scenarios, considering inflation, interest rates, project phasing, and other key variables.
4. **Recommended strategy** — deliver a final funding strategy and action plan.

A financial services team works directly with the client to explore funding options, avoid common pitfalls, and maximize return on investment, supported by detailed schedules and milestone tracking. Common funding solutions include grants, low-interest loans, traditional municipal funding, and user fees and other charges. The grant and loan application process is often complicated and time-consuming — applications typically require financial plans, environmental review documentation, project descriptions, cost estimates, and schedules — and structured support helps the client stay ahead of that process.

**Proven funding track record.** Jacobs is a leader in obtaining low-interest Water Infrastructure Finance and Innovation Act (WIFIA) loans for its clients: a **100% success rate with WIFIA loan approvals**, **more than $1.5B in municipal water and wastewater loans won since the program's inception in 2018**, and a cost of capital **as low as 1.14%** — historically low compared to conventional financing alternatives.

## Reuse guidance

The four-step funding-strategy process (Plan and prioritize → Identify options → Financial modeling → Recommended strategy) is a fully generic, reusable framework for any capital-planning or funding-support pitch, regardless of facility size or financing program.
