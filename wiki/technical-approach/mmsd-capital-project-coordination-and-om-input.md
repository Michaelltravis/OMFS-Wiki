# Capital Project Coordination and O&M Input Across CIP Phases

**CIP Implementation and Capital Project Support.** When it's time to execute the CIP, Jacobs offers end-to-end solutions—from planning and design through construction, commissioning, and ongoing operation. **Dedicated Capital Project Coordinators Liie Hill, PE, and Natalie Lenz, PE**, will exclusively support [CLIENT]'s CIP. These Capital Project Coordinators link day-to-day O&M with [CLIENT] Engineering/CIP for prioritization, constructability reviews, outage coordination, startup/turnover, and asset record updates.

Working with [CLIENT] project managers and third-party engineers, our Capital Project Coordinators will:

- Support the use of the AMS to identify common assets or adjacent assets that will likely need replacement shortly after a planned capital improvement, especially where there is the potential to save money in the long run.
- Assist in capital improvements scheduling to minimize capacity reductions and avoid conflicts with O&M activities in other areas (for example, scheduling contract required testing at critical conveyance sites to coincide with testing that will be required after the installation of new SCADA systems, which will occur during the next 10-year operating contract).
- Provide structured O&M input during all phases of CIP implementation including project development, planning, preliminary engineering, design, construction and commissioning, and post-construction/warranty phases. Jacobs is unique in the market to provide this insight because of our integrated One Water approach where our engineers make our operators better and our operators make our engineers better.
- Engage in and coordinate participation by other appropriate Jacobs O&M staff in project meetings, design workshops, documents and constructability reviews, testing, training, and outage/diversion planning. This step is critical for both Jacobs and [CLIENT]'s long-term success, making sure capital funds are used effectively, efficiently and everyone is engaged.
- Ensure management and operations staff are available for capital project support 24/7, with facility and conveyance O&M staff available for routine capital project support during weekday daylight hours and for special requests after hours and on weekends.
- Support operational readiness and functional acceptance testing, ensure new and modified assets are fully captured in the enterprise AMS, and verify that PM activities are loaded and ready once they start to be used.
- Track all labor time spent on [CLIENT] capital projects by [CLIENT] project number and submit quarterly reports of capital support labor in accordance with the Service Agreement.

**Expert Capital Project Planning and Coordination Support — Liie Hill, PE & Natalie Lenz, PE.** Liie Hill, PE, will serve as our lead Capital Project Coordinator and primary liaison to [CLIENT]'s project managers and third party engineers. She'll provide phase-by-phase CIP input from operations and maintenance and project-level tracking and quarterly report of the capital project support hours, required under the Service Agreements. She'll coordinate our staff to support facility assessments and [CLIENT] and third-party engineer capital planning efforts and execution. She'll assist with equipment startup, testing, integration, and operations for successful CIP projects.

Natalie Lenz, PE, will work with Liie as Capital Project Coordinator, drawing on 8 years of Jacobs experience supporting [CLIENT] watershed, conveyance, and facility planning projects to align capital improvements with [CLIENT]'s long-term environmental, regulatory, and operational objectives. With a deep working knowledge of [CLIENT]'s facilities and regulatory environment, she will work closely with [CLIENT] project managers, Jacobs' O&M teams, and third-party engineers to bring clear, data-driven input into project scoping, design reviews, and commissioning support so CIP projects are practical to operate, resilient, and ready for smooth startup.

Through this integrated CIP process, asset-management-driven reinvestment planning, and defined capital project support role, Jacobs ensures that [CLIENT]'s CIP is informed by real-time operational insight, is implemented with minimal disruption to ongoing operations, and is fully compliant with the capital program support requirements of the Agreement.

The "our engineers make our operators better and our operators make our engineers better" line is the reusable One Water positioning statement.
