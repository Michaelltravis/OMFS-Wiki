# Maintenance Staffing Approach and Drain-and-Clean Restructuring

We will staff each facility with dedicated maintenance teams sized for that facility's complexity and workload. Daily preventive and routine corrective maintenance will be performed by personnel assigned to each facility, not shared across distant sites. South Canadian's geographic distance from the northern facilities (45–50 minutes) and its new equipment profile demand a dedicated on-site team focused on warranty compliance, commissioning support, and a PM-heavy maintenance ratio from day one. The northern facilities — North Canadian, Deer Creek, and Chisholm Creek — will share specialized resources for electrical, I&C, and heavy corrective maintenance through a coordinated model that takes advantage of their relative proximity.

We're **also restructuring how drain and clean work gets done**. Operators — not mechanics — will execute the Schedule 15 drain and clean program, with mechanics following on for post-clean inspections and any preventive maintenance the drain-down reveals. We've sized the operations workforce specifically for this new approach, adding hourly operator capacity at every facility (detailed in our Staffing and Training Plan in Section 5 – Required Plans). The result for [CLIENT] — a southcentral US municipal water utility trust operating four wastewater treatment plants and a major pump station: more mechanic wrench time on PM, PdM, and corrective work that protects long-term asset value and operational reliability.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the principle that daily PM and routine corrective work is performed by staff assigned to the site rather than shared across distant facilities; the hybrid model where geographically clustered plants pool specialized electrical, I&C, and heavy corrective resources while an outlying plant gets its own team; the warranty-compliance and commissioning rationale for dedicating a team to a plant taking on new CIP equipment; and the drain-and-clean labor restructuring with its stated benefit in mechanic wrench time.
