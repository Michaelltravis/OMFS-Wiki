# Seven Steps to Set Up the PgM Framework for Success

Graphic asset ID: `309_007CAM_6` (client-specific: true). Banner across the deliverables row: "Increasing Capacity."

| Step / Goal | Purpose | Results & Deliverables |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Mobilize Program Management Team | Invest in orienting and empowering the PgM team, drawing on the 16,000+ hours Jacobs staff have already applied to assessing your system and developing the proposal | Program Management Framework initiated by project team |
| 2. Establish Leadership Alignment | Build team cohesion with INSIGHTS assessment. Charter to align on mission, vision, roles and responsibilities, conflict resolution mechanisms, and desired outcomes | Program Leadership Charter and Readiness Assessment |
| 3. Development of Holistic Strategies | In collaboration with [CLIENT], agree on strategies for each element of management, operations, and maintenance with a focus on driving transparency and building trust | Function strategies / Program Implementation Plan (PIP); documented communication strategy |
| 4. Governance for Decision-making Clarity | Structure accountability and clearly define decision-making, lines of authority, and transparent reporting | Governance Handbook |
| 5. Deployment of Defined Ways of Working | Establish detailed and integrated plans, processes, and tools to drive desired outcomes in compliance with the agreed overarching delivery strategy and function strategies | Program management platform and tools to be shared by entire team |
| 6. Development of Documented Baseline | Develop and ratify baseline scope, budget, and schedule aligned with [CLIENT] and used to measure our success | Baseline Report (scope, schedule, and budget) and Risk Register |
| 7. Charter, Align, and Train Entire Team | Align and educate the entire team and stakeholders to foster a One Team mindset | Team Charter and Training |

## Reuse guidance

Universal: all seven steps, their purposes, and their deliverables — this is a Jacobs-standard framework and transfers to any program-managed O&M contract.

Pursuit-specific: the due-diligence hours in Step 1, the client name in Steps 3 and 6, and the One Team theme line in Step 7.
