# Comprehensive O&M Plan Document Roster (30-Plan Framework)

Jacobs understands that effective O&M planning must be both detailed and adaptable. This approach combines proven best practices with innovative solutions, all delivered through open, transparent communication with the client to ensure fast, coordinated responses to any issues. Operations planning is viewed as a collaborative effort — where shared goals and teamwork drive high performance and reliability.

Jacobs' approach to developing a comprehensive O&M Plan for the client's facilities builds upon existing operations, emphasizing reliability, efficiency, safety, and continuous improvement. Through detailed evaluation of current facility conditions and procedures, Jacobs establishes a robust framework that integrates scientific process control, proactive maintenance and asset management, rigorous QA/QC, structured sampling and lab coordination, efficient solids management, and effective odor control strategies.

The list below is a representative list of Jacobs' O&M Plan elements. These plans are updated starting at transition and will be implemented by **Mack Mckenzie, CPO**, with ongoing support from the **Director of Operations and Jacobs' California Geographic Manager Howard Brewen** and **Manager of Operations Chris Catlin**. Together, this leadership team tailors and refines these plans to ensure the client's facilities operate at peak efficiency.

The O&M Plan also establishes clear protocols for internal and client-facing reporting and communications to ensure full transparency. Emphasis is placed on health and safety, supported by rigorous site security protocols and modern cybersecurity protections. All elements of the plan are formalized through facility-specific Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) that document best practices for execution, troubleshooting, and compliance.

## Reuse guidance

This ~30-item plan roster is fully reusable as a section-opening exhibit for demonstrating O&M planning comprehensiveness in any pursuit. Cross-check the roster against the target RFP's specifically enumerated required plans/programs and add or remove items as needed — some pursuits require additional named plans (e.g., an Odor Control Plan, a Confined Space Program) not listed here. The named leadership team (Mack Mckenzie, Howard Brewen, Chris Catlin) is this pursuit's actual proposed staff — replace with whichever CPO/Director/Manager is proposed for a different pursuit if these individuals are not staffed to it.
