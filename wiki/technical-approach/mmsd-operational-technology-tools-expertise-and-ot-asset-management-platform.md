# World-Class Operational Technology — OT Tools, Training, and the OT Asset Management Platform

**Delivering world-class operational technology and expertise.** Jacobs will equip onsite staff with modern OT tools, upgraded systems, and direct access to leading automation and controls specialists. Backed by decades of SCADA, process control, and OT modernization experience across critical infrastructure, we bring [CLIENT] a deep bench in software engineering, network architecture, cybersecurity, and life-cycle asset management.

We embed that expertise into practical, field-ready solutions. Our OT Asset Management platform, automated PLC backup and change-control systems, and secure access tools translate proven best practices from thousands of deployments into day-to-day capabilities — helping troubleshoot faster, reduce risk, and maintain compliance as standards evolve.

Training is a core deliverable, not an add-on. We'll provide targeted, [CLIENT]-specific training for onsite I&C and technology staff, focused on real operational needs such as wet-weather performance, network resiliency, and cybersecurity hardening. This ensures staff can confidently apply new tools in [CLIENT]'s operating environment.

**By pairing advanced technology with Jacobs' technical depth, we'll strengthen the capabilities of the [CLIENT] workforce and build a durable foundation for sustained operational excellence.**

**Jacobs OT Asset Management Software Platform.** [CLIENT] increasingly relies on advanced automation and control systems to operate its treatment and conveyance network. These OT assets — analytical instruments, PLCs, networked motor controls, HMIs, and field communications — are mission-critical, yet traditional CMMS platforms aren't designed to manage their complexity, interdependencies, and rapid configuration change.

Jacobs will close this gap with a comprehensive OT asset management platform tailored to [CLIENT]'s needs. Our system captures the full OT environment, from major components down to connectors, cabling, firmware, and configuration files, so teams can see asset condition, configuration health, and life-cycle risk in one place.

This end-to-end visibility strengthens resiliency by enabling smarter decisions, proactive maintenance, and a clearer understanding of how each OT component supports critical treatment and conveyance processes, as summarized in **Exhibit IV-31** (asset ID `375_007CAM_1`).
