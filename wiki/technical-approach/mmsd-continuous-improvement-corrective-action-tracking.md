# Continuous Improvement and Corrective Action Tracking

Jacobs' program fosters ongoing optimization through periodic audits, process modeling, and analytics-driven performance reviews. Each quarter, our team will evaluate process efficiencies and develop targeted improvement actions, such as optimizing aeration control, refining chemical feed curves, and using existing SCADA-integrated dryer and product temperature data to fine-tune dryer operating parameters. This includes focused review of product temperature trends monitored and archived to demonstrate compliance with EPA 503 requirements, while identifying opportunities to improve energy efficiency and operational consistency.

We'll log corrective actions identified through monitoring or audit reviews, track them to closure, and summarize them in quarterly reports. This provides [CLIENT] — a regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities and a heat-dried biosolids product line — with real-time visibility, auditable data, and measurable performance gains across all critical operational domains, from tunnel operations and energy management to biosolids product production, and PM/CM completion with evidence ensuring compliance, efficiency, and transparency in system performance.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the quarterly evaluate-act-close cycle, the insistence that improvement actions come from data the client already owns, and the commitment that corrective actions are tracked to closure and reported. That closure loop is what an evaluator scores; an improvement list with no closure mechanism reads as marketing.

Pursuit-specific: the example improvement actions and the domain list in the closing sentence. Rebuild the domain list from the client's own operating areas so the sentence reads as a scope commitment rather than filler.
