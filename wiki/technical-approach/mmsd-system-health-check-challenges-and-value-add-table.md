**Exhibit IV-4. Fresh perspective, built on more than 50 years serving the region and deep experience, will help [CLIENT] achieve its 2035 Vision.** Graphic asset ID `288_007CAM_6`.

_Health Check — We ranked issues using a color-coded scale ranging from no major issues (green) to more significant system risks requiring focused attention (red). We also outlined proposed value-added opportunities to address these needs and strengthen overall system performance._ Legend: No major issues · Minor improvements needed · Targeted improvements needed · Dedicated attention required.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the exhibit form. A findings-and-solutions grid across every asset group, with a color-coded severity legend, is the strongest way to show that due diligence actually happened—and it lets a challenger describe incumbent shortfalls as system conditions rather than accusations. Keep the discipline of numbering both sides so an evaluator can trace each finding to a remedy.

Pursuit-specific and high sanitization loss: the findings are this district's, and the paired facility identities (which WRF has which problem) carry proof value that generalization removes.
