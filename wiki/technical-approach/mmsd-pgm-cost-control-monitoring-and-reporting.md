# PgM-Driven Cost Control, Monitoring, Communications, and Reporting

Jacobs' PgM framework will be the backbone for how we **monitor, control, and communicate operational performance and costs** across [CLIENT]'s operations—[CLIENT] being a large regional sewerage district operating two water reclamation facilities and a regional conveyance system. In partnership with [CLIENT], we'll **align operational monitoring to the RFP's Standards of Performance exhibit**, jointly define key performance indicators (KPIs), and use integrated data from NEXGEN, SCADA, CMMS, Hach WIMS/LIMS, and financial systems to produce **one validated, single source of truth.** We'll provide [CLIENT] with real-time dashboards, automated exception tracking, and structured monthly/quarterly reviews that will drive corrective actions and continuous improvement.

**Quitterie Cotten**, who offers 28 years of wastewater experience, will help Keoki implement this program. See **_Section II – Statement on Staffing, Training, and Corporate Culture_** for more information on our Program Management team structure.

A critical PgM outcome is **shared-cost transparency and reporting**. Our framework establishes **complete, auditable shared-cost controls** for energy, chemicals, fuel, and biosolids handling—linking consumption and performance drivers directly to invoicing, inventory, and operational conditions. The system will **automatically flag KPI deviations**, maintain a **shared corrective-action log visible to [CLIENT]**, and trigger **immediate notifications for any condition that could impact permit compliance**, followed by written corrective summaries. PgM-led reporting will include **daily operations logs**, **monthly performance and reconciled shared-cost reports**, **quarterly and annual committee-ready summaries**, and an **Annual Performance and Optimization Report.**

**These reports are** generated from validated datasets and accessible to [CLIENT] through secure dashboards to support collaborative oversight, defensible cost allocation, and sustained performance improvement. **_Section 2.6 Monitoring, Tracking, and Reporting Operational Parameters_** provides a detailed discussion of our approach to delivering these tools.

We'll work hard to foster day-to-day communication between [CLIENT] and Jacobs staff to empower decision making on all levels and ensure immediate attention and response. Project Manager Kevin Dahl and the entire team will promote the flow of information and transparency. In addition to frequent informal daily communications, our PgM approach will include formal weekly, monthly, and quarterly meetings to review plant performance and maintenance activities in addition to the formal Governance Committees' meetings. Kevin will also ensure effective and transparent communication with the Commission and at public meetings as requested and directed by [CLIENT]. Our Executive Sponsors will support Kevin with these important communications as necessary. We provide information on how we'll communicate with the community and ratepayers in **_Section 2.2.6 Odor Control and Responding to Odor Complaints_** and in **_Section V, Regional Partnerships._ Exhibit IV-9** outlines communications with [CLIENT].

## Reuse guidance

Universal: "one validated, single source of truth" built by integrating named systems; auditable shared-cost controls tied to invoicing and inventory; automatic KPI flagging; a corrective-action log the client can see; and immediate notification of any condition that could affect permit compliance. The reporting ladder (daily logs → monthly reconciled reports → quarterly/annual committee-ready summaries → Annual Performance and Optimization Report) is reusable verbatim.

Pursuit-specific: system names, the performance-standards exhibit reference, named personnel and their experience, the governing board's name, and any cross-references.
