# Digital Tools for More Sustainable and Cost-Efficient Operations Table (Exhibit IV-30)

| **Digital Tool** | **How is it Implemented** | **Example Benefits for [CLIENT]** |
|---|---|---|
| **Intelligent O&M**<br>Advanced AI and Data Analytics Digital Tool | Turns [CLIENT]'s historical SCADA and laboratory data into predictive, operator-ready recommendations that reduce energy and chemical use while maintaining compliance and process stability. | Supports more efficient day-to-day operations and helps achieve sustainability and cost-reduction goals. At facilities managed by Jacobs, Intelligent O&M has contributed to a 10–30% reduction in power and chemical consumption. |
| **AquaDNA**<br>Smart technology and sensors for collection systems and pump stations for real-time system performance | Integrates real-time data from [CLIENT] sensors, gauges, and weather inputs into a machine-learning platform.<br>Detects and alerts CSO crew of blockages, I/I anomalies, and CSO risk conditions and visualizes conveyance, storage, and pump station performance for operator response. | Strengthens deep tunnel storage readiness and extends the useful capacity of the tunnel.<br>Prevents dry- and wet-weather overflows through early detection of pump or pipe anomalies. Improves pump station reliability and reduces emergency callouts.<br>Reduces manual field inspections and increases staff safety. |
| **AquaDNA Deragger**<br>Automated clearing of pump ragging | Installs smart algorithms that detect rag buildup and automatically clears pump impellers.<br>Provides performance monitoring and alerts through SCADA. | Reduces pump station failures and storage bypass risks.<br>Lowers maintenance labor and emergency callouts.<br>Improves reliability at historically high-ragging locations across [CLIENT]'s network. |
| **Digital Twin**<br>Whole plant process and hydraulic model connected to/from actual SCADA data system | Real-time model of [CLIENT]'s entire system (storage, conveyance, water reclamation facilities). Integrating SCADA, weather forecasts, flows data, and process models to quickly troubleshoot hydraulics and process issues/changes.<br>Simulates storage strategies, flow distributions, solids routing, and wet-weather responses. | Provides operators with a deeper understanding of the system and opportunities to test operating scenarios and controls.<br>Improves tunnel dewatering strategies to minimize CSOs and basement backups and enhances readiness for large storms and operational shocks. |
| **Hybrid Optimizer**<br>Chemical and energy optimization engine | Use the calibrated models together to optimize chemicals/energy and operations with real-time operating data.<br>Continuously recommends aeration, chemical, and solids strategies.<br>Provides operator dashboards to validate all recommended adjustments. | Advises operators on flow setpoints for WAS, digester feed, centrifuge feed, etc.<br>Lowers aeration energy at both water reclamation facilities, reducing shared-cost power bills.<br>Decreases disinfectant, bisulfite, ferric, and RAS chlorination chemical usage.<br>Improves forecast accuracy for high-load or wet-weather periods. |
| **WATS Model**<br>Sewer process, odor and corrosion modeling | Sewer-process model simulates biological, chemical, and physical transformations in [CLIENT]'s conveyance system.<br>Integrates [CLIENT] odor complaints, H₂S data, corrosion records, and hydraulics to identify high-risk segments and evaluates impacts of flow routing, pumping changes, and industrial contributions. | Reduces odor complaints and corrosion at known [CLIENT] hotspots.<br>Prioritizes targeted, cost-effective odor and corrosion mitigation investments.<br>Provides early insight into upstream impacts on both water reclamation facilities' performance.<br>Supports seasonal optimization of phosphorus removal for both facilities. |
| **Dragonfly and Argon**<br>CCTV defect coding and asset rehab planning tools | Dragonfly improves quality and cost-effectiveness of pipeline defect coding, helping optimize repair and replacement effort and with more consistent, objective data.<br>Argon supports more effective conveyance maintenance and can be used as a CIP support tool for linear assets. | Advanced data analysis and reporting provides insights into rehabilitation, repair, reinspection, defects, next steps, and cost estimates.<br>Approximately 30–40% cost savings compared to manual defect coding. |
