# Compliance Execution: Tracking, Reporting, and Dashboards

Jacobs will maintain a transparent and auditable system that consolidates operational, laboratory, and maintenance data into one defensible record for [CLIENT], a regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities. Using **Hach WIMS** integrated with [CLIENT]'s **SCADA** and **CMMS** platforms, compliance data will be validated daily, trended monthly, and summarized quarterly for joint review.

Dashboards will visualize permit performance, approaching limits, and parameter trends while linking results to process or maintenance events. Every report, including discharge monitoring reports, compliance maintenance annual reports, pretreatment program summaries, and air-permit documentation, will be prepared in [CLIENT]'s established formats and delivered 100% on time and in compliance.

All calibration records, chain-of-custody forms, and documentation will be managed through a secure, SharePoint-based electronic document management system accessible to [CLIENT] and Jacobs staff.

**Exhibit IV-39. Web-based compliance dashboard** (asset `209_007CAM_1`) — *A web-based compliance dashboard will monitor and track operating parameters and regulatory deadlines, ensuring performance requirements are maintained.*

## QA/QC and laboratory integration

Jacobs will ensure that field sampling, preservation, and chain-of-custody procedures meet certification standards. Calibration records will be automatically uploaded to WIMS, and sampling integrity will be verified through audits and proficiency testing. Our QA/QC approach follows The NELAC Institute standards and includes quarterly internal audits and an annual third-party review.

Jacobs will coordinate closely with [CLIENT]'s certified laboratories to align analytical methods, verify data quality, and perform joint internal audits. This collaboration will ensure seamless QA/QC integration from field collection through final reporting, maintaining data reliability and regulatory defensibility.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the daily-monthly-quarterly validation rhythm, the promise to report in the client's existing formats rather than the operator's, and the audit stack (quarterly internal, annual third-party, joint audits with the client's labs). Independent third-party review is the element that converts a data-integrity claim into something an evaluator can verify.

Pursuit-specific: the platform names, the specific report types the permit requires, and whether the client has its own certified laboratory to coordinate with. Where the client runs the lab and Jacobs does not, foreground the joint-audit and method-alignment language; where Jacobs runs the lab, expand the certification and proficiency-testing detail instead.
