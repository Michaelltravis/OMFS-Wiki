# Value-Added Innovations Menu for O&M Contracts

A menu of value-added innovations can be included within an O&M base fee (i.e., at no additional cost to the client over the contract term) to differentiate a proposal and demonstrate ongoing investment beyond routine operations:

- **Discounted engineering rates [up to X% savings compared to standard rates].** Out-of-scope engineering and consulting support offered at a discounted rate multiplier, letting the client tap the firm's broader technical bench (focused studies, grant support, and more) at reduced cost compared to standard/market rates. (Commercial pricing — the specific multiplier and resulting discount percentage are excluded from this wiki and must be repriced fresh for each pursuit.)
- **Annual innovation workshop(s) — $350,000 full-term value in the source pursuit (10-year term).** A recurring client/contractor workshop convening targeted subject-matter experts (process, biosolids, collections, odor, energy, digital tools, capital planning) to co-develop a focused agenda tied to current performance, recurring issues, and the client's next-step O&M and CIP priorities — keeping innovation proactive rather than reactive or ad hoc.
- **Regional support hours — $4,450,000 full-term value in the source pursuit (10-year term).** A defined block of supplemental hours (e.g., ~2,000 hours/year over the contract term) from the firm's regional bench of O&M resources and technical subject-matter experts, enhancing the on-site team's capacity to solve operational issues, optimize processes, and plan for the future.
- **Systemwide odor optimization — $100,000 full-term value in the source pursuit (10-year term).** Build and calibrate a sewer/collection-system process model using hydraulic model data plus targeted wastewater/H2S sampling; use the model to evaluate chemical dosing strategies (e.g., bioxide, in-pipe treatment technology, wet-well mixing technology) and complete an odor-control alternatives evaluation for the collection system and treatment facility (including scrubber review, conceptual layouts, cost estimates, and a technical memorandum). Reduces trial-and-error chemical dosing, gives a defensible basis for odor/corrosion-control cost decisions, and creates a systemwide roadmap for sequencing near-term changes and longer-term odor infrastructure investment.
- **SmartCovers and digital collections analytics — $60,000 full-term value in the source pursuit (10-year term).** Deploy relocatable smart manhole-cover sensors to pinpoint inflow/infiltration (I/I) and system surcharge; these can also be paired with hydrogen-sulfide sensors to pinpoint odor hotspots faster than traditional field investigations alone — providing more targeted data for rehabilitation, cleaning, and chemical-dosing decisions with less trial-and-error field work.
- **On-site jetter for rapid response — $80,000 full-term value in the source pursuit (10-year term).** Lease and house a jetter truck/unit on site, available for urgent blockages, hotspot cleaning, and other time-sensitive collection-system needs — improving response speed and operational flexibility without requiring the client to buy and maintain highly specialized equipment.

**Source-pursuit total:** these five value-added items plus the discounted engineering rate totaled $5,040,000 in value at no additional cost over the 10-year contract term (rounded to "$5 million" in the proposal's executive-summary graphic).

**Innovation as a pairing of routine execution with proactive improvement.** The value-added menu is best framed as more than routine O&M: disciplined day-to-day execution is paired with practical, proven innovation that helps improve reliability, reduce nuisance conditions, and support better long-term investment decisions. Structured innovation workshops create direct connectivity between on-site O&M leaders and regional/national subject-matter experts, and a strong link between field operations and capital planning turns recurring issues and operating data into actionable improvements rather than isolated fixes.

**Additional facility-specific improvement studies.** Beyond the standing value-added menu, targeted studies can be offered (typically delivered through the Annual Innovation Workshop or at Discounted Engineering Rates) to address a facility's specific improvement opportunities:

- **Incinerator (or other legacy process unit) options study.** Evaluate practical options for an existing, underused, or obsolete process area — including repurposing, removal, or alternative operational uses — to provide a clearer basis for future space, solids, and asset planning decisions.
- **Standby power and resiliency roadmap.** Evaluate existing standby power arrangements, generator readiness, and storm-related vulnerabilities, and compare practical resiliency improvements that reduce risk and improve continuity of service.
- **Targeted advanced predictive maintenance.** Identify selected critical rotating and electrical assets that warrant advanced predictive monitoring beyond the base preventive-maintenance program, and apply those tools only where they provide the greatest benefit.

## Reuse guidance

Select 3–5 items most relevant to the client's stated priorities (e.g., lead with systemwide odor optimization for a client with known odor complaints; lead with SmartCovers/digital analytics for a client focused on I/I reduction). The dollar figures above are the real, worked example from the source pursuit (kept as outcome/value figures under current sanitization policy) — useful for showing how a value-add bundle is priced and totaled, but develop fresh pricing/valuation for each new pursuit rather than carrying these numbers forward; do not imply a specific total value for a new contract without doing that math fresh. The discounted-engineering-rate multiplier and resulting discount percentage are true commercial pricing and are excluded here regardless of pursuit. The "additional facility-specific improvement studies" list is illustrative — swap in studies relevant to the target facility's actual legacy/obsolete assets, storm exposure, and critical-asset inventory rather than reusing the incinerator/standby-power/predictive-maintenance trio verbatim if they don't apply. Pairs well with the energy-management block (odor optimization modeling parallels the energy/chemical optimization approach), the CMMS/asset-management block (SmartCovers data feeds the same CMMS-driven decision-making; the standby-power and predictive-maintenance studies extend that block's asset-planning narrative), and `../win-themes/value-added-no-cost-enhancements-framing.md` (the "bundled value" sales technique for presenting this same menu).
