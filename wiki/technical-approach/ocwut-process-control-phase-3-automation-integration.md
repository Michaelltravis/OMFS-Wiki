# Phase 3 — Process Automation Integration and Demand-Responsive Aeration Control

In Phase 3, we'll embed resilient, repeatable automation into daily operations. Using the validated process models, refined control narratives, and qualified data foundation from Phases 1 and 2 will result in stronger permit compliance, better asset reliability, improved resource efficiency, and reduced operator task saturation as outlined in **Exhibit 1-24**. Our site visits showed all facilities running over-aerated, with air production exceeding true process demand — wasted blower runtime, unstable DO profiles, and higher energy cost. Phase 3 will deploy flow- and demand-responsive aeration control: accurate air flow measurement, valve and air distribution control, and continuous DO monitoring that make blower output automatically track real-time treatment needs. The goal is to reduce instances of overproduction and over-aeration and support better kWh/MG performance, lower cost, and more stable biology — directly supporting the RFP's energy management expectations.
