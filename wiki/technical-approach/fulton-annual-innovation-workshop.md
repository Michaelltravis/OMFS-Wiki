# JV Annual Innovation Workshop

JC Solutions' Annual Innovation Workshops, provided at no cost to [CLIENT], will bring together [CLIENT] staff, stakeholders, our O&M staff, and Jacobs utility experts. We will review the prior year's accomplishments and establish new goals and innovations that address [CLIENT]'s evolving needs and long-term vision. This annual meeting ensures that the team continuously looks forward to future needs and trends, helps [CLIENT] remain at the forefront of treatment technology and O&M practices, and eliminates complacency over time.

**$250,000 worth of investment by JC Solutions.**

## Reuse guidance

Use as a short value-added innovation commitment alongside the annual reporting cadence. Build the agenda from the new client's needs and omit any source-specific pricing or investment language. Pair with a detailed communications and reporting block where the workshop is part of annual governance.
