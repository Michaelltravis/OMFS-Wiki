# Operations Approach Built on Six Operational Priorities

Our approach to successful operations is built around what matters most to [CLIENT], a regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities and an extensive conveyance system: safe, compliant, cost-efficient, and reliable service—every day and in every condition. We'll go beyond routine operation to continuously optimize performance, regulatory outcomes, and chemical and energy use. Using real-time dashboards and Intelligent O&M tools, we'll give operators and [CLIENT] clear visibility into system conditions and performance drivers, with shared buy-in on how we act on the data. Just as important, we'll maintain clear communication and operating protocols between the water reclamation facilities (WRFs) and the collections team to manage wet-weather events smoothly and effectively—while protecting [CLIENT]'s reputation through environmental stewardship, responsive communication, odor control, and sustainable operations.

Drawing on decades of wastewater operations experience and deep familiarity with [CLIENT]'s interconnected system—from collection system and inline storage system (ISS) dynamics to wastewater treatment and biosolids product production—we'll deliver operating strategies crafted for each facility and that also strengthen performance system-wide. Our strategies directly support [CLIENT]'s priorities outlined in **Exhibit IV-10** and integrate process control, proactive maintenance, asset management, QA/QC, systematic sampling, real-time capacity management, odor control, and targeted energy and chemical optimization. We'll prioritize the system areas needing improvement, identified earlier in **Exhibit IV-4**, and our extensive offsite support resources will routinely audit the project and provide the onsite team with lessons learned, including those from our broader O&M work.

Facility-specific SOPs will document the O&M plan for consistent, safe execution. Equally important, we'll work side-by-side with operators, trades, and support staff to understand day-to-day challenges, provide practical tools and training, and clearly communicate the "why" behind each task to build accountability, alignment, and shared ownership of performance outcomes. In the seven subsections below, listed in **Exhibit IV-11**, we describe how we'll operate [CLIENT]'s facilities and elevate operations across the entire system.
