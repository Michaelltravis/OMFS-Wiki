# Corrective Maintenance Program — Breaking the Failure Chain

**Disciplined corrective maintenance isn't just about fixing what's broken; it's about preventing cascading consequences and building the data that makes the entire program smarter over time.**

At North Canadian, we've documented a pattern that illustrates why this matters: grit system downtime leads to primary clarifier bypass, which sends grit into aeration basins, accelerates wear on clarifier drive mechanisms and downstream pumping equipment, and creates conditions for compliance events. Reporting data shows this cycle repeating nearly every month. That pattern is the direct result of reactive maintenance, and it's exactly the kind of failure chain a structured corrective program breaks by addressing root causes, not just symptoms.

**Every corrective maintenance event will be tracked in NexGen with full documentation: failure mode, root cause, and operational impact recorded on every work order.** We'll apply Failure Mode and Effects Analysis to systematically eliminate repeat failures at the root cause. Each corrective event becomes an input to improve the overall maintenance program by adjusting PM frequencies, targeting PdM investments, or escalating chronic issues to capital planning. The result: corrective maintenance runs as a controlled process, not a reactive response. Our response and closure discipline is aligned with Schedule 5 KPIs: corrective work orders opened within 5 days of deficiency identification (CM-1), 100% of corrective work orders completed and closed within 60 days (CM-2), and Critical Assets returned to service within 2 days of failure (CM-3).

Facility-specific corrective maintenance priorities identified through our due diligence at [CLIENT] — a southcentral US municipal water utility trust operating four wastewater treatment plants and a major pump station:

- **North Canadian:** Grit system reliability, RAS pump availability (Phase 1 screw pump currently OOS), blower maintenance and condition monitoring following recent failure incident, H₂S-related equipment degradation across headworks, solids handling, and HVAC systems
- **Deer Creek:** Blower availability maintenance pending 2027 CIP, secondary clarifier mechanism issues, sand filter blower rehabilitation, algae management on secondary clarifiers
- **Chisholm Creek:** Bar screen reliability program, grit system evaluation and coordination with [CLIENT] to resolve design issues
- **South Canadian:** Maintaining SBR operability through CIP transition, commissioning support for new oxidation ditch equipment
- **System-wide:** Odor control equipment restoration, overhead crane inspections, equipment shaft guard replacements, generator reliability
