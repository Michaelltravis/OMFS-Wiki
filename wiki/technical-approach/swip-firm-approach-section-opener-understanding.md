# Firm Approach Section Opener — Integrated O&M Commitment and Facility Understanding

Jacobs will deliver an integrated, transparent, and results-driven project management approach that ensures 24/7 compliance, reliable production of advanced treated water, and seamless coordination across [CLIENT]'s facilities. With decades of experience operating facilities comparable in scale and technology — including membrane bioreactor (MBR), reverse osmosis (RO), UV advanced oxidation process (UV-AOP) systems, and large pump stations — Jacobs is uniquely equipped to manage the full scope of operations in accordance with the applicable state board orders (in this pursuit, State Board Orders R4-2021-0044 and R4-2023-0366). As one of the largest O&M services providers in North America, Jacobs will leverage the depth of its operations, engineering, asset management, maintenance, and compliance capabilities to meet [CLIENT]'s performance standards while driving long-term value and sustainability. Our team will integrate with [CLIENT] staff to uphold a community-first culture rooted in accountability, safety, and regulatory excellence.

## Jacobs understands [CLIENT]'s challenges and goals for its facilities

[CLIENT]'s sustainable water infrastructure program represents a significant advancement in water treatment and establishes [CLIENT] as a pioneer in water reuse. The 1.0-MGD advanced water treatment facility (AWTF) is the first of its kind to successfully treat both wastewater and stormwater, the first ever to receive log removal credits for pathogen reduction using MBR, and the first to do all of this in an underground facility.

The program plays a critical role in reducing [CLIENT]'s reliance on imported water supplies and promotes sustainability through the injection of recycled water into the local aquifer as a barrier to seawater intrusion and for aquifer replenishment. In addition, [CLIENT] is in a great position to be the first direct potable reuse (DPR) provider in the country — an amazing aspiration and one the full Jacobs team would love to help [CLIENT] achieve.

In preparation for this proposal, our team talked with your staff, thoroughly reviewed the RFP, scope of work, draft agreement and exhibits, and spent several days in the field visiting the facilities conducting due diligence, which has provided a deep understanding of the challenges and opportunities to innovate and improve the O&M of the facilities.

The facility-by-facility matrix that follows provides a concise summary of our understanding of your goals, challenges, and the resources and strategies Jacobs can bring to ensure we meet and exceed your expectations for exceptional O&M services. Across all sites, Jacobs recognizes the need to operate to [CLIENT]'s permits and KPI framework to reliably produce Product Water for non-potable and groundwater replenishment reuse project (GRRP) uses, implement CMMS-based asset management, and integrate alarms via the site SCADA/HMI platform (in this pursuit, Ignition), per the Scope of Services.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the four-move opener — (1) integrated/transparent/results-driven commitment naming 24/7 compliance and the technology stack, (2) recognition of what makes the client's program distinctive, (3) the sustainability/water-supply consequence of that program, and (4) proof of due diligence (staff conversations, RFP and draft-agreement review, several days of field visits) — is reusable for any O&M pursuit and is especially effective when displacing an incumbent, because it demonstrates site knowledge without a contract.

Pursuit-specific: the "first of its kind," "first to receive log removal credits using MBR," and "first underground facility" claims and the 1.0-MGD capacity belong to this client's facility; replace with the target client's own distinctions. The State Board order numbers, the Product Water/GRRP terminology, and the Ignition SCADA platform are pursuit-specific.
