# Owner-Governed SCADA/OT Support Approach with Incumbent System Knowledge

## Collaborative Automation and OT Support Approach Aligned with [CLIENT] Governance

**Jacobs' OT approach for [CLIENT], a regional municipal water utility trust operating four wastewater treatment plants and a major pump station, is grounded in a clear respect for owner control, operational transparency, and disciplined collaboration.** We fully understand [CLIENT] owns and governs the SCADA, IT, and cybersecurity infrastructure. As outlined in **Exhibit 1-23**, we will operate decisively within that framework as a trusted partner focused on field-level OT assets and seamless integration with your enterprise systems per Service Agreement 8.16 and Schedule 17.

## Jacobs Understands Your SCADA/OT Environment and Cybersecurity Expectations from Experience

**For more than a decade, Jacobs has supported [CLIENT]'s SCADA and OT environment.** We developed your SCADA Master Plan, built and maintain your SCADA Standards, completed your SCADA Vulnerability Assessment and 2022 Cybersecurity Risk and Resilience Assessment, and provide ongoing Wonderware/AVEVA application support, including direct coordination with Q-mation and AVEVA, to keep your Galaxy architecture running. We perform all your HMI programming today, and the same engineers and technicians who built that institutional knowledge will be on call for the O&M team from day one. They'll provide training and seamless integration into [CLIENT]'s existing system and **give you an operations partner that already knows your system inside and out.**

## SCADA, OT, and Cybersecurity Expertise at [CLIENT]'s Direction

SCADA, OT, and cybersecurity remain firmly under [CLIENT]'s governance, with Jacobs maintaining field-level assets at your direction. On-site I&C technicians handle day-to-day work, backed by on-call experts for troubleshooting, AVEVA/Wonderware support, capital projects, and any other needs [CLIENT] identifies.
