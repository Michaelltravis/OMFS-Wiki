# Chemical Usage Monitoring, Dosing Optimization, and Cost Allocation

Jacobs will track **chemical feed and consumption** to support efficient operations and accurate cost allocation for [CLIENT], a regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities. Automated metering, CMMS integration, and the Intelligent O&M chemical optimization module will record real-time dosing rates, flow-proportional usage, and cumulative consumption by process and by day. Deliveries and inventory will be reconciled against meter data and purchase records.

The system will continuously refine dosing strategies for hypochlorite, ferric chloride, polymer, bisulfite, and other key chemicals to maintain process stability and minimize waste. Monthly reports will document usage, cost, and correlation to process conditions, with analytics identifying opportunities for chemical-use reductions of 10 to 30% without affecting permit compliance.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the four-part mechanic — measure at the meter, integrate with CMMS, reconcile against deliveries and invoices, then report usage against process conditions monthly. Reconciliation against purchase records is the sentence that separates this from a generic optimization claim, because it tells the client the numbers in the monthly report can be audited.

Pursuit-specific: the chemical list and the savings range. Where the client shares chemical cost under a gainshare or shared-cost mechanism, say so explicitly and link the reduction target to the client's share of the benefit. Where chemical cost is wholly the operator's, present the reduction as a process-stability outcome rather than a savings claim.
