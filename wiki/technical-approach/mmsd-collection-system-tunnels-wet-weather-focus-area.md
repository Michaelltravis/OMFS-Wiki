# Collection System, Tunnels, and Wet Weather Flow Management — Focus Area Overview

[CLIENT]'s collection system, which conveys flows from separate and combined sewer systems, must perform under all conditions, from dry weather or routine storms to extreme wet weather and floods. [CLIENT] has long been a national leader in wet-weather management, starting with its inline storage system (ISS) and its pioneering use of deep tunnel storage. With nearly 30 entry points from the metropolitan interceptor system (MIS) into the ISS, operating the ISS before, during, and after a storm event is both complex and mission critical. Those operating decisions also need to be closely coordinated with [CLIENT] management.

Jacobs understands [CLIENT]'s goals of zero overflows by 2035, reduced basement backups, and lower energy use. Our approach supports those outcomes by strengthening the fundamentals: reviewing and refining SOPs and wet-weather procedures, improving tunnel dewatering protocols, and using rainfall and flow data to optimize decision-making. In dry weather, we'll focus on readiness through predictive maintenance (PdM) while minimizing unnecessary pumping and resulting energy use. In wet weather, we'll use rainfall patterns, forecast data, and real-time system conditions to balance available tunnel storage and WRF capacity.

As storms become more intense, such as the August 2025 1,000-year event, we'll prioritize proactive preparation, reliable forecasting, and resilient operations to protect customers and waterways. Jacobs will operate the ISS as a dynamic storage and conveyance system, actively managing capacity ahead of rainfall, throughout the event, and during recovery. As [CLIENT] integrates Storm Harvester, we'll refine forecasting tools and decision protocols to improve predictions and strengthen post-event analysis, drawing on our machine-learning experience at facilities like AlexRenew (Virginia) and Wilmington STP (Delaware). We'll manage the collection system and WRFs as one connected system ensuring coordinated decision-making with [CLIENT] management and optimized tunnel dewatering.
