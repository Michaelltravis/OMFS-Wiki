# JV Asset Management System and Technical Support

JC Solutions recognizes that [CLIENT], a county wastewater utility, seeks a proactive partner to maintain its facilities in a manner that ensures reliability, extends asset longevity, and protects its infrastructure investments. We are committed to preserving [CLIENT]’s capital investments—especially with new facilities coming online soon. We will develop an Asset Management System (AMS), initiating development at Transition, and it will include the required Long-term Maintenance Plan and Field Performance Testing Plan, developed in an integrated process to maximize the value of these plans. Our AMS will provide a comprehensive asset management strategy that applies preventive maintenance (PM) and predictive maintenance (PdM) with data-driven decision-making.

An important aspect about Jacobs is how tightly each project and each individual is networked into our internal operations. No project exists on an island, regardless of how large or how small, and that’s especially true of our asset management and maintenance teams. This is a big reason for our success—the interconnections and relationships we nurture drive job satisfaction, project performance, and in the end—client satisfaction, one of our most important metrics.

Exhibit 2-38. *Integrate Maintenance and Asset Management Support Provides Robust Resources for the Onsite Maintenance Team to Excel.*

Jacobs has successfully implemented more than 100 asset management systems in the US and worldwide. In fact, we literally wrote the book on water utility asset management! Jacobs subject matter experts have authored several industry publications at the forefront of Asset Management, including McGraw Hill’s *Water Infrastructure Asset Management: Adopting Best Practices to Enable Better Investments* and WEF’s *Implementing Asset Management: A Practical Guide.*

**Unique to JC Solutions: O&M Asset Management and Maintenance Technical Support Team.** Maintenance Manager Nathan Dahl and the onsite Maintenance team will be supported by robust resources focused on asset management and maintenance, as illustrated in Exhibit 2-38. These individuals will provide the local team the following programs and associated resources when needed:

1. Knowledge Management Program: Led by CMMS implementation experts to ensure accurate and accessible data across all asset management activities. Focuses on robust data governance, CMMS enhancement, and continuous staff training—embedding knowledge-sharing and data-driven decision-making into the organizational culture to drive operational excellence and consistency.

2. Predictive Maintenance (PdM) Program: Uses advanced condition monitoring technologies to prevent asset failures and enhance reliability including tools like vibration analysis, oil testing, and thermographic inspections. Reduces downtime and optimizes maintenance schedules, promoting a proactive approach to asset health.

3. Integration Program: Ensures smooth coordination between onsite teams and offsite technical support. Aligns asset management activities with strategic goals, connects asset management initiatives, and facilitates adopting new tools and processes to drive innovation and improvement.

4. Implementation Program: Delivers tactical expertise to translate strategy into operational success. Develops asset management plans, conducts condition assessments, and performs lifecycle cost analyses, ensuring asset management system (AMS) implementation aligns with ISO 55001 and industry best practices.

These four programs, guided by Graham Knowles, form the backbone of Jacobs’ O&M Asset Management team. Together, they deliver a structured, integrated approach that will not only meet [CLIENT]’s O&M needs but also ensure the organization’s asset management maturity continues to evolve toward world-class standards.
