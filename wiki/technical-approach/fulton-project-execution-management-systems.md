# Project Execution Through Integrated Management Systems

Our approach to project execution and administration is built on decades of delivering compliant, cost-effective O&M services for facilities similar in size and complexity—including deep expertise with membrane bioreactor (MBR) technology. As North America’s second-largest O&M service provider, Jacobs uniquely integrates operations, engineering, consulting, program management, and construction services, enabling us to deliver significant value across every phase of [CLIENT]’s facilities’ lifecycle. Beyond technical proficiency, we bring a partnership mindset grounded in accountability, transparency, and a community-focused, people-first culture.

[CLIENT] can confidently rely on JC Solutions for 100% compliance as we drive operational excellence, optimized performance, reduced risk, and sustained asset reliability well into the future. We’ve developed structured frameworks, processes, and a comprehensive suite of tools to plan, execute, monitor, and continually improve our operations.

Our health, safety and security framework supports compliance with Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) and other safety regulations, vulnerability assessments, job safety analysis, safety training, emergency preparedness and response, and other safety related functions. Our compliance and reporting program supports pollution prevention and waste minimization; it includes analysis and review checklists for all permits, report preparation for regulatory agencies, and environmental compliance training for all employees. Operations management documents treatment strategies and process control functions through standard operating procedures (SOPs), unit process control procedures (UPCPs), and treatment system optimization.

Maintenance and asset management integrates maintenance scheduling, documentation, and tracking; inventory control; and equipment condition assessment into a computerized database that allows us to maximize equipment performance and minimize downtime. Laboratory management uses standard procedures for sample collection, analysis, and documentation with our quality assurance/quality control (QA/QC) and chemical hygiene plans. Our human-resources program supports the success of all employees as individuals and as a team, including recruitment, orientation, and training; standard training needs assessments for all employees; training programs for certification, ethics, compliance, safety, management and succession; and performance appraisals and professional development.

Financial management uses systems and approaches to manage project cost through national purchasing power, documentation of contractual requirements, and continuous monitoring. Project management provides transparent communication including daily, weekly, monthly, severe weather, and annual client reports, internal measures of project performance, and plans to improve project performance beyond the requirements of our contract. A robust public education and community involvement program is developed in partnership with [CLIENT], including volunteering, charitable donations, and facility tours to benefit the community. Customer service demonstrates our commitment to be a good neighbor and responsiveness to community concerns.
