# Two Years of Due Diligence and an Approach Aligned to the Client's Own Values

Over the past 2 years, our team, including subject matter experts (SMEs) in O&M, wastewater and solids treatment, conveyance, asset management, system optimization, safety, and security has met with [CLIENT] staff—[CLIENT] being a large regional sewerage district operating two water reclamation facilities and a regional conveyance system—evaluated your facilities, and carefully reviewed all data provided. Our priorities align with [CLIENT]'s, and our operating approach is built to deliver safe, compliant, and reliable service. With strong emphasis on maintenance, we'll maximize the lifespan of [CLIENT]'s assets while optimizing long-term costs. **Exhibit IV-4** provides a high-level assessment of [CLIENT]'s facilities, summary of key findings, and our proposed value-added solutions to strengthen overall system performance.

**We understand your operations, environmental stewardship priorities, regulatory mandates, and long-term vision. Through discussions and more than 16,000 hours of due diligence, we assessed how best to meet your needs. Drawing on both regional and national expertise, our tailored approach delivers innovative, cost-effective operational strategies that enhance performance and align with your goals.**

Aligning our approach with shared values ensures that every step of our partnership reflects not just what we do, but who we are, together. With the One Team, One Water, One Vision approach illustrated in **Exhibit IV-3**, we align with your strategic objectives below:

- Commitment to cost-effective and compliant operations and management of [CLIENT] facilities
- Proactively manage assets to extend the life of [CLIENT]'s facilities
- Stay ahead of regulatory pressures and emerging contaminants
- Adopt climate-resilient and energy-efficient strategies
- Leadership in water quality innovation through engagement of industry leaders
- Integrated watershed management
- Regional partnerships and investments that benefit the community
- Partner with an O&M contractor with shared values

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-beat structure—what we did to learn your system, the hours it took, then your objectives restated as ours—and the "not just what we do, but who we are, together" line that turns a values list into a selection criterion. Pursuit-specific: the engagement period, the hour count, the SME disciplines listed, and the objective bullets.
