# PM/PdM as Front-End Investment — Evidence, Dashboards, and Material Readiness

Our approach starts with safety and regulatory compliance — they set the maintenance standard, priorities, and urgency. We won't "wait for thresholds" or visible deterioration to justify preventive work; we'll plan and execute PM/PdM as a disciplined, front-end investment to prevent failures, avoid unplanned outages, and reduce the likelihood of compliance-impacting events.

We'll document every work order with field evidence (photos, parts usage, meter readings), supervisor verification, and timely closeout. Real-time dashboards — shared with [CLIENT], a regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities — show PM compliance, CM backlog, labor, cost, and asset health, so no work goes unverified and no trend goes unseen. In addition, our team is experienced in tracking and reporting data in NEXGEN and supporting CMMS configuration improvements, both of which will be core to the maintenance program.

Finally, we'll proactively remove a known execution barrier raised by labor — parts availability — by strengthening material readiness (critical spares, min/max levels, kitting/staging, and tighter integration between planning and storeroom/procurement) so planned maintenance does not slip into reactive work.
