# Odor Control Baseline and Routine Monitoring

JC Solutions recognizes the importance of odor control at the [CLIENT] facilities, especially given the residential, recreational, and commercial nature of adjacent areas. Significant investments in odor control have been made at all three sites, and particularly at [FACILITY A], a WRF, and [FACILITY B], an environmental campus, which cover most, if not all, processes to capture and treat odors from plant operations. This approach, however, is not bulletproof. Although Monthly Operating Reports show no odor complaints in 2024, the conditions for which the existing odor control facilities were designed may have changed (e.g., odor characteristics, meteorological conditions), and as such, opportunities may exist to improve the performance of existing systems. As part of our proactive approach to O&M, we are including a value-add to study odor mitigation strategies to ascertain effective odor mitigation.

JC Solutions will routinely monitor the plants for odors including the presence of hydrogen sulfide (H2S) and nuisance odors using a portable meter. Performance of the existing odor control facilities will also be monitored. Operators will make routine patrols of the plant and the boundary using both the portable meters as well as their own senses to determine if H2S or other odor conditions are detectable. Once per shift, JC Solutions will make an off-site patrol to specified potential odor source locations in neighboring vicinities during the known high odor season using a portable H2S meter. Any detection of abnormal odor or H2S readings will be cause for JC Solutions to implement a corrective action plan. Written guidelines will be prepared for operators to follow if any of these elements are detected. JC Solutions will record each plant patrol finding, including any measured readings of noise, H2S, or detection of odor on an Odor Inspection Log. JC Solutions will sign the log for each patrol indicating time of patrol, weather conditions, wind speed, and direction.

## Reuse guidance

Apply only with the target facility’s existing odor-control system, boundary patrol locations, seasonal conditions, and complaint history validated. Preserve the JV identity in delivery commitments.
