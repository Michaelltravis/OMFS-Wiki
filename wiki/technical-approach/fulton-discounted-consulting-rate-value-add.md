# Discounted Rate for Engineering Services Provided Under the O&M Contract

We are proud to offer a discounted rate structure for Jacobs consulting services as part of this Wastewater Facilities O&M contract at the [CLIENT] facilities. This discounted rate structure would apply to sole-source consulting services connected to our wastewater O&M activities at [CLIENT] only and would not apply to any competitive pursuits. The discounted consulting rate structure (**Exhibit 2-6**) would last until the end of the 5-year O&M contract term and will be escalated by 2% for each fiscal year. An example of how the discounted rate could be applied includes engineering studies derived from our Annual Innovation Workshop. As an example of the potential savings, if the discounted rates shown in **Exhibit 2-6** were applied to a project typically priced at $500,000 for our engineering services, [CLIENT] could save almost 7.4% on average, which would equate to approximately **$37,000 in savings**.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the structure of the offer — (1) name the discount and tie it to this contract, (2) fence its scope to sole-source work connected to the O&M contract so it cannot be read as a blanket rate concession, (3) state the term and escalation, (4) name a concrete channel where the client would actually spend it, and (5) close with a worked example that turns a percentage into a dollar figure. That five-move pattern transfers to any pursuit where the operating entity can draw on an affiliated engineering practice.

Pursuit-specific: the exhibit number, the rate sheet it points to, the baseline it is discounted against (here, the client's current engineering MSA rates), the term, the escalation percentage, and every figure in the worked example. Do not carry the illustrative $500,000 / 7.4% / $37,000 triplet into another pursuit — rebuild it from that pursuit's own rate comparison.

Commercial note: the underlying hourly rate table on the same source page (Exhibit 2-6, DWR # 1–14) is commercial rate content and is deliberately not held in the wiki. Writers needing the actual rate structure go to the pricing lead, not to this block.
