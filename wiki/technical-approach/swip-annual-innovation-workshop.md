# Annual Innovation Workshop — No-Cost Continuous Improvement Program

Our Annual Innovation Workshops, provided at no cost to [CLIENT], a Southern California municipal owner of advanced water treatment, water recycling, and stormwater facilities, will bring together [CLIENT] staff, stakeholders, our O&M staff, plus our utility experts. We will review the prior year's accomplishments and establish new goals and innovations to address [CLIENT]'s evolving needs and long-term vision. This important annual meeting ensures we continuously look forward to future needs and trends, help [CLIENT] stay at the forefront of treatment technology and O&M practices, and eliminate complacency over time.

Jacobs' Annual Innovation Workshop drives continuous improvement and elevates O&M.

**What sets our Innovation Workshops apart from others?**

- Jacobs brings industry-leading experts in process, engineering, biosolids, hydraulics, pump stations, optimization, energy management, and virtually any other technical need — hand-selected based on the client's current needs. O&M-only contractors can't draw on these same resources.
- Our experts begin work BEFORE the workshop, learning your facilities and digging into opportunities so they come to the table ready to innovate and deliver realistic options. At the workshop, we listen and collaborate, then tailor innovative plans for the coming year to address your most pressing needs.
- Again and again, we've delivered results that improve operations and asset lifecycle performance plus provide cost efficiencies and savings, including energy savings opportunities valued at more than $1 million for the Wilmington WWTP.
