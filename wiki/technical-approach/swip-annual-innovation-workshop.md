# Annual Innovation Workshop — No-Cost Continuous Improvement Program

Our Annual Innovation Workshops, provided at no cost to the client, will bring together client staff, stakeholders, Jacobs' O&M staff, plus our utility experts. We will review the prior year's accomplishments and establish new goals and innovations to address the client's evolving needs and long-term vision. This important annual meeting ensures we continuously look forward to future needs and trends, help the client stay at the forefront of treatment technology and O&M practices, and eliminate complacency over time.

**What sets our Innovation Workshops apart from others?**

- Jacobs brings industry-leading experts in process, engineering, biosolids, hydraulics, pump stations, optimization, energy management, and virtually any other technical need — hand-selected based on the account's current needs. O&M-only contractors can't draw on these same resources.
- Our experts begin work BEFORE the workshop, learning the facilities and digging into opportunities so they come to the table ready to innovate and deliver realistic options.
- At the workshop, we listen and collaborate then tailor innovative plans for the coming year to address your most pressing needs.
- Again and again, we've delivered results that improve operations and asset lifecycle performance plus provide cost efficiencies and savings, including energy savings opportunities valued at more than $1 million for a reference wastewater treatment plant.
