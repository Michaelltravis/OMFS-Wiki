# Asset Management Program Overview — Understanding Client Goals and Value-Added Support Structure

## Understanding the Client's Asset Management Goals and Facility Needs

Jacobs recognizes that [CLIENT], a Southern California municipal owner of advanced water treatment, water recycling, stormwater, and groundwater injection facilities, is at the beginning of its asset management (AM) journey, and we are committed to supporting [CLIENT] in building a program that preserves its capital investments while ensuring long-term performance. During our site visit, Jacobs observed that while some elements of asset management are already in place, a full implementation has not yet been completed. We will coordinate closely with [CLIENT] to ensure full support for [CLIENT]'s Mentor Asset Performance Management (APM) program and will implement a robust Asset Management System (AMS) during the transition phase.

**Above and Beyond — Value-Added Extras: AquaDNA Deragger.** Jacobs will work with [CLIENT] during the transition to improve the performance of the coastal lift station by installing our AquaDNA Deragger at no additional cost (a $75,000 value). This technology will help ensure continuous operation and help reduce vac-truck cleaning frequency. **$75,000 embedded value by Jacobs.**

[CLIENT]'s advanced water treatment facility (AWTF) is in good condition; however, as a complex underground facility, it requires robust preventive maintenance (PM), predictive maintenance (PdM), and careful calibration programs to sustain current performance and reliability. By contrast, the urban runoff recycling facility is not currently in active use and contains many aging assets. We assume these will not require inclusion in the AMS unless directed by [CLIENT], beyond essential pickling and periodic pH monitoring of the membranes to maintain baseline condition.

Jacobs will also integrate the stormwater diversion structures, the coastal pump station, and the groundwater injection wells into the AMS. For the pump station—which requires routine vac-truck services—the AMS will be key to optimizing cleaning frequency in conjunction with utilizing **Jacobs' AquaDNA Deragger** (discussed in more detail in the subsection "Innovation and Advanced Techniques for Operational Efficiency") to adjust cleaning frequencies. At the groundwater injection site, Jacobs will apply the same asset management principles to ensure injection well reliability, system integrity, and regulatory compliance.

Our AMS will include a long-term Asset Management Plan developed through a collaborative, data-driven process. This plan will maximize the value of [CLIENT] assets by applying best practices in preventive and predictive maintenance, leveraging condition assessment data, and implementing optimization strategies that extend equipment life and reduce lifecycle costs.

## Integrated Asset Management and Maintenance Drives Delivery Excellence

Jacobs' projects are fully embedded into our interconnected internal network of subject matter experts, field professionals, and support teams. No project exists in isolation. This structure enables shared learning, resource efficiency, and exceptional client outcomes. [CLIENT]'s facilities will benefit from Jacobs' integrated approach to maintenance and asset lifecycle optimization.

**Above and Beyond — Value-Added Extras: Dedicated Regional Maintenance Technician.** In addition to the onsite Maintenance Technician on our [CLIENT] team, Jacobs will assign a separate, dedicated Maintenance Technician based in the Los Angeles area to support both this project and our West Basin project—ensuring rapid, expert response across all facilities. This shared-resource model provides local familiarity, operational continuity, and cost efficiency without compromising coverage. See the Key Personnel section for additional details. **$170,000 embedded value by Jacobs.**

## Dedicated Technical Support Structure

Jacobs' operations team will be supported by a national O&M Asset Management and Maintenance Technical Support Team. These four programs, guided by **Jacobs' Director of Asset Management for O&M, Graham Knowles**, form the backbone of Jacobs' O&M Asset Management team. Together, they deliver a structured, integrated approach that will not only meet [CLIENT]'s O&M needs but also ensure the organization's asset management maturity continues to evolve toward world-class standards.
