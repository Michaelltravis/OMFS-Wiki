# Transition Continuity and Staff Transfer

A smooth transition with a detailed plan and a dedicated transition team is one of the first steps in a successful partnership between [CLIENT] and JC Solutions. We have a thorough understanding of [CLIENT]’s needs and expectations and have developed a plan to deliver a high level of service on day one. We leverage the expertise of our transition experts to develop an approach that is tailored to the specific needs of the project and client, supported by additional resources that will supplement the local team. This helps confirm transition activities occur in parallel while project tasks are seamlessly implemented. Our transition plan reflects a highly organized process that couples our past experience transitioning operations with a focus on addressing specific project and client needs. It efficiently organizes the local staff and provides sufficient qualified resources in all disciplines to assure [CLIENT] of superior operations, maintenance, and administrative coverage for the managed assets. At the same time, this additional team of SMEs enables all Jacobs’ tools and strategies to be implemented at the project level without impacting the workload or effectiveness of the local team.

We also recognize that a facility may be undergoing a construction upgrade, with ongoing work likely to continue after the awarding of a new operations contract. During the commissioning phase, our team will support a seamless transition by managing operational aspects alongside the commissioning team. We will collaborate closely with [CLIENT] and the contractor, maintaining open communication to ensure alignment across all parties. Our approach involves coordinating with the contractor to understand operational needs, providing hands-on assistance to [CLIENT], and implementing a structured process to assume operational control, complementing the commissioning efforts while addressing concerns in real time.

JC Solutions’ transition is the first step in the transformation of [CLIENT]’s operations to a high-performing utility. One that is focused on leveraging the tools and capabilities of the larger Jacobs and driving ongoing improvement. As such, we invest significantly in the implementation of our systems, tools, and processes, coupled with a strong focus on training and the development of our culture. The result is a high-performing local team that is engaged, empowered to deliver on all project needs, fully integrated into the region, and well equipped to leverage the strength of Jacobs’ technical capabilities.

Transition planning will start immediately after notification of award to allow SMEs and teams to assemble, organize, and prepare to address all aspects of the transition on the contract date. Our approach includes the integration of due diligence and transition resources so that even prior to award, our transition experts are already engaged in the project. A key aspect of our transition is to provide full transparency through open and frequent communications in a structure adapted to the specific needs and expectations of [CLIENT].

We believe that the institutional knowledge of existing staff is a vital asset, and we are committed to offering competitive wages and benefits to qualified and interested candidates. At JC Solutions, we recognize the knowledge and invaluable contributions of the employees currently working at the facility. Our goal is to retain as many of these talented individuals as possible, leveraging their qualifications, capabilities, and the specific needs of [CLIENT]. Our commitment to local teams is evident in seamlessly transitioning over a dozen projects in the last three years, with a 90% conversion rate for qualified employees receiving job offers in the water sector’s O&M services.

Our approach to building a strong team focuses on a blend of existing employees who will transition to JC Solutions, existing JC Solutions resources, and targeted recruitment efforts to fill potential gaps. During the transition phase, we will leverage our extensive regional and technical resources to temporarily fill any unexpected vacancies, ensuring that operations remain uninterrupted from day one. Both existing and new employees will be supported with resources drawn from within JC Solutions. We will implement a proven six-step process: retain and recruit local talent; host introduction workshops; assess and interview remaining candidates; make employment offers; implement contract startup and operation; and initiate a continuous training and development program.

JC Solutions also understands the sensitivity of employees regarding transition and change and will provide inviting and welcoming informational workshops for employees and their families to better communicate who we are, how we are organized, what to expect, our benefits package, and the transition processes. JC Solutions’ unique transition process is patterned around listening to employees’ concerns with a specific focus on people. We routinely and successfully manage such transitions with care and will apply all our experience, qualified human resources, labor relations staff, and lessons learned to ensure it is smooth and seamless.

All key staff will participate in transition activities so everyone is familiar with the systems and tools being implemented, as well as with [CLIENT]’s assets. We will establish programs related to laboratory practices and testing, regulatory notification, and on-site communications plans. We will also finalize emergency response plans while having all required notifications, communications, and operational requirements fully functional by the commencement date. To ensure the success of this important partnership, we offer an onsite transition leadership team led by Transition Manager Mike Boven, who also holds a Georgia Wastewater Operator Class 1 Certification. Kelly Irving, Senior Operation Director, will assist Mike Boven; Kelly brings more than 30 years of experience in water and wastewater operations, management, and executive leadership, including successfully leading multiple transitions from public to private management and between contract operators. Most recently, she oversaw the complex transition of a regional utility serving 13 communities and approximately 50 employees under a 20-year contract.

A key focus of our transition is to provide full transparency through open and frequent communications with [CLIENT]. We will immediately implement a robust Communications Plan. Its elements include key stakeholders, team members, logistics, a knowledge transfer approach, communication methods including goals and preferred types, frequency, and objectives. Throughout the transition period, our transition manager and project manager will establish a regular schedule of meetings with [CLIENT] staff that meets expectations and provides an effective progression for our team to assume full responsibility for utility operations within an agreed-upon scope and schedule. We will document all activities, record minutes from regular meetings with [CLIENT], and log all required plans and documents to document completion of tasks.

Following hiring and onboarding, our O&M staff will observe all activities to learn from the existing [CLIENT] staff, including a review of all process areas, data gathering, metrics, and the CMMS. All staff will complete the required safety and operations training to ensure they have the knowledge necessary to safely and successfully perform all tasks assigned.
