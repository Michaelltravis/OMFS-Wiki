# Phase-by-Phase CIP Support Table (Exhibit 1-34)

We will provide services for CIP projects across every phase defined in Schedule 16, with the CIP Construction Manager coordinating all activities, as summarized in **Exhibit 1-34**.

**EXHIBIT 1-34.** *Jacobs' Approach to Support During Each Phase of CIP Implementation*

| PHASE | JACOBS' KEY ACTIVITIES | WHY THIS MATTERS TO [CLIENT] |
|---|---|---|
| **Preliminary Report (35% Design)** | Participate in alternatives analysis; identify O&M requirements, cost implications, and preferred manufacturers; begin MOPO development; provide plant data and facility tours for engineers; and flag potential Service Agreement modifications early. | O&M insight shapes design decisions before they're locked in—avoiding costly change orders and operability and maintenance issues downstream. |
| **Interim Plan (60% Design)** | Complete draft MOPO, provide input to Draft Sequence of Construction, finalize O&M impact evaluation, and begin Service Agreement Amendment negotiation if needed. | Construction sequencing reflects real operational constraints; no surprises at bid time. |
| **Final Plan (100% Design)** | Provide process performance data for final design, finalize MOPO, input to final Sequence of Construction and shutdown specifications, and execute Service Agreement Amendment if needed. | Design reflects actual operating conditions and shutdown realities. |
| **Bid Services** | Attend pre-bid meetings, provide facility access and tours for bidders, and assist with Request for Information (RFI) responses. | Bidders get accurate operational context, reducing risk of misunderstanding. |
| **Construction** | Execute MOPO, accommodate approved Sequence of Construction, execute shutdown plans, attend construction meetings, and participate in punch list development. | Full Schedule 5 compliance maintained throughout construction—no treatment disruptions. |
| **Startup** | Receive O&M and process operations information, prepare SOPs, participate in training, and operate new equipment during proofing period. | Staff are trained and SOPs are ready before new systems go live. |
| **Operate & Maintain** | Assume O&M of new assets, manage warranties and defective work via 2-year maintenance bond, and incorporate assets into NexGen with PM/PdM schedules and spare parts strategy. | Seamless transition from construction to sustained operations; warranty protection from day one. |
