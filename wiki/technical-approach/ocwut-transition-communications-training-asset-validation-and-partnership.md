# Transition Communications, Training, Asset Validation, and Long-Term Partnership

We’ll implement a transition communications plan that defines roles, responsibilities, communication pathways, meeting cadence, and regular coordination meetings with [CLIENT] to review progress, resolve issues, and confirm readiness. Knowledge transfer will follow a structured approach combining **documentation review, staff engagement, and direct observation of operations across all shifts,** capturing both formal procedures and the practical, experience-based knowledge that only comes from working alongside people who know these facilities.

During the transition period, we’ll prepare staff to assume their responsibilities safely, confidently, and effectively on the operational start date. Before performing independent duties, personnel will complete required safety training, job-specific operational training, and training on [CLIENT] systems, procedures, and work processes in accordance with the training plan.

During transition, our staff will shadow existing personnel and maintain a strong field presence across shifts to develop a detailed understanding of facility operations, maintenance practices, system behavior, and site-specific requirements. **This boots-on-the-ground approach will allow our team to observe operating conditions, workflows, decision points, and coordination practices in real time while respecting that the incumbent remains responsible for performing the work until the operational start date.** This familiarization will be reinforced through cross-training and systems training so personnel are prepared not only for their assigned roles but to provide reliable coverage across all facilities.

Training will continue beyond day one through refreshers, role-specific development, and ongoing reinforcement of safe operating practices, compliance requirements, and standard procedures, which will build confidence, promote consistency, and support reliable performance.

During the transition period, we’ll work with [CLIENT] to validate the baseline physical condition of the facilities as required under Schedule 19. This will include review and field verification of process equipment, structures, electrical systems, instrumentation, controls, and other critical assets so that facility condition is clearly documented and mutually understood before startup. Our team will use this process to identify discrepancies, confirm asset conditions, and support development of a defensible baseline for future accountability and asset management. We’ll coordinate the baseline physical condition of facilities report with [CLIENT], and it will be jointly signed prior to the operational start date in accordance with transition requirements.

Building directly on the baseline condition assessment findings, we’ll provide prioritized recommendations for capital improvements and reliability enhancements within 90 days of the operational start date. These recommendations will be grounded in documented field conditions, aligned with life-cycle planning, and structured to support [CLIENT]'s annual CIP budget development process consistent with Schedule 20 requirements.

Throughout the transition period, we’ll prioritize protecting continuity of operations and preparing for full compliance on the operational start date. We’ll validate monitoring, sampling, laboratory, reporting, and operational control processes during transition, identifying and resolving issues early so critical compliance systems are ready to function without interruption when Jacobs assumes responsibility on January 1, 2027.

**Our commitment extends beyond startup.** We’ll support [CLIENT] through disciplined documentation, transparent reporting, responsive communication and a continuous focus on safe, compliant, and reliable performance throughout the term of the service agreement.

**Jacobs has successfully transitioned hundreds of complex utility O&M contracts over more than 40 years, welcoming over 10,000 employees into our organization.** We’ve recently led large-scale transitions in Jackson, MS; Baton Rouge, LA; Waterbury, CT; West Basin, CA; and Southbridge, MA. In Jackson, we stabilized a complex multi-facility system serving 150,000 residents with no disruption to service. In Baton Rouge, we transitioned two major wastewater treatment plants simultaneously. In Waterbury, we completed the staff transition within 3 months of startup.

[CLIENT] will benefit from that experience, discipline, and proven process from day one.
