# CMMS-Integrated Inventory Management Value-Add for a Second Facility

*Presented in the source proposal under "Additional Items for Consideration" — modifications offered without a firm price, pending further scoping information from the client. The umbrella framing sentence is in [swip-scada-redundancy-upgrade-recommendation.md](swip-scada-redundancy-upgrade-recommendation.md).*

## Inventory Management for [SECOND FACILITY]

In recognizing the importance of [SECOND FACILITY], especially considering its role for DPR, [Firm] can extend our CMMS-integrated inventory management services to this facility, providing [CLIENT] a unified asset management system. This proactive system will support accurate part forecasting, timely reorders, prevent obsolescence, and ensure [CLIENT] gets the maximum value from these important assets. Dashboards and reports will provide real-time visibility into inventory status, usage trends, and procurement needs, empowering the team to make informed decisions while minimizing downtime and lifecycle costs. **[Firm]' approach will transform inventory control from a reactive function into a strategic asset management tool aligned with ISO 55001 standards and [CLIENT]'s operational goals.**

## Reuse guidance

This is a short, easy-to-adapt pitch for extending an existing or proposed CMMS-integrated inventory system to a facility that sits just outside the core RFP scope but is operationally connected to it — a second treatment plant, a plant that feeds the same treatment train, or a site the client is counting on for a future program. The structure is worth copying exactly. It opens by naming why the second facility matters to the client's own stated future rather than to the operator's convenience, offers a unified system across both sites as the benefit, lists four concrete capabilities the system delivers (accurate part forecasting, timely reorders, obsolescence prevention, and maximum value from the assets), adds dashboards and reports so the client can see the same data the operator sees, and closes on a bolded standards-anchored claim that reframes inventory from a back-office chore into strategic asset management aligned with ISO 55001. That last sentence is the line evaluators remember; keep it and keep the ISO reference, since it is what separates this from a generic parts-room promise. Replace [SECOND FACILITY] with the actual facility name, and mirror whatever driver the client has stated — resilience, reuse expansion, asset criticality — in the opening clause.
