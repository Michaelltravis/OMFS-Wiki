# CMMS-Integrated Inventory Management Value-Add for a Second Facility

*(Presented in the source proposal as one of the "Additional Items for Consideration" — suggested modifications offered without a firm price, pending further scoping information from the client; see `swip-scada-redundancy-upgrade-recommendation.md` for the umbrella framing sentence.)*

## Inventory Management for [SECOND FACILITY]

In recognizing the importance of [SECOND FACILITY], especially considering its role in [CLIENT]'s broader water reuse strategy, [Firm] can extend its CMMS-integrated inventory management services to this facility, providing the client a unified asset management system. This proactive system supports accurate part forecasting, timely reorders, and prevention of obsolescence, and ensures the client gets the maximum value from these important assets. Dashboards and reports provide real-time visibility into inventory status, usage trends, and procurement needs, empowering the team to make informed decisions while minimizing downtime and lifecycle costs. [Firm]'s approach transforms inventory control from a reactive function into a strategic asset management tool aligned with ISO 55001 standards and the client's operational goals.

## Reuse guidance

This is a short, easy-to-adapt pitch for extending an existing (or proposed) CMMS-integrated inventory system to a second facility that sits just outside the core RFP scope but is operationally connected to it (e.g., feeds the same treatment train, shares a reuse/recharge system). The ISO 55001 reference gives the pitch a standards-based credibility anchor that's reusable across pursuits. Replace [SECOND FACILITY] with the target pursuit's actual facility name and briefly state why it matters to the client's broader strategy (mirror whatever driver the client has stated — resilience, reuse expansion, asset criticality). No dollar value was attached to this item in the source pursuit.
