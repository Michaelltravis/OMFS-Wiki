# CIP Project Implementation, Construction Coordination, and the Dedicated CIP Construction Manager

[CLIENT]'s CIP portfolio during this contract term is substantial. [CLIENT] is a Southcentral US regional water utility trust operating four wastewater treatment plants and a major pump station.

- South Canadian's new 8.5-MGD oxidation ditch (online summer 2026)
- OSHG at Deer Creek and South Canadian
- Future Deer Creek capacity upgrades
- New North Canadian headworks with grit removal
- Odor control improvements across the system

These projects represent significant public investment. **We'll protect that investment by providing structured operational support at every phase, from early design input through commissioning, while maintaining full compliance with Schedule 5 Performance Requirements throughout.**

## Dedicated CIP Construction Manager

The CIP Construction Manager is a dedicated, required position (Schedule 10) whose sole responsibility is managing and coordinating the CIP scope defined in Schedule 16. This position serves as the interface between the General Manager, [CLIENT]'s consultants, construction contractors, and our on-site operations and maintenance team, coordinating activities so construction doesn't disrupt treatment processes or permit compliance. The CIP Construction Manager will be supported by our engineering and capital program management resources, bringing experience integrating major capital upgrades at operating wastewater facilities.

**Expert capital project planning and coordination support.** *Tanner Pipher, PE — CIP Construction Manager.* Tanner will serve as our CIP Construction Manager and primary liaison to [CLIENT]'s contractors, project managers, and third-party engineers during construction and startup activities. With more than 7 years of water and wastewater engineering experience — including startup of Bixby's new SBR facility and construction coordination across active Oklahoma wastewater plants — he connects design intent with field execution in a practical, low-disruption way. He'll coordinate our staff to support facility inspections, resolve field issues as they arise, and keep capital improvements aligned with operational, schedule, and compliance needs. He'll also assist with equipment startup, testing, and operational integration — so CIP investments deliver the performance [CLIENT] expects from day one.

*Liie Hill, PE — O&M Capital Projects Support SME.* Working alongside CIP Construction Manager Tanner, Liie will provide phase-by-phase CIP input from operations and maintenance, coordinate staff to support facility assessments and capital planning efforts, and assist with equipment startup, testing, and integration for successful project delivery.
