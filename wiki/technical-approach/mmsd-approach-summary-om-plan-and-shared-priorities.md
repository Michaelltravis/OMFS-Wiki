# Approach Summary: O&M Plan Built Around the Client's Stated Priorities

Jacobs shares [CLIENT]'s vision—[CLIENT] being a large regional sewerage district operating two water reclamation facilities, deep tunnel storage, and a regional conveyance system—for a forward-looking operations and maintenance (O&M) partnership grounded in transparency, collaboration, offsite support, and disciplined delivery of your expectations. Based on extensive engagement with [CLIENT] staff and a deep understanding of your facilities and system, we've developed a comprehensive O&M plan driven by key operating strategies that directly support your priorities—regulatory compliance, protection of [CLIENT]'s biosolids product brand, overflow prevention, cost and operational efficiency, an integrated approach to managing your system, safety, resiliency, and measurable progress toward your 2035 Vision.

Real-time, evidence-based reporting and open communication will build trust and foster a one-team partnership that aligns [CLIENT] and Jacobs around shared vision, goals, and solutions. Our objective: to drive consistent performance, shared prioritization, accountability, and resilience.

Our plan rests on a transparent Program Management (PgM) framework; embedded leadership and engaged support—an empowered leadership team backed by national staff to strengthen accountability and performance; and rigorous cost management—disciplined, transparent cost management with joint governance to validate savings and share measurable improvements over time.

Above all, we'll operate as a true partner so [CLIENT] has a "no surprises" path to **reliable, sustained performance** and **long-term community value**. **Exhibit IV-1** illustrates your priorities to achieve excellence and our O&M plan.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the "shared priorities → our plan delivers" structure, the "no surprises" promise, and the three framing commitments (transparent PgM, embedded leadership with national backing, rigorous cost management with joint governance). Pursuit-specific: the priority list itself, which should be lifted from the client's own strategic plan or RFP evaluation criteria and echoed in their words; the vision document name and target year; the biosolids product reference.
