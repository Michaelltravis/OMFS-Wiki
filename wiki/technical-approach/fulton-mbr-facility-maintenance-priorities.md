# MBR Facility Maintenance Priorities

Through our facility tours and initial assessments, we have identified several critical infrastructure components that we believe should be a focus of our AMS and our predictive and preventive maintenance planning. Condition assessments play a critical role in our industry-leading asset management and associated maintenance approach. Our field-trained maintenance specialists have completed condition assessments for 1 million water and wastewater plant assets.

Implementing a best-in-class maintenance practices is essential for the efficient and reliable operation of [FACILITY A], a larger Kubota MBR facility. Regular maintenance ensures optimal system performance, extends equipment lifespan, and maintains high effluent quality. Key maintenance activities include regular inspection and maintenance of the grit and screening facilities. In our experience the most susceptible membranes to damage are those on the bottom half of the stack. During the turbulence of the air scour cycle any grit or screenings in the tank encounter the lower units first creating the most damage. Regular screening effectiveness tests will ensure longevity of the equipment.

JC Solutions will be implementing PdM activities using data and monitoring tools to assist in timely and cost-effective maintenance. Regular inspections and cleaning prevent membrane fouling, a common challenge in MBR systems that can lead to reduced filtration efficiency and increased operational costs. Maintaining the UV systems for operation effectiveness, making sure hydraulic units are functioning properly for bulb cleaning and keeping an inventory of spare parts on hand. Utilizing air scouring as a primary cleaning method effectively removes debris from membrane surfaces, reducing the need for chemical cleaning and simplifying maintenance procedures. Periodic chemical cleaning is necessary to address persistent fouling and maintain membrane permeability, ensuring consistent treatment performance. Noted issues with the chemical feed system for the membrane treatment. Piping appears to be leaking around multiple fittings. An investigation of the entire feed system is required. Identify issues with screw press operations and repairs needed. Finalize project for the additional screw presses that are out of service.

Adhering to these maintenance practices not only enhances the efficiency of the Kubota MBR system but also contributes to the facility's compliance with environmental regulations and its commitment to sustainable wastewater treatment.

[FACILITY B], a Xenon membrane facility, has its biggest focus on the odor control system, which requires changing the media and regular checks to ensure the effectiveness of the system. The Xenon membrane system using Pneumatic valves requires special attention to the air dryer system. Effective moisture removal ensures consistent system operation and equipment longevity, along with repairing any air leaks in the compressed air system and blowers for the membranes. Any air leaks would disrupt the operational effectiveness of the system and will need to be addressed promptly.

Our maintenance approach at [FACILITY B] will focus on air dryer maintenance for keeping the pneumatic valves working properly; blower maintenance (vibration, precision alignment, continuous monitoring) for membrane system; special attention to the grit and screenings equipment; UV disinfection system maintenance and inventory control; training personnel on how to properly repair the hollow fibers membranes; media change out of the odor control system—a difficult process we will undertake and solve; chemical feed system piping maintenance; sensors and control systems, including regular calibrations of sensors and software updates; and centrifuge maintenance, including use of PdM tools to track system performance, which is particularly critical with the one unit out of service for repair, as observed on our site visit.

[FACILITY C], a smaller membrane facility, is a converted plant utilizing Xenon ZeeWeed technology. Again, effective grit and screenings removal will be especially important as will repairing failing units promptly to maintain compliance. Tank mixers will need to be checked regularly for proper mixing, and we will use oil analysis to determine when to change the lubrication to ensure proper maintenance with an eye to cost-effectiveness. The UV system is important and requires maintaining a proper inventory level of bulbs and replacement parts for timely repairs.

As this is an older facility, we will regularly inspect and maintain the physical infrastructure of the plant, addressing issues like concrete deterioration, pipe corrosion, and structural integrity before they become critical problems. Emergency Power generators and transfer switches will be tested monthly and maintained according to manufacturer specifications to ensure continuous operation during power outages, protecting both treatment processes and facility personnel. We will implement a comprehensive corrosion monitoring and prevention program including regular inspections, protective coatings application, and cathodic protection systems where appropriate to extend equipment life and maintain facility integrity. We will ensure alarms are functional and correctly calibrated through regular testing and verification procedures, maintaining accurate monitoring of critical parameters to prevent compliance issues and equipment damage.
