## Reuse guidance

Universal: the format. A health check that grades each system area, states the finding plainly, and answers it with a named solution is the strongest structural device available to a challenger, and it also works for an incumbent framing continuous improvement. Keep the discipline of one solution per finding.

Pursuit-specific: every finding and every solution. The percentage savings ranges (aeration 10–25%, chemicals 20–40%) are Jacobs program benchmarks — confirm the current registered values before restating them.
