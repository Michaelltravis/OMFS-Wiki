# Direct Potable Reuse Operations Staffing Model (Operator Tiers, Responsibilities, Assumptions)

## Direct Potable Reuse Operations

Our full team is excited about the potential for [CLIENT] to obtain approval for DPR and stands ready to help however possible to ensure success. With respect to operations at the facilities, the current DPR regulations require the following operators to be added to the overall operations, with some of the staff required at [SECOND FACILITY]:

| Operator | Responsibility | Assumption |
|---|---|---|
| **T5 Chief Operator and T3 (minimum) Shift Operator** | Entire treatment train | Because the scope of this RFP is limited to [FACILITY], it is assumed that [CLIENT] would provide the T5 operator(s) to meet this requirement, most likely located at [SECOND FACILITY]. |
| **AWT5™ Chief Operator and AWT3™ (minimum) Shift Operator** | [SECOND FACILITY], if LRVs or chemical control are provided here, 24/7 duty | This assumes that pathogen or chemical control is provided at [SECOND FACILITY], triggering these operator requirements. Because the scope of this RFP is limited to [FACILITY], our proposal assumes that [CLIENT] would provide the AWT5™ and AWT3™ staff to meet this requirement. However, in the future, [Firm] could provide these operators under an amendment should [CLIENT] decide to expand the scope to include [SECOND FACILITY]. |
| **AWT5™ Lead Operator and Two AWT3™ Shift Operators** | [FACILITY], 24/7 duty | Our proposal for this optional service includes additional staffing of an AWT5™ Lead Operator and two AWT3™ Shift Operators at [FACILITY] to ensure 24/7 operational coverage. |
| **AWT5™ Chief Operator and AWT3™ (minimum) Shift Operator** | Other location, 24/7 duty | With [CLIENT] currently evaluating DPR options, it is unknown if a DPR option will include any other treatment processes located offsite from either [FACILITY] or [SECOND FACILITY]. Should another location provide pathogen or chemical control, it will also be subject to staffing requirements listed above for [FACILITY] for DPR. If another treatment location is included in the future, [Firm] could provide these operators under an amendment should [CLIENT] decide to expand the scope to include it. Because of this unknown, our proposal only includes additional AWT5™ or AWT3™ staffing 24/7 at [FACILITY]. |

**We've provided a year 5 price of $662,997 for the Direct Potable Reuse Operations on Schedule B of the pricing form.**

## Reuse guidance

This table is a reusable template for scoping optional advanced-treatment staffing add-ons. Its structure is the transferable part: for each staffing line it names the certification tier the regulation requires, states the specific responsibility and duty cycle, and then documents in plain language the assumption or exclusion that drives whether the add-on is priced in, left with the client, or deferred to a future amendment. That assumption-documentation pattern — naming exactly what is out of scope, why, and the contractual path to bring it in — is broadly reusable for any optional-scope staffing proposal, not only DPR, and it protects the price without reading as evasive. Swap in the target pursuit's facility names for [FACILITY] and [SECOND FACILITY], confirm current operator certification requirements against the applicable state regulations before restating grades, and reprice the year-5 figure with the commercial lead. Keep the four-row shape even when only two rows apply: the value of the exhibit comes from showing the evaluator that every regulatory staffing trigger was considered, including the one at a location that does not exist yet.
