# Direct Potable Reuse Operations Staffing Model (Operator Tiers, Responsibilities, Assumptions)

[Firm]'s full team is excited about the potential for [CLIENT] to obtain approval for DPR and stands ready to help however possible to ensure success. With respect to operations at the facilities, current DPR regulations require the following operators to be added to overall operations, with some of the staff required at [SECOND FACILITY]:

| Operator | Responsibility | Assumption |
|---|---|---|
| T5 Chief Operator and T3 (minimum) Shift Operator | Entire treatment train | Because the scope of this RFP is limited to [FACILITY], it is assumed that the client would provide the T5 operator(s) to meet this requirement, most likely located at [SECOND FACILITY]. |
| AWT5 Chief Operator and AWT3 (minimum) Shift Operator | [SECOND FACILITY], if LRVs (log removal values) or chemical control are provided there, 24/7 duty | This assumes that pathogen or chemical control is provided at [SECOND FACILITY], triggering these operator requirements. Because the scope of this RFP is limited to [FACILITY], the proposal assumes the client would provide the AWT5 and AWT3 staff to meet this requirement. However, in the future [Firm] could provide these operators under an amendment should the client decide to expand the scope to include [SECOND FACILITY]. |
| AWT5 Lead Operator and Two AWT3 Shift Operators | [FACILITY], 24/7 duty | This optional service includes additional staffing of an AWT5 Lead Operator and two AWT3 Shift Operators at [FACILITY] to ensure 24/7 operational coverage. |
| AWT5 Chief Operator and AWT3 (minimum) Shift Operator | Other location, 24/7 duty | With the client currently evaluating DPR options, it is unknown if a DPR option will include any other treatment processes located offsite from either [FACILITY] or [SECOND FACILITY]. Should another location provide pathogen or chemical control, it would also be subject to the staffing requirements listed above for [FACILITY] DPR. If another treatment location is included in the future, [Firm] could provide these operators under an amendment should the client decide to expand the scope to include it. Because of this unknown, the proposal only includes additional AWT5 or AWT3 staffing 24/7 at [FACILITY]. |

**[Commercial figure redacted]:** The source pursuit stated a year-5 price for this staffing line explicitly "on Schedule B of the pricing form" (the commercial cost-proposal schedule) — a priced optional-staffing figure, not an outcome/savings result. ## Reuse guidance

This table is a reusable template for scoping optional DPR/advanced-treatment staffing add-ons: it shows how to (1) name the operator certification tier required by regulation, (2) state the specific responsibility/duty cycle, and (3) explicitly document the scope assumption or exclusion driving whether the add-on is included or requires a future amendment. This assumption-documentation pattern (naming exactly what's excluded and why, with a clear amendment path) is broadly reusable for any optional-scope staffing proposal, not just DPR. Swap in the target pursuit's actual facility names for [FACILITY]/[SECOND FACILITY], confirm current operator certification requirements against the applicable state's regulations (T5/T3/AWT5/AWT3 are California-specific; other states use different grade nomenclature), and reprice the commercial figure fresh. Pairs with `swip-dpr-readiness-expert-bench-and-monitoring.md` (the surrounding DPR narrative and named SME bench) and the Hull collection-system staffing blocks if a side-by-side, cross-region staffing-model comparison is useful.
