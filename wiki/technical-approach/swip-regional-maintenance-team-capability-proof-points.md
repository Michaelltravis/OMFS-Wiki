# Regional Maintenance Team Capability — Proof-Point Case Studies

**Expert Maintenance Resources — Proven, Mobilized, and Ready.** Jacobs' Regional Maintenance Teams are a proven asset to our O&M projects nationwide. These highly skilled teams bring a diverse range of technical expertise — including HVAC, electrical, SCADA, mechanical systems, pump repair, equipment installation, and structural and facility construction. Whether supporting routine maintenance, specialized repairs, or complex upgrades to treatment facilities and processes, our regional teams consistently deliver responsive, high-quality service that enhances reliability, extends asset life, and reduces downtime.

## Key West WWTP, FL — UV System Replacement

Jacobs successfully installed a new UV disinfection system in Key West, replacing an obsolete unit while maintaining uninterrupted treatment and full regulatory compliance. The project involved phased replacement of two channels — keeping one operational at all times — along with mechanical installation, electrical upgrades, and complete reprogramming of lost automation. With vendor coordination for programming and warranty support, the project was completed without violations and delivered very positive operational results.

## North Hudson Sewerage Authority — 18th St Pump Station ATS Replacement

Jacobs planned and implemented a temporary generator and Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) solution after the primary ATS failed. The team coordinated closely with site staff to maintain operations while developing a permanent replacement plan, which includes full removal and installation of a new ATS. (Before/after documentation available — see graphic reference.)

## Traverse City WWTF — New Equipment Room and HVAC Installation

Jacobs managed the layout and construction of a new equipment room at the Traverse City WWTF, working with a subcontractor to complete the structure. Following construction, we installed a mini-split HVAC system with heat pump to ensure proper climate control in the new space.

## Waterbury WTF — Instrument Panel Replacement

At the Waterbury Wastewater Treatment Facility, Jacobs removed and replaced deteriorated instrumentation panels, restoring reliability and integrity to the plant's monitoring and control systems, documented in before-and-after images.
