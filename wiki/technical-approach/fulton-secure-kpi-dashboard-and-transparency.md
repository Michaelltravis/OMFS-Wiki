# Secure KPI Dashboard and Transparency Framework

The dashboard is like a skilled business analyst, pulling data from unrelated sources like Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA), sensors, and devices, and relaying them visually. Any data source can be captured, including manual entries from phones and tablets. The dashboard is non-intrusive, read-only, and secure. Data in transit and at rest are fully encrypted. Role-based access controls specify who may view the data.

As we work side by side with [CLIENT] to build a successful, trusting partnership devoted to delivering safe, compliant, and efficient operations, you can expect from Day One a focus on collaboration, communication, and transparency. Effective communication is critical to our mutual success and that goal is reflected in our proposed procedures. In the first 60 days, we’ll work with you to develop an effective schedule of coordination, communication, and reporting.

Another useful feature of the dashboard is the ability to **view and track, in real-time, the location of individuals and physical assets on-site**. We have used this security feature in private industry and defense environments with great success. Physical location is tracked via access badges, which can be permanently associated with full-time staff or provided to visitors and contractors upon entry. Tags can also be associated with major assets, such as vehicles, to monitor their locations in real-time.

The KPI dashboard for O&M performance tracking is included in our approach.

A hallmark of JC Solutions’ approach is transparency and open communication. Our plan places significant emphasis on providing you full access to the information you want, so you can be aware of all aspects of your operations. [CLIENT] will receive a customized online dashboard to track key performance indicators (KPIs), providing you with real-time and historical trending information at your fingertips, including maintenance activity and work order status. It provides improved access and transparency and complements the written reports.
