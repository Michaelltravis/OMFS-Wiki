# Low-Pressure Sewer and Grinder Pump System O&M Experience

Building on our experience managing complex wastewater collection systems, we bring extensive experience evaluating, operating, and maintaining low-pressure sewer systems, including grinder pump networks integrated with broader wastewater infrastructure. Our team understands the unique operational, maintenance, and asset management considerations associated with these systems, including pump reliability, pressure system performance, electrical and controls integration, and lifecycle planning.

We have supported utilities with system assessments, capital planning, development of O&M programs, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and asset management strategies tailored to low-pressure collection infrastructure — directly applicable to systems with a large population of grinder pumps and associated low-pressure piping, including budgeting for routine pump replacement, SCADA and asset management integration, odor and grease control, and emergency response.

At [a reference client's low-pressure service area], our team initiated a targeted, out-of-scope evaluation to identify root causes and develop practical, long-term solutions to improve system reliability and performance — an example of the proactive, consultative support we bring beyond the base scope of an O&M contract.

Key personnel proposed for this role can bring direct experience managing **dual-pressure systems** that incorporate both residential grinder pumps and septic tank effluent pump (STEP) units, including collection systems with integrated force main and gravity infrastructure, multiple lift stations, and a substantial base of residential grinder pump installations — hands-on experience that provides a strong technical foundation for supporting similarly configured systems.
