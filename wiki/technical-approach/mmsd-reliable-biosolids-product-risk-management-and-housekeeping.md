# Reliable Biosolids Product Production — Strategic Risk Management, Chaff Handling, and Housekeeping

**Reliable Biosolids Product Production with Strategic Risk Management.** To help meet production and distribution goals for [CLIENT]'s biosolids product, we intend to collaborate with [CLIENT], a regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities, to evaluate the following opportunities to make production more reliable and consistent. To minimize risk to product quality, we'll work closely with [CLIENT] compliance and product marketing staff to plan and execute any proposed changes in a stepwise manner while carefully monitoring any changes.

- **Develop detailed and effective drying and dewatering SOPs and train operators in their execution to** mitigate the risk of producing product that is not compliant with the specifications. For example, operators must be trained on the new, more robust sieve analysis.

- **Carefully monitor silo storage capacity and work closely with [CLIENT] and its packaging contractor to mitigate the risk of reaching silo storage capacity** during low agricultural demand seasons, ensuring dryer production does not have to be curtailed.

- **Provide effective training and SOPs for staff on properly staging the product for distribution and delivering the product to the packaging facility** so they adhere to the requirements described in the RFP.

**Collecting Chaff from Product Production in Specialized Dumpsters for Disposal in a Landfill.** We'll work with [CLIENT] to investigate options to minimize chaff production, which would lower disposal costs. For transport and disposal, chaff could be hauled in standard vehicles or placed in roll-off boxes using disposable filter "socks" (the same type planned for other wastes). The sock is attached to the box and filled through an extended neck that keeps the dusty material contained. The roll-off is then hauled to the landfill, where the entire filled sock is tipped and disposed. This approach helps control dust during loading, transport, and unloading.

**Maintaining Clean Facilities.** The drying and dewatering (D&D) and silo facilities are difficult to keep clean because of dust and fine particles that escape during drying, processing, and storage. We'll develop detailed, OSHA-compliant SOPs so cleaning is performed consistently and effectively in accordance with housekeeping to manage dust as required in the Scope of Services. The D&D dust control system is aging, and leaks and plugging can worsen dust buildup. We understand [CLIENT] has ongoing and planned capital improvements. If those upgrades are not in place at contract start, Jacobs will evaluate system performance and recommend practical, low-cost actions to reduce dust, such as rebalancing or adjusting airflow rates.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the stepwise-change-with-monitoring commitment, which lets a challenger propose optimization without sounding cavalier about a product the client has built a brand around; the pairing of every risk with an SOP and a training commitment; and the honest treatment of an aging support system (name it, commit to low-cost fixes if the capital work slips).

Pursuit-specific: silo capacity and seasonal agricultural demand, the packaging contractor relationship, and the chaff sock method. Pairs with the dewatering and drying optimization block and the solids processing focus-area opener. Read `verbatim/mmsd-om-2028/pages/p0065.md` for the full passage.
