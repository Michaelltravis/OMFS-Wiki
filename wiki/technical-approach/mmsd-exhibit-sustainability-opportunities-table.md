# Exhibit IV-54 — Opportunities to Improve Sustainability for Operational Excellence

**EXHIBIT IV-54. OPPORTUNITIES TO IMPROVE SUSTAINABILITY FOR OPERATIONAL EXCELLENCE** (graphic asset ID `285_007CAM_5`)

| Energy Efficiency and Optimization | Decarbonization and Clean Energy Transition | Resource Recovery and Circular Economy | Resilience and Climate Adaptation | Funding and Partnerships |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Drivers:** Lower Opex with expected 10-20% energy reduction plus chemical optimization of 20-30%<br>- Repair and prevent air leaks in aeration systems<br>- Optimize process using real-time controls and AI-driven analytics to balance energy use<br>- Optimize chemical dosing, aeration control & distribution, and pumping | **Key Actions:** Maximize renewable energy and beneficial reuse<br>- Maximize RNG & biogas utilization, reduce flaring<br>- Update GHG inventory<br>- Continue development of biogas upgrading and CO₂ capture at the secondary-treatment WRF<br>- Transition fleet to EV/biogas as feasible | **High Value Opportunities:** Focus on byproducts<br>- Expand heat recovery from biosolids dryers, digesters, and cogeneration systems.<br>- Maximize digester gas utilization and minimize flaring<br>- Evaluate biosolids-product chaff and side-stream recovery to increase digester gas yield.<br>- Digest primary scum to boost biogas generation | **System Resiliency:** Incorporate climate adaptation into O&M planning<br>- Maintain reliable performance through extreme weather, flooding, or temperature changes<br>- Protect assets and staff, ensure treatment continuity, and enhance system redundancy | **Resources:** Leverage DOE/EPA and the state energy program (e.g., Wisconsin's Focus on Energy)<br>- Use proven Jacobs expertise to secure federal, state and utility funding (e.g. Wilmington)<br>- Tie sustainable O&M initiatives to external funding streams |

**DIRECTLY ADVANCES [CLIENT]'S 2035 VISION & 2050 FOUNDATIONAL GOALS**
