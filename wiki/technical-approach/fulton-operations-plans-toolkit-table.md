# Proven Operations Plans Toolkit — UPCPs, Sampling Plan, and Sample Tracking Tool

Supporting CPCS is a robust suite of tools built on our extensive experience and best practices. Summarized in **Exhibit 2-22**, these tools are the backbone of our process control strategy.

**Exhibit 2-22.** *JC Solutions' Tools for Operational, Process, and Compliance Success—Built on Best Practices and Decades of Experience*

| **PROVEN OPERATIONS PLANS** | **PLAN DESCRIPTION** | **BENEFIT TO [CLIENT]** |
|---|---|---|
| **Unit Process Control Procedures (UPCPs)** | UPCPs provide our "operations road map", establish key performance indicators (KPIs) and parameters we track, drive process adjustments based on results, direct continuous improvement, and clearly establish communication venues and related control of process adjustments. UPCPs also provide a level of detail for O&M personnel to make informed decisions. | We'll share the UPCPs with [CLIENT], promoting transparency. UPCPs provide confidence in consistent compliance with all permits and effluent requirements. Documented procedures and process targets are shared with [CLIENT] management, operators, maintenance, and laboratory staff. The detailed information provided maximizes efficiency and minimizes the potential for errors and downtime. |
| **Sampling Plan** | The Sampling Plan includes a well-thought-through and understood list of sampling locations, a sampling map, and parameters to be sampled at each location. It also identifies methods to be used, proper chain of custody techniques, and proper preservation procedures. | A properly written and implemented sample plan is critical to the compliant and efficient operation of a facility. Reliable, accurate data starts with representative sampling to achieve effective solids balances and process control. |
| **Sample Tracking Tool (STT)** | Our STT is used by operations staff to monitor and track process and compliance sampling and analysis requirements. This tool is developed and implemented at the start of each year; as samples are taken and analyzed in-house or sent to [CLIENT]'s laboratory or a third party, samples are checked off by the person who has pulled the samples and then checked off the same day by a second independent party to confirm that all process and compliance samples are taken as per requirements. | A key element of compliance is ensuring that samples and analyses are performed at the locations and frequencies outlined in the permit and scope of work. The STT provides the necessary quality assurance to make sure that nothing is missed by the onsite team. |

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-instrument structure (UPCPs / Sampling Plan / Sample Tracking Tool), the plan-description-plus-benefit column pairing, and the same-day independent second check inside the STT. Pursuit-specific: the exhibit number, the JV or firm name in the caption, and where samples are analyzed.
