# Maintenance and Asset Management SME Bench — On-Site Owners and National Support

Throughout the Maintenance Plan, each on-site owner is presented alongside the national or regional subject matter expert standing behind them. [CLIENT] is a southcentral US municipal water utility trust operating four wastewater treatment plants and a major pump station.

## Asset management expertise behind your on-site team

Asset management connects daily maintenance to long-term reliability, capital decisions, and audit-ready compliance — and it's a backbone of every successful Jacobs O&M project. On-site Asset Manager Amy Dembinski, CRL will draw directly on Graham Knowles and Jacobs' national O&M Asset Management Support Team, giving [CLIENT] local execution and the strategic depth to build a durable Asset Management System.

- **GRAHAM KNOWLES — *O&M Asset Management/CMMS SME.*** Graham and his team of on-call advisors support projects with SAMP development, AM Policy, criticality and risk workshops, and ISO 55001 alignment. He'll help the on-site team translate framework into field results and keep the program audit-ready as it matures. (Graphic asset ID 170_009385.)
- **AMY DEMBINSKI, CRL — *On-Site Asset Manager.*** Amy will be the day-to-day owner of [CLIENT]'s asset data, CMMS, and maintenance planning, bringing regional experience supporting O&M projects in Oklahoma and beyond. She'll build out the asset registry, PM/PdM/RCM plans, KPI and cost tracking, and capital-planning inputs that connect daily maintenance to long-term reliability.

## CMMS and NexGen expertise pair with direct client experience

A successful O&M program lives and dies by its CMMS — where asset data, work orders, planning discipline, and capital decisions come together. [CLIENT]'s NexGen implementation is already underway with Jacobs' support, giving [CLIENT] something no other proposer can offer: seamless continuity from implementation to long-term operation, with an SME who already knows how [CLIENT] wants NexGen configured, used, and evolved.

- **JANEANE GIARRUSSO, IAM — *CMMS/NexGen SME.*** Janeane brings deep NexGen implementation experience and longstanding relationships with NexGen's own team. As a key member of Jacobs' current NexGen implementation for [CLIENT], she has firsthand knowledge of the configuration choices, data standards, and workflows being built into the system right now — translating into faster answers and direct escalation paths no other proposer can match. IAM-certified and specialized in connecting CMMS configuration to asset management decision-making, she'll support the on-site team with NexGen optimization, data-standard enforcement, and workflow refinement long after implementation wraps. (Graphic asset ID 172_009385.)

## Maintenance execution expertise behind our on-site team

Strong maintenance execution turns asset management framework into field results. Our on-site Maintenance Manager Brian Daniels will lead day-to-day planning, scheduling, and work execution, backed by Jacobs' Regional Maintenance Support Team — condition assessment and reliability specialists who move between Jacobs O&M projects wherever targeted expertise is needed.

- **BRIAN DANIELS — *On-Site Maintenance Manager.*** Brian will own the day-to-day maintenance program — CMMS work order execution, PM/PdM scheduling, staff supervision, and emergency response. With recent O&M experience in Oklahoma, he'll build the planning discipline that shifts [CLIENT]'s program from reactive to proactive.
- **MIKE IDZIOR, CMRT, CRL — *O&M Regional Maintenance SME.*** Mike leads Jacobs' on-call bench for major maintenance, condition assessments, and specialized troubleshooting across mechanical, I&C, electrical, process, and chemical feed systems — stepping in when challenges go beyond routine and bringing in additional Jacobs specialists whenever warranted. (Graphic asset ID 171_009385.)

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the callout pattern itself — a one-paragraph lede stating why the discipline matters and what the pairing gets the client, then the national SME and the on-site owner side by side with concrete duties rather than titles. The three disciplines covered (asset management, CMMS, maintenance execution) map to the three places a maintenance section most needs bench proof.

Pursuit-specific and requiring re-clearance: all five names, their credentials (CRL, IAM, CMRT), their stated regional experience, the platform name, and the in-flight implementation relationship. The "no other proposer can match" framing depends entirely on that relationship being real for the target client.
