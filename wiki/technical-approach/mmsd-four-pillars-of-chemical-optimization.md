# Four Pillars of Chemical Optimization — Compliance, Transparency, Cost, Innovation

**Chemical Optimization — Focus Area.** Jacobs approaches process optimization through the four following governing concepts:

**JACOBS' FOUR PILLARS OF CHEMICAL OPTIMIZATION** (graphic asset `296_007CAM_1`) — *Regulatory Compliance:* compliance-first; historical client data; built-in safety margins. *Transparency:* shared decision-making; real-time performance data; open-book chemical pricing. *Cost Management:* passive system upgrades; active dosage adjustments; savings without risk. *Innovation:* industry-leading digital optimization tools; predictive AI recommendations; proven multi-millions in cost savings.

- **Regulatory Compliance** – No contract operator places greater emphasis on compliance—or has a stronger track record—than Jacobs. We take environmental compliance extremely seriously, and our optimization methods reflect this. Chemical dosages are intentionally set with a safety factor to ensure *consistent* 100% regulatory compliance under all operating conditions. Our analysis of historical best practices at your facilities under all recorded past operating conditions where compliance was *always* achieved informs our future dosage settings, not where it was achieved 99% of the time. If a lower-cost option introduces compliance risk, we do not pursue it.

- **Transparency** – Jacobs values [CLIENT]'s technical expertise, and transparent decision-making is fundamental to a strong partnership. We'll openly share the rationale behind all chemical optimization decisions through participation in process control meetings, real-time performance dashboards, open-book vendor pricing, and discussion of potential operational approaches. We are proud operators who respect and learn from the insights our client partners bring. Optimization will be a shared process, not a black box, and building trust with operational staff takes time and constant attention.

- **Cost Management** – [CLIENT], a regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities, and Jacobs share the goal of producing fully compliant water at the lowest responsible cost. We achieve this through both **passive** strategies in the form of one-time process alterations (e.g., refining chemical injection points, improving mixing, optimizing chemical selection) and **active** strategies that rely on our professional operations staff to implement dosage adjustments as plant conditions change. Our proposal outlines several opportunities in both categories that can deliver measurable savings without compromising performance.

- **Innovation** – Jacobs leads the industry in leveraging our in-house wastewater engineering resources and digital tools towards plant optimization. With more than 50 active chemical optimization deployments nationwide, our Intelligent O&M platform has delivered millions of dollars in recurring cost savings for our clients. Unlike legacy dashboards that simply display yesterday's data, our tools provide forward-looking dosage recommendations operators can act on with confidence, achieving significant reductions in chemical use while maintaining perfect compliance. [CLIENT]'s support in providing historical data has already enabled us to model similar opportunities for your facilities.

> "Jacobs was selected for the O&M of this $25M/year contract based on their ability to deliver reliability and cost optimization across this highly complex reuse system. Jacobs' ability to optimize our chemical budget using their digital tools really made a difference in their selection."
> — **Eric Owens, Assistant General Manager, West Basin Municipal Water District** (callout graphic asset `297_007CAM_1`)

Because of our leadership and practical applications of digital water, Jacobs was approached by the University of Wisconsin-Madison to develop and deliver a 20-hour course about data science and AI tools for water utility professionals, through their College of Engineering professional extension program. The classes cover a very wide range of technical, legal and managerial topics. Classes begin June 2026.

Our **Optimization SME John Rickermann, PE** will guide analysis of [CLIENT]'s performance data to identify opportunities for chemical optimization. John also leads Jacobs' intelligent O&M innovations team, which will offer advanced analytics, machine learning, and AI tools to the Project Manager and the onsite staff to support process optimization, asset management, and maintenance and reduce costs.
