# Exhibit: Communications and Reporting Cadence with the Client

**Exhibit IV-9. Communications with [CLIENT] — when, what, and who.** Graphic asset ID `380_007CAM_1`. Meetings and discussions provide an open exchange of information to ensure transparency and collaboration.

| When | What | Content | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Daily** | Daily Reports | Daily O&M report including required biosolids reporting and any air permit/odor deviations reporting within 24 hours | [CLIENT] / Jacobs supervisors and project management teams |
| **Weekly** | Weekly Status Meetings; Weekly Report | Review previous week's events, discuss future week plans, document decisions; weekly compliance/trend report | [CLIENT] designated representative(s); Jacobs Project Manager / DMP |
| **Monthly** | Monthly Review | Progress updates and collaboration, review of potential CIP impacts to operations | [CLIENT] / Jacobs project management leads; members of committees as noted; Jacobs managers from operations, OT/IT, maintenance, and/or asset management / planning / scheduling teams (where applicable); [CLIENT] board members |
| **Monthly** | Operations Committee Meetings | Process optimization, shared costs (energy, chemicals, odor, water) | As above |
| **Monthly** | Maintenance Committee Meetings | Maintenance performance review (PM, PdM, spares, shared asset risk) and biosolids task coordination (Class B, hauling, contingencies as applicable) | As above |
| **Monthly** | Joint Asset Management Group Review Meetings | Bi-monthly review of asset condition trends/updates, PM schedule updates, shared capital investment prioritization | As above |
| **Monthly** | Joint CMMS Governance Committee Meetings | CMMS/NEXGEN oversight and governance (monthly during implementation) | As above |
| **Monthly** | Joint IT Governance Meetings; Monthly Reports | IT / OT / SCADA / cyber oversight and governance; monthly O&M report with required procurement, air permit, and biosolids reporting plus summary of any functional readiness tests; sewer pipe inspection records; monthly presentation to [CLIENT] board members | As above |
| **Quarterly** | Quarterly Business Review; Executive Committee Review | Review quarterly progress, discuss future objectives and opportunities for continuous improvement; strategic oversight, resolve issues, approve recommendations | [CLIENT] / Jacobs leadership and project management teams; Jacobs regional management; members of committees as noted |
| **Quarterly** | Energy Cost Sharing Subcommittee; Joint CMMS Governance Committee | Energy purchasing, rate strategies and forecasts; CMMS governance and shared data risk management (quarterly post-implementation) | As above |
| **Quarterly** | Joint IT Governance; Quarterly Reports | Quarterly IT / OT / SCADA / cyber governance review, RACI compliance; quarterly report including annual base fee allocation to unit processes; quarterly procurement reports; quarterly capital project labor support expenses; stormwater quarterly visual inspection report | As above |
| **Annually** | Annual Innovation Workshop | Review prior year's accomplishments and set new goals for continuous improvement in the coming year (includes applicable external Jacobs SMEs) | [CLIENT] and Jacobs leadership teams; [CLIENT] / Jacobs project management teams; members of committees as noted; stakeholders as requested |
| **Annually** | Annual Joint Operations & Maintenance Review | Review staffing adequacy, performance incentives, shared metrics and costs | As above |
| **Annually** | Odor Protocol & Response Meeting | Odor events review, update annual odor control plan (more meetings if needed) | As above |
| **Annually** | Joint Asset Management Group | Annual review of asset condition trends, shared capital priorities | As above |
| **Annually** | Biosolids Annual Review | Biosolids disposal strategy, contingency planning by Maintenance Committee | As above |
| **Annually** | Annual Reports & Plans | Annual base fee by major accounts (due April 30); annual O&M summary report with projected next year's budget, energy use report and forecast, chemical use forecast (due July 31); annual required reports and updates to O&M plans, SOPs and SMPs, Workforce Development Plan, CMAR, IPP, insurance, letter of credit, etc. per the agreement | As above |

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the cadence ladder and the joint-committee architecture. The committees—operations, maintenance, joint asset management, joint CMMS governance, joint IT governance, energy cost sharing, executive—are the mechanism that turns "we'll be transparent" into a governance structure an evaluator can score. The 24-hour deviation-reporting commitment is a strong, portable compliance promise.

Pursuit-specific: report due dates, the base fee allocation reporting, board presentation expectations, and any row tied to scope the pursuit lacks. Pairs with [PgM-Driven Cost Control, Monitoring, and Reporting](mmsd-pgm-cost-control-monitoring-and-reporting.md).
