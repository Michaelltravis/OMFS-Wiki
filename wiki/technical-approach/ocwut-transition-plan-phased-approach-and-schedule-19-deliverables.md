# Transition Plan and Operational Readiness Approach — Phased Execution to Schedule 19 Deliverables

A successful transition is about protecting performance, maintaining compliance, and giving [CLIENT] confidence that nothing slips during change. From the moment of award through day one and beyond, **Jacobs will deliver a structured, transparent, and fully resourced transition that keeps your facilities stable** while building the foundation for long-term performance.

We'll **mobilize immediately upon notification of award**, activating a dedicated transition team supported by regional and technical specialists. This will allow transition activities to move forward in parallel with no disruption to ongoing operations. Every action during this period will focus on one outcome: **uninterrupted, compliant, and fully staffed operations on day one.**

We also encourage [CLIENT] to speak directly with clients who have selected Jacobs as their operating partner. Their firsthand experience with our transition process and resulting strong outcomes provides valuable insight into what partnering with Jacobs truly looks like.

## Structured, Phased Transition with Clear Accountability

Jacobs will execute the transition through **coordinated phases that move from planning to full operational control** while maintaining continuous alignment with [CLIENT].

During early transition, our team will establish governance, confirm communication protocols, and begin detailed validation of permits, compliance history, SOPs, asset data, and maintenance records. We'll finalize staffing assignments, confirm certification coverage, coordinate facility and systems access, advance required draft and final plans, and maintain disciplined tracking of all transition deliverables and commitments.

Prior to the operational start date, we'll complete the baseline physical condition assessment and initial inventory jointly with [CLIENT], identify chemicals and polymer with sourcing agreements, secure biosolids management equipment by December 1, 2026, review existing SOPs and standard maintenance procedures and update them to the more stringent of Jacobs' standards or [CLIENT]'s Standards of Maintenance, with all procedures finalized by December 21, 2026, and confirm all staff positions are filled and transition training is complete—all before January 1, 2027.

Transfer-of-service activities will follow three defined windows per Schedule 19. In the month prior to startup, we'll complete operational monitoring on multiple shifts, verify systems and compliance readiness, and confirm inventories are in place. During the first week after startup, we'll execute the operational handoff and verify all workflows are functioning as intended. For the remainder of the first month, we'll stabilize operations, reinforce training, and confirm that performance, compliance, and reporting meet the required standards.

Jacobs will meet every Schedule 19 deliverable deadline, not because they are contractual requirements, but because each one represents a meaningful step toward **giving [CLIENT] a fully prepared, compliant, and stable operation on day one**. By the established deadlines on October 15, November 2, and December 1 and the operational start date, all required plans, staffing actions, equipment, inventories, and jointly signed reports will be complete and delivered.

Within 90 days of startup, Jacobs will deliver prioritized capital and reliability recommendations grounded in baseline condition assessment findings and aligned with [CLIENT]'s CIP budget development process.

Jacobs has successfully transitioned multiple complex water and wastewater systems from previous operators. Recent examples include West Basin Municipal Water District and Jackson Water (JXN Water), where Jacobs assumed operational responsibility while maintaining uninterrupted service and workforce continuity. In both cases, utility leadership has recognized the effectiveness of Jacobs’ disciplined transition approach and the positive impact on employee engagement and operational confidence.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the opening framing (transition = protecting performance, maintaining compliance, and client confidence), the parallel-mobilization commitment, the invitation to call reference clients about the transition specifically, and the three transfer-of-service windows. The "not because they are contractual requirements, but because…" construction is the section's signature move and should survive rewrites.

Pursuit-specific: every date and schedule citation. Rebuild the deadline list from the new contract's transition schedule; if the new RFP does not define milestones, propose them and keep the same structure.
