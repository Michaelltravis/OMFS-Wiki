# Biosolids and Heat-Dried Product Production Monitoring

Jacobs will apply the same level of precision and transparency to biosolids and [CLIENT]'s heat-dried biosolids product production as to liquid treatment operations. [CLIENT] is a regional sewerage district whose solids stream is converted to a marketed fertilizer product rather than disposed. Monitoring will include **solids feed rates, dewatering efficiency, polymer use, dryer energy demand, and production output** by shift and by day. We'll use dryer and furnace data already integrated into SCADA for continuous monitoring of temperature, airflow, emissions controls, and product quality.

We'll routinely review product temperature trends monitored and archived for **EPA 503 compliance** to confirm compliance while identifying opportunities to **improve energy efficiency and operational consistency**. Monthly reports will summarize production, energy use, chemical inputs, and product output, supporting reconciliation, planning, and sustainability objectives. We provide similar reporting for JXN Water, as described in **Exhibit IV-36**.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the opening assertion that solids get the same precision and transparency as the liquid stream. Clients with product lines expect the solids section to be an afterthought, so leading with parity is an easy differentiator. The monitored-variable list by shift and day, and the pairing of a compliance record (product temperature for EPA 503) with an efficiency opportunity in the same sentence, both travel.

Pursuit-specific: the product and its process. Substitute digestion, composting, lime stabilization, or hauling as the client's route requires, and replace the reference reporting example with one the client can call. Where the client sells the product, add the market-facing quality metrics they track.
