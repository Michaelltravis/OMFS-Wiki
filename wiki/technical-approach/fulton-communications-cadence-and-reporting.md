# JV Communications Cadence, Reporting, and Dashboard Transparency

JC Solutions is dedicated to open and transparent communication with [CLIENT], recognizing this as key to a successful, collaborative partnership. Our communication plan keeps [CLIENT] informed, involved, and confident in project management while fostering a productive working relationship. This commitment ensures that [CLIENT] is fully aware of plant performance, maintenance activities, and significant developments.

| Cadence | Communication and reporting | Participants |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | Open exchange of information to ensure transparency and collaboration; Daily O&M Report | [CLIENT]/JC Solutions supervisors and project-management teams |
| Weekly | Review prior-week events; discuss future-week plans; document decisions; Weekly Compliance Report | Jacobs Project Manager and [CLIENT] designated representative |
| Monthly | Progress update and collaboration; review process and maintenance performance; review CIP and operational impacts; Monthly O&M Report; Monthly DMR for review; Monthly Subcontractor Utilization Report | [CLIENT]/JC Solutions project-management teams |
| Quarterly | Review quarterly process performance; discuss future objectives and continuous-improvement opportunities | Jacobs regional management and [CLIENT]/JC Solutions project-management teams |
| Annual | Review prior-year accomplishments and establish new goals and objectives for the following year to ensure continuous improvement; comprehensive report on operations, maintenance activities, compliance performance, and major accomplishments, highlighting key metrics, improvements, equipment replacements, and proposed optimizations for future efficiency; required annual financial reports | External Jacobs utility experts; [CLIENT] leadership team; [CLIENT]/JC Solutions project-management teams |

Beginning at Transition, JC Solutions will work with [CLIENT] to establish seamless communication channels and proactive engagement. These include informal daily updates for informed decision-making and rapid responses to emerging issues, as well as formal weekly, monthly, and quarterly meetings to discuss performance, maintenance schedules, and service updates. The Project Manager will be the key contact, ensuring regular communication with [CLIENT]'s Public Works Director or designated representatives, leadership, and the public as directed.

JC Solutions will deliver a comprehensive suite of reports to provide transparency into operational performance and regulatory compliance, giving [CLIENT] tools for proactive management and a successful partnership. By the fifth day of each month, we will deliver a comprehensive operating report with key operational data, compliance, staffing updates, safety metrics, maintenance activities, and significant alarms. This reporting provides visibility and supports the shared commitment to operational excellence.

Our Project Manager will also provide a detailed monthly Operations, Maintenance, and Asset Management Report covering O&M activities, staffing changes, training, health and safety, daily inspections, open maintenance work orders, equipment status, and permit compliance. We will confirm timely submission of the Georgia EPD Discharge Monitoring Report each month and immediately communicate permit violations or regulatory concerns, demonstrating transparency and regulatory compliance.

The annual report will comprehensively review the prior year's operations, capital improvements, financial performance, and plans for the upcoming year. It will be submitted by the end of the first quarter to guide long-term planning and investment decisions. We will also prepare severe-weather reports, wastewater verification, industrial monitoring, inspection reports, executive summaries, cost reviews, and specialized reports when unique circumstances warrant them.

[CLIENT] will have a customized reporting dashboard that tracks KPIs and provides real-time and historical trending information, including maintenance activity and work-order status, chemical usage, sampling, testing and compliance data, safety statistics, and more. It improves access and transparency into day-to-day facility status and complements the written reports. Our reporting approach affirms that communication and reporting remain aligned with [CLIENT]'s needs and expectations.

## Reuse guidance

Use as the governance and transparency core of a technical approach. Tailor cadence, reports, responsible parties, permit language, and KPI dashboard to the new contract. Preserve the full table only where the owner requires a formal communication rhythm.
