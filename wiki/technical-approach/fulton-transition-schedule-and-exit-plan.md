# Transition Schedule and Exit Transition Plan

Following the initial transition, we will enter a comprehensive integration phase. This critical period focuses on aligning all systems and processes with JC Solutions’ operational framework. Key activities will include extensive training sessions for all staff, updates to SOPs, and complete system integration. The final phase spans 12 months and is dedicated to finalizing all transition activities. Prior to implementation, our transition leadership will review the proposed transition schedule with [CLIENT] and modify it based on guidance and final contract award and execution dates.

| Phase | Timing | Core activities |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-start transition | 15 days prior to start | Finalize staffing plans, documentation, transition plans, and communication with existing staff. |
| Initial transition | 30 days starting at commencement | Shadow existing staff; conduct interviews and workshops; complete safety review, training needs, data, inventory, staffing, and systems setup. |
| Full transition and integration | 90 days from start date | Extensive staff training, SOP updates, and complete system integration. |
| Long-term transition | 12 months from start date | Finalize transition activities, monitor performance, gather feedback, and adjust as needed. |
| Extended support | Ongoing | Maintain project support and transition readiness. |

The transition schedule assigns task leads and start and completion dates to contract negotiations, chartering meetings, communications and reporting, staffing, process systems, contingency plans, technology and data systems, employee workshops and interviews, safety review, insurance and documentation, training plans, facility data review, inventory and asset assessment, emergency response, maintenance and asset management plans, QA/QC protocols, and extended SME support.

Although an Exit Transition Plan is something we hope will not be necessary, JC Solutions has developed a robust plan to ensure continuity of operations, seamless data and knowledge transfer, and accurate documentation. In accordance with the contract, JC Solutions will develop a detailed Exit Transition Plan within 90 days of the Commencement Date. This plan will outline all handback requirements and initiate transition activities no later than nine months prior to the end of the O&M term.

JC Solutions’ Exit Transition Plan will align with all requirements outlined in the RFP, Exhibit 6, and applicable contract articles. The plan includes detailed roles, responsibilities, and timelines to coordinate effectively with [CLIENT] and any successor operator. JC Solutions will provide a complete set of updated management plans, operating manuals, and historical maintenance records; a master inventory of project records; regulatory documentation such as permits, inspection reports, and Discharge Monitoring Reports; final asset-condition reports; inventory and registration documentation; and comparison of final asset conditions to baseline records.

JC Solutions will provide exit packages, training-needs assessments, interviews, and knowledge transfer for staff. Additionally, JC Solutions will waive non-compete restrictions solely for the purpose of enabling staff transitions to [CLIENT] or its selected contractor. All active permits, compliance documentation, and safety procedures will be transferred to [CLIENT]. JC Solutions will conduct a comprehensive compliance audit within 30 days of departure and provide continued support for regulatory reporting for up to 30 days post-departure.

JC Solutions will ensure uninterrupted service throughout the transition. Three months prior to contract end, we will conduct an operational readiness review to confirm tools, spare parts, and consumables are stocked for at least four weeks post-handover; vendor, service, and utility accounts are reconciled and properly closed; and final accounting tasks are completed and documented. As permitted by the contract, JC Solutions is prepared to provide up to 180 days of extended support post-exit at [CLIENT]’s request.

JC Solutions will maintain transition readiness throughout the contract by conducting annual updates to the Exit Transition Plan. These updates will reflect operational changes, system upgrades, or new regulatory obligations, ensuring the final handover is well-supported and executable with minimal disruption.
