# Facility Process Control Priority Matrix

**EXHIBIT 1-11.** *Process Control Priority Matrix by Facility* (graphic 113_009385)

| FACILITY | WHAT WE FOUND | WHAT WE'LL DO |
|---|---|---|
| **NORTH CANADIAN WWTP** | Poor air distribution across aeration basins; no blower VFDs; DO probes only at influent and end of train | Expand DO profiling across all basins; right-size basin service to actual loading |
| **NORTH CANADIAN WWTP** | Recent biological upset requiring plant re-seeding | Implement SRT-based wasting to stabilize solids inventory and biological activity |
| **NORTH CANADIAN WWTP** | Phase 3 plant (effectively just a flow equalization basin) discharges directly to combined effluent | Solids flux and state point analysis to protect clarifiers; manage FEB returns |
| **NORTH CANADIAN WWTP** | Sulfur dioxide system cannot match chlorine demand during high-flow conditions | Establish dosing protocols that maintain compliant disinfection independent of SO₂ capacity limit |
| **NORTH CANADIAN WWTP** | Floating sludge in Phase II chlorine contact basin; permanent sludge judge in place | Evaluate step feed implementation; assess CEPT for extreme wet weather protection |
| **DEER CREEK WWTP** | Light MLSS with excessive DO and early-stage foaming — indicators of young sludge age and over-aeration | Implement SRT-based wasting; reduce over-aeration to match actual biological demand |
| **DEER CREEK WWTP** | RAS pumps have VFDs but operating in static mode, not flow-paced | Flow-pace RAS pumps; transition to active solids management |
| **DEER CREEK WWTP** | Sand filters being manually backwashed | Restore automated filtration control with turbidity and TSS monitoring |
| **DEER CREEK WWTP** | Sludge holding tanks receive WAS and primary sludge with no mixing or aeration — source of extreme H₂S in belt press room | Improve timing, blending, and frequency of sludge transfers and pressing operations |
| **DEER CREEK WWTP** | Anoxic selector zones offline since 2019 (submersible mixers replaced, ready) | Re-implement selectors to improve settling and reduce aeration demand |
| **CHISHOLM CREEK WWTP** | All blowers running with excessive DO across aeration basins | Right-size aeration to actual biological demand; reduce energy consumption |
| **CHISHOLM CREEK WWTP** | Both headworks bar screens out of service | Restore screening capability; coordinate repair or replacement |
| **CHISHOLM CREEK WWTP** | Backwash return tank overflow piped directly to effluent — compliance risk during overflow events | Closely manage overflow risk; implement monitoring protocol |
| **CHISHOLM CREEK WWTP** | Carbon odor scrubbers not renewed or replaced | Assess condition and replace carbon media |
| **CHISHOLM CREEK WWTP** | Fewest historical permit excursions — most resilient facility in the system | Proactive risk reduction and stabilization, not recovery |
| **SOUTH CANADIAN WWTP** | Brand new activated sludge facility anticipated online July 2026 | Establish SRT targets and operating discipline from Day One |
| **SOUTH CANADIAN WWTP** | PSI MicroClor onsite hypochlorite generators have proprietary cartridges ~$20K each | Apply direct PSI operating experience from Jacobs' Pampa, TX facility to startup and cartridge management |
| **SOUTH CANADIAN WWTP** | New Huber disc thickeners — operations staff unfamiliar with equipment | Develop SOPs and deliver targeted operator training before startup |
| **SOUTH CANADIAN WWTP** | New Five Star cloth filtration system (60 total filters) | Establish automated backwash control; stock spare actuators |
| **SOUTH CANADIAN WWTP** | Oldest Komline Sanderson belt press in the [CLIENT] system remains in service | Optimize polymer dosing and belt conditioning to improve cake quality |
