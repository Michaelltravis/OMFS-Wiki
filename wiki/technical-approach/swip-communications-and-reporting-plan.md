# Communications and Reporting Plan — Structure, Cadence, and Deliverables

Jacobs is dedicated to open and transparent communication with [CLIENT], recognizing that this is key to a successful and collaborative partnership. Our communications plan will ensure the client remains fully informed, engaged, and confident in the management of the facilities—fostering trust, responsiveness, and mutual accountability. From day one, Jacobs will prioritize real-time visibility into plant performance, maintenance status, and regulatory compliance.

## Communication Plan

Starting at transition, Jacobs will collaborate with the client to establish clear communication protocols and engagement schedules. These will include informal daily check-ins to support responsive decision-making and issue resolution, along with formal weekly, monthly, and quarterly meetings to review performance, discuss maintenance schedules, and provide project updates, as illustrated below.

**Communication Structure**

| Cadence | Type | What | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily | Meetings & Discussions | Open exchange of information to ensure transparency and collaboration | Client Project Manager / Jacobs CPO |
| Weekly | Weekly Meeting | Review the previous week's events; discuss future week plans; document decisions | Jacobs CPO and client-designated representative |
| Monthly | Monthly Review | Progress update and collaboration; review process performance; review maintenance performance; review CIP and impact to operations | Client Project Manager / Jacobs CPO and Management Team |
| Monthly | Monthly Reports | Monthly O&M Report; Monthly DMR for review; Monthly Operations, Maintenance and Asset Mgmt Report | Client Project Manager / Jacobs CPO and Management Team |
| Quarterly | Quarterly Reports | Quarterly Regulatory Compliance Reports (per permit requirements); other discussions or reports based on client's preferences | Jacobs CPO and Management Team; Client Project Manager and staff as desired |
| Annually | Annual Innovation Workshop | Review prior year's accomplishments while establishing new goals and objectives for the following year to ensure continuous improvement | External Jacobs utility experts; Client Project Manager and Leadership Team; Jacobs' Leadership, CPO, and Management Team |
| Annually | Annual Reports | Comprehensive report summarizing the year's O&M activities including performance evaluations, capital and asset replacement summaries, and cost reporting | Jacobs' Leadership, CPO, and Management Team |

**CPO Mack Mckenzie** will serve as the client's primary point of contact, ensuring consistent communication with the client's designated manager, public works leadership, and other client stakeholders or community representatives, as directed.

## Reporting and Transparency

Jacobs will deliver a comprehensive suite of reports to ensure transparency into operational performance and regulatory compliance, providing the client with tools to proactively monitor and guide facility operations. These reports will also support planning, budgeting, and community engagement.

Key reporting deliverables include:

- **Monthly Operating Report.** Delivered by the fifth day of each month, this report will summarize key operational data including flows, volumes, pressures, fouling, compliance status, staffing, safety metrics, maintenance activities, and notable events or alarms. It reinforces our commitment to visibility, accountability, and continual performance review.
- **Monthly Operations, Maintenance, and Asset Management Report.** Prepared by the CPO, this report will cover all core operational areas, including PM/PdM activities, asset performance, staff training, safety meetings, inspection findings, work order status, and permit compliance. It will ensure the client has a clear understanding of asset health and facility status.
- **Monthly Discharge Monitoring Report.** Jacobs will confirm timely submission of the discharge monitoring report to the state water board through the applicable electronic reporting system. Any permit exceedances or compliance concerns will be communicated immediately, along with corrective actions and follow-up.
- **Annual Report.** Our annual report will be a comprehensive review of the previous year's operations, including O&M performance evaluations, capital improvements, financial performance, and plans for the upcoming year. We will furnish the client a draft within 30 days and final within 60 days after completion of each calendar year of operations.

**Additional Reports.** Jacobs will also prepare supplemental reports as requested by the client, such as:
- Recycled water quality and end-user summaries
- Executive summaries for public briefings
- Cost tracking or special project documentation
- Sustainability Report — energy and chemical usage

**Customized Digital Reporting Dashboard.** Jacobs will implement a secure, web-based dashboard tailored specifically to the client. This dashboard can provide real-time and historical trending on:
- Key performance indicators (KPIs)
- Work order completion and maintenance activity
- Chemical usage and inventory
- Sampling and compliance results
- Safety and training metrics
- Operational alerts and alarms

The dashboard complements our formal written reports, giving client staff immediate visibility into facility operations, performance, and compliance in real time. Our reporting strategy reinforces Jacobs' commitment to transparency, accountability, and open communication. It ensures client leadership is equipped with the data and insight needed to make timely, informed decisions and demonstrates our shared commitment to operational excellence and public trust.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the cadence table structure (daily/weekly/monthly/quarterly/annual with who/what) and the reporting-deliverables list are strong, comprehensive, and reusable across nearly any O&M communications-plan response. Pursuit-specific: adapt report names to the target facility's actual permit/report types (e.g., DMR vs. other discharge-monitoring formats) and confirm the client's preferred point-of-contact role title.
