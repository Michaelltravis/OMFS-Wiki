# Approach Summary — O&M Plan Built on the Client's Shared Priorities

Jacobs shares [CLIENT]'s vision for a forward-looking operations and maintenance (O&M) partnership grounded in transparency, collaboration, offsite support, and disciplined delivery of your expectations. Based on extensive engagement with [CLIENT] staff and a deep understanding of your facilities and system, we've developed a comprehensive O&M plan driven by key operating strategies that directly support your priorities—regulatory compliance, protection of [CLIENT]'s biosolids product brand, overflow prevention, cost and operational efficiency, an integrated approach to managing your system, safety, resiliency, and measurable progress toward your 2035 Vision.

Real-time, evidence-based reporting and open communication will build trust and foster a one-team partnership that aligns [CLIENT] and Jacobs around shared vision, goals, and solutions. Our objective: to drive consistent performance, shared prioritization, accountability, and resilience.

Above all, we'll operate as a true partner so [CLIENT] has a "no surprises" path to **reliable, sustained performance** and **long-term community value**. **Exhibit IV-1** illustrates your priorities to achieve excellence and our O&M plan.

The plan is organized around the operating strategies that carry those priorities:

- **Transparent Program Management (PgM) Framework** — real-time, evidence-based reporting and open communication that build trust and a one-team partnership.
- **Embedded Leadership & Engaged Support** — an empowered leadership team backed by national staff to strengthen accountability and performance.
- **Rigorous Cost Management** — disciplined, transparent cost management with joint governance to validate savings and share measurable improvements over time.
- **Wet Weather Management** — proactive, system-wide coordination that leverages forecasting to manage tunnel storage, pumping, and water reclamation facility (WRF) operations.
- **Advanced Digital Tools** — operator decisions guided and supported by digital tools for cost-effective and best-practice O&M.
- **Biosolids Management** — optimized, energy-efficient solids processing that will enhance reliability and renewable energy use.
- **Energy and Chemical Optimization** — predictive, system-wide optimization that lowers cost, maintains compliance, and accelerates progress toward your 2035 Vision's renewable energy goals.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the structure — shared vision sentence, priorities named in the client's own words, a short strategy-per-priority list, then a "no surprises" partnership close. The seven operating strategies are Jacobs-standard and transfer to any large O&M pursuit.

Pursuit-specific: the priority list, the long-range plan name, the branded biosolids product, and any tunnel/inline-storage language (drop for clients without deep-tunnel storage).
