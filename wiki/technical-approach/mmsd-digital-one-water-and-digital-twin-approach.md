# Digital One Water and the Digital Twin for Systemwide Optimization

At the core of our approach is giving operators clear, actionable insights while giving [CLIENT] — a large regional sewerage district — full visibility into what we see, decide, and do. Jacobs' Digital One Water platform turns system data into decision-ready information so operators can manage wet-weather flows, optimize treatment processes, sustain reliable production of [CLIENT]'s biosolids product, and anticipate equipment issues before they occur.

A key component of our toolkit is a digital twin, which provides a dynamic, real-time model of [CLIENT]'s infrastructure. It enables holistic management under varying conditions and supports adaptive operational decision-making, continuous compliance improvement, optimization of energy, nutrient removal, resource recovery, and strategic CSO mitigation. The digital twin does not automate plant operations. It elevates situational awareness by allowing [CLIENT] and Jacobs to evaluate operational scenarios together and respond proactively to changing conditions. **Exhibit IV-29** presents the benefits of Jacobs' digital twin to [CLIENT].

Real-time monitoring, PdM, and integrated controls will help the teams quickly understand current conditions, identify root causes, and take timely action. These tools don't replace operator judgment—they reinforce it by pairing real-time analytics and machine learning with proven operating experience to support proactive decision-making.

Our Digital One Water connects data across the full wastewater system—from the collection system and inline storage system to both water reclamation facilities—to drive measurable outcomes in permit compliance, CSO reduction, energy optimization and generation, enhanced biosolids product production, and lower carbon footprint. As shown in **Exhibit IV-28**, our integrated digital framework converts raw data into strategic insights, improving efficiency, strengthening compliance, and reducing operating risk. With this approach, Jacobs will help [CLIENT] extend its legacy of leadership in wastewater management with a system that stays resilient, transparent, and ahead of emerging challenges. Our Digital One Water Approach transforms raw data into strategic insights, helping [CLIENT] operate more efficiently, sustainably, and resiliently.

By leveraging the digital twin's capabilities, we'll partner with [CLIENT] to elevate performance, ensure system reliability, and build a foundation for sustainable, future-ready operations and a clear roadmap for system expansion and upgrades. In addition to our digital twin capabilities, we intend to leverage other advanced digital tools we have developed to support operators in making the right decisions in collaboration with [CLIENT]. **Exhibit IV-30** presents a summary of these tools, how we intend to implement them, and the benefits they will provide [CLIENT].

Digital One Water Approach** (asset ID `214_007CAM_1`) maps three capability sets onto a set of outcomes: One Water expertise (drinking water and reuse, wastewater, pumps and pump systems, conveyance and storage, water resources); lifecycle expertise (planning, design, construction, operations, compliance); and digital expertise (data analytics, transformation advisory, digital twins, AI and machine learning, integrated institutional knowledge) — producing Digital One Water outcomes in cybersecurity, predictive maintenance, energy and chemicals optimization, effluent quality management, operator training, automated operations, pipeline defect detection, overflow mitigation, and flood forecasting.
