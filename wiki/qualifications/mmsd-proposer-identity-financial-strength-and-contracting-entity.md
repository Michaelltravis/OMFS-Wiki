# Proposer Identity, Contracting Entity, and Financial Strength Opener

## Identity of the Proposer and Guarantor; Financial Assurances Requirements

Consistent client satisfaction and unmatched industry reputation has fueled our growth over the last 75 years, and for over 40 years in our Operations & Maintenance (O&M) business. Jacobs' historical growth, delivery excellence, and solid financial history provide us with the ability to meet the requirements of the largest and most complex projects and programs in the world. Our stable revenue and strong backlog, continuing profitability, and overall financial resources demonstrate our financial strength.

The combination of our robust financial infrastructure, disciplined management practices, and extensive experience with major projects and programs ensures our clients receive reliable, high-quality services. Our consistent financial performance underpins our capacity to deliver on commitments and underscores our reliability as a trusted partner.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the two framing paragraphs and the structure of the identity table (contracting entity, parent guarantor, corporate type, relationship to parent, authorizing officer). The OMI-is-a-wholly-owned-subsidiary-of-JEG language is the sentence clients look for and should not be reworded.

Pursuit-specific: the named contact person and authorizing officer, their contact details, and the tenure figures ("75 years", "over 40 years"). Confirm which Jacobs legal entity is contracting — on joint-venture pursuits the proposer line changes and the guaranty structure changes with it.
