# Maintenance and Asset Management Capability with Case Studies

Jacobs has implemented more than 100 asset management programs across North America, and our approach is built on a straightforward progression: get preventive maintenance locked in first, layer in predictive technologies (i.e., vibration analysis, motor signature analysis, infrared thermography, ultrasound, and oil analysis), and use the data to move toward condition-based decisions rather than fixed-interval assumptions. Corrective work orders open within 24 hours; we target closure within 30 days.

NexGen, being implemented now through Jacobs consulting, gives [CLIENT] full visibility into work order status, asset history, and maintenance costs. Our Asset Replacement Modeling (ARM) tool uses condition scores, asset age, and maintenance history to support capital planning decisions.

When Jacobs assumed operations of **Baton Rouge's North and South WWTPs** in 2023, the facilities faced a significant backlog of deferred maintenance, repair, and replacement needs. We immediately conducted a detailed inventory and condition assessment, developed targeted strategies to address critical equipment deficiencies, and launched a rigorous belt filter press review and repair program, **bringing the presses back to full effectiveness and significantly improving the system's capacity to handle biosolids**. We also conducted a comprehensive facility condition assessment and capital repair and replacement plan, aligning recommendations with the City's funding availability.

Since 2018, Jacobs has operated the **City of Waterbury's 27-MGD WWTP, 320 miles of sanitary sewer, and 20 pump stations** under a comprehensive O&M and asset management plan. We deployed CMMS from the start, implemented proactive maintenance practices, and supported capital improvements including a **$32M phosphorus upgrade and an ongoing $25M tertiary filtration project, all without service interruptions**. The City estimates up to **$12.7M in savings** compared to its prior operating approach, and the facility has maintained **99.57% permit compliance** over the past 5 years.

**Advanced Maintenance and Long-Term Reliability in Vancouver.** Jacobs has operated Vancouver's two wastewater treatment facilities (44 MGD total capacity) since 2016, when we replaced a 37-year incumbent operator. Our maintenance program integrates CMMS with Augury vibration analysis and Fluke SmartView thermal imaging, aligned with ISO 55001 asset management standards. We delivered a $25M multi-year SCADA modernization with zero service interruptions, earned $900,000 in utility rebates through targeted energy initiatives, and have recorded zero lost-time safety incidents since contract start. The facilities have received multiple NACWA Platinum Peak Performance Awards and recognition from the Washington Department of Ecology.

On the **Grand Traverse County Septage Receiving and Treatment Facility**, we helped the client address infrastructure and process issues tied to legacy design and startup challenges. After the facility's aeration basin collapse, our team supported emergency response and cleanup, then helped restore stable operations with a focus on reliability and maintainability. When the original membrane and solids handling systems proved difficult to maintain, **we reconfigured the treatment approach to reduce maintenance-intensive processes, simplify equipment needs, and improve long-term operability**. By converting the facility to operate as a sequencing batch reactor (SBR), discontinuing problematic fine screening, and repurposing existing equipment, **we reduced maintenance demands while lowering chemical and energy use and supporting more reliable performance**.
