# Southern California Reuse Expertise — Named SME Bench and Regulatory Relationships

## Proven advanced treatment and reuse experience in California

In Southern California, our team members provide treatment process support for projects like the Pure Water Project Las Virgenes-Triunfo and Chino Basin Programs, and Jacobs is the progressive design-builder for the Donald C. Tillman Advanced Water Purification Facility. Our subject matter experts in potable reuse and advanced treatment include Melanie Holmer, Michael Hwang, Jim Lozier, Larry Schimmoller, and Paul Swaim, who support these projects and will be available to [CLIENT]'s project — a coastal Southern California municipal advanced water treatment and potable reuse facility — to provide process expertise should challenges or opportunities arise.

Our Southern California teams have built strong relationships with the Los Angeles Regional Water Quality Control Board, the Division of Drinking Water, and have been very involved in the development of direct potable reuse regulations. Melanie Holmer served on WateReuse California's DPR working group, reviewing every draft of the DPR regulations and providing input on potential comments and project impacts. She is also supporting clients who are exploring the potential to shift from an Indirect Potable Reuse (IPR) project to a DPR project, similar to [CLIENT]'s ambitions. Melanie has also served as permitting lead or support for multiple IPR projects in planning, design, or operations in California. This familiarity will help streamline reporting, permitting, and compliance at every stage of the contract.

## Reuse guidance

The named SME roster and regulatory relationships (LA RWQCB, Division of Drinking Water, WateReuse California DPR working group) are Jacobs' actual standing bench and relationships — reusable for any Southern California potable or indirect potable reuse pursuit, but confirm the named individuals' current availability and project assignments before reuse, as staffing changes. The three named Southern California reference programs (Pure Water Project Las Virgenes-Triunfo, Chino Basin, Donald C. Tillman AWPF) are checkable and should be retained for any California reuse pursuit; substitute regionally closer programs elsewhere. If pursuing outside Southern California, replace the regional regulatory-body names with the correct state or regional equivalents and substitute SMEs from the appropriate regional bench.
