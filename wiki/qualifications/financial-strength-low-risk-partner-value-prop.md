# Financial Strength and Low-Risk Partner Positioning

Jacobs brings strong financial capacity and long-term stability to support [CLIENT] as a trusted O&M partner. With approximately $16 billion in annual revenue and a solid backlog, Jacobs has the financial strength to meet the requirements of large and complex programs, while also delivering the focused experience and hands-on expertise needed to successfully operate and manage systems comparable in size and complexity to [CLIENT]'s. Our Operations Management and Facilities Services (OMFS) group generates more than $100 million annually in water and wastewater O&M revenue, demonstrating the depth, scale, and reliability of our operations portfolio.

**OMFS revenue for the past 5 years (Exhibit 3-4, as of the Hull proposal — refresh annually):**

| Year | OMFS Revenue |
|---|---|
| 2025 | $495M |
| 2024 | $485M |
| 2023 | $435M |
| 2022 | $390M |
| 2021 | $376M |

Average revenue (2021–2025): $429M

Jacobs' annual audited financial reports are prepared in accordance with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP) and all relevant notes, and are included in our Form 10-K Annual Reports filed with the SEC. The Form 10-K reports for the last two fiscal years are available on Jacobs' Investor Relations – Filings and Reports page at https://invest.jacobs.com/financials/default.aspx#secfilings. Jacobs' financial stability, strong retained earnings, disciplined risk-management practices, and substantial bonding and insurance capacity ensure reliable, uninterrupted service throughout the term of the contract.

*Note: the Section 3 corporate-profile sidebar (Company at a Glance) states approximately $12 billion in FY24 revenue and a backlog of more than $2.4 billion, while the Financial Qualifications narrative states approximately $16 billion in annual revenue — both figures appear verbatim in the source proposal (pp. 6 and 10 respectively) and are reproduced faithfully here rather than reconciled. Confirm the correct current-year consolidated revenue figure with Jacobs corporate finance/IR before using either number in a live proposal.*

**Closing value-proposition callout pattern** (suitable for a boxed pull-quote at the end of a qualifications section):

> Jacobs offers [CLIENT] a proven, low-risk partner with demonstrated experience operating similar facilities in [REGION/REGULATORY CONTEXT] and comparable communities. Our integrated approach, experienced personnel, and deep technical support ensure reliable operations, strong regulatory performance, and the flexibility to respond to evolving system needs and community expectations.

## Reuse guidance

The financial-qualifications paragraph is largely boilerplate that most O&M RFPs require in some form — reusable near-verbatim. Per current sanitization policy, financial-qualification figures are kept in the wiki (they are not client-specific), but they are still time-sensitive: refresh the revenue, backlog, and 5-year OMFS revenue table from the latest corporate financial fact sheet before each new proposal rather than assuming the figures above are still current. The closing callout pattern generalizes well beyond financials — it can close out an entire Firm Qualifications section, not just the financial subsection. Pair with `omfs-corporate-scale-and-om-portfolio-proof-points.md` for the scale argument this section leans on, and with `corporate-entity-legal-qualifications.md` for the adjacent entity/legal/litigation content from the same Section 3.
