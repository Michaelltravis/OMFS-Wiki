# Financial Strength, Audited Reporting, and Low-Risk Partner Positioning

Jacobs brings **strong financial capacity and long-term stability** to support [CLIENT], a coastal New England municipal wastewater utility, as a trusted O&M partner. With approximately **$16 billion in annual revenue** and a solid backlog, Jacobs has the financial strength to meet the requirements of large and complex programs, while also delivering the focused experience and hands-on expertise needed to successfully operate and manage systems comparable in size and complexity to [CLIENT]'s. Our Operations Management and Facilities Services (OMFS) group generates more than **$100 million annually in water and wastewater O&M revenue**, demonstrating the depth, scale, and reliability of our operations portfolio. **Exhibit 3-4** presents the OMFS group's revenue over the past five years.

**Exhibit 3-4. OMFS revenue for the past 5 years**

| 2025 | 2024 | 2023 | 2022 | 2021 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $495M | $485M | $435M | $390M | $376M |

Average revenue: $429M. (Graphic ID `118_Hull_0091KO_2`.)

Jacobs' annual audited financial reports, prepared in accordance with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP) and all relevant notes, are included in our Form 10-K Annual Reports filed with the SEC. The **Form 10-K reports for the last two fiscal years** are included with this submission in accordance with the RFP's addenda and are also available on Jacobs' Investor Relations – Filings and Reports page at: https://invest.jacobs.com/financials/default.aspx#secfilings. **Jacobs' financial stability, strong retained earnings, disciplined risk management practices, and substantial bonding and insurance capacity ensure reliable, uninterrupted service throughout the term of the contract.**

**Closing value-proposition callout** (the boxed pull-quote that ends the qualifications section):

> Jacobs offers [CLIENT] a proven, low-risk partner with demonstrated experience operating similar facilities in EPA Region 1 and comparable coastal communities. Our integrated approach, experienced personnel, and deep technical support ensure reliable operations, strong regulatory performance, and the flexibility to respond to evolving system needs and community expectations.

## Reuse guidance

The financial-qualifications paragraph is near-boilerplate and reusable almost verbatim across O&M pursuits. The reusable technique is the three-layer proof: corporate revenue answers "are you solvent," the OMFS five-year series answers "is the O&M business itself healthy and growing," and the 10-K reference answers "can we verify it ourselves" — a small municipal evaluator worried about betting a 5- or 10-year contract on an outside operator needs all three, not just the first. Keep the bonding-and-insurance sentence; it is often the specific reassurance a procurement officer is scanning for. The closing callout generalizes well beyond financials and can close an entire Firm Qualifications section — swap the region and regulatory framing for the pursuit's own.
