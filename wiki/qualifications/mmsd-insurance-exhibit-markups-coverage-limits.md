**10. Make the following changes to Paragraph 9, Subsection (h):**

(h) Cybersecurity Insurance. Company shall maintain during the Term of the Service Agreement (and any extensions thereof) Cyber Liability Insurance with a minimum combined single limit of $2,000,000 per ~~occurrence~~ <u>claim</u> and in the aggregate, including but not limited to coverage for:

- i. Data breaches and privacy violations, including costs of notification, credit monitoring, and legal defense.
- ii. Network security breaches, including ransomware attacks, denial-of-service attacks, and system intrusions.
- iii. Errors and omissions related to cybersecurity services.
- iv. Media liability.
- v. Regulatory investigations and penalties.
- vi. Business interruption due to a cyber incident.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the coverage architecture Jacobs offers on a large multi-facility O&M contract — $1,000,000 combined single limit per accident on commercial automobile including client-owned fleet vehicles operated by the Company, $25,000,000 per occurrence and general aggregate umbrella/excess when combined with primary limits, $5,000,000 per claim or loss and $5,000,000 annual aggregate contractors pollution liability, and $2,000,000 per claim and in the aggregate cyber liability with the six enumerated cyber coverage grants. Also reusable are the recurring conversions: "per occurrence" to "per claim" on claims-made lines, continuous renewal for three years in place of purchased tail coverage, and excess-over rather than drop-down endorsement on exhausted underlying aggregates.

Pursuit-specific: paragraph and subsection numbering, whether the client's fleet is in scope, and the limits themselves, which must be reconfirmed each pursuit with risk and insurance.
