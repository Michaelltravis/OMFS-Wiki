# JC Solutions JV Additional Management and Leadership

JC Solutions (a Jacobs/CERM JV) places significant emphasis on transparency so [CLIENT] can be aware of all aspects of operations. Every management-team member will play an active role in building a partnership focused on collaboration, communication, transparency, and acting as an extension of the client's team and a contributing member of its community. The team will foster accountability and performance to provide reliable, compliant, and safe operations every day.
