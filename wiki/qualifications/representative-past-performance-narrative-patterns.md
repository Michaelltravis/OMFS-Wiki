# Representative Past-Performance Narrative Patterns (Recipe)

Every reference write-up in the source proposal's Exhibit 3-5 follows the same three-move structure: **Services Provided → Facilities Managed → Accomplishments**. The first move states the operating model, the second states the physical scope in comparable units, and the third states what changed because Jacobs was there. A blurb missing the third move reads as a contract listing rather than a reference.

**Pattern 1 — Holistic utility partnership takeover.** Open with the operating philosophy ("a holistic approach to utility operations, combining operations, asset management, technical services, planning, and regional support"), then the contract term and the physical scope (plant capacity, sanitary sewer miles, pump station count), then the partnership framing ("the City and Jacobs have worked as partners to manage, improve, and optimize..."). Land the accomplishment on a client-attributed outcome figure and a capital-program role, and close with a community-stewardship detail (volunteer hours on a river cleanup) that shows the operator is part of the town, not a vendor. Prose source: [../past-performance/project-waterbury-ct.md](../past-performance/project-waterbury-ct.md).

**Pattern 2 — Smooth incumbent-to-Jacobs operations transition.** Open with the selection (note unanimity or a competitive displacement if it applies), name the transition date and the fact that full operating responsibility was assumed from the prior operator, then list the scope. Build the accomplishment out of investments made rather than years served — a design-build award on the plant, a process optimization with a stated mechanism (wood amendment-to-biosolids ratio, blower operation by pile temperature), an odor control and dispersion study, new screening equipment — so a young contract reads as "actively investing," not "unproven." Prose source: [../past-performance/project-southbridge-ma.md](../past-performance/project-southbridge-ma.md).

**Pattern 3 — Turnaround inside a fixed early window.** Anchor the accomplishment to a stated window ("in the first 18 months of the contract") and make it a list of concrete, physical, verifiable actions — in-house clarifier rehabilitations, corrective action items inherited from the previous operator closed out, disinfection system upgrades, six 30-yard containers of trash and debris removed, landscaping and painting across the facility and pump stations. Physical, countable early wins are the most credible incumbent-displacement evidence available, because a skeptical evaluator can picture them. Prose source: [../past-performance/project-westerly-ri.md](../past-performance/project-westerly-ri.md).

**Pattern 4 — Regional system O&M with a workforce-development spine.** State the population served and the full service list (24/7 operations, maintenance, lab, regulatory compliance, industrial pretreatment, administrative support, all on computerized operations and asset-management systems), then describe the transition from the previous provider, then make training and retention the accomplishment — "implemented targeted training programs that strengthened workforce capability and retention" — before the process outcomes (reduced solids volume, chemical usage, and energy demand; thermal hydrolysis and Class A biosolids). Use where the client's own staff are being transitioned.

**Pattern 5 — Multi-decade DBO partnership.** Lead with the start year to make longevity the headline, then compress the middle years into the operating model (comprehensive operational strategy, computerized maintenance management, integrated capital planning and process optimization). Put the compliance restoration early ("restored regulatory compliance and resolved enforcement actions early in the contract and have maintained consistent compliance since"), then stack the capital upgrades and the optimization outcomes, and close on asset life extension. Longevity is only persuasive when paired with what changed during it.

## Reuse guidance

Use this recipe as a QC checklist over a draft reference write-up, not as text to paste. Draft from the verbatim sources — [../past-performance/client-references.md](../past-performance/client-references.md) for the Exhibit 3-5 and Exhibit 3-3 tables (real client names, owner contacts, sizes, years of service, and outcomes) and the `../past-performance/project-*.md` files for the expanded narratives — then check the draft against the three-move structure and the pattern that fits the reference's situation. Retain facility specifics (MGD, collection-system miles, pump and lift station counts, population served) and every outcome figure, since scope comparability and quantified results are exactly what make a reference persuasive; confirm each reference's confidentiality marking and contact details with the account team before external use. Pair with [reference-portfolio-comparability-and-similar-facilities.md](reference-portfolio-comparability-and-similar-facilities.md) for the prose that brackets the reference exhibit.
