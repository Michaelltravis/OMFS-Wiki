# Representative Past-Performance Narrative Patterns

**Reference-section intro framing pattern** (use to introduce the reference-projects exhibit before the individual project write-ups): Our [REGION] team partners with communities to successfully operate and maintain wastewater treatment facilities comparable in size and complexity to [CLIENT]'s system. While not all of the reference projects are located within [CLIENT]'s specific regulatory region, they demonstrate our extensive experience operating similar facilities under comparable regulatory frameworks and performance requirements.

Reusable narrative structures drawn from strong past-performance write-ups, each following a **Services Provided / Facilities Managed / Accomplishments** structure with client and location details replaced by placeholders.

**Pattern 1 — Holistic utility partnership takeover**
[CLIENT], operator of a [X MGD] water pollution control facility with approximately [X] miles of sanitary sewer and [X] pump stations, engaged Jacobs to develop a holistic approach to utility operations — combining operations, asset management, technical services, planning, and regional support under a multi-year agreement. Since transition, Jacobs and the client have partnered to manage, improve, and optimize the facility, collection system, and related utilities, with close monitoring of treatment processes and equipment, proactive maintenance, staff training, and long-term facility planning. The partnership has supported major capital improvement programs (e.g., a phosphorus/nutrient removal upgrade) and community environmental stewardship initiatives (e.g., volunteer support for a local waterway cleanup program).

**Pattern 2 — Smooth incumbent-to-Jacobs operations transition**
Jacobs was selected to operate and maintain a [X MGD] WWTP, [X] miles of collection system, and [X] lift stations under a multi-year contract, assuming full operating responsibility from the prior operator on a defined transition date. Since transition, the team has made targeted investments to improve facility and system operability and performance — for example, optimizing biosolids/composting operations, completing an odor control and dispersion study, and supporting continuous improvement planning — while bringing subject-matter expertise in specialty areas (e.g., biosolids management, odor control) to support ongoing plant operations.

**Pattern 3 — Regional collection-system O&M with workforce-development focus**
Jacobs provides O&M and management services for a wastewater system serving approximately [X] residents, including 24/7 operations, maintenance, lab services, regulatory compliance, industrial pretreatment, and administrative support, all supported by computerized systems for operations and asset management. After transitioning operations from the previous provider, the team implemented targeted training programs that strengthened workforce capability and retention, optimized biosolids management (reducing solids volume, chemical usage, and energy demand), and supported implementation of advanced biosolids-processing technology — improving overall plant performance and providing a more sustainable, cost-effective long-term solution.
