# JC Solutions JV O&M Resources — Maintenance, Compliance, Digital, Laboratory, and Energy

JC Solutions' O&M resources, led by John Rickermann, will be accessible to the Project Manager and management team so they can draw on specialized expert resources when needed. They also support the Transition Team. Together, onsite and offsite experts provide ongoing training and mentoring to existing staff to enable continuous growth and awareness of industry practices in day-to-day work.

|---|---|
| **Liie Hill** — 17 years of experience; CIP Manager | Works day to day with O&M personnel and processes, bringing insight into the operational impacts of planned projects. Uses utility operation, design, construction, and permitting experience to provide solutions that help bring client vision to life. Integrates capital projects into working treatment plants. |
| **Graham Knowles** — 20 years of experience; Asset Management | Provides asset-management leadership, oversight, consulting services, and support for utility-facility vision.
