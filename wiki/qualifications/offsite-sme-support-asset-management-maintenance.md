## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-role pattern — an asset management program leader, a regional maintenance leader, and a condition assessment leader — is the reusable core of any off-site O&M support bench for a multi-facility wastewater contract. The bullet grammar is also reusable: two or three capability bullets in the present tense, then one bullet that names what the SME will do for this client.

Pursuit-specific: the names, years, licenses, and the closing "will support [CLIENT] with…" bullet. Verify certifications (CMRT, CRL, IAM, BAMI-I, ISO 14001 lead auditor) before use; they are the credibility layer that makes this table read as more than a list.
