# On-Site Sodium Hypochlorite Generation Experience

On-site sodium hypochlorite generation (OSHG) is already part of how Jacobs delivers compliant disinfection. We operate PSI MicroClor® OSHG systems at active O&M contracts today, and that experience translates directly to what [CLIENT] is building. South Canadian's two PSI MicroClor® units are currently under construction; North Canadian and Deer Creek will follow in 2028 and 2029. When those systems come online, [CLIENT] won't need a contractor that's still figuring out the platform, as **our team already knows how PSI MicroClor® systems perform, where they're sensitive, and what it takes to keep them running reliably**.

Beyond operating these systems, **we've designed and commissioned them**. We've delivered PSI MicroClor® OSHG installations across the country, including the Hayden Bridge WTP for Eugene Water and Electric Board in Oregon (50 MGD, 1,500 ppd, commissioned 2019), Paradise Point WTP for Clark County Public Utilities in Ridgefield, Washington (7.5 MGD, commissioned 2016), the Rockwood Public Utility District's Main WTP in Portland (20 MGD, commissioned 2025), and Nashville Metro Water Services' Omohundro WTP (150 MGD, 9,600 ppd) and K.R. Harrington WTP (120 MGD, 7,200 ppd), both currently under construction. That design depth— knowing not just how to run these systems but how to specify, site, and commission them—means **we can support [CLIENT] through each capital improvement project from pre-construction coordination through startup and steady-state operations**.

PSI MicroClor® equipment is proprietary. That makes disciplined preventive maintenance, not reactive repair, the only responsible approach. We also understand the safety risks these systems carry and the operational requirements to manage them. Our teams in Pampa, Texas; Ontario, Oregon; Fort Irwin, California; and Rio Rancho, New Mexico, **(Exhibit 3-4)** have built their O&M programs around exactly that discipline: short-cycle PM on the failure points that matter most, tight residual control under variable demand, safe chemical handling protocols, and the documentation practices that keep compliance clean. We'll bring that same approach to [CLIENT]'s system.

## Reuse guidance

Use this pattern whenever the client is installing equipment the incumbent has never run: name the client's construction sequence back to them with dates, assert operating familiarity with the exact platform, then add design and commissioning depth so the claim covers the capital phase as well as steady state. The installation list is the proof and must be rebuilt from current records — several entries here were under construction at proposal time and will have commissioned since.
