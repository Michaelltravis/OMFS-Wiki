# Reference Portfolio Comparability and Similar Coastal Facilities

## References — comparability framing

Our Northeast team partners with communities to successfully operate and maintain wastewater treatment facilities **comparable in size and complexity to [CLIENT]'s system** — [CLIENT] being a coastal New England municipal wastewater utility. While not all of the reference projects are located within EPA Region 1 or New York State, they demonstrate our extensive experience operating similar facilities under comparable regulatory frameworks and performance requirements. **Exhibit 3-5** provides the information requested in the RFP, with expanded project descriptions included in the project-description appendix.

## Similar facilities

We operate and maintain wastewater facilities in **coastal communities comparable to [CLIENT]**, including Traverse City, Michigan; Key West, Florida; and Westerly, Rhode Island. These systems include similar regulatory requirements, residential proximity, wet-weather conditions, and odor sensitivity, requiring **responsive operations and strong community stewardship**. The similar-facilities appendix provides additional examples demonstrating our ability to manage facilities of similar size, complexity, and operating conditions. By partnering with Jacobs, [CLIENT] will gain an **experienced operator with a proven track record** managing systems of similar size and complexity, supported by the **resources, operational discipline, and depth of expertise** needed to deliver consistent performance and adapt to changing system demands.

## Reuse guidance

Use the first passage as the lead-in immediately above a references table or exhibit; it sets the terms on which the evaluator should read the list, rather than letting them score each reference against a geography checkbox on their own. Use the second passage wherever the RFP asks for "similar facilities" or "comparable communities" — its persuasive move is redefining comparability as a set of operating conditions rather than a capacity band, which is what lets a 27 MGD or 8.5 MGD reference support a 3 MGD pursuit without overreaching. Both passages are short and are meant to bracket the reference exhibit, not to stand alone. The reference tables themselves (Exhibit 3-5 owner contacts and Exhibit 3-3 representative experience) are captured verbatim in [../past-performance/client-references.md](../past-performance/client-references.md), and the full project write-ups live in the `../past-performance/project-*.md` files.
