# Oklahoma Experience and Regional Resources

In Jacobs, [CLIENT] will have a partner that already knows how to operate in Oklahoma. **We've operated a wastewater treatment facility for the City of Duncan continuously since 1995. Our 30-year relationship spans multiple contract renewals and represents one of our longest-standing municipal wastewater partnerships in the south-central U.S.** Our Duncan team has also earned recognition as **Oklahoma Water Environment Association's 2025 Municipal Plant of the Year.** We administer the City's Industrial Pretreatment Program (IPP), managing seven significant industrial users and four categorical industrial users under Oklahoma Department of Environmental Quality (ODEQ) oversight. Our team understands Oklahoma's regulatory environment and how ODEQ operates, and we've navigated the permitting, compliance reporting, and agency relationships that [CLIENT] relies on every day. Our Oklahoma experience also includes current operation of the **City of Bixby's water reclamation facility and 18 lift stations** — proof that we'll bring both active in-state operating depth and proven performance to [CLIENT].

**Exhibit 3-3** highlights Jacobs' south-central operations footprint, including in Oklahoma and neighboring states such as Texas, Kansas, Arkansas, Missouri, New Mexico, and Colorado. This regional network provides [CLIENT] with immediate access to specialized resources, technical advisors, and as-needed maintenance support in addition to a strong local team presence in Oklahoma.

**Exhibit 3-3.** *Local Roots with Regional Reach* — regional map carrying the callouts: Jacobs' deep pool of local and regional resources can support the full breadth of [CLIENT]'s current and future wastewater operations needs; regionally, we have more than **3,500 multi-discipline employees**; **379 O&M staff** at [CLIENT]; plotted with Jacobs O&M projects, Jacobs offices, and Jacobs corporate headquarters.

Over our **44 years of serving clients across Oklahoma**, we've developed a deep understanding of the regulatory, operational, and infrastructure landscape that shapes successful utility programs in the region. We understand the local environment [CLIENT] operates in, including state and federal permitting, evolving regulatory trends, treatment technologies, market conditions, and water quality standards. That knowledge lets us anticipate challenges early and deliver practical, proven solutions that protect system reliability and support long-term infrastructure investment. **See the Oklahoma Law section** for additional information on this experience.

We have worked closely with staff in the Utilities Department for more than a decade, providing trusted support across a wide range of disciplines. We have assisted the department by delivering uniquely skilled SMEs in areas including risk and resilience, human-machine interface (HMI) programming, cybersecurity, and business process improvements. Through this work, we have developed a strong understanding of [CLIENT]'s business processes, recognizing how critical secure, reliable systems and cybersecurity are to daily operations. Our long-standing partnership has also allowed us to gain deep familiarity with your facilities, your systems, and most importantly your people. In support of organizational continuity and workforce development, Jacobs has even developed training plans for new [CLIENT] staff across various divisions.

Building on this foundation, Jacobs will bring both local presence and regional strength to support [CLIENT]'s wastewater facilities. Our Oklahoma-based and surrounding regional offices offer access to more than 3,500 professionals specializing in engineering, operations support, maintenance planning, asset management, digital solutions, and program management. These teams are strategically positioned throughout the south-central U.S., enabling rapid response and scalable support tailored to [CLIENT]'s needs.

Project Manager **David Pitocchelli** and the on-site management team will guide day-to-day collaboration with [CLIENT] staff while connecting seamlessly to our broader technical network as needed. This integrated structure allows us to quickly deploy the right expertise, scale resources efficiently, and maintain continuity across operations, maintenance, and capital projects.

Combining local knowledge with regional depth and national technical leadership, we're a responsive partner who will support both immediate operational needs and long-term system improvements and deliver consistent performance, technical insight, and value throughout the contract. Selecting the right partner means choosing a team that has already delivered results in facilities with similar scale, scope, complexity, and operational demands. ## Reuse guidance

The structure transfers to any pursuit where in-state operating experience is an evaluation factor: name the longest in-state contract with its start year and renewal count, add a state-association award, name the state regulator and a program you administer under it, add a second active in-state contract, then widen to the regional bench with a number. The prior-relationship paragraph — SMEs already inside the client's department, training plans written for the client's own staff — is what turns a challenger bid into a known quantity, and it belongs early in a qualifications section, not buried.
