## Reuse guidance

Universal: the column structure and the editorial discipline behind it. Every row quantifies the excursion against its permit limit, states a root cause in one sentence, and closes with a completed corrective action. Reviewers score the corrective-action column, not the violation count — keep it specific (SOP rewritten, probe replaced, method changed) rather than generic ("retrained staff").

Pursuit-specific: all rows. Rebuild from the corporate violation tracker for the lookback window the RFP sets, and apply the RFP's own definition of "at-fault." Where the RFP asks for duration, carry it; where it asks only for the event, the duration text can be trimmed.
