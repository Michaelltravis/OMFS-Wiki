## Reuse guidance

Universal: the format — a one-line statement of what is being marked up, then numbered items keyed to the client's own paragraph numbers, each showing the exact tracked language. Also broadly reusable in substance are the four recurring Jacobs positions visible here: 30-day cancellation notice with a 10-day carve-out for non-payment; certificates rather than certified copies of full policies; additional-insured and waiver-of-subrogation status extended to the client's commissioners, directors, officers, and employees but not agents, with professional liability and workers' compensation carved out of the primary and waiver language; and deductibles and self-insured retentions carried as the Company's responsibility instead of requiring pre-approval, a surety bond, or a letter of credit.

Pursuit-specific: all paragraph and exhibit numbering, the certificate delivery address, the governing-law sentence, and whether the client's draft already contains any of these provisions.
