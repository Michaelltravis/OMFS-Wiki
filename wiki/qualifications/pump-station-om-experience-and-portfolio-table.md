# Pump Station O&M Experience and Portfolio Table (Exhibit 3-5)

Our team has extensive experience maintaining complex wastewater and potable water pump station networks like the Witcher facility **(Exhibit 3-5)**. Our O&M programs include daily monitoring, remote SCADA-based control, preventive maintenance, emergency response, and long-term life cycle planning. By focusing on sustainability and adapting to changing environmental conditions, we ensure pump stations will operate efficiently and effectively, contributing to the overall resilience of conveyance systems. Our commitment to quality service and risk management helps clients reduce costs and improve service quality, making Jacobs a trusted partner in your pump stations' O&M.

We currently maintain more than **700 pump stations at 75 plants, including 2,490 pumps**. Pumps range from 0.5 to 200 hp and include submersible and dry-well submersible pumps from manufacturers ranging from ABBA to Zoeller and everything in between, including Flygt, Trimmer, and Vaughan.
