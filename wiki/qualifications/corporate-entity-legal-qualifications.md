# Corporate Entity Details, Legal Standing, and Litigation/Termination Disclosure

## Company at a Glance

**Contracting entity:** Jacobs Solutions, Inc. (Jacobs) is the parent company. Operations Management International, Inc. (OMI), a wholly-owned subsidiary, will serve as the contracting entity for this RFP.

**Federal tax ID:** 93-0784940

**Principal office address:** 120 St. James Ave, Ste 500, Boston, MA 02116 | T: 508.273.6481

**Principal contact person:** Mark Phillips, Client Services Manager

**Entity information:** Date of incorporation 10/25/1980; state of incorporation California.

**Years providing services:** Jacobs delivers O&M services through different legal entities depending on the jurisdiction and contracting requirements. OMI has been engaged in providing contract operations, maintenance, and management services for 45 years.

## Legal Standing

OMI, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Jacobs Solutions, Inc., is in **good legal standing** and has no history of charges or convictions for crimes of moral turpitude, such as bribery or fraud, or for any other illegality relating to its operations. OMI is in good standing in its home state of California and is registered and authorized to conduct business in the Commonwealth of Massachusetts. OMI has not been the subject of any bankruptcy action. We are proud of our **long-standing history of delivering services in an ethical manner**.

## Litigation

Jacobs has approximately 370 operating companies and affiliates, with approximately 100+ operating entities in North America, and a combined global talent force of more than 42,000 and approximately $12 billion in revenue. Jacobs provides a full spectrum of services, including O&M, scientific, technical, professional, construction, and program management for business, industrial, commercial, government, and infrastructure sectors. Given Jacobs' size, disputes, claims, arbitrations, or lawsuits may arise from time to time and in the ordinary course of its business. However, the number of such instances is **minimal, especially considering the nature of work and the size of the company**. Jacobs seeks to avoid litigation and aims to resolve any such disputes in an amicable manner, whenever possible.

The company considers its claims to be confidential to the company and its clients. While the outcome of pending disputes is often difficult to predict, senior management and General Counsel believe that **no pending dispute, claim, arbitration, or lawsuit will result in a materially adverse impact** on the company's consolidated financial statements or its ability to perform its obligations under any contract. Any litigation that is considered to have the potential for a material impact on the company's operations is disclosed in Jacobs SEC filings, which can be found at https://invest.jacobs.com/overview/.

## Contract Termination

From time to time, and in the ordinary course of its business, Jacobs is subject to various terminations for convenience, claims and disputes, including, but not limited to, arbitrations and other legal proceedings. No such termination, claim, or dispute, including, but not limited to, arbitration and other legal proceeding is expected to have a materially adverse effect on the consolidated financial statements. Further, Jacobs does not track the information required by this question. However, based on information and belief, neither the Offeror nor any person to be assigned to this Project has had a contract terminated by [CLIENT], a coastal New England municipal wastewater utility, in the past 5 years.
