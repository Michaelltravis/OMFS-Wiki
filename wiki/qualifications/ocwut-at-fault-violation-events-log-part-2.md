## Reuse guidance

Universal: the pattern of closing each disclosure with a standing practice change rather than a one-time repair — weekly chemical strength testing, second-person checks on feed rate, marking the fields to be irrigated, rewriting the SOP. When an evaluator reads an at-fault log, these rows are the ones that answer whether the operator learns.

Pursuit-specific: every row. Rebuild from the corporate violation tracker for the RFP's lookback window; confirm each corrective action is closed and each root cause is stated as the site reported it.
