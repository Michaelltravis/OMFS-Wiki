# Septage Receiving Program Experience

Running a compliant, efficient septage receiving program takes more than a staffed attendant and a flow meter. It takes rigorous screening, clear hauler accountability, and active program management. We bring both the operational discipline and the programmatic know-how to do this well.

Jacobs has **operated the dedicated Grand Traverse County Septage Receiving and Treatment Facility since 2012**, managing all aspects of receiving, treating, and reporting on septage loads, including manifesting, sampling, Discharge Monitoring Report (DMR) submittals, and permit compliance. This purpose-built receiving station serves the broader Traverse City region, giving our team direct, hands-on experience with exactly the type of program [CLIENT] runs at its Witcher Pump Station receiving facility.

We also know how to turn a receiving program into a revenue driver. Jacobs has been the O&M partner to the **Lehigh County Authority (LCA) in Allentown, Pennsylvania** for 30 years, and we **overhauled their hauled waste program from the ground up**. That meant a market analysis, revised hauler permitting, updated metals limits, and a shift in lab testing accountability to the waste generators. The results speak for themselves: revenue from hauled waste increased **38% in the first year** and has grown **63% cumulatively**, generating nearly **$2.8M in offset annual revenue** for LCA.

We'll bring that same combination of operational rigor and program stewardship to [CLIENT]'s Septage Receiving Program at Witcher, keeping loads compliant, protecting the downstream treatment process, and looking for opportunities to strengthen the program over time.

**Turning a Receiving Program into a Revenue Driver at Lehigh County Authority.** Jacobs has operated LCA's high-strength industrial wastewater facility for 30 years, administering an active industrial pretreatment program and coordinating 60 to 80 truckloads of biosolids daily. We optimized LCA's waste hauler program to increase revenue by 38% in the first year, applying the same operational discipline and data-driven approach we bring to every contract. ## Reuse guidance

Reusable wherever septage, hauled waste, FOG, or high-strength waste receiving is in scope. The opening line — that a receiving program is more than an attendant and a flow meter — is a useful way to signal that the firm treats a minor scope item seriously, which evaluators notice. Two proof paths are offered and can be used separately: compliance discipline at a purpose-built facility (Grand Traverse), and revenue upside from restructuring the hauler program (LCA). The LCA callout restates the 38% figure in shorter form; use one telling per proposal. Only the Witcher references are pursuit-specific.
