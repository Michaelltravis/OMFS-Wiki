## Reuse guidance

Universal: the structure — two technical disciplines (electrical, process engineering) flanked by three relationship disciplines (funding, regulator coordination, corporate security) — is what turns an SME table into a local-presence argument. The reusable move is to place a former utility executive and a former state regulator on the bench and describe them by what they did before joining the firm, not by what the firm does.

Pursuit-specific: state PE registration numbers; the named local municipality where the funding SME served as Public Works Director (Bixby, a third-party reference that stays verbatim); the state agency and division named in the regulator-coordination entry; and the local university career fair. All of these must be replaced with the equivalents for the region being bid, and the entry is worth dropping entirely if no comparable local hire exists.

Numbers to register before reuse: five bond initiatives and $100M+ in capital improvements; 8 years as a Construction Permitting Engineer in the state water quality division plus one year at EPA's Clean Water State Revolving Fund; security programs supporting more than 40,000 employees; two FBI Director's Awards and the DoD James S. Cogswell Award.
