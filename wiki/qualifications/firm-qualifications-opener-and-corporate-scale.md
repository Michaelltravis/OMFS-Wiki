# Firm Qualifications Opener and Corporate Scale

[CLIENT], a southcentral U.S. municipal water utility trust operating four activated sludge wastewater treatment facilities and a major pump station, deserves a forward-looking team with boots on the ground operations supported by a local and national team of industry leading subject matter experts (SMEs) that can manage complex wastewater operations and keep critical facilities running every day. Jacobs brings exactly that. Our qualifications combine hands-on wastewater operations and maintenance (O&M) leadership, proven performance in comparable operating environments from the nation's largest and most complex facilities, and a management team backed by the technical depth needed to solve problems quickly and keep work moving.

Jacobs is one of North America's largest water and wastewater O&M service providers. Established in 1947 and headquartered in Dallas, Jacobs is a U.S.-based Fortune 500 company. We generated approximately $12B in revenue in FY24, with a backlog of over $2.4B in O&M services. We have 19,000+ U.S. staff and 42,000+ staff worldwide, making us one of the largest and most diverse companies in our industry. Our financial capacity, combined with our resources as the country's largest O&M, engineering, construction, and consulting firm, make it possible to successfully guarantee and deliver all aspects of [CLIENT]'s scope of services.

## Reuse guidance

The italic opener is a reusable section-opening device for any qualifications section: name the client's operating reality, say what that reality demands of an operator, then assert that the firm is that operator — three sentences before any corporate fact appears. Swap the descriptor clause for the new pursuit's profile. The corporate scale paragraph is pursuit-agnostic apart from the closing "[CLIENT]'s scope of services" clause; note this source states 19,000+ U.S. staff where other proposals in this library state 29,000+ — reconcile against the current fact sheet before reuse.
