# Financial Assurances: Letter of Credit, Guaranty, and Insurance

## B. Letter of Credit

Jacobs has the financial capability to meet the Letter of Credit requirements.

## C. Guaranty

The proposer, OMI, is the legal entity within Jacobs which primarily delivers O&M services for Jacobs throughout the United States. Jacobs is prepared to meet the financial assurance requirements of the Service Agreement by executing a Guaranty Agreement through OMI's parent company, JEG, the financial statements of which are available for review. The Guarantor will irrevocably, absolutely, and unconditionally guarantee the performance of all the Company's obligations under the Service Agreement, in a manner similar to that of other Guaranty Agreements executed between parties in the O&M industry.

## D. Insurance

Jacobs can provide the insurance requested by [CLIENT], the regional sewerage district, as evidenced by the letter issued by our insurers. Additionally, Jacobs has proposed an alternate insurance approach, which would provide additional savings to [CLIENT], as more specifically set forth in *Section VI – Exceptions and Requests*.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-part structure (letter of credit — guaranty — insurance), the guaranty sentence ("irrevocably, absolutely, and unconditionally guarantee the performance of all the Company's obligations"), and the broker-letter pattern of confirming coverage "substantially similar to" the RFP's insurance exhibit while reserving final compliance to the negotiated contract. That reservation is deliberate and should survive edits.

Pursuit-specific: the RFP number, the client's insurance exhibit reference (Exhibit J here), the procurement contact addressed in the letter, the letter date, and the cross-reference to whichever section carries exceptions. The alternate-insurance-approach sentence is a value play that must be reconciled with the exceptions section and the pricing volume before it is used.

Pairs with [Proposer Identity, Contracting Entity, and Financial Strength Opener](mmsd-proposer-identity-financial-strength-and-contracting-entity.md), which establishes OMI and JEG as the entities named here, and with the firm's broader financial-standing blocks ([financial-strength-low-risk-partner-value-prop.md](financial-strength-low-risk-partner-value-prop.md)) when the RFP wants narrative financial strength rather than assurance mechanics.
