## Reuse guidance

Universal: pairing two biosolids SMEs with complementary profiles — one covering the regulatory and beneficial-reuse side (Class A/B, PFAS, odor minimization), one covering the logistics and field-operations side (hauling, dewatering performance, landfill alternatives, CDL-level field credibility) — is the reusable structure for any solids-heavy O&M pursuit. It signals that the team has thought about the truck route as well as the permit.

Pursuit-specific: both names, the reference to the client's Solids Management Plan, and the final due-diligence bullet, which lists real findings from the existing operation. Replace those findings with the observations your team made during site visits; if no site visits occurred, drop the bullet rather than generalize it.
