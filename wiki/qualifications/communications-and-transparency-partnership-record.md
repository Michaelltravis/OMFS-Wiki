# Communications and Transparency Partnership Record

[CLIENT] will have clear visibility into how the system is performing and how Jacobs is managing it. Our management model and transparent reporting provide you with information to track compliance, maintenance, staffing, budget use, and corrective action without chasing information. We communicate early, document clearly, and bring forward issues with context and solutions. This level of transparency supports faster decisions, stronger regulatory coordination, and better protection of [CLIENT]'s assets, budget, and public trust.

**Nine Years of Transparent Partnership in Vancouver.** When Jacobs assumed operations of Vancouver's two wastewater treatment facilities in 2016, the City needed a partner they could trust with a major public utility. Over 9 years, we've built that trust through consistent performance, open communication, and active collaboration on capital decisions. The relationship has evolved from a contract handover into what the City's own engineering leadership describes as a genuine, transparent partnership. (Case study callout graphic ID `129_009385`.)

## Reuse guidance

Short, high-leverage, and almost entirely portable: the first paragraph needs only the client name swapped, and the five things the client can track (compliance, maintenance, staffing, budget use, corrective action) map onto most O&M reporting requirements without editing. The Vancouver callout is the proof and works best in challenger bids, where the argument is that the incumbent makes the client work for information.
