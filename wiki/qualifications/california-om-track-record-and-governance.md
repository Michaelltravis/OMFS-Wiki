# California O&M Track Record and OMFS Corporate Governance Structure

## Successful O&M project delivery in California since 1984

Jacobs has operated public utilities in California for over four decades, with contract O&M experience at 26 facilities statewide, including 11 municipal water and wastewater treatment plants. Our experience includes membrane filtration, reverse osmosis, UV disinfection, advanced oxidation processes, and indirect potable reuse, all directly relevant to [PROJECT].

## Corporate structure and organizational chart

Jacobs' Operations Management and Facilities Services (OMFS) group delivers O&M services for municipal water and wastewater facilities such as the [PROJECT] operations contract. As a core part of Jacobs' business, OMFS leads more than 300 O&M projects globally and provides direct oversight of all contract operations, safety, engineering support, and quality assurance activities.

The [CLIENT] [PROJECT] team will be fully embedded within this OMFS structure, ensuring strong governance, real-time access to technical expertise, and robust regional and corporate support. Our proposed team reports through Jacobs' integrated management hierarchy (Exhibit 2-1, graphic `104_008A26`).

Greg Fischer, President of OMFS and Design-Build, leads our O&M group and reports to our CEO Bob Pragada through our President of Global Operations, Patrick Hill. Our proposed Executive Sponsor Paul Rheault is Jacobs Vice President of Operations (West) and reports to Greg Fischer. Howard Brewen, California O&M Director, reports to Paul. Howard will serve as our Director of Operations at [PROJECT] with our Chief Plant Operator Mack Mckenzie reporting to him. In addition, Howard will have Chris Catlin, our Manager of Operations, supporting as part of the [PROJECT] Management Team. This governance framework ensures operational excellence, rapid escalation support, and full alignment with [CLIENT]'s expectations for quality, compliance, and innovation.
