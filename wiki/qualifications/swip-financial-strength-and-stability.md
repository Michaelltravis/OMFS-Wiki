# Financial Strength, Stability, and Comparable-Project Financial Track Record

Jacobs has the financial strength, stability, and experience to support [CLIENT] as a long-term O&M partner. The client requires a contractor with the resources and business resilience to ensure uninterrupted performance of its facilities. Jacobs will deliver that confidence.

With more than $15 billion in annual revenue and a robust backlog, Jacobs has consistently demonstrated the capacity to meet the financial demands of large-scale, technically complex projects across the globe. Our O&M business has operated successfully for over 40 years and is a key driver of our corporate strength. The financial reliability of Jacobs ensures that the client can count on us to meet all contractual obligations, even in the face of unanticipated financial pressures.

The operations team at Jacobs is backed by over 4,000+ employees and manages a $2.4 billion O&M backlog. We serve municipal, federal, and private clients with a broad array of facility services, from advanced water reuse systems to critical infrastructure operations. Our performance spans high-profile projects such as water and wastewater operations for **Tucson, San Diego, and Seattle**, as well as facility management at **Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport**.

Jacobs will also bring financial stability to this contract through our single-entity guarantor structure. The client can be confident that the named contract party is wholly backed by the full strength and resources of Jacobs, rather than a joint venture or multi-entity construct.

In short, Jacobs will provide the client with unmatched financial capacity and proven O&M leadership to ensure the long-term resilience and excellence of operations.

## Comparable Project Financial Performance

**Advanced Water Treatment Facility Experience.** Jacobs will leverage our extensive experience with facilities of comparable complexity to the client's facilities:
- Over 300 water and wastewater facilities under management across North America
- $1.6 billion annual revenue from O&M operations specifically
- Advanced treatment facilities including MBR, RO, and UV-AOP comparable to the client's technology

**Advanced Water Treatment Financial Track Record.** Jacobs will bring proven experience managing projects of comparable complexity, scale, and financial scope:
- **$36 million North Hudson Sewerage Authority** contract, a CSO program showcasing complex technical and financial management capabilities
- **$30 million annual Jackson Water contract**, proving our ability to deliver value on major municipal partnerships for water and wastewater systems

## Financial Strength and Stability

Jacobs has generated over $32 billion in revenue in the last 3 years, as demonstrated in our 10-K documents. We possess a strong financial position and credit rating. Our stable revenue and strong backlog, continuing profitability, and overall financial resources demonstrate our financial strength to the client. Our revenue has been steady throughout our history, based on successful project delivery, sound risk management strategies, and exceptional client satisfaction. We have strong retained earnings and a reasonable debt-to-capital ratio that leads to financial stability, reflected in our good credit ratings and banking relationships that demonstrate our financial flexibility. Our robust credit rating of 5A3 and assessment of "Stable Condition" and "Low-Moderate Business Risk" is demonstrated in the Dun & Bradstreet Credit Report for Jacobs.

## Financial Risk Management

**Comprehensive Insurance and Liability Coverage.** Jacobs maintains robust insurance programs consistent with industry O&M standards. Our practice includes taking full accountability for performance of services, including fines, penalties, and costs resulting from Jacobs' negligence. Our long-standing performance record demonstrates financial responsibility through minimal insurance claims for plant upsets or equipment failures.

**Outstanding Regulatory Compliance Financial Record.** Jacobs will deliver exceptional environmental and financial stewardship with our 99.98% compliance record over 20 years. This outstanding performance demonstrates our ability to avoid costly violations and maintain financial efficiency through proactive compliance management, directly relevant to the target facility's complex permit requirements.

**Contract Performance History.** Our proven track record includes:
- Not a single contract has ever been canceled due to compliance issues
- Zero contract terminations due to performance failures
- 99% customer contract renewal rate reflecting exceptional client satisfaction
