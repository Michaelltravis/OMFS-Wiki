# JC Solutions JV Consulting Resources — Digital, Solids, Energy, and Commissioning

| Consulting resource | Qualifications and support role |
|---|---|
| **John Karabias** — 17 years of experience; SCADA/Instrumentation | Leads Jacobs' strategic development for data, digital, and cyber solutions as a recognized technology thought leader. Identifies gaps in security controls and liaises with critical stakeholders in water treatment plants. Provides recommendations using best practices and lessons learned in strategic development of data, digital, and cyber solutions for clients. |
| **Todd Williams, PE, BCEE** — 43 years of experience; Biosolids | Biosolids subject-matter expert knowledgeable in WWTP solids processing systems, adaptive planning for emerging contaminants such as PFAS, and improvement options. Designed, evaluated, and provided operational services for compost-facility process, equipment, and odor-control systems on more than 100 projects. Has biosolids odor-control and biofilter-application experience. |
| **Wesley Chambers** — 6 years of experience; Energy Management | Experienced in integration of renewables, energy storage, and hydrogen. Specialist in front-end technoeconomic feasibility assessments and decarbonization planning and design, including energy-demand reduction and integration of carbon-free energy supply. |
| **David Oerke, PE** — 40 years of experience; Thickening and Dewatering Expert | Nationally recognized water and wastewater solids specialist with more than 40 years of experience in evaluation, design, and pilot testing of thickening and dewatering equipment. Completed designs for more than 150 solids-thickening and dewatering units. Develops internal Jacobs specifications for dewatering centrifuges, belt filter presses, and screw presses, and was lead author of the Jacobs Water Treatment Residuals Guidance Manual in 2018. |
| **Ashley Currey** — 23 years of experience; Commissioning | Commissioned nearly every water and wastewater DB/DBO project during her 20-year Jacobs tenure, with combined value over $1 billion. Her water-treatment experience ranges from $10 million to the $1.77 billion Northeast Water Purification Plant in Houston, described as the nation's largest PDB project. Delivered projects from Florida to California using commissioning tools and processes developed over two decades. |
