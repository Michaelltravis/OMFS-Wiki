# JC Solutions JV Executive Sponsors and Senior Supervisors

JC Solutions (a Jacobs/CERM JV) will use its Executive Sponsors and Senior Supervisors as the executive forum for overall performance review and issue resolution. They will oversee operational performance and focus on maintenance planning; facilitate collaborative decision-making and continuous improvements between [CLIENT] and JC Solutions; and provide the full breadth of the JV's expertise and corporate support to the partnership, reinforcing its commitment to exceptional results.

Active in the Atlanta community and serves on the Atlanta Regional Commission Board, engaging with the local community and contributing to regional planning and development. Drives strategic key-client relationships by establishing integration between operations, sales, and corporate functions to implement end-to-end solutions. |
