# Full-Service Lifecycle Capability and Capital Planning Support

## Full O&M services for water and wastewater treatment facilities

We deliver **comprehensive O&M services that extend beyond day-to-day operations**, integrating regulatory compliance, asset management, process optimization, and capital planning into a coordinated approach. Our teams are responsible for maintaining **permit compliance and reliable system performance**, while aligning operations with regulatory requirements, budget priorities, and community expectations, particularly in coastal and residentially sensitive environments. This **"more-than-an-operator" approach** supports [CLIENT]'s long-term infrastructure investment — [CLIENT] being a coastal New England municipal wastewater utility — and directly reinforces the on-site team described in the staffing and project management plan, ensuring **consistent, responsive, and reliable service**.

## Additional technical resources and capital planning support

We provide [CLIENT] with **direct access to extensive technical troubleshooting and capital planning resources** beyond the on-site O&M team. Capital improvement planning (CIP) is fully integrated into our Asset Management program, providing a **structured and defensible framework for prioritizing improvements, managing risk, and aligning investments** with operational needs. We have successfully delivered CIP programs and technical evaluations for wastewater facilities comparable to [CLIENT]'s.

These resources are primarily sourced from Jacobs' in-house engineering, construction, and water and wastewater consulting teams, providing **immediate access to subject matter experts** in process engineering, electrical and controls, structural, mechanical, regulatory compliance, and asset management. When specialized expertise is required, we supplement our team with pre-qualified affiliate partners and subcontractors. Routine technical troubleshooting and operational support are included as part of our base operating services. Larger studies, detailed design efforts, or capital project implementation support are provided as additional services upon client authorization, ensuring **transparency and cost control**.

Our vast network of resources, as shown in **Exhibit 3-6**, enables **rapid response to operational and compliance challenges** and supports continuous system improvement. These resources directly support the on-site team described in the staffing section, providing **additional capacity, technical depth, and rapid response capability**.

**Exhibit 3-6. Jacobs full-service offerings to support O&M projects and clients** (graphic ID `122_Hull_0091KO_1`) — five integrated service lines:

- **O&M** — asset management and CMMS; planning/scheduling; regional and major maintenance support; chemical optimization; QA/QC and project audits; safety excellence; subcontract management; technical O&M services; training and certifications; community engagement; project sustainability
- **Consulting** — cybersecurity; regulatory compliance; CSO/MS4/IDDE/CMOM; flood control; site security; energy management and efficiency; carbon strategy and management; technology and innovation; next-level, data-driven decisionmaking with AI and machine learning; lifecycle water management; licensing/permitting; technology selection; master planning
- **Engineering** — project management; owner's engineer; program management; design-build prequalifications; progressive design-build; design-build-operate; value engineering; operations and safety in design; process engineering and optimization (including IFAS and Bardenpho)
- **Procurement** — planning and strategy; global sourcing; materials and equipment procurement; expediting; transportation/logistics; field material control and inventory management; vendor/subcontractor administration
- **Construction** — commissioning and startup; development assistance; design-build (DB) and DB-operate (DBO); ongoing operations during construction; modular fabrication and installation; mechanical piping and electrical construction; general contracting; union and open-shop direct hire
