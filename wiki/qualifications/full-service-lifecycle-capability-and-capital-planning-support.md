# Full-Service Lifecycle Capability and Capital Planning Support

We deliver comprehensive O&M services that extend beyond day-to-day operations, integrating regulatory compliance, asset management, process optimization, and capital planning into a coordinated approach. Our teams are responsible for maintaining permit compliance and reliable system performance while aligning operations with regulatory requirements, budget priorities, and community expectations — particularly in coastal and residentially sensitive environments. This "more-than-an-operator" approach supports [CLIENT]'s long-term infrastructure investment and directly reinforces the on-site team described in the staffing and project management plan, ensuring consistent, responsive, and reliable service.

We provide [CLIENT] with direct access to extensive technical troubleshooting and capital planning resources beyond the on-site O&M team. Capital improvement planning (CIP) is fully integrated into our Asset Management program, providing a structured and defensible framework for prioritizing improvements, managing risk, and aligning investments with operational needs. We have successfully delivered CIP programs and technical evaluations for wastewater facilities comparable to [CLIENT]'s.

These resources are primarily sourced from our in-house engineering, construction, and water/wastewater consulting teams, providing immediate access to subject matter experts in process engineering, electrical and controls, structural, mechanical, regulatory compliance, and asset management. When specialized expertise is required, we supplement our team with pre-qualified affiliate partners and subcontractors. Routine technical troubleshooting and operational support are included as part of base operating services; larger studies, detailed design efforts, or capital project implementation support are provided as additional services upon client authorization, ensuring transparency and cost control.

Our full-service offering spans five integrated service lines that can be brought to bear on any O&M engagement:

- **O&M** — asset management & CMMS, planning/scheduling, chemical optimization, QA/QC & project audits, safety excellence, training & certifications, community engagement
- **Consulting** — cybersecurity, technology & innovation, regulatory compliance, flood control, site security, energy management & efficiency, data-driven decision-making (AI/ML), licensing/permitting, master planning
- **Engineering** — project/program management, owner's engineer, design-build, value engineering, operations & safety in design, process engineering & optimization
- **Procurement** — planning/strategy, global sourcing & prequalification, materials & equipment procurement, vendor/subcontractor administration
- **Construction** — commissioning & startup assistance, design-build/DB-operate, modular fabrication, mechanical/electrical construction, general contracting

This vast network of resources enables rapid response to operational and compliance challenges and supports continuous system improvement — providing additional capacity, technical depth, and rapid-response capability beyond the dedicated on-site team.
