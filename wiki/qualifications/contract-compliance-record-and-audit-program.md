# Contract Compliance Record and Audit Program

**Jacobs maintains a 99.98% compliance rate across our O&M portfolio. This record is reflected in Waterbury's 99.57% permit compliance rate over 5 years, Vancouver's back-to-back NACWA Platinum Peak Performance Award, and consecutive Outstanding Performance recognitions from the Washington Department of Ecology.** Behind those numbers is a structured program: standardized standard operating procedures (SOPs), rigorous laboratory management, and a dedicated team of compliance specialists supporting on-site staff at every Jacobs-operated facility nationwide.

Every Jacobs project is audited on a structured cycle within 6 months of contract start, with frequency adjusted based on performance. Audit teams work through 70 specific topics against contractual and regulatory requirements, and findings go to the project manager with a 30/60/90-day action timeline. We'll bring that same approach here. **Jeff Heroux** serves as our compliance lead on this project. As Director of Compliance and Regulatory Affairs, Jeff **provides oversight across all Jacobs O&M contracts and brings a dedicated team of 20 compliance specialists**.

**Regulatory Compliance Track Record at Dodge City, Kansas Water Reclamation Facilities.** 100% NPDES permit compliance since 2011 (North Facility). More than 6 years without a recordable safety incident. Zero deficiencies found in the 2025 internal compliance audit. Kansas Water Environment Association Plant of the Year and Safety Award. ## Reuse guidance

The first two paragraphs are the standard compliance-leadership pairing and are portable with only the compliance lead's name changed: portfolio rate, three named project-level proofs, then the program that produces the rate. The three proofs work because they are different kinds of evidence — a permit compliance percentage, a peer-association award, and a state regulator's recognition — so replace them in kind rather than substituting three of the same type. The Dodge City stat block is a compact four-stat callout and can be lifted alone into a compliance or safety section.
