# Biosolids and Land Application Capability with Case Studies

Across our O&M portfolio, Jacobs has designed hundreds of biosolids projects worldwide and completed more than 4,000 water and wastewater facilities. **Our biosolids O&M programs successfully manage the disposal and beneficial use of over 40,000 dry tons of solids every year.** Nearly all our treatment projects under contract operations involve some aspect of biosolids management and processing, including thickening; Class B and Class A digestion; fats, oils, and grease (FOG) and food waste; stabilization; dewatering, drying, energy resource recovery, and cogeneration; nutrient analysis for nuisance struvite/vivianite; side stream treatment; and biosolids disposal and beneficial reuse.

**In Wilmington, Delaware, we've been the City's O&M partner for more than 20 years, operating a 134-MGD wastewater treatment plant (WWTP), a renewable energy biosolids facility, and three pump stations** while simultaneously delivering a $134M capital improvement program. **In Vancouver, Washington, Jacobs operates two WWTPs totaling 44 MGD, manages the City's IPP, and supports an active incinerator O&M program, saving the City $1M annually** compared to the previous operator and earning multiple National Association of Clean Water Agencies (NACWA) Platinum Peak Performance Awards. **In Jackson, Mississippi, Jacobs manages three wastewater treatment plants and 98 lift stations** after assuming operations through a federal oversight process and quickly stabilizing a system that required urgent attention.

**From Process Challenge to Reliable Biosolids Performance.** In Vancouver, Jacobs turned biosolids processing into a dependable, high-performing operation. Through a strong O&M program, the plant processed 100% of the biosolids it produced through the incinerator year over year, excluding only planned diversions during annual outages. The team also drove measurable efficiency gains, including a recent 20% reduction in polymer costs by bypassing the Mannich system for emulsion totes. To strengthen reliability and control across the facility, Jacobs also leveraged broader technical resources to implement a plantwide SCADA and PLC upgrade with new automated programming—helping improve performance, reduce manual intervention, and keep this critical process under control.

**Biosolids Recovery – Turning Crisis into Control in Baltimore.** In 2024, Jacobs rapidly stabilized and transformed the solids operations at Baltimore's Back River WWTP—an underresourced, consent-driven facility facing an aggressive compliance timeline. By mobilizing an experienced on-site team supported by regional specialists and implementing a phased recovery strategy, we restored operational control while building long-term capability. In the first year alone, the team hauled out more than 4,500 truckloads of accumulated biosolids, reduced legacy sludge inventories exceeding 800 tons, and returned critical solids equipment to service— bringing centrifuges, thickeners, and settling tanks back online at full or near-full capacity. We established predictive maintenance through computerized maintenance management system (CMMS) implementation, collected thousands of samples to re-establish process understanding, and completed more than 3,200 training hours to strengthen workforce competency. Critically, Jacobs rebuilt staffing by hiring 22 permanent employees while retaining all existing City staff, creating a skilled, sustainable operation. The result was improved reliability and a clear path to compliance and resilience. (Case study callout graphic ID `125_009385`.)

**At Baltimore's 180-MGD Back River WWTP, Jacobs inherited a facility with a massive accumulated biosolids backlog** and only two of eight gravity belt thickeners operational. **Within 6 months, all eight units were fully restored, a centrifuge that hadn't run in nearly 9 years was returned to service, and more than 1,000 dry tons of accumulated biosolids were removed** —all while maintaining regulatory compliance under a consent decree. That same discipline in biosolids recovery and solids program management is exactly what [CLIENT]'s land application program requires of an operator every single day.
