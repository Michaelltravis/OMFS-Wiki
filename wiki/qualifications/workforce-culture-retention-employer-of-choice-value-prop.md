# Workforce Culture, Retention, and Employer-of-Choice Positioning

Our commitment to client partnership delivers **strong performance, cost efficiency, and long-term relationships**. We prioritize safety, ethics, and workforce development, creating a culture that attracts and maintains top talent. Jacobs has been recognized as one of the **"50 Best Companies to Work for,"** and our investment in our people results in **one of the lowest turnover rates in the industry**.

## Reuse guidance

Use this short passage to answer evaluation criteria concerned with staffing stability, key-personnel continuity, and transition risk on multi-year O&M contracts. Low turnover is a direct argument for continuity of institutional knowledge and reduced re-training and re-transition risk over the contract term — a more persuasive framing than citing the award on its own, so keep the award and the turnover claim in the same breath rather than splitting them. In an incumbent-displacement pursuit, this paragraph also does quiet work against the fear that a new operator means a new, unfamiliar crew every year; strengthen it in a live proposal by naming the project-specific retention plan from the staffing section. It is universal corporate language with no client identifiers, so it drops into a cover letter, executive summary, or firm-qualifications section unchanged.
