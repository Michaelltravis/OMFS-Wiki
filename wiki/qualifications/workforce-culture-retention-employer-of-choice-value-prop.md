# Workforce Culture, Retention, and Employer-of-Choice Positioning

Our commitment to client partnership delivers strong performance, cost efficiency, and long-term relationships. We prioritize safety, ethics, and workforce development, creating a culture that attracts and retains top talent. Jacobs has been recognized as one of the "50 Best Companies to Work For," and our investment in our people results in one of the lowest turnover rates in the industry.

## Reuse guidance

Use this to answer evaluation criteria concerned with staffing stability and transition risk on multi-year O&M contracts — low turnover is a direct, concrete argument for continuity of institutional knowledge and reduced re-training/re-transition risk over the contract term, which is a more persuasive framing than citing the award alone.
