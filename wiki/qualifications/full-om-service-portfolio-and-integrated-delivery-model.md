# Full O&M Service Portfolio and Integrated Delivery Model

Jacobs has provided full-service O&M contracts for water and wastewater systems since 1980. Our **O&M portfolio includes more than 300 O&M projects** involving water and wastewater treatment facilities, collection and distribution systems, water quality laboratories and meter reading, stormwater management, parks maintenance, fleet maintenance, streets and roads maintenance, groundwater remediation systems, building permit review and code enforcement, and facility management services.

Our multidisciplinary O&M model draws on deep technical expertise across operations, engineering, design, construction, procurement, asset management, data management, and systems integration. As a fully integrated firm, our O&M, engineering, and technical support teams routinely collaborate to enhance operability, efficiency, emergency response, and life-cycle value, supporting reliable, cost-effective O&M solutions that meet client needs day in and day out. Our operations staff have seamless, real-time access to engineering and technical specialists to help resolve challenges, optimize performance, and deliver innovative, practical solutions.

**As an industry-leading O&M service provider (Exhibit 3-2), we support some of the largest and most complex water and wastewater O&M projects in the U.S., including Jackson (145 MGD), Baltimore (180 MGD), Seattle (180 MGD), San Diego (100 MGD), Tucson (32 MGD), Baton Rouge (300 MGD), and Wilmington (134 MGD).** We apply proven technologies and practical solutions that improve operational performance, reduce risk, and lower long-term operating costs. Our deep bench of O&M resources nationwide ensures continuity of operations and highly qualified personnel regardless of local staffing shortages, illness-related absences, or other unexpected circumstances, reinforcing our clients' confidence in uninterrupted service.

The operational profile of [CLIENT]'s system, including four activated sludge treatment facilities running 24/7, pump station, approximately 170 permitted biosolids land application sites over 13,500 acres, and a capital improvement program actively reshaping the system mirrors the kind of integrated, multi-facility complexity we manage across our portfolio.
