## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-move disclosure pattern — cite the RFP paragraph that compelled the disclosure, state the lookback window and the permit-holder test, give the denominator (facilities operated) so the reader can scale the list, then offer the not-at-fault list on request. The table's column set (project, state, date/document type, notes, status) is reusable as-is.

Pursuit-specific: every row. Pull the current log from the corporate enforcement tracker on the day the proposal is locked, cut it to the RFP's lookback window, and re-verify each status. Match the RFP's own definition of "enforcement" — some solicitations ask only for formal enforcement orders, others for every NOV.
