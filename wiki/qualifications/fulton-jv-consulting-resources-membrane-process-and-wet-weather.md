# JC Solutions JV Consulting Resources — Membrane, Process, and Wet-Weather Support

JC Solutions' consulting resources, led by Rocco Koekemoer, will be accessible to the Project Manager and management team. They provide insight and draw on combined expertise in current technologies and regulatory-compliance knowledge to sustain facility O&M. They also support the Transition Team and guide specialized maintenance tasks for efficient service.

| Consulting resource or source-listed capability | Qualifications and support role |
|---|---|
| **Youngchul Choi, PE** — 30 years of experience; Membrane Technologist | Specializes in membranes and biological treatment, including hollow fiber and flat sheet. Maintains membrane performance through control of biofouling, biofilm growth, and sloughing. Uses modeling tools to simulate desalination, biological processes, and chemical treatments. |
| **Source-listed wastewater-process and resource-recovery capability** | Understands wastewater processes and resource-recovery strategies. Extends expertise from sewer processes in collection systems, wastewater treatment processes and technologies, and odor-control technology to stabilization, management, and marketing of biosolids products, and nutrient and energy recovery from wastewater. Engages with multidisciplinary research teams to develop next-generation solutions for resource-recovery facilities. |
| **Source-listed engineering-management and lifecycle capability** | Regional engineering manager for Jacobs' OMFS U.S. North and Canada Region. Leads engineers and operators to seek process efficiency, optimization, and operational excellence. Is involved in the project life cycle from initial concept planning through commissioning and operations of drinking-water, wastewater, and reuse facilities for municipal and industrial clients in multiple geographies. |
| **Source-listed consulting roster** | Youngchul Choi, PE — 30 years of experience, Membrane Technologist; Adrian Romero-Flores, PhD — 10 years of experience, Odor Control; Lee Tharps, PE — 19 years of experience, Wastewater Engineering; and Jackson Corley, PE — 7 years of experience, Wet Weather Management. |
| **Jackson Corley, PE** — 7 years of experience; Wet Weather Management | Has seven years of experience developing client solutions using Replica Operations Software, including evaluation of hydraulics and operations of water systems. Lead developer for Jacobs' AutoCAD tool that automatically calibrates physics-based models. Uses a proactive problem-solving approach for efficient operations and innovative solutions. |
