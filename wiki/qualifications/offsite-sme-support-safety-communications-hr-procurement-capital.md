## Reuse guidance

Universal: this five-role set answers the "who supports the site staff" question with functions rather than titles — safety program leadership, crisis and community communications, talent acquisition, purchasing and subcontract management, and capital project integration. All five are drawn from corporate support and are typically unpriced in the O&M fee, which is worth saying explicitly in the surrounding narrative.

Pursuit-specific: names, certifications, and state PE registrations. The HR bullet's phrasing ("bringing [CLIENT] industry-specific expertise") should be rewritten to name the local labor market the recruiter will actually work.
