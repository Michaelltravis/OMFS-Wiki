# Contract Exceptions - Insurance, Events of Default and Renewal Term

| Section | Issue | Proposed Revision |
|---|---|---|
| 8.02 | Jacobs can provide substantially similar insurance to that requested in the draft Agreement and with the limits specified in the draft Agreement. We request minor revisions to align with our corporate program. We assume that the property coverage requirement set forth in the RFP is only applicable as set forth in Section 4.06(c) and was not priced into this proposal. | |
| 9.02(a) | Events of default should not be inclusive of regulatory violations resulting from events beyond the contractor's control and Jacobs would like the default provision to be modified accordingly. | Persistent and repeated failure or refusal of the Company to perform timely any material obligation under this Agreement, unless such failure or refusal is clearly recognized, justified and excused by the terms and conditions of this Agreement; provided that failure by the Company to operate the Wastewater Facility in accordance with Applicable Law for more than ten (10) cumulative days in any Contract Year, except for failures resulting from Uncontrollable Circumstances, failure by [CLIENT] to approve Maintenance and Repair expenditures in excess of $3,000 or to implement non-elective Capital Expenditures, shall, ipso facto, constitute persistent and repeated failure or refusal; |
| 9.03 | [CLIENT]'s failure to approve properly submitted maintenance expenditures or implement non-elective Capital Expenditures can materially impact Contractor's ability to meet its obligations to [CLIENT] and should be considered acts of default. | (d) the persistent failure by [CLIENT] to approve Maintenance and Repair expenditure requests which are properly submitted by Contractor or the failure to implement non-elective Capital Expenditures. |
| 12.2 | Jacobs requests that any renewal option be at the mutual agreement of both parties. | The Term of this Agreement (the term) shall commence on the Commencement Date and shall continue for an initial term of ten (10) years, unless earlier terminated in accordance with the terms and conditions hereof. [CLIENT] shall have the right to extend this Agreement for an additional five (5) one-year periods, if so authorized by Town Meeting. The decision to enter into extension periods shall be at the mutual agreement of the parties and made in accordance with applicable procurement law at the time of renewal. During the extension periods, the Service Fee shall be adjusted in accordance with the methodology set forth in the RFP. The Company shall not be entitled to any other adjustments in the Service Fee during the extension periods, except as otherwise provided for in this Agreement. |

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the four positions themselves - insurance that is substantially similar to the requested coverage at the specified limits with minor alignment to the corporate program; events of default that exclude regulatory outcomes driven by circumstances beyond the contractor's control; a reciprocal default when the client withholds approval of properly submitted maintenance and repair expenditures or non-elective capital expenditures; and renewal by mutual agreement of both parties rather than a unilateral client option. The 9.02(a) redline is a good model of a symmetry argument: the contractor's default carve-out and the client's approval obligation are written into the same sentence.

Pursuit-specific: article numbers, the ten-day compliance window, the $3,000 maintenance and repair approval threshold, the ten-year initial term and five one-year extensions, and the Town Meeting authorization step, which is a New England municipal governance feature and should be replaced with the new client's approving body.
