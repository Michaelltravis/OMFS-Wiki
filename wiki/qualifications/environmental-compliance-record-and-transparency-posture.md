# Environmental Compliance Record and Transparency Posture

**99.98% Environmental Compliance Record.** We are proud to be recognized as one of the most trusted and transparent operators with a **99.98% environmental compliance rate, one of the highest in the industry.** Every day, we serve 12.6 million customers and manage over 1.3 billion gallons of water and wastewater treatment capacity. Our size, scope, and technical leadership demonstrate not only our capacity to deliver, but our deep-rooted commitment to compliance, accountability, and continuous improvement.

While permit exceedances and regulatory events may occur, as is expected with operations of this magnitude, we maintain a robust response process to address, report, and learn from every instance. We use more sophisticated and diligent compliance tracking systems than many of our competitors, which means our reporting may appear more extensive—not because we have more violations, but because we're more thorough and transparent in capturing and sharing data. This approach aligns with our culture of transparency and our belief that trust is built through honesty and continuous improvement.
