## Reuse guidance

This exhibit pattern — a facility-by-facility process and technology checkmark matrix plus a "Total Project Experience in Jacobs" aggregate row — is a highly reusable format for proving relevant experience on any water or wastewater O&M pursuit. Swap the reference project rows for the four to six references most technically and geographically similar to the new pursuit, and fill the "[CLIENT] FACILITY" row with the new pursuit's own facility name, MGD, and process checkmarks sourced from the RFP and scope documents; it should never remain a placeholder in a final draft. The aggregate "Total Project Experience in Jacobs" figures are firm-wide counts (for example, 123 plants with non-potable reuse experience and 53 with MBR) — verify they are current before reuse, as they are refreshed periodically. The West Basin footnote documents a live transition and its September 1, 2025 assumption-of-responsibility date; update the date or replace the note when reused.
