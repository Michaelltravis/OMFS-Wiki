## Reuse guidance

This exhibit pattern (facility-by-facility process/technology checkmark matrix plus a "Total Project Experience in Jacobs" aggregate row) is a highly reusable format for proving relevant experience across any water/wastewater O&M pursuit — swap the reference project rows for the four-to-six references most technically and geographically similar to the new pursuit. The "[CLIENT] FACILITY" row should be filled in with the new pursuit's own facility name, MGD, and process checkmarks (sourced from the RFP/scope documents), not left as a placeholder in the final draft.
