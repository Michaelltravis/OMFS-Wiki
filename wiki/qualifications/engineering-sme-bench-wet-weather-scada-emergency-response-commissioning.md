## Reuse guidance

Universal: the five disciplines here answer the risk questions an operations evaluator actually asks — what happens in a storm, who owns the control system, who writes and drills the emergency plan, who starts up new assets without disrupting service, and who connects capital decisions to asset data. The technique worth copying is the geographic anchor: the wet-weather SME is described first by state-based experience and state PE registrations, not by his employer.

Pursuit-specific and high-loss: two entries originally cited prior work performed for this same client — a large-scale AVEVA SCADA program and risk/resilience and emergency response planning across its water, wastewater, and solid waste systems. Those are incumbency-adjacent proof and cannot be transported to another pursuit; read `verbatim/ocwut-16-26/pages/p0093.md` and substitute the new client's own history or delete the claim.

Third-party references that stay verbatim: Tulsa Metropolitan Utility Authority, Sacramento Department of Utilities, and the Northeast Water Purification Plant in Houston. Pairs with the OT/cybersecurity entry in the process/compliance SME block.
