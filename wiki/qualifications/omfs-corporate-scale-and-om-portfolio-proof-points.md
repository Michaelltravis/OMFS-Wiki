# OMFS Corporate Scale and O&M Portfolio Proof Points

Jacobs is a leading provider of water and wastewater operations and maintenance (O&M) services in North America and worldwide, with more than 45 years of experience providing O&M services for water and wastewater systems — supporting complex facilities from startup and commissioning through long-term operations. Our O&M portfolio spans facilities across the United States, including a strong presence in the Northeast, demonstrating our ability to deliver consistent, high-quality operations across diverse systems and regulatory environments. Our portfolio of more than 300 O&M projects includes water and wastewater treatment facilities, collection and distribution systems, water quality laboratories, stormwater management programs, and groundwater remediation systems.

Founded in 1947 and headquartered in Dallas, Texas, Jacobs is structured as approximately 370 operating companies and affiliates, including more than 100 operating entities in North America, providing a full spectrum of services — including O&M, scientific, technical, professional, construction, and program management — across business, industrial, commercial, government, and infrastructure sectors. Our combined workforce includes approximately 700 staff in New England, 29,000+ staff across the U.S., and 42,000+ staff worldwide, providing the resources and financial strength needed to successfully deliver all aspects of [CLIENT]'s scope of services.

As a multidisciplined O&M provider, we bring a **"more-than-an-operator" approach**, integrating operations, process engineering, asset management, and technical services to optimize system performance, reliability, and compliance. Our experience directly aligns with [CLIENT]'s requirements, including operation of wastewater treatment facilities exceeding [X] MGD, management of collection systems greater than [X] miles, and operation of systems with multiple pump stations and low-pressure sewer networks.
