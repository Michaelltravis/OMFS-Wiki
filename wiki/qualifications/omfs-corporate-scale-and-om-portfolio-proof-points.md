# OMFS Corporate Scale and O&M Portfolio Proof Points

Jacobs is a leading provider of water and wastewater operations and maintenance (O&M) services in North America and worldwide. Established in 1947 and headquartered in Dallas, Texas, Jacobs is a U.S.-based Fortune 500 company with approximately $12 billion in revenue in FY24 and a backlog of more than $2.4 billion in O&M services. We have approximately 700 staff in New England, 29,000+ across the U.S., and 42,000+ worldwide, providing the resources and financial strength to successfully deliver all aspects of [CLIENT]'s scope of services.

We bring more than 45 years of wastewater O&M experience, including extensive experience in EPA Region 1 and New York. We operate and maintain facilities and collection systems comparable in size and complexity to [CLIENT], a coastal New England municipal wastewater utility, including systems exceeding 3 MGD, 40 miles of collection piping, multiple pump stations, and low-pressure sewer systems.

Our project organization chart, provided in the Project Staffing and Project Management Plan section, delineates lines of authority within the organization. Resumes for key personnel are included in the resume appendix.

Jacobs has more than 45 years of experience providing O&M services for water and wastewater systems, supporting complex facilities from startup and commissioning through long-term operations. As shown in **Exhibit 3-1**, our O&M portfolio spans facilities across the United States, including a strong presence in the Northeast, demonstrating our ability to deliver consistent, high-quality operations across diverse systems and regulatory environments. Our portfolio of more than 300 O&M projects includes water and wastewater treatment facilities, collection and distribution systems, water quality laboratories, stormwater management programs, and groundwater remediation systems.

As a multidisciplined O&M provider, we bring a **"more-than-an-operator" approach**, integrating operations, process engineering, asset management, and technical services to optimize system performance, reliability, and compliance. This approach directly supports [CLIENT]'s goals for reliable operations, regulatory compliance, and long-term system performance. Our experience directly aligns with [CLIENT]'s requirements, including operation of wastewater treatment facilities exceeding 3 MGD, management of collection systems greater than 40 miles, and operation of systems with multiple pump stations and low-pressure sewer networks.
