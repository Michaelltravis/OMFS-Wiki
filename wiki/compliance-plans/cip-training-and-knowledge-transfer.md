# CIP Training and Knowledge Transfer Before Operational Handover

**New systems and technologies demand new skills and we’ll build those skills before operational handover, not after.** For each CIP project, our training approach follows a structured sequence. **Manufacturer and vendor training:** We will coordinate with equipment manufacturers for both factory and on-site training during commissioning. For hypochlorite generation, this means hands-on training with PSI/Microclor® electrolytic cell systems, brine management, and control interfaces, leveraging our direct experience operating PSI units at similar facilities. For heat drying, manufacturer training will cover thermal system operation, emissions monitoring, material handling, and emergency procedures.

**Construction contractor and integrator coordination:** Jacobs staff will participate in startup and proofing activities, gaining hands-on familiarity with each system in its installed configuration before assuming full responsibility. **Process operations training:** Coordination with [CLIENT], a Southcentral municipal water utility trust, and its engineers to understand new treatment configurations, control strategies, and performance expectations for each upgraded process. **Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and Standard Maintenance Procedures (SMPs) development:** We’ll develop SOPs and SMPs for each new facility or process using construction contract-provided O&M information and engineer-provided process operations information, as required by Schedule 16 Startup Services.

Training will begin well before operational handover, with staff participating in commissioning, proofing, and startup activities to build hands-on familiarity. For our comprehensive training program structure, refer to our **Staffing and Training Plan earlier in this section.**

## Reuse guidance

Use the sequence when the operator must assume a newly constructed asset. Tailor the systems, vendors, startup phase, and applicable operating and maintenance procedures.
