# Emergency Preparedness and Response: Preparedness, Coordination, and Rapid Response

Jacobs will implement an integrated Emergency Response and Disaster Preparedness Program that meets the requirements of RFP Exhibit M, Section E and Article 3.03(e). Our approach emphasizes readiness, coordination, and clear communication to ensure effective response to unplanned events that may affect safety, operations, or regulatory compliance.

## Emergency Response Plan (ERP) Development

Within 60 days of contract execution, Jacobs will develop a comprehensive Emergency Response Plan (ERP) for both water reclamation facilities and the conveyance system. The ERP will define roles and responsibilities for Jacobs and [CLIENT] personnel, establish notification and communication procedures, and outline an incident command structure aligned with [CLIENT]'s expectations. Response protocols will address credible emergency scenarios, including fires, flooding, severe weather, chemical releases, confined space incidents, equipment failures, and other operational disruptions.

The ERP will also include procedures for safe equipment shutdown and restart, coordination with local emergency responders, and temporary operational adjustments during abnormal conditions. Jacobs and [CLIENT] will review and update the ERP annually and following drills or actual events.

## Training, Exercises, and Operational Readiness

Jacobs will maintain emergency readiness through regular training and coordinated exercises. Staff will be trained in communication procedures, evacuation and shelter-in-place protocols, and hazard-specific response actions. At least annually, Jacobs will conduct joint drills with [CLIENT]'s Risk Manager, Safety Advisor, and local emergency responders, followed by after-action reviews to document lessons learned and corrective actions.

Weather-related preparedness will be supported through pre-event inspections, verification of backup power systems, and post-event assessments to ensure safe and timely restoration of operations.

> "When Superstorm Sandy hit the New York harbor and the magnitude of the disaster became apparent, you were the first person that I reached out to for help. Fortunately for the North Hudson Sewerage Authority (NHSA), Jacobs, our partner of 22 years at the time, came to our assistance and in 24 hours provided an expert team to address the damage caused by the disastrous storm surge. Our main sewage treatment plant in Hoboken was pumped out; some 24 pumps were removed, rebuilt, and replaced; and temporary electrical control systems were constructed to enable primary treatment 48 hours later with full secondary treatment restored within 5 days of the system failure."
>
> — Fredric J. Pocci, PE, North Hudson Sewerage Authority Engineer

## Communication, Documentation, and Continuous Improvement

During any emergency, [CLIENT]'s PMR and Risk Manager will receive immediate notification, and Jacobs will maintain frequent communication with [CLIENT] and coordinate with local emergency services as needed. All incidents, drills, and corrective actions will be documented in Safety360 and incorporated into ERP updates and future training.

Emergency preparedness and response effectiveness will be reviewed annually through audits, tabletop exercises, and governance reviews with [CLIENT], with documentation maintained and available upon request.

## Commitment to Resilient Operations

Through disciplined planning, trained personnel, and coordinated response actions, Jacobs will support safe, resilient operations that protect [CLIENT] staff, the public, and the environment during emergency conditions.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the four-part structure (ERP development on a stated clock, training and exercises, communication and documentation, closing resilience commitment); the credible-scenario list; the annual joint drill with the client's safety staff and local responders followed by after-action review; the weather-preparedness triad of pre-event inspection, backup power verification, and post-event assessment; and the Superstorm Sandy testimonial, which is the strongest emergency-response proof in the library and travels to any pursuit where storm or flood response is a scored concern.

Pursuit-specific: the RFP article citations, the 60-day ERP window, the client's control-room and role names, and the scenario list — swap in seismic, wildfire, hurricane, or extreme-cold scenarios as the geography demands. Verify testimonial permission before external use.
