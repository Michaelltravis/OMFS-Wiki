# Biosolids Storage and Backlog Management

**Storage management is a direct compliance and financial risk driver at [CLIENT].** Schedule 14 limits storage to 3 days of production without prior written authorization, and Schedule 21 fees apply when storage exceeds 50 percent of total capacity without prior written authorization of the General Manager or their designee.

Jacobs will implement a structured Storage Management Protocol built on daily monitoring and proactive intervention at our 35 percent internal trigger. Our operators will track pad utilization each day, and our supervisors will review conditions daily. When utilization approaches 35 percent, the Operations Manager will evaluate hauling schedules, dewatering performance, and land application availability, and implement adjustments before risk develops.

If utilization approaches 50 percent, we'll seek prior written authorization from the General Manager, submit the required Service Approval Form with an action response plan, and implement corrective actions consistent with Schedule 21, protecting [CLIENT] from fee exposure while maintaining operational transparency. **We will not manage to the limit. We will manage well below it.**

When land application is not feasible, contingency disposal strategies will be activated to maintain system stability and prevent backlog.

## Reuse guidance

Use as the compliance-control companion to the processing and dewatering block. Confirm storage capacity calculation, authorization protocol, escalation authority, and disposal options before reuse. This is a solids-plan storage control, not a replacement for the separate Sludge Management Plan.
