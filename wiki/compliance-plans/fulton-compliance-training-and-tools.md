# JV Compliance Training and Oversight Tools

Competency is enhanced through a combination of tailored learning curriculum and on-the-job training with senior technicians, coupled with a positive culture that encourages and rewards engagement and development. Training will be performed by our qualified and experienced personnel, supported by a deep pool of local, regional, and national resources. This expertise will result in leading-edge training in O&M disciplines as well as NPDES and other federal, state, and local regulations.

Our suite of compliance tools will help achieve long-term compliance with environmental regulations and permit conditions. These tools include:

- A comprehensive training program, including project startup, video training modules, and ongoing training provided by web-based delivery mechanisms and onsite in-person training.
- NPDES, reuse, groundwater, drinking-water, and sample-tracking tools, with multiple layers of oversight to ensure sampling is performed as required.
- In-depth NPDES, reuse, groundwater, drinking-water, biosolids, industrial pretreatment, Risk Management Plan, laboratory, and air-permit evaluation tools to ensure all permit requirements are identified and fully understood.
- A standardized communication tool for project staff to promptly report excursions or other regulatory noncompliance issues to management and the Compliance & Reporting team, aiding in assessing incident severity and determining necessary support.
- A SharePoint site that provides access to project compliance tools and other tools and compliance-related information.

## Reuse guidance

Use with a confirmed training and compliance-tool set. Confirm whether NPDES, reuse, groundwater, drinking-water, biosolids, industrial pretreatment, Risk Management Plan, laboratory, and air-permit tools apply to the new pursuit.
