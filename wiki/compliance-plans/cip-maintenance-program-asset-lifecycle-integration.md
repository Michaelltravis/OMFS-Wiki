# CIP Maintenance Program and Asset Lifecycle Integration

Every new asset needs to be absorbed into the maintenance program from day one. Our approach to maintenance integration connects directly to our overall Maintenance Plan and leverages NexGen EAM as the single system of record. **CMMS Configuration and Asset Additions:** For each completed CIP project, we will configure NexGen EAM to incorporate new assets with complete documentation: manufacturer data, model and serial numbers, install dates, warranty terms, and criticality rankings. We’ll establish parent-child asset relationships linked to facility process areas, build Bills of Material from manufacturer documentation for efficient parts identification, and configure automated reporting so new assets are captured in KPI tracking and monthly reports from day one.

**Spare Parts and Consumables:** We’ll develop spare parts inventory strategies for each new facility before operational handover, identifying critical spares based on manufacturer recommendations and our experience, establishing minimum/maximum inventory levels in NexGen, and coordinating initial stocking with commissioning timelines. For hypochlorite generation, this includes proprietary electrolytic cell cartridges and brine system components. For heat drying, it includes thermal system consumables and emissions monitoring supplies. New consumables such as salt/brine for generation, fuel for drying, and polymer changes will be integrated into procurement schedules.

**Warranty Tracking and Compliance:** New CIP equipment carries manufacturer warranties that represent significant value to [CLIENT], a Southcentral municipal water utility trust. We’ll enter all warranty terms, conditions, and expiration dates into NexGen during commissioning and flag warranty-covered assets in the work order system, so corrective maintenance triggers a warranty review before any repair work proceeds. We’ll build warranty-required maintenance tasks into the preventive maintenance (PM) schedule and manage defective work claims through the 2-year maintenance bond per Schedule 16. Updated warranty lists will be included in monthly reports. **Long-Term Lifecycle Maintenance Planning:** We will update the Asset Management Plan after each CIP project, incorporating new assets into lifecycle cost projections and our Asset Replacement Modeling (ARM) tool. We’ll adjust risk profiles and criticality rankings, develop optimized PM/predictive maintenance (PdM) strategies starting with manufacturer recommendations and refining with NexGen operating data, and feed updated projections into the annual Facilities Status Report and CIP recommendations due each October 15.

## Reuse guidance

Use when commissioning must deliver assets into an established maintenance program. Tailor the EAM, required documentation, warranty instrument, lifecycle tool, and reporting deadlines.
