# Continuous Improvement, Baseline Compliance Audit, and Laboratory Compliance Tools

## Continuous improvement and compliance assurance

Within the first 90 days, Jacobs will complete a baseline compliance audit covering permit requirements, monitoring programs, laboratory systems, reporting processes, and site-specific risks at each facility. We'll document findings and track them through a corrective action program with defined responsibilities and timelines, which we'll share transparently with [CLIENT]. [CLIENT] is a Southcentral US municipal water utility trust operating four wastewater treatment plants and a major pump station under an ODEQ regulatory regime.

This connects directly to the Management Oversight QA/QC Plan required by Schedule 3, with a draft due October 15, 2026, and a final version due December 31, 2026. That plan will describe how Jacobs monitors daily operations, maintenance activities, regulatory compliance, and reporting obligations; how performance is reviewed by management; and how issues are identified, documented, escalated, and resolved.
