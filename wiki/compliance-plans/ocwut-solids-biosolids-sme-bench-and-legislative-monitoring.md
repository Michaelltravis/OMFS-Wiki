# Biosolids Expertise That Goes Beyond Day-to-Day Operations

**Todd Williams, PE, BCEE, WEF Fellow — Biosolids Engineering SME.** Todd brings 45 years of experience in biosolids and residuals management, holding two U.S. patents for biosolids treatment processes and having supported more than 100 biosolids management projects nationwide. Todd will advise the team on dewatering optimization, beneficial reuse alternatives, and long-term program development, including the drying capacity evaluation at North Canadian.

**Drew Mearns — O&M Biosolids SME.** Drew brings 34 years of hands-on biosolids experience spanning the full lifecycle from dewatering and storage through land application, disposal, and emerging beneficial reuse alternatives. Drew advises Jacobs' O&M projects nationally on cost-effective biosolids best management practices and is deeply familiar with national residuals management firms, their capabilities, and their pricing, giving [CLIENT] — a Southcentral US municipal water utility trust — direct access to the market knowledge needed to build a competitive and resilient program.

We are actively tracking the 2026 Oklahoma legislative session for potential regulatory changes affecting biosolids management and will continue this monitoring annually. Our compliance team will work closely with project staff to keep the program ahead of any changes.
