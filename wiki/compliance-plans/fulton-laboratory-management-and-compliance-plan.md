# Laboratory Management and Compliance Plan

Jacobs operates seven certified laboratories under the national TNI standard and is well-versed in the specific expectations of Georgia EPD and [CLIENT], a county wastewater utility. Our plan ensures full compliance with NPDES permits, reuse permits, stormwater and SPCC requirements, sludge-disposal regulations, and state and federal rules under the Clean Water Act. In alignment with Georgia EPD’s zero-tolerance policy for Sanitary Sewer Overflows, our monitoring program includes controls to detect early warning signs and drive rapid corrective action.

Our internal team of regulatory and industrial-pretreatment experts will work closely with onsite project staff to assure that Consent Order conditions, such as the Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan, are fully implemented. The industrial-pretreatment team will assist the project in monitoring [FACILITY A] influent for pollutants that have the potential to adversely impact the WRF and work closely with [CLIENT] in investigating the source of any slug loads that could lead to pass-through or interference.

At contract commencement, Jacobs will conduct a full laboratory-system startup, including QA/QC audits, method review, and LIMS validation. This ensures compliance from day one and establishes baseline documentation aligned with Article 50.6.

When required for specialized analysis outside our certification scope, we will contract with certified labs such as Eurofins Analytical and/or Pace Analytical Laboratories. These laboratories are approved by Georgia’s Environmental Protection Division.

Field sampling will follow EPA-approved methods under 40 CFR Part 136 and include immediate checks for turbidity, pH, temperature, and chlorine residuals, among others. JC Solutions’ operators will verify field results against regulatory thresholds in real time and notify [CLIENT] of any deviations as required by Article 50.14.

As specified in the contract, JC Solutions will conduct all routine and process-control sampling and laboratory testing required to manage facility performance. However, laboratory work associated with discharges for NPDES and Industrial Pretreatment Program purposes will continue to be performed by [CLIENT]. We will coordinate with [CLIENT] personnel to ensure seamless handoff of compliance-related data.
