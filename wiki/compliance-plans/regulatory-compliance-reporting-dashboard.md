# Regulatory Compliance Reporting and Real-Time Dashboard

A centralized, auditable compliance management system integrates operational, laboratory, and maintenance data into a single record. Client leadership receives real-time visibility into permit parameters, reporting status, and performance trends. Automated alerts identify approaching limits, enabling proactive response before an exceedance occurs.

All required regulatory submissions are prepared accurately and submitted on time in accordance with applicable state and EPA regional requirements, including:

- Discharge monitoring reports (DMRs) and NetDMR filings.
- Biosolids reports.
- PFAS sampling documentation.
- Combined sewer overflow (CSO) notifications (where applicable).
- Other required regulatory filings.

Records remain secure and audit-ready at all times.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the "centralized record → real-time client visibility → automated proactive alerts" structure and the list of common wastewater regulatory submission types. Tailor per pursuit: confirm which submission types actually apply to the target facility (e.g., omit CSO notifications for a fully separated collection system); name the actual reporting platform/dashboard tool being proposed rather than implying a specific vendor product unless confirmed. Pair with the Six-Point Compliance Program block (pillar 2, Transparency and Reporting) and the Tiered Communication and Reporting Protocol block in technical-approach.
