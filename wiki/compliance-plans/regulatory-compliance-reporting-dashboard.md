# Regulatory Compliance Reporting and Real-Time Dashboard

A centralized, auditable compliance management system integrates operational, laboratory, and maintenance data into a single record. As illustrated in Exhibit 5-7, a real-time compliance dashboard gives [CLIENT] leadership — [CLIENT] being a coastal New England municipality operating a WWTF and collection system — **real-time visibility into permit parameters, reporting status, and performance trends**. Automated alerts identify approaching limits, enabling **proactive response**.

All required regulatory submissions, including discharge monitoring reports (DMRs), NetDMR filings, biosolids reports, PFAS sampling documentation, combined sewer overflow (CSO) notifications, and other filings, will be prepared accurately and submitted on time in accordance with applicable state and EPA regional requirements. Records remain **secure and audit-ready at all times**.
