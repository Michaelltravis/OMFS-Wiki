# Hypochlorite Transport Operational Integration Plan

Schedule 18 requires a plan to transport hypochlorite generated at the Deer Creek facility to the Chisholm Creek facility. We will develop a detailed Hypochlorite Transport Plan in coordination with [CLIENT], a Southcentral municipal water utility trust, as the Deer Creek generation system design progresses. Because the system isn’t estimated for completion until 2029, a fully specified logistics plan would be premature at this stage. However, the planning framework will address logistics and cost-effectiveness: transport method, vehicle requirements, frequency, and route considerations weighed against the alternative of direct chemical delivery to Chisholm Creek. Our initial assessment, informed by our team’s operational analysis, is that hauling costs between facilities may be comparable to direct delivery, making the cost-benefit analysis a key decision point for [CLIENT].

The planning framework will address safety and compliance: chemical handling and transport protocols, DOT compliance with sodium hypochlorite transport, and spill response procedures. It will also address responsibility and delivery model: whether transport is handled by a chemical transport subcontractor, the hypochlorite system vendor, or through another arrangement. Jacobs does not anticipate self-performing bulk chemical transport between facilities. Finally, it will address contingency: temporary reversion to delivered chemical supply if transport is interrupted or the generation system is offline.
