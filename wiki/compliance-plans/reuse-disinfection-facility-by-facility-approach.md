# Reuse Disinfection — Facility-by-Facility Approach and Hypochlorite Transition

## Getting disinfection right — facility by facility

We operate reuse facilities of all sizes across the U.S., from compact multi-customer programs to the nation's largest advanced recycling plant. We know reuse success lives in the details — treatment performance, permit compliance, residuals handling, and how you work with each customer — and we'll bring that hands-on experience directly to [CLIENT]'s reuse agreements. [CLIENT] is a Southcentral US municipal water utility trust whose reuse program delivers to industrial and golf course customers from three of its wastewater treatment plants.

At each reuse point, we'll establish controlled chlorine dosing tied to measurable residual targets:

- At Deer Creek, our immediate priority is restoring the CL17 residual analyzer to functional status and transitioning the chlorine system from manual to automated control, giving operators real-time residual feedback for the Gaillardia reuse stream.
- At each facility, we'll implement chlorine and dechlorination sampling practices that focus on both free and total chlorine residual to better optimize dosing to reduce fecal counts.
- As OSHG comes online at South Canadian, we'll integrate it with SCADA to provide stable, traceable hypochlorite supply and closed-loop residual control.
- At South Canadian, the new PSI MicroClor generators will be the first to come online. Jacobs already operates PSI generator units at our Pampa, Texas, and Ontario, Oregon, facilities, which we'll apply to optimize brine management, cartridge maintenance scheduling, and generation volumes.
- During transition between disinfection platforms, redundant storage and dosing capability plus automatic cutover will maintain continuity.
