1. Listing of all permit/regulatory violations and penalties, including odor and noise complaints associated with wastewater-system operations: **Zero.**
2. Listing of any permit exceedance affecting the receiving waters: **Zero.**
3. Complete listing of all incidents where contract requirements were not met, including SSOs, permit violations, regulatory violations, odor, and noise: **Zero.**
4. Listing and description of any plant upsets, including their cause and effect: **Zero.**
5. All incidents where service fees were reduced for failure to meet contract requirements: **Zero.**
