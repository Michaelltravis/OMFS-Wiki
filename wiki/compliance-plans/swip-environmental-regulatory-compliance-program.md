# Environmental Regulatory Compliance Program, Dashboard, and Risk Management Approach

## Regulatory Compliance

Jacobs' environmental compliance record is unmatched in the industry. Our goal is perfect regulatory compliance and environmental protection across all our O&M contracts, and we will strive to achieve this standard for [CLIENT], a Southern California municipal owner of advanced water treatment, water recycling, stormwater, and groundwater injection facilities. To do so, Jacobs will tailor our Environmental Compliance Program to the unique requirements of [CLIENT]'s facilities and fully implement the program with transparency and rigor. Our Environmental Compliance Program will:

- Generate consistent compliance with all applicable environmental laws, regulations, and standards
- Alert [CLIENT] staff to situations that require special attention or escalation to regional or corporate support teams
- Generate legally defensible data and records

Jacobs' compliance experts bring decades of experience in environmental regulations applicable to water and wastewater operations, including the Clean Water Act, Safe Drinking Water Act, Clean Air Act, Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act, and Resource Conservation and Recovery Act. We also provide deep expertise in recycled water regulations (Title 22), groundwater recharge, biosolids, stormwater, risk management planning, and laboratory operations.

**99.98% — Jacobs' environmental compliance record is unmatched in the industry.**

Jacobs maintains trusted working relationships with regulators at the local, state, and federal levels, enabling effective communication and regulatory advocacy on behalf of our clients. For [CLIENT], we will ensure compliance with all facility-related permits—including NPDES, Title 22 GRRP, and AQMD—and will perform all required sampling, analysis, and reporting in accordance with state and federal guidelines.

We will also coordinate with [CLIENT] on any external factors that may impact permit compliance. Jacobs will address all regulatory requirements identified in the RFP scope, including:

- Preparing and submitting all required regulatory reports (e.g., DMRs, MORs, water recycling facility compliance records)
- Supporting development or updates to risk management plans and vulnerability assessments
- Assisting [CLIENT] in documenting and responding to industrial discharges affecting the system
- Performing all corrective actions and recommendations resulting from inspections by the Regional Water Quality Control Board, the Division of Drinking Water, or other agencies
- Ensuring timely payment of regulatory fees and permit renewals

Jacobs will implement a secure, web-based compliance dashboard to manage and track all regulatory deliverables, plant performance, and monitoring schedules. User access will be role-specific, ensuring both data integrity and [CLIENT] visibility. Working collaboratively with [CLIENT] staff, we will configure dashboard views and reports to support local oversight and performance reviews.

**Regular Internal Audits.** As discussed earlier in the subsection titled "In-House Procedures to Provide Accuracy, Integrity, And Quality Control," Jacobs will conduct routine compliance audits as part of our QA/QC Program.

**Environmental Risk Management Approach.** Our team assesses project risks through multiple lenses: regulatory compliance, financial exposure, safety, asset condition, and contractual performance. These factors are scored and aggregated to identify emerging risks, with results reviewed by Jacobs' regional leadership and used to develop targeted improvement plans.

**Open Communication and Reporting Transparency.** Transparency is fundamental to our compliance culture. Jacobs will maintain clear and thorough records of all operations and provide timely and complete reporting to [CLIENT] and regulatory agencies. Any permit violations, exceedances, or compliance concerns will be communicated immediately.
