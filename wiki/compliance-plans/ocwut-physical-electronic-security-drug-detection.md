# Physical and Electronic Site Security Plan with Drug Detection and Deterrence

## Physical and electronic security

**[CLIENT]'s wastewater facilities are critical infrastructure, and we will treat site security that way** — [CLIENT] being a Southcentral US municipal water utility trust. Our Security Plan will protect people, facilities, and operational assets through controlled access, routine inspection, disciplined documentation, and rapid response when conditions change. Site Security SME **Keith Waddell, CPP, CFE**, will support our team in aligning practical field security practices with [CLIENT]'s facility access, restricted areas, and documentation requirements. We'll conduct regular inspections of perimeter fencing, gates, lock functionality, and fence integrity. If we identify a disturbance, breach, or other security concern, we will secure the area, document the condition, report it promptly to the General Manager or designee, and complete or coordinate corrective action without delay. We'll keep gates closed and locked except when admitting authorized personnel or vehicles, and access will be limited to individuals presenting proper identification in accordance with [CLIENT] requirements. We will maintain secure access to AMAG-protected areas, server rooms, control rooms, and other restricted spaces except for approved entry.

Physical security and digital security must work together. We'll align site security practices with [CLIENT]'s electronic security requirements and Information Technology Systems implementation approach, so credentials, access controls, OT, and facility data are managed as one coordinated system. Keith will support this coordination by helping our team evaluate access control practices, restricted-space protocols, incident documentation, and security-sensitive communications through an operations lens. We'll support controlled access to SCADA and OT environments, disciplined account management, and secure handling of operational information in coordination with [CLIENT]'s IT and SCADA teams. We will follow [CLIENT]'s security requirements, support additional security measures developed during the contract term, and protect security-sensitive information through authorized channels only.

## Drug detection and deterrence

Because these facilities are safety-critical, workforce reliability matters just as much as technical capability. We'll implement a Drug Detection and Deterrence Plan aligned with [CLIENT]'s municipal Human Resources Workplace Standards. The plan will support a safe, dependable work environment through pre-employment screening, required background checks, employee and supervisor awareness training, monitoring procedures, and a process for documenting and reporting criminal convictions as required by the municipality. This approach will help protect employees, reduce workplace risk, and strengthen public confidence in the team operating [CLIENT]'s facilities.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the "critical infrastructure, and we will treat site security that way" opener; the four-verb breach response (secure, document, report, correct); the gates-closed-and-locked and identification-controlled access commitments; and the paragraph joining physical security to OT/SCADA account management, which answers most electronic-security requirements without claiming an IT scope the operator does not hold. Pursuit-specific: the access-control platform, the client escalation title, the IT/SCADA coordination model, and the HR workplace standards the drug and deterrence plan aligns to.
