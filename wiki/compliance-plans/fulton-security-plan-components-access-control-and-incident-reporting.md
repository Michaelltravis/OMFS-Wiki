# Security Plan Components: Access Control, Incident Reporting, and Information Protection

Our Security Plan will address the following components:

**Access Control and Surveillance.** Continuous oversight of all entry points and secure zones using surveillance cameras, intrusion detection systems, and access badge or code-based entry, as required. Security systems will be tested and maintained regularly.

**Incident Reporting and Response.** All incidents involving theft, vandalism, unauthorized access, property damage, or threats will be immediately reported, investigated, and documented in accordance with contract protocols. [CLIENT] personnel will be notified as required under Article 50.8.

**Annual Security Assessments.** The Project Manager will lead annual physical security and asset protection assessments to identify risks, ensure system readiness, and continuously improve site resilience.

**Information Protection.** SCADA/IT system access and data protection protocols will be implemented in accordance with [CLIENT]'s and our standards, ensuring alignment with cybersecurity policies and infrastructure protections.

**Construction Coordination.** Security will be maintained during capital improvement or construction activities, with access controls coordinated between JC Solutions, [CLIENT] staff, and third-party contractors.
