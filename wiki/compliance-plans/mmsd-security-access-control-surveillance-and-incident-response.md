# Access Control and Surveillance Management

Jacobs will manage [CLIENT]'s access control and surveillance infrastructure, including periodic, client-approved access audits to verify compliance. Surveillance systems at the two water reclamation facilities and the district's downtown parking structure will be centrally monitored using the Genetec platform at a location mutually determined with [CLIENT] leadership. Jacobs will perform PM, routine functionality checks, and coordinate closely with [CLIENT] IT to ensure timely resolution of outages. Key and lock control will be centrally managed using controlled issuance and inventory tracking to maintain accountability and alignment with [CLIENT] procedures.

# Incident Reporting and Response

Jacobs will maintain written, [CLIENT]-approved Site Security Procedures consistent with Exhibit M, defining responsibilities, escalation paths, and required staff training. These procedures will align with [CLIENT]'s timelines for reporting security events including, but not limited to unknown intruder or trespasser, theft, vandalism, drone incursion and facility breaches to ensure a coordinated rapid response and action through [CLIENT]'s Control Room or Process Monitoring Room (PMR); when necessary, relevant law enforcement agency will be contacted along with operations management.

Jacobs recognizes that [CLIENT]'s security responsibilities extend beyond treatment facilities to include entry gate access control, pump stations, conveyance assets, and remote monitoring locations. To support these requirements, Jacobs can integrate alarms from [CLIENT]'s SCADA systems into the security platform to provide redundant notification of critical events. This approach enables resilient, continuous monitoring of security conditions through the security control center in parallel with [CLIENT]'s Operations Center, providing a dual-layered approach to protection.

All security and emergency events will follow the same notification hierarchy through [CLIENT]'s PMR to maintain unified coordination across intruder, chemical, fire, or weather-related incidents through a documented security plan.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the four access-control commitments (manage the infrastructure, run periodic client-approved access audits, PM and functionality checks with IT coordination, centrally controlled key and lock issuance with inventory tracking); the security-event category list; the "security extends beyond the fence" paragraph; and the single notification hierarchy for intruder, chemical, fire, and weather events alike.

Pursuit-specific: the video management platform (Genetec here), the named monitored sites, the client's control-room name, and the reporting timelines, which must match the contract. The SCADA-alarm integration offer should be checked against the operational technology section so the proposal makes one consistent commitment about touching client control systems.
