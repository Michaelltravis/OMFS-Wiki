# Transition Due Diligence and Mobilization Governance Checklist

## Immediate Mobilization and Governance

Upon receiving award, Jacobs will activate our Transition Leadership Team and establish a formal governance structure with [CLIENT], a coastal New England municipality. Decision authority, communication cadence, issue escalation, and accountability assignments will be confirmed at the outset, so **progress remains transparent and measurable**. We will immediately complete structured due diligence, including:

- Review of NPDES permit requirements, monitoring/reporting obligations, and compliance history
- Detailed review of O&M manuals, SOPs, CMOM documentation, and historical performance data
- Verification of certified operator coverage and license/grade requirements
- Asset inventory validation and critical equipment condition assessment (including maintenance backlog)
- SCADA/telemetry review, remote access validation, alarm management testing, and cybersecurity verification
- Flood control readiness review (pumps/gates), standby power verification, and storm response procedures

All readiness milestones will be tracked against the Transition Schedule to ensure [CLIENT]'s facilities remain **stable and compliant throughout transition.**

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the sequence — activate the transition leadership team, stand up governance (decision authority, communication cadence, escalation, accountability) before any technical work, then run a documented due-diligence sweep whose results feed a tracked readiness schedule. The six checklist items are the standard sweep for any municipal wastewater O&M transition and can be lifted intact; they double as an answer to RFP questions that ask "what will you do in the first 30 days." Pursuit-specific: the permit type and regulator names, the flood-control and storm-response item (coastal/flood-exposed facilities only), and the cybersecurity item where the client's OT environment is contractor-managed.
