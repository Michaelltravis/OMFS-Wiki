# Solids Compliance Assurance and Related Program Support

Solids handling is one of the highest odor risks across the [CLIENT] system, and process control decisions related to sludge age, detention time, and solids handling directly influences odor generation. **We'll manage solids, process control, and odor as one integrated system.** This Solids Management Plan is fully aligned with the Odor Control Plan and Process Control Plan as required by Schedule 21.

Solids compliance is achieved through disciplined monitoring, review, and action. We'll track daily solids production, processing, hauling, storage levels, and dewatering performance at each facility. Our supervisors will review this data daily and initiate corrective actions before conditions create compliance risk.

Monthly reporting will provide clear visibility into solids performance, including production trends, hauling activity, storage conditions, and corrective actions, structured to align with [CLIENT]'s requirements while providing additional transparency.

**This plan is specifically designed to maintain compliance with both COM-3 and COM-5 through proactive management of solids and odor conditions.**

**We'll treat [CLIENT]'s solids program as a long-term performance opportunity.** We'll continuously evaluate dewatering performance, chemical usage, equipment condition, hauling efficiency, and process integration. **Every improvement will be evaluated based on its impact on compliance, cost, and community.**

[CLIENT]'s solids responsibilities extend beyond wastewater facilities to include residuals management, septage receiving, and industrial pretreatment support. Jacobs will execute each of these programs with the same discipline applied to biosolids operations.

At the Hefner Water Treatment Plant, we'll coordinate the collection and transport of lime residuals to approved recipients or [CLIENT]-designated locations while maintaining all required documentation and compliance tracking.

At the Witcher Pump Station, we'll operate the Septage Receiving Program in accordance with Schedule 6, including pH testing, manifest verification, load inspection, chain-of-custody documentation, and daily reporting. **This is a frontline compliance function that we'll manage with discipline every day.**

We'll also support the Industrial Pretreatment Program by providing qualified staff, coordinating inspections and sampling, and maintaining required documentation in alignment with program requirements.

## Reuse guidance

Use this integrated compliance posture where solids, odor, and process control must be managed together and the same operator supports related residuals programs. Tailor every named site, contract provision, related program, and reporting form. Pair with the hauling and storage blocks; retain the distinct Sludge Management and Operational Integration plan content for their own requirements.
