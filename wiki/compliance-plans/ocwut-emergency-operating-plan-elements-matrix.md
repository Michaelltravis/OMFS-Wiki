# Elements of the Emergency Operating Plan — Event Response Matrix

Our team will operate and maintain [CLIENT] facilities and utilities — [CLIENT] being a Southcentral US municipal water utility trust — under emergency conditions with a plan built around reliability, safety, communication, and continuity of operations. Our emergency operating plan will include staff training, emergency equipment readiness, defined response roles, and coordination procedures with the municipality and external agencies. Drawing on our experience supporting utilities through severe weather, flooding, and other system disruptions, we will establish immediate response actions that protect people first, stabilize operations quickly, and support efficient recovery.

At a minimum, the plan will address fires, floods, tornadoes, and power outages as required by the RFP and will also cover the broader emergency conditions most relevant to [CLIENT]'s facilities and collection system operations. These include chemical spills, asset and equipment failures, process failures, emergency communications, emergency equipment access, continuity of operations, and pipeline failures or blockages (**Exhibit 1-25**).

**Exhibit 1-25.** *Elements of our Emergency Operating Plan*

| EVENT | JACOBS' APPROACH |
|---|---|
| **Chemical spill response** | Individual safety data sheets (SDS) will guide proper clean-up and control measures. |
| **Asset/equipment failure response** | Identify the issue quickly, use available spare parts, and complete repairs or replacement to restore operations with minimal downtime. |
| **Fire response** | Train staff in prevention and initial response, coordinate with fire departments, and mobilize maintenance teams for recovery. |
| **Flood response** | Maintain flood control systems, adjust operations for changing influent conditions, and deploy additional staff to sustain operations. |
| **Tornado response** | Prepare for severe weather through monitoring, facility readiness, and staff staging. Secure assets, verify backup power, prioritize personnel safety, and rapidly assess and restore operations following the event. |
| **Pandemic/health emergency response** | Implement staffing contingency plans, cross-train personnel, and support workforce availability during extended events. |
| **Process failure response** | Monitor trends, perform additional testing, and adjust treatment processes with support from technical experts to stabilize performance. |
| **Power failure response** | Operate and maintain generators, prioritize critical loads, and sustain operations until power is restored. |
| **Emergency communications** | Maintain updated contact lists and clear notification protocols for staff, agencies, and stakeholders. |
| **Emergency equipment** | Maintain agreements for rapid access to rental equipment and specialty services when needed. |
| **Continuity of operations plan** | Maintain defined roles, staffing strategies, and external support to sustain operations during extended disruptions. |
| **Pipeline failure/blockages** | Isolate, bypass, and repair affected areas while communicating status and restoration timelines to [CLIENT]. |
