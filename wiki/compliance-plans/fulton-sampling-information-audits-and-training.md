# Sampling Information Management, Audits, and Training

Our program includes routine and special-condition sampling at key locations, including influent, effluent, reuse, in-process treatment steps, and stormwater and land-application-system discharge points. Sampling frequency, methods, and analytical targets will comply with chain-of-custody documentation, use of EPA-approved methods, internal audits and data reviews, corrective-action logs and procedures per Article 50.9 and Exhibit 5, NPDES and reuse permits, Georgia EPD’s sludge-disposal regulations, stormwater and SPCC plan requirements, SSO early-detection and reporting protocols, and the [FACILITY A] Consent Order.

We will use HACH WIMS to track and analyze laboratory results and operational data. This system enables real-time monitoring, regulatory trend analysis, automated reporting and alerts, and custom dashboards for [CLIENT] stakeholders.

All documents—including SOPs, QA/QC logs, calibration records, and compliance reports—will be maintained within a secure, version-controlled SharePoint site accessible to authorized [CLIENT] personnel. Document management will comply with the Open Records Act and all audit requirements specified in Article 50.12 and Article 14.

JC Solutions will perform regular audits of all laboratory processes, including equipment calibration, data verification, and sampling accuracy. These audits will ensure early detection of risks, immediate implementation of corrective actions, and alignment with Article 53 performance standards. Audit findings will be summarized in our monthly reports and reviewed with [CLIENT] during monthly performance meetings.

All laboratory and field personnel are trained in EPA and EPD regulatory requirements, laboratory safety and ethics, QA/QC procedures and documentation, and emergency response and spill containment. We support professional development through certification programs and require staff performing compliance testing to hold or pursue relevant Georgia EPD certifications. Our training logs and materials will be available for [CLIENT] review and aligned with Article 50.7(D) and Exhibit 4.
