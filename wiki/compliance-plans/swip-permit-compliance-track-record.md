# Summary of Jacobs' Permit and Compliance Record — Violations and Corrective Actions

In accordance with the RFP requirements, **Exhibit 2-24** provides a summary of any over-spills, out-of-compliance production, permit violations, and penalties associated with Jacobs' operations. To ensure clarity, this list is limited to incidents that were directly actionable by Jacobs in its role as operator and does not include items outside of Jacobs' responsibility or control. For each instance, the project, a description of the violation, the corrective action taken, and the date of occurrence are included.

**Exhibit — Summary of Jacobs' Permit and Compliance Record**

| Project Name | Violation Description | Corrective Action | Occurrence Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| City of Clovis | Chlorine, Total Residual Instantaneous Maximum limit is .01 mg/L and reported value was 0.08 mg/L at EFF-A. | Filtrate outlet valve on membrane tank 1 was adjusted. SOP was reviewed. | 1/13/2025 |
| City of Clovis | Chlorine, Total Residual Instantaneous Maximum limit is 0.01 mg/L and reported value was .012 mg/L at EFF-A. | The valves have been adjusted and are now in good working order. All operators now know to stop the chemical dosing if a valve fails. | 12/17/2024 |

The coliform sample collected on 10/4/2024 was supposed to be read out on 10/5/2024. Unfortunately, the sample was not ready until Monday morning, 10/7/2024.
