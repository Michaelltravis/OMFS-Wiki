# Site Security Plan and SSA Framework: Monitoring, Governance, and Technology

We are committed to providing a safe and secure work environment for our employees, clients, and the community while protecting critical infrastructure and public assets. We will develop and implement a comprehensive, site-specific Security Plan within 30 days of contract start, with updates provided annually. As the Operator, JC Solutions will assume full responsibility for the security of all wastewater treatment facilities and pump stations in the [CLIENT] service area. This includes enforcing security protocols without exception, maintaining secure perimeter fencing and gated entry points, and ensuring all doors, access panels, and entryways remain locked unless actively monitored. All personnel and visitors will be required to present valid identification and documentation authorizing access, with entry logs maintained at each facility.

Our Security Plan will provide access control and surveillance; security-awareness and training; incident reporting and response; annual security assessments; zero-tolerance policy enforcement; information protection; and construction coordination. Our Site Security Assessment methodology provides a comprehensive review of existing physical-security measures across all vertical assets, identifies gaps or potential risks, and defines targeted mitigation strategies. A well-rounded utility-security program is built on three important components: security monitoring, governance, and technology. These work in tandem to support daily operations, detect threats in real time, and ensure effective oversight and accountability.

The preliminary review identified zip code 30349 as having a “D+” safety rating in the 23rd percentile nationally; JC Solutions will conduct localized assessments to evaluate crime patterns and risks surrounding each facility. The source identifies **$34,000 worth of investment by JC Solutions** as a value-added extra for the Site Security Assessment/evaluation and recommendations.

Security monitoring functions as the “eyes and ears” of the operation, enabling rapid awareness of intrusions or unusual activity. JC Solutions will evaluate monitoring practices across perimeter security, facility access, fire-alarm integration, physical and electronic access-control systems, and sensitive in-building areas. Security governance provides the organizational framework for implementing and maintaining a successful security program, including leadership buy-in, policies and procedures, training, employee awareness, and administrative oversight. Security technology includes surveillance cameras, card readers, intrusion alarms, lighting, and video analytics to increase visibility, improve access control, and ensure secure perimeters.

Jacobs will perform in-person assessments of each vertical asset as part of the SSA. Each site will be documented, photographed, and evaluated using a structured, security-specific data-collection form. Assessment categories include perimeter deterrence, perimeter delay, perimeter detection, surveillance and CCTV response, secondary-layer delay, and secondary-layer detection. This assessment follows Crime Prevention Through Environmental Design (CPTED) principles: natural surveillance, natural access control, territorial reinforcement, and maintenance. Findings from the SSA will directly inform site-specific updates to [CLIENT]'s Security Plan and guide improvements in facility design, operations, and monitoring systems.
