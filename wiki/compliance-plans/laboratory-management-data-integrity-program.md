# Laboratory Management and Data Integrity Program

Reliable compliance begins with defensible laboratory data. A structured laboratory management program aligned with 40 CFR Part 136, the facility's NPDES permit, and applicable state requirements includes:

- Required influent and effluent sampling at specified frequencies.
- Use of EPA-approved analytical methods.
- Coordination of quarterly (or permit-required frequency) PFAS composite sampling with certified laboratories.
- Documented QA/QC procedures, including calibration, duplicate sampling, and control charting.
- Integration of laboratory data into process control and compliance dashboards.

All analytical data undergoes internal QA/QC review prior to submission. Laboratory equipment is maintained under preventive maintenance schedules, and staff maintain appropriate training and certifications.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the five-bullet QA/QC checklist and the "defensible laboratory data" framing, plus the practice of routing all lab data through internal QA/QC review before submission. Tailor per pursuit: swap in the correct state-specific permit citation and regulatory body; adjust sampling frequencies (influent/effluent, PFAS) to the target facility's actual permit conditions; confirm laboratory certifications required in the target state. Pair with the Process Control and Compliance Tool Set block (sampling plan/STT) and the Six-Point Compliance Program block (pillar 1).
