# Laboratory Management and Data Integrity Program

Reliable compliance begins with **defensible laboratory data**. A structured laboratory management program aligned with 40 CFR Part 136, [CLIENT]'s NPDES permit, and applicable state requirements includes — [CLIENT] here being a coastal New England municipality operating a WWTF and collection system:

- Required influent and effluent sampling at specified frequencies
- Use of EPA-approved analytical methods
- Coordination of quarterly PFAS composite sampling with certified laboratories
- Documented QA/QC procedures including calibration, duplicate sampling, and control charting
- Integration of laboratory data into process control and compliance dashboards

All analytical data will undergo internal QA/QC review prior to submission. Laboratory equipment will be maintained under preventive maintenance schedules, and staff will maintain appropriate training and certifications.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the "reliable compliance begins with defensible laboratory data" opening, the five-bullet QA/QC checklist, and the practice of routing all analytical data through internal QA/QC review before it leaves the facility. Tailor per pursuit: swap in the correct state-specific permit citation and regulatory body; adjust influent/effluent and PFAS sampling frequencies to the target permit's actual conditions; confirm the laboratory certifications the target state requires.
