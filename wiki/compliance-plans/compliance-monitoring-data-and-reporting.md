# Compliance Monitoring, Data, Reporting, and Excursion Notification

## Monitoring, data, and reporting

Every report Jacobs submits will be complete, accurate, and defensible. We will use centralized digital tools to consolidate data from SCADA, laboratory systems, and maintenance platforms. We'll make sure data is validated daily, trended to identify emerging risks, and reviewed by operations and compliance staff before any report is finalized. Dashboards will provide visibility into permit performance, parameters approaching regulatory limits, and the connection between process conditions and compliance outcomes. Jacobs' tracking toolkit includes our Sample Tracking Tool for compliance and process sampling accountability, CMMS-integrated work order and reporting workflows, and real-time performance dashboards that can give [CLIENT] direct visibility into permit performance, corrective actions, and operational trends at any time (**Exhibit 1-17**).

Key permit parameters including 5-day biochemical oxygen demand (BOD5), TSS, ammonia, and effluent toxicity will be tracked with early warning thresholds that trigger operator response before permit limits are approached. This is how compliance becomes predictable rather than reactive.

We'll complete, sign, and deliver NPDES monthly Discharge Monitoring Reports (DMRs) to [CLIENT]'s General Manager or designee at least 3 business days before the ODEQ submission deadline, consistent with MGT-1. When third-party laboratory results are pending, we'll submit the report noting what data is outstanding and when it will be available, exactly as Schedule 1 provides.

When permit excursions occur, Jacobs will notify [CLIENT] immediately (before regulatory agencies are contacted), document conditions and root cause, and submit all required regulatory notifications within required timeframes. We'll follow up promptly with a corrective action plan, including defined steps, responsibilities, and timelines shared transparently with [CLIENT]. You will never learn about a compliance issue from ODEQ before hearing it from us.

COM-1 requires 100% NPDES compliance at each facility, with up to 3.0% of the fixed monthly fee at risk per facility. MGT-1 requires DMRs delivered to the General Manager at least three business days before ODEQ submission. Jacobs treats both as baseline expectations, not performance targets.

**Monthly management, operations, and maintenance reports will include all elements required by Schedule 1, giving [CLIENT] real, actionable visibility into system performance, emerging risks, and the corrective actions underway every month.**
