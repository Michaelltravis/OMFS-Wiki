# Reuse Water Program — Understanding, Reuse Expertise, and Service Standard

## Reuse expertise behind your on-site team

Our integrated reuse water program will align operating setpoints to permit outcomes, provide defensible measurement and verification of performance, and leverage Jacobs' direct operational experience with the disinfection systems, including PSI hypochlorite generators, that will define [CLIENT]'s reuse program going forward. [CLIENT] is a Southcentral US municipal water utility trust operating four wastewater treatment plants and a major pump station under an ODEQ regulatory regime.

Your on-site reuse program draws on two complementary sources of Jacobs expertise. O&M Process SME EJ Hindy will provide day-to-day technical backup on chlorine dosing, residual monitoring, and disinfection optimization. Larry Schimmoller, PE, Jacobs' Global Principal for water reuse, will support the team across the full range of reuse needs.

**Larry Schimmoller, PE — Reuse Lead Engineering SME. We understand the challenges and goals of [CLIENT]'s reuse program.** Larry brings experience across dozens of reuse projects — including major advanced treatment programs in Los Angeles, Virginia, Arizona, and Colorado — and has served on national and regional committees shaping reuse research and policy, including chairing the development of direct potable reuse regulatory guidelines. For [CLIENT], he'll be the on-site team's escalation point on regulatory interpretation, water quality strategy, and customer agreements — connecting the program to Jacobs' broader reuse research, tools, and network.

## Three customers, three facilities, one standard

[CLIENT]'s reuse program serves three customers across three facilities: the OG&E Red Bud Facility at North Canadian WWTP, the OG&E McClain Facility at South Canadian WWTP, and the Gaillardia Golf and Country Club at Deer Creek WWTP (Exhibit 1-16).

**Each carry distinct operational demands.** At Deer Creek, the Gaillardia agreement requires on-site water sampling at designated sampling points to comply with reuse permit requirements — a more hands-on compliance obligation than the industrial reuse deliveries at the OG&E facilities. All three locations require chlorine treatment of reuse water in accordance with permit regulations.

## Our approach: reliable reuse, every delivery

Jacobs will operate [CLIENT]'s reuse program as a **regulated, continuously monitored service** that reliably meets reuse agreement water quality and quantity commitments while protecting public health and system integrity. Our approach combines **robust treatment and disinfection, distribution system control, targeted monitoring, and operational readiness** so reuse deliveries, including site-specific obligations at Gaillardia, are met.

**The common thread:** each reuse customer depends on reliable disinfection — and today, every facility faces a different gap in delivering it.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the two-tier SME model (process SME for daily dosing questions, global reuse principal for regulatory and agreement strategy), the "one standard, different gaps" common-thread device, and the four-part approach statement (treatment and disinfection, distribution control, monitoring, operational readiness). Pursuit-specific: the customer/facility roster, the sampling obligation embedded in any individual reuse agreement, and the disinfection platform named.
