# Risk Management and Environmental Emergency Programs

The North Canadian facility operates under both a **Risk Management Plan (RMP)** and **Process Safety Management (PSM)** requirements, given the presence of chemicals that trigger these thresholds. We'll maintain current RMP/ PSM documentation, conduct required program updates, and coordinate PSM compliance as an integrated component of the facility safety program.

At each facility, we’ll maintain a current **Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure (SPCC) Plan**, updated as required and integrated with the overall Emergency Operating Plan and the facility's day-to-day operational protocols.

**We’ll complete and submit annually by March 1 Tier II Emergency and Hazardous Chemical Inventory** forms for North Canadian, South Canadian, Deer Creek, and Witcher Pump Station—one of those firm, non-negotiable deadlines our team tracks and executes without prompting.

We’ll align emergency response planning with [CLIENT]'s Emergency Response Plan and [CLIENT]'s Emergency Operations Plan, with coordination across the Flood Emergency Response Plan, RMP, and SPCC Plan.

## Oklahoma Corporation Commission (OCC)—Petroleum Storage Tanks

[CLIENT]'s facilities include underground and aboveground petroleum storage tanks at North Canadian, South Canadian, and Chisholm Creek, all subject to OCC's Petroleum Storage Tank Program. These tanks used for regular unleaded and diesel fuel require active permit certification, routine inspection compliance, and meticulous recordkeeping under OCC regulations.

**We'll maintain current Petroleum Storage Tank Program Permit Certifications for all three facilities and integrate underground storage tank inspection and compliance activities** into our facility management protocols. We’ll incorporate spill prevention measures for these tanks into each facility's **SPCC Plan**, creating a unified approach to petroleum release prevention across the portfolio.

We understand how these regulations translate into sampling frequencies, process control targets, record-keeping standards, and reporting timelines, and we incorporate those requirements directly into plant operations, not as an afterthought.
