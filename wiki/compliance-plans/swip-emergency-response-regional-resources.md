# Regional and National Emergency Response Surge Capability

## Local and Regional Resources for Diversified Emergency Response

In a major emergency, it's wise not to have "all your eggs in one basket." A large event such as a wildfire or earthquake can result in fierce competition for operators, maintenance personnel, contractors, and materials. We bring [CLIENT] diversified support, with regional and national resources that we routinely deploy to support projects across the country.

When emergencies exceed local capacity, we deploy specialized crews with experience in critical pump replacements, pipe repair, generator failure, and SCADA restoration. These teams have responded to major emergency events across the country and are trained to act under pressure while adhering to safety and regulatory protocols.

Having a diversified response capability prevents over-reliance on a single resource pool—a vital advantage during widespread regional incidents where labor and materials are in high demand.

**Jacobs' Invaluable Regional Resources for Emergency Response:** At the request of the City of Waterbury, CT, during a water supply emergency in 2019, Jacobs staff (including Regional Director Kevin Dahl) mobilized and engaged with the city at no cost to help troubleshoot water supply restrictions and offer solutions.

## Proven Emergency Preparedness and Response for the Most Challenging Situations

Jacobs is the preeminent emergency response firm and a **certified FEMA Disaster Response and Recovery contractor**. Our regional team has successfully prepared for and managed a wide range of emergencies with regional O&M and consulting resources to any challenge presented. We are extreme weather experts and have received awards and client recognition for keeping systems in operation during disasters. As a FEMA contractor, we also bring the client deep access to specialized skills, contractors, and materials to handle even the most challenging emergencies.

**Benefits to [CLIENT]:** By aligning facility-specific Emergency Response Plan (ERP) procedures with the client's broader emergency management framework, Jacobs will enhance the client's ability to respond quickly and effectively to unplanned events. Close coordination with fire, police, and utility providers will reduce response times, improve communication, and ensure public safety in a highly populated urban setting. Integrating ERP procedures with the client's Operations Optimization Plan, Asset Register, and updated emergency-resource documentation will provide a comprehensive view of critical assets, preventive measures, and available tools. This integrated approach will reduce downtime, safeguard public health, and strengthen the client's overall resiliency in the face of emergencies.

**Jacobs' Emergency Response Supports Our Clients and the Community:** Our O&M team in Arizona at the Prescott Valley water and wastewater facilities stepped up in the early hours to man the stations when a multi-alarm fire broke out at an apartment complex under construction in the heart of Prescott Valley. They diverted flows so first responders could tackle the huge blaze, while also maintaining adequate pressure for residences and businesses. Town Manager Gilbert Davidson celebrated the team's abilities and commitment to excellence: "I want to recognize the water production and water distribution teams for their ongoing commitment to excellence," said Town Manager Gilbert Davidson in a letter sent to the project team in response to their efforts to assist with water flows during the fire. "Their leadership and attention to detail have been instrumental in achieving our goals and exceeding our customers' expectations. Your contributions have not gone unnoticed, and I thank you once again for your efforts."
