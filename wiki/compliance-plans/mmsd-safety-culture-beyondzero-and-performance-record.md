# Safety First: A Culture of Accountability and Prevention

Jacobs will bring [CLIENT], a Midwest regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities, a safety program built on personal accountability, strong leadership, and a commitment to preventing injuries and incidents. Our BeyondZero® philosophy guides day-to-day decisions, reinforces individual responsibility, and promotes a work environment where every employee is empowered to identify hazards and act before they cause harm. Based on our site familiarity, [CLIENT]'s facilities are generally well maintained and clean, providing a strong foundation on which to further reinforce consistent safety practices. At [CLIENT]'s facilities, this approach will translate into safe and reliable operations for staff supporting wastewater facilities, maintenance activities, laboratory functions, solids handling, and capital project interfaces. Our objective is clear: **zero injuries, zero incidents, and zero harm to people, property, or the environment**.

**EXHIBIT IV-50. JACOBS OUTPERFORMS AVERAGES IN TOP SAFETY CATEGORIES** (asset ID pending; stat-callout exhibit)

- Recordable Incident Rate (RIR) 5-Year Average*: Jacobs 0.19 — **60% BETTER** than the Industry Average of 0.62
- DART or Lost Time Injury Rate (LTIR) 5-Year Average*: Jacobs 0.056 — **72% BETTER** than the Industry Average of 0.20

*2018–2022 data from BLS

Jacobs' industry-leading safety performance (**Exhibit IV-50**) is supported by proven systems, comprehensive training, and transparent reporting. Safety360™ is Jacobs' integrated Safety Management System (SMS) that centralizes safety data, reporting, and performance tracking to support proactive risk prevention and continuous improvement. Through Safety360 and Jacobs' Learning Management System (LMS), [CLIENT] will have full visibility into training records, incident investigations, trends, and corrective actions.

Jacobs applies structured hazard identification and risk analysis methods to wastewater treatment, solids handling, chemical management, conveyance, and energy systems, and integrates the results into operating procedures, training, and safety programs to reduce risk and support safe, reliable operations.
