# Oklahoma Law and Regulatory Framework

Operating wastewater facilities in Oklahoma requires more than knowing permit limits. It requires understanding how federal, state, and local regulations translate into daily operational decisions and applying that knowledge consistently across every facility. We’ll bring that hands-on understanding and apply it from day one at [CLIENT].

[CLIENT]'s facilities operate within a layered regulatory framework that our team knows well. At the federal level, this includes the Clean Water Act, Clean Air Act, National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System permitting requirements, and biosolids management standards under 40 CFR Part 503, along with applicable stormwater and waste management standards.

In Oklahoma, these requirements are implemented through ODEQ under the OPDES program. The specific administrative rules governing [CLIENT]'s operations include:

- **OAC 252:606**: OPDES permitting, reporting, and compliance requirements
- **OAC 252:616**: Biosolids management standards applicable to Class B treatment, land application, and documentation
- **OAC 252:656**: Laboratory accreditation and certification requirements
- **OAC 252:515**: Operator certification requirements governing staffing qualifications
- **OAC 252:100**: Air pollution control regulations covering emergency generators
- **OAC 252:656 & 627**: Water reuse
