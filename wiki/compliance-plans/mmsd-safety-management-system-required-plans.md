# SMS and Required Plans

Jacobs' SMS complies with OSHA 29 CFR 1910 and will be aligned with [CLIENT] procedures. Thirty days prior to the Start Date, we'll submit a complete Safety Plan that includes:

- Confined space entry requirements, including permitting, continuous monitoring, communication, and rescue readiness
- Gas detection practices and calibration, as well as documentation of fixed and portable monitoring equipment
- Lockout and tagout procedures and verification of isolation
- Arc-flash protection, safe electrical work practices, and labeling compliance
- Chemical hygiene requirements and laboratory safety protocols consistent with [CLIENT] standards
- Housekeeping and combustible dust control in the drying areas that produce [CLIENT]'s biosolids product
- Daily pre-task planning, Activity Hazard Analyses, and safe work permitting
- Expectations for subcontractor safety, coordination, and oversight

Jacobs will work in close coordination with [CLIENT]'s Risk Manager and Safety Advisor to ensure consistent implementation, documentation, inspection practices, and reporting.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the OSHA 29 CFR 1910 alignment statement, the commitment to submit a complete Safety Plan on a stated number of days before the Start Date, and the eight-element contents list — this list maps cleanly onto most O&M RFP safety requirements and is the fastest way to demonstrate compliance without padding.

Pursuit-specific: the submittal lead time; the combustible dust line (keep it only where a heat-drying or dry-product operation exists, and name the product generically); the laboratory line (delete where the lab is not in scope); and the closing sentence, which should name the client's actual safety and risk roles rather than generic titles.
