# Regulatory Governance, Document Control, and Audits

We’ll implement an electronic document-management system to provide [CLIENT], a county wastewater utility, and our project team with full, complete, and real-time web-based access to the Services Management System, individual management plans, and supporting documentation.

One of our key initiatives is the establishment of a Regulatory Compliance Program, led and administered by our Compliance & Reporting team. The team comprises more than 20 subject-matter experts on federal and state regulations pertinent to facilities operated by our O&M team in Georgia. Their regulatory expertise includes domestic and industrial wastewater, drinking water, laboratory operations, biosolids, industrial pretreatment programs, risk-management plans, regulatory training, and the NPDES program. In 2024, the Compliance & Reporting team successfully completed 80 audits, and it will support the onsite Operations and Compliance Manager, Devin Trezevant, in achieving the goal of perfect reporting of non-compliance on the project.

We recommend a SharePoint-based electronic document-management system to manage all contract- and project-related documentation. This tool facilitates sharing documents in progress and provides a well-organized repository for all project records. Using SharePoint, documents contained in the Services Management System can be easily updated, tracked, printed, and downloaded in native file formats, including Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote, and PDF, and submitted to [CLIENT] no later than September 30 yearly.

The project will be audited by an experienced, independent auditor approved by [CLIENT] for compliance with the requirements of the Services Management System. The auditor will prepare and implement an audit schedule for each year of the contract, with at least one audit performed yearly. Typically, new projects are audited six months after startup, and the frequency of follow-up audits is determined by the project’s score across O&M and management areas. The auditor will submit the audit schedule to [CLIENT] for approval no later than 60 business days before the commencement of each fiscal year. The auditor’s report will be submitted to [CLIENT] within five business days after JC Solutions receives the report.

We evaluate project risk from a number of perspectives, including regulatory compliance, financial, asset management, safety, client relations, project manager strength, and general contract compliance, as measured by the annual review process. Each of those areas is overseen by subject-matter experts, tracking information and metrics that are collected, summarized, and aggregated to generate a composite risk score. Reports are then shared with regional leadership to validate the data and develop resolutions to address issues and bring projects into compliance.

## Reuse guidance

Use with a confirmed Compliance & Reporting team, electronic document system, independent-audit process, and risk-review governance. Revalidate all current dates, audit cadence, named personnel, standards, client approval requirements, and document platforms.
