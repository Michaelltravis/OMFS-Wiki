# Ongoing Safety Training/Certification and Underground/Confined Space Safety Program

**Comprehensive, Ongoing Safety Training and Certification.** Jacobs performs an assessment of safety training needs for each worker and project site starting at transition and then annually. Real-time data tracks and verifies that each employee has received the required training using our corporate employee learning and tracking program. We offer 100+ online safety courses and classroom or practical hands-on safety training programs, some of which are designed to meet specific OSHA regulations (example training topics: hazard communication, permit required confined space entry, bloodborne pathogens, lead, benzene, lockout/tagout, working from heights, PPE, and electrical safety). Prior to performing high-risk tasks, our safety team conducts classroom/practical training (NFPA 70E, permit required confined space, fall protection, scaffold, etc.).

**Underground and Confined Space Safety.** Because [FACILITY]'s facilities include lift stations, stormwater tanks, and diversion structures, Jacobs applies strict confined space entry and underground safety protocols. These include OSHA permit-required confined space training, atmospheric monitoring, rescue planning, and utility locate practices, ensuring safe work in or around subsurface environments.
