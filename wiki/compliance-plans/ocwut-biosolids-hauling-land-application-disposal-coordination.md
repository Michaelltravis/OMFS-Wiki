# Biosolids Hauling, Land Application, and Disposal Coordination

**[CLIENT]'s biosolids program requires continuous coordination to remain compliant and efficient.** We'll coordinate hauling to maintain storage within target ranges and adjust schedules based on production and site conditions. We'll maintain all required documentation for land application, including volumes, site locations, and compliance parameters required for Class B biosolids. We'll manage site selection and application sequencing in coordination with [CLIENT], while supporting the identification and development of additional permitted sites when needed.

We fully support [CLIENT]'s plan to integrate landfill disposal of biosolids by the 2029 target date. We'll monitor legislative activity that may influence this timeline and adjust planning accordingly. We have already begun building relationships with local landfill operators and will leverage our national project experience to establish a reliable and effective program.

**Schedule 14 requires biosolids to be managed to minimize odor at facilities, along transport routes, and at land application sites.** We'll implement controlled loading practices, covered transport, and strict manifest tracking. Spill prevention and immediate cleanup procedures will be enforced to meet regulatory requirements. **All external communications regarding biosolids will be coordinated through [CLIENT]'s Public Information staff as required by Schedule 14.** We'll maintain ready-to-deploy contingency disposal options to support operations when land application is not feasible.

## Reuse guidance

Use where land application relies on daily routing, documented loads, and public-facing odor discipline. Tailor disposal route, timing, site-development responsibility, communications authority, and applicable standards. Pair with the storage protocol; do not use it as the operational-integration narrative.
