# Safety and Site Security SME Bios Supporting the On-Site O&M Team

## Tracy James, ASP, CSP | O&M Safety SME

Jacobs' safety program at [CLIENT], a Southcentral US municipal water utility trust, will be reinforced by Tracy, a seasoned EHS leader with 20 years of experience developing and implementing health, safety, and quality programs across complex industrial and facility operations. Tracy brings hands-on expertise in OSHA compliance, risk assessment, incident investigation, training, and continuous improvement. For [CLIENT], he'll support the site team with proactive safety leadership, field-based coaching, documentation discipline, and practical measures that strengthen compliance, reduce risk, and sustain a safety-first culture in day-to-day operations.

## Keith Waddell, CPP, CFE | Site Security SME

Keith brings more than three decades of security leadership across critical infrastructure, high-security environments, and complex operating organizations. As Jacobs' Director, Security–Americas, he provides strategic guidance in physical security, site risk reduction, incident response, crisis management, and protection of people, property, and information. For [CLIENT], he'll serve as a trusted resource on site security planning, vulnerability assessment, and security management strategies — helping the team strengthen resilience through practical, integrated solutions that support safe and reliable operations.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the two-bio sidebar structure — credential line, breadth of experience, then a "for [CLIENT], he'll..." sentence that converts corporate depth into named on-site support. This is the pattern to use wherever an RFP asks how corporate resources back the site team. Pursuit-specific: every name, credential, title, and year count. Place these bios adjacent to the safety and security narrative rather than in the resume section, so evaluators read the capability at the point of the claim.
