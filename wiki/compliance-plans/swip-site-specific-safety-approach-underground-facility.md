# Site-Specific Safety Approach for a Below-Grade Treatment Facility and Employee Well-Being Program

## Site safety observations and unique safety needs

Jacobs understands that the [FACILITY] facilities present unique safety challenges due to their location and operational complexity. Of particular note, the advanced treatment facility extends three stories below ground, requiring a site-specific egress and regress plan to ensure safe access and evacuation under all conditions. To further safeguard personnel, Jacobs will maintain two-person minimum staffing at the below-grade facility, recognizing that confined, below-grade facilities require immediate support in the event of an incident. The site-specific safety program prepared following transition will address the unique environments of the advanced treatment facility, the water recycling facility, lift stations, and stormwater components.

Chemical management will also be a key focus of the safety program at [FACILITY]. The advanced treatment facility stores and uses several treatment chemicals, including sodium hypochlorite and calcium chloride, which require careful handling and preparedness. Jacobs will ensure that all chemical storage and feed systems are inspected and maintained as part of the preventive maintenance (PM) program, and emergency eyewash and shower stations remain fully operational and tested on schedule. Jacobs will also implement comprehensive chemical response plans that address spill containment, neutralization, and notification procedures, tailored to the specific chemicals in use at the facilities.

**Benefits to [CLIENT]:** This safety approach will provide the client with a program designed to meet the unique challenges of an underground, chemical-intensive water treatment facility. By requiring two-person staffing, maintaining fully functional emergency response systems, and applying tailored chemical hazard protocols, Jacobs will reduce risks to staff, prevent disruptions to operations, and ensure full compliance with state and federal safety regulations. These measures will also strengthen public confidence that the client is operating the facilities with the highest standards of safety and environmental stewardship.

## Continuous Improvement and Well-Being

Jacobs' safety program is built on continuous improvement, using real-time data and lessons learned to strengthen practices and prevent complacency. Leading indicators ensure we identify risks early, while lagging indicators provide accountability.

Recognizing that employee well-being is central to safe performance, Jacobs also integrates mental health into safety planning. Our **Mental Health Matters** program provides access to more than 1,200 trained champions worldwide who support staff in crisis, connect them to resources, and foster open dialogue about mental health. Supporting employee well-being helps sustain morale, productivity, and vigilance in safety practices.
