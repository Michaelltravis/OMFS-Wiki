# Capital Planning, Construction Support, and Targeted Maintenance Innovation Studies

## Technical Expertise for Capital Planning Efforts

Using our technical expertise, our contributions to capital planning include using the initial asset assessment and ongoing maintenance data to turn asset condition, criticality, lifecycle cost, and operational risk into a **practical, risk-ranked capital plan** for [CLIENT], a coastal New England municipality. Supported by integrated O&M, engineering, process, asset management, and digital resources, we will help prioritize renewal, resiliency, and strategic investments with defensible recommendations on scope, timing, sequencing, and funding needs. This ensures **day-to-day maintenance directly informs long-term capital decisions**.

## Support for Planning and Executing Construction Projects and Facility Upgrades

Jacobs offers expertise in actively supporting, planning, and executing construction projects and facility upgrades as desired by [CLIENT], including providing structured O&M input from project development through design, construction, startup, commissioning, and warranty. We regularly help clients with these types of improvements, helping ensure they are **practical to operate with minimal disruption to ongoing service**. Supported by Jacobs' broader engineering, construction management, and design-build bench, we can coordinate constructability reviews, outage and bypass planning, field support, testing, turnover, and asset record updates so new and modified assets are **fully integrated into [CLIENT]'s asset management program and ready for reliable operation on Day 1**.

## Innovative Opportunities for Enhanced Maintenance

We have identified several opportunities to strengthen maintenance planning, improve asset reliability, and support long-term stewardship of [CLIENT]'s wastewater facilities. If desired, one or more of the following innovative opportunities can be explored in our **Annual Innovation Workshop** or through our Discounted Engineering Rates:

- **Incinerator Options Study.** We will evaluate practical options for the existing incinerator area, including repurposing, removal, and alternative operational uses, providing a clearer basis for future space, solids, and asset planning decisions.
- **Standby Power and Resiliency Roadmap.** We will evaluate existing standby power arrangements, generator readiness, and storm-related vulnerabilities and will compare practical resiliency improvements that reduce risk and improve continuity of service.
- **Targeted Advanced Predictive Maintenance.** We will identify selected critical rotating and electrical assets that warrant advanced predictive monitoring beyond the base PM program and will use those tools where they provide the greatest benefit.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the logic chain — initial asset assessment plus ongoing maintenance data becomes condition/criticality/lifecycle/risk input, which becomes a risk-ranked capital plan with defensible scope, timing, sequencing, and funding recommendations, which the same team then helps deliver from design through warranty. The construction-support paragraph is the answer to any RFP question about whether the O&M contractor can support capital projects without a separate procurement. Pursuit-specific: all three named studies. They work because each one names a real asset or exposure at the target facility (a stranded incinerator, storm-exposed standby power, specific critical rotating equipment) — identify equivalents from site visits and the RFP's own stated problems rather than reusing these. Also confirm the offered delivery vehicle (annual innovation workshop and discounted engineering rates in the source pursuit).
