# Environmental Management, Transparent Reporting, and Sampling Strategy

Our commitment to environmental management is fully defined in our PlanBeyond Environmental Management System, which addresses the management and reduction of environmental impacts from our company’s operations. However, most significant environmental impacts and opportunities arise from the projects we deliver for our clients, which will be addressed in our Environmental Management Plan, which conforms to ISO 14001.

Transparency in reporting is central to our commitment to open communication. We’ll maintain clear and thorough records of all operations, verifying compliance with regulatory standards and full visibility for [CLIENT], a county wastewater utility. We summarize the full breadth of reports and documentation we will provide [CLIENT] later in this section under Communications and Reporting. As part of our approach to regulatory compliance reporting, we will specifically provide timely submission of the Georgia EPD Discharge Monitoring Report through NetDMR each month, with immediate communication of any permit violations or regulatory concerns, demonstrating our commitment to transparency and regulatory compliance.

Our Sampling and Laboratory Testing Plan is designed to ensure that operations in the [CLIENT] service area meet or exceed all applicable regulatory requirements while delivering highly defensible, timely, and transparent data. The program is rooted in practices successfully implemented on similar projects, including Spokane County, Washington; Clovis, California; and Traverse City, Michigan wastewater treatment plants, and integrates EPA-compliant sampling protocols, rigorous QA/QC procedures, and ongoing training programs.
