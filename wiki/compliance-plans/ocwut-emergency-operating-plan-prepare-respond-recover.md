# Emergency Operating Plan — Prepare, Respond, Recover, and Coordinate

## Protecting service, public health, and community confidence

Wastewater service can't pause during an emergency. When something goes wrong, [CLIENT] — a Southcentral US municipal water utility trust — needs a partner that will act quickly, communicate clearly, and keep operations under control. We will develop and implement an emergency operating plan that protects public health, supports regulatory compliance, and helps sustain community confidence when conditions are at their most challenging. We will coordinate this plan with [CLIENT]'s Emergency Response Plan, Flood Emergency Response Plan, and Risk Management Plan; the municipality's Emergency Operations Plan; and each facility's Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure (SPCC) Plan so response actions are aligned before an event occurs. This approach will help [CLIENT] reduce disruption, protect critical treatment functions, and recover more quickly when emergencies happen.

## Prepared, trained, and ready to act

Strong emergency response starts long before an event occurs. Our facility assessments identified specific vulnerabilities that will inform emergency planning priorities at each site, including flood risk at North Canadian's influent pump station, H2S conditions that restrict access to critical work areas, and redundancy gaps in key process equipment that require defined backup protocols. We will build on [CLIENT]'s existing procedures and strengthen them with a practical, facility-specific emergency operating plan tailored to your risks, assets, and operating conditions. Our team will review current emergency procedures and unit process control procedures, confirm emergency equipment availability, and identify critical assets and likely failure points so response efforts stay focused where they matter most.

We will prepare staff through routine drills, tabletop exercises, and scenario-based training covering severe weather, process upsets, power loss, staffing shortages, and other high-consequence events. Ahead of forecasted events, we will stage personnel, equipment, and materials to shorten response time and maintain continuity of operations. We will also maintain agreements for emergency equipment and specialty services so additional resources are available when needed without delay. This preparation will help [CLIENT] protect permit compliance, maintain treatment continuity, and respond with confidence under pressure.

## Rapid, structured response when it matters most

When an emergency occurs, we will activate a clear, practiced response structure focused on safety, operational stability, and communication. Our team will implement the emergency operating plan using defined roles, responsibilities, escalation procedures, and communication protocols from the outset. Project Manager David Pitocchelli and Emergency Response SME Megan Ellis will stay actively engaged during major events, providing leadership across operations, coordination, and executive communication so response actions remain aligned, timely, and focused on [CLIENT]'s priorities.

We'll prioritize the safety of staff and the public while maintaining operational control of the system. Operators will monitor performance continuously and adjust processes in real time to stabilize conditions and protect critical treatment functions. We will maintain direct communication with [CLIENT] leadership and coordinate with emergency responders and regulatory agencies as appropriate. Through Jacobs' 24-hour incident reporting process, we'll mobilize additional personnel and resources without delay. We'll respond rapidly to collection system emergencies such as blockages and backups to contain the issue, protect service continuity, and restore normal operations as quickly as conditions allow.

**Jacobs emergency reporting hotline** (graphic asset `102_009385`): Jacobs maintains a 24/7 emergency reporting hotline, giving [CLIENT], staff and residents immediate access to resources trained to respond quickly and effectively to emergencies and significant customer services issues.

## Fast recovery and return to full operations

Response is only one part of effective emergency management. Restoring full operations quickly and learning from each event matter just as much. Following an emergency, we will assess system conditions, prioritize repairs based on operational impact, and restore treatment capacity and system performance through a structured recovery process coordinated with [CLIENT] and regulatory agencies as needed. We will document actions taken, evaluate system performance, and conduct after-action reviews to identify improvements. We'll fold those lessons into updated procedures, training, and planning so [CLIENT]'s emergency readiness grows stronger over time. This approach will help your facilities recover faster, reduce repeat issues, and improve resilience across the full contract term.

## Coordination with the client and local responders

Effective emergency response depends on strong coordination before, during, and after an event. We will align closely with [CLIENT] leadership and integrate our procedures with local emergency response agencies to support a unified response. We'll establish clear communication protocols and escalation procedures so information flows quickly and accurately during an event. Our team will coordinate with fire departments, emergency management, and public safety agencies and participate in joint training exercises when appropriate. This coordinated approach will help [CLIENT] avoid confusion during critical situations, strengthen decision making, and keep the right people engaged at the right time.
