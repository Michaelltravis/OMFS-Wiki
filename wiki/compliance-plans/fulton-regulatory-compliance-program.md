# JV Regulatory Compliance Program and Transparent Reporting

Our goal for [CLIENT], a county wastewater utility, is perfect regulatory compliance and reporting and protection of the environment. We’ll strive to achieve these goals by tailoring our Environmental Compliance Program to the project’s unique needs and then fully implementing the Program with transparency. Our Environmental Compliance Program provides a proven blueprint for maintaining compliance with environmental, legal, and business requirements.

JC Solutions will support [CLIENT] in meeting permit requirements, including NPDES, air quality, water quality, and stormwater, by performing sampling, analysis, and reporting in accordance with applicable state and federal regulations. While JC Solutions will manage activities under our control to maintain compliance, we will also coordinate closely with [CLIENT] to address any external factors that may impact permit conditions. The Environmental Compliance Program will generate consistent compliance with all applicable environmental laws, regulations, and standards; alert [CLIENT] staff to situations that require special attention and possibly require assistance from regional, corporate, or other specialists to support onsite staff; and generate legally defensible data and records.

Also integral to environmental compliance, our compliance experts bring decades of experience and a consistent record of success in attaining environmental compliance for clients. They have expertise in all pertinent areas of environmental regulations—including the Clean Water Act, Safe Drinking Water Act, Clean Air Act, Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act, and Resource Conservation and Recovery Act—as well as recycled water, industrial pretreatment, laboratory management, and stormwater regulations. Our compliance experts also provide staff at project sites with extensive training on these topics and effective working relationships with regulators at the federal, state, and local levels, which will translate into effective advocacy for [CLIENT].

Most importantly, we are committed to providing complete transparency in reporting to the regulatory agencies and communities we serve, and we ensure the compliance and facility-performance data we gather and report to regulators, [CLIENT], and stakeholders is accurate.
