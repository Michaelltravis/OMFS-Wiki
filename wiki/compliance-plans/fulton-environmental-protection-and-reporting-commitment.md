# JV Environmental Protection and Transparent Compliance Reporting Commitment

JC Solutions, a Jacobs/CERM JV, guarantees the quality of its reporting and pledges full transparency in fulfilling [CLIENT]’s objective to operate in full compliance with all environmental regulations, statutes, and permits. Our goal for [CLIENT], a county wastewater utility, is perfect environmental compliance and reporting. We will strive to achieve that by tailoring our Environmental Compliance Program to [CLIENT]’s needs, then fully implementing it with integrity and transparency. The program is a proven blueprint for maintaining compliance with environmental, legal, and business requirements. It will generate consistent, predictable compliance with applicable environmental laws, regulations, and standards; alert [CLIENT] staff to situations that require special attention and possibly require regional, corporate, or other specialist assistance to support onsite staff; and generate legally defensible data and records.

A blueprint is one thing; applying it to its full potential is another. Jacobs’ environmental compliance team brings decades of experience and a consistent record of success in attaining environmental compliance for clients. Its professionals have expertise in all pertinent areas of environmental regulations—including the Clean Water Act, Clean Air Act, Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act, Resource Conservation and Recovery Act, biosolids management, industrial pretreatment, laboratory management, and stormwater regulations. Beyond an in-depth understanding of regulations, their strong working relationships with federal, state, and local regulators will translate into effective advocacy for [CLIENT]. JC Solutions is committed to providing complete transparency in our reporting to the regulators and communities we serve, and we guarantee the accuracy of the compliance and facility-performance data we gather and report to regulators, [CLIENT], and stakeholders.
