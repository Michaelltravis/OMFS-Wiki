# Integrated Prevention and Awareness

Security awareness is built into every employee's onboarding and annual training. Jacobs' BeyondZero® culture reinforces the principle that everyone shares responsibility for maintaining a safe and secure workplace. Staff will be trained to recognize suspicious activity, protect [CLIENT] property and data, and follow established notification protocols. In addition, Jacobs has completed more than 100 Risk and Resilience Assessments using the ANSI J-100 methodology. This expertise enables Jacobs to anticipate, evaluate, and respond effectively to potential threats and vulnerabilities.

# Active-Shooter and Workplace-Violence Preparedness

In accordance with Exhibit M and [CLIENT]'s Security Procedures, Jacobs will incorporate workplace-violence and active-shooter preparedness into its Safety and Security Plan. All employees will receive awareness training during onboarding and annual refreshers covering recognition of warning signs, communication protocols, and protective actions. Jacobs' Security and Safety Manager will coordinate onsite response, assist law enforcement, and ensure incident reporting and investigation meet required timelines. Jacobs has a robust active shooter and workplace violence prevention program that is based on recognition of human behaviors that are focused on grievance interruption. The information is based on the FBI publication _"Making Prevention a Reality, Identifying, Assessing and Managing the Threat of Target Attacks."_
