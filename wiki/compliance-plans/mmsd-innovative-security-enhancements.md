# Innovative Security Enhancements

Jacobs understands that [CLIENT] is implementing important upgrades, including new video cameras, the **Genetec Security Center** platform, and a visitor-enrollment and badging system. These investments establish a strong foundation for an effective, modernized security program. Based on our experience with similar utilities, Jacobs can offer several value-added recommendations that could further strengthen [CLIENT]'s protection and response capabilities.

## Shoreline Intrusion Detection

Jacobs will evaluate opportunities to enhance perimeter protection at the waterfront water reclamation facility, including **potential shoreline intrusion-detection solutions** identified through a site-specific vulnerability assessment. Technologies that may be considered include ground-based radar or microwave detection units integrated with existing camera systems. When configured, these systems can automatically cue corresponding camera views in the security office for rapid assessment.

Such technologies are effective in darkness, fog, snow, and other adverse conditions, and may be supplemented with optional acoustic loudspeakers to issue automated voice warnings and deter trespassers. Jacobs has implemented similar shoreline-protection solutions at waterfront utilities to improve early detection and response to vessels approaching restricted areas and would work collaboratively with [CLIENT] to determine the appropriate application based on site conditions, risk prioritization, and operational preferences.

## Mobile Video Capability

Jacobs recommends enabling Genetec Mobile for [CLIENT] security personnel. This feature allows staff to view live camera feeds and capture video directly from iOS or Android devices, with recordings automatically uploaded to the PMR. Mobile access improves situational awareness for roving officers and strengthens real-time incident documentation.

## Automated Security Workflows (Above and Beyond — Value-Added Extras)

Jacobs also recommends configuring Genetec Mission Control to establish predefined security-response workflows. During an incident, the system can guide personnel through required actions such as dispatching guards, notifying law enforcement, alerting nearby staff, locking doors, and activating relevant cameras. Automated workflows support a consistent, timely, and fully auditable response.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the opening move — acknowledge the client's own investment as a strong foundation, then offer enhancements that extend it rather than replace it — and the three enhancement patterns (a perimeter detection technology cued to existing cameras, mobile video for roving staff, and guided incident-response workflows). The Mission Control workflow list (dispatch guards, notify law enforcement, alert nearby staff, lock doors, activate cameras) is reusable verbatim for any platform with a workflow module.

Pursuit-specific: the client's actual platform and modules, the perimeter condition that justifies the detection technology (shoreline here; elsewhere it may be a rail corridor, open agricultural boundary, or public trail), and the control-room destination for mobile uploads. Keep every offer conditioned on a site-specific vulnerability assessment.
