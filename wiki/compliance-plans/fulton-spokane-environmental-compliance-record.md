# Spokane County Environmental Compliance Record — Permit-Exception Disclosure

For Spokane County Regional Water Reclamation Facility, Spokane County Utilities, Washington, the following is the disclosed listing of permit/regulatory violations and penalties, including odor and noise complaints associated with wastewater-system operations:

| Monitoring period | Parameter | Description / root cause |
|---|---|---|
| April 2020 | Cadmium, total (as Cd) | Industrial slug-load pass-through |
| July 2023 | BOD, carbonaceous, weekly/monthly average | Probable lab error during pH adjustment |
| February 2024 | Lead, total (as Pb) | Industrial slug-load pass-through |
| November 2024 | BOD, carbonaceous | Single daily value of 9.4 mg/L resulted in weekly-average exceedances. |
| December 2024 | Nitrogen, ammonia total (as N) | Recovery cleaning on membrane reduced nitrification. |
| February 2025 | BOD, carbonaceous | Single weekly-average value of 3.7 mg/L exceeded the permit limit of 3 mg/L. |

The same source record lists any permit exceedance affecting receiving waters as **Zero**; all incidents where contract requirements were not met, including SSOs, permit violations, regulatory violations, odor, and noise, as **Zero**; plant upsets requiring cause-and-effect description as **Zero**; and incidents where service fees were reduced for failure to meet contract requirements as **Zero**. This is a historical, project-specific disclosure in the JC Solutions (a Jacobs/CERM JV) proposal.
