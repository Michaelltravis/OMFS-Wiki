# Health, Safety, and Environmental Compliance Performance Record

Jacobs maintains a proven record of regulatory compliance with health, safety, and environmental regulations across our water and wastewater operations portfolio. Our BeyondZero safety program promotes a culture of caring, accountability, and proactive hazard identification, based on the principle that all incidents are preventable. Our goal extends beyond driving statistics to zero — we believe our "Culture of Caring℠" makes our people and communities safer and healthier, which we consider fundamental to our success. Through behavior-based safety practices, required supervisory observations, continuous training, and knowledge sharing across projects, we consistently perform better than industry averages and foster safe, compliant operations at every facility.

**Safety performance benchmarks** (refresh annually from current corporate safety data before reuse):
- OSHA Total Recordable Incident Rate (TRIR/TIR) — track a multi-year trend line, positioned against industry averages
- Experience Modification Rate (EMR) — track a multi-year trend line, positioned against the RFP's required threshold (if any) and industry averages

**Point-in-time historical trend data** (from a prior proposal cycle — re-pull current figures before reuse; do not present these years or values as current):
- TRIR/TIR by year: 2025 — 0.89; 2024 — 1.38; 2023 — 1.89 (a 3-year improving trend, each year below industry averages)
- EMR (Experience Modification Rate, per NCCI) by policy period: 7/1/2025–7/1/2026 — 0.45; 7/1/2024–7/1/2025 — 0.42; 7/1/2023–7/1/2024 — 0.45
- Environmental/NPDES compliance: a 20-year compliance record of 99.8% with NPDES permit requirements across the wastewater treatment plants operated nationwide, as reported in a prior proposal cycle

**Environmental compliance**: Jacobs is an industry leader in environmental compliance, with a multi-decade compliance record exceeding the vast majority of NPDES permit requirements across the wastewater treatment plants we operate nationwide. Minor violations occur only rarely in the ordinary course of operating water and wastewater utilities; when they do, all are minor in nature and are resolved quickly and transparently in coordination with regulators, with no impact to service delivery. (Example pattern: isolated administrative violations — such as an expired pesticide/herbicide applicator license — promptly identified, corrected, and closed out with the regulator, with no fine or resolution detail carried into proposal boilerplate.)

**Meeting and guaranteeing permit compliance**: Jacobs assumes full responsibility for meeting all permit requirements, including NPDES, air quality, biosolids, and stormwater permits. We perform all required sampling, analysis, and reporting in accordance with current state and federal regulations. Plant staff conduct routine permit-required analyses (e.g., pH, dissolved oxygen, and temperature monitoring), while specialized permit-required testing is completed by certified commercial laboratories, with samples transported under proper chain-of-custody to ensure compliance with hold-time requirements and data integrity standards. Beyond permit-required monitoring, operators conduct routine in-house process control testing that provides real-time operational data supporting proactive, data-driven decision-making — allowing the team to identify trends, anticipate process variability, and optimize treatment performance before issues arise. Compliance performance is actively tracked by regional process specialists who work with the on-site team to monitor trends, verify reporting accuracy, and address potential issues before they escalate. Through proactive monitoring, internal compliance audits, structured reporting controls, and regulatory engagement, Jacobs ensures continuous compliance and rapid corrective action should performance trends indicate potential risk.
