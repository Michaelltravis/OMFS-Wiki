# Land Application, Compliance Reporting, and External Communications

## Land Application Program Management

Managing [CLIENT]'s land application program — a Southcentral US municipal water utility trust — across approximately 170 sites and 13,500 acres requires daily coordination and disciplined execution. Jacobs will schedule and rotate sites based on suitability, weather conditions, soil conditions, hauling logistics, and storage levels. We'll maintain all required records and coordinate closely with landowners to support reliable operations.

We will work with [CLIENT] to develop and maintain a Sensitive Sites list, providing prior notification before any biosolids application at identified locations and applying additional controls to reduce community and regulatory risk. When conditions are not favorable at any site, we'll adjust or delay operations rather than create risk. When additional capacity is needed, we will support identification and permitting of new sites with [CLIENT] approval.

## Testing, Monitoring, and Compliance Reporting

Jacobs will complete all required sludge and biosolids testing and reporting in accordance with permits and Schedule 14 requirements. We'll collect samples at required frequencies, manage them under chain-of-custody procedures, and analyze them using approved methods. We'll review data for compliance before submission. We will maintain organized, accessible records to support audits, inspections, and regulatory review and will use this data to identify trends and adjust operations before issues develop.

## External Communications Protocol

All external communications related to sludge and biosolids management will be directed through [CLIENT]'s Public Information staff, as required by Schedule 14. Jacobs will train all staff and subcontractors on this requirement and establish clear internal escalation procedures. We will document all inquiries and complaints and track them to resolution.
