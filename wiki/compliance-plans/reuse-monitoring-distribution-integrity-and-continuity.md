# Reuse Monitoring, Distribution Integrity, and Operational Continuity

## Monitoring and compliance — continuous verification, not assumptions

We'll deploy **continuous residual monitoring at key reuse locations** — starting with restoring and calibrating the existing CL17 analyzers at Deer Creek and North Canadian that are currently offline or unreliable — and establish automated alarms for low or zero residual. At Deer Creek, where Gaillardia sampling is a permit requirement, we'll implement a scheduled grab-sample program with documented chain-of-custody at the designated sampling points. Data will flow into our compliance dashboards and laboratory QA/QC framework, so deviations are detected and corrected proactively. **Daily operator checks and routine sample verification across all reuse sites form the backbone of our compliance program.**

## Distribution integrity, cross-connection control, and end-user safety

Reuse piping, backflow prevention, and storage will be inspected and maintained under a preventive program. Pressure management and SCADA-enabled leak/surge detection will reduce contamination risk; annual cross-connection audits and customer outreach will support end-user safety and regulatory compliance.

## Operational continuity and contingencies so reuse stays online

The reuse plan includes redundancy (backup disinfection and storage), emergency-switching procedures, and prioritized restoration sequences, so deliveries continue during power events, equipment outages, or wet weather events. Operator training, SOPs for dosing and sampling, and documented cutover procedures for alternate disinfection modes will be delivered at startup and refreshed routinely.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: all three headings and their commitments — continuous residual monitoring with automated low/zero alarms, a preventive program covering piping, backflow prevention, and storage with annual cross-connection audits, and a contingency set built on redundancy, emergency switching, and prioritized restoration. Pursuit-specific: analyzer models and which sites currently have them offline; whether grab sampling is a permit requirement at any given delivery point.
