# Biosolids Program Proof Points

## Rapid Biosolids Program Stabilization in Jackson, MS

When Jackson's wastewater system was added to Jacobs' scope in 2025, the City faced immediate biosolids management challenges, including contractor procurement, dewatering limitations, and the need to rapidly establish a compliant land application program.

**Jacobs acted immediately to stabilize and implement a fully functional biosolids program under active operations.** We developed and issued a procurement for beneficial use of Class B biosolids, evaluated and onboarded contractors, and assumed full management of hauling and land application services. At the same time, we initiated the design of new dewatering systems capable of handling approximately 25,000 wet tons annually, aligning long-term capacity with system needs.

Despite tight timelines and ongoing operational demands, Jacobs delivered a seamless transition with no disruption to service. The program now supports compliant biosolids handling, improved system reliability, and a clear path toward long-term performance.

**For [CLIENT] — a Southcentral US municipal water utility trust — this means a partner who can step into complex conditions, establish control quickly, and build a biosolids program that performs from day one.**

## Beneficial Biosolids Use Award — Farmington, New Mexico

In October 2022, the City of Farmington Wastewater Treatment Plant in Farmington, New Mexico, was recognized as a Utility of the Future Today in the Beneficial Biosolids Use category at the Water Environment Federation's WEFTEC annual conference. Jacobs worked with the City to manage biosolids, reducing the volume of material going to a landfill, and applied a compositing process to produce solids that could be land-applied to hayfields.

**The result:**

- All solids leaving the Farmington compositing facility were land-applied in the first year
- Dewatering and composting the solids reduced the number of hauled loads
- The soil amendment improved the quality of the final agricultural product
