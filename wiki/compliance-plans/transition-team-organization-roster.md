# Transition Team Organization Roster

Jacobs' Transition Leadership Team, highlighted in **Exhibit 5-19** and led by Mike Boven, will oversee the integration process and serve as the primary interface with [CLIENT], a coastal New England municipality, maintaining a **practical, hands-on presence and clear accountability** throughout the transition. The chart pairs three transition leaders with a discipline bench drawn from regional and national resources, so each functional area a wastewater transition touches — operations, maintenance, safety, regulatory, process, biosolids, odor, energy, digital systems, capital projects, and funding — has a named owner from Day 1.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the shape of the chart — a dedicated Transition Manager who is not the eventual Project Manager, a Regional Operations Manager above both, and a named specialist for every discipline the RFP asks about — which answers the evaluator's real question ("who actually shows up during transition, and is safety/regulatory/odor someone's job or nobody's?"). Pursuit-specific: every name and credential, and which disciplines are shown at all (drop dewatering or odor control where the facility has neither; add stormwater or reuse specialists where it does).
