# Regulatory Compliance Program — Why It Matters, Leadership, and Governance

## Why compliance matters to [CLIENT]

Environmental compliance is the foundation of reliable wastewater operations. It protects public health, maintains regulatory standing, and shapes how the community experiences your facilities every day. [CLIENT] is a Southcentral US municipal water utility trust operating four wastewater treatment plants and a major pump station under an ODEQ regulatory regime.

**Jeff Heroux — Regional Compliance Manager.** Jacobs' compliance program at [CLIENT] will be backed by Jeff, our Regional Compliance Manager, with 25+ years leading environmental compliance at complex wastewater facilities nationwide. Jeff leads a national team of 20+ compliance specialists — a depth of resources no other proposer can match — integrated into day-to-day operations.

**99.98%** — Jacobs' environmental compliance record, one of the best in the industry.

At [CLIENT], compliance is tied to specific, known conditions: process variability driven by inconsistent controls, equipment that has limited operator access to critical areas, a laboratory operating without ODEQ certification, whole effluent toxicity test results that require attention, and a South Canadian facility that is mid-transition to a new process configuration. Our team will use these conditions as starting points from day one as we take on these challenges.

**[CLIENT] doesn't need a compliance program that reacts after limits are exceeded. You need a partner who knows exactly what's at risk, acts before conditions deteriorate, and builds a system that stays compliant because it's operated that way every day.**

Jacobs will deliver a structured, disciplined compliance program that integrates operations, maintenance, laboratory services, and reporting into one coordinated system with risks identified, prioritized, and addressed before they affect permit performance or trigger fee deductions.

## Governance and accountability

Reliable compliance starts with clear ownership, rather than shared responsibility that diffuses accountability until something goes wrong. Jacobs will establish a defined compliance leadership structure with clear roles across all functions. An environmental compliance manager will lead the program and serve as the primary interface with [CLIENT] and ODEQ, supported by facility operations leadership, our laboratory manager, and our regional and national compliance specialists.

**Compliance responsibility will be embedded across operations, maintenance, laboratory services, and management.** This structure creates clear ownership, rapid escalation pathways, and consistent accountability across the entire system.

The QA/QC manager, reporting directly to the project manager as required by Schedule 10, provides an independent audit function that validates what the compliance program is reporting. By conducting internal audits of financial performance, maintenance activities, operations, and environmental compliance separately from day-to-day operations, this role creates a built-in check and balance to confirm compliance data is accurate, self-reporting is complete, and corrective actions are tracked to closure. [CLIENT] will benefit from a compliance program that isn't policed by the same people doing the work.
