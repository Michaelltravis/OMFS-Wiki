# Layered Regulatory Framework and Stormwater Permit Administration

## Oklahoma law: regulatory knowledge and environmental compliance

Operating wastewater facilities in Oklahoma requires more than knowing permit limits. It requires understanding how federal, state, and local regulations translate into daily operational decisions and applying that knowledge consistently across every facility. We'll bring that hands-on understanding and apply it from day one at [CLIENT], a Southcentral US municipal water utility trust operating four wastewater treatment plants and a major pump station.

[CLIENT]'s facilities operate within a layered regulatory framework that our team knows well. At the federal level, this includes the Clean Water Act, Clean Air Act, National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System permitting requirements, and biosolids management standards under 40 CFR Part 503, along with applicable stormwater and waste management standards.

In Oklahoma, these requirements are implemented through ODEQ under the OPDES program. The specific administrative rules governing [CLIENT]'s operations include:

- **OAC 252:606**: OPDES permitting, reporting, and compliance requirements
- **OAC 252:616**: Biosolids management standards applicable to Class B treatment, land application, and documentation
- **OAC 252:656**: Laboratory accreditation and certification requirements
- **OAC 252:515**: Operator certification requirements governing staffing qualifications
- **OAC 252:100**: Air pollution control regulations covering emergency generators
- **OAC 252:656 & 627**: Water reuse

We understand how these regulations translate into sampling frequencies, process control targets, record-keeping standards, and reporting timelines, and we incorporate those requirements directly into plant operations, not as an afterthought.

## Stormwater

We'll prepare, administer, and maintain OPDES Stormwater Discharge Permits for each facility. Our team understands the distinction between [CLIENT]'s and the Proposer's respective responsibilities under Schedule 12 — [CLIENT] executes and submits; we prepare, obtain approvals, and administer — and we'll manage that division of responsibility clearly.
