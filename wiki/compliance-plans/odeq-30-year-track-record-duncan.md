# 30-Year State Regulator Track Record — The Duncan, Oklahoma Proof Story

## Jacobs in Oklahoma: a 30-year track record with the Oklahoma Department of Environmental Quality

Our team has 30 years of experience working with the Oklahoma Department of Environmental Quality (ODEQ), supporting land application programs, and maintaining compliance across Oklahoma as biosolids regulations have changed. The City of Duncan provides the clearest demonstration of our relationship with ODEQ and our Oklahoma regulatory experience. **Since 1995, Jacobs has provided full-service operations and maintenance for the City of Duncan's wastewater infrastructure under ODEQ oversight.** Our scope at Duncan mirrors [CLIENT]'s in similar ways: full-service WWTP operations, compliance sampling, laboratory services, biosolids handling, and a land application reuse program managing effluent across 38 irrigation pivot sites. That sustained record of compliance and operational performance earned the Duncan WWTP the **Oklahoma Water Environment Association's Municipal Plant of the Year award in 2025**.

During the 2018 catastrophic flooding event, **we kept the Duncan WWTP operational by repurposing equipment, securing mobile systems, and coordinating closely with ODEQ**. That's the kind of regulatory relationship that matters most when conditions are most challenging.

Since the start of the contract, we've also supported the City of Duncan with their Industrial Pretreatment Program (IPP). In 2025, when a permitted industry raised questions about revised limits stemming from our updated Technically Based Local Limits and Sewer Use Ordinance work, we engaged ODEQ directly on the City's behalf. **ODEQ confirmed our reasoning was sound, expressed confidence in us as the Control Authority representative, and commended the quality of our permit documentation**.

Beyond compliance, we've also delivered nearly **$5M in cost and energy savings for the City through targeted process improvements and operational optimization**, demonstrating that regulatory compliance and operational performance are not competing priorities.

When we led Duncan's disinfection upgrade, we partnered with ODEQ to convert Outfall 001 to gravity flow, eliminate chronic high-flow constraints, and negotiate less stringent cBOD limits — delivering a more reliable discharge system, reduced operational risk, and a permit that reflects real facility performance.
