# Sludge Program Contingency Planning, Upset Response, and KPI Tracking

## Contingency Planning and Upset Response

Jacobs will maintain contingency plans for the conditions most likely to create operational pressure, including equipment outages, dewatering upsets, weather-related restrictions, hauling disruptions, and site unavailability. Wet-weather events represent one of the most predictable contingency scenarios for [CLIENT]'s program — [CLIENT] being a Southcentral US municipal water utility trust operating four wastewater treatment plants and a Class B biosolids land application program. Extended rain periods limit land application availability while sludge generation continues, creating storage pressure. We will maintain pre-identified contingency disposal options, including landfill arrangements consistent with Exhibit B, so that wet-weather restrictions never result in storage threshold violations or fee exposure. Each contingency plan will define clear triggers, response actions, and communication steps so that response is immediate rather than improvised.

## Performance Tracking and Continuous Improvement

Jacobs will track key performance indicators (KPIs) including pad utilization, tons processed and hauled, odor incidents, hauling performance, compliance metrics, and site utilization. Corrective actions will be implemented when trends indicate risk, and monthly reporting will provide [CLIENT] with clear visibility into program performance.

**This approach maintains compliance with COM-3 and COM-5, protects [CLIENT]'s budget from fee exposure, and builds a sludge management program that becomes more reliable, efficient, and resilient over time.**
