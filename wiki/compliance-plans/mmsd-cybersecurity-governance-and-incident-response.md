# Business Networks and Infrastructure Cybersecurity Preparedness

Jacobs applies a unified cybersecurity approach that integrates automation controls, SCADA, and OT cybersecurity under a single leadership structure. This alignment eliminates silos, strengthens visibility across IT and OT environments, and ensures consistent application of security controls. While protection of [CLIENT]-owned systems is addressed in the Operational Technology Approach section, this section focuses on Jacobs' enterprise-level business cybersecurity capabilities that protect corporate networks, data, and collaboration platforms supporting [CLIENT] operations.

Recognizing the evolving cyber threat landscape facing water and wastewater utilities, Jacobs' integrated governance framework enhances resilience, accelerates incident response, and provides [CLIENT] confidence that cybersecurity risks are managed consistently across all layers of infrastructure.

## Cybersecurity Framework, Governance, and Incident Response

Jacobs' cybersecurity program is aligned with ISO 27001 and NIST/CISA standards, providing a globally recognized framework for managing information security risk through documented policies, regular audits, and continuous improvement. Jacobs maintains a comprehensive Cyber Incident Response Plan that defines roles, communication protocols, and restoration procedures, supported by a 24/7 global Security Operations Center for monitoring and rapid containment.

## Vulnerability Management and Network Protection

Jacobs employs automated patch management and vulnerability scanning to maintain systems within two major vendor releases, with all patches validated for authenticity. Layered defenses include next-generation firewalls, intrusion prevention systems, endpoint detection and response, and continuous network monitoring. Secure remote access is enforced through encrypted VPNs, multi-factor authentication, and zero-trust principles to prevent unauthorized access and lateral movement.

## Above and Beyond — Enhanced Cybersecurity Controls

- **Advanced Threat Protection:** Jacobs deploys full endpoint detection and response and network detection and response across all business networks and endpoints to identify and stop threats in real time.
- **Zero-Trust Remote Access:** Secure VPN access is provided through a zero-trust architecture that continuously verifies users and devices before granting network access.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the unified IT/OT leadership claim, the explicit scope boundary between client-owned systems and the operator's enterprise posture, the ISO 27001 and NIST/CISA alignment, the Cyber Incident Response Plan with a 24/7 global SOC, the patch-currency and layered-defense paragraph, and both Above and Beyond bullets.

Pursuit-specific: the name of the section carrying client-owned OT security, and any regulator- or client-mandated framework (AWIA, state cybersecurity rules) that should be cited alongside ISO 27001 and NIST/CISA. Where the client's IT organization will own remote access, say so rather than implying Jacobs sets the policy.
