# Security, Integration, Prevention, and Rapid Response

Jacobs' Global Security and Resilience team, supported by specialized technical experts, brings more than 100 years of combined operational and technical experience securing water, wastewater, and other highly sensitive government facilities across the United States. Jacobs is widely recognized as a leader in safety and security for the water sector, with experts who **helped develop federal security guidance for water and wastewater treatment facilities in collaboration with the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, the Water Research Foundation, and the Department of Homeland Security**. These standards continue to serve as the industry benchmark for protecting critical infrastructure, and [CLIENT] will benefit directly from the same proven practices that have shaped national policy.

Jacobs will deliver a fully integrated Safety and Security Program that protects people, assets, and information through a single, coordinated framework. Consistent with the mission-critical designation of [CLIENT] facilities as Department of Homeland Security Critical Infrastructure, our approach emphasizes prevention, real-time monitoring, and rapid response to maintain secure operations across all sites.

Aligned with RFP Exhibit M requirements, Jacobs' program integrates physical security and personnel safety into a proactive management system that ensures consistency, transparency, and accountability. Through dedicated staffing, established procedures, advanced digital tools, and close coordination with [CLIENT]'s Risk Management and IT teams, Jacobs will deliver a security program built on prevention, readiness, and continuous improvement.

# Security Management Structure

Jacobs' **Safety and Security Management Program (SSMP)** integrates dedicated personnel, technology systems, and corporate oversight to maintain a safe and secure environment across all [CLIENT] facilities. A **full-time Security and Safety Manager** will report to the Project Manager and lead SSMP implementation across all sites, coordinating directly with [CLIENT]'s Risk Manager to ensure full compliance with Exhibit M requirements. This role will be supported by two site-based positions: a **Safety and Security Coordinator at each of the two water reclamation facilities**. Together, these leaders will manage site safety initiatives and security operations, including access control, guard coverage, patrol scheduling, and daily coordination with plant staff.

The SSMP structure includes reach-back support from Jacobs' Corporate Health, Safety, Security, and Environment organization while maintaining direct, ongoing coordination with [CLIENT]'s Risk Manager, Safety Advisor, and Operations Officer. This integrated structure enables continuous improvement, rapid issue resolution, and focused oversight by experienced professionals from both [CLIENT] and Jacobs. Jacobs will also participate in quarterly joint security and risk management meetings with [CLIENT] to review incidents, technology performance, audit results, and emerging risks.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the federal-guidance authorship claim, the integrated people/assets/information framing, the SSMP name and its three components (dedicated personnel, technology systems, corporate oversight), the full-time manager plus site coordinator structure, the corporate HSSE reach-back, and the quarterly joint security and risk review.

Pursuit-specific: the RFP exhibit citation, the DHS Critical Infrastructure designation sentence (use only where the client's facilities carry it), the client counterpart titles, and the number of site coordinators. Where a client has not yet designated a security lead, offer the coordination structure anyway — it is a differentiator against operators who treat security as a guard-service line item.
