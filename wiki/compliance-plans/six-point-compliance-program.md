# Six-Point Compliance Program

Operations of [CLIENT]'s wastewater facilities — a coastal New England municipal WWTF and collection system — are conducted in full compliance with all applicable federal and state environmental requirements. Our objective is **predictable, uninterrupted compliance** that protects public health, coastal receiving waters, and long-term [CLIENT] interests. Jacobs' **Six-Point Compliance Program**, shown in Exhibit 5-6, integrates ethics, disciplined process control, laboratory practices, data integrity, auditing, and transparent reporting to deliver **defensible NPDES compliance**. This structured framework embeds compliance into daily operations rather than verifying it after the fact.

## Exhibit 5-6. Jacobs' six-point compliance program

**99.98% compliance is the result of a disciplined system, not a statistic.** Jacobs' Six-Point Compliance Program integrates ethics, disciplined process control, laboratory excellence, data integrity, auditing, and transparent reporting to deliver predictable and defensible NPDES compliance for [CLIENT]. That 99.98% compliance rate is maintained through rigorous process control, defensible data, and daily vigilance from certified experts.

1. **SOPs and Process Control Expertise** — consistency and precision at every operations stage; state-agency- and EPA-region-compliant sampling supported by proven QA/QC protocols; cross-functional teams for lab, reuse, stormwater, and biosolids.
2. **Transparency and Reporting** — insight into your system, visibility you can count on: immediate e-Reporting submission and permit violation alerts, and real-time data feeds to the client's web-based dashboard.
3. **Audits and Project Monitoring** — constant improvement through independent validation: internal baseline and ongoing audits drive improvement, and audit results are included in monthly client briefings.
4. **Oversight and Support by Global Experts** — compliance strength driven by ongoing oversight and support: 20+ dedicated O&M compliance and reporting SMEs supporting permit interpretation, training, rapid response, and future preparedness.
5. **Culture of Ethics** — we do the right thing even when no one is watching: transparency in all regulatory communications, and a reporting culture guided by honesty and defensibility.
6. **Compliance Training and Tools** — empowering our people to protect the environment: 30,000 hours of training provided in the last 3 years alone, plus standardized tools, tracking systems, and evaluation matrices.

## Regulatory Oversight and Change Management

Emerging regulatory developments, including nutrient limits, biosolids requirements, PFAS standards, and cybersecurity guidance, are actively monitored, with impacts evaluated and communicated to [CLIENT]. This **forward-looking approach** supports proactive planning rather than reactive compliance.

## Incident Response and Continuous Improvement

In the event of a permit deviation due to extreme weather, equipment failure, or abnormal influent conditions, response actions will be **immediate and transparent**. Corrective actions will be implemented promptly, required notifications will be completed, and each event will undergo documented root cause analysis. Findings will be incorporated into updated procedures to strengthen resilience and prevent recurrence.
