# Safety Leadership Engagement, Accountability, and Proactive Safety Management

**Leadership Engagement and Accountability**

Our commitment to safety starts at the top, with leaders like Senior Supervisors **Kevin Dahl**, **Tim Durham**, and **Kyle Holder**, Senior Strategic Supervisor **Benjamin Bagwell**, and Project Manager **Anthony Benavidez**, setting the tone. They work closely with onsite personnel, including Health and Safety Manager **Reginald McAlpine**, to make certain all staff are properly trained and fully accountable for their roles in safety. This includes regular safety meetings, inspections, and performance tracking to reinforce that every team member, from management to the newest employee, plays a crucial role in maintaining a safe work environment.

**Key Elements of Our Safety Program and Planning**

**Our comprehensive safety program and plan to improve the safety environment at [CLIENT]'s facilities is outlined below.** ([CLIENT] is a Southeast US county public works department operating multiple membrane bioreactor water reclamation facilities and associated pump stations.) These elements, along with our leadership's continuous advocacy, will help establish a whole culture of safety at your facilities. We use tablets to provide many of these documents and tools in an electronic format, improving efficiency in our day-to-day tasks, and tracking of our health and safety metrics and safety scorecard data (**Exhibit 2-41**).

Jacobs has a proven safety record, as evidenced by industry-low incident rates (see **Exhibit 2-40**), and this commitment to safety and active engagement will drive success at [CLIENT]'s facilities. Rather than reacting to near misses or accidents, we emphasize **proactive safety management**—identifying and mitigating risks before incidents occur. This approach is driven by leadership and embraced by every team member, creating a safety culture that prevents complacency from taking root.
