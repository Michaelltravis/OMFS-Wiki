# Laboratory Management and Sampling/QA-QC Plan

During our site visit, Jacobs observed that the onsite laboratory is in excellent condition and is a credit to [CLIENT]'s commitment to water quality and operational excellence. [CLIENT] is a Southern California municipal owner of advanced water treatment, water recycling, and stormwater facilities. We recognize that the primary role of this facility is to support process control and operational decision-making through in-house sampling and analysis. Regulatory compliance and reporting analyses will continue to be conducted by [CLIENT]'s third-party certified laboratory contractor. Jacobs will align our laboratory management approach with this framework, ensuring that onsite analyses provide timely, accurate data for operations, while maintaining seamless coordination with [CLIENT]'s external laboratory partners to meet all regulatory reporting requirements.

**Jacobs' Sampling and Laboratory Testing Plan** will ensure all facilities meet applicable regulatory requirements while generating timely, accurate, and defensible data. Rooted in proven best practices and regulatory expectations, our plan includes EPA-compliant sampling protocols, rigorous QA/QC procedures, and regular training for laboratory and field personnel.

Jacobs will perform all in-house process control sampling and laboratory testing necessary to effectively manage facility performance. For regulatory compliance, Jacobs will collect required samples and deliver them to [CLIENT]'s certified third-party contract laboratory in accordance with our rigorous sampling protocols.

Our Sampling and Laboratory Testing Plan will:

- **Maintain strict quality assurance/quality control (QA/QC) procedures** to ensure accuracy and consistency of results.
- **Follow chain-of-custody protocols** for all regulatory samples, providing clear documentation from collection through delivery to the contract laboratory.
- **Calibrate and maintain laboratory instruments** on a defined schedule to ensure precision and reliability.
- **Integrate laboratory results into the SCADA and CMMS for real-time visibility** and long-term performance tracking.
- **Conduct routine data validation and trend analysis** to support operational decision-making and early identification of process issues.
- **Prepare and organize laboratory records** to support [CLIENT] reporting requirements, audits, and regulatory inspections.
- **Provide and maintain laboratory equipment** at all facility locations.
- **Perform all required sampling per [CLIENT]'s permits** for the advanced water treatment facility, the water recycling facility, and the stormwater systems, and send all regulatory-required analyses to [CLIENT]'s third-party certified laboratory.
- **Conduct special sampling as needed for Title 22, potable reuse, or stormwater events**, with analysis performed by [CLIENT]'s third-party certified laboratory as required.
- **Provide support for toxicity, geochemical, or microbial sampling** based on regulatory conditions.

**Laboratories.** Prior to assuming operations, Jacobs will perform a full laboratory system startup, including QA/QC audits, method reviews, and data validation to establish a defensible compliance baseline. Our QA/QC program includes chain-of-custody tracking, use of EPA-approved methods, internal audits and data reviews, and corrective action logs.

**Document Control and Information Management Systems.** Jacobs will use HACH WIMS to track lab results and performance data, enabling real-time monitoring and alerts, automated compliance reporting, trend analysis across permit parameters, and custom dashboards for [CLIENT].

All regulatory documents—including SOPs, QA/QC logs, calibration records, and compliance reports—will be stored in a secure, version-controlled SharePoint site accessible to authorized [CLIENT] personnel. Document control will comply with all public records and audit provisions.

**Continuous Improvement through Regular Audits.** Jacobs will perform routine audits of all laboratory systems and sampling protocols, including calibration verification and data accuracy reviews. Findings will be presented during performance meetings and used to implement immediate improvements, ensuring continuous alignment with regulatory standards.

**Training and Certification Programs.** All field and laboratory staff will be trained in EPA, Title 22, and facility-specific permit requirements. Personnel will also complete training in laboratory safety, ethics, QA/QC documentation, and spill response.

Jacobs also uses proprietary QA/QC tools for lab data verification, including QC-Stats software and an internal auditing program. We participate in third-party proficiency testing programs to validate data integrity and laboratory performance, ensuring compliance with all state and federal standards. Our internal audit process reinforces adherence to regulatory expectations and supports early identification of compliance risks.

**Compliance Tools.** Our suite of compliance tools will help achieve long-term compliance with environmental regulations and permit conditions. These tools include a comprehensive training platform with video modules, in-person training, and annual refreshers; sample and report tracking tools for NPDES, groundwater, and recycled water programs; a Risk Management Plan and associated tracking tools; standardized escalation protocols for non-compliance reporting; and a secure SharePoint system for compliance documentation and reference materials.

**Benefits to [CLIENT].** Jacobs' laboratory management and sampling approach will provide [CLIENT] with reliable, defensible data to guide daily operations and ensure compliance with all permit requirements. By combining rigorous in-house process control testing with seamless coordination with [CLIENT]'s certified contract laboratory, Jacobs will reduce the risk of compliance violations while supporting proactive operational adjustments. Our integration of laboratory data into SCADA and CMMS systems will enhance real-time visibility, improve reporting efficiency, and strengthen long-term trend analysis. With a robust QA/QC program, secure document control, and ongoing staff training, [CLIENT] will benefit from increased regulatory confidence, streamlined audits, and operational transparency. Ultimately, this program will safeguard public health, protect the environment, and reinforce [CLIENT]'s leadership in sustainable water management.
