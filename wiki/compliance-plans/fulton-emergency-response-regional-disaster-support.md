# Emergency Response, 24/7 Reporting, and Regional Disaster Support

The JC Solutions team is committed to ensuring [CLIENT] wastewater facilities are fully prepared for any emergency. As a FEMA-certified Disaster Response and Recovery contractor, we bring proven expertise in managing critical events—including hurricanes, flooding, pandemics, power loss, and system failures—through comprehensive planning, real-time response, and rapid recovery. Our Emergency Response Plan will address federal, state, and local regulatory requirements while aligning with [CLIENT]'s specific expectations. This includes coordination protocols, contingency planning, crisis communication, and clear lines of authority for swift, unified emergency response. The ERP will be finalized within 60 days of contract award and updated annually per the contract.

| Event | Approach |
|---|---|
| Chemical spill | Site-specific SDS and emergency-cleanup protocols; immediate reporting per OSHA and local law. |
| Asset/equipment failure | Initial and periodic condition assessments; spares strategy for critical systems to enable quick restoration. |
| Fire response | Staff trained in fire prevention/suppression; coordination with local fire departments; recovery planning in place. |
| Flooding | Storm hardening and drainage maintenance; regional staff available for response and staffing support. |
| Hurricane | Emergency-power readiness; mock events (“dry runs”); continuity of operations during outages. |
| Pandemic/health emergency | Health and safety protocols, quarantine guidelines, emergency staffing, and access to regional support. |
| Process failure | Real-time SCADA and trend monitoring, immediate testing, chemical optimization, and use of regional SMEs. |
| Power failure | Coordination with [CLIENT] to deploy generators for 60 MGD capacity; 24/7 staffing until power resumes. |
| Emergency contacts | Posted emergency numbers, including key JC Solutions and [CLIENT] contacts, 911, hospitals, and first responders. |
| Emergency equipment | Pre-arranged 24/7 service contracts for vendor-supplied specialty equipment or rentals. |
| Continuity of Operations Plan | Clear succession, staffing-augmentation plan, subcontractor coordination, and emergency-notification protocols. |

We will provide immediate verbal notice to [CLIENT]'s Contract Administrator following any emergency and submit a written report within five business days outlining the event, actions taken, and recovery progress. In the case of personal injury or a major safety incident, Jacobs will immediately report known details and follow up with full documentation. We maintain a 24/7 emergency-reporting hotline, giving [CLIENT], staff, and residents immediate access to resources trained to respond quickly and effectively to emergencies and significant customer-service issues.

Our extensive national and regional network including more than 4,000 O&M and engineering professionals provides scalable emergency support. Our local Atlanta-based team and South Region staff are trained and equipped for rapid deployment to handle pump and pipe failures, SCADA outages, generator and backup-power needs, emergency procurement and logistics, and technical troubleshooting and compliance guidance. Our FEMA experience and certified teams have supported post-disaster recovery efforts across the U.S., including responses to Hurricanes Irma, Harvey, Maria, and major regional flooding events.

“When super storm Sandy hit the New York harbor and the magnitude of the disaster became apparent, you were the first person that I reached out to for help. Fortunately, for the North Hudson Sewerage Authority [NHSA], Jacobs, our partner of 22 years at the time, came to our assistance and in 24 hours provided an expert team to address the damage caused by the disastrous storm surge. Our main Sewage Treatment Plant in Hoboken was pumped out, some 24 pumps were removed, rebuilt and replaced and temporary electrical control systems constructed to enable primary treatment 48 hours later with full secondary treatment restored within 5 days of the system failure. It was teamwork at its best.” — Fredric J. Pocci, PE, North Hudson Sewerage Authority Engineer
