# Laboratory QA/QC and Compliance Dashboard

Because a great deal of the information necessary for permit compliance begins in the laboratory, we’ve developed a suite of quality-control tools for laboratory testing, including a QA/QC manual and the QC-Stats software program. We also participate in third-party proficiency-testing programs to ensure laboratory performance yields data of unquestionable integrity. The laboratory will be operated in compliance with the 2016 TNI standard. We also administer a comprehensive internal auditing program to verify the adherence of our projects to permitting and other regulatory requirements.

Thorough tracking is key to maintaining a record of perfect compliance and reporting. To facilitate our record of near-perfect compliance, we’ve developed a web-based compliance dashboard to manage project information and communicate with our staff and clients. The dashboard’s Report Tracking Tool lists all required reports and their due dates and can be enabled with reminders or push notifications to complete permits on schedule. Its Sample Tracking Tool lists all required parameters with sampling frequencies; the SCADA menu provides real-time SCADA screens to view plant performance; and the water-quality menu displays results from the monitoring database. Specific applications for recycled water and maintenance will be included.

An important feature of this web-based compliance dashboard is its security. As a first step, the dashboard is accessed by login and password. Access to data is granted at various levels, depending on the role of the user. Data is always encrypted, whether at rest or in motion. Finally, data is view-only: there is no control or ability to affect plant operations through this tool. Working with [CLIENT], a county wastewater utility, we’ll customize the dashboard views and the reports it generates, allowing staff to stay up-to-date and aware of issues affecting system performance.

## Reuse guidance

Use where laboratory QA/QC, compliance tracking, and a secure reporting dashboard are confirmed capabilities. Confirm the applicable TNI edition and each dashboard module before reuse.
