# Emergency Response Plan Approach and Key ERP Elements by Event Type

## Prevention of and Response to Emergencies

Protecting the safety of our employees, the public, and the environment is Jacobs' highest priority and a core value of our operations culture. Jacobs takes emergency preparedness seriously and brings [CLIENT] direct experience managing a wide range of emergency situations across the country.

Jacobs has extensive experience in the development and implementation of Emergency Response Plans (ERP). A well-rehearsed and actively maintained ERP ensures our teams are prepared to prevent hazardous conditions, minimize service disruptions, and restore operations quickly and safely while protecting public health, the water supply, and critical infrastructure assets. Our ERP will define response, recovery, and remediation actions for all anticipated emergency scenarios, incorporating reliability and communication features that enhance safety, coordination, and efficiency.

Sometimes the unexpected occurs. When that happens, Jacobs stands ready to respond with the superior services that are the cornerstone of our O&M project delivery. Jacobs has 2,400+ associates in the region who can be quickly deployed for routine needs, troubleshooting, or emergency support of this partnership when needed. We currently operate 12 O&M projects in the state, whose personnel can respond to any emergency in support of our onsite team or the client within a matter of hours. With 11 offices in the state, our team is equipped and committed to providing immediate, rapid, efficient, and effective assistance—whether for earthquakes, flooding, wildfires, hazardous materials, or equipment failure. Our experts understand the level of resources essential in your time of need and will adapt to provide the most technically effective and feasible solutions while delivering work products expeditiously and within budget.

## Emergency Preparedness Approach

Emergency preparedness begins with proactive assessment. Prior to any predicted event, Jacobs will assess expected impacts, prepare facility systems, and stage necessary staffing. Unit Process Control Procedures (UPCPs) will be reviewed to confirm equipment readiness and operational flexibility. Based on the severity of the threat, we will mobilize additional support staff from Jacobs' regional teams—drawn from facilities across the western United States and nationwide. During the event, we will continuously monitor operational conditions and initiate mitigation or response activities as soon as safely feasible.

Jacobs is equipped to respond immediately to unplanned events using local O&M personnel, regional technical teams, and established subcontractors. For emergencies such as pipeline failures or system blockages, Jacobs will respond within 30 minutes of notification. Our 24-Hour Incident Reporting Hotline connects projects to senior safety and emergency response managers who will initiate resource mobilization and incident tracking.

We recognize that [CLIENT]'s service area is a densely populated urban area with constrained access in many locations, requiring careful consideration of potential public interface during an emergency. Jacobs will coordinate closely with local fire, police, and city-designated emergency coordinators to align site-specific Emergency Response Plan (ERP) procedures with citywide emergency planning. We will also engage the regional electric utility when appropriate to strengthen preparedness and continuity of operations. ERP procedures will be fully integrated with the client's Operations Optimization Plan, Asset Register, and City-Provided Tools/Lab Equipment/Specialty Chemicals documentation, ensuring that facility-specific risks, critical assets, and preventive measures are directly addressed in emergency protocols.

## Elements of Our Emergency Response Plan (ERP)

Jacobs commits to operating and maintaining facilities under emergency conditions with a plan that emphasizes reliability, safety, and clear communication. The ERP will be reviewed and updated regularly in collaboration with the client and will include:

- Attention to the unique characteristics of the facilities that require access via elevator and/or stairs in an emergency, given the fact that the facility is three stories underground
- Emergency staff training in procedures and use of emergency equipment
- Coordination with the client and local agencies, including public safety
- Specialized protocols for weather, health, infrastructure, and utility emergencies
- Drawing on our extensive emergency response experience, including response to flooding, earthquakes, fires, and public health emergencies, our plan is designed to ensure readiness and minimize recovery time

**Key Elements in the ERP, by Event Type:**

| Event | Jacobs' Approach |
|---|---|
| Chemical Spill Response | Individual safety data sheets (SDS) will guide proper clean-up and control measures. |
| Asset/Equipment Failure Response | Identification of asset conditions, critical assets, and acquiring equipment spares that may be placed in inventory so emergency replacements can be made. |
| Fire Response | Facility staff will be trained on fire prevention and fire suppression. Local and regional maintenance staff are available for equipment/facility recovery operations. |
| Flood Response | Proper maintenance of facilities so flood control equipment and measures at the facilities are in top condition. Regional staff, properly certified and qualified, are available to fill in for staff shortages that may result from regional flooding. Jacobs' regional technical support staff are available immediately to optimize plant chemical dosing and operations should flooding affect incoming raw water. |
| Severe Weather Response | Maintenance of generators, mock drills, debris protection, and extended power contingency. |
| Pandemic/Health Emergency Response | A comprehensive employee health program, including mental/emotional health. Regional staff are available for assisting with operations and maintenance during extreme events, and an emergency staffing plan will help maintain safe operation under reduced staffing if required. |
| Process Failure Response | Continuous monitoring and evaluation of trends to proactively identify potential upsets. Response will include immediate additional testing to identify atypical contaminants, jar testing, and process optimization, which may be required based on changed water quality conditions. Conduct comprehensive source water quality testing to detect possible contamination. Procurement resources are available to remedy chemical shortages. Leverage Jacobs' technical resources to identify the issue and assist in the development of the recovery plan. |
| Power Failure Response | Generator startup procedures; sustained plant operations until utility power is restored. |
| Emergency Telephone Numbers | An emergency contact list with site numbers will be posted at each worksite and include project contacts, including the client, plant manager, safety manager, the Public Safety Department, police, fire, and ambulance for surrounding communities—emergency (911) and non-emergency numbers, area hospitals, and serious incident reporting and crisis management. |
| Emergency Equipment Inventory | If emergency equipment is a rental item or provided as a specialty service by a vendor, agreements for 24/7 service will be put in place in advance. |
| Continuity of Operations (COO) Plan | An effective COO makes sure of uninterrupted operations during immediate needs, such as a pandemic, and plans for longer range needs such as retirement, promotions, etc. The COO focuses on roles and responsibilities, notification requirements, subcontractor guidelines, quarantine guidelines, exposure reporting, and travel requirements, and identifies resources, both internal and external, to respond to staffing augmentation needs. |
| Pipeline Failure/Blockages | Train field crews on a comprehensive response plan for the various types of pipeline failure to guide them in appropriate response, whether it's bypassing, isolating, or venting/flushing and/or rodding sections. CPO will communicate condition to the client and provide updates as appropriate. |
| Advance Contracting | Establish in advance agreements for 24/7 service for any emergency rental equipment or services provided by service vendors. |
| Earthquakes | Public and employee safety is the top priority; staff follow posted instructions to seek shelter and avoid hazards, then calmly evacuate to designated areas, with the Safety Supervisor coordinating communication and re-entry only after structural assessments. |
