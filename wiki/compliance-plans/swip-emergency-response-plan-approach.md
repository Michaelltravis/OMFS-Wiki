# Emergency Response Plan Approach and Preparedness Method

## Prevention of and Response to Emergencies

Protecting the safety of our employees, the public, and the environment is Jacobs' highest priority and a core value of our operations culture. Jacobs takes emergency preparedness seriously and brings [CLIENT], a Southern California municipal water utility, direct experience managing a wide range of emergency situations across the country.

Jacobs has extensive experience in the development and implementation of Emergency Response Plans (ERP). A well-rehearsed and actively maintained ERP ensures our teams are prepared to prevent hazardous conditions, minimize service disruptions, and restore operations quickly and safely while protecting public health, the water supply, and critical infrastructure assets. Our ERP will define response, recovery, and remediation actions for all anticipated emergency scenarios, incorporating reliability and communication features that enhance safety, coordination, and efficiency.

Sometimes the unexpected occurs. When that happens, Jacobs stands ready to respond with the superior services that are the cornerstone of our O&M project delivery. Jacobs has 2,400+ associates in the region who can be quickly deployed for routine needs, troubleshooting, or emergency support of this partnership when needed. We currently operate 12 O&M projects in the state, whose personnel can respond to any emergency in support of our onsite team or [CLIENT] within a matter of hours. With 11 offices in the state, our team is equipped and committed to providing immediate, rapid, efficient, and effective assistance—whether for earthquakes, flooding, wildfires, hazardous materials, or equipment failure. Our experts understand the level of resources essential in your time of need and will adapt to provide the most technically effective and feasible solutions while delivering work products expeditiously and within budget.

## Emergency Preparedness Approach

Emergency preparedness begins with proactive assessment. Prior to any predicted event, Jacobs will assess expected impacts, prepare facility systems, and stage necessary staffing. Unit Process Control Procedures (UPCPs) will be reviewed to confirm equipment readiness and operational flexibility. Based on the severity of the threat, we will mobilize additional support staff from Jacobs' regional teams—drawn from facilities across the western United States and nationwide. During the event, we will continuously monitor operational conditions and initiate mitigation or response activities as soon as safely feasible.

Jacobs is equipped to respond immediately to unplanned events using local O&M personnel, regional technical teams, and established subcontractors. For emergencies such as pipeline failures or system blockages, Jacobs will respond within 30 minutes of notification. Our 24-Hour Incident Reporting Hotline connects projects to senior safety and emergency response managers who will initiate resource mobilization and incident tracking.

**Jacobs emergency reporting hotline.** Jacobs maintains a 24/7 emergency reporting hotline, giving [CLIENT], staff and residents immediate access to resources trained to respond quickly and effectively to emergencies and significant customer service issues.

We recognize that [CLIENT]'s service area is a densely populated urban area with constrained access in many locations, requiring careful consideration of potential public interface during an emergency. Jacobs will coordinate closely with the city fire department, police department, and city-designated emergency coordinators to align site-specific Emergency Response Plan (ERP) procedures with citywide emergency planning. We will also engage the regional electric utility when appropriate to strengthen preparedness and continuity of operations. ERP procedures will be fully integrated with [CLIENT]'s Operations Optimization Plan, the Asset Register, and the client-provided Tools, Lab Equipment, and Specialty Chemicals exhibit, ensuring that facility-specific risks, critical assets, and preventive measures are directly addressed in emergency protocols.

## Elements of Our Emergency Response Plan (ERP)

Jacobs commits to operating and maintaining the facilities under emergency conditions with a plan that emphasizes reliability, safety, and clear communication. The ERP will be reviewed and updated regularly in collaboration with [CLIENT] and will include:

- Attention to the unique characteristics of the facilities that require access via elevator and/or stairs in an emergency, given the fact that the facility is three stories underground
- Emergency staff training in procedures and use of emergency equipment
- Coordination with [CLIENT] and local agencies, including public safety
- Specialized protocols for weather, health, infrastructure, and utility emergencies
- Drawing on our extensive emergency response experience, including response to flooding, earthquakes, fires, and public health emergencies, our plan is designed to ensure readiness and minimize recovery time
