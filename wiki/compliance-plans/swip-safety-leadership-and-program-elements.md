# Safety Leadership Engagement, OSHA Audit Record, and Key Safety Program Elements

**Summary of OSHA Audits from the Past Five Years.** OSHA has issued two citations to Jacobs' water/wastewater O&M organization during the past five years. The violations were issued on August 25, 2020, and January 26, 2024. The August 25, 2020, citation was for a safety complaint regarding scheduled offline maintenance around a scrubber system at a PSM facility in Seattle (WA). A letter vacating the January 26, 2024, citation was signed on March 20, 2024. OSHA has not removed the citation yet. Documentation of both instances can be provided if requested.

## Leadership Engagement and Accountability

Jacobs' commitment to safety starts at the leadership level. **Vice President of Operations (West) Paul Rheault**, **Regional H&S Manager Bobby Hammond**, and **CPO Mack Mckenzie** together with **Director of Operations and Jacobs' regional Director of Operations Howard Brewen** and **Manager of Operations Chris Catlin** will set expectations, while **regional Safety Lead Trey Kane** will support day-to-day implementation. Leaders will conduct safety meetings, inspections, and site visits, modeling expected behaviors, and holding all staff accountable for their roles in maintaining safety.

Jacobs will also implement a **digital-enabled safety program** at [FACILITY]. Tablet-based tools will track observations, training compliance, and incident data in real time. This allows the team to quickly assess compliance, measure performance against KPIs, and respond proactively to emerging risks.

## Key Elements of Our Safety Program and Planning

Our comprehensive safety program and plan to improve the safety environment at the [CLIENT]'s facilities is outlined below. These elements, along with our leadership's continuous advocacy, will help establish a whole culture of safety at [CLIENT]'s facilities. We use tablets to provide many of these documents and tools in an electronic format, improving efficiency in our day-to-day tasks and tracking of our health and safety metrics and safety scorecard data.

| Element | Description |
|---|---|
| H&S Work Control Plan | Used to direct, conduct, and document work relative to H&S requirements. With a focus on accident prevention, the plan directs how to classify the type of work and uses activity hazard analysis and pre-task planning, daily safety meetings, and stop-work authority for unsafe activity afforded every associate without fear of reprisal. |
| Activity Hazard Analyses | In addition to preparing SOPs for operating system equipment, the project team is required to perform an activity hazard analysis for all high-hazard tasks. |
| Pre-Task Plans (PTP) | Used to discuss the work to be performed and comply with the OSHA regulation, PTPs make sure hazards are identified and the controls (work practices, personal protective equipment [PPE], training, etc.) involved with the safe performance of work are applied. |
| Inspections/BZOs | Managers' and supervisors' observations, coaching, and positive reinforcement allow employees to identify and correct site physical and health hazards. This program provides a real-time opportunity for leaders to engage with associates and provide feedback on safety program implementation. |
| PPE | Provides PPE to employees and others as required by each task. |
| Subcontractor Management | Manages risks associated with subcontractors' H&S using a systematic method that includes contract language plus prequalification based on safety performance. Requires most subcontractors to submit safety documents. |
| H&S Improvement Planning | Uses our safety audit scoring checklist and customized safety scorecard to look at parameters such as total recordable rate (TRR), at-fault motor vehicle incidents, training completion, Behavior Zero Observations (BZOs), and other performance data. |
