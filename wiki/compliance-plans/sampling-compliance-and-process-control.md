# Sampling — Compliance and Process Control Programs, Sample Tracking Tool, and Biomonitoring

## Sampling — compliance and process control

**Jacobs operates two distinct but integrated sampling programs, and both matter at [CLIENT].** Compliance sampling covers all NPDES permit-required sampling locations, parameters, and frequencies — influent, effluent, and in-process points at each facility — and stormwater and biosolids sampling. All compliance samples will follow EPA-approved methods with proper chain-of-custody, preservation, and documentation. Schedule 9's daily data reporting forms define the specific parameters [CLIENT] expects to be tracked at each facility. Jacobs will use these forms as the operational floor, not the ceiling.

Process control sampling is where the current program has the most room for improvement. Site observations confirmed that process testing is minimal at satellite facilities, in-line analyzers at North Canadian have been offline for years, and operators at Deer Creek and Chisholm Creek are relying on portable TSS probes without on-site balances or ovens for solids verification. Operators have been making treatment decisions without the real-time data they need. Jacobs will restore and expand process control sampling, including in-basin MLSS, DO, SVI, and MCRT monitoring, to give operators the visibility to manage treatment proactively, not reactively.

**To make sure nothing falls through the cracks, Jacobs uses a Sample Tracking Tool at every project.** This documented system records sampling locations, frequencies, parameters, and individual accountability for each sample pulled. Samples are checked off at collection and verified by a second independent party the same day, providing assurance that every compliance and process sample is taken as required, every time.

## Biomonitoring and whole effluent toxicity

We will conduct and manage biomonitoring, including whole effluent toxicity testing as required by NPDES permits, as a priority compliance obligation. Given recent WET test performance at North Canadian and Deer Creek, Jacobs will monitor these tests closely, maintain operational controls that minimize toxicity risk, and work proactively with ODEQ if additional testing or investigation is warranted.
