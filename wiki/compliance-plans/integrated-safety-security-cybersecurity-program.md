# Integrated Safety, Security, and Cybersecurity Program for Wastewater Facilities

Jacobs will implement a **fully integrated safety, security, and cybersecurity program** designed to protect personnel, safeguard [CLIENT]'s assets, and maintain **uninterrupted wastewater operations** in [CLIENT]'s coastal environment. The facilities of this coastal New England municipality operate in a flood-prone area, serve a dense residential community, and must meet strict NPDES permit requirements. Our program will support **safe, reliable operations under both routine and extreme conditions**.

Our objective is simple: **zero injuries, zero security breaches, and zero service interruptions.** Safety, physical security, and cybersecurity will be integrated into daily operations, maintenance planning, and emergency preparedness to **reduce risk and strengthen system resiliency**.

## Safety Leadership and Culture

[CLIENT]'s facilities will be operated under our **BeyondZero® safety philosophy**, which emphasizes proactive risk management and shared accountability. Safety leadership begins with the Project Manager and extends to all employees and subcontractors. Daily work activities will include pre-task planning, activity hazard analyses (AHAs), and clearly defined safe work procedures. All personnel will have **stop-work authority** and will be expected to exercise it whenever unsafe conditions are identified.

Safety metrics will be reviewed with [CLIENT] during routine meetings to support transparency and continuous improvement. Jacobs maintains an **Experience Modification Rate (EMR) below 1.0**, exceeding the RFP safety requirement, and our industry-leading safety performance is further illustrated in **Exhibit 5-10** (graphic asset `104_HHull_0091KO_1`): Jacobs outperforms averages in top safety categories, with a 5-year average Recordable Incident Rate (RIR) of 0.19 against an industry average of 0.62 — **60% better than the industry average** — and a 5-year average DART/Lost Time Injury Rate (LTIR) of 0.056 against an industry average of 0.20 — **72% better than the industry average**. Both comparisons use 2018-2022 data from the Bureau of Labor Statistics.

## Site-Specific Safety and Security Planning

Within **30 days of Notice to Proceed**, Jacobs will submit our Site-Specific Safety Plan and Site Security Plan for [CLIENT] review. The Safety Plan will address confined space entry, lockout/tagout, electrical safety, chemical handling, fall protection, gas monitoring, contractor coordination, and emergency response procedures. Given [CLIENT]'s vulnerability to coastal storms and flooding, the plan will also define **storm preparation protocols, staffing contingencies, and flood response procedures** to maintain operational continuity.

Security measures address both administrative and physical controls, including controlled facility access, secured gates and doors, visitor verification procedures, and annual security assessments. We will coordinate with local emergency responders to ensure familiarity with facility operations and **rapid response during emergencies**.

Physical security across the WWTF, pump stations, and collection system will also be evaluated. Existing deficiencies, such as holes in the WWTF perimeter fencing that allow potential unauthorized access, will be addressed through **immediate repairs and implementation of routine perimeter inspections**. Additional enhancements, including improved gate locking, signage, lighting, and surveillance, will be considered to strengthen long-term site security.

## Cybersecurity and SCADA Protection

We will implement a cybersecurity plan aligned with industry best practices for OT and industrial control systems, using a **defense-in-depth approach** to protect system integrity and maintain operational continuity. The plan will include system risk assessment, role-based access controls, secure network segmentation between business IT and SCADA environments, secure remote access protocols, and password governance. Continuous monitoring will identify abnormal activity and incident response procedures will define notification, containment, and recovery actions.

System backups and recovery capabilities will also be maintained to support **rapid restoration of operations** following a cyber or system event. Annual cybersecurity awareness training will be provided to on-site personnel to reduce risks associated with phishing, credential compromise, and other cyber threats.
