# Auditing, Inspections, and Continuous Improvement

Jacobs will apply a structured program of inspections and reviews to identify hazards before they lead to incidents. Supervisors and field leaders will conduct weekly inspections. Monthly reviews of safety performance will evaluate trends and corrective action progress. We'll complete a comprehensive annual audit at both water reclamation facilities. All findings will be documented, assigned, and tracked through Safety360 until closure. This consistent review process drives improvement and supports safe operations.

# Preliminary Safety Assessment

Jacobs conducted an initial safety review of both water reclamation facilities during proposal development. The sites demonstrate overall strong safety awareness and housekeeping. We observed opportunities for improvement in equipment guarding, railing continuity around aeration basins, arc-flash labeling, chemical storage organization, and certain confined space access points. These items will be verified with [CLIENT] during transition and incorporated into the Safety Plan. All corrective actions will be documented and tracked to resolution. A detailed assessment and photographs are provided in **Exhibit IV-51**.

**EXHIBIT IV-51. SAFETY ASSESSMENT — observations at the larger water reclamation facility**

- There are several areas around the aeration basins (West Plant) that have large gaps between railings, which are too large. Gaps between posts, or post and walls, should not exceed 4 inches. There are several areas where these gaps occur around valves and where metal railings meet concrete walls.
- In the Dryer building, there are several areas that need equipment guarding installed. Any rotating shaft or shaft end must be protected. This includes the dryer itself on the inlet side, which is not guarded from access.
- In the headworks building, all the screens have their guards flipped down. This allows the operators to view the screens to ensure they are operating effectively, but does present a hazard with its moving parts. Guards should be reinstalled or another effective control implemented to prevent contact with the moving parts of the screens.
- The channel outside headworks needs to have railing extended to protect against the fall hazard.

# Commitment to a Safe Operation

Jacobs will deliver a safety program that is consistent, disciplined, and continuously improving. Through strong leadership, clear procedures, comprehensive training, transparent reporting, and active engagement, we'll maintain a safe work environment for everyone who works in or visits [CLIENT] facilities.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the inspection cadence (weekly supervisor inspections, monthly performance reviews, comprehensive annual audit per facility, all findings tracked in Safety360 to closure) and the closing commitment paragraph. The structure of the preliminary assessment — walk the site, credit what is strong, name specific fixable conditions, commit to verifying them with the client during transition — is a reusable challenger device.

Pursuit-specific: every finding in Exhibit IV-51 and the accompanying photographs. Replace with observations from the actual pursuit walkdown, and have the capture and safety leads confirm each one before it goes to the client. Facility names are generalized here; restore descriptive references that match the pursuit.
