# Role-Based Safety Training, PPE, and Chemical/H2S Field Controls

## Training, personal protective equipment (PPE), and chemical safety

We will put safety leadership in the field from the start. Safety SME **Tracy** will work with leadership and supervisors to implement the Safety Program and Plan, verify site conditions, and tailor training to the work each employee will perform. Training won't be one-size-fits-all. Operators, mechanics, electricians, laboratory staff, biosolids personnel, and pump station crews will each receive training based on their assigned tasks, work locations, and exposure risks, with added focus before high-risk work begins.

We'll train staff to work safely in the conditions they'll face across [CLIENT]'s facilities — [CLIENT] being a Southcentral US municipal water utility trust — including chemical systems, confined spaces, H₂S-prone areas, electrical equipment, laboratory settings, biosolids handling areas, and maintenance work zones. We will provide role-based training during transition, refresh it annually, and update it whenever duties, equipment, or site conditions change. Training will cover confined space entry and rescue, lockout/tagout, fall protection, electrical safety, arc flash, hazard communication, chemical handling, emergency response, and first aid/CPR/AED. We'll pair that training with field verification, supervisor observations, and documented follow-up, so each employee understands what applies to their job, what PPE to use, when to use it, and how to use it correctly.

## Field controls for real chemical and process hazards

[CLIENT]'s facilities present real chemical and process hazards that require disciplined daily control, not stand-alone paperwork. During our site visit, H₂S levels at North Canadian and Deer Creek created access restrictions and safety concerns, including areas where personnel couldn't enter because of elevated gas readings. We will respond to those conditions with practical field controls. **As part of our startup investment, we'll equip staff with Blackline personal gas monitors for continuous H₂S monitoring, real-time alerts, and man-down detection.** This will give employees, supervisors, and project leadership better visibility into exposure conditions and a faster way to respond when conditions change.

We will also integrate safety, process control, and emergency response into routine operations, so field work matches the written plan. That approach will be especially important in areas with known H₂S exposure and odor-related safety concerns, including North Canadian and Deer Creek. We'll align our safety practices with our Risk Management Plan, Process Safety Management requirements where applicable, each facility's Spill Prevention Control and Countermeasure (SPCC) Plan, and related emergency response procedures so [CLIENT] gains a **stronger safety culture, stronger regulatory readiness, and better protection for staff, assets, and the environment.**
