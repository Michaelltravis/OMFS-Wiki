# Leadership, Training, and Incident Reporting

## Leadership, Roles, and Integration

Safety leadership is embedded in our on-site team. The Project Manager will hold overall accountability for safety performance and will serve as the primary point of communication with [CLIENT]'s Risk Manager and Safety Advisor. A dedicated **Safety and Security Manager** will implement the SMS, conduct inspections, coordinate training, and oversee investigations and documentation. Each facility will also have a safety coordinator to reinforce daily safe work practices and encourage peer engagement. Supervisors will lead pre-task briefings, hazard identification, and verification that safety procedures and permits are correctly implemented.

Jacobs and [CLIENT] will jointly review incidents, corrective actions, and trends during quarterly **Operations Governance Committee** meetings to promote continuous improvement.

## Training and Workforce Engagement

Employees assigned to [CLIENT]'s facilities will complete required OSHA and [CLIENT]-specific training, including confined space entry, lockout and tagout, fall protection, respiratory protection, combustible dust awareness, hazard communication, emergency response, chemical hygiene, and first aid and CPR. Job-specific and site-specific modules will supplement these core requirements. Annual refresher training will be tracked in the LMS and made available to [CLIENT].

Regular engagement through daily start-up discussions, weekly toolbox talks, and safety observations will reinforce awareness, support proactive hazard identification, and encourage open communication. All personnel will have stop-work authority.

## Incident Reporting and Investigation

Jacobs will meet [CLIENT]'s notification and reporting procedures for all safety incidents, near misses, injuries, or hazardous conditions. [CLIENT]'s Risk Manager, Safety Advisor, and Operations Officer will be notified immediately by phone. We'll provide a written summary within 12 hours, followed by a full investigation, including root cause analysis and corrective actions, within the required 48 to 72-hour period. All findings will be managed through Safety360 and reviewed with [CLIENT] during routine performance discussions.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the four-tier safety leadership structure (Project Manager accountable, dedicated Safety and Security Manager, per-facility safety coordinator, supervisors leading pre-task briefings), stop-work authority for all personnel, the engagement rhythm (daily start-up discussions, weekly toolbox talks, safety observations), and the escalating notification sequence.

Pursuit-specific: the client counterpart titles, the governance forum name and cadence, and the reporting clocks — these should mirror the contract's required timelines exactly. Where the RFP does not set a clock, the immediate-call / 12-hour-summary / 48-to-72-hour-investigation ladder is a strong default commitment.
