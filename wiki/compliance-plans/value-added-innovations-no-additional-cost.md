# Value-Added Innovations Delivered at No Additional Cost

## Innovation

Jacobs brings [CLIENT], a coastal New England municipality, more than routine O&M. We will pair disciplined day-to-day execution with **practical, proven innovation** that helps improve reliability, reduce nuisance conditions, and support **better long-term investment decisions**. Through structured innovation workshops, direct connectivity between O&M leaders and Jacobs' regional and national SMEs, and a strong link between field operations and capital planning, we will help [CLIENT] turn recurring issues and operating data into **actionable improvements rather than isolated fixes**. **Exhibit 5-22** demonstrates the breadth of value-added innovations we propose to bring [CLIENT].

**EXHIBIT 5-22.** *Value added innovations included in our base fee and delivered to [CLIENT]*

| Innovation | Value | What We Will Do for [CLIENT] | Benefit to [CLIENT] and Its Wastewater O&M |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Discounted Engineering Rates** | Up to 19% savings compared to standard rates | Provide out-of-scope engineering and consulting at a discounted 2.6 multiplier to aid in leveraging the full breadth of Jacobs' capabilities for [CLIENT] including acting on Annual Innovation Workshop priorities such as focused studies, grant support, and more. | Offers [CLIENT] the opportunity to leverage Jacobs' vast network of industry-leading experts to continually improve facilities performance at a significantly reduced cost compared to market value. |
| **Annual Innovation Workshops** | $350,000 | Convene an annual [CLIENT]/Jacobs workshop with targeted SMEs in process, biosolids, collections, odor, energy, digital tools, and capital planning. Co-develop a focused agenda tied to current performance, recurring issues, and [CLIENT]'s next-step O&M and CIP priorities. | Keeps innovation aligned to [CLIENT]'s priorities instead of becoming reactive or ad hoc. Gives [CLIENT] rapid access to specialist input and a practical path from operating issues to implementable improvements. |
| **Regional Support** | $4,450,000 | Supplement the on-site project team with approximately 2,000 hours of support from Jacobs' deep bench of regional O&M resources and industry-leading technical SMEs. | Enhances the capabilities of the on-site team with access to specialized expertise for solving operational issues, optimizing processes, and future planning needs. |
| **Systemwide Odor Optimization** | $100,000 | Build and calibrate a WATS sewer process model using hydraulic model data plus targeted wastewater/H₂S sampling; use the model to evaluate bioxide, In-Pipe Technology, Wet Well Wizard, and other operational or chemical strategies. Identifies the most effective operational and capital actions before money is spent, improving neighborhood protection and asset life. Creates a systemwide roadmap [CLIENT] can use to sequence near-term changes and longer-term odor infrastructure investments. |

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the exhibit's structure — innovation / quantified value / what we will do / benefit to the client — plus a bolded total row stating the aggregate value delivered at no additional cost over the contract term. That four-column form converts a list of nice ideas into a priced package an evaluator can score, and it is the pattern to copy even when every line item changes. Universal too: the lead-in framing that innovation comes from workshops, direct SME connectivity, and a link between field operations and capital planning, so recurring issues become "actionable improvements rather than isolated fixes." Pursuit-specific: every line item and every figure. Select items against the client's stated pain points (odor and I/I dominate here), price them for the actual term, and confirm the discounted-rate multiplier and the resulting savings percentage against the commercial volume — those two are true rate figures and must not be carried across pursuits.
