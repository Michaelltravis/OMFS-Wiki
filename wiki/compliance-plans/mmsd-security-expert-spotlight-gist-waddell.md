# Expert Spotlight: Forrest Gist, PE, CPP & Keith Waddell, CPP

## Jacobs' Global Security Leadership Supporting [CLIENT]

Forrest Gist, PE, CPP, and Keith Waddell, CPP bring more than 60 years of combined security experience supporting critical infrastructure across North America. Their leadership ensures [CLIENT] benefits from the same high standards used to protect complex utilities, government programs, and Fortune 500 operations.

As Jacobs' **Global Technical Lead for Security**, Forrest has delivered more than 250 security projects and is a Professional Engineer and Certified Protection Professional. He has designed and implemented advanced systems for utilities nationwide, including shoreline intrusion detection, Genetec Security Center integration, mobile video platforms, and automated incident-response workflows.

Keith Waddell serves as Jacobs' **Director of Security for the Americas**, supporting security and resilience programs for more than 40,000 employees. A Certified Protection Professional with 34 years of experience, Keith leads crisis management, business continuity, access and identity management, workplace violence prevention, and electronic countermeasures across high-risk environments.

Together, Forrest and Keith anchor the security program supporting [CLIENT], ensuring it is grounded in proven practices, resilient system design, and real-world operational experience.
