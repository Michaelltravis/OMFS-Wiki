# Petroleum Storage Tank Compliance and SPCC Integration

## Oklahoma Corporation Commission (OCC) — petroleum storage tanks

[CLIENT]'s facilities — [CLIENT] is a Southcentral US municipal water utility trust operating four wastewater treatment plants and a major pump station — include underground and aboveground petroleum storage tanks at North Canadian, South Canadian, and Chisholm Creek, all subject to OCC's Petroleum Storage Tank Program. These tanks used for regular unleaded and diesel fuel require active permit certification, routine inspection compliance, and meticulous recordkeeping under OCC regulations.

**We'll maintain current Petroleum Storage Tank Program Permit Certifications for all three facilities and integrate underground storage tank inspection and compliance activities** into our facility management protocols. We'll incorporate spill prevention measures for these tanks into each facility's **SPCC Plan**, creating a unified approach to petroleum release prevention across the portfolio.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: naming the second state agency that regulates fuel tanks, committing to permit certification, routine inspection compliance, and recordkeeping, and then folding the tanks into the facility SPCC Plans as one portfolio-wide release-prevention approach. Pursuit-specific: the OCC program name, which facilities hold tanks, and the underground/aboveground mix.
