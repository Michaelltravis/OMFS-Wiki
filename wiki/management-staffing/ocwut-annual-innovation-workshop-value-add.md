# Annual Innovation Workshop Drives Continuous Improvement and Elevated O&M

Our **Annual Innovation Workshops — provided at no cost to [CLIENT]**, a regional water utility trust operating a multi-plant wastewater system — bring leading-industry experts to your team each year to review the prior year's achievements and co-develop new goals aligned with [CLIENT]'s evolving needs and long-term vision. [CLIENT] staff, stakeholders, our on-site O&M team, and our utility experts all take part.

This forward-looking session keeps [CLIENT] at the leading edge of treatment technologies, optimization strategies, and modern O&M practices. We'll handpick industry leaders in process engineering, biosolids and dryer technologies/operations, odor control, hydraulics, wet weather flow management, pump station operations, digital optimization, and energy management to match your current challenges and opportunities.

Ahead of the workshop, we'll co-develop a full-day agenda with you that reflects your priorities and sharpens the focus on your priorities. Our specialists then prepare by learning about your facilities, analyzing performance data, and pinpointing improvement opportunities so they arrive with actionable ideas tailored to [CLIENT]. During the session, we listen first, then co-create plans for the coming year. The result is measurable gains in operations, asset lifecycle performance, and cost efficiency. **For example, a previous workshop at the Wilmington WWTP identified opportunities for more than $1M in energy savings.**

## From the WEFTEC Stage to Your Innovation Workshops

A clear demonstration of the depth and relevance of our expertise is our unmatched participation in national knowledge sharing events such as the Water Environment Federation Technical Exhibition and Conference (WEFTEC), where Jacobs consistently contributes more technical content and thought leadership than any other O&M provider. For example, Jacobs had 27 papers at the 2024 WEFTEC where our major O&M competitors had none. In 2025, Jacobs presented more than 60 workshops and remained a long-time sponsor of the annual operations challenge, where top wastewater professionals demonstrate their skills in collections, laboratory work, process control, maintenance, and safety. This level of engagement underscores the breadth and relevance of the expertise we can bring.
