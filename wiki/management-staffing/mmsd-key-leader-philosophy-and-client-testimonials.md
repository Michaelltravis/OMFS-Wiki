# Key Leader Management Philosophy, Personal Statements, and Client Testimonials

## Kevin Dahl, CRL — Project Manager

**Management philosophy**

- Fosters collaboration to challenge norms and eliminate complacency
- Creates a culture of connection where teams have the relationships and resources they need to succeed
- Integrates disciplines, listens to all voices, and embraces innovation to drive meaningful change

**Personal statement:** "I'm not afraid to make decisions and drive to targets. I want to know the direction things are moving. At the same time, I focus on consensus and a high-performing team—I'm not trying to micro-manage. Mentoring is important to me, as is getting everyone on the same page—unions, operations, engineering, and management. I can find a way to work with anyone. A healthy system is achieved not just by spending money on equipment, but by building a healthy culture. I'm confident we can achieve the vision of [CLIENT]."

**What clients are saying about Kevin:** *"I have developed a very positive relationship with Kevin since we started working together in 2018 at our wastewater utility. He is personable, responsive, transparent, and follows through on his commitments. Even more recently in 2023, the trust we have developed has expanded to our drinking water utility."* — Neil O'Leary, Mayor, City of Waterbury, Connecticut

## Rick Warner, PE, WEF Fellow — Deputy Project Manager / Director of Regional Partnership

**Management philosophy**

- Builds enriching public engagement processes that reflect stakeholder needs, expectations, and values
- Creates space for stakeholders and interagency and community partnerships that are critical to advancing innovative water management projects and programs

**Personal statement:** "I'm excited to partner with [CLIENT] and advance your 2035 Vision and the Strategic Plan. [CLIENT] and Jacobs are well-aligned. We're both innovative water sector leaders focused on bringing communities together around shared values and creating water solutions that are people-focused, resilient, inclusive, affordable, and transformative."

**What clients are saying about Rick:** *"Over the past 15 years, I've been privileged to work with Rick to create community partnerships for regional-scale water resilience and sustainability, transform organizational cultures, and establish hubs for research and innovation on today's critical water challenges. Rick's vision, strategic thinking, and stakeholder engagement were essential to creating and sustaining the transformative OneWater Nevada, Nevada Water Innovation Institute, and Partnering for Impact programs. I believe any water utility today would benefit tremendously from Rick's expertise."* — Krishna Pagilla, PhD, PE, The Ralph and Rose Hoeper Engineering Professor, Foundation Professor, and Director, Nevada Water Innovation Institute, University of Nevada, Reno

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-part key-leader card — philosophy bullets written as behaviors rather than qualifications, a first-person statement that says something a resume cannot (Kevin's "a healthy system is achieved not just by spending money on equipment"), and third-party corroboration. Two cards side by side on one page is an efficient way to spend a page on leadership without a resume dump.

Pursuit-specific: everything named. If a leader has no quotable client, either source one during capture or drop the quote line rather than paraphrasing praise.
