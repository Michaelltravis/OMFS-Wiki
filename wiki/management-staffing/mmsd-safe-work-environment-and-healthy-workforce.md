# Safe Work Environment, Safety Leadership, and Healthy Workforce

## F. Safe Work Environment

Jacobs and [CLIENT], a regional wastewater authority operating two large water reclamation facilities, share a single goal: that **every employee goes home safe each day**. We'll maintain a safe, compliant, and health-conscious workplace for all [CLIENT] and Jacobs personnel by embedding safety leadership, accountability, and care into every level of the team organization. Our approach aligns directly with the contractual requirements in §3.02(f) and Exhibit M and reflects Jacobs' culture of BeyondZero®, where safety is a core value built into planning, training, and daily behavior. Jacobs' **comprehensive safety and security programs** are provided in **_Section IV.A.4_** of this proposal.

### 1. Fostering a Safe Work Environment

Our **Security and Safety Manager, Travis Leech, will lead project safety, with support from Safety and Security Coordinators at each water reclamation facility**. Together, they'll provide site-specific leadership and assure alignment across all [CLIENT] facilities. A designated Safety Champion will support the Safety and Security Coordinators as an advocate to frontline staff, helping to reinforce Jacobs' safety culture and engagement throughout daily operations. Supervisors and team leads will participate in pre-task briefings, permitting, and job hazard analyses to maintain safe, compliant, and efficient work practices. These collective responsibilities will reinforce safety as a shared accountability across the organization.

Jacobs' proven safety management system combines structured procedures, real-time reporting tools, and continuous engagement to deliver industry-leading results. Our performance, which consistently exceeds industry averages, is achieved through transparency, training, and accountability. All new employees, including retained staff from the incumbent operator, will complete a site-specific orientation before beginning work. Task-specific training is provided for high-risk activities such as confined-space entry, electrical work, fall protection, and chemical handling. Ongoing refreshers and safety stand-downs maintain awareness and reinforce continuous improvement. Every employee has "Stop Work" authority that empowers them to halt unsafe work immediately. Jacobs recognizes and rewards proactive safety behaviors through regular recognition programs that reinforce engagement and accountability.

We track safety data, audit findings, and incidents using a system called IonCity, which provides analytics for trend identification, root-cause analysis, and corrective-action verification. This transparent system will provide [CLIENT] leadership real-time visibility into safety performance and outcomes. All activities will comply with OSHA and [CLIENT] requirements, including the procedures outlined in Exhibit M.

### 2. Healthy Workforce and Well-Being

Jacobs recognizes that physical safety and mental well-being are inseparable. Our Healthy Employee Focus promotes a holistic approach to care, addressing mental health, wellness, and work-life balance. Employees and their families have access to confidential counseling and mental health resources through Jacobs' One Million Lives initiative and EAP, along with comprehensive medical and wellness benefits that encourage healthy living. Jacobs also invests in programs that recognize and support employees beyond the workplace, including wellness challenges, family events, and volunteer opportunities that strengthen community connection. These initiatives create a work environment where people feel valued, supported, and motivated to perform at their best.
