# Integrated Asset Management and Maintenance Support Bench (Exhibit II-9)

**Exhibit II-9. Integrated maintenance and asset management support speeds problem-solving and improves performance** (graphic asset ID `250_007CAM_13`). The exhibit shows the onsite O&M team connected to offsite O&M support resources through three named leaders: John Loucks-Powell, CRL, Director of Maintenance & Asset Management; Graham Knowles, Director of Asset Management for O&M; and Mike Idzior, CMRT, CRL, Maintenance Support Lead. Offsite resources are organized into Regional Maintenance Support and Major Maintenance Support Teams, and an Asset Management Team comprising Knowledge Management, PdM, Integration Program, and Implementation Program groups.

We'll connect [CLIENT]'s maintenance and asset management staff to our asset management and maintenance experts, including planners, reliability engineers, CMMS specialists, heavy maintenance trades, and others, as shown in **Exhibit II-9**. Our experts will expedite decision making and problem solving to improve performance.

Our Regional Maintenance Support Teams are highly skilled maintenance professionals who support Jacobs' operations in the region. These mobile teams are available to help John and the onsite teams address maintenance needs, including complex PM and CM and emergencies.

**Benefit to [CLIENT]:**

- ✓ Avoids disruption to PM and CM activities
- ✓ Supplements staff when large or emergency tasks arise, so minimum staffing is maintained
- ✓ Helps speed equipment repair or startup
- ✓ Regional maintenance and asset management support increases potential to identify ways to improve energy and chemical efficiency

Our national Asset Management team will partner with John and the onsite team to strengthen maintenance data quality, optimize PM and PdM, and align day-to-day work with long-term asset strategies. They provide access to specialized tools, analytics, training, and implementation support that help reduce failures and downtime, improve lifecycle and capital decisions, and advance system performance.

**Benefit to [CLIENT]:**

- ✓ Provides a knowledge management program for CMMS data governance, standards, and ongoing training to enhance quality
- ✓ Supports advanced failure analysis
- ✓ Provides additional technical support for the PdM program using vibration, thermography, oil, and ultrasound integrated with NEXGEN
- ✓ Aligns resources, governance, and tool adoption so [CLIENT] and staff are confident and comfortable with processes
- ✓ Provides an implementation program delivering asset plans, condition assessments, and ISO 55001–aligned LCC/RCM studies that turn strategy into field execution

**Benefit to [CLIENT]:**

- ✓ Adding a part-time Director of Maintenance & Asset Management to take on administrative and strategic oversight tasks (governance, KPI management, PM optimization, RCA, NEXGEN data standards) allows our WRF and Conveyance Maintenance & Asset Managers to focus on day-to-day execution and exceeding the 85% PM/15% CM target

**Bill Desing will bring additional experts from our O&M and Engineering Support team to provide advanced technical assistance, select or redesign equipment, and coordinate with capital improvement projects as needed. Bill's team includes local staff in the region and global SMEs.**

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the two-column logic of Exhibit II-9 — onsite team on one side, named offsite bench on the other, each support layer carrying its own benefit line. Also universal: the argument that regional surge support is what protects the contractual minimum staffing level when a large or emergency job lands.

Pursuit-specific: the three named leaders and their credentials (CRL, CMRT); the CMMS/EAM platform the PdM data integrates with; the PM/CM ratio target; whether the engineering support lead's team can honestly be described as having local staff.
