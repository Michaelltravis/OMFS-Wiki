# University Scholarship Fund Commitment (Exhibit II-20)

**Exhibit II-20. Our commitment to the local university.**

Jacobs recently made an initial $2,500 contribution to support student learning and workforce readiness initiatives at the University of Wisconsin–Milwaukee (UWM), and during the execution of this contract with [CLIENT] plans to establish a $50K scholarship fund to advance education and career opportunities for students pursuing environmental, engineering, and sustainability-related fields. This long-term commitment reinforces our dedication to the region's future workforce and the local academic institutions that prepare them.

The contribution, dated April 3, 2025, was made to the Jacobs Environmental Engineering Scholarship Fund in recognition of 35 years of service in the region by Patrick Hill and Ron Williams.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the device — show a contribution already made, then state the larger commitment for the contract term. It converts a workforce-development promise into demonstrated behavior, and it works in qualifications and community-engagement sections as well as staffing.

Pursuit-specific: the institution, both dollar figures, the date, and the honorees. The check graphic is bespoke and must be re-rendered for any other pursuit.
