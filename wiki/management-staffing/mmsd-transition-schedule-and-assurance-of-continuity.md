# Assurance of Continuity and the Phased Pre-Term Schedule

## Assurance of continuity

Through proven systems, experienced personnel, and local familiarity, we'll maintain continuity across [CLIENT]'s facilities and services throughout the transition. Drawing on lessons learned from dozens of complex treatment and conveyance transitions, Jacobs will deliver a seamless, non-disruptive handover that is reassuring to employees and invisible to the public.

## Schedule for smooth transition of operations and related systems

Jacobs' transition approach is governed by a clearly defined, phased schedule that ensures all operational, staffing, and system milestones are achieved before March 1, 2028. While the Draft Contract does not specify a transition duration, Jacobs has developed an approximately 18-month pre-term schedule consistent with §3.02 (Mobilization and Staffing Transition) and §3.03(a) (CMMS Implementation).

The phased schedule emphasizes early engagement, structured onboarding, retention of institutional knowledge, and flexibility to deploy additional expertise as needed. Each phase is supported by Jacobs' governance and communication framework, providing [CLIENT] with transparency, accountability, and confidence in progress. The accompanying high-level schedule (**Exhibit IV-58**) illustrates how key activities will be sequenced and monitored to ensure full operational readiness.

All transition activities, decisions, and milestones will be documented and accessible, supporting a smooth progression to the operational start date.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the continuity promise, the four qualities the phased schedule emphasizes (early engagement, structured onboarding, knowledge retention, flexible expertise), and the commitment that every transition decision is documented and accessible. Pursuit-specific: the schedule duration, start date, and contract clause citations — and the observation that the client's draft contract left the duration open, which only applies where that is true.
