# Seamless Transition: Experienced Transition Team and Communications Plan

## Experienced Transition Team

Jacobs will deploy a proven transition team led by **Transition Manager Kelly Irving** and supported by **Transition Administrator Cheryl Reeves**. This team will manage all aspects of the transition, enabling the local team to remain focused on daily operations while new systems and processes are deployed.

Kelly brings more than 30 years of experience in water and wastewater operations, management, and executive leadership, including successfully leading multiple transitions from other contract operators. Recently, she oversaw the complex transition of a regional utility serving 13 communities and approximately 50 employees under a 20-year contract, navigating significant deferred maintenance and operational challenges with confidence and care. Kelly is currently leading a similarly complex transition for our client, JXN Water, as we were recently contracted to operate and manage the wastewater utilities for Jackson, MS, after taking over the water utilities in 2023.

In addition to Kelly, Cheryl Reeves brings over 30 years of experience, specializing in supporting complex operations and ensuring smooth transitions. Cheryl will be an integral part of the overall transition team leadership, bringing her extensive experience, including providing transition administration for 19 transitions at Jacobs. She excels at keeping onsite personnel organized, managing documentation, and coordinating with industry agencies to maintain compliance. Her proactive approach has been instrumental in minimizing disruptions and ensuring seamless handovers during multiple facility transitions. Cheryl's attention to detail ensures that nothing is overlooked, helping to keep all administrative duties on track. The transition team knows each other well, having worked together on four recent transitions, including our newest project in California, West Basin in El Segundo. Cheryl's expertise and familiarity with the team make her an invaluable asset during transition projects.

Together, Kelly and Cheryl form a complementary partnership that maximizes value for [CLIENT]. Kelly's executive leadership experience and transition management skills will drive systematic implementation of new processes and organizational structures, while Cheryl's administrative attention to detail will ensure our team is on track with each workstream. This dual approach allows for both immediate operational stability and long-term strategic improvements, ensuring [CLIENT] benefits from enhanced service delivery. Their combined experience provides the depth and breadth necessary to anticipate challenges, implement solutions efficiently, and deliver measurable improvements to [CLIENT]'s operations. Kelly and Cheryl will have access to a full team supporting the transition effort, as shown in Exhibit 3-4.

## Transparent Communications

Jacobs will implement a robust Communications Plan to keep City staff fully informed and aligned throughout the transition. Key elements include:

- **Integration of [FACILITY]'s alarms with the City's SCADA (Ignition v8.1) and 24/7 monitoring**, with 45-minute remote and 60-minute onsite response.
- **Weekly progress meetings**, with one meeting per month serving as the formal Monthly Operations Meeting attended by the Director and/or Manager of Operations and CPO.
- **Monthly Operations Reports** covering staffing, maintenance (CMMS work orders), CCP/LRV compliance, water quality, energy/chemical use, odor control, and corrective actions.
- **Continuous access for City personnel to real-time dashboards** and trend data.
