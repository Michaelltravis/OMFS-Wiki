# Staffing Strategy Opening and Organizational Structure

Our conversations with [CLIENT] staff, stakeholders, and union leadership over the past 2 years have given us a clear understanding of your operations and the condition of your facilities, as well as your expectations for a partnership throughout the contract. Beyond simply meeting permits and maintaining system reliability, you're seeking a partner that will lead by energizing the workforce, strengthening their sense of belonging, and advancing [CLIENT]'s long-term goals through the focused engagement of a high-performing, collaborative team. We've created our staffing approach to meet those needs. It's designed to deliver real, practical solutions to the staffing, training, cultural, and labor relations challenges you face—and to assure [CLIENT] of the leadership, workforce stability, and operational support you'll require for long-term success.

## Organizational structure

Jacobs' organizational structure, shown on **Exhibit II-2**, illustrates our staffing plan that complies with [CLIENT]'s Exhibit E and the pivotal roles we've added, which engages some of our top caliber staff to deliver transformative services and outstanding performance. Each leadership position is supported by a team to fulfill the requirements of the contract, and includes staff from within Jacobs, the current O&M employees who will transition to the Jacobs' team, our subcontractors, and our workforce development, internship, and apprenticeship programs, as presented in **Exhibit II-3**. Staff that represent positions Above and Beyond (AB) the required minimum staffing requirements are denoted on **Exhibits II-2** and **II-3** with the AB icons. **Staff resumes are included at the end of this section on page II-20**.

Transparency is a cornerstone of our approach. From staffing dashboards to key performance indicators (KPIs) and reporting on maintenance performance, we'll provide clear, consistent visibility into your operations. This openness will build trust, accelerate decision making, and make sure there are no surprises. Through decades of experience managing utility facilities, including nearly 200 current contracts, and augmented by more than 75 years in wastewater engineering, we understand the responsibility that comes with managing critical public infrastructure. **Our commitment is straightforward: deliver exceptional service from Day 1, while strengthening the workforce, systems, and culture that make [CLIENT] a nationally respected utility.** With a dedicated local Human Resources (HR) team, deep union partnerships, culture of belonging, and robust operations and maintenance (O&M) and consulting bench, we'll stabilize staffing, accelerate hiring, optimize operations, and build a workforce pipeline through apprenticeships, internships, and career pathways.

## One Water, One Team, One Vision

For Jacobs, this O&M contract is an opportunity to join [CLIENT] in promoting environmental stewardship and protecting public health. *One Water, One Team, One Vision* **(Exhibit II-1)** means we provide expertise in all facets of water, work as an extension of [CLIENT], and help progress your 2035 Vision for sustainable and resilient operations, zero overflows and basement backups, and a healthier region.

Exhibit II-1 carries the caption **COLLABORATION | COMMUNICATIONS | COORDINATION** over the three-part ONE WATER + ONE TEAM ([CLIENT] + Jacobs partnership) = 2035 VISION / ONE VISION construction.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the four-move opening (we listened → here is what you actually want from a partner → here is the structure that delivers it → here is what we will show you), the compliance-plus-above-and-beyond framing of the org chart, and the transparency-as-cornerstone paragraph. The "One Water, One Team, One Vision" equation graphic is a reusable device — substitute the client's own long-range plan as the third term.

Pursuit-specific: the 2-year listening window (only claim it if capture actually ran that long), the union-leadership reference, the RFP exhibit letter for minimum staffing, and the resume page cross-reference. The contract count and wastewater-engineering tenure must be re-verified against the registry before reuse.
