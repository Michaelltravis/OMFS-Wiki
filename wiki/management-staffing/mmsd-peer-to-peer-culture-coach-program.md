# Peer-to-Peer Culture Coach Program (Exhibit II-14)

**Exhibit II-14. Peer-to-Peer Culture Coaches.**

Our 2023 client survey confirmed that once again, workforce satisfaction and employee retention are top priorities. We introduced a peer-to-peer Culture Coach program where employee coaches facilitate discussions to strengthen relationships, deepen interpersonal trust, ensure effective communications, and sustain an atmosphere where employees feel valued, respected, included, and encouraged to bring their authentic selves. Our goal is for more than 80% of our O&M staff to have at least one in-person Culture Coaching session each year.
