# Seamless Workforce Transition — Union Engagement and Prior Transitions Map

Jacobs has a long record of successfully transitioning unionized workforces at large, complex water and wastewater utilities. Over 40 years, we've transitioned more than 11,000 union employees and worked productively with unions, including AFSCME, the Teamsters, IBEW, and the Operating Engineers. Our approach is grounded in early engagement, transparency, and respect for existing collective bargaining agreements. This foundation ensures continuity, stability, and trust throughout the transition process.

A seamless transition begins with proactive planning and experienced leadership. Recognizing the essential role union employees play in [CLIENT]'s operations, Jacobs has proactively met with union leaders and members to listen, understand their priorities, and discuss the working model that best supports their workforce. These conversations focused **on a wide variety of topics, including compensation and benefits, work-life balance, safety, training, and advancement opportunities.** These areas align with Jacobs' established employment practices and our long-standing commitment to a safe, supportive, and rewarding work environment.
