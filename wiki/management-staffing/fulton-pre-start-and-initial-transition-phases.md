# Pre-Start and Initial Transition Phases — HR Groundwork and the Badge Flip

**Pre-Start Transition (15 Days Prior to Commencement).** During this initial phase, we will focus on essential HR and administrative preparations to lay the groundwork for a successful project start. We will also commence the development of transition plans and continued engagement of key SMEs that will support the transition efforts. Key activities will include finalizing staffing plans, completing necessary documentation, and communicating with existing staff, in close coordination with the client, about the upcoming changes. This proactive approach establishes all logistical elements are in place ahead of the formal transition, allowing for a smoother start on July 1st.

**Initial Transition (30 Days Starting July 1st).** The initial transition phase will involve close collaboration between our new team and the existing staff. Our team will shadow current employees to gain insight into daily operations and best practices. This phase includes a "badge flip" process, where the majority of existing staff will officially transition to the new team under JC Solutions. This gradual integration helps preserve institutional knowledge and fosters continuity in operations during the transition.
