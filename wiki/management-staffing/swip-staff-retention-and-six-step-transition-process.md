# Seamless Transition: Staff Retention Approach and Six-Step Staff Transition Process

## Tapping the Value of Existing Staff

We believe the institutional knowledge of existing staff is a vital asset, and we are committed to retaining as many of these talented individuals as possible. At Jacobs, we recognize the invaluable contributions of the employees currently working at the facilities and will offer competitive wages and benefits designed to attract and retain them. **Our commitment to local teams is evident in seamlessly transitioning over a dozen projects in the last three years, with a 90% conversion rate for qualified employees receiving job offers in the water sector's O&M services.**

During the transition phase, Jacobs will leverage regional and technical resources to temporarily fill any unexpected vacancies, ensuring operations remain uninterrupted from day one. This proactive strategy not only addresses potential staffing gaps but also showcases the reliability and depth of Jacobs' resources, reinforcing our commitment to seamless operations for [CLIENT], a Southern California municipal water utility. Both existing and new employees will be supported with resources drawn from within Jacobs, and we will implement a **proven six-step process** (**Exhibit 3-3**) to minimize stress, maintain clear communication, and ensure a smooth workforce transition.

## Proven Approach to Supporting Existing Employees and Transition Success

In the first days of the transition, Jacobs will refine job descriptions based on a finalized needs assessment. A revised skillset list will outline the ideal experience and qualifications for each role, helping evaluate current employees, identify their best-fit positions, and highlight development or training opportunities to maximize potential.

### Exhibit 3-3. Jacobs' Staff Transition Process

**Jacobs' proven staff transition process addresses employee concerns and ensures smooth transfer of workforce.**

1. **Retain and Recruit Local Talent:** Our top priority is to retain the majority of the existing local team at [CLIENT]'s facilities. We will begin by thoroughly assessing the current staff to identify key talent and areas of expertise. By offering these individuals opportunities for continued employment and career growth with Jacobs, we aim to ensure continuity and stability. Alongside this retention effort, we will also implement a targeted recruiting process to fill any remaining gaps. This involves leveraging local job boards, professional networks, and outreach initiatives to ensure we attract qualified candidates who align with [CLIENT]'s needs and Jacobs' values.
2. **Host Introduction Workshops:** After identifying the key members of the existing local team and potential new hires, we will host introduction workshops. These workshops will provide an opportunity for the local team and new recruits to learn about Jacobs' culture, values, and operational expectations. By involving existing staff in these workshops, we foster a sense of inclusion, community, and collaboration from the outset. This ensures that everyone, both retained employees and new hires, are aligned with the project's goals.
3. **Assess and Interview Remaining Candidates:** We will conduct structured assessments and interviews for any remaining open positions or for employees interested in expanding their roles. This process allows us to evaluate each candidate's technical skills, cultural fit, and alignment with the project's long-term goals. By doing so, we ensure that those who join or remain on the team are not only qualified but also motivated to contribute to the ongoing success of [CLIENT] and Jacobs.
4. **Make Employment Offers:** For both the retained local staff and any new recruits, we will extend competitive employment offers that reflect their value to the organization. These offers will incorporate competitive salaries, comprehensive benefits, and opportunities for growth. For the existing staff, special attention will be given to recognizing their experience and contributions, which will support their smooth transition to Jacobs. This approach demonstrates our commitment to retaining talent and maintaining continuity.
5. **Implement Contract Startup and Operation:** Once employment offers are accepted, we will initiate the contract startup and operational processes. This step involves finalizing paperwork, clarifying roles and responsibilities, and ensuring compliance with all regulatory and contractual requirements. By retaining local staff who are already familiar with [CLIENT]'s operations, we will streamline the startup phase, ensuring minimal disruption and a smooth transition.
6. **Initiate Continuous Training and Development Program:** To ensure ongoing success, we will implement a continuous training and development program, designed to support both retained and newly hired staff. This program will include tailored training for specific job functions, mentorship opportunities, and ongoing professional development. By fostering a culture of learning and growth, we empower the team to adapt to new challenges, remain engaged, and continually improve performance.
