# Management Structure and Team Familiarity

[CLIENT] — a regional municipal water utility trust operating four wastewater treatment plants and a major pump station — has one of the largest wastewater facilities in the U.S. and deserves a high caliber team to ensure the success of the facility. We know the plants and understand the challenges they face, and our team brings meaningful history with your system. Project Manager **David Pitocchelli** knows these facilities firsthand from his previous work as project manager for this program, and Project Director **Steve Carpenter** brings deep local experience from his years leading the Hefner WTP. Assistant Project Manager **Alex Rodriguez** brings 13 years of hands-on wastewater and water operations experience and a personal connection to Oklahoma, where he began his career operating and maintaining community water systems. That familiarity matters. Rather than starting from scratch, we're bringing a real understanding of your system, regulations, your expectations, and the importance of getting things right from day one.

Our management team and required Schedule 10 staff for the O&M of Wastewater Facilities and Solids Management Program Services comprises highly experienced and dedicated professionals. **David**, **Alex**, **Steve**, Quality Assurance and Quality Control (QA/QC) Manager **Josh Alleman**, Maintenance Manager **Brian Daniels**, Asset Manager **Amy Dembinski**, Capital Improvement Program (CIP) Construction Manager **Tanner Pipher**, Laboratory Manager **Scott Golden**, North Canadian/Witcher Operations Manager **Tim Durham**, Chisholm Creek Operations Manager **Jose "Joe" Ramos**, Deer Creek Operations Manager **Paul Shropshire**, and South Canadian Operations Manager **Roy Aristizabal** are all committed to this program. We have also included additional key personnel, including Transition Manager **Kelly Irving** and Instrumentation and Controls (I&C) Technician **Robert Coons**.

**David** serves as your single point of accountability, supported by **Steve**, who provides senior-level, independent oversight between [CLIENT] and the project team. **Luke Lenard**, our Client Account Manager and Engineering SME Team Lead, brings over 7 years of [CLIENT] experience and Oklahoma-based delivery knowledge that will strengthen day-to-day responsiveness and long-term alignment. Together with Executive Sponsor **Kevin Dahl**, Strategic Advisor **Rick Warner**, Regional Manager **Ryan Harrold**, and Oklahoma Area Manager **Jacob Smith**, this corporate support team brings **executive oversight, engineering continuity, and Oklahoma-specific knowledge to the partnership as a built-in resource** throughout the contract. **Exhibit 3-6** shows our management structure and the corporate support team available to David, Steve, and Alex throughout the contract.

Our management team places significant emphasis on transparency, ensuring [CLIENT] has full visibility into every aspect of your operations. Every member is collaborative, communicative, and committed to acting as an extension of your team and your community. We also recognize the value of institutional knowledge in maintaining safe, reliable, and compliant operations. We look forward to having conversations with [CLIENT] to form the best team possible for long-term success. During this process, we've identified the following management and additional key personnel who provide experienced leadership and seamless transition. They'll foster a culture of accountability and performance to deliver reliable, compliant, and safe operations every day. Our organization chart, included in the **Staffing Plan** in **Section 5, Required Plans Submitted with the Proposal**, shows our approach for both on-site and off-site resources. Resumes for our key personnel follow this section.
