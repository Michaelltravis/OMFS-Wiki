# Training Topics by Staff Role (Exhibit 5-6)

| Staff role | Training topics |
|---|---|
| All staff | General health and safety awareness; safe driving techniques; fall protection; first aid and CPR; electrical safety and lockout/tagout prevention; chemical safety (hazard communication); PPE and safety-equipment use; activity hazard analysis; confined space; annual NPDES permit training; spill response; globally harmonized system (GHS); teamwork; contract synopsis (KPI); workplace violence; sexual harassment, cultural sensitivity, and ethics. |
| Jacobs University | 225 online courses on business skills, computer-desktop training, and IT topics; leadership/management, project management, and business-risk mitigation; project-management fundamentals; leading people; persuasive people speaking; Global Leadership Team Mentor Program. |
| Operations staff | Plant-specific process-control testing and monitoring; weekly process-control meetings; operations strategy; solids-handling operations and optimization; logbook and status exchange; SOP development; data management; check sheets/operator duties; NPDES permit; monitoring process KPIs by observing data trends; UPCP development; optimization plans for chemical, process, electrical, and sludge systems; site-specific odor-control system operations and monitoring. |
| Maintenance staff | CMMS/completing work orders; vibration analysis; equipment calibration; infrared thermography for electrical staff; arc flash for electrical staff; motor-current analysis for predictive maintenance; inventory control; pump repairs and PM tasks; reliability assessment. |
| Selected staff | Health and safety management for managers; supervisor and management training. |
