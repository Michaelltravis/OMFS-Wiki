# Mobilization Overview, Governance, and Communications

## Mobilization overview

Jacobs will implement a structured 200-point transition and mobilization plan immediately after contract award and concluding on March 1, 2028. This plan ensures all staffing, systems, governance, and readiness activities are completed before operations commence. The approach aligns with Exhibit E requirements and is anchored in Jacobs' steps to setup a PgM framework for success, provided in **Exhibit IV-8** in ***Section IV.A.1.2.1***. The detailed plan will be finalized jointly with [CLIENT] at contract award.

## Transition governance and communication

Jacobs will establish a three-tiered transition governance structure aligned with Exhibit P, including Executive, Operations, and Maintenance Committees. These committees will begin meeting within 10 business days of contract execution to guide implementation, ensure alignment, and maintain consistent communication between [CLIENT] and Jacobs leadership.

### Transition communications plan

Jacobs will implement a transition communications plan to provide transparency, clear expectations, and consistent engagement throughout the pre-term period. The plan defines stakeholder roles, meeting cadence, knowledge transfer protocols, documentation standards, and progress reporting. Key elements include:

- Defined stakeholders and points of contact
- Weekly progress meetings and bi-weekly governance reviews
- Structured knowledge transfer and documentation protocols
- A shared digital workspace for deliverables and records
- Dashboard reporting on schedule, staffing, and risk mitigation.

## Technology and CMMS integration

Jacobs recognizes CMMS integration as a critical pre-term activity. Our approach is described in detail in ***Section IV.C, CMMS Approach***.

## Integrated collection and treatment system modeling and system readiness

During the transition period, Jacobs will collaborate with [CLIENT] to evaluate the operation of the inline storage system and water reclamation facilities as an integrated system, particularly under increasingly intense rainfall conditions. Coordination with Storm Harvester will support understanding of system behavior before, during, and after significant storm events. Additional details are provided in ***Section IV.A, 2.2.1 Collection System, Tunnels, and Wet Weather Flow Management Focus Area***.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-tier governance structure with a start-meeting deadline measured in business days, the five-element communications plan, and the practice of treating CMMS integration and integrated wet-weather modeling as named pre-term workstreams rather than post-award surprises. Pursuit-specific: contract exhibit citations, committee names, the modeling partner, and whether the client operates deep tunnel or inline storage.
