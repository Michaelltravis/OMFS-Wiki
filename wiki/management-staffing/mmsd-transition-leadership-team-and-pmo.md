# Transition Leadership Team Profiles and Program Management Office Role

Jacobs' Transition Leadership Team (**Exhibit IV-57**) offers deep experience managing complex O&M transitions. Working as a unit, they'll ensure the workforce, systems, and operations are fully prepared from Day 1 through strong collaboration, proactive communication, and disciplined, detail-driven execution.

## Kelly Irving — Transition Manager

Kelly has more than 30 years of experience in water and wastewater operations, management, and executive leadership. She has led multiple transitions across both treatment and conveyance systems. Most recently, Kelly managed the transition of the City of Jackson, MS's wastewater operations (a Veolia transition), where 31 of 34 employees joined Jacobs and remaining vacancies were quickly filled, all while maintaining uninterrupted service and compliance. Her deep experience with startup planning, workforce integration, and organizational change will support a smooth, people-centered transition for [CLIENT].

## Mike Boven — Transition Deputy Manager

Mike is one of Jacobs' most experienced transition specialists, recognized for his hands-on approach to field implementation, technical coordination, and logistics. His background spans operations, maintenance, asset management, safety, SCADA, sludge management, odor control, and workforce development. He recently led the West Basin Municipal Water District transition (a Veolia transition), delivering a seamless badge flip for 49 employees while maintaining full operational and safety compliance. Mike's practical field-focused leadership will help ensure [CLIENT]'s transition is orderly, safe, and aligned with both field operations and project objectives.

## Kevin Dahl, CMRT, CRL — Project Manager

Kevin has more than 26 years of experience in water and wastewater operations and management. As Project Manager and a core member of the Transition Leadership Team, he'll work closely with [CLIENT] well before the start date to align staffing, operational strategies, and knowledge transfer with [CLIENT]'s goals. His background spans complex utility operations and operability reviews that enhance efficiency and reduce risk. Kevin led the transition of the City of Wilmington, DE's 168 MGD wastewater system (a Veolia transition) and has supported dozens of other major projects across the country. Kevin's collaborative and transparent leadership will help ensure an organized and effective transition for [CLIENT].

## Program Management Office (PMO) role

Supporting the Transition Leadership Team, Jacobs' PMO will provide oversight, structure, and coordination needed to manage all transition workstreams. The PMO will track deliverables, manage schedules, monitor risks, and ensure seamless integration between staffing, systems, training, and governance activities. It will maintain the master transition schedule, facilitate reporting dashboards, and prepare weekly updates and performance summaries for [CLIENT] and the Transition Leadership Team.

By centralizing progress tracking and documentation, the PMO ensures full transparency, disciplined execution, and seamless alignment with [CLIENT]'s governance framework and communication expectations.
