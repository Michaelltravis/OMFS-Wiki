# Compensation Package and Employee Satisfaction after Transition

## Compensation package

JC Solutions will offer employment to all interested and qualified current employees who would like to continue working at [CLIENT]'s facilities. [CLIENT] is a Southeast US county utility operating three membrane bioreactor water reclamation facilities and 33 pump stations. We recognize the importance of competitive wages and benefits to attract and retain quality staff. We have performed a regional salary analysis and will use that information to offer an improved pay rate scale for positions. **We intend to provide a comprehensive compensation package that provides for competitive recruiting and improved staff retention which has been tailored to the specific industry and geography.**

## Employee satisfaction

Jacobs regularly surveys staff members who have joined our firm after a transition. They are uniformly more satisfied with their work environment at Jacobs than their previous employer, as demonstrated in **Exhibit 2-45**.

We firmly believe that our commitment to our people and our intentional efforts to listen and act on their feedback has allowed us to develop a unique approach to attracting, retaining, and energizing our teams.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the pairing is what makes this work — a concrete compensation commitment immediately followed by third-party-style evidence (the post-transition satisfaction survey) that people who made this move were glad they did. Use the pairing, not either half alone.

Pursuit-specific: the regional salary analysis, the benefits summary, and the exhibit's survey vintage. Where an incumbent operator or civil-service pay scale sets the floor, say how the offer compares.
