# Standard Employee Benefits — Union CBA Alignment and Non-Union Package

Our comprehensive benefits program supports recruiting, retention, and wellbeing for union and non-union employees.

**Union employees — providing affordable benefits, respect for the agreement, clear communications commitment.** We'll honor the applicable CBAs for wages, hours, and benefits and engage union leaders through regular labor–management check-ins to maintain clarity on eligibility, enrollment, and leave policies. Typical benefits found in CBAs for our other projects and to be confirmed with [CLIENT] and the unions include:

- Medical/prescription, dental, and vision plan(s) and associated employee cost share
- Retirement programs with any applicable employer matching contributions
- Paid time off/holidays/leave provisions; short-/long-term disability, life, and/or accidental death and dismemberment insurance; leaves of absence language and FMLA coordination
- Any union-specific allowances or differentials
- Apprenticeship/education benefits referenced in the CBA

At Jacobs, our culture of caring and inclusion is foundational to who we are, and we support employees to be their best selves—physically, emotionally, financially, and socially. Our benefits program reflects this. It allows employees to make informed decisions and shape personal benefits, with options for carriers and coverages to suit individual needs. **To maximize affordability, Jacobs discounts health care premiums offered to our union employees and pays 80% of the cost of premiums.**

**Standard benefits for non-union employees.** For non-union employees, Jacobs will provide its standard employee benefits package:

- Medical, dental, and vision plan options with a range of employer/employee contributions
- 401(k) retirement plan and employer match after vesting
- Paid time off/holidays; short-/long-term disability, life, and/or accidental death and dismemberment insurance
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP) and well-being resources
- Licensing/education reimbursement based on identified limits and eligibility

**Well-being, safety, and leave — helping employees manage stress.** Jacobs' EAP and other mental health resources help employees with work-life balance and stress management. Onboarding materials explain leave policies and wellness resources for union and non-union staff.

**Enrollment, employee communication, and reporting — make it easy, keep it transparent.** We'll conduct benefits open-house sessions (timed around shifts and with an offsite option), distribute benefits FAQs, and provide individual enrollment support starting at transition. We'll update information at least annually. HR will track enrollment status and eligibility in our HRIS and include benefits participation and certification-bonus utilization in governance reporting, so [CLIENT] sees how benefits support retention and readiness.

**Benefits to [CLIENT]: CBA-aligned benefits and clear communication assure continuity and respect for union staff. Competitive, easy-to-understand benefits for non-union and union staff improve recruiting and retention. Covering exam fees and providing certification/degree bonuses will grow workforce skills aligned to [CLIENT]'s goals. Benefits and recognition programs are documented in HR dashboards and reportable to [CLIENT].**
