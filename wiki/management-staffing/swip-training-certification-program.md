# Training and Certification Program for O&M Personnel (AWTO-Focused, Incentive-Driven)

Jacobs will invest time and resources to provide comprehensive technical and practical training for the project team. This program is designed as a continuous learning environment that encourages staff to advance their skills and knowledge, ensuring top performance and regulatory compliance. A strong training program also reflects Jacobs' commitment to being the best possible steward of the client's facilities.

## Employee training

Jacobs will invest thousands of hours per year in an array of employee development, trainings, and incentives to improve quality and reliability of work by advancing the skills of its staff. Jacobs will provide the client with formal training plans, protocols, and associated schedule as part of the transition plan. Once the designated employee transition is complete and ongoing roles are established, Jacobs will finalize an ongoing staff training program. It provides continuous learning that supports staff in attaining certifications and credentials, empowering each with the skills and knowledge to help advance their careers and deliver top performance.

**Individual Training Needs Assessment:** Jacobs assesses each employee's training needs and develops an individualized plan based on their position, experience, and regulatory requirements. Training is delivered by experienced site staff with support from Jacobs' national O&M training resources and subject matter experts in potable reuse, advanced treatment, and regulatory compliance.

**Corporate training scale:** Jacobs operates one of the most comprehensive training and certification programs in the industry, supporting more than 300 O&M sites across North America. A centerpiece of staff development is building the largest bench of Advanced Water Treatment certified operators in California, a credential that has become increasingly vital as utilities expand into water reuse, indirect potable reuse, and direct potable reuse. Jacobs currently has 12 California AWTO certified employees, with several more on track, demonstrating a commitment to expanding this expertise and ensuring depth of coverage.

**Recognition:** Jacobs ranks high in employee satisfaction in a study by Newsweek and is the only O&M firm on the Top 100 "America's Most Loved Workplaces" list. Jacobs' commitment to investing in its employees through training and career development opportunities regularly earns global recognition as a highly desirable place to work.

## Structured training programs and compliance alignment

Every team member will receive training aligned with the State Water Resources Control Board, California Title 22, and AWTO certification requirements. Jacobs' programs encompass safety, ethics, operations and maintenance procedures, quality management, and certification preparation. Individualized and site-specific training is delivered as needed. To ensure regulatory awareness, Jacobs delivers targeted compliance training addressing permit responsibilities, reporting obligations, and the legal implications of violations under the Clean Water Act.

### Typical Jacobs O&M training topics (representative)

**Operations Staff:** Plant-specific process control testing and monitoring; check sheets/operator duties; weekly process control meetings; NPDES permit; operations strategy; monitoring process KPIs by observing data trends; solids handling operations and optimization; UPCP development; logbook and status exchange; optimization plan (chemical, process, electrical, sludge); SOP development; site-specific odor control system operations and monitoring; data management.

**Maintenance Staff:** CMMS/completing work orders; motor current analysis — predictive maintenance; vibration analysis; inventory control; equipment calibration; pump repairs and PM tasks; infrared thermography for electrical staff; reliability assessment; arc flash for electrical staff.

**All Staff:** General health and safety awareness; confined space; Smith safe driving; annual NPDES permit training; fall protection; spill response; first aid & CPR; globally harmonized system (GHS); OSHA 10-hour; crane safety, fire prevention; electrical safety and lockout/tagout prevention; teamwork; chemical safety (hazard communication); contract synopsis (KPI); PPE and safety equipment use; workplace violence; job safety analysis (JSA); sexual harassment, cultural sensitivity, and ethics.

**Selected Staff:** Health & safety management for managers; supervisor and management training.

**Jacobs University:** 225 online courses on business skills, computer desktop training, and IT topics; leadership/management, project management, and business risk mitigation; project management fundamentals; leading people; persuasive people speaking; global leadership team mentor program.

## Learning platforms and tracking

Jacobs' eLearning system, accessible to all employees, serves both as a knowledge platform and a centralized database for tracking completions and generating automated reminders for annual, safety, and regulatory refreshers. Jacobs' interactive operator training library ("Jacobs Operator") consists of 36 instructional volumes with interactive quizzes, accessible via a searchable module shelf with sample modules such as "Operator Math 101" and "Operator Math 201," each reachable via QR code for real-time, on-the-go access.

## Historical training investment

Jacobs' total compliance and regulatory training hours provided to O&M staff annually (a 20-year corporate trend): 1,875 hours (2005); 1,078 (2006); 2,989 (2007); 2,937 (2008); 2,965 (2009); 4,977 (2010); 4,455 (2011); 5,642 (2012); 9,196 (2013); 4,628 (2014); 4,787 (2015); 9,034 (2016); 10,285 (2017); 6,502 (2018); 8,155 (2019); 9,433 (2020); 12,641 (2021); 11,588 (2022); 9,185 (2023); 8,963 (2024).

## Operator certification incentives

Jacobs provides significant incentives and rewards for advanced certifications to elevate operations employees' skills:
- Covering the cost of training, preparation, and exam fees
- Potential promotions for operators-in-training (OITs) that complete the OIT period of six to 12 months and get their first operations or maintenance certification
- Providing a range of incentives for AWTO certifications, including covering costs and offering opportunities for career advancement

## Maintenance certification programs

Jacobs will help staff reach the next level and reinforce using the best practices that maximize asset life by driving certifications, including:
- **Certified Maintenance Reliability Technician (CMRT)** program, provided by the Society of Maintenance and Reliability Professionals (SMRP)
- **Certified Reliability Leader (CRL)** program, provided by the Association of Asset Management Professionals
- Potential bonuses for advancement in those programs for employees that complete next-level certifications

Jacobs employs more CMRT-certified maintenance staff than any other US water/wastewater service provider.

## Career development and advancement

Jacobs believes career development is essential to staff retention and building a culture of excellence. Jacobs' programs go beyond technical training to provide operators and maintenance staff with long-term growth opportunities.

- **Water Operator Certification and Renewal Training** — Through Jacobs' Institute of Learning, Jacobs has developed wastewater operations content aligned to the Association of Boards of Certification (ABC) "Need to Know" standards. Courses include classroom, field, and online instruction, supported by quizzes and practical demonstrations.
- **Maintenance Training** — Opportunities include Certified Maintenance Reliability Technician (CMRT) exam prep, hands-on instruction in predictive maintenance, training in vibration analysis, CMMS best practices, ultrasound, and troubleshooting. Jacobs employs more CMRT-certified maintenance staff than any other US water/wastewater service provider.
- **Broad Career Development Opportunities** — Employees benefit from leadership development programs, university partnerships, cross-training between plant and collections systems, succession planning, and structured mentoring.

Jacobs also encourages continuous learning and advancement through tuition and exam fee reimbursement, along with its **Certification Incentive Program**, which provides bonuses and salary adjustments tied to certification milestones. Recognizing the importance of the Advanced Water Treatment (AWT) Certification in the water reuse market, Jacobs supports staff in pursuing this credential. Jacobs currently has twelve California AWTO certified employees, with several more on track, and offers incentives at each stage of the certification process.

## Coordinate with the client

Jacobs will submit an Annual Staffing and Training Plan to the client outlining objectives, schedules, and planned content. Training activities will be coordinated with the client's staff and facilities schedule, and client personnel will be invited to participate in appropriate training modules.

## Peer-to-peer culture coaches

From Jacobs' 2023 client survey, Jacobs understands that workforce satisfaction and employee retention is a top priority. As a result, Jacobs is upping its game with a peer-to-peer Culture Coach program that Jacobs thinks of as "preventive maintenance for people." Employee coaches facilitate discussions to strengthen relationships, deepen interpersonal trust, ensure effective communications, and sustain an atmosphere where employees feel valued, respected, included, and encouraged to bring their authentic selves. Jacobs' goal is for more than 80% of its OM&M staff to have at least one in-person Culture Coaching session each year.
