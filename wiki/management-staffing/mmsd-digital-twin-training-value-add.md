# Digital Twin Operations as a Training Value-Add

## 4. Value-Add: Digital Innovation in Training — Digital Twin Operations

Jacobs can offer [CLIENT] an optional enhancement through our proprietary digital twin operations platform—a value-added innovation designed to optimize operations. If desired by [CLIENT], it would provide these key benefits:

- **Realistic Process Understanding** – Operators gain a clear, visual view of system dynamics and facility behavior under different flow and load conditions.

- **Optimization and Cost Reduction** – By simulating biological nutrient removal, chemical dosing, and energy consumption, the digital twin identifies efficiency gains and potential cost savings.

- **Immersive Simulation Training** – Connected to a training HMI interface, our digital tools enable operators to rehearse startup, shutdown, and emergency response scenarios in a fully controlled, risk-free setting.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-benefit structure (process understanding, optimization/cost reduction, immersive simulation training) and the "optional enhancement / if desired by the client" framing that keeps the offer from reading as scope creep.

Pursuit-specific: the processes named in the optimization bullet, and whether the training HMI is connected to the client's own SCADA/HMI or to a standalone training instance.
