# Exhibit 1-7. Communications and Reporting Framework

| When | What | Content / Purpose | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Daily** | Meetings & Discussions | Open exchange of information to ensure transparency and collaboration | [CLIENT] Designee / Jacobs PM and Plant Managers |
| **Daily** | Daily Operations Log | Daily documentation of facility operations, flows, process conditions, and any incidents or abnormal events | Jacobs Operations Staff |
| **Daily** | Odor Monitoring Records | Fence-line odor surveys and H₂S concentration readings recorded every 12 hours at the North Canadian influent (per the Odor Control Plan) | Jacobs Operations Staff |
| **Weekly** | Weekly Status Meetings | Review of previous week's operations, maintenance activity, upcoming scheduled work, open issues, CIP coordination needs, and action items | [CLIENT] Designee / Jacobs PM |
| **Weekly** | Weekly Report | Summary of production, compliance status, shutdowns, maintenance activities, and key events | [CLIENT] / Jacobs PM |
| **Monthly** | Monthly Review Meeting | Progress updates, performance review, CIP coordination, open issues, and continuous improvement discussions | [CLIENT] GM or Designee / Jacobs PM, Project Director |
| **Monthly** | Monthly Management, Operations, and Maintenance Report | Per schedule: staffing roster, safety incidents, facility inspections, daily flows/quality data, regulatory excursions with root-cause analysis, treatment process rotation schedule, biosolids production/land application data, odor chemical usage, fence-line survey results, complaint log, PdM/PM/CM task completion summaries, equipment out-of-service list with repair status, equipment rotation schedule run hours | Jacobs PM / QA/QC Manager |
| **Monthly** | NPDES Monthly DMR | Completed and signed DMR for each facility, submitted to [CLIENT] GM or designee at least 3 business days before the regulatory due date (MGT-1) | Jacobs Lab Manager / QA/QC Manager |
| **Monthly** | Monthly Payment Request | Self-reported compliance with the Performance Requirements, plus supporting financial documentation | Jacobs PM / Project Director |
| **Monthly** | M&R Fund Expenditure Report | Itemized purchases by facility, equipment/work order allocation, fund balance and projected expenditures for remainder of year | Jacobs PM / Administrative Specialist |
| **Monthly** | R&R Fund Expenditure Report | Itemized qualifying purchases by facility, equipment/work order allocation, fund balance and projected expenditures | Jacobs PM / Administrative Specialist |
| **Quarterly** | Quarterly Business Review | Review of quarterly performance trends, budget status, staffing adequacy, upcoming CIP impacts, continuous improvement initiatives, and strategic alignment | [CLIENT] Leadership / Jacobs Project Director, PM, Regional Management |
| **As needed** | Exception Reports | Reports of incidents, activities, or items relevant to contract execution, with root cause and corrective action documentation | Jacobs PM / QA/QC Manager |
| **Annually** | Annual Innovation & Performance Workshop ★ | Review prior year's accomplishments, set continuous improvement goals, discuss emerging technologies and optimization opportunities. Includes applicable Jacobs SMEs. | [CLIENT] Leadership / Jacobs Project Director, PM, Regional Management, SMEs |
| **Annually** | Annual Management, O&M Summary | Summarizes operations over the previous year, expected changes in the upcoming year, recommended modifications/improvements, anticipated maintenance procedure changes, CIP integration updates. Due October 1. | Jacobs PM / Project Director |
| **Annually** | Cost of Service Report | Financial information to support [CLIENT]'s cost-of-service documentation for expanding services to other communities. Due October 1. | Jacobs PM / Administrative Specialist |
| **Annually** | Annual R&R Report | Identifies, prioritizes, and plans major rehabilitation and replacement of assets. Includes proposed budget. Subject to GM approval. Due October 1. | Jacobs PM / Maintenance Manager |
| **Annually** | Annual M&R Report | Details plan for routine PM, CM, repairs, and replacements to maintain reliable day-to-day operations. Due October 1. | Jacobs PM / Maintenance Manager |
| **Annually** | Annual Quality Audit ★ | Jacobs' internal QA/QC audit of project performance across 70 topics (see the QA/QC Program section). Results shared with [CLIENT]. | Jacobs QA/QC Audit Team / Project Director |

★ Above and beyond the RFP's requirements.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the four-column When/What/Content/Who structure, the daily-to-annual ordering, and the ★ convention that separates contractual deliverables from volunteered ones. The two starred rows — the Annual Innovation & Performance Workshop and sharing the internal Annual Quality Audit results with the client — are the reusable no-cost value adds.

Pursuit-specific: every frequency, due date, schedule citation, and the odor-monitoring row, which exists because this pursuit had a specific odor requirement at a named influent point.

Pairs with [Reporting and Communication — The "No Surprises" Zippering Model](ocwut-reporting-and-communication-framework.md), which is the required lead-in, and with [Annual Innovation Workshop](ocwut-annual-innovation-workshop-value-add.md) and [QA/QC Program](ocwut-qaqc-program-oversight-and-annual-quality-audit.md), which detail the two starred rows.
