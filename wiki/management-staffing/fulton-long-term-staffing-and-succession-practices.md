# Long-Term Staffing and Succession Practices — Pipeline, Military Recruiting, and Incentives

**Long-Term Staffing/Succession Planning Practices.** ***Building a strong culture is Step 1.*** Effectively recruiting and proactively growing and retaining quality staff — while building future leaders — is critical to sustained O&M excellence. We have a plan for that!

Jacobs has created a Resource Planning Group focused on attracting, developing, and retaining high-performing O&M staff. Their efforts have **doubled the number of new Jacobs' O&M employees over the past year, lowered our turnover numbers, and focused on the promotion of our high-potential staff.** As a result, we have a proven approach to retaining our staff over time.

***We have already begun recruiting efforts for this important project.*** Internally we promote these opportunities via a range of internal communications, including virtual tools and onsite efforts. Externally, we use our national recruiting resources and will continue these practices in the future to fill vacant positions. Our efforts include:

**Partnering with local high schools, community colleges, and technical schools** such as Atlanta Metropolitan State College and ATC to create a pipeline of talent and offer intern and apprentice programs, as well as water and wastewater coursework to prepare students for the Operator Certification Exam and a career in the industry.

**At Ease, Jacobs' Military Recruitment Program.** The Jacobs' at Ease program partners with Hiring our Heroes and the US Department of Defense Skillsbridge programs for those nearing completion of military service, veterans, or their spouses with on-the-job experience over a 90–180 day period. They gain familiarity with O&M, Jacobs' systems and culture, and position competencies before officially joining the Jacobs workforce. This great new pool of talent for JC Solutions also benefits the men and women who have served our country.

**Recruiting tools best suited to O&M-focused candidates.** We focus on platforms such as Indeed and Zip Recruiters that work on smartphones instead of requiring a computer.

***Incentive and Recognition Programs.*** Jacobs provides a wide range of incentive and recognition programs to employees related to **job performance, safety-related behaviors, and exceptional team efforts.** Jacobs also provides **powerful rewards and recognitions to employees who obtain advanced certifications** in an operations, maintenance, or related technical field. Jacobs **helps employees cover the cost of training**, preparation and practice materials, and examination fees so they can concentrate on learning and developing, and provides **promotions and cash bonuses up to $1,000 for employees who pass certifications or earn an advanced degree** in a field related to their position from an accredited college or university degree program. JC Solutions can provide annual tuition reimbursement to help employees.
