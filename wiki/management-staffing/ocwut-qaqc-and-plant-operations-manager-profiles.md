# QA/QC Manager and Plant Operations Manager Profiles

## Josh Alleman, QA/QC Manager

**Josh** will support the QA/QC framework that keeps [CLIENT]'s operations, compliance, and reporting accurate, complete, and on time. An Oklahoma native with 22 years of water and wastewater experience, he brings **ODEQ coordination experience and a background spanning environmental sampling, DMR review, NPDES permit development, CMMS-driven maintenance oversight, and budget and contract performance**. As Project Manager at the City of Berryville, he conducted annual **internal audits, participated in ADEQ inspections, reviewed daily logs and lab work** for data accuracy, validated sampling results, and reviewed monthly and annual DMRs before submission. As Maintenance Supervisor at the City of Fayetteville, he aligned the CMMS with contract requirements, used **predictive technology to drive CIP decisions, and supported SSO sampling and state regulatory reporting**. As former owner of RMS Environmental, he managed a 10-year groundwater monitoring contract with Chesapeake and Devon Energy, **working directly with ODEQ on water permitting, data validation, and contamination remediation**. Josh will apply that same discipline to [CLIENT] — tracking performance metrics, identifying issues early, and giving the management team the verified, defensible information they need to manage compliance and contract requirements with confidence.

*"I help build quality into the work we do every day — not as an afterthought, but as part of how the team operates. That means clear checks, consistent follow-through, and accountability. [CLIENT] has set clear expectations and is looking for a partner who brings structure and transparency, which is exactly where strong QA/QC makes the biggest impact."*

## Tim Durham, Operations Manager — North Canadian/Witcher

**Tim** will lead operations at **North Canadian and Witcher**, overseeing process performance, compliance, and day-to-day coordination across the plant and pump station. Tim has **31 years of water and wastewater utility experience**, including large treatment systems, pump stations, capital improvements, and utility teams. As Project Director for the **City of Jackson, he leads more than 60 personnel and manages a $31M budget in a high-profile operating environment.** As Water Resources Director for Oconee County, he led wastewater plant expansions, distribution and collection operations, and long-term capital planning. As Assistant City Manager for Clarkesville, he oversaw water, wastewater, distribution, collection operations, and major public works projects. Tim brings experienced operational leadership, strong team coordination, and a practical understanding of how day-to-day plant operations connect to long-term reliability and capital needs. His experience will help keep North Canadian and Witcher operating reliably while supporting future upgrades and sustained performance.

*"Our experience in Jackson shows that investing in people is just as critical as investing in infrastructure — empowering our team to enhance system reliability, strengthen asset protection, and deliver the performance needed to rebuild public trust and ensure long-term operational resilience."*

## Jose "Joe" Ramos, Operations Manager — Chisholm Creek

**Joe** will lead operations at **Chisholm Creek**, overseeing day-to-day operations, compliance performance, and operator coordination. He has **30+ years of wastewater treatment and utility management experience**, including 25 in direct supervision, spanning treatment plants, lift station networks, municipal utilities, and full O&M contract delivery. He's **led plant startups and commissioning, mentored operations staff, developed process controls, and stepped in during emergency situations to restore compliance** and operational stability. In Oklahoma, he supported the **Bixby Water Reclamation Facility** through SBR commissioning and startup stabilization and stepped into **Duncan** during an emergency compliance situation restoring stable operations. As PM in **Gallup** and **Donna**, he was **fully accountable for contract delivery, safety, compliance, and daily operations** across water and wastewater plants, lift stations, and distribution and collection systems. Joe's Oklahoma experience, Class A operator license, and track record of hands-on compliance recovery and operator development make him the right leader to keep Chisholm Creek running reliably from day one.

*"I help teams turn good data into good decisions. Chisholm Creek is the type of facility where focused process control support and collaboration with operators can quickly improve day-to-day performance. I'm excited to contribute my operational experience to support [CLIENT]'s goals."*

## Reuse guidance

Universal: assigning a named operations manager to each plant (rather than one operations manager over the portfolio) and closing each bio with a "makes him the right leader to keep [plant] running reliably from day one" sentence. For the QA/QC role, the reusable structure is prior employer, then what he actually audited or reviewed there, then what he will do here.

Pursuit-specific: names, years, plant assignments, and the state regulator (ODEQ/ADEQ). Reference clients named here (Berryville, Fayetteville, Chesapeake and Devon Energy, Jackson, Oconee County, Clarkesville, Bixby, Duncan, Gallup, Donna) are kept verbatim and should be QC'd by the proposal team before external use.
