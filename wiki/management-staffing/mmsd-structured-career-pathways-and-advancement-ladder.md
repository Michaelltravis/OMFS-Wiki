# Structured Career Pathways and the Operator Advancement Ladder

#### 1.1. Structured Career Pathways Promote Advancement Opportunities

A structured pathway makes sure that every employee has a clear roadmap for growth, supported by targeted training and measurable milestones. It strengthens retention, builds institutional knowledge, and creates a future-ready workforce capable of meeting evolving regulatory and technological demands.

As part of our training plans, we'll offer structured career pathways that begin with entry-level roles and progress through licensed operator positions, specialized maintenance roles, and supervisory leadership positions. Our focus will be on attracting new employees and maintaining and expanding the skillset of the existing staff. We'll leverage our existing partnerships with local colleges, high schools, and other regional career-building programs, such as MATC, University of Wisconsin–Milwaukee (UWM), and Marquette. As part of our goals for employee advancement, we'll specify measurable promotion criteria, defined competencies, and certification milestones, all under the guidance and supervision of a seasoned manager and/or mentor. **Exhibit II-18** illustrates the "ladder" of advancement available to an entry-level employee interested in becoming a wastewater operator.

By integrating formal education, on-the-job experience, and performance-based incentives, this career pathway creates a transparent, motivating structure that benefits both employees and [CLIENT], which delivers operational resilience and long-term success.

**Exhibit II-18. Structured career pathways.**

| Level | What the role masters |
|---|---|
| Entry-Level Operator | Basic plant operations and safety protocols; standard operating procedures (SOPs), compliance; training in core wastewater treatment processes |
| Operator | Monitoring and adjusting processes; routine troubleshooting and repairs; completion of state-approved coursework |
| Senior/Lead Operator | Advanced treatment technologies; leadership in shift operations |
| Supervisory Operator | Regulatory compliance assurance; leading strategic initiatives |

_Each level has identified certification requirements where appropriate._
