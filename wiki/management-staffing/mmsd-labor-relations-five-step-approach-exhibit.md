# Five-Step Approach to Labor Relations (Exhibit II-7)

**EXHIBIT II-7. 5-STEP APPROACH TO LABOR RELATIONS**

| Step | What we do | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Understanding** | Plan our level of engagement with the union based on [CLIENT] needs. Spend time with the building trades, tour their training facilities, and learn about their apprentice programs. | COMPLETE |
| **CBA Review** | Identify adjustments to CBA to reflect new contract and operations. | COMPLETE |
| **CBA Review with Union** | Review current CBA to identify areas that require negotiation to accommodate Jacobs processes and benefits and [CLIENT]'s new contract. | COMPLETE |
| **New CBA by Day 1** | We've elected to voluntarily recognize the unions in support of [CLIENT] and expect to engage them in collective bargaining with enough timing to have a new CBA in place on Day 1 of project startup. | IN PROGRESS |
| **Leverage Union Partnership** | Support union efforts to recruit and attract journey-persons and enroll apprentices to staff positions. | — |
