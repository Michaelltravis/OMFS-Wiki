# Staffing Approach — Right-Sized Coverage and Welcoming Incumbent Staff

## Staffing Plan

Jacobs will staff [CLIENT]'s advanced water treatment and reuse facilities with the right balance of skill, experience, and coverage to ensure operations are not only in compliance but consistently meet the highest standards of safety, reliability, and performance. We will take into account the unique needs of each facility, the current condition of assets, and [CLIENT]'s operational objectives to ensure our staffing plan aligns with your priorities.

We anticipate that many existing operations and maintenance personnel may wish to continue serving [CLIENT]. Jacobs will welcome qualified incumbent staff into our team, recognizing the value of their facility knowledge and dedication. Our experience shows that transitioned employees thrive with Jacobs, benefiting from our collaborative culture, robust safety program, and comprehensive training opportunities.

Our proposed staffing and shift schedule will deliver:

1. The right number of staff per shift to complete work proactively
2. Team capacity to assist one another on complex or urgent tasks
3. No lone shift operations, ensuring both safety and operational resilience

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-part sizing rationale (unique needs of each facility, current condition of assets, client operational objectives), the incumbent-staff welcome, and the three staffing-delivery principles are reusable verbatim for any O&M staffing plan — they are especially strong in an incumbent-displacement pursuit, where existing plant staff are an audience for the proposal as well as a resource.

Pursuit-specific: confirm the intent to interview and hire incumbent personnel with the operations lead before making the commitment in writing, and check whether the RFP or a labor agreement sets terms for transitioned employees.
