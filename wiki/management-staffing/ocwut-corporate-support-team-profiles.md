## Reuse guidance

Universal: the five-role corporate stack itself — an executive sponsor with P&L-scale credentials, a strategic advisor with national-body standing, a client account manager who already knows the client, a regional manager who owns the bench, and an area manager who lives in the state. Also universal: showing "years (years with Jacobs)" so tenure and firm loyalty are visible in one number pair, and writing every bullet as a client benefit rather than a duty.

Pursuit-specific: names, license numbers and states, the state regulator, the prior-relationship claims, and the named reference projects (Jackson, Waterbury, The Villages, Bixby). Verify the "7+ years supporting [CLIENT]" claim against the actual account history for each new pursuit.
