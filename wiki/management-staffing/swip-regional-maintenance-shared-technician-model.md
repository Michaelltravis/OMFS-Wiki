# Regional Maintenance Team — Shared, Locally Embedded Technician Model

## Proven, Innovative Regional Maintenance Team Approach

Unlike other O&M firms that claim regional support but rely on borrowing staff from nearby projects, Jacobs maintains a standalone team of highly skilled regional maintenance professionals who directly support Jacobs' California operations. This mobile team will provide immediate, flexible support to address any of the facility's current or future maintenance needs as illustrated in **Exhibit 3-1**. If desired by [CLIENT], they will also assist with capital improvement project execution. This model has proven successful in addressing evolving maintenance requirements at advanced treatment facilities and will provide [CLIENT] with the reliability and responsiveness it expects from a long-term partner.

## Expanding the Model — Two New Locally Based, Shared-Time Positions

To further strengthen this model, Jacobs will expand our Regional Maintenance Team with two new Los Angeles–based positions — one dedicated I&C Technician and one dedicated Maintenance Technician — whose time will be shared between [CLIENT] and other local projects in the area. These new roles are in addition to the full-time Maintenance and I&C staff already assigned to the facility, giving [CLIENT] access to additional expertise without bearing the full-time cost of staffing them exclusively at the facility. By embedding these resources locally, Jacobs will eliminate mobilization delays, reduce travel expenses, and enable rapid response to both routine and urgent needs. This flexible deployment strategy provides [CLIENT] with the cost efficiencies of "on-demand" specialized support, while ensuring continuity, resilience, and direct access to technicians who are familiar with the client's systems, assets, and operational priorities.

## Visual Concept — Shared Dedicated Technician Venn Diagram

Two overlapping circles — "[CLIENT facility]" and "Other Local Area Projects" — with the overlap labeled "Shared Dedicated Technician: 1 I&C Technician, 1 Maintenance Technician." This simple visual communicates that the two new hires are a genuinely shared resource pool, not staff pulled away from other clients on an ad hoc basis.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the "standalone dedicated regional team, not borrowed staff" competitive positioning, the shared-technician cost/coverage mechanism (new locally based hires split between the pursuit facility and other local projects), and the Venn-diagram visual concept are all reusable for pursuits where a fully dedicated specialist isn't justified by facility size but rapid local response is still a client priority.

Pursuit-specific: only make the "standalone vs. borrowed staff" competitive claim where Jacobs' actual regional team structure supports it for the target region.
