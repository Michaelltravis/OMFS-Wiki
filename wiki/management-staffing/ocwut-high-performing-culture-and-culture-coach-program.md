# Building a High-Performing Culture

We won't just fill positions — we'll build a cohesive, high-performing team. At Jacobs, culture isn't a slogan; it will be the foundation that will drive safety, retention, operational performance, and client satisfaction. **Our leadership team has a proven track record of creating workplace environments where employees feel valued, supported, and motivated to deliver their best.** This will directly benefit [CLIENT] — a regional municipal water utility trust operating four wastewater treatment plants and a major pump station — with lower turnover, stronger compliance performance, and a workforce that takes ownership of results. Our approach to culture at the [CLIENT] facilities will include:

- **Valuing people.** We'll foster a workplace grounded in respect, inclusion, open communication, and mutual accountability. We'll implement our Peer-to-Peer Culture Coach program (**Exhibit 5-5**) at [CLIENT], which involves trained employee coaches facilitating discussions to strengthen relationships, deepen trust, and sustain an atmosphere where employees feel valued. Our goal is for more than 80% of O&M staff to participate in at least one in-person Culture Coaching session per year.
- **BeyondZero® safety culture.** Safety will be a core value embedded in every task, every shift, every day. All employees will have Stop Work authority, and our safety management system will combine pre-task briefings, real-time reporting, and continuous engagement to deliver results that consistently exceed industry averages. Employee satisfaction surveys at recent transitions (Jackson, MS and West Basin, CA) show significant improvement in workplace satisfaction after Jacobs takes over operations, which is a direct reflection of our culture and investment in people.
- **Training and certification** that build confidence today and capability for tomorrow. We will invest in every employee's growth through structured training programs, certification exam support, and continuing education, committing to make sure our team is ready to perform at the highest level today and prepared to take on the challenges ahead. As [CLIENT]'s facilities evolve through CIP investments and changing regulatory requirements, our workforce will evolve with them.
- **An ownership mindset.** We'll foster a culture where every team member is personally invested in the facilities' success and motivated to elevate performance every day. That means operators who don't just run a process but look for ways to optimize it, maintenance technicians who treat every asset as if it were their own, and supervisors who hold themselves accountable for outcomes, not just activities. When employees feel that level of ownership, it shows — through cleaner facilities, fewer breakdowns, faster response times, and pride in the work that [CLIENT] and the community will see firsthand.
- **Recognition, engagement, and feedback.** We'll recognize achievements in safety, innovation, teamwork, and community service. We'll use engagement surveys, open forums, and employee feedback to drive measurable improvements.

**Exhibit 5-5, Jacobs' Commitment to a Performance Culture Involves Every Level of Staff** (graphic asset 186_009385), describes the Peer-to-Peer Culture Coaches program: from our 2023 client survey, we understand that workforce satisfaction matters, and this year we're upping our game with a program we think of as "preventive maintenance for people." Our employee coaches will facilitate discussions to strengthen relationships, deepen interpersonal trust, ensure effective communications, and sustain an atmosphere where employees feel valued, respected, included, and encouraged to bring their authentic selves. Our goal is for more than 80% of our O&M staff to have at least one in-person Culture Coaching session this year and beyond.
