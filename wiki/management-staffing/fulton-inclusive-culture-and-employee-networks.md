# Inclusive Culture and Employee-Network Development

Jacobs’ Together Beyond global strategy creates a culture of inclusion and belonging where we all thrive by embracing many perspectives—critical elements to building a culture that retains talent. Its four strategic pillars are promoting business growth through different perspectives, building cultural awareness and engagement, developing the best talent, and leadership commitment and accountability. Our vision is to foster fairness and respect for all people, value the differences and similarities in backgrounds and skills, and maximize everyone’s potential.

Jacobs employee network groups support diversity and belonging by sharing information, ideas, experiences, and resources. These groups provide peer support, career development, and mentoring, and raise awareness through discussion groups and activities open to all. Each group has a senior executive sponsor to guide and reinforce the importance of their efforts and to mentor staff who may not otherwise have access to senior leadership.

Jacobs is also actively involved with the Hispanic Engineer National Achievement Awards Corporation, the National Society of Black Engineers, the Society for Hispanic Professional Engineers, the Women’s Transportation Seminar, and the Society of Women Engineers.

Our Jacobs Employee Networks play a critical role in fostering our Culture of Caring in the work they do. The groups are centered around offering employees an opportunity to collaborate with others around the world who share similar interests and explore opportunities that relate to their specific goals. The shared mission of our employee networks is to make, celebrate and develop Jacobs’ diverse employee population, provide career enriching development opportunities for all levels of talent, assist in the attraction and retention of top diverse talent, facilitate innovation at Jacobs, and contribute to the communities in which we live and work.

**Careers Network** — Empowering our employees across all career stages to maximize their potential and make Jacobs the industry leader and workplace of choice.

**Women’s Network** — Accelerating a cultural shift by empowering women and promoting gender equality.

**ACE** — Providing information, resources, and networking opportunities regarding physical, mobility and cognitive disabilities to disabled staff and to staff who provide caregiving services.

**OneWorld** — Providing an inclusive environment that actively nurtures and supports our diverse employees and clients across all ethnicities and cultures.

**Enlace** — Leveraging the company’s unique and vibrant Latino talent contributing to our company’s growth profitable strategy, attracting and retaining Latinos, while fostering leadership, community involvement, diversity and cultural pride.

**Harambee** — Positively impacting the black employee experience through recruitment, development, and retention of black talent.

**Prism** — Creating an environment where LGBTQI+ employees feel able and empowered to bring their whole self to work.

**VetNet** — Advocating for veterans and current military reserve members, including support for transitioning veterans.
