# Workforce Development Plan — Goals, Elements, and Delivery Partners

## H. Workforce Development

As your operator, Jacobs will support and strengthen [CLIENT]'s workforce development and capacity building program and bring new strategies for you to consider.

### 1. Elements of the Plan and Implementation Strategies

Our plan to execute the workforce development requirements cited in the scope of services includes these goals:

- Support [CLIENT]'s vision for regional equity and enhanced community benefits.
- Align with [CLIENT]'s foundation of operational excellence, regulatory reliability, and long-term employee growth.
- Incorporate strategies to sustain a local, diverse, and future-ready workforce to serve both [CLIENT] and other regional prospects.
- Promote O&M services as a model for professional career growth and technical advancement for people in the region and the surrounding area.
- Coordinate with [CLIENT] on outreach programs that promote [CLIENT]'s brand and raise awareness of water industry career opportunities.

**The following section and Exhibit II-17** present a workforce development plan designed to meet [CLIENT] requirements. We'll partner with [CLIENT] and our subcontractors PRISM and P3 Development Group to implement this plan.

**Exhibit II-17. Workforce development plan.** The plan is built on three tiers:

- **Career awareness and exploration** — high school outreach and STEAM career presentations.
- **Internships and co-op programs** — annual interns, [CLIENT]'s RISE program, and technical and lab placements. We'll offer structured internships, co-op opportunities, and pre-apprenticeship placements that lead directly to permanent employment within [CLIENT]'s workforce.
- **Apprenticeship program** — AFSCME trade apprenticeships for electricians, machinists, steamfitters, and auto mechanics.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the opening posture ("support and strengthen your program and bring new strategies for you to consider"), and the three-tier plan structure that moves a candidate from awareness through internship to apprenticeship and permanent employment.

Pursuit-specific: every goal bullet, the client's named internship program, the trade unions named in the apprenticeship tier, and the subconsultants delivering the plan. Where the client has no existing workforce program, invert the opening — lead with the plan you will stand up rather than the one you will strengthen.
