## Reuse guidance

Universal: the five-category structure (operations / maintenance / all staff / selected staff / corporate university) and nearly every row — this is the standard Jacobs O&M curriculum and needs no rewriting for a new pursuit.

Pursuit-specific: the process rows that name a facility's own unit processes (solids handling, site-specific odor control, plant-specific process control testing) and the permit rows (NPDES Permit, Annual NPDES Permit Training) — replace with the governing permit or regulation for the target system (WDR, Title 22, state drinking-water permit). Verify the Jacobs University course count (225) and the leadership course list against current corporate offerings at the time of the bid; the figure should be registered in `proof-points/registry.md` before it goes into body text.
