# Comprehensive Transition Support

As depicted in our transition team organization chart, Jacobs' transition team includes specialized experts across all key functions. These professionals bring a disciplined yet people-centered approach to mobilization and startup. During the transition period, this team will work side-by-side with [CLIENT] staff to ensure a smooth and efficient handoff. Their focus includes:

- **Leading the implementation of Jacobs' systems, tools, and standard processes** while maintaining focus on uninterrupted day-to-day operations.
- **Coordinating onboarding and training programs** that introduce employees to Jacobs' culture, safety principles, and operational excellence standards.
- **Integrating asset and data management systems** to align with [CLIENT]'s CMMS, SCADA, and reporting requirements.
- **Providing continuity and follow-through beyond the initial handover**, remaining available to [CLIENT] for ongoing support, optimization, and knowledge sharing.

This hands-on leadership approach ensures that [CLIENT]'s transition is guided by seasoned professionals who understand both the human and technical dimensions of change. By maintaining a dedicated presence throughout the process, Jacobs reduces risk, accelerates integration, and delivers a transparent and collaborative transition that reflects the professionalism and partnership [CLIENT] expects.

> Despite the many challenges presented by the COVID-19 outbreak, Jacobs implemented a smooth and successful transition from our previous operator. Jacobs assembled a transition team of HR, operational, and technical professionals to implement their plan safely and efficiently. The plan included constant communication with the owner; a safe, private, and remote process for employees onboarding to Jacobs; and a technical team that applied their engineering and operational expertise to ensure that operations continued without interruption. Following a successful transition, we look forward to a continued partnership with Jacobs for many years to come.
>
> **— Vincent R. Carroccia, Deputy Commissioner, Department of Public Works, City of Wilmington, DE, 302.576.3081**
