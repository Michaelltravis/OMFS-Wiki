# Staffing Model — Fewer Managers, More Doers

## Staffing model

We will deploy a workforce sized and structured to operate all [CLIENT] facilities safely, compliantly, and without interruption, as summarized in **Exhibit 5-3**. Our staffing approach will be built on three principles: **retain qualified incumbent staff, right-size coverage for each facility's operational complexity, and build resilience so that vacancies, absences, and emergencies don't compromise performance.**

We will retain qualified staff currently working at the facilities who wish to continue, preserving institutional knowledge and operational continuity. Details of our employee transition approach are provided later in this section under our **Draft Transition Plan**. We'll recruit for any positions not filled through retention through our dedicated resource planning group, local hiring networks, and national O&M talent pipeline.

## Fewer managers, more doers

**A staffing model built around wrench time and process control.** We've intentionally restructured the workforce mix from what's in place today, shifting investment out of management overhead and into the operators and technicians who keep the plants running. The result is a leaner leadership layer paired with the hands-on capacity needed to meet [CLIENT]'s drain-and-clean, process control, and 24/7 coverage expectations, as illustrated below.

| Workforce | Current count | Approx. Jacobs plan | What changes for [CLIENT] |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Salaried (management)** | 19 FTE | 13 FTE | Leaner leadership layer with six fewer mid-level and above managers for quicker decisions, clearer accountability, and overhead redirected to the field. |
| **Hourly (operators, mechanics, etc.)** | ~69 FTE | 83 FTE | Approximately 14 additional hourly doers focused where the work happens. |
| **Biosolids sub\*** | 13 FTE | 13 FTE | Provided by Synagro: 1 manager + 12 workers. |
| **Total FTE** | ~101 | 109 | A workforce sized to meet the RFP's drain-and-clean, process control, and 24/7 requirements without leaning on overtime or deferred maintenance. |

Where the added hourly capacity goes:

- **Drain-and-clean execution.** Operators, not mechanics, will perform the required drain-and-clean tank schedule, with heavy operator presence on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays for these tasks. Mechanics follow with quick post-clean inspections and any needed preventative maintenance.
- **More wrench time for mechanics.** Reassigning drain-and-clean to operators frees the maintenance team to focus on preventive, predictive, and corrective maintenance that keeps assets reliable and reduces unplanned breakdowns. We'll have four maintenance staff dedicated to Repair and Replacement (R&R) work.
- **More accurate process control data.** Operators will perform key process tasks (e.g., mixed liquor sampling) directly, rather than relying solely on in-line probes, improving the quality and reliability of data that drives day-to-day process decisions.
- **24/7 coverage at South Canadian.** Additional operators support the new requirement for round-the-clock staffing at South Canadian WWTP.
