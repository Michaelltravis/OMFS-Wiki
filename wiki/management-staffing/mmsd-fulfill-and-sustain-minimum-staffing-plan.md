# Plan to Fulfill and Sustain the Minimum Staffing Plan

Using our HR model, we'll staff and retain all 236 positions identified in RFP Exhibit E Minimum Staffing Plan by Day 1, meet Exhibit E's mandate to fill "Required Positions" within the 90-day grace period, and staff "Key Positions" at all times. We'll use temporary coverage when needed to maintain performance and review staffing status during governance and the annual joint reviews of the Minimum Staffing Plan. **Exhibit II-6** illustrates our framework for staffing certainty.

## Retain qualified incumbents — protect knowledge and morale during transition

During the transition pre-term phase, and approximately 60 days before Day 1, an **experienced transition team** will work closely with the unions to provide a stress-free transition to Jacobs. In section "***3.1 Process for Transitioning Union Employees***," we outline how this process will unfold. Our **Transition Manager, Kelly Irving,** will work closely with Brooke and our HR team to **identify the incumbent employees you most want to retain** and prioritize offers and onboarding for those individuals. (See ***Section IV.B Pre-term Transition Activities***.)

## A clear plan to fill vacancies — before they become an issue

From award forward, we'll track vacancies daily, launch postings immediately, and escalate hard-to-fill roles to our regional bench for interim coverage until permanently filled. This is how we'll avoid service risk and fee reductions tied to unfilled roles.

## Staffing for resilience — local core + regional bench + national SMEs

Our staffing model aims to ensure continuous staffing through four sourcing tactics:

1. **Foundational** — Current [CLIENT] employees and new hires from the local area.
2. **Regional O&M bench** — Provides temporary/emergency assignments, specialty skills, and training, as needed.
3. **National SME network** — Supports the local workforce with process, biosolids drying, odor control, energy, IT/OT/SCADA, asset management, and other specialty needs.
4. **Staffing contracts** — Several temporary employment providers will allow us to meet short-term needs and potentially convert promising short-term employees to long-term hires should the situation warrant such an approach.

## Systems and reporting that make staffing transparent

Our HR Information System (HRIS) and compliance workflows will track hiring status, onboarding, certifications, training, and license renewals. We'll embed staffing metrics (vacancies, time-to-fill, retention, training compliance) into the governance and reporting schedule and the annual staffing review. [CLIENT] will know about issues early and decisions will be traceable.

## Alignment with [CLIENT]'s broader goals

Our HR systems and practices will support [CLIENT]'s priorities, workforce development, and apprenticeship programs, which will be coordinated with the Workforce Development Plan described in ***Subsection H. Workforce Development Program***. This includes structured career pathways, on-the-job training, apprenticeships in key trades, and internships/co-ops, all of which will be reported annually to [CLIENT].
