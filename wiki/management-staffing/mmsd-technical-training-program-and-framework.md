# Technical Training Program and Five-Component Training Framework

Jacobs' proposed training programs are structured to develop and sustain a high-performing, certified workforce that operates safely, efficiently, and in compliance with national standards and [CLIENT]'s performance standards and technical training requirements under RFP §3.02(e,f) and Exhibit M. Our approach is data-driven and outcome-focused, providing the foundation for operational excellence, regulatory reliability, and employee growth.

**Commitment to learning, safety, and continuous improvement.** We plan to exceed the contractual requirement of 24 hours of technical training per employee per year, supported by comprehensive safety and leadership development. Training completion and certifications will be tracked through Jacobs' **Learning Management System (LMS)** and integrated with performance evaluations and project KPIs. This assures accountability, transparency, and measurable progress for both Jacobs and [CLIENT].

**Structured, accountable training framework.** As shown in **Exhibit II-12** (graphic asset ID `203_007CAM_3`), our training program includes five integrated components that sustain a culture of safety, technical excellence, and continuous learning: (1) Core Curriculum & Compliance; (2) Technical & Operational Skills Development; (3) Certification & Licensing Support; (4) Cross-Training & Succession Readiness; and (5) Performance Tracking & Reporting. Jacobs' technical training resources are supported by a global network of more than **100 certified instructors and SMEs** across operations, maintenance, laboratory, and asset management disciplines.

**Orientation** for new employees at Jacobs includes foundational courses in safety, environmental compliance, and emergency preparedness. We provide hands-on classroom and digital learning across operations, maintenance, and laboratory disciplines, reinforced by vendor-led instruction and simulations.

**Health and safety training** covers essential topics such as Arc Flash and NFPA 70E, confined-space entry, ladder safety, mental health awareness, and cybersecurity.

**Regional technical specialists coordinate technical training** on process operations, operator math, laboratory controls, troubleshooting, and disinfection techniques. These courses support foundational learning and advanced certification preparation, equipping employees with the technical skills needed to maintain [CLIENT]'s high standards.

We support staff in obtaining and maintaining professional certifications required to operate [CLIENT]'s facilities, covering exam fees, continuing education, and renewals. Quarterly reports to [CLIENT] will summarize hours completed, certification status, and correlation to operational KPIs.
