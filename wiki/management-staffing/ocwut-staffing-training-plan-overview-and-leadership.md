# Staffing and Training Plan Opener — Stable Workforce and Leadership Team

[CLIENT]'s facilities — a regional municipal water utility trust operating four wastewater treatment plants and a major pump station — demand reliable 24/7 operations, rigorous regulatory compliance, proactive asset stewardship, and a workforce that performs with excellence under both normal and emergency conditions. Meeting these demands will start with bringing in experienced leaders, qualified operators, and maintenance technicians and implementing a support structure that prevents gaps before they become problems.

Our staffing and training plan, which we developed in accordance with Schedule 10, will **deliver a stable, well-qualified workforce backed by structured training, cross-training across facilities and functions, on-call coverage, and regional resources**. Our plan reflects what we learned during our site visits to all five [CLIENT] facilities, analysis of the RFP and Service Agreement, and decades of experience staffing and managing multi-site wastewater O&M contracts of similar size and complexity across the country. The result will be **dependable operations without service gaps** and a team culture where people take pride in their work, stay for the long term, and grow.

## Organization Structure and Leadership Team

**Strong O&M performance begins with strong leadership.** We have assembled a leadership team with deep experience operating multi-facility wastewater systems, managing 24/7 workforces, and building high-performing cultures, because the caliber of leadership will directly shape the quality of daily operations, staff retention, and responsiveness to [CLIENT]. Project Manager **David Pitocchelli and our leadership team** will set the tone for a culture of accountability, responsiveness, and continuous improvement. They've done this before at complex, multi-facility systems, and they'll bring that same leadership presence and discipline to [CLIENT] from day one.

**Exhibit 5-1 summarizes our proposed leadership positions, each of which will meet or exceed the qualification and experience requirements defined in Schedule 10.** Our identified leadership team hold these qualifications, and any future vacancies will be filled with individuals who bring these credentials and certifications and the depth of experience [CLIENT]'s facilities demand. For detailed qualifications and resumes, **see Section 3, Experience and Qualifications of the Firm and Management Team**.
