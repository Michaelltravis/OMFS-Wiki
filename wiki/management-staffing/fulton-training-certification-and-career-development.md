# Training, Certification, and Career-Development Program

JC Solutions is deeply committed to attracting, developing, and retaining a high-performing workforce. As one of Newsweek’s Top 100 “America’s Most Loved Workplaces,” we invest heavily in our people to ensure longterm success at [CLIENT]’s facilities.

We offer comprehensive training programs that combine technical instruction, regulatory awareness, and professional development. From onboarding to advanced skill-building, our approach includes classroom sessions, field exercises, e-learning, and simulation-based training—ensuring our team is equipped to manage both routine operations and unexpected events.

We are investing 4,000 hours of training for the onsite team at [CLIENT]. To ensure our team remains current with evolving technologies, these training will include annual high-technology training programs tailored to emerging industry needs. This includes focused modules on MBR operations, delivered by subject-matter experts with deep hands-on experience. These trainings are designed to enhance operator proficiency, improve process performance, and maintain compliance across all treatment facilities. [CLIENT] staff will benefit from ongoing access to this advanced training, helping sustain a highly skilled, adaptable workforce.

$935,000 worth of investment by JC Solutions.

**Individual Training Assessments:** We assess each employee’s skills and tailor training plans accordingly, helping staff grow within their current role or transition into one that better suits their strengths.

**Structured Learning Paths:** Courses cover safety, ethics, operations, quality, certification, and compliance. Site-specific and on-demand training ensure adaptability.

**Simulation-Based Operator Training:** Operators practice real-world scenarios in a controlled environment, strengthening SOP-based response to malfunctions, peak flows, and emergency events.

**Regulatory Compliance Training:** Our early “crash course” and ongoing training ensure staff understand Clean Water Act responsibilities, permit compliance, and proper reporting protocols.

**Maintenance Skills Development:** Regional experts provide hands-on instruction in predictive maintenance (e.g., vibration analysis, CMMS, ultrasound). Staff are encouraged to pursue CMRT certification annually.

We also engage with local schools, colleges, and workforce programs to support talent development and build a pipeline of future O&M professionals. With a strong focus on safety, compliance, and technical proficiency, Jacobs ensures [CLIENT] staff are not only qualified—but prepared, confident, and resilient.

By providing numerous internal opportunities to our O&M staff to grow in their jobs and advance their careers, we see many of our staff remain long-term employees. Strategies include technical training and certifications; future leadership training programs; partnering with public and private universities, community colleges, and technical schools; Project Manager Intern Program; Leadership Team Mentor Program; cross-training programs; and succession planning.

Jacobs has developed its own wastewater operations training content through our Institute of Learning platform, covering a wide range of topics but generally aligned to the national Association of Boards of Certification (ABC) “Need to Know” set of standards per certification grade level. These classes were developed with the combined expertise of our operators and engineers, and provided through classroom, field, and online teaching methods. Online quizzes are used to allow students to demonstrate their learning, specific for each subject area. The intent is to improve the skills and competency of our operations staff and support their career growth. Providing training for operators developed by operators is an effective way to accomplish this.

JC Solutions’ approach to maintenance skills training and predictive maintenance techniques improves uptime and reduces corrective maintenance. **Certified Maintenance Reliability Technician (CMRT)** exam opportunities at least once a year are available for all maintenance staff. **Hands-on, in-person instruction for vibration analysis, alignment, oil analysis, CMMS best practices, ultrasound, and troubleshooting** is taught by our regional maintenance experts in each subject area. **Skills training**—from basic mechanic-in-training to advanced predictive maintenance training—supports continuous improvement of staff skills and maintenance processes. Our commitment to these programs has resulted in Jacobs employing more CMRT-certified maintenance staff than any other water/wastewater service provider in the U.S.

Cross-training will occur to help build our culture of growing and elevating skillsets and create synergy between the operations and maintenance teams focused on supporting each other. It also provides valuable redundancy during vacancies or emergencies, while creating opportunities for career growth. As part of our Individualized Training Plans, the management team will work with staff to identify ambitious individuals who aspire beyond their day-to-day job and will cater cross-training opportunities focused on these individuals to support their development.
