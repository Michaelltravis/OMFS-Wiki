# Cross-Training for Resiliency and Intern Program Support

### Cross-Training for Resiliency

The organization chart (**Exhibit 3-1**) reflects coverage for the [FACILITY] with cross-trained staff for flexibility. Jacobs believes that cross-training is essential to reliable plant operations and employee retention. We will develop our team members to their full potential, enabling them to work across multiple facilities and disciplines. This cross-facility approach increases [CLIENT]'s operational resiliency and creates a larger, more capable resource base.

In addition to onsite cross training, Jacobs will leverage our extensive network of projects across California. With nearby operations at West Basin, Soquel Creek, and other advanced treatment facilities, staff will participate in regional learning and knowledge sharing that directly benefits [CLIENT]. This California-wide bench of expertise not only enhances resiliency but also supports Jacobs' broader mission of advancing water reuse and developing the largest bench of AWTO certified employees in California. By sharing knowledge across projects, our teams strengthen each other and help "get the word out" about the value of water reuse, furthering the industry-wide effort to expand advanced water treatment capacity across the state.

**Sidebar callout — "At Jacobs, We Train to Retain"** (graphic asset `156_008A26`): *"Retention of newly-learned skills requires applied training materials customized to the participant's work environment, coupled with post-class assignments, all designed to help the participant apply what they have learned at the point of work. As a result, they become more empowered and engaged in their jobs. This focus and attention we give our employees has resulted in Jacobs being named by Fortune Magazine and Business Insider numerous times as one of the "50 Best Companies to Work for in America."*

### Supporting the [Client]'s Intern Program

[CLIENT]'s strong intern program is another important part of this strategy. Jacobs will involve interns in structured training alongside our operators, pairing them with mentors and providing feedback to [CLIENT] on their development. This builds the next generation of water and advanced water treatment professionals while supporting [CLIENT]'s long-term workforce pipeline.

We also recognize [CLIENT]'s interest in cross-training staff. While the scope of this request will be clarified during negotiations, Jacobs will work with [CLIENT] to identify targeted opportunities for knowledge transfer without overextending resources.

By combining cross-training within our Jacobs team, involving interns in structured development, and drawing on our broader California delivery team, Jacobs offers [CLIENT] a balanced approach that enhances resiliency, builds local talent, and strengthens long-term operational capacity.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-part close (cross-training rationale → named regional bench + "train to retain" proof point → intern-program commitment → balanced-approach wrap-up) is a strong drop-in pattern for ending any staffing-plan subsection, especially where the RFP signals interest in workforce development or an existing intern program. The "At Jacobs, We Train to Retain" callout is a reusable corporate proof point independent of any specific pursuit.

Pursuit-specific: replace [CLIENT]/[FACILITY] throughout; only include the intern-program paragraph if the target client actually operates or has expressed interest in an intern program (verify with the pursuit team before reusing verbatim — don't assume every client has one). The "nearby operations" project list (West Basin, Soquel Creek) should be swapped for whichever Jacobs projects are geographically closest to the new pursuit's facility.
