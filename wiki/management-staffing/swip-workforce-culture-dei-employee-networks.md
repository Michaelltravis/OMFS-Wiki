# Workforce Culture — TogetherBeyond Diversity Strategy and Employee Network Groups

## True belonging with diversity, equality, and inclusion

Jacobs' TogetherBeyond global strategy creates a culture of inclusion and belonging where everyone thrives by embracing many perspectives — critical elements to building a culture that retains talent. It is built on four strategic pillars: promoting business growth through different perspectives, building cultural awareness and engagement, developing the best talent, and leadership commitment and accountability.

Jacobs' diversity vision is to foster fairness and respect for all people, value the differences and similarities in backgrounds and skills, and maximize everyone's potential. Because the pursuit's service community is a truly diverse one, this is particularly important. Jacobs employee network groups share information, ideas, experiences, and resources. These groups provide peer support, career development, and mentoring, and raise awareness through a variety of discussion groups and activities that are open to all. Each group has a senior executive sponsor to guide and reinforce the importance of efforts and mentor staff who may not otherwise have access to senior leadership.

Jacobs is also actively involved with the Hispanic Engineer National Achievement Awards Corporation, National Society of Black Engineers, Society for Hispanic Professional Engineers, Women's Transportation Seminar, and Society of Women Engineers.

## Employee network groups

Jacobs Employee Networks play a critical role in fostering a Culture of Caring in the work they do. The groups are centered around offering employees an opportunity to collaborate with others around the world who share similar interests and explore opportunities that relate to their specific goals.

The shared mission of the employee networks is to make, celebrate, and develop Jacobs' diverse employee population, provide career-enriching development opportunities for all levels of talent, assist in the attraction and retention of top diverse talent, facilitate innovation at Jacobs, and contribute to the communities in which employees live and work.

| Network | Mission |
|---|---|
| ACE | Providing information, resources, and networking opportunities regarding physical, mobility, and cognitive disabilities to disabled staff and to staff who provide caregiving services. |
| Careers Network | Empowering employees across all career stages to maximize their potential and make Jacobs the industry leader and workplace of choice. |
| Enlace | Leveraging the company's unique and vibrant Latino talent contributing to the company's growth profitable strategy, attracting and retaining Latinos, while fostering leadership, community involvement, diversity and cultural pride. |
| Harambee | Positively impacting the black employee experience through recruitment, development, and retention of black talent. |
| OneWorld | Providing an inclusive environment that actively nurtures and supports diverse employees and clients across all ethnicities and cultures. |
| Prism | Creating an environment where LGBTQI+ employees feel able and empowered to bring their whole self to work. |
| VetNet | Advocating for veterans and current military reserve members, including support for transitioning veterans. |
| Women's Network | Accelerating a cultural shift by empowering women and promoting gender equality. |
