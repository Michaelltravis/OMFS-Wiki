# O&M Management Philosophy — Seven Critical Success Factors

Our approach to delivering results for [CLIENT], a regional water utility trust operating a multi-plant wastewater system, starts with seven tenets that define how we operate, collaborate, and continuously improve. These critical success factors, shown in **Exhibit 1-3**, create a holistic framework that connects every element of facility management into an integrated system focused on [CLIENT]'s priorities.

**1. Compliance & Ethics.** We uphold the highest standards of ethical conduct and regulatory compliance — without compromise. Our 99.98% compliance rate reflects the rigorous processes and tools behind every facility we operate. This commitment safeguards your reputation, protects the communities we serve, and reinforces the trust clients like [CLIENT] place in us.

**2. Transparency & Accountability.** We practice open, honest communication every day as the foundation of a strong partnership. Real-time dashboards, structured reporting, and rigorous QA/QC oversight will keep [CLIENT] leadership informed and in control — with no surprises. That transparency builds confidence in daily operations and strengthens collaboration at every level.

**3. Empowerment & Performance.** Our people drive our performance. We recruit, retain, and develop skilled operators, mechanics, and managers who take ownership of results. By investing in our teams and empowering them to lead, we deliver consistent, high-quality O&M performance across every facility we operate.

**4. Asset Protection & Reliability.** Your infrastructure is a long-term investment — and we treat it that way. Disciplined preventive and predictive maintenance, supported by CMMS data and condition-based planning, will protect [CLIENT]'s assets and reduce emergency failures — extending asset life and improving system reliability.

**5. Innovation & Best Practices.** We embrace innovation and continuously refine our methods by applying proven best practices. From process optimization to emerging technologies, we'll bring forward ideas that improve efficiency and reduce costs. The result is forward-thinking solutions that meet [CLIENT]'s evolving needs.

**6. Neighbor & Community Care.** We don't just operate facilities — we're part of the community. Proactive odor control at the North Canadian influent and across the system will reduce complaints and protect neighbors. Our team will also engage in local events, outreach, and partnerships that demonstrate [CLIENT]'s commitment to the communities it serves.

**7. CIP Integration & Continuity.** Major capital upgrades shouldn't disrupt daily operations. We'll coordinate closely with [CLIENT] Engineering and CIP contractors to maintain compliance and minimize disruptions — intentionally integrating each new asset into our O&M programs from day one for a smooth transition with no gaps in performance.

Exhibit 1-3 presents these as seven interconnected pillars that define how Jacobs delivers O&M and builds a culture of high performance (graphic asset ID 137_009385).

## Reuse guidance

Universal: all seven pillar headings and the benefit-carrying sentence structure inside each — every pillar ends in what the client gets, not what Jacobs values. Pillars 1, 2, 4, and 5 are essentially portable as written.

Pursuit-specific: the compliance percentage, the named odor location in Pillar 6, and the CIP coordination counterpart in Pillar 7. Where the pursuit has no active capital program, replace Pillar 7 with the dominant local issue (wet weather, biosolids, energy).
