# Care and Sensitivity Toward Transitioning Employees

Jacobs understands the sensitivity that comes with organizational transition and the uncertainty it can create for employees and their families. Our approach emphasizes **care, transparency, and respect** for every team member. To support this, we'll host informational workshops designed for [CLIENT] employees and their families to introduce Jacobs, explain how we're organized, outline what to expect during the transition, and review our benefits and the onboarding process.

These sessions are informational and listening forums. They create a comfortable, supportive environment where employees can ask questions, express concerns, and receive clear, honest answers. Our transition process is built around listening to employees' feedback, responding with empathy, and demonstrating commitment through meaningful action. Jacobs' transition team, including our experienced HR, labor relations, and employee engagement professionals, has managed hundreds of transitions across the country. We consistently apply lessons learned and best practices to ensure the process is **smooth, seamless, and people focused**.

## Compensation and benefits

Jacobs will offer employment to all qualified and interested employees of the incumbent operator who wish to continue their careers under our management. We recognize that competitive wages and benefits are critical to retaining and attracting quality staff. As part of our commitment to [CLIENT]'s workforce, Jacobs has performed a **regional salary analysis** and will implement a compensation structure tailored to the local labor market and wastewater industry. This ensures fair, competitive pay and benefits that improve recruiting success and long-term employee retention. Our approach is designed to support continuity and stability for transitioning employees.

Jacobs' comprehensive compensation and benefits package includes competitive salaries, health and wellness programs, retirement plans, and professional development opportunities. We've already begun communicating these offerings through employee workshops in collaboration with the unions and will continue to reinforce them through onboarding materials, providing clarity, reassurance, and transparency for all employees.

> Jacobs took responsibility of operating and maintaining Vancouver's wastewater treatment plants in 2016. As part of the transition process Jacobs put together a comprehensive transition plan which made the process very positive, smooth and seamless for not only the City but also the existing staff that became Jacobs employees. There was a high degree of transparency which continues to this day.
