# Performance Management Framework

## Understanding and Commitment to Performance Standards

[CLIENT]'s contract is performance-based. The Fixed Monthly Fee is directly tied to meeting the Performance Requirements defined in Schedule 5, and Jacobs takes that accountability seriously. Up to 32.4% of the monthly fee is at risk across Management, Compliance, Maintenance, and Staffing categories — a structure that aligns our priorities directly with [CLIENT]'s expectations for reliable, compliant operations.

We've studied these performance metrics carefully. They reflect [CLIENT]'s goals:

- Timely and accurate NPDES reporting (MGT-1)
- Clean and orderly facilities (MGT-2)
- Adequate spare parts inventory (MGT-3)
- 100% NPDES compliance (COM-1)
- Odor control (COM-3)
- Wet weather management (COM-4)
- Solids management (COM-5)
- Water reuse commitments (COM-7)
- Disciplined preventive, predictive, and corrective maintenance execution (PD-1 through CM-3)

We embrace these standards — and we'll build our internal management systems to track and exceed them.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-move pattern — state that the contract is performance-based and that fee is genuinely at risk, list the client's own metrics by their own IDs, then commit to building internal systems that track and exceed them. It costs half a page and proves close reading of the RFP better than any adjective.

Pursuit-specific: the at-risk percentage, the schedule number, and every metric ID. Where the RFP has no performance-fee mechanism, keep the metric list and drop the fee sentence.
