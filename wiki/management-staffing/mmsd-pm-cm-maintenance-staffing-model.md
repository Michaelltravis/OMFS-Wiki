# PM and CM Staffing Model — Wrenches, Not Waiting

Our proactive, reliability-centered maintenance program focuses on in-house delivery and aligns with [CLIENT]'s 85% PM/15% CM goal. We'll organize people, planning, and materials so crews spend their time on wrenches, not waiting. Our ISO 55001-aligned asset management framework, disciplined planned maintenance optimization model, and CMMS governance ties daily work to measurable outcomes. Dedicated onsite maintenance teams are supported by regional specialists and national experts for surge needs and after-hours coverage. For the full technical approach (asset management, governance, KPIs, and workflows), see *Section IV.A.iii Maintenance Approach and IV.D Predictive Maintenance (PdM)*. This section focuses on how we'll staff to deliver that approach.

**Maintenance staffing that meets and exceeds [CLIENT]'s goals.** As shown in **Exhibit II-9**, we'll provide the 79 maintenance staff required in Exhibit E, Minimum Staffing Plan. **We've added a key role to support our goal to optimize PM measures vs. CM needs: John Loucks-Powell as Director of Asset Management.** John will draw from his experience as a utility owner to direct teams' day-to-day execution, aiming to exceed the 85% PM/15% CM target.

We'll self-perform most PM and CM. For niche tasks (e.g., specialty coatings, large crane picks), we may engage prequalified subcontractors under our procurement and safety controls and using the same CMMS workflow as the work we perform.

**Leadership and governance.** John will serve on the Maintenance Committee and drive efficient daily operations. He'll provide staffing, PM lists, backlog burn-down, and reliability KPIs to [CLIENT] on a schedule (weekly planning, monthly KPI reviews, quarterly executive updates) to make sure decisions are followed through. We'll provide a dashboard where [CLIENT] can access staffing performance metrics (PM compliance, CM backlog/aging, MTBF/MTTR, availability, labor/parts cost, closeout lag). We'll document changes to PM lists, job plans, or staffing allocations and align resources by risk and workload based on the annual joint review of the Minimum Staffing Plan.

**Planning and CMMS.** A planner/scheduler and CMMS analysts will convert priorities into scheduled work with proof-of-work completion in NEXGEN (photos, meter readings, parts usage).

**Maintenance delivery teams.** We'll staff supervisors/leads and multi-craft technicians by facilities and across core disciplines (mechanical, electrical, and I&C). We'll cross-train teams where appropriate to flex between scheduled PM and priority CM, with designated duty leads for after-hours response. Safety coordinators, permit support, and operators will augment PM through routine rounds captured in the CMMS.

**Procurement and warehouse integration.** Procurement and warehouse staff will work with planning to kit recurring PMs and likely CMs, stage tools/LOTO, quality assurance/quality control (QA/QC) parts, and track long-lead items against the 4- to 6-week look-ahead. The goal is getting materials to technicians efficiently and maintaining PM schedules.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the sequence — accept the client's staffing minimum, name the added role that buys the performance improvement, then show the governance, planning, delivery, and materials functions that make the ratio achievable. The "wrenches, not waiting" line and the reliability KPI set carry across pursuits.

Pursuit-specific: headcount and the staffing-exhibit reference; the PM/CM ratio target; the CMMS platform name; the named leader and the committee he sits on; the reporting cadence, which should mirror the RFP's required meeting rhythm.
