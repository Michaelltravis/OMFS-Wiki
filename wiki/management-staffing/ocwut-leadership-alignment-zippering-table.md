## Reuse guidance

Universal: the six-tier spine (executive → operations → engineering/CIP → I&C/SCADA/cyber → dispute escalation → maintenance/asset approvals) and the three-column "their person / the interaction / our person" format. It answers "who do I call" and "what happens when we disagree" in one page.

Pursuit-specific: every name on both sides, the contract schedule and service-agreement citations, and the escalation trigger (15 business days here). Tier 4's parenthetical that the client retains control of SCADA is a pursuit-specific reassurance — restate only where true.
