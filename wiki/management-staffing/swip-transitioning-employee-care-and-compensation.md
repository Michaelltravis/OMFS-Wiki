# Seamless Transition: Care and Sensitivity Toward Transitioning Employees

## Care and Sensitivity Toward Transitioning Employees

Jacobs understands the sensitivity of employees regarding transition and change and will provide informational workshops for employees and their families to introduce Jacobs' culture, organization, benefits package, and transition process. Our approach is patterned around listening to employees' concerns with a specific focus on people. We routinely and successfully manage such transitions with care and will apply our experience, HR and labor relations expertise, and lessons learned to ensure a smooth and supportive process.

## Compensation Package

We have conducted a regional salary analysis and will use that information to offer a competitive pay scale tailored to the industry and local market. In addition to strong benefits, Jacobs provides education reimbursement, certification support, and incentive programs that reward performance, safety, and professional growth. This comprehensive approach ensures both competitive recruiting and long-term staff retention.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: all three pieces here are drop-in reusable for any O&M transition that displaces an incumbent operator. The family-inclusive informational workshop, the listening-first framing ("patterned around listening to employees' concerns with a specific focus on people"), and the compensation-package paragraph require no tailoring beyond the local market.
