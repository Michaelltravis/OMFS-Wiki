# Vacancy Management, Retention, and Staffing Resiliency

[CLIENT]'s service agreement is clear: inability to fill vacancies is not an excuse for failing to meet performance requirements. We take that seriously, and it dovetails with our overarching staffing philosophy. We'll design our staffing model to prevent vacancies before they occur, fill them quickly when they do, and maintain full operational coverage in the interim.

## Preventing vacancies: train to retain

The most effective vacancy strategy is keeping good people. Our retention approach will start with competitive compensation and benefits, then layer on career development, recognition, a positive culture, and the kind of leadership that makes people want to stay. This includes career-advancement pathways from entry-level through licensed operator, specialized maintenance, and supervisory positions; certification incentives that cover exam fees and preparation costs and provide promotions and bonuses for advanced certifications; and leadership development through Jacobs University workshops on communication, coaching, team performance, and management fundamentals.

## Filling vacancies promptly

When vacancies do occur, our dedicated resource planning group, focused exclusively on O&M recruiting, will act immediately. Over the past year, its efforts have doubled new hires across our O&M portfolio, reduced turnover, and promoted high-performing staff into new roles. Our vacancy-management approach includes immediate posting and recruiting through the centralized recruiting system, local job networks, veteran hiring programs, and targeted social-media outreach; internal mobility that offers openings first to qualified Jacobs O&M staff across the national portfolio; regional O&M-bench deployment for temporary or emergency coverage within days; and succession planning for every critical role, with cross-trained backups identified and prepared in advance.

## Staffing transparency

We'll track hiring status, onboarding, certifications, and license renewals through our HR information system and report staffing metrics to [CLIENT] as part of regular governance meetings. **[CLIENT] will know about staffing issues early, and every decision will be traceable.**
