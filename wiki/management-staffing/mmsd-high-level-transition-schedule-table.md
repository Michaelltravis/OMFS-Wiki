## Reuse guidance

Universal: the workstream taxonomy and the discipline of naming a task lead and a date range for every line, including the extended-transition rows that continue nine months past the start date — those rows are a differentiator against operators whose plan ends at go-live. Pursuit-specific: all dates, deliverable numbers, and contract exhibit references.
