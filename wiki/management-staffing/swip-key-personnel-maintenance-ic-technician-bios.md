# Key Personnel Bios — Maintenance Technician and I&C Technician

## Maintenance Technician — Richard Gwinn

Richard Gwinn will serve as the Maintenance Technician for the facilities. With over 30 years of experience in mechanical systems maintenance and a Class I Wastewater Operator license, Richard has taught wastewater classes at Spokane Community College and maintains over 50 hours of continuing education annually. His extensive background includes implementing CMMS systems, leading hands-on troubleshooting, and supporting capital upgrades. Richard's combination of technical skills, training experience, and ongoing professional development ensures responsive and proactive maintenance aligned with [CLIENT]'s asset management goals.

## Instrumentation & Controls Technician — Mayo Miller

Mayo Miller will serve as the onsite Instrumentation & Controls Technician for the facilities, bringing over 12 years of experience in wastewater management with expertise in SCADA, instrumentation, and controls. He has led operational technology teams supporting multiple SCADA platforms, implemented major control system and network upgrades, and managed CMMS and capital improvement projects. A certified Class III Wastewater Operator and recipient of the Arkansas Wastewater Manager of the Year award, Mayo will ensure reliable monitoring, compliance, and optimization of the facility's advanced treatment systems.
