# Retaining Existing Staff, Named Key Positions, and Backfill Resources

We intend to retain all the existing employees that [CLIENT], a Southeast US county wastewater utility, desires, preserving their invaluable institutional knowledge. For any staff who do not wish to join the JC Solutions team or that [CLIENT] does not wish to retain, we will provide staff for those positions. We have identified named staff for key positions and will work with [CLIENT] to identify whether they wish to keep staff currently in those positions or use the individuals from our team. Similarly, we have resources for all other positions should we need to replace any current staff in those positions.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the two-sided commitment — retain every incumbent the client wants, and carry named replacements for every position the client does not want filled by an incumbent — is reusable in any transition or incumbent-displacement pursuit where workforce continuity is an evaluation concern. It reads as low-risk to the client and non-threatening to incumbent staff, and it is a natural lead-in to the organization chart.

Pursuit-specific: the team name (JC Solutions is the Jacobs/CERM JV for this pursuit), the list of key positions, and the named individuals held in reserve.
