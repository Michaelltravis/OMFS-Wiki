# Zero-Tolerance Policy, Ethics Hotline, and Employee Feedback Loop

### 3. Zero Tolerance for Harassment, Discrimination, or Bullying

We maintain a safe, respectful, and inclusive workplace through a strict zero-tolerance policy embedded in our Code of Conduct and reinforced by training, proactive monitoring, and clear enforcement. We train all staff at onboarding and annually and include behavioral expectations in safety meetings and audits (**Exhibit II-16**).

**Exhibit II-16. Ethics hotline with secure portal to report harassment and discrimination concerns 24/7.** Employees can report unethical or illegal behavior, including harassment or discrimination, through Jacobs' anonymous third-party ethics hotline or secure online portal. Supervisors and HR teams are also trained to respond promptly and confidentially to complaints. Graphic asset ID: `264_007CAM_1`.

### 4. Employee Feedback: Engine for Continuous Improvement

We listen to our employees and respond with actions. We use engagement surveys, open forums, and employee networks to surface insights and convert them into measurable change. Anonymous surveys will inform inclusion, leadership development, and well-being initiatives, supporting our Culture of Caring.

**Benefits to [CLIENT]: Acting on employee feedback will strengthen engagement, retention, and innovation and help [CLIENT] realize sustained, high-performing operations.**
