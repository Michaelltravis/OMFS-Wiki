# Individualized Training Program and Cross-Training

Our training program will be structured to develop and sustain a high-performing, certified workforce that operates safely, efficiently, and in compliance with all applicable standards. **We have deployed training systems at more than 300 O&M sites and deliver more than 200 live and virtual sessions annually across our portfolio**, providing more than **50,000 hours of training** to staff across North America annually. We'll bring the full depth of that capability to [CLIENT].

Our training program for [CLIENT] will include five integrated components: (1) core curriculum and compliance—foundational safety, environmental-compliance, and emergency-preparedness training for every employee, starting during onboarding and refreshed regularly; (2) technical and operational skills development—hands-on, site-specific process-operations, SOP-based, equipment-specific, CMMS/work-order, and sampling/lab/process-control instruction; (3) certification and licensing support—exam preparation, continuing education, and renewal support for Oklahoma operator and laboratory licenses; (4) cross-training and succession readiness; and (5) performance tracking and reporting through Jacobs' Learning Management System (LMS), integrated with performance evaluations.

Compliance training will begin with an intensive orientation early in the transition and continue throughout the contract term, covering Clean Water Act responsibilities, NPDES permit requirements, data-gathering and reporting protocols, and the consequences of noncompliance. We'll keep all training records in our LMS available for [CLIENT] review.

Safety training, anchored in our BeyondZero® culture, is the foundation for every employee. This starts with site-specific orientation before any work begins and is reinforced through ongoing refreshers, stand-downs, and practical drills. Technical and process training will leverage Jacobs' Institute of Learning platform with 36 instructional volumes aligned to the ABC "Need to Know" standards. Developed by operators and engineers, the instruction will be delivered through classroom, field, and online methods. Maintenance staff will receive hands-on instruction from Jacobs' regional maintenance experts with annual CMRT exam support.

Schedule 10 requires a cross-training program so that competent staff are always available. Cross-training is already one of our core staffing strategies that helps reduce single-point dependency, improve emergency response, and create career-growth opportunities that reinforce retention. The program includes facility familiarization across North Canadian, Deer Creek, Chisholm Creek, and South Canadian; O&M cross-functional training to improve daily-operations and emergency coordination; relief-coverage development so coverage is never dependent on one individual; individualized training plans aligned to career goals and operational needs; and LMS documentation of qualifications and signoffs for functions at each facility.
