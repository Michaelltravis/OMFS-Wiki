# Six-Step Approach to Retaining Local Talent and Staffing the Contract

**1. Retain and Recruit Local Talent:** Our top priority is to retain the majority of the existing local team at [CLIENT]'s facilities. We will begin by thoroughly assessing the current staff to identify key talent and areas of expertise. By offering these individuals opportunities for continued employment and career growth with JC Solutions, we aim to ensure continuity and stability. Alongside this retention effort, we will also implement a targeted recruiting process to fill any remaining gaps. This involves leveraging local job boards, professional networks, and outreach initiatives to ensure we attract qualified candidates who align with [CLIENT]'s needs and JC Solutions' values.

**2. Host Introduction Workshops:** After identifying the key members of the existing local team and potential new hires, we will host introduction workshops. These workshops will provide an opportunity for the local team and new recruits to learn about JC Solutions' culture, values, and operational expectations. By involving existing staff in these workshops, we foster a sense of inclusion, community, and collaboration from the outset. This ensures that everyone, both retained employees and new hires, are aligned with the project's goals.

**3. Assess and Interview Remaining Candidates:** We will conduct structured assessments and interviews for any remaining open positions or for employees interested in expanding their roles. This process allows us to evaluate each candidate's technical skills, cultural fit, and alignment with the project's long-term goals. By doing so, we ensure that those who join or remain on the team are not only qualified but also motivated to contribute to the ongoing success of [CLIENT] and JC Solutions.

**4. Make Employment Offers:** For both the retained local staff and any new recruits, we will extend competitive employment offers that reflect their value to the organization. These offers will incorporate competitive salaries, comprehensive benefits, and opportunities for growth. For the existing staff, special attention will be given to recognizing their experience and contributions, which will support their smooth transition to JC Solutions. This approach demonstrates our commitment to retaining talent and maintaining continuity.

**5. Implement Contract Startup and Operation:** Once employment offers are accepted, we will initiate the contract startup and operational processes. This step involves finalizing paperwork, clarifying roles and responsibilities, and ensuring compliance with all regulatory and contractual requirements. By retaining local staff who are already familiar with [CLIENT]'s operations, we will streamline the startup phase, ensuring minimal disruption and a smooth transition.

**6. Initiate Continuous Training and Development Program:** To ensure ongoing success, we will implement a continuous training and development program, designed to support both retained and newly hired staff. This program will include tailored training for specific job functions, mentorship opportunities, and ongoing professional development. By fostering a culture of learning and growth, we empower the team to adapt to new challenges, remain engaged, and continually improve performance.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the six-step spine and the benefit clause at the end of each step. The order is the argument — retention comes before recruiting, and continuous training closes the loop so the process does not end at day one.

Pursuit-specific: labor-market channels, whether introduction workshops include families (the adjacent care-and-sensitivity passage on the same page does extend workshops to employees and their families), and any union or civil-service constraints on offers and assessments, which this Southeast county pursuit did not have.
