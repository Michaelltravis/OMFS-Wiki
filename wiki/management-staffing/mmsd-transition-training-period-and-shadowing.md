# Transition Training Period

Training is a critical bridge between mobilization and long-term operational excellence. During the transition period, Jacobs' O&M staff will complete structured onboarding and hands-on familiarization across [CLIENT]'s facilities, including the water reclamation facilities, collection system, tunnels, and pumping systems. Our objective is to ensure all employees, both transitioning and new, are fully prepared, credentialed, and confident before the March 1, 2028, Operational Start Date.

Following onboarding, Jacobs personnel will shadow [CLIENT] staff to capture institutional knowledge and gain firsthand understanding of process operations, data and reporting protocols, performance metrics, CMMS workflows, asset management practices, and production of [CLIENT]'s biosolids product. Training will cover biosolids blending, dewatering, drying, product classification, and silo storage, with a consistent focus on safety, reliability, and product quality.

Jacobs and trusted partners such as Mead & Hunt will provide collection system expertise and work closely with [CLIENT]'s laboratory staff to align quality systems and QA/QC procedures. All staff will complete required safety and operational training prior to startup, and role-specific development needs identified during transition will be incorporated into ongoing training plan.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-move structure — onboarding and familiarization across every asset class, shadowing with a named list of what is being captured, then partner-supported alignment of lab and QA/QC systems before startup. Pursuit-specific: the asset classes, the operational start date, the specialty process, and the subconsultant named.
