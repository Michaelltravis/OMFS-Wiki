# Exit Transition Plan — Asset Condition, Inventory, and Staffing Knowledge Transfer

**2. Asset Condition and Inventory Management.** In accordance with Article 53.2, JC Solutions will conduct a thorough assessment of all facility assets and generate detailed condition reports. This will include:

- Final inventory assessment of tools, equipment, spare parts, and consumables, with supporting documentation on all transfers or decommissioned items.
- Verification and documentation of vehicles and equipment registration, transfer, or termination.
- A comparison of final asset conditions to baseline records established at the project's start, with recommendations for capital improvements where applicable.

**3. Staffing and Knowledge Transfer.** JC Solutions will provide comprehensive staffing data to support a smooth transition. As outlined in Article 6.6(B), [CLIENT] — a county public works department operating multiple water reclamation facilities and pump stations — or its successor operator will have the right to offer employment to current JC Solutions' staff working at the Managed Assets. Our approach includes:

- Providing exit packages for staff, including placement or relocation support where applicable.
- Conducting training needs assessments for any employees transitioning to [CLIENT] employment or a successor firm.
- Coordinating interviews for staff interested in staying on post-transition and facilitating seamless knowledge transfer.

Additionally, JC Solutions will waive non-compete restrictions solely for the purpose of enabling staff transitions to [CLIENT] or its selected contractor.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the structure — asset condition measured against the baseline established at contract start, a final inventory of tools/spares/consumables with disposition documentation, vehicle and equipment registration transfer, and a staffing package that pairs employment rights with training needs assessments, interviews, and a non-compete waiver. The non-compete waiver and the capital-improvement recommendations are the two lines that read as unusually candid and are worth keeping.

Pursuit-specific: every article number (Articles 53.2 and 6.6(B) are this contract's handback and employment provisions), the term "Managed Assets," and whether exit packages with placement or relocation support are being offered. Confirm the non-compete waiver language with legal and HR for each pursuit.
