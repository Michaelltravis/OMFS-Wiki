# Two-Week Day/Night Operator Shift Rotations at the Two Larger Plants

**[FACILITY A] operations staff** will be overseen day-to-day by the Plant Manager, who will work eight-hour shifts, Monday through Friday. (See **Exhibit 2-15**.) There will be two groups of dayshift operators who will all work 12-hour shifts. A Main Lead Operator along with two Operators will work a total of seven days across two weeks, covering three days during the first week and four days the next week. Similarly, a Secondary Lead Operator and two Operators will also work shifts across two weeks, and their shifts will cover the four days in the first week and the three days in the second week not covered by the Main Lead Operator group. This ensures three day-shift Operators onsite every day of the week. For night shifts, we will have two groups, each consisting of one Lead Operator and one Operator. They will follow the same shift patterns across two weeks as the two day-shift groups. This ensures two Operators are onsite every night shift of the week.

**[FACILITY B] operations staff** will be overseen day-to-day by the Plant Manager, who will work eight-hour shifts, Monday through Friday. (See **Exhibit 2-16**.) Lead Operators and Operations will work 12-hour shifts and will follow the same shift patterns as at [FACILITY A], ensuring the required minimum staffing of three Operators during day shifts and two Operators during night shifts for every day of the week.
