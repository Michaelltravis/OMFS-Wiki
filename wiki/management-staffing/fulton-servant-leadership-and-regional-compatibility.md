# Servant Leadership, Certification Advancement, and Regional Compatibility

Our leadership approach is centered around empowering employees and fostering growth. We believe in servant leadership, where leaders prioritize the needs of their teams, provide guidance, and remove obstacles to success. Leaders at all levels are encouraged to be mentors and coaches, supporting career development through hands-on training and personalized career development. We also provide incentives for advancing licensing and certifications, such as higher compensation for achieving advanced O&M certifications, which motivates employees to continuously elevate their skills.

With 500 staff in our Atlanta office, Jacobs is one of the state’s largest local engineering design, construction, and operations firms. Our business model is based on repeat business and strong client relationships. We’ve provided water-system planning, design, and construction services for municipalities throughout Georgia for the past 65 years—if something isn’t right, we’ll fix it.

Jacobs operates in a variety of markets in the metro Atlanta area such as water consulting, transportation consulting, city services, program management, and so forth. We have been in this region for decades and intend to maintain a presence in this market for decades to come. Because of the wide variety of service offerings, our connectivity into the region is strong. We don't just offer O&M services. All of our service offerings are interconnected, so we are more connected to and invested in our client’s success than other O&M-only providers with fewer services and associated connections to clients in the local region, such as [CLIENT].
