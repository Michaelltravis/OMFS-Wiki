# Leadership Team Organization Chart — Named Roles and Role Benefit Statements

**Benefit to [CLIENT]: Our leadership model provides all required positions, strengthens positions where [CLIENT] needs it most, and applies Jacobs' subject matter experts' (SMEs) experience with large utility operations to drive greater reliability, operational efficiency, and long-term community value for the region.**

*Jacobs' organizational structure illustrates our compliance with [CLIENT]'s staffing plan (RFP Exhibit E), as well as the pivotal roles we have added which engages some of our top caliber staff to drive delivery of transformative services and outstanding performance.*

## Governance layer

- **[CLIENT]** — Executive Committee | Operations Committee | Maintenance Committee
- **Corporate Sponsors** — Bob Pragada, Greg Fischer. **Corporate Sponsors will back Jacobs' commitment, be available for executive reviews, and enable rapid escalation to clear obstacles and mobilize resources.**

## Group 1 — Project management

- **Project Manager** — Kevin Dahl, CRL. **Project Manager will be [CLIENT]'s chief point of contact, lead day-to-day activities, and deliver performance excellence, responsiveness, safety, and compliance with contract requirements and KPIs.**
- **Deputy Project Manager & Director of Regional Partnership** — Rick Warner, PE. **Deputy Project Manager will support the Project Manager with day-to-day operations and oversee the project support groups called for in the RFP. In addition to aiding succession planning, Rick's double hat as Director of Regional Partnership will bolster stakeholder and community partnerships by tapping his expertise in water utility partnerships.**
- **Transition Manager** — Kelly Irving. **Transition Manager will facilitate a seamless transition in the pre-contract term, including mobilization, CMMS functionality, labor and training requirements, and shift coverage, and work closely with [CLIENT] and Asset Management and Program Management Teams to assume the contract in March 2028.**

## Group 2 — Program management

- **Program Management & Initiation** — Keoki Sears; Quitterie Cotten, PE. Program Management will drive delivery excellence and achieve transparency, accountability, and collaborative communications by establishing clear governance, team chartering, robust tools, and effective processes.

## Group 3 — Human resources and office administration

- **Human Resources** — Brooke Winters, SPHR
- **Procurement & Warehouse Management** — Kim Wilson; Mike Mitchell

## Group 4 — Regional partnerships

- **Public Outreach Coordinator** — Toyin Ogunfolaju
- **Water Education Specialist** — Megan Myszka

## Group 5 — Maintenance

- **Director of Maintenance & Asset Management** — John Loucks-Powell, CRL
- **Maintenance & Asset Manager – WRF** — Frank Dipietrapaul
- **Maintenance & Asset Manager – Conveyance** — Monty Sedlak, PMP, PgMP, CSP

## Group 6 — Operations

- **WRF Operations Manager** — Aleksey Reznik
- **Conveyance Manager** — Eric Habermeyer, IAM
- **Wet Weather & Conveyance Director** — Susan Moisio, PE. **Director of Wet Weather & Conveyance will bring a utility owner's lens and conveyance system best practices and support for operations decisions during wet weather events.**

## Group 7 — Engineering and off-site support

- **Chief Engineer** — Bill Desing, PE. **Chief Engineer will lead the engineering and offsite SME team to optimize O&M of [CLIENT] facilities and systems and draw on Jacobs' water O&M, digital, and engineering technologists and SMEs to provide the design support, innovative solutions, and rapid responses that will maximize uptime and asset life cycle.**
- **Capital Project Coordinators** — Liie Hill, PE; Natalie Lenz, PE. **Capital Project Coordinators will minimize feedback loops between O&M and capital improvements, reduce rework, and keep facilities online during construction.**

## Safety, security, and technology

- **Security & Safety Manager** — Travis Leech, CSP
- **Technology Manager** — Luke Amory
- **WRF 1 Safety & Security Coordinator** — Dave Haverly
- **WRF 2 Safety & Security Coordinator** — Tracy James

## Asset management teams

**Asset Management Teams will provide the required 24/7 operations coverage of all facilities and at both water reclamation facilities with qualified staff, including the addition of the Director of Maintenance & Asset Management and Wet Weather & Conveyance Director.**

**Legend:** staff above and beyond [CLIENT]'s minimum staffing requirements are flagged with the AB icon. FTE = full time equivalent; WRF 1 and WRF 2 are [CLIENT]'s two water reclamation facilities.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the structure — a client committee layer and named corporate sponsors above the Project Manager, seven numbered functional groups, and a bolded benefit sentence attached to each leadership box. The benefit sentences for Transition Manager, Chief Engineer, Capital Project Coordinators, and Wet Weather & Conveyance Director are close to drop-in for any large multi-facility O&M pursuit.

Pursuit-specific: every name and credential, the contract assumption date, the RFP exhibit reference for minimum staffing, and the number of support groups. Facility-coordinator roles are named per facility — rename to the new pursuit's sites.
