# Refining Job Descriptions in the First Days of Transition

## JC Solutions' proven staff transition process addresses employee concerns and ensures smooth transfer of workforce

In the first days of the transition, we will refine job descriptions based on a finalized needs assessment. A revised skillset list will outline the ideal experience and skills for each role, helping evaluate current employees, identify their best-fit positions, and highlight development or training opportunities to maximize potential.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the sequence (needs assessment → refined job descriptions → revised skillset list → best-fit placement and development plan) travels to any O&M transition where incumbent staff will be carried over. The framing matters — the skillset list is used to place and develop people, not to screen them out.

Pursuit-specific: the timing claim ("in the first days of the transition") must match the transition schedule exhibit in the same proposal.
