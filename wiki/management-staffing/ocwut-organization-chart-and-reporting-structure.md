# Organization Chart and Reporting Structure (Exhibit 5-2)

**Exhibit 5-2** illustrates the full reporting structure for the [CLIENT] contract — a regional municipal water utility trust operating four wastewater treatment plants and a major pump station — from executive oversight and on-site leadership through operations, maintenance, laboratory, and support functions at each facility. The organization chart reflects clear lines of accountability, defined supervision for every shift and discipline, and direct integration of our regional support resources. All management positions will be filled by the start of the transition period. All remaining full-time employees will be hired and trained by the operational start date (Schedule 10).

**Corporate support (offsite).** Executive Sponsor Kevin Dahl, CMRT, CRL; Strategic Advisor Rick Warner, PE, WEF Fellow; Client Account Manager Luke Lenard, PE, PMP; Regional Manager Ryan Harrold; Area Manager Jacob Smith, CMRT.

**Offsite O&M / engineering SME support team.** O&M SME Team Lead John Rickermann, PE, CMRT; Engineering SME Team Lead Luke Lenard, PE, PMP.

**Project leadership (on-site).** Project Director Steve Carpenter; Project Manager David Pitocchelli; Assistant Project Manager Alex Rodriguez; Transition Manager Kelly Irving; QA/QC Manager Josh Alleman; CIP Construction Manager Tanner Pipher, PE; Asset Manager Amy Dembinski, CRL; Safety Manager (1 FTE); Administrative Specialist (1 FTE).

**North Canadian / Witcher.** Operations Manager Tim Durham; 3 lead operators; 18 WWTP operators; 2 Witcher operators; maintenance 4 PM mechanics and 4 CM mechanics; 4 lab technicians.

**Chisholm Creek.** Operations Manager Jose "Joe" Ramos; 2 lead operators; 10 operators; 2 PM mechanics; 2 CM mechanics; 2 electricians.

**Deer Creek.** Operations Manager Paul Shropshire; 2 lead operators; 10 operators; 3 mechanics.

**South Canadian.** Operations Manager Roy Aristizabal; 2 lead operators; 8 operators; 2 PM/CM mechanics; 1 electrician/I&C technician; 1 lab technician.

**Shared and specialty support.** Maintenance Manager Brian Daniels with maintenance administrative assistant (1 FTE) and planner/scheduler/parts clerk (1 FTE); Laboratory Manager Scott Golden; I&C Technician Robert Coons; Hefner WTP support (1 FTE).

**Biosolids (subcontracted).** Biosolids Manager (Synagro, 1 FTE) with 1 lead operator, 2 equipment operators, and 8 CDL drivers.

*Legend:* operations, maintenance, laboratory, and electrician/biosolids/I&C roles are color-coded; asterisked positions denote licensed PEs in states other than Oklahoma; double-asterisked positions denote subcontracted staff; named individuals with resumes provided are marked as team members.
