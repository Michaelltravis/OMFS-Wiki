# Step-by-Step Transition for New Employees and Retention of Institutional Knowledge

As noted earlier, our approach invests heavily in **people, systems, and culture**, ensuring that the workforce is empowered, supported, and aligned with industry-leading operational standards. Our transition plan emphasizes collaboration, transparency, and clear communication to minimize stress and maintain confidence among staff. Existing and new employees will receive structured communication, training, and onboarding to set expectations early and reinforce stability throughout the transition.

Throughout this process, we'll also leverage team members like Bill Desing, Marty Dierker, and Bill Farmer—each of whom brings valuable experience with [CLIENT]—to further strengthen continuity and ensure a successful start on Day 1. Deputy Project Manager Rick Warner has already relocated to the service area and Project Manager Kevin Dahl will join the local community in 2027, well ahead of the formal transition process.

We'll tailor our approach to [CLIENT]'s needs and expectations, ensuring that all employees are kept informed and involved throughout the process. The result is a **high-performing local team** that is engaged, empowered, and integrated into Jacobs' broader organization, drawing on our company's full breadth of resources to deliver safe, reliable, high-quality service from Day 1.

> The transition over to Jacobs has been quite seamless. The environment is welcoming and well-structured, the overall atmosphere feels refreshed and encouraging. I've appreciated the increased focus and support directed toward our department, which has made the change feel greatly beneficial. I'm looking forward to the future and growth with this company.
>
> **— Myesha Brandon, Former Veolia Biosolids Manager, JXN Water – Wastewater Facilities**

## Institutional knowledge is the asset we intend to keep

The institutional knowledge and technical expertise of [CLIENT]'s current workforce are essential to maintaining safe, reliable, and high-performing operations. Jacobs' goal is to retain as many skilled employees as possible, recognizing the value of their experience while applying our proven commitment to people, training, and process discipline to further strengthen performance and outcomes.

Existing employee knowledge is critical to successful production of [CLIENT]'s biosolids product. Successful production requires precise, coordinated execution across multiple process areas, including blended biosolids management, dewatering, drying, product classification, and silo storage. These activities must be performed safely, consistently, and with unwavering attention to product quality. Jacobs' transition approach is designed to preserve and leverage this deep operational knowledge while reinforcing best practices, safety systems, and process controls to ensure continuity and long-term success.
