# Project Leadership Triad Profiles

## David Pitocchelli, Project Manager

As your single point of accountability, **David** will be on site, engaged daily, and responsible for operations, maintenance, compliance, and client communication. He knows these facilities firsthand from **his previous work as project manager for this program** where he led targeted process improvements and strengthened operational reporting and communication. David brings **30 years of wastewater O&M leadership experience** managing similar multi-facility systems, including direct oversight of four regional facilities with 100+ MGD treatment capacity, peak wet-weather flows above 250 MGD, and 80+ staff. His experience aligns directly with [CLIENT]'s priorities. He has taken over underperforming contracts, stabilized operations, restored compliance, eliminated maintenance backlogs, and improved client communication and reporting. He's supported transitions with staff training, SOP implementation, and maintenance of continuous operations. His hands-on leadership focuses on strong teams, reliability, and measurable results.

*"Our team will know what success looks like and feel supported to do their best work. I'm excited to help [CLIENT] improve day-to-day performance and create a culture where compliance, maintenance, and communication move in the same direction."*

## Alex Rodriguez, Assistant Project Manager

**Alex** will support day-to-day contract delivery across operations, maintenance, compliance, and staff coordination. He brings **13 years of experience in wastewater and water operations**, laboratory analysis, biosolids handling, and utility maintenance and has managed multiple water and wastewater facilities. **Alex launched his career in Oklahoma, operating water systems in Enid and maintaining the infrastructure that Stillwater residents and two rural water districts depended on every day.** That experience gave him a firsthand understanding of Oklahoma communities and what they expect from their systems. Now, Alex is bringing his local knowledge combined with years of experience operating complex facilities across multiple states. He'll help connect daily plant needs with contract requirements by supporting David in maintaining reliable operations, coordinating staff activities, and keeping compliance, maintenance, and reporting on track.

*"I've always believed the best leaders stay connected to the work and to the people doing it. This contract brings together my Oklahoma roots and experience supporting reliable O&M across multiple facilities."*

## Steve Carpenter, Project Director

**Steve** will serve as a senior, independent resource to [CLIENT], providing oversight, technical guidance, and performance insight. Positioned between [CLIENT] and David on the organization chart, he'll increase [CLIENT] leadership's visibility into compliance, risk, and continuous improvement. Steve brings **29 years of water and wastewater utility experience**, including leadership of multi-facility O&M programs and direct management of large municipal treatment systems, **including as Plant Manager II for the 75-MGD Lake Hefner WTP in Oklahoma**. He operated the facility in a base load capacity at approximately **96% for 2 continuous years with no significant interruptions to service**. Steve will strengthen accountability, support transition and optimization efforts, and help align day-to-day operations with long-term system performance.

*"I'll support the project team and help [CLIENT] see risks and opportunities early. This partnership gives us the chance to pair strong day-to-day leadership with experienced guidance."*

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-role architecture — an on-site PM as single point of accountability, an assistant PM carrying day-to-day delivery, and a project director positioned *between* the client and the PM so oversight is independent rather than supervisory. That positioning sentence ("Positioned between [CLIENT] and David on the organization chart, he'll increase [CLIENT] leadership's visibility into compliance, risk, and continuous improvement") is the single most reusable line here and is worth lifting wholesale for any pursuit that offers an independent director.

Pursuit-specific: all names, year counts, facility references, and the quotes, which must be re-collected from the actual proposed individuals.
