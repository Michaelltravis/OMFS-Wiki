# HR Commitment — Award-Winning Culture and an Integrated Local + National HR Team

**We invest in our people because they're our greatest assets.**

## We bring an award-winning culture aligned with [CLIENT]'s values

We cultivate a workforce that feels respected, informed, and proud to serve our clients, which we'll carry forward to the region. For this project, our culture will promote higher engagement and retention, smoother union relations, clearer communication, and a stronger connection between employees and the community.

Jacobs consistently ranks among top firms in the industry, including *Forbes'* 2025 list of America's Best Employers — which tracks companies that are navigating unprecedented changes in the business landscape. Jacobs' culture aligns to published research that indicates employees value compensation, development, and advancement opportunities, meaningful work, supportive colleagues, and workplace well-being and flexibility. (www.forbes.com/lists/best-large-employers)

## HR built for [CLIENT] — project-specific, local resources, and backed by national reach

As illustrated in **Exhibit II-2**, our HR team will be integrated with onsite leadership and staff, reporting to Rick Warner, our Deputy Project Manager. We'll drive success through the following structure:

- **Integrated recruiting model (local + national):** Brooke Winters and Joette Anderson will post positions through Jacobs' centralized recruiting system; our corporate recruiters will widen the candidate pool and fast-track screening, offers, and onboarding.
- **Union coordination:** Jeff Williams will lead coordination with [CLIENT] leadership and unions to ensure we evaluate, interview, and onboard existing employees consistent with the collective bargaining agreement (CBA).
- **Hiring halls and local partners:** Where applicable, we'll use union apprenticeship programs, union hiring halls, and local trade partnerships to fill skilled roles quickly. This will foster more ownership of work quality.
- **Surge coverage:** Our regional bench and national O&M SMEs can provide interim coverage during peaks, wet-weather events, or outages. We'll work with the union to provide emergency coverage for unexpectedly vacant positions and avoid falling below minimum staffing levels.
