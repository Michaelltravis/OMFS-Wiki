# Wastewater Career Internship Pipeline Program (Exhibit II-19)

One successful program we propose to replicate under this [CLIENT] contract is a **Wastewater Career Internship Pipeline Program**, which we designed to create a direct pathway for high school graduates to enter apprenticeships or entry-level positions in wastewater operations and maintenance activities, laboratory services, and green infrastructure (groundskeeping and BMP maintenance). The program transitions into paid internships that can lead to long-term careers in the water sector. The program consists of two phases, as described in **Exhibit II-19**.

**Exhibit II-19. Wastewater Career Internship Pipeline Program.**

**_Phase 1: Identification and Engagement (High School Seniors)_**

- Introduce students to technical and administrative roles in wastewater facility management
- Partner with Title I schools and community organizations
- Host career awareness sessions and facility tours
- Offer pre-internship workshops on safety basics, environmental careers, and soft skills
- Prioritize students from underrepresented communities
- Collect applications and maintain a candidate roster for post-graduation placement

**_Phase 2: Internship Placement_**

- Students begin paid internships for mechanics, treatment plant operators, green infrastructure technicians, laboratory technicians, and administrative assistants
- Build pathways to union apprenticeships, entry level positions, and post-secondary education
- Duration: 8–10 weeks (summer) or semester-long

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the entire two-phase structure. Phase 1 builds the candidate roster before there is a vacancy; Phase 2 places paid interns and names the three exits. This is the most transferable workforce artifact in the section because it is specific enough to be credible and generic enough to run anywhere.

Pursuit-specific: the intern role list, the school partnerships, and the exit into a particular union's apprenticeship. Where the client runs its own internship program, position this as an extension of theirs rather than a parallel program.
