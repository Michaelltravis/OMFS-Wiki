## Reuse guidance

Universal: the table shape (firm, role) kept to four or fewer rows so each entry reads as deliberate rather than as a vendor list, and the practice of naming an individual alongside a firm when that person's knowledge of the client's system is the reason for the teaming.

Pursuit-specific: every row. Rebuild the roster from the pursuit's geography and the client's certified-firm directory, and check that each named firm has a signed teaming or subconsultant agreement before it appears in a submitted proposal.
