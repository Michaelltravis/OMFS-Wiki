# Workforce Development Team, Subconsultant Roles, and Transparent Reporting

### 2. Dedicated Workforce Development Team and Reporting

Rick Warner, Director of Regional Partnerships, will direct workforce development planning. Toyin Ogunfolaju will report to Rick and manage workforce development program planning and implementation. Our workforce development team will collaborate with our HR and operations leadership team and [CLIENT] to align career development initiatives with staffing needs, and to assure that internship and other entry-level trainees are onboarded in accordance with our hiring guidelines.

- PRISM will provide workforce development coordination and support our team in program planning, partnerships, tracking, and reporting.
- P3 will support our career awareness outreach efforts and capacity building activities.

We'll provide [CLIENT] with transparent reporting regarding workforce development that defines and tracks measurable outcomes. We'll submit a quarterly report/review to the governance committees and an annual report to [CLIENT] to maximize transparency, compliance, and accountability. The Workforce Development Coordinator will submit updates to the annual Workforce Development Plan by January 31 of each contract year and an annual report by March 31 of each contract year that details workforce development activities from the previous contract. **Exhibit II-21** provides examples of our outreach and mentoring strategies to help small businesses develop.

**Jacobs supports the growth of emerging small businesses through creative outreach and mentoring. Small businesses represent on average 30-35% of our total supply chain spend.**

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the structure — a director who owns the program, a coordinator who runs it, subconsultants with distinct and non-overlapping roles, and a two-deliverable reporting cadence with fixed calendar dates. The 30–35% supply-chain figure is a corporate proof point that travels.

Pursuit-specific: the named individuals, the subconsultant assignments, the governance committees receiving the quarterly review, and the submission dates.
