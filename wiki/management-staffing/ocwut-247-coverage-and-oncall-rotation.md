# 24/7 Facility Coverage and On-Call Escalation

## 24/7 facility coverage

North Canadian, Deer Creek, Chisholm Creek, and South Canadian will each be staffed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year with qualified operators. Our shift structure will use 8-hour shifts organized into overlapping groups to provide complete coverage while managing overtime costs and maintaining staff wellbeing. This model has proven effective at our similarly sized multi-facility contracts, where it provides adequate overlap for shift exchanges, sampling continuity, and communication between crews.

**We'll maintain day, night, weekend, and holiday coverage through a structured relief rotation.** We'll stagger supervisory coverage to extend leadership presence across shifts. During vacations, sick leave, and other planned or unplanned absences, we'll maintain coverage through trained relief operators, cross-qualified staff from other facilities, and if needed, regional bench resources deployed on short notice.

In the final staffing and training plan, we will provide detailed shift schedule tables, which are too lengthy to provide in this proposal. They will reflect the shift schedules described above.

## On-call rotation and emergency response staffing

We will implement an on-call rotation covering operations, maintenance, instrumentation and controls, and management escalation to keep all [CLIENT] facilities staffed with properly trained and qualified personnel at all times (Schedule 10). The on-call structure will provide:

- **Operations coverage** with designated on-call operators for each facility, rotating on a defined schedule to prevent fatigue and maintain readiness
- **Maintenance and I&C/electrical response** with on-call technicians available to respond to equipment failures, SCADA alarms, and instrumentation issues
- **Management escalation** so that the project manager, assistant project manager, or designated manager will always be reachable for rapid decision making
- **Backup escalation** if the first-call responder is unavailable, with a clear chain of contact and response-time expectations

This on-call structure, illustrated in **Exhibit 5-4**, **will support rapid, confident response to weather events, process upsets, power outages, odor events, equipment failures, and other unplanned conditions** — protecting [CLIENT]'s compliance, assets, and community.

**Exhibit 5-4, On-Call Escalation Framework** (graphic asset 185_009385), presents the escalation ladder as four tiers: Tier 1, on-call operator/technician; Tier 2, facility operations or maintenance supervision; Tier 3, project manager/assistant project manager; Tier 4, regional/corporate level.
