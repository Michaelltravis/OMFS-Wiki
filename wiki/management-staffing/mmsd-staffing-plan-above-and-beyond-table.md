## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-column shape (client-required onsite FTE | added onsite FTE | added offsite FTE) with a bolded total row, and the practice of footnoting what the totals exclude and what the offsite hours buy. This is the fastest way to show an above-and-beyond staffing offer without a price. Pursuit-specific: every number, the category names (they should mirror the client's own staffing exhibit line for line), and the exhibit cross-references.
