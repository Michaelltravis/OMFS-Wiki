# Licensing, Certification, Regional Support, and Subcontractor Integration

We will obtain and maintain all operator and laboratory certifications required to operate [CLIENT]'s facilities in Schedule 12. Our leadership team will hold the required Oklahoma licenses by the operational start date. We'll actively support every team member's professional development throughout the contract term, including incentives and support for certification advancement, such as bonuses for advancement through progressively higher certification levels. We'll also establish structured career pathways from entry-level roles through licensed operator, specialized maintenance, and supervisory leadership positions with defined competencies, measurable promotion criteria, and clear expectations at each level.

Jacobs provides incentives and rewards for advanced certifications to elevate operations employees' skills, including covering training, preparation, and exam fees; potential promotions for operators-in-training that complete the 6-to-12-month OIT period and obtain their first operations or maintenance certification; and support for Advanced Water Treatment Operator (AWTO)® certifications. We will help staff reach the next level and reinforce best practices that maximize asset life by driving certifications, including the Certified Maintenance Reliability Technician (CMRT) program provided by the Society of Maintenance and Reliability Professionals, the Certified Reliability Leader (CRL) program provided by the Association of Asset Management Professionals, and potential bonuses for advancement in those programs.

Our on-site team won't operate in isolation. We'll supplement [CLIENT] staff with 7,800+ hours per year of **dedicated support from our regional O&M resources and national SMEs**—providing depth, resilience, and specialized expertise that no standalone staffing model can match. This support structure includes regional operations support for temporary staffing coverage, emergencies, and specialized operational assistance; maintenance and asset-management SMEs for hands-on instruction, reliability assessments, predictive-maintenance support, and asset-management planning; process-engineering and optimization SMEs for troubleshooting, data-driven optimization, and process analysis; and compliance, safety, and regulatory SMEs for audit support, regulatory strategy, and training.

Specialized and emergency support for odor control, biosolids management, I&C/SCADA, energy optimization, and other specialty needs will be available from our national network of 40,000+ professionals. **This regional depth will be a strategic advantage for [CLIENT].** We'll solve problems more quickly and cover vacancies reliably, and the on-site team will have a safety net that prevents them from being overwhelmed during emergencies, major maintenance events, or unexpected staff departures.

Our staff will self-perform most O&M services at [CLIENT]'s facilities. Between our on-site workforce and regional support resources, we have the capability to handle nearly all operational, maintenance, compliance, and administrative functions in-house, providing consistent quality, faster response times, and direct accountability. We will engage a key subcontractor, Synagro, for biosolids hauling and disposal, and may work with other subcontractors on specialty services outside our core O&M scope. We'll integrate all subcontractors into our safety, quality, and performance-management systems, and they'll comply with all applicable service-agreement requirements.
