# Dual-Management Model — Project Manager and Deputy Project Manager

Project Manager Kevin Dahl will be the direct point of contact for [CLIENT]. He'll hold regular meetings with his leadership team to set priorities, communicate direction from [CLIENT], review progress, and complete outstanding action items. Kevin will work with his team to see that decisions are made on a predictable schedule, changes are controlled and transparent, and staffing/performance sync with evolving system needs.

Deputy Project Manager Rick Warner will focus on the six support groups shown on Exhibit II-2, enabling Kevin to stay focused on O&M performance and delivery. Exhibit II-4 captures the management style and chemistry enjoyed by this powerful duo, which will contribute to enhanced collaboration and management of the team.

To reinforce our commitment to your 2035 Vision, enhance facility performance, and support [CLIENT] as a vanguard public utility provider, we've assigned Kevin Dahl and Rick Warner, two of our highest-caliber water management leaders, to serve as Project Manager and Deputy Project Manager. As local residents, they'll become an extension of [CLIENT], available 24/7.

## Value of a dual-management model

Kevin and Rick bring a leadership approach that aligns directly with [CLIENT]'s values and priorities. Kevin will lead day-to-day operations with a steady, hands-on presence, while Rick will focus on key disciplines that support operations and maintenance teams and the strategic regional partnership and public outreach work. We're elevating regional partnership and public outreach to the Deputy Project Manager level because they are two of the priorities you've shared with us.

## Chemistry

After a year working together on this procurement, Kevin and Rick understand [CLIENT]'s goals and contract requirements. They've built a friendship and partnership that will result in more effective contract management for [CLIENT].

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the two-leader split and the three arguments that justify it — (1) the PM stays undiluted on O&M performance because the Deputy owns the support groups, (2) the client's own stated priorities are elevated to the Deputy's job title, and (3) the pair have already worked together, so the client is not paying for a new relationship to form. The "Chemistry" subhead is a small, disarming device worth keeping.

Pursuit-specific: names, the number of support groups, the priorities elevated to Deputy level, the length of the working relationship, and the residency/24-7 availability commitment. If the Deputy's second hat is a different discipline (asset management, compliance, industrial pretreatment), swap it and keep the logic.
