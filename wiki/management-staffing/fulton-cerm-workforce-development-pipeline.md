# CERM-Led Workforce Development and Technical-College Pipeline

JC Solutions is committed to building and sustaining a high-performing operations team at [CLIENT]’s facilities. Through ongoing professional development, workforce partnerships, and a dedicated staffing strategy, we ensure operators are prepared, empowered, and supported at every stage of their careers.

CERM brings deep expertise in workforce development, with a strong focus on cultivating the next generation of water utility professionals. The firm co-developed Georgia Piedmont Technical College’s Water Utility Apprenticeship program and Atlanta Technical College’s Civil Engineering Technologist program—both designed to meet the evolving needs of the water sector.

As an executive board member of The Water Tower Institute, CERM also plays a leadership role in advancing workforce training, practical research, and innovation. Operators in the [CLIENT] service area will benefit from access to these programs, which connect hands-on experience with formal instruction to support professional growth and operational excellence.

In collaboration with both institutions, JC Solutions will contribute to curriculum development focused on the industry's longterm needs—including maintenance, operations, laboratory work, and management. Our vision is to create more pathways for potential candidates to enter and thrive in the water sector, ultimately ensuring there is never a shortage of qualified resources.

JC Solutions intends to build on these strong relationships by partnering with both Georgia Piedmont Technical College and Atlanta Technical College to expand course offerings and workforce pipelines. Preliminary discussions have already begun to develop a new Class III Operator certification course, ensuring a continual supply of well-trained entry-level operators for [CLIENT]. The [CLIENT] will directly benefit from this proactive investment in talent development, gaining access to a growing pool of trained and job-ready professionals who are prepared to meet the demands of modern utility operations.

Lead by CERM, the JC Solutions team will partner with regional technical colleges to build an effective Workforce Development Program that includes Class III Operator certification courses and expands water/wastewater training opportunities, creating a continuous pipeline of qualified operators. The Program will include a comprehensive internship program for local residents with structured rotations and mentorship in plant operations, asset management, and community engagement. Our goal for the Workforce Development Program is to enhance operational excellence through a skilled local workforce trained specifically for [CLIENT] water utility needs while providing a strong community investment with preferential hiring of [CLIENT] residents and sustainable career pathways that address industry staffing challenges.
