# Staff Certification, Training, and Advancement Incentive Program

## Baseline Certification and Training Standard

A staffing plan should ensure adequate staffing in compliance with permit requirements and staff certification requirements. Leadership credentials should meaningfully exceed the minimum licensing requirement for the facility (e.g., key leadership holding a higher-grade wastewater operator license than the facility's minimum requirement) to demonstrate depth of oversight capability.

Baseline expectations for all staff:

- Operators and collections technicians obtain and maintain all required state licenses.
- Collections staff hold applicable industry certifications (e.g., NASSCO PACP/MACP for pipeline assessment).
- All staff complete OSHA 10 (or greater) safety training with documented certification.
- Startup/onboarding training includes site-specific standard operating procedures (SOPs), high-flow and bypass preparation, storm preparation, odor response, and emergency response procedures to ensure Day 1 operational readiness.

## Operator Certification Incentives

Provide meaningful incentives that encourage operators to pursue advanced certifications and continue developing their skills:

- Training preparation and exam fee coverage.
- Defined career-advancement pathways for operators-in-training (OITs) who complete the required OIT period (e.g., 6-12 months) and obtain full certification.
- Certification bonuses for achieving higher license/certification levels.

## Maintenance Certification Programs

Provide similar support to maintenance staff to advance technical expertise and apply best practices that extend asset life and improve reliability, through certification programs such as maintenance-and-reliability professional credentials (e.g., CMRT) and asset-management credentials (e.g., CRL), with incentives for achieving higher certification levels.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the framing of certification/training as a retention and compliance-risk-reduction tool (not just a compliance checkbox), the OIT career-pathway concept, and the certification-bonus incentive mechanic are reusable for any O&M staffing plan.

Pursuit-specific: swap in the actual state licensing classes and any discipline-specific certifications relevant to the pursuit's collection system/treatment processes; confirm actual incentive mechanics offered by the pursuing business unit before committing to specific bonus/fee-coverage language in a live proposal (no dollar figures are included here). Pair with `key-personnel-role-descriptions.md` for how these credentials show up in leadership qualifications.
