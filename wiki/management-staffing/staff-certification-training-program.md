# Staff Certification, Training, and Advancement Incentive Program

## Leadership Credentials

Project Manager Nathan Callison and Assistant Project Manager Tony Rose each hold a Massachusetts Class 6C Wastewater Operator License, **exceeding the licensing requirements for [CLIENT]'s facility.**

## Staff Certifications and Training

Operators and collections technicians will obtain and maintain **all required Massachusetts licenses**, collections staff will hold **NASSCO PACP/MACP certifications**, and all staff will complete **OSHA 10 or greater safety training** with documented certification. Startup training includes site-specific standard operating procedures (SOPs), high-flow and bypass plans, storm preparation, odor response, and emergency response procedures to ensure **Day 1 operational readiness**.

## Operator Certification Incentives

Jacobs provides meaningful incentives that encourage operators to pursue advanced certifications and continue developing their skills. We support this through training, preparation, and exam fee coverage; career advancement opportunities for operators-in-training (OITs) who complete the 6- to 12-month OIT period and obtain certification; and certification bonuses for achieving higher license levels.

## Maintenance Certification Programs

Jacobs provides similar support to maintenance staff to advance their technical expertise and apply best practices that **extend asset life and improve reliability**. We support this through certification programs such as CMRT (Society of Maintenance and Reliability Professionals) and CRL (Association of Asset Management Professionals), along with incentives for achieving certification levels.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the four-part structure — leadership credentials that exceed the facility's minimum licensing requirement, a baseline certification and training standard for all staff, operator certification incentives, and maintenance/reliability certification incentives — applies to any O&M staffing plan. The framing of certification and training as a retention and compliance-risk-reduction tool (rather than a compliance checkbox), the OIT career-advancement pathway, and the certification-bonus mechanic are directly reusable.

Pursuit-specific: substitute the state licensing classes actually required at the target facility (this pursuit's leadership held Massachusetts Class 6C, above the facility's requirement) and any collection-system or process-specific certifications; confirm which named leaders hold which licenses before repeating the "exceeds the requirement" claim; verify current OIT period length and incentive mechanics with the operating business unit.
