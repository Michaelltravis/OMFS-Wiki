# I&C Technician and Transition Manager Profiles

## Robert Earl Coons, I&C Technician

**Robert** will support instrumentation, electrical, and control system reliability across [CLIENT] facilities, maintaining and troubleshooting systems that keep treatment processes and facilities running reliably. Robert has **17 years of maintenance and electrical experience**, including regional support for treatment facilities and utility infrastructure. He is a **journeyman electrician with hands-on experience in preventive maintenance, electrical troubleshooting, testing with multimeters and other diagnostic devices, generators, transfer switches, transformers, and large main gear insulation and maintenance.** He supports maintenance and asset management in treatment facilities across Texas and Oklahoma. He provided **commissioning for Houston's 320-MGD Northeast Water Purification Plant and supported transition and startup at the Bixby Water Reclamation Facility and lift stations**. Robert will support preventive maintenance and respond to instrumentation and equipment issues that affect day-to-day plant and pump station performance.

*"Reliable I&C are essential to consistent plant performance. Drawing on hands-on experience, I take a proactive approach to maintenance and troubleshooting to keep critical systems operating at their best. I'll provide dependable support that minimizes downtime, improves reliability, and helps [CLIENT] maintain safe, efficient operations."*

## Kelly Irving, Transition Manager

**Kelly** has **30 years of experience in water and wastewater operations, management, and executive leadership**, with extensive experience leading complex O&M transitions. She recently **led transition of the Jackson water system in Mississippi,** where she coordinated workforce continuity, regulatory readiness, and operational startup in a high-risk environment under federal oversight while helping maintain compliant, uninterrupted service. She **supported transition of the West Basin recycled water facilities in California**, aligning staffing, asset readiness, and operational coordination across a multi-facility system serving municipal and industrial customers. Earlier in her career, she **led transition of a 225-MGD Detroit area regional wastewater system serving 13 communities** before assuming the role of General Manager. Her strengths in startup planning, workforce alignment, and operational readiness will help position the system for strong day-one performance.

*"The key to a successful transition is communication — across the transition team, with staff, and with the client. Just as important is having a dedicated team in place to support startup. When people are informed, aligned, and supported, you create the stability and confidence needed for a successful day-one operation."*

## Reuse guidance

Universal: naming a dedicated Transition Manager as a distinct person from the Project Manager, with a bio built entirely out of prior transitions at comparable or harder scale — and stating what each transition had to protect (workforce continuity, regulatory readiness, uninterrupted service). The escalating sequence used here — a system under federal oversight, then a multi-facility recycled water system, then a 225-MGD regional system serving 13 communities — is a strong ordering to copy.

Universal for I&C: pairing the trade credential (journeyman electrician) with a specific equipment list, then a commissioning credential at large scale. Pursuit-specific: names, years, states, and the quotes.
