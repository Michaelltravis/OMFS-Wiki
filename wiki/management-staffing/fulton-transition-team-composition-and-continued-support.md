# Transition Team Composition and Continued SME Support

**Transition Team.** To ensure the success of this important partnership, we offer an onsite transition leadership team led by Transition Manager **Mike Boven**, one of our most seasoned transition managers with a proven track record of smooth transitions. By having a fully staffed transition team, shown in **Exhibit 2-46**, we minimize distractions to the onsite team so that they can focus on day-to-day operations while JC Solutions' systems, tools, and processes are implemented along with training the onsite team.

The transition team is comprised of seasoned experts who have successfully transitioned numerous O&M facilities and who bring specific expertise in operations, maintenance, asset management, safety, SCADA control, sludge management, odor control, human resources, labor relations, and administration support. They will remain available after the transition period for questions, training, follow-up assistance, and continued support to the onsite project staff.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the discipline roll-call plus the post-transition availability commitment, which converts a transition team from a temporary cost into ongoing bench depth.
