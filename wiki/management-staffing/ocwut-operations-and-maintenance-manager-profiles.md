# Operations Manager and Maintenance Manager Profiles

## Paul Shropshire, Operations Manager — Deer Creek

**Paul** will lead operations at **Deer Creek**, overseeing process performance, compliance, and day-to-day coordination. He has **37 years of experience in wastewater treatment and pumping** and 7 years of operational and mechanical experience. As Senior Operations Manager at the **City of San Marcos WWTP**, he manages operations, maintenance, compliance, and regulatory responsibilities for a 24/7 operation; 11 staff; a $2.8M annual budget; flood response; and supports capital improvement planning. As **Division Manager of Wastewater Treatment for Austin Water, he managed two 75-MGD WWTPs and the Hornsby Bend biosolids facility, with indirect oversight of 132 skilled professionals** and a combined budget of $27.4M. He managed 126 wastewater pump stations, five remote WWTPs, and two water reclamation plants. Paul offers plant leadership, compliance and budget discipline, and operational knowledge across treatment plants, biosolids, pump stations, and reuse systems. His municipal wastewater operations and emergency response expertise will help keep Deer Creek operating reliably while supporting future improvements and performance.

*"Good plant leadership means focusing on the issues that affect performance most. I'm excited about the opportunity to strengthen Deer Creek's reliability while helping remove solids, reduce odors, and facilitate future improvements."*

## Roy Aristizabal, Operations Manager — South Canadian

**Roy** will lead plant operations, process control, compliance, and staff performance at **South Canadian**. Roy brings **25 years of water and wastewater experience** in large-facility operations and regulatory compliance. He supports technical problem solving, permit excursion investigations, management systems, and **commissioning and startup, including the 320-MGD Northeast Water Purification Plant expansion**. In leadership roles including Regional Director, Regional Manager, and Operations Support Director, he managed multi-project operations teams, supported operational improvements, and coordinated CIP implementation across treatment facilities, pump stations, and major transmission projects. As Operations Manager and Compliance Manager for SouthWest Water, he managed daily operations across the Houston area, 120 staff, and 140 service contracts while serving as a commissioning and startup manager for an 80-MGD expansion project. Roy will help maintain stable plant performance while supporting future improvements and operational readiness. He'll bring a disciplined approach to compliance and support both day-to-day plant performance and major system changes.

*"My management style is built around helping teams stay ready for change. I'm excited to support reliable operations today while helping South Canadian prepare for startup, transition, and long-term success."*

## Brian Daniels, Maintenance Manager

**Brian** will lead maintenance execution across [CLIENT]'s facilities, focusing on equipment reliability, preventive and predictive maintenance, and timely response to critical issues. He brings **30 years of maintenance experience supporting wastewater facilities, biosolids management, and 50+ lift stations**. He has led maintenance operations across mechanical and electrical systems, managed CMMS work orders, directed and scheduled staff, and applied predictive tools including thermography, vibration analysis, ultrasound, and motor testing. At Fayetteville Utilities, he **supported maintenance for two wastewater facilities, a satellite facility, and 56 lift stations** and was responsible for electrical systems, panels, conduit installations, and equipment diagnostics. He **supports projects in Bixby, Gallup, and Pampa with maintenance, electrical troubleshooting, and arc flash training.** Brian will help strengthen maintenance planning and field execution, improve equipment uptime, and support a more proactive approach to asset care across the plants, biosolids systems, and pump stations.

*"Maintenance should never be stuck in reaction mode. I'll help the team stay ahead of problems, use data to make smarter decisions, and protect equipment before failures affect operations. With [CLIENT]'s scale and complexity, a strong maintenance approach will make a visible difference."*

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the scale-matching move — every operations bio names a prior assignment whose plant count, staff count, and budget are at or above the pursuit's own scale, so the evaluator never has to wonder whether the person has run something this big. The maintenance bio's naming of four specific predictive technologies (thermography, vibration analysis, ultrasound, motor testing) is a reusable credibility device wherever a maintenance program is being promised.

Pursuit-specific: names, years, plant assignments, and the quotes. Reference clients (San Marcos, Austin Water and Hornsby Bend, Northeast Water Purification Plant, SouthWest Water, Fayetteville Utilities, Bixby, Gallup, Pampa) are kept verbatim and should be QC'd before external use.
