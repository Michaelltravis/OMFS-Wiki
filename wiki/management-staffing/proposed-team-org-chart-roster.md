## Reuse guidance

Universal: the two-table presentation (on-site positions with explicit FTE fractions summing to a stated total, then off-site disciplines with named specialists) is the clearest way to answer an RFP's "adequate staffing" and "lines of authority" requirements, and the 0.25-FTE intern/seasonal line shows how a small contract funds surge and development capacity without a full headcount.

Pursuit-specific: FTE counts scale with plant capacity, collection-system miles, and permit sampling load — this roster supported a 3.07-MGD WWTF plus a 42-mile collection system at 6.25 on-site FTE.
