What sets Jacobs apart is our deep bench of SMEs who can support our on-site teams to resolve issues quickly and provide innovative solutions. They bring expertise in regulatory compliance, asset management, SCADA, process optimization, and biosolids, among many other disciplines, and leverage lessons learned from years working with visionary clients like [CLIENT]. These resources **(Exhibit 3-7)** will be fully accessible throughout the partnership, ready to deploy specialized expertise whenever needed.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the framing sentence ("What sets us apart is our deep bench of SMEs who can support our on-site teams to resolve issues quickly"), the promise that the bench is fully accessible throughout the partnership at no extra step for the client, and the exhibit device of naming a person against every discipline the client might ever need — including the unglamorous ones (HR support, procurement, grants and funding) that signal the operator is backed by a full-service firm.

Pursuit-specific: every name, the state regulator row (ODEQ Coordination), and the legend flags for prior client experience and out-of-state PE licensure. The SME team lead entry should be re-cut so its bullets point at the new client's actual priorities.
