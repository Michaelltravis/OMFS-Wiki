# Staffing Coverage, Shift, and On-Call Structure

## Staffing Plan

Our staffing plan ensures **adequate staffing of the facilities** in compliance with the NPDES permit requirements and to meet its staff certification requirements, as demonstrated in our Key Staff and Technical Support Team profiles.

## Coverage, Shifts, and On-Call Structure

- **WWTF Base Coverage:** Monday through Friday, 7 a.m. – 3:30 p.m. (adjustable to [CLIENT] preference), with staggered start/stop coverage during construction or high-flow conditions.
- **Weekend/Holiday Coverage:** Two-person rounds daily; sampling and compliance requirements per permit.
- **24/7 On-Call:** One WWTF operator and one Collections Technician on rotation, with ≤20-minute phone response and ≤60-minute site response, meeting RFP requirements.
- **Storm Events:** Pre-staged teams at the WWTF and critical pump stations (on this pursuit, L Street and PS #3/#5), with provisions and sleeping arrangements maintained for extended events.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: framing coverage this way — a named weekday base window, explicit weekend rounds, and quantified on-call response-time commitments — gives evaluators a concrete, auditable answer rather than a general statement that the facility will be staffed appropriately. The four-tier structure (Base Coverage / Weekend-Holiday / 24-7 On-Call / Storm Events), the practice of stating explicit phone- and site-response time commitments and tying them back to the RFP's stated requirement, and the pre-staging-with-sleeping-provisions detail for extended weather events.

Pursuit-specific: adjust the base coverage window to the facility's actual operating hours and the client's stated preference; recalculate the ≤20-minute phone and ≤60-minute site response commitments to meet or beat whatever the specific RFP specifies; name the pursuit's own critical pump stations in the storm-event bullet; adjust emergency provisions to the region's actual extreme-weather risk profile (coastal storm surge versus inland flooding versus wildfire-driven power outages); confirm on-call rotation depth against the actual proposed FTE count.
