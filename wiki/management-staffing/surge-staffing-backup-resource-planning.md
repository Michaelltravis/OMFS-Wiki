# Surge Staffing and Backup Resource Availability

## Concept

Off-site regional and technical resources represent a portion of a broader internal bench available to support continuous, safe, compliant, and reliable operations. These resources provide technical expertise, supplemental staffing for special projects or innovation/process-optimization work, and can be **rapidly mobilized to respond to surge conditions**, including emergencies or severe weather events.

## Absence and Vacancy Coverage

- Planned absences and short-term vacancies are covered by qualified personnel from nearby projects or the regional/national staff pool.
- For unexpected absences, trained personnel are deployed from across the O&M organization to maintain safe and compliant operations with minimal disruption.

## Dedicated Resource Planning Function

Maintaining a **dedicated Resource Planning Group (RPG)** focused on attracting, developing, and retaining high-performing O&M staff is a strong differentiator. In coordination with the regional management team, this function supports rapid mobilization of qualified personnel to address both planned and unplanned staffing needs — essentially, an internal bench-depth guarantee that sits behind the named on-site and regional-support staff shown in the org chart.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the concept of naming a specific internal resource-planning function (rather than describing surge staffing only in the abstract) and the layered coverage logic (nearby projects → regional pool → national pool) are reusable for any O&M pursuit where evaluators will ask "what happens when someone is out or a crisis hits."

Pursuit-specific: verify the actual name and scope of the sponsoring business unit's resource-planning function before using this language in a live proposal; do not overstate mobilization speed/scope beyond what the region can actually support. Pair with `staffing-coverage-shift-oncall-plan.md` (which this function backs up) and `regional-technical-support-bench.md` (subcontractor governance for capability gaps outside the internal bench).
