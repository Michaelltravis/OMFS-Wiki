# Backup Resource Availability to Augment On-Site Staff and Fill Temporary Positions

The off-site Regional and Technical Resources shown in **Exhibit 4-1** represent a portion of Jacobs' broader internal bench available to support **continuous, safe, compliant, and reliable operations**. These resources provide technical expertise, supplemental staffing for special projects, or support for innovation and process optimization, and can be **rapidly mobilized to respond to surge conditions, including emergencies or severe weather events**.

Planned absences and short-term vacancies will be covered by qualified personnel from nearby Jacobs projects or our regional and national staff pool. For unexpected absences, we will deploy trained personnel from across our O&M organization to **maintain safe and compliant operations with minimal disruption**.

Jacobs maintains a **dedicated Resource Planning Group (RPG)** focused on attracting, developing, and retaining high-performing O&M staff. In coordination with our regional management team, the RPG supports **rapid mobilization of qualified personnel to address both planned and unplanned staffing needs**.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: naming a specific internal resource-planning function (rather than describing surge staffing only in the abstract) and the layered coverage logic — nearby projects, then the regional pool, then the national O&M organization — are reusable for any O&M pursuit where evaluators will ask what happens when someone is out or a crisis hits. The framing of the off-site org-chart tier as "a portion of a broader internal bench" turns the chart itself into evidence of depth.

Pursuit-specific: verify the current name and scope of the sponsoring business unit's resource-planning function before using this language in a live proposal; do not overstate mobilization speed or scope beyond what the region can actually support; point the Exhibit reference at the pursuit's own org chart.
