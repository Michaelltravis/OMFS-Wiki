# PM Schedule Discipline, CM Rapid Response, and PdM Integration

**PM staffing and schedule discipline.** We'll assign PM staff based on the asset-criticality scale we develop together, including the skill sets required for each task. Supervisors will be accountable for weekly completion and documentation standards in the CMMS. Our planners will manage a rolling 4- to 6-week planning cycle that locks in craft hours, access windows, and outage coordination—ensuring high-priority PMs are completed on time without disrupting critical corrective work.

To keep crews ready and productive, procurement and warehouse staff will participate in weekly planning, pre-kit jobs, and manage inventory aligned with the schedule. Pairing planning with kitting ensures maintenance teams are never stalled waiting for parts or materials, an essential factor in consistently meeting PM schedules.

**CM staffing — rapid response without diminishing PM.** We'll classify and manage CM under the contract's Level A/B model. Closeout will capture failure mode, root cause, corrective action, and steps to avoid recurrence. Supervisors will triage issues and assign work promptly, while the planner/scheduler will preserve the PM plan by slotting CM into reserved capacity and using the kits/parts pipeline to minimize downtime.

We'll prepare for wet weather/emergency coverage and minimize impacts on the overall PM schedule. This includes storm rosters, on-call rotations, and clear activation triggers coordinated with Operations. Details on wet weather/emergencies will be documented in our Emergency Response Plan, provided in *Section IV.A.4 Safety & Security*.

**PdM integration will maximize PM value.** We'll provide experts to work with the onsite maintenance teams to make sure [CLIENT]'s desired Level 3 PdM additive elements are optimized to deliver the maximum PM benefits of these tools. These experts, as illustrated in **Exhibit II-9**, will be fully integrated with the onsite maintenance teams and available to address challenges and support opportunities to elevate and expand these tools as [CLIENT] desires in the future.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-part answer to "how will you actually hold the PM ratio" — criticality-driven assignment with supervisor accountability, a rolling 4- to 6-week planning cycle with kitting, and reserved schedule capacity that absorbs corrective work instead of displacing preventive work. The storm-roster/on-call/activation-trigger sentence is a compact wet-weather staffing answer for any collection-system or high-flow pursuit.

Pursuit-specific: the CM classification model and its naming; the PdM maturity level the client has asked for and the technologies in scope; cross-references to the pursuit's own maintenance and emergency response sections.
