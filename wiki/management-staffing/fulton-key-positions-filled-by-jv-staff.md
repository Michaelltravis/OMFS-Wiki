# Leadership from JC Solutions Staff — Key Positions and Regional Backfill

## Leadership from JC Solutions Staff

A few key staff positions will be filled by JC Solutions employees and may include qualified existing employees as approved or supported by [CLIENT], a Southeast US county public works department. In the event of vacancies, we will leverage regional resources to ensure all positions are filled by skilled personnel on day one of the partnership.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-part staffing commitment — company-employed leadership, openness to retaining qualified incumbent employees with client concurrence, and regional resources as the vacancy backstop so every position is filled at commencement. This is the sentence that answers an evaluator's "what if you cannot hire enough people" question in a single move, and it belongs immediately after the compensation/retention material and before the transition team roster.

Pursuit-specific: the entity name (JC Solutions is the Jacobs/CERM JV for this pursuit), the client descriptor, and which positions are designated key personnel under the RFP.
