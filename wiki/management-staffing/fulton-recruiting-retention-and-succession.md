# Recruiting, Retention, and Succession Planning

Jacobs’ dedicated Resource Planning Group focuses on attracting, developing, and retaining top talent in the O&M industry. Over the past year, their efforts have doubled new hires, reduced turnover, and helped promote high-performing staff into new roles.

**Internal Transfers:** Open positions will be offered to current qualified Jacobs and CERM O&M staff, many of whom view [CLIENT] as a highly desirable location. We promote internal mobility through our digital platforms and provide relocation assistance where needed.

**External Recruiting:** Our full-time recruiting team leverages mobile-optimized tools and platforms targeted to O&M professionals. We also engage with veteran hiring programs such as Hiring Our Heroes and use targeted social media outreach to attract skilled talent.

**Retention and Succession:** In addition to structured training and certification incentives, we offer advancement pathways, performance recognition, and leadership development programs—creating a long-term strategy to support continuity and institutional knowledge.

Through this comprehensive approach, we will ensure [CLIENT] has a stable, skilled, and future-ready workforce.
