# JC Solutions JV Team Organization and Key Personnel

Our team for Operation & Maintenance Services for Wastewater Facilities & Pump Stations is composed of highly experienced and dedicated professionals. Project Manager Anthony Benavidez, Operations & Compliance Manager Devon Trezevant, Maintenance Manager Nathan Dahl, [FACILITY C] Plant Manager David Pitocchelli, [FACILITY B] Plant Manager Nathan Callison, and [FACILITY A] Plant Manager Mark Huggard will be fully dedicated to this project. We have also identified additional key personnel in Senior Supervisor Tim Durham, Senior Strategic Supervisor Benjamin Bagwell, Workforce Development and Training Lead Terrell Gibbs, and MBR Specialist and Wastewater Process Lead Scott Levesque, who will be instrumental in delivery.

Together, this team is committed to delivering high-quality and reliable services to [CLIENT]. Regional technical subject matter experts include John Karabias in Cybersecurity, John Rickermann in Intelligent O&M, Adrian Romero-Flores in Odor Control, and Mary Ann Rouse as Compliance and Reporting Manager. Transition Manager Mike Boven plays a crucial role in providing a seamless transition. Key personnel, subject matter experts, and support staff form a robust team dedicated to success.

Our organizational structure outlines a comprehensive staffing approach that meets and exceeds [CLIENT]’s requirements. We maintain current staffing levels and include key RFP-mandated roles. We do not anticipate a change to planned staff after year one. We intend to retain all existing employees that [CLIENT] desires, preserving institutional knowledge. For staff who do not wish to join the JC Solutions team or whom [CLIENT] does not wish to retain, we will provide staff for those positions.
