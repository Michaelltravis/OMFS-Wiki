# Transition Continuity — Addressing Transition Risk with a Proven Track Record

We recognize that transitioning to a new operator is a significant decision, and it's natural to have concerns—whether about potential impacts on service levels or the effect on the dedicated staff who keep your facilities running. We've addressed these considerations throughout our proposal so [CLIENT], a large Midwest regional sewerage district, can weigh them against the many benefits that Jacobs' fresh perspective, proven processes, and commitment to people bring to [CLIENT] and its customers. Jacobs is sensitive to the perception that risk is introduced during a transition, and we dedicate substantial resources to minimize impacts and respond to any challenge that may arise.

**We also encourage [CLIENT] to speak directly with clients who have selected Jacobs as their operating partner.** Their firsthand experience with our transition process, and the strong outcomes that followed, provides valuable insight into what partnering with Jacobs truly looks like.

## Continuity: enabling uninterrupted excellence in water services

**With Jacobs, transition is more than a handoff—it's the moment we begin building the foundation that will take your best-in-class utility to the next level. As we assume operational responsibility, we collaborate with [CLIENT] to ensure every step aligns with [CLIENT]'s priorities, values, and long-term vision. At the same time, we strengthen processes, elevate workforce capabilities, and introduce tools and practices that empower [CLIENT] and our joint staff for sustained success well beyond Day 1.**

Jacobs has successfully transitioned multiple complex water and wastewater systems that were previously operated by Veolia. Recent examples include West Basin Municipal Water District and Jackson Water, where Jacobs assumed operational responsibility while maintaining uninterrupted service and workforce continuity. In both cases, utility leadership has recognized the effectiveness of Jacobs' disciplined transition approach and the positive impact on employee engagement and operational confidence.

> Given the complexity of West Basin's operations, the transition period required extensive coordination and technical expertise. Jacobs deployed a deep bench of subject-matter experts and worked collaboratively with District staff to ensure a smooth transition from the previous contract operator of more than twenty years. Overall, the transition was managed very effectively, with minimal challenges, and resulted in a smooth and successful onboarding for a system of this complexity.
>
> **— Susanna Li, Manager of Engineering, West Basin Municipal Water District, 310.660.6238**

> In the throes of the water crisis, only Jacobs was willing to answer our calls for assistance. Since that time in December 2022, they have proven to be outstanding partners, delivering on their commitments and totally bought in to our shared mission of ensuring all Jacksonians have access to safe drinking water, all the time. Once again, Jacobs has stepped up and promised operational savings as they begin to operate our wastewater facilities. We look forward to expanding their mission, applying that same commitment to the people of Jackson with a focus on protecting public health and the environment with their wastewater expertise.
>
> **— Ted Henifin, JXN Water Interim Third Party Manager, 757.274.7904**

## Proven success transitioning complex projects

Jacobs brings real-world experience and an unmatched track record of success with the most complex and high-profile transitions in the industry. Our transition plan is based on more than 40 years of O&M delivery to leading utilities in the U.S., supported by refined processes and proven tools that allow us to transition projects consistently, confidently, and without disruption.

Our proven transition model, which focuses on supporting existing employees, has consistently produced exceptional outcomes. **In 2025, four transitions from Veolia resulted in an overwhelming number of staff choosing to join Jacobs, as shown on Exhibit IV-55.** These transitions included the West Basin Municipal Water District, the largest water reuse facility in North America with 58 FTEs, and the City of Jackson's wastewater system with 39 FTEs, which the incumbent operator highlighted as a reference project during [CLIENT]'s SOQ phase.

Our 200-point transition plan sequences critical activities—staffing, safety, SOP alignment, SCADA/IT readiness, asset-management integration, and other important activities well in advance of March 1, 2028. For [CLIENT], this translates into a structured, transparent, and proactive approach that ensures a seamless transfer of responsibility while safeguarding safety, compliance, and workforce stability.

**Jacobs recognizes that [CLIENT]'s top priorities during transition are uninterrupted operations, full regulatory compliance, and staff continuity.** [CLIENT]'s schedule reflects this commitment by allowing an unprecedented 18-month transition period, far exceeding the industry-standard 90 days. Combined with your procurement schedule and the extended shadowing period, this creates the conditions for an exceptionally smooth and low-risk transition.
