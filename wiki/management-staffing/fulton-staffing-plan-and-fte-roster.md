# Three-Facility Staffing Plan and FTE Roster

Our approach to delivery of the management, operations, and maintenance services of [CLIENT] facilities focuses on high-caliber leadership, reliability, compliance, and efficiency. Our staffing approach includes all of the required positions in the RFP, and we anticipate offering current qualified staff positions with JC Solutions.

Management, administrative, and safety staff will work eight-hour shifts, Monday through Friday. We will have two Administrative Assistants who will provide day-to-day support to the project, one located at [FACILITY A] and the other at [FACILITY B]. A Health & Safety Specialist will also be located onsite full-time and be responsible for all [CLIENT] facilities. At [FACILITY A] and [FACILITY B], the Plant Manager will work eight-hour shifts, Monday through Friday. Two day-shift operator groups will work 12-hour shifts across two weeks, ensuring three day-shift Operators onsite every day of the week. Two night-shift groups, each consisting of one Lead Operator and one Operator, follow the same shift patterns, ensuring two Operators are onsite every night shift of the week. At [FACILITY C], two day-shift Lead Operators alternate overlapping schedules, blending shorter shifts of 4 or 8 hours with longer 12-hour shifts to maintain daily supervisory coverage. Four day-shift Operators work rotating 12-hour shifts. Two night-shift Lead Operators alternate overnight supervision, each paired closely with a night-shift Operator who mirrors the rotation. This shift pattern provides balanced staffing at the smaller facility while ensuring a minimum of three day-shift Operators and two night-shift Operators seven days per week.

Maintenance staff will work eight-hour day shifts, with the majority Monday through Friday. One General Duty Mechanic and one Maintenance Assistance will work Sunday through Thursday, and a second General Duty Mechanic and Maintenance Assistance will work Tuesday through Saturday to provide weekend coverage. The Maintenance Manager will supervise the team, with support from the Maintenance Leads. The maintenance team will be responsible for all three plants, the lift stations, and water pump stations.
