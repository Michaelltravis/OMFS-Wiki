# Small Business Outreach, Mentor-Protégé, and Construction Academy (Exhibit II-21)

**Exhibit II-21. Creative outreach and mentoring strategies.**

Throughout the duration of this contract, we'll explore opportunities to support local workers and local businesses, and will lend our support, resources, and technical assistance to help small and local firms compete successfully for work under our O&M contract. Outreach to the small business community will be one of our strategies to identify local firm candidates to work on this contract. Accordingly, we hosted a Pre-Award Outreach Event in our local office in September 2025 to solicit interest in this O&M program.

> "It was a terrific opportunity to learn more about the important work that Jacobs is leading and to hear about the ways you are engaging partners to strengthen those efforts. I truly appreciated the thought and energy that went into the program, and I left with a deeper understanding of the potential opportunities ahead. Should the need ever arise, I would look forward to the opportunity to assist and help support Jacobs' efforts here in Wisconsin."
>
> — Pre-Award Outreach Event Participant

**Mentor-Protégé Program Queues up Subs for Future Work.** As the joint venture partner for Houston's $1.77B 320-MGD progressive design-build Northeast Water Purification Facility, we implemented a mentor-protégé program that spanned engineering through construction and commissioning phases. We trained engineering subconsultants in Bentley 3D and other design techniques for water treatment plants and tooled up construction subcontractors to allow them to win work on other projects. We also maintained a procurement portal targeting small and emerging firms and tailored construction work packages to subconsultant and subcontractor qualifications. For our successful engagement of small and diverse businesses, we were recognized as Construction Prime Contractor of the Year, City of Houston Champions of Diversity Award. Project procurement portal: https://www.newppexpansion.com/

**_Construction Academy Helps Increase SBE Participation in Omaha._** We established the Jacobs Construction Academy to provide technical assistance to small businesses in Omaha as part of our program management contract for Omaha Public Schools and our Omaha Public Schools Economic Inclusion Plan. Our team led 3-hour classes, twice a week on a variety of construction topics. As a result, local firms received nearly $43M in contracts and subcontracts.
