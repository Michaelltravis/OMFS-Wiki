# O&M Contract Transition Work Plan — Phased Schedule Structure

This is the structural skeleton of a three-phase transition work plan used to mobilize an O&M contract for a municipal wastewater treatment facility and collection system. Two anchor points drive all scheduling: **Award Date (Day A0)** and **Contract Start Date (Day C0)**. Durations below are expressed as offsets from these anchors so the plan can be re-dated to any pursuit.

## Phase 1 — Pre-Start Transition Activities (Award Date through Contract Start)

| Activity | Task Lead | Start (rel. to Award) | Completion (rel. to Award) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contract award and execution | Regional Director of Operations | A0 | A0+18d |
| Conduct chartering meetings with client and submit proposed Communications and Reporting Plan | Regional Director of Operations | A0+1d | A0+18d |
| Pre-start transition tasks: formalize startup and Transition Plan | Transition Manager | A0 | A0+25d |
| Review Project Manager and key staff details with client | Transition Manager | A0+7d | A0+25d |
| Finalize Staffing Plan and verify certified operator coverage/grade requirements | Transition Manager | A0+14d | A0+32d |
| Detailed review of NPDES permit, flood control readiness, general permit requirements, O&M manuals, SOPs, CMOM documentation, historical performance data | Compliance Lead | A0+7d | A0+46d |
| SCADA system review; cybersecurity verification; plan for remote monitoring and access transfer | Regional Support Leads | A0+14d | A0+64d |
| Joint facility walkthroughs and collection-system evaluations; baseline conditions documented; critical equipment condition assessment | Transition Team | A0+28d | A0+53d |
| Asset inventory validation (treatment plant, pump stations, collection system, low-pressure); verify spares/chemicals/solids inventory | Asset Manager | A0+28d | A0+64d |

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-phase structure (Pre-Start → Initial Transition [30 days] → Extended Transition [~6 months]), the activity categories within each phase (contractual/insurance, staffing plan and hiring, compliance/permit review, SCADA and cybersecurity, asset and inventory baseline, safety plan, training plan, odor/I&I programs, ongoing SME support), and the generic task-lead role titles are all directly reusable for any O&M contract transition plan of comparable scope.

Pursuit-specific: substitute the pursuit's actual Award Date and Contract Start Date for A0/C0 to regenerate real calendar dates; adjust durations if the pursuit's transition period is shorter/longer than the 30-day Initial Transition and ~6-month Extended Transition shown here. Confirm which task-lead roles actually exist in the proposed org structure for this pursuit (e.g., a smaller contract may combine Transition Manager and Project Manager). Compliance review items (permit names, CMOM) should be updated to the pursuit's actual regulatory regime. Pair with a staffing/org-chart block if one exists for full mobilization narrative.
