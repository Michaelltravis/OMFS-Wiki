# O&M Guiding Principles, Facilities Plans, and Networked QA/QC Support

At the heart of our approach is a robust O&M program focused on delivering safe, compliant, and reliable service while protecting and extending the life of [CLIENT]'s infrastructure. We take a comprehensive view of O&M—one that integrates performance optimization, regulatory compliance, proactive asset management, and stakeholder engagement.

JC Solutions’ operations strategy is driven by data-informed reliability and reinforced through advanced tools like Maintenance Connection, the CMMS software. As a recognized leader in Intelligent O&M, we bring proven opportunities to reduce chemical use, lower energy consumption, and optimize maintenance activities across complex utility systems. With more than 40 years of award-winning O&M experience, JC Solutions blends deep technical resources with practical, day-to-day operational know-how to deliver measurable results. We remain grounded in the fundamentals that define operational excellence—safety, responsiveness, reliability, sustainability, and transparency.

Our delivery model is guided by clearly defined principles and performance expectations. These principles are reinforced through close collaboration between onsite staff, regional management, and JC Solutions’ nationwide network of subject-matter experts. Our operations teams are supported rather than siloed and benefit from access to best practices, technical guidance, and real-time support. JC Solutions also applies a structured audit and performance-review program across all our O&M projects. This includes regularly scheduled audits that assess hundreds of operational tasks, benchmark against historical performance and peer facilities, and generate actionable insights. The results are used to identify trends, prevent performance dips, and promote continuous improvement.

We recognize that the Facilities Plans outlined in the RFP serve as a foundation for strong operational performance and shared success. JC Solutions will provide a comprehensive discussion of each required plan within the Approach section of this proposal. While some plan elements may require additional site-specific detail, we will submit clear, well-developed draft plans that outline our proposed strategies, tools, and methodologies for each area. These include but are not limited to Emergency Response, O&M, Staffing, Equipment Performance Testing, and Public Outreach. Each plan is based on Jacobs’ national experience and tailored to the needs of the [CLIENT] service area. We are fully committed to working in close collaboration with [CLIENT] to refine and finalize each plan. All plans will be submitted in both electronic and hard-copy formats, in full alignment with RFP expectations.

What distinguishes JC Solutions is how tightly each project and each individual is networked into our internal operations. No project exists on an island, regardless of how large or small it is. This is a big reason for our success—the interconnections and relationships we nurture drive job satisfaction, project performance, and in the end—client satisfaction, one of our most important metrics. From the outside, it can be hard to understand the inner day-to-day workings of contract operations. Our onsite team is in constant communication with technical specialists and senior leadership, continually assessing data, preventing incidents, addressing needs, and strategizing to improve performance. We attack project issues from the bottom up and the top down.

JC Solutions’ staffing approach is anchored in prioritizing health and safety, compliance, communications, and transparency with a targeted focus on supporting workforce development. Our approach has been developed with an aim for the long-term sustainability of high-quality services and continuous improvement.
