# Operations Management Training, Cross-Training, and Mentoring

Across Jacobs' O&M portfolio, **more than 200 live and virtual sessions** are delivered annually. This scale assures that [CLIENT] employees will have continuous access to high-quality, relevant courses to strengthen performance and compliance. The programs below align directly with [CLIENT]'s goals for operational readiness, compliance, and professional growth.

Jacobs' **Leadership and Professional Development Program,** anchored by Jacobs University, offers structured workshops and SME-led courses. It's designed to build confidence and competence in communication, financial management, and team leadership. *Frontline Fundamentals*, a key component of the 2026 course lineup, delivers short, real-time online workshops like *Driving Results* and *Interviewing Job Candidates* that teach practical management tools, and technical applications. Supervisors and emerging leaders will complete Jacobs' "Lead with Purpose" program, focusing on communication, coaching, and team performance.

**Cross-training and mentoring.** We'll institute a process to cross-train our O&M teams to elevate skillsets, provide redundancy, and create opportunities for career growth. For [CLIENT], cross-training will bring synergy that will allow teams to better support you and each other, address temporary staff shortages, and inspire the workforce, including:

- Our management team will develop individual training plans with training opportunities to help staff achieve their ambitions beyond their day-to-day jobs.
- Employees will rotate between operational disciplines to build versatility and systemwide understanding.
- Experienced staff will mentor new team members to accelerate learning and preserve institutional knowledge.

Our proposed mentoring program will complement formal training and certification initiatives and support employee progress along defined career paths while maintaining operational excellence, and includes:

- Pairing experienced professionals with emerging talent, enabling our management team to mitigate the risks associated with workforce shortages and aging infrastructure.
- Providing hands-on exposure to complex operational challenges, regulatory compliance requirements, and advanced technologies such as SCADA systems and membrane bioreactor processes.
- Equipping future leaders with both technical expertise and strategic decision-making capabilities they'll need to meet future challenges.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the move from corporate scale (annual session count, named programs) to individual benefit (individual training plans, discipline rotation, mentoring pairs), and the explicit link from mentoring to the client's real exposure — losing institutional knowledge as an experienced workforce retires.

Pursuit-specific: the session count and course lineup, which change yearly; the technologies named in the hands-on exposure bullet; whether the client's own staff are included in the cross-training and mentoring offer, which is a strong differentiator when the client retains some in-house positions.
