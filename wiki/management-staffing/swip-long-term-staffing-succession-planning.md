# Seamless Transition: Long-Term Staffing and Succession Planning Practices

Building a strong culture is the foundation of long-term success. Effectively recruiting, developing, and retaining high-performing staff, while proactively growing future leaders, is critical to sustained O&M excellence. Jacobs has a comprehensive plan to achieve this.

Our Resource Planning Group focuses on attracting, developing, and retaining skilled O&M professionals. Over the past few years, these efforts have doubled the number of new Jacobs employees in O&M, reduced turnover, and increased promotion of high-potential staff. We have already begun targeted recruiting for this project through internal promotions, external recruiting platforms, and partnerships with community colleges, technical schools, and military programs.

Retention is reinforced through competitive wages and benefits, incentive and recognition programs tied to safety and certifications, and career development support. Jacobs covers training costs, exam fees, and offers promotions and cash bonuses for advanced certifications, while also providing tuition reimbursement for accredited degree programs.

Finally, Jacobs emphasizes career development and succession planning. Through initiatives like cross-training, leadership programs, project manager internships, mentoring, and our Maintenance Excellence Initiative, we prepare staff to advance within Jacobs while strengthening operational resilience for our clients. Managers use formalized career pathways and succession planning tools to support employee growth across roles and departments.

Exhibit 3-6 highlights the range of programs Jacobs offers to train, retain, and grow our O&M staff, providing [CLIENT] with a stable, motivated, and long-term workforce.

### Exhibit 3-6. Staff Development and Succession Planning Programs

| Program | Description |
|---|---|
| **Technical Training and Certifications** | Staff keep up to date with our online electronic bookshelf of training materials and in-person training ranging from operations, maintenance, safety, compliance, and more to leadership development such as project management fundamentals, leading people, and persuasive public speaking. |
| **Future Leadership Development** | Our internal two-year training program focuses on project manager fundamentals, management, and leadership skills plus mentoring and networking opportunities. |
| **Global Future Talent Team** | By partnering with public and private universities, community colleges, and technical schools, our 20+ talent development staff manage strategies to attract and select graduates, interns, co-ops, and apprentices with positions that grow into full time upon graduation. |
| **Project Manager Intern Program** | This rotational program aims to give high-potential staff a three-month immersion on two different O&M project sites. They collaborate and build relationships with senior project managers while developing project management skills to take on new project management duties or assignments. After this six-month endeavor, participants should have a breadth of experience across our primary service delivery areas and could have opportunities to take on new project management duties or assignments — helping Jacobs build project manager bench strength to support growth. |
| **Leadership Team Mentor Program** | A selected group of 35 staff are paired with our global leaders for a year-long leadership training program focusing on a case study challenge that's presented at a capstone event. |
| **Maintenance Excellence Initiative** | A long-term strategy to significantly reduce maintenance costs and improve safety, reliability, and regulatory compliance, this program partners our leaders with staff to implement successful reliability-based strategies that improve our projects' operations as well as enhance our high-performance culture. |
| **Cross-training** | Jacobs will equip employees with cross-training at large sites as well as other local and regional projects through "walk in my shoes" opportunities. |
| **Succession Planning** | Jacobs will prepare succession plans to help employees reach desired advancement opportunities and goals. Managers use a formalized path and specific tools to further conversation on growth and development, leading to both linear and cross-departmental opportunities. |
