# Key Personnel Bios — Operations Leadership

## Section Intro — Onsite O&M Team

We have hand-picked a high-caliber team, reflecting our commitment to the success of [CLIENT]'s facilities. Key attributes of our team include:

## Director of Operations — Howard Brewen

Howard Brewen will serve as Director of Operations, bringing more than 22 years of experience in wastewater facility operations and management. He holds a California Wastewater Treatment Operator Grade V certification and a California Wastewater Collection Systems Operator Grade III certification, equipping him with the regulatory and technical expertise required to oversee the facility's complex infrastructure.

At the City of San Luis Obispo Water Resource Recovery Facility (WRRF), Howard managed daily O&M and led initiatives to improve energy efficiency and sustainability, including a $9.75M PG&E energy partnership and a $140M facility upgrade project. His experience managing $3.5M+ O&M budgets, integrating advanced treatment technologies, and establishing a successful wastewater operator intern program will directly benefit [CLIENT]. Howard will provide strategic oversight, lead coordination with [CLIENT] staff, and ensure reliable performance and regulatory compliance across the facilities.

## Manager of Operations — Chris Catlin

Chris Catlin will serve as Manager of Operations, bringing over 30 years of experience in water treatment and distribution, including leadership roles as plant manager, superintendent, and startup operator at advanced treatment facilities nationwide. He recently supported commissioning of the Soquel Creek Advanced Water Purification Facility, training operators and initiating compliance systems for membrane filtration, RO, and UV-AOP processes. Chris has extensive experience managing operations at facilities up to 120 MGD and has trained staff across multiple treatment technologies, from conventional surface water treatment to advanced reuse. He holds a California Water Treatment Operator T3 certification and an AWT5™, providing the highest level of compliance and technical expertise required for the facility's advanced treatment systems.

## Callout — Ensuring AWTP Compliance Through Proven Leadership

Jacobs will ensure that all positions assigned to the [CLIENT] project meet the permit regulations for AWTO certification and recognize the requirement for Chief Plant Operator (CPO) to hold an AWT5™ by December 2026. Our local project team will be supported with backup for positions through our substantial network of projects in California and our bench of subject matter experts who carry AWTO certification. Nearby projects at both West Basin and Gilroy have operators on staff with AWTO certification — and the number of AWTO certified employees continues to grow with our training and incentive plans for all California operators.

Jacobs brings added value through our unique culture of engaged management and multi-layered operational oversight. Our approach ensures full operational readiness and permit compliance through the direct involvement of senior leadership with extensive advanced water treatment credentials.

This **integrated, regionally connected leadership structure** — unique to Jacobs — ensures [CLIENT] benefits from immediate access to a deep bench of AWTO-certified professionals, management and oversight from an experienced team, and the excitement and focus around teammates achieving their next-level AWTO certifications.
