# Staffing Plan Philosophy — Onsite Team, Regional Bench, and Incumbent-Staff Approach

## Section Introduction

To ensure [CLIENT]'s facilities operate with the highest levels of safety, compliance, and reliability, Jacobs has identified a dedicated, high-caliber onsite team supported by deep regional and national expertise. Our staffing approach goes beyond the RFP minimums to provide added value in operations, maintenance, compliance, and innovation. [CLIENT] will benefit from a team with a proven track record operating advanced treatment facilities, deep experience with MBRs (membrane bioreactors) and Title 22 compliance, and a proactive maintenance philosophy that extends asset life and reduces downtime. Our structure is designed to ensure continuity, 24/7 responsiveness, and seamless integration with [CLIENT] staff — providing peace of mind that its critical water infrastructure is in capable, committed hands.

## 3.1 Staffing Plan — Four-Point Value Proposition

**Robust Team with AWTO Certifications.** Jacobs' onsite team of six dedicated staff will be led by our highly experienced Chief Plant Operator (CPO). Each member of our onsite team holds the required California State certifications, including Advanced Water Treatment Operator (AWTO) credentials, ensuring compliance with permit and regulatory mandates from day one. This certified team brings direct experience with the facility's relevant technologies, including MBR, RO, and UV-AOP, and will maintain robust 24/7 coverage across all facilities. Further enhancing operational resilience, the core team can call on our network of California AWTO-certified employees, including nearby resources at the West Basin facility in El Segundo, ensuring immediate backup and rapid support when needed.

**Additional Regional Support Dedicated to [CLIENT].** To enhance service flexibility and reduce costs, Jacobs will also hire two new locally based technicians — one Instrumentation & Controls (I&C) and one Maintenance — into our regional maintenance team. These positions are in addition to the full-time, dedicated Maintenance and I&C Technicians already assigned to the facility. By embedding these additional resources locally, Jacobs will provide [CLIENT] with rapid, on-demand technical support without the delays or costs associated with mobilization. This approach ensures the client receives responsive, expert maintenance services when needed in the most cost-effective manner, improving operational continuity while minimizing costs.

**Deep Bench of Technical Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) at the Ready.** Further strengthening this model is Jacobs' extensive technical bench of industry experts in MBR, potable reuse, asset management, and more. Our local and national experts will provide proactive support, including CMMS optimization, permit compliance, and process troubleshooting, ensuring [CLIENT] remains a leader in water reuse innovation.

**A Culture of Innovation and Continuous Improvement.** Jacobs uses real-world proven digital tools, best practices, and lessons learned across our portfolio to optimize operations. This deeply ingrained commitment to cultivating excellence will empower our [CLIENT] team to deliver consistent, high-performance operations while protecting the client's investment in sustainable water infrastructure.

## Regional and Technical Support Resources

Jacobs will supplement the full-time onsite operations team with a regional bench of **over 100 subject matter experts** in safety, regulatory compliance, advanced maintenance, asset management, and water reuse operations. This team is not committed to any one project but is a bench of shared resources leveraged to ensure each project is fully supported. This team provides on-demand support, ensuring projects receive specialized expertise and best practices. The team will assist with operations, maintenance, compliance, high-quality reporting aligned with State permit requirements, and engineering support to the [CLIENT] team. These experts will be available to client staff on an as-needed basis, providing immediate access to specialized technical expertise and industry-leading best practices. This in-house team ensures facilities operate at peak performance and efficiency, with direct access to specialized expertise when needed. We've shown some of the individuals in this large team who will be supporting [CLIENT] in the proposed project team organization chart (see `swip-project-team-org-structure.md`).

## Staffing Plan — Overall Approach

Jacobs will staff [CLIENT]'s facilities with the right balance of skill, experience, and coverage to ensure operations are not only in compliance but consistently meet the highest standards of safety, reliability, and performance. We will take into account the unique needs of each facility, the current condition of assets, and the client's operational objectives to ensure our staffing plan aligns with the client's priorities.

We anticipate that many existing operations and maintenance personnel may wish to continue serving [CLIENT]. Jacobs will welcome qualified incumbent staff into our team, recognizing the value of their facility knowledge and dedication. Our experience shows that transitioned employees thrive with Jacobs, benefiting from our collaborative culture, robust safety program, and comprehensive training opportunities.

Our proposed staffing and shift schedule will deliver:

1. The right number of staff per shift to complete work proactively.
2. Team capacity to assist one another on complex or urgent tasks.
3. No lone-shift operations, ensuring both safety and operational resilience.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the four-point staffing value proposition structure (certified onsite team → dedicated regional add-on hires → large SME bench → innovation culture), the incumbent-staff welcome/transition philosophy, and the three staffing-delivery principles (right-sized shifts, mutual-assist capacity, no lone-shift operations) are reusable for any O&M staffing plan regardless of facility type.

Pursuit-specific: adjust the onsite team headcount ("six dedicated staff"), certification types (AWTO/AWT3/AWT5 are California-specific — swap for the applicable state's operator certification scheme elsewhere), named technologies (MBR/RO/UV-AOP should match the actual facility's process train), and the SME bench size figure (100+) to the pursuit's actual scope and current firm-wide bench.
