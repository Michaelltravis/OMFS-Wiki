# Staffing Plan Philosophy — Certified Onsite Team, Added Regional Hires, and 100+ SME Bench

## Section Introduction

To ensure [CLIENT]'s advanced water treatment and reuse facilities operate with the highest levels of safety, compliance, and reliability, Jacobs has identified a dedicated, high-caliber onsite team supported by deep regional and national expertise. Our staffing approach goes beyond the RFP minimums to provide added value in operations, maintenance, compliance, and innovation. [CLIENT] will benefit from a team with a proven track record operating advanced treatment facilities, deep experience with MBRs and Title 22 compliance, and a proactive maintenance philosophy that extends asset life and reduces downtime. Our structure is designed to ensure continuity, 24/7 responsiveness, and seamless integration with [CLIENT] staff — providing peace of mind that its critical water reuse infrastructure is in capable, committed hands.

## 3.1 Staffing Plan — Four-Point Value Proposition

**Robust Team with AWTO Certifications.** Jacobs' onsite team of six dedicated staff will be led by our highly experienced Chief Plant Operator (CPO), Mack McKenzie, who has a California WW Grade V and AWT3™. Each member of our onsite team holds the required California State certifications, including Advanced Water Treatment Operator credentials, ensuring compliance with permit and regulatory mandates from day one. This certified team brings direct experience with the facility's relevant technologies, including MBR, RO, and UV-AOP, and will maintain robust 24/7 coverage across the facilities. Further enhancing operational resilience, the core team can call on our network of California AWTO certified employees, including nearby resources at the West Basin facility in El Segundo, ensuring immediate backup and rapid support when needed.

**Additional Regional Support Dedicated to [CLIENT].** To enhance service flexibility and reduce costs for [CLIENT], Jacobs will also hire two new LA-based technicians, one I&C and one Maintenance, into our regional maintenance team. These positions are in addition to the full-time, dedicated Maintenance and I&C Technicians already assigned to the facilities. By embedding these additional resources in the Los Angeles area, Jacobs will provide [CLIENT] with rapid, on-demand technical support without the delays or costs associated with mobilization. This approach ensures [CLIENT] receives responsive, expert maintenance services when needed in the most cost-effective manner, improving operational continuity while minimizing costs.

**Deep Bench of Technical Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) at the Ready.** Further strengthening this model is Jacobs' extensive technical bench of industry experts in MBR, potable reuse, asset management, and more. Our local and national experts will provide proactive support, including CMMS optimization, permit compliance, and process troubleshooting, ensuring [CLIENT] remains a leader in water reuse innovation.

**A culture of innovation and continuous improvement.** Jacobs uses real-world proven digital tools, best practices, and lessons learned across our portfolio to optimize operations. This deeply ingrained commitment cultivating excellence will empower our [CLIENT] team to deliver consistent, high-performance operations while protecting [CLIENT]'s investment in sustainable water infrastructure.

## Regional and Technical Support Resources

Jacobs will supplement the full-time operations team with a regional bench of over 100 subject matter experts in safety, regulatory compliance, advanced maintenance, asset management, and water reuse operations. This team is not committed to any one project but is a bench of shared resources we leverage to ensure each project is fully supported. This team provides on-demand support, ensuring projects receive specialized expertise and best practices. The team will assist with operations, maintenance, compliance, high-quality reporting aligned with State permit requirements, and engineering support to the [CLIENT] team. These experts will be available to [CLIENT] staff on an as-needed basis, providing immediate access to specialized technical expertise and industry-leading best practices. This in-house team will ensure [CLIENT]'s facilities operate at peak performance and efficiency, with direct access to specialized expertise when needed. We've shown some of the individuals in this large team who will be supporting [CLIENT] in Exhibit 3-1 (Proposed Project Team Organization).

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the four-point staffing value proposition structure (certified onsite team → dedicated regional add-on hires → large SME bench → innovation culture) and the "regional bench that belongs to no single project" framing are reusable for any O&M staffing plan regardless of facility type.

Pursuit-specific: adjust the onsite headcount ("six dedicated staff"), the certification scheme (AWTO/AWT3™/AWT5™ and California WW Grade V are California-specific), the named backup facility (West Basin, El Segundo), the process technologies (MBR/RO/UV-AOP), and the 100+ SME bench figure to the pursuit's actual scope and current firm-wide bench.
