# Client-to-Jacobs Values Alignment Table (Exhibit II-13)

**Exhibit II-13. Jacobs' corporate values align with [CLIENT]'s values and enhance our performance and collaboration.**

_Shared values enable effective work approaches and true collaboration._

| [CLIENT] values | Jacobs values | Impact on our service |
|---|---|---|
| Stewardship | **We aim higher.** | We'll make safety, environmental responsibility, and community service part of daily work to support [CLIENT]'s long-term sustainability goals. Our onboarding, shadowing, and mentorship for supervisors and emerging leaders will build skills fast. We'll track and respond to KPIs so standards are high and results are visible. |
| Quality | **Integrity — We do things right.** | Our leaders will set clear expectations, enforce the code of conduct, and provide real-time dashboards to build trust with [CLIENT]. |
| Collaboration | **We live inclusion.** | We'll plan together, meet often, and share information openly so we're aligned and win as one team. |
| Diversity | **We live inclusion.** | We'll hire inclusively, maintain respectful workplaces, and use workforce development partnerships to create opportunities and strengthen retention. |
| Innovation | **We challenge the accepted.** | We'll use digital tools, continuous improvement workshops, and expertise of our SMEs to translate lessons learned into safer, more efficient operations. |

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-column structure and the Jacobs values column. The pattern — client value, our matching value, what we will actually do about it — works in any section where a client publishes values or guiding principles.

Pursuit-specific: everything in column 1, and the actions in column 3 that name client programs, goals, or reporting products. Where the client's value has no clean Jacobs counterpart, restate the Jacobs value that comes closest rather than inventing one.
