# Internal Delivery and QA/QC Functional Support Matrix

| Support function | Source responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Regional process specialists | Set up process-control tools and Hach WIMS; hold weekly process meetings; look at data in WIMS and verify samples were taken on time and recorded in the database; analyze data for outliers and errors; pull data into the Jacobs process-control system and audit results against industry best targets; score data with internal KPIs; drive weekly process-control meetings; continually audit KPIs and treatment targets including coagulant dosage and alkalinity; verify consistent compliance with process goals and metrics; analyze trends and provide recommendations. |
| Regional maintenance specialists | Provide feedback and recommendations to PM/PdM schedules to optimize the maintenance program and extend asset life; generate audit reports and KPIs from the existing CMMS and provide Jacobs standards and best practices to [CLIENT]'s CMMS; review CM versus PM and open work orders; produce 30-, 60-, and 90-day reports; drive monthly maintenance-focused calls; assess tools and recommend cost-effective maintenance-program investments; maintain SCADA systems, networks, and cybersecurity components; and conduct ongoing review of sludge dewatering and solids handling. |
| Compliance and reporting | Provide general project oversight to verify regulatory and contractual compliance; provide instrumental project setup and ongoing compliance; conduct routine check-ins with clients; support quarterly business reviews; conduct recordkeeping and laboratory audits; assess whether samples are collected, tracked, and reported correctly, documents are maintained and backed up, data are defensible, and communications with [CLIENT] and regulators are documented and stored; and in cases of permit excursion or potential excursion, lead compliance audit and reporting to verify that compliance is done “by the book.” |
| Regional management and executive team | Obtain additional project resources as needed; confirm staffing, compliance, subcontractor, and emergency-response commitments are met; support project initiatives and [CLIENT] goals including community involvement and workforce development; serve as a resource in communication with regulators; engage with [CLIENT] staff on regulatory and compliance issues; confirm succession and training plans are in place; directly interact with [CLIENT] to support all project needs; and facilitate client satisfaction through speedy resolution to important issues. |
| Annual Quality Audit | Step 1: Planning and scheduling arranged by the audit team to conduct a site visit and generate an audit report. Step 2: Audit team, Regional Manager, and Plant Manager review findings. Step 3: 90-day fixed timeframe to address findings or identified areas of improvement, including any additional resources that may be needed. The main goal of the Annual Quality Audit is to confirm full compliance. The process also drives an ongoing improvement mindset. |
