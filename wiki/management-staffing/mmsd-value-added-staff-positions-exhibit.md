## Reuse guidance

Universal: the seven-group architecture, the placement of a named SME against every technical discipline (an evaluator can see there is a real person behind each), the asterisk convention for as-needed support, and the four benefit statements for groups 1–5, which are the most transferable sentences on the page. The 90-day fill requirement and the 85% PM/15% CM target are the two hardest commitments here — carry them only when the new contract supports them.

Pursuit-specific: all FTE counts, all names, the subcontractor list, the local office headcount, and the biosolids product reference. Facility labels WRF 1 / WRF 2 must be renamed to the new pursuit's sites.
