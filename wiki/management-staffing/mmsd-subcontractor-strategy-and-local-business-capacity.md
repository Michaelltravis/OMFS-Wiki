# Subcontractor Strategy — Building Local and Small Business Capacity

We'll balance self-performance requirements with the goal of finding meaningful opportunities for the region's local and small businesses to support this contract. Our subcontractor plan aims to build lasting capacity within the community through participation in [CLIENT]'s business capacity development initiatives, joint networking events, and mentor–protégé programs. Through close coordination with [CLIENT]'s procurement team and leveraging our subconsultants PRISM and P3 Development Group, we'll align strategies, share best practices, and expand opportunities for local suppliers and contractors. **Exhibit II-10** lists local strategic partners who will enhance Jacobs' services by contributing their expertise and insights into [CLIENT]'s facilities and service area.

**Documentation and reporting protocols.** We'll provide monthly and quarterly updates and an annual summary on subcontractor utilization, costs, and safety performance. Reports will document the level of underutilized business participation and its economic impact on the local community. To start the process of engaging local small businesses, we held a Pre-Award Outreach Event in our regional office in September 2025.

> "Big thanks to Jacobs for hosting such an informative procurement event for local and small businesses! Their team made the process clear, transparent, and even took time to assist vendors one-on-one and answer specific questions."
>
> **— Gabby Ramos, Contracts & Procurement Specialist I at West Basin Municipal Water District**

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the framing that subcontracting is capacity building rather than spend allocation, and the three-part reporting promise (utilization, cost, safety performance) with an explicit statement of economic impact on the local community — evaluators score this higher than a percentage commitment alone.

Pursuit-specific: the named capacity-building subconsultants; the client's own small business program name and thresholds; the outreach event and its date, which only works as proof if it actually happened before submittal. The quote is a real third-party testimonial and must be confirmed as permission-on-file in `testimonials/inventory.md` before external use.
