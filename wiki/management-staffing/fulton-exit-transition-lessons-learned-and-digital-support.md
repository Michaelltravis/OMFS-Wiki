# Exit Transition Lessons Learned and Digital Handover Support

**Lessons Learned.** JC Solutions' experience with exit transitions, such as Coos Bay, has highlighted the importance of early planning, thorough documentation, and proactive risk management. Structured communication protocols, detailed asset assessments, and collaborative planning with clients have proven key to success. These lessons will directly inform our approach at [CLIENT]'s facilities — a county system of water reclamation facilities and pump stations — ensuring a seamless transition.

**Additional Support.** Jacobs offers digital tools to support the Exit Transition Plan, including asset tracking and compliance reporting systems that streamline handovers. In alignment with Exhibit 6, we will provide continued training and technical support for [CLIENT] or successor operator, as needed, to ensure long-term continuity and performance.
