# Governance Committees and the Blue-Ribbon Panel

## Committees

We will comply with RFP Exhibit P – Governance. These [CLIENT] committees align with the Agreement's governance expectations and provide the forum for KPI review, preventive maintenance (PM) list/standard updates, CMMS change control, and the required annual joint review of the Minimum Staffing Plan. During the chartering session, we'll review and stand up these committees together, so they begin delivering benefits right away.

## Blue-Ribbon Panel

We suggest establishing a Blue-Ribbon Panel that would provide independent analysis and recommendations on [CLIENT]'s complex O&M needs. With your concurrence, [CLIENT] and Jacobs leaders will jointly select panel members and set the agenda for our annual Innovation Workshop, drawing on global water sector expertise. Expected focus areas could include treatment optimization, emerging contaminants, regulatory outlook, wet weather and combined sewer overflow (CSO) management, research needs, and financial forecasts.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the committee triad (executive / operations / maintenance), each jointly staffed and stood up at the chartering session rather than after award, and the framing that the committees are the forum where KPI review, PM standard changes, CMMS change control, and the annual staffing review actually happen. The Blue-Ribbon Panel plus annual Innovation Workshop is a reusable no-cost value-add for large, technically complex clients. Pursuit-specific: the governance exhibit reference, committee membership, and the panel's focus areas.
