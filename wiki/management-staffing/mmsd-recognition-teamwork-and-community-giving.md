# Recognition, Teamwork, and Community Giving Culture

### 5. A Culture That Celebrates Teamwork and Achievement

We recognize what matters: safety, innovation, teamwork, and community service. **Teamwork and empowerment are at the heart of Jacobs' culture and will power results at [CLIENT].** We strive to pursue industry recognition that reflects our teams' operational excellence and client satisfaction. These recognitions reinforce the value of collaboration across functions and levels.

**As noted in _Section II – F_, safety is the foundation of Jacobs' operations, and BeyondZero®** anchors health, safety, and well-being for employees and communities. **Through Beyond If℠, we celebrate ideas that deliver measurable client impact.** We encourage employees to challenge the status quo and develop transformative solutions for global challenges such as climate change and urbanization. Recognition programs highlight individuals and teams whose innovative ideas deliver measurable impact for clients and communities.

**Jacobs' commitment to social impact.** Through our **Collectively℠ program**, Jacobs empowers employees to give back via volunteering and charitable contributions. The company matches donations, rewards volunteer hours, and promotes campaigns aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goals. Since 2020, Jacobs employees have contributed over $10.9M to global causes. Each year our teams donate thousands of hours to benefit the local community, from river cleanups and community beautification efforts to charitable golf tournaments and holiday gift and meal drives. **At [CLIENT], we'll organize and sustain these efforts with the same discipline we bring to operations, turning community service into a standing practice that provides tangible benefits to the region, rather than a one-off event.**

Jacobs' community pride in the region is reflected in the number of community-based activities and volunteer programs we've participated in over the last 50 years. **_Section 4 – Regional Partnership captures some recent activities_**.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the BeyondZero® / Beyond If℠ / Collectively℠ program trio, the matching-donations and rewarded-volunteer-hours mechanics, the UN SDG alignment, and the $10.9M since-2020 figure.

Pursuit-specific: the local tenure claim (50 years here), the examples of local volunteer activity, and the cross-reference to a regional partnership section. On a pursuit where the client has its own community-benefit or equity program, name it in the closing promise so the discipline commitment attaches to something the client already runs.
