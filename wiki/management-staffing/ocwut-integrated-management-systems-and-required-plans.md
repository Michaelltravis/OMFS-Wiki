# Management Systems

Effective O&M depends on integrated management systems that connect operations, maintenance, compliance, safety, and administration into a coordinated whole. Jacobs will organize our management systems around the critical areas of service delivery shown in **Exhibit 1-9**, with each system supported by detailed plans developed per the contract schedule.

## Required Plans — Integration into an Overall O&M Framework

The contract requires Jacobs to develop 15 new plans and update nine existing plans, all coordinated with the [CLIENT] General Manager. We'll develop each plan as an integrated component of our overall O&M management system — not as standalone documents that sit on a shelf. Our Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) will flow from these plans and connect directly to CMMS work orders, operator round sheets, and training curricula.

**New plans (draft due November 2, 2026 / final due December 31, 2026):** Management Oversight and QA/QC Plan; Equipment Rotation Plan; Building and Grounds Maintenance and Housekeeping Plan; Safety Program and Plan; Emergency Operating Plan; Process Control Plan; Solids Management Plan; Odor Control Plan; Inventory Management Plan; Sampling and Testing QA/QC Plan; Corrosion Control Plan; Wet Weather Management Plan; Security Plan; Energy Efficiency Plan; Drug Detection and Deterrence Plan.

**Existing plans to update (reviewed annually with the [CLIENT] General Manager):** Stormwater Management Plan; Solids Management Plan; Flood Emergency Response Plan; Annual Drain and Clean Plan; Risk Management Plan; Industrial Waste Slug Management Plan; Process Safety Management Plan; SPCC Plan; Odor Control Plan.

Each plan will be reviewed annually and updated as conditions, regulations, or [CLIENT] priorities evolve. QA/QC Manager **Josh** will track plan development milestones and verify that all deliverables meet the agreed-upon schedule.

## Major Elements of Jacobs' Approach to Project Execution (Exhibit 1-9)

**Health, Safety, and Security.** A framework for compliance with the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) and other safety regulations, vulnerability assessments, job safety analysis, safety training, emergency preparedness and response, and other safety-related functions.

**Compliance and Reporting.** A program of compliance for pollution prevention and waste minimization. Includes analysis and review checklists for all permits, report preparation for regulatory agencies, and environmental compliance training for all employees.

**Operations Management.** Documents treatment strategies and process control functions through standard operating procedures (SOPs), unit process control procedures (UPCPs), and treatment system optimization.

**Financial Management.** Systems and approaches to manage project cost through national purchasing power, documentation of contractual requirements, and continuous monitoring.

**Maintenance & Asset Management.** Integrates maintenance scheduling, documentation, and tracking; inventory control; and equipment condition assessment into a computerized database that allows us to maximize equipment performance and minimize downtime.

**Project Management.** Transparent communication including daily, weekly, monthly, severe weather, and annual client reports, internal measures of project performance, and plans to improve project performance beyond the requirements of our contract.

**Laboratory Management.** Standard procedures for sample collection, analysis, and documentation with our QA/QC and chemical hygiene plans.

**Public Education, Community Involvement, and Media Relations.** A comprehensive public education and community involvement program developed in partnership with [CLIENT], including volunteering, charitable donations, and facility tours to benefit the community.

**Staffing & Training (Human Resources).** A program for the success of all of our employees as individuals and as a team, including recruitment, orientation, and training; standard training needs assessments for all employees; training programs for certification, ethics, compliance, safety, management and succession; and performance appraisals and professional development.

**Good Neighbor Programs.** A robust program for odor control and biosolids to be a good neighbor and responsive to community concerns.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the framing sentence that plans are integrated components rather than shelf documents, the SOP-to-CMMS-to-round-sheet-to-training chain, and all ten management-system definitions, which are stable house descriptions of the Jacobs O&M core.

Pursuit-specific: both plan lists, the plan counts, the due dates, and the named milestone owner. Where the RFP does not enumerate required plans, keep the Exhibit 1-9 wheel and drop the two lists.
