# Train to Retain — Training and Development Programs for O&M Staff (Exhibit 2-48)

Additionally, we strive to maintain competitive wages and benefits, recognizing it is a critical aspect of employee retention. ***Train to Retain.*** By providing numerous internal opportunities, highlighted in **Exhibit 2-48**, for our O&M staff to grow in their jobs and advance their careers, we see many of our staff remain long-term employees with JC Solutions. This approach benefits clients like [CLIENT] — a county wastewater utility — by providing a stable workforce and elevating existing staff's skills and quality.

| Program | Description |
|---|---|
| **Technical Training and Certifications** | Staff keep up to date with our online electronic bookshelf of training materials and in-person training ranging from operations, maintenance, safety, compliance and more to leadership development such as project management fundamentals, leading people, and persuasive public speaking. |
| **Future Leadership Development** | Our internal two-year training program focuses on project manager fundamentals, management, and leadership skills plus mentoring and networking opportunities. |
| **Global Future Talent Team** | By partnering with public and private universities, community colleges, and technical schools, our 20+ talent development staff manage strategies to attract and select graduates, interns, co-ops, and apprentices with positions that grow into full time upon graduation. |
| **Project Manager Intern Program** | This rotational program aims to give high-potential staff a three-month immersion on two different O&M project sites. They collaborate and build relationships with senior project managers while developing project management skills to take on new project management duties or assignments. After this six-month endeavor, participants should have a breadth of experience across our primary service delivery areas and could provide opportunities to take on new project management duties or assignments — helping JC Solutions build project manager bench strength to support growth. |
| **Leadership Team Mentor Program** | A selected group of 35 staff are paired with our global leaders for a year-long leadership training program focusing on a case study challenge that's presented at a capstone event. |
| **Maintenance Excellence Initiative** | A long-term strategy to significantly reduce maintenance costs and improve safety, reliability, and regulatory compliance, this program partners our leaders with staff to implement successful reliability-based strategies that improve our projects' operations as well as enhance our high-performance culture. |
| **Cross-training** | JC Solutions will equip employees with cross-training at large sites as well as other local and regional projects through "walk in my shoes" opportunities. |
| **Succession Planning** | JC Solutions will prepare succession plans to help employees reach desired advancement opportunities and goals. Managers use a formalized path and specific tools to further conversation on growth and development, leading to both linear and cross-departmental opportunities. |
