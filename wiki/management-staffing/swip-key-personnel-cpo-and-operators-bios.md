# Key Personnel Bios — Chief Plant Operator and Operators in Charge

## Chief Plant Operator (CPO) — Mack Mckenzie

Mack MacKenzie will serve as the designated Chief Plant Operator for the facilities. He holds a Grade V Wastewater Operator certification and AWT3™, bringing over 11 years of operational leadership across a range of advanced treatment plants throughout California.

Mack has served as CPO at multiple facilities, including his current role as project manager/CPO at the Soquel Creek Advanced Water Purification Facility, the 7-MGD Novato Sanitary District WWTP, and the 16-MGD Richmond Water Pollution Control Plant. He has extensive experience managing MBR, ultrafiltration, RO, and UV disinfection systems — technologies core to the pursuit facility. At Santa Paula and Fillmore, Mack led operations at full-scale MBR facilities, overseeing advanced automation systems, UV disinfection, chemical dosing, and biosolids handling. At the Soquel Creek Advanced Water Purification Facility, Mack oversees operations including membrane filtration, RO, and UV-AOP processes.

He has managed both day-to-day operations and long-term capital projects, supported regulatory compliance across multiple jurisdictions, and has trained operations staff in both theoretical and hands-on treatment processes. Mack has also implemented safety programs and served as a Site Safety Coordinator, contributing to strong compliance records.

His proven ability to supervise multidisciplinary teams, maintain asset performance, and navigate complex permit requirements will ensure the facility operates reliably and in full regulatory compliance from day one.

## Operators in Charge — Josh Hernandez, Christen Wood, and Jason Holst

Josh, Christen, and Jason will serve as Operators in Charge, providing coverage of the facilities. Each are certified and experienced in membrane filtration, UV disinfection, and compliance sampling. Their cross-training in the facility's specific treatment systems ensures operational resilience, particularly during off-hours and emergency response.

**Joshua Hernandez** — WW Treatment Operator Grade V, AWT3™. Josh brings nearly a decade of experience in wastewater treatment plant operations. He has overseen full plant processes, laboratory testing, and compliance reporting, with proven skills in troubleshooting, process optimization, and mentoring junior staff. Currently expanding his technical expertise in SCADA and PLC systems, Joshua combines hands-on operational knowledge with forward-looking skills in automation and data management. He holds a California Wastewater Treatment Operator Grade V license and an AWT3™, ensuring he meets all regulatory requirements to support reliable and compliant operations.

**Christen Wood** — WW Treatment Operator Grade V, AWT3™. Christen has 17 years of experience in the startup, operation, and optimization of water and wastewater treatment plants across the U.S. She has led commissioning of advanced reuse processes — including RO, ozone, and UV-AOP — and is skilled in troubleshooting, laboratory sampling and analysis, workforce training, and developing SOPs. Christen has also supported new and unique projects such as the Soquel Creek Advanced Water Purification Facility startup, where she provided hands-on operations, testing, and CMMS integration. She holds an AWT3™ and has passed the California Wastewater Operator Grade V exam, providing [CLIENT] with both proven expertise and operational resilience.

**Jason Holst** — Water Treatment Operator T3, AWT3™. Jason has eight years of experience operating and maintaining dynamic water/wastewater treatment plants. He is highly proficient in using SCADA systems to monitor and control plant processes. Jason is skilled in process control and chemical handling and is adept with laboratory equipment and techniques to analyze water quality. He has served as an operator at a number of California water treatment plants, including the Woodland Davis Regional Water Treatment Facility and the Twin Oaks Valley WTP. Jason is a Grade III Water Treatment Operator certified by the California State Water Resources Control Board and has an AWT3™ from the American Water Works Association California-Nevada Section.
