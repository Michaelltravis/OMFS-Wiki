# Employee Satisfaction After Transition and the Six-Step Workforce Transition Process

Jacobs consistently measures and tracks employee satisfaction across its operations, including projects that have transitioned from other employers. Staff members who have joined Jacobs after a transition consistently report **higher satisfaction and engagement levels** compared to their previous organizations, citing our supportive work environment, transparent communication, and strong career development programs.

**Staff satisfaction improves significantly after Jacobs takes over contract operations from Veolia:**

| Transition | Prior organization | Jacobs |
|---|---|---|
| West Basin, CA | 3.6 | 4.1 |
| Jackson, MS | 2.8 | 4.3 |

*Employee feedback from recent transitions shows an average 34% improvement in satisfaction after joining Jacobs, reflecting our strong onboarding, support systems, and people-focused culture.* (Graphic asset ID: `314_007CAM_1`)

Our success in building energized, high-performing teams stems from our commitment to listening to and acting on employee feedback. That philosophy will carry through to [CLIENT], ensuring that every employee feels heard, respected, and valued throughout the transition and beyond. Jacobs' **six-step process**, illustrated in **Exhibit IV-56**, provides a structured pathway for integrating [CLIENT] employees into our organization. Each step from talent retention and family outreach to onboarding and continuous development, supports employees through change, while ensuring uninterrupted operations. The result is a unified workforce that feels supported, motivated, and ready to deliver exceptional service on Day 1.

## Exhibit IV-56. Jacobs' proven six-step workforce transition process

1. Retain and Recruit Local Talent
2. Host Introduction Workshops
3. Assess and Interview Remaining Candidates
4. Make Employment Offers
5. Implement Contract Startup and Operation
6. Initiate Continuous Training and Development Program
