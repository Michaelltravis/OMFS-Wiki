# Subcontractor Scopes and Jacobs/CERM O&M Support Bench

Subcontractors will provide a range of specialty services as part of our staffing approach and commitment to utilizing local M/FBE firms. The following scopes of services have been identified for subcontracting and the firms teamed to provide them.

| Subcontractor | Scope of services | Certified MBE or FBE? |
|---|---|---|
| Operations Management International, Inc. (Jacobs) | All other required areas of Scope of Work not identified below | |
| Corporate Environmental Risk Management, LLC (CERM) | Sludge hauling, workforce development, support staffing, and procurement-assistance services | ✓ |
| Engineered Systems & Services, LLC (ESS) | Odor control and HVAC | ✓ |
| Full Circle Communications, Inc. | Community outreach | ✓ |
| KatyLady Building Maintenance Services, LLC | Janitorial | ✓ |
| Let Us Love Your Lawn, Inc. | Grounds maintenance | ✓ |
| Waste Eliminators, LLC | Sludge hauling | |
| Waste Water Industrial Solutions, Inc. (WWIS) | Tank cleaning and maintenance | ✓ |

We bring Jacobs’ and CERM’s deep bench of both regional O&M support resources and industry-leading technical SMEs to support [CLIENT] projects and ensure the onsite team has the backing they need to be successful—whether in solving an immediate issue, optimizing operations and maintenance, or planning for future needs and training the onsite team to elevate their own capabilities. Identified individuals were chosen for their alignment with the current needs and challenges at [CLIENT] facilities.

We have allocated 4,300 hours annually in our Base Fee for regional and technical support by our O&M Resources and Consulting Resources teams to support the project. These resources will be fully accessible to the Project Manager and onsite team, allowing them to draw on specialized expert resources whenever needed. JC Solutions also identifies $2,200,000 worth of investment in support of the project.
