# Transition Training Period — Onboarding and Ongoing Training Curriculum

**Transition Training Period.** Following hiring and onboarding, our O&M staff will observe all activities to learn from the existing [CLIENT] staff — a county wastewater utility — including a review of all process areas, data gathering, metrics, and the CMMS. All staff will complete the required safety and operations training to ensure they all have the knowledge necessary to safely and successfully perform all tasks assigned. We will also work with [CLIENT]'s laboratory staff to understand their processes, build on the efforts they have already implemented, and then provide training on our protocols to establish an effective sampling program.

Additionally, we will identify other needed training at onboarding, as well as ongoing training and development plans. This is a critical part of our transition plan that will encompass:

- H&S, including appropriate OSHA training
- Compliance
- Operations and technical procedures
- Administrative procedures
- Quality management
- Certification
- Other courses designed to improve skills
- Our code of conduct and ethics expectations
- Jacobs' 40-hour leadership training program for appropriate management and administrative staff
