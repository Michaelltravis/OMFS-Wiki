# Seamless Transition: Four-Phase Transition Model and Preliminary Schedule

## Transition Phasing and Timeline

Jacobs' structured transition model consists of four phases, with a preliminary 30- to 45-day schedule that will guide the transition and includes flexibility to extend it based on [CLIENT]'s needs (see Exhibit 3-5):

| Phase | Description |
|---|---|
| **Pre-Start Transition** | HR and administrative preparation, staffing plan finalization, and early staff communications. |
| **Initial Transition** | Shadowing of current staff, badge flip, and continuity planning to preserve institutional knowledge. |
| **Full Transition and Integration** | Alignment of SOPs, CMMS integration, extensive training, and system verification. |
| **Long-Term Transition** | Up to 12 months of monitoring, feedback, and performance optimization to fully embed Jacobs' systems and culture. |

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the four-phase model (Pre-Start / Initial Transition / Full Transition and Integration / Long-Term Transition) and its plain-language description column are a clean, generalizable framework for structuring any transition-plan section, and the task-level Gantt schedule is a strong ready-to-adapt template — its category groupings (Contract Negotiations and Pre-Award; Pre-Start Transition Activities; Initial Transition; Full Transition and Integration; Long-Term Transition) and task-lead role taxonomy apply broadly to municipal O&M transitions.

Pursuit-specific: every date in this schedule is anchored to this pursuit's specific award/commencement timeline (contract negotiations starting Nov 1, commencement in mid-January, long-term monitoring running through August) — regenerate all dates relative to the new pursuit's actual award date, and adjust task durations to match the new contract's required mobilization window (here, a compressed ~30-45-day pre-commencement window). Advanced-treatment-specific tasks (CCP/LRV compliance monitoring, RO/UV-AOP/membrane OEM service contracts, regulator coordination calls with DDW/RWQCB/SCAQMD, specialty chemical procurement) should be trimmed or replaced for a conventional (non-potable-reuse) WWTP pursuit. Pairs with `om-transition-workplan-phased-schedule.md` (a comparable phased-schedule pattern from a different source pursuit — compare structures before choosing one to lead with) and with `swip-transition-team-and-communications-plan.md` for the task-lead roles referenced throughout this schedule.
