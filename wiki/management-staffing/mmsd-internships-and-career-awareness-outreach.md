# Internships and Career Awareness Outreach

#### 1.3. Internships Peak Interest in Careers

Internships are critical to our recruitment strategies and have been a highly effective component of our operating model in the region and the surrounding geographic area, where we've engaged local colleges, technical associations, and nonprofit organizations such as the UWM and MATC, to attract students and provide insights into career pathways. We'll continue to work with the City and [CLIENT] as a participant in Camp RISE internship programs, and will review other proposed activities with [CLIENT], including participation in regional workforce programs, hosting career fairs and plant tours, developing bilingual recruitment tools, and supporting targeted outreach to underserved communities. Jacobs will provide technical instruction, facility tours, and mentoring opportunities that expose local youth to hands-on STEM and sustainability careers.

**Career pathways, internships and co-op programs.** Natalie Lenz, one of our proposed CIP Coordinators, helped students navigate career interests through [CLIENT]'s RISE Internship Program in summer 2025. Graphic asset ID: `243_007CAM_3`.

#### 1.4. Bolstering Career Awareness

In support of [CLIENT]'s goals for inclusive growth and technical advancement, we'll work with [CLIENT] to reinforce its brand and raise the awareness of job and career opportunities, particularly for under-represented communities through targeted outreach activities. We'll continue to invest in local partnerships to help build the next generation of water professionals to carry [CLIENT]'s legacy forward. This plan builds on the many outreach programs that [CLIENT] and Jacobs have developed or have supported in the region, such as _Camp RISE, Business Capacity Development Program, Waves of Change, the Water & Sewer School, Wisconsin Rural Water Association_ and _Fresh Coast Ambassador Program_.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the pairing of internships as a recruitment channel with career awareness as a brand-building service to the client — the second half is what separates this from a generic internship paragraph, because it offers to promote the client's brand, not Jacobs'.

Pursuit-specific: every named institution and program, the named staff member in the caption, and the state association reference. Where a proposed key person has already worked inside a client program, say so and name them — that caption is the proof.
