# Serving as an Engaged Regional Partner in Other Communities with Similar Contracts

Beyond facility walls and project boundaries, our purpose is to create a more connected, sustainable world. We act as community stewards and partners wherever we go—from O&M contracts for water reclamation facilities to large-scale infrastructure improvements. What follows are examples of where we served as an engaged regional partner for contracts with similarities to [CLIENT]'s operating environment and values, as well as where we've helped improve the surrounding communities.

## 1. JXN Water, Jackson, Mississippi

*Focused on public health, equity, transparency, and long-term stewardship while investing in the community and maintaining compliance and workforce solutions.*

Since assuming operations from the prior operator in the wake of the 2022 crisis that left residents without safe drinking water, Jacobs has been stabilizing and modernizing Jackson's water services. Based on our success, in 2025, JXN Water awarded Jacobs a 9-year contract to operate and maintain its wastewater treatment facilities. We're investing in community initiatives, workforce development, and environmental stewardship, helping develop a local workforce pipeline to support long-term system reliability. We:

- Partnered with Hinds Community College to deliver Water and Maintenance 101 training, host career fairs, conduct facility tours, and donated to the JXN Water Scholarship Fund, which will cover full tuition for two students focused on water-related careers.
- Created pathways for existing entry-level staff to gain additional technical training through a free custom college course.
- Established an engineering scholarship endowment at Jackson State University (JSU), matched by the university, providing perpetual support to students.
- Hired **six local interns across operations, maintenance, and engineering—three interns graduated and joined Jacobs as full-time staff**.
- Provided career 101 modules at National Society of Black Engineers events at JSU, a historically black college/university, established a $25K endowment scholarship for engineering students, matched by a $25K donation from JSU.
- In addition to our O&M role, we're the Lead and Copper Rule Compliance Program Manager for JXN Water. In advance of the upcoming Lead and Copper Rule Improvement, we partnered with the Mississippi State Extension service to provide early tap sampling and remediation to schools and childcare facilities in our distribution network through their "SipSafe" Program. We crafted materials and hosted informational sessions to help facilities understand upcoming sampling requirements and what they can do to keep their students safe. We helped bridge the gap between SipSafe and the Mississippi Low Income Childcare Initiative to help facilities across the state understand what's required from them and provide opportunities for testing.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the section-opening paragraph that frames community stewardship as something Jacobs does everywhere rather than something offered for this bid, and the example's own shape — a one-line italic theme, the situation, the outcome that proves the client kept buying, then countable community investments. The last bullet is the reusable move of showing work done outside the contracted scope because the community needed it.

Pursuit-specific: nothing in the example needs generalizing — JXN Water is a reference client, named verbatim, and the proposal team QCs reference use before external release. Select this example when the pursuit involves a takeover, a compliance recovery, or a client with strong equity and public-health commitments.
