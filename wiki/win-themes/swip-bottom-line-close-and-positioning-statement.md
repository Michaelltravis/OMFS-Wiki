# Bottom Line

Jacobs will operate and care for [CLIENT]'s advanced water treatment program like it is our own—shoulder-to-shoulder with [CLIENT] staff—bringing unmatched O&M rigor, instant access to top reuse engineers and other water treatment experts, and measurable value that will reduce risk, increase reliability, and accelerate [CLIENT]'s path to DPR leadership. We always stay ahead of the curve solving problems quickly and anticipating what's to come, providing the [CLIENT] peace of mind that the work is always being done the right way.

**Boxed positioning statement (full-width, closing the section):**

> *Jacobs is the [CLIENT]'s best-value partner who will act as an extension of your team; deliver reliable, compliant, and transparent performance; and position the [CLIENT] to lead the nation in advanced water reuse.*

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the two-part close is the transferable structure. The "Bottom Line" paragraph does three things in order — states the relationship ("operate and care for it like it is our own, shoulder-to-shoulder with your staff"), names the three proofs the section delivered, and ends on an emotional benefit ("peace of mind that the work is always being done the right way") that is deliberately not a metric. The boxed statement then compresses the same argument into one sentence built on three parallel verbs, which is the form an evaluator is most likely to lift verbatim into a consensus scoring narrative — writing it in the shape they need is the point of the device.

The phrase "like it is our own" and the "shoulder-to-shoulder" image are the two most reusable fragments in the passage; both do the work of an ownership claim without a promise that can be scored against. The final clause — position the client to lead the nation in what they are already trying to do — is the reusable close for any pursuit where the client has a public ambition: it flatters the ambition and attaches the proposer to it in the same breath.

Pursuit-specific: the three named proofs must match what the section actually argued, and the client's ambition in the closing clause must be one they have stated publicly, or the sentence reads as flattery. Place this immediately after the value-added exhibit in [swip-value-added-offerings-package.md](swip-value-added-offerings-package.md); it also works, lightly trimmed, as the closing paragraph of a transmittal letter — see [swip-transmittal-letter-opening-and-closing.md](swip-transmittal-letter-opening-and-closing.md) for the letter-length version of the same argument.
