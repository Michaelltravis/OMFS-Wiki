# Partnership Framing — "Extension of Staff," Regional Technical Bench, and Integrated O&M+Engineering

## Our Role — An Extension of Client Staff

"We will embed with [client] staff as a true operating partner—coordinating daily, meeting on weekly/monthly/quarterly cadences, and giving [client] leadership real-time visibility through a tailored KPI dashboard. Mack Mckenzie, our CPO, will serve as your single point of contact and will escalate decisions quickly in close coordination with our Management Team. We will stand up a contract-deliverables tracker so both teams can see every compliance and reporting milestone at a glance, improving planning, transparency, and accountability across the project."

**Companion graphic (asset `148_008A26`):** an interlocking two-piece puzzle graphic — left piece "Jacobs," right piece "the CITY" — visually literalizing the "extension of staff" partnership claim. Client-specific: the right-hand puzzle piece is labeled with the generic term for the client type and must be relabeled (e.g., "the Authority," "the District," "the Utility") to match the actual client entity for reuse.

**Benefits to [Client] callout:** *"Faster decisions, fewer surprises, and inspection-ready documentation with a one-team culture that reduces management burden on [client] staff."*

## Our Technical Bench and Regional Support

"The onsite team will never operate alone. We will surround [PROJECT] with a dedicated regional maintenance team and immediate reach-back to a large O&M technical bench for process, OT/SCADA, asset management, and regulatory support. This model, **which includes approximately 1,300 hours of technical and regional support annually in our offer**, will increase responsiveness, reduce life-cycle risk, and keep specialized knowledge local to your systems."

**Companion graphic (asset `149_008A26`):** a circular badge reading "Core Onsite Team / Regional and Technical Support (1,300 hours of support annually in our fee)" — a compact device for quantifying reach-back support as a specific annual hour commitment rather than a vague "backed by our national network" claim.

**Benefits to [Client] callout:** *"Local responsiveness plus national depth—quicker fixes, lower downtime risk, and better lifecycle performance more cost-effectively."*

## More Than an Operator — Integrated O&M + Engineering

"Beyond day-to-day operations, Jacobs will bring industry-leading process engineering, design-build experience, and automation expertise to solve issues before they become outages. We will implement a comprehensive O&M system—process control, sampling and laboratory QA/QC, asset management, safety, emergency response, and formal SOPs—so operations will be standardized, auditable, and continuously improved. We will pair QA/QC reviews with corrective-action tracking and secure document control to keep records inspection-ready for [client] and regulators."

**Benefits to [Client] callout:** *"Minimizing unplanned shutdowns, faster root-cause resolution, and consistent, audit-ready operations across [PROJECT] and its associated assets."*

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-part "embedded partner → quantified technical reach-back → integrated engineering capability" structure; the single-point-of-contact + KPI dashboard + contract-deliverables tracker combination as a concrete transparency mechanism; and quantifying regional support as an annual hour figure rather than a vague claim. Pursuit-specific: the puzzle graphic's client-side label, the named single point of contact (Mack Mckenzie, CPO, is this pursuit's real proposed single point of contact — confirm current assignment before reuse), and the specific annual support-hour figure (1,300 hours here) must all be confirmed against the real proposed offer before reuse — do not carry forward this pursuit's hour commitment as if it were a standard figure. Pair with `swip-onsite-team-staffing-and-shift-model.md` for the onsite team this bench supports, and with `swip-value-added-offerings-package.md` for the itemized breakdown of what the regional-support hours and discounted engineering rate are worth.
