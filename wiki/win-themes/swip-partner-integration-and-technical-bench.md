# Partnership Framing — Extension of Client Staff, Quantified Technical Bench, and Integrated O&M plus Engineering

## Our Role — An Extension of [CLIENT] Staff

We will embed with [CLIENT] staff as a true operating partner—coordinating daily, meeting on weekly/monthly/quarterly cadences, and giving [CLIENT] leadership real-time visibility through a tailored KPI dashboard. Mack Mckenzie, our CPO, will serve as your single point of contact and will escalate decisions quickly in close coordination with our Management Team. We will stand up a contract-deliverables tracker so both teams can see every compliance and reporting milestone at a glance, improving planning, transparency, and accountability across the project.

**Companion graphic (asset `148_008A26`):** an interlocking two-piece puzzle — the left piece "Jacobs," the right piece the client entity — literalizing the extension-of-staff claim.

**Benefits to [CLIENT] callout:** *"Faster decisions, fewer surprises, and inspection-ready documentation with a one-team culture that reduces management burden on [CLIENT] staff."*

## Our Technical Bench and Regional Support

The onsite team will never operate alone. We will surround [CLIENT]'s advanced water treatment program with a dedicated regional maintenance team and immediate reach-back to a large O&M technical bench for process, OT/SCADA, asset management, and regulatory support. This model, **which includes approximately 1,300 hours of technical and regional support annually in our offer**, will increase responsiveness, reduce life-cycle risk, and keep specialized knowledge local to your systems.

**Companion graphic (asset `149_008A26`):** a circular badge reading "Core Onsite Team / Regional and Technical Support (1,300 hours of support annually in our fee)."

**Benefits to [CLIENT] callout:** *"Local responsiveness plus national depth—quicker fixes, lower downtime risk, and better lifecycle performance more cost-effectively."*

## More Than an Operator — Integrated O&M + Engineering

Beyond day-to-day operations, Jacobs will bring industry-leading process engineering, design-build experience, and automation expertise to solve issues before they become outages. We will implement a comprehensive O&M system—process control, sampling and laboratory QA/QC, asset management, safety, emergency response, and formal SOPs—so operations will be standardized, auditable, and continuously improved. We will pair QA/QC reviews with corrective-action tracking and secure document control to keep records inspection-ready for [CLIENT] and regulators.

**Benefits to [CLIENT] callout:** *"Minimizing unplanned shutdowns, faster root-cause resolution, and consistent, audit-ready operations across the advanced water treatment facility, the urban runoff recycling facility, and stormwater assets."*

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-move sequence of embedded partner, quantified technical reach-back, and integrated engineering capability; the single-point-of-contact plus KPI dashboard plus contract-deliverables tracker combination, which turns "transparency" into three inspectable mechanisms; the sentence "the onsite team will never operate alone" as an opening for any bench narrative; and above all the technique of expressing reach-back support as a specific number of hours included in the offer, which converts an unfalsifiable claim into a commitment the client can hold you to.

Pursuit-specific: confirm the annual support-hour figure against the real proposed offer (do not carry 1,300 hours forward as a house number), name the actual single point of contact, relabel the puzzle graphic for the client entity, and adjust the closing benefits callout to the target system's asset list.
