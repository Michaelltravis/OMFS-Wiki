# Executive Summary — Exclusive Consulting Discount as Immediate Added Value

**Key features and benefits of our solution for [CLIENT], a county wastewater utility, include:**

**Exclusive Consulting Discount Adds Immediate Value.** As part of our commitment to long-term partnership and cost transparency, Jacobs is offering [CLIENT] an exclusive discounted rate structure for consulting services directly tied to our O&M work at [CLIENT]'s wastewater facilities. This discount allows [CLIENT] to access Jacobs' top-tier engineering and technical expertise—such as follow-up studies from our Annual Innovation Workshops—at significantly reduced rates.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the framing that ties a consulting discount to the O&M contract itself, and presents it as cost transparency and partnership rather than as a discount on a separate line of business. Pursuit-specific: whether the offer is approved, what services it covers, and the mechanism that generates the follow-on work (Annual Innovation Workshops here).

This is the final benefit item in the executive summary benefit sequence and reads best immediately after it — pair with [Executive Summary — Comprehensive JV Solution and Client Benefits](exec-summary-comprehensive-jv-solution-benefits.md), which carries the preceding eight benefit paragraphs including the added-value investment commitment. For a fuller, longer-form version of the same offer in a technical section, see [Discounted Engineering Services Offer to an O&M Client](discounted-engineering-services-offer.md). Read `verbatim/fulton-county-2025/pages/p0010.md` for the passage in place; the hourly rate table on p. 32 of the source is commercial content and is deliberately not captured here.
