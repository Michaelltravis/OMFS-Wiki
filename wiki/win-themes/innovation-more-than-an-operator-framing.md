# "More Than an Operator" Innovation Section Opening

## MORE THAN AN OPERATOR: OUR INNOVATIVE APPROACH TO O&M

[CLIENT]'s RFP — a regional wastewater utility trust operating a multi-plant system — emphasizes innovations supported by clear cost and return-on-investment (ROI) analysis. While our due diligence has allowed us to identify meaningful opportunities, the level of precision required to fully quantify lifecycle costs and ROI will be refined once we have access to detailed operating data, asset performance, and system conditions during contract execution.

That said, we're not waiting to bring value. Below are ideas that we're excited to implement. **Innovation is a key part of Jacobs' DNA.** Once we're on-site, we'll continue to uncover opportunities that make a real difference for [CLIENT]. The innovations below are aligned with [CLIENT]'s priorities and based on proven approaches we have successfully implemented at similar facilities. As we assume operations, we'll validate each opportunity with site-specific data and work collaboratively with [CLIENT] to confirm costs and quantify benefits. We also understand each will require [CLIENT]'s approval before implementation.

While our base program delivers the performance [CLIENT] expects, we also bring a set of focused innovations that can reduce costs and address known system challenges — unlocking measurable gains in reliability, cost control, and operational efficiency.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-beat structure — acknowledge the ROI-precision limit honestly, pivot to "we're not waiting," then promise validation, collaboration, and client approval before implementation. This is the opening that lets the rest of a Section 8 make specific, quantified offers without over-promising.

Pursuit-specific: the RFP's exact innovation/ROI language, the priorities named, and which of the innovations that follow are actually in the base fee versus offered separately.
