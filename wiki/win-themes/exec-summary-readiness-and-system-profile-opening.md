# Executive Summary Opening — "Ready for a Change" Positioning and System Profile

## Positioning [CLIENT]'s wastewater program for its next phase

*[CLIENT] — a coastal New England town of approximately 10,000 year-round residents — is ready for a change.*

The Town has invested heavily in resiliency, mechanical upgrades, electrical improvements, and collection system rehabilitation, clear evidence of its commitment to protecting essential infrastructure. But the Town is seeking more than regulatory compliance. It is looking for **stronger leadership, clearer communication, and a proactive asset-management approach** that anticipates issues before they become problems. Above all, the Town wants a **trusted partner**, one willing to take justified risks, stand behind its decisions, and safeguard the community's long-term interests.

Jacobs will deliver that change, **using the approaches listed in Exhibit 2-1**. The Town's wastewater system supports not only the Town's residents, but also a sensitive coastal environment and a community that has been valued across the greater regional metropolitan area for generations. Maintaining and improving the infrastructure that supports this environment **requires disciplined operations, thoughtful planning, and a long-term perspective**.

Serving approximately 10,000 year-round residents and an additional 5,000 seasonal residents, the Town's wastewater system includes a 3.07-MGD wastewater treatment facility, seven pump stations, a stormwater pumping station, 42 miles of gravity sewer, a low-pressure system, and an ocean outfall. These assets operate under conditions shaped by tidal influence, wet-weather variability, and seasonal demand, requiring **proactive management and operational discipline**.

## Your next chapter

By selecting Jacobs, the Town positions itself to strengthen leadership, improve transparency, and align capital investment with long-term performance. With Nathan Callison leading the on-site team, supported by Jacobs' technical resources, we will elevate operational performance, protect critical infrastructure, and deliver consistent, dependable service for decades to come.
