# Cover Letter Close — Named Leadership Team, Contract Prerequisites, and Commitments

**A team built for [CLIENT]'s success.** Our highly qualified team combines local leaders who are familiar with [CLIENT]'s facilities, an experienced regional management team, and a deep bench of technical experts. Leading the charge is Project Manager **David Pitocchelli**, who knows these facilities firsthand from his previous work as project manager for this program. Assistant Project Manager **Alex Rodriguez** combines Oklahoma roots with 13 years of multi-facility operations experience, including leadership of a 180-MGD facility for Seattle Public Utilities. Project Director **Steve Carpenter** provides independent oversight, drawing on his leadership of [CLIENT]'s Hefner WTP and his role in stabilizing complex systems such as Jackson, Mississippi. Strategic Advisor **Rick Warner, PE, WEF Fellow** brings 25 years of water and wastewater leadership and a trusted relationship with [CLIENT] leadership. Together, this team brings the experience, continuity, and accountability needed to deliver results.

We commend [CLIENT] for modernizing the contract to reinforce your operational priorities and hold the operator accountable for results. However, the contract by itself cannot fully achieve the performance transformation you envision. Two additional prerequisites are critical: pricing that responsibly supports the level of service expected, and a cultural evolution within the staff to embrace consistent, disciplined execution. Jacobs' proposal is designed not only to comply with the updated agreement but to support these broader conditions that ultimately determine success.

We are committed to serving as a trusted long-term partner to [CLIENT] and are deeply invested in your success, not only to deliver outstanding WWTP operations but also to help strengthen [CLIENT]'s future achievements through our ongoing partnership. Executive Sponsor **Kevin Dahl** will serve as the primary point of contact for this procurement. Jacobs acknowledges receipt of Addendum No. 1 issued on April 22, 2026, and confirms our ability and willingness to meet the tentative deadline and provide any additional documentation required to award the Service Agreement as specified in this RFP. This confirmation ensures alignment with project timelines and facilitates a smooth selection process. Upon award, we'll provide the required performance bond or irrevocable letter of credit, insurance documentation, and any other materials required under the RFP and Service Agreement.

Sincerely,

Kevin Dahl, PE, CMRT, CRL | Executive Sponsor

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the four-role leadership formula — a Project Manager with prior hands-on knowledge of these specific facilities, an Assistant PM carrying large-plant scale experience, a Project Director providing *independent* oversight, and a Strategic Advisor who holds the client relationship — each named in bold with one differentiating sentence. Also universal: the closing paragraph's mechanics (single named point of contact, addendum acknowledgment, confirmation of willingness to meet the award schedule, and the commitment to furnish performance bond or irrevocable letter of credit and insurance on award).

The middle paragraph is the distinctive move and is reusable as a pattern: praise the client's contract modernization, then state plainly that the contract alone will not deliver the transformation, and name the two conditions that will — here, responsible pricing and a cultural evolution among staff. Use it where the client has visibly reformed its procurement and where a candid tone will be read as partnership rather than presumption.

Pursuit-specific: all four names, tenures, and facilities; the executive sponsor; the addendum number and date.
