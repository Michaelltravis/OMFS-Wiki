Our outreach strategy will include a mix of digital and print tools, such as:

- **Printed Materials:** Distribute brochures and educational leaflets to community centers, libraries, and schools. These may include bilingual brochures and fact sheets.

- Press releases distributed in coordination with [CLIENT].
- Content contributions to existing neighborhood/HOA newsletters.
- Emergency alerts (as applicable) coordinated with local authorities.

From Treatment to Conservation: Advancing Understanding of the Water Cycle. We’re actively exploring ways to support the Chattahoochee Nature Center in expanding its water-focused educational programs and have already had initial discussions. One idea involves an interactive water cycle display overlooking Heron Pond—bringing the story of water to life and helping students and visitors understand how wastewater treatment protects the environment and sustains communities. We’re also considering making the butterfly exhibit a permanent feature, enhanced with content showing how the water cycle affects butterfly lifecycles. By connecting treatment plants to the health of our waterways, these efforts aim to deepen appreciation for water conservation and highlight the vital role of these assets.
