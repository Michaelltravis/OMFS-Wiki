# Project Narrative — Multi-Decade DBO Partnership With Sustained Award Recognition (Regional WWTP)

Jacobs has operated, maintained, and improved [CLIENT]'s 8.5-MGD wastewater treatment facility and related systems in the Midwest for over three decades under a design-build-operate (DBO) arrangement, achieving regulatory compliance, operational excellence, cost savings, and industry recognition throughout. Jacobs has continued to optimize performance and support the client's environmental goals while providing integrated services spanning operations, maintenance, asset management, capital planning, and technical support. A team of roughly a dozen staff operates the facility and supports pump stations and several satellite systems serving approximately 50,000 residents and local industry.

**Origin.** Jacobs assumed operations when the facility was under a noncompliance finding, implementing a comprehensive operational strategy and maintenance management program that restored compliance and resolved enforcement actions.

**Capital improvements.** Improvements have included treatment performance and compliance upgrades, comprising biological nutrient removal (BNR) improvements and installation of a large membrane bioreactor (MBR) system. The facility also includes digesters and dewatering and storage systems for solids handling.

**Technology and performance.** The plant uses MBR technology to produce high-quality effluent and handle peak flows up to roughly 17 MGD — well above its design rate. Following MBR installation, optimization efforts reduced electrical consumption by more than a third, with additional savings in chemicals and natural gas. Proactive maintenance and asset-management practices have extended membrane performance beyond its expected service life.

**Regionalized services.** The team operates several satellite wastewater treatment systems, providing regionalized solutions to industrial and municipal clients beyond the primary plant — a model worth highlighting where a pursuit has, or could develop, satellite/regional service potential.

**Regulatory and program management.** Jacobs restored and has maintained compliance with regulatory requirements, and developed and administers the client's industrial pretreatment program, including ordinance updates, industrial-user permitting, monitoring, and cost-recovery mechanisms.

**Recognition.** The facility has received five awards spanning roughly two decades, demonstrating sustained, not one-time, performance: two large-facility safety awards from the state water-environment association (one jointly with the client, in the most recent year, and one from an earlier year); a regional EPA "best operated and maintained facility" recognition; and two state engineering-society excellence awards (an engineering excellence award and a separate eminent-conceptor-for-engineering award, both from the same year).

**Community involvement.** The team supports environmental stewardship and education initiatives, including partnerships, student engagement, facility tours, and participation in regional water-resource events.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: framing a long-tenured contract around a single quantified technical achievement (here, a specific percentage reduction in electrical consumption tied to a named technology upgrade) rather than only "decades of good service," and citing a spread of awards across multiple years to prove sustained (not one-time) performance. The "regionalized satellite systems" angle is a reusable differentiator for any pursuit where consolidation/regionalization is on the client's radar — pair with the regionalization topic in `../technical-approach/annual-innovation-workshop-program.md`. Pursuit-specific: replace the technology (MBR) and the efficiency percentage with whatever is actually true of the reference project; do not reuse the MBR/energy-percentage combination for a facility that doesn't have comparable technology. Pair with `past-performance-narrative-structure.md`.
