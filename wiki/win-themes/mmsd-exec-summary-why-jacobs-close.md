# Why Jacobs: Your Trusted and Vision-Aligned Partner Driving Performance, Accountability, and Community Value

[CLIENT] — a Midwest regional sewerage district — has been clear about what success looks like: impeccable, timely, flawless compliance; visible PM completion; cost efficiency and value for ratepayers and regional stakeholders; sustained leadership aligned with its 2035 Vision; engaged leadership and strong union relationships; meaningful community benefits with real workforce pathways; and transparency, collaboration, and accountability across contract delivery. Jacobs' proposal is designed to deliver exactly that—through a delivery model built around disciplined execution and visibility.

## What's Next for [CLIENT] — With Jacobs at Your Side

- **Proven O&M leadership** and depth of technical resources supported by global SMEs, to strengthen [CLIENT]'s position as a vanguard utility and advance innovation, resilience, optimization, and resource recovery aligned with the 2035 Vision.

- **A transition that builds the platform and culture for higher performance and elevates [CLIENT]'s operations**—readying people, processes, and controls to accelerate reliability and continuous improvement, with continuity and compliance protected throughout.

- **A value-focused partnership that improves performance, controls costs, and delivers shared savings through optimization and innovation**—enabled by advanced digital tools and a PgM framework that ensures "no surprises" transparency with auditable controls, dashboards, and structured reviews.

- **Maintenance excellence** with PM proof-of-work transparency and elevated PdM to protect assets, prevent failures, and reduce compliance risk.

- **A stable, respected workforce and strong union relationships** built on early engagement and respect for collective bargaining agreements—protecting continuity and strengthening the culture that drives reliable operations.

- **Community benefits that are planned, delivered, and measured** through regional partnership, public engagement, and workforce development as structured, accountable programs and that support future research initiatives.

- **Safety-first operations reinforced by leaders during every shift** to protect our people and the public, reduce risk, and sustain compliance, supported by strong cyber/physical security for [CLIENT]'s assets and systems.

> At Jacobs, we challenge today to reinvent tomorrow—and [CLIENT] is exactly the kind of partner that makes that purpose real. We are committed to bringing the full strength of our people, our technology, and our global insights to help [CLIENT] deliver resilient operations, community value, and a future that sets the standard for utilities nationwide.
>
> —Bob Pragada, Chair & CEO, Jacobs

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the two-part close. Part one is the client's own success criteria played back as a single sentence with semicolons — compliance, PM completion, cost efficiency, leadership, union relationships, community benefits, transparency — followed by "our proposal is designed to deliver exactly that." Part two is the bulleted commitment list, each bullet a bolded claim plus the client consequence. The CEO statement is reusable across pursuits with the client name swapped.

Pursuit-specific: the success-criteria list, the 2035 Vision references, and the union and community-benefits bullets where a pursuit has neither.

Pairs with: [Executive Summary opening and six core attributes](mmsd-exec-summary-trusted-partner-opening-and-six-core-attributes.md) — the two blocks bookend the section and should always be edited together so the opening attributes and the closing commitments stay in the same order.

Note on overlap: the library holds several other executive-summary closing blocks (for example `exec-summary-innovation-commitment-and-closing-statement.md` and `swip-bottom-line-close-and-positioning-statement.md`) drawn from other sources; choose one close per section rather than stacking them.
