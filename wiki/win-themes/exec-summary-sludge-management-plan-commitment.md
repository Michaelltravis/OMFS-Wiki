# Executive Summary Required Plan — Sludge Management Plan, Generation Through Disposal

**Sludge Management Plan (Schedule 14):** An integrated, generation-through-disposal program covering all four facilities and ~170 permitted land application sites. Facility-specific strategies for North Canadian's H₂S and plastics issues, Deer Creek's hydrogen peroxide dosing protocol, Chisholm Creek's liquid transfer coordination, and South Canadian's construction-period continuity. All tied to a tiered Storage Management Protocol that keeps pad utilization well below Schedule 21 thresholds.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the shape of the commitment — one integrated program stated end to end ("generation through disposal"), then one named problem and response per facility, then a tiered storage protocol expressed against a contractual threshold rather than as a general intention. Evaluators reading a multi-plant solids scope are looking for evidence that the bidder knows the plants differ; this sentence pattern supplies that evidence in the space of an executive-summary bullet.

Also universal: the "tiered Storage Management Protocol" device, in which storage pad utilization is managed against defined trigger tiers so that the contractual threshold is never approached passively. Carry the device; re-derive the tiers.

Pursuit-specific: all four facility names and their conditions, the count of permitted land application sites, and both schedule numbers. Where the target contract does not number its schedules, cite the permit or the contract article that sets the storage limit instead.
