# Maintenance AI Assistant Powered by Palantir

The latest module added to the Intelligent O&M suite of tools uses large language model (LLM) AI on the Palantir Foundry platform to ingest and read only a specific plant's documentation and give users a search engine style interface to ask plain English questions and get answers in seconds. First deployed at our project at the Spokane County, WA, Regional WWTP, the system is designed to only use site-specific information, but in any format—CMMS, electronic manuals, PDF files, Word documents, scanned documents, CAD drawings, etc. The AI Assistant only uses this library for answering questions, and nothing is obtained from the internet unless it is specifically related to what the maintenance team wants to know.

## Maintenance AI Assistant Powered by Palantir

Wastewater treatment facilities have thousands of assets, and nearly every one of them comes with an equipment manual that defines what type of lubricant, repair parts, preventive maintenance, and other details are critical to maximizing the life of the asset. Much of this essential, frequently needed information like maintenance schedules, will be built into the CMMS. The maintenance team will still need to regularly refer to the vendor manuals, each being possibly hundreds of pages in length. Combined with the total number of assets, a mechanic might be faced with finding a needle in a haystack of information on a random shelf where plant documents are kept. The same kind of sorting problem happens when looking at equipment repair histories in CMMS—tools to support mechanics with this kind of plant-specific information have been unavailable. **Until now.**

Our maintenance teams are now able to access vast stores of their own information at incredible speed. Questions like, “What are the safety concerns when working with the microturbine?” are answered with a few summary bullets, and then the reference pages are listed and linked showing exactly where the information came from. This is a vital step in building trust in the validity of the results. Other broader questions are even more exciting, such as “What five pumps require the most maintenance based on work order history since 2017?” or “List the top ten most expensive repairs in Building 6,” all answered from a combination of CMMS and financial databases. Finally, years of mechanics comments written into closed work orders can now be analyzed for new insights like “Which preventive maintenance activities do electricians believe are the most effective?” or “What are the most common complaints about Clarifier No. 2?” The ability to leverage years of collective wisdom has been unlocked, and **all of these features make our maintenance team more efficient, while preserving and extending the life of [CLIENT]'s assets.**
