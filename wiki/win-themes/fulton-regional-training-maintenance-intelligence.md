# JV Regional Support, Training, and Maintenance Intelligence Benefits

JC Solutions will supplement onsite project teams with 4,300 hours of support from Jacobs' and CERM's deep bench of regional O&M resources and industry-leading technical SMEs, with a special focus on high-level technologies such as MBR. This enhances the capabilities of the onsite team with immediate access to specialized expertise for solving operational issues, optimizing processes, and planning for future needs through direct access to SMEs.

JC Solutions will invest 4,000 hours of comprehensive training for the onsite team, including specialized MBR operations modules delivered by subject matter experts. We will provide annual high-technology training programs tailored to emerging industry needs and evolving technologies. The result is enhanced operator proficiency leading to improved process performance and consistent regulatory compliance, along with ongoing onsite access to advanced training that creates a highly skilled, adaptable workforce without additional budget impact.

JC Solutions will implement our proprietary Intelligent O&M suite with Palantir integration that overlays the existing CMMS to optimize maintenance resources and work-order priorities. Advanced analytics tools determine optimal preventive-maintenance schedules based on work-order history and risk assessment. This maximizes operational KPIs and reduces maintenance costs through optimized resource allocation and scheduling guided by ISO 55000 principles and Jacobs expertise. The team gains immediate access to hundreds of vendor manuals and insights from thousands of work orders to perform PM optimization in a fraction of the time.

JC Solutions will conduct a comprehensive Site Security Assessment (SSA) to review existing physical-security measures, identify vulnerabilities, and develop targeted mitigation strategies. We will perform localized crime-pattern assessments around each facility to inform security recommendations and resource deployment. This enhances protection of critical infrastructure with a security framework that reduces vulnerability to increasing threats while maintaining operational continuity, and it decreases the risk of criminal incidents and associated liability through targeted security improvements informed by local crime-pattern analysis.
