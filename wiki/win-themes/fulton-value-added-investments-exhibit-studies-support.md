# Value-Added Investments Exhibit, Part 1: Studies, Regional Support, Training, Energy, and Security

The value-added offerings we highlight in Exhibit 2-4 are included in our Base Fee. The column labeled "Value of Investments Incorporated in Our Proposal" indicates the typical cost of these services if they were provided separately from the O&M contract under a stand-alone engineering/consulting services contract. Our goal is to demonstrate the breadth of additional services and value the JC Solutions team will bring to [CLIENT], a county wastewater utility. They reflect our commitment to not just meet but exceed your expectations.

**JC Solutions includes $8+ million worth of investments in the [CLIENT] O&M base proposal.**

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the exhibit architecture. Naming the stand-alone consulting value of every committed extra converts "value-added" from an adjective into a number, and the "Learn More" page column lets an evaluator verify each claim deeper in the proposal — reuse both conventions.

Pursuit-specific: every dollar value, hour commitment, facility count, utility name, and cross-reference page. The $235,000–$365,000 energy savings range and the 4,300 / 4,000 hour commitments must be re-derived for the new system before reuse.
