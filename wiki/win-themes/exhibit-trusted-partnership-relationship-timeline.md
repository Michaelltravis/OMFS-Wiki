## Reuse guidance

Universal: the device itself — a two-decade relationship timeline placed opposite the cover letter, mixing small early assignments with recent enterprise-scale programs, and marking at least one or two engagements "to present" so the relationship reads as current. It is the strongest available answer to the evaluator's unspoken question about a challenger bid: whether the firm will need a learning curve. Sequence the callouts so the most recent years carry the largest and most system-wide programs (asset management, SCADA, condition assessment, business continuity), which is what implies readiness to operate.

Pursuit-specific: every milestone, every year, the exhibit title, and the graphic asset ID. Where the account history is thinner, the same device works across a region or state rather than a single client.
