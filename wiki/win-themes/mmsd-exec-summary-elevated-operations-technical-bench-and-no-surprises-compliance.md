# Elevated Operations Driven by Technical Expertise and Innovation

## Cost-Effective, Compliant Operations That Protect [CLIENT]'s Reputation

We've listened carefully to [CLIENT] staff to understand your system and what success needs to look like in the future. [CLIENT] is a Midwest regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities and a biosolids production operation. Our approach is built to protect what [CLIENT] values most: permit performance, zero overflows, [CLIENT]'s biosolids product quality and reliable production, safe and resilient operations, cost effectiveness, and progress toward your 2035 Vision. We'll operate [CLIENT]'s complex network as one interconnected system—so decisions are coordinated, accountability is consistent, and performance improves across your facilities.

**Technical Excellence, Optimization, and Community-Facing Performance.** Jacobs' comprehensive O&M approach brings deep expertise in asset management, process optimization, digital operations, and system-wide process control. Operating as One Water, One Team, One Vision with open-book reporting and real-time monitoring for [CLIENT] staff, you'll always have clear visibility into what's happening, why it's happening, and what we're doing about it.

**Commitment to Compliance and "No Surprises" Performance.** Jacobs will operate every facility and control system to strictly adhere to permit conditions and [CLIENT]'s performance standards—supported by dedicated technical leadership, compliance tools, and a culture of accountability. PgM tools such as dashboards and committee-ready reports will connect operational conditions to outcomes and costs, so issues are identified early, communicated clearly, and resolved with documented corrective actions.

## Access to a Full Breadth of Technical Expertise to Strengthen Operations

By leveraging Jacobs' vast O&M and technical experts, our delivery model provides both the cost effectiveness and agility of a dedicated local team and the depth of a global enterprise—ensuring continuity, redundancy, and innovation. Through this structure, [CLIENT] gains a high-performing operations partner committed to delivering reliability, operational excellence, and continuous improvement throughout the agreement. **A proof of our commitment is our Annual Innovation Workshop where we bring our industry SMEs directly to your team** to share best practices and new technologies, explore future improvement initiatives and **proactively combat operator complacency over time.**
