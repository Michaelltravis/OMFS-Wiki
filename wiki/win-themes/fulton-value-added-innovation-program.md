# JV Value-Added Innovation Program Opening

JC Solutions' mission is to work alongside [CLIENT], a county wastewater utility, to deliver on its expectations and continue the important legacy of these vital facilities. Our SMEs in operations, maintenance, optimization, asset management, lab, SCADA/Operations Technology (OT), and safety have studied the facilities on our site visit and carefully reviewed the data provided. Many have prior experience with the facilities as well. Drawing on these resources, we have crafted a custom approach that includes numerous value-added benefits, especially when compared to the current and future operations.

Details of these near-term and long-term operations, process, and facilities improvements and their benefits are summarized in Exhibits 2-4 and 2-5, and we also provide a hyperlink to the location in this document where we describe them in further detail. We look forward to discussing these ideas with [CLIENT].

The value-added offerings we highlight in Exhibit 2-4 are incorporated in our proposal. Our goal is to demonstrate the breadth of additional services and value the JC Solutions team will bring to [CLIENT]. They reflect our commitment to not just meet but exceed expectations.

JC Solutions will complete a comprehensive Odor Emissions Study at contract start, including source sampling from odorous treatment processes and analysis for odor concentrations and reduced sulfur compounds. We will perform odor-dispersion modeling using site-specific hourly atmospheric conditions from the past five years to assess offsite impacts and identify priority improvement areas. Beyond our commitment to day-to-day proactive odor control, this provides focused identification of significant odor sources and dispersion patterns, with prioritized improvement recommendations to minimize community impact and complaints, particularly valuable for [FACILITY B], the second treatment facility, where conditions may have changed since the 2009 study.

JC Solutions will conduct a comprehensive evaluation of opportunities for alternative beneficial-reuse programs for biosolids, including the potential for Class B biosolids at [FACILITY A], the first treatment facility, from the aerobic digesters. The study completes a cost analysis comparing beneficial-reuse options and Class B production costs against current landfill-disposal expenses, potentially identifying significant long-term operational savings.
