# ISO 55001-Aligned Asset Management System for PM/CM Excellence

[CLIENT] — a Midwest regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities — wants a rigorous and accountable maintenance approach with the visibility needed to manage risk and maximize asset life. **We'll implement a practical ISO 55001-aligned Asset Management System**, governed through [CLIENT]-facing committees. It will drive reliability-centered maintenance and use NEXGEN as the system of record, with work orders closed using field evidence, supervisor verification, and timely closeout. Shared dashboards will track PM compliance, CM backlog/aging, cost/labor, and asset signals—so performance is auditable and actionable.

**Maintenance Certainty from Enhanced Leadership and Deep Bench.** As another Above-and-Beyond enhancement, **Jacobs has added a Director of Maintenance & Asset Management, John Loucks-Powell, CRL**—a proven leader who will align the maintenance teams and drive an asset-management-centered PM/CM delivery approach. John is backed by Jacobs' regional maintenance and specialty resources to support onsite crews during peaks, complex work, and emergencies—so potential staffing constraints won't impede execution. Jacobs' national asset management team will also provide tools, analytics, and implementation support to strengthen onsite capability.

**Reliability-Centered Maintenance to Eliminate Repeat Failures.** We'll call on our regional and national specialists to also bring their expertise in reliability-centered maintenance, PM optimization, predictive maintenance (PdM) strategies, CMMS/NEXGEN, and risk-based prioritization to our failure analysis. This ensures we identify true root causes, update work plans and maintenance intervals, prevent repeat issues, and embed lessons learned into ongoing operations.

**Reliability Enhancement Programs for Both Water Reclamation Facilities.** We've developed reliability enhancement plans for both WRFs to focus on the assets and systems that have commonly affected uptime. These programs will connect disciplined work execution with reliability analytics to improve equipment availability, reduce unplanned disruptions, and lower associated compliance risk.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the four-move structure — the system (ISO 55001-aligned, committee-governed, CMMS as system of record, evidence-based closeout, shared dashboards); the named leader who owns it; the specialist bench behind the leader; and the reliability analytics loop that stops repeat failures. The closed-loop Plan/Execute/Verify/Closeout/Learn graphic travels with it.

Pursuit-specific: the CMMS name, the named Director, and the assertion that facility-specific reliability enhancement plans already exist. In this pursuit the facilities are named in the source; the block generalizes them to "both WRFs" while keeping the substance.
