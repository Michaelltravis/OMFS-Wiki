# Executive Summary Required Plan — Solids Management Plan as One Coordinated System

**Solids Management Plan (Schedule 21):** Strategic framework for dewatering, storage, hauling, and disposal as one coordinated system with clear operational triggers and escalation protocols aligned with our Odor Control and Process Control Plans. Solids, process performance, and odor managed together—and positioned to guide [CLIENT] through the transition from land application to landfill disposal and the Deer Creek heat drying integration.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the cross-plan alignment claim. Naming the other plans this one is aligned with — odor control and process control — is what turns a required deliverable into evidence of integrated thinking, and it costs one clause. The second universal move is the forward posture: the bidder offers to guide the client through changes the client has already decided on, which reads as partnership rather than as scope expansion.

Also universal: "clear operational triggers and escalation protocols" as the mechanism by which a strategic framework becomes operable. Any solids, odor, or wet-weather plan summary in an executive summary can use that formulation, provided the underlying plan actually defines the triggers.

Pursuit-specific: the schedule number, the named facility for heat drying, and the disposal-route change. If the target client has no pending solids change, replace that clause with the risk the solids program is actually managing — hauling market volatility, land application permit renewal, or dewatering capacity, for example.
