# Local Workforce, Digital Twin, and Smart Collections Benefits

JC Solutions will partner with regional technical colleges to build an effective Workforce Development Program that includes Class III Operator certification courses and expands water/wastewater training opportunities, creating a continuous pipeline of qualified operators. The program includes a comprehensive Internship Program for local residents with structured rotations and mentorship in plant operations, asset management, and community engagement. It enhances operational excellence through a skilled local workforce trained for water-utility needs and strengthens community investment with preferential hiring of [CLIENT] residents and sustainable career pathways that address industry staffing challenges.

JC Solutions will create dynamic digital-twin models of treatment processes to simulate and optimize operations in a risk-free virtual environment, focusing on biological nutrient removal, chemical dosing, and energy reduction. We will implement a three-stage Digital Twin solution that analyzes hydraulics, optimizes control strategies, and provides a realistic simulator interface for operator training and process validation. The result is enhanced operational efficiency through optimized control strategies that reduce energy consumption and chemical costs while improving process stability, plus risk-free evaluation of seasonal adjustments, peak-flow management, and process modifications to meet evolving regulations without disrupting ongoing operations.

JC Solutions will integrate near-real-time data from multiple sources—SCADA, rain gauges, level sensors, and weather forecasts—onto the Palantir Foundry-powered AquaDNA platform for comprehensive pump-station-system monitoring. We will implement up to 15 machine-learning and AI use cases to process data and provide actionable analytics, including real-time optimization of pump-station and diversion-level set points. We will install automatic deragging devices at the four most vulnerable wastewater pump stations, one in each quadrant, to proactively detect and address potential pump-jamming issues. This enhances wet-weather management through predictive alarms and visualized pump-station operations, reducing compliance issues and operational inefficiencies; it improves response capabilities during critical events and proactively mitigates sewer overflows through early detection of jammed pump conditions at critical pump stations.

The investments will be completed within the first 24 months of the O&M contract. During transition, JC Solutions will collaborate with [CLIENT] to identify priorities and preferred timing for each investment.
