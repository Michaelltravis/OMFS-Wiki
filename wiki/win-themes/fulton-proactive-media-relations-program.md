# Proactive Media Relations Program

Our media relations strategy emphasizes proactive, accurate, and responsible communication. Our team can create print and digital content starting with a basic media kit for each facility covering the history, basics of operations, role in environmental sustainability and regional water management, and the communities it serves. This will serve as the foundation for media outreach and help ensure consistency across the service area. We will look for positive stories about staff, community partnerships, special events, behind-the-scene tours, and more to craft tailored media releases that generate coverage and can be amplified through social media.

Our focus would be on hyperlocal outlets, particularly the Appen Media Group, which has a robust multi-media presence in the service area of all facilities and presents a variety of opportunities to share stories. These include the newspapers Alpharetta-Roswell Herald, Johns Creek Herald, and Milton Herald; online news hub AppenMedia.com; social media channels with 50,000 followers/subscribers, and the Appen Podcast Network.

Additional outlets include North Fulton Neighbor, North Fulton News (AJC), GPB Radio and WABE (public broadcasting). Atlanta-based print and broadcast media would also be targeted but the chance of success is greater with local outlets.
