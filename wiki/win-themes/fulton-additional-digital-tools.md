# Additional Digital Tools for Next-Level Optimization

In addition to the included offerings, [CLIENT] may desire to explore some of Jacobs' other available digital tools, which have the potential for further significant optimization and cost savings. Because these items are beyond the requested scope of services, we briefly discuss their potential for savings, improving efficiency, and further safeguarding compliance and reliability. More detailed information can be provided should [CLIENT] find them of interest.

Jacobs can deploy Palantir's artificial intelligence to analyze operational data from multiple sources—SCADA, lab systems, and maintenance records—and transform it into actionable insights. Machine-learning algorithms specifically optimize power consumption across high-usage applications such as aeration, UV, and pumping, with push notifications for operator review. The tools enhance process stability and resource utilization through advanced analytics that identify patterns human operators might miss. They offer potential power-consumption reduction of 10–25% beyond traditional optimization methods, yielding over $250,000 in annual savings while maintaining full permit compliance, with potential savings of $1,600,000 over five years.

Jacobs can apply data-science and AI/Palantir techniques to optimize chemical dosing across all treatment processes using existing plant data and SCADA systems. A push-notification system provides dosage recommendations with comprehensive dashboards for tracking compliance limits and chemical usage. The approach can achieve 10–30% improvement in chemical dosage rates for sodium hypochlorite, sodium hydroxide, and Phos-sorb while maintaining full regulatory compliance. It can generate approximately $200,000 in annual savings to be credited entirely to [CLIENT] after setup and license fees, with potential savings of $1,100,000 over five years.

Jacobs can apply its AI-powered Dragonfly tool to assist with NASSCO PACE coding of sewer-inspection videos, combining artificial intelligence with human QA/QC for superior accuracy. The tool provides 30–40% labor savings compared to traditional manual defect coding. The team can generate comprehensive data analysis and reports using Argon software to create data-driven recommendations for collections-system optimization and support CIP development. This provides faster defect detection and CCTV reviews, allowing more pipe inspection in less time with consistent, objective data that reduces manual variability. It also improves capital planning through data-driven prioritization of rehabilitation needs and problem areas, helping reduce sewer backups, minimize I&I, and increase system capacity.
