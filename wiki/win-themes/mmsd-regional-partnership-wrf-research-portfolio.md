# Our Research and Innovation Credentials

Jacobs' team of operations, maintenance, and global technical SMEs offer research and innovation capabilities to help [CLIENT] deliver direct and substantial benefits to ratepayers and the broader region. Jacobs has helped utilities form research collaboratives and deliver utility management and water treatment innovations. We're a Founding Partner of the Leading Utilities of the World Network. In addition, since 2020, Jacobs has completed 40 WRF studies totaling $9.5M in partnership with utilities, academic institutions, and subconsultant partners. Select studies are shown in **Exhibit V-5**.

We look forward to partnering with [CLIENT] in our ongoing research work with WRF on topics that are of interest to [CLIENT] or would enhance your operations. Examples of that research:

**Partnerships/Organizational Culture/Leadership:** Approaches to Build Strong Partnerships and Solidify Successful Interagency Reuse Projects (in partnership with the Pacific Institute).

**Collection Systems:** Minimization of Odors and Related Corrosion in Collection Systems.

**Wastewater Treatment:** Development of Innovative Predictive Control Strategies for Nutrient Removal; Development of a Technology Roadmap for Sustainable Wastewater Treatment in a Carbon-Constrained World; WERF Energy Efficiency in Wastewater Treatment in North America.

**Residuals Processing and Resources Recovery:** Biosolids Processing Modifications for Cake Odor Reduction; Beyond Nutrient: Recovering Carbon and Other Commodity Products from Wastewater; Recovery of Plasmids and Rare Earth Elements from Wastewater.

**GHG Emissions/ESG:** Establishing Industry-Wide Guidance for Water Utility Life Cycle GHG Emission Inventories; Advancing the Understanding of Nitrous Oxide Emissions Through Enhanced Whole-Plant Monitoring and Quantification.

**Advanced Water Treatment/Water Reuse:** Bench-Scale Test of Alternative Treatment for Direct Potable Reuse; Integrating Real-Time Collection System Monitoring Approaches into Enhanced Source Control Programs for Potable Reuse (in partnership with Carollo Engineers).
