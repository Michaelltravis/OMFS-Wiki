# Odor Study Gap Analysis and Updated Odor Control Report Offer

## Odor Study Gap Analysis and Updated Odor Control Report

We'll take a structured, action-oriented approach to odor management planning. We'll review prior [CLIENT] odor studies — [CLIENT] being a regional wastewater utility trust — validate current findings against field conditions, and identify where data or scope gaps limit decision making. From there, we'll complete targeted fieldwork and technical analysis to close those gaps and develop an updated, implementation-ready plan with prioritized odor control actions.

Because odor control is a core performance requirement, this effort will focus on practical, cost-effective solutions that improve compliance, safety, asset protection, and community outcomes. Work will be delivered in phases, allowing [CLIENT] to act quickly on the highest-impact improvements while building toward longer-term systemwide optimization.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the four-step sequence — review prior studies, validate against field conditions, identify decision-limiting gaps, close them with targeted fieldwork — and the phased delivery promise that lets the client act early. Also universal is the framing of odor control as simultaneously a compliance, safety, asset-protection, and community issue, which is what justifies the study to a governing board.

Pursuit-specific: which prior studies exist, who wrote them, what triggered them, and whether odor control is named as a performance requirement in the contract.

This is one of the candidate studies offered under [the discounted engineering services offer](discounted-engineering-services-offer.md); pair the two so the study reads as funded rather than aspirational.
