# Cover Letter Value Bullets — Five Day-One Priorities from Due Diligence

We are pleased to submit a value-driven proposal built around what matters most:

**Addressing the highest priorities from day one.** Our due diligence provided a clear view of the operational gaps, and we are prepared to act immediately, by providing:

- **Odor and H₂S control.** The H₂S conditions at North Canadian and Deer Creek affect nearby residents, reduce asset life, and present a significant safety hazard to personnel. We'll address these conditions through targeted chemical addition at key points within the WWTFs, improve air changes with secondary auxiliary equipment, and point-source odor identification and remediation. These measures will support safe, reliable O&M activities across the facilities. Blackline Safety H₂S devices will serve as the primary personnel safety tool, providing continuous air monitoring and man-down notifications.

- **Process performance and compliance.** Fecal compliance exceedances, inadequate chlorination/dechlorination balance, and untended SCADA alarms and inaccurate instrumentation all point to a process control program that needs structure and accountability. Our structured process control program will have defined operating targets, flow-paced chemical dosing, and restored instrumentation—giving operators the tools to prevent permit excursions.

- **Deferred maintenance and asset condition.** [CLIENT]'s facilities' deferred maintenance has compounded over time, and the new contract rightly holds the operator accountable for thorough documentation. Our staffing approach will shift performing the tank drain and clean program along with minor PMs to operators. This shift allows our maintenance team to focus on preventative, predictive and corrective maintenance throughout the facilities. Within the first 90 days, we'll complete a comprehensive condition assessment, establish a defensible baseline, and transition maintenance from reactive to proactive using our Asset Replacement Modeling (ARM) approach.

- **Staffing stability and accountability.** Staff turnover and wage competitiveness have been well-documented challenges under the current operator. We'll prioritize retaining qualified incumbent staff, implement competitive compensation and career development pathways, and leverage regional support resources to ensure consistent, reliable operations.

**Transparent communication and performance measures that drive results.** We'll implement a robust communication framework supported by real-time dashboards and structured reporting aligned with [CLIENT]'s performance requirements. This approach provides full visibility into safety, compliance, maintenance, and cost ensuring [CLIENT] always remains informed and in control.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the structure — a "highest priorities from day one" umbrella bullet whose sub-bullets each open by naming an observed condition (not a capability), then commit to a specific corrective action with a named method, tool, or timeframe; followed by a transparency bullet that promises visibility rather than results. Also universal: the discipline of naming the incumbent's failures as system conditions ("well-documented challenges under the current operator") rather than as accusations.

Pursuit-specific: the facility names, the observed deficiencies, the named safety technology (Blackline Safety H₂S devices), the ARM asset-replacement approach if not offered, and the 90-day condition-assessment commitment.
