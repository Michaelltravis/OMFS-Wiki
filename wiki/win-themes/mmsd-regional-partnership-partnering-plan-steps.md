# Our Regional Partnering Plan

Our Regional Partnership Plan follows the steps described in **Exhibit V-1**.

**Charter the team** – including [CLIENT], Jacobs, and representative stakeholders to align with goals of [CLIENT]'s Public Affairs team.

**Develop and cultivate regional trust:**

- Coordinate with [CLIENT] and conduct research as needed to understand community concerns, vulnerabilities, and expectations
- Assign task leaders

**Develop implementation plans and schedule for specific goals and activities:**

- Branding and social media campaign
- Marketing materials
- Promotion of special events

**Measure and report progress using transparent reporting tools and dashboards:**

- Validate data
- Explore proven Jacobs tools, such as Simetrica, to monetize social value initiatives

**Evaluate effectiveness and recalibrate.**

We know that this plan works. It's rooted in our team's history of building relationships and leading effective outreach activities in the region, in particular, those with a focus on water quality improvements. We've delivered critical work to make a positive environmental, societal, and economic difference in the region — from the city's first Riverwalk design guidelines to the Waukesha Water Transition Project, the ongoing remediation and restoration of the Milwaukee River, and Estuary Area of Concern, and improvements at [CLIENT]'s larger water reclamation facility. These projects (captured in **Exhibit V-2**) reflect our commitment to building a better future for the City and the surrounding region.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the five-step loop (charter → cultivate trust → implement on a schedule → measure and report → recalibrate), the assignment of task leaders, and the commitment to publish progress on a dashboard. Evaluators discount community-benefits sections because they are unfalsifiable; the measurement step is what removes that objection.

Pursuit-specific: the four named regional projects, the client's public affairs team name, and the exhibit numbers. Where the pursuit region offers no comparable local project record, drop the "we know this plan works" sentence rather than weakening it with distant examples — the local-record block and the other-communities examples carry that load instead.
