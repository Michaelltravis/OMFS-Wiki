# AquaDNA Smart System Optimization and Pump-Station Deragging

## AquaDNA: Smart Technology for System Optimization

While the collections system beyond the pump stations is not part of the scope of services for this contract, we recognize that overflows present significant management challenges that can lead to compliance issues and operational inefficiencies, especially during wet weather events. To address these challenges, we’re including Jacobs’ Palantir-driven AquaDNA tools as part of our comprehensive solution for [CLIENT]'s facilities.

AquaDNA uses smart technology and sensors for wastewater collection systems to understand performance in real time, alert operators to potential incidents, and help avoid spills, floods, overflows, and backups that could harm customers and the environment. While wet weather is primarily managed through the collection system—with wet weather management tanks only becoming available at [FACILITY A] after construction completion—AquaDNA offers an immediate solution by visualizing pump station operations, monitoring critical sewer levels, and providing predictive alarms.

Our offering integrates near real-time data from multiple [CLIENT] systems onto the AquaDNA platform, powered by Palantir’s Foundry. These integrated data sources include pump station SCADA information, available rain gauge data, level sensors, flow meters, and public weather forecasts. After integration, both the onsite JC Solutions team and [CLIENT] personnel will receive customized software access to this powerful monitoring system.

## Enhanced Pump Station Management with AquaDNA “Deragger”

As part of our solution, we propose installing automatic deragging devices at four of the most vulnerable wastewater pump stations, one in each quadrant, to proactively detect and address potential ragging issues and help mitigate sewer overflows in advance. Our AquaDNA “Deragger” is an exclusive partnership with a sewage pump deragging vendor that implements pump reversals upon detection of flow or power changes through dynamic waveform monitoring—a unique approach in the industry.

This technology has been successfully implemented at several sites, including our newest project in Wilmington, DE. With nearly 100 installations in the U.S. and a decade of operating experience, this is a proven approach that delivers dramatic results. For example, at our Rio Rancho, NM project, eight sewage pumps fitted with this control system significantly reduced previously chronic sanitary sewer overflow (SSO) events.

## Advanced Analytics and Optimization

## AquaDNA Smart System Optimization

Our AquaDNA solution delivers comprehensive protection against costly overflows and system failures through real-time monitoring and predictive analytics powered by Palantir’s Foundry platform. JC Solutions’ investment includes annual licensing costs, equipment/installation for automatic deragging devices at four vulnerable pump stations, flow and level measurement instrumentation, and ongoing calibration and optimization by JC Solutions experts. The system’s dynamic waveform monitoring technology proactively implements pump reversals upon detection of flow or power changes—a unique approach proven across nearly 100 U.S. installations that dramatically reduces emergency callouts, decreases power consumption, extends equipment life, and prevents sanitary sewer overflows. By integrating SCADA information, rain gauge data, level sensors, and weather forecasts, our machine learning algorithms continuously optimize pump station setpoints, transforming raw data into actionable intelligence that enhances operational efficiency and maximizes system performance.
