# Leveraging an Ongoing Riverfront Estuary Renewal to Enhance Regional Benefits

Jacobs proposes to advance a Fishing Pier and River Access concept tied to the ongoing EPA sediment cleanup on the Milwaukee River, in partnership with [CLIENT]. Building on our role in the $450M Milwaukee Estuary restoration, we would help align [CLIENT], the City, County Parks, the Urban Ecology Center, and private partners to assess and deliver this amenity. Our support would include feasibility study, design, permitting, construction oversight, funding support, and stakeholder coordination. By pairing river access with sediment cleanup and habitat restoration, [CLIENT] can expand public use of the river while demonstrating leadership in environmental stewardship and community partnership.

The concept exhibit organizes the vision into typologies and typical sections:

- **Connection:** kayak trail system, fishing, public access
- **Culture:** entertainment, education, community space
- **Gathering:** outdoor programming
- **Memory:** fishing, ice harvest; **History:** river history; **Healing**
- **Ecology:** wildlife, natural assets, fish habitat, erosion control
- **Water Quality:** mosquito control, invasive control, biological process, pollutant removal

Graphic reference: `317_007CAM_3` (Exhibit V-3, fishing pier and river access vision board with typologies and typical sections; client-specific: true).

## Reuse guidance

Universal: pick one above-and-beyond idea and render it as a designed thing — a graphic, a scope of services, a named partner list — rather than a bullet. The typology framing (connection, culture, gathering, memory, history, healing, ecology, water quality) is a reusable way to show an amenity delivering social and environmental value at once.

Pursuit-specific: the river, the cleanup program and its value, the partner organizations, and the amenity type. Coordinate with the capture team before committing Jacobs to unpriced design and permitting support, and confirm the offer's cost treatment matches the spec sheet.
