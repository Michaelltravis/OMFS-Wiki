# Intelligent O&M Toolset Included in the Base Fee at No Additional Cost

Based on our success with this approach, **we have integrated this toolset into our base fee** with the goal of improving maintenance metrics by approximately 10—reducing maintenance overtime and greatly reducing the level of effort on our Maintenance Manager in planning the team's week. For consulting clients, this is typically worth over $100,000 per year for setup, annual licensing, and support fees, but for this program we know it will more than pay for itself, so it is included at no additional cost to [CLIENT].

Like the planner/scheduler module discussed above, this module is worth over $100,000 per year or more when deployed for our consulting clients, but given the tremendous advantages to both our team and yours, we have included this as part of our base bid at no additional cost to [CLIENT].
