# Replica Digital Twin for Process Optimization and Operator Training

## Replica Digital Twin: Modeling for Process Optimization and Employee Training

Jacobs' Replica modeling tool creates dynamic digital twins of treatment processes, enabling risk-free testing of operational strategies before implementation. This powerful simulation capability could support process optimization, troubleshooting, and capacity planning without disrupting operations. Our experts have already begun and would continue developing calibrated models for each facility to provide valuable insights into biological nutrient removal optimization, chemical dosing strategies, and energy reduction opportunities—particularly valuable for evaluating seasonal adjustments, peak flow management, and process modifications to meet evolving regulatory requirements.

The proposed Replica Digital Twin solution for [FACILITY B], a wastewater reclamation facility, and [FACILITY A], a wastewater reclamation facility, provides three key stages. First, staff would gain a realistic understanding of how [FACILITY A] improvements function. Second, models would optimize control strategies and process operations, potentially further reducing power and chemical costs.

We’ll use our Replica Digital Twin technology to create dynamic virtual replicas of [CLIENT]'s facilities that enable risk-free testing of operational strategies before implementation. This powerful tool delivers three game-changing benefits: providing staff with realistic understanding of facility improvements, optimizing control strategies to reduce power and chemical costs, and offering hands-on simulation training for operators to master both routine procedures and emergency scenarios.

By calibrating against historical data and incorporating controller logic, Replica will help the JC Solutions team identify inefficiencies, improve process stability, and enhance operational readiness—ultimately delivering safer, more efficient, and cost-effective plant management.

## Replica Digital Twin: Proven Ability to Avoid Unintended Consequences in Interconnected Systems Like [CLIENT]'s

Finally, a realistic simulator would offer operators hands-on experience in facility management, enhancing operational readiness.

Replica would simulate flow dynamics and pressure conditions, calibrated against historical data to ensure realistic representation. This analysis would reveal inefficiencies, confirm design integrity, and identify proactive measures to reduce system failures. By incorporating controller logic along the main flow path and analyzing control loops, Replica would identify opportunities to refine operational parameters—improving process stability, reducing energy consumption, and minimizing malfunctions. At [FACILITY B], the model could also validate new membrane designs.

Connected to a realistic HMI interface, Replica would create an effective training environment where operators could practice routine procedures and experience equipment failure scenarios. This tool-based training would enhance operator proficiency and readiness, contributing to safer and more cost-effective plant management.

Jacobs’ Replica™ Digital Twin can manage system complexity and optimize operations. The Replica™ model serves as a “single source of truth,” with the ability to create scenarios around all components of the system for better decision-making. We’ve developed Replica™ Digital Twin models for a range of clients similar to [CLIENT]. For the Tillman AWPF, Replica™ optimized the flow balance between the original WWTP and the new AWPF, maximizing the capture of available flows to the AWPF. The team also used Replica™ to model system hydraulics and controls to optimize membrane sizing. Once construction and startup are complete, it will be used to troubleshoot equipment issues, optimize operations, and train plant operators.
