# Public Information Displays, Tours, and Speakers Bureau

A designated Public Information Display will be installed at the facility entrance or administrative building. It will include:

- Educational content from WEF and Georgia WPCA.
- Plant process schematics.
- Current project brochures and fact sheets.
- Contact information for community questions.

Inviting people into the facility can broaden engagement efforts. Open houses and tours can be planned around specific events/celebrations such as Clean Water Week, Public Works Week, Earth Day, or other related events. Getting creative and linking them to something less obvious such as a “spooky tour” on Halloween or a spring tour that celebrates new vegetation at the facility is a fun way to engage people with the process.

Regardless of the timing or even if they are regular events, facility tours should be planned and organized to provide a highlight of the operations and connect them to people’s everyday lives. Print and digital maps highlighting key areas, interactive or hands on displays that explain “hidden” processes (e.g. membranes), and well-versed, personable guides go a long a way to creating a memorable experience. Our team would assess current practices for tours and then based on experience supporting other facilities and insights from industry peers would develop a specific tour plan for each facility, help select the best guides, and support training as needed.

A Speakers Bureau will be formed with technical and community outreach staff available to speak at career days, neighborhood association meetings, community events, and [CLIENT] Board meetings. Presentations will be customized to the audience and pre-approved by [CLIENT] when required.
