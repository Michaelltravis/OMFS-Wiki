# Process Optimization, Cybersecurity, and Asset-Management Benefits

JC Solutions will deploy Jacobs' process engineers to collaborate with onsite operators to optimize plant operations while maintaining compliance and minimizing energy use, chemical consumption, biosolids disposal, and membrane replacement costs. We will implement systematic testing and optimization of fermentation zones, chemical dosing, swing zones, and membrane-performance tracking across all three facilities. We will deploy Jacobs' SCADA/OT expertise to develop custom membrane monitoring reports for the new Kubota system at [FACILITY A], the first treatment facility, similar to the InSight reports currently provided for the other membrane types at [FACILITY B], the second treatment facility, and [FACILITY C], the third treatment facility.

This work reduces chemical consumption through optimized fermentation processes and enhanced biological phosphorus removal. It supports longer membrane life and lower maintenance costs through improved mixed-liquor filterability, strategic surface wasting, and data-driven performance monitoring; decreased energy costs through ammonia-based aeration control, strategic equalization, and real-time power management; and higher-quality biosolids processing with improved cake dryness that reduces hauling and disposal costs.

JC Solutions will implement a comprehensive NIST-aligned cybersecurity program including a Risk and Resilience Assessment to identify vulnerabilities, develop site-specific security plans, and conduct penetration testing. We will deploy a 3-2-1-1 backup strategy with immutable cloud storage while securely integrating critical operational systems—SCADA, LIMS, CMMS, and GIS—for data management. This provides enhanced protection against sophisticated cyber threats through proactive defense strategies and real-time monitoring, preventing lateral movement between critical systems. It also minimizes downtime and data loss during emergencies with disaster-recovery capabilities and validated system backups, ensuring operational continuity through natural or man-made disasters.

JC Solutions will implement an ISO 55001-aligned Asset Management System tailored to the system's needs that enhances reliability, optimizes asset lifecycles, and establishes data-driven decision-making processes for continuous operational improvement. The approach delivers the value of ISO 55001 standards without certification costs while reducing operational risks and extending asset lifespans through Jacobs' proven approach implemented for hundreds of water-utility clients.

JC Solutions will host an annual workshop with handpicked subject-matter experts to focus on future priorities important to [CLIENT]. The workshop drives innovation and avoids complacency by focusing on continuous-improvement initiatives.
