# 4. Thames Tideway Program, London, UK

*Delivered lasting socio-economic benefits—leaving a legacy.*

An aspiration of the UK's largest water infrastructure undertaking was to develop a labor force and leave a positive legacy for local communities and the construction industry. Jacobs helped the client develop the skills, education, and employment approach to fulfill that goal. We helped establish an apprenticeship program, develop incentivized schemes in partnership with contractors and an industry-leading education program. Procurement strategies supported local businesses and community engagement programs helped build trust. Efforts that helped achieve these socioeconomic benefits include:

- Employed 1,000 previously unemployed people, with a predicted additional value to society of $4.17M by the end of the project.
- 150+ participants included a client-based apprenticeship scheme with 1 in 50 apprenticeship targets, realizing over 150 to date, delivering $542K additional social value.
- Achieved 25% local employment to date, delivering $2M in social value.
- Employed 37 people with convictions, delivering $2M in additional social value.
- Engaged over 80,000 young people in STEM through a digital education program, delivering $6.95M in additional social value.

> ...the delivery has been excellent and Jacobs' contribution to that has been exemplary. From the early engagement with local schools and colleges through to the development of our strategies in this area and then on into implementing those strategies with our Main Works Contractors, the performance and commitment have been of the highest order. The Jacobs team's passion for making a difference and ability to handle difficult situations with sensitivity have been key elements of embedding a successful skills and employment program at the heart of Tideway's Legacy... Above all, Jacobs has helped Tideway provide many young people with significant career-enhancing opportunities.
>
> — Roger Bailey, Tideway Chief Technical Officer

## Reuse guidance

Universal: attaching a monetized social-value figure to every social commitment. A community-benefits section that says "we hired locally" competes with every other bidder saying the same thing; one that says "25% local employment, delivering $2M in social value" does not. The client-executive quote placed at the end of the examples closes the section on someone else's voice, which is the right place to spend a testimonial.

Pursuit-specific: the program itself is a UK capital program, not a US O&M contract — say plainly that it is offered as evidence of method, not of comparable scope, or a US municipal evaluator will discount it. Keep the quote only where its permission status is on file.
