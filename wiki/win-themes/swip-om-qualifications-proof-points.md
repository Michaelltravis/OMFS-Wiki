# O&M Qualifications Proof Points — Scale, California Track Record, and Governance Model

## Jacobs' O&M Qualifications for Advanced Water Treatment

Jacobs will bring four decades of operations and maintenance (O&M) delivery and one of the industry's largest integrated operations organizations to [CLIENT], a Southern California coastal municipality. We will apply proven experience across 300+ O&M projects, supported by more than 4,000 O&M professionals nationwide and a strong California track record operating advanced treatment systems (MBR, RO, UV-AOP) comparable to this program. Our references—including our recent selection to operate West Basin's Edward C. Little complex, Pure Water Soquel, an advanced water purification (indirect potable reuse) project using MF–RO–UV-AOP, and Twin Oaks Valley WTP, a 100-MGD submerged-membrane drinking water facility (DBO/long-term O&M), and others—demonstrate our advanced water O&M leadership in California including our performance under stringent regulatory regimes, 24/7 reliability expectations, and multi-train advanced processes. We will integrate this project directly into our OMFS governance model that sustains a 98% client retention rate and a 99.98% environmental compliance record.

Our O&M team combines operations know-how with in-house engineering, SCADA/OT and IT/cybersecurity expertise, asset management, and design-build resources so the [CLIENT] gains both day-to-day operational excellence and on-demand problem-solving depth. This model—refined on complex California advanced water treatment and reuse programs—will give [CLIENT] rapid access to specialized capabilities when needed without losing focus on core O&M.

**Benefits to [CLIENT] callout:** *"Best-in-class AWT O&M pedigree; fast, integrated problem solving via access to our builders, engineers and consultants; and governance that anchors reliability, compliance, and value."*

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the four-move structure — scale claim, narrow to named comparable reference projects, land two hard retention/compliance percentages, then pitch the integrated in-house bench — and the state-footprint map with years of service at each marker, which makes regional density and longevity legible at a glance instead of as a list of client logos. The "we will integrate this project into our [governance model] that sustains X% retention and Y% compliance" sentence is the reusable hinge: it converts a corporate statistic into a promise about this contract.

Pursuit-specific: refresh every firm-wide figure before use; replace West Basin/Edward C. Little, Pure Water Soquel, and Twin Oaks Valley WTP with references matched to the target facility's process train and regulator; and rebuild the map for the state or region where the firm's density actually supports the claim.
