# O&M Qualifications Proof Points — Scale, California Track Record, and Governance Model

**Section header:** "Jacobs' O&M Qualifications for Advanced Water Treatment"

**Paragraph — scale claim, then narrow to comparable references.** "Jacobs will bring four decades of operations and maintenance (O&M) delivery and one of the industry's largest integrated operations organizations to [CLIENT]. We will apply proven experience across 300+ O&M projects, supported by more than 4,000 O&M professionals nationwide and a strong California track record operating advanced treatment systems (MBR, RO, UV-AOP) comparable to [PROJECT]. Our references—from our recent selection to West Basin's Edward C. Little complex to Pure Water Soquel—an advanced water purification (indirect potable reuse) project using MF-RO-UV-AOP and Twin Oaks Valley WTP—a 100-MGD submerged-membrane drinking water facility (DBO/long-term O&M), and others—demonstrate our advanced water O&M leadership in California including our performance under stringent regulatory regimes, 24/7 reliability expectations, and multi-train advanced processes. We will integrate the [PROJECT] into our OMFS governance model that sustains a 98% client retention rate and a 99.98% environmental compliance record."

**Second paragraph — pitch the integrated in-house bench.** "Our O&M team combines operations know-how with in-house engineering, SCADA/OT and IT/cybersecurity expertise, asset management, and design-build resources so the client gains both day-to-day operational excellence and on-demand problem-solving depth. This model, refined on complex California advanced water treatment and reuse programs, will give [CLIENT] rapid access to specialized capabilities when needed without losing focus on core O&M."

**Benefits to [Client] callout:** *"Best-in-class AWT O&M pedigree; fast, integrated problem solving via access to our builders, engineers and consultants; and governance that anchors reliability, compliance, and value."*

**Companion graphic — California O&M footprint map (asset `147_008A26`).** A state-of-California map plotting Jacobs offices, municipal O&M projects, and private-sector O&M projects as three distinctly colored/sized markers, with the target site marked and each marker labeled with years of service at that location (source example markers: Crescent City 6 years, Red Bluff 5 years, Lincoln 1 year, Davis-Woodland 11 years, Auburn 33 years, Turlock 20 years, Soquel Creek 4 years, Clovis 16 years, Gilroy 41 years, Fort Irwin 20 years, West Basin, Twin Oaks 19 years). A callout box beside the map states: "Jacobs is a Top Provider of O&M Services in North America including Similar California Projects." This device makes regional density and longevity legible at a glance rather than as a bare list of client logos.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the "scale claim → narrow to comparable, named reference projects → two hard retention/compliance percentages → integrated in-house bench pitch" structure, and the state-footprint map-with-years-of-service device (adaptable to any state/region where the firm has a dense O&M presence). Pursuit-specific: verify the 300+ projects, 4,000+ professionals, 98% retention, and 99.98% compliance figures are current before reuse (these are firm-wide stats that should be refreshed periodically, not per-pursuit). Reference projects (West Basin/Edward C. Little, Pure Water Soquel, Twin Oaks Valley WTP) must be replaced with facilities genuinely comparable in process type (MBR/RO/UV-AOP, membrane filtration, DBO) and regulatory rigor to whatever is being pursued — do not claim California-specific experience for an out-of-state pursuit. Pair with `swip-transmittal-letter-value-bullets.md` (bullet two, "Proven Performance," is the condensed version of this same claim) and with `wiki/qualifications/` blocks for the full corporate-scale narrative this proof point draws from.
