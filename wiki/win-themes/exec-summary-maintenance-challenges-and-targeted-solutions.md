# Executive Summary — Maintenance Challenges and Targeted Solutions Table

|Key current and future problems and concern|Our solution & benefit|
|---|---|
|Compliance — A past compliance issue involving discharge exceedances highlights the need for a more proactive and accountable approach as a repeat would be unacceptable—and a potential public relations setback for [CLIENT].|Our team will maintain the highest standards through rigorous QA/QC protocols, certified lab procedures, and multi-layered verification.|
|[FACILITY A], a wastewater reclamation facility — Membrane fouling, ineffective grit/screening, chemical feed leaks, UV and screw press issues.|Implement best-in-class preventive and predictive maintenance (PdM), inspect feed systems, optimize air scour and cleaning cycles, and repair/restore screw press operation to ensure high membrane performance and equipment longevity.|
|[FACILITY B], a wastewater reclamation facility — Aging odor control systems, pneumatic valve failures, air system leaks, sensor reliability, centrifuge downtime.|Prioritize air dryer, blower, and membrane system maintenance. Replace odor media, fix air leaks, and use PdM tools to monitor centrifuge performance—ensuring system uptime and community satisfaction.|
|[FACILITY C], a wastewater reclamation facility — Infrastructure aging, poor grit/screenings removal, limited corrosion prevention, mixer and UV system upkeep.|Perform regular structural inspections, apply protective coatings, use oil analysis on mixers, and manage UV system inventory for cost-effective reliability and regulatory compliance.|
|Pump Stations — Outdated “can” designs, limited telemetry, generator readiness, odor system upkeep.|Transition to modern submersible designs, upgrade communication systems, test emergency power with load banks, and implement odor media replacement schedule—improving reliability and reducing emergency maintenance.|
|Predictive Maintenance (PdM) — Limited early fault detection and response capacity.|Deploy PdM technologies (vibration, thermography, ultrasonic, MCSA) to reduce downtime and extend asset life. Focus on critical rotating equipment and electrical systems.|
|Site Housekeeping & Appearance — Inconsistent cleanliness, disorganized storage, public-facing asset aesthetics.|Establish Facilities Appearance Standard (FAST), integrate tasks into CMMS, and enforce routine cleaning and landscaping to enhance operational pride and community perception.|
