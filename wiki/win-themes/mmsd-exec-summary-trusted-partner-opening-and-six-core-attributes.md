# Executive Summary Opening — Trusted Partner Framing and Six Core Attributes of the Approach

## Jacobs: A Trusted Partner for the Next Decade of Performance Excellence

[CLIENT] — a Midwest regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities and a biosolids production operation — designed this procurement to secure a long-term operations and maintenance (O&M) partner that will bring fresh perspectives, innovation, and greater accountability—one that will strengthen treatment performance, enhance maintenance discipline, and improve asset integrity and reliability. Additionally, meeting evolving regulatory requirements and accelerating progress toward your 2035 Vision and strategic goals are priorities. Jacobs will deliver exactly that—meeting every requirement and advancing outcomes through Above and Beyond improvements from Day 1.

*Caption: One integrated [CLIENT]–Jacobs team aligned to your 2035 Vision—built on transparency, disciplined delivery, and shared accountability.*

## Our Approach Is Built Around [CLIENT]'s Priorities

Our approach includes six core attributes, wrapped around Above and Beyond Value-Added Strategies, to achieve [CLIENT]'s vision for successful, systemwide O&M.

**Programmatic framework.** Establishes the foundation for transformational delivery: cost control, stakeholder alignment, collaborative decision-making, and accountability.

**Technical innovations with measurable value and savings.** Systemwide optimization aided by digital tools will protect compliance and community trust, while delivering transparent, measurable cost savings across [CLIENT]'s interconnected system.

**People-first leadership and performance culture.** Proven leadership and performance culture that translates into safe, consistent, reliable operations and strengthened by our strong, collaborative relationships.

**Seamless transition.** Drawing on our success in transitioning operations from the current operator, we'll ensure uninterrupted service, retain critical knowledge, support existing staff, and stand up the systems and processes that drive immediate performance improvement.

**Rigorous asset management.** ISO 55001-aligned asset management including capital improvement project support, plus reliability and NEXGEN expertise, will deliver visible PM/CM accountability, eliminate repeat failures, and improve uptime.

**Regional partnership for community benefits.** Above-and-beyond programs will align with [CLIENT]'s vision and deliver measurable outcomes in equity, sustainability, and economic growth while supporting [CLIENT]'s research initiatives.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the challenger opening that names, in the client's own words, why the contract is being competed, then answers it with "meeting every requirement and advancing outcomes … from Day 1." Also universal: the six-attribute wheel (programmatic framework, technical innovations, people-first leadership, seamless transition, rigorous asset management, regional partnership), which becomes the running order of the rest of the executive summary and can be re-labeled to match a different RFP's evaluation criteria.

Pursuit-specific: the 2035 Vision reference, the "One Water, One Team, One Vision" tagline, the NEXGEN CMMS name, and the assumption that an incumbent operator is being displaced.
