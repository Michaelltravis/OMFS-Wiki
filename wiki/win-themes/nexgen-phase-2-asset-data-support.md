# NexGen Phase 2 Asset-Data Implementation Support

## NexGen Phase 2 Implementation Support

We can help complete the wastewater treatment plant key asset data needed to support NexGen Phase 2 implementation, including installation dates, replacement costs, impact indices, and expected useful life. This work would help strengthen the quality and completeness of the asset data foundation needed for more effective enterprise asset management.

This support will keep [CLIENT] — a regional wastewater utility trust — on track for full NexGen Phase 2 implementation, support broader enterprise use of the platform, and provide continued access to our asset management professionals. A more complete and decision-ready asset dataset can also improve lifecycle planning, replacement forecasting, capital prioritization, and long-term asset stewardship.

## Reuse guidance

Use where an owner has an active EAM rollout whose asset records need completion. Confirm the platform, phase, fields, and implementation relationship before reuse.
