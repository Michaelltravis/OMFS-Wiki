# Asset Management, CMMS, Inventory, and 72-Hour Repair Recommendations

We will implement an ISO 55001-aligned asset management program with CMMS-driven preventive maintenance (PM)/predictive maintenance (PdM) using the [CLIENT]'s Mentor APM system, then track PM compliance and reliability KPIs, and deliver recommendations within 72 hours for large/emergency repairs to protect warranties and asset life. We will stand up a modern inventory program, including barcoding/QR codes, labeled bin locations, mobile access, reorder and obsolescence KPIs, and support annual budget planning for maintenance, replacements, and capital improvements.

**Benefits to [CLIENT] callout:** *"Longer asset life, fewer reactive repairs, clearer budget forecasts, and faster parts availability to shorten outages."*

## Reuse guidance

Universal: three moves carry to any O&M pursuit. First, anchoring the program to a recognized standard (ISO 55001) rather than to an in-house methodology, which lets a technically literate evaluator place the offer immediately. Second, the explicit commitment to work inside the client's own CMMS — this is a quiet but powerful trust signal on a recompete, because it says the client's data and history stay the client's. Third, and most valuable, the 72-hour repair-recommendation commitment: a hard, numeric service level attached to the moment a client is most anxious about an outsourced operator, with a stated reason (protecting warranties and asset life) that shows the clock exists for the client's benefit rather than as a procedural flourish. The inventory sentence is a compact model for describing a stockroom program — barcoding and QR labeling, bin locations, mobile access, reorder and obsolescence KPIs — and it closes on annual budget planning for maintenance, replacements, and capital improvements, which is what converts an asset-management paragraph into a financial-planning benefit for a public owner.

Pursuit-specific: confirm the 72-hour turnaround is achievable and priced for the target scope, since it will be read as a contractual commitment; name the client's real CMMS or asset performance management platform; and adjust the standard reference if the client works to a different asset-management framework.
