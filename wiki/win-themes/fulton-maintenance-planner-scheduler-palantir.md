# Maintenance Planner/Scheduler Powered by Palantir

## Maintenance Planner/Scheduler Powered by Palantir

Managing limited maintenance resources, both labor and materials, is a major focus for the team, and getting it right makes a big difference with preserving assets and meeting budget. Most existing computerized maintenance management systems (CMMS) can only store work orders and asset information, but they do not have any way to optimize the priorities of work orders for the maintenance team. This becomes a laborintensive process by the maintenance planner/scheduler or maintenance manager.

Instead, Jacobs has developed a very unique support tool that is an overlay on top of any CMMS that makes optimal use of limited maintenance resources (labor and parts) to maximize KPIs and also drive down costs. The most direct way to optimize maintenance resources is through proper planning and scheduling, but the procedures are extensive and complex and require a planner/scheduler or maintenance manager to hold several, often competing, metrics or variables in mind simultaneously. This is unrealistic for most people unless they have many years of experience and a very strong understanding of asset management theory.

We will implement our proprietary Intelligent O&M suite with Palantir integration that overlays your existing CMMS to optimize maintenance resources and work order priorities. It provides advanced analytics tools that determine optimal preventive maintenance schedules based on work order history and risk assessment. It accesses hundreds of vendor manuals and insights from thousands of work orders to perform PM optimization in a fraction of the time—maximizing operational KPIs and reducing maintenance costs through optimized resource allocation and scheduling guided by ISO55000 principles and Jacobs expertise.

$600,000 worth of investment by JC Solutions.

Using Jacobs' maintenance planning best practices written as an algorithm within the Intelligent O&M suite of tools, the maintenance manager can set the schedule, revise the suggested labor pool, and revise specific work orders, and then issue the plan to staff. There are numerous filters and analysis tools that can be used to optimize metrics along with way, which were co-developed with Palantir based on ISO55000 principles and Jacobs' expertise. The tool also provides us with sophisticated insights into where more preventive maintenance is needed based on work order history and risk, or where less is appropriate—an analysis that used to require a lot of time and the insights of a very select group of industry experts.
