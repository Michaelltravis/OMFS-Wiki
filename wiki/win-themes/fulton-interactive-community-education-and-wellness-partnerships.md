# Interactive Community Education and Wellness Partnerships

- **Interactive GIS and Augmented Reality Experiences:** Develop GIS-based maps with AR overlays to visualize watersheds, facility locations, and water flow. Visitors can engage with digital content via mobile devices for an enhanced learning experience.
- **Signature Butterfly Exhibit Expansion:** In partnership with CNC and [CLIENT], we propose expanding the beloved butterfly exhibit into a permanent, hands-on feature. This captivating space will serve as an entry point to environmental education—connecting pollinators, water quality, and ecosystem health in a way that resonates with all ages.

**Local Craft Breweries.** The popularity of craft breweries offers a different way to tap into a strategic partnership. An active regional craft brewery community already has existing supporters and connections to the Chattahoochee and local environment. It also presents a fun, relaxed way to talk about water quality and the importance of clean water for beer. The partnership could be as simple as a pop display on the water reclamation process at existing events such as the Topside Tap Trail (a curated experience at breweries across Roswell, Alpharetta, and Sandy Springs), Paddle & Pints, or Farm-to-Table Dinners. Or it could be elevated into a focused media and social media campaign on the beer/clean water connection while leveraging participation in the events. These events offer opportunities to highlight the role of clean water in disease prevention, treatment, and community wellness.

**[CLIENT] Public Works’ Fall Garden Festival.** Host interactive booths focused on water’s essential role in agriculture and gardening. Activities may include sponsoring water-saving tools (e.g., rain barrels) for community gardens and offering guidance on sustainable irrigation and reducing water consumption in horticulture.

**Local Food Security Organizations.** Collaborate with groups such as Angie’s Kitchen, North Fulton Community Charities, and food centers supported by the Atlanta Community Food Bank to support access to clean water for food preparation and distribution and partner on community garden initiatives, linking clean water access to food security and sustainable agriculture.

**Health and Wellness Awareness Initiatives.**

**Breast Cancer Awareness Month.** Partner with advocacy organizations to sponsor local events, distribute water at awareness walks, and emphasize the critical role of hydration and clean water in overall health and recovery.

**Sickle Cell Foundation of Georgia.** Support community health by sponsoring screenings, educational events, and fundraisers.
