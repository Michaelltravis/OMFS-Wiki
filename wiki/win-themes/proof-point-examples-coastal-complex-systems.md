# Proof-Point Examples — Track Record in Coastal and Complex Wastewater Systems

## Proven operations in coastal and complex systems

We have a **demonstrated track record of improving wastewater system performance** through disciplined operations and proactive problem solving. We have delivered similar results for comparable communities, including:

- **Town of Westerly, RI:** Resiliency planning and biosolids dewatering upgrade
- **South Huron Valley Utility Authority (SHVUA), MI:** Stacked tray grit removal system and biosolids dewatering upgrades
- **Traverse City, MI:** Grant funding support and low-pressure collection system improvements

At the Town of Southbridge's WWTP and collection system, our team improved reporting transparency, reduced long-standing odor issues related to biosolids composting, and optimized chemical and power usage, **enhancing system performance, community conditions, and operational efficiency**.

[CLIENT]'s wastewater system — a coastal New England municipal system — operates under conditions that require disciplined execution. Seasonal population changes, tidal infiltration, and wet-weather variability introduce operational complexity that must be actively managed. We address these challenges through **proactive monitoring, coordinated operations, and disciplined execution**. Our team strengthens reliability through standard operating procedures (SOPs), preventive and predictive maintenance, and improved monitoring of pump stations and collection system assets.

For the Town of Westerly's WWTP, Jacobs improved odor control by upgrading an aging biofilter system from organic media to engineered media with a significantly longer service life. This reduced replacement frequency, **improved system reliability, and delivered over $134k in cost savings**. Our disciplined approach improves compliance stability, reduces variability, and supports **predictable long-term operating costs**.
