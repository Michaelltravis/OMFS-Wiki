# Safety, Emergency Preparedness, and Risk Management

Supported by our regional team of safety professionals, the onsite team will implement a robust, documented Safety Plan/Program and Emergency Response Plan/Program, conduct regular drills, and leverage Jacobs' FEMA disaster-response experience and regional network to secure materials and staffing during extreme events. Our environmental risk approach will track regulatory, safety, asset condition, and financial indicators and drive targeted improvements.

**Benefits to [CLIENT] callout:** *"Safer operations, faster recovery from events, and measurable risk reduction across facilities."*

## Reuse guidance

Universal: this is the pattern for covering safety in an executive summary that has no room for a safety section — one sentence that stacks four commitments (documented safety program, documented emergency response program, regular drills, disaster-response reach-back) and one sentence that defines risk management as an indicator-tracking practice. Two elements deserve to be carried forward almost verbatim. The first is the framing "supported by our regional team of safety professionals, the onsite team will implement…," which makes clear that safety is not delegated to a six-person crew's spare attention. The second is translating a disaster-response credential into what it buys the client — securing materials and staffing during extreme events — rather than leaving it as a badge; that sentence answers the question every utility owner has after a storm or a wildfire season. Naming the four indicator families (regulatory, safety, asset condition, financial) is what elevates "risk management" from a heading to a method, and it also quietly ties the safety narrative back to the asset-management and compliance programs elsewhere in the section.

Pursuit-specific: confirm the disaster-response credential and swap the hazard framing for the target region's real exposures, then make the drill cadence and seasonal-preparedness commitments match what the operations plan actually funds. Where the RFP has a dedicated safety evaluation criterion, this paragraph is the summary and the full program belongs in `wiki/compliance-plans/`.
