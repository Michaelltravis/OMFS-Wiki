# Community Internship and Workforce Development Partnerships

Newsletters and channels from the municipalities served by the facilities as well as environmental newsletters, such as Green Matters (Atlanta Regional Commission e-newsletter) also present opportunities to share positive content. Any content created for the media could also be leveraged on [CLIENT]'s social media channels or even developed into mini-campaigns that would link back to published/posted stories or link to [CLIENT]'s website. All media announcements will be pre-qualified with [CLIENT] prior to release. In the event of a major incident or concern, we will coordinate closely with [CLIENT]'s Communications Department to ensure consistent messaging and timely public updates.

Internship programs offer a powerful opportunity to cultivate local talent while supporting workforce readiness and long-term career growth. We are deeply committed to building local capacity through strategic partnerships and hands-on training. Our proposed Internship Program will provide meaningful employment and professional development for recent high school graduates and college students, with preference given to [CLIENT] residents. CERM brings a strong track record in workforce development and established partnerships with regional institutions such as ATC, Atlanta Metropolitan State College, GPTC, and others. Interns will benefit from structured rotations, direct mentorship, and exposure to plant operations, asset management, and community engagement—all designed to build real-world skills and inspire interest in environmental careers. All intern candidates will be jointly reviewed by JC Solutions and [CLIENT], with final selection approved by [CLIENT]'s Contract Administrator, ensuring alignment with program goals.

We have also initiated discussions with ATC to form a strategic partnership with Jacobs and CERM focused on expanding access to high-demand training in water and wastewater operations. ATC currently offers three Level III courses—Intro to Wastewater, Water Distribution and Backflow, and Water Distribution Concepts—and is developing two additional courses to meet increasing industry demand. While current classes are limited to City of Atlanta staff, ATC is actively working to broaden enrollment and align its curriculum with Georgia Operator certification pathways. Through this collaboration, we can shape future-ready curriculum, strengthen [CLIENT]'s water sector talent pipeline, and support both in-person and virtual instruction to reach a wider pool of aspiring professionals.

Strategic Partnerships and Collaborations. To ensure continuity and long-term impact in community relations, education, and public communication, we have identified additional audiences, engagement activities, and partnership opportunities. These initiatives will strengthen community ties, promote water stewardship, and support [CLIENT]'s commitment to sustainability and public service.
