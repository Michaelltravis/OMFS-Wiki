# Cover Letter Close — Structured Transition, Workforce Continuity, and Long-Term Partnership

A successful contract begins with a smooth transition and stable, supported workforce. Jacobs will implement a structured transition plan focused on knowledge transfer, workforce continuity, safety alignment, and seamless, uninterrupted service, while advancing staff training, succession planning, and alignment with [CLIENT]'s culture, goals, and community values. This approach minimizes operational risk, maintains continuity of service, and positions the team for long-term performance and resiliency. From Day 1, we will maintain regular communication with [CLIENT] leadership and support continuous improvement through initiatives such as our Annual Innovation Workshop.

Jacobs is committed to being a trusted, long-term partner to [CLIENT], a coastal New England municipality. We look forward to working collaboratively with [CLIENT] to deliver safe, reliable, and forward-looking wastewater services for the community.

Jacobs acknowledges receipt and review of all addenda issued for this solicitation.

The letter is signed by the Project Manager and the Regional Operations Manager, each with a direct phone number and email address, and carries a boxed executive attestation from the Executive Vice President confirming authority to bind the firm.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the sequence of naming the transition risk before the client has to, answering it with four named commitments (knowledge transfer, workforce continuity, safety alignment, uninterrupted service) plus a workforce-development follow-on (training, succession planning, cultural alignment), then converting all of it into the three outcomes an evaluator scores — minimized operational risk, continuity of service, long-term performance and resiliency. The Day-1 communication promise and a named recurring forum turn the commitment from a sentiment into a schedule. Closing on partnership rather than a request for the award is the right register for a transmittal letter.

Pursuit-specific: every commitment here has to be defensible in the transition and staffing sections — do not carry a promise into the letter that the transition plan does not detail. Replace the Annual Innovation Workshop with whatever continuous-improvement forum is genuinely being offered, and match the "culture, goals, and community values" phrasing to language the client uses about itself. The addenda acknowledgment belongs in the letter only where the RFP or the forms package asks for it there; confirm signatory authority and current direct contact details before issue.
