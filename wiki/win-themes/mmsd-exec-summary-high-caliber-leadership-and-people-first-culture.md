# High-Caliber Leadership Will Create a People-First, High-Performance Team

We've assigned **Kevin Dahl as Project Manager and Rick Warner, PE as Deputy Project Manager (DPM), two of our highest-caliber water management leaders**, who will align with [CLIENT]'s management and remain available 24/7. [CLIENT] is a Midwest regional sewerage district operating two large water reclamation facilities and a biosolids production operation. **Kevin will lead day-to-day operations with a steady, proven, hands-on presence. Rick's Above and Beyond role is a key differentiator: in addition to other responsibilities, we're intentionally elevating regional partnership and public outreach to the DPM level so these priorities receive dedicated leadership attention**—while also strengthening the cross-functional disciplines that directly support your operations and maintenance teams. In this way, Rick will back up Kevin with selected management needs, so Kevin can stay focused on daily O&M performance and culture building.

Kevin and Rick will build a **people-first, high-performance culture** grounded in employee development and respect—because strong culture drives safety, retention, and results. Our team has engaged [CLIENT] staff, stakeholders, and union leadership and understands the workforce and labor-relations realities that must be managed proactively. **We'll be ready to perform from Day 1.** Our robust training approach is tailored to staff roles and career pathways and includes a workforce development program to build a sustainable labor pool. Rick will also oversee workforce development and community outreach efforts with a dedicated team of specialists.

Our key leadership team covers the critical areas of expertise for elevated performance on [CLIENT]'s complex system.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the structure — name the two leaders, state their availability, explain the deliberate division of labor, then connect leadership to culture, to safety and retention, and to Day 1 readiness. The sentence about having already engaged staff, stakeholders, and union leadership is a strong proof of preparation and transfers to any pursuit where the team has actually done that work; delete it if they have not.

Pursuit-specific: every name, the roster table, and the decision to elevate regional partnership to the DPM. Where a pursuit has no union workforce or no community-benefits requirement, the DPM rationale needs rebuilding around the priority that pursuit's client actually has.
