# Leadership Team Introduction and "Extension of Client Staff" Coordinated-Operations Narrative

## Leadership-Driven Operations and Accountability

Jacobs' approach begins with **strong, accountable leadership** supported by the experienced on-site team described in the staffing/project management plan section. The [PROJECT MANAGER], [REGIONAL OPERATIONS MANAGER], and [ASSISTANT PROJECT MANAGER] (shown together in a leadership-team exhibit) bring the experience, technical depth, and leadership needed to deliver **reliable and compliant operations** to the client.

Successful wastewater operations depend on leadership that not only maintains performance, but also **proactively manages operational, regulatory, and safety risks**. Jacobs provides that leadership through disciplined operational practices, clear accountability, and direct access to technical resources that strengthen decision-making and accelerate problem-solving.

The [PROJECT MANAGER]'s leadership emphasizes **accountability, workforce development, and continuous improvement**, fostering a culture where operators take ownership of their facilities — reflected in strong housekeeping practices, organized workspaces, and pride in daily operations, driving improved compliance, safety, and performance.

The [PROJECT MANAGER] is supported by the [REGIONAL OPERATIONS MANAGER], [ASSISTANT PROJECT MANAGER], and Jacobs' **regional and national technical resources** that strengthen on-site decision-making and performance. The [ASSISTANT PROJECT MANAGER] plays a critical role in day-to-day operations, reinforcing safety, responsiveness, and operational discipline across the facility and collection system. This leadership structure ensures **consistent daily performance supported by deep technical expertise**.

**Reusable trust-building line:** "Our leadership team will ensure we operate as **an extension of [the client's] [staff/team]**, maintaining transparency, responsiveness, and alignment with [the client]'s goals through organized communication and performance tracking." This "extension of your staff" phrase is deliberately repeated later in the section (see the Annual Innovation Workshop framing) to reinforce a partnership rather than vendor relationship.

## Coordinated System Operations and Data-Driven Performance

**Values bridge (firm-wide, reusable near-verbatim):** "We recognize water's role in the natural cycles of our planet; its connections to land, food, and energy; and perhaps most importantly, its influence on our cities, our societies, and our cultures. Water shapes how communities grow, how people live, and how we plan for a resilient future."

"We use this perspective to help our clients implement integrated solutions to solve the most pressing water management challenges. We call this our **OneWater approach**, and it means we bring the people, capabilities, and solutions to overcome the challenges facing [the client]."

Our approach is supported with **data-driven tools and real-time monitoring**, including performance dashboards and maintenance tracking systems. These tools provide **clear visibility into system performance**, allowing our team to identify trends, anticipate issues, and respond proactively before they impact operations.

This **coordinated, data-driven approach** (paired with the Traditional-vs-Enhanced O&M comparison exhibit — see `differentiator-traditional-vs-enhanced-om-comparison.md`) improves flow management, reduces overflow risk, strengthens compliance, and enhances system resilience in the client's operating environment.

## The Jacobs Difference: Expertise That Drives Results

Jacobs delivers more than traditional O&M services by combining experienced on-site leadership with **a deep bench of regional and national Subject Matter Experts (SMEs)**. Our team includes specialists in process optimization, biosolids management, odor control, asset management, regulatory compliance, maintenance reliability, and digital systems. These experts actively support the on-site team, providing **targeted solutions and accelerating implementation of improvements**.

By connecting real-time operational insights with engineering and asset management expertise, we **translate daily performance data into informed capital planning and system improvements**. Our approach aligns operational decisions with long-term investments, **enhancing system reliability, extending asset life, and delivering cost-effective performance**.
