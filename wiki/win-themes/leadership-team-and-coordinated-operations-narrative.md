# Leadership Team Introduction and "Extension of Client Staff" Coordinated-Operations Narrative

## Leadership-driven operations and accountability

Jacobs' approach begins with **strong, accountable leadership** supported by the experienced on-site team described in Section 4 – Project Staffing and Management Plan. Project Manager Nathan Callison, Regional Operations Manager Scott Mangold, and Assistant Project Manager Tony Rose (shown in **Exhibit 2-2**) bring the experience, technical depth, and leadership needed to deliver **reliable and compliant operations** to [CLIENT], a coastal New England town of approximately 10,000 year-round residents.

Successful wastewater operations depend on leadership that not only maintains performance, but also **proactively manages operational, regulatory, and safety risks**. Jacobs provides that leadership through disciplined operational practices, clear accountability, and direct access to technical resources that strengthen decision-making and accelerate problem-solving.

Nathan's leadership emphasizes **accountability, workforce development, and continuous improvement**. He fosters a culture where operators take ownership of their facilities, which is reflected in strong housekeeping, organized workspaces, and pride in daily operations, driving **improved compliance, safety, and performance**.

Nathan is supported by Scott Mangold, Tony Rose, and Jacobs' **regional and national technical resources** that strengthen on-site decision-making and performance. Tony plays a critical role in day-to-day operations, reinforcing safety, responsiveness, and operational discipline across the facility and collection system. Our leadership structure ensures **consistent daily performance supported by deep technical expertise**.

Our leadership team will ensure we operate as an **extension of [CLIENT]**, maintaining transparency, responsiveness, and alignment with [CLIENT]'s goals through organized communication and performance tracking.

## Coordinated system operations and data-driven performance

We recognize water's role in the natural cycles of our planet; its connections to land, food, and energy; and perhaps most importantly, its influence on our cities, our societies, and our cultures. Water shapes how communities grow, how people live, and how we plan for a resilient future.

We use this perspective to help our clients implement integrated solutions to solve the most pressing water management challenges. We call this our **OneWater approach**, and it means we bring the people, capabilities, and solutions to overcome the challenges facing [CLIENT].

Our approach is supported with **data-driven tools and real-time monitoring**, including performance dashboards and maintenance tracking systems. These tools provide **clear visibility into system performance**, allowing our team to identify trends, anticipate issues, and respond proactively before they impact operations.

This **coordinated, data-driven approach** (highlighted in **Exhibit 2-3**) improves flow management, **reduces overflow risk, strengthens compliance, and enhances system resilience** in [CLIENT]'s coastal environment.

## The Jacobs difference: expertise that drives results

Jacobs delivers more than traditional O&M services by combining **experienced on-site leadership with a deep bench of regional and national Subject Matter Experts (SMEs)**. Our team includes specialists in process optimization, biosolids management, odor control, asset management, regulatory compliance, maintenance reliability, and digital systems. These experts actively support the on-site team, providing **targeted solutions and accelerating implementation of improvements**. By connecting real-time operational insights with engineering and asset management expertise, we **translate daily performance data into informed capital planning and system improvements**.

Our approach aligns operational decisions with long-term investments, **enhancing system reliability, extending asset life, and delivering cost-effective performance**.
