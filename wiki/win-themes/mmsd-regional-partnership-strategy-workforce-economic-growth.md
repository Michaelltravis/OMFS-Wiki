# Strategy 02 — Workforce Development and Economic Growth

*Workforce and economic development for [CLIENT], facility staff, city and regional residents, union members, and the seven-county region means creating pathways to jobs, education, and business growth that will strengthen local economies, promote equity, and build the local workforce. Our plan reinforces activities that will catalyze economic growth and development and prepare future workers for high-demand water sector jobs. We'll leverage this contract to create equitable career pathways, support small business growth, and drive sustainable regional development.*

**Deepen Union Partnerships**

- Leverage union relationships to backfill critical positions and support new career paths.
- Develop apprenticeship program tied to [CLIENT] needs.
- Support pre-apprenticeship training for under-represented groups.

**Enhance Internships and Apprenticeships**

- Scale up [CLIENT]'s internship programs by introducing Camp RISE-style initiatives to teach basic skills (estimating, scheduling, safety, and contract management) and provide certification prep and project exposure.
- Offer rotational internships across Jacobs to broaden career exposure.
- Deploy wastewater treatment training modules and certification guides to staff and interns.
- Incorporate best practices from Jacobs' global internship programs that lead to long-term career growth.

**Expand Scholarship and Academic Partnerships**

- Establish a $50K scholarship targeted at UW-Milwaukee environmental studies candidates that builds on UW-Milwaukee sponsorships to date.
- Partner with MATC to mirror Wisconsin's state-certified wastewater operator program.
- Provide senior staff as teachers for related courses.
- Expand career pathways with 3-month internships that are integrated with [CLIENT]'s summer intern and Operator-in-Training programs.

**Academic and Research Partnerships**

- Connect [CLIENT] programs to STEAM, research, and hands-on projects, and to innovation hubs through university and nonprofit partnerships.
- Create a physical hub for onsite research and maintenance activities to support staff and intern training.

**Community Engagement and Career Exploration**

- Integrate PRISM's Youth Career Exploration Days with the Fresh Coast Ambassador program.
- Build on [CLIENT]'s RISE internship program and engage youth through STEM outreach and PRISM Career Exploration Days.
- Offer hands-on learning activities, workshops, and mentorship for K-12 schools.

**Small Business Growth**

- **Establish Jacobs Academy** to provide technical training and mentoring for small businesses, including compliance, bidding, business development, and project delivery.
- **Capacity Building:** Strengthen local firm capacity by publishing lower-tier contract opportunities, linking firms to general contractors, and offering OSHA training, financial planning resources, and leadership development.
- **Hands-On Learning:** Provide real-world project exposure to build confidence and capability (equipment demonstrations, GI projects, and maintenance training).

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the six clusters — unions, internships and apprenticeships, scholarships and academic partnerships, research partnerships, youth career exploration, and small-business capacity building — and the discipline of naming a dollar figure on at least one commitment so the strategy is not entirely made of verbs. "Jacobs Academy" and the capacity-building bullet are reusable named offers for any pursuit with a small-business participation goal.

Pursuit-specific: the universities and technical colleges, the state operator-certification program, the client's own internship program names, and the scholarship amount. The "seven-county region" phrasing must be replaced with the pursuit's actual service-area description.

Pairs with: [Strategy 01, community health and environmental enhancement](mmsd-regional-partnership-strategy-community-health-environment.md) and the other-communities workforce examples, especially [JXN Water](mmsd-regional-partnership-engaged-partner-jxn-water-jackson.md) and [San Marcos](mmsd-regional-partnership-engaged-partner-san-marcos.md), which supply the proof that these programs produce hires.
