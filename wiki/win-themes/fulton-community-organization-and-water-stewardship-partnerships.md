# Community Organization and Water Stewardship Partnerships

**Strategic Collaborations with Charitable and Community Organizations**

**Senior Services North Fulton.** Serving seven senior centers across Alpharetta/Crabapple, Atlanta, Milton, Roswell, and Sandy Springs, this partnership provides multiple engagement avenues, including staff volunteer opportunities through programs like Meals on Wheels; participation in seasonal and ongoing programming, including summer and holiday events; water conservation support through the sponsorship and distribution of water-saving kits (e.g., low-flow showerheads and faucet aerators), potentially integrated into Aging in Place events; and educational seminars for senior residents on managing water use, understanding utility costs, and the vital role of treatment facilities in delivering safe, reliable water.

**Chattahoochee Nature Center.** Finding environmental and/or water adjacent partners can help spread the stories of these facilities and putting staff out in the community is another way to get outside the gates. The Chattahoochee Riverkeeper (CRK) and Chattahoochee Nature Center (CNC) are natural choices. We can develop a list of events and contacts for each organization and leverage existing relationships to determine which ones would be the best fit. This could include participating in and collaborating on workshops and seminars, field trips, and classroom presentations on topics such as water resources, protecting water quality, supporting natural biodiversity, and more with both organizations.

Jacobs already fields a team in CNC’s annual Water Drop Dash 5K and one of our Atlanta-based senior leaders serves on the Board of Trustees. CRK’s annual Sweep the Hooch cleanup is an opportunity for staff to put its message of environmental protection and stewardship into action and highlight the commitment to the service area. The event takes place in March/April and provides an easy way to get involved and make a difference.

Here are some additional ideas designed to deliver immersive educational experiences connecting water management with environmental conservation:

- **“From River to Tap” workshops:** Partner with CNC to create an interactive workshop that follows the journey of water—from the Chattahoochee River through the treatment process to clean, reusable water. These workshops may include live demonstrations and hands-on activities, such as filtration experiments and UV light treatment simulations.
- **World Water Day/Earth Day Programs:** Co-host engaging events with hands-on activities, facility tour giveaways, and expert talks on water conservation and wastewater innovation. Activities may include a “River to Reuse Challenge” where families track household water use and explore water treatment processes.
- **Annual Clean Water Festival:** Partner with CNC to host an interactive festival featuring eco-vendors, plant tours, conservation workshops, and STEM-based activities such as “Build Your Own Filtration System” and “Ask an Operator” sessions.
