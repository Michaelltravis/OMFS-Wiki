# Compliance Systems and Laboratory Program

We will deploy Hach WIMS integrated with LIMS and our Sample Tracking Tool (STT) to centralize data, automate reporting, and ensure no sampling requirement is missed. Our ELAP-aligned sampling plan, chain-of-custody, and regular audits will produce defensible data and on-time submittals for the advanced water treatment facility, the urban runoff recycling facility, and stormwater.

**Benefits to [CLIENT] callout:** *"Fewer data gaps, easier audits, and reliable compliance packages ready for [CLIENT] and regulators."*

**Companion graphic — "Our Six-Point Compliance Program" (asset `152_008A26`).** A hexagonal cluster of six points arranged around a central hub reading **99.98% Compliance**: SOPs and Process Control Expertise; Transparency and Reporting; Audits and Project Monitoring; Oversight and Support by Global Experts; Culture of Ethics; Compliance Training and Tools.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the paragraph is a model of compliance writing that survives technical scrutiny, because every clause names a thing rather than an intention — a WIMS platform, a LIMS, a sample tracking tool, an accreditation-aligned sampling plan, chain-of-custody, audits. The phrase "ensure no sampling requirement is missed" is the client's real fear stated plainly and then answered by the tooling that precedes it, and "defensible data and on-time submittals" is the two-word summary of what a regulator-facing owner buys from a compliance program. The six-point hexagonal graphic is the more portable asset: six named pillars orbiting one hard percentage gives a compliance section a memorable center of gravity, and the pillar names (SOPs and process control, transparency and reporting, audits and monitoring, global expert oversight, ethics culture, training and tools) are generic enough to reuse across any O&M pursuit while specific enough to look deliberate.

Pursuit-specific: verify the 99.98% environmental compliance figure is current at proposal time; replace ELAP with the target state's laboratory accreditation program; confirm which WIMS and LIMS the client already runs, since proposing to deploy a platform the client has already licensed reads as inattention; and list the actual reportable facilities in the final clause.
