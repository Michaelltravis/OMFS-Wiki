# Above and Beyond: Value-Added Investments and Savings

## Built-In Investments to Elevate O&M and Unlock Significant Savings

Jacobs is committed to delivering meaningful and measurable improvements from the start of the contract. As part of our Base Fee, we are investing—at no additional cost to [CLIENT], a Midwest regional sewerage district—in a suite of high-impact value-added improvements that strengthen resilience, optimize operations, and unlock long-term savings. **These include additional onsite and offsite resources beyond your minimum requirements**, advanced digital tools for operational insight, energy and chemical optimization pilots, solids processing enhancements, reliability improvements, and targeted studies that address [CLIENT]'s most pressing operational challenges. Many of these offerings represent services that would ordinarily require stand-alone consulting contracts. Details are provided on **pages IV-2 and IV-70.**

**We'll collaborate with [CLIENT] to prioritize these items and deliver them within the first 24 months to accelerate performance improvements and establish a strong foundation for the decade ahead.**

*Exhibit (asset 363_007CAM_3) — $107M in Value-Added Investments and Savings over the 10-year Term, Included in Base Fee:*

| Category | Value |
|---|---|
| Investments | $4.6M |
| Savings | $58M |
| Onsite support | $19.2M |
| Offsite support | $15M |
| Best practices | $4.9M |
| Regional partnership | $5.3M |
| Studies | $154K |

*Potential Future Savings — $21–53M:*

| Category | Value |
|---|---|
| Process improvements | $12–33M |
| Future SCADA/OT/improvements | $8–17M |
| Future optimizations | $1–3M |

*Savings and Investments over 10 years of up to $160M.*

## Additional Offerings to Expand Savings and Innovation

**Our team has also identified an additional 25+ future-ready ideas with the potential to generate significant savings, reduce operational risk, and strengthen system performance.** Jacobs will work with [CLIENT] to explore these additional opportunities that further expand the impact of the O&M contract and shared savings. Details are provided on pages **IV-2** and **IV-73**.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-tier framing — what is committed and priced inside the base fee, what is credible upside, and what is a pipeline of identified ideas — plus the promise that the client picks the order and gets the first tranche inside 24 months. The single most reusable sentence is that many of these offerings "would ordinarily require stand-alone consulting contracts," which converts an unpriced extra into a comparison against the competitor's fee.

Pursuit-specific: every figure, the category split, and the cross-references to pages IV-2, IV-70, and IV-73. These figures are value-added investment and savings outcomes, not fee or rate data, and belong in the proof-point registry with an as-of date and an approved-for-external-use flag before they go into a new proposal.
