# Seamless Transition Will Lay the Foundation for Uninterrupted and Continuous O&M Services

[CLIENT] — a Midwest regional sewerage district — has a schedule that provides approximately 18 months for pre-term transition, intentionally structured to minimize risk and protect uninterrupted operations. **Jacobs will execute our proven transition approach that delivers continuity, staffing stability, and strong Day 1 performance.** We'll sequence critical staffing, systems, and governance activities well ahead of the contract start, so **handover occurs without disruption to service, safety, or compliance.**

**Expert Transition Team, Leadership, and Processes Ensure Day 1 Readiness.** Our transition will be led by **senior transition manager Kelly Irving** and a dedicated team experienced with complex wastewater O&M handovers. We emphasize early engagement, clear communication, and disciplined readiness so people, processes, and systems are fully prepared before operations begin—establishing a strong foundation to elevate performance throughout the contract term.

**Workforce continuity is central to success. Jacobs brings deep experience transitioning and supporting unionized workforces**, and we will focus on continuing to build and align our relationship with the local unions to ensure success. In parallel, our PgM team will mobilize during the pre-term period to establish governance, control tools, and management processes—positioning [CLIENT] for a seamless handover on March 1, 2028.

*Exhibit (asset 319_007CAM_3) — Sampling of Jacobs transitions from the incumbent operator. Prior transitions: Southbridge; Woonsocket; Goderich; Waterbury; Westerly; South Huron Valley Utility Authority; Wilmington; Vancouver; Gresham; Farmington; West Melbourne; Jackson (1 WWTP). 2025 transitions: West Basin (4 reuse plants); Jackson (3 WWTPs).*

> "Given the complexity of West Basin's operations, …Jacobs deployed a deep bench of SMEs and worked collaboratively with District staff to ensure a smooth transition from the previous contract operator of more than 20 years. Overall, the transition was managed very effectively, with minimal challenges, and resulted in a smooth and successful onboarding for a system of this complexity."
>
> —Susanna Li, Manager of Engineering, West Basin Municipal Water District, 310.660.6238

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the sequence — read the client's own transition runway back to them as a deliberate risk-reduction choice, name the transition manager, commit to sequencing staffing/systems/governance ahead of the start, then prove it with a map of comparable handovers and one client's words. The union-continuity sentence is the piece most often needed and most often written weakly; this version commits to relationship-building without promising terms.

Pursuit-specific: the 18-month duration, the March 1, 2028 start date, and the local union relationships.

Keep verbatim: West Basin Municipal Water District and every utility named on the transitions map are other Jacobs clients cited as evidence, not the pursuit client — names, locations, facility counts, and the reference contact stay as written.
