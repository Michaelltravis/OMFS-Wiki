# Strategy 03 — Sustainability and Resilience

*We support [CLIENT]'s position as an industry model for climate resilience and sustainable water management and will promote, accelerate, and embed practices that create long-term environmental, social, and economic stability, while preparing systems and communities to withstand future shocks and stresses such as climate change, flooding, and resource scarcity.*

**Nature Positive Solutions**

- Engage Jacobs' SMEs to advance [CLIENT]'s shift toward regenerative, nature-positive solutions that support climate resilience and adaptation, sustainability, and progress toward the 2035 Vision and the 2025-2027 Strategic Plan.
- Combine [CLIENT]'s leadership with Jacobs' expertise to elevate land protection as a cornerstone of regional climate resilience and sustainability.
- Combine our nature-focused expertise and established regional partnerships to enable [CLIENT] to acquire and protect river corridor lands.
- Use Great Lakes and Milwaukee Watershed collaborations to identify priority conservation areas and integrate nature-based solutions into land acquisition strategies.
- Focus with [CLIENT]'s input on where we can deliver ecological and community benefits by embedding biodiversity restoration, flood risk reduction, and water quality improvements.

**Expand Integrated Watershed Planning**

- Improve water quality and enhance flood risk management on a regional scale.

**Leverage Partnerships**

- Work with the Great Lakes Watershed and Milwaukee Watershed initiatives to strengthen regional sustainability and resilience leadership.
- Work with our subconsultant partners P3 and PRISM to create green job opportunities resulting from nature-positive planning efforts and enhance public engagement by involving community-facing organizations.

**Support Business Case Development**

- Quantify social and environmental returns on investment for GI (e.g., Greenseams), renewable energy, and resilience projects, using Jacobs' tools, such as Simetrica-Jacobs.
- Embed social value in decision making to attract funding and enhance partnerships.
- Practice transparent reporting aligned with global sustainability standards.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the argument that a sustainability strategy earns its place only when it changes an investment decision — hence the business-case cluster and the commitment to reporting against global standards. Also universal: routing green-job creation through the local subconsultant partners rather than claiming it directly, which makes the economic benefit local by construction.

Pursuit-specific: the watershed initiative names, the client's land-conservation program, the vision and strategic-plan titles, and the subconsultant names. Where the client has no land-acquisition program, replace that cluster with their largest capital sustainability commitment.
