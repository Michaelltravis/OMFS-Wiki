# Comprehensive O&M Enhanced by Award-Winning Consulting and Program Management

With the Jacobs team, [CLIENT] — a Midwest regional sewerage district — can count on a partner that brings comprehensive O&M services, enhanced by award-winning consulting and program management expertise, including:

- **Local knowledge and national expert resources** allowing us to mobilize quickly, deliver performance excellence, and make improvements faster, without surprises or disruption.

- **Resilient, interconnected operations**—built into daily decisions—to support [CLIENT]'s sustainability and integrated watershed goals, reduce risk, protect public health, and safeguard water resources in all conditions.

- **One-team culture with clear accountability and visible results** to unify Jacobs and transitioning staff around shared expectations, strong support, and pride in [CLIENT]'s mission.

- **Safety first and always**, with a zero-incident culture reinforced by our leaders on every shift and supported by rigorous training—plus strong cyber/physical security—to protect [CLIENT]'s people, assets, and systems.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: all four bullets. They are the compressed form of the "operator plus full-service firm" positioning and work in any O&M executive summary or cover letter where the client's fear is that an operator will show up thin.

Pursuit-specific: "integrated watershed goals" reflects a regional sewerage district's remit — replace with the pursuit client's own stated environmental goal. The third bullet presumes staff transitioning in from an incumbent operator; drop or reword for a greenfield or first-time-outsourcing pursuit.
