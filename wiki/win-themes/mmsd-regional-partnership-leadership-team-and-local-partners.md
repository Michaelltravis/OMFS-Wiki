# Community-Focused Leadership Team to Drive Positive Long-Term Outcomes

Our regional partnership leadership team offers a strong local focus. The caliber of their experience demonstrates our commitment to provide the resources and structure to ensure transparency, accountability, and collaboration, and to align with [CLIENT]'s 2035 Vision:

- **Rick Warner** is a Milwaukee resident with extensive experience managing water infrastructure programs. He brings a strong track record of forging partnerships in the water industry, and excels at connecting municipalities, workforce leaders, and community organizations to deliver sustainable solutions.

- **Toyin Ogunfolaju** is a government relations and social value specialist who has led initiatives that have embedded sustainable social, economic, and workforce solutions into major infrastructure programs. Her focus is on prioritizing equity and local talent development, drawing on her expertise developing workforce development programs, advancing workforce inclusion, and building partnerships with communities, educational institutions, and local organizations.

- **Megan Myszka** brings experience in stakeholder engagement and public involvement for major infrastructure projects. She has managed outreach for complex, high-profile initiatives and infrastructure plans, and excels at developing inclusive public involvement and communication strategies aimed at building trust with diverse communities.

- **PRISM Technical Management and Marketing Services (PRISM)** has a legacy of driving local supplier and small business growth in Milwaukee, directing over $1.7B to local firms and providing mentoring, certification support, and compliance services for many clients, including [CLIENT]. They are a trusted leader in the region and will guide supplier/small business development to foster economic development.

- **P3 Development Group (P3)** is a Milwaukee-based firm specializing in strategic communications and community engagement. They excel at crafting clear messaging, managing media relations, and building trust with diverse communities—and offer experience working with [CLIENT].

- **Mike Tate** is a Milwaukee-based government and public affairs specialist who will support our community outreach work, drawing from his 25 years of experience working in the Milwaukee public sector.
