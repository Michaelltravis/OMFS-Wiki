## Reuse guidance

Universal: totalling the value-added column and then immediately committing to a delivery window and a client-led prioritization conversation. The total makes the offer concrete; the 24-month window makes it believable; letting the client sequence it makes it a partnership rather than a bundle.

Pursuit-specific: every dollar value and the total, the membrane and platform names, the facility and pump-station counts, the four deragging locations, and the cross-reference pages. Re-sum the total from the rows actually offered.
