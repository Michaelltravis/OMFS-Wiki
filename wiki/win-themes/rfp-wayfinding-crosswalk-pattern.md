## Reuse guidance

What is universal is the device, not the rows: a short lead-in that states the response follows the RFP's prescribed format (cite the exact RFP page range), then a two-column table whose left column repeats the evaluator's own scoring questions word for word and whose right column gives the page, page range, or envelope where the answer sits. Grouping the rows by the RFP's own section numbers lets a scorer work the table top to bottom without hunting, and the same table run internally before submittal is a fast compliance check — any blank right-hand cell is an unanswered requirement.

Three things must be rebuilt per pursuit. First, the left column: transcribe the new solicitation's evaluation criteria and minimum-qualification questions verbatim; paraphrasing loses the recognition effect that makes the device work. Second, the right column: freeze pagination first, then populate, and re-verify after any late repagination. Third, the jurisdiction-specific row groups — local preference, service-disabled-veteran preference, per-entity Joint Venture financials, state security/immigration affidavits, contract-compliance utilization plans, and insurance certificates — which exist here because of Georgia and county purchasing rules and will differ elsewhere.

Substitute the bidding entity's legal name for [PROPOSER]. In the source proposal this was JC Solutions, the joint venture of Jacobs and Atlanta-based minority-owned CERM; where a JV bids, keep the JV name in the crosswalk (the RFP itself asks whether the executive summary names the legal entity and business type, and asks for financials from each JV member), and let the executive summary carry the split between Jacobs' operating capability and the local partner's presence and workforce commitment.

Pairs with the executive-summary blocks in this folder, which supply the content the first row group points to. Read `verbatim/fulton-county-2025/pages/p0004.md` through `p0007.md` for the complete table as submitted, including the original page-number targets.
