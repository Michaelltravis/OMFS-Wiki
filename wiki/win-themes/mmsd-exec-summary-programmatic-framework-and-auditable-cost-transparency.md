# Programmatic Framework for Unified Governance, Accountability, and Delivery

[CLIENT] — a Midwest regional sewerage district — structured this contract to deliver accountability and transparency, especially for shared costs and maintenance outcomes, across its complex, interconnected system. Jacobs' program management (PgM)-based operating model will do just that—unifying governance, delivery management, and cost control across [CLIENT]'s facilities. Our PgM framework creates a single source of truth for operating, maintenance, and financial systems and ties performance and cost to verifiable data, shared key performance indicators (KPIs), and disciplined reporting—with dashboards, exception tracking, and structured monthly/quarterly reviews that drive timely corrective action.

**Auditable, Transparent, Shared Cost Controls and Rapid Issue Response.** A critical PgM outcome is shared cost transparency. Jacobs will establish complete, auditable controls for energy, chemicals, fuel, and biosolids—linking consumption and performance drivers directly to invoicing, inventory, and operational conditions. The system will automatically flag KPI deviations, maintain a corrective action log visible to [CLIENT], and trigger immediate notifications for any condition that could impact compliance—followed by a written corrective action summary.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the whole passage. The logic — client structured the contract for accountability, so here is an operating model that produces accountability as a by-product rather than as a promise — transfers to any multi-facility or shared-cost pursuit, and the PgM framing is a genuine differentiator against operators who offer only a monthly report.

Pursuit-specific: the shared cost categories (energy, chemicals, fuel, biosolids) and the "complex, interconnected system" descriptor. Replace the KPI/reporting cadence with the cadence the RFP requires.
