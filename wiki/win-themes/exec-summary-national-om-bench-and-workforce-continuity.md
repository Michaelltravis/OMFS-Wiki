# Executive Summary Credential Beat — National O&M Bench, ENR Rankings, and Workforce Continuity

## A team built for this program

Jacobs has provided full-service water and wastewater O&M since 1980, supporting some of the largest and most complex public systems in the U.S.—including Baltimore (180 MGD), Wilmington (134 MGD), Baton Rouge (300 MGD), and Jackson (145 MGD). We bring 4,000+ O&M staff nationwide and a 98% contract renewal rate since 2013—validating our disciplined approach to O&M excellence and superior client satisfaction. **ENR** ranks us #1 in Sewer & Waste, Wastewater Treatment, and Sanitary & Storm Sewers — reflecting the integrated design, consulting, construction, and O&M we bring to every client.

But credentials alone don't run your plants. People do. We've assembled a high-performance, qualified leadership team with direct knowledge of [CLIENT]'s facilities, their operational challenges, and Oklahoma's regulatory environment. We'll retain qualified incumbent staff and invest in their growth—structured training, certifications, and mentorship from our regional SMEs—so the team running your plants tomorrow is stronger than the one there today.

### Stat callouts carried alongside this beat

- 99.98% environmental compliance record
- 40 years O&M group experience
- Combined wastewater capacity managed
- 10+ major O&M projects with 100+ MGD or 80+ staff
- Proven culture — safety, compliance, and people first
- 1000's of assets under management

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the structure — credentials stated as peer-scale operating contracts with capacities, then a hard pivot to people, then the incumbent-retention and staff-investment promise. The sentence "But credentials alone don't run your plants. People do." is a reusable hinge in any executive summary where corporate scale risks reading as impersonal, and it is especially effective in a challenger bid where incumbent staff are the audience as much as the evaluators.

Pursuit-specific: the state named in "Oklahoma's regulatory environment," the four peer contracts, and every corporate figure. The stat callouts are set as graphics in the source and should be re-set with verified current values, not copied.
