# How We Will Deliver Results — Compliance, Reliability, Transparency

The [CLIENT] can count on Jacobs to meet and exceed your expectations for compliance, reliability and transparency, including:

- **Operate to Permits and KPIs.** We will run the full train, track critical control points (CCPs)/log reduction values (LRVs), and issue monthly compliance/KPI reports. We will verify alarms remotely and integrate alarm callouts with Ignition, documenting alarm functionality each month. We will trend injection-well performance (head pressure, specific capacity, backwash intervals) to protect capacity and regulatory confidence.
- **QA/QC and Audits.** We will deploy a formal QA/QC program with internal audits and data reviews tied to corrective actions for durable improvements.
- **Reporting and Dashboards.** We will produce MORs/DMRs/annual reports and a customized dashboard showing KPIs, work-order status, chemical/energy intensity, compliance results, and safety/training metrics—so the [CLIENT] will have real-time visibility into performance.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-commitment skeleton — operate to permits and KPIs, QA/QC and audits, reporting and dashboards — is the shortest complete answer to a compliance evaluation criterion, and each bullet carries a frequency ("monthly compliance/KPI reports," "documenting alarm functionality each month") that makes the promise auditable. The dashboard content list (KPIs, work-order status, chemical/energy intensity, compliance results, safety/training metrics) transfers to any O&M pursuit unchanged and is worth reusing closely, because it shows the client exactly what they will be able to see. The phrase "so the [CLIENT] will have real-time visibility into performance" is the hinge that converts a reporting deliverable into a transparency win theme.

Pursuit-specific: CCP/LRV tracking and injection-well trending belong to potable-reuse permits — replace them with the parameters the target permit actually regulates; confirm the SCADA/alarm platform; and rebuild the dashboard mockup around the target facility's real parameters before showing it.
