# Compliance, Reliability, and Transparency Narrative

## How We Will Deliver Results — Compliance, Reliability, Transparency

"The client can count on Jacobs to meet and exceed your expectations for compliance, reliability and transparency, including:

- **Operate to Permits and KPIs:** We will run the full [treatment] train, track critical control points (CCPs)/log reduction values (LRVs), and issue monthly compliance/KPI reports. We will verify alarms remotely and integrate alarm callouts with [SCADA platform, e.g., Ignition], documenting alarm functionality each month. We will trend injection-well performance (head pressure, specific capacity, backwash intervals) to protect capacity and regulatory confidence.
- **QA/QC and Audits:** We will deploy a formal QA/QC program with internal audits and data reviews tied to corrective actions for durable improvements.
- **Reporting and Dashboards:** We will produce MORs/DMRs/annual reports and a customized dashboard showing KPIs, work-order status, chemical/energy intensity, compliance results, and safety/training metrics—so the client will have real-time visibility into our performance."

**Companion graphic:** a screenshot-style "Example WWTP" dashboard mockup showing a plant-status header bar (BOD5, TSS, TRC, pH, flow, solids, an edit control), an effluent-concentration gauge (green/yellow/red zones), a monthly-average trend line with max/min reference lines, and a most-recent-month detail trend — captioned "Customized Dashboard for Real-Time Visibility into Our Performance." No graphic asset ID was legible on this exhibit (appears to be a live product screenshot rather than a numbered Jacobs graphic template); flag as `graphic-id: unreadable` if cataloged for design reuse.

**Benefits to [Client] callout:** *"Confident compliance, 100% visibility, and data-driven decisions that reduce operational and regulatory risk."*

## Training, Cross-Training, and Knowledge Transfer

"Training for our staff will consist of in-house training, and training through water and wastewater associations such as the Water Environment Federation (WEF), the American Water Works Association (AWWA), and the WateReuse Association. We will also provide training for our associates through private sources such as the American Water College, which got its start in California in 2006 and provides water and wastewater training for all 50 states. Its many course offerings include California-approved specialized training courses such as advanced water treatment.

We will cross-train across [primary facility] and [secondary facility], pair [client] interns with mentors, and implement a formal training plan (EPA/Title 22/ELAP sampling, safety, QA/QC, SOPs) with documented completion and incentives."

**Benefits to [Client] callout:** *"Staff stability and improved morale with a resilient, multi-skilled workforce; reduced reliance on overtime/contractors; and continuous capability growth for [client] staff and interns."*

## Site-Specific Readiness and Reliability

Organize by asset, one short paragraph each:

- **[Primary treatment facility].** Verify PLC/SCADA versions and license ownership, finalize point lists and CCP/LRV displays, and add server redundancy for continuous operations. Conduct monthly peak-flow stress tests so the client will know membranes can handle design peaks.
- **Stormwater/Lift Stations.** Validate alarm point lists, implement monthly alarm testing, exercise valves, and increase [pump station] vac-truck cleaning to every eight weeks; apply advanced de-ragging tools driven by Intelligent O&M capabilities to maximize uptime.
- **[Secondary/legacy recycling facility].** Uphold preservation protocols, coordinate OEM services, and integrate facility data when units return to service—including potential diluent roles.
- **Injection Wells.** Trend performance and embed telemetry in [primary facility] dashboards to document compliance and protect injection capacity over time.

**Benefits to [Client] callout:** *"Higher runtime and fewer nuisance alarms; protection of well capacity; and smoother transitions as systems cycle in and out of service."*

## Asset Management, CMMS, Inventory, and 72-Hour Repair Recommendations

"We will implement an ISO 55001-aligned asset management program with CMMS-driven preventive maintenance (PM)/predictive maintenance (PdM) using the client's [CMMS platform, e.g., Mentor APM] system, then track PM compliance and reliability KPIs, and deliver recommendations within 72 hours for large/emergency repairs to protect warranties and asset life. We will stand up a modern inventory program, including barcoding/QR codes, labeled bin locations, mobile access, reorder and obsolescence KPIs, and support annual budget planning for maintenance, replacements, and capital improvements."

**Benefits to [Client] callout:** *"Longer asset life, fewer reactive repairs, clearer budget forecasts, and faster parts availability to shorten outages."*

## Compliance Systems and Laboratory Program

"We will deploy [WIMS platform, e.g., Hach WIMS] integrated with [LIMS] and our Sample Tracking Tool (STT) to centralize data, automate reporting, and ensure no sampling requirement is missed. Our ELAP-aligned sampling plan, chain-of-custody, and regular audits will produce defensible data and on-time submittals for [all reportable facilities]."

**Benefits to [Client] callout:** *"Fewer data gaps, easier audits, and reliable compliance packages ready for [client] and regulators."*

A strong reusable framework for organizing any compliance-program narrative into six named pillars anchored to one hard performance statistic.

## Safety, Emergency Preparedness, and Risk Management

"Supported by our regional team of safety professionals, the onsite team will implement a robust, documented Safety Plan/Program and Emergency Response Plan/Program, conduct regular drills, and leverage Jacobs' FEMA disaster-response experience and regional network to secure materials and staffing during extreme events. Our environmental risk approach will track regulatory, safety, asset condition, and financial indicators and drive targeted improvements."

**Benefits to [Client] callout:** *"Safer operations, faster recovery from events, and measurable risk reduction across facilities."*
