## Reuse guidance

Universal: the column heading "WHY THIS MATTERS TO [CLIENT]" and the discipline it enforces — no leader earns a row unless there is a client-specific reason for them to be there (prior employment with the client, prior PM of the same program, state licensure, ownership of a named contract schedule). The corporate-bench paragraph is equally reusable: name the regional manager, executive sponsor, and maintenance specialist as individuals, say what problem each one absorbs, and close on the fee inclusion.

Pursuit-specific: all six names and roles, the facility and program references in column two, and the schedule numbers cited for KPI oversight. Where the target RFP numbers its schedules differently, re-map them.
