# Cover Letter Differentiators — Innovation, Large-Utility Scale, and a Culture of Doing Things Right

**Future innovations that lower costs and improve performance.** Beyond the services defined in the base scope, we bring proven tools and capabilities to reduce costs and improve performance, including Intelligent O&M analytics for chemical and energy optimization, structured odor evaluation programs, and accelerated implementation of [CLIENT]'s NexGen EAM platform. These are practical, implemented solutions already delivering results for utilities of similar size and complexity. Full details are provided in Section 8 Innovative and/or Alternative Recommendations.

**More than an operator: integrated support from a proven partner to large utilities.** We deliver O&M services for some of the most complex utility systems in the country. In Jackson, Mississippi, we stabilized and now operate three WWTPs and 99 pump stations following emergency federal oversight—demonstrating our ability to restore performance in highly challenging environments. Jacobs has provided full-service water and wastewater O&M since 1980, supporting some of the largest and most complex public systems in the U.S.—including Baltimore (180 MGD), Wilmington (134 MGD), Baton Rouge (300 MGD), and Jackson (145 MGD). For [CLIENT], this means seamless integration between operations and capital improvements—from startup and commissioning to long-term asset integration—without requiring [CLIENT] to bridge that gap.

**A culture of doing things right.** Ethics and accountability are foundational to how we operate. Our expectation of "perfect compliance and perfect reporting of non-compliance" drives behaviors that align with our long-term partnership mindset and commitment to transparency. As a result, we are the go-to O&M firm for communities looking to make a change.
