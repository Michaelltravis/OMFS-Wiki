# Energy Management Strategy and Operating Actions

JC Solutions will develop a comprehensive Energy Management Strategy for the wastewater facilities and associated pump stations. Our approach goes beyond traditional energy-efficiency measures to identify significant cost-saving opportunities through strategic rate-structure optimization, demand management, and innovative operational strategies.

JC Solutions will analyze all three WRFs and the 28 pump stations to optimize electricity costs without capital investment. Our specialized team will review complex electric-utility rate structures, evaluate strategic demand-management opportunities, negotiate preferred utility classifications, and implement operational timing adjustments that align energy consumption with the most favorable rate periods. This strategic initiative delivers immediate financial impact with projected annual savings of $235,000–$365,000 across the wastewater system. Benefits extend beyond cost reduction to include enhanced operational visibility, improved system resilience, reduced exposure to rate volatility, and the foundation for future sustainability initiatives—transforming energy from an uncontrollable expense into a strategic asset for [CLIENT].

Our team will conduct a detailed review of current electricity rate structures for all three treatment plants and 28 pump stations within the [CLIENT] system. We will model electric-utility costs across all relevant rate structures to identify least-cost options for each facility, taking into account the unique operational characteristics of each site. By analyzing historical consumption patterns, we can determine significant opportunities for rate optimization and identify facilities where alternative rate classifications could yield immediate savings without capital investment.

JC Solutions will develop facility-specific strategies to reduce peak-demand charges across the facilities. Our approach includes modeling backup-generator deployment for strategic peak shaving, with careful consideration of emissions thresholds and noise constraints to ensure regulatory compliance and community harmony. We will evaluate battery energy storage systems (BESS) for peak-demand reduction through detailed financial-feasibility analysis that considers current rates and future projections. For viable energy-asset deployments, we will create automated control strategies that optimize battery charging/discharging cycles aligned with rate structures and operational needs.

Leveraging our established relationship with the electric utility, JC Solutions can negotiate preferred rate classifications for the facilities to maximize cost savings. Our team will coordinate directly with utility representatives to identify interconnection requirements for any proposed energy assets, ensuring smooth implementation of recommended solutions. Utility tariff structures are notably complex and often require active daily or hourly management to optimize—a capability our team excels at providing. We will also identify all available utility incentives and rebate programs that could benefit [CLIENT] and the required application process to secure these additional financial benefits.

The Energy Management Strategy will include comprehensive analysis of process timing and sequencing to minimize coincident peak demands across all facilities. We will identify strategic load-shifting opportunities that take advantage of time-of-use rate differentials while maintaining operational performance. To ensure long-term success, JC Solutions will develop staff-training protocols for ongoing energy-management best practices that empower the onsite team to maintain optimization over time. Additionally, we will create recommendations for a real-time monitoring and response system capable of managing daily and hourly energy consumption in alignment with rate structures and operational priorities.
