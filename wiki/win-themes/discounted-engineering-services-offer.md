## Reuse guidance

Universal: the offer structure — access to technical specialists throughout the contract, scoped to the facilities the O&M team already runs, justified by continuity across operations, design, and construction rather than by price alone. Also universal is the move of following the offer with a short menu of candidate studies, so the offer reads as immediately usable.

Pursuit-specific: the commercial terms, facilities covered, and list of candidate initiatives. Route any fee, multiplier, or rate detail through the pricing lead; it is intentionally omitted from this reusable block.

The specific studies offered under this discount in the source proposal each have their own block: [odor study gap analysis](odor-study-gap-analysis-and-updated-report.md), [Replica hydraulic model](replica-hydraulic-model-system-optimization.md), and [NexGen Phase 2 asset data support](nexgen-phase-2-asset-data-support.md).
