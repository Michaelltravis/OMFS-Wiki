# Energy Management Investment Value and Cost Savings

## Investment Value and Cost Savings

Based on our experience with similar facilities and Georgia Power's rate structures, we project the following financial impacts from our Energy Management Strategy.

- Rate structure optimization: $120,000 - $180,000 annually
- Peak demand management: $75,000 - $125,000 annually
- Operational timing adjustments: $40,000 - $60,000 annually
- Total potential savings: $235,000 - $365,000 annually

Potential cumulative 10-year savings of $2.5 - $4 million (accounting for utility rate escalation).

Enhanced facility performance and extended equipment life through optimized operations. Reduced carbon footprint through more efficient energy utilization. Improved budgetary predictability for energy costs.
