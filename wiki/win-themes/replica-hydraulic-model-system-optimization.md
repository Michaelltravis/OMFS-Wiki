# Replica Hydraulic Model for System and Facility Optimization

## Replica Hydraulic Model for System and Facility Optimization

We can use our hydraulic modeling tools, together with Replica Process modeling already being provided (see cost proposal for details), to evaluate how flow moves through the treatment system under dry and wet weather conditions. This effort can extend beyond the plants to include key collection system interfaces and the system's major regional pump station, helping [CLIENT] — a regional wastewater utility trust — better understand hydraulic constraints, wet weather risk points, pump station operating strategies, and plant flow management opportunities. We recognize [CLIENT]'s current initiative with Black & Veatch for the collection system model. Our effort can leverage this work and provide a combined hydraulic and process perspective, supporting the development of more effective operating procedures, setpoints, and sequencing strategies across the system.

This effort will help improve compliance, reliability, and operational performance by establishing distinct operating strategies for dry and wet weather conditions. Potential benefits include improved wet weather response, better pump station sequencing and wet well level management, more reliable flow distribution through the plants, reduced power consumption, and stronger systemwide planning for odor mitigation and hydraulic bottlenecks.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the combined hydraulic-plus-process framing (most competitors offer one or the other), the explicit acknowledgment of the client's in-flight modeling contract with another firm, and the benefit list expressed as things operators will do differently — dry- and wet-weather operating strategies, pump sequencing, wet well levels, flow distribution, power draw.

Pursuit-specific: the named pump stations and collection system interfaces, the incumbent modeling consultant, and whether process modeling is in the base fee.
