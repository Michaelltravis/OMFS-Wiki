# Strategy 01 — Community Health and Environmental Enhancement

Our Regional Partnership plan includes four strategies. We'll explore with [CLIENT] those ideas within each strategy that have the greatest impact and bring the most value to [CLIENT]; **Exhibit V-4** presents details of this plan. Each strategy separates ideas we are committing to — **going above and beyond** — from the further ideas we would pursue with you, labeled **aiming higher**.

*Improving quality of life through clean water, flood risk reduction, and green infrastructure (GI) strategies, while promoting equity and sustainability.*

**Rain Barrel/GI Training Program**

- Train and certify local contractors and small businesses to install rain barrels, rain gardens, and other GI.
- Create an online portal to schedule and host interactive workshops.
- Build on [CLIENT]'s and Reflo's "Green Schools" partnership. Introduce GI for stormwater capture. Support educational partnerships and outdoor learning spaces and provide materials for participating schools.

**Public Access and Recreation**

- Leverage the EPA Sediment Removal project for restoring public access to the river. Plan and design a public fishing pier to enhance riverwalk and recreation access. (Exhibit V-3)
- Focus on underserved communities to provide access to green spaces.

**Riverwalk Partnerships**

- Partner with WaterMarks and Riverkeepers to transform the Riverwalk.
- Add environmental education and stewardship information to public spaces along the Riverwalk.
- Encourage local participation in sustainability, water conservation, and ecosystem health awareness activities.

**Celebrate Water Stewardship and Community Pride**

- Sponsor public art installations and educational signage along river corridors.
- Promote and sponsor water stewardship and sustainability focused activities.
- Sponsor public-focused incentives (neighborhood challenges, Fresh Coast Guardians).

**Strengthen Flood Management and Habitat Restoration**

- Hold community-focused workshops on expanded flood management projects.
- Ideate further opportunities within the Kinnickinnic River corridor.
- Provide technical expertise for nature-based solutions.
- Implement stormwater modeling and predictive analysis to maximize water quality benefits.

**Neighborhood Revitalization**

- Foster local economic, funding, and grant opportunities.

**Advance Sustainability Goals**

- Introduce innovative energy and resource recovery strategies.
- Pilot GI technologies.
- Expand climate resilience activities.

**Support Business Case Development**

- Use our Simetrica-Jacobs model to measure, monetize, and communicate social value.
- Develop key performance indicators and performance dashboards.
- Publish success stories on website.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the strategy's internal shape — a one-line italic definition of what the strategy improves, then themed clusters of ideas, each cluster small enough that the client can select from it. Also universal: closing every strategy with a "support business case development" cluster, which promises measurement and monetized social value rather than goodwill.

Pursuit-specific: the named local nonprofits and programs (Reflo, WaterMarks, Riverkeepers, Fresh Coast Guardians), the named river corridors, and the tie to a specific federal sediment-removal project. Where the pursuit region has no such programs, name the client's own outreach programs instead — the ideas must attach to something already in motion.
