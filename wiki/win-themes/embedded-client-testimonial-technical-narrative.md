# Embedding a Client Testimonial Pull-Quote Inside a Technical Narrative Section

Rather than confining client testimonials to a dedicated past-performance appendix, a testimonial can be placed as a sidebar pull-quote directly inside the body of a technical-approach section — positioned next to the specific capability it validates (here, the Collections O&M subsection) so the evaluator encounters third-party validation at the moment they are evaluating that exact claim.

**Illustrative sanitized example** (structure and genuine proof-point content preserved; reference client and individual generalized):

> "For the past 32 years, Jacobs has been our contract operator for [REFERENCE CLIENT]'s Wastewater Treatment Plant and Collections System. Over the decades, Jacobs has provided strong technical personnel who have improved our process tremendously, and reduced costs. The Jacobs team has won Plant of the Year and Collections of the Year every year since 2012 from [a regional water environment association]. They work closely with our staff, and we think of them as an extension of [our organization]. We're very pleased with Jacobs' service and look forward to many more years of partnership."
>
> — [PUBLIC WORKS MANAGER], [REFERENCE CLIENT] WWTP

## What makes this testimonial effective

- **Multi-decade tenure figure** (32 years) is a concrete, hard-to-fabricate credibility marker that a generic "we have great client relationships" claim cannot match.
- **Named, verifiable awards** (an annual "Plant of the Year" / "Collections of the Year" recognition from a real regional industry association, sustained every year since a stated year) — specific enough to be checked, which makes the claim more credible than an unverifiable superlative.
- **Relationship language** ("we think of them as an extension of the City") reinforces the win theme that the operator functions as embedded staff rather than an arm's-length contractor.
- **Placement discipline**: the quote appears in the Collections subsection specifically, not in a generic "about us" location — it validates the collections-system track record right where the reader is evaluating that claim.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the technique of placing a short (3-6 sentence), attributed, role-only client testimonial as a sidebar inside a technical-approach subsection — matched to the specific capability being discussed — rather than saving all testimonials for a past-performance appendix. The pattern of leading with a tenure figure, following with a specific outcome (cost reduction, process improvement), and closing with named/verifiable award recognition is a reusable structure for any long-tenured reference relationship.

Pursuit-specific: source a fresh, currently-authorized quote from an actual reference client for the target pursuit — do not reuse this quote's substance (tenure length, award history, attributed language) for a different client, since doing so would misrepresent another firm's actual client relationship. Confirm the reference client and individual have consented to being quoted in a new proposal. Pair with `past-performance-narrative-structure.md` (the fuller Appendix B project-description pattern, which also uses a client pull-quote but in a different, dedicated-appendix placement) and with `collection-system-om-program.md`, the technical-approach block this specific testimonial was placed alongside in the source proposal.
