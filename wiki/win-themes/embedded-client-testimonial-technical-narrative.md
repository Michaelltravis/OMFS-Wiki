# Embedding a Client Testimonial Pull-Quote Inside a Technical Narrative Section

**This is a recipe, not prose.** The prose it describes — the verbatim testimonial and its attribution — lives in [../technical-approach/collection-system-om-program.md](../technical-approach/collection-system-om-program.md), under "Collection System Operations."

## The pattern

Rather than confining client testimonials to a dedicated past-performance appendix, place a testimonial as a sidebar pull-quote directly inside the body of a technical-approach section, next to the specific capability it validates — in the source proposal, alongside the Collections O&M bullet list — so the evaluator encounters third-party validation at the moment they are evaluating that exact claim.

## How to build one

1. **Open with a tenure figure.** "For the past 32 years, Jacobs has been our contract operator for the City of Auburn's Wastewater Treatment Plant and Collections System." A multi-decade number is a concrete, hard-to-fabricate credibility marker that a generic "we have great client relationships" claim cannot match.
2. **Follow with a specific outcome in the client's own words.** In the source quote: strong technical personnel who "improved our process tremendously, and reduced costs."
3. **Name verifiable third-party recognition.** "Plant of the Year and Collections of the Year every year since 2012 from the California Water Environment Association" — specific enough to be checked, which makes it more credible than an unverifiable superlative.
4. **Close on relationship language that carries the win theme.** "We think of them as an extension of the City" reinforces the embedded-staff positioning rather than an arm's-length contractor.
5. **Attribute by name, title, and organization.** Mengil Dean, Public Works Manager, City of Auburn WWTP, CA.
6. **Enforce placement discipline.** The quote belongs in the subsection whose claim it proves, not in a generic "about us" location. Keep it to three to six sentences so it reads as a sidebar and does not interrupt the narrative column.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the six-step construction above and the placement discipline — a short, attributed, capability-matched testimonial inside a technical subsection rather than saved for an appendix. The sequence (tenure figure → specific outcome → named award recognition → relationship language) is a reusable template for any long-tenured reference relationship. Pursuit-specific: source a fresh, currently authorized quote from a real reference client for the target pursuit, and confirm consent from the quoted individual. Pair with the prose block named in `pairs-with` above, and with the past-performance narrative pattern for the fuller, dedicated-appendix placement.
