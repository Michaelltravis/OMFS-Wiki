# Cover Letter Opening — Decade-Long Relationship, System Challenges, and Baldrige Credibility

This procurement represents a critical opportunity for [CLIENT], a regional municipal water utility trust, to elevate operations and maintenance (O&M) through a more proactive, transparent, and asset-focused approach. Over the past decade, Jacobs has worked closely with every division within the Utilities Department, building a deep understanding of your facilities, business processes, and the people who operate them.

Through our site visits and due diligence, we have identified the key challenges facing the system today—and we understand the future challenges [CLIENT] must also navigate. Over the term of this partnership, [CLIENT] will be managing a changing biosolids and disposal strategy, major treatment expansion and disinfection upgrades, increasing pressure to optimize power and chemical use, and the need to protect significant capital investments as new assets are brought online. Jacobs brings the accountability, structure, operational discipline, and integrated technical resources to address both priorities: delivering measurable improvements from day one while helping [CLIENT] plan, integrate, and sustain the improvements that will define the system's long-term performance.

With more than 40 years of O&M experience in Oklahoma and ongoing engineering work across the state, we have established strong working relationships with the Oklahoma Department of Environmental Quality (ODEQ) and other regulatory agencies. As shown in the timeline on the following page, we have a long history of supporting [CLIENT]—including the Hefner Water Treatment Plant (WTP) expansion, North Canadian Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) work, the SCADA System Master Plan, and Deer Creek improvements—meaning we understand not only your system, but also your expectations. This familiarity enables us to transition seamlessly and begin delivering results immediately.

[CLIENT] is ambitious and positioned for performance excellence. As the only company in the water and wastewater industry to receive the Malcolm Baldrige National Quality Award, we understand what it takes to build a culture of accountability, discipline, and continuous improvement. Through our partnership, we'll help position [CLIENT] to achieve that level of recognition in a way that reflects your people, priorities, and long-term vision.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the four-move opening — (1) frame the procurement as the client's opportunity, not the firm's; (2) show due diligence by naming the client's future challenges, not just today's problems; (3) convert prior non-O&M work into system knowledge and a regulator relationship; (4) close on shared aspiration using the Baldrige award. Pursuit-specific: the decade of prior work, the named prior projects, the state tenure figure, the regulator name, and the list of forward-looking challenges (biosolids strategy, expansion and disinfection upgrades, power and chemical optimization, protecting new capital assets).
