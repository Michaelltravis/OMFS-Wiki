# We're Inspired by Your Vision

True to your 2035 Vision, [CLIENT] is doing tremendous work to improve community health and the environment, build the next generation of water professionals, and become a national model for climate resilience and sustainable water management:

- **Community Health and Environment:** As a sustainability leader, you're cutting greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions and working with community partners to advance climate resilience and resource recovery. You're taking a proactive role in preventing flooding and restoring habitat with such projects as the Kinnickinnic River Corridor transformation and taking every opportunity to also revitalize neighborhoods with public amenities as you do so.

- **Workforce and Economic Development:** You're working hard to build the pipeline of water professionals through internships, training, and career pathways through such programs as Fresh Coast Ambassador & Fresh Start, as well as your on-the-job training partnership with Employ Milwaukee and the Water Equity Task Force.

- **Sustainability and Resilience:** You're positioned, via the 2035 Vision and 2025-2027 Strategic Plan, to be a national model for climate resilience and sustainable water management through integrated watershed planning, renewable energy goals, green infrastructure (GI), and promoting equity.

- **Research:** You're investing in a $13M clean-water research lab at one of the two water reclamation facilities and piloting innovative technologies such as the Proteus Upflow Media Filter and GI Performance Testing. And you're implementing a research program to explore new ways to optimize operational performance.

We're inspired by your vision—and by all your ongoing initiatives, which we plan to support. **Exhibit V-4** presents our ideas for furthering your goals, building on the foundation you've established, to go "above and beyond" and then maybe even "aiming higher." **Exhibit V-3** is one of those "above and beyond" ideas.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the four categories (community health and environment; workforce and economic development; sustainability and resilience; research), the all-second-person voice, and the closing pivot that promises to build on the foundation rather than replace it. The "above and beyond / aiming higher" two-tier label is a reusable device for separating committed enhancements from aspirational ones.

Pursuit-specific: every program name, the strategic-plan and vision-document titles, the research lab investment figure, and the named pilot technologies. If the client has no published vision document, substitute the board's adopted strategic priorities or the capital plan's stated goals — but the bullets must quote the client's own language or the flattery shows.
