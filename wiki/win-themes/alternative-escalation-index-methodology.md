# Alternative Escalation Index Methodology as a Priced Innovation

## Alternative Escalation Index Methodology — Saving [CLIENT] $620,000 Over the First Contract Term

As an alternative to the RFP's CPI-U South escalation, Jacobs proposes an approach that better reflects actual wastewater industry cost drivers. Using an index such as CPI Water/Sewer, or a blended CPI/ECI methodology, more closely aligns annual adjustments with real labor and operating cost trends, reducing the need to carry contingency for mismatches between general CPI and utility-specific escalation.

By aligning escalation with actual cost conditions, we can lower the Base Fee through reduced pricing contingency. This approach provides an estimated savings to [CLIENT] — a regional wastewater utility trust — of approximately $155,000 annually, or $620,000 over the first contract term.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the argument that a general consumer price index does not track utility labor and operating costs, that the mismatch is priced as contingency, and that a better-matched index therefore lowers the client's fee. That mechanism travels to any O&M contract with an escalation clause.

Pursuit-specific: the index the RFP specifies, the alternative index or blend proposed, the annual and term savings figures, and the contract term length used to extend them. Register both figures in `proof-points/registry.md` before restating them, and have pricing confirm the arithmetic against the current bid.

The $155,000-per-year and $620,000-per-term figures are outcome savings offered to the client, not Jacobs fee or rate data, and belong in the narrative. Pairs with [the innovation section opening](innovation-more-than-an-operator-framing.md).
