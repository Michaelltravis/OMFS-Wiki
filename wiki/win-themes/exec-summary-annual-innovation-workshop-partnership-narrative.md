# Executive Summary Partnership Beat — Continuous Improvement Through an Annual Innovation Workshop

## Continuous improvement through an Annual Innovation Workshop

We believe successful wastewater operations are built on **strong partnerships**. Our team will operate as an **extension of [CLIENT]'s staff** — [CLIENT] being a coastal New England municipal wastewater utility — maintaining open communication, transparency, and alignment with [CLIENT]'s priorities while proactively identifying opportunities to strengthen long-term system performance and resilience.

We reinforce this approach through our Annual Innovation Workshop, a structured, collaborative forum that brings together [CLIENT] staff and Jacobs SMEs to review system performance, evaluate prior-year accomplishments, and establish **targeted goals for the coming year focused on continuous improvement** while avoiding complacency over time. Drawing on expertise in process engineering, biosolids, collections, odor control, energy management, and digital optimization, these sessions focus on high-value opportunities aligned with [CLIENT]'s priorities.

By connecting **day-to-day operations with technical expertise and forward-looking planning**, we help [CLIENT] move beyond reactive operations toward **continuous, measurable improvement**. This process ensures alignment with evolving regulatory requirements, improves decision-making, and provides a **clear path from operational challenges to implementable solutions**.

> *"The Annual Innovation Workshop was very informative and it was amazing how much information was packed in 8 hours. It was like attending a WEFTEC workshop in your own backyard with all the presentations that were custom made for your facility."*
>
> — Firooz Fath-Azam, Former South Huron Valley System Manager
