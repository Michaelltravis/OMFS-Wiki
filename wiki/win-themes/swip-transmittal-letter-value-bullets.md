# Transmittal Letter — Opening Framing and "Jacobs' Value" Bullet List

**Letterhead (top of page, right-aligned):** Jacobs' issuing-office address and phone number head the transmittal letter above the recipient block — e.g., "555 South Flower St, Suite 200, Los Angeles, CA 90071, P: 213.228.8255." This is the proposing office's address, distinct from the "Organization Name and Contact Information" box near the signature (which gives the corporate/contracting office address instead) — include both, they can legitimately differ.

**Header block:** Submitted via OpenGov Procurement Portal. Addressed to [CLIENT] — Public Works Department, Engineering and Street Services Division, [CLIENT ADDRESS].

**Subject line:** "RE: Proposal for [SOLICITATION NO.] – Operation, Maintenance, and Management Services of the [CLIENT]'s Sustainable Water Infrastructure Project (SWIP) Advanced Water Treatment Facilities (AWTF)"

**Salutation:** "Dear [CLIENT CONTACT NAME], PE, and Evaluation Committee:"

**Opening paragraph — credit the client's investment, then commit to the mission.** "[CLIENT] is setting the national standard for sustainable, locally sourced water through its visionary investment in the Sustainable Water Infrastructure Project (SWIP). Jacobs is proud to submit our proposal to operate and maintain these critical facilities. We are committed to ensuring reliable groundwater replenishment today and supporting your readiness for future Direct Potable Reuse (DPR)."

**Second paragraph — state the integrated value proposition and name the compliance anchor.** "Jacobs offers [CLIENT] a unique blend of operational rigor, advanced treatment expertise, and seamless integration across advanced water treatment operations, engineering, and deep regional support. Our proposed team will function as a true extension of [CLIENT] staff, with a focus on compliance, transparency, and continuous improvement. We will deliver safe, high-quality Product Water while maximizing uptime, protecting SWIP assets, and maintaining performance under [CLIENT]'s permits, including State Board Orders R4-2021-0044 and R4-2023-0366."

## "Jacobs' Value to [CLIENT]" — nine-bullet value list

Introduce with: "Jacobs stands out as [CLIENT]'s most experienced and forward-thinking partner, offering:"

- **Certified, Experienced Team** – Led by our designated Chief Plant Operator Mack McKenzie (WW Grade V, AWT3™ progressing to AWT5™), and supported by Director of Operations Howard Brewen (WW Grade V) and Manager of Operations Chris Catlin (AWT5™, T3), Jacobs will provide 24/7 staffing with a six-person core team and immediate local surge capacity. All staff will be cross-trained across the facilities.
- **Proven Performance** – Jacobs has delivered over 300 long-term O&M projects across North America, including Title 22 GRRP and Indirect Potable Reuse (IPR) projects in Ontario, Clovis, and Soquel Creek. We are currently supporting DPR planning and execution for Pure Water Los Angeles and Valley Water.
- **Unmatched Advanced Water Treatment Expertise** – Jacobs operates facilities comparable to SWIP, with experience spanning every unit process from MBR to RO to UV-AOP. We bring a deep bench of experts in reverse osmosis, instrumentation, and SCADA to ensure optimal system health and regulatory performance.
- **DPR Readiness Leadership** – Jacobs is California's most engaged firm on DPR implementation. Our subject matter experts include WateReuse DPR Working Group leaders and project engineers for Pure Water Los Angeles, Pure Water Southern California, and Valley Water. We will support the client's DPR planning through strategic engineering, regulatory alignment, and forward-looking data collection across SWIP and Arcadia WTP.
- **Integrated Technical Depth** – Our team includes Jacobs specialists in engineering, design-build, automation, cybersecurity, laboratory QA/QC, and asset management. This allows us to deliver not just operations but rapid access to solutions when challenges arise.
- **Innovation and Value-Added Services** – As part of our base fee, Jacobs will deliver co-planned innovation workshops, operator training and incentives, performance optimization studies, asset inventory setup, and access to discounted engineering services. These offerings will steadily improve system performance and reduce risk.
- **Transparent, KPI-Driven Reporting** – We will implement a customized dashboard and issue monthly compliance and performance reports. These will include CCP and LRV tracking, work order status, chemical and energy KPIs, and injection well health indicators to ensure the client has complete visibility and audit-ready documentation.
- **Frictionless Transition and Continuous Compliance** – We will deliver a seamless handoff from the current contract to day one of operations in January 2026. Our team will retain institutional knowledge by engaging qualified incumbent staff, coordinate with the client through onboarding, and establish systems, schedules, and emergency readiness before startup.

**Closing paragraph:** "Jacobs' proposal reflects our commitment to operational excellence, regulatory compliance, and shared success. We will be [CLIENT]'s trusted partner. More than an operator, we will be a collaborator working to advance your vision for sustainable water supply and environmental leadership."

**Signature block:** Sincerely, [signature] — Paul Rheault, Vice President of Operations (West). E: Paul.Rheault@jacobs.com. P: 541.408.2362.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the letterhead + separate signature-block organization-contact-box pattern (two legitimately different Jacobs addresses can appear in the same letter — proposing office vs. contracting/corporate office), the two-paragraph opening arc (credit investment → state integrated value + compliance anchor), the nine-bullet value-list structure (team credentials → track record → technical depth → forward-looking readiness → integrated capability → no-cost value-add → transparency/reporting → transition risk mitigation), and the boilerplate box format most public-agency procurement portals require. Pursuit-specific: `[CLIENT]`, the client contact/addressee, the solicitation number, and the addenda list must be replaced for every new pursuit. The named Jacobs staff (Mack McKenzie, Howard Brewen, Chris Catlin, Paul Rheault, Jim Huentelman), their certifications, and the reference projects (Pure Water Los Angeles, Pure Water Southern California, Valley Water, Ontario, Clovis, Soquel Creek, Arcadia WTP) are real and should only be reused if they are in fact the individuals/references being proposed for the new pursuit — confirm current assignment and certification status before reuse. Pair with `swip-om-qualifications-proof-points.md` for the track-record claims behind bullet two, and with `swip-value-added-offerings-package.md` for the substance behind the innovation/value-added bullet.
