# Transmittal Letter — "Our Value to the Client" Eight-Bullet Value List

Jacobs stands out as [CLIENT]'s most experienced and forward-thinking partner, offering:

- **Certified, Experienced Team** – Led by our designated Chief Plant Operator Mack McKenzie (WW Grade V, AWT3™ progressing to AWT5™), and supported by Director of Operations Howard Brewen (WW Grade V) and Manager of Operations Chris Catlin (AWT5™, T3), Jacobs will provide 24/7 staffing with a six-person core team and immediate local surge capacity. All staff will be cross-trained across the facilities.

- **Proven Performance** – Jacobs has delivered over 300 long-term O&M projects across North America, including Title 22 GRRP and Indirect Potable Reuse (IPR) projects in Ontario, Clovis, and Soquel Creek. We are currently supporting DPR planning and execution for Pure Water Los Angeles and Valley Water.

- **Unmatched Advanced Water Treatment Expertise** – Jacobs operates facilities comparable to [CLIENT]'s advanced water treatment facilities, with experience spanning every unit process from MBR to RO to UV-AOP. We bring a deep bench of experts in reverse osmosis, instrumentation, and SCADA to ensure optimal system health and regulatory performance.

- **DPR Readiness Leadership** – Jacobs is California's most engaged firm on DPR implementation. Our subject matter experts include WateReuse DPR Working Group leaders and project engineers for Pure Water Los Angeles, Pure Water Southern California, and Valley Water. We will support [CLIENT]'s DPR planning through strategic engineering, regulatory alignment, and forward-looking data collection across the advanced water treatment facilities and [CLIENT]'s companion water treatment plant.

- **Integrated Technical Depth** – Our team includes Jacobs specialists in engineering, design-build, automation, cybersecurity, laboratory QA/QC, and asset management. This allows us to deliver not just operations but rapid access to solutions when challenges arise.

- **Innovation and Value-Added Services** – As part of our base fee, Jacobs will deliver co-planned innovation workshops, operator training and incentives, performance optimization studies, asset inventory setup, and access to discounted engineering services. These offerings will steadily improve system performance and reduce risk.

- **Transparent, KPI-Driven Reporting** – We will implement a customized dashboard and issue monthly compliance and performance reports. These will include CCP and LRV tracking, work order status, chemical and energy KPIs, and injection well health indicators to ensure [CLIENT] has complete visibility and audit-ready documentation.

- **Frictionless Transition and Continuous Compliance** – We will deliver a seamless handoff from the current contract to day one of operations in January 2026. Our team will retain institutional knowledge by engaging qualified incumbent staff, coordinate with [CLIENT] through onboarding, and establish systems, schedules, and emergency readiness before startup.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the eight-bullet architecture and its sequence. Each bullet names a capability in bold, then delivers the client-facing consequence in one or two sentences — never a capability alone. The order is deliberate and transfers to any O&M cover letter: who will actually be on site (named, certified) → that we have done this many times (portfolio count plus like-for-like references) → that we know this specific technology (unit-process by unit-process) → that we are ahead of where the client is going (regulatory/technology readiness) → that the local team has a corporate bench behind it → what the client gets at no additional cost → how the client will see performance → how the risk of changing operators is retired. The transition bullet is the incumbent-displacement move: it names the retention of qualified incumbent staff, which converts the evaluator's biggest objection into a Jacobs commitment.

Pursuit-specific: `[CLIENT]`, the program and facility descriptors, the named staff and their grades/certifications, the core-team headcount and shift model, the reference project list, the portfolio count, and the day-one date. The permit-driven items in the reporting bullet (CCP and LRV tracking, injection well health indicators) are specific to a Title 22 groundwater replenishment reuse permit — for a conventional WWTP pursuit, substitute the applicable NPDES/effluent KPIs and keep the dashboard-plus-monthly-report structure.
