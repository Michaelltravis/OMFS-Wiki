# Executive Summary — Comprehensive JV Solution and Client Benefits

**Our solution is comprehensive, focused on regulatory compliance, optimization and asset preservation, and delivers value today and tomorrow.**

JC Solutions delivers a comprehensive O&M strategy grounded in deep expertise across MBR systems, asset management, and process optimization. Our partnership with [CLIENT], a county wastewater utility, is built on a foundation of exceptional regulatory compliance—and so much more. We bring a forward-looking vision powered by advanced digital tools, intelligent O&M platforms, and data-driven decision-making that optimize performance and manage costs. Backed by Jacobs' extensive technical resources and CERM's strong local presence and workforce development leadership, we are fully committed to safeguarding [CLIENT]'s infrastructure and serving the community well into the future.

**Meaningful Partnership Built on Transparency, Accountability and Investment in Community.** Our approach is grounded in transparency, accountability, and continuous improvement—the same model that has driven a 98% client renewal rate across our portfolio. We will work side by side with [CLIENT] to tackle operational challenges, implement innovative O&M strategies, and proactively plan for the future. Beyond plant operations, we're committed to community engagement—expanding on the successful outreach at [FACILITY B], a wastewater reclamation facility, and partnering with the Chattahoochee Nature Center to deliver impactful education programs that foster the next generation of water stewards.

**Proven Regulatory Compliance and Environmental Stewardship.** With a 99.98% compliance rate across hundreds of regulated facilities, our track record speaks for itself. We will apply our rigorous internal audit protocols and QA/QC processes to ensure full compliance at all three of [CLIENT]'s facilities—integrating proactive sampling, noise and odor mitigation, and continuous monitoring to meet all EPA, Georgia EPD, and [CLIENT] standards.

**Expert Staff, Locally Grounded and Regionally Backed.** Our team includes highly qualified, licensed professionals with deep knowledge of advanced wastewater technologies, pump station operations, and the unique complexities of [CLIENT]'s system. Many have direct experience at [FACILITY A], a wastewater reclamation facility, and [FACILITY B], bringing valuable institutional knowledge. They're supported by Jacobs' unmatched regional network of 1,200+ Georgia-based staff—offering expertise in SCADA, MBR optimization, capital planning, commissioning, and more.

**MBR Experience and Expertise to Protect and Maximize Your Return on Investment.** Our global and Atlanta-based MBR experts—like Scott Levesque, PE, and Youngchui Choi, PE—provide deep handson knowledge in MBR system design and operations. From membrane life extension and optimized chemical dosing to enhanced effluent quality, we offer proven, practical solutions that protect [CLIENT]'s investment while delivering performance that meets the highest operational and public expectations.

**Seamless Transition with Proven Talent Retention.** We understand the value of continuity and staff retention during transition. Led by Transition Manager Mike Boven—who uniquely brings experience with both Jacobs and Veolia—we are equipped to manage a smooth, informed transition that eliminates disruptions and preserves institutional knowledge. Our recent transitions of Veolia-operated sites this past year in MA, MS, and CA involving over 100 FTEs illustrate our ability to deliver rapid, successful handovers without compromising service quality.

**Fresh Perspectives Paired with Advanced Technologies for Elevated O&M.** We bring a revitalized team equipped with fresh perspectives and cutting-edge tools. Through Intelligent O&M, Replica™, and an ISO 55001-aligned Asset Management Program, we will optimize asset performance, improve maintenance efficiency, and reduce costs. Our use of advanced analytics, SCADA integration, and predictive modeling empowers real-time decision-making while safeguarding long-term infrastructure value.

**Workforce Development that Builds Local Capacity.** Workforce stability starts with investment. We provide structured training, career development, and certification incentives to retain and grow local talent. Our partner, CERM, enhances this through community-based workforce programs that strengthen [CLIENT]'s labor pipeline and support long-term operational resilience.

**Over $6.7 Million in Added Value from Day One.** Our base proposal includes more than $6.7 million in digital tools, technical resources, and innovation investments—delivering immediate and lasting value to [CLIENT]. These enhancements are designed to improve service delivery, extend asset life, and keep costs down—without increasing [CLIENT]'s budget.
