# Executive Summary Opening — JV Partnership Value Proposition and Combined Strengths

_Driving efficiency, ensuring compliance, and extending asset life — JC Solutions offers the value, innovation, and commitment to help [CLIENT] meet today's needs while preparing for tomorrow's challenges. Our team is equipped with next-level O&M tools and strategies designed to elevate daily operations, reduce costs, and protect your capital investments by extending the life of existing and new assets. We bring a long-standing track record of regulatory compliance, with a history of meeting and exceeding permit requirements across multiple jurisdictions. Our partnership model is rooted in transparency and backed by a deep bench of local and national resources, enabling rapid reach-back for expert support and continuous improvement. We've successfully deployed advanced technologies that competitors only aspire to, and our in-house membrane bioreactor (MBR), SCADA, and system optimization experts are just a phone call away._

**We're more than an operator: we're your strategic partner for wastewater excellence and compliance.**

Jacobs and the Atlanta-based, minority-owned firm CERM have formed a joint venture, JC Solutions (A Jacobs/CERM JV), to deliver compliant operations and maintenance services for the wastewater facilities and pump stations in [CLIENT]'s northern service area. This joint venture is a natural evolution of our partnership and a perfect alignment of complementary strengths. Built on over 20 years of combined experience across water, education, aviation, and transportation projects, our partnership brings together complementary strengths: Jacobs' deep O&M expertise and national best practices, and CERM's strong local presence, workforce development programs, and community partnerships. Jacobs is the only fully integrated O&M firm in North America—U.S.-based, Fortune 500, and built for stability. With $12 billion in FY24 revenue and a $2.4 billion O&M backlog, we bring unmatched scale and staying power. Our 4,000+ U.S. O&M professionals and 45,000 global staff make us the industry's largest and most diverse operations partner. CERM was founded in 1995 as a minority-owned engineering, environmental, and program management consulting firm, and has grown into an award-winning organization with 100+ professionals serving the southeastern U.S. CERM offers robust workforce development programs and strong local and regional partnerships. Together, we bring a shared commitment to regulatory compliance, environmental stewardship, and operational excellence.

**By combining Jacobs' national technical leadership with CERM's deep local roots and capacity-building expertise, we are uniquely positioned to deliver consistently compliant and reliable services, optimize life cycle costs, and maintain [CLIENT]'s assets to the highest standards.**

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the italic value-proposition lead (efficiency, compliance, asset life), the "more than an operator" positioning line, the "complementary strengths" JV justification, and the closing bold sentence that fuses both partners' contributions into a single client outcome. This pattern works for any joint-venture or prime/sub pursuit where a national firm pairs with a local or diverse-business partner.

Pursuit-specific: the legal entity name, the partners' names and characteristics, the corporate scale figures, the service-area description, and the technology callouts (MBR, SCADA) that must match the client's actual process train.
