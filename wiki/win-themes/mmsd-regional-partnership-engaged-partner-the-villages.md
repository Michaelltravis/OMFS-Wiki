# 3. The Villages, Florida

*Strengthening workforce, culture, and community connections.*

Jacobs has provided O&M and technology services for 13 water treatment systems, four wastewater treatment facilities, collection and distribution, meter reading, and solid waste collection for this group of 55-plus communities north of Orlando. Our community engagement and workforce development activities include:

- Organized Resident Academy Program, briefings, classes, and tours for the public to educate them on the services taxes and fees supports. Approximately 250 residents visited public works and treatment facilities on recent tours.
- Created a Wastewater Operator Development Program that produced four newly licensed operators in one year, promoted from within the local workforce.
- Designed and delivered a college-level wastewater operations course in partnership with Lake-Sumter College, leading to operator licensure.
- Led student mentorship initiatives linking classroom learning to real-world utility operations.
- Sponsored high school athletics scholarship, golf tournament, barbecues, and Middleton Mingle event to connect families and residents to utilities and strengthen ties with key stakeholders.
- Participated in Government Day civics event to educate students and residents on utilities.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the resident academy is the transferable idea — a structured program that walks ratepayers through the facilities their fees pay for, reported with an attendance number. So is the operator development program framed as internal promotion, which speaks directly to an incumbent workforce worried about a new operator's intentions. The social sponsorships at the end deliberately sit below the workforce items; they are texture, not argument.

Pursuit-specific: the scope list and the named college. Where the pursuit is a single large facility rather than a portfolio, cut the scope sentence to one line so the community content carries the block.
