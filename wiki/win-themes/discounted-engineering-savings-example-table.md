## Reuse guidance

Universal: the layout — paired rows at the discounted and market multiplier for the same scope and hours, a percentage and dollar delta per row, and a rolled-up annual and multi-year value in the bottom right. The closing sentence about no cap on hours is what makes the total credible as a floor rather than a ceiling.

Pursuit-specific: every figure, the work packages chosen (pick ones the client has already said it needs), the term used for the multi-year rollup, and the client's procurement constraints.
