### `103_0037AJ` — Jacobs outperforms averages in top safety categories (horizontal)

* **Figures on the graphic:** Recordable Incident Rate (RIR), 5-year average — industry 0.62 vs. Jacobs 0.19, "Jacobs is 60% better than industry average". DART or Lost Time Injury Rate (LTIR), 5-year average — industry 0.20 vs. Jacobs 0.056, "Jacobs is 72% better than industry average". Footnote: "*2018-2022 data from BLS".
* **Registry:** PP-0311 (Jacobs RIR 0.19), PP-0312 (industry RIR 0.62), PP-0313 (60% better), PP-0314 (Jacobs DART/LTIR 0.056), PP-0315 (industry DART 0.20), PP-0316 (72% better), PP-0496 (BLS 2018-2022 window). Figures confirmed current by the maintainer 2026-10-12.
* **Style:** Jacobs blue gradient panel, dark-blue title band, white industry bars vs. orange Jacobs bars, white down-arrows and callouts, Jacobs wordmark under the Jacobs bars.
* **Reuse:** the corporate version of the Hull Exhibit 5-10 chart (`104_HHull_0091KO_1`) and of the Santa Monica Section 2.14 benchmark graphic; use this one for any new pursuit. Full-width placement in a safety / compliance section. Refresh when corporate EHS publishes a newer BLS window (the footnote year range is the tell).
* **Used by blocks:** `wiki/compliance-plans/integrated-safety-security-cybersecurity-program.md`, `wiki/compliance-plans/swip-safety-plan-performance-record.md`.
