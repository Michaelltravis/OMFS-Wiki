# Resume — Keoki Sears (Program Management Advisor)

**Program Management Advisor – Keoki Sears**

**Education and Licenses**

- MS, Construction Engineering and Management
- BS, Civil Engineering

**Experience Summary**

- Directs Jacobs' $3B program management portfolio for the Americas, provides performance assurance for major programs.
- Built the management systems, tools, and frameworks used across Jacobs globally.
- Two decades leading multi-billion-dollar water/energy/transportation programs.

**Select Projects**

- Program Director, SuedLink–SouthLink – The Wind Powerline, Würzburg, Germany
- Rapid Response Leader, PG&E 10k Undergrounding Program, United States
- Strategic Logistics and Supply Chain Lead, NEOM – The Line, Saudi Arabia

**Benefit to MMSD:**

Keoki, a nationally recognized program management expert, will lead our program management framework to bring a transparent, program-management-style approach to services. He'll develop processes, tools, and governance structure during mobilization and due diligence phases to drive accountability and transformational delivery performance.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the argument this card makes is that an O&M contract deserves program-management discipline, and that the firm can supply the person who wrote the firm's own frameworks. That is a strong differentiator against an incumbent running the work as routine operations. The reusable sentence pattern is "will lead our program management framework to bring a transparent, program-management-style approach to services," followed by the three deliverables — processes, tools, and governance structure — and the phase in which they appear (mobilization and due diligence). Use this card wherever a client has signalled that reporting, accountability or transparency is an evaluation issue.

Pursuit-specific: the portfolio value and the project list. The named programs are intentionally outside water — a wind powerline in Germany, a utility undergrounding program, and a linear megacity in Saudi Arabia — which buys scale credibility but risks reading as remote from a wastewater evaluator. Keep this card adjacent to operator- and engineer-level water cards, and consider substituting a water program if one of comparable scale is available.
