# Nathan Dahl
## Maintenance Manager

### Professional background
- Jacobs Experience: 10 years
- Total Experience: 15 years
- Class II Wastewater Treatment Operator, Georgia; Grade III Wastewater Treatment Plant Operator, Washington; Jacobs Certified Rigging and Signaling.
- Heavy Wheel Vehicle Recovery Sargent Specialist Course; Combat Life Saver Course; Public Communications Camp; PNCWA Inland Empire Quarterly Meeting; PNCWA Tour: Riverside Park Water Reclamation Facility; PNCWA 2024 Fall Conference; PNCWA 2023 Conference.
- 2023 | PNCWA Eastern Washington Treatment Plant Operator of the Year.

### Why Nathan?
- Possesses over 10 years experience in the maintenance of MBR plants and currently serves as the Maintenance Supervisor for Spokane County Utilities’ MBR (Veolia) Plant.
- Over 15 years of supervisory experience including five years in the United States Army as a Motor Sargent.
- Currently serves as the Eastern Washington Regional Director for Pacific Northwest Clean Water Association (PNCWA) and won PNCWA 2023 Eastern Washington Treatment Plant Operator of the Year.

Nathan is a motivated and experienced project manager with over 15 years of proven leadership in supervising, training, and managing operations and maintenance activities. Skilled in overseeing diverse teams and ensuring efficient execution of projects to meet and exceed goals. Adept at troubleshooting, repairing, and maintaining a wide range of equipment, including water and wastewater treatment plant machinery, vehicles, and material handling systems. Strong background in budgeting for facility maintenance, implementing preventative maintenance programs, and providing essential training to enhance team productivity. Recognized for exceptional communication skills, attention to detail, and a solution-oriented approach to complex challenges.

### Responsibilities
Nathan, as the Maintenance Manager for Spokane County Utilities Plans, brings his expertise to the County. He schedules and directs the maintenance of mechanical and electrical equipment, supervises personnel and materials for wastewater treatment facility repairs, and estimates costs and time for maintenance tasks. Nathan diagnoses malfunctions, inspects buildings and equipment, and ensures proper use of tools and safety equipment. He excels at interpreting and working with blueprints and other technical drawings.

Nathan’s skills include mechanical repairs on treatment plant engines and pumps. He collaborates with Project Manager Anthony Benavidez to plan workloads, review projects, and enforce safety regulations. Organized and detail-oriented, Nathan keeps records, prepares reports, and provides training. His open-door policy ensures smooth communication and operation of the facilities.

- Knowledge of the methods, materials, equipment, and tools used in the construction, repair, and operation of engines, pumps, and other mechanical equipment related to wastewater treatment plant operations and procedures
- Knowledge of the principles and practices of an effective mechanical, structural, and grounds preventive maintenance program and operations of internal combustion engines and electrical pump motors
- Basic electrical knowledge involved in working on pumps, motors, and other equipment; equipment, tools, and materials used in maintaining and repairing wastewater treatment plant and related equipment

### Relevant experience
**Maintenance Supervisor/Certified Washington Wastewater Operator III | Spokane County Regional Water Reclamation Facility, Spokane County Utilities | Spokane, WA.** Since 2014, Nathan has been an integral team member, serving as a Mechanic, Specialist, Mechanic, Lead, and now as the Maintenance Supervisor. His responsibilities include maintaining, troubleshooting, and repairing water treatment plant equipment, managing company preventative maintenance programs, budgeting for facility maintenance, providing employee training, and serving as Maintenance On-Call. He currently supervise three direct reports, providing training, mentoring, and career guidance to support their professional development. Nathan excels in planning, scheduling, and directing the maintenance of various equipment. His key responsibilities include diagnosing malfunctions, determining effective corrective actions, and performing skilled mechanical repair work on equipment. Nathan assigns, coordinates, and supervises personnel and materials for maintenance and repair tasks, estimates costs and time for all maintenance, repair, and construction work, and inspects buildings and equipment for necessary maintenance and repair. Additionally, Nathan prepares rough sketches for maintenance, repair, and construction jobs, ensures the proper use and care of tools, materials, and safety equipment, and reads and interprets blueprints, drawings, sketches, plans, specifications, and mechanical illustrations.

**Motor Sargent | United States Army | Fort Lewis, WA.** Nathan oversaw a staff of 15 soldiers and ran the combat field medical batch course for the United States Army. He maintained over 15 ambulances, Humvees, diesel vehicles, and equipment. He managed maintenance and repair of light and heavy tactical vehicles, supervising maintenance, and recovery operations, investigating accidents, monitoring tool and parts inventories, and ensuring optimal workshop conditions.

Nathan received a number of awards and honors during his time serving including: Army Commendation Medal; Army Achievement Medal; Army Good Conduct Medal; National Defense Service Medal; Iraq Campaign Medal; Global War on Terrorism Expeditionary Medal; Global War on Terrorism Service Medal; Army Overseas Service Ribbon; Army Service Ribbon.
