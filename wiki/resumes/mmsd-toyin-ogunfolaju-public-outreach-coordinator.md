# Resume — Toyin Ogunfolaju (Public Outreach Coordinator)

**Public Outreach Coordinator – Toyin Ogunfolaju**

**Education and Licenses**

- BA, Government & Public Policy
- BS, Nutrition

**Experience Summary**

- North America Director of Social Value; 23 years in stakeholder engagement, government relations, social value consulting, and workforce development.
- Led social value and workforce strategies for major infrastructure programs for Maryland Transit Administration, Philadelphia International Airport (PHL), Port Authority of New York and New Jersey (PANYNJ).
- Develops workforce development programs, advances workforce inclusion, and builds partnerships with unions, educational institutions, and community organizations.

**Select Projects**

- Deputy Program Director; Capacity Enhancement Program for PHL; Philadelphia, PA
- Program Management Consultant, PANYNJ Workforce Development Program, NY and NJ
- Program Management Consultant, Maryland Transit Administration, Workforce Development Program, Baltimore, MD
- EPA Environmental and Climate Justice Community Change Grants Program, Various Locations, United States

**Benefit to MMSD:**

Toyin will turn MMSD and community priorities into actionable workforce and outreach programs that strengthen regional partnerships, create jobs, and build the next generation of operators. She's designed and implemented successful strategies that prioritize equity and local talent development.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: many proposals promise community engagement and workforce development in narrative and staff neither. This card answers that by naming a corporate-level director as the owner of both, with a project list drawn from programs where social value was a contractual obligation rather than a courtesy. The benefit paragraph's chain — translate client and community priorities into programs, strengthen regional partnerships, create jobs, build the next generation of operators — is the reusable formulation, and the operator-pipeline ending is what ties social value back to the client's own staffing risk. Use it on any pursuit where the client faces an aging operator workforce or has published workforce, equity or local-hire commitments.

Pursuit-specific: the named programs are transit, aviation and port authority work, not water; that buys programmatic credibility but must be paired with water-side cards so the team reads as a utility team. The partnerships named in the third bullet (unions, educational institutions, community organizations) should be made concrete with the pursuit's actual local partners — technical colleges, union locals, and community groups — in the accompanying workforce development section rather than left generic.
