### Summary
Roy will serve as Operations Manager for the South Canadian facility, helping lead safe, compliant, and dependable operations through one of the most important transitions in the OCWUT program: the move from the existing SBR plant to the new activated sludge wastewater treatment facility. He has 25 years of water and wastewater experience with a strong focus on operations leadership, regulatory compliance, commissioning and startup, SOP development, operator training, and process stabilization. His background includes support for major capital programs, operational testing of new systems, startup planning, permit excursion investigations, and implementation of management systems that strengthen plant performance and accountability. His experience makes him well suited to support South Canadian's startup, staff readiness, operational turnover, and long-term performance as the new facility comes online.

### Relevant Experience
**Regional Technical Specialist | City of Houston | Northeast Water Purification Plant (NEWPP) | Houston, TX.** Roy collaborates with project managers to solve technical problems, report and investigate permit excursions, develop scope for work expansion opportunities, implement management systems, and support commissioning and startup. Roy's work on the **320-MGD NEWPP expansion project** included developing SOPs, training employees, supporting operational testing, and establishing regulatory reporting practices. This experience is directly relevant to South Canadian's startup and turnover into full operations.

**Operations Support | City of Duncan | Wastewater Treatment and Reuse O&M | Duncan, OK.** Roy collaborates with the project manager to help commission new chemical systems and chlorine contact chamber. Roy also helps with emergency operations through severe weather events and flooding.

**Regional Director | City of Beeville | Beeville WWTP | Beeville, TX.** Roy managed an operations staff of more than 100 across eight projects in Texas, including operational improvements and implementation of CIP projects. His oversight included support for major infrastructure work such as the City of Beeville WWTP expansion. This role highlights his ability to lead teams, support operational readiness, and coordinate improvements across multiple sites.

**Regional Manager | North Harris County Regional Water Authority | NHWCRA | Houston, TX.** Roy managed an operations staff of 43 across more than 21 projects, including support for operational improvements and CIP implementation tied to major transmission and pump station work associated with the NEWPP expansion. This experience reinforces his ability to work through large-scale system changes while maintaining operational continuity.

**Operations Support Director | San Jacinto River Authority | SJRA SWPP | Houston, TX.** Roy managed an operational support advisor team that worked with regional and project managers to solve technical problems, investigate permit excursions, develop scope for work expansion opportunities, and implement key operational improvement programs. This role demonstrates his strength in bringing structure, technical support, and problem-solving discipline to operating projects.

**Operations Manager / Compliance | City of Houston | SEWPP | Houston, TX.** Roy managed daily operations for all Houston-area operations with a staff of 120 and 140 service contracts. **Operations Specialist | MWH Constructors | Houston, TX.** Roy supported design review, commissioning-document development, SOP writing, dry testing, wet testing, controls integration, vendor training, safety training, process training, and technical support on new systems.
