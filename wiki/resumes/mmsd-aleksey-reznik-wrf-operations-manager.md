## Reuse guidance

Universal: two devices travel from this card. First, the four-line project list that is the same facility four times at ascending titles — licensed operator, shift supervisor, operations manager, O&M project manager — which shows an internal career ladder more convincingly than any narrative about staff development, and reassures incumbent staff that promotion is real. Second, the certification reciprocity line with a filed date, which converts a licensing risk into a closed item before award; on any out-of-state operations manager, state the filing explicitly rather than promising to obtain certification.

The benefit paragraph sequences four things in the right order for an operations role: optimize daily performance, maintain compliance, help staff adopt digital tools, and reduce energy and chemical use. The digital-tools clause is framed as helping staff adopt rather than installing, which is the right posture when the existing workforce is being retained.

Pursuit-specific: the facility size (168 MGD) and capital program value should be swapped for the most closely comparable facility in the candidate's history; the reciprocity date and the certification list must be current at submittal, and the endorsement categories must match the pursuit state's own certification scheme.

Related blocks: [John Loucks-Powell](mmsd-john-loucks-powell-director-maintenance-asset-management.md) and [Liie Hill](mmsd-liie-hill-capital-project-coordinator.md) also draw on the Wilmington contract, so check the numbers across all three cards for consistency before a proposal uses more than one.
