# Scott Levesque, PE

## MBR SPECIALIST AND WASTEWATER PROCESS

### Professional Background

Why Scott?

- Jacobs Experience: 13 years
- Total Experience: 33 years

- He reviewed Water Environment Federation’s Membrane Bioreactor (MBR) Manual of Practice and membrane-related portions of Design of Municipal Wastewater Treatment Plants.
- He served on the committee that developed ANSI/AWWA Standard B130, Membrane Bioreactor Systems and later chaired the committee for its revision.
- He has designed and evaluated municipal and industrial MBR processes on five continents ranging from 0.1 to 55-mgd capacity.

Scott has 33 years of experience in biological nutrient removal (BNR) and biological process simulation. He serves as Jacobs’ global resource for MBR. He has worked extensively with all aspects of MBR technology: design, troubleshooting, pilot testing, membrane procurement, and membrane replacement/upgrade. He has expertise in a wide range of additional treatment technologies and process equipment for wastewater, potable water, and residuals. Scott has assisted municipal and industrial clients to develop and compare process alternatives, resulting in cost-effective solutions.

### Responsibilities

Scott will provide engineering input to the O&M team, including process simulation and analysis to determine how to operate the plants for maximum efficiency at minimum cost. He will continue to provide training to Jacobs’ O&M staff in the areas of biological nutrient removal and membranes. He will serve as a link between plant staff and Jacobs’ extensive range of subject matter experts.

### Relevant Training

- M.S., Environmental Engineering, Georgia Institute of Technology
- B.S., Environmental Engineering, University of Florida

### Certifications/Licenses

- Professional Engineer: Georgia, Ohio

### Relevant Experience

Lead Process Engineer | Johns Creek Environmental Campus | Fulton County, GA. Prepared an RFP for the County to pre-select and procure a membrane system for its 15-mgd MBR facility, the Johns Creek Environmental Campus. Assisted the County to prepare an RFP to solicit design/build proposals for the same project.

Lead Process Engineer | Yellow River Water Reclamation Facility On-Call Process Support | Department of Water Resources | Gwinnett County, GA. Worked with GCDWR staff to address operational issues at the plant, including its MBR process. Reviewed and analyzed data to investigate occasional episodes of increased effluent ammonia. Developed strategies to maximize biological phosphorus removal, thereby minimizing the expense of chemical phosphorus removal. Updated the plant’s Operations Model, which is built on Jacobs’ proprietary Pro2D2 simulation platform.

Lead Process Engineer | Yellow River Water Reclamation Facility Expansion | Department of Water Resources | Gwinnett County, GA. As part of expansion of the County’s Yellow River Water Reclamation Facility from 14- to 22-mgd capacity, compared an MBR process to a conventional activated sludge process followed by granular media filters, demonstrating that the MBR process would have lower lifecycle cost. Prepared a request for proposals to pre-select and procure a membrane system for the MBR process. Performed simulation modeling to develop the project mass balance. Evaluated capacity of existing treatment facilities with and without aeration improvements to develop a plan for construction sequencing.

Subject Matter Expert | Assessment of Existing Tertiary Treatment Facilities | Department of Water Resources | Gwinnett County, GA. Assessed existing physical/chemical treatment facilities, including solids contact clarifiers, rapid mix and flocculation tanks, high-rate clarifiers, and granular media filters. Developed alternatives to improve or replace these facilities. Presented a workshop on chemical phosphorus removal, alternative coagulants, and tertiary treatment approaches at other facilities. Advised DWR on jar testing protocols.

Senior Technical Consultant | W.B. Casey Water Resource Recovery Facility Capacity Analysis and Plant Expansion Evaluation | Clayton County Water Authority | Clayton County, GA . Evaluated plant capacity constraints, developed influent and effluent design bases, and evaluated liquid and solids treatment process alternatives using economic and non-economic criteria. Selected liquid and solids processes for plant expansion from 24 to 32-mgd capacity. Developed a preliminary engineering report for solids improvements that include primary sludge thickening, sludge screening, anaerobic digestion, sludge storage, dewatering, cake receiving, and thermal drying. Part of the Jacobs team acting as owner’s agent for implementation of biosolids improvements using a progressive design build approach.

Wastewater Process Specialist | Linwood Water Reclamation Facility Process Analysis | Department of Water Resources | Gainesville, GA. Performed facility analysis of this 5-mgd MBR plant, which was experiencing nitrification inhibition. Characterized raw wastewater and developed process simulation model. Determined that the most likely cause of inhibition was quaternary ammonium compounds (QACs). Developed strategies to mitigate the impact of QACs. A year later, updated the evaluation and developed a plan to expand aerated sludge holding and dewatering.

Lead Process Engineer | Regional Water Reclamation Facility | Spokane, WA. Investigated seasonal decline in membrane performance that had affected plant capacity. Developed and implemented capital improvements to achieve required plant capacity. These improvements include - increased membrane area, increased mixed liquor pumping capacity, polymer feed to secondary treatment to improve mixed liquor filterability, and bioreactor swing zones to increase aerobic volume and improve winter nitrification.

Lead Process Engineer | Regional Water Pollution Control Plant Membrane Replacement | Traverse City, MI. In 2013, Suez (now Veolia) 500C membranes that had been installed in 2004 were approaching end of life. Developed a plan for phased replacement with 500D membranes to maximize use of existing assets while minimizing risk associated with impaired permeability due to gram positive bacteria. Negotiated pricing and scope of supply with Suez to avoid cost and time associated with competitive membrane procurement.

Senior Technical Consultant | Fowler Water Reclamation Facility Expansion | Forsyth County, GA. Oversaw expansion of an existing MBR facility from 2.5 to 5-mgd (max monthly average). Existing membrane tanks were reconfigured to maximize capacity. Older generation membranes (Suez 500C) membranes were replaced with the latest generation membranes (Suez 500D). Designed a sidestream process for mixed liquor fermentation. Assisted with membrane performance testing and enhanced biological phosphorus removal process commissioning. Wrote an Operations Guide for the plant.

MBR Facility Lead | Changi WRP Expansion | Singapore Public Utilities Board, Singapore. Led the Liquid Module 3 MBR design, which increased plant capacity by 53 mgd (annual average), making it one of the five largest MBR facilities in the world. The MBR process uses center-flow band screens, deep step feed bioreactors, and DuPont immersed membranes.

MBR Subject Matter Expert | Rikers Island Feasibility Study | New York City, NY. Developed concepts to convert four existing wastewater treatment plants to MBR technology. Capacity of existing plants varied from 80 to 275-mgd (annual average). Also developed concepts for a new, 705-mgd MBR facility to replace those four WWTPs. Results of this study will inform decision making by NYC Environmental Protection relating to replacement or upgrade of existing WWTPs.

MBR Subject Matter Expert | Technical Analysis of Biological and Advanced Water Treatment Processes at the Joint Water Pollution Control Plant | Sanitation Districts of Los Angeles County, CA. Advised Jacobs staff on implementation of large MBR designs. Performed a survey of 14 large, North American MBR facilities. Conducted a literature review focused on wet weather performance and biofouling control of MBR processes.

MBR Subject Matter Expert | Tapia Water Reclamation Facility Membrane Bioreactor Evaluation | Las Virgenes-Triunfo Joint Powers Authority | Malibu, CA. Evaluated conversion of conventional activated sludge to MBR technology. Due to space limitations, developed a concept to integrate an immersed membranes facility into existing rectangular secondary clarifiers. Coordinated with several engineering disciplines to define the concept in sufficient detail for cost estimation.

MBR Subject Matter Expert | Mary’s Creek Water Reclamation Facility | Fort Worth, TX. Advising project team on matters relating to preliminary treatment, biological nutrient removal, process simulation, MBR process configuration, and membrane pre-selection for a 10-mgd (annual average) greenfield WRF that will produce reuse water for nearby customers.

Subcommittee Chair | Membrane Bioreactor Systems Standard (ANSI/AWWA B130). In 2023, oversaw revision of the MBR standard first published in 2013.
