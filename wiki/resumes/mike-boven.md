### Relevant Experience

**Transition Manager | 30- to 40-MGD Reuse Facility, West Basin Municipal Water District | Los Angeles, CA.**
Served as Transition Manager for the operational transition of the West Basin project to Jacobs, coordinating startup deliverables, internal teams, and client communication to ensure a smooth handover of an approximately 30- to 40-MGD system. Maintained compliance with Jacobs' policies and proactively resolved gaps to reduce risk, support operational readiness, and strengthen long-term service reliability.

**Transition Manager | Water and Wastewater Treatment Facilities, Town of Goderich | Ontario, CAN.**
Served as Transition Manager for the transition of Goderich's water and wastewater operations to Jacobs, overseeing onboarding, procurement, startup planning, and implementation of Jacobs' standards across a 3.1-MGD surface water treatment facility, a 2.0-MGD wastewater treatment plant, and associated infrastructure. Also coordinated stakeholder communication and cross-border logistics, including Canadian work permits for specialized personnel, to keep the transition on schedule and minimize disruption.

**Transition Manager | 1.5-MGD Reuse Facility, Santa Monica SWIP | Santa Monica, CA.**
Served as Transition Manager for the handover of Santa Monica's SWIP facility to Jacobs operations, coordinating startup activities, staff onboarding, operating procedures, and regulatory readiness for this advanced water reuse facility designed to recycle 1.0 MGD of municipal wastewater and treat up to 0.5 MGD of stormwater, urban runoff, and brackish groundwater for beneficial reuse. Managed schedules, deliverables, and stakeholder communication while addressing staffing, budget, and site-access challenges to support compliance and operational continuity.
