# Scott Golden
## LABORATORY MANAGER

### Why Scott?
- **Compliance-driven laboratory leadership.** Scott brings direct experience managing compliance sampling, verifying regulatory data, and preparing defensible reports that support permit compliance and protect data integrity. His background in DMR preparation, pretreatment coordination, and laboratory oversight aligns well with OCWUT's need for accurate, audit-ready reporting.
- **Integrated laboratory and process control expertise.** Scott connects laboratory results with plant operations, using analytical data to support process control, operational consistency, biosolids handling, and compliance-focused decision-making. His experience helps ensure laboratory information is used effectively to support reliable performance across the system.
- **QA/QC and reporting discipline.** Scott has maintained QC programs, chain-of-custody practices, internal verification processes, and documented procedures that support accurate, timely, and defensible laboratory performance. He brings the structure needed to support OCWUT's Sampling and Testing QA/QC requirements and long-term accountability.
- **Strong Oklahoma credentials and operational support experience.** Scott holds Oklahoma Class A wastewater and water operator licenses and laboratory certifications, and he has supported startup and early operations on complex utility systems. That combination positions him to lead a laboratory program that supports both regulatory compliance and day-to-day operating performance.

### Summary
Scott will lead OCWUT's laboratory program with a focus on accurate reporting, strong data integrity, and direct support for plant performance and permit compliance. He brings 21 years of water and wastewater operations and laboratory-related experience, including compliance sampling, regulatory reporting, QA/QC practices, and laboratory-driven process control. His background combines Oklahoma operator and laboratory credentials with hands-on experience supporting startup, SOP development, process optimization, and operational decision-making in active utility environments. For OCWUT, Scott will implement a disciplined laboratory program that supports the Sampling and Testing QA/QC Plan, strengthens internal verification, and provides reliable data for process control, biosolids performance, odor management, and regulatory reporting.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the argument that a laboratory manager is an operations role. Each of the four "Why Scott?" bullets pairs a lab discipline (compliance sampling and DMR preparation, analytical data feeding process control, QC programs and chain of custody, state credentials) with the operating consequence, and the summary lists the four things the lab data is actually used for — process control, biosolids performance, odor management, and regulatory reporting. That list is drop-in language for any lab-manager or lab-plan section. Portable scale proof: a utility system serving 150,000 residents, a 4.5-MGD activated sludge plant with an industrial pretreatment program, a 10-MGD surface WTP, and regional technical support to 18 water and wastewater facilities.

Pursuit-specific: the "Sampling and Testing QA/QC Plan" title in bullet three and the summary — replace it with the new client's named plan; the Oklahoma-credential bullet, which only earns its place in an ODEQ pursuit (swap the state and regulator elsewhere and check the bench actually has in-state licenses); and every "For OCWUT" clause. The Duncan entry is the strongest single paragraph for an Oklahoma pursuit because it carries ODEQ compliance, DMRs, and pretreatment in one place.
