# Tanner Pipher, PE
## CIP CONSTRUCTION MANAGER

### Why Tanner?
- **Construction-Phase and Startup Support Experience.** Tanner has supported bidding, construction services, and startup activities for active wastewater and water facility projects, helping move improvements from design into operation with minimal disruption to ongoing performance.
- **Strong Coordination and Field Problem Solving.** Tanner works directly with contractors, engineers, and plant staff to resolve field issues, support implementation, and keep projects aligned with operational, schedule, and compliance needs.
- **Bridges Design, Construction, and Operations.** Tanner has supported submittal reviews, RFIs, inspections, construction coordination, and startup-related activities that help connect design intent with field execution and reliable facility operation.
- **Aligned with OCWUT's CIP Priorities.** Tanner's experience with startup support, active facility construction coordination, and treatment system improvements positions him to support South Canadian commissioning and broader facility upgrades in a practical, low-risk way.

### Summary
Tanner will support OCWUT's capital program as CIP Construction Manager by helping coordinate construction, startup, and operational integration of facility improvements in active wastewater environments. He brings more than 7 years of water and wastewater engineering experience and a strong background in construction services, startup support, mechanical evaluation, and field coordination for treatment plant, lift station, and biosolids-related improvements in Oklahoma. That experience allows him to connect design intent, contractor execution, and plant operating needs in a practical way that supports reliable delivery and minimizes disruption to ongoing performance. For OCWUT, Tanner will help identify risks early, resolve field issues, and support low-risk implementation of capital improvements as South Canadian moves into operation and other facility upgrades advance.

### Relevant Experience
**Project Engineer | City of Bixby | Bixby Water Reclamation Facility and Lift Stations O&M | Bixby, OK.** Tanner supported the initial startup of the 2.8-MGD Bixby Water Reclamation Facility, helping bring a newly constructed SBR facility and associated lift stations into operation. Following startup, he performed targeted analyses related to residuals disposal and mechanical equipment, supporting reliability and operational refinement. This experience reflects the kind of startup coordination and troubleshooting that will support OCWUT as South Canadian moves into operation.

**Process Mechanical Engineer | City of Duncan | Duncan Wastewater Treatment and Reuse O&M | Duncan, OK.** Tanner supported the Duncan chlorination/dechlorination project as part of the bidding and construction services team, serving in a process mechanical engineering role. He helped bridge design intent and field execution during improvements to a 4.5-MGD activated sludge wastewater treatment plant with reuse infrastructure and associated lift station assets. His role supported construction coordination and implementation of treatment system improvements within an active Oklahoma wastewater facility.

**Project Engineer | City of Tulsa | Northside Wastewater Treatment Plant Digester Improvements | Tulsa, OK.** Tanner supported construction-phase delivery of anaerobic digester improvements to address corrosion and hydrogen sulfide impacts. He worked alongside contractors and operations staff during construction to coordinate inspections, support field activities, and resolve issues as they arose. His role helped maintain construction progress and system reliability in an active wastewater facility.

**Project Engineer | City of Tulsa | AB Jewell Water Treatment Plant Clarifier Improvements | Tulsa, OK.** Tanner supported construction services for upgrades to a 120-MGD treatment facility, coordinating with design teams and contractors to deliver improvements while maintaining operational continuity. He facilitated submittal reviews, responded to RFIs, and helped resolve field conflicts, ensuring construction activities remained aligned with design intent and operating needs.

**Project Engineer | Regional Metropolitan Utility Authority | Haikey Creek Wastewater Treatment Plant Biosolids Program | Broken Arrow, OK.** Tanner supported planning and development of a biosolids program to transition the facility to standalone operations. He contributed to evaluation of biosolids treatment alternatives, coordinated technical analysis, and supported stakeholder engagement throughout the planning process. He also helped resolve a solids wasting bottleneck at the plant, which led to reconfiguring the facility's wasting treatment train to improve process control, automation, and treatment flexibility. This work helped inform a $40M capital improvement program and reflects Tanner's ability to connect operational challenges with practical, long-term facility improvements.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the CIP Construction Manager role framing itself — an O&M team member whose whole job is bridging design intent, contractor execution, and plant operating needs "with minimal disruption to ongoing performance." That risk-reduction promise ("identify risks early, resolve field issues, and support low-risk implementation") is the reusable argument for carrying an engineer on an operations org chart, and it works on any pursuit where the client has an active capital program. The Haikey Creek entry is the model for how to end a junior resume: a specific operational problem, the fix, and the capital consequence.

Pursuit-specific: the OCWUT and South Canadian references, and the all-Oklahoma project set — the concentration is the differentiator on an Oklahoma pursuit and a liability elsewhere, so on out-of-state pursuits substitute in-state projects or reframe around the facility types rather than the geography. The PE license state must match the pursuit state or be paired with a reciprocity statement.
