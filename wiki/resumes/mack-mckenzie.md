# Mack Mckenzie
**Chief Plant Operator | WW Grade V, AWT3™**

## Why Mack?
- Chief plant operator/project manager for multiple wastewater facilities in California
- Specific experience managing membrane bioreactor and ultrafiltration/reverse osmosis facilities
- Deep knowledge of federal, state, and local regulatory and reporting requirements
- Extensive experience with capital construction projects and maintenance of treatment facilities

## Professional background
- Total Water/Wastewater Experience: 11 years

## Education
- Associate of Science (AS), Business Administration | Southwestern College
- AS, Real Estate | Southwestern College
- BS, Business | California State University, Channel Islands (in progress)

## Certifications/Licenses
- Wastewater Treatment Plant Operator Grade V
- Water Treatment Operator T2
- AWT3™
- Distribution System Operator D2

## Membership/Affiliations
- Member, California State Water Resources Control Board - Wastewater Needs Assessment Advisory Group (2024 - 2027)

## Summary
Mack is a seasoned water and wastewater treatment professional with over 11 years of experience managing complex treatment facilities. He holds advanced certifications including Wastewater Treatment Plant Operator Grade V, Water Treatment Operator T2, AWT3™, and Distribution System Operator D2.

## Relevant experience

**Project Manager | Soquel Creek Advanced Water Purification, Soquel Creek Water District | Soquel, CA.** Jacobs is leading the long-term operations of Soquel's 1.3-MGD advanced water treatment facility. As Project Manager, Mack is responsible for permit compliance, operations, lab and maintenance supervision, process control changes, staff scheduling, budget, hiring, safety procedures, and creating monthly operational and regulatory reports. Since this is a new facility, Mack is creating the standard operating procedures and sampling schedules for staff to follow.

**Project Manager | Novato Sanitary District WWTP | Novato, CA.** Mack was responsible for the operation, maintenance, and laboratory at the Novato Sanitary District's 7-MGD WWTP. Mack supervised a staff of 11 employees and one intern within four departments: Operations, Maintenance, Laboratory, and Administration. In addition to being the Project Manager, Mack served as Chief Plant Operator (CPO) for the facility and was responsible for producing all regulatory and operating reports. His duties included fostering a safe work environment, meeting contract deliverables, maintaining client satisfaction, presenting at monthly board meetings, supporting regulatory and lab compliance, addressing routine and emergency maintenance issues, capital planning, budget compliance, managing large capital projects, implementing efficiency programs, and developing long-term strategic plans. Mack managed employees operating under a collective bargaining agreement, and helped to develop a new internship program.

**Chief Plant Operator (CPO)/Assistant General Manager | Richmond Water Pollution Control Plant | Richmond, CA.** Mack was responsible for the operation, maintenance, collections system, and laboratory at the Richmond Water Pollution Control Plant, a facility designed for 16-MGD treatment of the City's sewage. He supervised 27 employees within five departments: Operations, Maintenance, Collections, Laboratory, and Administration. Mack's duties included fostering a safe work environment, meeting contract deliverables, maintaining client satisfaction, presenting at monthly board meetings, ensuring regulatory and lab compliance, addressing routine and emergency maintenance issues, capital planning, budget compliance, managing large capital projects, implementing efficiency programs, and developing long-term strategic plans. Mack also trained operations staff in wastewater theory and practical application.

**Maintenance Manager | South Bay International Wastewater Treatment Plant (SBIWTP) | San Diego, CA.** Mack was responsible for maintenance operations of a 25-MGD chemically enhanced primary treatment (CEPT) wastewater treatment plant with secondary clarification at a federally owned facility in San Diego. The International Boundary and Water Commission (IBWC) governs the SBIWTP, which treats sewage on the U.S. side of the Mexican border but generated in Mexico. He supervised and directed scheduled and unscheduled maintenance activities of eight direct reports and managed very large capital improvement projects. In addition to maintenance, Mack trained wastewater operations personnel in operations theory and practical applications.

**CPO | Limoneira WWTP, Limoneira Co. | Santa Paula, CA.** Mack was responsible for the operation and maintenance of the Limoneira WWTP, an extended aeration wastewater treatment plant where he managed two public water drinking water systems. He maintained operation and maintenance of all equipment and processes involved with the activated sludge system, including pumps, motors, controls, blower system, meters, and valving assemblies. He also provided routine and emergency maintenance of lift stations and 20 miles of sewer collection lines and manholes. Mack oversaw the dewatering system, multiple (1 MG) aeration ponds, sand filtration system, and UV disinfection. He created all standard operating procedures (SOPs), operational checklists, inventory distribution, sampling schedules, employee scheduling, and all safety training and prepared all required monthly, quarterly, and annual maintenance and sampling reports to state, federal, and local agencies for all three water systems.

**Lead Operator/Wastewater Maintenance Supervisor | Santa Paula Water Recycling Facility (WRF) | Santa Paula, CA.** Mack operated and maintained the Santa Paula WRF, a 3.4-MGD MBR facility with combined anoxic and aerobic aeration system. He supervised employees maintaining operations of all equipment and processes involved with the activated sludge system and SCADA system. Mack oversaw the Huber gravity belt thickeners, screw press dewatering system, drum type screens, MBR trains, Bray actuators, Ozonia UV disinfection trains, Flygt mixers, carbon media odor silo, Aerisa odor blowers, K-Turbo blowers, flow totalizers, meters, VFDs, PLCs, and HMIs. Mack was the designated Site Safety Coordinator, was first aid/CPR certified, and was part of the Community Emergency Response Team (CERT). He also completed state compliance reporting.

**Wastewater Operator/Collections Supervisor | Fillmore Recycled Water Facility | Fillmore/Santa Paula, CA.** Mack operated and maintained the Fillmore Recycled Water Facility, a 2.4-MGD MBR facility. He supervised employees maintaining operations of all equipment and processes involved with the activated sludge system and SCADA system. Mack oversaw the screw press dewatering system, rake type screens, MBR trains, Bray actuators, Trojan UV disinfection trains, Flygt mixers, odor biofilters, flow totalizers, meters, VFDs, PLCs, and HMIs. He completed line cleaning and CCTV recording of 33 miles of sewer collection systems and created reports for the client. Mack was the facility's designated Site Safety Coordinator, where he was part of the CERT and was first aid/CPR certified. He purchased all equipment and parts required for the CMMS program and managed an ultrafiltration/reverse osmosis plant treating industrial wastewater.
