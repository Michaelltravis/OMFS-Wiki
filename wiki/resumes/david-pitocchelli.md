# David Pitocchelli
## PROJECT MANAGER

### Why David?
- **Experienced Wastewater Operations Leader.** David brings 30 years of related water and wastewater operations experience, exceeding the Schedule 10 minimum for the Project Manager role and including leadership of large, multi-facility systems.
- **Direct Oklahoma City and Multi-Facility Management Experience.** David has direct experience managing the Oklahoma City wastewater system and has led operations for multiple wastewater facilities with peak flows up to 250 MGD and staffs of more than 80 personnel.
- **Strong Compliance, Maintenance, and Performance Focus.** David has restored regulatory compliance, implemented SOPs and training programs, and used CMMS-based maintenance planning to improve reliability, reduce backlog, and strengthen long-term asset performance.
- **Well Suited to Serve as OCWUT's Onsite Representative.** David's experience in utility leadership, workforce management, operational oversight, and stakeholder coordination aligns well with the Project Manager role's responsibility for overall facility management and liaison with the General Manager.

### Summary
David will serve as OCWUT's Project Manager and onsite representative, with overall responsibility for safe, compliant, and reliable operation of the County's wastewater facilities. He has 30 years of water and wastewater operations leadership, including direct experience managing the Oklahoma City wastewater system, multi-facility operations with peak flows up to 250 MGD (combined), and teams of more than 80 staff. David has a proven record of restoring compliance, strengthening maintenance performance through CMMS-driven programs, improving operational reliability, and leading workforce development in complex utility environments. His experience positions him to provide day-one leadership, maintain 24/7 operational continuity, and serve as a responsive liaison to the General Manager or designee while advancing long-term system performance.

### Relevant Experience

**Project Manager | OCWUT | Wastewater Facilities Management | Oklahoma City, OK.** David will leverage his direct experience managing the OCWUT system to deliver day-one stability and performance. He managed four regional wastewater treatment facilities totaling 111 MGD with peak flows up to 250 MGD (combined), leading more than 80 staff. He restored regulatory compliance through targeted process and chemical-feed improvements, implemented a CMMS-driven maintenance program that eliminated deferred maintenance backlog and improved equipment reliability, and developed and enhanced the biosolids production and reuse program. He also led odor-control strategy development and strengthened operational reporting and communication with the client. Since serving in this role, David has continued to support wastewater projects as a senior operations subject matter expert, helping project teams improve compliance, maintenance performance, workforce effectiveness, and day-to-day reliability while mentoring project managers and site leaders across complex utility environments. This direct OCWUT experience gives David a clear picture of the facilities, the people, and the performance expectations and positions him to step into day-one leadership without disruption.

**Wastewater Manager | The Villages | Water, Wastewater, and Public Works O&M | The Villages, FL.** David managed wastewater operations across four treatment plants and associated collection systems serving more than 130,000 residents, with responsibility for daily operational oversight, compliance performance, staffing coordination, maintenance planning, biosolids management, and capital project coordination.

**Project Manager | City of Pasadena | City of Pasadena Wastewater Facilities | Pasadena, TX.** David led operations for two wastewater treatment plants (10 MGD and 14 MGD), improving system performance while positioning the utility for future growth. He strengthened workforce accountability, modernized CMMS and data systems, achieved measurable reductions in energy and chemical costs through process optimization, and supported long-term system consolidation planning.

**Senior Regional Water/Wastewater Manager | Various | Southeast Region.** David provided multi-facility operational leadership across the Southeast region, directing day-to-day operations while reinforcing compliance protocols, safety programs, and workforce training. He supported capital facility upgrades and construction coordination, maintaining compliant operations through active construction periods.

**Wastewater Manager | Forsyth County | Multi-Facility Operations | Suwanee, GA.** David oversaw daily O&M for six wastewater facilities and associated conveyance systems. His responsibilities included maintenance planning, prioritization, inspections, budget development, and performance management across a diverse portfolio of assets.

**Project Manager / Port of Texas City | Port of Texas City Water/Wastewater Systems | Texas City, TX.** David managed operations of a municipal water system and supported wastewater planning, implementing CMMS and data system upgrades and delivering cost savings through energy and chemical optimization.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the resume architecture — a "Why [name]?" bullet block that maps the individual to the RFP's named role responsibilities, a one-paragraph role commitment written in future tense ("David will serve as…"), then reverse-chronological relevant experience, an explicit "Meets Minimum Qualifications" crosswalk to the RFP's stated minimums, and a client reference with live contact details. That structure is reusable for any key-personnel resume.

Pursuit-specific: every client name, the Schedule 10 minimum-qualification mapping, and the "direct experience managing your system" claim, which is the single strongest differentiator here and does not travel. Reuse the prior-experience paragraphs (The Villages, Pasadena, Forsyth County, Port of Texas City) freely; rewrite the OCWUT paragraph for the new client.
