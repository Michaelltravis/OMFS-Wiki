# Resume — Susan Moisio, PE (Wet Weather and Conveyance Director)

**Wet Weather and Conveyance Director – Susan Moisio, PE**

**Education and Licenses**

- MS, Civil Engineering
- BS, Civil Engineering
- Professional Engineer: NY, OH

**Experience Summary**

- 37 years of experience in water; 16 years with Cincinnati's Collections Division responsible for CSO/sanitary sewer overflow (SSO) operation and management.
- Strategic adviser for the London Thames Tideway Tunnel CSO program and critical to success of Miami-Dade Water and Sewer Ocean Outfall Legislation Program and wastewater planning for VCS Denmark.
- Technology Fellow, U.S. Water Alliance's One Water Council; named one of the top 40 influencers and one of 2025's most powerful women in water by Global Water Intelligence.

**Select Projects**

- Design Suitability Oversight Group, Thames Tideway Tunnel Program, London, U.K.
- Sr. Technical Consultant, VCS Integrated Model, Odense, Denmark
- Sr. Technical Consultant, Evansville Water and Sewer Utility Integrated CSO/SSO Overflow Control Planning and Regulatory Negotiations Assistance, Evansville, IN
- Sr. Technical Advisor, Puerto Rico Aqueducts and Sewers Authority, Long-Term Control Plan Controls Development and Negotiations

**Benefit to MMSD:**

Susan brings a utility owner's lens and expertise in conveyance system best practices and support for operations decisions during wet weather events. She'll direct experts in conveyance, sustainability, and wastewater technology to help MMSD innovate and address current and future challenges.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the opening bullet is the model for an owner's-lens claim — not "worked with utilities" but sixteen years inside one large combined-sewer utility's collections division with named responsibility for CSO and SSO operation. Where an evaluator is a public utility staffer, that line is worth more than the international project list beneath it. Third-party recognition is the second device: a fellowship and two named industry listings supply external validation the proposal itself cannot assert, and belong in the experience summary rather than in a bio footnote.

The benefit paragraph does two things worth copying — it puts the person on the operations decision path during wet weather events (an operations role, not an advisory one) and it makes her a conduit to a bench of specialists in conveyance, sustainability and wastewater technology.

Pursuit-specific: the regulatory-negotiation projects (Evansville, Puerto Rico long-term control plan) are the ones to foreground when the client is under a consent decree or negotiating a long-term control plan; the international CSO projects foreground scale and technical depth. State PE registrations should include or be reciprocal to the pursuit state. Recognition claims carry a year and must be re-dated at each reuse.
