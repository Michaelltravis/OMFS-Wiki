### Professional Background

Scott brings leadership and operational management experience across the Northeast U.S. and Canada. He manages portfolios of operations and maintenance projects while maintaining strong relationships with clients, staff, vendors, and subcontractors. Scott focuses on aligning operational strategies with business objectives while supporting process improvements and cost optimization across chemicals, maintenance, utilities, and overtime. He applies advanced analytics to identify operational trends and root causes and supports project transitions and startup activities. Scott's experience includes leading project transitions and supporting operational performance across municipal and industrial facilities.

### Relevant Experience

**Regional Manager/Startup Manager | Southbridge WWTP | Town of Southbridge, MA.**
Led startup and regional oversight for a 5-year O&M contract covering a 3.77-MGD WWTP, 48 miles of collection system, and 11 lift stations, including the successful transition from the previous contractor on February 1, 2025. Directed staffing, system integration, and operational standardization to ensure compliance and continuity of service. Drove performance improvements and cost efficiencies, including enhanced composting processes to support product marketability and revenue potential. Implemented innovative technologies and provided strategic leadership to optimize operations, workforce efficiency, and long-term system performance.

**Startup Manager | Wastewater O&M, City of Waterbury | Waterbury, CT.**
A 10-year agreement to manage, improve and optimize the 27-MGD activated sludge facility, 310-mile sewer collection system and 20 pump stations that experience flows greater than 70 MGD during wet weather. Also assisting with the $25 million phosphorous upgrade that includes tertiary filtration and permanent chemical addition.

**Geographic Manager of Operations | Jacobs | Philadelphia, PA.**
Oversees a portfolio of projects for Jacobs' Operations Management Facilities Services (OMFS) in the Northeast U.S. and Canada. Focuses on maintaining a positive working relationship with all Jacobs staff, clients, vendors, and subcontractors. An expert on contract management and project cost optimization of chemicals, maintenance, utilities, and overtime. Helps clients identify opportunities for cost savings and assists projects with improving process strategies. Uses Advanced Analytics to identify root causes and turnover trends for the U.S. and Canada. Recently managed the successful transition of the Southbridge WWTP to Jacobs. Instrumental in accomplishing transitions (badge flips) at NASA Goddard, Honeywell in Wilmington, Delaware, and the cities of Westerly, Rhode Island, and Southbridge, Massachusetts.
