# Resume — John Loucks-Powell, CRL (Director of Maintenance & Asset Management / MMS Advisor)

**Director of Maintenance & Asset Management/MMS Advisor – John Loucks-Powell, CRL**

**Education and Licenses**

- Associate, Specialized Technology
- Certified Reliability Leader (CRL)
- FEMA Disaster Management for Water and Wastewater Utilities Certification

**Experience Summary**

- 30+ years at Wilmington WWTF as an operator, lead operator, maintenance manager, and capital/O&M advisor. As Maintenance Manager, he increased planned maintenance completion rate to 85%, surpassing the 80% KPI benchmark, reduced emergency repairs by 25%, and achieved zero lost-time safety incidents over 4 consecutive years of major construction and maintenance activity.
- Leads asset management/CMMS programs and predictive maintenance for CSOs/WWTFs to reduce unplanned downtime, speed recovery, and maintain transparency on risk, backlog, and life-cycle cost.
- Former AFSCME member with strong field presence.

**Select Projects**

- O&M Consultant/Capital Projects Lead, City of Wilmington, DE, WWTF O&M
- Maintenance Manager, City of Wilmington, DE, WWTF O&M
- Operator/Storekeeper/Parts Mechanic, City of Wilmington, DE, WWTF O&M

**Benefit to MMSD:**

John will help build a transformational asset management program that drives accountability and transparency and achieves a sustained 85% PM/15% CM maintenance delivery, using his O&M experience from an owner's lens.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: this card is the template for a maintenance leader's resume because every claim is a number with a consequence. The structure — a single career at one large facility, a progression of job titles inside that career, then the metrics achieved in the senior role — is more persuasive on an O&M pursuit than a list of employers. Keep the four metrics together in one bullet; they reinforce each other (planned work up, emergency work down, no lost-time injuries during a heavy construction period). The "Former AFSCME member with strong field presence" line is a deliberate union-credibility signal and is worth carrying wherever the workforce is organized.

The second bullet states the purpose of asset management in client terms — reduce unplanned downtime, speed recovery, maintain transparency on risk, backlog, and life-cycle cost — and is directly reusable as approach language in a maintenance section, not only in a resume.

Pursuit-specific: the 85% PM / 15% CM split is stated here as a sustained delivery commitment. Check the target client's own maintenance KPI, and align or explain the difference rather than importing this ratio unexamined. The Wilmington project list and the AFSCME reference should be refreshed to match the pursuit's labor context.
