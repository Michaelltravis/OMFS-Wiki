# Howard Brewen
**Director of Operations | WW Grade V**

## Why Howard?
- Recognized leader in water/wastewater industry known for his innovative and sustanable [sic] approach to management
- O&M project leader who excels in building strong partnerships and delivering exceptional client service
- Unique blend of private sector entrepreneurship and extensive municipal experience
- Experience at community and state levels bridging interdisciplinary networks including city councils, environmental regulatory agencies, and government officials

## Professional background
- Jacobs Experience: 7 years
- Total Experience: 24 years

## Education
- BS, Animal Science | University of California, Davis

## Certifications/Licenses
- California Wastewater Treatment Operator Grade V
- California Wastewater Collection Systems Operator Grade III

## Summary
Howard has 22 years of water/wastewater operations and management experience. As a senior O&M leader, Howard excels in building solid client relationships based on a solutions-delivery approach so that clients' needs are thoroughly understood and met. His innovative and sustainable approach to management has earned him recognition in the industry. With a strong background in both private sector entrepreneurship and municipal water and wastewater management, Howard's leadership is characterized by a commitment to mentorship, sustainability, and efficiency.

## Relevant experience

**Director | Clovis WWTP/WRF, City of Clovis | Clovis, CA.** The 2.8-MGD Clovis WWTP is a California Title 22 Quality treatment facility with effluent suitable for discharge into the environment. Treatment includes MBR with centrifuges for biosolids processing and a belt filter press (BFP) as back up. In his role as Jacobs' O&M Director of California, Howard is involved in all facets of facility operations from liaising with clients and budgeting, to permitting and certifications.

**Director | Edward C. Little Water Recycling Facility (ECLWRF), West Basin Municipal Water District | El Segundo, CA.** Operation for the 40-MGD ECLWRF has just changed over from the previous operator to Jacobs. Howard's leadership has been critical to this transition process, and he has worked closely with onsite staff to maintain operational continuity throughout the process. Avoiding facility interruptions allowed the ECLWRF to continue to conserve enough recycled water to meet the drinking water needs of up to 80,000 households for a year.

**Director | Woodland Davis Regional Water Treatment Facility (RWTF), Woodland Davis Clean Water Agency | Woodland, CA.** Howard provides leadership for the operational staff of Woodland's three RWTF, which includes a river intake and pump station, 36-inch 5.1-mile raw water pipeline, a 30-MGD WTP, finished water pump station, and a 36-inch, 7.8 mile distribution pipeline. In his role as Director, Howard is involved in all facets of facility operations from liaising with clients and budgeting, to permitting and certifications.

**Director | Twin Oaks Valley WTP, San Diego County Water Authority | San Marcos, CA.** Howard provides senior leadership to the operational staff of this 100-MGD submerged membrane filtration WTP. The plant's features include fine screening, membrane filtration, ozone disinfection, biologically active carbon contactors, chlorine addition, and two 7.5-mg clearwells. As Director, Howard is involved in all facets of facility operations from liaising with clients and budgeting, to permitting and certifications.

**Superintendent | Water Resource Recovery Facility (WRRF), City of San Luis Obispo | San Luis Obispo, CA.** Responsible for the daily O&M of the WRRF, including but not limited to the following: administering all wastewater treatment, reclamation, and effluent disposal activities; work oversight; review and evaluation of facility personnel; compliance with federal, state, and local water biosolids and air quality regulations; management of an annual $3.5M line item budget and an annual $600K to $3M capital improvement project (CIP) budget.

**Wastewater Treatment Operator | WRRF, City of San Luis Obispo | San Luis Obispo, CA.** Howard was responsible for all WRRF processes, including the O&M of bio-filters, activated sludge systems, and disinfection systems using chlorination and dechlorination methods. He conducted basic laboratory practices and principles for compliance with regulatory standards. Additionally, Howard led capital improvement projects (CIP) and special projects, which included planning, coordination, and implementation of upgrades and enhancements to the facility's infrastructure and processes. These projects aimed at improving efficiency, reducing operational costs, and establishing long-term sustainability of the wastewater treatment operations.

**Wastewater Collections Operator | Wastewater Collection Systems and Storm Drains, City of San Luis Obispo | San Luis Obispo, CA.** Howard managed operations, maintenance, and emergency repairs of wastewater collection systems and storm drains for the City of San Luis Obispo. This included routine inspection and cleaning of sewer lines to prevent blockages and overflows, as well as performing preventive maintenance to ensure system reliability. Howard conducted emergency repairs to address urgent issues such as pipe breaks or severe blockages, coordinating with other City departments and contractors as needed. He oversaw the maintenance of storm drain systems to ensure proper drainage and mitigate flooding risks during heavy rainfall. Howard also implemented best practices for safety and regulatory compliance, and maintained detailed records of all maintenance and repair activities to support operational efficiency and regulatory reporting.

**Manager | WRRF Intern Program, City of San Luis Obispo | San Luis Obispo, CA.** Howard established a robust Intern Program for individuals interested in obtaining state certification as a wastewater treatment operator, where they volunteer a minimum of 1,840 hours as Operators in Training (OIT). The program has successfully placed over 90% of its interns in permanent jobs within the industry.

**Principal Lead | WRRF Energy Efficiency Project, City of San Luis Obispo | San Luis Obispo, CA.** Acting as the principal lead, Howard collaborated with Pacific Gas and Electric (PG&E) in a $9.75M Energy Efficiency Project, the first of its kind as a public/private partnership in the state of California. The project included the design and replacement of equipment that was non-compliant or non-efficient from an energy or regulation perspective. The overall goal of the program was the integration of process technology, energy usage, and instrumentation system optimization to reduce overall energy usage at the facility.

**Core Leadership Team | WRRF Upgrade Project, City of San Luis Obispo | San Luis Obispo, CA.** Member of the core management team overseeing a $140M facility upgrade project to deliver the latest in water resource recovery technology, processes, and sustainability as well as incorporation of research sites and an educational learning center. This included not only design decisions but also the integration of new standard operations procedures, operations optimization, new treatment equipment, and the collection of data used in predicting trends allowing operations to be proactive, not reactive. The core management team was responsible for the decision-making process for all aspects of the project.
