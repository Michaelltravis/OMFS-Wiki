# Resume — Rick Warner, PE (Deputy Project Manager / Director of Regional Partnership)

**Deputy Project Manager/Director of Regional Partnership – Rick Warner, PE**

**Education and Licenses**

- MS, Civil Engineering
- BS, Civil Engineering
- Professional Engineer: WI, NV

**Experience Summary**

- 38 years of experience with municipal utility leadership – a former water utility engineering and resource planning manager.
- National leader in public utility planning and regional partnerships with expertise in potable reuse, regulatory frameworks, stakeholder engagement, and integrated water resource management.
- Design of 100+ projects with $750M+ implemented, and multiple $50M+ programs.
- Founder of Nevada Water Innovation Institute, founder of Partnering for Impact, past WEF President, WEF Fellow, chair for NWRI panels, and longtime collaborator with AWWA and EPA.

**Select Projects**

- Strategic Growth Leader – Water, Jacobs, United States
- Founder and President, Warner and Associates, Community Engagement, Regulatory Frameworks, Financing, and Stakeholder Engagement Consulting, Reno, NV
- Engineering and Resource Planning Manager – Water, Washoe County Utility, Capital Improvement Planning and Management of Drinking Water, Wastewater Collection and Treatment, Biosolids Management and Water Recycling Facilities, Washoe County, NV

**Benefit to MMSD:**

Rick relocated to Milwaukee to build a long-lasting partnership with MMSD. He'll lead six support teams (Program Management, HR, Security & Safety, Procurement & Warehouse Management, and Public Outreach) so Kevin can focus on O&M performance. Rick will bring successful strategies for partnership-building to elevate regional collaboration and drive clear communications that advance your 2035 Vision and build stakeholder trust for the long term, using his proven partnership building strategies. He'll also oversee our public outreach, including outreach for odor issues in communities.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the relocation sentence — a named senior leader who has already moved to the client's city — is one of the highest-value commitment devices in the bank, and it opens the benefit paragraph rather than being buried. Equally reusable is the explicit allocation of support functions to the Deputy PM so the PM is free for daily operations; list the functions by name rather than saying "corporate support." The owner's-side career history ("a former water utility engineering and resource planning manager") is the credibility hinge for a partnership argument and should be stated in the first bullet whenever the candidate has it.

Pursuit-specific: the society offices and awards, the state PE registrations, and the named support teams (which must match the pursuit's org chart). Note that the source lists six support teams but names only five; confirm the sixth against the org chart before reuse. The final sentence attaches public outreach and odor complaint response to this role because odor was a live community issue on this pursuit — drop or retarget it elsewhere.
