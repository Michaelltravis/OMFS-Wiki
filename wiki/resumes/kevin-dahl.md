# Kevin Dahl, PE, CRL, CMRT
## REGIONAL DIRECTOR OF OPERATIONS

### Why Kevin?
- Total Experience: 26 years

### Education
- MS, Environmental Engineering, University of Connecticut
- BS, Civil Engineering, University of Connecticut

### Licenses/Registrations
- Professional Engineer: CT
- Certified Reliability Leader (CRL)
- Certified Maintenance Reliability Technician (CMRT)
- Class IV Wastewater Treatment Operator: CT, RI
- NEWEA Class IV Wastewater Collection Systems Operator

### Professional Background

Kevin supports public agencies and industrial clients using his experience in wastewater operations, engineering, and management. His expertise includes project administration, process control analysis, personnel training, budgeting, and cost control. Kevin conducts operability reviews during planning and design to support efficient operations and reduce risks during construction, commissioning, and startup. As Regional Director of Operations, he oversees municipal operations contracts in the region including safety, compliance, financial performance, and maintenance. Kevin also supports new municipal operations pursuits, capital planning activities, and development of client relationships.

### Relevant Experience

**Project Manager | Norwalk WWTP, City of Norwalk | Norwalk, CT.**
Manages the overall O&M of the Norwalk WWTP and sanitary sewer (collection) system. Prepares and is responsible for meeting annual budgets. Tracks and improves financial and contract performance including generating new work, and manages a staff of 25 associates. Responsible for the facility meeting federal, state and local regulatory performance limits. Serves as a liaison between the City of Norwalk, regulatory agencies, and Jacobs.

**Transition and Regional Manager | Wastewater O&M | City of Waterbury | Waterbury, CT.**
A 10-year agreement to manage, improve and optimize the 27-MGD activated sludge facility, 310-mile sewer collection system and 20 pump stations that experience flows greater than 70 MGD during wet weather. Also assisting with the $25 million phosphorous upgrade that includes tertiary filtration and permanent chemical addition.

**Project Manager | West Haven WWTP, City of West Haven | West Haven, CT.**
Oversaw O&M of full-service contract, including $40 million upgrade to the WWTP with City and selected contractors to include IFAS technology for enhanced nutrient removal including consulting on operations and best management practices. The 15-year contract included operation of a 12.5-MGD municipal WWTP including approximately 140 miles of sanitary sewer. The system included 13 sewage pump stations.
