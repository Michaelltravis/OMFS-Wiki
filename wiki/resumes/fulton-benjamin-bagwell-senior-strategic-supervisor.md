Plant Superintendent | Yellow River WRF | Gwinnett County DWR | Lawrenceville, GA. Managed the 24/7 operation of Gwinnett’s Yellow River WRF (22-mgd) for 26 years. Duties included supervising 25 employees, operations, and maintenance. Responsible for preparing and maintaining a $6 million annual operation’s budget. Prepared monthly DMR to State and ensured that NPDES was consistently met. Trained the operations’ staff and conducted facility tours. Served as the main operations’ contact during the $240 million plant improvement project that completely replaced an existing 12-mgd Activated Sludge Plant with a 22-mgd Advanced MBR Facility. Worked with engineers, contractors, and staff in completing and startup of projects. The membrane is over 14 years old and still performing as new.

Wastewater Manager | Crooked Creek WRF, Gwinnett County DWR | Lawrenceville, GA. Frontline supervision for the 24/7 operation of the Crooked Creek WRF. Staff size ranged from 20 to 30 employees. Completed safety and equipment inspections and was on-call year-round to be available to staff for process decisions and handle high flows, inclement weather and process upsets.
