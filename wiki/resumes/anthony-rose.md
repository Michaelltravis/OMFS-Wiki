Supported the startup and execution of a 5-year O&M contract for a 3.77-MGD WWTP, 48 miles of collection system, and 11 lift stations, including transition from the previous contractor on February 1, 2025. **Operations Technician | Wastewater Treatment | Fall River, MA.**

Collected samples and conducted laboratory testing to ensure compliance with water quality standards.
