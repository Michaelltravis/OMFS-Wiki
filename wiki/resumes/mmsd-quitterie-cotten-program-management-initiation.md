**Benefit to MMSD:**

Quitterie's led our procurement phase with MMSD and for continuity, she'll build on relationships with MMSD to help assure smooth chartering and transition and help plan and develop long-term solutions.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the continuity claim. Most proposal teams disappear at award; naming the person who led the procurement phase as a continuing member of the delivery team answers an unspoken evaluator question about whether the people in the room will be the people on site. The sentence pattern is short and should stay short — led the procurement phase, will build on the relationships already formed, carries them through chartering and transition into long-term planning. Pair it with a broad technical summary so the person reads as a practitioner rather than a pursuit manager.

Pursuit-specific: the capital-delivery figure and its geography ("utilities across the western United States deliver more than $1B in capital improvements") and the Oregon and Washington project list, which should be swapped for regionally comparable work when the pursuit is elsewhere. The PE registration state should match or be reciprocal to the pursuit state where the role signs or seals anything.

Related blocks: [Kelly Irving, Transition Manager](mmsd-kelly-irving-transition-manager.md) owns mobilization mechanics while this role owns relationship continuity; [Keoki Sears](mmsd-keoki-sears-program-management-advisor.md) owns the governance framework. Use all three only where the client has a large, phased transition; on smaller pursuits keep the transition manager and fold this content into the PM card.
