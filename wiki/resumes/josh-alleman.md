# Josh Alleman
## QA/QC MANAGER

### Why Josh?
- **Oklahoma Roots and Direct ODEQ Experience.** An Oklahoma native, Josh brings firsthand knowledge of Oklahoma's regulatory environment through direct ODEQ coordination on discharge compliance, understanding the standards OCWUT operates under from years of working within them.
- **Demonstrated Contract Delivery Performance and Financial Oversight.** Josh has a track record of keeping contracts on track and finances accountable, negotiating a 10-year plus 5-year contract renewal with 3 years still remaining, conducting annual budget reviews, driving CIP decisions through predictive technology data, and reviewing DMRs and biosolids reports before submission to keep reporting accurate and defensible.
- **Proven QA/QC Oversight Across All Four Pillars.** Josh has delivered the full QA/QC Manager scope in practice: CMMS-driven maintenance audits, budget monitoring and contract negotiation, NPDES permit formation, DMR review, IPP management, field sampling, and CIP and corrective action tracking.
- **Independent Compliance Perspective.** His background owning his own environmental contracting firm gives Josh the objective, independent mindset the QA/QC Manager role requires, identifying performance gaps before they become compliance issues and giving OCWUT a reliable first line of assurance on program performance.

### Summary
Josh will serve as QA/QC Manager, reporting directly to the Project Manager and providing first-level audit and assurance support to the project management team and OCWUT across financial performance, maintenance activities, operations, and environmental compliance. As a former Oklahoma City hazmat crew volunteer with more than 20 years of water and wastewater experience, Josh brings direct ODEQ coordination, firsthand familiarity with OCWUT's North Canadian treatment plant through his industrial pretreatment (IPP) compliance work, and a background spanning CMMS-driven maintenance oversight, budget monitoring, contract negotiation, DMR and biosolids reporting, and environmental sampling and data validation. He will work closely with the team to track metrics, identify issues early, and support OCWUT with data reporting and analysis beyond standard contract reporting requirements.
