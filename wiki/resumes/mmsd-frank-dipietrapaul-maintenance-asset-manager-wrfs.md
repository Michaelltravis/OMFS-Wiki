# Resume — Frank Dipietrapaul (Maintenance & Asset Manager, Water Reclamation Facilities)

**Maintenance & Asset Manager (WRFs) – Frank Dipietrapaul**

**Education and Licenses**

- Millwright Apprenticeship, Northern California Joint Apprenticeship Training Center
- Level 1 Operator

**Experience Summary**

- 25+ years of experience in wastewater facility maintenance and management; former Division Manager for San Francisco Public Utilities Commission (SFPUC).
- Oversaw long-range maintenance, construction, safety, and budgets and has managed various WWTF O&M teams.
- Managed $50M+ in capital/major maintenance; supervised approximately 50 union and salaried staff.

**Select Projects**

- Director of Reliability and Maintenance Group for O&M, Jacobs, Pacific Northwest
- Division Manager, San Francisco Public Utilities Commission Wastewater Enterprise, San Francisco, CA
- Project Manager, City of Vancouver, WA, WWTF O&M
- Project Manager, City of Gresham, OR WWTF O&M

**Benefit to MMSD:**

Through his experience, Frank will keep treatment systems running safely and efficiently. He'll focus on delivering daily PM milestones to support long-term reliability.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the career arc is the argument — a millwright apprenticeship, then operator certification, then supervision of roughly 50 union and salaried staff and $50M+ of capital and major maintenance at a major municipal utility. On a pursuit where the client's maintenance workforce is organized and skeptical of an incoming contractor, a maintenance manager who came off the tools is a credibility asset, and the apprenticeship line should be kept at the top of the credentials rather than buried. The benefit paragraph is deliberately plain and operational — keep systems running safely and efficiently, deliver daily PM milestones — which is the right register for a site-level role sitting beneath a program-level director.

Pursuit-specific: the public-agency employer (SFPUC) and the Pacific Northwest O&M project list, which should be swapped for facilities comparable in size and process to the pursuit. "Level 1 Operator" should be restated as the equivalent certification in the pursuit state, with reciprocity status if the certification is not yet transferred.

Related blocks: [John Loucks-Powell](mmsd-john-loucks-powell-director-maintenance-asset-management.md) is the program-level director this role reports into and carries the quantified maintenance outcomes; [Monty Sedlak](mmsd-monty-sedlak-maintenance-asset-manager-conveyance.md) is the parallel conveyance-side role. Using all three shows a maintenance organization rather than a single maintenance manager, which is the point on a multi-facility pursuit.
