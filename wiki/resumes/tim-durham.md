# Tim Durham
## OPERATIONS MANAGER — NORTH CANADIAN/WITCHER

### Why Tim?
- **Deep wastewater operations and utility leadership experience.** Tim brings 31 years of experience leading water and wastewater operations with direct responsibility for plant performance, staff leadership, regulatory compliance, and system reliability.
- **Strong fit for maintenance-intensive wastewater and solids operations.** He has managed wastewater treatment and collection systems and led upgrades involving oxidation ditch improvements, clarifier rehabilitation, and biosolids processing equipment, which aligns well with OCWUT's operational and residuals-management needs.
- **Proven manager of teams, budgets, and capital improvements.** Tim has overseen large teams, major budgets, and significant infrastructure improvements. He brings the planning discipline and operational judgment needed to support long-term, uninterrupted service.
- **Experienced public-sector partner focused on dependable service.** His background as a utility and municipal leader demonstrates an ability to work closely with owners, support community expectations, and keep essential services operating safely and effectively.

### Summary
Tim will serve as Operations Manager for the North Canadian and Witcher facilities, helping lead safe, compliant, and reliable wastewater operations across a complex public utility environment. He brings experience managing water and wastewater treatment systems, utility staff, budgets, capital programs, and day-to-day operations. His background includes leadership of wastewater treatment plants and collection systems, biosolids process improvements, regulatory coordination, asset management, and planning for system growth and resilience. Tim has led multidisciplinary teams, directed major rehabilitation and expansion efforts, and managed utility operations in both municipal and contract-service environments. He's well equipped to support OCWUT's goals for dependable operations, strong client partnership, disciplined maintenance and capital coordination, and continuous service at the North Canadian/Witcher location.

### Relevant Experience

**Project Director | City of Jackson | O.B. Curtis & Fewell WTPs O&M | Jackson, MS.** Tim oversees O&M for two drinking water plants with a combined capacity of 70 MGD, along with wells and elevated storage tanks, within a high-profile emergency operating environment. He manages a $31M budget and leads a team of more than 60 staff while helping maintain compliant, uninterrupted service under difficult system conditions. Although water-focused, this experience demonstrates Tim's strength in high-visibility utility leadership, large-team supervision, budget accountability, and operational resiliency—capabilities directly relevant to supporting OCWUT's service expectations at North Canadian/Witcher.

**Director | Aiken County Public Service Authority | Horse Creek WWTP | Aiken, SC.** Tim managed a 20-MGD wastewater treatment plant and collection system, later expanded to 26 MGD, with responsibility for operations, planning, and improvement of critical treatment infrastructure. He coordinated design and upgrade efforts including conversion of lagoon systems to flow equalization basins, improvements to the Ovivo oxidation ditch, rehabilitation of secondary clarifiers, and biosolids process upgrades (i.e., belt presses, thickening, and dryer processes). Tim led utility planning including a rate study and sewer system mapping to support capital improvements, operational reliability, and future growth. This experience closely aligns with OCWUT's need for disciplined wastewater operations, maintenance coordination, and long-range asset planning.

**Water Resources Director | Oconee County Water Resources | Calls Creek WWTP, Distribution and Collection System | Oconee, GA.** Tim led major expansion and improvement for the Calls Creek WWTP, first increasing capacity from 0.67 MGD to 1.5 MGD through PDB delivery and then advancing a further expansion to 3 MGD under CMAR delivery. Tim oversaw utility operations across treatment, distribution, and collection functions, supporting coordinated service delivery and long-term capacity planning. Under his leadership, the distribution and collection crews earned GAWP Gold Awards, reflecting strong operational standards and team performance. This experience demonstrates the combination of plant leadership, system integration, and continuous-improvement mindset needed for the North Canadian/Witcher role.

**Assistant City Manager | City of Clarkesville | Clarkesville, GA.** Tim oversaw planning, budgeting, and operational management for eight departments, including water treatment, wastewater treatment, distribution, collection, streets, sanitation, parks, and pool operations. He directed department heads, full-time staff, crew leaders, and seasonal personnel while managing water and sewer system projects, plant upgrades, and broader public works initiatives. Tim secured and administered grants and funding, supported construction delivery, and helped implement utility programs. This highlights his ability to manage complex public utility operations, coordinate diverse teams, and align day-to-day operations with community and client expectations.
