# Christen Wood
**Operator | WW Operator Grade V, AWT3™**

## Why Christen?
- 17 years of experience in the startup and operations of water and wastewater treatment plants, with a focus on cultivating sustainability practices with the front-line workforce
- Expertise in wastewater treatment startup and troubleshooting, laboratory sampling and analysis, workforce development training, and public outreach
- Proven experience in the startup of advanced water recycling processes such as RO, UV-AOP, and ozone

## Professional background
- Jacobs Experience: 3 years
- Total Experience: 17 years

## Education
- MBA, Organizational Leadership | University of Findlay
- BS, Life Science Biology | University of Findlay

## Certifications/Licenses
- AWT3™
- Wastewater Treatment Plant Operator Grade V (exam passed, license not yet issued)
- Wastewater Treatment Professional Operator, Class IV, Ohio Environmental Protection Agency
- Professional Operator, Wastewater Treatment Class IV, Association of Board of Certification (ABC)

## Summary
Christen is a highly passionate and experienced leader within the water and wastewater industry specializing in startups, workforce development, and process optimization. Since 2008, she has been working to improve the quality of water treatment operations throughout the country in laboratory, operations, and management roles.

## Relevant experience

**Startup and Transition Operator | Soquel Creek Advanced Water Purification Facility, Soquel Creek Water District | Santa Cruz, CA.** Treatment process included microfilters, RO, ozone, UV-AOP, and chemical addition. Christen reviews control narratives and other design documents to develop operations guides, SOPs, round sheets, and other front-line job aids. She performs 24/7 startup operations, including state-required performance testing of all unit processes. Christen performs extensive troubleshooting of instrumentation, provides guidance for sampling and analysis, and assists in implementing CMMS software and documenting startup conditions.

**Wastewater Treatment Operator | Southerly Renewable Energy Facility Treatment Upgrades, Northeast Ohio Regional Sewer District | Cleveland, OH.** A $170M upgrade to biosolids processing, including fluidized bed incineration, this project included nine dewatering centrifuges, six piston pumps, and grease processing upgrades. Christen's specific responsibilities included: startup and troubleshooting of individual equipment and optimization of overall process; recommending process changes for optimized operations; collection and analysis of samples for process control; helping with integration of new systems while operating existing processes; and working with operators to think creatively about continuing operations in the face of equipment issues, process upsets, and increasingly stringent incineration requirements.

**Startup Operator | Wapakoneta Water Treatment Facility (WTF), City of Wapakoneta | Wapakoneta, OH.** Christen initiated startup of the Wapakoneta WTF's new lime softening process, provided feedback to vendors on operability concerns, and developed routine operations procedures. She trained operators and management on new processes, sampling, troubleshooting, and required lab analyses and developed a detailed Operations Guide as an alternative to traditional operations and maintenance manuals. Christen also provided checklists and procedures that are prioritized to typical operations and troubleshooting.

**Plan Developer | Cyanide Preservation Study, City of Red Bluff | Red Bluff, CA.** Christen developed a method and SOP for cyanide preservation and hold-time study. She worked with regulators and client to achieve cost-effective alternatives to new NPDES permit limits.

**Process Control Operator | Woonsocket Wastewater Treatment Facility, City of Woonsocket | Woonsocket, CT.** Christen analyzed operational staffing issues, provided feedback, training, and process control recommendations for the Woonsocket facility. She developed SWOT analysis for each of three shifts, edited existing Unit Process Control Plans (UPCP), and developed new units for aeration blowers and all chemical feed systems. She developed a comprehensive operator round sheet to utilize for future training and documentation. She also provided solids contingency planning and high flow event documentation and training.

**Operator | Staff Augmentation, Alexandria Renew | Alexandria, VA.** Christen provided staff augmentation during retirement and COVID-19 absences. Christen's responsibilities included: serving as part of the operations team providing process control support, troubleshooting, and monitoring; analyzing routine process control lab samples; assisting operators with diurnal and wet weather process adjustments; using her knowledge of wastewater processes to assist operators; encouraging collaborative learning to deepen understanding of the facility and its biological, chemical, and physical impacts of the treatment process; and providing examples of laboratory analysis and routine calculations done within the plant to demonstrate formulas and conversions used on operator certification examinations.

**Superintendent | Upper Tuscarawas RBC Replacement and Wastewater Treatment Upgrades, Summit County Department of Sanitary Sewer Services | Akron, OH.** For this $19M upgrade to existing wastewater treatment facility, changing biological treatment system from rotating biological contactors (RBCs) to biological nutrient removal, Christen oversaw construction of the facility while operating the existing plant. She suggested changes to allow fully independent operation of the two activated sludge trains and ensured 24/7 operations during controls programming downtime, operating the plant fully by hand. She organized sampling schedules and built spreadsheets for tracking performance throughout the facility. She troubleshot issues with biological nutrient removal, recommended operational changes, and documented success. She communicated successes and challenges to upper management and provided wastewater, process, and troubleshooting training to O&M staff on operation of the biological nutrient facility. During and after plant startup, she provided hands-on, on-the-job training. Under Christen's operations leadership, the facility drastically improved its water quality and NPDES permit compliance.

**Construction Manager and Startup Operator | Industrial Facility | Marinette, WI.** Christen provided technical support for a new industrial groundwater remediation facility, with emphasis on removal of arsenic. She oversaw construction and provided feedback on the operability of systems and equipment, and she provided laboratory equipment procurement and development of targets and methods.

**Contributor | Wastewater Level 4 Training Modules.** Christen developed PowerPoint modules for training high-level wastewater operations. Modules included MBR, Advanced Phosphorus Removal, Advanced UV Operations and Maintenance, Advanced Nitrification, and Advanced Foaming. She utilized a diverse network of operators to assist in learning their biggest challenges and converted their solutions into training guidelines, offering fictional case studies to improve student engagement.

**Operator | Prescott Valley Staff Augmentation | Prescott Valley, AZ.** Christen supplemented Prescott Valley's staffing during a transition in lead operator staffing and staff shortages. She served as interim laboratory operator, completing daily process control and reportable data. She coordinated sampling and analysis between multiple providers and assisted in improving the efficiency of existing standard operating procedures. This led to a reduction in materials, time, and training required for these processes. Christen assisted the existing staff in understanding laboratory testing requirements and their application to the wastewater process. She organized the laboratory area to remove expired and dangerous chemicals, optimize equipment efficiency, and improve workflow.
