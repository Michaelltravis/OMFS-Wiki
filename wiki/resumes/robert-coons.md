# Robert Earl Coons
## I&C TECHNICIAN

### Why Robert?
- **Hands-On I&C Field Experience Across Treatment Plants and Lift Stations.** Robert brings 17 years of field experience maintaining, troubleshooting, and calibrating process instrumentation, sensors, PLCs, and SCADA connected control systems across wastewater treatment facilities and lift station networks, exceeding the Schedule 10 requirements for OCWUT's I&C Technician.
- **Startup, Commissioning, and Operational Readiness Experience.** Robert has supported startup and commissioning at both new construction and active treatment environments, helping bring control systems, instrumentation, and electrical infrastructure online reliably, which directly supports OCWUT's South Canadian WWTP as it comes online.
- **Electrical Depth that Supports I&C Work.** As a journeyman electrician, he brings hands-on expertise with motors, generators, transfer switches, VFDs, conduit, and testing equipment, the electrical foundation that keeps instrumentation and control systems performing in operating facilities.
- **Owner Collaboration and Cybersecurity Compliance Awareness.** Robert has worked directly with city personnel on IT/OT field needs across multiple projects, coordinating system changes, shutdowns, and communications network support in environments where owner compliance and coordination are non-negotiable.

### Summary
Robert will support OCWUT's facilities as an I&C technician, maintaining and troubleshooting the I&C and electrical systems that operators depend on for safe and reliable daily operations. He brings 17 years of hands-on field experience across treatment plants, lift stations, and utility environments, including maintaining and calibrating process instrumentation and sensors, PLC maintenance and repair, SCADA connected field support, and communications network troubleshooting. Robert has worked directly with city personnel on IT/OT field coordination and brings the cybersecurity compliance awareness OCWUT's internal systems require. His journeyman electrician background supports both instrumentation needs and broader system reliability. For OCWUT, Robert will keep field systems responsive, support startup readiness at South Canadian WWTP, and reduce downtime across plant and pump station assets.

### Relevant Experience

**Regional Mechanic | City of Bixby | Water Reclamation Facility and Lift Stations | Bixby, OK.** Robert supported startup, transition, and ongoing O&M activities at the 2.8-MGD facility and its 18 SCADA-monitored lift stations. Working across both the treatment plant and the collection system, Robert maintained, troubleshot, and calibrated process instrumentation and sensors throughout the lift station network, with each station equipped with a PLC he maintained and repaired. At the multifacility plant, he diagnosed and resolved electrical faults across the SCADA connected radio communication network, responding to power-related outages to restore connectivity and keep remote monitoring fully operational. He coordinated with the city's on-site representative, supporting IT/OT field needs and informing city personnel of system status. His electrical expertise was central to keeping this new facility reliable under active construction, including managing power surges from the local utility that caused recurring equipment problems across the plant and lift stations.

**Maintenance Specialist | City of Houston | Northeast Water Purification Plant | Houston, TX.** Robert supported ongoing maintenance at the 320-MGD multi-facility plant. He was the primary field technician when systems weren't performing and diagnosed faults by reviewing single line drawings, isolating individual components and working with manufacturers to resolve issues. He maintained and repaired PLCs, monitored equipment to calibrate process instrumentation and sensors, and handled low-voltage electrical field work to keep SCADA signals reading accurately back at the control panels. He worked with city personnel and engineering firms on warranty and field resolution work.

**Regional Maintenance Technician | City of Clovis Wastewater Treatment Plant | Clovis, CA.** Robert commissioned and maintained all mechanical and electrical systems on a brand-new centrifuge for this multi-facility new plant startup. He performed instrumentation calibration and sensor commissioning, installed a new PLC, set up the SCADA system, and coordinated with multiple trade contractors to integrate new systems across sub-plants.

**Project Manager | General Contractor | Private Electrical Contractor | CA and TX.** Robert managed up to 50 employees and built broad across commercial electrical construction and maintenance, including motors, generators, transfer switches, PLCs, and networked energy management systems. He coordinated across multiple trade contractors, managed city inspectors, and routinely handled network and router connectivity.

**Regional Maintenance Support | City of San Marcos | WWTP O&M | San Marcos, TX.** Robert provided regional maintenance support across the city's multi-facility plant wastewater system, including the remote Little River plant, maintaining and troubleshooting electrical systems, PLCs, SCADA equipped lift stations, and process instrumentation. He coordinated directly with city personnel before any system shutdowns or startups.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the whole shape of this resume — four "Why Robert?" bullets that each translate a craft skill into an owner benefit, then a summary that closes on downtime and startup readiness rather than on tools. The four bullet headings (field I&C experience across plants and lift stations; startup/commissioning and operational readiness; electrical depth supporting I&C; owner collaboration and cybersecurity awareness) are a reusable frame for any I&C technician, maintenance technician, or electrician resume. Individual experience paragraphs carry portable proof: 2.8-MGD facility with 18 SCADA-monitored lift stations each with its own PLC, a 320-MGD purification plant, centrifuge commissioning with new PLC and SCADA setup, and up to 50 employees managed in electrical construction.

Pursuit-specific: the Schedule 10 reference in bullet one, the South Canadian WWTP startup hook in bullet two and the summary, and every "For OCWUT" clause. The cybersecurity/IT-OT bullet is worth keeping wherever the client owns its own network and requires coordination before shutdowns; drop it where the client has no OT security posture to speak of. Note the small source infelicity in the electrical-contractor entry ("built broad across commercial electrical construction") — repair the sentence before reuse.
