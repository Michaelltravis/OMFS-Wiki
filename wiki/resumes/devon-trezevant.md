# Devon Trezevant
## Operations (& Compliance) Manager

### Professional background
- Jacobs Experience: 2 years

- Class I Wastewater Operator, Georgia; Class II Drinking Water Operator, Georgia
- Transformational Leadership Training; Emotional Intelligence (EQ) Leadership Training; GE MBR Training; Membrane Treatment Technologies; Drinking Water Treatment.

### Why Devon?
- Committed emphasis on achieving significant MBR improvements in operational efficiency and regulatory compliance, delivering exceptional results through a proactive and results-driven approach.
- Extensive experience leading multiple O&M projects throughout Georgia, including Fulton County, providing reliable and high-quality service through strong leadership, effective communication, and a collaborative team environment.
- Proven ability to drive strategic innovation, leveraging emerging technologies and sustainable practices to optimize wastewater operations while exceeding performance benchmarks and anticipating future regulatory needs.

Devon is a highly motivated and seasoned Water and Wastewater State Director with over a decade of proven success in leading dynamic projects across the southern United States. He excels in profit and loss management, permit compliance, health and safety protocols, and process control. Devon consistently delivers exceptional results while fostering positive relationships with clients and stakeholders. With a strong focus on operational efficiency, regulatory compliance, and team collaboration, Devon is dedicated to driving excellence and achieving success in challenging O&M projects.

### Responsibilities
Devon is an O&M Consultant Professional, qualified to serve as the Operations Manager for the County. He excels in guiding MBR plant operations and maintenance, supporting client and business development, implementing training programs, and building strong staff relationships. Devon evaluates treatment plant processes, analyzes operational procedures, and advises the Project Manager. He ensures client satisfaction, maintains accurate records, prepares reports, and keeps data updated.

Devon aims to keep his technical skills sharp and stay informed on field developments. He communicates effectively, assists in preparing O&M manuals, provides consulting services, reviews cost and staffing estimates, conducts performance evaluations, and participates in proposal processes. Active in contractor organizations, Devon offers technical and management assistance. He reviews plant records, controls sludge systems, optimizes energy use, administers right-to-know programs, monitors chemical usage, evaluates polymers, and manages facility maintenance.
