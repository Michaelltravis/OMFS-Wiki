# Amy Dembinski, CRL
## ASSET MANAGER

### Why Amy?
- **Brings strong wastewater asset management experience across multi-site O&M programs.** Amy has supported wastewater treatment plants, lift stations, and systemwide maintenance programs in Oklahoma, Mississippi, Washington, Connecticut, and Texas, giving her directly relevant experience for OCWUT's multi-facility wastewater portfolio.
- **Turns asset and maintenance data into practical decisions.** Amy supports asset inventories, PM/PdM/RCM task development, KPI tracking, cost and labor reporting, life-cycle planning, and NexGen EAM implementation that improve maintenance efficiency, asset visibility, and repair and replacement decisions.
- **Supports condition, risk, and capital planning efforts.** Amy works with condition-assessment and maintenance data in the CMMS to support risk-based prioritization, capital-planning discussions, and long-term asset stewardship across wastewater systems and pump stations.
- **Brings transition-ready experience that supports compliance and reporting discipline from day one.** She has supported startup and data-migration efforts across multiple Jacobs projects, helping teams establish usable asset data, train users, track contract deliverables, and maintain documentation that supports audit readiness and stronger maintenance reporting.

**YEARS OF EXPERIENCE** Jacobs: 18 Total: 18

### Education
- MS, Computer Information Systems, Boston University
- BS, Computer Information Systems, University of Houston
- Graduate Certificate, Data Science with SQL and Tableau, Cornell University

### Certifications/Licenses
- Certified Reliability Leader (CRL)

### Summary
As Asset Manager for the OCWUT program, Amy will help the Project Manager and site teams strengthen asset visibility, maintenance planning, and long-term reliability across the wastewater facilities, Witcher Pump Station, and solids management program. She brings 18 years of experience in water and wastewater asset management, CMMS administration, maintenance-program development, KPI reporting, transition implementation, and EAM best-practice application. Her background includes multi-site wastewater systems, pump stations, industrial pretreatment support, startup and transition asset-data setup, and development of preventive, predictive, and reliability-centered maintenance programs. At OCWUT, she will support asset inventory, maintenance planning, condition and risk data integration, capital-planning decision support, and reporting that connects day-to-day maintenance work to reliability, compliance support, and life-cycle stewardship. She will work directly with project managers, site teams, and client representatives to align asset data, maintenance priorities, EAM configuration, and capital recommendations with program goals and regulatory requirements.

**Asset Manager | City of Bixby | Bixby Water Reclamation Facility and Lift Stations | Bixby, OK.** Amy supported asset management for this 2.8-MGD water reclamation facility and 18 lift stations, which Jacobs operates 24/7 under a full O&M contract that began before final facility acceptance. Amy's role covered asset inventory, PM/PdM/RCM task development, condition-assessment and risk data coordination, KPI and cost tracking, capital-planning decision support, life cycle planning, and maintenance documentation, all within an active Oklahoma DEQ regulatory environment. Her work supported regulatory audit readiness and helped the team maintain the consistent records needed for compliance reporting, coordinating directly with City staff and project leadership to align asset priorities with program goals.

**Asset Manager | JXN Water | Wastewater System O&M | Jackson, MS.** Amy supported asset management activities for this project. She supported a comprehensive condition assessment and strategic improvement plan focused on restoring system reliability and preventing bypasses and integrated broader program management and regulatory support. Amy provided CMMS data management, asset inventory, condition-assessment data integration, KPI reporting, and maintenance documentation that helped establish a data-driven baseline for risk-informed maintenance planning, capital priority development, and long-term reliability planning.

**Asset Manager | City of Vancouver | Westside and Marine Park WWTPs O&M/DB | Vancouver, WA.** Amy supported asset management and CMMS administration for this multi-facility wastewater program. Amy provided asset registry updates, maintenance planning for PM/PdM/RCM activities, KPI and cost tracking, life cycle planning, and CMMS-based reporting that helped the team prioritize repairs, replacements, and capital needs while improving maintenance.

**Asset Manager | City of Waterbury | Waterbury Water and Wastewater System O&M | Waterbury, CT.** Amy supported asset management, including CMMS administration, asset inventory, PM/PdM planning, KPI and cost tracking, condition assessment data integration, and contract deliverable tracking that supported audit readiness, maintenance planning, rehabilitation and replacement decisions, and long-term capital investment planning.

**Asset Manager | City of San Marcos | San Marcos Wastewater Treatment Plant O&M | San Marcos, TX.** Amy supported CMMS administration, asset inventory and maintenance planning, monthly KPI and cost reporting, and life cycle and capital planning inputs that helped the team track performance, support reliability improvements, and document work consistently.
