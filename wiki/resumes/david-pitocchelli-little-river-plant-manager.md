- 40-Hour Hazwoper Training; 8-Hour Incident Command Training; First Aid and CPR Certified; FWRA Wastewater Fundamentals; Practical Personal Management; Wastewater Disinfection; DDA Georgia Six Hour Defensive Driving Improvement; GWRA Wastewater Collection Systems; GWRA Advanced Math; GWRA Membrane Technology.

### Why David?
- Proven experience managing numerous wastewater facilities ranging from 4-mgd to 250-mgd, ensuring efficient and compliant operations.
- Skilled at fostering interpersonal relationships to fortify business objectives and create high-yield workforce, enhancing client satisfaction and team performance.
- Strengthened best management practices for operational procedures, maintenance activities, and capital improvements by revising numerous plans, delivering cost effective and high-quality improvements.

David is a results-driven professional with over 30 years of experience in the utility operations industry. He has a proven track record of managing daily O&M for large wastewater facilities, restoring compliance, reducing work order backlogs, and enhancing workforce productivity. David excels in leading teams, implementing proactive maintenance practices, and improving operational reliability, consistently delivering cost savings and high-quality results for clients. His strategic approach and dedication to excellence make him a valuable asset in driving projects to successful completion and achieving client satisfaction.

### Responsibilities
Drawing on his extensive experience serving as Project Manager for various wastewater facilities, David is ready and excited to serve as the Little River Plant Manager for the County. David’s experience includes managing his staff by establishing workloads, prioritizing work assignments, evaluating employee performance, interpreting and enforcing policies and procedures, and resolving staff conflicts.

Serving as a liaison to the general public, David ensures positive community relationships, and responds to inquiries from the public and media. His role also involves developing and monitoring the budget, approving purchases and procurements, analyzing wastewater sample reports, and directing procedural and operational changes. Furthermore, David oversees the maintenance of electrical, instrumentation, and mechanical equipment, determining effective solutions for repair or replacement. He provides instruction to staff on policies, procedures, and regulations governing treatment plant operations. David also prepares bid specifications for new collections and services, oversees construction, prepares statistical reports, and reviews engineering designs, recommending changes for operational efficiency and safety.
