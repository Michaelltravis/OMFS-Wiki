## Reuse guidance

Universal: the pattern is a capital coordinator whose entire project list is the client's own work and in-state work. Where that is available it should be assembled deliberately — list the client's projects by their actual project names, because an evaluator recognizes them and the recognition does the persuading. The second half of the benefit paragraph carries two further local proofs: a named internship program the candidate led engagement with, and a named local subcontractor on this very contract, which ties an individual resume to the team's community and small-business commitments.

The stated knowledge of "the client's standards and process" is the operative claim — not general design competence, but familiarity with how this owner reviews, approves and builds. Carry that phrasing on any recompete or displacement bid where team members have worked for the client as consultants.

Pursuit-specific: everything on the project list, the state permitting program named (Wisconsin Pollutant Discharge Elimination System), the PE registration state, and the internship and subcontractor names. Where no prior client work exists, this card should be replaced rather than softened — a coordinator without local knowledge should lead with capital delivery depth instead.
