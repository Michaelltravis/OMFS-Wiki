### Relevant Experience

Account Manager/Water and Sewer Utility Executive Consultant | Rockdale County Department of Water Resources | Conyers, GA. Responsible for supporting to the overall organization, planning, direction, and management of the activities of the Rockdale County Department of Water Resources; providing support in the effort to provide for an adequate, safe, and efficient water supply and wastewater service for Rockdale County; collaborating with the County leadership in formulating and implementing departmental policies and long-range plans, programs, and capital improvement plans. Managed the update, development and implementation of the Rockdale County’s 2040 Water and Sewer Master Plan in an effort to strategically plan for the County’s water and sewer needs for the future. Details included growth estimating, capital improvements forecasting, and financial planning.

Account Manager | Rockdale County Stormwater Department (RCSD) | Conyers, GA. Responsible for supporting the financial vitality and service delivery for CERM’s staff augmentation support of engineering personnel to the component of the RCSD CIP. The CIP short-range plan consisting of a collection of projects to improve the County’s ability to collect and convey stormwater as well as design and install best management practices to protect and/or improve water quality.

Project Principal | Water Meter Upgrade & Installation, DeKalb County Department of Watershed Management | DeKalb County, GA. Responsible for the contract management, resource planning, and overall program progress reporting to ensure project delivery of DeKalb County’s water meter upgrade and installation program. The program consisted of sight pre/post inspections, site improvement coordination, metering infrastructure replacement and verification, and service renewals.

Account Manager | Consent Decree Program, DeKalb County Department of Watershed Management | DeKalb County, GA. Responsible for the financial vitality and service delivery for CERM’s component of the DeKalb County Consent Decree Program (DCCDP). The DCCDP is a systematic collection of projects to improve the County’s ability to collect, convey, and treat its wastewater. CERM managed a number of collections/conveyance projects within this program to satisfy the consent agreement between DeKalb County, The Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), and the Georgia Environmental Protection Division (GaEPD).

Technical Director/Task Coordinator | Water and Sewer Master Plan, Mobile Area Water and Sewer System (MAWSS) | Mobile, AL. CERM was tasked with assisting Jacobs with the preparation of a comprehensive risk-based water and wastewater system master plan. This effort resulted in the first time MAWSS has developed a comprehensive plan versus their prior approach of a fragmented plan focused on a particular area of their system. The master plan provided the road map for implementation of capital and operational improvements for the next 10 years and beyond. Specific responsibilities include providing technical expertise and oversight for system growth (i.e., population projection, water demand, and sewer capacity) projection planning for the MAWSS Water and Sewer System Master Plan, which include historical trend analysis, current water and wastewater treatment operations analysis, growth estimating, capital improvements forecasting, and financial planning.

Project Principal | Municipal Separate Storm Sewer System (MS4) and Stormwater Management Plan Compliance Program, City of Lithonia | Lithonia, GA. Responsible for successful resource planning and project delivery for the City of Lithonia’s MS4 Management Plan and Compliance Program. CERM’s effort included conducting the City’s MS4 Inspection and Inventory Updates, developing the City’s maintenance and repair plan, and producing the City’s annual report.

Deputy Commissioner Office of Linear Infrastructure Operations | City of Atlanta Department of Watershed Management | Atlanta, GA. Responsible for providing executive level technical and operational department direction for the Office of Linear Infrastructure Operations, an operation responsible for the repair and maintenance of City of Atlanta’s 2,700+ miles of water lines, 2,200+ miles of sewer lines 1,100+ miles of storm lines 24,000+ hydrants, 47,000+ manholes, 35,000+ catch basins, and 62,000+ valves. Responsible for managing a $45 million operating budget as well as implementing operating policies and procedures, planning and managing budgets, directing operations, developing leadership and technical talent, developing and implementing capital projects, and monitoring and measuring performance of the operations of the Office to ensure efficiency and effectiveness.

Watershed Director – Distribution Systems | City of Atlanta Department of Watershed Management | Atlanta, GA. Responsible for managing a $15 million operating budget as well as providing direct management responsibility for the day-to-day operations of the water distribution system (2,700+ miles of water lines, 24,000+ hydrants, and 62,000+ valves) for the City of Atlanta.

Deputy Director of Operations and Engineering | Rockdale County Department of Water Resources | Conyers, GA. Managed the day to day operations of the engineering, water distribution and collections O&M program, treatment works (which to include a 22.1-mgd water treatment plant, five wastewater treatment facilities) and the County’s Water and Sewer Capital Improvement Program.
