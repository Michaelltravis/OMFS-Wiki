# Resume — Brooke Winters, SPHR (Human Resources)

**HR – Brooke Winters, SPHR**

**Education and Licenses**

- MS, Human Resources and Business Management
- BS, Anthropology
- Sr. Professional in Human Resources (SPHR)

**Experience Summary**

- HR business partner supporting O&M and contracts for Jacobs.
- 21 years of experience, including payroll, benefits, HRIS, employee relations, full cycle recruiting, training and development, and onboarding.

**Select Projects**

- HR Business Partner, West Basin Municipal Water District O&M for Recycled Water Treatment Facilities, Carson, CA
- HR Business Partner, Lincoln-SMD1 Wastewater Treatment and Reclamation Facilities O&M, Lincoln County, CA
- HR Business Partner, Town of Southbridge Wastewater Facilities O&M, Southbridge, MA
- Associate Business Manager/HR Delivery Partner, Greater Prudhoe Bay Producers O&M, Prudhoe Bay, AK

**Benefit to MMSD:**

Brooke will be dedicated to this contract and begin by supporting a seamless transition for MMSD and employees. She'll focus on fully staffing facilities with the best employees available. As a key link between onsite managers and our corporate recruiting resources, she'll help widen the candidate pool and fast-track screening, offers, and onboarding.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the benefit paragraph is the reusable unit and does three things in order. It commits the person to this contract rather than sharing her across a region. It names the transition as her first job, and names both parties she serves during it — the client and the employees — which matters when incumbent staff are being offered employment and are reading the proposal. Then it explains the mechanism by which a dedicated HR partner produces a staffing result: she is the link between site managers and corporate recruiting, and the consequence is a wider candidate pool and faster screening, offers and onboarding. Most HR cards stop at "supports recruiting"; this one states the throughput improvement, which is what a client short of operators actually wants.

The full-cycle experience list (payroll, benefits, HRIS, employee relations, recruiting, training and development, onboarding) is worth keeping because it shows the role covers everything a transferring employee will ask about in the first week.

Pursuit-specific: the supported-contract list should be swapped for the most comparable facilities by size and workforce type; the named clients are other utilities cited as evidence and stay verbatim. Where the HR partner is regional rather than dedicated, say so plainly and state the coverage model instead of borrowing the dedication claim.
