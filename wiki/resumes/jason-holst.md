## Summary
Jason is a plant operator with eight years of experience operating and maintaining dynamic water and wastewater treatment systems. He is skilled in process control and chemical handling and has served as a laboratory operator.

## Relevant experience

**Operator | Woodland Davis Regional Water Treatment Facility (RWTF), Woodland Davis Clean Water Agency | Woodland, CA.** Jason served as an operator for Woodland's three RWTF, which includes a river intake and pump station, 36-inch 5.1-mile raw water pipeline, a 30-MGD WTP, finished water pump station, and a 36-inch, 7.8 mile distribution pipeline. The greenfield treatment plant uses conventional, high-rate ballasted flocculation, ozone, high-rate BAC filtration, and granular activated carbon (GAC) adsorption. The treated water is conditioned to blend with groundwater sources without causing corrosion problems.

**Operator | Twin Oaks Valley WTP, San Diego County Water Authority | San Marcos, CA.** Jason served as an operator for this 100-MGD submerged membrane filtration WTP. The plant's features include fine screening, membrane filtration, ozone disinfection, biologically active carbon contactors, chlorine addition, and two 7.5-MG clearwells.

**Water Treatment Plant Operator | Multiple Locations | CA.** Jason served as an operator for multiple facilities, including a 53-MGD desalination plant, a 3-MGD modular desalination plant, and a 6-MGD advanced water treatment plant. In his work at these facilities, he gained experience with media filtration, micro-filtration, diffused air filtration with centrifuge, reverse osmosis, UV treatment, and post-treatment. Jason operated pumps, valves, air compressors, and conducted daily inspections of tanks, pumps, and other related equipment. He conducted plant start-ups and shutdowns via SCADA systems and had remote monitoring responsibilities. Jason supervised chemical inventories and deliveries, and was responsible for daily regulatory laboratory testing and sampling.

**Laboratory Operator | Emerald Cloud Lab | Austin, TX.** Jason performed various laboratory tests and analyses, while generating troubleshooting tickets to document discrepencies [sic] during customer-ordered experiments.
