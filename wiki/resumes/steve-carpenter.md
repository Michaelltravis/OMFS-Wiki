# Steve Carpenter
## PROJECT DIRECTOR

### Why Steve?
- **Multi-Facility Operations Leader.** Steve has led regional O&M portfolios spanning 15+ facilities, including reuse facilities, WWTPs, WTPs, biosolids facilities, lift stations, and distribution systems, with oversight of more than 100 staff. He brings the structure and accountability needed to manage OCWUT's diverse treatment assets as one integrated, high-performing program, and the leadership needed to support site teams across the full system.
- **Independent Program Leadership Across All Facilities.** Steve will provide OCWUT with a single, experienced leader focused on system-wide performance rather than day-to-day operations. He'll connect performance across treatment plants, pump stations, and biosolids operations, mentor site leaders, support retention, and link local teams to regional resources, managing OCWUT's full system as one integrated, high-performing program.
- **Direct Oklahoma Operating Experience at Scale.** As Plant Manager II for Oklahoma City's 75-MGD Hefner WTP, Steve maintained approximately 96% base load capacity for two consecutive years with no significant service interruptions—operating in the same community and regulatory environment as OCWUT.
- **Trusted Advisor Focused on Compliance.** Steve will serve as a direct resource to OCWUT leadership, bringing an objective view of performance, risk, and compliance. His experience leading large municipal utilities under state and federal oversight will identify issues early, guide corrective actions, implement best practices, and provide continuous improvement so the program keeps advancing over time.
- **Bridging Operations, Capital, and Strategy.** Steve understands how today's decisions affect long-term system performance. He'll work alongside OCWUT to align operations with the capital program and evaluate approved innovations, so projects deliver expected outcomes without creating operational challenges.

### Summary
Steve will serve as Project Director, providing OCWUT with senior independent oversight, performance visibility, and technical guidance across the contract. Steve brings 29 years of water and wastewater utility experience, including leadership of multifacility O&M programs, startup and transition support, and direct Oklahoma operating experience. Steve will help OCWUT strengthen accountability; keep leadership informed of performance, risk, and compliance issues; and align day to day operations with long-term asset, capital, and program goals. His independent oversight and accountability across the contract will give OCWUT leadership clear visibility into performance, priorities, and areas requiring corrective action.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the Project Director framing — "a single, experienced leader focused on system-wide performance rather than day-to-day operations," an objective view of performance/risk/compliance for client leadership, and a bridge between operations and the capital program. That role definition is reusable verbatim on any multi-facility O&M pursuit where the client worries a contract operator will disappear behind the site staff.

Pursuit-specific: the Oklahoma proof (Lake Hefner, Duncan, Bixby) is what makes this resume land on a same-state pursuit and should be replaced with the equivalent local experience elsewhere. The 96% base load capacity claim carries its own stated time window (two consecutive years) — keep the wording and the window together.
