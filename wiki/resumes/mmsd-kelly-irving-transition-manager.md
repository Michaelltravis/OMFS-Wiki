# Resume — Kelly Irving (Transition Manager)

**Transition Manager – Kelly Irving**

**Education and Licenses**

- AAS, Hydrology & Water Pollution Control
- AA/AS, Liberal Arts & Sciences/Community Health
- Wastewater Class 1 Operator: GA; Class A Wastewater Treatment Certification: MI, MN; Type IV Land Application of Biosolids Certification: MN; Grade 4 Wastewater Treatment Certification: IA

**Experience Summary**

- Jacobs' Senior (Sr.) Director of Transitions and 40-year veteran of utility O&M transitions with insights to drive integration and performance culture.
- Broad O&M delivery expertise as lead for due diligence, legal/financial coordination, operational readiness, and on-the-ground mobilization.

**Select Projects**

- Transition Manager, JXN Water O&M, Jackson, MS
- Transition Manager, West Basin Municipal Water District O&M for Recycled Water Treatment Facilities, Carson, CA

**Benefit to MMSD:**

Kelly will lead a seamless mobilization that will preserve institutional knowledge, maintain compliance, and set the tone for a positive culture shift. Her focus on transition will allow Kevin to maintain focus on daily O&M from Day 1, while she implements Jacobs' proven systems and tools for success.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: this is the standard Jacobs transition-manager card and travels to any O&M transition pursuit. Two elements carry the weight — the four-part scope of transition work (due diligence, legal/financial coordination, operational readiness, on-the-ground mobilization), which reassures an evaluator that transition is a discipline and not an afterthought; and the division-of-labor sentence that keeps the project manager on daily operations from Day 1. The three transition outcomes named in the benefit paragraph (preserve institutional knowledge, maintain compliance, set the tone for a positive culture shift) map directly onto the anxieties of a client changing operators, and should be kept in that order.

Pursuit-specific: the operator certifications by state, and the named transition projects — JXN Water (Jackson, MS) and West Basin Municipal Water District (Carson, CA) are real transitions at other utilities used here as evidence and should be kept verbatim, but refreshed to the most recent and most comparable two or three. Swap the project manager's name in the benefit paragraph.
