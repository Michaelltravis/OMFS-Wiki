# Resume — Travis Leech, CSP (Security and Safety Manager)

**Security and Safety Manager – Travis Leech, CSP**

**Education and Licenses**

- BS, Occupational Safety and Health

**Experience Summary**

- National O&M Health and Safety Director overseeing approximately 300 sites across U.S. and Canada; supports a 4,000+-person O&M organization.
- Experienced Sr. Health, Safety, Environment (HSE) Manager across major employers including water/wastewater ($1.7B contracts; 1,100-person workforce).
- Expertise in behavior-based safety, hazard analysis, SMS, industrial hygiene, audits, training, incident investigation.

**Select Projects**

- Health and Safety Director, Jacobs O&M Services, United States, Canada, and Puerto Rico
- Sr. HSE Manager, Jacobs Government Technology Operations, United States
- HSE Manager, Jacobs Technology Group, Tullahoma, TN

**Benefit to MMSD:**

Travis will bring his security and health and safety expertise to MMSD facilities, driving our BeyondZero® safety culture. He'll bring industry expertise in site and cyber security to reduce risks and protect staff and critical systems.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: this is the corporate safety-leadership card and travels to any O&M pursuit that asks who owns safety above the site. The persuasive work is done by the two scale figures in the first bullet — approximately 300 sites and a 4,000+-person O&M organization — which convert a safety program from a promise into an operating system the client is buying into. The second bullet's pairing of contract value with workforce size ($1.7B contracts; 1,100-person workforce) is the reusable device for establishing that the candidate has carried safety at the scale of the pursuit. Keep the third bullet's discipline list intact; evaluators score against behavior-based safety, hazard analysis, industrial hygiene and incident investigation as named elements.

Pursuit-specific: the site count, workforce size and contract value are point-in-time figures and must be refreshed. The closing sentence attaches cyber security to this role because critical-systems protection was a live concern on a large regional district with SCADA-controlled conveyance; retarget or drop the cyber clause where the RFP does not ask for it. BeyondZero® is the Jacobs program name and carries the registered mark on first use.
