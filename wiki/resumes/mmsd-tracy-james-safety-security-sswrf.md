# Resume — Tracy James (Safety and Security, South Shore Water Reclamation Facility)

**Safety and Security, SSWRF – Tracy James**

**Education and Licenses**

- BS, Kinesiology and Health

**Experience Summary**

- 20 years of experience developing and implementing occupational safety, security management, and emergency response programs such as at JXN Water, where, in less than a year, Tracy implemented rigorous safety and compliance programs that helped restore operational stability without interrupting service.
- Drives continuous improvement via risk assessments, audits, and KPIs, assures OSHA compliance; supervises security and incident reporting systems.
- Proficient in strategic planning and hands-on field leadership.

**Select Projects**

- Health and Safety Manager, JXN Water, O&M Services for Water Treatment and Wastewater Treatment Facilities, Jackson, MS
- Health and Safety Manager, City of Baton Rouge, LA, O&M Services for Wastewater Treatment Facilities
- Health and Safety Manager, City of Pembroke Pines, FL, Utilities O&M for WTF and WWTF

**Benefit to MMSD:**

Tracy will establish a SSWRF-specific safety/security program featuring immediate communication, vigilant monitoring, and responses that comply with our contract.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the first bullet is the model for how an outcome belongs inside a resume rather than beside it — the assignment, the clock ("in less than a year"), what was implemented, and the consequence for the utility ("helped restore operational stability without interrupting service"). Keep all four parts; a resume bullet that states only the role loses the argument. The second bullet's mechanism list (risk assessments, audits, KPIs) is what makes the claim auditable, and the pairing of safety with security and incident reporting supervision is the reusable framing wherever the RFP treats site security as part of the safety scope.

Pursuit-specific: the facility named in the benefit paragraph, and the project list. JXN Water (Jackson, MS), City of Baton Rouge LA and City of Pembroke Pines FL are real assignments at other utilities cited as evidence and are kept verbatim; JXN Water in particular is the reference that carries the turnaround story and should be retained wherever the client's situation involves recovering stability or displacing an operator. Where the pursuit has a single treatment facility, drop the facility qualifier from the role title and write the program as site-specific rather than facility-specific.
