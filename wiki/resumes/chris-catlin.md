# Chris Catlin, PE
**Manager of Operations | T3/AWT5™**

## Why Chris?
- 30+ years of hands-on and management experience in a wide range of water treatment and water reuse processes
- Recently started up and commissioned the Soquel Creek Advanced Water Purification Plant in Santa Cruz, CA
- Operation and management experience with drinking water plants that utilize conventional surface water treatment, biologically activated carbon, membrane filtration, reverse osmosis, nanofiltration, ozonation, advanced oxidation, and air stripping/decarbonization

## Professional background
- Jacobs Experience: 13 years
- Total Experience: 30+ years

## Education
- MS, Civil and Environmental Engineering | University of Iowa
- BSE, Chemical Engineering | University of Iowa

## Certifications/Licenses
- Professional Engineer: IA (#P12163); MN (#25686)
- Water Treatment Operator T3
- AWT5™
- Class 4 Water Treatment Certification (MN, IA, AZ, NM)

## Memberships
- AWWA, Member and Contributor

## Summary
Chris has 30+ years of experience in water treatment and distribution, and has served as an operator, lead operator, system manager, design engineer, and operations consultant.

## Relevant experience

**Acting Manager: Staff Augmentation | Norwood WTP, North Miami Beach Water | North Miami Beach, FL.** Chris acted as plant manager for three years for 30-MGD reverse osmosis, nanofiltration, lime softening treatment plant. Plant also included air stripping for VOC removal and chloramination disinfection. Chris managed a staff of 25 operators, maintenance staff, and lab personnel.

**Acting Plant/Water Quality Manager | North WTP and Santan Vista WTP, City of Gilbert | Gilbert, AZ.** Chris provided management services on an interim basis (approximately one year) for a water utility serving a population of 220,000. System included two main treatment plants and three satellite plants. Treatment included ozonation, conventional surface water treatment, ballasted sand clarification, and arsenic removal.

**Startup Operator/Interim Manager | Soquel Creek Advanced Water Purification Facility, Soquel Creek Water District | Santa Cruz, CA.** Chris provided start up operations support and management for the 2-MGD water reuse facility utilizing ozonation, membrane filtration, reverse osmosis, advanced oxidation, and chloramination for disinfection. Chris trained operators, set up lab testing program, and initiated CMMS.

**Superintendent: Operations and Maintenance | Minneapolis WTP, Minneapolis Public Works Water Treatment and Distribution Services | Minneapolis, MN.** Chris managed a 120-MGD surface water treatment plant, including a staff of 140 comprising operations, maintenance, and water quality departments within the water utility. Plant included lime softening, membrane and conventional filtration, activated carbon adsorption, recarbonation, chloramination, and fluoridation. Chris optimized treatment and residuals handling and trained operators, supervisors and lab staff in state of the art operations. Chris also created a Renewal and Replacement program for sustainability.

**Treatment Operator | Seattle Cedar Water Plant, Seattle Public Utilities | Seattle, WA.** Chris operated the treatment plant during staffing shortage. Treatment included chlorination, ozonation, UV disinfection, and pH adjustment.

**Design Engineer and Project Manager | Howard R. Green Consulting Engineers | Saint Paul, MN.** Chris provided design engineering, engineering study and analysis, master planning, and construction oversight services for municipal clients. Work included a variety of design, construction oversight, and start up of surface water treatment plants, groundwater treatment plants, and membrane treatment plants. Plant flow rates ranged from 1- to 30-MGD.

**Startup and Commissioning Operator | Joint Water Purification Plant (JWPP), Arapahoe County Water and Wastewater Authority (ACWWA)/Cottonwood Water and Sanitation District | Denver, CO.** Chris served as start-up and commissioning operator for this plant utilizing reverse osmosis, microfiltration, advanced oxidation. Chris provided start up and commissioning services, operations, and operator training.

**Startup and Commissioning Operator | Catskill-Delaware Water Ultraviolet Disinfection Facility, NYC Department of Environmental Protection (NYCDEP) | New York City, NY.** Chris provided start up and commissioning services for this 2,200-MGD UV treatment plant. He operated the plant during start up and trained City staff on operations.
