**Select Projects**

- Labor Relations Manager, Jacobs, U.S.
- Employee Relations Advisor, HR Delivery Organization, Jacobs, U.S.

**Benefit to MMSD:**

Jeff will manage CBA negotiation and maintain open dialogue with union management to address concerns before they escalate. He'll make sure our O&M personnel foster positive union relationships and are comfortable managing labor issues.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: on any pursuit with an organized workforce, this is a required card, and the reason is narrow and specific — a client changing operators wants to know who will sit across the table from the union, and whether that person has done it before under exactly these conditions. The load-bearing phrase is "negotiated 8 CBAs as part of successful O&M contract transitions": not eight negotiations in general, but eight during transitions, which is the relevant test. Refresh the count rather than dropping it.

The benefit paragraph carries two reusable commitments. The first is escalation prevention — open dialogue with union leadership to address concerns before they escalate — which speaks to a client who fears a grievance-heavy first year. The second is more unusual and worth keeping: the corporate labor specialist's job includes making site O&M managers comfortable handling labor issues themselves, which promises capability transfer rather than dependence on a distant expert.

Pursuit-specific: the CBA count and the union context. Where the pursuit's workforce belongs to a specific international or local, name it in the accompanying staffing narrative and state whether the existing agreement will be assumed or renegotiated — this card supports that discussion but does not substitute for it.
