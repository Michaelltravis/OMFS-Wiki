# Resume — Kevin Dahl, CRL (Project Manager)

Brief resumes for the minimum key staff identified in Exhibit E and our "Above and Beyond" staff follow.

**Project Manager – Kevin Dahl, CRL**

**Education and Licenses**

- MS, Environmental Engineering
- BS, Civil Engineering
- Wastewater Operator Certification: WI (301069); Class IV Wastewater Treatment Operator: CT, RI
- NEWEA Class IV Wastewater Collection Systems Operator
- Certified Reliability Leader (CRL)

**Experience Summary**

- Led 1,000+ union/craft/salaried staff across multiple O&M contracts and 10+ major municipal utility projects, achieving permit compliance while aligning maintenance–operations–capital to reduce risk and total life-cycle cost.
- Jacobs' Vice President of Operations (East Region) responsible for multiple municipal O&M contracts, applying program management best practices, maintaining safety and compliance, planning, maintenance, and operations aligned with owner objectives.
- Builds trustworthy, skilled teams, mentors supervisors and operators, partners with unions, and develops future leaders. Has successfully transitioned nearly a dozen utility operations and maintenance programs.

**Select Projects**

- Transition and Regional Manager, City of Wilmington, DE, Wastewater Treatment Facility (WWTF) O&M
- Assistant Project Director, North Hudson Sewerage Authority Hoboken and West New York WWTFs, Hoboken, NJ
- Project Manager, City of West Haven, CT, WWTF

**Benefit to MMSD:**

With 26 years of water and wastewater O&M experience, Kevin will bring his transformational leadership to implement the contract and elevate people, processes, and assets. He'll unify planning, maintenance, and operations and strengthen the workforce. He'll also provide transparent, KPI-driven safety, compliance, and reliability aligned with MMSD's objectives and 2035 Vision. Kevin's authorized to make contract decisions, which will help prevent potential delays waiting for guidance from corporate executive-level staff.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the three-part structure of the experience summary — span of workforce led, corporate operations role with contract authority, and people/union development — is the reusable pattern for any named project manager on a large union-workforce O&M pursuit. The "Benefit to [client]" paragraph is the persuasive unit; it names years of experience, the three things the PM will elevate (people, processes, assets), the transparency device (KPI-driven), and the decision-authority claim that differentiates a resident PM from a corporately-supervised one.

Pursuit-specific: education, license numbers, state certifications and the project list. The Wisconsin operator certification number and the CT/RI Class IV licenses were current for this 2028 bid only. The final sentence must be re-pointed at the new client's own strategic plan or vision document.
