# Mayo Miller
**I&C Technician**

## Why Mayo?
- 12 years of experience in wastewater management, including project management, SCADA, instrumentation and controls, communication, and networking
- Well-versed in regulatory compliance, with expertise in capital improvement planning, maintenance, and management
- Excels in mentoring and managing personnel, which earned him the prestigious title of Wastewater Manager of the Year for the Northwest District from the Arkansas Waterworks & Water Environmental Association

## Professional background
- Jacobs Experience: 12 years
- Total Experience: 12 years

## Education
- MS, Physics | University of Arkansas
- BS, Physics and Mathematics | Westminster College

## Certifications/Licenses
- Arkansas Class III Wastewater Operator

## Summary
Mayo has over a decade of experience in wastewater management and is well-versed in regulatory compliance, capital improvement planning, maintenance, and management. He oversees regional support for water and wastewater sites' operational technology, including SCADA, controls, instrumentation, and more.

## Relevant experience

**Operational Technology Lead | Jacobs | South Region US.** Mayo leads a team in the South Region specializing in operational technology. His team supports a variety of SCADA applications such as Ignition, FactoryTalk, AVEVA (Wonderware), iFIX, VTScada, and more. They also specialize in instrumentation and control (Allen Bradley, Schneider, Siemens, etc.). Automation and control support ranges from safety, compliance, reliability, and efficiency to labor reduction and cost savings. His team plays a significant role in supporting networks, firewalls, servers, and more.

**Project Manager | CMMS Implementation, City of Berryville | Berryville, AR.** Mayo was responsible for budgeting and utilizing financial toolkits and supervised personnel to ensure adherence to contractual requirements and regulatory obligations. Mayo successfully implemented a CMMS, enhancing maintenance operations and tracking. This role also involved coordination with city mayors and officials, ensuring effective communication and collaboration. Additionally, Mayo was responsible for generating detailed reports for clients and providing key information and updates.

**Assistant Project Manager | Capital Improvement Plan, City of Fayetteville | Fayetteville, AR.** Mayo specialized in capital improvement planning and execution, skillfully leading projects for the City from inception to completion. Committed to workplace safety, Mayo implemented robust safety programs and enabled strict adherence to regulatory standards, including precise compliance reporting.

**SCADA Supervisor | I&C Implementation, City of Fayetteville | Fayetteville, AR.** Mayo installed, troubleshot, and repaired various instrumentation, such as level detectors, flow meters, and sensors. He was actively involved in the troubleshooting, modification, and implementation of PLCs to enhance operations control and data collection. Additionally, Mayo played a significant role in a large-scale network and SCADA upgrade, which included updating firewalls, servers, network switches, and human-machine interfaces, among other components.

## Reuse guidance
Best fit for pursuits emphasizing SCADA/OT modernization, multi-platform instrumentation and controls expertise, network/firewall/server support, or CMMS implementation. His "Wastewater Manager of the Year" award (Arkansas Waterworks & Water Environmental Association, Northwest District) is a usable proof point for management/mentorship win themes.
