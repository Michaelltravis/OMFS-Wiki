# Kelly Irving
## TRANSITION MANAGER

### Why Kelly?
- **Proven Transition Leader for Complex Utility Systems.** Kelly brings 30 years of wastewater operations experience and a strong record of leading mobilization and transitions of complex, multi-facility utility systems. She knows how to guide organizations through change while protecting service continuity and operational control.
- **Disciplined, Low-Risk Approach to Mobilization and Startup.** Kelly leads structured transition programs that align staffing, systems, regulatory readiness, reporting processes, and startup planning with demanding implementation schedules. She emphasizes clear milestones, accountability, and early risk identification to reduce startup risk.
- **Strong Day-One Readiness and Workforce Alignment Focus.** Kelly understands how to align staff during transition, clarify responsibilities, support onboarding and training, and establish the systems needed for immediate performance. She helps utilities begin service with stable teams and prepared processes.
- **Aligned with OCWUT's Transition Priorities.** OCWUT needs a Transition Manager who can coordinate due diligence, staffing readiness, required plans, startup activities, and stakeholder communication in a disciplined and transparent way. Kelly's background positions her to help OCWUT move into operations with confidence and low implementation risk.

### Summary
Kelly will serve as Transition Manager, leading a structured, low-risk mobilization that prepares OCWUT wastewater facilities, staff, systems, and support processes for successful day one operations. She brings 30 years of wastewater operations leadership and direct experience managing complex utility transitions, including in high-risk and multi-facility environments where continuity, compliance, and workforce alignment are critical. Kelly has led transition efforts that required disciplined planning, clear accountability, and coordination across operations, maintenance, regulatory, and client teams to maintain service continuity while establishing stable long-term operations. She will coordinate staffing, regulatory readiness, required plans, reporting systems, and startup activities ahead of the January 1, 2027 service start, helping OCWUT enter operations with confidence, clear accountability, and a strong operational foundation.

### Relevant Experience

**Senior Director of Transitions | Jacobs | North America.** Kelly leads mobilization and transition planning for water and wastewater O&M programs across Jacobs' national portfolio. She coordinates due diligence, staffing alignment, regulatory readiness, operational startup planning, and transition execution to support low-risk service commencement. Her role includes establishing transition milestones, accountability, readiness tracking, and reporting processes so staffing, systems, and operational controls are in place before operations begin. Kelly will apply this same disciplined approach to staffing readiness, required plans, startup coordination, and transition execution so OCWUT enters operations with clear accountability and low implementation risk.

**Transition Lead | JXN Water | Public Drinking Water and Wastewater Facilities O&M | Jackson, MS.** Kelly led transition efforts in a high-risk operating environment under emergency federal oversight, helping stabilize a large and complex water system during a change in service delivery. She coordinated workforce continuity, regulatory readiness, operational control, and transition activities associated with turnover from the incumbent provider. Kelly's work focused on maintaining compliance, preserving essential service, identifying transition risks early, and establishing a stable operating foundation from day one.

**Transition Support | West Basin Municipal Water District | West Basin Recycled Water Facilities | CA.** Kelly supported transition of a complex, multi-facility recycled water system serving municipal and industrial customers, including aligning staffing, asset readiness, compliance expectations, and operational coordination across treatment facilities to maintain uninterrupted service. She supported readiness, accountability, and continuity across a diverse utility portfolio with multiple stakeholders and operating interfaces.

**Regional Vice President/Project Leadership | North Central U.S.** Kelly led a portfolio of regional water and wastewater operations contracts across multiple states for an O&M firm. She was responsible for ongoing delivery and performance and startup of numerous projects spanning 29 years. She aligned staffing, performance expectations, management systems, and operational processes to support smooth mobilization, successful startup, and stable contract performance. This reflects her ability to lead large-scale transitions while building workforce alignment and operational consistency across multiple sites.

**General Manager/Transition Manager | Regional Wastewater System | Detroit, MI.** Kelly led the complex transition of this large-scale, 225-MGD wastewater system before assuming the role of General Manager. This included treatment facilities, interceptor networks, and wet-weather infrastructure serving 13 communities. She led operational performance, workforce management, and system reliability across complex assets with decades of deferred maintenance while coordinating staff and resources to maintain continuous service. This gives her a strong operating foundation for transition leadership, including understanding how to align people, processes, and system needs to move large utilities into stable, accountable operations.
