# Paul Shropshire
## OPERATIONS MANAGER - DEER CREEK

### Why Paul?
- **Deep wastewater operations leadership experience.** Paul brings 37 years of managerial experience in wastewater treatment and pumping, plus operational and mechanical experience in wastewater and industrial environments. He's held long-term responsibility for plant performance, staff leadership, budgets, and system reliability.
- **Strong fit to manage complex treatment, pumping, reuse, and biosolids operations.** He has managed wastewater treatment plants, more than 126 wastewater pump stations, remote treatment facilities, water reclamation plants, and biosolids facilities, aligning well with Deer Creek's treatment, solids, odor-sensitive, and reuse-related operational needs.
- **Proven manager of teams, budgets, and capital coordination.** Paul has overseen 24/7 operations, direct and indirect staff ranging from plant teams to large utility divisions, and budgets from individual plant contracts to major utility-wide operations. He has also supported capital planning, design coordination, and out-of-scope improvement work.
- **Experienced public-sector operator with strong stakeholder awareness.** His background includes communication with engineering teams, city departments, consultants, public information staff, and nearby communities, which is especially relevant to Deer Creek's high-visibility setting and odor-sensitive environment.

### Summary
Paul will serve as Operations Manager for the Deer Creek facility, helping lead safe, compliant, and dependable wastewater operations at one of OCWUT's most publicly visible and operationally sensitive sites. He brings decades of wastewater treatment and pumping leadership, with direct experience managing treatment plants, pump stations, reuse-related systems, biosolids facilities, operating budgets, emergency response, and multidisciplinary teams. His background includes oversight of wastewater treatment and reclamation facilities, beneficial reuse programs, biosolids management, capital planning coordination, and communication with stakeholders across utility, engineering, and public-facing functions. That experience positions him well to support Deer Creek's need for strong process oversight, disciplined staffing and maintenance coordination, odor-sensitive operations, and reliable service through current and future facility upgrades.

### Relevant Experience
**Senior Operations Manager | City of San Marcos | San Marcos WWTF O&M | San Marcos, TX.** Paul manages operations, maintenance, compliance, and regulatory responsibilities at the 9-MGD San Marcos Wastewater Treatment Facility (WWTF), with direct oversight of 11 full-time employees across three shifts supporting 24-hour operations. He prepared a $2.8M annual spending plan, managed $1.2M in out-of-scope filter rehabilitation work, supported capital improvement planning, and coordinated emergency flood response during two historic rain events. This experience aligns well with Deer Creek's need for consistent operational leadership, staffing oversight, capital coordination, and dependable service under changing conditions.

**Division Manager of Wastewater Treatment | City of Austin | Walnut Creek WWTF, South Austin Regional WWTF, and Hornsby Bend Biosolids Facility O&M | Austin, TX.** Paul managed O&M for the Walnut Creek WWTF and the South Austin Regional WWTF, both permitted at 75 MGD, along with the Hornsby Bend biosolids treatment and management facilities. These operations also supported beneficial water reuse for major customers and community uses. He had direct responsibility for a combined $27.4M budget and indirect oversight of 132 skilled licensed professionals. This role demonstrates leadership of large, complex wastewater, solids, and reuse operations that align strongly with OCWUT's treatment and residuals management needs.

**Division Manager, Lift Stations and Remote Treatment | City of Austin | Walnut Creek WWTF, South Austin Regional WWTF, and Hornsby Bend Biosolids Facility | Austin, TX.** Paul prepared, implemented, and oversaw operations and maintenance for more than 126 wastewater pump stations, five remote wastewater treatment plants, two water reclamation plants, and five air-phase odor and corrosion filtering sites. He oversaw workload, training, safety, equipment needs, and vehicle acquisitions for 23 employees; coordinated with engineering and planning teams on design specifications; and led development of emergency response plans for hazardous-material spills and wastewater overflows. He also supported improvements that reduced costs and strengthened contingency planning during mechanical and electrical failures. This experience is directly relevant to Deer Creek's need for disciplined operations management, odor-sensitive site awareness, and close coordination between operations, maintenance, and engineering.

**Millwright & Treatment Plant Mechanic | City of Austin | Austin Water Utility | Austin, TX.** Paul led multidisciplinary team members performing maintenance and support at a 50-MGD WWTF, 126 wastewater pump stations, nine remote WWTPs, and five vapor-phase odor and corrosion control units. He helped develop hazardous-material and emergency-response programs adopted utility-wide. This early hands-on maintenance background strengthens Paul's ability to lead operations with a practical understanding of equipment reliability, field response, and safe execution of critical work.
