# Jose "Joe" Ramos
## OPERATIONS MANAGER - CHISHOLM CREEK

### Why Joe?
- **30 years of wastewater operations and utility management experience.** Joe's treatment and utility experience includes 25 years of management and supervision across wastewater plants, lift stations, and municipal utility operations, well exceeding Schedule 10's 5-year requirement for Operations Manager.
- **Proven operations manager at facilities of similar size and complexity.** Joe has held direct operational responsibility for WWTPs and multi-system utilities, including a 112-MGD WWTP, a 320-MGD expansion, and full O&M contract delivery across water, wastewater, and lift station systems.
- **Direct Oklahoma experience and ODEQ familiarity.** Joe has worked on the ground at both Bixby and Duncan — including emergency compliance recovery — giving him firsthand knowledge of Oklahoma's regulatory environment and OCWUT's operational expectations.
- **Operator, trainer, and compliance leader in one.** He has trained operators, developed SOPs, managed permit compliance, and supported facility startups and transition, bringing the operational discipline and team leadership Chisholm Creek needs from day one.

**YEARS OF EXPERIENCE** Jacobs: 12 Total: 30

### Education
- HS Diploma; Los Fresnos, TX
- 574 Hours Water Training; TEEX and TWUA
- 536 Hours Wastewater Training; TEEX and TWUA

### Certifications/Licenses
- Class A OK WW Operator License
- Texas Class A Water Operator
- Texas Class A WW Treatment Operator

### Summary
Joe will serve as Operations Manager for the Chisholm Creek facility, helping lead safe, compliant, and reliable wastewater operations at a process-driven treatment plant that requires disciplined operating oversight, strong staffing coordination, and dependable compliance performance. He brings more than three decades of treatment and utility experience, including project management, operations supervision, plant startup support, operator training, lift station oversight, and regulatory compliance across municipal wastewater systems. He's held daily responsibility for wastewater plants and lift stations, operator supervision, process compliance, reporting, and contract delivery. That experience positions him to support OCWUT's goals for dependable day-to-day operations, strong operator leadership, disciplined maintenance coordination, and continuous service at Chisholm Creek.

### Relevant Experience
**Operations Manager | City of Houston | Northeast Water Purification Plant | Houston, TX.** Joe started work on this project during the commissioning phase and helped with equipment commissioning. He was responsible for the NPDES permit and made sure all permit parameters were met. Joe prepared and submitted discharge monitoring reports. He led the operations team once the plant was ready for process startup.

**Operations Manager/Consultant | JXN Water | Public Drinking Water and Wastewater Facilities O&M | Jackson, MS.** Joe observed operations to determine the source of treatment issues. He helped during the transition phase once an O&M contract was in place, including assisting facility staff. Joe mentored staff in operational duties to maintain plant compliance with state regulations. He trained staff on dosage calculations and chemical pump calibration drawdowns to make sure proper feed rates were being applied, preparation of stock solutions, and optimal dosage rates.

**Regional Support | City of Bixby | Bixby Water Reclamation Facility and Lift Stations O&M | Bixby, OK.** Joe provided short-term operational support for the 2.8-MGD Bixby Water Reclamation Facility and its 18 associated lift stations. He assisted with equipment commissioning and operational stabilization as the new SBR facility transitioned to full operations.

**Regional Support | City of Duncan | Wastewater Treatment and Reuse O&M | Duncan, OK.** Joe provided short-term support during an emergency situation to help plant staff bring operations back into compliance. The facility includes an activated sludge treatment process, 38 effluent reuse irrigation pivot sites, and an industrial pretreatment program, comprising a multicomponent system where operational stability and compliance performance directly affect the surrounding community.

**Operations Consultant | AlexRenew | WWTP | Alexandria, VA.** Joe helped operate this 112 MGD-WWTP and identify maintenance issues. He helped operate the plant for 6 months with minimal training and supervision. He identified several maintenance issues and helped staff with operational questions.

**Project Manager | City of Gallup | Gallup WWTP | Gallup, NM.** Joe was responsible for overall operations including contract delivery, safety, compliance, and daily oversight of WWTPs and lift stations, and maintained strong client relations. This demonstrates Joe's ability to lead wastewater operations in a municipal environment with responsibility for both treatment performance and service delivery.

**Project Manager | City of Donna Public Utilities WTP, WWTP, and Lift Stations | Donna, TX.** Joe led a team of O&M professionals and was responsible for project delivery, making sure all safety procedures were followed, and compliance with state and federal regulations. He oversaw the daily operations of the billing department, meter reading, the distribution and collection department, lift stations, and the water and wastewater treatment plants.
