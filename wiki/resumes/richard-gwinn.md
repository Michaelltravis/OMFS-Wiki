# Richard Gwinn
**Maintenance Technician**

## Why Richard?
- 30+ years of facility maintenance and management experience, including at water and wastewater facilities
- Expertise executing CMMS systems including Maximo, Infor EAM, and Maintenance Connection
- Excells [sic] at efficiently installing, repairing, and troubleshooting systems for water and wastewater facilities

## Professional background
- Jacobs Experience: 1 year
- Total Experience: 30+ years

## Education
- General Studies | California State University, Long Beach

## Skills
- Arc Flash, Confined Space, and Forklift Certified
- PLC/RTU troubleshooting
- AutoCAD

## Summary
Richard is a regional maintenance specialist and supervisor with over 30 years of hands-on facility management experience. His responsibilities range from condition assessments and predictive maintenance to scheduling and planning future projects. He has over 20 years of experience with CMMS.

## Relevant experience

**West/Southwest Regional Maintenance Specialist and Supervisor | West/Southwest Region, US.** In his role as Regional Maintenance Specialist and Supervisor, Richard schedules and plans project work for Jacobs' O&M projects, repairing and maintaining many water/wastewater treatment facility components. He performs condition assessments using Aces/Echo and performs predictive maintenance (PdM) using PdM equipment to assess longevity of equipment. Richard uses CMMS to track work completion for corrective and preventive maintenance and is proficient in adding and removing assets. He is also experienced in mechanical and electrical repair.

**Park Maintenance/Facility Manager | Riverside, CA.** Richard served as department manager for various ride control systems, water treatment systems, facility plumbing, and electrical systems to complete direct project contributions and daily tasks. He provided technical oversight and direction for troubleshooting, repair, and preventive maintenance of water treatment, ride control PLC/RTU, and park facilities. Richard oversaw the safety, reliability, availability, regulatory compliance, and quality aspects of the facility. He led and obtained support for programmed engineering source data (SCADA), repairs on critical components, HMI, PLC, RTU, sensors, valves, and actuators.

**Engineering Services Manager | Disney California Adventure East Team | Anaheim, CA.** Richard served as department manager for various attractions and maintenance craft for direct project contributions and daily tasks. He provided technical oversight and direction for troubleshooting, repair, and preventive maintenance and oversaw the safety, reliability, availability, regulatory compliance, and show quality aspects of attractions. Richard led and obtained support for program repairs requiring engineering source data and repairs on critical components ahead of planned work.

**Technical Specialist Task Force | Project Shanghai | Shanghai, China.** Richard led local Chinese team members in a repair support program requiring engineering source data and repairs on critical components ahead of planned work. He coordinated, scheduled, and directed supporting teams, field crews, contractors, vendors, and supplemental laborers on projects and daily tasks. Richard also led maintenance planning and scheduling actions in a safe and methodical manner that focused resources for maximum efficiency.

**Sound Mechanic | Disney California Adventure East Team | Anaheim, CA.** Richard trained team members in technical competence; read and evaluated detailed instructions, blueprints, flowcharts, schematics and engineering documents; and passed all compliance necessary for technical and safety training. He utilized PLC, control, and repair of circuits and motors through troubleshooting skills while working alone and as part of a team. In his role, Richard evaluated job scope, determined needed parts/materials, and coordinated with vendors. He demonstrated expertise using hand tools, meters, gauges and other associated test and measurement equipment, as well as the ability to measure current, voltage, and amperage. Richard operated under Hazard Analysis Critical Control Point (HACCP) guidelines and has a full understanding of the National Electric Code.
