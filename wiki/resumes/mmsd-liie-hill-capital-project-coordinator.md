**Education and Licenses**

**Experience Summary**

- 17 years' experience using real-time operational insight to inform capital project planning, design, permitting, construction, startup, and commissioning.
- Engineering Manager who has supported capital planning and delivery for Jacobs' wastewater and water O&M portfolio, including contracts valued at $692M total.
- Coordinates complex capital projects with ongoing O&M and advises on O&M during design. While overseeing a $150M capital improvement program at Wilmington WWTF, she used our digital twin and helped shorten construction duration by 40% and reduce costs by 20% for a secondary treatment upgrade like the planned SSWRF aeration upgrades.

**Select Projects**

- Engineering Manager, Philadelphia, PA, Water Department O&M Services
- Project Engineer, City of Farmington, NM, WTF Improvement Program

**Benefit to MMSD:**

Liie will support integration of capital planning, design, and construction with operations to avoid compliance issues and operations impacts. She'll bridge gaps between operations and engineering.

## Reuse guidance

Universal: the third experience bullet is the model for how a resume should deploy a technology claim. It does not say the firm has a digital twin; it names the program, the program value, the tool, and two outcomes with their percentages, then closes the loop by naming the client's own planned project the outcome would apply to. That final clause — "like the planned [client project]" — is the transferable move, and it is what turns a past-performance number into a forecast the evaluator can act on. Use it wherever the client has published a capital project comparable to one in the candidate's history.

The role itself is also a reusable idea on any pursuit where a large capital program runs concurrently with operations: a named coordinator whose job is to keep construction from causing compliance excursions, and to put operations input into design before it is too late to use.

Pursuit-specific: the 40% schedule and 20% cost figures belong to the Wilmington secondary treatment upgrade and must travel with that attribution; the $692M portfolio figure is corporate rather than personal and should be labeled as portfolio support. PE registrations and the operator license should be checked for reciprocity in the pursuit state.
