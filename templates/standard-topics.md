# Standard topics — what Jacobs includes whether or not the RFP asks

Derived from the four extracted proposals (Hull 2026, Santa Monica SWIP 2025, OCWUT 2026, Fulton County 2025): a topic is listed when it appears in at least three of the four. `work/new_content_plan.py` copies each family's rows into the matching section of a pursuit's `content-plan.md`, where the proposal manager ticks what to include. Edit this file freely — it is the house list, not a rule.

Format rules the scaffold depends on: `## <family-key>` starts a family; `### <group>` is a sub-heading carried into the plan; each table row is `| topic | preferred block | why it is always there |`. Block paths are relative to the wiki root; the scaffold warns when one does not exist.

Family keys: `cover-letter` · `exec-summary` · `qualifications` · `staffing` · `approach`. Compliance, safety, security and QA/QC sit inside `approach` because that is where Jacobs proposals carry them.
